A Florida Chapter report of the Forest Fire Lookout Association, headed 2015 and beginning 2016, says John Seifert moved to Florida and operated the DeLeon Springs tower, and then the Seville fire tower.[^1] The same report credits his early work with the first working list of Florida lookouts, which was obtained in 1991.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page for DeLeon Springs gives the county as Volusia County.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the DeLeon Springs site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Florida Chapter report on remaining fire lookouts, 2015 and beginning 2016, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/cr-fl-2015.pdf (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: DeLeon Springs - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/deleon-springs.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
