The Hinds Lookout Tower is in Hinds County, Mississippi. The National Historic Lookout Register gives its elevation as 464 feet (141 meters) and describes a 100-foot Aermotor with a straight staircase inside.[^1] OpenStreetMap lists the same spot as the Raymond Lookout Tower, and the register gives "Raymond" as a possible other name.[^2][^1]

The register does not say when the tower was built. It lists the build date as unknown and names the Mississippi Forestry Commission as administrator.[^1] We found no account of who staffed the tower or of any fire it watched.

The register records a site visit on July 9, 2022. Its note says the Forestry Commission still seems to be using the site.[^1] The tower was registered the next day, July 10, 2022, as US 1659, MS 24, with Jason Johns as nominator.[^1]

We found no public access information. Because the register points to ongoing agency use, ask the Forestry Commission before going near the site.

[^1]: Hinds Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ms/hinds-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Raymond Lookout Tower, OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/357997213 (accessed 2026-10-09).
