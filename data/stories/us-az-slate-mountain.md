Slate Mountain, in Coconino County near Flagstaff, once carried a fire lookout on its summit.[^3][^1] A lookout-history listing places it instead in Navaho County (the listing's spelling) under the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Coconino listing and the hiking accounts point to Flagstaff, so the Navaho entry looks like the outlier.[^4][^3][^1]

The tower was taken apart some time ago. Forest growth has taken over the old access road, leaving only a trail to the top.[^2][^1] That trail is 2.2 miles long and follows an old jeep road up an 853-foot climb from a trailhead at 7,362 feet.[^2]

One hiker who searched the top for the lookout found no evidence of it, though the writer conceded the search may have been inadequate.[^1] Few large trees stand on the summit, which opens onto broad views across northern Arizona.[^2]

To reach the trailhead, drive US 180 northwest out of Flagstaff for about 26 to 27 miles, then turn onto Forest Road 191 and continue about two miles to the signed trailhead.[^2][^1] The sources disagree on the road. One says a low-clearance vehicle can manage it in all but the worst conditions, while another, who found it rocky, recommends a high-clearance vehicle.[^2][^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a ground lookout that is gone.[^3]
