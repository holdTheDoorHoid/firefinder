Pettibone Ridge is in Idaho County, Idaho.[^1][^3] It sits about nine miles southeast of the Moose Creek Ranger Station, in the Nez Perce National Forest.[^2][^3] Some sources also call it Indian Ridge (Pettibone).[^5]

Sources disagree on the build year. A Moose Creek ranger district inventory, which is not dated, gives 1934 for a tower 30 feet tall with an L-4 cab.[^5] Later lists, which draw on Ray Kresek's book, date the tower to about 1939.[^2][^3][^4][^5]

Until 1957 the Bitterroot National Forest oversaw the site; that year it passed to the Nez Perce National Forest.[^5]

The lookout was destroyed in 1950.[^2][^3][^4][^5]

Idaho Fire Lookouts describes the site as hike-in and lists the lookout as destroyed.[^4] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains on the ridge today.

Because it is a hike-in site on national forest land, it appears to be open to the public. We read nothing that restricts access.[^4][^3]
