Shoshone Peak had a short life as a lookout. Its tower was a 40-foot pole structure with an L-4 cab, built in 1931 in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest about 25 miles north of Kellogg, in Shoshone County.[^1][^2] The two pages that describe it disagree on the peak's elevation: one gives 5,425 feet and the other 5,238 feet.[^1][^2]

That same year a wildfire burned the tower. firelookout.com names the fire as the McPherson Fire, and the tower was never replaced.[^1][^2] A University of Idaho photo from the Civilian Conservation Corps collection shows a replanting crew on slopes above the Coeur d'Alene River, land the 1931 McPherson fire had burned.[^3]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page is a July 2023 post. Its writer says little is left to see beyond concrete footings, which the post links to the tower's very short life.[^2] The page marks the site as hike-in but gives no trail details. Neither page says whether visitors are welcome or whether anything beyond the footings remains.[^1][^2]
