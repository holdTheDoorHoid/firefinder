The provincial tower layer lists a Pinetorch Fire Tower, and its comments place the point on Ishpatina Ridge, which the record calls the highest known point in Ontario.[^1] The layer records the tower's construction year as 1935.[^1]

A volunteer list of Ontario's still-standing towers names an Acadia Tower at Pinetorch Lake, Temagami, and describes it as an MNR repeater tower.[^2] A Canadian lookout directory also files Pinetorch Lake, Temagami, under the name Acadia.[^3] The same list gives an Ellis Tower on Ishpatina Ridge, so these sources do not show whether the ridge point and the Pinetorch Lake structure are the same tower.[^2][^1]

The list says the Ontario MNR decided, as of 2013, to take down many of these towers, and that the ministry no longer maintains them and asks people not to climb them.[^2] No source found describes public access to the Pinetorch Lake structure today.
