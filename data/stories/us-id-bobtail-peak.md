Bobtail Peak Lookout was a 30-foot pole L-4 tower.[^1][^2] It stood on Bobtail Peak in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, about 17 miles north of Wallace, Idaho, at an elevation of 4,128 feet.[^1][^2]

The tower went up in 1934.[^1][^2][^4] The only staff member the Idaho Fire Lookouts page names is Charles Huelleman, in 1934.[^2] The page cites a Wallace Press-Times item dated June 7, 1934.[^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1955.[^1][^2][^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Idaho lookouts now marks the site as gone.[^3]

A 2022 post by Idaho Fire Lookouts describes the site as completely overgrown.[^2] Locating even the cement footings took the visitors a while, and they also found an old whiskey bottle that they judged to be from the tower's era.[^2] The post lists the site as drive-up.[^2] No source says whether the land is open to visitors today, and none describes anything else that remains.[^2][^3]
