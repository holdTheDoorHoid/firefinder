Burnt Granite is a former lookout site in Clackamas County, in the Mount Hood National Forest.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register dates a Forest Service cabin here to about 1929 and says it was no longer standing by 1950.[^2] The firelookout.com listing gives the same two dates in fewer words.[^1]

The register puts the site at 5,057 feet, or 1,541 meters.[^2] It also has a panoramic photograph of the lookout labeled 6-21-34, which we read as June 21, 1934.[^2] Ron Kemnow's listing dates the panorama photos to June 29, 1934, so the two sources disagree on the day.[^3]

Kemnow's listing marks the site as removed, with no date.[^3]

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go to it. The Oregon Hikers Forum thread from June 2026 is a hikers' discussion board, not an official source.[^4] One reply says the Hoodland Trails and Orogenesis Collectives put heavy effort into getting the trail open again, most of it in the past two years.[^4] The same thread says the trail ends at FR 4651, a forest road, and that old maps show it running down to the riverbank, though that stretch has not been rebuilt.[^4] A poster says the trail has disappeared from the Forest Service's digital inventory, and that further work on it would not be approved.[^4] Another poster reports no remnants of the trail along the ridge or up to it, other than possibly a few blazes.[^4]
