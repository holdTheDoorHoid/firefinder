Oak Flat sat in Douglas County, Oregon, within the Umpqua National Forest.[^2][^3]

The tower went up in 1933 at the westernmost point, looking down on the mouth of Copeland Creek.[^1] The structure was a 10-foot pole L-5 tower, topped by a 10 by 10 Aladdin lookout house.[^1][^3]

Panorama photos were taken there on August 21, 1936.[^3] A Roseburg News-Review item from August 1938 says Maurice Reid of Idleyld Park had spent the past few weeks at the station before returning Tuesday.[^3]

The register says the tower was removed in 1964.[^1] The Fire Lookout website says it was destroyed that same year.[^2] The two accounts differ on how it ended, but both put its end in 1964.[^1][^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register listed the site on March 26, 2011, as US 884, OR 251.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
