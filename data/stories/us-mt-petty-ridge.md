Petty Ridge was a US Forest Service lookout site in Missoula County.[^2][^3]

The site was set up in the 1920s, when a D-6 cupola cabin stood on a log crib.[^1][^2] In 1933 an improved structure went up there, a 50-foot pole L-4 tower.[^1][^2]

That 1933 structure was destroyed in 1963.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^3]

A 2019 summit report titled Petty Mountain uses the keyword Petty Ridge Lookout, and it says the 1933 improved structure was destroyed in 1963.[^2] The report describes a concrete base, some ground wire, glass and rotting wood as all that is visibly left.[^2] It also notes a hitching rail for horses that might have belonged to the old facility.[^2]

The report describes two routes to the summit, both starting from trailheads on Petty Creek Road.[^2] The Petty Pasture route is about 10.25 miles round trip, and the Printer Creek route is about 13 miles.[^2] Its directions use Forest Service roads and trails.[^2]
