The Georgia list of lookout towers numbers the Fort Valley entry GA104 and places it in Peach County, at 32.56094 north and 83.85040 west.[^1] The list gives its elevation as 493 with no unit stated; read as feet, that is about 150 metres.[^1] The entry also carries the tag gfc-336.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register's Georgia page counts 29 registered lookouts, and Fort Valley is not one of them.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Georgia, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/GA (accessed 2026-10-21).
