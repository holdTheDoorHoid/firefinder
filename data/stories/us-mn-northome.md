Northome's first lookout went up in 1912.[^1][^2] It was an 80-foot ladder tower with an enclosed ladder, and the Minnesota Forest Service owned it.[^1][^2] A second tower, tower #81, followed in 1930.[^1][^2] It stood 94 feet tall, an Aermotor ladder design.[^1][^2]

A 1934 survey describes a 100-foot state tower two miles southwest of Northome, on U.S. Highway 71.[^1][^2] The survey and the tower #81 entry give different heights, and the sources do not show whether they describe the same structure.[^1][^2]

The Koochiching list marks tower #81 as removed, with no date given.[^1] Its spot, a little west of town on Highway 71, is now a state ARMER site.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Northome tower as gone, at 47.86591, -94.31458.[^3] No source describes anything else at the site, and none says whether the public can visit it.
