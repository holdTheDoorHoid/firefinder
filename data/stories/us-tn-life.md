The Life fire tower is in Henderson County, Tennessee, and shares its name with Life, an unincorporated community in that county.[^1][^2] The community had a post office from 1881 until 1934.[^1]

Tennessee Landforms gives the tower an elevation of 620 feet and the number 8-13. Those are district and tower numbers from a 1976 list of state forest towers.[^2]

The Tennessee Division of Forestry (TDF) built 208 fire lookout towers, with construction running from about 1930 to about 1970.[^3] The sources do not say when this tower went up, what design it used, or how tall it is.[^2][^3]

None of the sources describe staffing or fires at this tower.[^2][^3]

The sources disagree about whether the tower still stands. Landforms lists it as standing.[^2] The 2014 National Register document lists the Tennessee towers that were still standing on March 26, 2014. Its only Henderson County entry is Natchez Trace, and Life is not on the list.[^3] No newer record turned up, so the tower's current condition is unknown. The Tennessee index of the National Historic Lookout Register, which counts 76 registered lookouts in the state, does not name it.[^4]

The sources say nothing about whether the tower can be climbed. The 2014 document says in general terms that surviving towers are often in poor repair and that visitors are often cautioned about the risks of going up them.[^3] Do not climb this tower. Check with the Tennessee Division of Forestry before going to see it.
