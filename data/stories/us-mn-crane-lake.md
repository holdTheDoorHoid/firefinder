The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Crane Lake tower in Saint Louis County, Minnesota, at 48.24983 north and 92.53585 west, and marks it as gone.[^1]

TopoZone shows a Crane Lake Lookout Tower on the Kabustasa Lake USGS topographic map, and includes it in the St. Louis County towers listing.[^2] It gives the tower's coordinates as 48.2496278 north and 92.5357156 west, nearly the same point as the Association's.[^1][^2] The map gives the approximate elevation as 1,365 feet, or 416 meters.[^2] The same page lists nearby towers, among them the Elephant Lake and Kabetogama lookouts.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.

[^1]: Minnesota Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Crane Lake Lookout Tower Topo Map MN, St. Louis County (Kabustasa Lake Area), TopoZone, https://www.topozone.com/minnesota/st-louis-mn/tower/crane-lake-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
