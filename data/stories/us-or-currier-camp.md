Currier Camp was a Forest Service camp on the Fremont National Forest, in Lake County, Oregon. The Oregon lookouts directory gives its location as 33S-15E-14.[^1]

The camp shows up in a 1917 plan. A Lake County newspaper, quoted in the forest's history, described a telephone line that was to run from the supervisor's office in Lakeview to Bald Mountain, passing Ingram Station, Currier Camp, Silver Lake and Oatman station.[^2]

In the 1922 season, the same history says, a trunk road was finished to Long Creek and north to Currier Camp.[^2]

A Silver Lake Leader item from September 4, 1924 reported that the Forest Service employed a lookout at Camp Currier that year.[^1]

The history also lists Currier Camp among the base camps that crews used.[^2]

Do not confuse the camp with Currier Guard Station, a Forest Service rental cabin that dates to 1933 and served as an outpost of the Paisley Ranger District.[^3] The sources read for this story do not link the two, so the cabin should not be assumed to stand on the camp's site.[^3]

The directory gives no current status or access details for the camp.[^1]
