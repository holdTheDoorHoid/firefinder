The Penney Farms fire tower was ordered in 1928 and reported erected by March 1929. A Jacksonville Journal item from June 1928, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, says the Black Creek Forest Protective Association had ordered equipment that included an 80-foot tower, to be raised at the center of its property in Clay County.[^1] A March 1929 Florida Times-Union piece, quoted there too, says the tower had been put up not far from the Penney Farms community center, on high ground more than 200 feet above sea level, beside the road west to Gainesville.[^1]

The 1929 piece says the tower was manned day and night by one or more men keeping watch. It adds that fire-fighters were immediately available nearby, and that the tower's attendants were on their way to fires at a moment's notice.[^1] The 1928 plans also called for hiring a state forest ranger for that year.[^1]

In January 1948 a Bradford County Telegraph item reported that a woman and her brother had moved the week before to the Penney Farms fire tower, where they worked.[^1]

No source I read describes the tower's site today, or says when the tower came down. No source I read says whether the site is open to visitors.[^1]

[^1]: Penney Farms - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/penney-farms.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
