Anchor Hill is a state-owned hill in Clearwater County, about ten miles north-northwest of Two Inlets, according to a 1938 survey description.[^1] The 1938 directions end at a fire tower and station on the hill, 0.15 mile up a side road from a signed turn.[^1]

A county compilation lists a 65-foot ladder tower on the hill for 1929, and dates a survey mark there to 1938.[^2][^3] The owner column lists the Minnesota Forest Service, which the county footnote says is now the state Department of Natural Resources.[^2]

The lists also give a tower for 1952, with a height of 80 feet, and call it the relocated Lone Lake tower from Aitkin County.[^2][^3] A 1964 survey note filed under that same entry calls the tower a four-legged steel structure with a small cabin at the top, about 100 feet tall, so the two heights disagree.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list marks Anchor Hill as gone, without a date.[^4]

No source describes the site now, including what remains on the hilltop or whether visitors can reach it.
