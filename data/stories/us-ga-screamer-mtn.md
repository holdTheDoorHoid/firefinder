The Georgia lookout list includes a tower on Screamer Mountain in Rabun County, placed at 34.8727, -83.37547.[^1] The list's elevation figure is 2966, while Wikipedia gives the mountain as 2,972 feet (906 m), so the two sources do not quite agree.[^1][^2] Wikipedia describes the mountain as near Clayton, Georgia, and says the whole mountain was bought in 1971 by Modern States Life Insurance Co. That is the only ownership fact we found.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Screamer Mountain, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screamer_Mountain (accessed 2026-10-21).
