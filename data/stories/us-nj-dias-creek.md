Dias Creek Station stands in Middle Township, Cape May County.[^2][^3] The registry calls it the southernmost tower in New Jersey.[^1]

The registry dates the tower to 1944, when a 60-foot Aermotor with a metal cab measuring 7 by 7 feet went up.[^1] A June 24, 1968 state bid notice for structural steel to extend the Dias Creek lookout tower places it on Route 47.[^3] The registry says the tower was raised to 100 feet in 1970.[^1] Wikipedia's state list gives the height as 100 feet, or 30 meters.[^2]

The registry's data box gives an elevation of 13 feet (4 meters), though its text says 15 feet above sea level and calls it the lowest lookout in the state.[^1] Wikipedia gives the same 13 feet (4.0 meters).[^2]

The registry says the tower has recently been put back into service, because the area is growing fast and needs quick fire detection.[^1] It does not give a date for that return. The registry recorded the site on March 15, 2004.[^1]

I did not find notable fires connected with this tower in the sources I read. Access rules are not described either, so check with the New Jersey Forest Fire Service before going.
