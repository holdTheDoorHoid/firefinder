Centerville is a lookout in Reynolds County, Missouri, that the Central US Lookouts site files under the Mark Twain National Forest.[^1] The site calls it a tree crow's nest lookout.[^1] Its platform sits in a tall tree rather than on a tower.[^1]

The survey marker is on the east end of a high, wooded ridge, at the top of a prominent hill about three miles north of Centerville.[^1] The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the marker in 1938.[^1] The description places it 70 feet southwest of the Forest Service platform, and about half a mile northeast of a Civilian Conservation Corps camp on the Centerville-Bixby road.[^1] Because that 1938 description already mentions the platform, the platform was in place by then.[^1]

No source I could read describes what remains of the platform today, or whether the public can visit the site.[^1]
