Pilot Mountain, a peak near Brevard in Transylvania County, North Carolina, was judged a good site for a fire lookout in 1922.[^2][^3] A November 1922 newspaper item calls the peak Pilot Knob. It says the peak's contour made a steel tower necessary and describes a planned cabin with windows all around the structure.[^2]

Five fire lookouts once stood in Transylvania County, and one of them was on Pilot Mountain.[^1] The county library says the tower on Pilot Mountain has since been removed.[^1]

Today the peak lies within Pisgah National Forest, and the Art Loeb Trail crosses it.[^3] A user-written summit guide gives the shortest approach as about two miles one way from Gloucester Gap.[^3] It describes the top as a small clearing with views that include Looking Glass.[^3] The same guide says the tower has been removed, though many maps still label it, and it does not mention any remains.[^3]
