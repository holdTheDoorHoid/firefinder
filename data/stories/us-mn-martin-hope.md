An earlier lookout on this site was a wooden tower that its namesake built, according to a county list of Minnesota Forest Service towers.[^1] In 1911 a 50-foot steel ladder tower replaced it.[^1] The same list says that tower was among the first towers the new Minnesota Forest Service put up.[^1]

The sources disagree about where the tower stood. The Cass County list puts it in Cass County, and a statewide list of Minnesota towers does too.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association puts it in Crow Wing County and lists it as a platform.[^2]

The Association's list marks the site as gone.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the Martin Hope site today or whether the public can reach it.
