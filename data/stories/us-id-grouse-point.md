Grouse Point is a former lookout site in the Clearwater National Forest in Idaho.[^1] Firelookout.com lists an L-4 tower there for 1934, and says it burned that same year.[^1] The Forest Service's list of Clearwater lookouts places a Grouse Point L-4 tower six miles south of the Powell Ranger Station, and says it burned in 1934.[^2]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site says the Pete King Wildfire burned this tower.[^3] The Forest Service list also has a second Grouse Point, three miles north of the Lochsa Ranger Station.[^2][^4] Firelookout.com dates that one to 1933, describes it as a 30-foot pole L-4 tower, and says the 1934 Pete King Fire burned it.[^4] A second Idaho Fire Lookouts page says it burned in the Pete King Fire too.[^5] Because both towers carry the Grouse Point name, check which one a source describes before you rely on it.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks this Grouse Point as gone.[^6]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page lists the site as a hike-in and says it burned, but it does not describe what remains.[^3] We found no source that describes the site today, so we cannot say whether any footings or other traces survive, or whether the public may visit.
