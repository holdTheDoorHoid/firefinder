The tower began its working life as the Ila Tower in Madison County.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register says it was originally put up in 1952 for the Georgia Forestry Commission, and that a crew of oil well derrick builders from Texas assembled it.[^1][^2] The Register describes it as an 80-foot steel Aermotor LS-40 tower with a metal cab measuring 7 by 7 feet.[^1]

In 1973 the tower was relocated to Walton County, and an outside catwalk was added during the move.[^1][^3]

The Register places it on the Fairwoods Stewardship and Educational Forest, which belongs to Don and Edith Shedd.[^1] It describes the tower as privately owned.[^1] It was registered on September 9, 1997, and the Register notes that it is the first Georgia fire tower it has listed.[^1]

The sources I could read do not say who staffed the tower in Walton County or whether it ever watched a particular fire. The Register lists no fire history for it.[^1]

The sources do not describe the tower's condition now. The Register names no visiting arrangement for this privately owned site, so the owners control access.[^1] Anyone curious should ask them first, and should not climb the tower without their clear permission.
