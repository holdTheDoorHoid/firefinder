The Pine Top Lookout Tower stands at the MacClenny Forestry Station, on U.S. 90 west of Glen St. Mary in Baker County.[^1][^2]

A January 1957 newspaper report, reprinted by the Forest Lookouts site, describes the new county fire control unit's headquarters as an office building, a lookout tower, a garage and a warehouse.[^2] Baker County voters had given their approval in a referendum. The state then built the buildings and furnished the equipment, and the county was to share part of the operating cost through a special two-mill tax levy.[^2]

The 1957 report does not say when the tower went up, or whether it was among the buildings the state put up.[^2] The register gives the builder only as a guess. Its nominator wrote, "I believe it was a WPA project," and we found no source that confirms it.[^1] The same entry says the tower stood on a turpentine camp that has since closed.[^1]

The register added the tower in June 2026, on a nomination by William Wyatt Williams.[^1] It lists the Florida Forest Service as administrator and does not describe public access.[^1] Treat the grounds as restricted, and ask before you visit.
