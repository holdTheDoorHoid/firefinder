Green Butte rises about 11 miles southeast of Sweet Home in Linn County, to an elevation of 3,512 feet.[^1] Oregon has several peaks with this name, including ones in Douglas and Lake counties, so this story covers only the Linn County butte.[^3]

The earliest record is from 1912.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page for the Linn County site notes that an alidade, a surveying sighting device, was established there that year.[^2] Firelookout.com lists a camp at the site in 1912 as well.[^1] A cabin went up in 1916, about 600 feet west of the summit.[^1]

Firelookout.com says the lookout was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page marks the site as removed, without giving a date.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list shows the butte as gone.[^3]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains on the butte today, or whether the summit is open to visitors. Check access with the managing agency before going.
