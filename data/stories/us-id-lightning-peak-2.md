A ground cabin was built on Lightning Peak in Valley County in 1933.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com calls it an L-4 cab, and the Forest Lookouts page describes a 14 x 14 ground cabin.[^1][^3] The peak sits in the Payette National Forest, 18 miles east of Yellow Pine, at 8,528 feet.[^1][^2]

A University of Idaho archive photograph captioned "Old Lightning Peak Lookout" is dated August 1957.[^4] The archive files it under the Payette National Forest and Valley County.[^4] The caption therefore places a lookout on the peak in 1957, but the archive entry does not say which cab it was.[^4]

The sources disagree on when it was destroyed. Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both give 1960.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page, which cites Kresek, gives 1963.[^3]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. The Idaho site tags the peak as hike-in.[^2] Whether the public can reach it now is not stated in any source, so check with the Payette National Forest before any trip.[^2]
