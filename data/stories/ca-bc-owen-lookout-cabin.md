Owen Hill lookout sits in the Regional District of Bulkley-Nechako, about 30 km from Houston by forest road.[^5][^2] Built in 1957, it was staffed from May to September for about 20 years.[^1] Dick Aitken and Bruce Campbell were the lookout men for most of that time.[^1] John Keefe, a forest ranger from 1948 to 1961, said the lookout men's communication link inside the Ministry of Forests was important.[^1] Nanika, a lookout built in 1959, could not get word to Houston, so it routed its traffic through Owen.[^1]

Owen was last manned in 1983.[^1] In 1989 it was considered a secondary lookout, for emergencies only.[^1] In 1990 a new lookout, Story lookout, was built on a shoulder of Morice Mountain, a site with a better view than Owen's.[^1] By 2000 Owen was in poor shape, and some clean-up was done because people were talking about turning it into a recreation site.[^1]

Owen Lookout was designated a recreation site in 2009.[^2] In 2012 the province chose it, with four other lookouts, to restore on the basis of location, accessibility, community use and historical significance.[^3] The Houston Hikers Society also took part.[^3] Its own page says it received a centennial fund grant from the Ministry of Forests to lead the restoration, while the government release calls the society a partner in the work.[^2][^3]

The Houston Hikers page, which is undated, lists a wide deck for tenting, a wood stove, tables and chairs, and an outhouse at the site.[^2] The page also describes the view over Morice Mountain and the Morice River valley as good.[^2] The trail is about 2 km long and rated moderate.[^4] The society asks visitors not to leave trash at the camping sites.[^2]
