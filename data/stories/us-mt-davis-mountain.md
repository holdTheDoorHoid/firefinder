Davis Mountain Lookout is in Lincoln County, Montana, on the Kootenai National Forest, about 25 miles east-northeast of Libby.[^1][^2]

The present tower is a 60-foot steel structure with a 7-by-7-foot cab and ladder access. It has an accompanying log cabin. Both were built about 1931.[^1][^2]

The Register does not say who staffed the lookout or which fires it watched. I found no staffing dates or fire reports for it.

Both the Register and firelookout.com say the tower and cabin have been abandoned for several years.[^1][^2] Neither gives the year the lookout was closed, or says whether it was ever moved or restored.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association still lists the tower as standing.[^3] That conflicts with the Register and firelookout.com, which both describe the tower and cabin as abandoned for several years, and the sources do not settle which is current.[^1][^2] The Register registered it on January 9, 2021, as US 1505, MT 83, on a nomination by Kyle Stetler, and names the US Forest Service as administrator.[^1] It gives the elevation as 6,058 feet, while the other page gives 6,053 feet.[^1][^2]

The sources do not say whether the public may visit or climb it. Because they describe the tower as abandoned, check with the Kootenai National Forest before any trip, and do not climb the tower.
