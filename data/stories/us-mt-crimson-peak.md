Crimson Peak, in Powell County, Montana, had a lookout about 11 miles northeast of Seeley Lake, at an elevation of 8,229 feet.[^1] Both lookout listings place it on the Flathead National Forest.[^1][^2]

The listing describes the cabin as a 10' wooden L-4 cab and dates it to the 1930s.[^1] It says the cabin was abandoned in 1956 and burned in 1959.[^1]

A USGS name entry lists both the Flathead and the Lolo national forests as related terms, without saying which one holds the peak.[^3] The forest assignment therefore rests on the two lookout listings.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as gone.[^4] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
