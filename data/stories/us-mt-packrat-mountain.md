Packrat Mountain is listed under Powell County, Montana, on the Forest Lookouts index of Montana sites.[^1] Its own page names the Flathead National Forest as the agency.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of standing Montana lookouts does not include Packrat Mountain.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the public may visit it.

[^1]: Packrat Mountain - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts, https://montanalookouts.weebly.com/packrat-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Montana Lookouts (Standing), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/mt-st/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
