The Stony Creek lookout stood on Potts Mountain in Giles County, near the Appalachian Trail.[^1] A map label began showing the name "Stony Creek Lookout Tower" on this ridge in 1965.[^1] Be careful with the name, though. Nearly everything the author found about Potts Mountain refers to a different lookout farther north, in Craig County, that is not on the Appalachian Trail.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Giles County tower as gone, and gives "Big Stony" as another name.[^2]

The site is a former lookout.[^1] The author could not locate the old Potts Mountain Trail up to it, which has been abandoned and overgrown.[^1] The page includes a photo of one of the footings.[^1] The page gives the detour from the Appalachian Trail as 0.1 mile.[^1] From Wind Rock, a scenic spot just off the trail, the tower site can be seen on the hillside.[^1]
