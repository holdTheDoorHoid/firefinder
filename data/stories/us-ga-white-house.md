White House is a fire tower site recorded in Randolph County. A Georgia tower list on tnlandforms.us places it at 31.81163, -84.78566, gives an elevation of 503 feet (153 m), and tags it "gfc-317".[^1] The name is the list's own. The list says names for towers numbered 73 and above are its creation, and this one is number 151.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register's Georgia page does not include this tower. That page says it has 29 registered Georgia lookouts.[^2]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Georgia | National Historic Lookout Register, NHLR, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ga/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
