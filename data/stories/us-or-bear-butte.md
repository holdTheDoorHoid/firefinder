Bear Butte, also called Baldy or Bear Flat, is in Klamath County, Oregon, about 18 miles west of Silver Lake.[^1][^4] The Klamath Forest Protective Association had a lookout built here.[^1] By about 1932 the site had a firefinder on the summit and a cabin below it.[^3][^4]

The NHLR says the 1949 house replaced an earlier structure that dates back to at least 1930.[^1] A 1953 Coast and Geodetic Survey recovery note describes a 9-by-9-foot house built on a group of large boulders at the summit.[^2] The 1949 lookout house was built for $177.83.[^2] Firelookout.com describes the cab as wooden, 9 by 9 feet, and dates it to 1949.[^4]

The lookout also shows up in fire reports. A 1937 newspaper item credits the Bear Flat lookout with spotting a fire and putting it out.[^2] In August 1947 the lookout on Bear Butte passed news of a fire that had burned about four acres on the Klamath Indian Reservation to the association.[^2]

The NHLR says the lookout was last staffed in 1966.[^1] A note from that year says it was not permanently manned that season.[^2] Firelookout.com says it was abandoned in 1966.[^4]

The butte sits on Weyerhaeuser Company land.[^1] In 1965 the company granted the association a permit for its existing installations.[^2]

Today the cabin is in poor shape.[^3] A June 2025 visit report says the cabin is worsening and only just holding up.[^3] The NHLR listed the site on June 22, 2022, with Brad Eells as nominator.[^1] Because the butte sits on company land and the cabin is only just standing, do not enter or climb it.[^1][^3] The land belongs to Weyerhaeuser, so ask the company before going to the site.[^1]
