The Foley Lookout Tower is listed under Taylor County, Florida, and bears the name of Foley, an unincorporated area in the same county.[^1][^2]

Jerry S. Foley started the place as a company town for a lumber firm, the Brooks-Scanlon Lumber Company.[^2] A Taylor County history site notes the Brooks-Scanlon Lumber Co. site there, and dates the community's post office from 29 June 1929 to 29 August 1964.[^3]

OpenStreetMap describes the tower as a lattice observation tower and lists the site at 82 feet (25 m) above sea level.[^4] Its edit note calls the structure a communication mast, a label that no other source here confirms.[^4]

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.
