Mastadon Mountain stands at 5,905 feet, about 10 miles northwest of Avery in the St. Joe National Forest.[^2] A camp was set up at the site in 1926, and a shake cupola cabin followed in 1929.[^1][^2][^3]

Two lookouts appear in the records. Ronald Frank was reported in 1967 by the Kellogg Evening News, and Loren Baker was listed in 1968.[^1]

In 1963 the cupola gave way to an R-6 flat cab on a concrete base.[^2][^3] One county list calls the concrete a first floor,[^3] and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the structure as a two-story cab.[^5]

The Idaho lookout site and firelookout.com both say the cab was removed in 1978.[^1][^2] A Shoshone County list instead gives the 1980s and says the lookout was destroyed.[^3] The sources disagree on the date.

A June 2021 hiking guide says the summit is bare apart from scattered remains of an old lookout tower.[^4] That guide spells the peak Mastodon, not Mastadon.[^4] Its route is a loop that starts at the Foolhen trailhead, reached by following FR-225 along Slate Creek.[^4] No source we found describes the site in more detail.
