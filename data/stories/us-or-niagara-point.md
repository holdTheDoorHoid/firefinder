Niagara Point is in Yamhill County, inside the Siuslaw National Forest.[^4][^3] A 1940 Coast and Geodetic Survey station description places the survey station at the center of a U.S. Forest Service 30-foot wood lookout tower, about six miles southeast of Blaine, by the headwaters of Niagara Creek.[^3]

Sources disagree about when the tower went up. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register and firelookout.com both give 1937 and describe a 30-foot pole tower of the L-4 type.[^1][^2] The Oregon Lookouts page points to 1936 instead.[^3] It records panorama photographs from October 1936 and describes a crude wood tower that month.[^3] A December 1936 Forest newsletter item says the lookout house had recently been finished and would be skidded 13 miles and raised with a gin pole.[^3]

The register pages say the tower came down in 1948.[^1][^2] One calls it removed and the other destroyed.[^1][^2] The Oregon Lookouts page lists it as removed but gives no date.[^3] When the U.S. Geological Survey checked the station in 1954, it found the station destroyed, with no lookout on the top.[^3]

We found no source describing what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors. Do not expect a tower there to climb.
