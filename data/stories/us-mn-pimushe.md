The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower called Pimushe in Beltrami County, at 47.52708 north and 94.52680 west, and marks it gone.[^1]

The same name belongs to a lake.[^3] Pimushe Lake sits between Pennington and Blackduck, in Beltrami County, and a real-estate listing describes its shoreline as undeveloped.[^3] A boat-ramp listing for the lake gives the access type as boat ramp and places the access within the Chippewa National Forest.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the tower site, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1]
