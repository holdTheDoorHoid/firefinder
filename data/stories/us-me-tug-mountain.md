A Maine Forest Service panorama dated 1918 is centered on the Tug Mt. fire tower in Township 30 MD, so a tower stood on the mountain by that year.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone, at 44.88622 north and 67.83753 west in Washington County.[^2]

A trip report dated May 21, 2026 calls the summit the former fire tower site.[^3] The writer parked along Route 9 on the north side of the mountain, where there is shoulder room, followed a logging road, and then made a 0.2mi rough bushwhack to the top.[^3] The writer found nothing of the tower beyond some cables and an anchor pin.[^3] The report also describes a narrow view north from a ledge just north of the summit.[^3]

No source we read says whether the land is open to the public, and no source we read describes the site beyond that one trip report.[^3]

[^2]: Maine Fire Towers, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/me/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Tug Mountain Trail Conditions, New England Hiking Reports, https://nehikingreports.com/mountains/tug-mountain (accessed 2026-10-10).
