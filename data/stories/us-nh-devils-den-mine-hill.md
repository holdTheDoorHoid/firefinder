Mine Hill, a low hill with steep sides, lies on the east side of Massabesic Lake in Auburn, New Hampshire.[^1]

A wooden fire lookout tower stood on the summit from 1940 to 1989.[^1][^2] The Manchester Water Works ran the tower, and it owns the western side of the hill.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's New Hampshire list marks the site as gone.[^3] The hill is also the site of Devil's Den, an 80-foot cave that extends into the hill.[^2]

Today, four cement footings and other pieces of the tower remain on the highest point.[^1] A 2025 visitor saw the tower's footprint and took a picture of one of the cement footings.[^4]

Visitors park at a pull-off on Route 121 in Auburn.[^1] The route to the top follows unmarked woods roads and paths.[^1] That road runs over Manchester Water Works land.[^4] A private house stands at one edge of the summit clearing, but NH Family Hikes says the side with the tower site is public property.[^1]
