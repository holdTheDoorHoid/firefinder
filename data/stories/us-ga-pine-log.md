In February 1938, a newspaper report reprinted on the Forest Lookouts site said a fire tower would be built atop Pine Log Mountain once a truck trail reached the top.[^1] The road was to be 4.8 miles long and run up the east side of the mountain, and CCC workers from Cartersville were building it.[^1] The tower was to be 50 feet (15 m) tall, on five acres Col. Sam Tate had given to the state forestry division.[^1] The article describes a plan, not a finished tower.[^1]

The tower list places the site in Cherokee County, at an elevation of 2,314 feet (705 m).[^2] The Georgia Trust places the mountain in Bartow County instead.[^4] Wikipedia says the summit now has a radio tower.[^3] No source we read describes a fire lookout on the summit today. Wikipedia also says a paper company owns the land and rents it to the state to manage.[^3] The Trust says that lease has ended and the property is up for sale.[^4]

In September 2026, Georgia's Department of Natural Resources said it had reached a deal to buy 14,640 acres in Bartow and Cherokee counties.[^4] The Trust's update expects the property to reopen as a wildlife management area in spring 2027.[^4] We could not find a source that says whether the public can reach the summit today.
