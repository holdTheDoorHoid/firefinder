Sterling's fire tower stood in Polk County, near the St. Croix River.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register places it at 45.62956, -92.81351, and lists it as gone.[^3]

A local blog about the tower says it was the third one to stand in Sterling.[^2] The first went up in 1932, just north and west of the town hall.[^2] An 85-foot tower replaced it at the present site in the late 1930s, and a 100-foot tower followed in 1954.[^2]

The writer's uncle, Maurice, worked the tower in the 1940s and also spent many years with the DNR.[^2] The writer spent a season there in 1970, from March to October, while working for DNR fire control out of Grantsburg.[^2] Smoke sightings went out as compass bearings.[^2] The Grantsburg tower took a second bearing, and the two were crossed on a map at the ranger station to place each fire.[^2] The 1970 season was quiet, with no smokes apart from the local dumps.[^2]

No source I could read describes what, if anything, is left at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors. OpenStreetMap still carries a point named Sterling Lookout Tower at the spot, but a map marker does not show whether a structure stands.[^4] Since the register lists the tower as gone, do not plan a climb around this site.
