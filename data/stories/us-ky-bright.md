The Bright site in Letcher County is known, in the sources I read, from a 1931 newspaper report about a plan.[^1] The Mountain Eagle printed on May 21, 1931 that Chap King, the local chief fire warden, expected a fifty-foot steel lookout tower to be erected on a high point near where a state highway crosses.[^1] The item looks ahead to the tower rather than reporting one finished.[^1] The report added that a watchman would staff the tower during the risky part of the year and warn of any fire nearby.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts listing names the Jefferson National Forest as the agency.[^1] The Jefferson National Forest reaches into Letcher and Pike counties in Kentucky, according to the Encyclopedia of West Virginia.[^2]

The listing gives the tower's status as removed, with no date.[^1]

No source I read describes the site today, including whether any remains survive or whether the public can reach it. Ask the Jefferson National Forest before going.
