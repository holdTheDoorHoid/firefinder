Incline Ridge lies in Shoshone County, Idaho, about nine miles east of Fernwood, on the St. Joe National Forest.[^1] Its elevation is 4,900 feet.[^1] The listings show a camp at the site in 1933 and an L-4 cab in 1934.[^1][^2][^3] The lookout was destroyed in 1957.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as drive-up, although the lookout was destroyed in 1957.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
