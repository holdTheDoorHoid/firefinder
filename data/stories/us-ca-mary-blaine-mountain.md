Forest Lookouts files Mary Blaine Mountain under Trinity County, in the Trinity National Forest.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association gives the county as Siskiyou/Trinity, so the two sources do not agree on the county.[^2]

A July 1924 report named John Adler, a University of California student, as the lookout for that summer.[^1] In 1926 the Forest Service built a telephone line from its Crawford Creek ranger station to the lookout.[^1] That July a medical student from Portland, George Addler, was posted there.[^1] A paper also said the station lay in Trinity County but was simpler to reach through Sawyers Bar and Cecilville than along the trails.[^1] The two reports spell the surname differently and give different first names, so they may describe different people.[^1]

In 1931 a Forest Service crew cleared the trail to the lookout station.[^1] A July diary entry describes digging holes for telephone poles to carry wire to the lookout building.[^1]

In November 1931 a ranger reported to the Supervisor that he had heard the Trinity Forest supervisor planned to abandon the lookout.[^1] He asked whether a position could be set up and financed on the Klamath Forest, since the lookout also served that forest.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association files the site under its Ground type and lists it as gone.[^2]

We found no source that describes the site today, or whether visitors can reach it. Before any trip, check with the Trinity National Forest about access.
