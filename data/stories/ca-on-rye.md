The Rye tower site is listed at an elevation of 327 m (1073 ft) in Unorganized Centre Parry Sound, Parry Sound District.[^1] Firelookout.ca says the first tower here was wooden, a frame of hewn spruce timbers, with posts 20.1 m (66 ft) long set in concrete footings and steadied by guy wires bolted into rock.[^1] The page gives a total cost of $310.10.[^1] It then says a new steel tower appears to have been built on the site, and it refers to a Department of Lands and Forests tower detection system map that shows a 30.5 m (100 ft) steel tower at this location.[^1]

No source describes the site today. None says what, if anything, still stands there, or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Rye Fire Tower, Firelookout.ca, https://firelookout.ca/lookouts/?slug=rye-fire-tower (accessed 2026-10-20).
