Firelookout.ca places the Clarke Fire Tower in unorganized Division No. 18, Saskatchewan, at 54.66494, -104.77897.[^1]

A list of Saskatchewan towermen on websiteservice.ca gives Sam Roberts for 1970 to 1989, with four towers: North Cabin, Holbein, Clarke and Thunder Mountain.[^2] The entry adds that he filled in for his father in 1981 before taking the job on full time.[^2] The page itself asks readers for corrections, so treat its dates as informal.[^2]

No source we found describes what remains at the Clarke site today, so whether it still stands is unknown.
