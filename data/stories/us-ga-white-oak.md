The White Oak tower is recorded in Camden County. A Georgia tower list on tnlandforms.us places it at 31.032222, -81.733611, gives the site an elevation of 8 feet (2.4 m), and carries the note "topo 1959".[^1] That note most likely dates the USGS map on which the tower was found, which is not the same as a build date.[^1] The name is the list's own, since the list says names for towers numbered 73 and above are its creation, and this one is number 390.[^1]

A community called White Oak lies in Camden County, where it is described as being along the coastal highway corridor.[^2] No source I found ties the tower to that community.

No source I found describes what remains at the site today.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: White Oak, Georgia, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Oak,_Georgia (accessed 2026-10-21).
