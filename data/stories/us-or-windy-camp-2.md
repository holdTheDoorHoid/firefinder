The Windy Camp site is in Douglas County, about 18 miles east of Myrtle Creek.[^2] The register lists the Douglas FPA as the administrator.[^1]

The first setup dates to 1922. The register and firelookout.com describe a crow's nest and a tent cabin.[^1][^2] The Oregon lookouts site describes a different first structure, a 10-by-12 log dwelling with a shake roof, set 50 feet up in a tree.[^3]

In 1933 a 30-foot round fir pole tower with an L-4 cab went up.[^2][^3] The register says it replaced the crow's nest and tent cabin.[^1] A 12-by-24 pole barn with two stalls followed in 1937.[^3]

Both the register and firelookout.com say the tower was gone by 1959. Neither gives a more exact date.[^1][^2]

A separate Windy Camp, eight miles southeast of Mount Hood in Hood River County, has its own record and is a different place.[^4]

No source I read describes what remains at this site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
