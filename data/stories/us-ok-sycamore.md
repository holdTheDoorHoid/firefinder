The Sycamore lookout stood on Winding Stair Mountain, in Le Flore County, Oklahoma, inside the Ouachita National Forest.[^1] The Winding Stair Mountains form a ridge in Le Flore County.[^2] In June 1934 a Texas newspaper reported that a fire tower would be erected on the mountain that autumn, to protect newly acquired federal land.[^1] By October 1936 a 28-foot wooden tower was in use there.[^1] The Daily Oklahoman reported that a 33-foot (10 m) steel tower would replace it on the highest peak.[^1] A 1936 description placed the Sycamore tower over a federal triangulation station at 2,428 feet.[^1] In 1936, the tower guards posted there were Tom Doyle of Summerfield and Sebe Johnson of Whitesboro.[^1] The listing marks the tower as removed.[^1] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.
