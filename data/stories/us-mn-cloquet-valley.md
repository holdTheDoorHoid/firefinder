The state forest service put up tower number 105 in the Cloquet Valley in 1930.[^1] The county catalog describes it as an 80-foot Aermotor stairway lookout that stood along County 4, just south of Whiteface Reservoir.[^1]

In 1952 the Coast and Geodetic Survey recorded the tower as steel construction, roughly 85 feet tall, with a base 20 feet on a side.[^2] The survey placed it about 33 miles north of Duluth.[^2] The catalog and the survey disagree on height, 80 feet against about 85 feet, so treat the figure as approximate.[^1][^2]

The state catalog marks the tower as removed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Cloquet Valley tower as gone.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
