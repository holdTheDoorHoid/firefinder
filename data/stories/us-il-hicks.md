Hicks was a lookout in Hardin County, Illinois, on the Shawnee National Forest.[^1] The Central US Lookouts page files it under that county and forest and gives its name as Hicks (ELO).[^1]

A National Archives photograph from November 1938 captions it as an emergency lookout tower in the Secondary Lookout System.[^3] The picture is part of the Forest Service records the archive holds.[^3]

The archive places the photograph in Pope, Illinois, within the forest, while the Forest Fire Lookout Association puts the site in Hardin County.[^3][^2] The association's list also calls the structure a platform and marks it gone.[^2] The archive caption calls it a tower, so the two descriptions do not match.[^3]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it can be visited.
