The tower on Bare Hill in Plymouth County, Massachusetts, is called the Carver Fire Tower.[^1][^4] The Massachusetts Bureau of Forest Fire Control administers it, and the register calls it the tallest and largest lookout in the state.[^1] It is in Myles Standish State Forest.[^5]

The current tower was built in 1988.[^4] The register describes an 80-foot steel tower with angled cab windows and no door out onto the catwalk.[^1] It counts this as the third tower to watch over this section of the forest.[^1]

The site had older towers, and the sources give different dates for them.[^2][^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says a 38-foot tower moved from Chelmsford was erected here in 1918, and that a 68-foot iron tower went up in 1930.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Massachusetts chapter says the Chelmsford tower arrived in 1919, and that the tower built in 1932 mostly protected the state forest's own land.[^3] That post places the older tower along Lower College Pond Road, while the register gives its location as SW Line Road.[^2][^3]

The register's visit report from March 24, 2014 says the tower has been unstaffed for several years.[^1] The same report says the backup solar panels had been damaged and that the tower needed paint.[^1]

The older tower shut down after the 1986 season.[^2][^3] In 2002 it was sold at auction, taken down, and moved to a farm in Minnesota.[^2][^3]

The 2014 visit report is the most recent one we could read, so we cannot confirm the tower's condition now.[^1] The register notes that there is no NHLR sign at the site and no signed directions to the tower.[^1] A DCR post invited people to an event at this tower on Sunday, July 28, from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m., where visitors could tour one of the agency's 42 towers used statewide.[^5] No source we read describes regular public access, so check with the Bureau before visiting.[^1][^5]
