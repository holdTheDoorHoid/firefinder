The Dead Mountain lookout was in the Willamette National Forest, near Oakridge, Oregon.[^1][^2] A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description from 1953 places a survey mark about three miles northeast of Oakridge, a quarter mile south of the ruins of a tower that burned.[^1]

The dates are patchy. firelookout.com gives 1904 for a camp at the site and 1923 for a fire finder, and says the lookout was abandoned in the 1940s.[^2]

Newspaper items quoted on the Forest Lookouts page name people at the station across the late 1920s and 1930s.[^1] In August 1928 a hiking group spent a day at Dead Mountain with Robert Dunning, who was at the lookout. A family was at the lookout in August 1929, and another spent the summer of 1932 there. In May 1935 three men were posted to Dead Mountain as lookout firemen, and in September 1936 Jay Putnam was stationed there in that role.[^1]

The 1953 description mentions the ruins of a tower that burned. Its directions end at a road turnaround by those ruins.[^1]

We found no source that describes the site today or whether visitors may reach it. In July 2025 Cascadia Wildlands joined a tour near Oakridge, hosted by the Forest Service and a local collaborative, and wrote about the Dead Mountain project area, where thinning and controlled fire are planned.[^3] That article is about the forest, not the lookout site.[^3] Ask the Willamette National Forest about access before you go.
