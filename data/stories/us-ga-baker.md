The Georgia Forestry Commission owned a lookout tower in Baker County, Georgia, and sold it at auction in September 2006.[^1] The buyer paid $20.00 and had 30 days to take the tower down.[^1] The Forest Lookouts listing now marks it as removed.[^1]

A blog post from July 2017 calls it a former Georgia Forestry Commission lookout tower in Baker County.[^2] A photo credited to Michael Rivera on Wikimedia Commons is dated July 15, 2017.[^3] Its camera position sits close to the coordinates the statewide list gives for the Baker tower, so the photo probably shows this site.[^3][^4] The photo's file name carries the number GA216, which that list gives to a tower at Fargo in Clinch County, so the match is likely but not certain.[^3][^4]

We could not find a source that describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
