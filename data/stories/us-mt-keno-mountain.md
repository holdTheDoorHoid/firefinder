Keno Mountain's tower stood in the Kootenai National Forest, about 15 miles west of Yaak, in Lincoln County, Montana.[^1] The page gives its elevation as 6,542 feet.[^1]

The firelookout.com page says it was built in 1936 as a 45-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1] L-4 is the name for a 14-by-14-foot frame cab that a lookout could live in, and the register notes these cabs could be placed on top of tall fire towers.[^3]

The page leaves the end date open with a question mark, and says the tower has long since disappeared.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list also marks it as a gone tower.[^2]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.
