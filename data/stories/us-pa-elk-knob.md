Elk Knob, in Lycoming County, Pennsylvania, is listed as a former lookout site.[^1] According to the register, a "bald tower" was in use around 1917 and does not stand there now. It gives no coordinates for the site.[^1]

The regional lookout listing describes the same place as an "unimproved bald top observation point" established before 1918.[^2] The two descriptions differ on whether anything was built, and the sources we read do not say which is right.[^1][^2]

The statewide list, last updated in December 2008, records a bald site with no build year, not standing, and used circa 1917.[^3]

No source we read describes the site as it is today, or whether it can be visited.

[^1]: Elk Knob Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites register, Forest Fire Lookout Association, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/pa/elk-knob-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Pennsylvania (A-F) Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/pennsylvania-a-f.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Pennsylvania Fire Lookouts, State Listing Page, firelookout.ipage.com, https://firelookout.ipage.com/lo-pennsylvania.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
