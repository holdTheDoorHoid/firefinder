Dedrick Point was a lookout station in Trinity County, California.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a ground site rather than a tower.[^2]

Newspaper notes from 1933, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, show the station under construction that year.[^1] A Courier-Free Press item from September said horses would haul lumber to Balsam grove for the fire lookout, which was making fast progress.[^1] Several horses had reached Dedrick on their way up the creek to a C.C.C. camp on the spike road.[^1] By October the Courier Searchlight reported that the new station at Balsam grove was about two-thirds finished and had a cement foundation.[^1]

The September item also said that Mr. and Mrs. John Biddle would be in charge of the lookout.[^1] Crews were working on the lookout road in June 1934.[^1] A June item said the road joined the lookout with Canyon creek road, and by August it was open to the lookout.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list marks Dedrick Point as Gone.[^2]

None of the sources we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it. The association's list gives the point's coordinates as 40.84610, -123.05230.[^2]
