The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list calls this tower Bath (Possum Walk) and places it in Walker County.[^2] The community was known as Possum Walk until 1887, when its post office gave it the name Bath.[^4]

A Coast and Geodetic Survey description dated 1935 places a survey marker under a bolt in the roof of a lookout house here, so a roofed lookout house stood on the site by then.[^1] The same description puts the station about 10 miles south of Huntsville and about 8 miles northwest of New Waverly.[^1] The Texas lookout index lists the tower under Sam Houston National Forest, in Walker County.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^2] The community's post office closed in 1905. By the early 1990s, all that was left of it was Union Hill Baptist Church and a cemetery.[^4] No source describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout or whether the land is open to visitors.
