Ellsinore was a lookout tower in Carter County.[^1] The Central US Lookouts record lists it under Mark Twain National Forest, at 27N-3E-33, a township, range and section code.[^1]

A Daily American Republic report dated February 2, 1935, which the page quotes, says the Ellsinore Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC) crew had put up a 100-foot (30.5 m) lookout tower, though the passage does not say where it stood.[^1] Joseph Blake, superintendent of the Ellsinore CCC camp, was quoted in the same report as saying that CCC crews did all of the Forest Service's real work.[^1] He added that the CCC as a body might not last, but the work it was doing was lasting.[^1]

The record now lists the tower as removed.[^1] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Ellsinore - Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/ellsinore.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
