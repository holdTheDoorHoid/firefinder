The White Rock fire tower went up in 1914 on a spot known as the "white rocks," near Pond Park in the Mont Alto forest reservation, in Franklin County.[^1][^2]

A June 1914 newspaper item said the state forest academy at Mont Alto was putting up a 50-foot tower so fire wardens could scan South Mountain and spot fires early. The tower was to be linked to the academy by telephone.[^2] The Weebly lookout index gives a different height, 35 feet for a wood tower put up that year.[^2] The sources disagree on the height, so this story notes both figures.

In March 1920 a forest fire burned fifty to sixty acres near the tower at Pond Park. Nearby residents fought it, and the academy's roughly thirty students joined them before it was under control.[^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes a wooden tower built in 1914 and removed in 1932.[^1] The register entry dates from September 2008 and gives no coordinates for the site.[^1]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it can be visited.
