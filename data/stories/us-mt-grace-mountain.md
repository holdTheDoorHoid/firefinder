Grace Mountain was a lookout site on the Flathead National Forest in Powell County, Montana, about 21 miles northeast of Seeley Lake.[^1][^2]

The firelookout.com page dates the site only to the 1930s and says its history is unknown.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places it at 47.35361 north, 113.11579 west, lists its type as unknown, and marks it gone.[^3] The Forest Lookouts page gives its survey location as township 18 North, range 12 West, section 3.[^2]

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
