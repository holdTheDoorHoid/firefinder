Bull Run Rock is in Baker County, Oregon, about 10 miles southwest of Unity, at 7,873 feet.[^2][^1]

The cabin dates from 1934.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page lists panorama photographs taken on September 9, 1935.[^3]

The sources name different national forests. The Forest Lookouts page places the site in the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest, while the register says Malheur National Forest.[^3][^1] The register lists the U.S. Forest Service as administrator, and gives a registration date of November 19, 2019.[^1]

The register and firelookout.com both say the cabin was abandoned in 1937.[^1][^2] That date sits badly with an August 1938 newspaper item quoted on the Forest Lookouts page. The item describes a lightning fire four miles south of the station, reported from Bull Run Lookout.[^3] The sources do not explain the difference, so both dates are reported here as they appear.

The register says the cabin is gone.[^1] It shows a 2008 photograph of the site, but we could not view the image, so this story does not describe it.[^1] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
