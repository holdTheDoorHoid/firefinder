Cascade is a fire lookout site in Wayne County, Missouri.[^1] The Central US Lookouts page lists the agency as the Missouri Department of Conservation.[^1] A 1963 survey description says the site lies on state property.[^1]

In 1940 the Missouri Conservation Commission acquired 15 acres here for a fire tower.[^1] Its annual report for 1939 to 1941 lists a 52-foot (about 16 m) pole tower.[^1] A state manual for 1945 to 1946 says the Commission owned the 15-acre site.[^1] In June 1946 a newspaper item said roads were under construction at the Cascade tower, which had been erected earlier, while other facilities there were still unfinished.[^1]

The Commission's report for fiscal 1953 to 1954 says a replacement tower was put up at Cascade.[^1] A 1963 survey description gives the structure as steel with an observation cabin at the top, about 110 feet (about 33.5 m) overall.[^1]

In April 1963 a fire crossed the tower site and wiped out 55,000 pines that had been planted by hand that same spring.[^1] A May 1, 1963 newspaper item said the flames ran across the site, but the tower and the other buildings escaped major damage.[^1] A report for fiscal 1981 to 1982 mentions that the roof of the tower garage was replaced.[^1]

In November 2022 the tower sold at auction for $4,024.12, and the sale set March 31, 2023 as the deadline for its removal.[^1]

No source I could read says whether the tower still stands.[^1] The sale required removal, and I found no source confirming that the work was finished.[^1] Public access to the site today is not described in any source I found.[^1]

[^1]: Cascade - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/cascade1.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
