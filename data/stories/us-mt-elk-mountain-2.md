Elk Mountain Lookout sits in Lincoln County, Montana, on the Kootenai National Forest, about 28 miles east-northeast of Libby.[^2] The register gives its elevation as 6,588 feet, and firelookout.com gives 6,587 feet.[^1][^2] The register lists the US Forest Service as administrator, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list also calls the site Elk Patrol.[^1][^3]

The register says the site was developed in 1923 with a 10-by-10-foot frame cab and a roof platform.[^1] In 1940 a 10-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab was built about a quarter mile southwest of the summit.[^1][^2] The register also notes an L-6 cab about a mile northeast of the lookout.[^1]

The sources I could read do not say who staffed the lookout or record any notable fires there.

Vandalism and decay have taken a toll, and the register says the tower has fallen into ruin.[^1] Firelookout.com says the same.[^2] The association's Montana list still marks the site as standing, so the sources disagree about its condition.[^3]

A travel listing for the Elk Patrol Trail says the old structure is now closed to the public.[^4] A Visit Montana listing for the Elk Mountain Trail describes a hike that leads up to Elk Mountain and the old lookout, and it does not mention a closure.[^5] The Visit Montana listing is silent on closure, so please treat the site as closed, and do not climb the ruins.
