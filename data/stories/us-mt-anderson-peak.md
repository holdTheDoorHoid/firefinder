Anderson Peak is a lookout site in Judith Basin County, about nine miles north-northeast of Neihart, at an elevation of 7,687 feet.[^1] The Lewis & Clark National Forest is listed as its agency, and a Forest Lookouts page gives its location code as 15N-9E-17.[^1][^2]

Firelookout.com describes a 50-foot pole tower of the type it calls L-4, dated to 1935.[^1]

The sources disagree about how the tower ended.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com says it was destroyed in 1968.[^1] A Forest Lookouts page lists it as removed.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks it gone and lists its coordinates as 47.06590 north and 110.60412 west.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the peak today, and none says whether the public can reach it.
