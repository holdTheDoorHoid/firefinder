Experimental Spur was a 60-foot crow's nest, a platform built in a tree, in the Kaniksu National Forest about 12 miles north-northeast of Priest River, Bonner County, Idaho.[^2] The sources date it to about 1928.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

It was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1][^3][^4] Later accounts say it survived for a while: firelookout.com says it was still there in the 1980s,[^2] the Idaho Fire Lookouts team reports that Ray Kresek's book placed it there as of 2015,[^1] and WillhiteWeb says the tree was still there in 2022.[^4]

WillhiteWeb says the platform seems to be missing, though the tree itself is still there.[^4] The Idaho Fire Lookouts team found the tree because the telephone wire was still staked into its trunk. Apart from that wire and the ladder stakes, they wrote, nothing remains of the platform.[^1]

The road ends about a quarter mile short of the tree.[^1] WillhiteWeb describes parking at a switchback at 2,680 feet on the Gisborne Mountain Road and hiking 0.6 miles one way along an abandoned spur road.[^4] Neither source says whether the land is open to the public.[^1][^4]
