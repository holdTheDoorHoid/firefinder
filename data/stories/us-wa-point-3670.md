Point 3670 is a lookout site in Skamania County, within the Gifford Pinchot National Forest.[^1] An L-4 cab went up in 1934, about a quarter mile north of the summit, where the main road ends.[^1][^2][^3] Photographs from July 4, 1934 show trees recently cut to open the view to the northwest.[^3][^4] A benchmark was placed in 1937.[^3]

In May 1964 air patrols were expected to replace the coverage the lookout system had provided, according to a Longview Daily News report quoted by Forest Lookouts.[^4] The 37 stations manned in earlier years were to drop to six manned lookouts, and Point 3670 was one of the six, mainly to provide communications coverage.[^4] In June 1965 the plan narrowed again: Point 3670 would be the only staffed lookout in the Spirit Lake and Lewis River areas, and aircraft would do the other patrolling.[^4]

In 1971 the Forest Service burned the lookout house, which it no longer needed.[^4][^3] The register says the cab was destroyed that year.[^1][^2]

The site sits at the end of Forest Road 31. Willhite describes the road as good gravel, though trees block the view from the site.[^3] His 2020 photographs show the site at the end of the road.[^3] That year new culverts were being replaced along the whole access road, which Willhite says shows the Forest Service plans to keep it for many years.[^3] He speculates that the work may point to a timber sale soon.[^3] None of the sources used for this story describes remains on the ground today.
