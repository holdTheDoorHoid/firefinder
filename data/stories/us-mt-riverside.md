A 30-foot pole tower that the lookout's page calls an L-4 went up at Riverside in 1934.[^1] The site lies in the Flathead National Forest, about 12 miles southeast of Hungry Horse, in Flathead County.[^1]

The page says rising water from Hungry Horse Reservoir covered the site, and it dates the loss to 1950.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list also marks the tower as gone.[^2]

Those dates conflict with the dam's history. The Park Service dates the completion of Hungry Horse Dam to 1953, which falls after the 1950 date on the lookout page.[^3] The same history says the reservoir flooded one fire lookout tower, which the Bureau of Reclamation rebuilt at another site, but it does not say which tower that was.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains of the site today. If the lookout page is right, the site now lies under the reservoir.[^1] No source says whether the spot is open to the public.
