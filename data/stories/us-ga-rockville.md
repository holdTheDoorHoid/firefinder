Rockville Lookout Tower is listed in Putnam County, Georgia.[^1] It appears on the same numbered list of Georgia lookout towers kept on the tnlandforms.us website, which gives each entry a name, coordinates, county and elevation.[^1] The entry places the tower at 33.34319, -83.19738, with an elevation of 673 and no unit printed (673 feet would be about 205 m).[^1] Its last field, which the page's header labels "GFC number", is blank.[^1] The map on that page was last marked as revised on 22 March 2016.[^1]

No source found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it. Check who owns the land before going, and do not climb any structure you find there without permission.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
