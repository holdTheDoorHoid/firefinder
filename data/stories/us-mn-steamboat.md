The Steamboat tower was also known as the Steamboat Hill lookout, and it stood in Hubbard County.[^1][^2] It was a 100-foot (30.5 m) Aermotor stairway tower, and the Minnesota Forest Service numbered it state tower 59.[^1]

The Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list gives the site as 47.06813, -94.73484 and marks the tower as gone.[^2]

In 1952 the tower was moved from its hill and erected at Farris, a rail village that had once been called Graceland. The site lay west of Cass Lake, in the northeast of Hubbard County.[^1][^3] A 1959 survey note for the Farris tower gives an overall height of 110 feet, while the compilation's own description says 100 feet, so the two figures disagree.[^1] The 1959 survey notes also say the Steamboat tower had apparently been dismantled.[^1][^3] The Fire Lookout Association lists the Farris site as gone too.[^2]

We found no source that describes what stands on the hill or at Farris now. A photograph in the 2025 compilation carries the caption "Footing at Steamboat site," and another is captioned "Footing at Farris site."[^1] No source we read says whether either place is open to the public, so ask the local land manager about access before you go looking.
