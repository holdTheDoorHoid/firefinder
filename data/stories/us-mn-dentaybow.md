Dentaybow was a fire lookout tower in Koochiching County.[^2] The county list says the township's name combines the surnames of three of its homesteaders, Densmore, Taylor and Bowman, one syllable each.[^2] The tower belonged to the Minnesota Forest Service, now the state Department of Natural Resources, and the lists date it to 1952.[^2][^3] The list describes an 80-foot ladder tower at the junction of Highway 65 and what it calls Ut 36.[^2]

The county list also offers a guess that the tower began as the Rapid River Ranger Station tower in Lake of the Woods County, and may later have moved east of Ludlow to become West Pine Island.[^2] The list says this may or may not be so, and it notes that both towers carry the number 77.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list marks the tower as gone, with no date.[^1] The county list says it was removed and that the site is now private property.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the site today. Because the land is private, any visit would be on someone else's property.[^2]
