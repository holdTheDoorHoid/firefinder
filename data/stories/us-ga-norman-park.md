The Georgia tower list places a Norman Park lookout in Colquitt County, at an elevation of 358 feet (109 m), and gives the site the number gfc-229.[^3] Forest Lookouts also has a Norman Park page under Georgia and Colquitt County.[^1]

A Digital Library of Georgia photo record dates a picture to about 1955 and places it at a forestry unit site in Colquitt County, near Sunset School, off Thomasville Road.[^2] Its description also mentions fire-fighting gear and a fire tower in the photo's backdrop.[^2] The record does not name that tower or say it stood at Norman Park.[^2] Its map point, 31.18839, -83.76885, is not the point the tower list gives for Norman Park, so the photo may show a different tower.[^2][^3]

We could not find a source that describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
