The Girard Lookout Tower is in Burke County, and the National Historic Lookout Register lists the Georgia Forestry Commission as its administrator.[^1] The Register gives 1940 as the build year, but its description puts construction around the late 1930s or early 1940s.[^1]

The tower is an 80-foot steel tower of Aermotor LS-40 type, with a cab seven feet square. A second Georgia list records the same design for the Burke County site.[^1][^2] Mike Finch, Jr. nominated it, and the Register lists its registration date as January 3, 2013.[^1]

The sources I read do not say who staffed the tower or whether it watched any particular fire.

According to the Register, the bottom steps have been removed to prevent entry, and the site appears to be abandoned.[^1] I found no newer source on its condition.

Nothing in these sources suggests the public can visit or climb the tower, and the removed steps point the other way.[^1] The Register names the Georgia Forestry Commission as administrator, so that is the office to contact about it.[^1] Until then, treat the structure as off limits.
