The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Kelliher tower in Beltrami County as gone, at 47.941 N, 94.456 W.[^1] A 2025 compilation of Minnesota fire towers gives it Minnesota Forest Service number 121. It describes a tower 80 feet tall, of ladder design, that stood in the town of Kelliher itself.[^2]

A 1934 survey note used the tower's summit as an observation point and places that tower in the west of town, so it was standing by then.[^2]

The same entry includes a second-hand account of its removal. It says the tower was reportedly damaged while being taken down, and that it now sits with other retired towers at a local place nicknamed a tower graveyard. The compilation presents this as hearsay, so treat it as unconfirmed.[^2]

We found no source that describes the Kelliher site today, so we cannot say whether any part of the tower survives in town or whether the site is open to the public.

[^1]: Minnesota Lookouts (By County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/mn-co/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Minnesota Fire Lookout Towers, 2025 compilation (Kelliher Fire Tower entry), Wikimedia Commons, https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a4/Minnesota_Fire_Lookout_Towers_3-2025-Aug_25.pdf (accessed 2026-10-10).
