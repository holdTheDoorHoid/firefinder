Alberta's wildfire register dates the Ansell tower to 2013.[^1] It is a steel tower, 30 m (98 ft) tall, standing at 967.7 m (3,175 ft) above sea level.[^1]

The tower shares its name with the Ansell locality, which began in 1911 as a siding built by the Grand Trunk Pacific Railway to support coal mining and transport on the Alberta Coal Branch line.[^2] Wikipedia describes a fire and bear lookout on the site, Ansell Tower, that the province runs, with services coming through Edson.[^2] A lookout history site files Ansell under the Edson Forest.[^3]

Across the province, Alberta says each lookout site is staffed by a lookout observer, generally from April to October.[^4]

The register lists the tower as active, with a weather station on site and access coded "drive".[^1] Wikipedia's present-tense description suggests the tower is still in place.[^2] No source describes public visits, so treat it as a working fire-watch tower.
