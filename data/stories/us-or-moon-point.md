Moon Point is a former lookout site on the Willamette National Forest in Lane County, Oregon, about 13 miles south-southeast of Oakridge.[^1][^2][^4] The Forest Service administers the land, and the register gives the site's elevation as 5,138 feet, or 1,566 meters.[^1]

The register says a tower was built on the site in 1930.[^1] Firelookout.com dates the tower only to the 1930s, which fits that year.[^2]

The lookout was abandoned in 1969, a date that Forest Lookouts also gives.[^1][^3]

Today the point is reached on foot.[^4] The Forest Service lists the trailhead as open, and the Moon Point Trail, number 3688, starts there and runs through wet and dry meadows past rocky cliffs.[^4][^5] The agency's directions begin on Highway 58, two miles east of Oakridge. They then run 18 miles on Road 21, about 7 miles on Road 2129, and a final 1.5 miles to the trailhead.[^5] From the point, the Forest Service says, the view takes in the Middle Fork Ranger District and the Umpqua National Forest.[^4]

The register describes the lookout as gone.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
