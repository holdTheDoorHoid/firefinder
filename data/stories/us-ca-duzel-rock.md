Duzel Rock is a fire lookout in Siskiyou County, California, northeast of Etna.[^1][^3] The Register puts its elevation at 6,041 feet (1,841 meters).[^1]

In early 1931 a newspaper reported that state rangers planned a 12-mile telephone line from Etna to the rock, and that the state forest department meant to set up a lookout station there.[^2] A paper in April 1931 said a standard lookout house was planned for Duzel Rock, a spot that overlooks timber both inside and outside the Klamath National Forest.[^2] The sources I could read do not give a completion date, so the exact build year is not settled here.

The newspapers spell the name "Duzzle Rock" in one item and "Duzel Rock" in the other.[^2] The Register and CAL FIRE use "Duzel."[^1][^2][^3]

The Register accepted the lookout on November 27, 2016, as US 1152, CA 113.[^1] Its page says the lookout is administered by the California Department of Forestry & Fire Protection and sits within CalFire's Siskiyou Ranger District.[^1]

A July 16, 1953 photograph by Vern F. Knedler shows the lookout looking northeast. The photo belongs to a California Division of Forestry collection that the Cal Fire Museum keeps, and the archive marks it as public domain.[^4]

On September 7, 2019, a fire started near the lookout. CAL FIRE's Siskiyou unit reported it at 15 acres, and it was contained three days later.[^3]

An April 2020 photo blog post says a fire lookout tower is on top of the rock. It also says the route up crosses private land, where locked gates have been installed and the people there do not like visitors passing through.[^5]

The Register does not say whether the lookout is staffed today. Please do not try to reach the tower over private land or past locked gates. Check with CAL FIRE's Siskiyou unit before you plan any visit.
