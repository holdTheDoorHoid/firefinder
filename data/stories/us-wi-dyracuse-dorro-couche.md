Dyracuse Lookout is an 80-foot Wisconsin standard tower in Adams County.[^1] The register gives its build year as 1939 and describes an external protected ladder.[^1] It says the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources administers the tower, and that the lookout was once named Dorro Couche, after a nearby lake.[^1]

The tower stands on Dyracuse Mound, inside the Town of Rome's recreation area.[^2] The town says that area opened on May 29, 1982, and takes its name from the mound.[^2] The town also says it became the first in Wisconsin to build a motorcycle recreation area with money from the Motorcycle Recreation Program, which the DNR operates.[^2]

The register gives Tyler Bormann as the nominator and records the tower's registration on August 16, 2017.[^1] The sources read do not say who staffed the lookout, when it was last used as a lookout, or whether any fires were reported from it.

The sources disagree about who controls the mound. A rock-formation guide says the land is privately owned and not open to the public.[^4] The town describes the mound as lying inside its recreational area, which the town operates, and the register lists the DNR as administrator.[^1][^2] The sources read do not settle the question, so check with the Town of Rome before going near the tower.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^3] The town's page says the park opens at the beginning of April, weather permitting.[^2] The sources read do not say whether the cab is open to visitors, and the tower should not be climbed without permission.
