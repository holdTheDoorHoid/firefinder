The Beavertown Tower Site is in Snyder County, Pennsylvania, at an elevation the register gives as 2,098 feet.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it as US 192, PA 21.[^1] Henry Isenberg nominated it, and the register records its registration date as September 16, 2008.[^1]

The register describes the structure as a steel M-M tower that went up in 1949.[^1] It says the tower is no longer standing.[^1]

Today, TopoQuest, a topographic map site, places a Beavertown Fire Tower at the same coordinates the register gives.[^1][^2] TopoQuest puts its elevation at 2,096 feet, two feet below the register's figure.[^1][^2] Its map entry does not say whether any structure still stands. No source we read describes what remains at the site or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Beavertown Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/pa/beavertown-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Beavertown Fire Tower, TopoQuest, https://topoquest.com/place-detail.php?id=1213136 (accessed 2026-10-10).
