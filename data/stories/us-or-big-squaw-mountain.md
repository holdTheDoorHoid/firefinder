Big Squaw Mountain is in Douglas County, Oregon, on the Umpqua National Forest.[^1][^2] The lookout stood at 4,878 feet, on the divide between the Little River and the South Umpqua River.[^1][^2]

A July 1932 newspaper report said nine new lookout houses were to be built, one of them on Big Squaw Mountain.[^2] The site's register entry gives 1932 as the build year and describes an L-4 cab on a 20-foot tower.[^1] The Forest Lookouts list, drawing on Umpqua National Forest files, puts an L-4 cab on a 10-foot timber tower in 1933.[^2] A photograph of the view north from the lookout is dated August 12, 1933.[^4]

In 1934 several acres were cleared to improve the view from the lookout, and the record tags that work as CCC.[^2] In 1941 the lookout was manned for 80 days, and its reports went to the Steamboat Bridge Ranger Station. A forest line tied its communications into Pacific Telephone and Telegraph.[^2]

Both the register and Fire Lookout date its abandonment to 1958 and list the site as gone.[^1][^3] The sources disagree on the tower's height. The register and Fire Lookout both give 20 feet, while the Forest Lookouts list says 10.[^1][^2][^3]

None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. None says whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
