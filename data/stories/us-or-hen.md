The Hen lookout was a crow's nest in Wallowa County, Oregon.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places it near 45.729 N, 117.3593 W, and uses the term Crowsnest for the structure.[^3] Firelookout.com dates the crow's nest to the 1930s and says it was abandoned, and the association's list gives the same type and status.[^1][^3]

The sources disagree about which forest it belonged to.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com names the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest.[^1] The Oregon list on Weebly names the Wallowa National Forest and gives the location as 3N-44E-19.[^2]

The Oregon list also gives September 4, 1936 as the date of panoramic photos made at the site.[^2] The photographs are the only dated entry on the Oregon list for the site.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the public can reach it.
