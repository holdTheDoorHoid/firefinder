Peterson Point Lookout stood in St. Joe National Forest, nine miles south of St. Maries in Benewah County, at an elevation of 4,161 feet.[^1] An L-4 tower went up on the site in 1938.[^1][^2] It was destroyed in 1954.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts and firelookout.com agree on both dates.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's regional register gives its coordinates as 47.17538 north and 116.55230 west, and it marks the tower as gone.[^3] Firelookout.com also lists a different Peterson Point, a camp in the Nez Perce National Forest, 21 miles east-southeast of Kooskia, that was abandoned in 1941, so the two should not be confused.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.
