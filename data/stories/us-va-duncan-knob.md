Duncan Knob Lookout is a 60-foot Aermotor tower in Bath County, Virginia. The National Historic Lookout Register says it was built in the late 1930s and is the second of two towers in the county.[^1] Its special feature is a wrap-around catwalk.[^1]

The Register's table gives the elevation as 3,844 feet, but its description says 3,819 feet, so the exact figure is unclear.[^1]

A Forest Fire Lookout Association list of Virginia towers marks it as standing.[^2] The Register lists a registration date of January 23, 2017, under the number US 1165, VA 31.[^1]

Ownership reads differently across sources. The Register names American Tower Corporation as the administrator, while an eastern lookouts site places the tower in the George Washington National Forest.[^1][^3] The sources do not explain how the two fit together.

The sources read do not give a staffing history or name any fires the tower watched. They also do not say whether the public may go up it, so anyone planning a visit should check with the forest office or the administrator first.
