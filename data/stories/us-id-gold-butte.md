Gold Butte is a lookout site about six miles east of Elk River in Clearwater County, Idaho.[^1] Sources name the Potlatch Timber Protective Association as the agency behind it.[^1][^3]

A lookout was established there in 1915.[^3] The association's 1915 annual report, as quoted on Kemnow's page, says about an acre and a half was cleared of timber to improve the view, and that the platform stood about 80 feet above the ground.[^3] Rex Kamstra's firelookout.com page gives the same 80-foot platform.[^1]

Kamstra's page lists a log cabin for 1923 and a 74-foot pole tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab for 1933.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the same cabin and tower dates.[^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts names Ben Thomas as the only staffer it records, for 1919.[^2] A May 1919 Clearwater Republican item, quoted on Ron Kemnow's Forest Lookouts page, places a Ben Thomas as the staffer at Gold Hill Lookout. That page flags the location as possibly wrong, so the link to Gold Butte is uncertain.[^3]

Kamstra's page says the tower was destroyed in 1966.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives 1956 instead, so the two sources disagree.[^2] Kamstra's 1923 entry also says the cabin was still standing, but the page does not say when that was seen.[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under drive-up.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the tower as gone.[^4] None of the pages I checked describes what remains at the site today or whether the ground is open to visitors.
