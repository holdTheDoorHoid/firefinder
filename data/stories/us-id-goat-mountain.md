Goat Mountain Lookout stood in the Kaniksu National Forest, about 13 miles northeast of Bonners Ferry in Boundary County, Idaho.[^1][^3] The site is listed at an elevation of 6,779 feet.[^1][^2]

The lookout was a cupola cabin, dated to 1924.[^1][^2] Charley Forslund is listed as lookout personnel for 1933 to 1935.[^1][^2]

Rex's Forest Fire Lookout Page and the Idaho Fire Lookouts site both say the lookout was destroyed in 1965.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts marks it as gone in the same year.[^3]

A Boundary County article, first published in 2011 and updated in 2024, notes that traces of other county lookouts survive, including a concrete box on Goat Mountain.[^4] The article does not tie that box to this lookout by name.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in, but no source we read says whether it is open to visitors today.[^2]
