Thirteenmile Mountain sits in Ferry County, in the Colville National Forest, about 10 miles south-southeast of Republic.[^1][^2] In 1929 the site was a camp.[^4] Panoramic photos from September 26, 1934 show lumber ready for building the cab.[^4]

In 1935 a 10-foot pole tower with an L-5 cab replaced the camp.[^1][^4] The 1934 panoramas call the ridge Thirteen Mile Ridge, not mountain.[^4] A 1936 survey party described the top as bare and grassy, and locals called it Thirteen Mile Mountain.[^4]

In 1942 the lookout was used by the AWS, from March 27 to May 9.[^3][^4] In 1945 a survey party found it still standing.[^4]

Later in the 1940s the tower and cab came down.[^1][^4] The FFLOS entry says removed, while firelookout.com and Willhite say destroyed.[^1][^2][^4]

None of the sources cited here says what, if anything, is left on the summit today or how to reach it.
