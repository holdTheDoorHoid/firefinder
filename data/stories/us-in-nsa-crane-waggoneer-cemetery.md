The northern Crane tower is near Waggoner Cemetery and a lake on the Navy base.[^1] The Register calls it Waggoneer Cemetery, lists it as US 1836, IN 16, and captions its photo as half-demolished.[^2]

A September 1939 Outdoor Indiana map shows a tower near the lake at Crane, and Indiana Fire Towers counts that tower among the Crane-area towers.[^1] It does not say for certain that this is the same tower.[^1] The Navy base was originally established as an ammunition depot in 1941.[^3] The sources I read give no build year for this tower.

The Forest Service says the depot operated four towers during World War II, but its numbered list does not clearly match this northern tower to any one of them.[^4] The sources I read say nothing about who staffed it or what fires it watched.

Indiana Fire Towers says that as of 2025 only the bottom part of this tower remains, and that its satellite photo appears to show the cab missing.[^1] A 2004 satellite image on the same page appears to show the whole tower.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association marks the tower as standing with an asterisk. Its footnote says the asterisk flags towers that have lost their cab but still have their main structure.[^5] The two descriptions do not fully agree on how much of the tower is left.[^1][^5]

The Crane towers sit on restricted federal Naval property.[^1] Indiana Fire Towers says visitors cannot get in or climb them.[^1] The Register recorded this tower on August 2, 2026.[^2]
