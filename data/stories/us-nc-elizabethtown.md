Elizabethtown is a lookout tower in Bladen County, North Carolina.[^1][^2] A North Carolina tower list on tnlandforms.us gives its coordinates and county.[^2] The Forest Lookouts entry names only the county, with no agency, date or height.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.

[^2]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
