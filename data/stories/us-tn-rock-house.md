Rock House was a fire tower site in Fentress County, Tennessee, at an elevation of 1,687 ft (514 m).[^1] The Tennessee Landforms list gives its tower number as 4-1, a number from a 1976 list of state forest towers.[^1][^2] The list marks the tower as moved.[^1]

The list says the tower was relocated to Pickett in 1972, and that concrete footings and an old cabin survive at the original site.[^1]

A separate entry for Pickett, in Fentress County at 1,720 ft (524 m), says the tower there is standing and that it came from the Rock House site nearby.[^3] The list was last revised in January 2026.[^2]

No source found says whether the public can visit either the Rock House site or the Pickett tower.
