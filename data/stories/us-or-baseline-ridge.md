Baseline Ridge sits in Washington County, Oregon, about 14 miles west-northwest of Hillsboro.[^2] The Forest Lookouts record lists the site under the Oregon Department of Forestry.[^1]

A Tillamook District annual report, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, says a secondary lookout tower 70 feet tall was built in 1932 on the Scoggin-Sein Creek ridge, in section 34 of township 1 north, range 5 west.[^1] No other construction date or height appears in the sources consulted.

The same page gives an activation date of October 19, 1942, and names the Portland Filter Center.[^1]

The fate is recorded in two ways. The Forest Lookouts page marks the tower Removed, with no date or method given.[^1] The firelookout.com listing marks it Gone and describes a 1930s crow's nest.[^2] That description does not match the 70-foot tower in the 1932 report, so the two records disagree on what kind of structure stood here.

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the ridge today, or whether the site is open to the public.

[^2]: Baseline Ridge Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com, https://firelookout.com/or/baselineridge.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
