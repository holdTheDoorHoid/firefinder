The Sycamore lookout was a fire tower in Cheatham County, Tennessee.[^1] Tennessee Landforms gives its elevation as 679 feet, or about 207 meters, and lists it under the number 6-9, marked removed.[^2] Landforms explains that its table numbers are district-and-tower codes from a 1976 state forest list, and it links them to the Division of Forestry's 208 tower sites of about 1976.[^2]

The Tennessee Division of Forestry (TDF) built 208 fire lookout towers from about 1930 to about 1970, according to the National Register form for those towers.[^3]

The Forest Lookouts page for Sycamore gives the county and no further details.[^1]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the Sycamore site, or whether the public can visit it. Please do not assume a tower stands there.
