A Georgia lookout list posted on tnlandforms.us places a lookout at a point it calls Homerville, in Clinch County. The list's unlabelled number for the point is 173, which this record reads as feet of elevation (52.7 m).[^1] The list was last revised in March 2016.[^1]

A Wikimedia Commons photograph dated 7 June 2014 is captioned as a Georgia Forestry Commission tower in Homerville, Clinch County.[^2] Its recorded camera position lies north-west of the list's point, so the photo may not show the exact listed site.[^2][^1]

A 2018 Vanishing Georgia post describes a Homerville building that was once the Timber Protection Organization office. The post says the building was then home to the Ware Visiting Nurses Service.[^3] Readers commenting on the post add memories that are not verified. One recalls a watch tower on the same lot. Another commenter links the TPO to the Georgia Forest Commission and says the Commission also had a fire tower. A third commenter says the old office appears to sit at the junction of Cogdell Highway and Highway 84.[^3]

The 2014 photo caption is the latest dated source we found that describes a standing tower here.[^2] No source describes what remains of the tower today, or whether the site is open to the public.
