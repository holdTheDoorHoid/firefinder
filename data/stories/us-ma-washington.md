The Whitney family built a 90-foot stone tower in 1896 on their estate.[^1] That estate is now October Mountain State Forest, which is located primarily in the town of Washington.[^1][^2] The Washington Historical Commission holds a photograph titled Whitney Tower, dated about 1920 to 1925 and placed on October Mountain.[^3] The record does not say outright that it shows this tower; the match rests on the title and location.

The tower was a fire detection station for only a few years. Its staffing ended in 1914, when a new tower went up at a different spot.[^1]

The original tower was still a public viewing spot until 1946, when it was judged unsafe and taken down.[^1]

In 2005, New England FFLA members (the Forest Fire Lookout Association) surveyed the land and located the former tower site. Four very large footings remain there, in a clearing on land the State of Massachusetts owns.[^1]

The forest came into state ownership through the Whitney estate, and the first 11,000 acres were bought in 1915. Public access began that same year.[^2] The footings sit on state land, but no source we read gives a route to them, so ask the forest before you go looking.[^1]
