The Perkinstown Lookout Tower stands in Taylor County, Wisconsin, on Forest Road 121.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts site dates it to 1934, when a 100-foot steel tower was erected.[^2] The state's historic property record calls it a 100-foot fire tower with a communications building at its base.[^1] We found no source that says who designed it or why it was built.

In December 2015 the Star News of Medford reported that the Perkinstown and Rib River towers were staffed 15 to 20 days each spring, which the paper tied to the highest fire risk.[^3] One of the last two fire spotters in Taylor County had worked the Perkinstown tower for nine seasons.[^3] We found no record of a particular fire the tower spotted.

The Star News placed the tower in the Chequamegon National Forest and said the Medford branch of the DNR managed it.[^3] The Forest Lookouts site lists the agency as the national forest's Medford District instead.[^2] The two sources disagree about who ran the tower.

The Star News said a December 16 press release laid out the DNR's plan to close every remaining state fire tower.[^3] WXPR reported that the expense of replacing the towers and modern technology were among the reasons for the decision.[^4] A 2019 chapter report described the DNR's tower divestment as underway and said it was not yet clear whether the agency would keep any of the towers.[^6]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list shows the tower as standing.[^5] We found no recent report on its condition and no current information on public access, so ask the Medford district before going near it.
