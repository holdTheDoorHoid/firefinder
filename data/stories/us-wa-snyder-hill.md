The lookout stood on Snyder Hill's east ridge, close to the summit, in Colville National Forest.[^1][^3] The site lies about five miles northeast of Ione, in Pend Oreille County.[^1][^2]

From about 1937 the site held a 10-foot pole lookout with an L-4 cabin.[^1][^2][^3] A platform patrol tower stood half a mile west, on a knob of the west ridge.[^1][^3] Aerial pictures from 1955 still show the cab.[^3]

The lookout came down in 1958. firelookout.com and Willhite Web say it was destroyed that year, while Former Fire Lookout Sites says it was removed.[^1][^2][^3] Rex's Fire Lookout Page also marks it as removed.[^4]

Willhite Web's recent photos show four footings, part of a stove, a stovepipe still standing, and many anchors around the site.[^3] No source describes anything else standing on the ridge. A phone line once ran south from the lookout toward Molybdenite Mountain.[^3] A longtime owner of valley property told Willhite Web the line survives on his land.[^3]

The summit is wooded and offers no views, Willhite Web says, while the former lookout sites around it have partial views in two directions.[^3]

To get there, drive over the Ione bridge spanning the Pend Oreille River, then go 1.4 miles on Sullivan Lake Road to Snyder Hill Road, FR 050.[^3] The gravel road runs 8.7 miles before reaching a ridge junction, and the final mile, overgrown in places, is walked.[^3] Two gates on the road are subject to seasonal closure.[^3] Willhite Web suggests going between July and August 14, when both gates are open.[^3]
