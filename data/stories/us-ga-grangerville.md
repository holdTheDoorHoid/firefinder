The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers has a "grangerville" entry in Wayne County, at coordinates 31.48164, -81.73453.[^1] It gives an elevation of 78. The list prints no unit, so that figure reads as feet.[^1]

The entry's last field is "gfc-808." The page's "More info" list describes its tower list as giving a "GFC number," and the page links to the Georgia Forestry Commission, so the label is probably a Forestry Commission number.[^1] Names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own creation, so "grangerville" may be a label rather than a name the tower carried.[^1]

No source I could read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
