Bluehill Pond appears on the Canada Lookouts index under the heading for Newfoundland lookouts. The page gives only the name. It has no construction date, design or history.[^1]

Terra Nova National Park sits in eastern Newfoundland beside the Trans Canada Highway and was established in 1957.[^2] A 2023 travel blog describes a fire tower at Ochre Hill in the park. It also describes a nearby Blue Hill Lookout, where visitors can sit in Parks Canada red chairs and take in the views, and it names Blue Hill with Ochre Hill, Salton's Brook and Sandy Pond in a passage on the park's dark-sky status.[^2]

No source found describes a tower at the Bluehill Pond site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1]
