Mount Seneca is a 2,106-foot (642 m) summit in Cattaraugus County.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's New York list shows a platform tower on Mount Seneca and marks it gone.[^1]

The park's Wikipedia article describes three fire lookout towers, Summit, Mt. Tuscarora and Mt. Irvine, and it does not mention a Mount Seneca tower.[^3] The article also says all the fire towers were given up for aerial surveillance in 1971.[^3] It lists a Bear Caves trail to Mt. Seneca, 4.0 miles long.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains of the Mount Seneca platform today. The Bear Caves trail is listed, but none of the sources says whether any structure or view is still there.
