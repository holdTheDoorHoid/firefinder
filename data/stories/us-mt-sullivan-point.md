Sullivan Point is a lookout site in Ravalli County, one mile west of Victor, at an elevation of 3,500 feet.[^1] The firelookout.com page calls it a 1930s observation tower that was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1] That page gives only decades for both dates, so the exact years are not known from these sources.[^1]

A Forest Lookouts page lists it as a private co-operator site under the land description 8N-21W-36, and sorts it under Ravalli County.[^2] Both pages describe it as a private site in Ravalli County.[^1][^2]

The firelookout.org list gives its coordinates as 46.41069, -114.16477 and marks the tower as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can go there.
