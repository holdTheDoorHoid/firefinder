The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower named Zion, Mount, in Trinity County, Texas, and marks it as gone.[^1] A Texas lookout-tower list gives the same county and coordinates under the name "mt zion."[^2] A gazetteer entry places a Mount Zion Lookout in Trinity, Texas, at 31.17741 north and 94.93604 west, with an elevation of 318 feet.[^3]

No source we read says what, if anything, stands at the tower's listed spot, or whether that spot is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3]

[^2]: Texas lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/txtowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Mount Zion Lookout, Mapcarta, https://mapcarta.com/21772004 (accessed 2026-10-21).
