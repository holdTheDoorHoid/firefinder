Lejeune is listed as a lookout tower in Onslow County, North Carolina, on tnlandforms.us's list of North Carolina lookout towers. The list places it at 34.61083 N, 77.28972 W.[^1]

A Waymarking.com listing is titled Lejeune Fire Tower.[^2] The name matches Camp Lejeune, a Marine Corps installation in North Carolina. Wikipedia dates the approval of its construction to April 1941, on land in Onslow County.[^3] No source we read ties this tower to the base.

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the tower today, or whether the public may visit the site.
