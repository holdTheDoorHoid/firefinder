The Chunklick Lookout is a tower in Harlan County, Kentucky, at an elevation of about 3,419 feet (1,042 meters). The National Historic Lookout Register lists it as US 1015, KY 9.[^1] The sources do not say when the tower was built, who built it, or who looks after it today.

The Register's entry rests mainly on one visit, made on August 6, 2014, by Robert Wittenback, who also nominated the tower. The Register says the tower was still standing as of August 2014 but was in poor shape: heavily rusted, with most of its wooden stairs and cabin floor gone. The same visit noted that the tower needed paint, that nearby electronic sites were encroaching on it, and that logging and surface mining were happening in the immediate area.[^1]

That visit also recorded that the tower was not staffed, with no Register sign posted and no signed directions to the tower.[^1]

No source describes the fires the tower watched, its daily work, or any later repair, closure or removal. A Forest Lookouts page for Chunklick gives only the county heading and no history.[^2]

The Register accepted the tower on September 2, 2014. The most recent condition report in the sources is from that August visit, so the tower's current state is unknown.[^1] The sources do not say whether the public can reach or climb it. Given the missing steps the 2014 visit described, anyone planning a trip should first learn who manages the site and whether it is open to visitors, and should not climb the tower.
