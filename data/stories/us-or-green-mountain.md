Green Mountain's lookout stood about 10 miles east of Seaside in Clatsop County, at an elevation of 2,044 feet.[^1] Firelookout.com dates the station to 1933, when a 40-foot pole observation tower and a cabin went up.[^1]

The sources disagree about who ran it.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page names the Oregon Department of Forestry and gives the site's survey location as 6N-8W-8.[^2] Firelookout.com names the Northwest Oregon FPA instead.[^1]

Firelookout.com says the lookout was abandoned in 1965.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page marks it as removed, without a date.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list shows the tower as gone.[^3]

A different Green Mountain in Clatsop County, about nine miles west of Vernonia, has its own history, so do not mix the two.[^4]

No source we found describes what remains on this summit today, or whether it is open to the public. Check access with the managing agency before going.
