Look Rock Observation Tower stands in Great Smoky Mountains National Park, reached from Foothills Parkway West.[^1] In January and February 1966, newspaper reports said Hart Construction Co. of Sevierville was apparently the low bidder on a fire observation tower on Look Rock, and that the Department of the Interior had then awarded Hart the contract. The tower was to sit near the parkway in the Blount County section.[^2] A 2024 article says the tower was constructed in 1967.[^3]

A National Park Service plan for the Foothills Parkway describes it as an observation and fire lookout tower on Chillhowee Mountain.[^4] Its National Register record gives a period of significance of 1966 to 1967, lists it on October 12, 2017, and files it under the park's own historic resources.[^5] A 2014 study of Tennessee fire towers, which covers mostly the state's Division of Forestry towers, still lists it among the surviving ones.[^6] The two groupings differ, so these sources do not make clear which program the tower belonged to.

Visitors reach the tower on a trail of about 0.8 miles round trip from the Look Rock Tower Trailhead; the park describes the trail as paved but steep.[^1] A parking tag is needed to park for more than 15 minutes.[^1] The tower also serves as a site for air quality monitoring and houses park radio system equipment.[^3] The nearby campground and picnic area had been closed for several years for lack of funding and had only just reopened when the 2024 article appeared.[^3]

The sources I reached do not describe the tower's staffing or any recent closure, so check with the park before planning a visit.
