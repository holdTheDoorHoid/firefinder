Bland Mountain lies about 2.5 miles east of Days Creek in Douglas County.[^2] The Douglas FPA planned a supplementary lookout there in 1941, and by 1942 the site had a tent camp.[^2] A 1944 association report said the site had been used as a lookout for three years, that the man there had been living in a tent, and asked for a 14-by-14-foot house on a 30-foot tower as the standard design.[^2]

A 14-by-14-foot cab went onto a timber tower in 1947. The register says that tower was 25 feet, while Forest Lookouts says 20 feet.[^1][^2] The catwalk and roof were replaced in 1967.[^2]

Sources differ on the next big change. The register and firelookout.com date the change to 1996, when the cab was lowered onto a recycled 20-foot metal tower.[^1][^3] Forest Lookouts and Cheryl Hill's 2015 visit date the new tower to 1997, built from a water tower salvaged from the state forestry office in Canyonville.[^2][^4] The register lists the lookout as registered on March 31, 2003.[^1]

The register's description says the Douglas association staffs it.[^1] Firelookout.com reports summer staffing into the late 2000s, and says the site is now on emergency status.[^3]

When Cheryl Hill visited in June 2015, the lookout was not regularly staffed and the cab was boarded up.[^4] The approach runs 2.3 miles along BLM Road 7.0 from Days Creek Road, then a gated spur road and about half a mile of uphill walking.[^4] The Cheryl Hill standing list shows the site as accessible to the public.[^5] The cab is on emergency status, so please keep off the tower and treat the cab as closed.[^3][^5]
