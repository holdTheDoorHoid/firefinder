Denaro is an unincorporated rural settlement in the south-central portion of Amelia County, Virginia.[^1] Early in the twentieth century, it served as a post village.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list gives a tower called Denaro at 37.24910, -77.99065 and marks it as gone.[^2] Visitors should not confuse it with the Amelia Tower Site. The Association gives that site different coordinates, 37.33242, -77.98437, and its register page gives 1965 as the build year and about 2004 to 2005 as the removal period.[^2][^3]

No source I could read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
