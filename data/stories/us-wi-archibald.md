The Archibald lookout tower stood in Oconto County, Wisconsin, at the land description 32N-15E-11.[^1] The Central US Lookouts directory lists the Chequamegon-Nicolet National Forest as the agency.[^1] A map site places the tower in the Doty area, near Lakewood.[^2]

The directory lists the tower as removed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin county list marks it gone.[^3]

The OpenStreetMap node for the tower is tagged as an observation tower with an elevation of 435 meters, and it sits at 45.2627464, -88.5867724.[^4] The county list gives the site as 45.26287, -88.58689.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
