Humboldt Mountain Lookout sits in the Tonto National Forest in Maricopa County, with the Cave Creek Ranger District as its cooperating district.[^1] The FAA built the lookout in 1958 to Forest Service specifications, beside a much larger air traffic radar dome.[^1] The register says a CL-100 R-6 metal flattop cab with a catwalk took the place of a 1940 L-4 cab that stood on a stone foundation.[^1]

The register gives the lookout a registration date of July 20, 2002.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^3]

The Forest Service records a 2023 project on Humboldt Mountain for an AT&T FirstNet communications site.[^2] The proposal would add 20 feet to the tower and install an equipment cabinet and a propane generator, and the decision was signed on March 2, 2023.[^2] The project page lists its status as Completed.[^2]

The register describes the station as in active service, but these sources do not say whether anyone staffs the lookout or whether visitors can reach it.[^1] I found no 2026 notice on staffing or access. Because the mountain also carries a communications site, check with the Cave Creek Ranger District before you go, and do not climb the tower.[^2]
