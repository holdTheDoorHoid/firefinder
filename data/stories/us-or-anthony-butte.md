The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register says a 30-foot pole L-4 tower was built on Anthony Butte in 1937.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the same height and date.[^3] The butte sits in Union County, about 20 miles south-southwest of La Grande, within the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest.[^3]

Two local papers from September 1933 describe a 14-by-14-foot lookout house being built on the butte.[^2] The Baker Democrat-Herald put the tower at 35 feet, and the Record-Courier at 30.[^2] The sources do not agree on the date, and none says whether the 1933 tower is the one the 1937 date describes.[^1][^2][^3]

In July 1933 a Baker paper said Dean Bidwell would be running the station that summer.[^2] Firelookout.com names Neil Taylor for 1940 and Maurice Larive for 1942.[^3]

An AWS cabin was added in 1942 and a cupola trailer was set up in 1975.[^1][^3] Both the register and firelookout.com say the lookout was gone by 1977.[^1][^3] Rex's page marks the site only as "Removed."[^2]

The register includes a photograph captioned as the Anthony Butte site in 2018, credited to Rex Kamstra, but the caption does not say what it shows.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, is on the summit today, or how to reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
