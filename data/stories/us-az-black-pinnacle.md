Black Pinnacle Lookout sits on a black rock pinnacle overlooking the north end of Canyon de Chelly National Monument, in Apache County, Arizona.[^1][^2] The register describes a wooden ground house, with metal steps climbing the rock to reach it.[^1]

The register dates the site to 1936 and says it still serves as a major detection point.[^1] The register lists the Navajo Indian Reservation as the administering body, with the Bureau of Indian Affairs as a cooperator, and records its registration on July 20, 2002.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Arizona list shows the lookout as a standing ground lookout.[^3]

I did not find a recent staffing report, access rules or rental information for the site. Because it lies on Navajo land, anyone interested should ask the administering office before planning anything.[^1]
