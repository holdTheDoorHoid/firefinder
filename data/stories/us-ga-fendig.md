The Fendig lookout tower is listed in Brantley County, Georgia, at 31.33780, -81.83887, with an elevation of 82, which we take to be feet (25 m) because the list states no unit.[^1] The Georgia lookout list that includes it was last revised in March 2016 and draws on USGS name databases and topographic maps.[^1]

No source we found describes the site today, so what remains there and whether it is open to the public are unknown.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
