The Stone Mountain lookout stood in Guildhall, in Essex County, Vermont.[^1] In August 1922 the Bennington Evening Banner reported that work had begun on two fire towers and their cabins in Essex County, one of them a tower to be built new on Stone Mountain in Guildhall.[^1] The paper said the tower would give watchers a view of wooded country that no other tower could see.[^1]

A state report for the term ending June 1938 says work began on a new lookout cabin with a log shelter, fireplaces and tables, and that the old cabin was moved to serve as wood storage.[^1] A biennial report covering 1949 and 1950 says the tower's ventilator was replaced with a weatherproof cap.[^1]

A 1948 item in the Essex County Herald says a man was employed at the fire tower, and that his family spent the summer at Stone Mountain with him.[^1] In May 1971 the News and Citizen reported that a woman starting a state job would be stationed on Stone Mountain and would run the fire tower.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Stone Mountain tower as gone.[^2] It also lists a relocated site at West Topsham, in Orange County, as a radio tower, also gone.[^2] The directory gives no date or reason for either change.[^2]

Beyond a status line, the directory gives no description of either site and says nothing about visitor access.[^2]
