Benton is a fire tower in Benton County, Mississippi, and the Forest Lookouts page for it names the Mississippi Forest Service.[^1] A National Geodetic Survey data sheet for the station describes the structure as a metal cab on a four-legged skeleton frame, 110 feet (33.5 m) high, and credits a 1966 description by the US Coast and Geodetic Survey.[^2] The sheet says the tower belongs to the Mississippi Forest Service, but the road sign it describes reads Mississippi Forestry Commission-Benton.[^2] The sheet's 1966 date is when that survey first observed the station; it gives no build date for the tower.[^2] The station description puts the tower 0.2 mile south of US Highway 72.[^1]

The sheet's history also logs a 2009 condition report that rates the station good, but nothing I found says whether the tower still stands or whether visitors can reach the site.[^2]

[^2]: NGS Data Sheet EG1649, Benton Fire Tower, National Geodetic Survey, NOAA, https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=EG1649 (accessed 2026-10-21).
