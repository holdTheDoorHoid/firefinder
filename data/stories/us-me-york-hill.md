York Hill in Franklin County is on the Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of standing towers.[^1] That list is the only source I found that says a structure is still up there. It gives the point at 44.59676 north and 69.97355 west.[^1]

Another York Hill, a summit near Weld, is listed at 1,440 feet and sits about 33 kilometers west of that point, slightly north of due west, by my own calculation from the two coordinates.[^2] The name alone does not settle which hill the tower is on.

The Eastern US Lookouts page for Maine has a York Hill entry in Franklin County, but it gives no build date, height or history.[^3]

I found no build date, design, owner, staffing or access rules, and no newspaper or Forest Service account of the tower. Treat this record as thin until someone checks the site or a Maine Forest Service map.

[^2]: York Hill in Franklin County ME (Weld Area), MountainZone, https://www.mountainzone.com/mountains/maine/franklin-me/summits/york-hill/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^3]: Maine - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/maine.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
