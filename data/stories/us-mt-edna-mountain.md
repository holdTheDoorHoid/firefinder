Edna Mountain is a Lincoln County site in the Kootenai National Forest.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists its position as 48.69140 north and 114.96767 west.[^2]

A short listing dates a pole tower on the mountain to 1937.[^1] The same listing gives 1958 as the year the tower was gone.[^1]

The association's Montana list still shows the tower as gone.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, is left on the summit today, or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Edna Mtn. Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/ednamtn.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
