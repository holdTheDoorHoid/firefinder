Trail Butte is in Coos County, on the western edge of the Elliott State Forest.[^1][^2][^3] Sources disagree about when the first lookout went up. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says it was built in the 1920s and abandoned in 1965,[^2] and Rex's Fire Lookout Page describes a 1920s cupola cabin.[^3]

The clearest record is from 1942. A May 1942 Forest Log item said work on a new lookout house, 16 by 16 feet with a glassed cupola, was about to start, and a road to the site had been finished.[^1] A 1942 Oregon Department of Forestry annual report says the association built it and that the Air Warning Service paid for the ground cabin.[^1] A 1943 Coos Bay Times item credits the CCC with completing it instead.[^1]

Newspaper items name lookouts there in 1945, 1950 and 1954.[^1] In August 1953 lightning set off at least five fires in the protective association's area, and the Trail Butte station recorded 26 strikes in sight of its tower.[^1]

The register and Rex's page both give 1965 as the year it was abandoned.[^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts page says it was destroyed that year instead.[^1]

A web history of the Elliott forest's lookouts says the forest had four fire lookouts and that only one remains, the Cougar Pass tower, as a dilapidated shell.[^4] The other three are known now only from a few old photos, recollections and written accounts.[^4] The same page mentions the old Trail Butte lookout ground house in a 1956 photo caption, but does not say whether Trail Butte was one of the four.[^4]

No source I found describes what remains at the Trail Butte site, or whether visitors may go there.
