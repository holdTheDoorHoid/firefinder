The Boulder Lookout Tower was in Vilas County.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it at 46.10659, -89.71434 and marks it as gone.[^2] No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^2]: Wisconsin Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
