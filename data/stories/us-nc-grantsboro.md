The statewide list of North Carolina lookout towers places a tower named Grantsboro in Pamlico County, at 35.12972, -76.84083.[^1]

A 1999 House bill, Pamlico Fire Funds, proposed $40,000 for the 1999-2000 fiscal year, to go to the Pamlico County Fireman's Association for a fire training tower and for grading its training site.[^2] The bill describes a firefighter training structure, not a lookout, and it does not name Grantsboro as the site.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Grantsboro entry's location today, or whether the spot is open to the public.

[^2]: Pamlico Fire Funds (House Bill 673, 1999), North Carolina General Assembly, https://library.ncleg.net/Sessions/1999/Bills/House/PDF/H673v1.pdf (accessed 2026-10-21).
