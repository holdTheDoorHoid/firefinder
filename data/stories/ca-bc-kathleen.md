Mount Kathleen is a little-known mountain in the hills above Summerland, British Columbia.[^1] Canada's Fire Lookouts lists a Kathleen lookout in its British Columbia index.[^2]

A hiking blog says the Kathleen fire lookout went up in 1954 and was abandoned within about two decades.[^1] The same blog says the tower deteriorated and was eventually taken apart.[^1] In the early 1990s, a local snowmobile club put up a shelter on the lookout's old foundation.[^1]

The blog does not say whether that shelter still stands. No other source read describes the site today.

According to the blog, forestry and gravel roads lead in from Peachland or Summerland.[^1] The blog says most of the way up can be driven, and that with the right choice of route an SUV can get within about two kilometres of the summit.[^1] Hiking there mostly means following ATV tracks, and the blog strongly suggests a mountain bike for visitors who travel under their own power.[^1] No source read names the land manager or describes gates or closures, so check the land status before you go.

[^2]: British Columbia Index, Canada's Fire Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/british-columbia-index.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
