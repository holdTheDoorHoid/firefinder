A Central US Lookouts page for Morris places the tower in Shawano County, Wisconsin.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list gives its point as 44.82051, -89.07102 and marks the tower gone.[^2]

OpenStreetMap has a node named Morris Lookout Tower, tagged as an observation tower.[^3]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
