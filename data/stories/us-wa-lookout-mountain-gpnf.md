Lookout Mountain rises to 5,475 feet in Lewis County, Washington, about 10 miles south of Mount Rainier.[^1][^2] The register gives the tower's height as 10 feet and calls it a wooden L-4 tower, built in 1932.[^1][^2]

Sources disagree about the forest. The register and firelookout.com place the site in the Gifford Pinchot National Forest, while an index on washingtonlookouts.weebly.com lists it under the Snoqualmie National Forest.[^1][^2][^3] The register names the U.S. Forest Service as the administering agency.[^1]

The tower was gone by 1968. The register says it was removed that year, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's regional list also marks the tower as gone.[^4]

The sources say nothing about what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
