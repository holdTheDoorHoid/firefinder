Black Rock Mountain stands in Trinity County, in the Shasta-Trinity National Forest.[^1] A 2025 hike report places the lookout in the Yolla Bolly-Middle Eel Wilderness.[^4] The sources give the summit as 7,765 feet, 7,769 feet and 7,792 feet, so the exact figure is not settled.[^1][^2][^3]

According to the register, the Trinity National Forest had the lookout put up in 1935.[^1] The register describes a cabin built to California Region 5 plan BC-301, raised on a 20-foot open timber tower.[^1]

The early staffing record comes from newspaper items.[^2] In the summer of 1932, G. H. McCausland was stationed at the lookout, and the station closed for the season that October.[^2] A 1933 item says Leon D. Duey succeeded the previous lookout.[^2] In 1936 the lookout logged 25 visitors between June 22 and October 20, and 14 of them were Forest Service employees.[^2] In June 1946 a paper said Ora Snyder had been a lookout there for two years before moving to a clerk-typist post.[^2]

The sources we found do not record a fire that struck the lookout itself. A 2025 hike report crosses ground that burned in the 2020 August Complex on the way up, but it does not mention the tower burning.[^4]

In 2017 the register judged the structure to be in poor condition, though it still stood.[^1] Photos from August 2005 describe it as abandoned and in bad shape, and a caption says the tower is not very safe to climb.[^3] The register recorded it on April 24, 2020, as US 1419, CA 161, after a nomination by Brad Eells.[^1]

A May 2025 hike report found the lookout dilapidated.[^4] Snow still half buried the stairs, which fell well short of the platform, and much of the platform sat around the bottom of the struts rather than the top.[^4] The writer turned back without climbing it.[^4]

The 2025 hike reached it on foot from the West Low Gap Trailhead.[^4] The lookout sits on National Forest land that the U.S. Forest Service administers.[^4][^1] Our sources say it is not very safe to climb, so please keep off the structure and check with the Shasta-Trinity office before planning a trip.[^3][^4]
