The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Charles City (Roaches Corner) tower in Charles City County as standing, placed at about 37.39 north and 77.05 west.[^1] Its entry gives no National Historic Lookout Register number, build date, builder or staffing details.[^1][^2] I could not find a readable history for the site, so I cannot say when the tower went up, who built it, or whether anyone watched from it for smoke. The listing is the only record I could read, so its status is the association's view rather than a fresh inspection, and it does not say whether the tower is public, private or closed.[^1] Anyone curious should ask the landowner or county officials before going to look, and should never climb a tower without permission.

[^1]: Virginia - Forest Fire Lookout Association, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Virginia - National Historic Lookout Register, National Historic Lookout Register, https://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
