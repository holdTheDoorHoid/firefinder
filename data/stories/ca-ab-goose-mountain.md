The Goose Mountain Ecological Reserve sits about 43.5 km north-west of Swan Hills, Alberta.[^3] OpenStreetMap, the community-edited map, has a point called Goose Mountain Fire Lookout at about 54.75 N, 116.03 W.[^4] The edit comment on that entry labels it a fire and forest tower located near Swan Hills.[^4]

Alberta Parks says the public reaches the reserve by the Goose Mountain Fire Tower road from Swan Hills, and warns that the road becomes very hard going when wet.[^1] The Town of Swan Hills describes that route as a private oilfield road that weather can affect.[^2] The reserve has no facilities, and overnight camping, open fires and motorized vehicles are not allowed there.[^1]

No source we read describes the fire tower itself today. None says whether visitors may climb it, so this story does not say the tower can be visited.
