Green Mountain sits in the Nez Perce National Forest, in Idaho County, Idaho.[^1][^2] The first structure on the site was a log cabin with a cupola, built around 1930.[^1][^2][^3] The tower that stands there now was built in 1956. It is 41 feet of treated timber with an L-4 cab.[^1][^4][^5]

Idaho Fire Lookouts dates the switch to emergency status to 1971.[^3] The National Historic Lookout Register says it has not been regularly staffed since the early 1970s, but the lookout is often used on an emergency basis because of the area's high fire frequency.[^1]

The Forest Service history says the L-4 is maintained for emergency use.[^5] Firelookout.com says the tower serves emergency fire detection all summer long.[^4] The lookout was registered on the National Historic Lookout Register on August 25, 1997.[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as Drive-Up.[^3] When its writers visited in 2020, the tower was staffed because of a nearby fire.[^3] That was a single visit during a fire, so it does not show that the tower is open to visitors. The sources do not give a rental arrangement. Check with the Nez Perce National Forest before going, since the tower is kept for emergency use.
