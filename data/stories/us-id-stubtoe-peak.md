Stubtoe Peak is a lookout site in the St. Joe National Forest, at 5,700 feet.[^1][^2] The earliest entry in the site history is a crow's nest in 1918. A cabin with a pole tower followed in 1928.[^1][^2]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site lists three people who staffed the lookout. It gives John "Red" Dennis for 1919, and names Ray Darby and Mark Oliver without years. All three come from Bert Russell's book Hardship & Happy Times.[^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1955.[^1][^2] The Idaho Fire Lookouts site labels the site drive-up.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the tower as gone.[^3]

None of the sources I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2][^3] With the tower gone, there is no lookout to climb.[^2]
