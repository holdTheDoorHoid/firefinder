Heards Mountain sits in southern Albemarle County, Virginia, and Civilian Conservation Corps crews helped put a fire tower on it.[^1][^2] Camp Albemarle, a CCC camp at White Hall, was set up in 1933 and ended operations in 1942.[^1] Its engineer, J. Harvey Bailey, said the camp put much of its effort into fighting forest fires, building truck trails and upgrading secondary public roads and bridges to reach remote peaks and valleys.[^1]

The camp's early projects included an 80-foot steel fire tower on Carter's Mountain near Monticello, and crews also put up comparable towers on Heards Mountain.[^1][^2] Crews ran telephone wire through the woods to connect those installations with fire wardens and crew members.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list marks the Heards Mountain tower as gone.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today.[^1][^2][^3]
