Cold Springs Lookout stood eight miles northwest of Riggins, Idaho, in the Nez Perce National Forest, at 6,927 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

The site began in 1930 with a 14-by-14-foot log cabin.[^1][^3] In 1958 a 41-foot treated timber tower with an R-6 cab was built.[^1][^2][^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page labels the site drive-up.[^2]

The Forest Service list says the cabin was removed and that the tower was destroyed in 1979.[^3] Two other pages say the tower was torn down that year.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the tower as gone.[^4]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to the public. Check with the Nez Perce National Forest before going.
