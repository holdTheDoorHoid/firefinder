Brown Creek #2 stood in Clearwater County, Idaho, at about 4,279 feet.[^2][^3] Its earliest record is a log cabin with a crow's nest, dated 1913.[^1][^2] In 1934 a 40-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab went up at the south end of the ridge.[^1][^2] That same year a patrol point about a mile to the south-southeast, a 135-foot tree platform, was abandoned.[^1][^2]

Records place the lookout with the Clearwater Timber Protective Association, and a county index gives its survey location as 34N-5E-03.[^3] The tower was destroyed in the 1960s.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks it as gone and gives its position as 46.32278 north, 115.82422 west.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts marks the site as drive-up, but no source describes what remains on the ridge today.[^2]
