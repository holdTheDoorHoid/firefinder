Boardman Airport lies about four miles southwest of the city of Boardman in Morrow County, Oregon.[^2] The Army Air Forces built the field about 1942, and it was known as the Boardman Flight Strip.[^2] Military aircraft on training flights from the Boardman Bombing Range could land there in an emergency.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a "Beacon Tower" at the airport, marked as standing and described as relocated from Malheur National Wildlife Refuge.[^1] We could not confirm that move from any other source. The listed point also sits about 1.5 km east of the airport's published coordinates, so it may mark a structure near the field rather than the field itself.[^1][^2]

The refuge does have lookout towers of its own. The Oregon Hikers field guide describes two Civilian Conservation Corps lookouts there, both dating from the 1930s.[^3] One overlooks the refuge headquarters, and the other stands at P Ranch near Frenchglen.[^3]

The airport is operated by the Port of Morrow.[^2] We found no report of when a tower might have moved, who owns it, or whether the public may visit it, so treat the Boardman entry as unverified.
