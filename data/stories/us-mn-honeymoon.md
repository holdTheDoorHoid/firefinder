Honeymoon was a stairway lookout on Honeymoon Mountain in Cook County, near Honeymoon Creek, and the county inventory lists the U.S. Forest Service as its owner.[^1] The inventory gives its height as probably around 100 feet.[^1] It also records a 1952 survey note describing the tower as approximately 125 feet high, with four legs and steel construction, so the two figures disagree.[^1]

The inventory marks the tower as removed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list also shows it as gone.[^2]

No source we checked describes the Honeymoon Mountain site today.
