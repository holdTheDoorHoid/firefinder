The Adams Creek lookout was a mountain cabin in Alberta, and the name now covers a newer cabin as well.[^1] A visitor's photo caption dates the Adams Creek Fire Lookout to 1940.[^3] Alberta's register gives 2005 as the construction year of the active Adams Creek cabin, a 5 m (16 ft) structure at 2,177.6 m (7,144 ft).[^2]

A 2004 Alberta report, written as a letter, says the old cabin had been replaced years ago by a newer structure. The forest service then removed it to the grounds of the Grande Cache museum, where it was to be renovated and put on display.[^1] The letter says the forest service is making real progress in recognising its history, because the old removal method would have been simply to "torch" the structure.[^1]

A visitor's photo caption places the old cabin at Bird's Eye View Interpretive Park, not at the museum grounds the 2004 letter names.[^3][^1]

Today the register still lists the mountain cabin as active, with a weather station on site. Its access mode is recorded as "fly", which points to access by air.[^2]
