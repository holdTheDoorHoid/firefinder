A list of Georgia fire towers that tnlandforms.us published, last revised in March 2016, places a tower labelled "tifton" (list number GA207) in Tift County, at 31.49091, -83.4388.[^1] The list gives its elevation as 364 with no unit; read as feet, that is about 111 m above sea level.[^1] The page says its author made up the names for towers numbered 73 and above, and GA207 falls in that range, so "tifton" is a label rather than a documented name.[^1]

Georgia has kept reducing the staff on its fire towers since the late 1980s.[^2] A Georgia Forestry Commission report from 1987 counted 93 towers with full-time staff and 73 with seasonal staff.[^2] A 2006 article drew on a forestry commission spreadsheet to report that 137 of the state's 144 surviving towers were unstaffed.[^2] The same article reported that towers were being sold at auction, and that some were sold as scrap metal.[^2]

No source describes what remains at this site today, or whether the public can visit it.
