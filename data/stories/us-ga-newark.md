The only source found for the Newark tower is a compiled list of Georgia towers. It places the tower in Thomas County at 30.81477, -83.90993.[^1] Newark is number 248 on that list, and the page says that names for towers numbered 73 and above are the author's own creation, so the name may not be a historic one.[^1] The page's map was revised on 22 March 2016.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 29 registered Georgia towers, and Newark is not one of them.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists two Georgia entries, neither of them Newark.[^3]

No source found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.

[^2]: Georgia, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ga/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Georgia, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ga/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
