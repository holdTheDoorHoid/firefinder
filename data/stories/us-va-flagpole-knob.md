Flagpole Knob is a summit in Rockingham County, Virginia, at 4,190 feet (1,277 m).[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts website lists it among its Virginia lookouts, with Rockingham County as the location.[^1]

Forest Road 85 covers 3.6 miles to the summit, along the top of the Alleghany Mountains, where the ridge follows the Virginia and West Virginia border.[^2] The Virginia Department of Wildlife Resources, which lists the summit on its wildlife trail, describes the road as steep, rocky and deeply rutted, and says only experienced off-road drivers should attempt it.[^2] Visitors can park at Briery Branch Gap and walk or bike the 7.2-mile round trip, which the same page calls highly technical and strenuous.[^2]

The U.S. Forest Service's North River Ranger District manages the site, and the page lists the access requirement as free and daily.[^2]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains of a lookout on the summit today.
