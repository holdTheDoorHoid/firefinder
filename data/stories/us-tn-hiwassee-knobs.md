The Hiwassee Knobs Fire Tower is in Monroe County, Tennessee, and the Tennessee Division of Forestry administers it.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register records it as US 992, TN 13 and says it was registered on March 14, 2014, after a nomination by Ron Stafford.[^1]

The sources read do not say when the tower was built, what its design was, who staffed it, or which fires it watched.[^1][^2][^3]

The tower also appears as Madisonville. Tennessee Landforms lists a Madisonville tower in Monroe County, and OpenStreetMap has a node by that name about 22 metres from the Register's coordinates, which suggests the two names describe the same structure.[^3][^4][^1]

The sources also differ on its height above sea level. The Register gives 1,332 feet (406 metres), OpenStreetMap gives 410 metres, and Tennessee Landforms gives 1,383 feet.[^1][^4][^3]

Tennessee Landforms lists the Madisonville tower as standing.[^3] The Register page has no visit report, so these sources do not settle public access or climbing rules.[^1] Anyone planning a trip should check with the Division of Forestry first.
