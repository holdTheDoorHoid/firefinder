Chilcoot Mountain lies about 24 miles southwest of Oakridge, in the Umpqua National Forest of Douglas County, Oregon, at roughly 1,520 meters.[^1][^3] Forest files cited on a lookout history page record a one-room log tool house on the site in 1923.[^2] The register dates the first lookout, a six-by-six platform in a tree, to 1930, and says a tent went up close by.[^1]

A September 1930 Roseburg News-Review item described how the watch worked.[^2] The lookout spent long daily stretches watching from a nearby fir, reached by ladder, and a telephone on the small platform linked it to the forest's other stations.[^2] The register and firelookout.com both say an L-4 cab replaced the platform in 1931.[^1][^3]

Rex's page says the lookout was dismantled in 1962.[^2] The register says it was removed or destroyed in 1964, and firelookout.com says it was abandoned that year and is now gone.[^1][^3] The sources do not agree on the year the lookout came down.[^1][^2][^3]

firelookout.com places the cab at the northwest tip below the summit.[^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains there today, or whether the site is open to visitors. Check with the Umpqua National Forest before planning a trip.
