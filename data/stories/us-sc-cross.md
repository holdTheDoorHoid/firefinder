The Cross Lookout Tower was one of the South Carolina Forestry Commission's fire towers, in Berkeley County.[^1] The state's tower network began in 1930 with a tower near Camden.[^2] When a smoke was spotted, two towers each recorded its direction, and the fire lay where the readings crossed on a map.[^2]

The commission's own history dates the closing of its fire tower system to October 1, 1993.[^2] A Post and Courier history dates the system's retirement to 1994, so the two accounts differ.[^3]

A Pastmaps summary of a 2011 USGS topographic map of Cross puts the tower close to the shoreline.[^4] The Forest Lookouts site lists Cross as removed, with no date.[^1] Those two sources together do not show when the tower came down.

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the land is open to visitors.
