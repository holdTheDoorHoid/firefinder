The Brushy Mountain lookout site sits in Alleghany County, Virginia, on land in the George Washington and Jefferson National Forests.[^1] Its registry numbers are US 923 and VA 9, and the site stands at 3,015 feet (919 m).[^1]

The U.S. Forest Service built the tower in 1939.[^1] It was a CT-2 model, 40 feet high and built of wood, with a 14-by-14-foot cab, a catwalk and a shingled roof.[^1]

According to the register, the tower came down before 2011.[^1] The site was added to the register on April 10, 2011, with Ron Stafford as nominator.[^1] A second listing of Virginia lookouts still marks the tower as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today or whether the public can reach it.
