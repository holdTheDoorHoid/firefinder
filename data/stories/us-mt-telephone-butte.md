Telephone Butte rises about 14 miles west of Missoula, in Missoula County and the Lolo National Forest, at an elevation of 6,885 feet.[^1] The site's record places a 30-foot pole L-4 tower there in 1934.[^1]

The record says the tower was abandoned in 1947 and is now gone.[^1] It gives no reason for the abandonment.[^1] The state-wide list of Montana lookouts also marks the Telephone Butte tower as gone, with coordinates but no history.[^2] Only the firelookout.com record gives dates, so the 1934 and 1947 dates rest on a single source.[^1][^2]

No source read for this story describes what, if anything, remains on the butte today.[^1][^2] The sources also do not say whether the land is open to visitors, so anyone interested in the site should check with the Lolo National Forest before making a trip.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Telephone Butte Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/telephonebutte.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
