Cedro Peak rises above Cedro Canyon in the Sandia Ranger District of the Cibola National Forest, and a lookout has stood on its summit since at least 1922.[^2][^4] The register gives the first lookout as a 25-foot log tower from the 1920s. A 1935 newspaper called the old tower a 30-foot pole tower.[^2]

In 1929 the Department of Commerce got a permit to build a 40-foot steel tower topped with a beacon light, to help airplanes through the gap between the Sandia and Manzano mountains. The Forest Service was to use it as a lookout too.[^2][^4]

In 1935 a Civilian Conservation Corps crew built a new tower, a cabin and a road to the top. The register dates the replacement to that year and calls it a 45-foot metal tower.[^1][^2][^4] A 1935 newspaper said the new frame rose more than 45 feet above the summit, and a 1945 article described a 50-foot tower. The 1945 figure does not match the register's 45 feet.[^1][^2]

The lookout was staffed in fire season in the 1940s.[^2] In 1945 a chief lookout kept watch with an assistant, a dog named Bing.[^2] In May 1946 fire guards went on duty there for the season.[^2] Water still had to be packed in, the 1935 coverage notes.[^2]

In 1969 AT&T erected a large transmission tower at the peak. The current 14-by-14-foot cab sits on top of the new electronics building beside it.[^1][^3][^4] The register lists 1969 as the build year, but that date belongs to this cab, not the first lookout.[^1]

A 1988 thematic National Register evaluation found the lookout not eligible for listing because it was too young.[^3] A 2020 New Mexico chapter report says it should now be re-evaluated.[^3]

Today the lookout is not staffed.[^4] A personal blog says the Forest Service now tracks wilderness fires mostly by satellite, and that the lookout is closed off and the access road is gated.[^4] Until about a decade ago, the blog says, a fire watcher could be visited there.[^4] Visitors are also not allowed to use the stairway to the balcony.[^4] The blog is undated, so check the current status with the Sandia Ranger District before planning anything.
