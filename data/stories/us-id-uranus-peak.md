The tower stood in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, on Uranus Peak in Shoshone County, Idaho.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the summit about 12 miles north-northeast of Kellogg.[^1] Two sources give its elevation as 4,977 feet.[^1][^2]

Built in 1931, it was a pole tower 40 feet high, with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2][^3]

A photo in the University of Idaho's Civilian Conservation Corps collection shows four CCC men on a lookout tower.[^4] The caption on the back reads "Our fire tower on Uranus Peak."[^4] The record gives the date as approximately 1938.[^4]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page lists two known staff: Lorenzo Lanter under 1935, and Ernest Milot under 1937.[^2]

Three sources date the tower's destruction to 1950, though Forest Lookouts writes it as c.1950.[^1][^2][^3] The association's list marks the site as gone.[^5]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains on the summit. Idaho Fire Lookouts classes the site as hike-in, the only access detail we found.[^2]
