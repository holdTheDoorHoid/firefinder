Emery Hill sits at 5,406 feet, about five miles east of Hungry Horse, in the Flathead National Forest.[^1] The firelookout.com record describes a 30-foot L-4 tower dated 1932.[^1] A 1933 Flathead Courier item, quoted on a lookout history site, says 11 miles of road were planned to reach two lookout sites, one on Emory hill (the item's spelling) and one on Desert mountain.[^2] The same item says most of the crew was finishing a 1.5-mile stretch of Desert mountain road, clearing and grading, and predicts the work would help safeguard timber in the Coram experimental forest.[^2]

The firelookout.com record lists the tower as gone in 1948, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list also marks it gone.[^1][^3]

None of the three cited sources says what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
