The Little War Gap Fire Tower is in Hawkins County, Tennessee, on a rocky outcrop overlooking Little War Gap.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register gives its build year as 1947 and its elevation as 2,329 feet, or 710 meters.[^1] Tennessee Landforms lists what appears to be the same tower under the shorter name War, at 2,401 feet.[^2] Because that figure differs from the Register, we use the Register's number.

The Register also describes a watchman's cabin at the site.[^1] The sources we read do not name who built the tower, who staffed it, or any fire it watched.[^1]

A state nomination of Tennessee's fire towers includes the tower in a table of surviving towers as of March 26, 2014.[^3] Tennessee Landforms also marks it as standing.[^2] The Register says the tower and its cabin were in bad shape as of 2019, and that the site was registered on March 24, 2020.[^1]

The Register lists private owners, and we found no public access.[^1] Our advice: do not climb the tower, and ask the owners before going near it.
