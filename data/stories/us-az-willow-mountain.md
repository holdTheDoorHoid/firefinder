Willow Mountain rises to 7,817 feet in the Natanes Mountains of Graham County, Arizona, on the San Carlos Apache Reservation.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site with a two-story cab type and a Standing* status.[^2] Its key says the asterisk marks towers whose structure survives after the cab is gone.[^2] The Mountain Field Guide says remnants of the old lookout remain at the summit and that it no longer operates as a staffed tower.[^1] OpenStreetMap tags the tower as an abandoned building.[^3] Taken together, the sources point to a structure left at the top, possibly without its cab, but I could not find a recent photo to confirm its condition.

I also could not find when the lookout was built or who staffed it, so those parts of its history remain open.

Every part of the mountain sits on San Carlos Apache tribal land.[^1] Visitors need a recreation permit and must follow tribal rules before they hike, drive, camp or explore.[^1] The same guide points to an older lookout guide that calls the destination extremely isolated and suggests four-wheel drive.[^1] Check with the San Carlos Apache Tribe before you plan a trip, and do not climb any remaining structure.

[^2]: Arizona Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/az/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
