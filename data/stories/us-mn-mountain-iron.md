A fire tower called Mountain Iron once stood in St. Louis County, Minnesota.[^1] The name matches the city of Mountain Iron, which lies within Saint Louis County.[^2] A 1941 map of the Superior National Forest shows a tower under this name, and the county list calls it likely a state tower, although its owner entry carries a question mark.[^1]

The 1941 map is the earliest dated record we found for this tower.[^1] A 1948 survey note mentions an abandoned fire tower, and the county list files that note under this entry.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone and places it near 47.56 north, 92.64 west.[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.
