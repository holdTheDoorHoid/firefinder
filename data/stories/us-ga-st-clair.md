A fire lookout tower is recorded at St. Clair, in Burke County, Georgia.[^1] A list of Georgia towers gives its coordinates as 33.16881, -82.18355, and its elevation as 362 (no unit is given; read as feet, that is 110.3 m).[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register lists 29 lookouts in Georgia, and St. Clair is not among them.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists two Georgia sites, neither of them at St. Clair.[^3]

None of the sources read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether it is open to the public. Do not assume a tower stands there. Check with the landowner or the managing agency before going.
