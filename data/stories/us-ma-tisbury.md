The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists Tisbury's lookout at the Great Plains, in Dukes County.[^1] It says the original location of the tower is unknown, although it gives coordinates for that area.[^1]

In 1916, a 48-foot tower went up after fifteen acres were bought, and a mile of telephone line was run out to it.[^1] A new 68-foot tower went up in 1930.[^1] The register's last operating date for the site is 1944, when the taller tower was dismantled.[^1]

I found no source that describes the Great Plains site today, or whether the public may visit it. A February 2022 Martha's Vineyard Times report on a Vineyard Haven house fire mentions firefighters working from Tisbury's fire tower.[^2] That tower was in use that year, but the report does not say where it stands or connect it to the old lookout.[^2]
