Flagtail Mountain Lookout stands in Grant County, Oregon, on the Malheur National Forest, about 23 miles southwest of the town of John Day.[^1][^2] Its first lookout was a crow's nest in a tree, which the register dates to the early 1920s. firelookout.com dates its use as the first lookout to 1922.[^1][^2] Wikipedia dates the first lookout tower to the 1920s.[^3]

A 20-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab went up in 1933.[^1][^2] The present cab is a 14-by-14-foot R-6 flat-top with a catwalk.[^1] The register dates it to 1960, while firelookout.com dates it to 1966.[^1][^2] Wikipedia also records other structures from 1933 and 1960.[^3] This story cannot settle the year of the cab.

The lookout was registered with the National Historic Lookout Register on January 8, 2003.[^1][^3]

Staffing has continued. A 2023 local news report listed Flagtail among the lookouts staffed on the Blue Mountain Ranger District through the summer.[^4] The register says veteran observer David Sheffel was staffing it as of 2025.[^1] firelookout.com describes it as staffed every summer.[^2]

The forest says there is public parking at its lookouts, and that visitors are welcome during daylight hours.[^4] It also asks visitors to respect the staff after hours and when fire activity is high.[^4] Because the tower is staffed, check with the Malheur National Forest before you go.
