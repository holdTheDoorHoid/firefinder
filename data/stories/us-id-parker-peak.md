Parker Peak rises to 7,670 feet about ten miles southwest of Porthill, in Boundary County, on Kaniksu National Forest land.[^1][^2]

Both firelookout.com and the Idaho Fire Lookouts site describe a log cabin lookout on Parker Peak, built in 1937.[^1][^2] The only lookout staff either site names is Jesse Daniels, listed for 1936.[^1][^2][^3] That date falls a year before the build date.[^1][^2] The sources we read do not explain the gap.

Both sites say the cabin was abandoned in the 1960s, without giving a single year.[^1][^2] The association's Idaho list marks the lookout as gone.[^4] We found no source that describes what, if anything, is still on the summit.

Visitors reach the summit on foot.[^2][^5] The Idaho site calls it a hike-in site.[^2] A trail guide describes more than 5,820 feet of climbing from the West Side Road to the summit.[^5] The same guide says there used to be a fire lookout on the summit in 1939.[^5] Since the association lists the lookout as gone, a visitor should not expect to find a standing cabin.[^4]
