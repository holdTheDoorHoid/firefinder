P Ranch Lookout is a 72-foot Aermotor tower on the Malheur National Wildlife Refuge, about a mile east of Frenchglen in Harney County.[^1][^2] The Civilian Conservation Corps built it in 1933 close to where the original P Ranch had stood.[^1][^4] The same work demolished most of the ranch buildings, and in 1935 the site became part of the adjacent refuge.[^4] The main ranch house burned in 1947.[^4]

Its purpose is not clear. One listing calls it primarily a wildlife-observation tower, and a 2012 visitor wondered whether it was ever used for spotting fires, since the area has few trees.[^2][^3] I found no source that records a fire spotted from it.

The register says the tower is shut to visitors for most of the year because of bird nesting habitat, though it stays available for emergency use.[^1] A 2012 visit found the lowest stairs overgrown, and that visitor's listing marked the tower as abandoned.[^3] By April 2024 the tower was standing, and a visitor found the area around its base closed because of wildlife.[^4] The same visitor could see it from the road, the parking lot and the Riverview trail, and enjoyed watching turkey vultures roost on it at night.[^4]

If you go, the view from the road or the trail is the way to see it. Please respect the closure around the base.
