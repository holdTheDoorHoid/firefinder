The Lauder fire tower was a light steel lookout in the Unorganized South Nipissing area of Nipissing District, in northeastern Ontario.[^1] The register gives the site an elevation of 426 m (1,398 ft).[^1]

Most Ontario fire towers were erected in the decades from the 1920s to the 1950s, a peak period for fire protection in the province.[^2] A 1963 Tower Detection System map from the Department of Lands and Forests places a 24 m (80 ft) steel tower here.[^1]

The North Bay topographic map (sheet 31L) no longer shows the tower.[^1] By 1985 the lower ladder was gone, and that year the tower was recommended for demolition.[^1] The register flags its coordinates as approximate.[^1]

The register is the only tower-specific source found, and it does not describe what remains at the site today, whether the tower still stands, or whether the site is open to the public.[^1]
