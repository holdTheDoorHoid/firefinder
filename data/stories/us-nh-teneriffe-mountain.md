Teneriffe Mountain is the highest point in Milton, New Hampshire, with its summit at 1,090 feet (330 m) above sea level.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list places it in Strafford County, gives its type as unknown, and marks the lookout as gone.[^2]

The Forestry Commission's report for 1911-12 was published in Concord in November 1912.[^3] It lists Teneriffe Mountain, Milton, under its temporary lookout stations.[^3] Teneriffe does not appear in the report's main list of stations, the one that names a watchman for each.[^3]

No source I read describes what remains on the summit today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
