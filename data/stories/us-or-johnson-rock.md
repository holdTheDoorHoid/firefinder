Johnson Rock sits in the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest in Union County.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com says the site was developed about 1938 with a wooden L-4 tower.[^2] According to the register, the present tower took the place of an earlier L-4 built in 1938.[^1]

Built in 1952, the present tower is 82 feet tall, of open timber construction, with a 14-by-14-foot L-4 cab on top.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com lists lookout personnel for 1954 and 1958, and for several years in the 1980s.[^2] It also says the tower is staffed every summer.[^2]

The register added Johnson Rock on July 4, 2022.[^1] I found no rental listing, public access rules or dated staffing record for the lookout. Before visiting, check with the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest, since the tower is staffed in summer.[^2]

[^1]: Johnson Rock Lookout, National Historic Lookout Register (nhlr.org), http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/or/johnson-rock-lookout/ (accessed 2026-10-08).
[^2]: Johnson Rock Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/or/johnsonrock.html (accessed 2026-10-08).
