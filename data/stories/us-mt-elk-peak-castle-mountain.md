The Castle Mountains are a detached range east of White Sulphur Springs in Meagher County, Montana, and Elk Peak is their highest point.[^3] At one point the peak carried the official name Castle Peak.[^2]

A May 1922 item from the Great Falls Tribune, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, reported plans for a seven-mile telephone line to the forestry lookout station on Elk Peak, the summit of the Castle Mountains.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page lists the Lewis and Clark National Forest as the agency.[^1]

In July 1938 the Independent Record reported that Joseph W. Nawrocki and Arnold H. Chloupeck had begun summer work, and that Nawrocki would serve as foreman lookout on Castle Mountain.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page lists the site as removed.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today. According to Wikipedia, roughly 30,000 acres of the Castles had no roads as of 1995.[^3]
