Tripod Peak Lookout stands on an 8,082-foot summit about 10 miles south-southwest of Cascade, in Valley County, Idaho.[^1][^2] The peak first hosted a lookout in 1921, when the National Historic Lookout Register says a California Region 5 Plan 4A cabin was set on a six-foot open timber tower.[^1] Firelookout.com describes that first lookout as a six-foot wooden tower with a live-in cab on top.[^2]

The first cab was later replaced by an R1/L-4 cabin on a short timber tower. Idaho Fire Lookouts dates that change to 1934, and firelookout.com places it in the 1930s.[^1][^2][^3] A concrete base came next. The register and Idaho Fire Lookouts date it to 1950, while firelookout.com says 1956.[^1][^3][^2] The current two-story R-6 flat cab was built in 1977.[^1][^2]

The register says the Southern Idaho Timber Protective Association staffs it during fire season, and firelookout.com says the same association has staffed it.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts labels it hike-in staffed.[^3] An undated employment page from that association, however, lists two fire lookouts on Brundage and Lookout mountains among its responsibilities, and the page does not name Tripod.[^4] The current season is therefore unconfirmed.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^5] The National Historic Lookout Register added it on October 3, 2020.[^1]
