Within Lincoln County, Georgia, Chennault is recorded as an unincorporated community.[^1] State Routes 44 and 79 meet there, and the community lies northwest of Lincolnton, the seat of the county.[^1]

The Georgia lookout tower list gives an entry numbered GA382 at a point labelled "chennault" in Lincoln County, tagged "topo".[^2] The list's legend says it also covers towers found on 1:100,000 USGS topo maps, and it notes that names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own creation. GA382 falls in that range, so the name here is the compiler's.[^2]

Measured from the coordinates, a registered tower that the FCC record lists as constructed in 2014 lies about 1 mile (1.5 km) from that point.[^2][^3] A Cellular Finder page for FCC tower #1290427 gives its address as 1469 Graball Road, Chennault, places it at 33.9203, -82.5887, lists it as 294.9 feet (89.9 m) tall, and gives its constructed date as June 29, 2014.[^3] No source ties that modern structure to the list's point.[^2][^3]

No source found says what, if anything, stands at the list's point today, or whether it is open to visitors.
