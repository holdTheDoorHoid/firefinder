Sounding Knob sits on Jack Mountain east of Monterey, in Highland County, Virginia.[^1] In 1934 a Civilian Conservation Corps camp was set up to build a road to the top, and a planned 47-foot tower was changed to an 80-foot steel one at no extra cost to the county.[^4] A Highland newspaper item from March 1935 describes the crew building the tower for reporting fires in the county.[^4]

The sources disagree on the build year. The register gives 1935, while the owner, Hammill "Skip" Jones, says 1934.[^1][^2]

The register says the tower was manned until the 1960s.[^1] Local papers name a lookout watchman who moved into the tower in 1948 and was still on duty in 1950.[^4] Jones says fire towers fell out of use in the 1960s because small airplanes became more practical, and this one sat in disrepair until 2002.[^2]

The register says the tower was removed in 2002, and that Steve Good of Highland Welding bought it and kept its parts.[^1] Allegheny Mountain Radio gives the same year.[^2] A Recorder item dated July 24, 2004, reports the tower was dismantled, which is a different year.[^4]

In 2016 Skip Jones bought the tower and had it restored. The steel parts went to Ashland, Virginia, to be re-galvanized, the bolts went to Illinois for the same treatment, and new hemlock steps replaced the old ones.[^1][^3] It was put back up at Sounding Knob in 2017, with a dedication ceremony on July 8.[^1] The Virginia Association of Counties says it now overlooks Monterey from the top of Jack Mountain.[^5] None of these accounts says how far it now stands from its first spot.[^1][^2][^5]

According to the register, Skip Jones privately owns the tower.[^1] As of November 2020, the register says, the public was welcome during daylight hours.[^1] A January 2021 tourism piece from the Virginia Association of Counties invites visitors to enjoy the views from sunrise to sunset, up a gravel drive off Sounding Knob Road.[^5] Jones told the radio station in 2017 that the tower was fenced in, with plans to open it from time to time.[^3] Because the tower is privately owned, check with the owner before you go.
