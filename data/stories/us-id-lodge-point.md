Lodge Point is in the Nez Perce National Forest, about 15 miles east-southeast of Kooskia.[^2][^3] It lies in Idaho County,[^2] and the sources give its elevation as 4,761 feet.[^1][^2] The Idaho County list gives its location as township 32 North, range 6 East, section 14.[^4] The FFLA's Idaho list places it at 46.11351, -115.67204.[^5]

The lookout was a 20-foot tower with an L-4 cab, dated to 1933.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

The sources agree that it was abandoned in 1950.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Firelookout.com lists the site as gone,[^2] and so does the FFLA's Idaho list.[^5] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the abandoned site under its drive-up category.[^1]

Firelookout.com and the FFLA list say the tower is gone, but none of the sources describes the site today or says whether the public may visit it.
