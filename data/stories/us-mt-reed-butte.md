Reed Butte is a lookout site about five miles east of Stevensville in Ravalli County, at an elevation of 4,739 feet.[^1] The listing names the Montana Department of Natural Resources and Conservation as the agency.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list records a tower at 46.50872, -113.97952, marked as gone.[^2]

One record places the year 1972 beside the words tower and gone, but does not say whether that year marks the building or the removal.[^1] A county index puts the site in section 27 of township 9 North, range 19 West, and marks it as removed.[^3]

The listing that describes the site says nothing about what remains there or whether the ground is open to visitors.[^1] Anyone thinking of visiting should ask the agency first.
