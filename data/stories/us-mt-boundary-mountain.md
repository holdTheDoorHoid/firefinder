Boundary Mountain is a former lookout on the Kootenai National Forest, in Lincoln County, Montana, about 19 miles east-northeast of Libby.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 5,950 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the site at 48.4336, -115.13869, and files it among the county's lookout structures and sites. That list calls it a ground lookout rather than a tower.[^2]

The page gives only the decade, the 1930s, for a cabin and a crow's nest about two miles east, and asks whether they are still there.[^1]

Firelookout.com marks the lookout as abandoned, while the Forest Fire Lookout Association list marks the site gone.[^1][^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
