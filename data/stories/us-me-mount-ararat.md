Mount Ararat, in the town of Topsham in Sagadahoc County, Maine, had a fire lookout tower by 1932.[^3] The Forest Commissioner's annual report describes a 1931 decision to give districts without a tower one, and it records that Mt. Ararat received a lookout.[^1] The same report also mentions new 47-foot steel stations, but it does not say whether Ararat's was one of them.[^1]

A 360-degree panoramic map centered on the tower was made by the Maine Forestry Service and is dated 1932.[^3]

The sources we read do not say who staffed the tower, what it watched, or when it was refurbished or taken down.

Today the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing, and it carries no mark for a missing cab.[^2] The Eastern US Lookouts page we read gives history but no current status, so its condition today is not confirmed.[^1] We found no public access information for the site, so ask the landowner before going near it.
