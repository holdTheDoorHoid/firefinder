The Tabernacle tower stood in Tipton County, Tennessee, and the state index marks it removed.[^1]

The tower shares its name with the Tabernacle neighborhood, which an 1882 Memphis newspaper item, reprinted by Genealogy Trails, places about four miles (6.4 km) from Covington.[^2]

The index numbers the tower 9-17, a district and tower number from a 1976 state list.[^1] It gives the elevation as 420 feet (128 m) and ties the site to National Geodetic Survey datasheet FE2637.[^1] TopoZone lists the spot as a tower on the USGS Gift map, at 35.5106 N, 89.5459 W, with an approximate elevation of 400 feet (122 m).[^3] The two elevations differ, and neither source explains the gap.[^1][^3]

The index adds that numerous towers are gone, and that most former tower sites now sit on private land.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^3]

[^2]: News Stories: Fires, Floods, Train Wrecks and other Calamities in Tipton County, Genealogy Trails, https://genealogytrails.com/tenn/tipton/news_fires.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Tabernacle Lookout Tower Topo Map in Tipton County TN, TopoZone, https://www.topozone.com/tennessee/tipton-tn/tower/tabernacle-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
