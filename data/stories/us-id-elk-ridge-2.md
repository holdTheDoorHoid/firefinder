Elk Ridge is in the St. Joe National Forest, about three miles east-northeast of Calder in Shoshone County, Idaho.[^1][^2]

Both pages describe a camp from the 1930s.[^1][^2] The camp used two patrol points: one half mile west atop Billy Goat Hill, and another two miles northeast atop O'Neill Hill.[^1][^2]

The two pages give different elevations, 3,320 feet and 3,220 feet.[^1][^2]

Both pages call the site abandoned, but neither gives a date for that.[^1][^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, and neither says whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page marks it as a hike-in site.[^1]

Idaho has other lookouts named Elk Ridge. A different one in Bonner County, on the Kaniksu National Forest, is a separate record and should not be mixed up with this one.[^3]
