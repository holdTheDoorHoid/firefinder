Wolf Point was a lookout in Lane County, Oregon, about 16 miles southwest of Eugene, at an elevation of 1,716 feet.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site files it under the Western Lane district of the Oregon Department of Forestry.[^2]

Work began in 1949 on a 14-by-14-foot lookout house on a 40-foot tower.[^2] The firelookout.com listing describes that tower as a wooden one.[^1] A 1950 report to the state gave the total cost of the improvement as $2,955.61.[^2]

In 1969 a replacement was under way.[^2] It was a portable tower whose cabin would be taken to headquarters for winter storage, which was meant to limit weather wear and vandalism.[^2] A small trailer house would provide the living quarters.[^2]

In January 1972 the Forest Log reported that the Wolf Point lookout station had burned to the ground that winter, and that the cause was under investigation.[^2] In 1972 a 35-foot steel Aermotor tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab was erected, with the trailer house.[^1][^2]

Both lists mark Wolf Point as gone.[^1][^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. None says whether the site is open to the public, so treat access as unknown.
