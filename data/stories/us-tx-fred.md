The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower named Fred in Tyler County, at 30.56652, -94.19422, and marks it as gone.[^1]

The name belongs to a small community at the southeastern tip of Tyler County, lying seven miles south of Spurger.[^2] A post office opened there in 1881, and Wiley Cunningham was its first postmaster.[^2] In 1883 the settlement counted thirty residents, two churches, a general store and a school, and by 1913 there were seventy-five people.[^2] In the late 1960s the count stood at 349; from the early 1970s to 2000 it was 239.[^2] During the 1960s its post office was one of just three still in service in Tyler County, and a new brick building for it opened in 1976.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
