Sand Creek Lookout stood in the Colville National Forest in Pend Oreille County, at about 4,560 feet.[^1][^2] The Register and firelookout.com both date it to 1937 and describe a 30-foot wooden L-4 tower.[^1][^2] WillhiteWeb gives the same year and tower size.[^4]

The tower is gone, according to the Register, firelookout.com and Forest Lookouts.[^1][^2][^3]

Eric Willhite has not visited the site.[^4] He says its location still needs confirming. It may be on the lower bumps to the west, on Sand Creek Mountain, which sits on the west side of Sullivan Lake.[^4] Footings likely remain, he says, and recent Forest Service maps label those western bumps.[^4] A 2014 Forest Service map shows the lookout location, according to his captions.[^4]

The Register lists the Forest Service as administrator of the land.[^1] None of the sources we read describes a road or trail to the site today.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
