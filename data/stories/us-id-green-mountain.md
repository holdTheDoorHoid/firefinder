Green Mountain is a crow's nest lookout in Clearwater County, Idaho, at 5,244 feet, about eight miles northeast of Elk River.[^1][^2] It was listed under the Potlatch Timber Protective Association, known as PTPA.[^2][^3]

The first mention we found is in the association's 1914 annual report. That report places a lookout on Green Mountain in section 1, township 40 north, range 3 east. It adds that the lookout had no telephone and reported daily by way of Elk Butte, which sits four miles off.[^3]

In June 1915 the Elk River Sentinel reported a planned lookout on Green Mountain, with about five and a half miles of new telephone line tying it into the Elk Butte line.[^3] The paper also said the top was not bare, and that a lookout would take the form of a ladder run up a large hemlock.[^3] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both date the crow's nest to 1915.[^1][^2] The 1914 mention and the 1915 date do not fully agree, and the sources do not settle which is the start.[^1][^2][^3]

Both register sites record the lookout as abandoned in 1930.[^1][^2] The statewide list classes it as a crow's nest with an abandoned status.[^4]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today. Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site Drive-Up, which is the only access detail in the sources.[^2]
