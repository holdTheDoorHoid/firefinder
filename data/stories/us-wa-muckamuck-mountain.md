Muckamuck Mountain sits in the Okanogan National Forest, in Okanogan County, Washington.[^1][^2] The register lists the summit at 6,370 feet.[^1]

The lookout was a D-6 cupola cabin built in 1922.[^1][^2][^3] A 1922 entry, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, says a standard house was built on the mountain that October.[^4]

Bert Julian was the lookout in 1924, according to Willhite.[^3] A 1924 item quoted by Forest Lookouts says Julian picked up wireless messages on his radio set, with a reach from Annapolis to Cavite, P.I.[^4] FireLookout.com lists Julian for 1923 and 1929, and Robert McIntosh for 1959.[^2] Those 1923 and 1929 dates do not match the 1924 date in Willhite and the 1924 item.[^2][^3][^4] Willhite also says panoramic images were taken from the cupola in 1930.[^3]

The cab was destroyed in 1974 after 52 years, according to Willhite and FireLookout.com.[^3][^2] The register says it was removed that year, and it notes that a snow-measuring pole still stood on the site in September 2001.[^1]

Willhite visited in May 2020 and found little left but wire and nails.[^3] He reports that much of the timber has regrown, views are still available by moving around the summit area, and cattle wander the mountain along informal tracks.[^3]

His route went up Forest Service Road 3825 for 6 miles to a trail that is not signed, then 1.6 miles one way to the summit, with about 1,200 feet of gain.[^3] The sources read mention no closure or permit for that route.
