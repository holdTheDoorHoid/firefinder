Number 9 Mountain is in Aroostook County.[^1] Maine Memory places the lookout in a township it calls TD R2 WELS.[^2] The 1914 forest commissioner's report, as transcribed by Forest Lookouts, puts the station in Township 9, Range 3.[^1] The sources do not agree on the township.

The first lookout on the mountain was a wood tower, built in 1914.[^1] A year later a log crib tower with a cab replaced it, according to Forest Lookouts, though a Maine Memory caption calls the replacement a wood tower.[^1][^2] In 1919 a 36-foot steel tower went up there.[^2][^3]

The watchman in 1917 was W.B. Hussey of Patten, and James M. Pierce of Houlton was chief warden.[^2][^3] A 1916 forest commissioner's report also calls the mountain Bald Mountain.[^1] We found no notable fire on the mountain in the sources we read.

The Maine Memory exhibit, credited to the Maine Forest Service, says the steel tower was removed in 1993.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine list still shows a standing tower here.[^4] We could not reconcile the two, so the 1993 removal date is the first thing to check.

We found no current access information for Number 9 Mountain, and no notice that visitors may go there. Check locally before you set out, and do not climb any tower you find on the mountain.
