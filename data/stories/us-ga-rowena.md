Rowena Lookout Tower is named on a Georgia list of lookout towers, which places it in Early County at 31.34712, -84.71381.[^1] The list gives the site a code, gfc-207, and its note says that gfc-nnn codes come from an older Georgia Forestry Commission list that described 203 towers.[^1] The list carries a revision stamp of 22 March 2016.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register counts 29 registered lookouts in Georgia, and Rowena is not one of them.[^2] The register does list a different Early County tower, the Early County Fire Tower in Blakely, with its own coordinates.[^3] The Georgia Forestry Commission built that tower in 1952, and it was staffed until 1987.[^3]

No source found describes what remains at the Rowena site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1]
