Smith Point's lookout was a 100-foot pole tower with an L-6 cab on top, plus a ground cabin for living quarters [^1][^4]. It went up around 1934, and every source that dates it agrees on that year [^1][^2][^3][^4]. The site lay about nine miles west-northwest of Canyon R.S. [^1].

The Forest Service's Clearwater list gives 1941 as the year the lookout was abandoned [^3]. The other sources say the 1940s [^1][^2][^4]. The tower fell down in the 1950s [^1][^2][^3].

The sources do not agree on who ran the site. Firelookout.com names the Clearwater-Potlatch Timber Protective Association [^1]. The county list gives the Clearwater Timber Protective Association [^4]. Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the Clearwater National Forest as the land [^2].

No source describes what, if anything, is left on the summit today. Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as hike-in [^2]. None of the sources says whether the land is open to visitors, so check with the managing agency before going.
