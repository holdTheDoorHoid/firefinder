The Anderson City lookout is listed as a Georgia Forestry Commission tower in Worth County.[^1] A Georgia lookout tower list at tnlandforms.us gives the site an elevation of 416 with no unit given, coordinates of 31.38823, -83.85457, and the Commission number 215.[^2]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^2]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
