Clark Mountain, also called Clark Peak, is a former fire lookout about four miles southeast of Thompson Falls, Montana.[^1][^2] The tower was a 20-foot pole with an L-4 cab, built in 1934.[^1][^2][^3] Sources disagree on the forest: firelookout.com places it in the Lolo National Forest, while a Forest Lookouts page places it in the Kootenai National Forest.[^1][^2]

The tower was in use the following summer. In June 1935 a Sanders County newspaper reported that Art Turk had been sent out as the first lookout of the season in the Thompson Falls district.[^2] That September a crew burning brush around the lookout lost control of the fire in a high wind, and 48 men had to help put it out.[^2] Margaret Sullivan was employed as the lookout in 1943.[^2] Firelookout.com and a 2021 Sanders County Ledger column both say the tower was staffed until 1969.[^1][^3]

The Ledger column says the tower was destroyed in 1970. Firelookout.com says it was destroyed in the 1970s.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, is left on the hill, and none says whether the public can reach the site.
