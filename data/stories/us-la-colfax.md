The Colfax lookout was a 100-foot Aermotor steel tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab, built in 1936 by the Civilian Conservation Corps in Grant Parish, Louisiana.[^1] The register places it inside Kisatchie National Forest.[^1] The CCC crews ran the tower until 1942, when Forest Service personnel took over.[^1]

In 1983 the tower was placed under a special use permit held by the Louisiana State Forestry Department, and it ran as a cooperative tower with the Forest Service.[^1] In 1988 a Mr. Thorne bought it.[^1]

The register lists the tower as a former lookout, registered on February 24, 2011, as US 856, LA 3.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site records a move in 1993 to a tourist attraction in Bentley, so the tower no longer stood in the forest.[^2] No source we read describes what stands at that attraction today, or whether visitors can climb the tower.
