The Point 118 lookout was a cupola trailer in 1967, on the Lolo National Forest in Missoula County.[^1][^2][^3]

The firelookout.com record marks it gone in 1968, and the Montana index lists it as a trailer site with its status gone.[^1][^3]

A 2020 amateur-radio report on pnwsota.org places a Missoula County communication site on Point 118, about 200 feet from the summit.[^4]

The same report notes that the access road is permanently gated, so visitors must go on foot or by bicycle.[^4]

No source we found describes the lookout itself today, and the 2020 report says nothing about a lookout.[^1][^3][^4]
