The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes a steel Aermotor tower that the U.S. Forest Service owned.[^1] A 1940 map of the Hallton area already labels a Zimmerman Hill Fire Tower.[^4] The map label shares the Zimmerman name with the register entry, but no source says they describe the same tower.[^1][^4]

A map listing places the tower in Elk County.[^2] Its registered date on the Former Fire Lookout Sites register is September 16, 2008.[^1]

The register says the tower has been removed.[^1] A trail listing says it was removed in the 1990s and that its location is still evident.[^3]

The route is FR228, which the trail listing also calls Zimmerman Fire Tower Road.[^3] It is a graded dirt and gravel track through the Allegheny National Forest, so the land is public.[^3] The listing carries a "Permanently Closed" label, so check the road's status before you drive in.[^3]
