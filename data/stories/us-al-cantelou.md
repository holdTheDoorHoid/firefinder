The Cantelou Fire Tower stood on a bluff west of Montgomery, in Montgomery County, Alabama.[^1] Its cab measured 12 feet by 12 feet, according to the register that lists the site.[^1] The register places the site at about 32.34 degrees north and gives its elevation as 323 feet (98 m).[^1]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the site as US 1612, AL 2, and records a registration date of January 16, 2016.[^1] Al Pelham nominated it, and the same page credits him with the photograph.[^1]

According to the register, the land was later put to use for a water tank once the tower came down.[^1] No source I read says whether that tank still stands, what the site looks like today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1]

[^1]: Cantelou Fire Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/al/cantelou-fire-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
