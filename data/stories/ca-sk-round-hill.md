Round Hill is a heavy steel fire tower in Saskatchewan, 24.4 m (80 ft) tall.[^1] Canada's Fire Lookouts indexes the Saskatchewan site as Round Lake (Hill).[^2]

The Round Lake page records that in 1963 the Round Lake tower was taken down and rebuilt at Long Lake, but no source confirms that the Round Hill structure is the same tower.[^3]

A towermen list on the websiteservice.ca survey places Round Hill among the towers covered by two observers: one listed from 1943 to 1963 with seven towers, and another from 1986 to 2001 with six.[^4]

In April 2013 Wildfire Today reported that the Saskatchewan Ministry of Environment was shifting its lookouts from human observers to cameras.[^5] A union release reported a March announcement that observers would come off 42 towers before the 2014 fire season.[^6] None of these 2013 reports names Round Hill among the affected towers.

Today the Saskatchewan Public Safety Agency says the province has 42 wildfire detection towers, each with a camera, and that its system mixes software with human monitoring.[^7] No source describes the Round Hill site today, or says whether the public can reach it.
