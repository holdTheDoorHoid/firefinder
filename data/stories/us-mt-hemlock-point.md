Hemlock Point was a lookout cab in Missoula County, Montana, on the Lolo National Forest, 25 miles northwest of Seeley Lake.[^1][^2] Its elevation is listed at 7,780 feet.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page gives its land-survey location as 20N-18W-25.[^2]

Firelookout.com dates the cab to 1947 and says it was destroyed in 1963.[^1] It was an L-4 cab, a design a Sanders County history describes as a 14-by-14-foot wood-frame cab that could sit on the ground or atop a tower.[^4] That history also says the L-4 was the design people chose most often for living in a lookout.[^4] The statewide list classes Hemlock Point as a ground site and marks it gone.[^3]

The sources we read do not describe what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
