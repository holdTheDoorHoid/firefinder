Dubakella Mountain is a former lookout site in Trinity National Forest, in Trinity County, California.[^1][^2] A 1961 federal survey description places the lookout house about 12.5 miles south-southeast of Hayfork.[^2]

By November 1921 the mountain already had a working lookout.[^1] A newspaper report from that year, quoted on Forest Lookouts, says the lookout W.C. Duncan saw the smoke from a fire that a hunter started after he became lost.[^1] The fire had grown to about a quarter acre before Duncan worked it and made it safe.[^1]

In November 1931 a telephone line from Peanut to the lookout was finished.[^1] In September 1932 Assistant Forest Supervisor James Farley planned a visibility study with Lookout W. Duncan.[^1]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey's 1961 description calls it a wood building that the Forest Service had stopped using as a lookout. The survey said the building was partly torn down.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as gone.[^3]

The federal survey record for the site still carries the name, but its only history entry dates from 1961.[^2] No source describes what remains there today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
