The Sawtooth Valley lookout stood in Custer County, Idaho, about five miles southeast of Obsidian.[^2] The earliest date our sources give is 1935, when a fire finder sat on a barn roof.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a rooftop lookout.[^4]

The sources disagree about the national forest. Forest Lookouts names the Sawtooth National Forest, while FireLookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both name the Challis National Forest.[^1][^2][^3] We did not find a source that settles the question. Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the elevation as 6,904 feet, while FireLookout.com leaves it unknown.[^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts says it does not yet have a record of who staffed the lookout.[^3]

Forest Lookouts and FireLookout.com both give 1951 as the year the site was abandoned.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the same year.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list also marks the site as abandoned.[^4]

None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. One listing carries a "Drive-Up" label, which suggests a car can reach the site, but the label does not say what the site is like now.[^3]
