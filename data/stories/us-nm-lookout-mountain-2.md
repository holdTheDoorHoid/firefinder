Lookout Mountain sits at 33.39061, -105.81148 in Lincoln County, New Mexico.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it among New Mexico lookouts as a ground site that is gone.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site files the lookout under the Lincoln National Forest.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: New Mexico Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Lookout Mountain (Lincoln), Forest Lookouts, https://westlookouts.weebly.com/lookout-mountain-lincoln.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
