Newton Mountain lies in Lincoln County, six miles west-southwest of the Sylvanite Ranger Station, in the Kootenai National Forest.[^1] The lookout website gives its elevation as 6,538 feet, and records a camp at the site in 1921. An L-4 tower followed in 1934.[^1]

The same website entry records the tower as destroyed in 1959.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list classes the site as a tower and gives its status as gone.[^2]

A county list of Montana lookouts gives the mountain's legal location as 34N-34W-23.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today.
