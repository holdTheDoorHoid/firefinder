Mud Pond Mountain has a former lookout site in Aroostook County, Maine.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register places the tower along the American Realty road west of Ashland, at the 22-mile marker, at an elevation of 1,381 feet.[^1] The register puts the site near 46.67 north and 68.67 west, and numbers it US 79, ME 5.[^1]

The register calls it a very old wooden tower. Local sources said it was still standing in 1998.[^1] The register does not name those sources, so the claim is secondhand.[^1] The site was added to the register on September 15, 2008, with Bob Spear as the nominator.[^1]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today. We also could not confirm whether the land is public or open to visitors.

[^1]: Mud Pond Mountain Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/me/mud-pond-mountain-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
