Skookum Peak sits at 3,261 feet in the Colville National Forest, in Pend Oreille County, Washington.[^1]

The lookout went up in 1937. It was a 10-foot pole tower topped with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2][^3]

It was abandoned in the 1950s.[^1][^2][^3] One listing calls the tower gone, and another lists it as removed.[^2][^4]

At the site, WillhiteWeb's undated photos show footings, bed frames, what may be a stovepipe, and a flagpole or telephone pole still standing.[^3]

Skookum Peak itself is public land, but the road from Bead Lake Road crosses private parcels.[^3] A gate and trespass signs block that road, and the WillhiteWeb author left out a route to avoid trouble with neighbors.[^3]

If you plan a visit, stay off posted private land and check the current access with the Colville National Forest first.
