The Forest Lookouts site has a short entry for a Becker County lookout named Pickerel. It says only that the tower was removed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association also lists the site as gone.[^2]

A Becker County compilation of towers lists a Juggler Lake tower and says it was formerly noted as Pickerel.[^3] The same entry says the site was also indicated on a 1958 USGS map.[^3] A 1964 survey reference called it an abandoned lookout tower.[^3] The compilation says the angle-iron footings left behind suggest a short tower.[^3] A separate A-Z list of Minnesota lookouts repeats the Juggler Lake entry as formerly Pickerel.[^4] We found no independent source for the link between the two names.

According to the compilation, the site is south of Highway 113, across from the ARMER tower.[^3] The sources we read do not say whether the footings can still be seen or whether the site is open to the public.[^3]
