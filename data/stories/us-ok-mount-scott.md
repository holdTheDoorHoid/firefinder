Mount Scott rises to 2,464 feet (751 m) in the Wichita Mountains National Wildlife Refuge, in Comanche County, Oklahoma.[^1][^2] A Forest Lookouts list credits Civilian Conservation Corps workers with a stone tower on the mountain.[^1]

A historical marker about the refuge says it hosted three CCC companies. The marker credits the camps with dams, roads, buildings, fences and trails, and with many recreation facilities that remain in service today.[^3]

The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service maintains the area.[^2] Cars and bicycles can reach the summit on a three-mile paved road, and hikers may go, though the mountain has no formal trails.[^2] The same article says the paved road is open to walkers and cyclists only from 6 to 9:30 a.m.[^2]

None of the sources says whether the stone tower still stands.
