Glen was a fire lookout in Aitkin County, Minnesota.[^1] The Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC) erected it in 1938.[^1] The county survey describes a 100-foot stairway tower made by the McClintock-Marshall Company, and lists it as tower 36.[^1][^3] A statewide list gives the same 1938 date and stairway design.[^3]

The survey lists the owner as "Mn MFS," which stands for the Minnesota Forest Service, now the Forestry Division of the state Department of Natural Resources.[^1][^4]

DNR records mention a Long Lake tower at the same spot.[^1] The survey says that name may have been another name for Glen, or an earlier tower.[^1] The hamlet of Glen and Long Lake were both nearby.[^1]

A 1960 survey recorded a steel tower on four legs, topped by a metal observation room, with an overall height of 110 feet.[^1] That is ten feet more than the 100 feet the survey gives for the tower as built in 1938, and no source we read explains the difference.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the site is open to visitors.
