Salmon Mountain, in the Mount Hood National Forest of Clackamas County, Oregon, carried an L-4 cab lookout built in the 1930s.[^1][^2] An Oregon Lookouts page lists panorama photos dated March 10, 1934 under the site's entry.[^3]

The cab came down in 1962. The register says it was removed, firelookout.com says it was destroyed, and Oregon Hikers says it was decommissioned that year.[^1][^2][^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list also marks the site as gone.[^5]

The sources do not agree on elevation. The register gives 3,768 feet, Oregon Hikers gives 4,020 feet for the site, and firelookout.com gives 4,960 feet.[^1][^4][^2]

Today the site is a viewpoint at the end of the Salmon Mountain Trail, number 787, in the Salmon-Huckleberry Wilderness.[^4] Oregon Hikers rates the hike moderate, at 10 miles with 1,960 feet of gain.[^4] The guide says the western view is gone, though the northern view still reaches the Salmon River valley and Mount Hood.[^4] No source we read says whether anything of the cab remains on the ground.
