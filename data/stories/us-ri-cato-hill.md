Cato Hill is in Providence County, Rhode Island.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Rhode Island list gives each tower a county, coordinates, a type and a status.[^1] For Cato Hill it gives 42.00533, -71.51565 and marks the tower gone.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.

[^1]: Rhode Island Fire Towers, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/ri/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
