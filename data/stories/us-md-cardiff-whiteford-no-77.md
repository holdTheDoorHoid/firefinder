The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a site called Cardiff (Whiteford) (No. 77) in Harford County, Maryland. The list classes it as a ground site rather than a tower, and marks it as gone.[^1]

The name links the site to Cardiff and Whiteford, two communities in northern Harford County.[^1][^2] The Whiteford–Cardiff Historic District, a national historic district, covers parts of those communities, which were linked with slate production from the late 1800s into the early 1900s.[^2] The district was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 2005.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains of the site today, so check who owns the land before visiting.
