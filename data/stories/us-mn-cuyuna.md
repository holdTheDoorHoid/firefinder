The state forest service put up an 81-foot ladder tower at Cuyuna in 1938.[^1] The county catalog lists it as the first lookout on the site.[^1]

In late August 1947 the Brainerd Daily Dispatch reported that the old tower had been taken down the week before, after it was declared outdated and shaky.[^2] A new 90-foot steel tower was going up near the village of Cuyuna, about half a mile off the Cuyuna-Crosby road.[^2] The paper also said Vernon Pratt was in charge of the tower.[^2]

The catalog suggests the 1947 tower may have been the first tower at Waskish, but it is only a guess.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Cuyuna tower as gone.[^3]

A map page on the Pastmaps site, titled 1973, describes a Cuyuna Lookout Tower as standing over the state forest, in the present tense.[^4] That blurb is undated and conflicts with the association's listing, so treat it with caution. No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
