The Simpson Lookout Tower is a 100-foot Aermotor LS-40 tower in Simpson County, and the Mississippi Forestry Commission administers it.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register entry gives no construction date, so the sources available for this entry do not record the tower's early years.[^1][^2]

The register lists its elevation as 530 feet (162 metres).[^1] OpenStreetMap, which maps the structure as an observation tower, gives a slightly lower figure of 157 metres.[^2] This record uses the register's figure and notes the gap.

The register added the tower on May 19, 2026.[^1] Nothing I could read reports its condition since that listing, so its present state is not confirmed.[^1][^2]

Today the register gives no hours, climbing policy or visitor contact.[^1] Anyone interested in the site should ask the Mississippi Forestry Commission first, and should not climb the tower without permission.
