Little Mountain stands at the south end of Mount Vernon in Skagit County, Washington.[^1][^3] In 1935 a 100-foot wooden tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab and a shake cabin went up on its summit.[^1][^2][^3]

In 1942 an Aircraft Warning Service cabin was added to the summit.[^1][^3] Willhite says it was there to spot enemy planes that might come near the coastal waters nearby during World War II.[^3]

The tower came down in 1980. The register says it was removed, while firelookout.com and Willhite say it was destroyed.[^1][^2][^3] An index on washingtonlookouts.weebly.com also marks the site as removed.[^4]

Today the mountain is part of Little Mountain Park, which Willhite calls the largest park in Mount Vernon, covering 480 acres.[^3] A 1.5-mile paved road leads to the top, where there are two viewpoints. The park also has numerous hiking trails, one of which climbs the mountain from the bottom of the park just after the entrance gate.[^3] The register names the Washington Division of Forestry/DNR as the administering agency, while Willhite describes the park as Mount Vernon's.[^1][^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains of the tower or the cabins on the summit today.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
