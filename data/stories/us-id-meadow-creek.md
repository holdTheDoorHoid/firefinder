Meadow Creek was a camp in Idaho County, about 19 miles east of Elk City.[^2][^4] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both date the camp to 1931, while Forest Lookouts gives c.1930.[^1][^2][^3]

The sources disagree on the forest. Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts name the Nez Perce National Forest, while Forest Lookouts names the Payette National Forest.[^1][^2][^3]

The camp was abandoned in 1939.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as drive-up.[^1] None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, remains there now.

Do not confuse it with the Meadow Creek Lookout in Valley County. That one is a separate, standing cab that was built in 1933 and is on the National Historic Lookout Register.[^4][^5]
