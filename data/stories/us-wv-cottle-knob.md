Cottle Knob sits in Nicholas County, West Virginia.[^1] The Eastern lookouts site files the lookout under the West Virginia Division of Forestry.[^2] I could not find a construction date, a designer, or any list of who staffed it. The register dates its own entry to September 16, 2008.[^1]

The records do not agree on what became of the tower. The register says plainly that the tower "has been removed."[^1] On the Eastern lookouts page, the fate is given as a single word: "Removed."[^2] A West Virginia tower list on firelookout.org shows the original knob as gone, but also a standing tower at Pocahontas County High School, coded as a relocated Cottle Knob tower.[^3] The list marks that entry with an asterisk, which it applies to towers whose cab has gone while the structure and supports remain.[^3]

I found no source that explains a move to a school or dates one. Nothing I found describes the knob today, and nothing says whether the school's tower can be seen or visited.
