Storm Creek Lake is a steel lookout tower near West Helena, in Phillips County.[^1] The Coast and Geodetic Survey described it in 1953 as a four-legged steel structure carrying a glass-enclosed cab, about 110 feet (33.5 m) overall.[^1] The station sat about 3.0 miles north and 1.0 mile east of West Helena, 75 feet east of the center of State Highway 44. Its survey point was the center of the cab roof.[^1]

Statewide, the Encyclopedia of Arkansas counts 122 known fire towers at their peak and says 48 were still standing in 1994, though it is unknown whether any were in use.[^3]

No source I found says whether the tower still stands or what remains at the site.[^1]

In June 2022 the Storm Creek Lake unit of Mississippi River State Park opened a newly rebuilt campground. The article describes the unit as largely undeveloped until then, and it does not mention the lookout.[^2]
