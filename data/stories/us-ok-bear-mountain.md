The Bear Mountain Lookout is in McCurtain County, Oklahoma.[^2] The National Historic Lookout Register describes an 80-foot steel tower of Aermotor make, which the Civilian Conservation Corps put up in the 1930s. The register puts the site at 817 feet.[^1]

The register gives no more exact date than the 1930s.[^1] The Oklahoma Forestry Services history supplies some context.[^3] The Civilian Conservation Corps let the state build more fire towers in that decade, and at one time 23 state fire towers stood within its fire protection area.[^3] Today, the history says, only three towers are still staffed, and the others now house radio repeaters.[^3] The history does not name Bear Mountain, so we cannot say how the tower fits into that picture.[^3]

The register was entered on January 2, 2026, with a nomination by Monty Graves, who also supplied its photographs.[^1] The Oklahoma Department of Agriculture, Food, and Forestry administers the site, according to the register.[^1]

We found no account of who staffed the tower or of any fire it helped with. The sources we read do not describe its condition today or whether the public may visit. Ask the administering department before going near it.
