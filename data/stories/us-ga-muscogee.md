A statewide list of Georgia lookout towers gives a tower called Muscogee Lookout Tower in Muscogee County, at about 32.59 degrees north and 84.91 degrees west.[^1]

The list gives the tower the code gfc-310. It says the gfc-nnn numbers come from an earlier list by the Georgia Forestry Commission, one that covered 203 towers.[^1]

The same list says that names for towers numbered 73 and above are its own creation. This tower is numbered 72, so, by the list's own account, the compiler did not make up its name.[^1]

The list page carries a revision stamp of 22 March 2016.[^1]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything remains there or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers (GA tower list with coordinates, elevation and GFC number), tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
