Rimrock North is a former lookout site on the Mount Hood National Forest in Hood River County, Oregon, at about 4,100 feet.[^1][^2] The register says an L-4 cab went up on the site in 1934, and firelookout.com gives the same year.[^1][^2]

Both sources date the cab's abandonment to 1941 and say it no longer exists.[^1][^2] The register files that under "Removed," with the note "abandoned 1941."[^1] The U.S. Forest Service is the administering agency, and the site was added to the register on September 18, 2019.[^1]

No source I read describes the register's point as it looks now.[^1][^3] A hiking guide to a Rim Rock Fire Lookout Site on Surveyor's Ridge describes a makeshift path that climbs a rock outcrop to what remains of the lookout's concrete footings.[^3] The guide does not say whether it means this register entry. Its map point lies close to the register's point, while a firelookout.org list places Rimrock North farther north.[^3][^1][^4] A 7.9-mile Surveyors Ridge hike reaches that site, the guide says.[^3]
