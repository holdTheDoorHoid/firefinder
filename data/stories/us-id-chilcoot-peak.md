Chilcoot Peak, at 8,998 feet in Valley County, Idaho, got a fire lookout in 1933. Three sources give that year, and two of them describe a ground cabin.[^1][^2][^4] The Forest Lookouts page gives 1934 instead, for an R-1 lookout cabin, and says the first fire season it was staffed was 1935.[^3] The firelookout.com page lists the Boise National Forest as the agency.[^1]

Sources date its destruction to 1969. None of them says why.[^1][^2][^4]

A 2016 ascent reported several foundations on the summit.[^4] The peak lies on the western side of the Frank Church River of No Return Wilderness, and the climbing guide rates the route class 1 and says few peakbaggers visit the area.[^3][^4] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page files the site as a hike-in.[^2] No source we found describes the site in more detail than that 2016 report.
