The Sardis Lookout Tower stood in Winn Parish, Louisiana.[^1][^2] The Wikipedia list of the state's fire lookout towers places it there, with Atlanta listed beside it.[^1] A separate index of Louisiana lookouts also names a Sardis lookout in Winn Parish.[^2] The list marks the tower as torn down.[^1]

For background, the same list says funds for fire protection work were directed in 1915, and that 39 fire towers had been built in Louisiana by 1943 and 56 by 1950.[^1]

The list's newest mention of the tower is a 2012 USGS map, and it adds that aerial imagery does not show it.[^1] It also places the site just northwest of today's water tower.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to the public.
