Stone Johnny Lookout stood in Bonner County, Idaho, six miles north of Oldtown, in the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^4] The site was developed with a firefinder in 1930.[^1]

In July 1935 the Newport Miner reported, as quoted by Forest Lookouts, that the Forest Service was building a new station at the crest of the range.[^2] The paper added that the station sat nearly on the state line and would overlook the country to the south and east.[^2] Firelookout.com describes the tower as a 25-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, built in 1935.[^1]

The only named staff member we found is Valarie Croopler, listed for 1967.[^1][^3]

Sources give 1969 as the year the tower was destroyed.[^2][^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list also marks the site as gone.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site.[^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page tags it "Drive-Up" but gives no current condition.[^3]
