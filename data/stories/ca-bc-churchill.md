Churchill was a lookout in Fraser-Fort George, British Columbia, south of Giscome.[^1] The Canada Lookouts page for it gives a position of latitude 54.067 and longitude 122.267 (west), at an elevation of 938 m (3,077 ft).[^1]

The page also quotes a Prince George Citizen item dated May 16, 1962. The item names him as Axel Edvin Lindberg, a 63-year-old forest lookout man who had been stationed at the lookout since May 1 and had staffed it the year before.[^1] It says he fell ill while working on a trail to the lookout, and that he died of a heart attack at the Churchill Lookout.[^1]

A British Columbia guide to fire lookouts says the province's network held more than 300 lookouts at its height.[^2]

No source found describes the site today. Whether anything remains on the ground, and whether the public can reach it, is unknown.
