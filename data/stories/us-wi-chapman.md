Chapman was a lookout tower in Marinette County, Wisconsin.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list places it at 45.71964 north, 87.85614 west, and records the structure as a tower.[^1]

Most of the state's towers went up in the 1930s.[^2] The DNR's forest bureau director said the towers had turned into a safety concern, and the agency planned to rely more on airplane monitoring.[^2] In 2016 the state resolved to shut down the final 72 lookout towers still in use.[^3] A 2019 report from the association's Wisconsin chapter said every one of the towers was then due to come down.[^4]

The association now marks Chapman as gone.[^1] No source I reached describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public, so treat the spot as unverified.
