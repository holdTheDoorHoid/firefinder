The Georgia Forestry Commission's older tower list gives the Desser tower the number 224, and it places the tower in Seminole County.[^1] The number comes from an older Commission list that described the locations of 203 towers.[^1]

Desser is a crossroads south of Donalsonville, along the route to Lake Seminole.[^2] The tower list gives the site an elevation of 140 feet, at 30.88528 north and 84.86256 west.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 29 lookouts in Georgia, and Desser is not among them.[^3]

Statewide, the Commission moved away from staffed towers in the late 1980s and relied on aerial patrols instead. Many towers were sold off as scrap.[^4]

No source we found describes the site today, including whether any part of the tower remains.
