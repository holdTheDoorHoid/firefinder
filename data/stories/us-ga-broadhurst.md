Broadhurst is a lookout site in Wayne County, Georgia. A statewide list of lookout towers places it at 31.459 N, 81.921 W, with an elevation of 61 feet (18.6 m).[^1] The Eastern US Lookouts website has a page for it, but that page gives only the county.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register's Georgia index lists 29 registered lookouts, and Broadhurst is not one of them.[^3]

No source describes the site today, so I cannot say whether a tower, footings or nothing remains, or whether the place is open to visitors. Anyone planning a visit should first find out who owns the land, and should not climb any tower that is closed or posted.
