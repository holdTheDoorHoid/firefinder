Colesburg is one of the unincorporated communities of Camden County, Georgia.[^1] A marker road-trip list from Visit Kingsland places a marker for the Fairfield and Bellview plantations of Gen. John Floyd at the corner of Colesburg Thompkins Road and US-17.[^2]

An online list of Georgia lookout towers places a tower at Colesburg, at 16 ft (4.9 m) elevation, and tags the entry BLL-65230 and gfc-844.[^3] The list's point lies about 140 m (460 ft) from the coordinates that the marker list gives for that corner.[^2][^3]

No source we found describes the site today, so what stands at the listed point, if anything, is not known.
