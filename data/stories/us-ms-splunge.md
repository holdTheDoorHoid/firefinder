The Splunge Lookout Tower stands in Monroe County, Mississippi, at about 513 feet above sea level.[^1] Splunge is an unincorporated community in the same county, and Wikipedia says the tower is listed on the National Historic Lookout Register.[^2]

The register describes a 90-foot Aermotor tower with a parallel staircase, 13 landings and 14 flights of stairs.[^1] Aermotor built its towers from galvanized steel.[^3] The register gives no build date.[^1] I found no record of who staffed the tower or which fires it watched.

The register's site visit took place on July 17, 2022, and the tower was registered on July 18, 2022, with Jason Johns as nominator.[^1]

At that visit, the bottom flights of the stairs were missing.[^1] Without those bottom flights, the stair cannot be reached from the ground. An online lookout index files it under Mississippi as Splunge, in Monroe County.[^4]

The register names the Mississippi Forestry Commission as administrator, and I found no later report on the tower's condition, repairs or public access.[^1] The map database marks it as a lattice tower with a site elevation of 157 meters.[^5] Check with the Forestry Commission before planning any visit.
