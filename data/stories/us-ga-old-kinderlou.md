Kinderlou Tower is a 54-foot (16 m) former fire lookout at Grand Bay, in Lowndes County, Georgia.[^1][^3] A 2012 photo essay dates its service as a lookout for nearby Kinderlou Forest to 1939 through 1993.[^1] The New Georgia Encyclopedia says it was once a fire tower watching over Langdale Forest Products timberland.[^2]

The essay says Harley Langdale Jr., a Valdosta businessman, donated the tower to the state.[^1] The encyclopedia, last revised in 2016, places the tower at the end of a half-mile boardwalk at the Grand Bay Wetland Education Center.[^2] Two agencies, the Coastal Plains Regional Educational Service Agency and the Georgia Department of Natural Resources, jointly run the center.[^3] The encyclopedia says the tower was modified so visitors can observe the wetland's plants and animals.[^2] It also says the public can have interpretive experiences of the Grand Bay Wildlife Management Area.[^2]

The 2012 essay said a Georgia Outdoor Recreation Pass was then required for access, so check current rules before you go.[^1] It says the view from the top repays the climb, but cautions visitors in summer that red wasps nest on the structure.[^1] Its directions from Valdosta start with U.S. Highway 221 north for about 10 miles.[^1]
