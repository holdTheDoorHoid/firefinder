Clarkton is a town in Bladen County, North Carolina.[^3] A North Carolina lookout listing places a Clarkton tower in that county and pairs it with the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources.[^1]

The listing says the Civilian Conservation Corps erected a 120-foot tower there in 1941.[^1] It cites a 1942 state biennial report for that date.[^1]

A list of North Carolina lookout towers gives the site's coordinates as 34.48861 and -78.67083.[^2]

No source I read describes the site today.
