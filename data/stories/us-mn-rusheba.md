The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Minnesota lookouts includes a Rusheba tower in Chisago County and marks it as standing, at about 45.70° N, 92.93° W.[^1]

The one source we could reach says little more. We did not find when the tower was built, who put it up, what it looked like, or whether it was ever staffed as a lookout. We also found no source that explains the name.

We have no current information about who owns the site or whether visitors may climb the tower. The list shows that the structure stands; it does not say that the public is welcome. Please do not climb it, and check with the people who manage the land before you visit.

[^1]: Minnesota lookouts list, Forest Fire Lookout Association (firelookout.org), https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
