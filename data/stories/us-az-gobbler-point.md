Firelookout.org's Arizona list places Gobbler Point in Greenlee County. It gives the type as unknown and marks the site gone.[^1] The Forest Lookouts Arizona index files the point under the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Additional Arizona Information, Firelookout.org, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/az/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: ARIZONA - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts, https://westlookouts.weebly.com/arizona.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
