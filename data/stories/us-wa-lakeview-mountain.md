Lakeview Mountain, in Yakima County, Washington, had an L-4 cab built in 1935.[^1][^2]

The cab was abandoned in 1945, ten years after it was built.[^4][^1] The pages we read do not describe who staffed it or any fires it saw.

Left standing alone for years, the cab decayed and eventually collapsed.[^4] Other pages agree that it has collapsed.[^1][^2][^3]

Today the site is ruins.[^3] One history page says a significant amount of debris remains.[^4] The Former Fire Lookout Sites record lists the Yakima Indian Reservation as both location and administrator.[^1]

Wilderness status covers the north half of the mountain, while the south side is Yakama Nation land.[^4] Treat the site as permission-only. The pages we read describe no public route to it, so ask the tribe before you go.[^4][^1]
