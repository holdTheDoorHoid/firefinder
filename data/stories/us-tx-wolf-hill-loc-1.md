The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list includes Wolf Hill (Loc 1), a tower in Angelina County, and marks it as gone.[^1] The list gives its coordinates as 31.10875, -94.53024.[^1]

The same list has a second Wolf Hill entry, Loc 2, also in Angelina County and also marked as gone, at 31.11944, -94.55111.[^1]

None of the sources read for this story describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Texas Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
