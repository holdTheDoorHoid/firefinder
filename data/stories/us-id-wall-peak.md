Wall Peak is in Kootenai County, in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1][^2] The Willhite Web page calls it a large, long mountain that lies north of Interstate 90, stretching from Fourth of July Summit toward Cataldo.[^3]

A camp is recorded there in 1931.[^2] In 1933 a 30-foot pole tower, fitted with an L-4 cab, went up.[^2][^3] The firelookout.com listing also names patrol platforms atop Swanson Mountain, a quarter mile and two miles east.[^2]

Charles Scott is named as the lookout in 1944.[^3] The same listing adds Art Bandon for 1946.[^2]

The sources disagree about how the tower ended. Idaho Fire Lookouts dates its destruction to 1947.[^4] The Willhite Web page gives 1947 as the last year of the lookout.[^3] The firelookout.com listing says instead that the tower was put up for public sale in 1953 and then removed.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the tower as gone.[^1] Photographs on the Willhite Web page are captioned as four footings.[^3] Its author came in on a road from Fourth of July Summit. Gravel roads reach a point just under the summit on the south side, and from the road's highpoint the route climbs the southeast slope.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts calls the site a hike-in.[^4] No source we read says whether the site is open to visitors now.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
