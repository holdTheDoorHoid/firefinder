Saddle Guard Station is a former fire lookout in the Siuslaw National Forest, in Lane County, Oregon, about 16 miles north-northeast of Florence.[^1][^2] The register gives its elevation as 2,297 feet (700 metres), and firelookout.com gives the same figure.[^1][^2]

The register dates a pole L-4 tower on the site to 1937 and says it no longer stands.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the same year and the same design, and lists the tower as gone.[^2]

The register's photo is captioned as a Saddle Guard Station lookout panorama dated 4-18-34, three years before that build date.[^1] The sources do not explain the difference.

The register names the U.S. Forest Service as the administering agency, and dates the entry to November 15, 2010.[^1]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains of the site today.[^1][^2]
