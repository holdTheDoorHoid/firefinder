The Jones Lake Lookout Tower stood in Bladen County, North Carolina.[^1][^2] The North Carolina Division of Forest Resources is listed as its agency.[^1]

The Civilian Conservation Corps erected the tower in January 1935. It was a 99-foot 9-inch steel tower with a two-room ground cabin, and the work cost $1,531.97.[^1] The funding came through the Emergency Conservation Works program.[^1] The page credits these details to the Department of Conservation and Development's sixth biennial report, covering 1934 to 1936.[^1]

A 1937 Gastonia Daily Gazette item quoted on the same page says a state-operated tower near the area had been supplemented by a 100-foot steel tower put up by the Resettlement Administration. The same item credits the project forester with building up a skilled crew to head off and put out fires across a 30,000-acre project.[^1] The page does not connect that item to the 1935 tower, so the two heights may describe different structures.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page marks the tower as removed.[^1]

Today, an OpenStreetMap record still holds a tower point at the lake, tagged as having no public access.[^3] That is a map entry, not a report on the site. No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, or whether anyone may visit it.
