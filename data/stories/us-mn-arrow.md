Arrow was a fire lookout in Lake County, Minnesota, on land of the Superior National Forest.[^1][^2] The booklet codes the owner as USFS and places the tower at about 47.85 north and 91.31 west.[^2][^4] The Forest Lookouts page also lists the site as 62N-86W-14.[^1]

A Boundary Waters history article names Arrow, along with Insula and Malberg, as one of the lakes where fire towers once stood southeast of the Fernberg Lookout.[^3] The Lake County booklet lists the tower as removed.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone.[^4]

No source I read describes the Arrow site today.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
