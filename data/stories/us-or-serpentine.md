Serpentine is a lookout site on the Siskiyou National Forest, about 17 miles west-southwest of Grants Pass in Josephine County, Oregon. The two summits there stand at 4,282 and 4,492 feet.[^1]

A 40-foot pole tower of the L-4 type went up on the north summit in 1935.[^1] In 1962 a 41-foot treated timber tower with an R-6 flat-roof cab was built on the south summit.[^1][^2] The Oregon Lookouts page says that tower took the place of an older one roughly a mile north, but does not say whether that was the 1935 tower.[^2]

The tower's fate is recorded in three different ways. One listing calls the site ruins.[^1] A status line on the Oregon Lookouts page reads "Removed."[^2] The county directory marks the tower as gone.[^3]

No source describes the site now beyond those undated notes. Anyone thinking of visiting should check with the Siskiyou National Forest first.[^1]
