Goat Peak was a lookout in Lincoln County, Montana, in the Kootenai National Forest, about 5 miles southwest of Troy.[^1] The Fire Lookout website gives its elevation as 5,416 feet.[^1] The association's Montana list gives the coordinates as 48.40648, -115.97373.[^2]

The lookout there was an L-4 cab, which the website dates to 1933.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a ground-level structure, not a tower.[^2]

The website calls the lookout gone but gives no date for its removal.[^1] The association's Montana list also marks it gone.[^2]

Wikipedia says a different Goat Peak, in the North Cascades of Washington, is topped by a Forest Service lookout that is in operation.[^3]

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today, such as footings or a foundation, or whether the public can visit it. Anyone planning a trip should ask the Kootenai National Forest about access before heading out.
