Grouse Mountain's fire lookout stood eight miles east of Trout Creek in Sanders County, Montana, at an elevation of 5,924 feet.[^1] The firelookout.com page dates the tower to 1935 and describes it as an L-4 tower.[^1] The two web sources name the forest differently. firelookout.com gives only the Kootenai National Forest, while the local history lists the Cabinet National Forest first and then the Kootenai.[^1][^2]

A 1941 item from the Sanders County Independent-Ledger, quoted on the local history page, says a man named Ray Bierwagen had been home for a weekend from his lookout. The item calls the site Grouse Peak rather than Grouse Mountain.[^2]

The tower is listed as gone in 1955 on firelookout.com, and both the local history and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list show it as removed or gone.[^1][^2][^3]

No source consulted describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach the ground where it stood.[^1][^2][^3]
