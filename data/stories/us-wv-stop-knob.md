The Stop Knob tower once stood close to Powellton, in Fayette County.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register records the site at 2,305 feet (703 m) and says it was registered on January 26, 2017, as US 1666, WV 57.[^1]

By October 1926 the tower was served by telephone from the Montgomery central office.[^2] In April 1929, according to a Charleston newspaper, the Southern West Virginia Fire Protective association set aside $400 to build quarters for the observer at Stop Knob.[^2]

The register says the tower was moved to Peters Mountain in Monroe County.[^1] The state list marks the Stop Knob site as gone.[^3]

A 2004 Associated Press story describes the Hanging Rock tower on Peters Mountain as a wooden cabin from the 1930s, set on a ledge at one of the mountain's high spots.[^4] The register for the Hanging Rock Lookout says the state built that tower in 1934.[^5] The two accounts disagree about when the cabin was rebuilt after a 1996 fire.[^4][^5] The AP story says the following year, and the register says 1999.[^4][^5] Neither source mentions Stop Knob, so we cannot say whether the tower that moved to Peters Mountain is the structure there now.[^4][^5]

No source we read describes what remains at the original Stop Knob site, or whether that site is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3]
