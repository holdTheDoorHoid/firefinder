Cowpar Lake is a steel fire lookout in Alberta.[^1] The provincial register lists the tower at 30 m (98 ft) tall, on a site 553 m (1,814 ft) above sea level, on map sheet 73M/16.[^1]

The register gives 2013 as the year the tower was constructed.[^1] Alberta runs 127 wildfire lookout sites, located for good visibility so fires can be spotted and reported.[^2] Each site has a lookout observer on duty, typically from April to October.[^3]

The register still lists Cowpar Lake as active. Its access mode is recorded as "fly", and it records no weather station on site.[^1] No source describes the site now beyond that entry, so visitors should not assume the public can go up the tower.[^1]
