Caribou Ridge sits in Boundary County, Idaho, with Bonners Ferry about six miles to its northeast.[^1] The site is listed under the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^3] Forest Lookouts gives its legal location as township 61 north, range 1 west, section 9.[^3] It sits at an elevation of 4,725 feet.[^1][^2]

Both lookout sites list a cabin here in 1933.[^1][^2] Both also name Robert Saarf as a lookout in 1934.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts ties that name to the Bonners Ferry Herald, dated 06/07/1934.[^2]

Both sites describe the site as abandoned, without giving a date for that.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association instead lists the site as gone.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site as a hike-in site.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains there today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
