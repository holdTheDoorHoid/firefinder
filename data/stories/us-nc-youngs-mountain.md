Youngs Mountain is a summit in Rowan County, North Carolina, at an elevation of 1,089 feet (332 m) [^2]. The county's own history spells it Young's Mountain [^1].

In 1949 the county reached a cooperative agreement with the state [^1]. A 48-foot (15 m) wooden lookout tower was erected on the mountain in September 1954 [^1]. The following year, the 73-foot High Rock tower went up at Healing Springs, in Davidson County, and it watches over the eastern half of Rowan County [^1].

The tower on Young's Mountain was taken down in June 1968 [^1]. No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the site is open to visitors. Do not confuse it with the other Youngs Mountain near Lake Lure, which has a hiking trail on the north side of the lake [^3].
