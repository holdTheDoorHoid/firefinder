The Mount Lassic lookout stood on Goat Hill, south of the Mount Lassic summit.[^1] The summit's official name, Signal Peak, traces back to a heliograph station that stood on it around 1900.[^2] A concrete survey pillar on top is not the lookout, though the writer first wondered whether it was part of one.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site with the type Ground and the status Gone.[^3] The 2015 Lassics Fire reached the edge of the road that climbs toward the site, and a 2023 hike found a lot of standing dead and burned trunks farther up it.[^4]

A 2023 blog hike went up South Goat Hill Road, a high-clearance road the writer found legal to drive.[^4] At the end of the road, the writer saw only a scaffold that may have supported weather equipment.[^4] A second road or trail also reaches the site, but motor vehicles are no longer allowed on it.[^4]

The summit is the highest spot in the Mount Lassic Wilderness, which the Forest Service lists under its Mad River District.[^2][^5]
