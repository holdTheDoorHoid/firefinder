The Eastern US Lookouts site files this Walton County tower as Mossy Head (Sandy Mtn).[^1] It describes a 100-foot Aermotor tower, about 30.5 m tall.[^1] A map gazetteer lists a Mossy Head Lookout Tower and a Sandy Mountain Lookout Tower at almost the same point.[^2]

The listing places the tower in the Choctawhatchee National Forest.[^1] Wikipedia says Congress transferred that national forest to the War Department on June 27, 1940, and that Eglin Air Force Base has occupied the land ever since.[^3]

According to that page, the tower was auctioned off and taken down in autumn 2008.[^1]

TopoQuest also lists a Sandy Mountain Lookout Tower point, but a map name does not show what stands there.[^2] No source I found describes what remains on the ground today or whether the public can reach the site.[^1][^2]
