Cline Knob is a former lookout site in Claiborne County, Tennessee, at 1,864 feet (568 m) above sea level.[^1][^2] The Tennessee lookout list marks its tower as moved.[^1]

A note on the site's entry credits Ron Stafford with information indicating that a tower here was moved to Lafollette.[^2] The list's Lafollette entry is in Campbell County and sits at 1,240 feet (378 m).[^3]

A list of Tennessee Division of Forestry towers still standing as of March 26, 2014, printed in a National Register document, includes an extant tower named Lafollette in Campbell County.[^4] That document does not say whether this is the tower moved from Cline Knob.[^4]

No source reached describes what, if anything, remains at the Cline Knob site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
