Mount Collins is near Matachewan, Ontario.[^1] The Canada Lookouts index files the tower under the North Bay District.[^2] The index records that a UHF radio-telephone set was fitted to the Mount Collins tower in 1937, citing a Department of Lands and Forests report.[^2]

The Forest Rangers site says Ontario's fire towers were the responsibility of the former Department of Lands and Forests, which gave way to the Ministry of Natural Resources in 1972.[^3] The same index also has an entry named Raymond at "Mount Collins - Matachewan", so the two names may refer to one tower. The sources do not say.[^2]

A Firelookout.ca record places the observer's cabin and the tower both standing in 2011, with the cab undamaged.[^1] No source found describes the site after 2011, or whether the public can reach it.
