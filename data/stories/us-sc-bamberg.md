The Bamberg tower is a steel lookout about 100 feet tall, with a 7-by-7-foot cab, built around 1936.[^1][^2] The register says it appears to be a Bethlehem Steel tower, but that is a tentative identification.[^1]

The sources disagree about who controls it now. The Eastern US Lookouts page places it under the South Carolina Forestry Commission,[^2] while the register lists a private owner for the tower and its ground residence.[^1]

The register notes a keeper's house close by, an original Forestry Commission sign, and Forestry Commission radio repeaters that the tower still seems to carry.[^1] It says the tower still stands in its original location and looks to be in fair to good condition.[^1]

The register gives no public access arrangement, and I found no record of fires, staffing or a later sale. Because the site is privately owned, please do not climb the tower or enter the grounds without the owner's permission.

[^1]: Bamberg Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/sc/bamberg-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Bamberg, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/bamburg.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
