Lion Rock is a former lookout site on Table Mountain, 16 miles north of Ellensburg in Kittitas County.[^2][^3] It began in the 1920s as a camp with a crow's nest, a platform built up in a tree.[^1][^3] A 40-foot pole L-4 tower was built there in 1932.[^1][^3]

Earl Johnson was listed as the lookout-fireman in June 1933, according to a Leavenworth Echo item quoted on the Forest Lookouts page.[^4][^2] In May 1938, plans for a combined garage and woodshed were approved for the site.[^3][^4]

A new 40-foot L-4 replaced the first tower in 1948.[^1][^2][^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says the first tower's cab was apparently salvaged and moved to Sugarloaf Lookout in 1949, and firelookout.com says it was apparently moved, without a date.[^1][^2] WillhiteWeb says only that it may have been.[^3]

The tower came down in 1967. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says it was removed, while firelookout.com says it was destroyed.[^1][^2]

The Table Mountain fire torched the area in the fall of 2013. The blaze left patches of green, and many summit trees made it through.[^3] An undated WillhiteWeb photo caption reads "One footing left," and that is the only remnant the sources describe.[^3]

On the pages we read, the site is public. WillhiteWeb lists it as drive-up with good gravel access, a campground nearby, and many dispersed campsites around the summit.[^3] The Mountaineers give the route as Forest Road 35 to Forest Road 35-124, and list day hiking as a suitable activity.[^5]
