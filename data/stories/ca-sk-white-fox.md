The White Fox Fire Tower stands close to Tobin Lake, off Highway 35 in Torch River No. 488, Saskatchewan.[^2] The Firetowers of Saskatchewan list gives it as a heavy steel tower, 24.4 m (80 ft) tall, and places it by its legal land location, LSD 7-17-53-12-W2.[^1]

In 2026 the Saskatchewan Public Safety Agency told a technical briefing that the province runs 42 wildfire detection towers, and that cameras are installed on all of them.[^3] Detection staff monitor the camera images, and software can flag something that may be smoke for staff to assess before a fire is confirmed.[^3]

A firelookout.ca page, a user submission, says the cupola (the page spells it "copula") was taken off the tower sometime after 2009, making way for a thermal detection system.[^2]

The same page says the lookout cabin is still there, though its condition is unknown.[^2] It describes access to the site as unrestricted and the access trail as in good condition, but the tower is behind a safety fence.[^2]
