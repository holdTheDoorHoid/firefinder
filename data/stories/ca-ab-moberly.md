The Alberta wildfire register lists Moberly as a cabin and gives 1967 as its year of construction.[^1] The register puts the summit at 1,676.2 m (5,499 ft).[^1] A 2014 trip report places it in the Rock Lake area of the Willmore Wilderness.[^2]

The only firsthand account in the sources is that report, written in March 2014. Its writer saw a lookout building and an official plaque on the summit, and noted that trees cover the top, so the views are limited even when the weather is good.[^2]

Access is where the sources differ. The register codes the site as reached by road.[^1] The 2014 writer found the Rock Lake road unmaintained and beyond what their car could handle, which added 2.2 km one way to the trip.[^2]

No source describes the site today. The register marks the lookout as active.[^1] Alberta bars the public from climbing lookout towers, treats lookout dwellings as private, and asks visitors not to count on a tour of the cabin.[^3]
