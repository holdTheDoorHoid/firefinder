Pole Mountain Lookout was a cabin on the ground, not a tower.[^1][^4] A log cabin is recorded a quarter mile west in 1921, and a cupola cabin in 1930.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com calls it Pole Mtn. Fire Lookout Cabin, so searches may use that name.[^1]

The site lies in Shoshone County, Idaho, within St. Joe National Forest, six miles southwest of Red Ives ranger station, at an elevation of 6,601 feet.[^1] Its patrol point, three miles to the south-southeast, is also listed.[^2]

The lookout was gone by 1955.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's register marks it as a ground site that is gone.[^2][^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.
