Bishop Mountain rises above the Targhee National Forest in Fremont County, Idaho, about 25 miles north-northeast of St. Anthony.[^1][^2] The steel Aermotor tower on its summit was built in 1937, though at least one source gives 1938 for the tower and cabin together.[^1][^2][^3] The National Historic Lookout Register records it as 72 feet tall with a 7-by-7-foot cab; other sources describe an 84-foot tower instead.[^1][^2][^3]

The lookout earned its keep during the Yellowstone fires of 1988, when it saw extensive service.[^2][^3] No documented staffing history survives for who worked the tower day to day.[^3]

Bishop Mountain was added to the National Historic Lookout Register in 1991, nominated by Targhee National Forest's supervisor and forest archaeologist.[^2] It remains on standby status under the U.S. Forest Service's Ashton Ranger District, and the Register notes it is available for rental.[^2] A nearby cabin is bookable through the federal recreation.gov system.[^3]

Today the tower still stands on its forested summit, a working relic of Idaho's fire-watch era rather than a museum piece.
