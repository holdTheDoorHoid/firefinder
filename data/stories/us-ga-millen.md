Millen serves as the county seat of Jenkins County, Georgia.[^1] The TN Landforms list of Georgia lookout towers has an entry named millen in Jenkins County, at 32.76804 north and 81.91692 west, with an elevation of 241 feet (73.5 m).[^2] Its codes point to data from the Georgia Forestry Commission (GFC).[^2] The list says the commission provided coordinates for 118 towers, coded BLL, and that an older commission list described 203 towers, coded gfc.[^2] This entry carries both codes, BLL-64699 and gfc-105.[^2]

The list says names for towers numbered 73 and above are its compiler's own.[^2] This one is numbered 178, so the name is a label rather than a recorded one.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
