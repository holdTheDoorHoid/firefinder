Big Burn Point's lookout was a log cabin rather than a tower.[^1][^5] It was built in 1929 in the Nez Perce National Forest, about 14 miles east-southeast of Grangeville in Idaho County.[^1][^2][^3]

The lookout stood at 5,700 feet, in township 30 North, range 5 East, section 33.[^1][^3][^4]

The sources describe the cabin as abandoned.[^1][^2][^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a ground lookout and marks it gone.[^5]

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site hike-in.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
