A volunteer's list of Ontario fire towers that still stand includes the Manning Tower, which the list gives as being at Chagama Lake, Elsas, Ont.[^1][^2] The list uses a star for towers whose cupola was removed and which now serve as communications towers; the Manning entry has no star.[^1]

According to the list, the Ontario Ministry of Natural Resources (MNR) had decided by 2013 to take down many of the towers that still stand.[^1] The list adds that the bottom 6 m (20 ft) of ladder was removed because climbing is hazardous, that the MNR no longer keeps the towers up, and that visitors should not climb them.[^1]

The list's "still standing" label may be out of date. No source I found describes what stands at the site today, or whether it can be reached.

[^1]: Ontario Fire Towers Still Standing, forestrangers.github.io (volunteer list), https://forestrangers.github.io/ontariofiretowers/stillstanding2.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Ontario Fire Towers Still Standing, geocities.ws (ontarioftl), https://www.geocities.ws/ontarioftl/stillstanding.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
