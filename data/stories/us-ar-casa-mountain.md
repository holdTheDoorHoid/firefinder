A website called Forest Lookouts lists a Casa Mountain lookout in Perry County, Arkansas.[^1] The listing gives only that name and county.[^1] No source gives a construction date, height, owner, staffing or description, and nothing in the sources checked confirms that a tower stood on the mountain.[^1]

The Encyclopedia of Arkansas lists the state's fourteen lookouts on the National Historic Lookout Register, and Casa Mountain is not among them.[^2]

No source describes the site today.[^1] Whether anything remains there and whether the public can visit are both unknown.[^1]

[^1]: Casa Mountain, Forest Lookouts (Arkansas listing), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/casa-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Forest Fire Lookouts, Encyclopedia of Arkansas, https://encyclopediaofarkansas.net/entries/forest-fire-lookouts-7905/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
