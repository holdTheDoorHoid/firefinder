Mount Emerine sits at 8,616 feet in Granite County, Montana, in the Deerlodge National Forest.[^1][^2] Both sources date a pole platform tower with a log cabin to the 1920s, and a 20-foot pole L-4 tower replaced it in 1932.[^1][^2]

Sources disagree on how far the site is from Philipsburg. Newspapers gave 22 miles in 1917, 25 miles in 1922 and about 18 miles in 1931, while firelookout.com gives 19 miles to the southwest.[^3][^2]

A 1917 newspaper account already describes a lookout station on the peak, so the 1920s tower was not the first lookout there. In that account, a man posted there grew so lonely that he quit, and a woman took the job. She watched through the summer with her two dogs as protectors.[^3] In 1922, lightning destroyed 1,500 feet of telephone line leading to the lookout station.[^3] Jerry Churchwell served as a lookout on the tower during the summers from 1961 to 1964.[^1]

Driggs Construction of Anaconda, Montana, built a 20-foot treated timber R-6 flat tower in 1964.[^1] Both sources say that tower has since been removed and the log cabin remains, but neither dates that report.[^1][^2] A 1931 newspaper says lightning completely destroyed the lookout station that June.[^3]
