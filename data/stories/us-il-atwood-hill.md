Atwood Hill was an 80-foot steel fire tower in Union County, Illinois, on land of the Shawnee National Forest's Jonesboro Ranger District.[^1]

Sources disagree on when it went up. A 1938 Coast and Geodetic Survey recovery note describes a new survey mark placed 20 feet south of a fire tower at the site, so a tower was already standing by then.[^1] Bob Frakes, an Illinois lookout historian, groups Atwood with towers built in 1940, and says their cabs had room for a lookout to spend the night.[^2]

The Forest Service put the tower up for sale in March 1974.[^1] Bids were to open at the ranger station in Jonesboro, and the buyer had to take the tower out of the forest within 180 days.[^1] Officials said expanded aerial fire spotting had left the tower outdated.[^1] Frakes says almost every Illinois tower was dismantled or sold off in the early 1970s.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts index now lists Atwood Hill as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes the site now, so we cannot say what, if anything, remains on the ridge or whether visitors may go there. Check with the Shawnee National Forest before planning a trip.
