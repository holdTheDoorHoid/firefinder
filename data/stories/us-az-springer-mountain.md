The Springer Mountain Lookout stands on the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest in Navajo County, Arizona, at about 7,153 feet.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register says the Civilian Conservation Corps built it in 1933.[^1] It is a 30-foot MC-24 tower with a 12-by-12-foot cab, and the Register says the national forest remodeled the cab in 1967.[^1]

The Register accepted the lookout on March 4, 2002, as US 424, AZ 31, and lists the Lakeside Ranger District as its cooperator.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Arizona list shows the tower as standing under the same register number.[^2]

A July 2005 Arizona Highways feature mentions a lookout working at Springer Mountain on the Lakeside Ranger District.[^3] The same piece says a lookout decides whether visitors may climb a tower, and that visitors should ask first.[^3] That is a general description of Forest Service practice, not a statement about this tower today.[^3]

The Register says the cab was remodeled while the tower was in active service, which does not tell us its status today.[^1] The sources I read do not say who staffs the tower now, what fires it has watched, or whether it is open to visitors. Anyone planning a visit should ask the Lakeside Ranger District first.[^1][^2][^3]
