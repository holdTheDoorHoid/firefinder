Wapaloosie Mountain sits in Ferry County at 7,018 feet, and the Colville National Forest is listed as its agency.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts gives the land location as 37N-34E-35.[^5] The earliest record we found is a crow's nest at the site in 1920.[^1][^2] A Forest Service photograph in the Crossroads Archive, captioned Wapaloosie Mountain Lookout, shows a lookout in front of a tent shelter, and the collection is marked public domain.[^4]

Firelookout.com and WillhiteWeb both say the crow's nest was abandoned in the 1920s.[^1][^2] Firelookout.org's list of Washington lookouts also marks the site as a crow's nest that was abandoned, and places it at 48.66477, -118.46430.[^3]

WillhiteWeb says the trail that now runs a short way below the summit dates from the 1920s or before.[^2] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout itself, or whether the summit is open to visitors. Check with the Colville National Forest before you go.
