Liz Butte is in the Clearwater National Forest.[^1] The earliest structure listed there is a log cabin, dated to 1929.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com says the cabin is still there.[^2]

In the 1930s a 30-foot pole tower with a cab went up on the site.[^1][^2][^3]

The sources disagree about what became of that tower. Idaho Fire Lookouts says the tower was taken away, but its page does not know when.[^1] The Forest Service list says it is in ruins.[^3] Firelookout.com marks it as gone,[^2] and so does the FFLA's Idaho list.[^4]

Both Idaho Fire Lookouts and firelookout.com record emergency use in the 1950s.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts reports that the cabin still stands, that it was at one time rented out for recreation, and that it is presently not offered for rental.[^1] The same page tags the site as drive-up.[^1]

Only the Forest Service list describes what remains of the pole tower, as ruins. None says whether the public may visit the cabin, and the drive-up tag does not settle that.
