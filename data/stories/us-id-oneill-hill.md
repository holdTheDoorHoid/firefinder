O'Neill Hill Lookout stood about a mile southwest of St. Joe, in Benewah County, at an elevation of about 3,360 feet.[^1][^2]

The site had a cabin and a platform tower by 1939.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts places it in the St. Joe National Forest.[^2] The lookout was abandoned in 1942.[^1][^2] The firelookout.org Idaho list also records it as a platform lookout with the status abandoned, and gives its location as 47.30348, -116.36920.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^2] None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, remains on the hill today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
