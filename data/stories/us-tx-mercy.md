The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower named Mercy in San Jacinto County, Texas, and marks it as gone.[^1] A Texas lookout-tower list gives the same name, county and coordinates, and notes a survey datasheet, BL2183, for the site.[^2]

The name belongs to a small place in the East Texas piney woods.[^4] The Handbook of Texas places the locale between Farm Roads 1725 and 945, fifty-five miles north of Houston.[^3] It says the locale was once linked by tram railroad to Fostoria, where timber was processed into lumber, and that its post office ran from 1915 to 1918.[^3]

The Handbook dates the Mercy oilfield's discovery to 1942 and says the field has produced more than 13 million barrels of crude oil.[^3]

The Handbook notes scattered buildings at the Mercy site.[^3] Both place-history pages describe the Mercy locale and its oilfield, not the tower itself.[^3][^4] No source we read says what, if anything, stands at the tower's listed spot, or whether that spot is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3]
