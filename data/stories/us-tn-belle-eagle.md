Tennessee Landforms lists Belle Eagle as a Haywood County tower at 368 feet and marks it removed.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site gives the agency as the Tennessee Division of Forestry, District 8, and also marks the tower removed.[^2]

A National Register study describes the division's surviving towers as examples of its first organized effort to build a coordinated forest-fire management plan.[^3] The same study says the division built 208 fire lookout towers in the period between about 1930 and 1970.[^3]

Belle Eagle is an unincorporated rural community in northern Haywood County, seven miles north of Brownsville on Tennessee State Route 54.[^4] No source describes what, if anything, remains of the tower on the site, or whether visitors can reach it.
