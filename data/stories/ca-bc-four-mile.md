Four Mile is listed among the British Columbia lookouts on the Canada Lookouts website.[^1] The entry places it in Bulkley-Nechako, at 55.272 north and 127.550 west, and gives an elevation of 686 with no unit.[^1]

The federal geographical names database records a Four Mile Mountain at 55.275 north and 127.553889 west.[^2] Its name matches the lookout's and it lies close to the listed point, but the sources do not say whether they are the same site.[^1][^2]

No source found describes the site today, so nothing is known about what remains there or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Four Mile, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/four-mile.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Four Mile Mountain, Canadian Geographical Names Database, Natural Resources Canada, https://geogratis.gc.ca/services/geoname/en/features/c7ab3118c6cd11d892e2080020a0f4c9 (accessed 2026-10-20).
