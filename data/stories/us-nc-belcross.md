Belcross was a North Carolina state lookout in Camden County.[^1][^3] The lookout page names the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources as its agency, and a statewide list gives the same agency.[^1][^2] The tnlandforms.us list places the site at 36.32694 north and 76.12194 west.[^3]

The lookout page marks the tower as removed.[^1] Mapcarta, a map site that draws on OpenStreetMap, still lists a "Belcross Lookout Tower" near Camden, typed as a tower.[^4] That entry gives no date or condition, so it does not show that a structure stands.[^4]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or says whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Because the tower is listed as removed, a visit should not be planned around climbing it.[^1]
