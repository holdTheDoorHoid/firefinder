Firelookout.ca lists the Canoe Lake Fire Tower at the address Canoe Lake 165, Saskatchewan.[^1]

A list of Saskatchewan towermen on websiteservice.ca says its names are "in alphabetical order by FIRST NAME," and it gives Canoe Lake against five names with no dates: Alexi Iron, Beaver Iron, Bradford Iron, Delfous Iron and Jerry Iron.[^2] The same list places Clement Durocher at Canoe Lake from 1989 to 2002, and Kevin Durocher there from 1999 to 2002.[^2] Both entries say the men went to aerial detection.[^2] The page itself asks readers for corrections, so treat its entries as informal.[^2]

No source we found describes what remains at the tower site today, so its condition is unknown.

[^1]: Canoe Lake Fire Tower, Firelookout.ca, https://firelookout.ca/lookouts/?slug=canoe-lake-fire-tower (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Firetowers of Saskatchewan: Firetowermen and Women, websiteservice.ca, https://www.websiteservice.ca/firetowermen.htm (accessed 2026-10-20).
