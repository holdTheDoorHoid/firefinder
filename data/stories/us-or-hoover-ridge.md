Hoover Ridge is a former lookout site in the Willamette National Forest, about two miles east of Detroit in Marion County.[^2][^1] The register lists the U.S. Forest Service as its administrator.[^1]

In April 1931 a newspaper item, quoted on a lookout history page, said Hoover Ridge would be one of eleven new emergency lookout stations on the Santiam district.[^3] A June 1933 item named Hoover point among the peaks where new lookout houses were to go up that summer.[^3] No source says whether that point is Hoover Ridge.

The sources disagree on the first build year. The register and firelookout.com both date the tower and cabin to 1932; the tower is 26 feet tall, with an L-6 cab.[^1][^2] The Weebly history's only construction entry is 1936, with no month given.[^3]

Abandonment came in 1948, and the register and firelookout.com both list the lookout as gone.[^1][^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site now.
