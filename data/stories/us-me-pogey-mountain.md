Pogey Mountain lies in Piscataquis County, Maine, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists its tower as gone.[^1]

The earliest record I found is a Forest Commissioner's report for 1916, quoted on a Maine lookout list.[^2] The list files one passage under 1915, saying a station was put up on Pogey Mountain, in Township 4, Range 9, because the spot gave a view over more land.[^2]

That entry calls the structure a station, while the association's list calls it a tower.[^1][^2]

The list files a second entry under 1916, from the same report: a telephone line of one and a half miles, run from a Lincoln Pulp Wood Company line to the station.[^2]

No source I could read describes what remains on the mountain today, or whether it is open to the public.

[^1]: Maine Fire Towers (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/me/me-co/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Forest Lookouts: Maine, Forest Lookouts (Ron Kemnow), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/maine.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
