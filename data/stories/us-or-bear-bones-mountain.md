Bear Bones Mountain stands at 4,910 feet in Lane County, Oregon, within the Willamette National Forest.[^1] Firelookout.com places it 14 miles south of Oakridge.[^2]

The sources disagree about when the lookout was built and what kind of building it was. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says a ground L-5 cabin went up in 1934.[^1] Firelookout.com also gives 1934 but calls the building an L-6 cab.[^2] Forest Lookouts gives 1933 for a 10 x 10 ground house built to plan FC-2.[^3] A panorama photograph captioned as the Bear Bones Mountain lookout is dated 26 August 1933, which suggests a lookout was standing by then.[^1][^3]

In September 1935 the Eugene Register-Guard reported that C. A. Paddock had been sent to the Bearbone lookout station as an emergency lookout fireman.[^3]

A 1953 Coast and Geodetic Survey description, reproduced on Forest Lookouts, places the station on the summit.[^3] It puts a standard disk in bedrock 24 feet east of the lookout house's southeast corner, which implies that the lookout house was still standing in 1953.[^3]

Both the register and firelookout.com say the cab was gone by 1964.[^1][^2]

The 1953 description gives directions that end at a trail up the mountain, but that is a survey note from 1953, not a current access report.[^3] None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether that survey disk is still there.
