Siwash Peak Lookout stood on a summit about four miles southeast of Avery, in the St. Joe National Forest, at roughly 5,800 feet [^1]. In 1931 an L-4 cab went up on the peak [^1][^2]. A 10-foot patrol tower was put up about a mile north, on North Siwash Peak [^1][^2].

The cab and the patrol tower were both destroyed in 1961 [^1][^2]. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list now marks the site as gone [^3].

In an August 2025 article, Idaho Fire Lookouts said the summit looked stripped bare at first [^2]. A closer search turned up tie-downs and a flagpole. The writers also found a lone piece of cement, probably from a corner, and two concrete blocks that had formed the cabin's base, both since knocked off the mountainside [^2]. Beyond those pieces, no source describes what remains on the summit today.

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in [^2]. Both sources place it on St. Joe National Forest land [^1][^2]. No source describes a trail or a route up to it.
