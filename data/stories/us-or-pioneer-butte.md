Pioneer Butte is in Benton County, Oregon, on the east slope of Mary's Peak.[^1][^3] In July 1940 Dahl Kirkpatrick, the Siuslaw National Forest supervisor, announced a fire lookout there to protect the Corvallis watershed.[^3] The plan called for a 14-by-14-foot house on a 52-foot wooden tower.[^3] By September 1940 construction was nearly finished.[^3] Lookouts had staffed the butte from tents in 1940, and the new tower was first used in 1941.[^3]

The register and firelookout.com give a different picture: a 41-foot timber tower with living quarters, built in 1941 and removed before 1968.[^1][^2] The register lists the Oregon Department of Forestry as the administering agency, although the 1940 announcement came from the Siuslaw National Forest.[^1][^3]

The tower was later removed.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the butte today.
