The Canada Lookouts website dates a lookout building on Bulldog Mountain, in the Central Kootenay area of British Columbia, to 1950.[^1] Its listing gives the position as latitude 49.383 and longitude 118.083.[^1]

The same site marks the lookout as removed, without giving a date.[^1]

A Valley Voice issue dated 6 September 2018 reported a fire on Bulldog Mountain and a structural protection specialist assigned to it.[^2]

No source I found describes what remains on the mountain today, or whether the site is open to visitors. Do not assume a lookout still stands there.

[^1]: Bulldog Mountain, Canada Lookouts (Ron Kemnow), https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/bulldog-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: The Valley Voice, Volume 27, Number 18, 6 September 2018, https://www.valleyvoice.ca/_PDF_2016/ValleyVoice180906web.pdf (accessed 2026-10-20).
