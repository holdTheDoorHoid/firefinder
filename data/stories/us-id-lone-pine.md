Lone Pine Lookout was a 30-foot pole patrol tower in Idaho County, about six miles southwest of the Moose Creek Ranger Station.[^1][^2]

Most sources date the tower to 1934.[^1][^2][^3][^4] An undated inventory of lookouts on the Moose Creek Ranger District, quoted by the Forest Lookouts site, instead gives 1932 for a 30-foot tower with an L-4 cab. We have not settled which year is right.[^4]

The tower was abandoned in 1950.[^2][^3][^4] According to the Forest Lookouts site, the site passed from the Bitterroot to the Nez Perce National Forest in 1957.[^4] Other listings name the Nez Perce as the forest today.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^5] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it is open to visitors. Anyone planning a visit should ask the Nez Perce National Forest first.
