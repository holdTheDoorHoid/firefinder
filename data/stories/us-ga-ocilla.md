A Georgia lookout list on tnlandforms.us includes a tower named ocilla, placed in Irwin County.[^1] It gives the position as 31.59298, -83.19303 and an elevation of 340, without saying whether that is feet or metres.[^1] The entry carries two codes, BLL-64707 and gfc-516. The page's notes say GFC provided coordinates for the 118 BLL-coded towers, and that the gfc numbers come from an older GFC list that described 203 towers.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 29 registered lookouts in Georgia, and Ocilla is not among them.[^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register lists two former lookout sites in Georgia, Lafayette and Yonah Mountain, so the tower is not recorded there as a former lookout.[^3]

No source I could read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Georgia, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/GA (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Georgia, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://www.firetower.org/lookouts/us/ga/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
