The Madison River lookout stood inside Yellowstone National Park, about two miles east of West Yellowstone, at 6,700 feet.[^1] Rex Kamstra's firelookout.com page calls it a tower, while the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the structure as a platform.[^1][^3]

A privately run Google Sites page about the lookout says the tower was built in 1935.[^2] The same page says it was marked on older topographic maps but no longer appears on newer ones. It also lists other names for the site, including Promontory Fire Lookout and Yellowstone Building 348.[^2]

Both the firelookout.com page and the association's list mark the lookout as gone.[^1][^3] The Google Sites page says it burned down at some point, but it gives no date.[^2]

The same page adds that its concrete footings show up in the most recent Google Maps imagery.[^2] The page says the road to the lookout was abandoned long ago, and that almost no road or trail reaches it now.[^2] The site lies inside the national park, but visitors should not expect a route to it.[^1][^2]
