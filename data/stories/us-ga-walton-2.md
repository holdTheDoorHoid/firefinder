A Georgia tower list compiled by Tom Dunigan includes a lookout called walton in Richmond County. The list gives its elevation as 353 and its coordinates as 33.483029, -82.070492.[^1] The entry is marked "topo". The page says towers 1 to 72 come from a USGS name database. Other towers, it says, were identified on 1:100,000 USGS topo maps and NGS data sheets.[^1] For towers numbered 73 and above, it says, the names are the compiler's own. This entry is GA404, so the name walton may not be a historical one.[^1]

The same list has a separate Walton Lookout Tower in Dooly County, at 32.06045, -83.66600. This story does not cover that tower.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
