The Blue Rock lookout site is in Jackson County, Oregon, within the Rogue River National Forest. It borders the Sky Lakes Wilderness.[^1][^2]

Planning for a lookout here came early: a 1913 district fire plan hoped to place a lookout man at Blue Rock, linked by telephone.[^3]

The register and firelookout.com date the first tower to 1934.[^1][^2] The Oregon page puts it at about 1935.[^3] The register gives 45 feet, the Oregon page gives 35 feet, and a 1936 newspaper calls it a 40-foot tower, so sources disagree on its height.[^1][^3]

In August 1940 the lookout spotted a smoker-caused fire in Blue Canyon, near Horseshoe Lake, and it was put out before it spread.[^3] The lookout was staffed for 85 days in 1941.[^3] From August 1942 to June 1943 the site was an AWS station, and in the winter of 1942 a couple served there as observers.[^3] A 1952 newspaper item still refers to the Blue Rock lookout station as a workplace.[^3]

In 1962 the old tower was taken down to clear the way for a replacement.[^3] The 1963 replacement stood 53 feet tall, was built of treated timber, and had a flat-topped R-6 cab.[^1][^3]

That tower was moved to Robinson Butte.[^1][^2] The register and firelookout.com date the move to 1973, while the Oregon page and the Robinson Butte page say 1974.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The Robinson Butte page says the tower there came from Blue Rock, and the Oregon lookout list shows a standing tower at Robinson Butte.[^4][^5]

The Oregon list marks the original Blue Rock site as "Tower Gone."[^5] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains there, or whether the public can reach it today.
