In November 1922 the Asheville Citizen-Times reported that the Pisgah National Forest was considering building lookouts soon.[^1] One planned tower, on Big Chestnut Knob between Caldwell and Burke counties, was described as probably a steel structure with an enclosed room at the top.[^1] A December 1922 item said a tower on Big Chestnut Knob was likely.[^1] The reports name Big Chestnut Knob, and the sources do not say whether that is the summit this record calls Chestnut Mountain.[^1]

A USGS topographic map of the area, portraying 1993 and published in 1995, is listed by Pastmaps, which describes it as showing a Lookout Tower on Chestnut Mountain.[^2] The forest listing gives Avery, Burke and Caldwell counties for the area.[^1] A list of North Carolina towers places the Chestnut Mountain tower in Caldwell County.[^3]

No source I could read describes the summit today, the tower's condition, or whether the public may visit. Check with the Pisgah National Forest before going up, and do not climb any tower without permission.
