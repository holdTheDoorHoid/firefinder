Summit Rock is a site in Klamath County, Oregon. The Forest Lookouts site lists it under the Rogue River National Forest, with the location 28S-6E-36.[^1] The one dated entry is from 1959. It calls the site undeveloped and says it served as a point for emergency and patrol observation.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Summit Rock, Forest Lookouts (Oregon), https://oregonlookouts.weebly.com/summit-rock.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
