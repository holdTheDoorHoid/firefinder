Mims Chapel, a community about a mile north of Lake O' the Pines, lies on Farm Road 729 in the northwest of Marion County.[^1] The community is thought to have begun around 1843, with a log church on the site.[^1] In 1847 Jefferson S. Nash built Texas's first iron furnace close to the community.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower at Mims Chapel in Marion County as standing.[^3] A page on the Central U.S. Lookouts site lists the Mims Chapel tower under the Texas Forest Service.[^2]

The sources checked give no build date, no design, and no account of staffing, fire use or changes for this tower, so most of its history is still unknown.

Nothing found so far says whether the tower is open to visitors or who owns the land it stands on. Before planning a visit, check with the Texas Forest Service about access.
