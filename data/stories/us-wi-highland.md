The Highland Lookout Tower stands in Douglas County, Wisconsin, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a standing tower.[^1] OpenStreetMap records an elevation of about 380 meters at the tower's location.[^2]

We could not find when the tower was built or who built it. We also found no record of whether it was ever staffed, which fires it watched, or whether it has been moved, rebuilt or changed since the association listed it. Our searches turned up its name, county and coordinates, and little else.

We found no public access information, and the sources do not say who owns the land. Before going near the tower, anyone interested should check with the landowner or land manager, and should not climb it.

This story rests on two listings, so treat it as a starting point rather than a full history.
