Prohibition Mountain is in Larimer County, in the Roosevelt National Forest.[^1]

A 1931 item from the Fort Collins Express-Courier, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, previewed a Sunday outing by a mountain club to the peak.[^1] It named the peak as the Manhattan ranger station's fire lookout, and said that from it one could see everything between the Medicine Bow range and the plains.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as abandoned.[^2] Its entry gives the structure type as unknown and carries no register number.[^2] It places the site at 40.73921 and -105.60670.[^2]

No source we read says what, if anything, stands on the summit today, or whether visitors may go there. Check with the Roosevelt National Forest before heading out.
