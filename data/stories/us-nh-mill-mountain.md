Mill Mountain is a peak in Coos County, New Hampshire, where a fire lookout tower once stood. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list places the tower at 44.59626, -71.38622 and marks it as gone.[^1] A New Hampshire fire-tower history lists a Mill Mountain at 2,517 feet in Stark, under a heading for mountains that once had a fire tower.[^2] The two sources do not say outright that they describe the same peak, but both indicate that no tower stands there now.[^1][^2]

The same history says that on June 9, 1983, all of the remaining towers in the state were closed.[^2] Today no source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the summit can be visited.

[^1]: New Hampshire Fire Towers (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nh/nh-co/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: History of NH Fire Towers, NH Family Hikes, http://www.nhfamilyhikes.com/firetowerhistory.php (accessed 2026-10-21).
