The register says a tower went up on Huckleberry Mountain in Wallowa County in 1930.[^1] An Enterprise newspaper item from August 1930 reported that materials for a lookout had been packed in.[^3]

The firelookout.com list names lookouts on the summit from 1936 to 1946.[^2] In a February 1937 account, lookout-fireman Mills, stationed on Huckleberry Butte, described a July 14 fire that he reported and was sent to. He found a large white fir burning in the top and, with a local cooperator, felled the tree to examine the burned top.[^3]

The register dates the loss to the 1950s, and firelookout.com dates the abandonment to that decade.[^1][^2]

The Oregon lookout list marks the site as gone.[^4] We found no source that describes the summit today, and no current access information. The register and that list give different coordinates for the summit, so the exact spot is in some doubt.[^1][^4] The land is part of the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest.[^1]
