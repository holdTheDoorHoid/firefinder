The National Historic Lookout Register describes the Bar ōf Ranch Tower as the former Charlotte Fire Tower, moved to the Bar ōf Ranch, a private ranch outside Clinton in Van Buren County, Arkansas.[^1] It is a 100-foot MC-39 tower, and the register says it is on private property.[^1]

The register does not say when the Charlotte tower was built or who built it. It also does not say when it was moved or where it stood before.[^1] The page also describes Camp Charlotte, a Civilian Conservation Corps camp tied to the Soil Conservation Service. It stood near Charlotte in Independence County and was occupied from August 1935 through 1939.[^1] It does not say whether the camp built the tower, so this story does not connect the two.[^1]

We found no record of who staffed the tower or which fires it watched. The move to the ranch is the only change to the tower's history that the register records.[^1]

The register numbers the entry US 1850, AR 29, records its registration date as August 16, 2026, and lists Bill Eoff as administrator. It gives the elevation as 760 feet (232 m).[^1]

Today the register describes the ranch as a private ranch best known for its chuckwagon races, which draw an estimated 20,000 attendees a year.[^1] Because the tower sits on private property, we found no public access. Please do not climb it, and ask the owner before any visit.

[^1]: Bar ōf Ranch Tower (Relocated), National Historic Lookout Register (nhlr.org), http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ar/bar-ōf-ranch-tower-relocated/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
