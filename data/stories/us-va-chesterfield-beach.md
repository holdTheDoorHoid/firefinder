A tower called Chesterfield (Beach) once stood in Chesterfield County, Virginia, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone.[^1] The Association gives its position as 37.35030, -77.62454.[^1] A Fire Tower Enthusiast Group post on Facebook describes a former tower known as Beach/Chesterfield, lying west of the hamlet of Beach.[^2]

No source I could read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Virginia Fire Towers (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/va-co/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Former fire tower site post (Beach/Chesterfield), Fire Tower Enthusiast Group, Facebook, https://www.facebook.com/groups/589436025051211/posts/1034859237175552/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
