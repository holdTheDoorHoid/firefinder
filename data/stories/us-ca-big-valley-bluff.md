Big Valley Bluff is a summit on the north rim of the North Fork American canyon, in Placer County, east of Emigrant Gap.[^1] It lies within the Tahoe National Forest.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower at the site and marks it as gone.[^3] A personal note on GitHub gives 1979 as the year it was constructed.[^2] We found no Forest Service record that confirms that date, so treat it as unconfirmed.

In 2003 a North Fork trails blog wrote a letter to the forest asking for a vehicle closure.[^1] The writer called the spot the old TNF lookout site and wanted cars, trucks, SUVs and off-road vehicles stopped at least a quarter mile north of it.[^1] People were driving onto the summit and making unofficial roads.[^1]

A second 2003 post from the same blog says a Forest Service lookout tower once stood right at the cliff's edge, and that only a few big concrete piers are still there.[^4] A trail listing for the site says the structure was taken down, leaving only its footings.[^5]

The site is reached by a dirt road.[^5] The trail listing describes an out-and-back trail, 1.9 miles long, to an old fire-watch outpost, and warns that the road gets no upkeep after rain or snow.[^5] User reports on that page, including ones from October 2025, list the trail as open.[^5] We found no Forest Service notice about current access, so check with the Tahoe National Forest before you go.
