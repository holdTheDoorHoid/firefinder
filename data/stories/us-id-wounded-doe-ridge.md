Wounded Doe Ridge is a former lookout site in Idaho County, Idaho.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts and a Forest Service list of Clearwater lookouts both put it in the Clearwater National Forest.[^1][^4] A firelookout.com page names the Clearwater/Nez Perce National Forests and places the point 19 miles southwest of the Powell Ranger Station.[^2]

The start date is disputed. A 1981 history of the Lochsa Ranger District, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, says the point was first manned in 1923.[^1] Ranger Ralph Hand set up a tent camp there with Jess Mosher as lookout, and communication ran over emergency wire strung to a telephone line.[^1] The Forest Service's 2016 list of Clearwater lookouts and firelookout.com both give 1933 for a camp.[^4][^2]

In 1939 Ranger Hans Roeffler set up another tent camp, with Keith McGee as lookout.[^1] The radio planned for the site did not work well, so McGee and Daland Broker, then at Rhoda Point, improvised mirror-flash signals instead.[^1]

Nobody staffed the point after 1939, and it was eventually taken off the fire plan.[^1] The firelookout.com page and the Forest Service list both mark it abandoned in 1939.[^2][^4]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit. One Idaho listing tags the point "Hike-In", and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's list calls it a camp that was abandoned.[^3][^5]
