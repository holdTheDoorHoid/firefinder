Peavine Mountain sits in Josephine County, about 19 miles northwest of Grants Pass.[^3][^1] Lookout use began with a rag camp in 1920, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register describes a camp and tree platform in the 1920s.[^1][^2] A 40-foot L-4 tower replaced that early platform in 1933, according to the same register.[^2] A Weebly summary dates a 40-foot round timber tower to 1934 and describes an R-1 style cab, so the year and design differ between sources.[^4]

In 1961 a 41-foot treated timber tower with an ODF live-in cab went up about three-quarters of a mile northeast of the original site.[^2][^3] One summary calls that tower laminated timber instead.[^4]

The lookout was staffed for decades.[^4][^3] A Weebly entry says it was staffed 120 days in 1941 and reported to the Rand Ranger Station, and firelookout.com says the 1961 tower was still being staffed into the 2000s.[^4][^3]

Inspectors found dry rot in the tower, and it was burned to the ground on November 20, 2006.[^2][^3] The National Historic Lookout Register describes the tower as condemned.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register recorded the lookout on March 31, 2003, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register recorded the site on March 26, 2011.[^1][^2] The two registers give different elevations for the site, 3,756 feet and 3,870 feet.[^1][^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register lists the location as a former lookout site.[^2] None of the pages read says what remains on the mountain today or whether visitors can reach it, so this story does not describe either.
