Hogue Mountain's lookout stood about a mile southwest of Eastport in Boundary County, on land in the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^3] The site sits at 4,320 feet.[^1][^2]

The lookout was a 10-foot L-4 tower, dated 1937 by firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts names Glenn Seeliger as a staffer in 1938, citing a Bonners Ferry Herald item from that August.[^2] Firelookout.com lists staff from 1944 through 1951, starting with Barbara Reuter and Margaret McNamara in 1944.[^1]

Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both date the tower's destruction to 1958.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts lists the site as "Removed" and gives no year, so the sources disagree on how the tower ended.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site now, or whether it is open to visitors. The Idaho lookout list also marks it as gone.[^4]
