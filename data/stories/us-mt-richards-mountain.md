Richards Mountain lies in the Kootenai National Forest, about 15 miles east-southeast of Libby in Lincoln County, Montana. The page gives its elevation as 6,001 feet.[^1]

Firelookout.com calls it a fire lookout cabin. The page dates the cabin to the 1930s and marks it gone.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a ground lookout, not a tower, and also marks it gone.[^2]

The same association list includes a separate Richards Peak in Sanders County, which it lists as standing. That is a different site.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left on the mountain, or whether the area is open to the public. Ask the Kootenai National Forest about access before going out.

[^1]: Richards Mtn. Fire Lookout Cabin, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/richardsmtn334.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
