The Seamore Mountain Lookout Site is in Walker County, Alabama, at about 794 feet (242 meters).[^1] The register lists it as US 1624, AL 5 and gives a registration date of July 24, 2016.[^1] A summit listing for Seamore Mountain in Walker County, near Carbon Hill, gives coordinates that match the register's almost exactly.[^1][^2]

The register says the Civilian Conservation Corps built the lookout as a 100-foot steel tower, around 1941.[^1] It was fairly short-lived, the register says, and was removed by 1960.[^1]

Today, the register says, the tower's foundations were moved from their original spot and now act as gateposts on a property.[^1] The register names a private administrator for the site, so treat the land as private and ask the owner before visiting.[^1] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the original spot.

[^2]: Seamore Mountain in Walker County AL (Carbon Hill Area), MountainZone, https://www.mountainzone.com/mountains/alabama/walker-al/summits/seamore-mountain/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
