The Forest Lookouts website files a Beulah tower under Taylor County. Its page quotes Butler Herald reports from 1949, one of them about a lookout tower to be built near Rupert.[^1] The quoted reports never use the name Beulah, so that page is the only source tying them to this tower.[^1]

In a report dated September 22, 1949, concrete was being poured for the foundations, and The Melton Vault Co. won the contract after submitting the lowest bid.[^1] The same report quotes a plan to build the towers in-house to save money.[^1]

By the December 22, 1949 report, a one-room headquarters for the Taylor County Forest Protection Unit had been finished on an acre of land donated for the lookout tower. Local citizens gave the materials, and several firms are named among the donors.[^1] That report hoped one tower would be running by New Year's, but it does not say which tower.[^1]

The tnlandforms.us list gives the Beulah Lookout Tower a site in Taylor County and lists its elevation as 653.[^2]

No source we found describes the Beulah site today, so we cannot say whether any part of the tower remains or whether the land is open to visitors.
