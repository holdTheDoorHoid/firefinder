Pinnacle Hill is in Hempstead County, Arkansas, and the Forest Lookouts directory lists its tower under the Arkansas State Forestry Commission.[^1] In February 1938 the commission announced that work on a forest fire lookout would soon begin about seven miles directly west of Patmos.[^1] The commission planned to use the tower to cross-check with other towers nearby.[^1]

By March 15, 1938, a Hope Star report said construction had begun on a new steel lookout on Pinnacle Hill, which the paper called an exceedingly high point.[^1] The directory header gives the site as 14S-25W-35, while the March report gives the code 35-14-24. The two do not match, and we could not tell which is right.[^1]

Those two 1938 newspaper items are the only record we found of this tower. No source we found describes the site today, so we cannot say whether anything remains on the hill.

[^1]: Pinnacle Hill lookout, quoting two 1938 Hope Star reports, Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/pinnacle-hill.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
