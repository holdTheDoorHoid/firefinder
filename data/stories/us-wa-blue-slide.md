Blue Slide is a former lookout site in Yakima County, Washington, at 6,785 feet (2,068 m).[^1][^2] The site got its start in 1942, when a D-6 cupola cabin was trucked up from the Darland Lookout.[^1][^3]

In 1946 a 30-foot treated timber tower with an L-4 cab replaced the cabin.[^1][^3] In 1960 a 40-foot treated timber tower with an R-6 flat cab replaced that one.[^1][^3]

I found no source that names who staffed Blue Slide or what fires it watched.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

The sources disagree about how the tower ended.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The Register says it was removed in 1971.[^1] firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year, though its heading gives the dates as 1942 to 1946.[^2] Willhite and a Weebly page both quote an April 4, 1972 Oregonian item, which says the station toppled over a cliff after its guy wires were cut, a $5,000 loss.[^3][^4]

The forest is disputed as well.[^1][^3][^4] The Register and the Weebly page place the site in Wenatchee National Forest, while the 1972 quote names Snoqualmie National Forest.[^1][^4][^3]

Willhite's photo captions show several sets of footings and an emergency cabin, with no dates given.[^3]

Getting there is rough.[^3] Willhite's route leaves the Ahtanum Road and follows a gravel road that grows rockier as it climbs. He says a high-clearance 4WD is needed, or the trip is a hike.[^3] The Forest Service lists a Blue Slide Trail that starts at the Grey Creek dispersed camping area and ends at the Divide Ridge West Trail. The part I read does not mention the lookout.[^5]

I found no closure notice for the site in any of these pages.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
