The Pinewood Fire Tower started out in Pinewood, Minnesota, about 20 miles west of the Bemidji fairgrounds.[^2] The Beltrami County report dates it to 1934 and lists its owner as "Mn MFS."[^1] A 1960 survey note in the same report describes a steel tower with four legs and a metal observation room on top.[^1]

Sources disagree on its height. The county report gives 79 feet 6 inches, while the 1960 survey note gives an overall height of 90 feet, and a 2022 Lakeland PBS story calls it 90 feet tall.[^1][^2]

In 2004 the tower went to the Beltrami County Fairgrounds, near Bemidji.[^1][^3] Dave Quam, who moved it, said he learned it was slated for scrap and then began raising money for the move.[^2] He says private donations paid for it.[^3] A 2016 article said he saved it from demolition.[^4]

The tower now stands next to the Forest Education Center, on the fairgrounds.[^4] Quam, who oversees the tower, says it has drawn over 1,000 climbers on each fair date since 2004.[^4][^3] Climbing is allowed during the county fair, and no more than four people may be on the tower at once.[^2] In 2022 the schedule had it open Wednesday through Saturday until 9 p.m. and Sunday until 3 p.m., and closed in rain, lightning or strong winds.[^2] A 2016 article said it could be visited by appointment from May 1 to October 1, and was also open during regular fair hours when weather allowed.[^4]
