The Attala (Kosciusko) Lookout Tower is in Attala County, Mississippi, and the Mississippi Forestry Commission administers it.[^1] The Register describes a 100-foot Aermotor LS-40 with a straight internal staircase and lists the build year as unknown.[^1]

The tower's history is better documented from the late 1940s. In December 1948 a Clarion-Ledger report said the Mississippi Forestry Commission was building the first fire-danger rating station in central Mississippi at the Kosciusko lookout tower.[^2] The station was meant to let tower staff work out each day's fire danger from wind speed and humidity.[^2]

The Register records a site visit on July 9, 2022, and says the Forestry Commission has vacated the site.[^1] The Register added the tower to its listing on July 10, 2022.[^1]

A lookout history site's 2023 note lists the tower as standing, based on Google Earth imagery.[^2] We found no later record of its condition, and no source says whether the public may visit.

Because the Forestry Commission has left the site, anyone curious should check the current owner before going near it. Do not climb or enter the tower without permission.
