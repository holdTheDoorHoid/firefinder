In February 1951 the state forestry department was building a new forest fire observation tower on Stewart Hill, near Wyassup Lake, to cover most of New London County.[^1] The Day reported that the new tower would take the place of the existing Fort Hill tower outside Mystic, and that the Fort Hill tower would keep operating until the replacement was ready.[^1] Wyassup Lake is in North Stonington, in New London County, Connecticut.[^2]

In February 1968 the State Park and Forest Commission invited bids to paint and repair six forest fire towers for work between April 1 and June 30 that year. The list included the Stewart Hill tower, which stood 75 feet tall.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts website marks the tower as removed.[^1]

Today, no source we read describes the site. We cannot say what, if anything, remains on the hill, and we found no information on public access. Please ask the local town office before visiting.
