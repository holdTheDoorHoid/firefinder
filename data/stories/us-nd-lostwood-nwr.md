Lostwood National Wildlife Refuge in Burke County, North Dakota, has a 100-foot steel lookout tower that is listed as standing.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts site says it was erected in 1936.[^2] The refuge's 2005 brochure gives the same year and says the tower was built under the Works Progress Administration.[^3]

According to the brochure, the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service manages the refuge.[^3] Its map marks a lookout tower, but it does not say whether visitors may climb it.[^3]

We found no record of who staffed the tower or which fires it watched, and no recent report on its condition. A 1988 refuge brochure says vehicle and hiking trails give visitors access during portions of the spring, summer and fall.[^4] That older source is about the refuge's trails, not the tower. Check with the refuge office before visiting, and do not climb the tower unless the refuge says it is open.
