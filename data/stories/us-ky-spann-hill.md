Spann Hill was the planned site of a fire lookout in Wayne County.[^1] A bid notice in the Clinton County News from September 1954 said bids had been opened for 60-foot (18.3 m) fire lookout towers.[^1] The notice added that the steel towers would be put up soon, and that one would stand near Spann on land given by Raymond C. Troxell.[^1] The same notice placed a second tower on the Clinton County line, which the Forest Lookouts site covers as Savage.[^3]

A map website, Mapcarta, lists a Spann Lookout Tower in Wayne, Kentucky, and types it as a tower. It is likely the same place, but that entry does not say whether a structure stands there now.[^2]

Beyond that map entry, no source I could reach describes the site today, including whether it is open to visitors.
