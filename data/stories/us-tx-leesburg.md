The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower under the name Leesburg in Camp County. It gives the position as 33.00542, -95.05547, and marks the tower as gone.[^1]

A community with the same name lies seven miles west of Pittsburg, in western Camp County, along the Louisiana and Arkansas Railway and State Highway 11.[^2] According to the Texas State Historical Association, the name comes from the John Lee family, who were early settlers in the area.[^2] In 1874 the town was one of three put forward as the county seat, and it placed second behind Pittsburg.[^2] That same year a post office under the name Leesburgh opened. The spelling later became Leesburg, around 1900.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.
