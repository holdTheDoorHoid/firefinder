Box Springs Mountain sits in Riverside County, and the register lists its location as Moreno Valley.[^1] The California Department of Forestry built a lookout there.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register calls it the only structure that department built in Riverside County, and gives its elevation as 3,080 feet (939 meters).[^1]

We found no build date in the sources we read.

A lookout station stood on the mountain in 1951, but the sources do not say whether it is the station the register describes. A newspaper dated October 31, 1951 reported that an Air Force transport from Truax Field in Madison, Wisconsin, had crashed on the mountain the night before, killing six servicemen. A ranger posted in a lookout station on top of the mountain saw the flames go up.[^2]

The register records its registration on August 10, 2009, and lists the Riverside County Administration Center as administrator.[^1]

Today part of the range is a county park.[^3][^4] UC Riverside describes Box Springs Mountain Park as 3,400 acres with miles of trails, and trailheads that start near the campus.[^3] The Box Spring Mountain Reserve, run by the Riverside County Regional Park and Open-Space District, covers part of the range.[^4] No source we read says what, if anything, remains of the lookout on the mountain today, or whether the former lookout site is open to visitors. Ask the county park before you go.
