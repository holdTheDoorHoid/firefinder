Willow Creek is a lookout site in the Bridger National Forest, in Sublette County, Wyoming, at about 43.10 degrees north and 109.87 degrees west.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it among its unknown and undocumented sites, which means it has not confirmed that the site ever worked as a routine fire lookout.[^2] The name also belongs to a separate Forest Service guard station on the Pinedale Ranger District, which a Forest Service history says was constructed by the Civilian Conservation Corps in the early 1930s.[^3]

The clearest account we found is a Pinedale Roundup story, dated September 29, 1960, and reprinted on the West Lookouts site.[^1] It follows a 60-year-old man who got separated from his fishing party and spent Sunday night below the summit of the lookout.[^1] According to the article, former forest ranger Harmon Shannon said he had hiked up to the Willow Creek lookout.[^1]

A 1968 map of the Fremont Lake North area, as described on the Pastmaps website, shows a Willow Creek Lookout.[^4] Pastmaps is a secondary description rather than the map itself, so treat it as supporting evidence, not proof.

West Lookouts marks the site as "Removed," but none of the sources we read gives a date for its removal or says who took it down.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
