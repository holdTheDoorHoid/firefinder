The Linn Fire Patrol built a lookout on Tom Rock in 1953, south of Mill City.[^1][^3] A March 1953 Albany newspaper item had called it a new station that would protect the Thomas Creek basin and bring the patrol's count to eleven lookouts.[^3]

The register lists a 10-foot enclosed base carrying an L-4 cab.[^1] Another description puts a 14-foot-square lookout house on a 10-foot enclosed tower.[^3]

The register and Rex's Fire Lookout Page both date the end to the 1970s, but differ on how: the register says it was removed, while Rex's page says it was destroyed.[^1][^2] That same page gives the lookout's life as 1952 to the 1970s, a year earlier than the 1953 start in the other sources.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, or whether the spot is open to visitors.
