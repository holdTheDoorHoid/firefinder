The Civilian Conservation Corps built the Greens Cross lookout in Bertie County in August 1934.[^1] The tower was a steel structure of 99 feet 9 inches with a two-room cabin, and the Emergency Conservation Work program paid for it.[^1] The North Carolina Division of Forest Resources is listed as the agency.[^1] The state's tower index also places the lookout in Bertie County.[^2]

Between 1962 and 1964 the lease on the site expired, and the lookout was relocated to another site.[^1] Later entries on the same page give the height as 100 feet, three inches more than the 1934 figure, so the record gives two heights.[^1]

In 2010 the 100-foot Aermotor MC-39 was taken down and moved so it could be restored and resold.[^1]

Today, no source I could read describes what remains at the original Greens Cross site or at the later one. None says where the tower stands now, or whether it was ever restored.[^1]
