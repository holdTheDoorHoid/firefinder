The Nepco Lake tower stood on high ground in Wood County, Wisconsin, close to the border with Adams County.[^1][^2] It was 60 feet tall and belonged to the Nekoosa-Edwards Paper Company, according to a 1970 newspaper report quoted by Forest Lookouts.[^1]

For several years before that report, the company mostly kept the tower as a backup lookout and did not staff it regularly.[^1]

A report dated November 30, 1970 says Wood County police, on a routine patrol early one Sunday morning, found the tower apparently blown over.[^1] Police were examining whether someone had taken out some of the support bolts at the base.[^1]

The Wisconsin lookout list marks the tower as gone, and lists it under the name Nursery (Nepco Lake).[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Nepco, Forest Lookouts, quoting The Daily Tribune (1970), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/nepco.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Wisconsin Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
