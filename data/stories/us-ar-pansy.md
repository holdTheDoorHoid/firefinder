The Pansy lookout stood on state-owned land beside State Highway 15 in Cleveland County, Arkansas, about 1.35 miles north of the community of Pansy.[^1] The survey calls it a tower of the Arkansas Forestry Commission, Unit 4, No. 6.[^1] The directory lists it as "(Pansy ?)", with a question mark, so the name is provisional.[^1]

A National Geodetic Survey description, written in 1942, says the tower was about 100 feet (30.5 m) high, built of steel and topped by an enclosed cabin.[^1] The survey places it about 65 feet west of the highway's center line.[^1] Because the 1942 description treats it as an existing tower, it stood by then, though no source gives a build date.[^1]

No source we found describes the site today, so we cannot say whether a tower, footings or anything else remains on the ground.
