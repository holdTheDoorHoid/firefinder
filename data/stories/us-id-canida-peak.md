Canida Peak sits in Boundary County, Idaho, about six miles west of Porthill.[^1] The lookout pages list it under the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1] The lookout stood on the peak's easternmost point, about 800 feet south of the Canadian border.[^1][^2]

A ten-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab is listed for 1936.[^1][^2] The lookout pages name people known to have worked the tower from 1934 through 1950, among them Roy Hawks in 1934 and Roger Minnich in 1949 and 1950.[^1] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page also lists Howard Green for 1934, citing a Bonners Ferry Herald item from that year.[^2]

Three pages call the tower gone, and the Forest Lookouts entry says it was removed.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, stands on the peak today, or whether visitors may go there. One lookout page tags the site as hike-in.[^2]
