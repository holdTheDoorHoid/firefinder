North Pinhead Butte sits in the Mount Hood National Forest in Wasco County, Oregon, at about 5,447 feet (1,660 m).[^1][^2]

The most specific early record is a January 1928 letter from a forest ranger at Wapinita to the Warm Springs forest supervisor.[^3] It says a telephone line and a trail had been built to the top of North Pinhead the year before.[^3] The letter also planned to occupy these points only during periods of high fire hazard.[^3]

Both sources date the cabin on the site to the 1920s.[^1][^2] Both say it was gone by 1945.[^1][^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site in August 2010, with Ray Kresek as nominator.[^1] Neither the register nor firelookout.com says whether anything remains on the ground today or whether the public can reach the site.[^1][^2]
