Pickett's fire tower stands inside Pickett CCC Memorial State Park in Fentress County, Tennessee.[^1][^3] Wikidata records it as a fire lookout tower named after the park.[^1] A travel guide says the Civilian Conservation Corps built it in the 1930s.[^3] The guide gives no exact year, design or height, and we found none in the other sources we read.

According to the Tennessee Encyclopedia, the Civilian Conservation Corps also put up lookouts and ranger stations in Pickett's forested parts, though the entry does not name this tower.[^4] The land became a state forest in December 1933, and a CCC company settled in Pickett County in 1934.[^4]

A Tennessee lookout-tower list on tnlandforms.us shows it standing at an elevation of 1,720 feet.[^2]

The travel guide places the tower close to the road inside the park, with parking, and says the top is open for a 360-degree view.[^3] It adds that the interior is due a clean, and that the caution signs at the base say climbing is at your own risk.[^3] We could not open the state park's own pages, so we cannot confirm whether climbing is allowed or what shape the tower is in today. Check with the park before you go, and do not climb past any closed sign.
