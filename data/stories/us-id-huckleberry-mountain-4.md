Huckleberry Mountain sits about 20 miles southeast of St. Maries in Shoshone County, Idaho, on St. Joe National Forest land.[^1] The site's elevation is 5,662 feet.[^1]

FireLookout.com dates the first lookout to 1933 and says it was a 20-foot pole tower topped by an L-4 cab.[^1] A list credited to Kresek gives 1936 for that tower, and Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the same year.[^2][^4] A 20-foot treated timber tower with an R-6 flat cab replaced it in 1974 or 1975; the sources differ on the year.[^1][^2][^4]

FireLookout.com reports that staffing continued through the 1995 season, after which the lookout went to emergency status.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists known staff years, starting with 1944.[^4]

The tower was sold at auction in 2013.[^1] Bidding in a GSA auction ended on October 24, 2013; the maximum bid submitted was $425.[^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts reports a sale for under $500 but dates the sale to 2014, which disagrees with the 2013 dates.[^1][^2][^4] In October 2014 a private owner took the tower down.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's 2014 chapter report said the new owners planned to put it back up in north-central Idaho.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts reports that ruins remain at the site: some timbers from the R-6 tower and cement footings from both towers.[^4] Its page carries a Drive-Up tag.[^4] The sources I could read do not say whether the public can reach the site now. I could not confirm from them whether the tower was re-erected, or where it stands today.
