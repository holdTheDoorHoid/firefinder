In 1915 Louisiana earmarked money for fire protection work, which made it possible to hire people to staff lookouts.[^1] The Athens lookout stood in Claiborne Parish and was run by the Louisiana Forestry Commission.[^2] The Forest Lookouts record places it in Section 11 of Township 19 North, Range 7 West.[^2]

A 1965 Coast and Geodetic Survey description of the station says surveyors found it and that it was in good order.[^2] A National Geodetic Survey listing of stations without complete data sheets has two rows for PID CQ2989, which is the same station. One codes its surface mark as reported destroyed; the other says the station has no geodetic control.[^3] The 1965 description and the destroyed-mark row disagree about the marker's condition.[^2][^3]

The tower itself is gone.[^1][^2] The Wikipedia list says it was torn down, and that USGS maps showed it in 2012 but not in 2015.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page marks its status as removed.[^2]

No source reports what is on the ground at the site today, and neither marker record gives a visit date. The Wikipedia list places the old tower at 32°39′28″N 93°3′35″W, and its notes say the tower does not appear in the oldest aerial photos of the area.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register of former lookout sites lists six in Louisiana, and Athens is not among them.[^4] With no tower standing, there is no structure left to climb.[^1][^2]
