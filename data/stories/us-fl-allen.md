Allen is a fire lookout tower in Santa Rosa County, Florida.[^1] The Eastern US Lookouts index gives its location as the survey-style code 5N-29W-34.[^1] The Jackson's Ramblings blog gives the tower's address as 3778 Greenwood Rd, Jay, FL 32565, on County Road 164, and its coordinates as roughly 30.896 north and 87.158 west.[^2]

The blog says Allen is, for now, not listed on the National Historic Lookout Register.[^2] The register's Florida list names 31 lookouts, and Allen is not one of them.[^3]

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the tower is open to visitors. Check access before you go.
