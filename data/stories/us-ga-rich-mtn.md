A Georgia lookout tower list places a tower called rich mtn in Gilmer County. The list gives the elevation as 4,047 with no unit, which is read here as 4,047 feet (1,234 m).[^1] The Rich Mountain Wilderness, in Gilmer County, is part of the Chattahoochee National Forest.[^2] It was designated in 1986, and the U.S. Forest Service governs it.[^2] Its 9,476 acres are part of the 13,276 acres that make up the Rich Mountains.[^2]

A Komoot route page says the summit once held a Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC) fire tower, though the tower may no longer be there.[^3] Treat that as unverified.

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
