Brush Mountain Lookout stands on Brush Mountain in the Six Rivers National Forest, Humboldt County, California, at about 4,023 feet (1,226 meters).[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register says it was built in 1979. It lists the lookout as US 1206, CA 122, registered on June 9, 2017 after a nomination by Jason Barnes.[^1]

The build date is disputed. A 2008 annual report from the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Western Region says a brand new lookout was completed and opened on Brush Mountain that year.[^2] The sources we read do not say whether the 1979 date refers to an earlier cab, so we cannot tell which date fits the structure standing now.

Staffing is described two ways. A North Coast Journal feature, undated in the copy we read, says Brush Mountain is the only fully staffed lookout in Humboldt County, from June through mid-October, and that it welcomes visitors.[^3] The same article lists Brush Mountain among lookouts staffed only in extreme fire danger.[^3]

The sources do not describe the tower's design or any fires it has watched. We found no account of it being moved, burned or taken down.[^1][^2][^3] The feature says it is easily reached from Friday Ridge Road, southeast of Willow Creek.[^3] Because the dates and the staffing reports disagree, check with the Six Rivers National Forest before a visit.
