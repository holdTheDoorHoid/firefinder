Sandpoint Baldy is in Bonner County, Idaho, about seven miles northwest of Sandpoint, on the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1] A camp went up there in 1921, and a cupola cabin followed in 1922.[^1][^2][^3]

Newspaper items gathered on Forest Lookouts name two lookouts. A September 1927 report of a lightning storm, from the Pend Oreille Review, names Clarence Sperling and says one strike landed no more than 35 feet from the lookout house.[^3] In June 1929 two papers reported that Charles Stidwell was heading to the station for the summer, and an August 1931 paper said he had been stationed there.[^3] The notices call the place "Bald mountain" or "Mt. Baldy," and Forest Lookouts files them under this lookout.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts says the cabin has since been removed.[^2] Firelookout.com lists the lookout's dates as "R.I.P. 1922-?", so the removal date is unknown.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as gone.[^4]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, or whether the public can reach it.
