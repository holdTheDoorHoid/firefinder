The Jessie Lake lookout stood in Itasca County, inside the Chippewa National Forest.[^1][^2] A statewide list of Minnesota fire lookouts gives 1936 in its year column for the tower there.[^1] The Coast and Geodetic Survey described a survey station in 1959. It lay about a mile west of the lake, near the lookout tower, and its description mentions the tower's southeast leg and a small wooden building nearby.[^2]

A survey note from the same year describes the tower as a four-legged steel observation tower, 120 feet tall, and calls it a U.S. Forest Service lookout.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Minnesota towers marks the Jessie Lake tower as gone.[^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the site is open to visitors.
