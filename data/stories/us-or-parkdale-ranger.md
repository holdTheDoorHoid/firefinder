The Parkdale lookout stood on the grounds of the Parkdale Ranger Station, in Hood River County, in the Mount Hood National Forest.[^1][^2]

In 1933 the Forest Service erected a 36-foot steel tower there, with a 7-by-7-foot cab.[^1][^3] Firetower.org says that cab was moved from the Mount Wilson lookout.[^1] A 1923 Oregonian account says the Mount Wilson lookout men had just finished a new cabin and steel tower there. It describes that lookout house as six feet square, not seven.[^4]

Firetower.org and firelookout.com both say the tower is gone.[^1][^3] The Oregon lookout history site lists it as removed.[^2]

The station building is on the National Register of Historic Places, listed April 11, 1986, as part of a group of Depression-era buildings in Oregon and Washington.[^5] None of the sources we read describes what remains of the tower, or whether the grounds are open to visitors.[^1]
