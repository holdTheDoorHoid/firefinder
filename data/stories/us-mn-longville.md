Longville's fire tower stands in Cass County, Minnesota, in the Foot Hills State Forest.[^1] The sources do not agree on when it went up. A local lookout history dates it to 1929 and describes a steel Aermotor tower, 81 feet high, with a ladder for access.[^1] A county survey of the towers says the tower standing today was erected in 1925, and that an earlier open-cab tower may have stood on the site first.[^2] The same survey is less sure that the tower was an Aermotor.[^2] The survey lists the owner as Mn MFS.[^2]

Newspaper items quoted on the lookout history page show a man assigned to the tower in 1937 and again in 1940.[^1] In April 1937 the Brainerd Daily Dispatch reported that Harry McKeebe would have charge of the tower that summer.[^1] In October 1940 the same paper reported that he had started work there, because the woods were dry and full of duck hunters.[^1] In February 1939 the county board also authorized the state Division of Forestry to build a telephone line that would connect to the forest service line running into the Longville lookout station.[^1] The sources we read do not name any notable fire the tower watched.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as Standing*, a starred label its legend uses for towers that keep their structure but have lost the cab.[^3] The county survey reports that the cab no longer exists and that antennas are mounted on the framing.[^2] The kjackson.us tower page also reports that the cab is absent and that the tower carries many antennas.[^4]

That same page places the tower behind the city garage in Longville, with radio equipment at its base.[^4] We did not find a public visit route, so treat the site as restricted. Please ask the town before going near it, and do not climb the structure.
