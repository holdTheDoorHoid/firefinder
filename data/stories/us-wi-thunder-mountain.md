Thunder Mountain Tower stands in Marinette County, Wisconsin, on the privately owned Thunder Mountain Ranch.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register lists it as US 1413, WI 48, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list shows it as standing.[^1][^2]

The earliest record we found is a September 1926 item from The Milwaukee Sentinel, quoted by the Forest Lookouts site. It said building a glass-and-steel lookout on the summit was about to begin. Fire Warden Joseph Bletz expected telephone links to other towers the county had recently put up, all running by early October.[^3] The item also called the summit "the highest point in Wisconsin." The sources we read do not test that claim, and none of them gives a completion date for the tower.[^3]

A June 1927 notice in the Rhinelander Daily News, also quoted by the Forest Lookouts site, mentions tools stolen from a "Thunder Lake fire tower."[^3] That is a different name from the Register's, so the sources do not confirm it was the same site, although the Forest Lookouts page files it under this tower.

We found no names of lookout staff and no record of fires the tower watched.

The Register says the tower was registered on March 25, 2020, on the nomination of David Paluch, and gives the site elevation as 1,404 feet (428 meters).[^1]

Today the tower sits on private land.[^1] The Register's only note on it is that it "sits in the privately owned Thunder Mountain Ranch," which it names as administrator.[^1] We found no information on public access, so please do not climb it or approach it without the owner's permission.
