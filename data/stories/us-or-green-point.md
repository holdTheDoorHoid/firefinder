Green Point is a summit in Lincoln County, Oregon, about seven miles northeast of Lincoln City, inside the Siuslaw National Forest.[^1][^2]

A crow's nest lookout went up on the summit in 1933.[^1] In 1939 a Civilian Conservation Corps crew built a 53-foot pole tower of the register's L-4 type, along with a garage.[^1][^3] A January 1940 Eugene Guard item, quoted by the Forest Lookouts site, said the new house and tower would add fire protection near Neskowin.[^3]

In 1942 the lookout saw brief wartime use for aircraft surveillance.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site gives an activation date of August 3, 1942, for the Portland Filter Center.[^3]

The register and firelookout.com both say a windstorm destroyed the tower in 1951.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts history ends with the single word "Removed" and gives no date.[^3] The sources differ on how the tower came down, so we report the windstorm and note the one-word entry.[^1][^2][^3]

The register says a laddered tree from the crow's nest lookout is still there.[^1] None of the sources we read describes the rest of the site, or says whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2][^3]
