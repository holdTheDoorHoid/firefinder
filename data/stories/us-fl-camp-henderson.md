Camp Henderson is a lookout listed in Santa Rosa County, Florida.[^1] It is not on either of Florida's lookout registers.[^2][^3] The National Historic Lookout Register counts 31 Florida lookouts, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register counts six former ones.[^2][^3]

No source describes what remains at the Camp Henderson site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
