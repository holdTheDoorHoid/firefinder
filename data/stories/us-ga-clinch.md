The Clinch lookout is listed on the tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers, which places it in Clinch County.[^1] Its entry gives two labelled numbers, BLL 65196 and GFC 822.[^1] The page carries a revision stamp of March 2016.[^1]

No source I could read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
