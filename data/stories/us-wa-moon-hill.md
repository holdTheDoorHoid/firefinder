Moon Hill is a summit in Pend Oreille County, Washington, at about 3,945 feet.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com places it five miles northeast of Usk, in the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1] WillhiteWeb says the summit is private timber company land.[^2]

The earliest date either source gives is 1929, when there was a crow's nest.[^1][^2] Both sources say it was abandoned in the 1930s.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as abandoned too.[^3] WillhiteWeb notes that the 1941 Metsker map shows nothing at the site.[^2]

No source describes a lookout structure on the summit today. WillhiteWeb reports a small communication site at the top, a short walk from the road.[^2] The page gives a hiking distance of 1.5 miles and an elevation gain of 1,000 feet.[^2] WillhiteWeb saw no gate signs in 2021 and said there was no issue with walking up the road.[^2] Missing gate signs are not permission from the owner, though, so ask the timber company before you go.
