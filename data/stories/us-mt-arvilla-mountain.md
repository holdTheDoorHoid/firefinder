Arvilla Mountain is in Sanders County, Montana, about 6 miles west of Plains, on the Lolo National Forest.[^1][^2][^3] The Fire Lookout Association gives its elevation as 5,138 feet and its position as 47.44189 north, 115.02183 west.[^1][^3] The Montana listing gives the survey location as 20N-27W-34, meaning township 20 north, range 27 west, section 34.[^2]

The association records a 30-foot L-4 tower dated 1935, standing about two miles north of the summit.[^1]

The association says the tower was destroyed in 1962.[^1] A second Montana listing marks the site as removed but gives no date.[^2] The two records differ on how the tower ended, though both agree it is gone.[^1][^2][^3]

The association's list marks the site "Tower Gone."[^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^3] We found no information about public access to the site.
