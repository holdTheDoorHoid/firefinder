In October 1948, The Monroe News-Star reported that two old towers would be moved into Jackson Parish. One was to come from Sikes in Winn Parish to the Hood's Mill community, five miles south of Chatham, and the move was expected to get under way in about 45 days.[^1]

In 1971, the National Geodetic Survey (NGS) first recorded a survey station on the Sikes Lookout Tower.[^2] The NGS record places the station about 3 miles east of Sikes and 0.35 mile south of State Highway 126.[^2] Its 1971 description says the observation cabin is square and gray, built of metal, and glazed on all four sides. It gives the cabin's height as 110 feet (33.5 m) and says four channel-steel legs support it.[^1]

Recovery notes filed by the US Power Squadrons in 1990 and again in 1991 both describe the station as in good condition. The 1991 note is the latest condition record in these sources.[^2]

No source describes what stands at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.

A Louisiana tower list built from USGS names and NGS data sheets also carries a second entry, Sikes Observation Tower, marked historical and placed further north.[^3]
