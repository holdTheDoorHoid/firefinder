Buffalo Mountain is one of the summits in Union County, shown on the Williamsport SE USGS topographic quad map.[^1] Its elevation is roughly 2,073 feet (632 meters) above sea level.[^1] A Department of Forestry report for 1912 to 1913 said the bare top was high enough for a lookout station at that time and that a small tower would be of great benefit.[^2] It also noted that a fire road led to the summit.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register later describes a bald tower in use around 1917 that no longer stands.[^3] That entry was added on September 16, 2008, and nominated by Henry Isenberg.[^3]

Today, no source describes what, if anything, remains at the tower site, and none says whether the summit is open to visitors.
