A Georgia lookout list posted on tnlandforms.us places a lookout tower at a point it calls Homer, in Banks County. The number on the list for this point is 910, which this record reads as feet of elevation (277 m).[^1] The list was last revised in March 2016.[^1] Its entry carries no street address and no notes.[^1]

Homer is the county seat of Banks County, which lies in Georgia's Northeast region.[^2] Homer's Old Banks County Courthouse holds a listing on the National Register of Historic Places.[^2]

The list does not say whether the tower stands within the town or somewhere in the county outside it.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public. Check with Banks County government before going to look for it.
