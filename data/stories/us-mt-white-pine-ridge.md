White Pine Ridge was a fire lookout on the Kootenai National Forest in Sanders County, Montana.[^1][^2] It went up in 1934 as an L-4 cab on a ten-foot tower.[^1][^2] The Sanders County Ledger describes the L-4 design in general: by a wide margin the favorite live-in lookout, a 14-by-14-foot frame cab with windows on every side.[^4]

In August 1936 the Sanders County Independent-Ledger reported that Della Barrett and Irene Mohl went up to the Whitepine lookout to visit Irene Mohl's father, W. L. Mohl.[^2] In July 1939 a severe windstorm came with a lightning storm.[^2] The wind took one cup off the lookout's anemometer, yet the instrument still read above 45 miles an hour, and many trees and snags were blown across trails and roads.[^2]

The cab was destroyed in 1955.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page also lists the site as removed.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks the ground site as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
