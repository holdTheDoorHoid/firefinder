Masons Knob is a summit in Roanoke County, Virginia, on the Bent Mountain topographic quadrangle.[^1] MountainZone gives its approximate elevation as 3,153 feet (961 m).[^1] A Summits on the Air record gives 3170 feet and a prominence of 560 feet, and lists the summit in Franklin and Roanoke counties.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower at Masons Knob, at 37.17472, -80.03224, and marks it gone.[^3]

The Blue Ridge Parkway has a Masons Knob Overlook. From the overlook, the knob stands above the land around it.[^4] According to the same guide, John Mason took up residence at the base of the mountain in 1750. During the French and Indian War, his house served as a stockade during Indian raids.[^4] No source describes what, if anything, remains of a tower on the summit today.
