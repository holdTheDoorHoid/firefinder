The Dunbar Lookout stood in Marinette County, and the National Historic Lookout Register dates its construction to October 1939.[^1] The register describes a 100-foot Wisconsin standard design with a protected ladder on the outside, and it lists the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources as the administering agency.[^1] The register added the tower in June 2017.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list now marks the tower as gone.[^2] In December 2015 the DNR announced it would retire all 72 of its fire towers, and Door County Pulse reported that unsold towers would be torn down.[^3][^4] Neither of those reports names the Dunbar tower.[^3][^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]
