Hart's Hill is in Middlesex County, Massachusetts, and it once carried a fire watch tower on its summit.[^1][^2] The register describes it as a 48-foot iron tower with spiral stairs and a cab of 10 by 10 feet. It says the tower was built on town park land and first operated in 1913.[^1] The register also says the town of Wakefield contributed $350 toward its cost.[^1]

A postcard in the Lucius Beebe Memorial Library's collection tells the story a little differently. It describes the tower as built at the summit in 1913 by the state Forestry Department, and it puts the height at about 50 feet.[^2] The register gives 48 feet, so the two sources differ slightly on height. The register credits the town with the land and the $350, while the postcard credits the state with building the tower.[^1][^2] Both may be right, but no source we read explains the difference.

The register says a new cab was built in 1958. The tower closed at the end of the fire season in 1964, and it was dismantled in 1968.[^1] It has not stood since then.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on Hart's Hill today, or whether visitors may go up to the summit.[^1]
