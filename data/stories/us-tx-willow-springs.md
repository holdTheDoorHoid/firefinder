The settlement of Willow Springs sits on Farm Road 946, roughly seventy miles north of Houston, in the middle of San Jacinto County.[^1] A Texas lookout roster lists the Willow Springs tower under the Texas Forest Service.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the tower at 30.66147, -95.21966 and marks it as gone.[^3]

The sources disagree on the tower's fate. According to the Handbook of Texas, the lookout tower is one of a small number of structures still at the settlement.[^1] That entry was last revised on June 1, 1995, so it may describe the site as it was before any later change.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register counts five registered lookouts in Texas: Chandler, Love's, Pine Mills, Spring and Woodville. Willow Springs is not among them.[^4]

No source found describes the site today or says whether it is open to visitors.
