Tom Dunigan's list of Georgia lookout towers includes a tower called Fort Benning S, placed in Chattahoochee County at 220 feet (67 m) of elevation.[^1] The list gives it the note "topo 1950 army", the same note it gives Fort Benning E.[^1] A third, plainly named Fort Benning entry sits on the same list.[^1]

Fort Benning is an Army post near Columbus, Georgia.[^2] Wikipedia says the post serves more than 120,000 people each day, among them active-duty troops, family members, reserve soldiers, retirees and civilian employees.[^2] Its roughly 182,000 acres sit mostly in Chattahoochee County, Georgia, with the rest in Russell County, Alabama.[^2]

No source describes the site today, and none says whether a tower still stands there.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Fort Benning, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Benning (accessed 2026-10-21).
