Mount David rises in Chelan County, in the Wenatchee National Forest.[^2][^3] On July 2, 1931, the Wenatchee Daily World reported that a lookout was to be stationed there that week, but the opening had been postponed because of snow.[^3]

The cab went up in 1933. Former Fire Lookout Sites and firelookout.com both describe it as an L-4 cab built that year.[^1][^2] In June 1933 the Leavenworth Echo named Magnus Bakke as the Mount David lookout.[^3]

Jim Currie was the lookout in 1948, and he bragged that his station was the roughest in the district.[^4]

The cab came down in 1974. Former Fire Lookout Sites and firelookout.com both give that year for its removal, and Willhiteweb says it was burned, with a helicopter's assistance, to clear away debris.[^1][^2][^4]

Willhiteweb says panoramic photos were taken in 1974, but its own captions and the Forest Lookouts page date the panoramas to August 22, 1934.[^3][^4]

You reach the site on foot. A hiker's trip report on Willhiteweb describes crossing the White River on a big bridge, then climbing 3000 feet of switchbacks to the ridge.[^4] On the summit, the same report found old remains of a lookout, and it says the outhouse hole, which it puts at 500 feet deep, is still there.[^4]
