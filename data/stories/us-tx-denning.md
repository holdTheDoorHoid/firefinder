The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Denning tower in San Augustine County, Texas, and marks it as gone.[^1] It gives the coordinates as 31.46359 and -94.21039.[^1]

The name matches the community of Denning, which the Texas State Historical Association places in the county's northwest, along Farm Road 3409 and some eight miles to the west of San Augustine.[^2] The listed point lies south-west of the association's own San Augustine point, so the lookout and the community may not be in the same place.[^1]

As background, a Texas Escapes history of fire towers says the Forest Service started putting up metal lookout towers at key points in the woods.[^3]

No source I read describes what remains at the Denning site today, or whether it is open to the public.[^1]
