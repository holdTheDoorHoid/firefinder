Threemile Point is a former lookout site in Ravalli County, Montana, about 12 miles northeast of Stevensville.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association gives its position as 46.5914 north and 113.8723 west.[^3]

Both lookout pages list the Bitterroot National Forest as the agency.[^1][^2] The lookout there was a pole platform tower with a tent cabin, built in the 1930s.[^1]

The tower came down in 1961. Firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year, while the montanalookouts page says it was removed. Both give the same year.[^1][^2]

The Association lists the site as gone, with a platform as its type.[^3] We found no source describing what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.
