A Georgia lookout list on tnlandforms.us includes a tower called roopville, in Carroll County, at coordinates 33.44895, -85.13711, with a value of 1289 in its elevation column.[^1] The list does not state a unit for that column, and its note says names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own, so the name may not be a historical one.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
