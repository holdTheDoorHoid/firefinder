Butt Mountain Lookout stands in Giles County, Virginia, at an elevation of 4,225 feet (1,288 m).[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register lists the Jefferson National Forest as its administrator.[^1] The lookout was registered on March 14, 2014, on the nomination of Wes Alvin.[^1] It gives no build date or builder for the tower, and describes it as steel with wooden steps.[^1]

A 1964 Coast and Geodetic Survey description places a survey mark at the site of Butt Mountain Lookout Tower No. 59. The same description says the mark is on the property of two private landowners.[^2] That conflicts with the register, which names the Jefferson National Forest as administrator.[^1][^2] The sources do not settle who owns the ground, so check with the Forest Service before assuming it is public land.[^1][^2]

The register's visit report from January 3, 2017 is the most recent condition record I found.[^1] The 2017 visit report says the bottom two stair flights are gone, the cab floor has disappeared, no windows remain, and the cab is pocked with holes from guns and BB guns.[^1] The same report marks the tower as not staffed.[^1] None of the sources I read names a fire the tower watched.[^1][^2]

Getting there means passing a gate about half a mile from the tower, then a Jeep trail that the register calls vigorous; it says a high-clearance four-wheel-drive vehicle is required.[^1] A visitor listing describes the same rough road and calls the climb sketchy and dangerous.[^3] The 2017 report found the stairs and cab floor missing, so the tower should be treated as unsafe to climb, as the visitor listing also warns.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list also shows it as standing.[^4]
