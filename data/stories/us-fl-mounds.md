The Mound Lookout Tower stands in Wakulla County, Florida, on the St. Marks National Wildlife Refuge, on a small rise that locals refer to as the Indian Mound.[^1] The Register describes an 80-foot tower that has been standing since at least 1935.[^1] It registered the tower on December 15, 2014, as US 1053, FL 7, on the nomination of Brian Pippin.[^1]

The 1935 date is the earliest one given. The Register does not say when the tower was built or who built it.[^1] The refuge was established in 1931 to provide winter habitat for migratory birds, but none of the pages we read ties the tower to that work.[^2]

OpenStreetMap has a tower node called Mound Lookout Tower, tagged as an observation tower with a height tag of 25.[^3] If that figure is in meters, it is close to the Register's 80 feet, which is about 24.4 meters.[^1]

We found no source that names who staffed the tower, what fires it watched, or whether it was ever closed or moved. The Register describes it as standing.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page for the tower carries little more than a header: Mounds, Wakulla County, and the St. Marks refuge.[^4] The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service administers the tower, but none of the pages we read say whether the public may climb it or visit the top.[^1] Check with the refuge before going out, and do not climb the tower.
