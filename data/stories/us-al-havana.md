The Havana Lookout Tower is in Hale County, Alabama, and the Forest Lookouts site lists it under the Alabama Forestry Commission.[^1]

OpenStreetMap, a volunteer map, tags the spot as a lattice observation tower at 135 m (443 ft) above sea level.[^2] TopoZone, a topographic map site, gives an approximate elevation of 443 feet (135 m) and lists the feature as a tower on the USGS Ingram map.[^3]

Statewide, a 1819 News opinion column on Alabama's historic towers puts the number still standing at probably under 75.[^4] The same column says many are badly worn and unsafe, and that climbing them is against the law.[^4]

No source we found describes the Havana site now, or whether the public can reach it. Do not climb this tower.
