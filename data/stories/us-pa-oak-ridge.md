The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register lists an Oak Ridge Tower Site on US 403 and PA 232 in Tioga County, Pennsylvania, and dates its entry to September 16, 2008.[^1] The entry describes a bald tower that was used around 1917 and is no longer standing.[^1]

The trail guide places the Oak Ridge Trail in Tioga State Forest near Wellsboro and describes it as a 4-mile out-and-back walk that is easy.[^2] The trail follows the ridgeline of Oak Point Mountain and has an overlook of the Wellsboro area.[^2] The guide says the service road where the trail begins carried mostly logging and fracking trucks as of September 2021.[^2]

The register gives only the county and highway for this site, so I cannot tell whether the trail passes the old tower.[^1]

No source I read describes what remains of the tower site today.

[^1]: Oak Ridge Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites (Forest Fire Lookout Association), http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/pa/oak-ridge-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Oak Ridge Trail - Tioga State Forest, PA, MyHikes, https://myhikes.org/trails/oak-ridge-trail (accessed 2026-10-10).
