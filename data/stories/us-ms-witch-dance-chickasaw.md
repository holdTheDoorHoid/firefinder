The Witch Dance (Chickasaw) Lookout Tower stands in Chickasaw County, Mississippi, in the Tombigbee National Forest.[^1] The register lists the U.S. Forest Service as the administrator and describes a 100-foot Aermotor tower with diagonal stairs, at an elevation of 549 feet (167 meters).[^1] It gives the build year as unknown, and we found no other source that dates the tower or names who built it.[^1]

The tower shares its name with Witch Dance, a stop on the Natchez Trace Parkway.[^2] The National Park Service describes that stop as a place with picnic areas, trailheads, and miles of hiking and horseback trails.[^2] The Park Service page says the two trailheads near the horsetrail picnic area enter the Tombigbee National Forest, and that the Forest Service maintains the trails there.[^2]

The register notes that the bottom flight of stairs has been removed, but it gives no date for that change.[^1] We found no record of who staffed the tower or whether it ever spotted a fire.

The sources we read do not say whether the tower is open to visitors today, so please do not try to climb it. If you want to see it, ask the Forest Service about access first.

[^1]: Witch Dance (Chickasaw) Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ms/witch-dance-chickasaw-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Witch Dance, Milepost 233.2, National Park Service, https://www.nps.gov/places/witch-dance.htm (accessed 2026-10-09).
