Pinewood was a state forest lookout in Hickman County, Tennessee, at about 807 feet (246 m).[^1][^2] A federal survey station named PINEWOOD has directions that run from the Pinewood General Store along a gravel road, with the lookout on the right.[^2] The same description says a survey mark stamped 1953 sits at the center of the tower's base, which suggests the tower was standing by that year.[^2] A National Register study of Tennessee's forestry towers says the Civilian Conservation Corps is reported to have been responsible for building 98 lookout houses and towers in the state during the 1930s.[^3]

Tom Dunigan's Tennessee list numbers the tower 7-7, a state forest tower number from a 1976 list, and marks it removed.[^1] A 2010 USGS topographic map, shown on Pastmaps, still labels a Pinewood Lookout Tower in the area.[^4] No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.

Tom Dunigan's page says most former tower sites are now privately owned, and it advises against climbing the towers.[^1] Please ask the landowner before going onto any former tower site.
