Tabernacle is a Shelby County tower that the Forest Lookouts Texas list credits to the Texas Forest Service.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as standing, at about 31.67 N and 94.24 W.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page for the tower gives only the county and the agency.[^3] I did not find when it was built, what its design or height is, or who has staffed it.

According to its publisher's summary, Fire in the Piney Woods by Edward Cavallerano covers where Texas's lookout towers stand and how they were designed. The summary I read does not mention Tabernacle, and I did not read the full text.[^4] Access is unknown from these sources, so do not climb the tower without the landowner's permission.
