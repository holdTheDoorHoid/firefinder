Moccasin Point lies in the Nez Perce National Forest, in Idaho County, Idaho.[^1][^2][^3] It sits 28 miles south-southwest of Elk City at 6,569 feet.[^1] The Nez Perce list places the point at township 24 north, range 7 east, section 12.[^4] That list was compiled in April 2010 by Cindy L. Schacher, an archaeological technician.[^4]

An L-4 cab went up on the north point around 1930, a date the Forest Service list marks as approximate.[^1][^3][^4] By about 1960 the cab was gone, and the sources agree on that.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page tags the site as a hike-in, and as both abandoned and gone.[^2] The Fire Lookout website titles its page "Moccasin Point Fire Lookout Cabin."[^1]

None of the sources describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
