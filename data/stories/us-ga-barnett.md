Barnett Lookout Tower stood in Warren County, Georgia.[^1] The Georgia list of lookout towers numbers it GA008 and places it at 33.48347 north and 82.76292 west.[^1] The OpenStreetMap record gives the same name and puts the tower at nearly the same spot, at 33.4834694 north and 82.7629156 west.[^3]

Barnett is an unincorporated community in Warren County, so the community and the tower share a county.[^2] The OpenStreetMap record is tagged as an observation tower and as demolished, so it does not describe a standing tower.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]

[^2]: Barnett, Georgia, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnett,_Georgia (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Barnett Lookout Tower (node 358748502), OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/358748502 (accessed 2026-10-10).
