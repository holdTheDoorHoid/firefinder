Sagebrush Lookout stands on National Forest land in Lemhi County, Idaho, at 7,147 feet (2,178 metres).[^1] The sources do not agree on the forest's name. The National Historic Lookout Register places it on the Salmon-Challis National Forest,[^1] while Idaho Fire Lookouts and firelookout.com both say Salmon National Forest.[^2][^3]

They also disagree on when it was built. The register says its 4A, 14-by-14-foot cab dates to 1926.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts describes a two-story log cabin in 1923, followed by a 14-by-14-foot R-4 cab on a stone basement in the 1930s.[^2] firelookout.com gives the R-4 cab's date as the 1930s, and a second hobbyist site agrees.[^3][^4] The sources do not say whether the 1923 cabin and the 1926 cab are the same building.

The earliest staffing record in these sources is a 1924 forest lookout report, which shows two men stationed at the point that season, one of them an emergency worker.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists emergency service in 1948 and last staffing in 2000.[^2] Newspaper items from 1952 and 1953 mention the lookout being manned and supplies carried up to it.[^4] A 1966 comment in the register's visit reports recalls a summer on the lookout from early July to mid-September, when the first fire was found on July 4 and groceries came in by horseback every three weeks.[^1]

By the late 2000s the cab was in poor shape. A visit report from July 2009 describes packrats inside, a door that does not latch, one shutter missing, and "not much of a catwalk left."[^1] firelookout.com says the lookout "has been abandoned for a number of years."[^3] The register added it on July 22, 2010, noting that it is "badly in need of adoption and restoration."[^1]

The register's visit reports note that the trail crosses a stream more than fifteen times, that no sign points the way, and that one commenter called the trail poor and advised a good map and GPS.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site "Hike-In Abandoned."[^2] Nothing in these sources says the cab is open to visitors, so treat it as an abandoned structure and do not climb it.
