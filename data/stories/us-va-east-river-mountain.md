The fire tower on East River Mountain stands on that mountain in Tazewell County, south of Springville.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a standing tower at 37.18056, -81.37787.[^2]

The sources we read do not say when the tower was built, who designed it or how tall it is. We found no construction record for it.[^1][^2]

What the sources do describe is repeated vandalism.[^1] In October 1967 Chief Fire Warden Charlie W. Dunford reported that vandals had damaged the tower again, breaking glass in its windows, smashing a door and slashing a mattress.[^1] Dunford also said the towers were checked now and then and were staffed only for several weeks in the spring and fall fire seasons.[^1]

Two years later, in October 1969, Dunford called the damage extensive.[^1] He added that the cabin at the foot of the tower had been destroyed again and again, and that someone had tried to burn it at least once.[^1]

The association still lists the tower as standing, but its list gives no date and describes no condition.[^2] We found no recent report, photo or statement about public access.[^1][^2] The 1969 report names the Forestry Department as the tower's agency, but we found nothing newer on who owns it.[^1] Please ask the owner before going near it.

[^1]: East River Mountain, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/east-river-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Virginia Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
