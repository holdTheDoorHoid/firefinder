A Georgia lookout tower list includes the McCollum Lookout Tower in Coweta County, Georgia, with an unlabelled elevation of 996 (about 304 m if the figure is in feet), near 33.46678, -84.69132.[^1] A topographic map directory also lists a McCollum Lookout Tower among the towers in Coweta County, with the USGS quadrangle name Madras.[^2] The tower list was last revised in March 2016.[^1]

For context, the Georgia Forestry Commission's history says the first fire observation tower went up on the Ware Hotel in Waycross, and it suggests a 1928 forestry fair in that city may have prompted it.[^3]

The tower list and the map directory are the only sources we found that name this tower.[^1][^2] No source we could reach describes what stands at the McCollum site today or whether it is open to visitors.

[^3]: History of the Georgia Forestry Commission, Georgia Forestry Commission, https://gatrees.org/resources/history-of-the-georgia-forestry-commission/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
