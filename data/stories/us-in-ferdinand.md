The Ferdinand Fire Tower stands in Ferdinand State Forest in Dubois County, Indiana.[^1] Workers from a Civilian Conservation Corps camp erected it in 1935, according to the National Historic Lookout Register and a fire-tower hobby site.[^1][^2] A Forest Lookouts listing names the Indiana Department of Conservation as the agency.[^6]

The present tower was not the first in the area. The Register and a 2009 report from the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Indiana chapter both describe an earlier tower to the southeast, with a small dwelling for its lookout.[^1][^3] The Register puts it less than a mile away, and the 2009 report puts it about half a mile to three-quarters of a mile away.[^1][^3] The sources do not settle what happened next. The hobby site quotes a February 1983 article that treats it as an open question whether the tower was moved or whether plans were changed after the foundations were laid.[^2] The Register suggests the tower may have been moved, while the property manager interviewed in 2009 could not confirm either account.[^1][^3]

The Register says the tower worked with other towers to locate forest fires, and that local firefighters answered the reports.[^1] It was last used for fire detection in the late 1950s or early 1960s.[^1] I could not find any source naming the people who staffed it or a particular fire it watched.

The tower was restored in the summer of 2009 with AARA funds, which the 2009 report says the State of Indiana received to restore lookout towers.[^1][^2][^3] The wooden steps were replaced with treated wood, and new fencing went up.[^3]

The Register registered the tower on July 22, 2026, as US 1833, IN 14, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Indiana list shows it as standing.[^1][^5] The hobby site describes the tower as climbable up into the cab, but I found no official word that climbing is allowed.[^2] The state's trail map marks a Fire Tower trail, asks visitors to stay on marked trails, and shows a seasonal gate, with recreation-area access through the gatehouse only.[^4] Check with the state forest before you go, and stay off the tower if it is closed or posted off limits.
