The Georgia lookout tower list at tnlandforms.us gives a Cedar Springs Lookout Tower in Early County, with an elevation of 253 and no unit given (read here as feet, about 77 m), and places it at latitude 31.22657 and longitude -85.03215.[^1] The list says its first 72 towers are named from a USGS name database, and this one is entry GA014 in the list.[^1]

Cedar Springs is also the name of an unincorporated community in Early County.[^2]

Since the late 1980s, Georgia has steadily trimmed its fire-tower personnel and turned to aerial patrols, which officials consider cheaper and more effective.[^3] A 2006 newspaper report, citing a forestry commission spreadsheet, found that 137 of the state's 144 surviving towers were no longer staffed.[^3] That article does not mention this tower.[^3]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, whether a tower still stands there, or whether the public can reach it.
