Alpha is one of the Georgia lookout towers listed in Fulton County. The tnlandforms.us list places it at 34.07, -84.26 and gives its elevation as 1,246, which we take to be feet (about 380 m) because the list states no unit.[^1] Forest Lookouts also lists it under Fulton County.[^2]

The tnlandforms list was built from USGS topographic maps and NGS data sheets. For towers numbered 73 and up, the author says the names were made up for the list, so "Alpha" may be a working label rather than an official name.[^1]

No source we found describes the site today, including whether a tower still stands or the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Alpha - Forest Lookouts, easternuslookouts.weebly.com, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/alpha.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
