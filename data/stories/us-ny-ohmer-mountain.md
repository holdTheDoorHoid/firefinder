In October 1911 the state Conservation Commission built a wooden fire lookout on Ohmer Mountain, in Saratoga County.[^2][^3]

The commission's 1916 annual report said it could not work out how to operate the Ohmer station. It also said plans were under way for a steel tower and a new station on Hadley Mountain.[^1]

The wooden lookout stopped operating in 1916, because arrangements to keep it up could not be made.[^3]

The replacement went up on Hadley Mountain, also in Saratoga County, as a prefabricated steel tower made by the Aermotor Corporation.[^3][^4]

Sources disagree on the date. Wikipedia gives 1917 for the Hadley tower, but reports that a plaque at the base of the trail to the tower dates the original wooden tower to 1916 and the current steel tower to 1920.[^4] A Glens Falls Chronicle report also gives 1917, and says the tower remained active until 1990.[^5]

The Hadley tower was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 2001.[^4] It still stands on the summit, and hikers reach it on a 1.8-mile trek.[^5] Each summer a local committee hires a summit steward to help and inform visitors, and the committee says as many as 20,000 hikers a year make the climb.[^5]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Ohmer Mountain site today.
