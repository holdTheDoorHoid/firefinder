The Newton Lookout Tower stands in Newton County, Mississippi, and the Mississippi Forestry Commission (MFC) administers the site.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register lists it as a 100-foot Aermotor with a straight stair on the inside.[^1] Aermotor made several lookout models at set heights, but the sources we read do not say which model this tower is.[^4]

No build date turns up in the sources. The register lists the year as unknown.[^1] OpenStreetMap maps the structure as a lattice observation tower and gives its elevation as 146 meters, where the register says 485 feet, or 148 meters.[^2][^1] A lookout history site lists a Newton entry under Newton County and nothing more.[^3] We found no record of the staff who worked the tower or of any fire it watched.

The register records a site visit on July 9, 2022, when all the stairs were intact.[^1] The entry was filed on July 10, 2022, on the nomination of Jason Johns.[^1] It also says the MFC is actively using the site, as part of its Region 3, Area 32 work.[^1]

We have no current condition report and found no public access arrangement for the site. Treat it as a working state site until the Forestry Commission says otherwise, and ask them before going near it. Please do not climb it.
