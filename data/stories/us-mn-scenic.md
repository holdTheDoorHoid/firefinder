Scenic's tower went up in 1934. It is a 100-foot steel stairway tower in Scenic State Park, in Itasca County, Minnesota.[^1][^2] The A-Z list describes its design as an International Derrick stairway tower and says a nearby CCC forestry camp erected it that year.[^2] We found no account of why it was placed where it stands, and no record of the crew beyond that camp.

The park also has a CCC-era service yard on the western shore of Sandwick Lake, with buildings dated 1934 and 1935.[^3]

The sources do not agree on the height. The Central US page and the A-Z text both say 100 feet, but the A-Z table's height column reads 86.[^1][^2]

In the sources we could read, we found no record of who staffed the tower or which fires it watched.

The A-Z list says a study of the tower's historical significance was carried out in 2019.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^4]

The A-Z list says the tower is not open for climbing, although the Fire Tower Trail leads to it.[^2] If you visit, stay on the trail, do not climb, and ask park staff about the tower before you go.
