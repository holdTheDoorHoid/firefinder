The Carter lookout stood in Oconto County, Wisconsin, in the Nicolet National Forest.[^1][^2] A 1934 Coast and Geodetic Survey station description, reproduced on the Forest Lookouts site, describes a square steel frame tower with a closed cabin on top.[^2]

The tower was erected in 1934.[^1][^2] The survey station described that year sits at the center of the tower.[^2] It lies about a quarter mile west of State Highway 32 and about 1 1/4 miles southwest of the community of Carter.[^2]

The Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources administered the tower, with the U.S. Forest Service as a cooperator.[^1]

The tower was removed in 1975.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as gone.[^3]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added it on March 3, 2019, on the nomination of Brecken Young.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
