Darby Knob is in Tuolumne County, on the Calaveras Ranger District of the Stanislaus National Forest [^1]. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register dates its lookout to about 1914 [^1]. A photograph from that year, taken by J. T. Jardine, shows a heliograph lookout station near Calaveras Station [^1]. The caption also places a telephone at the tower's top, a heliograph at ground level, and a horse and shovel ready for fire duty [^1].

The register gives no removal date and lists it as unknown [^1]. A California lookout list on the Forest Lookouts website marks the site as removed [^2].

Today the register describes the site as a private housing development [^1]. It lists a private land owner among the cooperators, so visitors should treat the ground as private property [^1]. No source found describes what, if anything, remains on the site.
