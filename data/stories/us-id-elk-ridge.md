The Elk Ridge lookout sat on the Idaho-Montana border in the Kaniksu National Forest, about nine miles southeast of Clark Fork in Bonner County, at 5,020 feet.[^1]

The site started in 1936 with a pole platform and a tent.[^1][^2][^3] In 1940 a 40-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab replaced that camp.[^1][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts has not yet recorded who staffed this lookout.[^2] A 1948 item from the Sandpoint News-Bulletin, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, says three men spent a Tuesday at the lookout in August 1948.[^3]

The tower came down in the 1950s.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com says it was removed, while the Forest Lookouts page, citing Kresek, says it was destroyed.[^1][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts describes the site as largely forgotten.[^2] The main remains are concrete piers and embedded eye hooks.[^2] No source says whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3] The Idaho listing calls it a hike-in site.[^2]
