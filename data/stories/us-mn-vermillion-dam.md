The Civilian Conservation Corps built the Vermillion Dam lookout in 1934.[^1][^2] The register describes an 80-foot stairway lookout, while the Forest Lookouts site calls it an 80-foot steel tower; both give the same height.[^1][^2] The Minnesota Department of Natural Resources administers it, and the register gives its elevation as 1,470 feet.[^1] It is in St. Louis County.[^2][^4] The sources disagree on the forest: Wikidata puts it in Kabetogama State Forest, while Forest Lookouts names the Superior National Forest.[^2][^4]

The pages we read record no staffing dates, fire reports or structural changes, so its working life is largely undocumented here. The register and the Forest Fire Lookout Association spell the name differently: the register uses "Vermillion" with two Ls, and the association's list uses "Vermilion" with one.[^1][^3]

The register added it to its list on January 8, 2021.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as standing.[^3] The pages we read do not describe public access or a rental, so check with the Minnesota DNR before you go, and do not climb it unless it is open to visitors.
