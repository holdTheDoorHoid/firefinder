Horse Mountain was a fire lookout in the Kootenai National Forest, about 17 miles south-southeast of Libby in Lincoln County.[^1][^2]

The site began as a camp in 1914. A log cabin and a 50-foot three-tree platform followed the next year.[^1] A cab went on top of the platform in 1921.[^1] In 1941 a 50-foot pole tower of the type the source calls L-4 replaced the platform.[^1] The page dates the first structure to 1915 through 1941 and the tower to 1941 through 1975.[^1]

That tower stood until 1975, when it was destroyed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the public may visit. Check with the Kootenai National Forest before going.
