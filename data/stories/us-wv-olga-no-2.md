According to the Register, the Olga Coal Company put up three towers in McDowell County, and Olga No. 2 is one of them.[^1] The Register's entry gives a registration date but no construction year, and the other sources read for this story do not supply one.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites record for Olga No. 1, a neighboring tower, says that tower's first day in service was October 12, 1939, and that the Olga Coal Company built it too.[^3]

A 1986 newspaper report, quoted on the Forest Lookouts website, describes a 40-foot "Olga Fire Tower" on Olga Coal Company property, with a small cabin that the Department of Agriculture maintained.[^4] The observer then in charge had held the post for five years and was officially on the payroll for only six months a year.[^4] The report does not say which of the three Olga towers it means, so it cannot be tied to Olga No. 2 with certainty.[^4]

The Register places Olga No. 2 on private land in McDowell County and names Alawest as its administrator.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's West Virginia list marks the tower as standing and lists the Olga No. 1 and Olga No. 3 sites as gone.[^2] The Register added the tower on July 15, 2022.[^1] Its page describes no public access, so this story does not recommend a visit.[^1]
