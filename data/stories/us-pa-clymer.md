The Clymer tower site is in Indiana County, Pennsylvania, at an elevation the register gives as 1,697 feet.[^1]

The tower came from a site called Chickaree.[^1][^2] The Chickaree register entry, for a place in Cambria County, says a steel Aermotor tower was built there in 1921 and moved in 1930, when it was renamed Clymer.[^2]

A 1930 item from The Indiana Gazette, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, describes moving the old Chickaree tower, a steel structure 60 feet tall, to a spot near Clymer.[^3] The item also said the new spot would improve what the towerman could see.[^3]

The Clymer entry says the tower was built in 1930, which conflicts with the Chickaree entry's 1921 build date.[^1][^2] The 1930 newspaper item describes a move rather than a build, so the Clymer date looks like the year the tower arrived.[^3]

The register says the tower was removed in 1978 and recorded the site on September 16, 2008.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
