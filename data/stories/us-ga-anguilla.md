The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia fire towers places a site it calls "anguilla" in Glynn County, at 31.25656, -81.60941, with an elevation listed as 14.[^1] The page says names for towers numbered 73 and above are its author's own creation, and this entry is numbered 313.[^1] The name matches Anguilla, a Glynn County place that Wikipedia calls unincorporated.[^2] The list also tags the site with the code BLL-65259, and the page says the Georgia Forestry Commission provided coordinates for 118 towers under BLL codes.[^1]

Georgia's forestry department was adding lookout towers in the late 1940s.[^3] Its biennial report says that at the end of 1948, 22 new towers were in use or nearly finished, and that the towers were paid for with state and federal money in the cooperating counties.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today, and none says whether visitors can reach it.
