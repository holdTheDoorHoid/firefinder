The Sliderock Ridge Lookout site is on Challis National Forest land in Custer County, Idaho, about 38 miles west-northwest of Challis.[^1] The lookout's history pages date its construction to 1934.[^1][^2][^3]

The sources disagree about what was built. The Fire Lookout site and the Idaho Fire Lookouts site describe a 20-foot log crib platform.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page records the 1934 building as a log cabin and credits that detail to Kresek.[^3] None of the pages we read explains why the lookout was put up or who designed it.

We found no documented list of the people who staffed it. The Idaho Fire Lookouts site says it has no staffing history on file yet.[^2] We also found no notable fires tied to the site in the pages we read.

All three history pages say the lookout was abandoned in 1950.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list still shows it as standing, with the type recorded as a log crib.[^4] That list is the only source that speaks to the structure's present state. We found no recent photo or condition report, so we cannot confirm what remains.

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site describes the site as a hike-in.[^2] We found no closure notice or permit rule for it, but no source describes a trail or the route up. Treat it as an abandoned structure rather than a lookout open for climbing, and check with the Challis National Forest about access before you go.
