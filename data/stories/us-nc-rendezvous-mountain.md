The Rendezvous Mountain lookout sits in the state educational forest of the same name in Wilkes County.[^1] The register dates its 50-foot Aermotor tower and 7-by-7-foot cab to 1936, when the North Carolina Forest Service put it up.[^1] A local newspaper gives the same year, and a cabin built by the Civilian Conservation Corps in the early 1930s stands nearby.[^2]

A state biennial report covering 1934–36, as quoted by the Forest Lookouts site, says the Civilian Conservation Corps erected a 59-foot-3-inch steel tower in October 1934.[^3] The register and the newspaper both give 1936, so the sources conflict on both the date and the tower's description.[^1][^2]

A 1950–52 state report says the Division of Forestry maintained and operated the tower and a towerman's residence on the site.[^3] The register lists the lookout as registered on April 2, 2008.[^1]

The Wilkes Journal-Patriot reports that the tower is no longer used.[^2] A 2023 Wilkes Record column describes it as still standing but unsafe to climb.[^4] The register notes that the tower is now restricted for electronics use.[^1]

A rustic cabin sits just below the tower, where the people who manned it lived when they were off duty.[^4] A 2023 column says a visiting party, led by a staff guide, got inside the cabin to look at old photos of the men who worked there.[^4] The Forest Lookouts site gives the 1934 tower's height as 59 feet and 3 inches, while the register gives 50 feet for its Aermotor tower, so the height is still in question.[^3][^1]
