In July 1965 a St. Cloud Times item, quoted on the Central US Lookouts site, said a new 100-foot fire tower would be erected in Mille Lacs County.[^1] It was to be called the Milaca tower and placed about seven miles south and two miles east of Onamia.[^1] The item speaks of a tower to come, not one already standing.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Mille Lacs tower at 45.96816, -93.62024, marked gone.[^2]

Do not confuse this site with Mille Lacs Kathio State Park. A 100-foot observation tower there was originally a fire tower near Isle, Minnesota, and was moved into the park in 1981.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists that tower as a separate entry, marked standing.[^2] Visitors can climb it.[^3]

Nothing in these sources describes what remains at the 1965 site near Onamia today.[^1][^2]
