A lookout tower once stood on Hogback Mountain in Rockbridge County.[^1] A Virginia lookout-history page lists it under the George Washington National Forest and marks it as removed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's state list also marks it as gone.[^2]

Do not confuse it with the other Hogback Mountain, a high point on the Appalachian Trail inside Shenandoah National Park.[^3] Civilian Public Service workers built a lookout and a cabin on that summit in the winter of 1942-43, and the tower was removed in 1962.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the Rockbridge site today, or whether it is open to visitors. A state wildlife management area in northern Rockbridge County includes a ridge named Hogback, but none of the sources we read ties this lookout to that ridge.[^4]
