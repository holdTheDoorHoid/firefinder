Pool was a fire lookout in Montgomery County, Texas.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places it at about 30.52 north and 95.72 west, and marks it as gone.[^1] The Central US Lookouts site lists its agency simply as National Forest, without naming a specific forest.[^2]

Across East Texas, the Forest Service moved from spotters in the treetops to metal lookout towers, because watching from trees was seldom effective.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
