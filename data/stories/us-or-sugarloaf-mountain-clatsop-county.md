Sugarloaf Mountain, in Clatsop County, Oregon, is a former lookout site.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register lists its location under the ODF Clatsop FPA and says the Oregon Department of Forestry administers the site.[^1][^2]

A tree crow's nest went up on the site in 1921, and a patrol point was set on rocks close by.[^1][^2] The register describes only that, so the sources give no further design detail.[^1] Use of the lookout ended in the 1930s, and the register lists it as gone.[^1][^2] The register recorded the site on October 15, 2019.[^1]

The register number is where the sources disagree.[^1][^4] The firetower.org page gives US 1768 to this Clatsop site, at 871 metres (2,858 feet).[^1] The firelookout.org county list gives the same number to a Curry County Sugarloaf Mountain at different coordinates.[^4] Oregon Lookouts also documents a separate Curry County lookout on Sugar Loaf mountain, which was being built in March 1926 near the headwaters of the Sixes River.[^5]

Today, the register says the site is gone, and no source I found describes what remains.[^1] A climbing trip report places the mountain on private timberland, so visitors should not assume the ground is open.[^3]
