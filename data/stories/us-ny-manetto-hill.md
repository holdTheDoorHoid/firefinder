The hamlet of Plainview, in the Town of Oyster Bay in east central Nassau County, took its name from the view of the Hempstead Plains from the top of the Manetto Hills.[^1] The name is older still. A 1695 deed from the Bethpage Purchase already names "Mannatto Hill", and the settlement later became known as Manetto Hill.[^1]

In 1885, Manetto Hill residents applied to the United States Postal Service for a post office. Several accounts say the application was refused because a similar name was already taken upstate, and the hamlet was then called Plainview.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's New York county list places a platform tower on Manetto Hill, in Nassau County, at 40.80363, -73.45383, and marks it gone.[^2]

A wider change followed on September 1, 1959: the Conservation Department ended its active role in forest fire control in Nassau and Suffolk counties, and every active fire tower there was closed.[^3]

No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
