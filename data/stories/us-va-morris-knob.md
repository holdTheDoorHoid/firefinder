The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Morris Knob tower in Tazewell County, Virginia, and marks it gone.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register says the tower was first built in Tazewell County under the Morris Knob name and is now at the Matthews State Forest office.[^3]

The register lists the tower as registered on January 23, 2017, as US 1161, VA 27, and names the Virginia Department of Forestry as its administering agency.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list shows the relocated tower as standing in Grayson County, at 36.64290, -80.95941.[^2]

Matthews State Forest spans 566 acres in Grayson County, a short way west of Galax.[^4] Its office is at 106 Forestry Lane.[^4]

Trails south of Route 58 are closed to hunting Monday through Saturday, from the first Saturday of October to the first Saturday of January, so that closure applies as of October 10, 2026.[^4] The forest office, at (276) 236-2322, handles facility reservations, so ask there about the tower before you visit.[^4] Do not climb it unless the office says visitors may.
