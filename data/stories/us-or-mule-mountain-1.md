Mule Mountain #1 was a fire lookout in the Willamette National Forest, about 13 miles northeast of Oakridge in Lane County, Oregon.[^1] A tree crow's nest was set up on the peak around 1924.[^2]

The lookout cab, an L-4 design, was built in 1930 at about 5,550 feet (1,692 m).[^3][^1][^2] Panorama photographs dated 5 September 1933 were taken by James Rittenhouse.[^2]

The original site was closed in 1952, and its duties passed to a spot about a mile southeast.[^2] The register and firelookout date the cab's move to 1953, to a spot at 5,470 feet (1,667 m). Firelookout adds that the cab may have stood on a 52-foot tower, according to USGS data.[^3][^1]

A Weebly page for the second site gives a different sequence. It describes a 41-foot timber tower carrying an L-4 model cab, put up in 1952 roughly a mile southeast of the original lookout.[^4] The year and the height do not match the register, so the sources disagree on these details.[^3][^4]

The register says the relocated cab was abandoned in 1968 and no longer stands.[^3] The second-site Weebly page says the lookout was burned to dispose of it on 22 August 1968.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at either site. A Forest Service page currently shows the Mule Mountain Trailhead shut because of the Cedar Creek Fire.[^5]
