A steel fire tower was built on Lightning Peak in Bonner County in 1939.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts page describes an 80-foot Aermotor LX-24 with a 7x7 cab and credits the detail to Kresek.[^1] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both give 80 feet for the steel tower, and the Idaho site lists the peak at 3,050 feet in the Kaniksu National Forest.[^2][^3]

The same Forest Lookouts page quotes a 1933 newspaper report of a 70-foot tower built at the Little Lightning creek station. It does not say whether that tower stood on this peak.[^1]

Few people are named. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists Garreth Rouse as known staff in 1949, citing a Bonners Ferry Herald item from July of that year.[^3] The Forest Lookouts page quotes a 1941 Sandpoint Bulletin item that identifies Lynn Dawnson with the Lightning Creek lookout.[^1] A 1928 Pend Oreille Review item, also quoted there, reports that smoke blocked the Lightning Creek lookout's view of the Pack River and Upper Grouse Creek districts.[^1]

Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both give 1953 as the year the tower was gone.[^2][^3] The Idaho site also tags it "Removed," and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks it as a gone tower.[^3][^4]

No source I read describes what, if anything, is still on the peak. The Idaho site tags the peak as hike-in.[^3] It does not say whether the public may go up, so check with the Kaniksu National Forest before any trip.[^3]
