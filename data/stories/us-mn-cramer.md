Cramer was a lookout in Lake County, Minnesota. The county compilation lists it as a ladder tower 80 feet tall, built in 1938 and owned by the Minnesota Forest Service.[^1]

The site sits northeast of what is now George Crosby Manitou State Park, and Cramer Lake lies close by.[^1] The compilation says Cramer Township and the rail hamlet were named after a homesteader.[^1]

The compilation's table calls the tower a ladder type, but a 1952 survey note describes it as a four-legged steel tower that the Forest Service used as a lookout.[^1] The compilation marks it as removed, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's list also shows it as gone.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
