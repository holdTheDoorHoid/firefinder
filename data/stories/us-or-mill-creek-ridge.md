Mill Creek Ridge, in Wasco County, had a lookout built in the 1930s.[^1][^2][^3] The register describes a 20-foot pole L-4 tower with a cabin.[^1] Rex's notes record panorama photographs taken on July 10, 1937.[^3]

Rex's notes quote a 1949 forest supervisor's report saying the state took over ownership of this station and Postage Stamp in March 1950, as part of its protection duties.[^3] The cabin received interior and exterior paint in 1954.[^3]

The register says the lookout was removed in 1956, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2] Rex's notes quote a Barlow District report for 1956, logged in February 1957, saying it was taken down and burned the previous fall.[^3]

Oregon Hikers places the old lookout about two miles east of the ridge's westernmost prominence, which its waypoint marks.[^4] I found no source that describes what remains at the site, or whether the spot is open to visitors.
