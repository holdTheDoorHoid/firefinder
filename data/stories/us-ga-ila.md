The tower began at a site in Madison County, which Forest Lookouts calls Ila.[^1] A Georgia tower list puts that site at 912 feet (278 m) above sea level.[^3] The same Forest Lookouts page ties the Madison County tower to the Georgia Forestry Commission and gives 1952 as the year a tower was erected there.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register says the tower was first put up in 1952 as the Ila Tower, for the Georgia Forestry Commission. The register adds that a Texas crew that built oil-well derricks put it together, and it describes an 80-foot (24 m) Aermotor LS-40 steel tower with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab.[^2]

In 1973 it was relocated to Walton County, and an outside catwalk was fitted during the move.[^1][^2]

Today the register places it at Fairwood, on the Fairwoods Stewardship and Educational Forest in Walton County.[^2] That forest belongs to Don and Edith Shedd, and the register describes the tower as privately owned.[^2] Don Shedd nominated it, and the register listed it on September 9, 1997.[^2]

No source I read describes what remains at the Madison County site today. The Fairwood tower's condition is not described in any source I read either. Because it stands on private land, ask the owners before you go, and do not climb it without their clear permission.
