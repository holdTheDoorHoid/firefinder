Eightmile Point is a former fire lookout in Wasco County, Oregon, on the Mt. Hood National Forest.[^1] Rex's Fire Lookout Page lists a crow's nest there in 1930.[^1]

Forest Lookouts describes the early setup. The fire finder sat on a log jammed into a crack in the summit rock, and early staff stayed in a tent.[^2]

The sources do not agree on what the crow's nest sat on. Rex's page says it stood atop a minor rock knob.[^1] A 2022 visitor's account says it was built atop a fir, in the 1930s.[^3] That account says there is not much history on the crow's nest.[^3] It presumes the lookout was abandoned in the 1940s with the rest of the area's crow's nests, though it offers that only as a guess.[^3] Rex's page marks it abandoned but gives no year for that.[^1]

The 2022 account says the crow's nest is no longer standing.[^3] The writers found only fragments, among them wire, boards and an insulator.[^3] They could not tell which tree had held the lookout. They found the general area with help from other enthusiasts who were also looking for it that day.[^3]

The account describes a walk of less than half a mile up the trail from the Fifteenmile Trail trailhead, reached by forest road, and it mentions no closure.[^3] The 2022 account is the latest source we found, and it reports only remnants.
