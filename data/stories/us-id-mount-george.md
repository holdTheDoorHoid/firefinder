Mount George is in Idaho County, Idaho, at 7,747 feet, about 16 miles north-northeast of Magruder R.S.[^1]

The first lookout was a crow's nest, which Forest Lookouts dates to about 1924, citing Kresek.[^1][^3] A July 1932 newspaper item, quoted by Forest Lookouts, shows a lookout on the mountain reporting snow.[^3] In 1941 a ten-foot L-4 cab tower went up on the site.[^1][^2][^3] Fire Lookout and Idaho Fire Lookouts describe it as a pole tower, while Forest Lookouts calls it a timber tower.[^1][^2][^3]

Fire Lookout and Forest Lookouts place the mountain in the Bitterroot National Forest. Idaho Fire Lookouts names the Nez Perce National Forest instead.[^1][^2][^3]

Fire Lookout and Idaho Fire Lookouts list lookout staff from 1952 to 1961: Donald Woods, William Moen for part of a season, Dick Montag, Dave Warris, Lee Anderson and Tony Rasmussen.[^1][^2] A 1962 newspaper item, quoted by Forest Lookouts, places Rasmussen on the mountain in 1961.[^3]

The lookout was lost in 1969. Fire Lookout and Idaho Fire Lookouts say it burned down.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts says the Forest Service removed the lookout by burning that year, so the sources differ on how it ended.[^3]

None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in.[^2]
