Piney Mountain Lookout is a lookout tower in Fannin County, Georgia.[^1] The register says the tower was put up to watch Piney Mountain and the land around it.[^1] The Georgia Forestry Commission administers it.[^1]

The register gives the site's elevation as 2,396 feet (730 m).[^1] A Georgia tower list on tnlandforms.us gives 2,409 for the same coordinates but states no unit, so this story follows the register.[^2]

The register was entered on August 23, 2017.[^1] The photograph and the nomination are both credited to Justin Peel.[^1]

The sources I read give no build year, builder or design for the tower, and they describe no fires it watched or the people who staffed it. They also do not say whether the public may climb it, so please do not climb it without checking with the Georgia Forestry Commission first.
