The Oregon Department of Forestry's Southwest Oregon Unit Office in Grants Pass had a lookout tower on its grounds.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register says it was a 40-foot tower with an L-6 cab, built sometime in the 1930s.[^1] firelookout.com calls it a wooden observation tower and gives its dates as "R.I.P. 1940s-1940s," which would put the build in the 1940s instead.[^2] The two sources also disagree on elevation. The register gives 1,157 feet, and firelookout.com gives 1,000 feet.[^1][^2]

The register records the lookout as removed in 1949.[^1] Its entry includes a photo of the lookout from the 1940s, courtesy of the Rex Kamstra collection.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the office today. The tower stood at a state forestry office, and the sources do not say whether those grounds are open to visitors.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Grants Pass Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/or/grants-pass-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Grants Pass Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/or/grantspass.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
