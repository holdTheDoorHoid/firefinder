Speckled Mountain, in Oxford County, has no trees on its summit and sits inside the Caribou-Speckled Wilderness of the White Mountain National Forest.[^1][^3][^4]

A fire tower went up there in 1914. The Sun Journal's roundup of Maine towers says it was replaced in 1917, 1919 and 1933.[^1]

Watchmen got to the tower up an old jeep track. That track was closed in the 1970s, and hikers now walk it.[^2]

In 1987 the tower was relocated to Streaked Mountain, to the east, and it still stands there in Buckfield.[^2][^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine list marks the Speckled Mountain tower as gone.[^4]

Today the summit is bedrock ledges where the tower stood, and Section Hiker says only the foundations remain.[^2][^3] Pieces of the old communication wire, some fitted with white insulators, still show up on the trail: some hang from trees and some lie on the ground.[^2]

The summit sits in a federal wilderness area and is reached by hiking trails. The state's hike guide describes an 8.2-mile loop by the Bickford Brook and Blueberry Ridge trails, and Section Hiker describes a route up the Cold Brook Trail.[^2][^3]
