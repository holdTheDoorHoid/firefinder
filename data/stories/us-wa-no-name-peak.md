No Name Peak Lookout stood in Pend Oreille County, Washington, about 10 miles north-northwest of Newport.[^1][^2] The site lies within the Kaniksu and Colville National Forests, and the U.S. Forest Service administered it.[^1][^2]

The tower went up in 1935 as a 40-foot pole with an L-4 cab.[^1][^4] A WillhiteWeb photo is captioned "No Name Peak Lookout in 1940".[^4] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it as US 1516 and registered it on December 17, 2012.[^1]

Sources disagree on how the tower ended. The Former Fire Lookout Sites says it was removed in 1955, and FireLookout and WillhiteWeb say it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2][^4] Washington Lookouts gives only the word "Removed", with no date.[^3]

WillhiteWeb's trip report describes a route from Mystic Lake, along an old road that is overgrowing, then up a ridge.[^4] The same report warns that a hiker who strays into a home site's backyard has gone too far and might be shot.[^4] The hike is 1.8 miles one way, with about 1,200 feet of climb.[^4] Its site notes list footings, anchors, cable and glass, and say there were no views at the summit in 2023.[^4] The same page lists access as "Any vehicle", which does not fit the hike it describes.[^4] None of the sources says whether the site is open to the public.
