Tillicum Peak sits in Pend Oreille County, in the Kaniksu National Forest, and Willhite lists the lookout elevation at 5,746 feet.[^1][^2]

The Forest Lookouts index describes the site as a patrol platform tower.[^1] Willhite says the peak was said to be a patrol point for Orwig Hump Lookout, which sits about two miles southeast of Tillicum.[^2][^3]

Orwig Hump's own record gives a 15-foot tree platform in 1932, a 40-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab in 1934, and removal of the tower in 1961.[^3]

Willhite notes that the maps record nothing at Tillicum Point, though a trail has run across the summit since at least 1932.[^2] He adds that the summit rises more than 400 feet above Orwig Hump, which would have obstructed much of that lookout's view.[^2]

Willhite had not yet visited the summit, and a trail between the two sites appears on the current Forest Service map.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today.
