Lookout Mountain Lookout is a fire lookout on the Bitterroot National Forest in Ravalli County, Montana, at about 7,830 feet.[^1][^2] The U.S. Forest Service administers it, and its cab looks down on the upper West Fork of the Bitterroot River.[^1]

The cab is an R-6 flattop with a catwalk.[^1] The Register and firelookout.com describe a 10-foot concrete base,[^1][^2] but the Forest Lookouts site calls it a cinder block base.[^3]

Both the Register and firelookout.com give 1969 as the year the cab was built.[^1][^2] The cab did not start on this ridge, however. It first stood at Antrim Point, another site on the same forest, and was moved here in 1974.[^1][^2][^3][^4] A firelookout.com page about Antrim Point says an R-6 cab replaced the older L-4 cab there in 1968, a year before the 1969 build date given elsewhere.[^1][^2][^4]

The Register says the lookout is regularly staffed during the fire season,[^1] and firelookout.com says it is staffed in the summer.[^2] The sources read say nothing about which fires it watched or who staffed it in earlier decades.

The Antrim Point page says the cab still stands on this ridge.[^4] The Register accepted it on April 24, 2009, as US 791, MT 48, after a nomination by Gary Weber.[^1]

Because it is staffed during fire season, check with the West Fork Ranger District before planning any visit.[^1] The sources read do not mention a public rental for it.
