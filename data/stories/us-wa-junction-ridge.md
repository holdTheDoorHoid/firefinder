Junction Ridge was a crow's nest on the east point of a ridge in Chelan County, Washington.[^1][^2] The site sits in the Wenatchee National Forest, and one lookout site places it about 12 miles northwest of Stehekin.[^1]

A directory dates the crow's nest to 1933 and says it was abandoned in 1940.[^1] A page on the same lookout repeats both dates and credits them to the Kresek Inventory.[^3]

Panorama photographs are dated September 19, 1934.[^2] WillhiteWeb says the photos were made somewhere other than the east point, and not from a crow's nest. It suggests the crow's nest may still have stood on the east point then, and that the photos came from one of the knobs further west, perhaps from the spot where a larger lookout was planned.[^3]

The Forest Lookouts directory marks the site as gone, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Washington list shows it as abandoned.[^2][^4]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the ridge is open to visitors. The WillhiteWeb page lists the lookout's elevation as unknown.[^3]
