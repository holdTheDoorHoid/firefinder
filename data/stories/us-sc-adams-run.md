The Forest Lookouts index lists Adams Run as a South Carolina Forestry Commission tower in Charleston County.[^1][^2]

The commission's first fire tower went up in August 1930, near Camden.[^3] A large share of early towers used wooden beams, and steel later replaced every wooden one.[^3]

A July 15, 1949 item, which Forest Lookouts quotes from the Aiken Standard, names Mrs. Thelma Garvin as the towerlady at Adams Run.[^1] It reports that she took third prize in the 1948–49 Towersite Improvement contest.[^1]

The towers were not normally staffed after dark.[^3] Starting in the 1960s, women filled most operator posts, and their husbands often worked as firefighters.[^3] Often the post stayed in one family, passing from parents to grown children.[^3]

The Forestry Commission shut down its lookout network on October 1, 1993.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the Adams Run site, or whether visitors can reach it.
