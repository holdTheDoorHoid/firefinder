The Snow Shoe fire tower stands in Centre County, Pennsylvania, in and around the borough that shares its name.[^2] The first tower on the site was a 60-foot Aermotor erected in 1921.[^2][^1] A 1926 newspaper report, quoted on the same lookout history page, put the tower at about 2,000 feet and said it sat practically within the borough limits.[^2]

In 1977 a debris fire got out of control around the tower and forced the operator out of it to avoid the smoke.[^2] I found no record of who staffed the tower in other years, or for how long.

The current tower is a newer structure. The register says it was built around 2018 as part of Pennsylvania's program to modernize its fire tower fleet, and that it replaced the 60-foot Aermotor.[^1] A 2018 Pennsylvania chapter report from the Forest Fire Lookout Association says the Commonwealth is replacing many of its older towers with new structures, a job to be done in several phases over several years.[^3] The 1921 and 2018 dates do not truly conflict, since they belong to different structures on the same site.[^1][^2]

The register lists the tower as US 1255, PA 24, registered on December 6, 2017, and administered by the Pennsylvania Bureau of Forestry.[^1] I did not find a source that says whether the tower is staffed today or open to visitors, so check with the Bureau before planning a visit.
