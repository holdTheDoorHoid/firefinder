Bear Creek was a fire lookout in northern Idaho.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says a 20-foot pole tower of the L-4 type went up in 1934.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts and Rex's Fire Lookout Page give the same year and tower type.[^3][^4]

We found no staffing history. Idaho Fire Lookouts says it does not yet have a documented record of who staffed the tower, and it asks readers who know to get in touch.[^3]

The tower ended about 1961.[^1] Three sites give that year as the date it was destroyed.[^2][^3][^4] The register says it was removed about that time, so the sources differ on whether it came down or was destroyed.[^1] Firelookout.com lists the agency as Priest Lake TPA/IDL.[^2]

None of the sources we read describes the site today, so we cannot say what, if anything, remains on the ground. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^3] We found no rules about access, so check with the managing agency before going.
