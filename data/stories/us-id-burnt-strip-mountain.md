Burnt Strip Mountain is in Idaho County, Idaho, within the Bitterroot National Forest, about 10 miles north-northeast of the Magruder Ranger Station, at an elevation of 7,489 feet.[^1][^2]

The earliest record is a cabin dated about 1939.[^1][^3] The Forest Lookouts listing also mentions a 50-foot tower, which it says was torn down in 1956, with its usable parts salvaged.[^3]

Two sources give 1955 as the year the lookout was destroyed.[^1][^2] It is not clear from the sources whether the 50-foot tower and the lookout were the same structure.

Idaho Fire Lookouts describes the site as a hike-in.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
