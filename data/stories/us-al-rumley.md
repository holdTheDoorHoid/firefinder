The Rumley Lookout first stood in Sumter County, Alabama.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register says the Civilian Conservation Corps erected it in the late 1930s, and it gives 1936 as the build year.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site lists the Alabama Forestry Commission under the tower's name.[^2]

In 2005 the tower was relocated to Perry Lakes Park, in Perry County, and rebuilt for bird-watching.[^2] Students from Judson College and Auburn University's Rural Studio, with help from the Alabama Forestry Commission and Perry County, moved and restored it.[^1][^2] The rebuild left out the 7-by-7-foot cab.[^1] The register says this was done to create an open-sky canopy effect for bird-watching.[^1]

Today the 100-foot (about 30 m) Aermotor MC-39 stands in Perry Lakes Park, which the register places on US 852 and AL 30.[^1] The register says the tower is promoted as the nation's tallest bird-viewing tower, and that the Perry County Commission administers it.[^1] It was listed on the register on December 16, 2009.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, is left at the original Sumter County site today.
