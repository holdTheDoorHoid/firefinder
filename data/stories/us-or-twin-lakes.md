Twin Lakes was a lookout on the Umpqua National Forest in Douglas County, Oregon.[^2][^3]

The register describes a rough crow's nest platform with an Adirondack shelter from the early 1920s.[^1] Forest Lookouts describes a different early set-up: five wood posts with a 12-foot platform, around 1922.[^3]

The register and firelookout.com both put a 14-foot tower on the site in 1930, and firelookout.com calls it an L-4 tower.[^1][^2] A July 1930 item quoted by Forest Lookouts described a new tower of poles cut on the ground.[^3] The same page gives a 15-foot tower with a gable-roofed cab for about 1938, so the sources do not agree on the tower's height or date.[^3]

An August 1927 item quoted by Forest Lookouts named Harold Church as a Twin Lakes lookout.[^3] In July 1938 a supply plane passed low enough over the station that the faces of its lookout crew could be seen.[^3]

The register dates the tower's removal to the 1970s.[^1] firelookout.com says it was destroyed during the 1970s.[^2] Forest Lookouts gives 1962 as the year it was removed.[^3] The dates disagree by roughly a decade.[^1][^2][^3]

Nothing I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
