Quitman, founded in 1839, is the county seat of Clarke County, Mississippi, and lies beside the Chickasawhay River.[^3] The Quitman Lookout Tower is listed in that same county.[^2]

OpenStreetMap maps a node called Quitman Lookout Tower and tags it as an observation tower of lattice construction, with an elevation of 112 metres (about 367 feet).[^1] The map entry gives no build date or history.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site lists the tower under Mississippi, with Clarke County as its only location detail, and gives no build date, history or agency.[^2]

We found no source that says when the tower was built, who staffed it, whether it has seen fires, or whether it still stands today. No source describes the site now, and access is unknown.
