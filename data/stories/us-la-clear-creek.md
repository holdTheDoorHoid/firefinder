The Clear Creek Lookout stood in the Kisatchie National Forest in Louisiana.[^1] Sources disagree about the parish. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives Sabine Parish,[^1] while the Forest Lookouts list gives Grant Parish.[^2]

The Civilian Conservation Corps built the tower in 1935.[^1] It stood 100 feet tall, was made of steel, and was an Aermotor LS-40 model with a 7-by-7-foot cab.[^1]

From 1941 to 1945 it served as both a fire lookout and an observation tower, the years U.S. Army Camp Livingston was built, occupied and closed.[^1]

The register recorded the site on April 4, 2011, on the nomination of Mark H. Gutzman.[^1] A 2011 Louisiana chapter report says the site joined the register that April.[^3]

The tower came down in the early 1980s, as most other Louisiana towers did when the state turned to overflights instead.[^1] The Forest Lookouts list also marks it as removed.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.[^1]
