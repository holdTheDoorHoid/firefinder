Silver Butte is in Clearwater County, Idaho, about 26 miles northeast of Orofino, at 4,680 feet.[^1] The lookout is listed under the Clearwater Timber Protective Association.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page gives a crow's nest in 1915 and a platform tower in 1927, and credits both to a source it names as Kresek.[^2] The sources do not say whether the platform replaced the crow's nest.[^1][^2]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page says it does not yet have records of the lookout's staffing.[^3]

Three of the pages list the lookout as abandoned, with no date given for when that happened.[^1][^3][^4] None of the pages says the platform was taken down.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Today, no source describes what, if anything, remains at the site.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page labels the spot drive-up, but it does not say whether the public may visit.[^3]
