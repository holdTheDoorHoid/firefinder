The Mars Hill Lookout Tower is in Winston County, Mississippi.[^1] The Register gives its elevation as 674 feet (205 meters).[^1]

The Register describes a 100-foot Aermotor LS-40 with a straight internal staircase, 14 landings and 15 flights.[^1] It lists the build date as unknown and names the Mississippi Forestry Commission as administrator.[^1] We found no account of who built the tower, when it went up, or who staffed it.[^1]

The tower was registered on July 10, 2022, as US 1662, MS 27, after a site visit on July 9, 2022.[^1]

The visit notes are the main evidence about its condition. At that visit the lower treads had been removed and many treads were missing.[^1] The Register also says the site was vacated by the Forestry Commission and is possibly owned by a timber company.[^1] We could not confirm the owner from any other source.

Because the site may be owned by a timber company, treat it as private land. Do not climb the tower, and do not go onto the property without asking the owner first.

[^1]: Mars Hill Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ms/mars-hill-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
