Flat Creek Lodge is a hunting and fishing property in Emanuel County, about nine miles from downtown Swainsboro, set in a 2,000-acre pine forest.[^1][^2] The fire tower on the grounds did not start there.[^1] The Georgia tower list records the lodge entry as a tower moved from Oak Park, and it keeps a separate Oak Park Lookout Tower in the same county, tagged gfc-111.[^1]

A March 2007 magazine profile of the lodge, then about five years old, lists "a decommissioned fire tower from Oak Park in Emanuel County" among its features.[^2] By then the tower was already at the lodge and described as out of use.[^2]

Today the lodge's website advertises pheasant shoots sold as packages.[^3] The shoot uses a "concealed central tower" from which birds are released.[^3] No source ties that shooting structure to the old fire tower, and no source describes what, if anything, remains of the fire tower at the site now. No source says the old tower is open to visitors or describes its condition, so do not climb it.
