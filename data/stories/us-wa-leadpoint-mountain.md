The Leadpoint Mountain lookout was an L-4 tower built in 1938 on the Colville National Forest in Stevens County, Washington.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com places it about 10 miles northwest of Metaline Falls.[^2]

In April 1939, a Colville Examiner item quoted on the Forest Lookouts page reported that crews were working on telephone line to link lookout stations, and that a 35-mile section was being built to the Leadpoint lookout.[^4]

The tower came down in the 1950s. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register, WillhiteWeb and Forest Lookouts say it was removed, while firelookout.com says it was destroyed.[^1][^2][^3][^4] We found no source that names anyone who staffed it or any fire it watched.

The exact spot is uncertain. WillhiteWeb says the Metsker map shows the Leadpoint name at the north end of Red Top Mountain, and that the Kresek inventory places the lookout on that summit.[^3] Ray Kresek nominated the site for the Former Fire Lookout Sites register, which listed it on December 16, 2012.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
