Rowe Gap is a former fire lookout site in Franklin County, Tennessee.[^1] The Tennessee Division of Forestry ran it, and the state's 1976 tower list gives it as tower 10 in district 5.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts lists a Franklin County tower under the name Keith Springs, in the same district.[^2] The Keith Springs page does not mention Rowe Gap, so the link between the two names is an inference from matching county and district, not a statement by either source.

The Division's national register form says it built 208 fire lookout towers between about 1930 and 1970.[^3] Its largest number, in the late 1960s, was 208 towers in operation.[^3] The form includes a list of towers still standing on March 26, 2014, and Rowe Gap is on it.[^3]

The Keith Springs entry on Forest Lookouts says the tower was dismantled and removed on November 3, 2017, and was to be reassembled at a location in Bedford County.[^2] Tennessee Landforms marks it removed as well.[^1] No other source here confirms that the tower went to Bedford County.

No source describes what remains at the Franklin County site, such as footings or a base, or whether the spot is open to visitors.[^1][^2] Tennessee Landforms says many lookouts statewide have been removed and most of their sites are now on private land, but it does not say that about Rowe Gap.[^1] Check who owns the land before going to look.
