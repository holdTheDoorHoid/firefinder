The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list places a Peters Mountain lookout in Alleghany County, at 37.72055 N, 80.14389 W. The list gives its type as unknown and its status as gone.[^1]

Peters Mountain, 52 miles (84 km) long, lies on the border of Alleghany, Giles and Craig counties in Virginia and Monroe County in West Virginia.[^2]

Hanging Rock, another fire tower on the mountain in Monroe County, West Virginia, is listed at 37.50448 N, 80.45302 W, a different point from the Virginia record.[^3][^4] A Forest Lookouts history says the Hanging Rock tower last served for fire detection in 1972 and that a fire destroyed it in February 1996.[^4]

No source we read describes what remains of the Virginia lookout today, or whether the public can reach the site.
