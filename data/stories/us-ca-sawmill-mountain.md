Sawmill Mountain sits in the Sierra Pelona range, within the Angeles National Forest near Lake Hughes, in Los Angeles County.[^1][^2] In 1929 the Los Angeles County Forestry Department was building a fire lookout there as part of a wider fire-protection scheme for the county's north, especially Pine Canyon.[^1]

The tower was a 30-foot tower of galvanized steel, with a cabin for the lookout man.[^1] The plan also called for about four miles of trail up from Pine Canyon and about four miles of telephone line.[^1] A March 28, 1929 newspaper report said the tower was under construction.[^1] A November 1934 newspaper report says it had been erected in 1928, and a hiking site says the lookout was established in 1929, so the start date is unclear.[^1][^2]

A November 1934 report says the county had dismantled the tower because it no longer matched the new detection plan.[^1] Two sources say it was moved to Tejon Mountain in 1935.[^1][^2] A county annual report says a Tejon Lookout Tower was erected in December 1936, and a Tejon site record dates the piers there to 1936, so it is not clear that the Sawmill tower ever went to Tejon.[^3][^4]

A hiking site reports that cement blocks are the only remnants on the summit.[^2] The summit is on Angeles National Forest land, and the same site gives a forest road, FR 7N23, as a route to it.[^2] The Fire Lookout index lists the Sawmill Mountain tower as gone.[^5]

At Tejon Mountain, a site record notes that remnants of a wooden cabin are still there.[^4]
