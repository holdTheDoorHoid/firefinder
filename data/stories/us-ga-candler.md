A Georgia lookout tower list places a tower called candler in Candler County, at 32.38893, -81.98161.[^1] Its elevation column reads 221, with no unit given.[^1] The row also carries the codes BLL-64689 and gfc-109, along with the word usgs.[^1]

The list's Google map is marked as revised on 22 March 2016.[^1] The list says names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own creations. Candler is numbered 273, so its name may not be a historical one.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
