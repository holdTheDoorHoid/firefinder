Spencer Butte is a 4,247-foot summit in the Gifford Pinchot National Forest, in Skamania County, Washington.[^1] The Forest Service administers the site.[^1]

The register and firelookout.com date the site's development to the 1920s, and its first structure was a D-6 cupola cabin.[^1][^2] WillhiteWeb and the Forest Lookouts list give 1920 as the start.[^3][^4] A 20-foot timber tower with an L-4 cab followed in 1938, a year all four sources give.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Two other sources disagree about the tower's date. The Forest Service trail page and Oregon Hikers both give 1935,[^5][^6] and a WillhiteWeb photo caption places the tower under construction in 1936.[^3]

The register says both structures have been removed, and firelookout.com says both were destroyed.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts list says the lookout was removed.[^4] Some remains are still on the summit.[^3][^5][^6] A WillhiteWeb photo caption mentions two lookout footings, and the same page notes that some bolts, nails and glass remain.[^3] Oregon Hikers describes a concrete foundation at the top, and the Forest Service says the remains of a lookout are on the summit.[^6][^5]

Accounts of the top differ. Oregon Hikers calls it grassy, while WillhiteWeb says trees have again covered the broad summit, leaving no views.[^6][^3] The Forest Service says the canopy mostly hides the skyline.[^5]

The Forest Service lists Trail 30 as the Spencer Butte Trail.[^5] From FR 25, the paved FR 93 runs about 7.6 miles to a high saddle with parking at the south trailhead.[^3] Trail lengths differ: Oregon Hikers says about 1.5 miles from either FR 93 trailhead, WillhiteWeb gives 1.6 miles one way, and the Forest Service lists 3.2 miles one way from the south.[^6][^3][^5]
