The register places this 110-foot tower at the Ivor and Unity Road crossing in Southampton County, Virginia, near Vicksville and Sedley, where it had stood since the 1930s.[^1]

The tower's owners, Michael and Christina Upton, gave it to the Cherokee Ridge Alpine Trail Association (CRATA).[^1] The association began dismantling it in 2021 to move it to Alabama, and the register does not say when the move was finished.[^1]

The tower now stands at Overlook Park in Tallapoosa County, at the south end of Lake Martin.[^1] The register describes a full restoration that put in steel and wooden steps, flooring and fencing, and says it opened to the public in November 2025.[^1] A Lake Martin magazine article calls it newly constructed and says volunteers worked to reconstruct it.[^2] That could fit a rebuild from moved parts, but the register is the only source we found that names Virginia.

The register titles it the Jimmy K. Lanier Fire Lookout Tower (Relocated).[^1] A 2012 Alfa Farmers article calls Jimmy K. Lanier of Eclectic CRATA's treasurer and former president, and credits him with the idea for restoring the Smith Mountain Fire Tower.[^3] We found no source that explains why this tower carries his name. Will Sprinkle nominated it, and the register added it on August 5, 2026.[^1]

Today the tower is open to the public, and the register says it is easy to reach by car at the south end of Lake Martin.[^1] We found no current hours or visitor rules, so check with CRATA before you go.
