Fox Mountain sits in the Modoc National Forest in Modoc County, California.[^1] The register says the lookout was built in 1936 as a California Region 5 Plan BC-301 cabin on a 10-foot enclosed timber tower.[^1] A 1948 Coast and Geodetic Survey station sheet describes a 15-foot tower instead, so the two sources disagree on height.[^1][^2]

A November 1944 work-project budget describes a lookout observation unit with living quarters underneath.[^2] It also lists a one-car garage, storage, water storage, a windcharger, a latrine, and a cistern that was then in place.[^2]

The staffing record is thin. The Weekly Adin Argus reported in October 1938 that Lewis Page and his wife had been at the lookout that summer.[^2] Henry K. Keene is named as fire guard there in 1939.[^2] In June 1947 the same paper said Ben Bath of Adin would be stationed on Fox Mountain.[^2]

From 1942 through 1946 the lookout made 22 first discoveries, according to the Forest Lookouts history page.[^2] A June 1947 inspection found the cabin generally in good condition.[^2] It also found that none of the shutter bolts worked well.[^2]

The register gives no removal date and lists it as unknown.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list marks the site as gone.[^3]

The register page carries a 2008 photo of the site, but its caption says nothing about what the photo shows.[^1] A 1948 survey sheet said good Forest Service roads ran from the town of Adin to the station.[^2] We found no source describing what stands at the site now or whether visitors can reach it.
