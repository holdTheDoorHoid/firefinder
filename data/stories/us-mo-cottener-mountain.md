The lookout that the Central US Lookouts index calls Cottener Mountain stood in Madison County, Missouri, in what the index lists as the Clark National Forest.[^1] That forest was named after Champ Clark, a state legislator, and was established on September 11, 1939.[^2] In 1973 it was administratively combined with the Mark Twain National Forest, and in 1976 it was absorbed by that forest.[^2]

According to the index, the tower was taken down, moved and put back up on private land.[^1]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the original site now, or whether that spot is open to the public.
