Ron Kemnow's Forest Lookouts site includes a Big Game Ridge lookout in Teton County, on the Teton National Forest.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association places Big Game Ridge in its unknown or undocumented group, not among its lookouts.[^2] It says it will move a site onto its lookout lists if evidence or documentation turns up showing regular use as a wildland fire lookout.[^2]

Wikipedia describes Big Game Ridge as lying on the southern border of Yellowstone National Park, and its Mount Hancock article says a peak on the ridge is in the park.[^3] The Kemnow page places the lookout in the Teton National Forest.[^1] The two accounts do not agree, and this story cannot settle which is right.

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public.
