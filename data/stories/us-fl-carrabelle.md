The Forest Lookouts site files the Carrabelle tower under Franklin County, Florida, and gives no builder, height, or construction date for it.[^1]

The only dated event in the sources I read is a state surplus sale. On September 14, 2005, bids were opened to sell the tower, and the winning bid was $1,100.[^1] The state's general notice on fire tower sales says the high bidder is expected to take the tower apart and haul it away, typically in sections, using a crane the buyer provides.[^3]

Nothing I found says whether this tower was bought, moved, or left standing where it was. An OpenStreetMap entry marks a lattice-built observation tower at the spot, but that is a map record, not a report on its condition.[^2]

I found no source about public access or climbing rules. Treat the site as unverified, do not climb any tower there, and check with the Florida Forest Service or the current landowner before any visit.
