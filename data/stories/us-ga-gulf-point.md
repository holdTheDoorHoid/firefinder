The Georgia lookout tower list places a tower called gulf point in Walker County, at coordinates 34.62555, -85.46756.[^1] Its elevation column reads 2197, and the entry does not state a unit.[^1] The list's compiler says the names of towers numbered 73 and up are their own invention, so this name may not match a historical one.[^1] The entry also carries the notes BLL-41108, map and gfc-704.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
