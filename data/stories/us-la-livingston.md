The Livingston Fire Tower stood in Livingston Parish, Louisiana, and a Wikipedia list of Louisiana fire lookout towers includes it.[^1][^2]

The same list gives some background on Louisiana's towers.[^1] The first steel fire tower went up near Bogalusa in 1922, and fifty-six towers had been built by 1950.[^1]

The list says the Livingston tower was torn down between 2016 and 2017, and it points readers to a Google Street View image of the tower.[^1]

The Central US Lookouts page for Livingston gives a later date. It lists the tower as removed in January 2018.[^2] The two sources disagree on the year.

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it. Both sources treat the tower as gone, so there is nothing to climb.[^1][^2] Before visiting the spot, ask Livingston Parish about access to the land.

[^1]: List of fire lookout towers in Louisiana, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_fire_lookout_towers_in_Louisiana (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Livingston - Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/livingston.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
