A Georgia lookout list on tnlandforms.us includes a tower named ochlocknee, placed in Thomas County.[^1] It gives the position as 30.97774, -83.99301 and an elevation of 272, without saying whether that is feet or metres.[^1] The entry also carries the code gfc-230. The page's notes say these gfc numbers come from an older GFC list that described the locations of 203 towers.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 29 registered lookouts in Georgia, and this tower is not one of them.[^2] The register also lists a Thomas (Thomasville) Lookout Tower as its own entry.[^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register lists two former lookout sites in Georgia, Lafayette and Yonah Mountain, so this site is not recorded there as a former lookout.[^3]

No source I could read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Georgia, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/GA (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Georgia, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://www.firetower.org/lookouts/us/ga/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
