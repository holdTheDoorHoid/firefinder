Buzzard Roost was a 100-foot lookout tower in Perry County, Indiana. The Forest Service's list of Hoosier National Forest towers places it in the Lafayette district, now the Tell City district, and notes a latrine at the site.[^1][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Indiana inventory lists the tower as gone.[^2]

When the Indiana Fire Towers author visited in June 2024, the old site was a clearing overgrown with weeds on the north side of a road, at or very near the tower's presumed spot. The author walked around the area for a few minutes and saw no evidence of tower bases.[^3] The author had stopped at the Buzzard Roost Overlook, which is in the same section as the old tower site, to look at the Ohio River.[^3] No source I could read describes what, if anything, stands at the site beyond that visit.[^3]
