Fairview Mountain sits in Lane County, Oregon, inside the Siuslaw National Forest.[^1] The first structure here is hard to date. An Oregon lookouts history places an open platform on round pole legs at about 1919, with railroad spikes used as steps on the northwest corner.[^1] Fire Lookout describes a 15-foot pole tower with a 6-by-6-foot platform, and dates it to the 1930s.[^2] The two accounts disagree on the date, and the sources we read do not say whether they describe the same structure.[^1][^2]

Road work came in 1934. A November 1, 1934 item from the Eugene Register-Guard, quoted by the Oregon lookouts history, says roads to Fairview Mountain and Cummins Peak were under construction that year.[^1]

Two of the sources list the lookout as abandoned.[^2][^3] Fire Lookout adds that the tower was still there in 1994, which is the most recent description we found.[^2] None of the sources we read says what became of it after that, or when it came down.[^1][^2][^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1]
