A small lookout cabin once stood on the summit of Lassen Peak, which a local history calls the highest point on the Lassen Forest.[^2][^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register dates the cabin to 1912 and describes it as a wood ground cabin, 12 feet 8 inches square.[^1] Newspapers from 1913 suggest a later start.[^1][^2] A June 1913 report says the house was built in sections, carried up the mountain by pack animals, and planned at 10 by 10 feet.[^2] A December 1913 report says three such houses went up that summer, one of them on the summit.[^2] A local history says a first attempt in 1912 did not work, and that a new design came a year later.[^4]

Local history names W.H. Packard of Woodland as the lookout's first and only occupant.[^3] Another account says he manned it for two seasons.[^4] A 1920 obituary in the Woodland Daily Democrat calls Marshall M.F. Packer a former lookout on Mt. Lassen, who left a day or so before the eruption.[^2] The sources do not say whether the two names belong to one man.[^2][^3]

The USGS dates the start of the 1914 eruptions to May 30, 1914, when a phreatic explosion broke out at the summit.[^5] Newspaper reports from June 1914 say the summit lookout had already been destroyed, and a June 14 report added that, so far, the only damage was to the lookout house.[^2] The local history puts the key damage on September 13 and says the cabin was gone seventeen days later.[^3] The register says the lookout house was completely destroyed over the course of that summer and was never rebuilt.[^1] The sources disagree about when it was lost, placing the loss anywhere from June to September.[^1][^2][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout on the summit today.[^1][^3] The register says the peak is administered by the National Park Service in Lassen Volcanic National Park.[^1] The trail to the summit starts at the peak parking area and runs 2.5 miles one way.[^6] That trail listing comes from a 1981 application and may be out of date, and a vehicle pass is required to enter the park. Check with the park before you go.[^6]
