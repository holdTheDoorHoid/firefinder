Green Point is a former lookout on the Clearwater National Forest, at 5,872 feet in Idaho County.[^1][^2]

The site lies nine miles southwest of the Kelly Creek ranger station, according to firelookout.com and a Forest Service list of the forest's lookouts.[^1][^3] That list places it in section 34, township 39 north, range 8 east.[^3] The statewide list gives its coordinates as 46.67590 north and 115.42910 west.[^4]

The lookout was an L-6 cab, built in 1934.[^1][^2][^3] The statewide list puts it among ground lookouts rather than towers.[^4] Every source I found lists it as gone.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts marks the site as "Hike-In," which points to a walk to reach it.[^2] No source I found describes what, if anything, remains there today, or whether visitors may enter.
