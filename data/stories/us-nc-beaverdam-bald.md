Beaverdam Bald is a summit in Cherokee County, North Carolina, rising to about 4,226 feet (1,288 m).[^2] It sits near 35.25 north and 84.12 west, and it appears on the McDaniel Bald topographic quadrangle.[^2]

The forest lookout register lists a lookout on Beaverdam Bald in Cherokee County, gives Cherokee National Forest as the forest, and marks the lookout as removed.[^1]

Nothing in these sources describes what remains on the summit today, or whether it is open to visitors.
