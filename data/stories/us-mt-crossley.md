Crossley is a lookout site in Glacier National Park, in Glacier County, Montana.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site gives the agency as the park and the survey location as 36N-18W-12.[^1] Panorama photographs were taken there on August 7, 1935.[^1]

At about the same time, the National Park Service was running a panoramic photography project that began in 1934 with an identical camera.[^2] The Park Service's history of that work assumes the project had the same purposes as the earlier Forest Service effort, which mapped fire-detection coverage from the lookouts then in service.[^2]

Across the park, aerial observation from 1954 led to many fire lookouts being razed or removed.[^3] Nine fire lookouts remain in the park today.[^3]

The Forest Lookouts page is the only source we read for the site itself, and it does not describe what remains there today.[^1]
