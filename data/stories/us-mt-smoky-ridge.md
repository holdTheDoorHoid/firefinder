Smoky Ridge is 9 miles north-northeast of Whitefish, in the Flathead National Forest and Flathead County, Montana.[^1] The Montana list puts its coordinates at 48.52250 north and 114.25723 west.[^2]

The firelookout.com page describes a 30-foot pole L-4 tower, built in 1935 on a southeast spur about half a mile below the summit.[^1] The page gives the elevation as 6,650 feet.[^1]

The same page says the tower was destroyed in 1977.[^1] The Montana list marks it as a tower that is gone.[^2]

Today, no source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the tower at the site, and none says whether the public may visit it.[^1] The page's text is brief, and it gives no reason for the 1977 destruction.[^1]

[^1]: Smoky Ridge Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/smokyridge.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
