A Georgia lookout tower list on the tnlandforms.us website includes a site called signal mtn, in Polk County.[^1] The list gives its elevation as 1,286 without naming a unit.[^1]

Wikipedia describes Signal Mountain in Rockmart as the most prominent mountain in Polk County. It gives that peak an elevation of 1,290 feet (393 metres).[^2] Neither source says whether it is the same peak as the list entry.

No source describes the site today, so it is not known what, if anything, remains there, or whether visitors may go up.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Polk County, Georgia, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polk_County,_Georgia (accessed 2026-10-21).
