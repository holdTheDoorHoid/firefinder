Kaniksu Mountain Lookout stood on Kaniksu Mountain in Boundary County, in the Kaniksu National Forest, about 13 miles north of Upper Priest Lake.[^1] Sources disagree on its elevation: one page gives 6,814 feet and the other 5,814 feet.[^1][^2]

Two pages date the camp and its crow's nest to 1929.[^1][^2] A photograph in the Priest Lake Historical Photograph Collection shows Jim Evans on a lookout at Kaniksu Mountain.[^4] The University of Idaho collection dates the photo to 1927 and marks that date as approximate.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts also lists Evans as staff in 1927.[^2] That date falls before the 1929 build date, so the sources do not line up.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association list calls the site a crow's nest, now abandoned.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts files it as a hike-in site.[^2] No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
