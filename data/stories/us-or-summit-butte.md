Summit Butte is a lookout site in Oregon. The Forest Lookouts site lists it under the Walker Range Patrol and a private timber company.[^1] The first entry is from 1927. Because of high fire-hazard weather that year, an intermediate lookout was set up near a Shevlin-Hixon logging camp.[^1] Another intermediate lookout was stationed there in August 1929, and an emergency lookout in August 1930.[^1] Hunter took panorama photos in February 1934.[^1]

In 1940 the Walker Range Patrol staffed the lookout for 110 days. The reporting station at LaPine was linked to it by telephone.[^1] In June 1943 a Bulletin item quoted on the page said Aleene Barton would be stationed at Summit Butte as a fire lookout for the summer. She had a former home in Bend and had come from La Grande.[^1]

The last entry describes a tree crow's nest with a camp. By 2006 the tree had rotted to almost nothing, leaving a few boards and the usual tin cans.[^1]

No source describes the site after 2006, and none says whether the public can visit it.

[^1]: Summit Butte, Forest Lookouts (Oregon), https://oregonlookouts.weebly.com/summit-butte.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
