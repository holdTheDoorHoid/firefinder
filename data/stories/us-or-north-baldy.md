North Baldy is a second lookout point in Wheeler County, Oregon, roughly a mile north of the South Baldy lookout.[^1] The Oregon Department of Forestry is the agency named for it.[^1][^2]

The South Baldy lookout has the better-documented history. Its current lookout went up in 1958-59, a 14x14 L-4 cab on a 40-foot open timber tower.[^3] The register says the site had been used for fire detection since at least 1936, and that South Baldy was staffed to at least 1964.[^3] The Oregon Department of Forestry also administers South Baldy.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the North Baldy site today, or whether visitors can reach it. Check with the Oregon Department of Forestry before planning a trip.
