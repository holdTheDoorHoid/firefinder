Limpy Prairie is listed as a lookout site in the Umpqua National Forest in Douglas County.[^1][^2] The site page gives its survey location as 27S-1E-10.[^1][^2]

The only dated entry on the site's page is a panorama photograph from August 15, 1933, with no description.[^1] From 1933 to 1935 a Forest Service effort in Oregon and Washington set out to record panoramic views at each of the region's fire lookouts.[^3] A panorama taken at the site in that year suggests a lookout stood there by 1933, though the sources do not say when it was built.[^1][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and we found nothing on whether it is open to the public.[^1] Check with the Umpqua National Forest before planning a trip.
