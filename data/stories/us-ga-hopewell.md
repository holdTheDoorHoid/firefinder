Hopewell is the name a web list of Georgia lookout towers gives to a tower in Baldwin County, at 33.06468, -83.28156.[^1] The list says names for towers from number 73 onward are its author's own, so this name is a label from the list rather than one taken from a map database.[^1]

The list also notes that the Georgia Forestry Commission supplied coordinates for 118 towers, labelled BLL-nnn, and that an older commission list covered 203 towers, labelled gfc-nnn.[^1] This tower's entry carries BLL-41084 and gfc-612, one number from each list, which suggests the commission's records covered it.[^1] According to the list's notes, the work began because the FFLA site listed no Georgia towers in 2010.[^1]

No source I found describes the site today, so whether any structure remains, and whether the public may visit, is unknown.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
