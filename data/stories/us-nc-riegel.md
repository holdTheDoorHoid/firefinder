The Civilian Conservation Corps built the Riegel tower in the Green Swamp of Brunswick County, North Carolina.[^1][^2] A 1940 state report, as the Forest Lookouts page quotes it, says Camp P-73 erected the tower on October 11, 1938. The report describes a 120-foot creosoted wood tower with an inside stairway; in metric, that is about 36.6 m.[^1]

Camp P-73 finished a four-room frame house at the tower in January 1939. The page gives the house's size as 30 by 36 feet, and that entry spells the tower's name "Riegal."[^1]

The page dates the end of staffing at the tower to November 2005.[^1] No source I read describes the site today, including whether the tower still stands or whether the public can reach it.

The page's entry header names the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources.[^1]
