The Molde lookout stood in St. Louis County, Minnesota.[^1] Wikidata classes the site as a fire lookout tower at about 1,384 feet above sea level.[^2]

A county table lists a 100-foot stair tower here, dated 1934, and says it was a CCC tower to begin with.[^1] The same table says the tower came from a site in Morrison County and that the Goose Lake tower was moved to this site in 1952. A survey note from that year describes a four-legged steel tower about 80 feet high, with a lookout cage on top.[^1]

A photo description on Wikimedia Commons places a tower west of Two Harbors and says it was moved there in 1955 or 1956 from the Camp Ripley Military Reservation.[^3] The table and the photo description disagree on the date and origin, so both are given here as the sources' own claims.

The site also held an earlier tower.[^1] The county table calls it tower number 103 on a 1939 list, and says it was moved in 1958 to Lake of the Woods County, where it became the Bankton tower.[^1]

The county table marks one tower here as standing.[^1] A photo dated 30 September 2011 shows a tower at the site, but the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Molde site as gone.[^3][^4]

No source describes what remains at the site now. Before any visit, check who owns the land, and do not climb any tower without permission.
