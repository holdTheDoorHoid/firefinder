Dusk Peak is a 3,241-foot summit in Olympic National Forest, Mason County, about 16 miles west of Hoodsport.[^1][^2]

Information about the lookout itself is scarce.[^3] Firelookout.com lists its history as unknown.[^1] Willhite says the Lookout Inventory marks the site "Information Unknown," and that his search of the summit turned up no lookout history.[^3]

Willhite says maps show the name changing from Dusk Peak to Dusk Point in the late 1960s.[^3] The registry still uses Dusk Peak.[^2]

A 1953 USGS map shows trails but no roads near the summit.[^3] Willhite thinks the lookout was probably reached on foot, since the summit road did not arrive until the 1960s.[^3]

The registry says the lookout is now gone.[^2]

Forest Road 23 is a well-maintained gravel road that stays open when possible, though storms can block it.[^3] The last road to the summit, FR 080, is under a mile, rough, and best walked.[^3] Willhite reports views in most directions, and someone has cut brush to keep the view open.[^3] Willhite also says dirt was bulldozed around the summit when logging used it, for tower placements and log landings.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout at the summit today.
