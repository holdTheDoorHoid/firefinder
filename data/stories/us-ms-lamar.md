The Lamar Lookout Tower is in Lamar County, Mississippi.[^1] Purvis is the county seat,[^2] and the register places the tower six miles west of Purvis.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register gives 1957 as the build year.[^1] Its description says employees of the Mississippi Forestry Commission completed the construction, and it calls this the first tower in the county.[^1]

The register describes the tower as all steel and says it rises 100 feet among longleaf pines, which is about 30.5 meters.[^1] The same account adds that the Pontiac Refinery's cat cracker was easy to pick out from 80 feet up.[^1]

We found no record of who staffed the tower or of any fire it watched. The register does not say either.

The register puts the site at 379 feet (116 meters).[^1] It lists the tower as registered on May 11, 2022, as US 1571, MS 6.[^1] The nomination came from Jennifer Payne, who also supplied the photograph on the register page.[^1]

The register lists the location as private land, administered by a private owner.[^1] It names the Mississippi Forestry Commission as a cooperator.[^1]

We found no later account of the tower, so we cannot say what shape it is in today. Because the site is private and we found no public access described, anyone interested should ask the landowner before going near it.[^1]
