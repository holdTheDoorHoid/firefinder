The Stokes tower was a 100-foot ladder tower in Itasca County, and the compilation lists its agency as the U.S. Forest Service, Chippewa National Forest.[^1][^3] A Forest Service handbook places it about four and a half miles north of Marcell.[^2] The 2025 compilation puts it off Highway 38, north of Marcell.[^1]

A 1959 survey note describes the tower as a four-legged steel frame with an observation room on top, and gives its overall height as 115 feet (35 m).[^1] The compilation's own description gives 100 feet, so the survey note and the description disagree on its height.[^1]

The compilation says the tower was taken down in the 1972 tower sale.[^1] The Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone.[^3]

A photograph in the 2025 compilation carries the caption "Footing at Stokes site."[^1] We found no source that describes the site today, and none says whether the land is open to visitors.
