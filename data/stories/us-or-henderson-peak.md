Henderson Peak sits at 1,170 feet in the Siuslaw National Forest, about 14 miles southeast of Florence in Douglas County.[^1][^2]

The register dates a 10-foot platform and a cabin it labels AWS to 1942.[^1][^2] The Oregon lookouts list describes the first structure on the summit as a roughly 12-foot round timber tower with an open platform, but it gives no date.[^3] The sources do not explain how the two accounts fit together.

In 1958 a forest crew erected a 40-foot steel tower with a 7-by-7 steel cab.[^3] The register says that tower replaced the 1942 platform and cabin.[^1]

Newspaper items quoted on the Oregon list show the lookout staffed in the 1940s.[^3] In February 1943 the Coos Bay Times named Oliver Cowan as the person in charge.[^3] In October 1947 the same paper reported that William Sylvester had been relieved of his summer post, and that the lookout had closed on October 1.[^3] The list gives 1972 as the last season it was staffed.[^3] It also gives an activation date of October 5, 1943, and names the Eugene Filter Center beside it without explaining why.[^3]

The register says the lookout was used for emergencies in the 1970s.[^1] In 1973 the lookout was put to use as an emergency station, then taken down when the season ended.[^3] The register and firelookout.com both now list it as gone.[^1][^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, and none says whether the public can reach it. PeakVisor, a summit database, says a trail called National Forest Development Road 4811-025 leads to the summit, but it does not mention the lookout.[^4]
