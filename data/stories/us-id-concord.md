Concord was a camp in the Nez Perce National Forest, about 20 miles southwest of Elk City in Idaho County.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Service list dates it to about 1930, and firelookout.com gives 1930 as well.[^2][^3] The Forest Service list places the camp in section 18 of township 26 North, range 7 East.[^3]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page gives only the 1930s and lists the site as a hike-in.[^1]

Every cited source that gives a status describes the camp as abandoned.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

None of the cited sources describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
