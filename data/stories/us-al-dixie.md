The Dixie lookout site is in Escambia County, Alabama.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives its elevation as 285 feet, or 87 meters, and its coordinates as N 31.130565° W 086.727767°.[^1] The register dates the tower to the 1930s, gives its cab as 12 by 12 feet, and says it was removed around 1980.[^1]

Al Pelham nominated the site, and the register recorded it on January 16, 2016.[^1] The page credits Pelham with the photo at the top, and it carries the registry number US 1610, AL 1.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1] Anyone who wants to look for it should check with local land managers first.

[^1]: Dixie Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/al/dixie-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
