The Georgia lookout tower list places a tower labelled "border" in Echols County, at 30.597508 degrees north and 82.669327 degrees west.[^1] Its elevation is listed as 126, with no unit given; in feet that would be 38.4 m.[^1] The page says the list was prompted by a gap: the FFLA site had no Georgia entries in 2010.[^1] Its names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own, so "border" is the compiler's label.[^1]

The entry's note reads "topo". The list says it located its towers using 1:100,000 USGS topo maps and NGS survey sheets, and the map on the page is marked as revised on 22 March 2016.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 29 registered Georgia lookouts, and this site is not among the names on its state page.[^2]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything remains or whether it is open to visitors.
