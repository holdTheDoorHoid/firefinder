Dog Mountain stands in Lake County, Oregon, in what the register calls the Fremont National Forest.[^1] The same forest appears in a 2022 trip report as the Fremont-Winema National Forest.[^3]

The first lookout here was an alidade in the teens, and a D-6 cupola house followed in 1926.[^1][^2] Members of a nearby CCC camp built a wood-frame garage in 1934.[^2] The 1947 cabin is described two ways. The register calls it an L-4 cabin costing $2,700, while Forest Lookouts describes a 14-by-14 Aladdin-style house for the same price.[^1][^2]

In 1918 Bertha Covert staffed the lookout, one of many women who took such jobs during the manpower shortage of World War I.[^1] Forest Lookouts reports that a couple from Malin were on duty there in 1956 and spent the summer there again in 1960.[^2]

A 1995 fire facility proposal called the 1947 house structurally unsound, with numerous code violations.[^2] A new two-story house went up beside it in 1997.[^1][^2] The old cabin is gone, and only its concrete slab and tie-down bolts are left.[^2] The net contract, including modifications, came to $82,213.92, and lookout duties moved to Drakes Peak while the work went on.[^2]

The register says the present cab is still in active service.[^1] Rex's page says it is staffed every summer, but a visitor in June 2022 reported that it seemed still locked up for the season.[^4][^3] That same visitor found the road runs all the way to the summit and that there is no gate.[^3]

Sources also disagree on the size and elevation. Forest Lookouts says the cab measures 15 feet 4 inches on each side, while the register gives 15 by 15 feet.[^2][^1] The register gives the elevation as 6,890 feet, and Rex's page gives 6,936.[^1][^4]

Before a visit, check with the Lakeview Ranger District, which the register names as the cooperator.[^1]
