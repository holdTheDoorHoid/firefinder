Midvale was the first name of the community now called East Glacier Park, in Glacier County, Montana.[^1][^2]

The Great Northern Railway platted the community in the 1890s.[^2] Residents renamed it Glacier Park in 1913 so it would not be mistaken for a different Midvale in southern Montana, and in 1949 the official name changed to East Glacier Park.[^2] The community was home to Glacier Park Lodge and the Glacier Park Transportation Company's garages, and it made its living serving tourists and people who lived nearby.[^2]

A lookout at Midvale (East Glacier) appears in a listing that dates it only to the 1930s. The listing describes it as a platform atop a garage, at 4,825 feet (about 1,471 m).[^3] It does not say which garage held the platform.[^3] The same listing marks the lookout as abandoned and gone.[^3]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known what, if anything, remains of the platform, or whether the site is open to the public.
