The Arvon fire tower stood in Baraga County, about ten and a half miles east of L'Anse, in the western portion of a wooded ridge that runs east and west.[^1] A 1929 L'Anse Sentinel report already places an accident at the tower, so it was in use by then.[^1]

A 1956 survey described a steel tower about 110 feet high, with four legs and a small house on top.[^1] The Michigan fire tower directory, which lists the same tower, records no cab.[^2] The two sources disagree on that point.[^1][^2]

A towerman worked the tower in 1930.[^1] A notice in the L'Anse Sentinel said Mr. Auge was employed in that role at the Arvon fire tower.[^1] In 1932 the Conservation Department and L'Anse Township were building a new road to the tower.[^1]

The Michigan fire tower directory lists the tower's status as Down.[^2]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
