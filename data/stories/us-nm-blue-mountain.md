Blue Mountain was a lookout site in Socorro County, New Mexico, in the Cibola National Forest.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site at 33.65962, -107.43708 as a platform and marks it gone.[^2]

The same list has a second entry, "Blue Mtn," almost on the same spot, which it calls a crow's nest and also marks gone.[^2] The two entries may describe one site, but the list calls one a platform and the other a crow's nest, so the kind of structure is unclear.[^2]

The list also includes a separate tower called San Mateo. The association shows it as standing and lists it on the NHLR as US 668, NM 22.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the Blue Mountain site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Blue Mountain, Forest Lookouts, https://westlookouts.weebly.com/blue-mountain1.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: New Mexico Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
