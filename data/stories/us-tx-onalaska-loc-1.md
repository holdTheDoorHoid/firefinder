In 1935 the Coast and Geodetic Survey described a Polk County station placed directly under the middle roof bolt of the Onalaska fire lookout tower.[^1] The description therefore puts the tower in place by 1935.[^1] A Texas lookout list shows the Onalaska tower under the Texas Forest Service.[^2] The survey page gives no coordinates, so matching it to Loc 1 rests on the name and county.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Onalaska (Loc 1) site as gone.[^3] It also lists a separate Onalaska (Loc 2) in Trinity County as standing, with "Pvt" in place of coordinates.[^3] Because Loc 2 is in another county, it is a different site, and the two records should not be confused.[^3]

No source describes the site now, so it is not known what, if anything, remains of the Loc 1 tower or whether the land is open to the public.
