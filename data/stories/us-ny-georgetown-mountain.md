The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list calls this site Georgetown Mountain and marks its tower as gone.[^1] The New York chapter places the tower on Morrow Mountain, about three and a half miles north of the village of Georgetown, in Madison County.[^2] The state's forest page gives the mountain's height as 2,142 feet and calls it the county's high point.[^3]

The Civilian Conservation Corps built the tower in 1940 through its Camp S-103 at DeRuyter.[^2] It was a 67-foot, six-inch International Derrick tower.[^2] Once aerial detection arrived, the tower was closed at the end of the 1970 season.[^2]

The tower was later removed.[^2] According to the same chapter list, a New Hartford resident recently bought the dismantled parts and hopes to re-erect the tower at his camp in Oppenheim, New York.[^2]

Morrow Mountain is now part of Morrow Mountain State Forest, which covers 1,290 acres and is open year-round at no charge.[^3] The forest is primitive, with few amenities, and hiking is allowed anywhere on the property unless it is posted otherwise.[^3] The state's directions name Fire Tower among the roads that reach the forest.[^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the tower on the summit today.
