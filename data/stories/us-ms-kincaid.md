The Kincaid Lookout Tower is in Grenada County, Mississippi.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as a 100-foot International Derrick tower, and gives the site's elevation as 490 feet, or 149 meters.[^1]

The register gives no build date for the tower.[^1] The Forest Lookouts index lists a Grenada County tower under the name Gore Springs.[^2] Neither source links that entry to this tower, so we do not treat them as the same site.[^1][^2] Neither source names a staff member or a fire the tower watched.[^1][^2]

The register lists the tower under the Kincaid name, recorded on July 19, 2022, as US 1685, MS 44, on the nomination of Jason Johns.[^1] The Mississippi Forestry Commission administers the site.[^1] The register's only site visit is noted as 2/5/22.[^1]

The sources we read do not say whether the tower still stands, or whether it has been moved, burned or taken down.[^1][^2] The register gives no access information.[^1] Anyone planning to see the site should first ask the Mississippi Forestry Commission, which administers it.[^1]

[^2]: Gore Springs, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/gore-springs.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
