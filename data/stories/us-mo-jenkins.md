The Jenkins lookout stood in Barry County, Missouri, on a towersite that the Missouri Conservation Commission acquired in fiscal 1948–49 [^1]. The site was 43 acres when it was acquired, and the 1949–50 state manual listed it at 42.5 acres [^1].

A 1961 Coast and Geodetic Survey station record describes a wooden frame on four legs, topped by a white observation room [^1]. The same record puts its height at about 90 feet [^1]. The survey directions start at Leann and send drivers north on State Highway 39 [^1].

The commission's annual report for fiscal 1956–57 records major repairs to the tower [^1].

A 1974 USGS topographic map, published in 1977, describes the Jenkins Lookout Tower as one of the local landmarks [^2].

The Central US Lookouts page now lists the tower as removed [^1].

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
