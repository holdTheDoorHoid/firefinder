The Reid Lookout Tower is in Calhoun County, Mississippi.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register gives its elevation as 512 feet (156 meters).[^1] It lists a 100-foot Aermotor tower with stairs set on a diagonal.[^1]

The register lists the build date as unknown.[^1] It names no builder, no year of construction, and no fire the tower watched.[^1] Its only physical details are the height, the Aermotor design and the staircase.[^1]

The register recorded the tower on July 21, 2022, as US 1686, MS 46, on the nomination of Jason Johns.[^1] The Mississippi Forestry Commission administers the site.[^1] The register records no site visit for this tower.[^1]

We could not confirm whether the tower still stands. The register gives no current condition for it.[^1] We found no other source that describes the tower. The register also gives no access information, so it does not show whether the public may visit.[^1] The Mississippi Forestry Commission, which administers the site, would be the agency to ask.[^1]

[^1]: Reid Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ms/reid-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
