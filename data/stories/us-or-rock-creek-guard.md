The Rock Creek Guard Station lookout is listed in Wasco County, Oregon, within the Mount Hood National Forest.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says a 100-foot crow's nest and camp were put up in 1925, and that the U.S. Forest Service built a cabin there in 1932.[^1]

Rex's Wasco County page places the guard station in the Mount Hood National Forest at the land-survey location 4S-11E-25.[^2] The register's own coordinates, however, read 43.329841° N and 123.000339° W.[^1] Those points lie well to the southwest of Wasco County, so the sources we read do not settle which location is right.

The register says the lookout is now gone.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon index also marks the crow's nest as gone.[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains of the structure at the site, or whether the site is open to the public.
