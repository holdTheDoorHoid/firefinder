Bledsoe was a fire lookout tower in Bledsoe County, Tennessee.[^1] The Tennessee lookout-tower site gives the tower's elevation as 1,836 feet, under the name Bledsoe, and marks it as removed.[^1]

The site numbers its towers by state forest district and tower.[^1] The Bledsoe entry carries the code 3-5, which the site reads as tower 5 in district 3, based on a 1976 roster of towers.[^1]

According to the same site, towers were still going up in Tennessee during the 1950s.[^1] The Encyclopedia of Scott County, citing the University of Tennessee, reports a statewide peak of 208 lookout towers, with 142 still standing in 2014.[^2]

The site says many of its listed towers have been taken down, and that most of their sites are now privately owned.[^1] The site also says unofficial names were assigned to towers absent from the USGS maps and the state forestry list, so not every name in its table is official.[^1]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
