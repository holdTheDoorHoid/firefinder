The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list shows a Dismal Swamp tower in the City of Suffolk as standing, placed at about 36.68 north and 76.49 west.[^1] A species list on the Old Dominion University website carries two photo captions, "View from fire tower" and "Ditch as seen from fire tower," so someone photographed the swamp from a fire tower at some point.[^2] I cannot tell whether that is the same tower the list shows, and the captions do not say where it stood, who built it or when it was used.[^2] The Fish and Wildlife Service describes the refuge as the largest unbroken surviving part of a wetland forest that once spanned more than a million acres.[^3] The land came under protection in 1973, and the federal refuge was created in 1974.[^3] I could not confirm whether the tower sits inside the refuge or whether visitors may go near it, so check with refuge staff first.[^3] Do not climb any tower without permission.
