The Pinto Point lookout was a 40-foot L-4 pole tower erected in 1934 on the northwest point, in the Kootenai National Forest.[^1] The site lies about 20 miles southwest of Eureka, Montana, at an elevation of 5,311 feet.[^1]

In August 1955 the Inter Lake reported that the lookout had been manned as an extra precaution, which made three lookouts in the district.[^2]

An Evergreen item from September 1955 says the Pinto Mountain Lookout reported a lightning-caused fire on Pink Mountain. Smoke chasers from Big Creek Cabin went to work, and the fire was out by September 10.[^2] The 1955 reports use different names for the lookout, calling it the Pinto Lookout and the Pinto Mountain Lookout.[^2]

Firelookout.com says the tower was abandoned in the 1970s and destroyed in 1990.[^1] The Montana list also marks it as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it can be visited. Ask the Kootenai National Forest before going.
