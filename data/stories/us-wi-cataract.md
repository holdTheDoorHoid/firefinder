The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower called Cataract in Monroe County, Wisconsin, and shows it as standing.[^1] The listing gives coordinates, but no build date, builder or height.[^1]

I found no record of who built it or when. WJFW says Wisconsin once had 119 fire towers at its peak, and that many of the survivors were built in the 1930s by Civilian Conservation Corps crews.[^2] Nothing I read ties Cataract to that crew.

Across Wisconsin, towers were staffed from May through September for decades.[^2] I found no record of who staffed Cataract or for how long.

WJFW reports that 72 towers still in service were retired in 2015, and that the Wisconsin DNR now watches the forests from airplanes.[^2] The article does not name Cataract, so I cannot say whether it was one of those 72. The Forest Fire Lookout Association still shows it as standing.[^1]

I could not confirm who owns the site or whether the public may visit it, so check with the landowner before planning a trip.
