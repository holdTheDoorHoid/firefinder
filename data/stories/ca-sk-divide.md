Divide Fire Tower is listed in Meadow Lake No. 588, Saskatchewan, at about 53.87 N, 108.43 W.[^1] A Department of Natural Resources annual report for 1951 to 1952 says the tower was remodelled with new cables and aerials as part of a maintenance programme, so it was already standing by then.[^2] A 1965 entry records that the earlier lookout was replaced by a steel tower of newer design.[^2]

Towermen lists name several people connected with Divide. Vallery Morin is listed there from 1998 to 2002, and the entry notes a move to aerial detection.[^3] April Swartz is listed at Park Ridge and Divide, and John Rule's entry names Divide among four towers.[^3]

No source we found describes the Divide site today. Firelookout.ca gives no height, status or visit details for it.[^1]
