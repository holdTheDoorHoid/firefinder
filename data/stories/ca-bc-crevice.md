The Crevice fire lookout is listed in the East Kootenay, and a BC hiking guide lists it under Corbin.[^1][^2]

OpenStreetMap labels it an abandoned BC Wildfire Service fire lookout tower. Its tags describe a tower with a red roof, and its map note reports significant rat droppings inside as of 2023.[^3]

Across the province, a 2012 provincial news release said new technology, public reporting and aerial detection had replaced the lookouts. It counted 320 lookout locations in British Columbia, with 20 considered active.[^6]

A place listing puts the tower on the Couldrey Forest Service Road, in the Upper Flathead/Elk Valley area of the Regional District of East Kootenay.[^4] An urban exploration listing marks the site as confirmed abandoned, but shows no recent confirmation.[^5]

Beyond those listings, no source describes the site today, and none says whether the public may visit it.[^3][^4][^5]
