The Mashmayer Lookout Tower is near Pineview in Escambia County, Alabama, at an elevation of 321 feet (98 meters).[^1][^2] The Alabama Forestry Commission administers it, and the register describes a 120-foot metal tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab.[^1]

The build date is uncertain. The register's "Built" field gives 1939, but the same entry says the tower was built in 1938.[^1] We found no other source that settles it, so treat the date as either 1938 or 1939.

The National Historic Lookout Register listed the tower on January 27, 2010, as US 873 and AL 50, and calls it the 50th lookout listed in Alabama.[^1]

We found nothing on who staffed the tower, what fires it watched, or when it stopped being used. The register page does not describe public access.[^1]

A drone video posted to Rumble calls the tower abandoned and places it a few miles off I-65, a few miles south on AL 113.[^2] We found no source saying the public may visit or climb it, so do not climb it.
