Cronin Mountain lies in the Bulkley-Nechako area of British Columbia, and the Firelookout.ca directory lists the site as the Cronin Mountain Fire Lookout.[^1][^3]

Canada Lookouts' entry titled Cronin, in the same district, gives latitude 54.935 and longitude 126.77.[^1] The entry names no tower beyond Cronin, so linking it to this lookout is an inference.

A 2026 guide places the Cronin Fire Lookout at Smithers among lookouts confirmed to have standing structures or likely to still stand without upkeep.[^2] The same guide says that less than a hundred BC fire lookouts are left standing today, out of more than 300 that once existed around the province.[^2]

Beyond the guide's listing, no source I read describes the site today or says whether the public can reach it. The guide adds that most of the lookouts it lists are hard to reach, often needing a high-clearance 4WD or an off-road vehicle.[^2] Do not climb the tower unless its access is confirmed as public.
