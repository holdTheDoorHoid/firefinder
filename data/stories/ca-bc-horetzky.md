The Open Canada Fire Lookout Index lists a Horetzky Fire Lookout in Bulkley-Nechako, British Columbia.[^1] Its pin sits on Mount Horetzky, an official name for a peak north of Smithers, on the north side of the Babine River above Shanagh Creek.[^1][^2][^3] The provincial names record lists the name as adopted, with the date 5 July 1951.[^2]

The pin lies within about 40 m (about 130 ft) of the official point.[^1][^3] The index gives a name and a point, with no tower type, build year or height.[^1] The entry may have been made from the mountain's name, though no source says so.[^1][^2]

I found no source describing a tower or cabin on Mount Horetzky, so treat the listing as unconfirmed.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, stands at the summit today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
