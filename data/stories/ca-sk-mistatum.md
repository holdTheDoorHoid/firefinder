Canada Lookouts files the Mistatim tower under its Hudson Bay Region heading.[^1]

In 1960 a tower cabin was built at the Mistatim site, and a 24.4 m (80 ft) steel tower was erected.[^1] The record for this comes from the Department of Natural Resources annual report for 1960-61, which lists the cabin and the steel tower in the same entry.[^1] The Firetowers of Saskatchewan page says that department is now called Saskatchewan Environment and Resource Management, or SERM.[^2]

The Firetowers of Saskatchewan page lists the same tower under the spelling Mistatum, at 52 degrees 57 minutes 30.3 seconds north and 103 degrees 22 minutes 53.0 seconds west.[^2]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether any part of the tower stands or whether the public can visit.
