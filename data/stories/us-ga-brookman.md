A Georgia lookout tower list places a tower called Brookman in Glynn County, at 31.18905 and -81.65867.[^1] Its entry carries the code gfc-848.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, and the only source for this tower is that list of names and positions.[^1]
