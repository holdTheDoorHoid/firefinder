The city of Los Angeles put up the lookout on San Vicente Mountain, working with the county forestry department.[^1] A September 1924 Van Nuys News item, quoted on the same page, reported that a lookout was planned on Mt. San Vicente, with two fire rangers detailed and a telephone system to be installed.[^1] The item put the area covered at more than 400 square miles, roughly 27 by 15 miles.[^1] On the same mountain, the Army ran Nike missile control site LA96C from 1956 to 1968.[^2] The Nike site is now San Vicente Mountain Park.[^2] Visitors walk about ten minutes on a wide dirt road from the parking area.[^3] The marker notes restricted entry, with vehicles past one point limited to handicapped permits.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains of the 1924-era lookout today.
