High Springs was a lookout on the Nez Perce National Forest, about 7 miles northwest of Moose Creek Ranger Station, in Idaho County.[^1][^3]

The site is listed at 6,509 feet.[^1]

A 20-foot tower with an L-4 cab went up in 1935.[^1][^3] The L-4 was a pre-cut frame house measuring 14 feet by 14 feet.[^4] Forest Service style notes say that design was produced between 1929 and 1953.[^4]

The lookout was abandoned in 1939.[^1][^2][^3] One Forest Lookouts page says dynamite was used to blow the tower down in 1957.[^3]

Firelookout.com and the FFLA list both record the site as gone.[^1][^5]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as hike-in.[^2] I found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
