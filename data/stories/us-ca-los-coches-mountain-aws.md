Los Coches Mountain lies in Santa Barbara County, inside the Los Padres National Forest.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list places the site at 34.98311, -120.25728.[^1]

The list gives the site type as AWS, short for Aircraft Warning Service.[^1][^3] That was a civilian service tied to the Army's Ground Observer Corps during World War II, and a Wikipedia summary says it was deactivated on May 29, 1944.[^3]

The same list marks the site as gone, with no date.[^1]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
