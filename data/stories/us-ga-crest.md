The Georgia tower list places a Crest Lookout Tower in Upson County, at 32.96680, -84.44437, with an elevation of 1,175 feet (358.1 m).[^1] A map site lists a Crest Lookout Tower on Firetower Road, near Molena, at nearly the same coordinates.[^2]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether a tower still stands there or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Elevation of Crest Lookout Tower, Firetower Road, Molena, Upson County, Georgia, MAPLOGS, https://elevation.maplogs.com/poi/crest_lookout_tower_firetower_road_molena_upson_county_georgia_usa.632134.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
