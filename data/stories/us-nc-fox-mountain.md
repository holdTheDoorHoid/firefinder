In February 1956 the Iredell County forester told the county commissioners that a fire detection tower on Fox Mountain would be finished that month.[^1] The tower had been an electric transmission tower in Rowan County.[^1] Mill Power Supply Co. of Charlotte donated it to Iredell's forestry service, and it was moved to Fox Mountain.[^1] Love Valley, Inc., and the Forestry Service were rebuilding it on the mountain at the time.[^1]

In November 1958 the tower spotted smoke, and an Iredell smoke chaser brought the blaze under control.[^1]

By 1968 the district forester had indicated that the tower would be closed. He explained that the department had built more permanent and higher towers.[^1] County commissioners passed a resolution asking him to keep it running, and representatives of some volunteer fire departments came to ask the same.[^1] The source does not say whether the tower stayed open.[^1]

No source describes the Fox Mountain site today or says whether any part of the tower remains.[^1]
