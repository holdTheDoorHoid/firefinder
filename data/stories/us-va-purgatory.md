This tower began on Apple Orchard Mountain in Bedford County, Virginia, before it was moved to Purgatory Mountain.[^1]

A lookout was on duty there in 1933.[^2] In August that year, a newspaper item quoted on the Forest Lookouts page described the lookout, William Dawson, treating a boy who had been bitten by a rattlesnake and was headed for the tower.[^2] Dawson put on a tourniquet and phoned a different camp to request a suction pump.[^2]

The register dates the move to Purgatory Mountain, in Botetourt County, to 1957-1958.[^1] A Blue Ridge Parkway history page on willhiteweb.com gives a wider window, placing the move anywhere from 1955 to 1958.[^3] That page says the tower was reportedly 60 feet (18 m) tall, with a 14-by-14-foot (4.3 m) cab.[^3] The register's 1957-1958 falls inside that wider window.[^1][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list still shows the Purgatory Mountain entry as a tower marked gone.[^4] No source read says what, if anything, remains on Purgatory Mountain today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^3][^4]
