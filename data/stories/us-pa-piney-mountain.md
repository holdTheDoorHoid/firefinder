The Piney Mountain Tower Site was a fire lookout in Adams County, Pennsylvania, listed by the Former Fire Lookout Sites register as US 425, PA 254.[^1] The register dates the steel tower to 1914 and says it was taken down in 1933.[^1]

The register added the site on September 16, 2008, on the nomination of Henry Isenberg.[^1] It gives no coordinates for the location.[^1]

The register does not describe what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1] It also says nothing about public access, so check with the land manager before going to look for the site.[^1]

[^1]: Piney Mountain Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/pa/piney-mountain-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
