Brooks Knob lies in Edmonson County, Kentucky.[^1] The only source I found is a Forest Lookouts page for the knob, which lists the tower as removed, without a date.[^1]

That page also reproduces a National Geodetic Survey data sheet. The sheet gives the designation "BROOKS KNOB LOOKOUT TOWER," the identifier HA1380, and the Rhoda USGS quadrangle, shown with the year 2019.[^1] The designation itself names a lookout tower.[^1]

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it. Ask local officials about access before any visit.

[^1]: Brooks Knob, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/brooks-knob.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
