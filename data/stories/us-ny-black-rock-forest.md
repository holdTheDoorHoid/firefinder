The Black Rock Forest Fire Tower stands in Orange County, New York, at an elevation of 398 feet.[^1] The register gives two build dates. Its description says the tower went up in 1929 as a private lookout on the Stillman property, while its Built field gives 1933.[^1] The register lists it as an Aermotor LS-42, 80 feet tall, with a metal cab measuring 7 by 7 feet.[^1]

The forest's history explains who owned the land. Dr. Ernest G. Stillman established the forest in 1927, and Harvard University owned it from 1949 until 1989.[^2] The register says ownership passed to the Black Rock Forest Consortium in 1989.[^1]

The register says restoration was finished everywhere but the roof.[^1] A visit report dated May 1, 2007 found that the tower had no roof. It marked the tower as staffed sometimes, and noted a surveillance camera and weather instruments mounted on it for the forest's use.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association still lists the tower as standing, though its entry carries an asterisk whose note I could not read.[^3]

The sources I reached do not record a fire that struck this tower.

The 2007 report says the tower is not open to the public.[^1] It describes a locked lower landing, with entry only by the forest manager's permission, so please contact the Black Rock Forest office rather than climbing it.[^1]
