The Chetek Lookout was a 100-foot Aermotor tower with an inside corner ladder, in Barron County, Wisconsin.[^1] The Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources administered it.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register dates the tower to 1951 and says it was built at its current location that year.[^1] It also suggests the tower may have been moved there from another site in 1951.[^1]

Two newspaper items from late 1953, dated November 29 and December 2 and quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, describe it. The November item calls it a new fire lookout tower that had been under construction for several months and would open soon.[^2] The December item calls it the newest link in a chain of fire towers.[^2] The two items also disagree on its place: a mile east of Mud Lake in one, and about five miles north and slightly east of Chetek in the other.[^2]

The November item said the tower would be manned by a son of the Brann family.[^2] The December item says Carl E. Bram had been its fire watcher through the fall and reported three smokes during the dry weather.[^2]

The register added the tower in 2017, on the nomination of Tyler Bormann.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list marks the tower as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it is open to visitors.
