Linfor Hill stands about six miles northwest of Kellogg, in Shoshone County, Idaho, within the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1] A 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab went up there in 1934.[^1][^2][^3]

A 20-foot platform on Thomas Hill, about a mile to the southeast, served as a patrol point for the Linfor tower.[^1][^2][^4]

John Gunn is the only lookout the sources name, for 1944.[^1][^2]

The sources disagree about how the tower ended. Firelookout.com says the tower was put up for public sale and taken down in 1950.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts says that year it was either taken down or burned.[^2] The Weebly list records it as gone by 1950, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks it gone as well.[^3][^5]

Idaho Fire Lookouts reports that a visit turned up cement footings and tie-downs, and otherwise only the view.[^2] The site is listed as hike-in, which means it is reached on foot.[^2] No source says whether the ground is open to visitors or what rules apply there.[^1][^2]

The Thomas Hill platform has toppled, yet its concrete footings, pole legs and railings all remain identifiable.[^4]
