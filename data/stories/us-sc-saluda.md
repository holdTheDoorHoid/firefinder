The Saluda Lookout is a lookout tower in Saluda County, South Carolina, and the Forest Lookouts site lists it under the state Forestry Commission.[^1]

OpenStreetMap, a volunteer map, tags the spot as a lattice observation tower at 168 m (551 ft).[^2] TopoQuest, a map site, gives the elevation as 547 feet (about 167 m) and lists the feature as a tower on the USGS Saluda North map sheet.[^3] The latest edit to the OpenStreetMap entry is labelled "Add/modify communication mast."[^2]

The Forestry Commission, which the Forest Lookouts page names for this tower, shut down its fire-tower network on October 1, 1993, a system-wide closure that no source we found ties to this particular tower.[^1][^4]

No source we found describes the site now, or whether the public can reach it. Do not climb the tower unless a source says it is open.
