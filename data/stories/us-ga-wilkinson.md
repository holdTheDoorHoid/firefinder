The Georgia tower list includes a Wilkinson Lookout Tower in Wilkinson County, at 32.82959, -83.23932, with an elevation of 512 feet (156 m).[^1] The entry carries two codes, BLL-41093 and gfc-617. The list's notes say the Georgia Forestry Commission supplied coordinates for 118 towers under the BLL code, and recorded the sites of 203 towers in an older list under the gfc code.[^1]

OpenStreetMap, a map that volunteers edit, marks a Wilkinson Lookout Tower at almost the same coordinates as an observation tower with lattice construction.[^2]

No source says what condition the tower is in today or whether it is open to the public, so check before planning any visit.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Wilkinson Lookout Tower (node 358748762), OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/358748762 (accessed 2026-10-21).
