Prospect Peak Lookout stands in the Bridger-Teton National Forest in Sublette County, Wyoming, about 36 miles west of Pinedale.[^1][^4] The National Historic Lookout Register says it was built in 1941, alongside two nearby lookouts, Cabin Creek Peak and Deadman Mountain.[^1] A local newspaper reported in July 1941 that L.W. Roberts and Dick had just completed a lookout cabin for the Forest Service, one the summer fire guard would use.[^2]

The Register describes it as a smaller four-panel ground cabin, not a tall tower.[^1] Staffing was limited. The Register says the lookout was apparently staffed only in times of high fire hazard.[^1] A July 1941 item said staff would be posted there once the Cora weather station reported dry air and high fire danger, and that phone and radio links ran to Afton.[^2]

The lookout was registered on May 10, 2022, as US 1570, WY 22, with Brad Eells as nominator.[^1]

In a July 6, 2025 post, the Forest Fire Lookout Association said the lookout was still wrapped while the Horse Fire burned nearby.[^3] The sources read do not say whether the cabin was damaged, whether it is open to visitors, or whether it is rented out. The Register lists the Forest Service as its administrator.[^1] Anyone planning a visit should check with the Bridger-Teton National Forest first.
