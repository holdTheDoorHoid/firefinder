The tower that now stands at Matthews State Forest began life in Tazewell County as the Morris Knob tower.[^1] The register says it now stands at the forestry office, and it calls the tower, figuratively, a museum of forestry history for the state's rural southwest.[^1]

The register lists a registration date of January 23, 2017, with the number US 1161, VA 27, and Kristin Reynolds nominated the tower.[^1] The sources checked do not say when the tower was built, who built it, or when it was moved.[^1][^2]

The state forest is 566 acres in Grayson County, just west of Galax, and its office is at 106 Forestry Lane.[^2] The forest began with a 1993 gift of its first parcel from Judge Jack Matthews and his wife, Clare, and later donations brought it to its current size.[^2]

Hunting closes all trails south of Route 58 every day except Sunday, from the first Saturday in October through the first Saturday in January, so check before you go.[^2] The register does not say whether the tower can be climbed, so this story does not suggest it.[^1] The forestry page gives the office number, (276) 236-2322, for reserving facilities, so call ahead to ask about the tower before a visit.[^2]
