The Cold Springs lookout stood on a peak in Trinity County, California, on South Fork Mountain.[^1][^2] In April 1934 the Blue Lake Advocate reported that the new tower was finished and ready to be set on its foundation, and that it was all wood.[^1] A Forest Service road had been opened to the springs, and lumber was being hauled there for the new station.[^1]

The station was meant to take the place of the Blake lookout, which also stood on South Fork Mountain.[^1] A May 1934 item said the Blake lookout would be abandoned and replaced by the new Cold Springs station.[^1]

A 2021 blog post says the site sits on the edge of the area the 2015 Route Fire covered.[^2] The same writer found that the tower itself was gone by then.[^2] A few wood struts and other remnants were still on the spot, and the writer said it was easy to see where the lookout had stood.[^2]

OpenStreetMap marks the spot as a fire lookout building tagged as ruins.[^3] The blogger noted that the site has a road, and walked up road 2N43 from a parking spot on its far side.[^2] No agency page we read says whether visitors are welcome there now.
