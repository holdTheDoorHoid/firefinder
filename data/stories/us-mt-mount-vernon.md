Mount Vernon was a lookout site in Lincoln County, Montana, on the Kootenai National Forest, about 16 miles south of Troy.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 5,580 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list gives the type as crowsnest and places the site at 48.2294 north, 115.89975 west.[^2]

Firelookout.com titles its page a fire lookout tower, but the text describes a crow's nest with a tent cabin, dated to 1934.[^1]

The site was abandoned in 1942.[^1] The association's list gives its status as Abandoned.[^2] Neither source calls the site gone.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes the site today. The only pages we found are short listings, so we cannot tell from them what, if anything, remains on the ground, or whether the public may visit it.
