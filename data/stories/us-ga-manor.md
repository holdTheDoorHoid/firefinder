The Georgia tower list places a tower labelled "manor" in Ware County, at 31.08758 N, 82.53614 W. The list gives its elevation as 149 with no unit; read as feet, that is about 45 m.[^1] The page says names for towers numbered 73 and above are its author's own. This entry is number 132, so "manor" is a label, not an official name.[^1]

Manor is an unincorporated community in Ware County, southwest of Waycross on U.S. Route 84.[^2] The list's map was last revised in March 2016, and the entry carries the tag gfc-834.[^1]

The state forestry agency's history ties its first fire observation tower to a hotel in Waycross, possibly because of a 1928 forestry fair in that city.[^3] The list has a separate entry labelled "ware hotel", tagged "first tower", which appears to be the same tower.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
