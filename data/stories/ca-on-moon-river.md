The Moon River Fire Tower is listed on firelookout.ca at Parry Sound, Ontario, at an elevation of 234 m (768 ft).[^1] The record gives its coordinates as 45.08639, -79.83167.[^1] The name points to the Moon River, which runs from Lake Muskoka, at Bala, into Georgian Bay south of Parry Sound.[^3]

Parry Sound also has a fire tower on Tower Hill, just outside the downtown.[^2] The Museum on Tower Hill says the first structure was meant to serve both as a public viewing tower and a fire lookout.[^4] The first tower came down in 1973; the current one dates from 1975.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains on the Moon River site today, or whether the public can reach it.
