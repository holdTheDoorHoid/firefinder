Adams is a fire lookout tower in St. Lucie County, Florida, west of Fort Pierce.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page gives its location as St. Lucie County and 36S-38E-5. The second part reads as a survey-style township, range and section code.[^1]

A December 1973 item, credited on the page to The News Tribune, reports a break-in at the house at the Adams tower, where several household items were taken.[^1] That is the only dated event the page describes.

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1]

[^1]: Adams, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/adams1.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
