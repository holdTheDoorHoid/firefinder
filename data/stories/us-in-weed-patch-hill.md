Weed Patch Hill Lookout stands on the hill of the same name inside Brown County State Park, in Brown County, Indiana.[^1]

The first plan was for a steel tower eighty feet tall. A state annual report announced one for the hill. The Register dates that report to 1926, while a Forest Lookouts transcription dates it to 1923.[^1][^3] A 1928 report says an eighty-foot steel tower was erected there in 1927.[^1] Dating it is harder than that. The Register gives about 1927, but the wooden sign at the site says the 1930s. A Frank Hohenberger photograph in the Lilly Library's collection is dated July 14, 1927, which would put the tower before the park opened in 1929.[^1][^2]

The heights do not match either. The 1928 figure is eighty feet, but a November 1941 item in the Culver Citizen describes the tower as 90 feet above the ground, and the Register says 90 to 100 feet.[^1][^3]

In 1944 the Brown County Democrat named Bennie Petro as in charge of the fire tower at the state park. That September a two-way radio link went in between Weed Patch and the Hickory Ridge tower.[^3] I found no source that names the fires it watched.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Indiana list and a fire-tower hobby site both list the tower as standing.[^2][^4] The Register says the tower is now used for both communications and public observation, and that it sits along the main park road.[^1] The hobby site says the cab is not open, but the tower can be climbed partway.[^2] I found no official rule on climbing, so ask park staff before you go up.

The Register added the tower on January 2, 2026, as US 1786, IN 10.[^1] The same month, the Friends of Brown County State Park announced a fundraising drive to restore it, with a goal of finishing by the park's 2029 centennial.[^1][^5]
