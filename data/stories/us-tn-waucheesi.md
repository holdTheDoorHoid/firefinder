Waucheesi Lookout Tower stood on Waucheesi Mountain in Monroe County, Tennessee.[^1] A USGS topographic map, portrayed in 1957 and published in 1959, labels it there.[^1]

The Tennessee lookout list gives the site an elevation of 3,692 feet.[^2] It marks the tower as removed, with the note "cnf removed."[^2]

The Forest Service signed a decision on 03/16/2021 for a project at a "Waucheesi Communication Tower" on the mountain.[^3] The project calls for a new radio system, a microwave dish, backup power and an HVAC unit inside the shelter.[^3] The project is recorded as completed.[^3] It lies about 4.3 miles northeast of Coker Creek, in Cherokee National Forest.[^3]

None of these sources says whether the communication tower stands where the lookout did. No source reached describes what remains of the lookout itself, and none says whether the summit is open to the public.
