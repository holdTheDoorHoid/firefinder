Powhatan (Loc 1) is a former fire tower site in Powhatan County, Virginia.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone and places it at 37.55298, -77.93304.[^1] That makes it the northern of the county's two listed sites. By our calculation from the coordinates, it is about 1.9 km from the Powhatan Court House entry, which carries the same status at 37.54759, -77.91298.[^1]

The Eastern US Lookouts page for Virginia lists one Powhatan tower, spelled POWHATTEN in capitals.[^2] Beneath that entry, the page records that a 100-foot McClintock-Marshall steel tower sold for $1,255.00 in a 2005 eBay auction.[^2] The note does not say which of the county's two sites it describes, so it may not refer to this one.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
