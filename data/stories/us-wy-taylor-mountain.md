Taylor Mountain is a Teton Range peak, with Teton Pass roughly two miles to its southeast and the towns of Wilson and Jackson nearby.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Taylor Mountain site in Teton County under its heading "Unknown/Undocumented Sites."[^2] Its note says that any of these sites shown to have been used as a regular wildland fire lookout would be moved to the association's lookout lists.[^2]

The Teton National Forest was adding lookout stations in the early 1940s.[^3] A Jackson Hole Courier notice from April 1941 reported four new lookout houses being built, and an August 1942 notice counted 17 fire lookout stations that year.[^3] In July 1954 the Teton Forest judged it advisable to staff the Monument Ridge and Munger Mountain lookouts, and it listed five more positions to be manned within a week.[^3]

A hiking route starts at the Coal Creek Trailhead, just west of Teton Pass on US 22, and runs about 10 miles (16 km) round trip to the summit.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, stands at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
