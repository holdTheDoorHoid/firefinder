Pelham's fire tower stands on Mount Lincoln, a high point on the Pelham Dome or Pelham Hills, an upland plateau that overlooks the Connecticut River Valley [^3]. The register counts the current tower as the fourth on the site, and dates the first to 1913, when the Amherst Mountain Club put one up [^1]. In January 1921 the State Forester's report for 1920 recorded that a 50-foot tower from Hanson had been moved to Pelham and had replaced an older 30-foot wooden tower [^2].

The register describes the present tower as 68 feet of steel, with a 10-by-10-foot cab and no catwalk [^1]. Wikipedia refers to the original tower as 80 feet tall [^3]. The two sources may not describe the same structure, so their heights are not directly comparable [^1][^3].

Register visit reports from 2016 describe the tower as staffed sometimes, and one August visit records it as staffed [^1]. That same visitor found the tower in good condition, with fresh paint [^1]. Wikipedia says the original tower "remains in seasonal use" [^3].

The tower was registered on June 17, 2004, as US 585, MA 12 [^1]. The summit sits in the University of Massachusetts Amherst's Cadwell Memorial Forest, and the register notes that the forest's trail map shows the tower and how to reach it [^1][^3]. The Metacomet-Monadnock Trail crosses the summit [^3].

The register gives the summit's elevation as 1,174 feet, while Wikipedia gives 1,240 feet [^1][^3].
