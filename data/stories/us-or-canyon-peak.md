Canyon Peak sits about nine miles west-northwest of Cave Junction, in Josephine County, at 4,903 feet.[^1][^3] A trail was built up to the summit, and in 1941 a 14-by-14 L-4 lookout cab went up on top.[^2][^1] The Josephine County list calls it an L-4-36 ground house built that year.[^4] The U.S. Forest Service administered the lookout, which sits in the Siskiyou National Forest.[^1]

Planning papers from May 1940 described the position as inaccessible country and named lightning as its main risk.[^2]

The only staffing we found comes from Medford Mail Tribune items, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page. It reported in June 1958 that a man had been named to the post but had not yet been sent there.[^2] A camping party that stopped at the lookout in July 1958 found the husband of one member stationed there for the summer.[^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says the cab was removed sometime in the 1960s.[^1] Another site says it was abandoned in the 1960s and is now gone.[^3] The Josephine County list marks its removal with a question mark.[^4]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1]
