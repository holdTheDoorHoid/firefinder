Thorn Creek Butte, in Grant County, Oregon, carried a lookout on the Malheur National Forest, about 22 miles southwest of John Day.[^2][^1] The register and firelookout.com both date its L-4 cab to 1933, but an Oregon lookouts history gives 1934 for an "L-4 ground house."[^1][^2][^3]

Panorama photographs were taken there in 1935.[^3] In 1938 the Malheur asked to build a garage and storage building at the site, and the request was approved.[^3] In August 1940 a Canyon Creek camp was building a garage at the lookout.[^3]

Both the register and firelookout.com say the cab was removed in 1958, after the site had been left unused for several years.[^1][^2] A 1958 recovery note, labelled on the Oregon Lookouts page as a U.S. Geological Survey note, says the tower was destroyed and the Forest Service survey mark was not found.[^3]

A 1933 Forest Service survey description says the point lies on a truck road.[^3] The register lists the spot as a former site, administered by the U.S. Forest Service.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
