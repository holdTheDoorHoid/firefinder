Gibson Point Lookout was a 30-foot pole tower that the sources call an L-4, built in 1933 in the St. Joe National Forest, about nine miles north of Avery, Idaho.[^1][^2] Its listed elevation is 5,490 feet.[^1] A patrol point stood 1.5 miles to the south-southeast, atop Marcus Cook Point.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list places the tower in Shoshone County.[^3]

Both lookout pages date the tower's destruction to 1953, and they agree on the years they give.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list marks the tower as gone.[^3]

None of the three pages describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as Drive-Up, but that label does not say whether the site is open to the public.[^1]
