Big Creek Lookout stood on a knoll at the end of a ridge in Kittitas County, in the Wenatchee National Forest.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com places it 16 miles west-southwest of Cle Elum.[^2] WillhiteWeb says the site was picked to watch one particular area rather than a wider region.[^3]

The phone line reached the site in 1930, and an L-4 cab was built in 1931.[^3][^1] The FFLOS register gives the same 1931 date.[^1]

Firelookout.com lists Bruce Anderson as a lookout in 1933.[^2] A September 1936 article from the Bothell Citizen, quoted on two of these pages, says Clare Moyer spent two months as a forest-fire lookout at Big Creek.[^3][^4] It describes one small fire during what it called a very quiet season.[^3][^4]

The register and both other pages say the cab was gone by 1955.[^1][^2][^3] None of them says how it came down.[^1][^2][^3]

WillhiteWeb says the site is now overgrown with forest and out of sight of the hiking trail close by.[^3] Photo captions on the same page name a foundation and stove parts.[^3]

Reaching it means a long drive on forest roads.[^3] WillhiteWeb says the road is steep and rocky near its end.[^3] The walk is under a mile and gains about 600 feet.[^3] The trail begins on an abandoned spur road and soon enters old-growth forest.[^3]

The register lists the site at 5,495 feet, or 1,675 meters.[^1] WillhiteWeb says the lookout stood at 5,368 feet, and that the 5,495-foot point lies about five minutes further up the ridge.[^3]
