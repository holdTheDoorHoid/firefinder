Jungle Point sits at 4,000 feet in the Clearwater National Forest, 14 miles east-northeast of Kooskia in Idaho County.[^1][^2]

An L-4 tower went up here in the 1930s, half a mile southeast of the summit.[^1][^2][^3] A Forest Service list compiled in June 2016 places it in Section 15, T33N, R6E.[^3]

All three sources call the lookout abandoned, and two of them mark it gone.[^1][^2][^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains of the tower today. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in, so it is reached on foot.[^2]

Another Jungle Point, a camp on the Nez Perce National Forest that was abandoned in 1942, is a separate site.[^4] It lies 13 miles south-southeast of Grangeville, while this one lies 14 miles east-northeast of Kooskia.[^1][^4]
