Larch Butte is a lookout site in the Clearwater National Forest, about 13 miles east-northeast of Pierce in Clearwater County.[^1][^3] Its listed elevation is 5,539 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

The lookout was a 30-foot tower with an L-4 cab, built in 1933.[^1][^2][^3]

A Clearwater National Forest lookout list, compiled in June 2016, marks it abandoned. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page and firelookout.com both list it as gone.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts classes the site as a hike-in.[^2] No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
