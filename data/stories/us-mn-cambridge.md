The Cambridge Lookout Tower stands in Isanti County, Minnesota, and the Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list still shows it as standing.[^1] The list places it under Cambridge, at about 45.56 degrees north and 93.23 degrees west.[^1] A Wikidata entry classes the structure as a fire lookout tower, names it after the city, and records it as located in Cambridge.[^2]

I could not find when the tower went up, who built it, or why it was put there. The directory gives only a name, a county, coordinates, a type and a status, and the Wikidata entry I could read has no dates or builders either.[^1][^2] Because no source I could read covers its design, its staff or any fires it watched, this account leaves those out rather than guessing.

Wikimedia Commons has a photograph of the tower, shared under a Creative Commons license that asks for credit and share-alike terms.[^3]

I also could not confirm whether the public may climb the tower or visit it. Before going, check with the city of Cambridge or the owner of the land, and do not climb a tower that is closed or private.
