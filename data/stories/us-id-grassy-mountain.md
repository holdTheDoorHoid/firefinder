The first lookout on Grassy Mountain was a log cabin with a cupola, put up in 1924 about 17 miles north of Kellogg in Shoshone County, Idaho.[^1][^2][^3] Sources do not agree on when the work began.[^2][^4] The Idaho Fire Lookouts site quotes a packer, Ken Maryott, who recalled the lookout being built in 1927 or 1928.[^2] Photo captions on WillhiteWeb.com put construction work in 1925 and 1926.[^4]

In 1936 a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab replaced the cabin.[^1][^3][^4] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page lists two people as known staff for 1927, Bliss Haynes and Glenn Maryott, each tagged "(Diary)".[^2]

Sources also differ on when the tower was destroyed. Idaho Fire Lookouts and WillhiteWeb.com give 1962, with WillhiteWeb.com allowing for the late 1950s, while firelookout.com says the late 1950s.[^1][^2][^4]

Today, Idaho Fire Lookouts says only the view remains.[^2] WillhiteWeb.com says that as of 2025, logging several years earlier had opened a full 360-degree view, that the site can be driven to the very top, and that several Forest Service roads access the mountain.[^4] It also says the site should be open for quite some time.[^4]
