Ben Parton is a high point in Sevier County, Tennessee, inside the Great Smoky Mountains National Park, near Silers Bald.[^2] The Tennessee lookout-tower list gives its height as 4,822 feet and tags it "historical."[^1] That list covers lookout towers and former locations on the Tennessee side of the park.[^1]

A travel guide gives the high point as 4,859 feet, so the two heights disagree.[^2][^1]

The guide says the point has no dedicated trail or trailhead of its own, and that it is reached by way of the Miry Ridge Trail system.[^2] It also says the top has no overlook platform and that the area is firmly backcountry-hiker terrain, suited to hikers who already handle the park's long ridge trails rather than first-time visitors.[^2] No source we read describes a tower or other structure left at the top today.

[^1]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Ben Parton Lookout, Smoky Mountains Travel, https://smokymountains.travel/listing/hiking/ben-parton-lookout (accessed 2026-10-21).
