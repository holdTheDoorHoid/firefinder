Elmira Peak appears in Ron Kemnow's Forest Lookouts listing as a lookout site on the Kaniksu National Forest, in Bonner County, Idaho.[^1] The listing gives its location as township 59 north, range 1 west, section 2.[^1] The page sits under the Idaho and Bonner County headings of the Forest Lookouts site.[^1]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts history of Jay Peak lists a 50-foot pole tower there from 1930.[^2] It also records a camp atop Elmira Peak, four miles southwest of Jay Peak.[^2]

No source found describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today.
