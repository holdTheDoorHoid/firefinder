Forest Lookouts lists an Alaqua tower in Walton County, Florida. Its entry gives the code 2N-20W-4, in the usual township, range and section format, though the page does not explain it.[^1]

Topozone, a topographic map site, lists an Alaqua Lookout Tower in Walton County. It shows the tower on the De Funiak Springs West USGS map, at 30.7051902 north, 86.2341107 west, with an approximate elevation of 197 feet (60 meters).[^2]

We found no source that gives a build date, design, height, staffing, or any later history. No source describes what remains at the site today, and we found nothing about public access.
