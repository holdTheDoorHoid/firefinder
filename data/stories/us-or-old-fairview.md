Old Fairview is a former lookout site in Douglas County, in southwest Oregon, at about 4,648 feet.[^1] The register lists the Douglas FPA as the agency that administered it.[^1][^2]

The Douglas FPA bought a 50-foot treated timber tower and a 14-by-14-foot lookout house for the site in 1956.[^3] A three-quarter-mile road was built to reach the new lookout.[^3] The 1956 report says the tower was finished that fall and the house would follow before the next fire season.[^3] The 1957 report says the house was finished that year.[^3] The register and firelookout.com both give 1956 as the build year, even though the house was not done until 1957.[^1][^2][^3] They describe a 50-foot wooden tower meant for living in.[^1][^2]

The register's photo caption covers the years 1956 to 1975.[^1] The Forest Lookouts history lists 1975 as the year the lookout was discontinued.[^3]

The tower was taken down in 1976, and its parts were reused at the new Mt. Scott lookout.[^3]

The register and firelookout.com both describe the tower as gone.[^1][^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site, and none says whether the place is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3] With no tower standing, there is nothing to climb.[^1] Anyone planning a trip should check current access with the managing agency first.
