Thorp Mountain Lookout sits on the summit of Thorp Mountain in Kittitas County, Washington.[^1][^3] The register places it in the Wenatchee National Forest.[^1] HistoriCorps says the Okanogan National Forest was combined with the Wenatchee National Forest in 2000, and TrailChick uses the name Okanogan-Wenatchee National Forest.[^3][^5]

The U.S. Forest Service built the lookout in 1930 as a 14-by-14-foot gable-roofed L-4 cabin.[^1][^3] HistoriCorps describes it as one of the few surviving gable-roofed L-4 cab lookouts in the Northwest.[^3] The register lists it with a registration date of September 1, 2001, and HistoriCorps says it was listed on the register that year.[^1][^3]

According to the register, the lookout got a complete refurbishment in summer 2007.[^1] firelookout.com dates its extensive maintenance to about 2008, and TrailChick says the 2008 renovations did not necessarily follow the original design.[^2][^5] The sources give different years for the work.[^1][^2][^5]

Staffing is where the sources disagree.[^1][^2][^5] The register says the lookout is currently staffed seven days a week.[^1] TrailChick, updated in March 2024, says it is no longer volunteer staffed because of carbon monoxide from the stove, though the Forest Service still uses it in periods of high fire danger.[^5] firelookout.com describes it as available for emergencies.[^2]

HistoriCorps planned a volunteer repair project on the lookout, which it describes as deteriorated.[^3] The two sessions were set for September 7–13 and September 16–22, 2026, with the Mountains to Sound Greenway Trust as partner.[^3]

TrailChick lists Thorp as a hike in, though it gives driving directions to the trailheads.[^5] The Washington Trails Association route from Thorp Creek covers 7.4 miles round trip with 2,484 feet of gain.[^4] A WTA note dated 8.24.26 says that trail was closed because of the Three Queens Fire.[^4]

The sources also give three elevations.[^1][^2][^4][^5] The register lists 5,582 feet, firelookout.com and TrailChick list 5,854 feet, and WTA gives 5,884 feet as the route's highest point.[^1][^2][^5][^4]
