The Hollofield tower stood on the northeast edge of Ellicott City in Howard County, reached from Church Lane.[^1] The state's record lists its installation date as unknown.[^1] The 9.43-acre parcel came to the state by a deed dated December 5, 1935.[^1]

A draft Department of Natural Resources report dated April 2007 says the old forestry tower was taken down, and a modern communications tower now stands in its place.[^1] The top section of the old tower, with its cab removed, is now a viewing platform at the Merkle NRMA, according to the same report.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Hollofield site as gone.[^2] It also lists a tower at Patuxent River Park as relocated from Hollofield, and marks it standing. The association marks towers whose structure survives without the cab with an asterisk.[^2] The state report names Merkle and not Patuxent River Park, so the two sources disagree about where the upper tower went.[^1][^2]

Patuxent River Park's own listing names an observation tower that has an elevator.[^3] The sources we read do not connect that tower to Hollofield.

The site sits in Patapsco Valley State Park, which the report describes as public parkland. The report recommends keeping the land within the park and as a site for communications equipment.[^1] The tower itself is no longer at the site. Inside the fence at its base are a propane tank, a backup generator and an equipment building made of concrete block.[^1]
