The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists a tower at the Mineral Lake site in Ashland County.[^1] It was built around 1933, likely by the Civilian Conservation Corps.[^1] The register places it along Camp Six Creek, inside the Chequamegon-Nicolet National Forest, and says the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources administered the site.[^1]

A Wisconsin Historical Society photograph record is titled for a CCC camp called Mineral Lake, but none of the sources I read ties that camp to the tower.[^1][^3]

The site entered the former-lookout register on December 31, 2023, as US 2101, WI 5.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list marks the tower as gone.[^2]

OpenStreetMap has a point named Mineral Lake Lookout Tower, tagged as an observation tower, but a map point does not show whether anything stands there.[^4]

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^4]
