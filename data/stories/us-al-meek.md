The US Geological Survey described the Meek tower in Winston County in 1966. Its station record gives directions from a schoolhouse: 2.6 miles north to a T-road, then 0.1 mile along a gravel road to the tower.[^1] The same record describes a four-legged steel structure about 100 feet high, with a glass-enclosed observation platform at the top, and gives the tower's name as Meek Fire Tower 1966.[^1] The station record also names the Arley topographic quadrangle, dated 1983.[^1]

A map site that uses USGS topographic maps also lists a Meek Lookout Tower in Winston County, on the Arley quadrangle, at an elevation of 823 feet. The station record gives no coordinates, so it cannot confirm that the two are the same structure. The listing gives the name but says nothing about whether the steel still stands.[^2]

No source describes the site today, so what remains there, and whether the land is open to visitors, is not known from these sources.[^1]

[^2]: Meek Lookout Tower, AL, TopoQuest, https://topoquest.com/place/alabama/tower/meek-lookout-tower/164375 (accessed 2026-10-21).
