Big Pine Hill, in Lackawanna County, once carried a steel fire-lookout tower on its summit.[^1] A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description written in 1955 gives the tower's height as about 90 feet (27 m).[^1]

When a survey crew revisited the station on August 6, 2004, they found the tower had been taken down.[^2] A smaller wooden observation platform had been built in its place, and the surveyors noted it could not serve as a geodetic control point.[^2] The station's landmark had been destroyed as well.[^2]

A hiking guide says the Pinchot Trail, which lies in Lackawanna State Forest, features an excellent vista from Big Pine Hill.[^3] The trailhead is set among pines, with plenty of parking.[^3] No source says whether that platform still stands.
