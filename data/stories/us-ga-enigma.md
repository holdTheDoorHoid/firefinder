Enigma is a small South Georgia community at the northwest corner of Berrien County.[^1] Roughly 14 km (9 miles) east of Tifton, it lies along U.S. Highway 82.[^1] A compiled list of Georgia lookout towers gives a tower under the entry GA222, "enigma?", at 31.46776 north and 83.30709 west, with an elevation of 336 and no stated unit. Read as feet, that is 102 m.[^2] The compiler named towers numbered 73 and above personally, so "enigma?" is a label, question mark included, rather than a name recorded on the tower.[^2] The entry's note reads "firetower rd", and the page does not explain it.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register's Georgia page names 29 lookouts, and none is called Enigma.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, stands at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
