The Hematite Fire Tower stands on the Land Between The Lakes NRA, and the U.S. Forest Service administers it.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register places it in Lyon County, Kentucky.[^1] A Forest Lookouts page that lists the tower as Curry Hollow (Hematite) gives Trigg County instead.[^2]

The Register describes an 80-foot Aermotor LS-40 tower, also known as Curry Hollow or Wildland Tower #1.[^1] It gives 1930 as the build year.[^1] Its description says only that the Civilian Conservation Corps built the tower in the 1930s, in what was known as the Kentucky Woodlands Wildlife Refuge.[^1] A related tower, Wildland #2, was built by the Corps in 1937 for the state division of forestry, and it first stood in the same refuge area.[^3]

The Register gives November 3, 2009 as the registration date, with Trenton C. Girard as nominator.[^1]

The sources read do not say who staffs the tower, whether it is open to visitors, or whether the public may climb it. They also name no fires it watched.[^1][^2][^3]

Anyone planning a visit to the Land Between The Lakes should check with the Forest Service about the tower before going.
