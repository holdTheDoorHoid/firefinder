High Divide is a site in Del Norte County, California, that a forest lookout listing places under the Siskiyou National Forest.[^1]

A May 1940 entry from the forest's guard-placement plans describes what the position could see. The entry says the view covered a lightning area, and it notes man-caused fires several miles to the south.[^1] It also says that once the roads were improved, the position would be worth more for fire suppression than it was then.[^1]

A California lookout directory lists a Low Divide (Stone Corral) in Del Norte County and marks its tower as gone.[^2] No source in this record says whether that is the same place as High Divide.[^2]

No source in this record describes what, if anything, remains at the High Divide site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
