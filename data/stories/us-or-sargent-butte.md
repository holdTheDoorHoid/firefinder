Sargent Butte sits in south central Oregon, in Wheeler County, about five miles northwest of Mitchell.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register puts the site at 44.608970 north and 120.227510 west, at an elevation of 3,315 feet (1,010 m).[^2] The Oregon Lookouts page lists its township, range and section as 11S-21E-17.[^3]

The register names the Oregon Department of Forestry as the administering agency.[^2][^3] The tower was built in the 1940s.[^2][^1] The register describes it as now gone, and so does the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list.[^2][^4]

The register lists the site as registered on July 16, 2011, with Ray Kresek as the nominator.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the butte today, or whether visitors can reach it.
