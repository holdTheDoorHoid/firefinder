Hickman Hill was a fire lookout tower on a hill southwest of New Albany, in Floyd County, Indiana.[^1] It also went by the Floyd County Lookout Tower and the Floyd Lookout Tower.[^1]

The Indiana Fire Towers site says the tower was constructed by 1935.[^1] The site dates a map that shows it to 1935 in one place and to 1936 in another.[^1] A 1951 topographic map of the Lanesville area also shows the tower.[^1] The site's author thinks it was probably standing in 1947 and 1951. The author guesses it may have been a private tower, noting that it was not on a major state park or state forest property, and says the Department of Conservation's annual reports do not discuss it much.[^1]

A Forest Service list of the state's fire towers, under a heading for the Indiana Department of Natural Resources, names Hickman Hill at Lanesville.[^2] The same list says towers marked with an asterisk were still standing in 2003. Hickman Hill has no asterisk.[^2]

Federal surveyors set a survey disk beneath the tower, stamped 1937. Their 1947 record describes the disk as being in good condition.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the tower as gone.[^4] The Indiana site's list of towers places it among those no longer standing.[^5]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the hill today. The federal survey database still carries the disk, but it rates the disk's stability as questionable or unknown, so the listing does not show that anything is left.[^3] We found nothing on public access, so check who owns the hill and whether visiting is allowed before going there.
