Goat Butte is a high point in Yakima County, at about 7,487 feet.[^1][^2] The lookout sat on the Yakima Indian Reservation, in what WillhiteWeb calls the Mt. Adams Recreation Area, a Yakama Nation area.[^3][^4]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register dates the Region 5 cab to 1930, and firelookout.com gives the same year.[^3][^2] WillhiteWeb says the cab was built in the 1930s, on the highest point.[^4]

WillhiteWeb reports that a 1946 USGS party said the lookout house had been given up in 1940.[^4]

Both the register and firelookout.com now describe the cab as ruins.[^3][^2] WillhiteWeb says a few ruins may still remain.[^4]

Getting in requires a Yakama Nation permit for the Mt. Adams Recreation Area, according to WillhiteWeb.[^4] The approach details are old: a 1946 USGS party took six hours to reach the site by horse, with the last water three miles before it.[^4] No source I found describes the site in more detail than that.
