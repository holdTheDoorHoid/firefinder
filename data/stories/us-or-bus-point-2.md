Bus Point's lookout was a crow's nest high in a large tree, in the Mt. Hood National Forest, in Wasco County, Oregon.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com's page title calls it a tower, but both pages we read describe a crow's nest in a tree.[^1][^2]

The nest was 95 feet up the tree.[^2] Firelookout.com gives the site an elevation of 3,333 feet.[^1]

Firelookout.com dates the lookout to 1933.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page includes panorama photos dated November 7, 1933.[^2] From 1933 to 1935 the Forest Service in Oregon and Washington ran a project to collect panoramic photos from lookouts across the region, and the Bus Point picture may belong to that survey, although the sources we read do not say so.[^4]

None of the pages we read names an observer, or a fire that the crow's nest watched.

Firelookout.com describes the lookout as abandoned, yet still in place.[^1] A 2013 post also calls it abandoned.[^3] Its author could not find the crow's nest on a July 2013 visit, but the post says the first person to find the author's cache did find it.[^3]

Firelookout.com lists the agency as the Mt. Hood National Forest.[^1] We found nothing about current access. The nest is high in a tree, so please do not try to climb to it.
