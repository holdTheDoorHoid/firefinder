Montgomery Peak, west of Red Bluff in Tehama County, already had a lookout at the Montgomery ranch by 1930.[^1] In July 1930, two members of a rural fire department were installing a telephone on the peak to make reports from the lookout quicker.[^1] Before that, the lookout had to cover several hundred yards to reach the ranch headquarters and report a fire.[^1]

A Corning paper reported in May 1932 that the county had just finished a lookout on Montgomery Peak.[^1] The new 20-foot station had an observation floor on top and living quarters below.[^1] Elmer Peasley was due to go on duty on June 1.[^1] The 1930 and 1932 reports may describe one station or two.[^1]

The Fire Lookout Association's California list gives the structure as an enclosed tower and marks it gone.[^2] No source we found describes what, if anything, remains on the peak today or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^2]: California Lookouts county list, Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/ca/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
