The Forest Lookouts page places the Indian Trail lookout in Laurel County, in the Daniel Boone National Forest.[^1]

The oldest source we found about the tower is a memorandum of January 6, 1936, quoted on the same page. It was written by a radio technician working on what the memo calls the Cumberland National Forest in Kentucky.[^2] The writer was at the Indian Trail tower to inspect a telephone line and the antenna installed on it.[^2] A radio set in the lookout picked up an experimental Forest Service station in Portland, Oregon, and the writer reported that the signal could be read for roughly 95 percent of the listening time.[^2]

The memo calls the forest the Cumberland National Forest, while the Forest Lookouts page names the Daniel Boone National Forest.[^2][^1]

The Forest Lookouts page marks the Indian Trail tower as "Removed," with no date.[^1] No source we read describes what remains at the site today or whether it is open to visitors, so ask the Daniel Boone National Forest office before going to look for it.

[^2]: Radio technician's memorandum, January 6, 1936, quoted from a Forest Service Bulletin on Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/indian-trail.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
