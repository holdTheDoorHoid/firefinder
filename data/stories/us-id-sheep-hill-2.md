Sheep Hill Lookout lies deep within the Frank Church River of No Return Wilderness on the Nez Perce National Forest, about 24 miles southeast of Elk City.[^1][^3] The site was in use as a camp by the early 1920s, and a log cabin with a cupola went up in 1928.[^3][^1]

In 1960 an R-6 cab replaced that cabin.[^1][^3] The sources do not agree on how tall its base is. The registry gives 7 feet, a Forest Lookouts history page gives 8 feet of cinder block, and Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts give 10 feet.[^1][^4][^3][^2] Keeping Watch lists the lookout as "Built: 1928" with an R-6 cabin, which conflicts with the 1960 date given elsewhere.[^5][^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts' known-staff list includes Doris and Bert Babcock in the 1960s and 1970s, and Betsy Booth in 2010.[^2] A 1975 item from the Recorder Herald, quoted on that same history page, reports that the Salmon National Forest would furnish a radio for installation at the lookout, which sits on the Nez Perce, to provide a radio link with Butte Point lookout or direct to the North Fork river patrol.[^4] The National Historic Lookout Register listed the site on August 25, 1997.[^1]

Today the lookout is reported as staffed, but none of these pages dates that status. Firelookout.com describes summer staffing every year, and Keeping Watch describes seasonal staffing.[^3][^5] Idaho Fire Lookouts describes it as a hike-in site that is actively staffed.[^2] Reaching it means a hike into wilderness, so plan for that before you go.
