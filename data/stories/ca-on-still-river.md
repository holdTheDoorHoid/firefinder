Firelookout.ca places the Still River Fire Tower at Still River, in the unorganized Centre Parry Sound area of Parry Sound District, central Ontario, at 45.834596, -80.574689. It describes the tower as steel and gives its elevation as 203 m (666 ft).[^1]

Wikipedia says the Parry Sound Forest Fire District was founded by Ontario's Department of Lands and Forests in 1922.[^2] The district's headquarters in Parry Sound was the central location for 21 fire tower lookouts, and Still River is among the other 20.[^2] A 1969 list on an Ontario fire tower history page counts 18 towers for the district and also names Still River.[^3] The two counts disagree.[^2][^3]

Most Ontario towers were built in the 1920s to 1950s. They fell under the Department of Lands and Forests, which was succeeded by the Ministry of Natural Resources in 1972.[^4]

The 1969 list says only four towers were still actively manned: Ardberg, Go Home, Stormy Lake and Boulter.[^3] Wikipedia names the same four, as Ardbeg, Go Home, Stormy Lake and Boulter, and says they were phased out in the 1970s, as aerial firefighting techniques came into use.[^2] Still River is not one of the four.[^2][^3]

A fan-run list of towers still standing does not name Still River.[^5] No source we found describes the site today or says whether the public may visit it.
