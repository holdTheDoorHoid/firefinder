Yazoo Lookout Tower is in Yazoo County, Mississippi, and the register gives its elevation as 386 feet (118 meters).[^1] The Mississippi Forestry Commission administers the site.[^1] The register describes a 100-foot Aermotor LS-40 tower, and OpenStreetMap tags the structure as an observation tower with lattice construction.[^1][^2]

The register lists the build date as unknown.[^1] I found no source that says who erected the tower or when, and none that records staffing or notable fires.

The site is registered as US 1733, MS 50, with a registration date of July 5, 2023.[^1] The register gives a site visit date of July 2, 2023. Its description says the Commission still maintains the site, which also holds the original work shed and a small office or residence.[^1]

That description suggests the tower may still stand, though no source says so directly.[^1] The register says nothing about public access, so this story makes no claim about visiting. Please ask the Mississippi Forestry Commission before going, and do not climb the tower.

[^1]: Yazoo Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ms/yazoo-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Yazoo Lookout Tower, OpenStreetMap node 357997314, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/357997314 (accessed 2026-10-09).
