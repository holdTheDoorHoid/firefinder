Midnight is on the websiteservice.ca list of Saskatchewan fire towers, at 53°38′22.2″ N, 108°22′39.5″ W.[^1] The card for the site names John Rule as towerman, and its photos carry captions dated 1963.[^1]

A towermen list on the same website places several people at Midnight. The list credits Maurice Bourassa with gathering its information, and it asks readers for corrections.[^2] Chris Rule is listed there from 1968 to 1970, and the list puts the word "unofficially" beside the Midnight name.[^2] Tracy Lockhart is listed for Midnight and Birch from 1977 to 1994.[^2] Hilda Switzer Lundgren is listed for seven towers, Midnight among them, with service dated 1957 and 1970 to 1994.[^2] John Rule's entry names Midnight among four towers, without dates.[^2]

The sources do not describe the Midnight site today or say whether the tower still stands.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Firetowers of Saskatchewan, websiteservice.ca, https://www.websiteservice.ca/firetowers_page4.htm (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Firetowermen and Women, websiteservice.ca, https://www.websiteservice.ca/firetowermen.htm (accessed 2026-10-20).
