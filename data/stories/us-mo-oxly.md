Oxly is a fire lookout in Ripley County, Missouri, listed by the Forest Lookouts site among its Missouri towers.[^1] The name is shared with a community in southeastern Ripley County, classed as a census-designated place. It sits beside Logan Creek and Missouri Route 142, roughly seven miles to the east of Doniphan.[^2]

A map website lists an "Oxly Lookout Tower" in Varner Township, typed as a tower.[^3] This map entry does not show whether a structure still stands, and no source found describes what remains at the site today.[^3]
