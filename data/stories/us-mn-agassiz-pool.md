A Minnesota lookout list kept by the Forest Fire Lookout Association places a tower called Agassiz Pool in Marshall County and marks it gone.[^1] The same list has a second Marshall County tower, Agassiz HQ, also marked gone.[^1]

The Agassiz name is shared with Agassiz National Wildlife Refuge, which the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service says was established in 1937 and sits in northwestern Minnesota.[^2] The refuge's hunting map for 2025 labels a pool called Agassiz Pool.[^3]

No source I could reach describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Minnesota Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Agassiz National Wildlife Refuge: About Us, U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service, https://www.fws.gov/refuge/agassiz/about-us (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Agassiz National Wildlife Refuge hunting map, 2025, U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service, https://www.fws.gov/rivers/sites/default/files/documents/2025-07/agassiz_hunt25_online.pdf (accessed 2026-10-10).
