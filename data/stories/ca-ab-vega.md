Vega's lookout is a steel tower, 30 m (98 ft) high, at an elevation of about 713 m (2,339 ft).[^1] The provincial data gives 2003 as its construction year.[^1] A photograph of the tower on the Canada Lookouts list for Alberta carries the same year, credited to Tim Klein and the Alberta ESRD online collection.[^2]

The lookout shares its name with Vega, an unincorporated community in central Alberta.[^3] One history of Alberta's lookouts says steel came into use for towers in the 1920s and steadily took over from wood.[^4]

Alberta's own description says every wildfire lookout site keeps an observer on duty, mostly from April to October.[^5]

The data lists Vega as active and gives its access mode as "drive".[^1] It does not say whether the public may visit, so treat the tower as a working fire-watch structure and do not climb it.
