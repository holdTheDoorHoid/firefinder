The site lies in Boone County, at 1,965 feet (599 m) above sea level.[^1] The register lists it as the Piney Knob Tower Site, US 1662 and WV 53, and records its entry on January 26, 2017.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site names the West Virginia Division of Forestry as the agency for the site.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's West Virginia list marks the tower as gone.[^2]

Statewide, towers were manned for roughly eight to fourteen weeks in each spring and fall season, the periods of greatest fire risk.[^4] Over time the state wound down its own tower network, and the final ones shut at the end of the 1990 fall fire season.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
