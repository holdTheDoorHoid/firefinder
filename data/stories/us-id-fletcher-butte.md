Fletcher Butte is a summit in Valley County, Idaho.[^1][^3] Mapcarta places it near the Williams Creek Summit locality, about five miles east of Ola, an unincorporated settlement in Gem County.[^3] A Forest Lookouts index lists the butte under the Boise National Forest, with the legal location 10N-2E-22.[^1][^2] Mapcarta puts the summit at 5,669 feet (1,728 m).[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, stands on the summit today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3] Check the Boise National Forest's current notices before any trip, because none of these pages covers access to the summit.
