A Georgia list of lookout towers names a site called "sapelo" in McIntosh County, at 31.59140, -81.41809.[^1] The list numbers its entries from GA001 to GA427, and this site is GA262. It carries the code gfc-133, which the list's note ties to an older Georgia Forestry Commission list of 203 towers. The list's author also says that names for towers 73 and above are the author's own creation, so the name may be a label rather than an official one.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register counts 29 registered lookouts in Georgia, and this site is not one of them.[^2]

The place name also appears in the Sapelo Island Lighthouse, a Georgia lighthouse that stands near the island's southern end.[^3]

No source found describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1]
