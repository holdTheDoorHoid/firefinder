Wynn Lake was a fire tower in Saint Louis County, Minnesota.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list gives its coordinates as 47.57281 north and 92.31066 west, and marks the tower as gone.[^1]

The Central US Lookouts page for Minnesota heads its entry Wynne Lake, although its navigation list reads Wynn Lake.[^2] It gives the land reference as 59N-16W-25 and lists the site as state land.[^2]

That page also credits a photograph dated March 26, 1934, from the Library of Congress Historic American Buildings Survey. Its caption places the tower near Duluth, on Rice Lake Road.[^2] The page gives no build date, so the 1934 date should not be read as the year the tower went up.[^2]

The caption's location does not obviously match the coordinates on the other list, and we did not settle which is right.[^1][^2]

No source we found describes the site today, or says whether it is open to the public.

[^1]: Minnesota Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Minnesota - Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/minnesota.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
