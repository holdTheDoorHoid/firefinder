The only source found for the New Lacy tower is a compiled list of Georgia towers. It places the tower in Bacon County at 31.53639, -82.36833.[^1] The entry gives a datasheet reference, BS0276.[^1] New Lacy is number 347 on that list, and the page says that names for towers numbered 73 and above are the author's own creation, so the name may not be a historic one.[^1]

The name is also used locally. Bacon County's revised 2019 wildfire protection plan refers to a New Lacy district with its own fire chief.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 29 registered Georgia towers, and New Lacy is not one of them.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists two Georgia entries, neither of them New Lacy.[^4]

No source found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.
