Turner Mountain is a summit in Surry County, North Carolina.[^1] In 1954 the Coast and Geodetic Survey described a survey station at the center of the top of a lookout tower on the mountain, about 6 miles south of Mount Airy. At about 85 feet (26 m) tall, the steel tower stood on four legs and ended in an enclosed observation platform.[^2]

Later notes from 1986 and 1987, kept by the North Carolina Geodetic Survey, record the station as recovered as described.[^2] A list of North Carolina fire towers names Turner Mountain in Surry County, with its survey number. The list also says it may include a few observation towers.[^3]

Today the highest point in Dobson Township is Turner Mountain, which a township article describes as the site of several communications towers.[^4] The article gives the elevation as 1,275 feet, while TopoQuest gives the summit as 1,587 feet.[^1][^4]

No source describes what remains of the 1954 lookout today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
