Park Ridge is a fire tower site in Saskatchewan [^1]. The Firetowers of Saskatchewan listing spells the name as one word, "Parkridge," and gives its position as 54 degrees 25 minutes 33.0 seconds north and 108 degrees 36 minutes 3.1 seconds west. It records nothing else about the site [^1].

The Canada Lookouts site lists Park Ridge among its Saskatchewan lookouts [^3].

A towermen list on websiteservice.ca places the name April Swartz beside "Park Ridge, Divide," with no years given [^2]. The table is hard to read, so that pairing may not be exact.

No source I could read describes what, if anything, stands at the Park Ridge site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Firetowers of Saskatchewan, websiteservice.ca, https://www.websiteservice.ca/firetowers_page4.htm (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Firetowermen and women, Firetowers of Saskatchewan, websiteservice.ca, https://www.websiteservice.ca/firetowermen.htm (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^3]: Saskatchewan Lookouts, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/saskatchewan.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
