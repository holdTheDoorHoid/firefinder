Elder Mountain rises to about 1,850 feet (564 m) in Marion County, roughly 5 miles northwest of Chattanooga, and it held a Tennessee state forest lookout tower.[^1][^2][^3] The state's survey numbers the tower 3-28, a district-and-tower code from the Division of Forestry's 1976 list, and a Forest Lookouts list places it in District 3.[^1][^2][^4]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the station in 1962, when it was a regular lookout cab on an 80-foot (24 m) four-legged steel tower.[^3] The 1962 directions ran up a private road that began at a keep-out sign, and the station sat beside a gravel road.[^3]

The state survey marks the tower as removed.[^1][^2] Elder Mountain is now a community on top of the mountain, with its own volunteer fire department.[^5] The sources used here do not describe what, if anything, remains at the tower site. The 1962 route was a private road, and no source describes public access to the site today, so do not assume the site is open to visitors.[^3][^5]
