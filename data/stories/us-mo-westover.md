Westover, an unincorporated community on Dry Creek in Crawford County, lies roughly seven miles south-southeast of Steelville.[^3]

A fire lookout station at Westover shows up in a 1944 study of place names in five southeast Missouri counties.[^2] The study lists it as the "Westovee Lookout Station" (its spelling), says it was named for the community, and notes that the tower watched for fires in the surrounding forest.[^2] It sources the listing to a Clark National Forest map.[^2]

The Central US Lookouts site gives the tower's location only as a survey code, 37N-3W-18, lists its agency simply as the National Forest, and marks the tower as removed.[^1]

In 1973 the Clark and Mark Twain national forests, Missouri's only two, were merged into a single forest, the Mark Twain.[^4] In the Mark Twain forest, a number of towers and their dwellings were declared surplus and removal began. By 1991 only 22 towers still stood there.[^4]

No source I found describes what remains at the Westover site today.
