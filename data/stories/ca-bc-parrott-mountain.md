Canada Lookouts lists Parrott Mountain as a British Columbia lookout, at 54.017 N, 126.383 W.[^1] A 1944 item in the same entry places it near Burns Lake.[^1]

In May 1939, The Interior News reported that two men were cutting trail to the proposed lookout on the mountain.[^1] A June 1939 item in The Province said that twelve men in the Prince Rupert district would build a lookout on Parrott Mountain as part of the government's summer forest programme.[^1] Both reports describe the work as planned.[^1]

In October 1944, the Forest Branch News Letter reported that it had been sent a story by Lookoutman F.W. Lindsay, who was stationed at Parrott Mountain.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains on Parrott Mountain today, or whether the summit is open to visitors.

[^1]: Parrott Mountain, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/parrott-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
