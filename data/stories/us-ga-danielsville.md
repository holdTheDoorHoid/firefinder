The Georgia Forestry Commission's history says the first fire observation tower went up on the Ware Hotel in Waycross, possibly as a result of a 1928 forestry fair in that city.[^1]

A Georgia lookout tower list places a tower named "danielsville" in Madison County, at about 34.1 degrees north and 83.2 degrees west.[^2] The list gives it no GFC number. Its name is also a label, because the list says names for towers numbered 73 and above are its compiler's own, and this one is numbered 176.[^2]

Georgia has cut its tower staff since the late 1980s in favor of air patrols.[^3] By 2006, a forestry commission spreadsheet showed that 137 of the state's 144 still-existing towers were no longer staffed, and 119 of those were listed as antenna towers, which are usually unoccupied.[^3]

Across the state, towers were being disposed of through auctions, scrap sales and sales to hunting clubs and similar groups.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
