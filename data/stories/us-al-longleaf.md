The Longleaf Lookout Tower stands in Butler County, Alabama, on the property of its owner, Jerry Watson, which the register calls Georgiana.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as a 100-foot International Derrick model Huxford fire tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab, built in 1939.[^1]

The register says the Alabama Forestry Commission declared the tower surplus in 2006. Watson then bought it, moved it and restored it.[^1] The register lists Watson as the owner and gives a registration date of December 18, 2009.[^1]

A second record complicates the picture. The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register lists a Longleaf Fire Tower Site in Spring Hill, about 0.7 kilometers from the coordinates given for the moved tower.[^1][^2] That entry says only the stands remain there.[^2] The two records do not say outright that the Spring Hill site is where the tower stood before it moved. That link is my inference from the matching name.

I found no account of who staffed the tower or which fires it watched.

Today the tower stands on private property.[^1] Neither record says whether the public may visit or climb it, and I found no public visiting arrangement.[^1][^2] Anyone interested should ask the owner before going near the property.

[^1]: Longleaf Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register (nhlr.org), http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/al/longleaf-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Longleaf Fire Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register (firetower.org), http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/al/longleaf-fire-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
