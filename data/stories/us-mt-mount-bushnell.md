Mount Bushnell, once called Lookout Peak, sits about eight miles south of Thompson Falls, on the Sanders and Mineral county line.[^2][^1] A gabled L-4 cab went up there in 1931.[^2][^1] Among live-in designs, the L-4 cab was by far the most popular, a wood frame cab with windows on every side.[^1]

The lookout was destroyed in 1958.[^2][^1] The Sanders County Ledger and Forest Lookouts agree on both dates.[^1][^2]

Today, a 2021 Sanders County Ledger hiking story says the base of the old lookout still sits at the top of the peak.[^3] The same story describes a hike to Mount Bushnell, and Forest Lookouts places the site under the Lolo National Forest.[^3][^2] No source we read describes a standing structure there.
