The Backbone Ridge lookout stood at the north end of its ridge in the Trinity National Forest, about 15 miles northwest of Weaverville and 8 miles north of Helena.[^1] A 1961 National Geodetic Survey record describes a wood lookout house with a chimney 13 feet tall.[^1] The survey station was set at the top center of that chimney.[^1]

A July 1938 Blue Lake Advocate notice reported that newlyweds Mr. and Mrs. Normal Olsen were living at the Backbone Ridge lookout.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Backbone as a gone ground site.[^2] A California table of lookout sites also lists it as an LO site.[^3] That table's key defines an LO site as one whose building has been removed, possibly leaving no evidence behind, or whose status is unknown.[^3]

No source we found describes what remains on the ridge today, or says whether the site is open to visitors.
