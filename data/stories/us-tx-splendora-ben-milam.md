The Splendora lookout, which the Forest Lookouts site also calls Ben Milam, stood in Montgomery County, about 13 miles east-southeast of Conroe.[^1] A National Geodetic Survey record describes a steel tower on four legs, standing on a concrete base, with a cab on top and an overall height of 100 feet.[^1] The page credits the Texas Forest Service as the agency.[^1]

A survey note says the station is probably the same as the Ben Milam Lookout Tower, marked T.F.S., and prints 1942 beside that name.[^1] The survey recovered the station mark in 1952.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas index marks the tower as gone.[^2] No source we read describes what remains at the site, or whether the land is open to visitors.
