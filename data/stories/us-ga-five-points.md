The Five Points Lookout Tower is listed as a fire tower in Jones County, Georgia, at 33.01263, -83.66045, with an elevation of 592, which we take to be feet (180.4 m) because the list states no unit.[^1] That list was last revised in March 2016 and draws on USGS name databases and topographic maps.[^1] A separate Georgia lookout list gives the Georgia Forestry Commission as the tower's agency.[^2] A mapping site also knows it as Five Point Lookout and tags it as a tower in OpenStreetMap data.[^3]

No source we found describes the site today, so what remains there and whether it is open to the public are unknown.
