Strawberry Mountain Lookout stood in Bonner County, Idaho, about 20 miles northeast of Sandpoint, in the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1]

The lookout was a 40-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, dated to about 1934.[^2] A Sandpoint News Bulletin article from June 21, 1962, quoted by Forest Lookouts, says the lookouts were put up by the Civilian Conservation Corps during the 1930s.[^2] According to the same article, the Forest Service dropped the towers from use and upkeep in 1950, when it moved to aerial patrol.[^2]

The paper listed Strawberry Mountain among six abandoned lookouts that were in the process of being toppled and destroyed.[^2] Forest Lookouts gives its status as "Removed."[^2] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts simply call it gone, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list reads "Tower Gone."[^1][^3][^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site.[^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page tags it "Hike-In" but gives no current condition for the site.[^3]
