Mullikin Mountain rises to 2,891 feet in Clearwater County, Idaho.[^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts prints the elevation with the abbreviation CTPA, which the page does not explain.[^2] A lookout once stood on it.[^1][^2] Both main write-ups give one dated entry, 1926, and say the history is unknown.[^1][^2] Neither gives a build date.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list places the site at 46.60521 north and 116.22955 west, and lists its type as unknown.[^3]

Both write-ups describe the lookout as abandoned.[^1][^2] The association's list marks it gone.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts has no documented staffing history for it.[^2]

No source describes what remains on the mountain today, and none says whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts' Drive-Up tag describes how the site is reached, not whether it is open.[^2]
