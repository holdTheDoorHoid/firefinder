The Texas Forest Service owned a fire lookout tower in Anderson County that the sources call Montalba.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as Montalba (Wilkerson Mtn), at 31.91906 north and 95.70855 west, and marks it as gone.[^2]

A 1982 USGS topographic map of the Wilkerson Mountain area, which shows the towns of Montalba and Elmwood, has a Lookout Tower among its features.[^3]

A Texas Forest Service notice dated January 15, 1993, asked for sealed bids to buy, dismantle and remove its lookout towers. The notice listed the Montalba tower among those for sale, and bids were due on Tuesday, February 23, 1993.[^1] The text is credited to the Tyler Morning Telegraph.[^1]

The notice does not mention Wilkerson Mountain, so the tower's link to that place rests on the association's listing, Montalba (Wilkerson Mtn).[^1][^2]

The notice put the tower up for sale for dismantling and removal, but it does not show that the work was done.[^1] The association's list marks the tower as gone.[^2]

Nothing we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.[^1]
