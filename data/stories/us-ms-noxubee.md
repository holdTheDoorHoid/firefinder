The Noxubee Lookout Tower stands in Noxubee County, Mississippi, on a site that was once a county work center for the Mississippi Forestry Commission (MFC).[^1] The register describes an MC-39 Aermotor tower that is 100 feet tall.[^1] The MC-39 was one of several Aermotor lookout models sold at a range of set heights.[^4]

The register gives no build date and lists it as unknown.[^1] It puts the elevation at 249 feet, or 76 meters, while OpenStreetMap gives 74 meters. We have not settled that small gap.[^1][^2] OpenStreetMap maps the structure as a lattice observation tower too.[^2] A lookout history site lists a Noxubee entry under Noxubee County, and the part we read gives no further detail.[^3] We found no record of staff or of any fire connected with this tower.

The register was filed on July 4, 2022, on the nomination of Jason Johns.[^1] It lists the MFC as a cooperator and says the site is now privately owned.[^1] It does not say when the work center closed or when the land changed hands.

Today the tower sits on private land.[^1] We found no information about public access, so ask the owner before any visit. Please do not climb it.
