Cayuse Hill is in Missoula County, Montana.[^1] Firelookout.com describes a 40-foot pole L-4 tower there, dated to 1932.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page places the site on the Lolo National Forest.[^1]

The dates do not fully agree. A September 1933 newspaper item reports that a 40-foot tower had been hauled to Cayuse the week before, but it names the Clearwater forest rather than Lolo.[^1] A March 1934 newspaper item describes Civilian Conservation workers building a road from the Nine Mile area toward the lookout, which it places on the Lolo forest.[^1]

The end is also reported differently. Firelookout.com says the tower was destroyed in 1966.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page lists it only as removed.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today. Firelookout.com says the site is on private property, and we found no source describing public access.[^2]

[^1]: Cayuse Hill, Forest Lookouts, https://montanalookouts.weebly.com/cayuse-hill.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Cayuse Hill Fire Lookout Tower, Rex's Fire Lookout Page, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/cayusehill.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
