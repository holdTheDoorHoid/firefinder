The Forest Lookouts directory files a fire tower near Medoc under Halifax County, North Carolina, and lists the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources as its agency.[^1] A list of North Carolina towers gives the site as 36.24777, -77.87388.[^2]

The one dated record is a county decision rather than a finished tower. On October 16, 1947, according to the directory, the county agreed to work with the state forestry service on a third fire tower, planned for a spot near Medoc.[^1] The directory credits the Roanoke Rapids Herald for the item.[^1] The state's own history dates its first fire tower to 1926, in Harnett County, so the Medoc decision came a couple of decades into that program.[^3]

Today no source describes what, if anything, remains near Medoc, or whether the public can reach the site.
