Hee Mountain lies in McCurtain County.[^1] A U.S. Forest Service project on the Ouachita National Forest, with a decision signed in 2009, carries the name Hee Mt. Tower.[^2]

The project, titled Hee Tower Fuel Reduction Burns, proposed controlled burning across 455 acres to cut fuel build-up.[^2] The Forest Service signed its decision on February 24, 2009.[^2] The record lists the Kiamichi Ranger District as the lead management unit and marks the project completed.[^2]

The record gives two location details that it never reconciles. It lists McCurtain County and a legal land description, but it also places the site about five miles southwest of Buffalo, Oklahoma.[^2] The exact position is therefore not settled by that record alone.

A map database, TopoQuest, lists Hee Mountain as a summit at about 1,302 feet above sea level.[^3]

No source describes the tower's condition, access or use today.
