Chetco Peak lies in Curry County, in the Siskiyou National Forest, at 4,540 feet, about 18 miles east-northeast of Brookings.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com says the lookout stood on a minor point 1.5 miles east-southeast of the prominent summit.[^2]

The register dates the tower to 1936 and describes a 10-foot tower made of redwood, with a live-in cab.[^1] Firelookout.com also gives 1936.[^2] Forest Lookouts gives 1937 for a 10-foot tower and lookout cab, and says panorama photographs were taken on June 26, 1937.[^3] The register's own panorama is captioned 6-25-1935, earlier than either build date, so the sources disagree about when the tower went up.[^1]

A 1940 guard-placement plan for the Siskiyou National Forest, quoted by Forest Lookouts, described the position as one that apparently did not get much use, with lightning the main risk.[^3] In 1941 the lookout was staffed for 84 days and reported to the Redwood station by radio.[^3]

The register says the lookout was removed in 1950.[^1] Firelookout.com says it was abandoned in the 1950s and is gone.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list also shows the tower as gone.[^4]

The sources we read do not describe what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether visitors can reach it.
