Bass Hill was a fire lookout in Alexander County, in the Shawnee National Forest.[^1] A 1942 description calls it a Forest Service fire tower about three miles northwest of Elco.[^1]

The tower was completed in March 1934 as an experimental structure of western larch timber.[^1][^2] A National Archives caption says it was among the first of eight towers made from western larch, and it spells the name "Base Hill."[^2] An archive photograph dated June 1941 shows the tower.[^2]

The Central Illinois lookout list marks the tower as removed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Illinois list shows it as "Tower Gone."[^3] According to the Forest Service, Trigg is the only one of the forest's sixteen fire lookouts that remains.[^5]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. Wikidata places the lookout inside the Shawnee National Forest.[^4]
