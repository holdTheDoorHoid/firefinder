Marble Point was a Lolo National Forest lookout in Mineral County.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com places it about 1.5 miles east of an earlier point two miles north-northeast of Sheep Mountain.[^1]

The tower went up in 1936. One account gives it as a 50-foot pole tower and the other as a round timber tower, and both give it an L-4 cab.[^1][^2]

A July 1948 item in the Mineral Independent, quoted on Forest Lookouts, said that Fern Bonnell, a St. Regis school teacher, had taken over the summer lookout duties. It said she had already worked the station for the three summers before that.[^2]

Firelookout.com lists three more known lookouts: Wally "Bigfoot" Hahn in 1950, Mack Mage in 1953 and Jim Bookie in 1954.[^1]

The lookout was destroyed in 1965.[^1][^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to the public. The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^3]
