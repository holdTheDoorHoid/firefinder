Baxter was a lookout tower in Jasper County, Mississippi.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site lists it under Jasper County and the Mississippi Forest Service.[^1] A 1970 National Geodetic Survey description calls it a state forest service lookout tower. It places the tower roughly 6 miles north-northeast of Montrose, 4 miles southwest of Garlandville, and 0.4 mile north of State Highway 15.[^1] The survey describes a four-legged structure with a small house on top, 100 feet (30.5 m) tall.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site marks the tower as removed, but it gives no date for the removal.[^1] Research found no source describing what, if anything, remains at the site today or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Baxter - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/baxter.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
