The Mattawawan fire tower is a heavy steel lookout near Mattawan, in Nipissing District in northeastern Ontario.[^1] The register's oldest date is 1963, when the Department of Lands and Forests' Tower Detection System Map placed a steel tower of about 30.5 m (100 ft) at this spot.[^1]

The register says the lower ladder had been removed by 1985, and that the tower was put forward that year as a repeater site.[^1] The North Bay topographic map (31L) no longer shows it.[^1]

The register gives no current condition, and its latest date is 1985.[^1] An undated caption on a fire-tower fan site, mirrored from GeoCities, credits the North Bay MNR for its photos and calls one "Mattawan Tower", saying vandalism has left it without a roof.[^2] The caption gives no location, so it may or may not be this tower.[^2] Neither source says whether the public can reach the site.[^1][^2]
