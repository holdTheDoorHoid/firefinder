Stokesville is a tower site in Charlton County, Georgia, listed as entry 346 in a list of Georgia lookout towers kept at tnlandforms.us. The entry places the site at 30.39657 N, 82.09431 W.[^1] The list's author says names for towers numbered 73 and above are the author's own creation, so the name Stokesville may not come from local use.[^1]

The list was started because the FFLA site listed no Georgia towers in 2010, and it was revised in March 2016.[^1] It draws on several kinds of sources: the Georgia Forestry Commission supplied coordinates for 118 towers, an older commission list described 203 towers, and other people provided more locations.[^1]

No source describes the site today, including whether a tower still stands there or whether the ground is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
