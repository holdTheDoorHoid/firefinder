The Forest Lookouts site lists a tower at Semmes in Mobile County, Alabama.[^1] The Mobile News-Item reported on December 11, 1929 that Harry F. Smith, division forester, had announced the 100-foot lookout tower would go on a five-acre plot at Semmes, not on a 10-acre tract 12 miles from Mobile on the Lott road.[^1] The notice gave fire detection as the tower's purpose.[^1] The same notice said the smallest of the two county towers was then going up at Citronelle.[^1]

A TopoQuest map record lists a Semmes Lookout Tower in Mobile County at an elevation of 242 feet.[^2] That record gives a location, not a build date or a status.[^2]

No source we read describes the site today, so we cannot say whether the tower still stands, whether any part of it remains, or whether the public can see it.
