The Thompson Point lookout stood in Clearwater County, Idaho, about six miles west of the Canyon Ranger Station.[^1] It was built in 1934 as a 40-foot pole platform, with its cabin at the north end of the ridge, about half a mile north of the summit.[^1][^2] One list gives the date as c.1934.[^3] The site sits at 3,995 feet.[^1][^2]

The Clearwater Timber Protective Association is the agency listed for the site.[^3]

The tower was destroyed in 1958.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list files it as a tower and now marks it as gone.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left on the ridge today. Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site drive-up, but none of the pages says whether the land is open to visitors.[^2]
