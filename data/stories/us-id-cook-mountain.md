Cook Mountain, about 25 miles east-northeast of Pierce in Clearwater County, Idaho, began as a tree-top lookout in 1917.[^2] Ron Kemnow's listing credits Paul Wohlen with building it, citing a Clearwater National Forest history, and describes a platform set on two trees cut to about 15 feet.[^3]

A 25-foot tree platform is listed for 1919, and an L-4 cab appears by 1941.[^1][^2][^4] The Forest Service's Clearwater list gives the lookout's elevation as 6,534 feet.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts, Rex Kamstra and the Forest Service list all give 1963 as the year the lookout was abandoned, and the Forest Service list calls it gone.[^1][^2][^4] Ron Kemnow's page says it was removed, without a date.[^3]

The sources differ on what kind of lookout it was. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list still classes Cook Mountain as a ground lookout, while the Forest Service list and the Idaho and Rex Kamstra pages describe tree platforms.[^5][^4][^1][^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors. Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site "Hike-In," which describes how it is reached rather than whether it is open.[^1]
