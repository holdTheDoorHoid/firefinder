The tower the directories call Sentinal Point stood in Price County, Wisconsin.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list places it at 45.97078, -90.08133 and marks it gone.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page for the site spells the name Sentinel Point and gives only the county.[^2] The sources spell it two ways, so records are worth searching under both names.

An OpenStreetMap point for the tower is named Sentinal Point Lookout Tower. It carries an elevation of 511 meters and is tagged as an observation tower.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
