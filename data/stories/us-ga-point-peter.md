An online index of Georgia lookout towers lists a tower named Point Peter in Oglethorpe County, at 34.00068, -83.01381.[^1] The index's map was last revised on 22 March 2016, so its entries may be older than today.[^1]

Georgia's fire towers date to the early twentieth century, and their number peaked at more than 200 in the 1960s.[^2] According to a 2006 Savannah newspaper report, 137 of the state's 144 surviving towers were no longer staffed.[^2] The article also described auction sales, with some towers going for scrap metal and others to hunting clubs or other groups.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association had catalogued 360 tower sites, current and former, around the state.[^2]

No source found describes the Point Peter site today, including whether a tower still stands there or whether the public can visit.
