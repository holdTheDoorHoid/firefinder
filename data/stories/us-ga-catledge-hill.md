The Georgia lookout tower list at tnlandforms.us gives a tower called Catledge Hill Lookout Tower in Brooks County, with an elevation of 206 and no unit given (read here as feet, about 63 m), and places it at latitude 30.70048 and longitude -83.59460.[^1] The list says its first 72 towers are named from a USGS name database, and this one is entry GA013 in the list.[^1]

A forest lookouts directory page also names the tower Catledge Hill and places it in Brooks County.[^2] That directory's state index files Catledge Hill under Florida, but Brooks County is in Georgia, so it is listed here under Georgia.[^2][^3]

Since the late 1980s, Georgia has steadily trimmed its fire-tower personnel and turned to aerial patrols, which officials consider cheaper and more effective.[^4] A 2006 newspaper report, citing a forestry commission spreadsheet, found that 137 of the state's 144 surviving towers were no longer staffed.[^4] That article does not mention this tower.[^4]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, whether a tower still stands there, or whether the public can reach it.
