Bruce Ridge had a fire lookout in Flathead County, Montana, on the Flathead National Forest.[^1][^2] The two main sources disagree about when the site was first developed.

Firelookout.com says the site had a camp in 1922, and an L-4 cab atop a minor crest of the ridge in 1939.[^1] The Forest Lookouts Montana page lists a 1930 entry under the site's name: the first pre-cut lookout house that the Superior Building Company built on the Flathead Forest.[^2] The sources do not reconcile the 1930 and 1939 dates, so both appear here.

Firelookout.com says the cab was abandoned in 1948 and is gone.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page says the site was removed, but gives no date for that.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a ground lookout that is gone.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Bruce Ridge Fire Lookout Cabin, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/bruceridge.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Bruce Ridge, Forest Lookouts (montanalookouts.weebly.com), https://montanalookouts.weebly.com/bruce-ridge.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association (firelookout.org), https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
