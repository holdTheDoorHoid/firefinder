Falls Point Lookout stood on the Nez Perce National Forest in Idaho County, about 30 miles east-southeast of Kooskia.[^1][^4] It sat a short distance southwest of Selway Falls, along the Selway River, at an elevation of 5,069 feet.[^1][^2][^4]

Sources give 1933 as the build year.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The lookout was an L-4 cab set on a log base, which firelookout.com describes as a 10-foot log crib about a mile north of the summit.[^1][^2][^4] The Weebly page gives the date as circa 1933 and describes a gable roof.[^3]

The Forest Service says the lookout went out of use and was burned in September 1963.[^4] It also suggests that other lookouts nearby, with a wider view, may have led to the decision to remove this one.[^4]

The sources disagree on when use ended.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com says the lookout was abandoned in 1950.[^1] The Weebly page says it fell into disuse because its view was limited, under a September 1963 entry, and gives no separate year.[^3] All of them agree it burned in 1963.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site "Drive-Up" and "Burned Down" without explaining either tag.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today or whether the road to it is open.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
