Durdenville is listed among Georgia lookout towers in Emanuel County, at about 32.530° N, 82.202° W.[^1] Its only note on the list is the word "topo". The map on the page was last revised on March 22, 2016.[^1]

Emanuel County was formed on December 10, 1812, and Swainsboro is its seat.[^2]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
