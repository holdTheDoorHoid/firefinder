The Lenawee lookout stood in Bayfield County on Forest Service land inside the Chequamegon-Nicolet National Forest.[^1][^2] A 1953 survey station description by the Coast and Geodetic Survey, reproduced by Central US Lookouts, places the tower on that site and puts a survey mark 41 feet south of its southeast leg.[^1] That description means the tower was in place by 1953.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^3] The Central US Lookouts page also shows a "Removed" label under the lookout's name, with no date or explanation.[^1]

A Wisconsin DNR timber sale named for the tower, the Lenawee Tower sale, covers a 103-acre sale area under a contract that ends June 30, 2028.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, stands on the site now, or whether visitors may go there.
