Round Butte stands at an elevation of 3,272 feet (997 m), about 5 miles southwest of Madras in Jefferson County.[^1][^2] The Register places it on the Deschutes National Forest and lists the U.S. Forest Service as the administrator.[^1]

A cabin was built there in the 1940s.[^1][^2] Both sources date it only to that decade, with no year.[^1][^2] The Register says the cabin was removed in 1956, while FireLookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2]

The Register recorded the site on July 16, 2011, after a nomination by Ray Kresek.[^1] Both sources list it as a former lookout, and neither gives a later date for the structure.[^1][^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, and none says whether the site is open to the public.
