Rosemead is a fire tower in Bertie County, North Carolina.[^3] A history page for the state's lookouts lists the tower under the North Carolina Forest Service and says the State Forest Service erected a 120-foot (36.6 m) tower there in 1948.[^1] The Coast and Geodetic Survey gives a different height.[^2] Its 1959 description calls the tower a square metal cabin on a four-legged steel structure, 110 feet (33.5 m) overall.[^2] The same description places the lookout in the village that the survey spells "Rosemeade," about five miles southeast of Powellsville and five miles west of Colerain.[^2]

None of the sources checked describes the site today. The survey record has one history entry, a 1959 observation, and it still lists the mark as current survey control.[^2] A 2021 list of North Carolina towers places Rosemead at 36.18277, -76.86638 in Bertie County.[^3]
