Spruce Mountain rises to about 5,647 feet, or 1,721 meters, on the North Carolina side of the Great Smoky Mountains National Park.[^1] A trail guide files the Spruce Mountain Trail under Swain County, North Carolina.[^2]

A 2019 hiking account says a fire tower and a ranger's cabin were built on top of the mountain four years after Congress created the park, and that electricity was installed too.[^3]

The tower did not last. Tennessee Landforms lists it as removed.[^1] The trail guide says the tower is now gone, and that its view was once among the best in the park.[^2] The 2019 account says only foundation blocks remain, covered in blackberry brambles.[^3] A 2010 hiking account reached the old clearing, found it thick with blackberries, and did not try to find the foundation.[^4]

Today the site lies inside the national park and is reached on foot.[^1][^3] The 2019 account describes the trail as a hiking destination off Balsam Mountain Road, 1.2 miles in and out.[^3] The trail guide describes a ridge route from Polls Gap, 5.5 miles toward the summit.[^2] The two accounts do not agree on the distance. The 2010 account reached the old tower site from Cataloochee, so these sources do not settle which county the site is in.[^4][^2]

No tower stands there now.[^3]
