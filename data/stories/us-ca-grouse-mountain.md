Grouse Mountain is a lookout site in Humboldt County, California.[^2] A 1956 item, quoted by Forest Lookouts, places the lookout in the Six Rivers National Forest and says the U.S. Forest Service reported on it.[^1] The firelookout.org index lists the site as a tower that is gone.[^2]

Construction began in 1934.[^1] A March 1934 Blue Lake Advocate item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, said two men were stationed on the mountain while they located the new station.[^1] By August, foundations were going in and building materials were being hauled to the site, and a Shasta Courier item predicted that three lookout towers in the lower Trinity district would soon be finished, if work kept pace.[^1]

A ranger was posted at the lookout for the 1956 season.[^1] A Eureka Humboldt Times item called it the first season since 1947, which suggests no one was stationed there in the years between.[^1]

Forest Lookouts marks the page as removed but gives no date or detail about what was taken down.[^1] The firelookout.org index marks the site as gone.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, and none says whether the site is open to visitors.

[^2]: California Lookouts (Alphabetical List), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/ca/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
