The Poyner (Fincastle) lookout is a standing tower in Henderson County, Texas.[^1][^2] The listing's second name, Fincastle, is a community that lies on Farm Road 315 about four miles north of Poyner.[^2][^3]

The Handbook of Texas spells the nearby community Poyner, while Wikipedia uses Poynor for the town, which it places in southeastern Henderson County.[^3][^4] Fincastle was settled in 1848, and a post office opened there in 1855.[^3] By 1884 it had three steam cotton gins and three churches.[^3] The Handbook says the town declined after the Texas and New Orleans Railroad was built south of it in 1900 and 1901, because commerce moved to other centers.[^3]

We found no build date for the tower. No source we read names its builder or gives its height, and we found no list of people who staffed it or of fires it watched. The Forest Lookouts page for Fincastle gives only a two-word note, which says the tower is standing.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Poyner (Fincastle) tower as standing, with coordinates in Henderson County.[^2] We found no later record of its condition, and no statement about public access. Anyone interested should ask local officials before going, and should not climb the tower without permission.
