The Former Fire Lookout Sites record says the Olga Coal Company built the tower it lists as Olga No. 1, and gives its first day in service as October 12, 1939.[^1] The record names Alawest as the administrator and puts the site at 2,458 feet (749 metres) above sea level.[^1] A Wikipedia article on Coalwood, West Virginia, says the Olga Coal Company name dates from 1947, when the company was sold and renamed.[^2] That is eight years after the tower's recorded first day in service, and the sources read do not explain the difference.[^1][^2]

A 1986 newspaper report, quoted on the Forest Lookouts website, describes a 40-foot tower on land the Olga Coal Company owned.[^3] It says the Department of Agriculture looks after the tower and a small cabin, and that the observer then in charge had held the post for five years.[^3] The report calls the structure the Olga Fire Tower and gives no number, so it cannot be tied to Olga No. 1 with certainty.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's West Virginia list marks the site as gone.[^4]

No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to the public.[^4]
