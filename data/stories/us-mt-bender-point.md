Bender Point sits in the Beaverhead National Forest, at 8,439 feet.[^1] The firelookout.com listing puts it 17 miles northwest of Wisdom.[^1] A 1952 paper gave the distance as 15 miles as the crow flies, or 25 miles by trail.[^2]

A watchman was posted on the spot each summer for nearly ten years before a tower stood there.[^2] A 1952 Dillon paper said there were no living quarters, just the equipment needed for lookout work.[^2] Earlier notices name Fred Haskins, who gave up the post for the season in 1940, and Clifford Kearney of Dillon, placed there in 1941.[^2] Robert Hilton, of Banville, Illinois, took up the station in 1944.[^2]

The Forest Service began a tower in 1952.[^2] In September the Dillon Daily Tribune described it as about 30 feet high, with a small house on top for the person on watch.[^2] By early October a four-man crew under Mickey Durant was building it.[^2] In January 1953 the paper reported it complete.[^2] Firelookout.com dates the 30-foot pole L-4 tower to 1953, so the build ran from late 1952 into early 1953.[^1][^2]

Sonny York had the watch in 1951.[^2] In July 1953 the paper reported Bill Wilcox of Butte in charge when the lookout opened.[^2] In September it said Don Smith of Butte had served there over the summer.[^2] The paper names both men for 1953 and does not say how the season was split.[^2] Later seasons brought Milton Andrews of Yoakum, Texas, in 1954, Homer Meyer, a forestry student at the university in Missoula, in 1956, and Benny Jones, a college student, in 1957.[^2] When Richard Pease had to go back to his teaching job in Lima, Terry Browning replaced him in 1958.[^2] In July 1963 the Forest Service staffed the Bender and O'Dell lookouts.[^2]

Firelookout.com lists a 20-foot treated timber tower for 1964 and says the site was gone by 1976.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list also shows the tower as gone.[^3] Forest Lookouts marks the structure removed, without a date.[^2]

We found no source describing what remains at the site today, or whether visitors may reach it.[^1][^2]
