The Georgia tower list places a Winokur Lookout Tower in Charlton County, at 30.98884, -82.02456, with an elevation of 72 feet (22 m).[^1] The entry is tagged gfc-830. The list's notes say the gfc code marks towers from an older Georgia Forestry Commission list, which recorded the sites of 203 towers.[^1]

A Forest Lookouts index places the name Winokur under Georgia. The tower's page on that site gives only the name and the county, Charlton County.[^2]

No source describes the site today, and none says whether it is open to the public, so check its condition before planning any visit.

[^2]: Winokur - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/winokur.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
