Scotty Peak stands in the Kootenai National Forest, about 15 miles north of Noxon in Sanders County, Montana.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 6,779 feet.[^1]

The entry for the peak dates a camp there to 1935 and gives 1942 as the year the site was abandoned.[^1]

A 2021 Sanders County Ledger feature says the county was the site of Montana's first two Forest Service lookouts, and it puts the county's total at 82.[^2]

No source found describes the site today. That includes what, if anything, remains on the summit and whether the peak is open to visitors.
