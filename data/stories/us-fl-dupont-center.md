The Eastern US Lookouts listing gives this lookout the name Dupont Center and places it in St. Johns County.[^1]

The name belongs to a place near St. Augustine.[^2] A DuPont House history page on Abandoned Florida says the place got its name when Cornelius DuPont and his wife donated land the government needed to widen the highway.[^2]

No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.

[^1]: Dupont Center - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/dupont-center.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: DuPont House, Abandoned Florida, https://abandonedfl.com/dupont-house/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
