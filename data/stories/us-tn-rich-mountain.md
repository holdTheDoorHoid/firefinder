Tennessee's lookout register lists a Rich Mountain tower in Campbell County, at 2,369 feet, and marks it removed.[^1] The register also notes that many Tennessee towers have been removed and that most of their sites are now private property.[^1] Check who owns the land before going. No source describes what remains at the site today.

Do not confuse it with the Rich Mountain near Hot Springs, North Carolina, which WillhiteWeb describes as a standing lookout.[^2]
