Kirby Lookout Tower is a name on OpenStreetMap, the open map database, which places its point at 34.16 N, 93.63 W.[^1] The map tags it as a lattice structure with an observation purpose.[^1] Its name matches the community of Kirby, an unincorporated place in northern Pike County, Arkansas, on U.S. Route 70, halfway between Daisy and Glenwood.[^2] The map entry once carried place-name tags from a GNIS import (the U.S. Geographic Names Information System), and a later edit removed them.[^1]

No source I read describes the tower today. The map entry was last edited more than two years ago, so it may not show what is there now.[^1] Do not climb any tower you find at the site.
