The White Rock lookout sat in Carter County, Tennessee, at a listed elevation of about 4,094 feet.[^2] The Forest Lookouts site files the tower under the Cherokee National Forest.[^1]

Both pages list the tower as removed.[^1][^2] A Forest Service project covering White Rocks mountain in Carter County, on the Watauga Ranger District, began scoping on October 21, 2024, and in February 2025 the agency approved taking down an existing communications tower there and building a new one.[^3] The project page now lists it as completed.[^3]

No source ties that communications tower to the old lookout, so it is not known whether the new structure stands where the lookout did.[^1][^2][^3] No source describes what is at the site today or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
