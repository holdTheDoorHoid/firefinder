The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list marks Livingston (Loc 2), in Polk County, as gone.[^1] A Texas lookout towers list last revised in January 2011 gives a tower named livingston at 30.73547, -94.90426, the same coordinates the association uses for Loc 2.[^2]

A Forest Lookouts page titled Livingston gives the county as Polk and the agency as the Texas Forest Service.[^3] In November 1930 a group of landowners and the Texas Forest Service signed cooperative agreements covering intensive fire protection on 250,000 acres in Polk, Tyler, Hardin and Liberty counties, including Polk County, where this tower is listed.[^4]

The association also lists a second Livingston tower, Loc 1, as gone.[^1] No source we read describes what remains at the Loc 2 site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^3]
