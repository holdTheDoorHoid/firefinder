Buffalo Ridge was a fire lookout in Amherst County, Virginia, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list records its tower as gone.[^1] The Eastern US Lookouts page for the site names only the county.[^2] A USGS topographic sheet dated 1969 and published in 1971 places the ridge where the James and Tye rivers meet, on the border of Amherst and Nelson counties.[^3]

The state's forestry agency later put up fire watchtowers at important sites across Virginia.[^4] Those towers often sat on privately owned land that owners had leased to the government.[^5] Once the leases ended, most landowners sold the towers for scrap metal, though some were left standing.[^5] The same post puts the peak at around 150 towers statewide and says perhaps ten still stand and can be reached today.[^5]

No source I read says what, if anything, remains at this site today or whether the ground is open to visitors.
