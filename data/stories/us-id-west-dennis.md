West Dennis was a short lookout tower on the St. Joe National Forest, in Benewah County.[^1][^2] Rex's Fire Lookout Page and Firelookout.com both date it to 1931, when it stood 10 feet tall with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2] Rex's page gives its location as the code 43N-3W-9, which in the standard land-survey format means section 9 of township 43 North, range 3 West.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts reports the same 1931 tower, but lists the forest as the Clearwater National Forest.[^3]

The tower is listed as gone.[^3][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts notes that Rey Kresek's book, Fire Lookout Towers of the Northwest, lists it that way but gives no date for its removal.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list also marks it as gone.[^4]

A September 2022 post from Idaho Fire Lookouts describes a brief hike to the site.[^3] On that visit the writers reported eye-hole supports for guide wires, a piece of lumber that was likely from the tower, and a benchmark.[^3]
