Dutchman Peak Lookout sits on the summit of Dutchman Peak in the Rogue River National Forest, near the California–Oregon line, about 13 miles southwest of Ashland.[^1][^2][^3] The 1927 cabin is a D-6 cupola design of conventional wood-frame construction.[^2][^3] Rangers keep watch for fires from a cupola that rises above the cabin roof.[^5]

In the summer of 1927 the Applegate Ranger District put a worker on the peak, while the ownership of the land was still being debated.[^3] According to the nomination, that worker, Alex Schichtl, probably put up the structure with at least one helper.[^3] Schichtl apparently spent each summer there until about 1937, and his supplies came in by pack string along a rough trail.[^3]

A five-person Civilian Conservation Corps crew built a garage and woodshed just below the lookout in 1937.[^3] By then the Ashland/Applegate Loop Road, built in 1935 and 1936, gave vehicles access to the summit.[^3] Robert L. "Slim" Dowell took over lookout duties in 1938 and came back each summer for the duration of World War II.[^3] In 1942 the Forest Service repurposed the CCC garage as a home for Aircraft Warning Service observers, and Dowell and his wife served as spotters that year and in 1943.[^3]

A Forest Service historic preservation team fixed the foundation and put on a new roof in 1999.[^3] The lookout entered the National Historic Lookout Register on August 2, 1991, as US 19 (OR 5), with Barbara Mumblo of the Forest Service as nominator.[^1] Its National Register of Historic Places record is dated December 29, 2000.[^4]

A 2017 Jefferson Public Radio feature said the lookout can be reached by road in summer and sits not far from the Pacific Crest Trail. It also noted that an original Osborne firefinder, a 360-degree map invented in 1911, is still in place.[^5] A firelookout.com page, which is undated, says the lookout is still staffed in summer.[^2] The register says the lookout is a go-to stop for drivers on the Mt. Ashland Loop Road and for Pacific Crest hikers.[^1] Sources give the summit elevation as anywhere from 7,364 to 7,418 feet.[^1][^2][^3][^5]

I did not find a 2025 or 2026 staffing schedule. Before visiting, ask the Applegate Ranger District about the current season and whether the cabin is open to the public.
