Greenwater is a fire tower on the Firetowers of Saskatchewan website, which places it at 52°29′09.0″ N, 103°27′42.0″ W.[^1] The site attributes its information to Maurice Bourassa's work.[^1]

A Big River-area history says Saskatchewan's Department of Natural Resources set up fire towers roughly fifty miles apart across the north of the province.[^2] The same history treats the destructive fires of the early 1900s as a warning of what would follow without some form of control, and says the distance between towers was set so that towermen could keep in radio contact.[^2] It also lists the standard kit for such a tower: a fire finder, a radio set and binoculars.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the Greenwater site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
