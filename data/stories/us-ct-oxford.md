The Forest Lookouts website has a Hunters Mountain (Oxford) entry in New Haven County, but the entry does not mention a tower, so it is unclear whether it refers to this site.[^1] Mapping sites call the structure the Oxford Lookout Tower, and ExpertGPS lists it in Oxford.[^2][^3][^4]

TopoZone gives its coordinates as 41.4120408°N, -73.1295549°W and an approximate elevation of 764 feet (233 m), and places the tower on its Southbury topographic map.[^3] OpenStreetMap gives an elevation of about 748 feet (228 m) and tags the structure as an observation tower.[^2] The two elevations differ by a few metres.[^2][^3]

No source I found describes the site today.
