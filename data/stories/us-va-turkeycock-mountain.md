Turkeycock Mountain is a summit in Virginia's Blue Ridge foothills, and it lies in both Franklin and Henry counties.[^1]

A November 1949 newspaper story, quoted on the Eastern U.S. Lookouts page, reports that a 72-year-old man died in a fire that destroyed a four-room frame cottage.[^2] The clipping also notes that one of the man's sons, a fire-watcher, had gone to work at the fire tower atop Turkey Cock Mountain, not far from the home.[^2]

The sources give different points for the site. The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the tower at 36.82143, -79.70826, while Wikipedia gives the mountain's summit as 36.82917, -79.68694.[^3][^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list shows the tower as gone.[^3]

Part of the mountain lies within the Turkeycock Wildlife Management Area, and Wikipedia says that area is open to the public.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the tower on the summit, or whether the tower site lies inside the public area.[^3][^1]
