Archer is a fire lookout in Alachua County, Florida. The Eastern US Lookouts index gives its location as the survey-style code 11S-18E-9.[^1]

The US Geological Survey's water-data website has a site named "Archer Fire Tower," placed at about 29.544 north and 82.522 west.[^2] A historic-map site for the Archer area names an "Archer Forestry Site Tower" among local legacy sites.[^3] The sources do not say whether these two names refer to the same structure.

Archer is on neither lookout register. The National Historic Lookout Register names 31 lookouts in Florida, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists 6 former lookout sites in the state.[^4][^5]

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
