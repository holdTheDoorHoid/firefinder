The Brunet Lookout Tower was in Sawyer County.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it at 45.94618, -90.77030 and marks it as gone.[^2]

A Sawyer County Record article reported that the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources planned to take 72 of its fire towers out of service in 2016, including five in Sawyer County.[^3] The article named the county's towers as Hayward, one in the Town of Sand Lake, Pipestone, Swayne and Connors Lake.[^3] Brunet was not among them.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]
