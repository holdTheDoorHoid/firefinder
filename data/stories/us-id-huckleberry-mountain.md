Huckleberry Mountain stands about 14 miles south-southwest of Sandpoint in Bonner County, Idaho, at 4,356 feet.[^1] A May 1925 Northern Idaho News item reported that a new lookout was being built about three miles northwest of Careywood.[^3] The two lookout-history pages describe the 1925 structures as a cabin and barn.[^1][^2]

A January 1927 fire wardens' report said one lookout on the mountain covered the Careywood, Granite and Cocolalla district.[^3] The same report noted four miles of telephone line running from the lookout to a utility's main line.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts says it does not yet have a documented staffing history for it.[^2]

Both history pages list the lookout as abandoned.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho index marks the site as gone.[^4]

No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the public can visit it.
