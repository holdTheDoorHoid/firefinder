Silverdale was a fire lookout tower in Hamilton County, Tennessee, run by the Tennessee Division of Forestry.[^1][^2] Tennessee Landforms gives it as tower 14 in district 3, using the state's 1976 numbering.[^1][^2]

The Division as a whole built 208 fire lookout towers between about 1930 and 1970, according to the national register form for the state's towers.[^3] Silverdale does not appear on that form's list of towers still standing on March 26, 2014.[^3]

Tennessee Landforms also lists an NGS datasheet identifier, FC1846, beside the tower, and says these identifiers can be looked up with an NGS datasheet tool.[^1]

Both Tennessee Landforms and Forest Lookouts mark the tower removed.[^1][^2]

No source describes what remains at the Silverdale site, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2] Find a Grave lists a Chattanooga cemetery, Cofer Cemetery, under the alternate name Silverdale Lookout Tower Cemetery.[^4] That listing does not say whether the cemetery sits on the old tower site, so treat the name as a lead, not a confirmed location.
