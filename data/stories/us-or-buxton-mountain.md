Buxton Mountain sits in Washington County, Oregon, and the Oregon Department of Forestry administers the site.[^1]

A 1939 camp program planned a mile-long road to the summit and a 100-foot lookout tower with a cabin.[^2] The tower went up in 1941 as a 100-foot wooden L-6 tower.[^1][^3]

Records show the lookout was activated on March 9, 1942, under the Portland Filter Center.[^2] A 1942 inspection report said that, because of logging and fire hazard, the warden would keep both observers and hire a fire guard, and that all three staff would also track aircraft, not only watch for fires.[^2]

In October 1963 lookout Marcia Yeater spotted a smoke near Manning.[^2] A Forest Log account says the person who came on the radio admitted burning trash beside a house.[^2]

Work on a new 99-foot tower began in 1962.[^2] By 1963 a timber tower with a 7x7 cab was finished, with living quarters in a concrete block ground cabin.[^2] Rex's page gives the cab as 9x9 feet instead.[^3]

One 1940 line in the Oregon Lookouts record gives the tower as 113 feet, while the other records give 100.[^2][^1][^3]

The register and Rex's page say the tower was staffed into the 1970s and removed in 1998.[^1][^3] The register says only a foundation piece remains.[^1]

The register's photo caption describes an area between two fences on site.[^1] No source we found says whether visitors may enter it or how to reach it.[^1]
