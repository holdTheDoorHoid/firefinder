Cold Spring Mountain, in Mendocino County, carries a closed fire lookout east of Point Arena, with views over the Coast Range.[^4][^1] The lookout has a CDF 809R cab on an enclosed 29-foot steel tower.[^1] The NHLR says the cabin follows a post-1950 design, with angled windows.[^1]

The station was built in 1965 and is administered by the California Department of Forestry.[^1][^3] Cal Fire nominated it, and the register listed it on January 12, 2002, as US 397, CA 48.[^1]

The lookout was last staffed in 1990, according to the NHLR.[^1] A Forest Lookouts history page records a 1990 note that budget cuts would keep it unstaffed that fire season.[^2]

The NHLR says the station is closed and describes its condition as good.[^1] A Peakbagging.com photo captioned as taken in 2006 says the lookout appears to be in good condition.[^3] A visitor's trip report on Bay Area Peaks says the old lookout is securely boarded up.[^4]

The sources give different elevations. The NHLR lists 2,645 feet, while Peakbagging.com lists 2,739 feet.[^1][^3] None of the sources read describes a notable fire spotted from this lookout, and none describes a public visiting arrangement, so treat the tower and cab as off limits.
