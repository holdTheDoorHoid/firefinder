Brushy Lookout stands on Cibollito Peak in Cibola County, New Mexico.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register places it on the Isleta-Acoma Indian Reservation.[^1] Other lists give the name as Brushy (Cebollita).[^2]

The Register describes a 50-foot open-framed steel tower made by Aermotor, capped with a 7-by-7-foot observation cab; the site also holds a residence cabin.[^1] The Register says the construction date and the current use are unknown, and the sources read do not supply either.[^1]

The Register names the Isleta-Acoma Indian Agency as the administrator, and a Forest Lookouts list gives the same name.[^1][^3] The Register accepted the lookout on July 7, 2022, as US 1649, NM 48.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's New Mexico list shows the tower as standing.[^2]

The sources read say nothing about who staffed the tower, what fires it watched, or whether it was ever abandoned. Nothing found describes a nomination or a rebuild either.

Today the tower is listed as standing.[^2] Because the Register places it on reservation land, access may require permission, and the sources give no visitor rules.[^1] Before going anywhere near it, ask the administering office named in the Register, and do not climb a tower that is closed or posted.[^1]
