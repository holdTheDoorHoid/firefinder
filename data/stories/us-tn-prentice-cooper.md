The Prentice Cooper Fire Tower stands in Marion County, Tennessee.[^1] The register lists the Tennessee Department of Agriculture as its administrator and dates the tower to the 1930s, when it was meant to guard the new Prentice Cooper State Forest.[^1]

The register gives only the decade, so no exact build year is recorded. The state began buying land for the forest in 1938 and dedicated it in 1945, which leaves the date open.[^3]

A photograph captioned 1948 shows a state fire tower in the forest.[^3] A Tennessee Division of Forestry weather station on Walden's Ridge, at 1,920 feet, describes a 100-foot fire tower just west of its equipment, but it does not name the tower, so we cannot confirm it is this one.[^4] The Tennessee list marks the tower as standing.[^2] The register says it was registered on August 28, 2017.[^1]

We found no record of who staffed the tower or which fires it watched.

Today the forest has more than 30 miles of hiking trails, but it is often closed for deer and turkey hunts, so check before you go.[^3] The Tennessee list advises against climbing these towers, because steps are often removed or rotten.[^2]
