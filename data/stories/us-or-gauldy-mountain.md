Gauldy Mountain stands in Tillamook County, about 21 miles south of Tillamook.[^1][^2] A pole platform tower was built there in 1933.[^1][^2][^3]

Panorama photographs were taken from the site in 1936.[^1][^3] In 1937 it was replaced by a 30-foot pole tower with a live-in cab.[^1][^2][^3]

The register lists NW Oregon FPA as the administrator, while the lookout history names the Oregon Department of Forestry.[^1][^3] Those two names may describe different periods; our sources do not settle it.

The register and the lookout history say the tower was removed in 1948.[^1][^3] A firelookout.com listing says it was destroyed that year.[^2]

The register shows a 2009 photo of the site but gives no description of what it shows.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains there today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
