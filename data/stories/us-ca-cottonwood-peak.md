Cottonwood Peak sits west of Henley, in the Klamath National Forest.[^1] In August 1912 Forest Supervisor W. B. Rider and Ranger W. Gott inspected the peak as a possible lookout site, with a plan to tie it into the telephone line, the Ashland Tidings reported.[^1] No source describes the site today, including whether any structure remains or whether the summit is open to the public.
