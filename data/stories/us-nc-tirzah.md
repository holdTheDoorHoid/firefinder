Tirzah is a lookout tower in Person County, North Carolina.[^1] A list of the state's lookout towers, whose map was last revised in June 2021, gives the name Tirzah and places it in Person County.[^1] OpenStreetMap, the open map database, has a tower under the name Glenns Mountain Fire Tower and tags it as an observation tower.[^2] Our records treat the two names as one site, but neither source names the other, so that link is ours and not one a source confirms.[^1][^2]

We found no build date for this tower, and no record of who built it or what design it is. We also found no record of who staffed it or which fires it watched.

Neither lookout register we checked lists it. The National Historic Lookout Register lists 31 lookouts in North Carolina, and Tirzah is not among the names on its page.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists only two former sites in the state, Big Stamp and Frye Knob.[^4]

The sources therefore do not show how the tower is kept, or whether it still stands as the map shows it. A map entry is not an inspection. We found no statement about public access. Anyone curious should check with the landowner or the local authorities before going to the site, and should not climb a tower unless it is open to the public.
