Ball Butte is in Latah County, Idaho.[^3] The sources spell its name two ways. Firelookout.com and the Idaho lookout list say Ball Butte.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts calls it Bald Butte.[^2]

The lookout was a crow's nest.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com dates it to the 1930s and names the St. Joe National Forest as the agency.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the same 1930s date for a crow's nest.[^2] Forest Lookouts adds that the nest sat in a tree.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts has not yet documented who staffed the lookout.[^2]

Three sources list it as abandoned.[^1][^2][^3]

The pages describe its history and status, but none describes the site today, so we cannot say whether anything remains or whether the spot is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^4]
