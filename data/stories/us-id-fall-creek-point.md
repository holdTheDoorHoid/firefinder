Fall Creek Point Lookout was a two-story log cabin with a cupola in the Nez Perce National Forest, about 11 miles west of Elk City in Idaho County.[^1][^4]

Sources date the cabin to about 1930.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Three of them describe it as a two-story log structure with a cupola.[^1][^2][^3] The site stands at 4,929 feet.[^1][^3]

The cabin was abandoned in 1950.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Firelookout.com and the Idaho Fire Lookouts page both list it as gone.[^1][^3] The Forest Service list gives the same 1950 date.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page tags the site as drive-up, which is the only access detail the sources give.[^3] The Forest Service lists it among the Nez Perce National Forest lookouts.[^4]
