The Harvard fire tower is a steel lookout in Worcester County that overlooks the Harvard Forest.[^1] The register gives 1969 as the year the current tower was built, and it describes a 70-foot steel structure with a 10-by-10-foot cab.[^1] The current tower replaced a 68-foot tower built in 1921.[^1]

The register lists the Mass. Bureau of Forest Fire Control as administrator, with District 8 as a cooperator.[^1] Since 1911 the Bureau has helped cities and towns prevent, spot and put out forest fires, and it now works under the name DCR Bureau of Forest Fire Control and Forestry.[^2]

Statewide, a network of 42 operating fire towers is staffed by trained observers. They use alidade tables, binoculars and topographic maps to pinpoint where a fire is.[^3]

The register describes the tower in the present tense.[^1] Its detail fields do not include a current staffing record or public access arrangement.[^1] Until the state confirms access, please don't climb it.
