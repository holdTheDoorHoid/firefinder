Pond Ridge is a former fire tower site in Clay County, West Virginia. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it as US 1665, WV 56, with a registration date of January 26, 2017.[^1] Brian M. Powell nominated the site.[^1] The register gives its coordinates as 38.480278 north and 80.882778 west, and its elevation as 1,807 feet, or 551 meters.[^1] It names the West Virginia Division of Forestry as the administering agency.[^1] In the register's numbering, the next entry is the Stop Knob Tower Site (US 1666).[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list marks the Pond Ridge tower as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]
