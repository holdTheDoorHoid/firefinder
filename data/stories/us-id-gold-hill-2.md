The Gold Hill lookout in Idaho County was a 30-foot L-4 tower built in 1931 on the Clearwater National Forest.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5] It stood about five miles east of the Lochsa Historic Ranger Station, at an elevation of 6,449 feet.[^4][^2]

A 2018 Forest Service post shares a 1938 photograph of Wayne Tautfest inside the lookout.[^4]

Sources disagree on how the tower ended in 1954. Firelookout.com, Idaho Fire Lookouts and a 2016 Forest Service-hosted list all say it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2][^3] The 2018 post says the tower was removed in 1954 instead.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, and none says whether it is open to the public. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^2]
