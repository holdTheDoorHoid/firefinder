Cherry Hill was a lookout in Nevada County, California, on the Tahoe National Forest.[^1]

According to the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, the lookout went up in 1933 as a BC-301 cabin, a California Region 5 plan, on a 16-foot enclosed timber tower.[^1] The register gives the site's elevation as 5,050 feet (1,539 meters), and its coordinates as N 39.417046° W 120.797783°.[^1]

Brad Eells nominated the site. The register recorded it on April 1, 2020, under the numbers US 1958 and CA 112.[^1] The register's photograph of the lookout is credited to the Mark Swift Collection.[^1]

The register lists the removal date as unknown.[^1] No source we read says what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.[^1]

[^1]: Cherry Hill Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ca/cherry-hill-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
