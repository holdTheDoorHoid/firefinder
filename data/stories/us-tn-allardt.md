A December 2015 blog post places the Allardt fire tower along State Route 296 in central Fentress County, Tennessee.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site files it under the Tennessee Division of Forestry, in the division's District 4.[^2] The Tennessee Landforms tower table gives its elevation as 1,697 feet and numbers it 4-4. Those numbers are district and tower numbers taken from a 1976 list.[^3]

The sources do not say who built the tower, when it went up, or how tall it is. The only dated status record is a multiple property survey document that lists Allardt among Tennessee's surviving fire towers as of March 26, 2014.[^4] The blog post is newer but does not describe the tower's condition, and none of the sources says whether it can be climbed. Check with the Tennessee Division of Forestry before going to see it, and do not climb it.
