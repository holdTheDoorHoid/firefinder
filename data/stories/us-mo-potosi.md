The Central US Lookouts guide lists a Potosi lookout in Washington County, Missouri.[^1] The guide names the Missouri Conservation Commission as its agency, and quotes the annual report of the Missouri Department of Conservation for fiscal 1949–50, which records that the department obtained 80 acres for a lookout.[^1] The department owned 15 fire towers in 1941 and operated 102 by 1966.[^2]

No source I could reach describes the site today, so it is not clear whether a tower still stands on that land, or whether the public can visit.

[^1]: Potosi, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/potosi.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Shooting the Smoke, Missouri Department of Conservation, https://mdc.mo.gov/magazines/missouri-conservationist/2022-07/shooting-smoke (accessed 2026-10-21).
