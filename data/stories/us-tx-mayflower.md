Mayflower is an unincorporated community in Newton County, Texas, where State Highway 87 meets Texas Recreational Road 255.[^3] A tower listed as Mayflower appears on the Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Texas lookouts, which gives its county, its coordinates and the word "Standing."[^1] The list gives one line for the tower. It says nothing about when the tower was built, who built it, how tall it is or what it looked like, and we found no other source that does.[^1]

The place has older names too. Settlers called the land "The Survey," and the post office that opened there in 1903 was first named Surveyville before it was renamed Mayflower.[^4] A historical marker near Mattox Cemetery says the burial ground "later served the Survey community, also known as Mayflower."[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists five registered lookouts in Texas, and Mayflower is not one of them.[^5] We found no record of who staffed the tower or of any fire it watched.[^1]

We cannot say what the tower looks like today. The Forest Fire Lookout Association list is undated, and we found no recent photo or statement of its condition, so its standing is unconfirmed.[^1] We also found no information about public access. Anyone curious about it should ask the landowner first, and should not climb a tower that is closed, private or unmarked.
