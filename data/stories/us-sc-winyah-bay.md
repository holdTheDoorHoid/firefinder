The Winyah Bay lookout is located along U.S. Route 17 south of Georgetown, South Carolina, in Georgetown County.[^1] The South Carolina Forestry Commission put up a 100-foot Aermotor tower there in 1937.[^1]

A Forest Lookouts page says a Civilian Conservation Corps group repainted the living quarters at the tower site in 1938 and 1939, citing a state forestry report.[^2]

We did not find any account naming the people who staffed the tower or any fire it watched. The register calls the tower presently inactive.[^1] It was added to the register in April 2017 and uses a photo dated January 2014.[^1]

We found no report after the 2017 registration on whether the tower still stands. The register places it along the highway.[^1] It does not describe any visit or climbing access, and we would not climb it.[^1]

[^1]: Winyah Bay Lookout, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/sc/winyah-bay-lookout/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Winyah Bay, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/winyah-bay.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
