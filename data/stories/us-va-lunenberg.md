The Lunenberg Lookout is a 110-foot Aermotor tower in Lunenburg County, Virginia.[^1][^3] The Register says it was built in the 1930s but gives no exact year.[^1] The Register spells the county "Lunenberg," while Wikidata uses "Lunenburg."[^1][^3]

A 2015 chapter report from the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia group lists a "Lunenberg Tower" as private. The report says towers not yet on the Register would be submitted once their owners and history had been looked into.[^2] The Register added this tower on January 23, 2017.[^1]

The association's Virginia list shows the tower as standing.[^4] The Register says the owner asked that the coordinates stay unlisted, so this account gives no location beyond the county.[^1] The sources read do not describe staffing, fires, or public access. Because the owner is private, anyone interested should ask first and should not climb the tower.
