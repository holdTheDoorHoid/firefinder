De Kalb sits in Bowie County, Texas, within the Texarkana metropolitan statistical area.[^1] The 2020 United States census counted 1,527 residents.[^1] Central US Lookouts records a Texas Forest Service lookout at Dekalb under Bowie County.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the tower at 33.51394 north, 94.61701 west and lists it as gone.[^3] No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.
