Nopone was a fire lookout tower north of Gainesville, in Hall County, Georgia.[^1][^2] A list of Georgia lookout towers gives its elevation as 1,480 feet.[^2]

In December 1955 a member of the Hall County Forest Fire Protective Association, Mary Etta Evans, went to her station at the Nopone tower.[^1] She became ill and collapsed there.[^1] She stayed up on the tower for several hours in freezing weather before she was rescued.[^1]

The rescue was difficult.[^1] Strong winds and icy ladder steps made it hard to reach her. Gainesville firefighters reached her, and she was brought down in a Davis rescue harness with the help of tie lines.[^1]

The account comes from an Associated Press report that The Macon News printed.[^1]

No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]
