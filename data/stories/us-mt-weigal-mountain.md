Weigal Mountain is a former lookout site in the Kootenai National Forest, in Lincoln County, Montana, about 25 miles east of Libby.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 5,765 feet.[^1]

Firelookout.com dates the tower to 1938 and describes it as a 50-foot pole L-4 tower. It also says the lookout was never manned.[^1]

A memoir by Forest Service employee Leon L. Lake implies that the Weigal Mtn. Lookout had a telephone line. Lake could not get a call through the lookout because lightning had damaged its phone, so he rode back to Boundary Station.[^4] The same account places a one-acre fire at the head of Cripple Horse Creek that the memoir associates with the Weigal Mountain lookout.[^4] The memoir places this episode within its account of a bad fire season in July 1940.[^4] It does not say who worked the lookout, so it neither confirms nor settles firelookout.com's statement that the lookout was never manned.[^1][^4]

Firelookout.com says the tower was destroyed in 1960.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
