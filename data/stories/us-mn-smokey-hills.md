The Smoky Hills lookout in Becker County is a windmill tower, erected in 1926.[^1] A photo caption on Wikimedia Commons ranks it among the older lookouts still standing in Minnesota.[^2]

Wikidata places the tower in Smoky Hills State Forest, at an elevation of 1,647 feet.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list spells the name "Smokey Hills," while the Central page, the Commons file and Wikidata follow the state forest's spelling, "Smoky Hills."[^1][^2][^3][^4]

The sources I reached do not say who built the tower, why it was put up in 1926, or what the term "windmill tower" describes in this case. The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^3]

No source I reached describes current access, climbing rules, or staffing. Smoky Hills is a state forest, so check with its managing agency before planning a visit, and stay off the tower if it is posted as closed.[^4]
