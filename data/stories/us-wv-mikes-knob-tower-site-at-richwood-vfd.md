The Mikes Knob lookout was built in 1941 on Mikes Knob, in West Virginia.[^1][^4] A Forest Lookouts history credits the Civilian Conservation Corps with the labor.[^2] The U.S. Forest Service administered the site, and the Register gives its elevation as 4,243 feet, or 1,293 meters.[^1]

The Register's photo captions include a 1946 image of a fire finder in use at the site.[^1] We found no staffing history or notable fires in the sources we read.

The Register says the tower has been moved to the Richwood Volunteer Fire Department, and a February 2014 photo caption shows its top atop the Richwood VFD.[^1] The blog says the move was made for preservation.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a standing tower at Richwood VFD, marked as relocated from Mikes Knob.[^3]

The sources disagree on the county. The Register and the blog say Pocahontas, while the Forest Lookouts page and the FFLA list say Greenbrier.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Today the blog says the tower is not open to the public.[^4] Because it stands at a fire station, ask the department before going near it.
