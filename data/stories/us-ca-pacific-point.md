Pacific Point is a lookout site on Mendocino National Forest land.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it in Colusa and Lake counties, as a site of the type it calls Ground, and marks it gone.[^1][^2] Its coordinates on that list are 39.21389 and -122.59731.[^1] Forest Lookouts lists the site in Lake County.[^3] Its peakbagging table places it on the Hough Springs topographic map and labels it an LO site.[^4]

The table's key says LO means the structure is gone and little or nothing may remain, or that the site's condition is not known.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the site is open to visitors.
