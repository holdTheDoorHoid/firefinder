The Eastern US Forest Lookouts site lists a lookout called Blichton, in Marion County, Florida, and gives its map position.[^1]

The community takes its name from Dr. Simeon Hardee Blitch, a doctor and dentist who later represented Marion County in the state Senate.[^2] It grew up around a crossing of two wagon trails, which are now State Highways 326 and 27.[^2] Its post office, which used the spelling Blichton, ran from 1888 until 1917.[^2] The community is spelled Blitchton today.[^2]

No source found describes what stands at the lookout site now, or whether the public may visit it.
