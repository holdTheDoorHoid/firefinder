Wildcat Mountain sits in the Willamette National Forest in Linn County, about 12 miles north-northeast of McKenzie Bridge.[^1][^2] In 1934 a 14-by-14 L-4 lookout house went up there, and the Oregon lookouts site credits three men from the Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC) with building it and staffing it.[^3]

In 1939 Eugene P. Smith was the lookout fireman on the mountain.[^3] In July 1956 Richard Weber was up at the lookout with his brother Leland, who was the lookout attendant that year.[^3]

The cab came down in 1965. The register and the Oregon lookouts site say it was removed, while firelookout.com says it was destroyed.[^1][^2][^3] The site went onto the Former Fire Lookout Sites register on May 16, 2011, on the nomination of Ray Kresek.[^1]

The register tells the history of the cab and says nothing about the site as it stands now.[^1] None of the sources I read says whether anything remains on the mountain, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
