Diamond Point rises in the Okanogan National Forest, 29 miles north-northwest of Winthrop, at 7,916 feet.[^1][^2] In December 1930 materials for a 14 x 14 foot L-4 cab were carried in along a 12-mile trail.[^3][^4] Most sources call it an L-4 cab, but a Washington Lookouts page calls it an Aladdin lookout house, and the sources do not reconcile the two names.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

A July 1931 Wenatchee newspaper item, quoted by Washington Lookouts, reported that three new lookouts had gone out the week before, and that R.G. Dykstra of Omak had gone to Diamond Peak.[^3] The page does not say whether that name refers to this site.[^3]

Panorama photos were taken from the site on September 17, 1934.[^3] The register credits them to the National Archives Pacific Northwest Region.[^1][^3]

WillhiteWeb quotes a lookout book that tells of Howard Culp, a lookout here whose date is not given.[^4] He had grounded his telephone once a storm was closing in, then, after what seemed a safe interval, switched it back on, and a bolt struck.[^4] He regained consciousness about two hours later, as a trail crew came up.[^4] The Winthrop Ranger Station asked the crew to check on him after he went quiet.[^4]

The register and FireLookout.com both say the cab was destroyed in 1952.[^1][^2] A WillhiteWeb photo is captioned 1946, so the building stood at least that long.[^4]

WillhiteWeb says trails still reach the site, though recent fires have cut the final mile to a faint path.[^4] Its photos show an outhouse seat, a double chair, and nails, glass and hinges, and several views are captioned 2016.[^4] No source describes a standing structure today.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

The route starts at Highway 20 west of Winthrop, up the West Chewuch River Road.[^4] The guide then follows Eightmile Creek Road to the Billy Goat Trailhead, and it says an ordinary car should manage that drive.[^4] From there the hike is listed at 12 miles with more than 4,600 feet of climbing, over several passes and then cross-country up the ridge.[^4] No source mentions a closure.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
