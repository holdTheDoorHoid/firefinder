The Skookum Prairie lookout was an L-5 cab on a southwest slope in the Umpqua National Forest, Douglas County, Oregon.[^1][^2][^3] It stood about two and a half miles west-southwest of the Garwood Lookout, at an elevation of 5,855 feet.[^1][^2]

The register and firelookout.com both date the cab to 1933.[^1][^2] Panorama photographs were taken there on August 2, 1933.[^3] That September, a Roseburg newspaper reported that the CCC would build a 10-by-10 lookout before the snow flew, the standard size for a secondary lookout.[^3] A stream about a quarter mile east of the lookout served as its water source.[^3]

The cab's end is described two ways. The register says it was removed about 1958, while firelookout.com says it was destroyed about that time.[^1][^2] The sources differ on how it ended, but both place the end around 1958.[^1][^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the site as US 907, OR 274, and registered it on March 29, 2011.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, or whether the land is open to visitors.
