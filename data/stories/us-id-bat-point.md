Bat Point sits on the Nez Perce National Forest, about 20 miles southeast of Elk City in Idaho County. The site stands at 5,984 feet.[^1]

In 1934 a lookout with an L-6 cab went up on a 60-foot pole tower, set about a quarter mile southeast of the highest point.[^1][^2][^3]

The tower was destroyed in 1957.[^1][^2][^3] A statewide lookout listing now marks it as gone.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^2] No source we found describes what, if anything, remains on the ground today.

A National Geodetic Survey station description from 1946, reproduced on the Forest Lookouts page, places a bronze disk stamped BAT 1945 in a boulder on a timbered ridge below the tower. Its trail directions run about four miles from the end of truck travel to a fork, then about a mile farther on the left fork to reach the lookout.[^3]
