Dry Meadow Tower Site was a lookout in Pike County, Pennsylvania, at an elevation the register gives as 1,420 feet (433 m).[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists a steel tower of code "M-M," erected in 1934 and taken down in 1975.[^1] The Pennsylvania statewide list, last updated in December 2008, gives the same build and removal years for a steel M-M tower that is no longer standing.[^2]

Because both sources give the same dates, 1934 and 1975 are the best-supported facts we have about this tower.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the tower at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.

[^1]: Dry Meadow Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites register, Forest Fire Lookout Association, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/pa/dry-meadow-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Pennsylvania Fire Lookouts, State Listing Page, firelookout.ipage.com, https://firelookout.ipage.com/lo-pennsylvania.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
