Lone Pine Fire Tower stands in the Wayne National Forest in Lawrence County, at about 294 meters (964 feet) of elevation.[^1][^4] The U.S. Forest Service administers the site, and the National Historic Lookout Register added it on September 3, 2014, as US 1025, OH 14.[^1]

I could not find a construction date, a design, or a builder for this tower. The Lawrence County entry on the Forest Lookouts site has only the name, the county, and the register listing, with no history.[^2] So its early story is not recorded in the sources I could reach.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Lone Pine as standing.[^4] A 2015 report from the association's Ohio chapter said there is no firm count of the former Ohio towers still standing, so the list's standing entry is worth confirming before anyone plans a visit.[^3] The same list labels the Wayne National Forest headquarters tower as relocated from Snake Ridge.[^4] The 2015 report noted that one Forest Service tower had been restored and was standing at that headquarters in Nelsonville, not on its original site.[^3]

The sources do not say whether the public can reach or climb the tower today, so anyone planning a visit should contact the Wayne National Forest first.
