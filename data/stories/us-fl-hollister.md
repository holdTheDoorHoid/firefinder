The Hollister Fire Tower is on State Road 20, on the east side of Hollister. The community is unincorporated and lies in Putnam County, Florida.[^1][^3] The register gives its elevation as 85 feet (26 meters).[^1]

The sources I could reach do not say when the tower was built or who designed it.[^1][^2] The register gives no construction year, and the regional lookout list for Hollister gives only the tower's name and county.[^1][^2] The state's lookout network was already growing in the 1930s, when a history of the Florida Division of Forestry counted 22 lookout towers by 1932.[^4] I found nothing that links this tower to that early group.[^1][^4]

I found no record of who staffed the tower or which fires it watched.[^1][^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register added the tower on July 24, 2016, as US 1139, FL 22.[^1]

Today the register describes the tower in the present tense and names the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services as administrator.[^1] It does not describe the tower's condition or say whether the public can visit or climb it, so check with that agency before any trip.[^1]
