The Peyton Lookout Tower lies east of Peyton, an unincorporated community in Claiborne County, Mississippi.[^2] Peyton's post office ran from 1900 to 1935.[^2] OpenStreetMap, the open map database, records the tower as a lattice observation tower, with its map point about 126 m (413 ft) above sea level.[^1]

No source describes what stands at the site today, and none says whether the site is open to the public. Do not climb or enter the tower without first confirming that it is open to visitors.
