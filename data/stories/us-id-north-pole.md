North Pole is a peak in the Gospel Hump Wilderness, about 17 miles southwest of Elk City, on the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1][^2]

A fire lookout went up on the peak in 1927.[^2] It was a cupola cabin, and one page files it under the D-6 design.[^1][^3] It was abandoned in 1950.[^1][^3][^4] The climbing guide says it was taken out of service that year and destroyed later, but it gives no date for the destruction.[^2]

According to the same guide, little beyond the foundation survives.[^2] A SummitPost write-up says the summit has no register, and that the only things on it are the leftovers of an earlier lookout and a heap of stones.[^5] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the site as Gone.[^6]

The climbing guide describes a hike of 2.5 miles from Wildhorse Campground, with 1,225 feet of elevation gain. The guide also calls the peak easy to reach on foot, though upkeep on the trail is light and it is still in fair shape.[^2]
