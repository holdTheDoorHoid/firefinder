The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower called Blackwater NWR – Gum Island, in Dorchester County, Maryland.[^1] A Gaia GPS listing describes Gum Island as a wetland within the refuge.[^3] The refuge dates to 1933, when the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service says it was set up for migratory birds.[^2]

The association's list marks the Gum Island tower as gone.[^1] The same list has two more Blackwater towers, Little Blackwater and Wolf Pit Pond, and it marks both as gone too.[^1]

A visitor page from Friends of Blackwater NWR has a public-use section that covers the visitor center, the Wildlife Drive and the land and water trails.[^4] No source we read describes Gum Island as open to visitors, so ask refuge staff before going looking for the site. No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the tower there today.
