The Deppe fire tower is in Onslow County, North Carolina, at 34.83694 north and 77.30083 west.[^1] A North Carolina historical marker near Jacksonville describes the Hofmann Forest as an 80,000-acre tract for research, spanning Jones and Onslow counties, and a photograph taken in October 2009 shows the Deppe tower in the background.[^2] The Forest Lookouts listing ties the tower to the Hofmann State Forest and to the state's Division of Forest Resources.[^3]

Sources disagree on when the tower was built. The Forest Lookouts listing quotes a 1938 state report describing a creosoted wood tower of 120 feet (37 m), built by the Civilian Conservation Corps at Camp S-65.[^3] A WCTI reel on Instagram credits the Aermotor Corporation with building the tower in 1949, and notes that the firm also made windmills.[^4] The two dates do not match, and we found nothing that settles the question.

The WCTI reel is undated in our copy, but it features a drone pilot who used a drone to explore the tower in Onslow County.[^4] It also says the site has a memorial to a forest firefighter who died in 1972.[^4] No source we read says whether the public may climb or visit the tower. Ask the state Division of Forest Resources before going, and do not climb the tower without permission.
