Dry Lake Lookout stands on the San Carlos Apache Reservation in Graham County, Arizona, and the San Carlos Agency administers it.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register says the Larson Corporation built the present tower for the agency in 1984.[^1]

The tower is 80 feet of steel, with an 8-by-8-foot metal cab and a catwalk on three sides.[^1] It replaced a 54-foot Aermotor tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab, which the Register says had been in service since 1930.[^1]

The Register lists the San Carlos Agency as its only cooperator and gives no staffing history. I found no source that names anyone who worked the tower or describes a fire it reported.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Arizona list still shows the tower as standing.[^2] The Register recorded the tower on April 1, 2005, as US 633, AZ 57.[^1]

These sources do not say whether visitors may reach the tower, drive to it or climb it. Anyone thinking of a visit should ask the San Carlos Agency first, and should not climb a tower that is closed or private.[^1]
