The Bone Mountain lookout site is in Coos County, Oregon, about 19 miles southeast of Myrtle Point.[^1][^2] The Coos County Fire Patrol association administered the lookout.[^1][^3]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register and firelookout.com both say a tower was built on the site in 1935.[^1][^2] Newspaper items quoted on the Forest Lookouts page describe a lookout there before that date.[^3] In 1930 a patrol man went to the Bone Mountain lookout on Eden Ridge, and in 1931 a Marshfield man was to take charge of it for the summer.[^3] A 1933 item reports that a CCC crew would build a five-mile road to it.[^3] A 1934 item describes a trail to the high point, meant to protect fir and cedar from fire.[^3]

The sources therefore differ on when a lookout first stood there. The 1930 and 1931 items describe one before the 1935 build date, and the 1934 item says a tower was still to come. None of the three says what that earlier lookout was.[^1][^2][^3]

Both the register and firelookout.com date the abandonment to 1942, and the register says the lookout is no longer there.[^1][^2]

The register records the site as US 1807, OR 530, with a registration date of November 11, 2019.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^3]
