The lookout stood at about 3,600 feet in Missoula County, Montana, beside the old Condon Ranger Station.[^1] Firelookout.com describes a 90-foot steel Aermotor tower, built in 1949.[^1] The station itself is older. The Forest Service built its compound in the early 1920s, and the two-story log residence went up in 1923 for $3,330.10.[^2][^3]

The station was a working base for decades. Montana Trail Briefs says it served as the central base for forest rangers, lookout crews, and fire guards for over 40 years, until a new ranger station was built near the highway and airstrip in 1965.[^3] Bigfork Eagle adds that several lookouts and fire guards still used the old site as their principal base after the newer station opened.[^4]

Firelookout.com reports that the tower no longer stood by 1975.[^1] Forest Lookouts (Montana) lists it as "Removed," and the Forest Fire Lookout Association marks it "Tower Gone."[^7][^6] A Forest Service photo from 1956 was taken from the lookout tower, so the tower was standing then.[^4] A correction note from Montana Ranger Stations places the tower east of the truck shed, not north, and dates the truck shed to the summer of 1939.[^5]

The ranger's residence is now a cabin the Forest Service rents year-round. It costs $85 a night, sleeps eight comfortably, and is booked through Recreation.gov.[^2] Montana Trail Briefs says most of the outbuildings from the 1920s compound still stand.[^3] The Forest Service notes that not all of the original buildings remain.[^2]

To reach it, turn east off Highway 83 between mileposts 48 and 49 onto Condon Loop Road 124, then drive about 2.5 miles.[^2] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains of the tower on the site today.
