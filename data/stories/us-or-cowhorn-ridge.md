Cowhorn Ridge is a site in Oregon's Umpqua National Forest. The Forest Lookouts directory, a listing of Oregon lookout sites, files it under Douglas County and gives its location as survey code 30S-2E-17.[^1]

The spot was readied for a lookout, but no structure was put up there.[^1] The directory describes the spot as a patrol lookout point, and it records no other use.[^1]

Both names contain Cowhorn, so do not confuse it with Little Cowhorn Mountain, a separate lookout on the Willamette National Forest. Oregon's Standing Lookouts list gives that tower a build year of 1960 and an abandoned status.[^2]

Nothing is recorded as standing on the site, and the directory gives no details on access or visits by the public.[^1]
