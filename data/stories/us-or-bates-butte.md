Bates Butte is a small hill in Deschutes County, Oregon, inside the Deschutes National Forest.[^1] By July 1915 Ranger Oney had built a spur telephone line about a third of a mile long, from the Crane Prairie line up to the summit. The hill, which lies near the Big River Station, served him as a secondary lookout point, and the first call came over the line on July 7.[^1]

A May 20, 1924 entry records that William Beaton had been shifted for good from a temporary post on the butte to Paulina Peak.[^1] An August 5, 1927 entry reports that extreme fire danger led to an intermediate lookout being posted on the butte.[^1]

No source describes the hill today, including whether anything remains on it or whether visitors can reach the top.
