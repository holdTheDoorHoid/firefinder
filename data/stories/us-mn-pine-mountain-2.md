The Pine Mountain Lookout Tower is listed in Deerfield Township, Cass County, about four miles west of Backus.[^1] A map database gives the feature type as a tower and gives the spot an elevation of 1,608 feet (490 metres).[^1]

The Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list marks Pine Mountain, in Cass County, as gone.[^2] A map reprint site describes a 2010 USGS map of Backus as naming the Pine Mountain Lookout Tower, alongside Evergreen Cemetery and the Paul Bunyan trail.[^3] That shows the name was on a map in 2010. It does not show whether a structure stood at the spot.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
