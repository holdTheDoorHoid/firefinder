Lookout Mountain is a former lookout site in Wallowa County, Oregon, inside the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest.[^1][^2]

A 1932 timeline on Weebly says a tower with a cabin was erected that year, and a 1954 forest history credits Charles V. Marks with helping put up the cabin and tower in 1932.[^1] The register gives a later sequence: a 14-by-16-foot cabin in 1933, then a 50-foot pole tower with a cab in 1934.[^2][^3] The register dates the 82-foot treated-timber tower that followed to 1964.[^2][^3] A 1963 annual report, quoted on Weebly, mentions one new tower on the mountain, so the sources disagree on that year.[^1]

The 1954 history says Marks kept the lookout post through the close of the 1952 season and gives his total time there as 20 years.[^1] firelookout.com names a Charlie W. Marks for several seasons between 1936 and 1951, which gives a different middle initial, and lists other lookout staff from 1955 to 1967.[^3]

On August 24, 1967, the Record-Courier reported a 1,100-acre blaze in the Imnaha-Snake country that began when it destroyed the Lookout Mountain tower.[^1] The paper said heat from the cabin blaze set the tower alight, and that the tower dated from 1964.[^1]

The register and firelookout.com both say the 82-foot tower was destroyed in a 1967 forest fire and never replaced.[^2][^3] Right after the fire, a forest official said the tower's legs were still standing, with no sign of the cabin.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
