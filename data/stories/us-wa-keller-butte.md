Keller Butte sits on Colville Indian Reservation land in Ferry County, Washington.[^1][^4] The register gives its elevation as 4,800 feet, the trail guide gives 4,811 feet, and a fire lookout page gives 4,831 feet.[^1][^2][^4]

Keller Butte first hosted a camp lookout in 1932.[^1][^2] A July 1932 newspaper item said a man would soon be sent to the new station.[^3] In 1933 the first Civilian Conservation Corps camp for the district opened with 75 men, and one crew was to build a road from the summit to the new lookout tower.[^1][^3]

In 1935 the CCC built an 80-foot, three-legged wooden tower with a 7-by-7 cab and an L-4 ground cabin for living quarters.[^1][^2][^3] An August 1935 newspaper had called it a 90-foot tower, but the register and a 1960 inspection both give 80 feet.[^1][^3]

In 1943 the Colville Examiner reported that many teachers were working as summer fire lookouts, and Miss Josephine Volger, a teacher at the Leadpoint school, was stationed on Keller Butte.[^1][^3] A November 1960 inspection found the old tower and its shelter unsound and unsafe, and it proposed a 65-foot steel tower with living space at the top.[^3] The current lookout, a 67-foot steel live-in tower, went up in 1964 and is staffed each summer.[^1][^2][^4]

A plaque at the summit honors Ramona Peone, who spent more than 40 years as a fire lookout, including at Johnny George.[^1][^2] In 2021 the Chuweah Creek fire started from lightning on July 12.[^1] The fire ran over the north shore of Buffalo Lake and across the Keller Butte Road north of the lookout, and it was eventually contained at more than 36,000 acres.[^1] The lookout survived unharmed, the trail guide says.[^2]

Today the site is on tribal land, and non-tribal visitors need a Colville recreation permit, which the trail guide gives as $80 a year.[^2] The guide suggests reaching Keller from the south shore of Buffalo Lake along BIA 21, then over to Keller Butte Road, and it warns that wet weather can leave the clay roads impassable.[^2] The 2021 fire may also affect access from the north side.[^2]
