Cross Trails was a lookout in the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1] The Forest Service's 2010 list of Nez Perce lookouts places it about 20 miles south-southwest of Elk City, Idaho, in Township 25 North, Range 6 East, Section 26.[^2] Firelookout.com gives 27 miles, in the same direction.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list gives its position as 45.4761 north and 115.69144 west, and lists its type as unknown.[^3]

Two lookout sites list its history as unknown.[^1][^4] The Forest Service list gives its status as abandoned, and Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it as abandoned in its history.[^2][^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list is the only one we read that marks it gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
