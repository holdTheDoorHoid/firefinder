Stafford Lookout Site sits in the Wenatchee National Forest, in Kittitas County, Washington, roughly 12 miles north-northeast of Cle Elum.[^1][^2]

A Leavenworth newspaper article names B. D. March as the Stafford Creek lookout-fireman in 1933.[^2][^3] WillhiteWeb says the place was a camp at that time unless an earlier structure existed.[^3] Ground-level panoramas followed in 1934.[^3] The site's L-4 cab went up in 1935.[^1][^2][^3] In 1938 the Forest Service was working on a garage and woodshed combination there, WillhiteWeb reports.[^3]

The register says the lookout was abandoned in 1966 and no longer stands.[^1] FireLookout.com also lists it as gone.[^2] WillhiteWeb dates the abandonment to around 1966.[^3] WillhiteWeb also reports that some inventories wrongly name the site Standup Ridge Lookout.[^3]

Directions run from the North Fork Teanaway Road to the Stafford Creek Road (9703).[^3] WillhiteWeb says a high-clearance vehicle is best and that the road can be walked, and it gives the hike as one mile one-way with about 700 feet of gain.[^3] A 2020 hiker describes an old road of roughly a mile leading to the old lookout site.[^4] WillhiteWeb's photos from October 2, 2021 show footings and anchors at the site.[^3] No tower is left to climb.[^1]
