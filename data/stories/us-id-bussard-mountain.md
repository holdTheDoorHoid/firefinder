The Bussard Mountain lookout stood in Boundary County, about 14 miles north-northeast of Bonners Ferry, inside the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^3] The tower went up in 1935: a 30-foot pole structure with an L-4 cab, at an elevation of 5,968 feet.[^1][^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1955.[^1][^2] One Idaho register uses the word "Removed" for the tower instead of "destroyed."[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone, and one lookout guide labels the site drive-up.[^4][^2]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
