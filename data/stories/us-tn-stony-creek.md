The Stony Creek lookout stood in Carter County, at an elevation of 2,251 feet, or about 686 meters.[^1] The Tennessee Division of Forestry (TDF) built 208 fire lookout towers from about 1930 to about 1970.[^2] The National Register form for those towers lists a Stony Creek tower in Carter County in its table.[^2]

Tennessee Landforms calls the tower historical, while the Forest Lookouts page says it was removed.[^1][^3] Neither source gives a date for its removal.

The same form reports that many of the 208 towers have been taken down or are waiting to be demolished.[^2]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the Stony Creek site, or whether the site is open to visitors. Please do not assume a tower stands there.
