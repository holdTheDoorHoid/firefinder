Bear Mountain stands in Siskiyou County, in the mountains between Mount Shasta and the Modoc Lava Flat.[^1] The state added Siskiyou, the county's name, to tell it apart from the other Bear Mountains.[^1]

In August 1933 a crew from the Slagger Civilian Conservation Corps camp was building an 18-foot tower and lookout house near Bartle, according to the Dunsmuir News.[^2] A 1948 survey description records a standard lookout house on a 20-foot enclosed wooden tower, on the highest point of the mountain.[^2] The register dates the present tower to 1975 and describes it as a steel-column tower with a standard 732-6A cab.[^1]

A July 1956 item says that while her husband fought fire, she manned the lookout tower.[^2] A June 1959 item names a temporary lookout.[^2] In 2008 the Forest Service staffed the station while the Black Fox lookout was closed for maintenance.[^2] The register notes occasional staffing by Forest Service personnel.[^1] A 2006 photo note adds that the state forestry agency often staffs it during fire season.[^3]

The register puts the station some six miles from a paved road. It has solar panels and no generator backup, and earlier equipment was struck by lightning.[^1] The 2006 photo note describes the lookout as in good condition.[^3] No source read for this story gives visiting rules or rental terms, so none are offered here.
