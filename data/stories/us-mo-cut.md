Cut was a lookout tower in Wayne County.[^1] The Central US Lookouts record lists it under Mark Twain National Forest, at 28N-5E-21, a township, range and section code.[^1] The record marks the tower as removed.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
