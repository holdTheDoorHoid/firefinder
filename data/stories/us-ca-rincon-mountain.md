Rincon Mountain is in Ventura County.[^1] Most of what the sources describe is the tower that left it. The original lookout on Nordhoff Peak was destroyed by wildfire in 1948, and the Carey post places the loss in the Wheeler Springs Fire.[^3][^2] Around 1950 a tower and cabin were moved by helicopter from Rincon Mountain to replace it.[^3][^2] The register gives that Nordhoff structure a build date of circa 1950.[^3] That structure burned in the 1970s.[^2][^3] The 2012 blog post found only the steel frame still standing, above the Tower campground on the ridge that shares its name.[^2] Visitors still come from Lion Canyon, Gridley Canyon, Rose Valley and Pratt, the post says.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains on the Rincon Mountain summit today.

[^2]: Lookout! No. 12: Nordhoff Peak, Craig R. Carey blog, https://www.craigrcarey.net/2012/05/lookout-no-12-nordhoff-peak/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Nordhoff Peak Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites (FFLOS register), http://www.firetower.org/lookouts/us/ca/nordhoff-peak-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
