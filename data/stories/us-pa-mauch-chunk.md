The Register places the Mauch Chunk tower in Weiser State Forest in Carbon County, and says Civilian Conservation Corps crews built this Aermotor steel tower in 1936.[^1] Earlier, a fire tower that had stood on Lewis Behrens's land at Christman's was moved in 1925 to a higher point on Bear Mountain, above Mauch Chunk.[^2][^3]

A July 1925 newspaper item described a steel tower, 60 feet high, being erected for the district, and said it had formerly stood at Christman's.[^2] The new site, the item said, was on a mountain locally known as Jew's Head; it does not name Bear Mountain.[^2] The Register's 1936 date does not mention that earlier tower, and I found no source that says whether the CCC tower replaced it.[^1][^2]

The Register says Righter Reabold staffed the tower for 17 years.[^1] A May 1940 item named S. Aubrey Pry as towerman at the Mauch Chunk fire tower.[^4] A May 1943 forest fire that surrounded the tower was reported while Eleanor Steidle was manning it.[^4] That page does not say which site those items describe.[^4]

The Register listed the tower on January 15, 2018, with a nomination from Wyatt Cassidy, and says the Pennsylvania Bureau of Forestry administers it.[^1] I could not find a readable current source on its condition or on whether the public may climb it. Check with the Bureau before you go, and do not climb the structure until its status is confirmed.
