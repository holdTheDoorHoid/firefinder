Pistol Creek Lookout stands on the Flathead Indian Reservation, eight miles south-southeast of St. Ignatius in Lake County, Montana.[^2][^3] The Confederated Salish and Kootenai Tribe administers it.[^1]

The tower was not built where it stands.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register says it was originally constructed at another site, and that the 53-foot treated timber tower with an R-6 flat cab was built here around 1970.[^1][^3] None of the sources I could open names the first site or the year it went up there.

In July 1974 the Char-Koosta News reported that two of the reservation's four fire lookouts had been badly hit by vandals over the winter.[^4] Crews found the Pistol Creek lookout "nearly destroyed," with its stove, windows and firefinder broken.[^4] A 1979 Flathead Courier notice names a lookout who had five years of duty at Pistol Creek.[^4]

The tower is still standing. The register added it on April 12, 2022, as US 1544, MT 107, and says it is staffed during the summer.[^1] The tribe's page says the tower is now staffed only during emergencies.[^3]

No source I could read gives visitor rules, so ask the Confederated Salish and Kootenai Tribe before heading up.[^3]
