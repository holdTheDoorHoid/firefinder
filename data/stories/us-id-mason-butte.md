Mason Butte sits in Latah County, Idaho, at 3,745 feet, about 12 miles northwest of Orofino.[^1] The Potlatch Timber Protective Association ran the site.[^3] It began in 1917 with a log cabin and a tree platform.[^1][^3]

Two sources date a 67-foot pole tower with an L-6 cab to 1933.[^1][^3] A 1962 Potlatch report says instead that the Civilian Conservation Corps built a 90-foot wood tower in 1934.[^3] The sources disagree on the height and the date.[^1][^3]

Staff included the Ash family, who were stationed there for the summer of 1952.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts also lists Bob and Phylis Dudley around 1950 and Fraser Hubbard in 1980.[^2] A 1960 forest fire did not destroy the tower.[^1]

Dates for the replacement steel tower also differ. The 1962 Potlatch report says a war-surplus steel tower replaced the old one that year.[^3] Firelookout.com gives 1963 and an 86-foot steel tower.[^1] The lookout was staffed until 1986 and removed in 1999.[^1][^2][^3]

The 1999 removal is the last event the sources record.[^1][^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it is open to visitors.
