The Civilian Conservation Corps built the High Windy lookout in Buncombe County in September 1934.[^1] The steel lookout stood 45 feet 9 inches high, had a one-room tower cabin, and was paid for by the Emergency Conservation Works program at a cost of $968.90.[^1] The page credits those details to the state's sixth biennial report, which covered 1934 to 1936.[^1] The state's tower index also lists High Windy in Buncombe County.[^2]

The page lists High Windy as removed, but it gives no date for the removal.[^1]

Today, no source I could read describes what, if anything, is left at the High Windy site, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1]
