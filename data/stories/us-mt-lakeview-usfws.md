Lakeview is a 100-foot steel Aermotor tower on the Red Rock Lakes National Wildlife Refuge, in Beaverhead County, Montana, at about 6,700 feet.[^1]

The refuge dates to April 22, 1935, when President Franklin D. Roosevelt established it as a migratory waterfowl refuge.[^2] The tower's build date is unclear. The Forest Lookouts Montana page gives 1937 as the build year for a 100-foot steel tower with a cab, placed above the headquarters.[^3] The Fire Lookout Tower site says it does not know when the tower was built, and it presumes the tower was meant mainly for watching wildlife.[^1]

The sources I read name no staff and no fires the tower watched.

The refuge's visitor page lists hiking, fishing, hunting, photography and wildlife viewing among the activities on offer there.[^4] I found no source that says whether the public may climb this tower. The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as standing.[^5] Until the refuge confirms otherwise, treat the tower as off limits, and ask refuge staff before you go.
