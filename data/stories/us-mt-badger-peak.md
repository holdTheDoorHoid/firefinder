Badger Peak Lookout stands on a sharp top about six miles east-northeast of Lame Deer, on the Northern Cheyenne Reservation in Rosebud County, Montana.[^2][^3] The National Historic Lookout Register dates the tower to 1930 and describes it as a steel tower.[^1] A second listing, firelookout.com, dates the steel tower only to the 1930s.[^2]

A station description on one lookout history site says summer staff came from the forest service of the Northern Cheyenne Indian Agency, part of the Department of the Interior.[^3] The register now lists the lookout as administered by the Northern Cheyenne Agency of the Bureau of Indian Affairs.[^1]

Both the register and firelookout.com put the last known standing date at 2016.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's standing list still shows the tower as standing.[^4]

The route on that description runs east from Lame Deer on State Highway 8, turns onto a forest road, reaches a wire gate, and follows a bulldozed grade to the top of the hill.[^3] The sources give no visiting rules, and the lookout sits within the Northern Cheyenne Reservation.[^1][^2][^3]
