Forest Lookouts lists a Tate tower in Liberty County, under the Apalachicola National Forest.[^1] The entry dates the tower, a guard's cabin, a garage, fencing and landscaping to 1937, and credits Civilian Conservation Corps members with completing them.[^1]

A Forest Service handbook on Florida says that when the Apalachicola unit came under Forest Service control, it was among the last remote areas of north Florida, and that an intensive CCC program has transformed it.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts entry gives no height, material, status or current condition.[^1]
