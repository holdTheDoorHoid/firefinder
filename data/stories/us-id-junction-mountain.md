Junction Mountain Lookout stands in Clearwater County, Idaho, within the Clearwater National Forest, about two miles southwest of the Kelly Creek Ranger Station.[^1][^2] The first lookout here was an L-4 cab on a log crib base, dating to about 1931.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts prints that earlier date as 19341, which looks like a typo.[^3]

The present tower went up in 1955. It is a 53-foot structure of treated lumber with an L-4 cab, and it replaced the older log crib lookout on the site.[^1][^2][^3]

According to the register, the windows and lower stairs are gone.[^1] Sources date its abandonment to the 1970s.[^2][^3] The register recorded the site on September 2, 2014.[^1]

Staffing history is undocumented.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts says it does not yet have one for this tower.[^3] The same site lists it as a hike-in.[^3] No source read gives visitor access rules or a recent condition report, so check with the Clearwater National Forest before going.
