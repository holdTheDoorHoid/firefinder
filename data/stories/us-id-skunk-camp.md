Skunk Camp was a lookout in Lemhi County, Idaho, on the Salmon National Forest, about 40 miles northwest of Salmon.[^1][^2] The firelookout.com page gives its elevation as 7,229 feet.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts page summarizes a 1924 Salmon Forest lookout report. It says one person was stationed there, with no telephone communications.[^1] The same page quotes a 1932 Recorder Herald item about two men putting up new lookout structures on Bluenose and Skunk creek, with Forest Service oversight.[^1] The item names the creek, not this lookout, so the link to Skunk Camp is not certain.

A cabin is dated to 1935 on the Forest Lookouts page, on firelookout.com and on the Idaho Fire Lookouts page.[^1][^2][^3] The lookout was destroyed in 1970.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Lookouts page also gives its status as Removed, with no date.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists its kind as ground and its status as gone.[^4]

Today, no source describes what, if anything, remains at the site.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page calls the site hike-in, meaning it is reached on foot.[^3] None of the sources says whether the public may visit.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
