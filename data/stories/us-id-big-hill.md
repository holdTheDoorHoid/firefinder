Big Hill sits near the east end of a ridge, about 16 miles east of Kooskia, Idaho.[^1] Its lookout was a 50-foot tower with an L-4 cabin on top.[^3][^4]

Sources disagree on the year it went up.[^1][^3][^4] Louis Hartig's 1981 history of the Lochsa Ranger District says the Selway National Forest built it in 1932, at a cost of $2,000.[^3] Two other listings give 1934.[^1][^4]

In 1934 the point was an important spotting post for fires. That year a fire closed in, and the lookout had to leave the cabin. A badly burned tower leg had to be replaced.[^3]

Listings say the lookout was abandoned in the 1950s.[^1][^4]

Around 1960 the cabin and tower were declared surplus. Lightning protection and other salvageable material were recovered, and the tower was then burned down.[^3][^4]

Today the lookout is listed as gone.[^1][^2] The St. Joe-Clearwater listing also tags the site "Drive Up."[^2] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
