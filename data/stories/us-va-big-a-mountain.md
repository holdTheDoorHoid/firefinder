Big A Mountain sits in Russell and Buchanan counties, about four miles northwest of Honaker.[^1][^2] A peak-listing page on SummitPost calls it the highest point in Buchanan County.[^3]

A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description written in 1963, copied on the Forest Lookouts site, names the site the Big A Mountain Fire Tower.[^1] It says the tower was about 100 feet high, with a glass-enclosed cabin on four steel legs.[^1] The sources I read give no construction date, builder, or design history for it.[^1][^2][^3]

None of the sources names a lookout, a staffing period, or a notable fire.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list marks the tower as standing, though it gives no NHLR number for it, unlike several other towers on the same list.[^2] The SummitPost page mentions only the phone and law enforcement towers on the peak and does not mention a fire tower, so the tower's current state is uncertain.[^3]

The same SummitPost page describes the summit as privately owned.[^3] No source names the tower's owner.[^1][^2][^3] Treat the tower as off limits: do not climb it, and ask the summit's owner before any visit.
