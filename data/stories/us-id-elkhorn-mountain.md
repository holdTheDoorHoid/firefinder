The Elkhorn Mountain lookout stood at 7,907 feet in Idaho County, 17 miles south-southwest of Magruder Ranger Station, in the Bitterroot National Forest.[^1]

The first cab was an L-4, built in 1933.[^1][^3] In 1959 an R-6 flat cab on a 10-foot concrete base replaced it.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page dates a 10-foot cinder-block base and an R-6 cab to 1957, built by Dave Langley and a crew of two, with materials packed in from the Salmon Mountain base camp.[^3] The sources disagree on the year and on the base material.

Named lookouts run from 1959 to 1980.[^1][^2] Michael Long is listed for 1959, and Dave Garner for the early 1960s.[^1][^2] A 1963 Ravalli Republic item calls the coming season his third.[^3]

In 1969 a Ravalli Republic item said Scott Keating, a Gonzaga engineering student, would man the lookout.[^3] The last name in the Idaho list is Alexia Cochrane, for 1980.[^2]

The lookout was destroyed in 1991.[^1][^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it as a hike-in site.[^2] No source says whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
