Tony Peak is nine miles east-southeast of Libby, in Lincoln County, Montana, in the Kootenai National Forest.[^1][^2] The lookout, a 10-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, went up in 1934, and it was the only lookout the peak ever had.[^1] Nobody had staffed it since the late 1960s, and vandals had badly damaged it.[^1]

The register says the tower was nearing collapse when the Forest Fire Lookout Association came to Libby for its 1999 western summer meeting.[^3] After a visit to the site, the association began restoration work to slow the decay.[^3] The register lists the lookout as registered on May 12, 2000, and calls it one of eight hip-roofed L-4 lookouts on the Kootenai National Forest.[^3]

FireLookout.com says the tower burned in the summer of 2014.[^1] It is the only source we read for that fire. No source we read describes what remains on the peak today, or whether it is open to visitors.
