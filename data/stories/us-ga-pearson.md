A compiled list of Georgia lookout towers, kept on tnlandforms.us, places a Pearson tower in Atkinson County at 31.27239, -82.83176.[^1] The list gives its elevation as 194, with no unit stated.[^1]

Neither the National Historic Lookout Register, whose Georgia list has 29 towers, nor the Former Fire Lookout Sites register, which lists two Georgia sites, includes a Pearson entry.[^2][^3]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything remains or whether the public can reach it.
