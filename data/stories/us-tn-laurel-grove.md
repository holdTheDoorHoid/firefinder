Laurel Grove Lookout Tower stands in Anderson County, Tennessee, and the Tennessee Landforms list gives its elevation as 1,743 feet.[^1] The TopoQuest listing places the same site at 1,712 feet above sea level, so the two sources differ on its height.[^2]

A 2014 National Register study of Tennessee fire towers lists Laurel Grove among the towers still standing that year.[^3] According to the same study, the Tennessee Division of Forestry built 208 fire lookout towers from about 1930 to about 1970.[^3] The sources I reached do not say when this tower was built, who built it, or whether it was ever staffed.

Access is not recorded in these sources. Check with the land manager before planning a visit, and do not climb it unless the site is confirmed open to the public.

[^1]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Laurel Grove Lookout Tower, TN, TopoQuest, https://topoquest.com/place/tennessee/tower/laurel-grove-lookout-tower/1647771 (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^3]: Tennessee Division of Forestry Fire Lookout Towers, 1933 to 1975, National Register multiple property documentation form, Tennessee Valley Archaeological Research, https://npgallery.nps.gov/pdfhost/docs/NRHP/Text/64501237.pdf (accessed 2026-10-09).
