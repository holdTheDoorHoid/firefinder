The Eagles Nest (McPhee) Tower Site is in the San Juan National Forest, in Montezuma County, Colorado, at an elevation of 7,227 feet.[^1] The register dates the wooden tower to the 1930s.[^1] The U.S. Forest Service administers the site.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added it on September 18, 2009.[^1]

The register says the tower has been taken down.[^1] The Colorado list marks it removed under the same register number, US 535, CO 7.[^1][^2]

The two sources disagree about where the site is. The register gives its coordinates as 37.533000 north and 108.547000 west.[^1] The Colorado list gives 37.47791 north and 108.54572 west, several kilometres further south.[^2] Nothing I read settles which point is right.

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Eagles Nest (McPhee) Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites register, Forest Fire Lookout Association (firetower.org), http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/co/eagles-nest-mcphee-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Colorado lookout list, firelookout.org, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/co/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
