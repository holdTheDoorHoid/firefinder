The Dunlap Lookout Tower stands in Sequatchie County, Tennessee, and the Tennessee Division of Forestry administers it.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register gives the site an elevation of 2,372 feet (723 meters).[^1]

The register describes a traditional Aermotor tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab.[^1] At its base is an old house that may once have been the watchman's home.[^1] The register also says the site may be known as Bennett or Signal Mountain.[^1] A regional list of Tennessee towers shows a Bennett tower in Sequatchie County at 2,372 feet, which may be the same structure, though these sources do not confirm it.[^2][^3]

The register lists the tower as US 1291, TN 32. Victoria Oliver nominated it, and the register accepted it on February 19, 2019.[^1] The register reports that the tower was open to visitors in 2017, though that may no longer be the case.[^1]

The sources do not say when the tower was built, who staffed it, or which fires it watched, and they do not describe its condition today. A regional guide advises against climbing Tennessee's towers and notes that steps are often removed or rotten.[^2] Check with the Tennessee Division of Forestry before any visit, and stay off any tower that is closed or on private land.
