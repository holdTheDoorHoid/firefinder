The Matson (N) Lookout is a tower in Marathon County, Wisconsin, administered by the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources.[^1] The Register gives its height as 94 feet, while a 2021 DNR listing says about 90.[^1][^2] The Register also says it was built in 1951, has an inside corner ladder, and appears to be an Aermotor. It was historically called N Tower.[^1] The 2021 auction catalog gives a Plover address that the sources read do not reconcile with Marathon County.[^2]

The tower was renamed Matson in 2005 to honor John Kenneth Matson, known as Ken.[^1] Ken spent 45 years as a DNR fire lookout, most of them in this tower, and he kept climbing until he was 81.[^1] The Register says the tower was staffed on and off during spring fire seasons through 2009, and that it was still used as a Marathon County radio tower until about 2012.[^1] The sources read do not say which fires it watched.

The Register lists a registration date of August 28, 2017, with Tyler Bormann as nominator.[^1] In 2021 the DNR offered it for removal in a surplus auction that ran from August 18 to September 2.[^2] The catalog described the tower as in good shape apart from substantial surface rust on the cab, said its communications equipment was no longer in use, and set a removal deadline of December 8, 2021.[^2] As of November 2021, a Forest Fire Lookout Association report counted 27 Wisconsin towers still planned for removal.[^3]

The Association's Wisconsin county list still shows the tower as standing.[^4] None of the sources read confirm whether it came down after the 2021 auction, so its current status is unknown.[^2][^4]

The 2021 listing describes a fenced site, with the first section of the cab ladder removed to prevent unauthorized access.[^2] The sources read give no public visit route, so they give no basis for visiting or climbing it.
