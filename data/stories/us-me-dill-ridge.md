Dill Ridge sits in Lakeville Plantation in Penobscot County, Maine.[^3] A 1931 panoramic map from the Maine Forestry Service is centered on the fire tower there, so a tower stood on the ridge by that year.[^1]

A Forest Commissioner's report for 1967 and 1968, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, says the Dill Ridge tower had been moved to Almanac Mountain. The same report says the watchman's camp at Dill Ridge had been sold.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives the move as 1967.[^3] Its wording, "moved back," suggests the tower had earlier stood on Almanac, but none of the sources I read describes an earlier move.

Almanac Mountain, also in Lakeville Plantation, became a lookout station in 1914.[^4] A crew was at work erecting a 48-foot steel lookout tower there in April 1921, though the sources do not say whether it is the structure that later came from Dill Ridge.[^4] In 2008 the observer's camp was burned in the fall. In 2009 the tower cab was removed, leaving only the steel tower on the site.[^4]

The association's index lists the Almanac Mountain tower as standing.[^5] No source I found describes the Dill Ridge site today.
