Powhatan (Powhatan Court House) is a former fire tower site in Powhatan County, Virginia.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone and places it at 37.54759, -77.91298.[^1] By our calculation from the coordinates, it sits about 1.9 km southeast of the county's other listed site, Powhatan (Loc 1), which the same list also marks as gone.[^1]

The Eastern US Lookouts page for Virginia has only one Powhatan entry, spelled POWHATTEN in capitals.[^2] Beneath it, the page records that a 100-foot McClintock-Marshall steel tower sold for $1,255.00 in a 2005 eBay auction.[^2] The note does not say whether it describes this site or the Loc 1 site, so it may not refer to this one.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
