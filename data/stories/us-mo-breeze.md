Forest Lookouts lists a Breeze tower in Butler County, Missouri, under the Mark Twain National Forest, and marks it as removed.[^1]

FS 3113 is an unpaved Forest Service road in the Poplar Bluff Unit of the forest.[^3] A spur road, FS 3113A, runs north from it to the former towersite.[^2]

A survey marker dated to 1962 is at the site. On old topographic maps, the 1980 Williamsville edition shows the tower, but the editions just before and after leave it off.[^2]

The trail listing says only one website claims the tower was removed, and it gives no date for that.[^2]

Today the spur leads to a turnaround loop beside the tower's foundation piers. Old foundations for the tower's lodging cabin and garage sit on the west side of the path nearby.[^2] The listing gives the spur's length as 0.7 miles.[^2]
