Roberts was a fire lookout tower in Humphreys County, Tennessee, at an elevation of 584 ft (178 m).[^1] The Tennessee Landforms tower list gives it the code HUT003, ties it to the federal survey mark FD1631, and marks the tower as removed.[^1]

The mark, called DUCK, sits on top of a concrete monument and is stamped DUCK 1952.[^2] A Tennessee Valley Authority recovery note on its datasheet, dated 1973, says the mark was in good condition about half a mile north of the Duck River.[^2] The same note reports that no lookout tower stood at the spot.[^2]

Taken together, the removal entry and the 1973 note suggest that no tower stood at this survey mark by 1973.[^1][^2]

No source found describes what, if anything, remains of the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.

[^2]: NGS Data Sheet for survey mark FD1631 (DUCK), National Geodetic Survey, NOAA, https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=FD1631 (accessed 2026-10-21).
