The Mississippi Forestry Commission put up a lookout tower in Washington County, on a road running between Wilmot and the Swiftwater Church.[^2] A Delta Democrat item dated October 18, 1961, reproduced on the Forest Lookouts site, reports that the tower had been completed.[^2] The same item describes a small cabin on top, with glass windows and a trapdoor that swings up. It says the tower would be used in fire season, November to March, to watch for fires across the county.[^2]

The register describes a 100-foot Aermotor tower.[^1] It gives the build date as unknown, so the 1961 item is the only dated source for construction.[^1][^2]

The register spells the name Wilmont, while the 1961 item and the Forest Lookouts page use Wilmot.[^1][^2]

The register entry rests on a site visit recorded on July 8, 2022, and the tower was registered on July 10, 2022.[^1] The sources I could read give no condition after that visit, so the tower's present state is not confirmed.[^1][^2]

Today the register says the site appears to be privately owned.[^1] Treat it as off limits unless the owner gives permission, and do not climb the tower.

[^2]: Wilmot, Forest Lookouts (Eastern US Lookouts), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/wilmot.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
