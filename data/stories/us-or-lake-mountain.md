An L-5 cab was built on the northernmost point of Lake Mountain, in the Siskiyou National Forest.[^1] The site lies about 16 miles east-southeast of Cave Junction, in Josephine County.[^2] The sources disagree on the year. The register and a Siskiyou National Forest inventory give 1937, while a lookout history site and a Josephine County blog say 1938.[^1][^4][^3][^5] The history site and the blog both describe a 10 by 10 cabin, and the blog calls it a ground house.[^3][^5]

In 1941 the lookout was manned for 89 days and filed its reports with the Redwood Ranger Station.[^3]

The cab blew down in a strong windstorm in 1953.[^1][^3] The county blog gives 1954 as the removal year and says a wind storm destroyed the house.[^5]

The register says ruins may remain at the site.[^1] The history site adds that about fifty years after the storm, much of the debris still lay on the hillside.[^3] No source I found describes access to the site today, so check with the Siskiyou National Forest before you go.
