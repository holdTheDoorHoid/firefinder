Dead Horse lookout is a crow's nest in the Fremont National Forest, about 18 miles northeast of Bly in Lake County, Oregon.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the site an elevation of 8,064 feet.[^1]

Firelookout.com dates the crow's nest to 1913.[^1] A separate Forest Lookouts page, for a point called Deadhorse Rim in the same national forest, describes a patrol lookout used there in July and August 1911. It also quotes a 1915 document that said fire-finding instruments would be placed on Dead Horse Rim.[^2] That page does not say whether the rim and the crow's nest were one site.[^2]

The most recent date Firelookout.com gives is 1993, when the crow's nest was still there.[^1] The page now calls the lookout abandoned.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list has an entry named "Dead Horse Lake" that it marks as an abandoned crow's nest. That name differs from Firelookout.com's, so the list may not describe the same site.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^3] Anyone who finds the structure should not try to climb it.
