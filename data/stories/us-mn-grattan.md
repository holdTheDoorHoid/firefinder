Grattan was a state fire lookout in Itasca County, Minnesota, on a hill at the south end of Moose Lake.[^1] The state lookout was erected before 1941.[^1] Survey notes call it the Grattan Fire Tower.[^1] The site lies southeast of Northome.[^1]

The statewide list also names the site Moose Lake-Grattan, and says a small hamlet called Grattan was nearby.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it simply as Grattan, at nearly the same coordinates.[^1][^2]

Do not confuse it with a separate Moose Lake tower in Carlton County.[^1] That one, an 80-foot stairway design, is now used for communications.[^1]

The statewide list also has a tentative entry, with no coordinates, that places a possible lookout near Coddington Lake, northwest of Dora.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
