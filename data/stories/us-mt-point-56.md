Point 56 was a fire lookout cabin in the Flathead National Forest, six miles northwest of Big Salmon Lake in Flathead County, Montana.[^1] The site's listed elevation is 7,483 feet.[^1] The firelookout.com page dates the cabin only to the 1930s and calls it an L-4 cab.[^1] Working with the U.S. Forest Service, the Forest Fire Lookout Association completed a national inventory that found more than 8,000 lookouts in 49 states at one time.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list files Point 56 under Ground and places it at 47.66410, -113.47460.[^2] The firelookout.com page says the cabin was abandoned and is now gone, and the list agrees that it is gone.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. Anyone thinking of visiting should ask the Flathead National Forest about access before going.
