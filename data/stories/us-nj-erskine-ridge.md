The Ringwood Company built this wooden lookout to protect its forested land from wildfire, and it was one of a number the firm put up.[^1] The register estimates it was probably 40 feet tall or less.[^1]

A local resident, Ralph Colfax, operated the lookout.[^1] The register, citing Section 2 Chief Fire Warden Frank Scardo, says a fire destroyed it in 1954.[^1]

The site sits inside Ringwood State Park, a park of 4,444 acres in Passaic County.[^2][^1] The register says no road is known to have reached the lookout, though a trail once ran from Camp Yawpo to it.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether that trail can still be followed.

Bob Spear nominated the site to the register, which listed it on April 16, 2014, as US 1550, NJ 2.[^1]
