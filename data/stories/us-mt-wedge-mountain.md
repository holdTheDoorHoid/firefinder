Wedge Mountain is a former lookout site in the Flathead National Forest, in Flathead County, Montana, about 12 miles northwest of Polebridge.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places it at 48.89081 north and 114.49942 west.[^2]

Firelookout.com dates a platform tower and a camp to the 1930s and calls the site abandoned.[^1] The association's list also records a platform there, marked abandoned.[^2]

Wedge Mountain appears in the early panoramic photograph records. The Forest Lookouts Flathead County page gives a September 7, 1935 entry, "Panorama photos taken," beside the name.[^3] The National Park Service's list of 1930s panoramic lookout photographs has two Wedge Mt. entries, both dated 9/7/1935 and both taken by the photographer L. Moe.[^4]

The NPS history of the panoramic project says the earlier Forest Service photographs were meant for maps of how much ground the lookout system could watch for fires.[^5] It assumes that the later NPS project, which began in 1934, had the same purpose.[^5]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the site today, or whether visitors may reach it.
