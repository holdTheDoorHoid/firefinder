Goat Peak lies in the Beaverdell Range of British Columbia's Okanagan Highland, near Beaverdell.[^2][^1] A fire lookout stands on the summit at 1747 m (5732 ft).[^2]

The Life is Adventure site calls it an abandoned firewatch tower.[^1]

Reaching it means driving forest service roads.[^1] A 4x4 can drive the Boyer FSR out of Beaverdell to the base of the lookout, and other visitors may have to walk about 5 km of the road.[^1] The same writer turned back 5 km short of the base because of sketchy dugouts on the now deactivated forestry road.[^1]

A trip report from 2 July 2026 says the hikers reached the road's end in under two hours.[^2] It found a visitors' log inside the lookout.[^2] The report gives a total distance of 11.3 km and 399 m (1309 ft) of climbing. Its round-trip time, however, includes a vehicle ride down the road for part of the way.[^2]
