Davis Mountain stands in the Deschutes National Forest, in Deschutes County, Oregon, about 13 miles west of La Pine.[^1][^2] It sits right off the Cascade Lakes Highway, between Davis Lake and the Wickiup Reservoir.[^4]

A Forest Service crew was building the tower in late 1932.[^3] A report from that November, quoted on Kemnow's page, says the tower was complete but the wind was too strong to start work on the lookout house, which was still to be erected on top.[^3] Most pages give 1933 as the year the tower was built.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The November 1932 report and that 1933 date differ by a year, and these sources do not show which one marks the finished tower.[^1][^3] The lookout house, a 14-by-14 L-4 hip-roof cab, was completed in 1934.[^3] A 16-by-18 garage was also built.[^4]

In June 1936 the lookout received hazard sticks, balanced scales and wind recording instruments.[^3] In August 1950, Tom Morris was the lookout when an abandoned campfire was blamed for one of two fires that started on Davis Mountain near the east shore of Davis Lake.[^3] He spotted the fire and got it under control, and it was confined to less than an acre.[^3]

The sources disagree about how the tower ended.[^1][^2] The register says it was later removed, while Kamstra's page says it was later destroyed.[^1][^2]

Willhite's visitor guide says not much remains at the summit.[^4] Its captions mention a footing out of the ground and a footing with an eyebolt.[^4] The same guide says the summit is an easy drive-up in a high-clearance 4WD vehicle, if the road has been brushed out, and that a decent road takes you about halfway.[^4] The site is on national forest land, so the Deschutes National Forest is the place to ask about current road and access conditions.[^1]
