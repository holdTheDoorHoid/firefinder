Grouse Mountain is a peak in the Mogollon Mountains of Catron County, New Mexico, and it lies inside the Gila Wilderness.[^2] A Forest Lookouts page places its lookout in the Gila National Forest.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower on the mountain as gone.[^1]

Wikipedia gives the Holt Apache Trail as the route to the peak, about seven miles southeast of Mogollon.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the tower, or whether the old site is open to visitors.
