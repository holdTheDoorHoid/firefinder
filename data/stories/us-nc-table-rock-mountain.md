Table Rock is a peak on the eastern edge of Linville Gorge in Burke County, North Carolina.[^4] The tnlandforms.us list of North Carolina lookout towers includes an entry for Table Rock Mountain, with its coordinates and county.[^1]

The tower is gone. A 2022 trip report describes the foundation of an old fire tower still on the summit.[^3] NC Tripping says visitors can explore a stone foundation that once served as a fire-tower base.[^2]

The summit lies in Pisgah National Forest, on the Grandfather Ranger District.[^4] A trail climbs about a mile to the top, most of it along the Mountains to Sea Trail.[^3] NC Tripping says drivers cover 9.5 miles on a service road from NC-181 before reaching a paved road to the parking area, and that four-wheel-drive vehicles are preferred.[^2]
