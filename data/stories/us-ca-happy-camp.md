Happy Camp Lookout stands on Happy Camp Mountain in Modoc County, in the Modoc National Forest, at 6,067 feet.[^1] The register says a primary lookout was set up there in 1911, with Eli Dale as the first lookout man.[^1] A 1920 district newsletter item, quoted on a California lookouts page, says Dale took up the post in 1912 instead, so the start date is not settled.[^2]

Dale kept the job for years. A 1919 item says he had been the lookout for seven years, and a 1920 California district newsletter calls the station the only permanently manned lookout on the Forest.[^2]

The original structure was replaced in 1950.[^1] In 2000 an electrical fault started a fire that destroyed the lookout while it was unattended that night.[^1] A replacement built in 2001 was made accessible, with a wider-than-usual catwalk and a motorized chairlift running from ground level to the catwalk.[^1] Until the Vetter Mountain rebuild was finished in 2019, the register listed it as the newest lookout in Region 5's inventory.[^1]

Photographs dated August 1, 2019 appear on a California lookouts page, and the register added the site to its listings on October 22, 2019.[^2][^1] We found no source that says whether the public can visit or climb it today. Ask the Modoc National Forest before any trip, and do not climb a closed or private structure.
