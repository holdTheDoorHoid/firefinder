The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Moquah lookout as a tower in Bayfield County, in northern Wisconsin, and marks it as gone.[^1] The entry gives it no National Historic Lookout Register or Former Fire Lookout Sites number.[^1]

A reproduction of a 1947 topographic map of Moquah marks a Moquah fire tower on its sheet.[^2] OpenStreetMap carries a point named Moquah Lookout Tower and tags it as an observation tower.[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1]

[^2]: 1947 Map of Moquah, Delta, WI, Pastmaps, https://pastmaps.com/map/moquah-bayfield-county-wi-usgs-topo-1947 (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Moquah Lookout Tower, OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/353894187 (accessed 2026-10-10).
