Paradise Peak is a former lookout site in Ferry County, Washington, on the Colville National Forest, at 5,838 feet.[^1] The register dates the lookout to around 1930.[^1] It was a platform tower about 30 feet tall, built from three trees and reached by a ladder.[^1] A rag camp stood near its base.[^1]

Locals regard the site as separate from the Skalawag Ridge lookout, and the register says there are records to back that up.[^1] The register also suggests it may instead have been the patrol lookout used alongside Skalawag Ridge.[^1] The site was listed on the register on April 3, 2018, on the nomination of Rod Fosback.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Washington index lists the platform as gone.[^2]

The register says large stones that once sheltered the fire ring from the wind were still there.[^1] No source we read says whether the site is open to the public or how to reach it.[^1][^2]
