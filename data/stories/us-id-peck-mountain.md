Peck Mountain, in Adams County, Idaho, held a fire lookout on Payette National Forest land.[^1] Firelookout.com puts it about 10 miles northwest of Council, while a 1954 U.S. Geological Survey station description, quoted on Kemnow's site, gives 12.5 miles.[^2][^4]

The first lookout went up in 1919: a platform in a 30-foot tree, with a cab and a frame cabin for living quarters.[^2][^3][^4] The register describes a different first lookout, a roofed 6-by-6-foot platform with a 6-by-8-foot ground house, built by the Southern Idaho Timber Protective Association.[^1]

According to the register, the Civilian Conservation Corps put up a 74-foot Aermotor steel tower in 1935, topped with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab and joined by a ground cabin and a garage.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts and Rex's page both give the tower a height of 45 feet instead.[^2][^3] Kemnow's site dates the tower to 1934, a year before the others.[^4]

Known staff lists name Roy Nelson and A. Patton for 1942, and Satterfield for 1956.[^2][^3]

The register entry is dated March 1, 1999.[^1] A Forest Fire Lookout Association Northern Idaho/Montana chapter annual report for 2006 says parts salvaged from the ground cabin at Peck Mountain helped finish the lightning protection at Square Mountain Lookout.[^5]

The tower came down in 2007.[^2][^3][^4] Kemnow's site says it was taken apart that September and held at the Council Ranger Station until a new location could be found.[^4]

The sources give little detail on the site as it stands now. A rider's check-in on a motorcycle club's challenge page says the last two miles of the climb are off-pavement, and that there is "not much left of the lookout."[^6] The same club page describes the summit as a brief walk from forest roads, and asks riders to respect private land nearby.[^6]
