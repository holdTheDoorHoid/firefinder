Miller Peak is the highest mountain in the Huachuca Mountains, about 10 miles south of Sierra Vista in Cochise County.[^1] Sources give its height as 9,470 feet or 9,466 feet.[^1][^2] The Coronado National Forest manages the Miller Peak Wilderness, which covers 20,190 acres.[^1]

A May 2, 1962 item from the Tucson Daily Citizen, reprinted on the Forest Lookouts site, said the Miller Peak Lookout would be flown up by helicopter, since the snow was still deep on the mountain's upper slopes.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the lookout as a ground site, now gone.[^4] A SummitPost overview says the summit once held a lookout tower, and is now a scatter of rock and light brush.[^2] The two sources disagree on what the structure was, and we could not settle it.

Visitors can reach the summit on foot.[^2] The Crest Trail follows the range crest, and a spur leads steeply from it to the top. The writer also calls the hike short.[^2]
