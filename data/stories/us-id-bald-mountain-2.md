The Idaho Fire Lookouts site files this Bald Mountain under its St. Joe-Clearwater region and marks the tower as destroyed in 1972.[^1]

The site's history starts with a log cabin with a crow's nest, dated to 1914.[^1] By 1923 it had an observation tower with a log cabin, and by 1933 the tower was a 50-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1]

The page says staffing for this lookout is not yet documented.[^1] A reader comment on the same page, however, says the commenter was a paid staffer at the Bald Mountain lookout for the Idaho Department of Lands in the summer of 1987 or 1988, though not sure of the date.[^1] That is years after the 1972 date.[^1] The comment is a personal recollection, and it has not been checked against records.[^1]

No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether visitors can reach it.[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the rented Big Baldy lookout its own page, at 5,334 feet, against the 5,036 feet listed for this tower.[^2][^1] The National Historic Lookout Register places a Bald Mountain lookout in Latah County, Idaho, whose 1960 tower was refurbished in 1998 and is on the Clearwater National Forest cabin rental program.[^3]
