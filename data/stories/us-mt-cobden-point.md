Cobden Point is a former fire lookout about eight miles southeast of Superior, Montana, on the Lolo National Forest in Mineral County.[^1][^2]

The tower was under construction by the spring of 1934. The Missoulian reported that a crew was building a lookout tower near Cobden to watch the timber for fires.[^2] The Lolo forest had also ordered materials for ten new lookout towers that year, and one listed location was Cobden knob, which may be this point.[^2] The finished lookout was an L-4 observation cab on a 30-foot round timber tower.[^1][^2]

Records name a few lookouts: Buddy Campbell in 1950, William Oliver in 1953, and Lloyd Reesman and Mabel Vann in 1957.[^1] Firelookout.com lists the tower as gone by 1967, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association also marks it gone.[^1][^3] Neither source says how the tower came down.

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site, and none says whether the public can reach it.
