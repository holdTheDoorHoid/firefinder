The Wolf Camp Butte lookout was a wooden L-4 tower built in the 1930s, overlooking the Salmon River in Clackamas County, Oregon.[^1] The register places it in the Mount Hood National Forest.[^1] A photograph in the register is captioned 1940.[^1]

The register says the tower was removed in 1955.[^1] firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^2] The Oregon Lookouts page lists it only as removed, with no date.[^3] The sources do not agree on how the tower ended, so the story keeps both descriptions.

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site on September 21, 2010, with Ray Kresek as nominator.[^1]

PeakVisor places the summit inside the Salmon-Huckleberry Wilderness of the Mount Hood National Forest.[^4] We found no source that describes what, if anything, is left at the summit now, or whether the public can reach it.
