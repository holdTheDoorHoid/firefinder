Georgia's forest lookout listings include a tower at Blount, in Monroe County.[^1][^2] A Georgia tower list gives its position as 33.17611° N, 83.95861° W and an elevation of 679, with no unit stated (about 207 m if feet).[^2]

Blount is an unincorporated settlement in Monroe County. The community took its name from James Henderson Blount (1837–1903), a U.S. congressman from Georgia, and a post office operated there from 1887 until 1906.[^3]

No source I found describes the tower site today, so I cannot say what remains there or whether the public can reach it.[^1]
