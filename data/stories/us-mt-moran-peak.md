Moran Peak is a lookout site in Flathead County, about 7 miles west-southwest of Polebridge.[^1] It is listed under the Flathead National Forest, at an elevation of 7,404 feet.[^1] Firelookout.com dates its L-4 cab to 1930 and marks the lookout as abandoned and gone.[^1]

A 1912 item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site from The Columbian, describes a lookout man stationed on "Moran mountain." We take that to mean this site.[^2] A telephone line linked him directly to the Moran ranger station.[^2] A second lookout about twenty miles north would send him the location of any fire.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list also marks the site as a ground lookout that is gone.[^3] The Forest Lookouts page carries a bare "Removed" label, with no date or reason given.[^2]

We found no source describing what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.
