Slate Rock was a lookout on the Willamette National Forest in Linn County, Oregon, about eight miles southwest of Detroit as the crow flies.[^1][^2][^3] The register gives its elevation as 4,523 feet.[^1]

Panorama photographs were taken there in May 1934.[^3] The cab was built in 1935, and the Forest Lookouts page says the CCC constructed a 10-by-10 L-5 lookout house.[^1][^2][^3]

Two 1944 newspaper items name people working the post.[^3] One said two Mill City high school boys were working for the forestry department that summer, and that one of them served on the Slate Rock lookout.[^3] A later August item said a worker had been relieved from the lookout after some time there.[^3] The two items name different men, so the record of who staffed the lookout is unclear.[^3]

The register and firelookout.com both say the cab was gone by 1965.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page marks the site as removed.[^3]

A 1947 Coast and Geodetic Survey description placed the lookout house on a long, low ridge with timber cover. It described a single-story building with a pointed roof.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site now, or whether it is open to visitors.
