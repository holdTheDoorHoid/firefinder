Plum Creek was a fire lookout in Koochiching County, Minnesota.[^1] A Koochiching County report on Minnesota fire lookout towers says it was erected in 1930 and that the Minnesota Forest Service owned it.[^1] It was a ladder tower 94 feet tall, and the report gives its state number as 83.[^1]

The report also gives a second name, the Park Avenue lookout. Highway 1, the road through the area, was known as Park Avenue.[^1]

The report marks the tower as removed.[^1] The report says the site's buildings went to Northome, to the west, where they now form part of the forestry station there.[^1] The Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list also marks the tower as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
