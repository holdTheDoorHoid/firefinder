Stubblefield Butte is a lookout tower in Harney County, Oregon, about 20 miles southeast of Burns. The agency that lists it is the U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service.[^1][^2]

Civilian Conservation Corps workers built the tower in the 1930s.[^3] It is a 50-foot tower with a wooden platform.[^1] One page says its main use was watching wildlife.[^1]

The sources we read name no one who staffed the tower and no fire it watched.[^1][^2][^3] A 1995 archaeological assessment treats the place as a site in its own right, the Stubblefield Lookout Tower Site (35Ha53).[^4]

A blog visit in April 2024 found the stairs still in place. The writer reported that the area has since been shut to visitors over wildlife, and suggested Highway 205 as the best spot to see the tower. The tower is not on the National Historic Lookout Register.[^3]

In August 2026 OPB reported that the Stubble and Jackass Butte fires, which scorched part of Harney County near the Malheur refuge, claimed two or more historical structures dating from the 1930s.[^5] The article does not name them, so we cannot tell whether this tower is one of them.[^5]

The 2024 visit is the most recent report we found that shows the tower standing, so check before relying on it.[^3] Access is reported closed, so do not try to reach the tower.[^3]
