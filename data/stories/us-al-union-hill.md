The Union Hill Lookout Tower was erected in 1964 on a high hill in eastern Morgan County, Alabama.[^1][^2] A 1964 newspaper report, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, called it the county's first and only fire tower and said it was made of steel.[^2] The register describes an Aermotor MC-39 steel tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab, one of a limited number of 80-foot towers in Alabama.[^1]

Reports from spring 1964 tracked its progress. In March it was nearing completion; by April it was in operation, though not quite finished.[^2] A notice from July 1964 said the new tower had not yet been manned, because there were not enough funds or people to staff it.[^2] The sources read do not record any fire it spotted, or whether it was staffed in later years.

The register says the tower and its surrounding buildings were sold to a private owner in 2019, and that access now requires the landowner's permission.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page gives August 2019 as the month the site was deeded to private ownership, and it lists the Alabama Forestry Commission as the earlier administrator.[^2]

The register's photographs were taken in November 2021, and the sources do not say what condition the tower is in now.[^1] Because the owner controls access, anyone curious about it should ask the landowner first.[^1]
