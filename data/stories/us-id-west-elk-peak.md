West Elk Peak lookout stood on the peak of that name in Shoshone County, about five miles north of Clarkia.[^1] Firelookout.com lists the agency as the St. Joe National Forest and the elevation as 5,054 feet.[^1] A tower went up at the west end of the peak in 1935, according to Firelookout.com, Idaho Fire Lookouts and Rex's page.[^1][^2][^3]

Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both name East Elk as a patrol point linked to the tower.[^1][^2]

Sources disagree on how the tower ended in 1957.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts say it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2] Rex's page, which cites Kresek, says it was removed.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under hike-in.[^2] None of the four sources describes what, if anything, remains on the peak today.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
