In a Wisconsin Public Radio report, DNR officials said the state would no longer use its 72 fire lookout towers, most of them built in the 1930s.[^1] A notice dated January 6, 1983, quoted from The Capital Times, invited sealed bids for a fire tower at the Seeley site in Sawyer County.[^2] Bids were to be received at the DNR's Park Falls area headquarters.[^2] The notice placed the tower in Section 6, Township 42 North, Range 7 West, and described the steel tower as about 84 feet tall.[^2] The excerpt does not say what the bids were for.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Seeley tower as gone, with its point at 46.14777 north and 91.28392 west.[^3] An OpenStreetMap point for the site is tagged abandoned and in ruins, but no photo or written description confirms what is there.[^4]

No source read describes the site today beyond that map tag, or whether visitors can reach it.
