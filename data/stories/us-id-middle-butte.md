Middle Butte, in Idaho County, was a cabin rather than a tower.[^1][^4] Firelookout.com lists a cabin for 1920 and says the site was abandoned in 1930.[^1] Its elevation is 5,388 feet, and the site lies seven miles southwest of the Lochsa Ranger Station on the Clearwater National Forest.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the same two dates and labels the site hike-in and abandoned. It files the site under the St. Joe-Clearwater region.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page, which lists it under Idaho County, gives its land description as 34N-8E-9.[^3] The state index lists the site as ground-level and gone, placing it at 46.30118, -115.47060.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at Middle Butte today, or whether the spot can be reached.
