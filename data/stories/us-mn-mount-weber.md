Mount Weber, also called the Greenwood Lake lookout, was a fire tower in Lake County, Minnesota.[^1]

It was tower number 142, a 56-foot Aermotor ladder tower erected in 1925.[^1][^2] The county list gives the owner as Mn MFS, the Minnesota Forest Service, which is now the Department of Natural Resources' forestry division.[^1][^3] Survey notes from 1934 describe the Mount Weber station as sitting under a fire lookout tower.[^1]

The alternate name points to Greenwood Lake, and Wikipedia traces that lake's name to George C. Greenwood, a pioneer merchant.[^4]

The county list says the tower was removed and that it may have passed to the U.S. Forest Service.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone.[^5]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.
