A 1952 survey note describes the Beck station as reached by driving south from Monico on U.S. Route 45 for 2.6 miles, then turning right through a gate onto open ground.[^1] By then a Wisconsin Conservation lookout tower had been built directly over the station mark.[^1] The surveyors found the mark as it had been described in 1940, and in good condition.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page lists the Wisconsin Department of Conservation as the agency.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list marks the tower as gone.[^2]

A 2010 USGS topographic map of the Monico area, as described on the Pastmaps website, still labels a Beck Lookout Tower.[^3] A map label does not prove that a tower was standing that year. No source we read describes the site today or says whether the public may visit.
