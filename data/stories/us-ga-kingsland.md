A tower named "kingsland" appears in Camden County on the tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers, which gives it GFC number 846.[^1] The page says its map was revised in March 2016.[^1]

Kingsland, the city that shares the tower's name, became incorporated in 1908.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register counts 29 registered Georgia lookouts, and its Georgia page does not name Kingsland.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register lists two former Georgia sites and does not name Kingsland either.[^4]

No source describes the site today, including whether it is public.
