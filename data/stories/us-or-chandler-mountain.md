Chandler Mountain sits about six miles southeast of Sweet Home, in Linn County, at 2,498 feet.[^1][^2] The register says the Linn FPA administered it, and Forest Lookouts names the operator as the Linn County Fire Patrol.[^1][^3]

The register and firelookout.com both say a 10-by-10-foot cabin went up in 1915, about a mile and a half east of the site.[^1][^2] Both also describe a two-story L-4 cab built on the mountain in 1952.[^1][^2]

Forest Lookouts tells the 1952 story differently. It says a 10-foot tower went up that year about a mile and a half west, on a point called Chimney Rock, and that duties were to move there from Chandler Mountain.[^3] The same page lists a cupola-style lookout house built in 1939.[^3]

In July 1932 a Roseburg News-Review item, quoted by Forest Lookouts, reported that Fred Bateman had left for the Chandler lookout, where he would be stationed for the summer.[^3] In 1964 an Albany Democrat-Herald notice said the mountain would be in use that year only in emergencies.[^3]

The register says the two-story cab was removed in 1970, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list shows the site as gone, with a two-story cab.[^4]

The sources we read do not describe what, if anything, stands on the summit today, or whether visitors can reach it.
