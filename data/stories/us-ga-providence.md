A Georgia lookout index lists a tower named providence in Stewart County, with an elevation of 662. The index gives no unit; read as feet, that is about 202 m.[^1] The index's author says names for towers numbered 73 and above are the author's own creation, so the name may not be an official one.[^1] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
