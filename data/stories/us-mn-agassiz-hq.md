The Agassiz Headquarters fire observation tower is in the Agassiz National Wildlife Refuge, in Marshall County, Minnesota.[^1] A 2005 Fire Lookout Association report says the Agassiz Refuge Fire Tower opened in early June that year. We take that to be the same tower, though the report does not say where it stands.[^2]

The association's Minnesota list marks Agassiz HQ as gone.[^3] The refuge's visit page, however, says the 100-foot tower near headquarters can be climbed, and that a key is available from the office during office hours in snow-free months.[^4] A caption on a refuge photo gives the height as 99 feet and says the tower's view takes in the refuge's western half.[^5] The sources disagree, and we could not date any of them, so we cannot say which is current.

We found no source that describes the site on the ground now. If you plan a visit, the refuge page advises calling ahead to check that facilities are open.[^4]
