A lookout tower site in Montgomery County, Georgia, appears on a lookout-tower list under the name "mt vernon?", and the question mark is part of that name.[^1] Mount Vernon is the county seat of Montgomery County.[^2] The row for the site ends with the words "fire tower rd", and the list gives coordinates and an elevation for it.[^1] The list's map was last revised in March 2016.[^1]

No other source I could reach mentions a tower at this site, so the question mark in the name is a real caveat. Because the list itself carries that mark, the list does not settle what the site was called.

No source describes the site now, so I cannot say whether a tower, a foundation or any other trace remains there, or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^2]: Montgomery County, Georgia, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montgomery_County,_Georgia (accessed 2026-10-21).
