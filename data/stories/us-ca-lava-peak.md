Lava Peak sits in Lassen County at about 6,613 feet, on land administered by the Lassen National Forest.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register dates the lookout to about the 1940s.[^1] It describes an 8x8, observation-only cabin on the ground, with a wood catwalk.[^1]

The register's condition note is dated May 2025. By then the walls had fallen in, while the platform stayed largely intact and most of the roof had come to rest about 50 feet downhill.[^1] The register calls the site a standing ruin.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list marks it as gone.[^2]

As of that May 2025 note, the site is a ruin with a mostly intact platform. The sources we read do not say how to reach it or whether the public may visit, so check with the Lassen National Forest before any trip.[^1]
