Whitimore Tower is in Elk State Forest, Cameron County.[^1] The storymap gives a build year of 1921, the Aermotor Windmill Company as builder, and a height of 47 feet.[^1] I found no second source for those details.

Wikipedia's entry on the Aermotor company says the firm also made galvanized steel fire lookouts under the name "Aermotor Corporation."[^2]

Most of Elk State Forest's land was obtained from timber firms in the early 1900s, and the lumber era there ended in 1915.[^3] The same article says sparks from steam trains started fires that held back the forest's recovery after the lumber companies left.[^3] The sources I read do not connect this tower to any particular fire.

I found no staffing, access, or fire records for Whitimore in the sources I could open. Two listing pages that mention the tower, one on a trail site and one with a photo caption, were blocked when I tried to read them.

Ask the Elk State Forest office about access before you visit, and do not climb the tower unless it is open to the public.
