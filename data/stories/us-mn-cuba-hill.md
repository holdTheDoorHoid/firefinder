The Cuba Hill lookout is listed in Cass County, Minnesota.[^1] Wikidata classes it as a fire lookout tower, about 1,434 feet above sea level, inside the Chippewa National Forest.[^2]

I could not find a dated record of when the tower was built, who built it, or how tall it is. The sources I could reach give only its location, elevation and status, so its history here is thin. A search turned up a blog about Minnesota's old fire towers that seems to cover this one, but the blog pages would not open for me, so I have used none of its claims.

I found no record of who staffed the tower, or of any notable fire it was used for. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's standing list includes it.[^3]

I found no current statement about public access or climbing. Before any visit, check with the forest's managers, and do not climb the tower without permission.
