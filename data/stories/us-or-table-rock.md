Table Rock is a site in the small Table Rock Wilderness, in Clackamas County.[^4] A lookout camp was in use there by 1916.[^2] A February 1916 newspaper item, quoted on the Oregon Forest Lookouts page, names lookout camps at High Camp, Highland Butte, Table Rock, Ogle Mountain and Mountain Home Rock.[^2] A 1920 account has a forest ranger making observations at Table Rock, looking for evidence of fires already burning.[^2]

What was built there, and when, is not clear from the sources. Firelookout.com lists the structure for 1937 as unknown and says the site was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1] A 1946 Mount Hood National Forest map still listed the site as a state lookout, so the exact end date is uncertain.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon index marks the site as gone.[^3]

A popular trail in the wilderness leads to what one regional guide calls an old lookout site, with views of Mount Jefferson and Mount Hood.[^4] The guide does not say what, if anything, still stands there.[^4] No other source we read describes the site today.
