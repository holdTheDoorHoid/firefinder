Haydenburg Ridge is in Jackson County, Tennessee, at about 994 feet.[^1] The state's tower numbers are pairs of district and tower, and this one is 4-20.[^1] A Forest Lookouts index places it under Jackson County and the Division of Forestry's district four.[^2]

We could not find when this tower was built, what it was made of, or who worked it. A table of towers still standing, dated March 26, 2014, lists Haydenburg Ridge in Jackson County.[^3] Tennessee Landforms also marks it as standing.[^1]

A directory listing calls it the Tennessee State Forestry Division's Haydenburg Fire Tower, places it near Cordell Hull Lake, and gives an address of 2030 Riley Creek Road in Whitleyville.[^4] The entry carries no date, so it does not show whether the tower is in use today.

We found no visitor information for Haydenburg Ridge. Tennessee Landforms advises people not to climb the state's old towers, because steps are often removed or rotten, so please keep off this one.[^1]
