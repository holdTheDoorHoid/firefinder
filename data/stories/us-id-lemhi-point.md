Lemhi Point sits in the Frank Church River of No Return Wilderness, on Payette National Forest land about 45 miles east of Riggins in Idaho County. Its elevation is 6,821 feet.[^1]

Firelookout.com dates a 20-foot pole platform there to the 1930s.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the same decade and says the site was a patrol point for the Sheepeater lookout.[^2]

The platform is now in ruins. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as abandoned, and firelookout.com describes it as presently in ruins.[^1][^2]

No source describes what, if anything, is left beyond those ruins. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in, so reaching it means walking in, and no source describes a closure.[^2] Check with the Payette National Forest before you plan a trip.
