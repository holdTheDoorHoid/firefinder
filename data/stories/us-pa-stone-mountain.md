The Stone Mountain tree tower stood in Huntingdon County, Pennsylvania.[^1] Forest Lookouts dates a tree observation post, roughly 40 feet off the ground, to 1907.[^2]

The register dates construction to 1907, notes the tower was still in use around 1917, and reports that it has since come down.[^1] It is listed as US 497 and PA 326, filed on September 16, 2008, with Henry Isenberg as the nominator.[^1]

The Stone Mountain Hawk Watch overlook lies inside Rothrock State Forest, on the boundary between Huntingdon and Mifflin counties.[^3] A lot sits at the summit, and autumn is the busiest season, when people come to watch hawks migrate.[^3] No source we read ties that overlook to the old tower, and none describes what, if anything, remains at the tower site today.
