Chicken Peak Lookout sits inside the Frank Church River of No Return Wilderness on the Payette National Forest, in Idaho County.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com places it about 12 miles north of Big Creek R.S.[^2]

The cabin is a 14-by-14-foot R4-D8 design, a pre-CCC style with a wooden body and a steep pyramid roof with no catwalk.[^1][^4] The register, Idaho Fire Lookouts and Keeping Watch all date it to 1930.[^1][^3][^4] Firelookout.com says 1933 for its 14-by-14 log cab, so the build year is not settled.[^2] According to the register, it is the only known survivor of its pre-CCC style and the oldest lookout on the forest.[^1]

Staffing ended in 1969. The register says the cabin was staffed until that year, and Idaho Fire Lookouts gives 1969 as the last year staffed.[^1][^3] Forest Lookouts says the site was staffed into the late 1960s.[^5] Idaho Fire Lookouts has no documented list of who staffed it, so the names of the lookouts are not known from these sources.[^3]

Keeping Watch records the cabin as abandoned in 1969.[^4] Firelookout.com says it has been abandoned for many years but is still there.[^2] The register says the cabin needs care and describes it as a cultural treasure.[^1]

Keeping Watch holds videos and photos of the 2021 Dixie Jumbo Fire, including one titled "Dixie Jumbo Fire From Chicken Peak," dated July 12, 2021.[^4] None of the sources read says whether the fire reached the cabin.[^4]

The National Historic Lookout Register recorded the lookout on December 1, 1999, on the nomination of Steve Stoddard of the Idaho Chapter, FFLA.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it as a hike-in and as abandoned.[^3] Because it sits in wilderness and no source read says whether the cabin may be entered or climbed, check with the Payette National Forest before planning a visit.[^1][^3]
