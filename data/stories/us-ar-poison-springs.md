The Poison Springs Fire Tower is a 100-foot steel Aermotor tower in Ouachita County, Arkansas, on the Poison Springs State Forest.[^1][^2]

The register says the Civilian Conservation Corps built it in 1938.[^1] Camden News items from early 1935, reproduced on the Central US Lookouts page, describe plans for a state steel tower near Chidester. A March 21, 1935 notice said construction would start that Saturday.[^2] I found no source that reconciles those newspaper plans with the register's 1938 date, so I treat the build date as disputed.[^1][^2]

The planned site was on the old Lester mill company property, near the old Poison Springs Civil War battlefield.[^2] A 1941 Coast and Geodetic Survey station description, copied on the same page, describes a steel tower with a closed-in cab and a height of 100 feet.[^2]

The register says the tower served the state forest and surrounding counties until 1985.[^1] It does not name the people who staffed the tower.[^1]

The register says the steel tower is now a radio relay site for the Arkansas Forestry Commission.[^1] I found no source describing public access, and a working radio site is not a place to assume you can climb. Ask the Arkansas Forestry Commission before you visit.

[^2]: Poison Springs (#92), Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/poison-springs.html (accessed 2026-10-08).
