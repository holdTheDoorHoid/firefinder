The Georgia lookout tower list on tnlandforms.us gives an entry numbered GA136 at a point labelled "chester" in Dodge County.[^1] The entry gives coordinates, a county and an elevation, but no details of the structure itself.[^1]

The name is the compiler's own label rather than a record name. The list draws on a USGS name database and on towers found on 1:100,000 USGS topographic maps, and it says names for towers numbered 73 and above are its own creation.[^1] GA136 falls in that higher range, so "chester" was chosen by the compiler.[^1] The page was revised in March 2016.[^1]

No source found describes what, if anything, stands at the point today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
