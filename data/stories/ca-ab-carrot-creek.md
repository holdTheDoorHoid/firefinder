Carrot Creek is a steel fire lookout in Alberta. The provincial register lists the tower at 30 m (98 ft) tall, on a site 1,056 m (3,464 ft) above sea level, and marks its location as verified.[^1]

The register gives 2014 as the year the tower was constructed.[^1] Builders began using steel for lookout towers in the 1920s, gradually moving away from wood.[^2] A news explainer on Alberta's wildfire lookouts says the first permanent ones went up in 1915. The lookouts have since been refurbished or swapped out, but most sit in the same general areas.[^3] The same explainer puts most towers at about 30 m (98 ft) in height.[^3]

Today the register lists Carrot Creek as active, with an access mode of "drive", meaning the tower is reached by road, and a weather station on site.[^1] Lookouts are not open to public viewing, the province says, but its workers will greet hikers who come across a site.[^3] Alberta bars the public from climbing the tower, citing safety.[^4] The register says nothing about the tower's condition or surroundings today.[^1] No other Carrot Creek source was found.
