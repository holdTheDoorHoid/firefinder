Rock Creek Butte Point is a lookout site listed under Del Norte County, California, on the Forest Lookouts site.[^1] That site quotes a Klamath National Forest chronology entry dated October 1, 1942, which notes a temporary cabin erected on the road near the ridge top, west of Rock Creek Butte.[^1] The listing files the entry under Aircraft Warning Service.[^1]

A Forest Service research page describes a research natural area on the Klamath National Forest that takes in the butte's higher slopes and summit. The area was established in 2007 and covers about 500 acres.[^2] That page places the butte in westernmost Siskiyou County, while the lookout listing gives Del Norte County. The two sources give different counties, and it is not certain they describe the same place.[^2][^1]

No source found describes the lookout after 1942, or what, if anything, remains at the site today. Whether the site is open to the public is not stated.

[^1]: Rock Creek Butte Point, Forest Lookouts, western US (Ron Kemnow), https://californialookouts.weebly.com/rock-creek-butte-point.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Rock Creek Butte, Research Natural Areas, USDA Forest Service Research and Development, https://research.fs.usda.gov/psw/rnas/locations/rock-creek-butte (accessed 2026-10-21).
