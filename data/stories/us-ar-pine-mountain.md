Pine Mountain is a lookout site in Franklin County, Arkansas, in the Ozark National Forest.[^1] OpenStreetMap maps a tower with this name at about 35.63 N, 94.37 W. It is tagged as an observation tower with lattice construction.[^2] The map record gives the site an elevation of 430 metres.[^2]

At its peak, between the 1930s and the 1970s, Arkansas had more than 120 fire towers.[^4] The counts since then also disagree. Preserve Arkansas puts the number still standing at about 40, out of the lookouts it has documented.[^3] Most of the state's towers are steel frames with metal cabs, a design especially vulnerable to rust.[^3] The Encyclopedia of Arkansas says 46 were still standing in 2023.[^4]

The forest-lookout listing and the map record say nothing about the tower's condition or whether visitors can reach it today.[^1][^2]
