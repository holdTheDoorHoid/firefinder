The Cove Mountain Lookout stands on Cove Mountain in Wythe County, Virginia, near Max Meadows.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes an 83-foot steel tower with a 14-by-14 cab.[^1] It lists the tower as built in 1941.[^1]

The Register gives no builder and no reason for the tower.[^1] A 2010 visit report on the Register passes on an older local resident's memory that the tower was finished before 1945, with materials carried up the mountain by convicts. We could not check that account, so we have not treated it as established.[^1]

The same report says the tower has been unused for many decades.[^1] We found no record of who staffed it or of any fire it watched.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list still marks the tower as standing.[^2] The 2010 visitor wrote that the steel structure, stairs, and rails all seemed to be in very good condition.[^1] The same visit noted some rotting of the wood at the doorway and most of the glass broken.[^1]

The Register registered the tower on September 3, 2014.[^1]

The Register says the tower is no longer in use, and that only the landowner can permit access.[^1] We found no public access, and the 2010 report notes there is no access road.[^1] Anyone interested should ask the landowner first and should not climb the tower without permission.[^1]

[^1]: Cove Mountain Lookout, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/va/cove-mountain-lookout/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Virginia Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
