The Forest Lookouts site places McCormick in Pope County on the Shawnee National Forest, and its 1972 notice describes the tower as steel.[^1]

On August 18, 1972, the Southern Illinoisan ran a notice inviting bids to buy this tower, along with seven other steel towers on the forest.[^1] An Illinois Country Living profile of Bob Frakes, a historian of fire lookouts, reports that he visited 17 Illinois sites before nearly all of the state's towers were removed or sold early in the 1970s.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists McCormick as a tower that is gone.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors. The Forest Service says that of the 16 fire lookouts put up on the Shawnee National Forest, only Trigg Lookout Tower remains.[^3]
