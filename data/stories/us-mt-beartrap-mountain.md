Beartrap Mountain is a lookout site in Lincoln County, Montana, about 11 miles southwest of Eureka, on the Kootenai National Forest.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 5,625 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places it at 48.78357 north, 115.24665 west and lists the type as crowsnest.[^2]

Firelookout.com dates the crow's nest to 1936.[^1] A Lincoln County list prints a 1936 tree crow's nest in the Bear Creek Ridge entry, just above Beartrap's, so that year may belong to the neighboring site instead.[^3]

The association marks the site Abandoned.[^2] On the same line as its 1936 date, firelookout.com adds that the crow's nest was still there, with no separate date for that note.[^1] The two sources do not settle whether anything stands on the mountain today.

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
