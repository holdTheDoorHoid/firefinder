The Clatskanie Mountain lookout stood in Columbia County, Oregon, and the Oregon Department of Forestry administered it.[^1] In 1954 a steel Aermotor tower with its cab went up on the site.[^1][^3] The tower was 60 feet tall with a 7-by-7 steel cab, and it replaced the older Benson Lookout.[^2] Some sources give a height of 65 feet instead.[^4][^5] When it was finished, the tower had cost $2,325.[^2]

The sources we read do not name anyone who staffed the tower or any fire it watched. A 1955 annual report, quoted on the Weebly history, describes a porch and kitchen fittings added to the living quarters.[^2]

In January 1984 the tower was taken down and moved to Magness Tree Farm, where it was to be rebuilt for teaching and as a historic display.[^2] The sources disagree about where the farm was. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register and Firelookout both say near Woodburn,[^1][^3] the Weebly history says near Wilsonville,[^2] and a 2021 Hopkins newsletter says Sherwood.[^4]

The tower then went to Hopkins Demonstration Forest, a 140-acre working forest just outside Oregon City.[^4][^5] The newsletter says restoration there took nearly ten years.[^4]

Today the tower stands at Hopkins. A 2025 article from Mt Hood Territory calls it a highlight of the forest, says it has been restored to historic standards, and says visitors can climb it for views.[^5] The newsletter said the forest hoped to open the tower regularly, and it listed public tours and open houses that included a tower visit.[^4] Mt Hood Territory says the forest is open seven days a week, but the sources do not describe a year-round schedule for tower climbs, so check the forest's tour dates before you go.[^4][^5]
