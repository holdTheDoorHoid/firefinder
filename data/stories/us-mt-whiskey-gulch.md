The Whiskey Gulch lookout site lies about 17 miles east-northeast of Missoula, in Missoula County, Montana.[^1][^2]

A firelookout.com page dates it to the 1920s and describes it as a 20-foot platform tower.[^1] The same page names the Flathead National Forest as its agency.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list also types it as a platform, and gives its coordinates as 46.95496, -113.66395.[^2]

Both sources describe the lookout as abandoned.[^1][^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Whiskey Gulch Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/whiskeygulch.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
