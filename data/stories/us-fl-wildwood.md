The Forest Lookouts website lists a tower called Wildwood in Sumter County, Florida, with the location code T19S-R22E-2.[^1] The listing's only dated entry is from 2008, when the tower was sold at auction for $2,000.[^1]

A Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services page on surplus equipment says that when the Florida Forest Service no longer needs a fire tower, the tower goes up for sale as surplus property.[^2] The high bidder is expected to take the tower apart and move it to a new site, and buyers usually bring their own crane and flatbed to take it down in sections.[^2] That is the process the state describes today, not a record of what happened at Wildwood.[^2]

The listing says nothing about what, if anything, remains at the Wildwood site today, or whether the tower was taken down after the 2008 sale.[^1]

[^1]: Wildwood - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts website, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/wildwood1.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Surplus Equipment and Fire Towers, Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services, https://www.fdacs.gov/Forest-Wildfire/For-Communities/Surplus-Equipment-and-Fire-Towers (accessed 2026-10-21).
