The Flag Staff tower was a steel lookout on Flagstaff Mountain above Mauch Chunk, in Carbon County.[^2] The Anthracite Forest Protective Association finished erecting it in 1922, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives the same build year.[^1][^2]

Sources disagree on its height. An April 1922 Wilkes-Barre Record item put the new steel tower at fifty feet.[^2] A Forest Leaves note from the same year gave the tower as 42 feet, with an enclosed cab.[^2]

The register says the tower was removed in 1929.[^1] It was added to the register on September 16, 2008.[^1]

We found no source that describes the site today, or whether it is open to visitors. The one landmark we found for the site is Flag Staff Park, and the Forest Leaves note puts the tower 1,500 feet west of it.[^2]
