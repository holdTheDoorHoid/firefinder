Oreton, in Vinton County, Ohio, was a mining town tied to the New York Coal Company.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association registers the Oreton Tower Site as US 1581, OH 27, at an elevation of 960 feet (293 meters), with a registration date of September 3, 2014.[^1]

The association's Ohio list marks the tower as gone.[^2]

A route guide says a short, dead-end gravel road in Vinton Furnace State Forest once led to the Oreton Lookout Tower.[^4] A Find a Grave page for Burns Cemetery places a gate near the location of the "defunct Oreton fire tower" in the Vinton Experimental Furnace.[^5]

The same route guide, citing the Ohio Department of Natural Resources, says the state forest is open to visitors daily from 6 am to 11 pm.[^4] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the tower site, or whether it can be reached on foot or by car.[^1][^2]
