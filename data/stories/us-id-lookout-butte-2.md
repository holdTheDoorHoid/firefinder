Lookout Butte in Clark County, Idaho, is listed under the Caribou-Targhee National Forest.[^1] Its location code is 10N-42E-12.[^1]

Another Lookout Butte exists in Idaho. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list places one in Idaho County, at 46.04591, -115.65941, and marks it as a standing tower.[^2] Check the county and coordinates before you search for this one, since the two names are the same.

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Clark County site today, or whether visitors can reach it. Ask the Caribou-Targhee National Forest about access before planning a trip.

[^2]: Idaho Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/id (accessed 2026-10-21).
