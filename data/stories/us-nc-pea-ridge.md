The Pea Ridge lookout tower is in Polk County, North Carolina.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts index files it under the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources.[^1] A North Carolina tower list gives its coordinates as 35.33277 and -82.12472.[^4] The same list draws on North Carolina fire towers but says it may include a few observation towers.[^4] TopoQuest gives the site an elevation of 1,112 feet (339 m) and says the USGS 1:24,000 topographic map Pea Ridge, NC shows it.[^2] OpenStreetMap, a volunteer-edited map, tags the spot as a lattice observation tower.[^3]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, so its condition and whether the public may visit are unknown.
