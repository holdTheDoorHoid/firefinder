Harvey Creek Lookout Site sits in the Colville National Forest, in Pend Oreille County, Washington, about nine miles east of Ione.[^1][^2] Its elevation is 5,921 feet, or 1,805 meters.[^1]

The site had a platform tower and a camp by 1937.[^1][^2][^3] WillhiteWeb says the site was used for only a short time and had few improvements.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the U.S. Forest Service as its administrator.[^1]

The lookout fell out of use in the 1940s.[^1][^2][^3]

The register says the lookout is gone.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the ground now. WillhiteWeb also reports that the author found no USGS map that shows the site.[^3] On Metsker maps, Harvey Mountain and an old trail appear, with no lookout marked.[^3]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site on April 17, 2020, as US 2025 and WA 496, on the nomination of Tammy McLeod.[^1]
