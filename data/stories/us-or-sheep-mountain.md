The lookout stood on Sheep Mountain, at 7,728 feet (2,355 meters), in the Malheur National Forest of Grant County, Oregon.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the site as US 1090 and records its registration on July 16, 2011.[^1]

A 1932 item quoted on the Forest Lookouts page planned a new lookout house on the mountain, along with a telephone line.[^2] The tower that followed was built in 1933: a 20-foot pole with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2] The firelookout.com page gives the pole as 15 feet instead.[^3] We have used the 20-foot figure, which the register and the Forest Lookouts page both give.[^1][^2]

The Forest Lookouts page records a few staffing snapshots.[^2] CCC men staffed the lookout in 1936.[^2] In 1939 Max O'Brien, the telephone operator, was stationed there, and Mrs. O'Brien was in charge of the switchboard.[^2] In July 1953 Mr. and Mrs. Dick Martin and Mike were stationed at the lookout.[^2]

A 1953 entry on the same page says a new wooden tower had been set up beneath the existing house, after the old tower was "stubbed" in 1947.[^2]

The register says the tower is now gone.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page marks it as removed, but records no date for that.[^2]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts page quotes a survey description, determined in 1933, which says a Forest Service disk marks the point, sunk in concrete just beneath the tower's center. It does not say whether that disk survives.[^2] No source we read says whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
