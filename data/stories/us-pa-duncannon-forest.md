Built in the 1940s, the tower began as a tourist attraction beside a restaurant on the far side of the Susquehanna River, at Water Gap in Dauphin County.[^1][^2][^4] It stands about 110 feet (33.5 m) tall.[^1][^3][^4] Its name suggests a fire lookout, and Wikidata does list it as one, but the travel and hiking pages describe a tourist tower from the start.[^1][^2][^3]

Road work on routes 322 and 22 put the tower in line for demolition.[^1][^2] The owner of the Old Sled Works decided to save it, and it was taken apart and moved to the business's property in Duncannon, Perry County.[^1][^2] French Wikipedia gives 1999 as the year of that move.[^4]

Today the Old Sled Works says it is permanently closed.[^5] When the shop was open, visitors could pay a small fee to climb the tower, and the Appalachian Trail runs two blocks away.[^1] The sources do not say whether the tower can be climbed now or what condition it is in, so treat it as closed to climbers for now.
