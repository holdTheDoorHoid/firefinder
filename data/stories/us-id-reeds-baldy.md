Reeds Baldy lies in the Saint Joe Mountains of Benewah County, Idaho.[^4] The Forest Lookouts site for Idaho lists it as a patrol lookout and gives its survey location as 47N-1E-31.[^1]

A page about the St. Joe Baldy lookout on firelookout.com says there were patrol points on nearby peaks, one of them atop Reeds Baldy, 2 miles northeast of St. Joe Baldy.[^2][^3]

The two sources differ on its elevation. The Idaho climbing guide gives 6,153 ft, while PeakVisor gives 6,132 ft (1,869 m).[^3][^4]

Today the climbing guide describes a summit route along an unmaintained ridge that begins on the road north of Saint Joe Baldy. It says the trail was once maintained as far as Latour Peak.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
