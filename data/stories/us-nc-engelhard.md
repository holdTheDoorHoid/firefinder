The earliest record of the Engelhard state fire tower that I found is a newspaper report from April 1956, quoted on the Eastern US Lookouts page for the site.[^1] It describes about 4,000 acres of woodland burned over in Hyde County on a Sunday afternoon.[^1] Gale winds drove the blaze northeast from the tower's vicinity toward Swan Lake, covering some four and a half miles.[^1] The paper says heavy rain that came after the gale winds spared many thousands more acres from burning.[^1]

The North Carolina Digital Collections holds a record titled "Fire Tower at Englehard."[^2] Its title spells the name as Englehard, with the e and l swapped, and the page I could read gave no date or description.[^2] The tnlandforms list places Engelhard in Hyde County.[^3]

No source I found describes the tower or its site today, so I cannot say whether anything of it remains, or whether the public may visit.
