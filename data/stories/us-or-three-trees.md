Three Trees was a lookout in the Siskiyou National Forest, in Curry County, Oregon, about 15 miles north-northeast of Brookings.[^1][^2] The first cab, a 10-by-10-foot L-5, went up in 1934.[^2][^3]

According to the register, the 1934 cab gave way in 1940 to an L-4 tower set on a spur roughly a mile west-southwest of the summit.[^1] Rex's Fire Lookout Page gives the same two dates.[^2] In 1941 the site was manned for 84 days, and reports went by telephone through West Coast Telephone Company to the Gold Beach station.[^3]

The tower was used in an emergency in 1965.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts lists the site as removed, without a date.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list marks the tower as gone.[^4]

The register recorded the site on April 21, 2011, on the nomination of Ray Kresek. It gives the elevation as 2,960 feet, or 902 meters.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
