Tatakose appears in the Firetowers of Saskatchewan survey, which places it at about 54.56 N, 109.60 W.[^1] Canada's Fire Lookouts lists the name in its Saskatchewan index.[^2]

A towermen list on the survey ties one observer to Tatakose from 1989 to 2002. The site name carries a question mark in that entry, which also notes that the observer went to aerial detection.[^3]

In April 2013 Wildfire Today reported that the Saskatchewan Ministry of Environment was shifting its lookouts from human observers to cameras.[^4] A union release reported a March announcement that observers would come off 42 towers before the 2014 fire season.[^5] None of these 2013 reports names Tatakose among the affected towers.

Today the Saskatchewan Public Safety Agency says the province has 42 wildfire detection towers, each with a camera.[^6] No source describes the Tatakose site today, or says whether the public can reach it.
