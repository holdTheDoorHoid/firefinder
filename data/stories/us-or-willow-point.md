The Willow Point site lies in the Fremont National Forest in Lake County, about 10 miles southeast of Lakeview.[^1][^2] An earlier 20-foot wooden tower and ground cabin came first, in the 1930s.[^1]

Sources do not agree on when the 25-foot L-4 tower went up.[^1][^2][^3] The register and firelookout.com date it to 1940.[^1][^2] The Oregon lookouts site says the material had been bought by November 24, 1932, and that the tower was completed in 1933.[^3]

In 1940 the lookout became a fire-weather station. Observations were taken at 8 a.m., noon and 4:30 p.m., and the readings were phoned to the Lakeview Ranger Station at 4:30 each day.[^3]

In 1952 a new 10-foot tower went up on Crane Mountain, about a mile west. It replaced the Willow Point lookout, and the garage was moved there.[^1][^4][^3]

The Willow Point tower came down in 1953. The register and firelookout.com say it was destroyed, and the Oregon lookouts site says it was removed.[^1][^2][^3] The register also gives a removal date of 1972 without saying what came down, and firelookout.com says destruction came in 1972 without naming the structure.[^1][^2]

None of the sources I read describes what, if anything, is left at the Willow Point site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
