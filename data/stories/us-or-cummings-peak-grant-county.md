Cummings Peak is in Grant County, Oregon, about 11 miles west-northwest of Mt. Vernon.[^2] The site sits at 3,939 feet, or 1,201 meters.[^1]

The Oregon Department of Forestry administered the site, according to the Forest Fire Lookout Association.[^1] The Oregon Lookouts page heads its entry Cummings Creek, although its own site list also shows Cummings Peak.[^3]

A cabin went up on the site in the 1930s, according to the Forest Fire Lookout Association.[^1] Firelookout.com also gives the 1930s and a cabin, without a more exact year.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association says the cabin was abandoned and no longer stands.[^1] Firelookout.com lists the cabin as abandoned.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association registered the site on November 1, 2019, at the nomination of Tammy McLeod, as US 1777 and OR 517.[^1]

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it. Ask the Oregon Department of Forestry before planning a trip.
