The DeSoto tower stood in Becker County, just south of Itasca State Park.[^1][^3] The Becker County survey describes it as an 86-foot steel tower.[^3] The survey says the tower was named for an early explorer, spelled DeSota there.[^3]

A forestry record refers to it as tower #70, in the Park Rapids district.[^3] The survey also cites a 1936 newspaper article about fire detection near Itasca State Park that mentioned the tower.[^3]

The Central US Lookouts page says the tower was removed, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone.[^1][^2] According to an old-timer cited in the survey, state rangers dismantled the tower so it could be moved elsewhere.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public.
