Dooly County, Georgia, is home to Richwood, an unincorporated community.[^1] It started as a lumber town, and its name probably reflects the dense woodland that once covered the spot.[^1] A post office there operated from 1887 until 1947.[^1] The New Georgia Encyclopedia's Dooly County entry lists Richwood among the county's communities.[^2]

A list of Georgia lookout towers on tnlandforms.us includes a tower it calls "richwood" in Dooly County.[^3] Its entry gives an elevation of 363, with no unit stated, and two codes: "BLL-41114" and "gfc-330".[^3] The list is a Google map that its header dates to a 2016 revision.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today.
