The Duck Lake lookout was an alidade and log cabin about half a mile west of the lake.[^1][^2] It stood about 12 miles northwest of McCall in Valley County, Idaho, on Payette National Forest land.[^1][^3] The record dates the cabin to 1920, and all three lookout pages agree that it was abandoned in 1923.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the site as a ground site that is gone.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts labels it hike-in.[^2]

The Forest Service describes a trailhead on Lick Creek Road, about 18 miles from McCall.[^5] From that trailhead, the walk to the lake is a little under a mile and a half each way.[^5] The agency also cautions drivers to take care on the narrow, rough road.[^5]

None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, remains at the old site, which is half a mile west of the lake.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
