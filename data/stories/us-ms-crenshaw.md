The Crenshaw Lookout Tower is a 100-foot Aermotor tower on the Sanders family's property outside Crenshaw, in Panola County, Mississippi.[^1] The register puts its site at about 362 feet, or 110 meters.[^1] It gives no build date, and we found none in the other sources we read.[^1]

Aermotor, the maker the register names, also made fire lookout towers from galvanized steel.[^2] Wikipedia says hundreds of one 7-by-7 model were used in the southeastern United States, but the register does not say whether this tower is that model.[^2][^1]

The tower did not begin on this land. Before it came here, the tower stood some miles to the southeast, the register notes.[^1] In 1991 the Sanders family bought it from the Mississippi Forestry Commission for $105.05.[^1] That state agency was created in 1926 and was authorized to prevent, control and extinguish forest fires.[^3] At first the family considered reselling the tower as scrap, but decided instead to put it up on their own property.[^1] The register also says the original footings were reused at the new site.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register listed it as US 1604, MS 9 on June 19, 2022, on the nomination of Jason Johns.[^1]

The register lists the location as private property.[^1] It describes no public access, and it does not say who has staffed the tower or which fires it watched.[^1] Anyone interested should ask the family before going near it, and should not climb it.
