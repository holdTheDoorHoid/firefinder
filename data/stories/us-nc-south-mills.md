South Mills is a lookout tower site in Pasquotank County, North Carolina, listed with coordinates on a North Carolina lookout-tower list published by tnlandforms.us.[^1] The Forest Lookouts website says the State Forest Service erected a 99-foot, 9-inch tower there in 1944, with co-operation from the U.S. Forest Service.[^2] That entry does not say whether the 1944 tower is the one on the site now.[^2]

A history of forestry in Wake County describes a 120-foot steel tower at Knightdale that was erected in 1938.[^3][^4] Forest Lookouts gives the same erection date and says the Civilian Conservation Corps put it up.[^4] In 1971 the house and site at Knightdale were sold.[^3] In 1972 the tower was removed and stored in Clayton, and it was later moved to South Mills.[^3]

A Facebook post says the South Mill tower was built in 1972 and stands 120 feet tall.[^5] The post's 1972 is the year the Knightdale tower was taken down, while both histories give 1938 as its erection date. If the post means that same tower, the sources conflict on its date, and this story follows the histories.[^3][^4]

On March 22, 2018, bidding opened at an auction of state surplus property.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page places the tower on state-owned land.[^2]

The Facebook post is the only source we read that says in so many words that a tower stands there now.[^5] None of the sources we read says whether visitors may go there.
