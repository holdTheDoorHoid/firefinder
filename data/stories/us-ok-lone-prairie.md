Lone Prairie is a fire lookout tower in Delaware County, Oklahoma, listed on the Forest Lookouts site.[^1]

A USGS topographic map sheet, with a date portrayed of 1971 and a published date of 1972, marks a Lone Prairie Lookout Tower among its landmarks. The sheet covers a Delaware County survey area, with Lake Eucha as its most prominent feature to the north.[^2] The map-history site that reproduces the sheet describes the tower as overlooking Elkhorn Prairie.[^2]

A Joplin News Past Facebook post is titled "Long Prairie Fire Tower going up!" and may describe this tower under another spelling.[^3]

The Forest Lookouts page gives the tower's name and county, but no source I read describes what remains at the site today.[^1]
