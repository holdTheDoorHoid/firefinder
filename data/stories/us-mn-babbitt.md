The Babbitt Lookout is in Saint Louis County, and the Minnesota lookout list marks it as standing.[^3] The county's list of fire towers, a PDF kept on Wikimedia Commons, describes its cab as octagonal.[^1] That list also says the tower appeared on Superior National Forest maps from 1928 to 1966.[^1] A map listing is not a construction date, though, and we did not find a source that says when the tower was built or who built it.

A National Geodetic Survey station description written in 1952 also names a "Babbitt Lookout Tower," which suggests the structure stood by then.[^2] The county list's owner column reads "USFS?" with a question mark, so ownership is not settled in our sources.[^1]

The sources disagree about its status. The county note says "Standing" near the top but ends with "Removed," and gives no date for either.[^1] The Minnesota list still marks it as standing.[^3] We found no record of a fire or a rebuilding, and no current access notice.

Because the status is disputed, check with whoever manages the site before you visit, and do not climb the tower unless it is open to the public.

[^2]: Babbitt Lookout Tower, National Geodetic Survey station description (PID SH0813), Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/babbitt.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
