Dam Ridge Lookout stood in the St. Joe National Forest.[^1] Its L-4 tower went up in 1934.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the site's elevation as 5,050 feet and calls it a hike-in site.[^2] The page says it has no documented staffing history for the lookout.[^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1954.[^1][^2] Both lookout-history pages give the same 1934 and 1954 dates.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list places the site in Shoshone County and marks the tower as gone. It gives the site's coordinates as 47.40417 and -115.97206.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it. Anyone planning a trip should check with the St. Joe National Forest first.
