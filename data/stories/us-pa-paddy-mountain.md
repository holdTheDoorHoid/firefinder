Paddy Mountain in Centre County had a fire observation tower that the register calls a tree tower. The register dates it to 1910 and says it was still in use around 1917.[^1] A 1910 entry on the Eastern US Forest Lookouts site puts the viewing platform 25 feet off the ground and cites a Department of Forestry report dated December 31, 1917.[^2]

The same site quotes the Pennsylvania Department of Forestry's report for 1910 and 1911, which says two fire observation towers went up, one of them atop Paddy Mountain.[^2]

The register says the tower is no longer standing.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the ground is open to visitors. Check who owns the land before you go looking for it.

[^1]: Paddy Mountain Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites register, Forest Fire Lookout Association, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/pa/paddy-mountain-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Paddy Mountain - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Eastern US Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/paddy-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
