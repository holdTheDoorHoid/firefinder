The Little Switzerland Lookout is a 93-foot tower standing on the Little Switzerland ski hill in Slinger, Washington County, Wisconsin.[^1][^2]

According to the National Historic Lookout Register, the tower was built by the Wisconsin Conservation Corps and moved to its present site in 1935.[^1] The Register thinks it is most likely an Aermotor LS-40 and says it has an internal stairway.[^1]

A Tower Heritage Center article says motorcycle races ran on the hill from 1938 to 1940, before the ski hill was established on December 7, 1941.[^3] Wikipedia gives December 7, 1941 as the opening date.[^2] A 2019 Milwaukee Journal Sentinel article also gives 1935 as the move year.[^4] That is before the ski hill was established, and no source read says where the tower stood before it moved, so the date is worth checking before anyone repeats it.[^1]

The sources read say nothing about who staffed the tower or what fires it watched.

Firelookout's Wisconsin list still shows the tower as standing.[^5] Its original owners ran the ski area until March 10, 2007.[^2] After a full remodel in 2012, new owners reopened it that fall as a year-round restaurant with winter skiing.[^2]

The Register names Little Switzerland Ski Hill as the tower's administrator.[^1] A 2019 Journal Sentinel article calls the Mountain Fire Lookout Tower the only Wisconsin fire tower open to the general public for climbing and says nothing about climbing at this one, so anyone who wants to see this tower up close should ask the ski hill first.[^4]
