Cascade Point Lookout sat in the St. Joe National Forest, about 1.5 miles west of Cascade Peak and eight miles east of Red Ives G.S., in Shoshone County, Idaho.[^1]

Its listed elevation is 6,084 feet.[^1][^2]

The lookout went up in 1935 as a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2] One county list gives the pole as 20 feet, so the height is in dispute.[^4]

It was destroyed in 1961, according to the firelookout.com and Idaho pages.[^1][^2] A county list also has it gone by 1961,[^4] and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the tower as gone.[^3]

The Idaho site labels the location a drive-up site.[^2] None of the sources describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public.
