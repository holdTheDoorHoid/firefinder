Vie Mountain rises to about 7,360 feet in Chelan County, on the Wenatchee National Forest.[^1][^2] Panorama photos taken on October 3, 1934 came before the cabin, and Willhite reads them as showing a camp.[^2][^3] The L-5 cab was built in 1935.[^1][^4][^2]

The Forest Service burned the lookout in 1953, according to Willhite and Forest Lookouts.[^2][^3] The register lists the cab as removed that year, not burned.[^1] Firelookout.com says it was destroyed.[^4] Willhite reports that the stove pipe, roof cap and hardware remain at the site and have not moved since the fire.[^2]

The site is on Forest Service land.[^1] Willhite describes a long, scary dirt approach road, then a hike of 7.5 miles or more each way.[^2] Some trees have grown over the site's views, though a short walk to the high point still offers plenty of them.[^2] Willhite also reports that fires destroyed the trails, which have since all but disappeared.[^2]

The Register recorded the site as US 1429, WA 329 on November 9, 2012.[^1]
