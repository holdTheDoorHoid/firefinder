Pike Mountain rises to 7,710 feet in Cassia County, Idaho, about 26 miles southeast of Twin Falls.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the Sawtooth National Forest as the agency, while the 1941 newspapers name the Minidoka National Forest.[^1][^3]

A CCC work list from April 1941 included a fire lookout on top of Pike Mountain.[^3] A notice dated July 11, 1941 said a shelter, a telephone and a fire finder would go up there, with a ranger or CCC enrollee on duty that summer.[^3] A July 23, 1941 newspaper report said the lookout was fully set up and in operation.[^3]

The firelookout.com listing also gives a camp at the site in 1935, several years before those accounts. The sources do not explain that entry.[^1]

A newspaper report dated July 30, 1941 says three lightning fires on a Sunday evening burned more than 80 acres of Minidoka forest land in the west Cassia division.[^3] The report also says this blaze was the first the new lookout sent to the local office.[^3] In December 1960 a newspaper reported that two emergency lookouts had been manned that fire season, one of them on Pike peak in the Magic Mountain area, which may be this site.[^3]

A 1961 entry lists the lookout as a trailer.[^1][^3] The sources list the site as gone.[^1][^2][^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site now. Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under drive-up.[^2]
