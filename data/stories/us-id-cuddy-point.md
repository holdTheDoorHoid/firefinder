Cuddy Point Lookout stood on the Payette National Forest in Washington County, Idaho, about 16 miles west-northwest of Council.[^1] The site sits at an elevation of 7,715 feet.[^1][^2] The Payette National Forest is listed as its agency.[^1][^2][^3]

Around 1932 the lookout took the form of a cabin with a roof platform.[^1][^2][^3] It is recorded as a ground lookout rather than a tower.[^4] The only named staff member in these records is William Davis, listed for 1942.[^1][^2] The lookout was last staffed in 1944.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the site as gone.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site a hike-in.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the site today, and none says whether visitors are allowed there. Anyone planning a trip should ask the Payette National Forest about access before setting out.
