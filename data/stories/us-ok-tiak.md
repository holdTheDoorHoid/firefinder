The Forest Lookouts site lists a lookout called Tiak in McCurtain County, Oklahoma, with the Ouachita National Forest named on the same entry.[^1] Mapcarta, a map site, places a tower of that name in McCurtain County and puts the town of Haworth 3½ miles east of it.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register names a Tiak Ranger District as one of the two administrators of the Jadie Tower, a different lookout.[^4] The register lists five registered lookouts in Oklahoma, and Tiak is not among them.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit. Ask the land manager before going, and do not climb a tower that is closed or private.
