Fitzwater Point is in the Fremont National Forest in Lake County, about 17 miles southwest of Lakeview.[^1][^2] A Civilian Conservation Corps camp at Dog Lake listed a 1934 project to build a lookout here.[^3] The cab that ended up on the point, though, was built in 1934 at Coleman Point, where a crow's nest platform had come first.[^4] It was moved to Fitzwater Point in 1948 and rebuilt on a rock foundation.[^3][^4] The register gives 1949 for the move.[^1]

A 1956 record gives the station's air marking as F-21.[^3] A 1960 newspaper item named James Jacobsgaard, of Holland, Michigan, as a lookout new to the job.[^3]

The register says the cab was gone by 1967, and firelookout.com gives the same year.[^1][^2] A letter from the Fremont National Forest to the regional office, dated May 13 and listed under 1968, said that "Fitzwater Point has been burned."[^3] The register does not say the cab burned, and its 1967 date falls a year before the letter, so the accounts do not agree on how the cab ended.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3] The house sat on a rock foundation, but no source says whether any of it is still there.[^3]
