Horse Creek was a platform rather than a tower, in Alexander County, Illinois.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association files it under lookout sites and non-lookout structures, and gives its coordinates as 37.27056 north and 89.38889 west.[^1][^2] The Association lists the site as gone.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.

[^2]: Illinois Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/il/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
