Frenchman Butte, in the Clearwater National Forest, once carried an L-4 cabin on a pole tower.[^1] Three sources give 1934 and a 40-foot tower.[^1][^2][^3] A 1981 history of the Lochsa Ranger District, quoted on a lookout-history website, instead gives 1933 and a 50-foot tower, built by Earnest Hartman and Richard Stadtman at a cost of $915.[^4] The sources disagree on the year and the height, and this story cannot settle which is right.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Seasonal use continued until the late 1940s or early 1950s, after which the site came off the fire plan.[^4] The Forest Service list says it was abandoned in the 1940s.[^3] A garage added to the site in 1936 burned down around 1958.[^4]

Its end came around 1960.[^3][^4] The 1981 history says the stairway had become unsafe by 1960, so the tower was knocked over and burned after its lightning protection had been salvaged.[^4] The Forest Service list says it was destroyed in 1960.[^3] The firelookout.com page and the Idaho Fire Lookouts page both say it was gone by 1963.[^1][^2]

Today, the Idaho Fire Lookouts page labels the site drive-up, but it says nothing about what is left there.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site now.
