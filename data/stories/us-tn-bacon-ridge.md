The Bacon Ridge tower stood in Roane County, at an elevation of 1,385 feet.[^1][^2] Its code on the tnlandforms list is 2-19, and the same page says codes like this are district and tower numbers from a 1976 list of state forest towers.[^1] The page also says the state's Division of Forestry had 208 tower sites around 1976.[^1]

The tnlandforms list marks the tower as removed.[^1] According to the same list, many Tennessee towers have been taken down, and the sites of most of them now belong to private owners.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the Bacon Ridge site, or whether the public can reach it, so please ask the landowner before visiting.

[^1]: Tennessee lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Bacon Ridge, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/bacon-ridge.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
