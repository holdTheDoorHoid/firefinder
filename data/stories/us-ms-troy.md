The Mississippi Forestry Commission put up a standard Aeromotor tower, 100 to 150 feet tall, in Pontotoc County, Mississippi, in the 1930s.[^1] It stood in the middle of a pasture, half a mile north of where Goodfood Road meets Toxish Road.[^1]

Looking north from the site, the Pontotoc skyline could be seen 8.08 miles away at ground level, and at night the town's street lights and radio towers were visible.[^1] The register lists it as US 694, MS 1 and records its listing on October 19, 2010, nominated by Danny White.[^1]

The register describes the tower in the past tense, so it appears to be gone.[^1] It does not say what, if anything, remains in the pasture today or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Troy Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ms/troy-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
