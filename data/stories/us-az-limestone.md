Limestone Lookout stands on the Fort Apache Indian Reservation in Navajo County, and the White Mountain Apache Agency administers it.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register says it was built in 1936 as a 123-foot Aermotor tower with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab.[^1] The register also calls it the tallest fire tower in Arizona. I could not check that ranking against a second source, so treat it as the register's claim.[^1]

According to the register, the lookout remains active, and the same site also serves as a helitack crew base.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Arizona list still shows the tower as standing.[^3]

The register entry is dated February 18, 1997.[^1] A West Lookouts history page records that an October 2016 investigation into reported vandalism and looting at a lookout tower found the property to be Limestone, and that the Bureau of Indian Affairs would decide what action to take.[^2] I found no later report of that decision.

Limestone is on tribal land, and I found no public visiting information for it.[^1] Anyone curious about a visit should ask the White Mountain Apache Tribe before going, and should not treat the tower as open to climb.
