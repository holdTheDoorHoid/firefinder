Firelookout.ca lists a Pickle Lake Fire Tower with a Pickle Lake, Ontario address.[^1] Two visitor notes on that entry disagree about what is there.[^1]

One note, from a person who says they work out of Pickle Lake, reports that the tower is definitely gone. That writer says they compared historical maps showing the tower's location and has worked in Pickle Lake since 2022 without seeing a tower on that spot.[^1] Another note says the tower is still standing, but that the pinned location is almost certainly wrong. It places the tower on a ridge near that point, though the writer does not know exactly where.[^1]

A volunteer list of still-standing towers includes a Pickle Lake Tower, with the note that it was removed from Atikokan.[^2] That note suggests the tower was moved from Atikokan to Pickle Lake.[^2] No source found says what, if anything, remains at the site today.

[^1]: Pickle Lake Fire Tower, Firelookout.ca directory entry, https://firelookout.ca/lookouts/?slug=pickle-lake-fire-tower (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Ontario's Fire Tower Lookouts, still-standing towers list, forestrangers.github.io, https://forestrangers.github.io/ontariofiretowers/stillstanding2.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
