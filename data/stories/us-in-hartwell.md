Hartwell Fire Tower stands in Pike State Forest, in Pike County, Indiana, and overlooks the Patoka River valley.[^1] A federal survey description from 1946 calls it a silver, four-leg steel tower with a cabin at the top, about 100 feet high.[^2]

Its build date is not settled. The Hartwell page at Indiana Fire Towers gives a window of 1935 to 1952 and says the tower replaced an earlier one south of Highway 64.[^3] The same site's history page says the Hartwell tower was completed in 1935.[^4] A structural survey note says it was constructed before 1952.[^3] A 1936 Pike County map shows an earlier fire tower off old I-64, southwest of Augusta.[^3]

Michael Crews, an interpretive naturalist at Lincoln State Park, says the Lincoln, Ferdinand and Hartwell towers worked together to triangulate fires.[^5] I found no source that names who staffed Hartwell or when it last watched for fires.

The register's description is in the present tense, and I found no source reporting that the tower was removed or moved.[^1]

The register records a December 15, 2014 registration as US 1050, IN 9, nominated by Floyd Knight.[^1] It lists the Indiana Division of Forestry as the administrator.[^1] A June 2022 Pike State Forest map, as read by Indiana Fire Towers, marks the tower closed.[^3] I found no source that says whether it is open to visitors, so treat it as closed and do not climb it.
