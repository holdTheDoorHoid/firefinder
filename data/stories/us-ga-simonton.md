A list of Georgia lookout towers places a tower named simonton in Gwinnett County, at about 33.93 degrees north and 83.96 degrees west.[^1] The list gives its elevation as 1,121 with no unit printed; read as feet, that is about 342 m.[^1]

The list's map notes say that towers 1 to 72 come from a USGS name database, and that the rest were found on 1:100,000 USGS topographic maps and NGS data sheets.[^1] Simonton's entry carries the note "datasheet DF2743".[^1]

For towers 73 and above, the page says the names are the compiler's own creation. This tower is number 112, so "simonton" is the list's own label for it.[^1]

The page's map was revised on 22 March 2016, so the list is more than ten years old.[^1]

No source we found describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything remains there or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
