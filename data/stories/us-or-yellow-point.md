The firelookout.com listing places Yellow Point in Josephine County, Oregon, about 28 miles southwest of Eugene, at an elevation of 1,665 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association index also lists it in Josephine County.[^3] The Forest Lookouts county index, however, files it under Douglas County, and its page names no county.[^2] The Western Lane FPA is credited with the site.[^1][^2]

The firelookout.com listing describes a cabin here from the 1930s, which it says was abandoned in 1953.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page tells a different story. In May 1943 the Fourth Fighter Command approved Yellow Point as an observation post for the Aircraft Warning Service.[^2] A memorandum gave it the code name Eugene 69, to change to Charlie Zero Seven after June 1.[^2] The page's header gives May 29 as the activation date, but the memorandum set noon on May 20.[^2] The Army deactivated the post at 1800 hours on October 16, 1943.[^2]

In 1944 the Western Lane FPA built a tree platform at the point, at a cost of $14.65.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association index lists the site as ground-level and gone.[^3] The Forest Lookouts page marks it as removed.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether it is open to the public.
