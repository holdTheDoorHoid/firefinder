Buck Mountain is in the Kaniksu National Forest, 11 miles southeast of Bonners Ferry in Boundary County, at 5,600 feet.[^1]

The sources do not agree on when the first lookout was built. The firelookout.com page describes a log cupola cabin from the 1920s.[^1] A Pend Oreille Review item dated August 1, 1929, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, says the lookout was partly complete by then.[^3] A 1962 Sandpoint News Bulletin report, also quoted there, says the six lookouts it names, Buck Mountain among them, were built in the 1930s by the Civilian Conservation Corps.[^3]

The firelookout.com page names Pat Parsons as known lookout personnel in 1933.[^1] It gives 1962 as the year the lookout was abandoned.[^1] The 1962 report says the lookouts had gone unused and unmaintained since 1950, the year the agency began relying more on aerial patrols.[^3]

The sources disagree on what survived. The firelookout.com page says a 30-foot pole platform from the 1940s was still there,[^1] while the 1962 report describes the towers being toppled and burned.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site today.
