The Peshtigo Lookout Tower stood in Marinette County, as both the Forest Fire Lookout Association and the Forest Lookouts site list it.[^1][^2] OpenStreetMap records its elevation as 192 metres and tags the structure as an observation tower.[^3]

The tower shares its name with Peshtigo, the biggest town in the area the 1871 fire hit.[^4] The fire struck northeastern Wisconsin on October 8, 1871, and it ranks as the deadliest wildfire on record.[^4] This story covers the tower, not the fire.

Across the state, the Conservation Department set up an engineering division in October 1931 to design new towers. It began replacing the old windmill-type towers in 1932.[^5] Wisconsin retired its lookout towers in 2016 and stepped up aerial detection to back up citizen reports.[^5]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Peshtigo tower as gone, with coordinates 45.08833, -87.75731 and no National Historic Lookout Register (NHLR) or Former Fire Lookout Sites Register (FFLOS) number.[^1]

Nothing I read says what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
