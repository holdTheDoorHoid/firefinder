The Greenfield tower stands in Monroe County, Wisconsin, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as standing.[^1] A lookout-history site describes an approximately 84-foot steel tower, which is about 25.6 meters, and says it was erected in 1933.[^2] That is the only build date we found, and we could not confirm it from a second source.

The same site files the tower under the Wisconsin Department of Conservation.[^2] Its location line reads "18N-2W-9", which looks like a township, range and section in the federal land survey rather than an agency name.[^2] We have corrected the agency field in our records for that reason. The page names the agency but does not say who built the tower.[^2]

In the sources we read, we found no record of who staffed the tower or which fires it watched. The association's list gives no register number for it, and we found no later report on its condition.[^1]

We found no public access information and no record of a rental. Check with the land manager before going near the tower, and do not climb it.

[^2]: Greenfield - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/greenfield.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
