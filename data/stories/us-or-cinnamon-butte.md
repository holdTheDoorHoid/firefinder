Cinnamon Butte's lookout stands in the Umpqua National Forest, about 19 miles north of Crater Lake.[^3] The first lookout here was an L-4 cab on a timber tower, completed by the Civilian Conservation Corps in 1934.[^1][^2] Sources disagree on that tower's height: Forest Lookouts gives 35 feet, while firelookout.com and the register give 30.[^1][^2][^3]

In 1955 an R-6 tower was first erected on Buster Butte. In 1976 it was moved to Cinnamon Butte, where it still stands.[^1][^3][^4] The register gives the moved tower as 44 feet, while firelookout.com says 41.[^1][^3]

Staffing goes back a long way. A 1939 newspaper counted Cinnamon Butte among two manned lookouts in its district.[^2] By 1961 a Forest Service observer, Alan Madson, had been posted there for five summers.[^2] The register lists the site as registered on March 31, 2003.[^1]

Firelookout.com says the lookout is staffed in the summer.[^3] A 2023 visitor reported a road climbing to the summit, with a 1.5-mile dirt stretch leading up from a gravel road.[^4] That visitor also wrote that the lookout gave permission to climb, so ask the staff before going up.[^4] The register says the lookout remains active.[^1]
