Grand Ridge is listed as a fire lookout in Jackson County, Florida.[^1] The Forest Lookouts website that holds the listing gives the county and nothing else for the site.[^1] The town of Grand Ridge lies in the same county and had 882 residents at the 2020 census.[^2]

No source found describes the site today. It is not known whether a tower, footings or nothing at all remains there, and no source says whether the land is open to the public.

Anyone planning a trip should ask the land manager about access first, and should not climb any tower without permission.
