Black Mare is a lookout site in Valley County, Idaho, on the Payette National Forest, about 16 miles southeast of McCall.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 8,724 feet.[^1] Keeping Watch, from the Center for Digital Inquiry and Learning, gives the coordinates as 44.8201333, -115.7892.[^4]

A log cabin lookout is recorded at the site in 1925.[^1][^3]

An August 1944 Salt Lake Tribune item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, said one family was covering two lookouts.[^3] It named a woman and her son who watched for fires from Black Mare.[^3]

The lookout was abandoned in 1946.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both describe the site as ruins, and Idaho Fire Lookouts calls it a hike-in site.[^1][^2]

We found no source that describes what is left at the site today, such as foundations or cabin walls. No source we read says whether the public may visit, so check with the Payette National Forest before you go.[^1][^2][^3]
