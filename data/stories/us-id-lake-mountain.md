Lake Mountain stands about 14 miles southwest of Salmon, in Lemhi County, Idaho, on the Salmon National Forest.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts says the lookout is located at the head of Lake Creek, south of Williams Creek.[^3]

The site had a camp in 1918.[^1] A 1923 Salmon Herald item says a trapper, Paul Penslin, had gone up to the lookout for the fire season to assist firefighters.[^3] By 1924 a telephone line connected the lookout with Salmon.[^3]

Sources disagree on the first log cabin.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com gives 1928, while Forest Lookouts lists a flat-top log cabin in 1931.[^1][^3]

The L-4 cab's date also varies.[^1][^3] Forest Lookouts says an L-4 cab was added in 1934.[^3] Firelookout.com says the old cabin was converted to a garage in 1938, with an L-4 cab added on its roof.[^1]

In October 1938 a Recorder-Herald item, quoted by Forest Lookouts, reported that a CCC crew from the Forney camp had completed a new lookout building.[^3] The building stood on an eight-foot log base and had a railed platform all the way around.[^3]

Joseph Howard had charge of the station in August 1941.[^3] Edwin Peterson was the lookout in July 1951.[^3]

In September 1955 the Post-Register said the lookout was still occupied because it served as a relay station for the radio at Copper Creek Ranger Station.[^3]

In August 1973 vandals did extensive damage.[^3] The Recorder-Herald reported that the lookout had not been opened that season, that 14 windows were broken, and that the damage was estimated at $800.[^3]

Sources disagree on what happened next. Forest Lookouts says the lookout was removed in 1974.[^3] Firelookout.com says it was destroyed in 1974.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it as destroyed.[^2]

No source describes what remains on the summit today, or whether the public can reach it.
