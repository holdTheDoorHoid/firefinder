The Forest Lookouts index lists Aynor as a South Carolina Forestry Commission tower in Horry County.[^1][^2]

The commission's first fire tower went up in August 1930, near Camden.[^3] A large share of early towers used wooden beams, and steel later replaced every wooden one.[^3]

The towers were not normally staffed at night.[^3] The public reported fires to them, and they also kept field crews in touch.[^3] Starting in the 1960s, women filled most operator posts, and their husbands often worked as firefighters.[^3] Often a tower stayed in one family, with grown children replacing their parents on watch.[^3]

The Forestry Commission shut down its lookout network on October 1, 1993.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the Aynor site, or whether visitors can reach it.
