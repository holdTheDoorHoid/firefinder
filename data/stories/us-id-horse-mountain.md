The Horse Mountain lookout stood in the Payette National Forest, in Valley County, Idaho, 22 miles east of Big Creek G.S.[^1][^3]

Sources date its construction to 1934.[^1][^2][^3] Most describe a 10-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts describes a 10-foot log crib with an L-4 cab instead.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists Mr. and Mrs. Krause, newlyweds, as staff for 1956.[^2] The same page also lists two staff entries dated 2012. That is more than three decades after the tower was destroyed, so those entries cannot belong to this lookout, and we have left them out.[^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1978.[^1][^2][^3]

None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site a hike-in.[^2]

Do not confuse this tower with the other Horse Mountain Lookout, in Adams County, Idaho.[^4] The National Historic Lookout Register lists that one as US 1434, built in 1935 with a 30-foot open timber tower.[^4]
