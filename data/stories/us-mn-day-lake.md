Day Lake was a fire tower in Itasca County, Minnesota, in the Chippewa National Forest.[^1]

A Coast and Geodetic Survey description from 1934 refers to the tower, and a letter from the Forest Service confirmed that it was the Day Lake tower.[^1] The survey point was at the center of the tower, about 19 miles north-northwest of Grand Rapids.[^1]

A 1947 recovery note puts the height at roughly 100 feet. It describes steel angle iron with a wooden section on top, about 10 feet on a side.[^1] A Minnesota tower list gives the same height for a ladder tower with a square cab, lists it as an Aermotor, and calls it a CCC tower.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^2]

A roadside marker near Suomi describes the remains of the Day Lake CCC camp along Highway 38, including a concrete shower and an outdoor stone stairway and chimney.[^4] The marker does not mention the lookout.[^4] No source we read describes what remains of the lookout itself, or whether the site is open to the public.
