The Forest Lookouts list of Minnesota towers places a tower at Wagner Lake in Itasca County, at township 149 north, range 28 west, section 1.[^1] The same list dates the tower's removal to 1976.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list gives the site's coordinates as 47.75539 north, 94.16396 west and marks the tower as gone.[^2]

We found no source that describes the site now, so we cannot say what remains there or whether the ground is open to visitors.

[^2]: Minnesota fire lookout list, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
