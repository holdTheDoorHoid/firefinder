The Spalding Bay lookout sat 9 miles west-southwest of Moran Junction, at an elevation of 7,065 feet in Teton County, Wyoming.[^1] Forest Lookouts places it within Grand Teton National Park.[^2] Willhite Web counts three known former lookout sites inside the park boundary and lists Spalding Bay among them.[^3]

A Jackson's Hole Courier item dated July 30, 1942 says Fred Brown was on duty at the Spalding lookout for the Forest Service.[^2] The same item mentions his family at the lookout, where guests visited them on a Sunday.[^2]

Firelookout.com lists the lookout as gone, as does the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wyoming list, which gives its type as unknown.[^1][^4] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
