The Mellen Lookout Tower is in Ashland County, Wisconsin.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts site lists it under the county and nothing more, so it gives no build date, builder, design or staffing history.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing, at about 46.30 north and 90.62 west.[^2] Its list shows no National Historic Lookout Register number for the tower.[^2] OpenStreetMap records a tower named Mellen Lookout Tower at an elevation of 571 meters.[^3]

The sources we read do not say when the tower was built, what it looked like, who watched from it, or whether it ever spotted a fire. We did not find a Wisconsin Historical Society record for it, so its early history remains unknown.

Today the tower is listed as standing,[^2] but we found no information on public access or current condition. Before going near it, ask the land manager whether the tower is open. Do not climb it without clear permission.

[^2]: Wisconsin Lookouts (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/wi-co/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
