Pleasant Pond Mountain is in Somerset County, Maine, and rises to 2,460 feet.[^1] The Appalachian Trail runs over its summit.[^1]

The site's history starts in 1909, when a camp was established there, and a wooden lookout station stood at the site by 1910.[^1] A 24-foot steel tower went up in 1917, and the inventory for 1920 lists a 36-foot steel tower.[^1] A Maine Forestry Service panoramic map dated 1918 is centered on the Pleasant Pond Mt. tower and places it in Caratunk Plantation.[^3]

In 1951 the tower was taken down and re-erected on Barren Mountain, in Piscataquis County.[^1][^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the Pleasant Pond Mountain tower as gone.[^2]

Photographs on the site page show a lookout footing with a white blaze on it and two more footing locations where bolts were placed in the rocks, but the page does not say when they were taken.[^1] No source we read describes a structure on the summit now.

According to the Barren Mountain page, the tower itself still stands on Barren Mountain without its cab.[^4]
