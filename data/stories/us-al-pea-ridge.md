The Pea Ridge lookout site is in Shelby County, Alabama, at an elevation the register gives as 843 feet, or 257 meters.[^1] According to the register, Kimberly-Clarke first had this wooden tower built, and the State of Alabama later assumed control of it.[^1] The register describes it as a 60-foot pole tower and lists it as standing in 1971.[^1]

Andrew Zerbe nominated the site, and the register added it on July 24, 2016, as US 1623, AL 4.[^1] The register lists its coordinates as N 33.155000° W 086.936100°.[^1] The register names Mayfair Baptist Church as the site's administrator.[^1]

The register says the tower no longer remains.[^1] No source we read describes what is on the site now or whether visitors may go there, so check with Mayfair Baptist Church before going.

[^1]: Pea Ridge Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/al/pea-ridge-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
