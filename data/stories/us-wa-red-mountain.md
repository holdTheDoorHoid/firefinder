Red Mountain is a former lookout site in Kittitas County, Washington, in the Wenatchee National Forest.[^1] It sits at 5,703 feet, about 17 miles northwest of Cle Elum.[^1][^2]

The register says the lookout was built in 1932 with an L-5 cab.[^1] The other source calls it an L-6 cab, so the two disagree on the model.[^1][^2] Both say the cab was abandoned in 1948.[^1][^2]

Panoramic views labeled as Red Mountain Lookout and dated August 22, 1934 are credited to the National Archives Pacific Northwest Region.[^1] The register listed the site on October 28, 2012, on the nomination of Ray Kresek.[^1]

The register says the cab is now gone.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Washington index also lists the site as gone.[^3]

The register page includes a photo captioned "Debris."[^1] No source we read describes the site today or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]
