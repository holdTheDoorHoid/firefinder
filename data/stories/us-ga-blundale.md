Georgia's forest lookout listings include a tower at Blundale, in Emanuel County.[^1] A Georgia tower list places it at 32.75348° N, 82.3875° W and gives an elevation of 371, with no unit stated (about 113 m if feet). The same list carries the code gfc-112 for it.[^2]

Blundale is an unincorporated settlement in Emanuel County. A post office there operated from 1901 until 1951, and the Stillmore Air Line Railroad had a depot there.[^3]

No source I found describes the tower site today, so I cannot say what remains there or whether the public can reach it.[^1]
