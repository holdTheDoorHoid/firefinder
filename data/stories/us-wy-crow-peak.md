Ron Kemnow's Forest Lookouts site includes a Crow Peak lookout in Park County, on the Shoshone National Forest.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association places Crow Peak in its unknown or undocumented group, not among its lookouts. It gives the site coordinates of 44.51892, -109.94338.[^2] The association says it will move a site onto its lookout lists if evidence or documentation turns up showing regular use as a wildland fire lookout.[^2]

Crow's Nest is a different site, in Sublette County, which the same association lists as gone.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the summit today, or whether the site is open to the public.

[^1]: Crow Peak - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Ron Kemnow, westlookouts.weebly.com, https://westlookouts.weebly.com/crow-peak.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Wyoming Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wy/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
