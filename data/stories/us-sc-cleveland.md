Cleveland was one of the South Carolina Forestry Commission's fire towers in Greenville County.[^1] The commission's history says the towers were where the public reported fires and a link for crews in the field, and that most tower sites covered 10 acres and had, besides the tower, a year-round home for the operator.[^3] The commission ended its fire tower network on October 1, 1993.[^3]

The Forest Lookouts site lists the Cleveland tower as removed.[^1] A blog post about mapping the state's towers says many of the towers that appear on lists no longer exist, and gives the Cleveland Tower north of Marietta as one example.[^2] The same post says Firetower Road in Cleveland, SC now dead-ends at a hunt club, and that the tower there is gone.[^2] The post does not say whether either of these is the same tower as the commission's Cleveland entry.[^1][^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the land is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
