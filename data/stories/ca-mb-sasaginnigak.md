Sasaginnigak Fire Tower stands beside Sasaginnigak Lake, in Atikaki Provincial Park, Manitoba.[^1] The Manitoba Historical Society says most of the province's old lookout towers are gone, and that this one is among the few still in place.[^1]

A 1933 newspaper item, quoted on a lookouts website, mentions a forestry lookout station at Lake Sasaginnigak, though it does not say whether that station was this tower.[^2] The item says a carrier pigeon carried word of 14 hours of rain from the station to a base at Lac du Bonnet.[^2] Forestry officials said the rain had ended the fire threat in that area.[^2]

Today, the most recent description I found is the Manitoba Historical Society page, revised in 2018, with a May 2018 photo of the tower.[^1] A lodge on the lake advertises a hike to the fire tower, with views over the water.[^3] No source I found describes the tower's condition after 2018.
