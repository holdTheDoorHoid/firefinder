Parr Hill is one of the Saskatchewan fire tower sites recorded in the Firetowers of Saskatchewan listing [^1]. That listing gives its position as 52 degrees, 17 minutes, 17.9 seconds north and 102 degrees, 3 minutes, 16 seconds west, and it leaves blank the height, year of construction and names of towermen for the site [^1]. The Canada Lookouts index of Saskatchewan towers also carries a Parr Hill entry [^2].

The province's early towers appear in the forestry reports that Canada Lookouts quotes [^2]. The 1914 report lists one 24.4 m (80 ft) lookout tower at Nisbet, and the 1915 report says a tower had been ordered for the Beaver Hills Forest Reserve [^2].

No source I could read describes what, if anything, stands at Parr Hill today, or whether the public can visit the site.

[^1]: Firetowers of Saskatchewan, websiteservice.ca, https://www.websiteservice.ca/firetowers_page4.htm (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Saskatchewan, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/saskatchewan.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
