The Finland lookout, in Lake County, was built in 1950 as Lookout Tower #394.[^1][^2][^4] It was a 120-foot International Derrick stairway tower.[^2] It replaced an earlier 100-foot Aermotor tower, #107, which was moved to the Askov site in 1951.[^2]

According to the Friends of Finland, after 76 years newer technology had left the tower no longer serving its purpose, and its condition had become unsafe.[^1] The group reported in July 2026 that the tower was quietly taken down in late June.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as removed in 2026.[^3]

An OpenStreetMap point for a Finland Lookout Tower still exists, but it was last edited about six years ago, before the removal.[^5]

Wikidata lists the tower as lying within Finland State Forest.[^4] The Friends also posted images taken before, during and after the demolition, but their text does not say what remains on the site.[^1] We found no source saying whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^3][^4]
