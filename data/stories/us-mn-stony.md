The Stony lookout began as the Isabella lookout, a Forest Service tower in the Superior National Forest in Lake County.[^1][^2][^3]

A 1934 survey note mentions a survey station near the base of the Forest Service lookout tower Isabella.[^1] By 1951 the tower was named Stony.[^1] The compilation gives its approximate position as 47.6040 north and 91.4347 west. The Fire Lookout Association's list gives 47.60407 and -91.43476, so the two positions differ by less than 0.0001 degree.[^1][^2]

A 1968 note says the tower was in good shape but was not operating.[^1] The Lake County table marks it as removed, and the Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone.[^2][^3]

We found no source that describes the site today, and none says whether it is open to visitors.
