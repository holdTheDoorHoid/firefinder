A list of Georgia lookout towers gives a tower the name "Smithville Lookout Tower" and places it in Lee County, at about 31.89 degrees north and 84.21 degrees west.[^1] The list gives its elevation as 350 with no unit printed; read as feet, that is about 107 m.[^1]

The name matches that of the town of Smithville.[^2] A 2017 wildfire protection plan for Lee County, prepared by the Georgia Forestry Commission, says the town lies in the northern part of the county and was incorporated in 1863.[^2]

The list's note on names covers towers 73 and above. This tower is number 50, so the note does not apply to its name.[^1]

The page's map was revised on 22 March 2016.[^1]

No source we found describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything remains there or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Community Wildfire Protection Plan, Lee County, Georgia, Georgia Forestry Commission, https://gatrees.org/resources/lee-cwpp-2017 (accessed 2026-10-21).
