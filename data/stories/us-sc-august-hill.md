August Hill was a South Carolina Forestry Commission fire lookout in Spartanburg County.[^1] The station sat on the crest of a low, round-topped hill, about 6 miles (9.7 km) east-southeast of Landrum.[^2]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the station in 1957. Its description calls the lookout a 10-foot cabin on four structural steel legs, about 100 feet (30 m) in height.[^2] Surveyors recovered the station in 1982 and again in 1983, each time as described in 1957.[^2]

Across South Carolina, the commission's history says women staffed most towers from the 1960s on, and their husbands often worked as firefighters.[^3]

The state ended its fire tower network on October 1, 1993.[^3] The lookout-history site lists August Hill as removed.[^1]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains on the hill today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
