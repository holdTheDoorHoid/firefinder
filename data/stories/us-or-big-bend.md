The Big Bend lookout stood on Big Bend Mountain in Multnomah County, Oregon, within the Mount Hood National Forest, at about 4,017 feet.[^1][^2]

The Forest Service built it in 1933 as a wooden tower with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts site records panorama photographs taken on June 20, 1934, by James Rittenhouse.[^3]

In 1941 the lookout was staffed for 79 days. Its reports went to the Zig Zag Ranger Station, and communication was by Pacific Telephone and Telegraph.[^3]

The sources disagree on when it came down. Forest Lookouts says it was removed around 1949, while the register and firelookout say it was gone by 1950.[^1][^2][^3] A 1957 Zigzag District historical report, quoted by Forest Lookouts in a February 1958 entry, named the lookout point as a potential heliport.[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
