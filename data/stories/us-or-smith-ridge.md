Smith Ridge stands at 4,165 feet in Douglas County, in the Umpqua National Forest, about 28 miles northeast of Canyonville.[^1][^2]

The register dates the tower here to 1936, an 8-foot pole L-4 tower, and says it was removed in 1967.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the same year and says the tower was destroyed.[^2] The Forest Lookouts history page dates an L-4 cab on a short tower to 1942, and says the tower was enclosed to create a storage area.[^3] These sources disagree on the first date, and on whether the 1967 loss was a removal or a destruction.[^1][^2][^3]

In August 1962 a News-Review item listed Smith Ridge among the Umpqua lookout posts manned that summer by college students.[^3]

Today the site is reached by the Forest Service's Smith Ridge Trail, number 1561.[^4] The trail climbs steeply to the former lookout site, where the lookout itself no longer stands. From there the views take in the Dumont Creek and Straight Creek drainages.[^4] To reach the trailhead, drive about eight miles on Forest Road 2813; parking is limited to a small turnout.[^4] The trail can stay snow-covered from late fall into early or mid spring.[^4]
