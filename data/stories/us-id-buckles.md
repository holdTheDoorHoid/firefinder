Buckles is in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, in Kootenai County, about 10 miles northeast of Hayden Lake, at 4,737 feet.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page lists the code 52N-2W-16 for it, which reads as township 52 North, range 2 West, section 16.[^3]

The first structure listed there was a crow's nest, around 1929.[^1][^3] A pole tower with an L-4 cab followed in 1931.[^1][^3] The sources disagree on its height. The firelookout.com page gives 30 feet, while the Forest Lookouts page, which cites Kresek, gives 20 feet.[^1][^3]

In 1934 a 40-foot pole tower with an enclosed cab went up about a quarter mile to the south.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts puts that distance at half a mile.[^2] The site was destroyed around 1955.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as drive-up.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains there today.
