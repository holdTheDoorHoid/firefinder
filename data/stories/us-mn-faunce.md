The Faunce Lookout Tower stands in Lake of the Woods County, Minnesota, and the Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list shows it as standing.[^1] A local tourism guide describes it as a 100-foot stairway tower that the Civilian Conservation Corps built in 1935.[^2] The tower sits at a campground roughly 12 miles south of Williams on County Road 2.[^2]

The guide gives no further design details, and it names no CCC unit, company or individual workers.[^2]

The current tower replaced an older one about three miles to the south, along Faunce Forest Road.[^2] The directory lists a separate Faunce site at roughly that distance as gone.[^1] Neither source dates the older tower.[^1][^2]

The guide also links the site to the lost village of Faunce, and a post office in Faunce operated from 1917 to 1938.[^2][^3]

The guide lists Norris Camp as a separate site and suggests visiting it on the same trip as the tower.[^2] It says the camp's history began in 1933 with the CCC program, and that the camp now serves as the base of the Red Lake Wildlife Management Area.[^2]

I did not find records in these sources of who staffed the tower or of any notable fires.[^2][^3]

For today, the directory lists the tower as standing.[^1] I did not find current climbing or access rules, so check with local land managers before visiting, and do not climb a tower that is closed or private.
