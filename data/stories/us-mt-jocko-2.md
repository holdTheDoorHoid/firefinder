The Jocko #2 Lookout stood in Lake County, Montana, at about 5,818 feet.[^1] The register places it in the Flathead National Forest.[^1] Firelookout.com puts it on the Flathead Indian Reservation, 14 miles southeast of St. Ignatius.[^2]

The register dates the 60-foot steel tower to 1934.[^1] Firelookout.com gives 1935 instead, and says the tower went unused until 1960.[^2] We have kept the register's date, but the two sources do not agree.

The register says the tower was taken down, possibly in the mid-1960s.[^1] Firelookout.com says it was gone in 1965.[^2]

The register says the removal left nothing but concrete footings, and its photo is captioned as a footing.[^1] We found no source that describes access to the site. Firelookout.com places it on the Flathead Indian Reservation, so ask the land managers before you go.[^2]

Other lists call the same spot Jocko #1.[^1][^3] A different, standing Jocko #2 in Missoula County is listed separately, so check the coordinates before you go looking.[^3]
