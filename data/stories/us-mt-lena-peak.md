Lena Peak sits in Powell County, Montana, about 22 miles north of Seeley Lake, on the Flathead National Forest.[^1][^2] The firelookout.com page gives its elevation as 8,364 feet.[^1]

That page lists an L-4 cab in 1930 and a cabin in 1947.[^1] It says the site was abandoned in 1961 and is now gone.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site lists the lookout as removed.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list classes the site as a ground site that is gone.[^3]

No source we read describes the site today, including whether any remains are visible or whether the public can reach it. Anyone planning a visit should check with the Flathead National Forest first.
