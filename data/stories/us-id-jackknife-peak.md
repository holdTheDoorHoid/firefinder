Jackknife Peak is about six miles south of Lakeview, in Kootenai County on the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1][^2] The lookout site sat at 5,242 feet.[^1][^2]

Records describe a pole platform tower on the peak in the 1920s.[^1][^3] A 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab was built here in 1931.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists two known staff members: Mr. Al Hafterson in 1932 and Mr. Mick Clumper in 1937.[^2]

Sources date the tower's loss to about 1950.[^1][^2][^3] Photos of the lookout are credited to the Fire Lookout Museum.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains of the tower or the site today. Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site drive-up.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the lookout as gone.[^4] Ask the Coeur d'Alene National Forest about access before you go.
