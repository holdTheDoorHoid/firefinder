Richmond Peak lies in the Lolo National Forest, about 10 miles north of Seeley Lake in Missoula County, Montana. The page gives its elevation as 6,780 feet.[^1]

The site was established in 1929 with a 20-foot pole platform and a tent cabin. The 20-foot pole L-4 tower was built in 1935.[^1]

A circa 1959 photograph in the University of Montana's Bud Moore collection depicts the lookout from the period when Moore was helping Ranger Horace Godfrey. The catalog entry says it appears to be a 20-foot tower.[^2]

The firelookout.com page says the lookout was destroyed in 1965.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Montana lookouts marks Richmond as a tower that is gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the peak is open to the public. Ask the Lolo National Forest before going up.
