Tom Dunigan's list of Georgia lookout towers has a second Swainsboro entry, called "swainsboro old," with an elevation of 310. The list gives no unit; if the figure is feet, it is about 94.5 m.[^1] By its listed coordinates, the entry lies about 1.5 km (under a mile) north-west of the other Swainsboro entry.[^1] The entry is tagged "datasheet," and the list's links include an NGS datasheet lookup.[^1]

The same list has a separate Flat Creek Lodge entry, which it says was moved from Oak Park.[^1] A 2007 Georgia Trend article described a decommissioned fire tower from Oak Park at that lodge, which sits in a 2,000-acre pine forest about nine miles from downtown Swainsboro.[^2]

No source reached describes what remains at either site today, or whether the public may visit. The Georgia Forestry Commission's Emanuel County unit is at 296 Glenwood Road in Swainsboro.[^3]
