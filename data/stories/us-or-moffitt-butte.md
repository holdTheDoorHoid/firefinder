Forest Lookouts, a site that tracks Oregon's lookouts, files Moffitt Butte under Klamath County and gives its location code as 24S-11E-07.[^1] Its only dated entry is from 1940. That year the Walker Range Patrol held the lookout in emergency status, with phone links to LaPine.[^1]

The Summit Butte lookout carries the same township and range code, 24S-11E.[^2] In 1940 the Walker Range Patrol manned it for 110 days, and reports went by telephone to LaPine.[^2]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains on the butte today, or whether the summit is open to visitors.

[^2]: Summit Butte, Forest Lookouts (Ron Kemnow), https://oregonlookouts.weebly.com/summit-butte.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
