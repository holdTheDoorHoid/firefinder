The Forest Lookouts site lists a lookout called Sobol in Choctaw County, Oklahoma.[^1] Mapcarta, a map site, places a tower of that name in Choctaw County and puts the community of Sobol two miles north of it.[^2]

Mapcarta puts that community in the southeast of Pushmataha County and lists it as unincorporated, so the name is shared with a community in another county.[^2] The register's Oklahoma list names five lookouts, and Sobol is not one of them.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit. Ask the land manager before going, and do not climb a tower that is closed or private.
