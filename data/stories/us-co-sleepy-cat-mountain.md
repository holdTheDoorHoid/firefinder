Sleepy Cat Peak lies in the Flat Tops of northwest Colorado, east of Meeker, inside the White River National Forest.[^1] The lookout sources call the site Sleepy Cat Mountain, in Rio Blanco County.[^2][^3]

In July 1913 a Routt County newspaper reported that the White River service had set up a lookout station on the mountain, with a telephone line to it.[^2]

A December 1913 item describes a guard posted on the summit through the fire danger period. From there the guard could see about a third of the forest, and the summit was also connected by phone to the supervisor's office.[^2] The same item says the forest's officers planned to use a heliograph to help report fires.[^2]

In July 1914 several forest officers, including the White River supervisor, were at the Sleepy Cat ranger station testing a new heliograph station.[^2]

A June 1919 item reports a 30-foot tower on the peak, about 9.1 metres tall.[^2] It adds that a lookout man was posted there in dry summer spells to keep an eye out for fires.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as removed, without giving a date.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit now, or whether the public may go up there. Anyone planning a visit should check with the White River National Forest first.
