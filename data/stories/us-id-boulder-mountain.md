Boulder Mountain in Bonner County began as a camp in 1915. The site sat in the Kaniksu National Forest, about 8 miles north-northwest of Nordman, at an elevation of 5,654 feet.[^1][^2]

In 1927 a cupola cabin went up on the site. Firelookout.com, Idaho Fire Lookouts and the Forest Lookouts list agree on all three dates.[^1][^2][^3]

An accidental fire destroyed the cabin in 1935, and no replacement was built.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
