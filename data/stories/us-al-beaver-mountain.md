Beaver Mountain Lookout Tower stands in St. Clair County, Alabama, at an elevation of 1,087 feet (331 m).[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register says the Alabama Forestry Commission administers the tower and that it was built in 1948.[^1] The Register also describes Beaver Mountain as St. Clair County's first fire tower.[^1]

A notice quoted by the Forest Lookouts site, credited to The Anniston Star and dated October 15, 1948, said the tower would be dedicated on top of the mountain the following day.[^2] The newspaper page itself was not among the sources I could check, so the dedication date rests on that quoted notice.

The sources I could read do not say who staffed the tower, what fires it watched, or how it has changed since. Our record lists it as standing, but no source I read confirms its condition today. Neither source says whether the public may visit, so anyone planning a trip should ask the Alabama Forestry Commission first.

The Register gives January 3, 2015 as the date the tower was registered, under US 1047, AL 59.[^1]
