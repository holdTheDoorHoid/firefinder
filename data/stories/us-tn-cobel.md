Cobel was a fire lookout tower in Hickman County, Tennessee.[^1] Tennessee Landforms, which lists the location or former location of the state's lookout towers, gives its elevation as 857 feet and files it under the state number 7-10.[^1] That number is a district-and-tower number taken from a state list made in 1976.[^1]

A National Register survey of the Tennessee Division of Forestry's fire towers names Cobel among the lookouts built of wood.[^2] The same survey says the vast majority of fire lookout towers built in Tennessee in the 1930s were steel-frame.[^2]

The survey also says steel towers replaced the Cobel tower and Moore's Switch, another wooden tower in Hickman County, in the 1940s or 1950s.[^2] It credits that date to an undated Division of Forestry source.[^2] The survey's list of towers still standing on March 26, 2014 does not include Cobel.[^2] Tennessee Landforms now marks the tower as removed.[^1]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^2] Landforms adds that many towers are gone and that most of their sites are now on private land.[^1] None of the sources I read says whether the site is open to visitors, so check who owns the land before going to look.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, Tennessee Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Tennessee Division of Forestry Fire Lookout Towers, 1933 to 1975, National Register multiple property document, NPGallery (National Park Service), https://npgallery.nps.gov/pdfhost/docs/NRHP/Text/64501237.pdf (accessed 2026-10-21).
