Higgins Hump had a crow's nest, and the Clearwater National Forest list records its type that way.[^3] The list places it in Section 13, about 16 miles east-northeast of Kooskia, at an elevation of 4,475 feet.[^1][^3] The list draws on Ray Kresek's book Fire Lookouts of the Northwest, Forest Service records, and the firelookout.com site.[^3]

The crow's nest went up in 1934, a year that the association's page, the Idaho Fire Lookouts page and the Clearwater list all give.[^1][^2][^3] The site was abandoned in 1941.[^1][^2][^3] The association's statewide Idaho list gives its status as abandoned.[^4]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page calls it a hike-in, and it does not say whether the ground is open to visitors.[^2]
