By June 1931 a telephone line ran from the Blake lookout to a ranch on the Mad River. A five-man crew from Hayfork and Hyampom had built it.[^1] In 1933 the Forest Service finished another line over the nearby ridges, linking the Mad River with Hyampom, the Blake lookout and Iaqua Buttes.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page dates that clipping to 1976, but its text describes 1933.[^1]

In May 1934 a notice said the Blake lookout on South Fork Mountain would be abandoned and moved, with a new lookout at Cold Springs taking its place.[^1] The notice names South Fork Mountain, while the FFLA list places the Blake site on Blake Mountain. FFLA also lists a South Fork Mountain tower in Shasta County, so the name alone does not show which site the notice meant.[^1][^2] The notice announced a move, but these sources do not confirm that it happened.[^1]

The FFLA list describes the Blake Mountain site as a ground site that is now gone.[^2] PeakVisor gives the summit as 5,909 feet and says a trail called Last Chance Trail leads to the top.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, stands on the site today.
