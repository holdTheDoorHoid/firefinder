Cannibal Mountain sits in the Siuslaw National Forest in Lincoln County, Oregon, at 1,946 feet.[^1] It lies about 22 miles south-southeast of Newport.[^2]

The first lookout went up in 1934 as a 40-foot pole L-4 tower.[^1][^4] A May 1934 item in the Eugene Register-Guard, quoted by the Oregon Forest Lookouts site, said a tower was planned for the mountain in the Waldport district.[^3]

In August 1948 the Forest Service asked for bids on a prefabricated, treated 53-foot tower with a 14-by-14-foot cab.[^3] That taller tower replaced the first one the same year.[^1]

The tower was staffed until 1973, and the Oregon Forest Lookouts site calls it the last of the forest's staffed lookouts.[^3]

In 1975 a buyer took the tower away for salvage and converted its cab into a chicken house.[^2][^3][^4]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register instead says the tower served in emergencies in 1977 before it was taken down.[^1] That date conflicts with the 1975 removal given by the other three sources, so this account follows 1975.[^2][^3][^4]

None of the sources describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether visitors can reach the site.
