Bull (Bull Pond) Hill was a fire lookout in Orange County, New York.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list places it at 41.34979, -74.07186 and marks it as a tower that is gone.[^1] The state's Department of Environmental Conservation says New York used more than 100 fire towers, over nearly a century, to keep watch on its forests.[^2]

The county list leaves the hill's entry blank in its NHLR/FFLOS column, which holds register numbers for towers that have one.[^1] Graham Mountain, another Orange County tower on the same list, is marked as standing and carries a register number.[^1]

A 2011 list of the state's Bureau of Forest Fire Control detection stations, covering 1909 through the 1990 fire season, does not include Bull Pond Hill.[^3] Its Orange County entries are Graham Mountain, Pocatello Mountain and Sterling Mountain.[^3]

No source we read describes the site today, including whether anything remains there or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
