Eagle Springs is a lookout tower in Moore County, North Carolina.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts list pairs it with the state's Division of Forest Resources and says a 120-foot (37 m) tower was erected there in 1944.[^1] A North Carolina tower list on tnlandforms.us gives its coordinates and county.[^2]

A Pinestraw Magazine article, which its page dates to August 2018, is the only later mention we found.[^3] In it, a Scotland County ranger says almost all of the state's 100 fire lookout towers are out of service.[^3] He says three remain in use, one of them at Eagle Springs.[^3] The article does not say whether that is the Moore County tower described here.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
