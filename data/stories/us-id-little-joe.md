Little Joe Lookout was a fire lookout in the St. Joe National Forest, about eight miles northeast of the Red Ives Ranger Station, at roughly 7,052 feet.[^1][^2] The site is listed in both Shoshone County, Idaho, and Mineral County, Montana.[^1]

A camp is recorded on the site around 1924, and a 14-by-14-foot log cabin followed in 1930.[^1][^2][^3] Two sources also note a patrol point two miles south, on Crooked Ridge.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts names John Simmons as a staff member and cites a book, Hardship & Happy Times, by Bert Russell.[^2]

The lookout was destroyed in 1953, the year all three sources give for its end.[^1][^2][^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains on the site. Idaho Fire Lookouts tags it Drive-Up, but no source says whether a road reaches it now.[^2]
