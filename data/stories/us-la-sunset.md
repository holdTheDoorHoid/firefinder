Sunset Lookout Tower is mapped in Rapides Parish, Louisiana, on the USGS Melder map.[^1][^2] Its coordinates are 31.0338 N, 92.6574 W, with an approximate elevation of 194 feet (59 m).[^1] A Louisiana tower list built from USGS names and NGS data sheets lists the tower at nearly the same coordinates.[^2] OpenStreetMap tags the structure as a lattice tower used for observation.[^3]

No source here describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.

[^2]: Louisiana lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/latowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
