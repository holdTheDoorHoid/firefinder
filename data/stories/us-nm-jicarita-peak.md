Forest Lookouts describes Jicarita Peak as the site of a Forest Service lookout station in Taos County, on the northern side of Pecos National Forest land.[^1] An August 1914 Albuquerque Journal account, quoted there, says the station was hard to occupy before mid-June, because snowdrifts left the way impassable until then.[^1] The same account describes a Forest Service-built trail that winds up through thick spruce.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Jicarita Peak site as a rock shelter, and as gone.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, is left on the summit today.

Forest Lookouts lists the peak under the Pecos National Forest, with the Carson National Forest after it.[^1] Forest Trail 161 leads from NM 518 to the Alamitos Trailhead.[^3] SummitPost says camping, hiking, and climbing there are free, and it tells visitors to look up fire rules for the Carson National Forest before going.[^3]
