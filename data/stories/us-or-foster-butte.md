Foster Butte is a summit in Lake County, Oregon, at 42.936812° N, 120.972211° W, with an elevation of 6,755 feet (2,059 m).[^2] It appears on the USGS 1:24,000 topographic map named for it.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page lists its location code as 30S-15E-29, which reads as section 29, township 30 South, range 15 East.[^1]

Forest Lookouts describes the butte as a secondary observation or emergency lookout point in 1936.[^1] The same page notes that panorama photographs were taken on August 17, 1936.[^1] It files the butte under Lake County, listed alongside Fort Rock.[^1]

No source we found describes what, if anything, stands on the summit today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
