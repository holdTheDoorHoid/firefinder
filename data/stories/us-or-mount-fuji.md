Fuji Mountain sits in Lane County, in the Willamette National Forest, about 18 miles southeast of Oakridge.[^1][^2] The site dates to 1919, when it was set up as a tent camp with a fire finder stand.[^1] That year an alidade was put in place to help locate fires.[^3]

Sources disagree on the first cabin. The register says a D-6 cab was erected in 1926.[^1] A September 1926 newspaper item reported two lookout houses built, one on Fuji, and a January 1927 item said one was placed there.[^3] The firelookout.com page dates a cupola cabin to 1928.[^2]

Edgar Buchanan worked as the lookout on Fuji in the summer of 1923.[^3] Orlo Flock was on his way to the summer station there in 1934.[^3] A 1935 general inspection raised whether lightning protection on old lookout houses needed upgrading, and used Fuji Mountain as its example.[^3]

In 1959 an R-6 ground cabin replaced the older cabin.[^1][^3] The register and firelookout.com both say it burned nine years later, in 1968.[^1][^2] Rex's Fire Lookout Page gives August 26, 1968, as the date the lookout house was removed.[^3]

The summit is now a former fire lookout site. The Oregon Hikers guide describes a short out-and-back hike from the Upper Trailhead, after a 10.5-mile drive on FR 5883.[^4] No source I read describes what remains of the cabin or its footings.
