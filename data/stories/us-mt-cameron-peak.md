Cameron Peak is a former fire lookout in Mineral County, Montana, on the Lolo National Forest.[^1][^2] The site listing dates an L-4 tower there to 1936.[^1]

The same listing says the tower was destroyed in 1962.[^1] The Forest Lookouts list of Montana towers marks it as removed, with no date, and gives the land description as township 20 North, range 30 West, section 36.[^2] The sources differ on how the tower ended, but all of them treat it as no longer standing.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list gives its status as gone and its coordinates as 47.44331 north and 115.36447 west.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and we found nothing about public access.[^1][^2][^3]
