The Bruce County Fire Tower is a steel tower in Tobermory, in the Municipality of Northern Bruce Peninsula, Ontario.[^1] Its site is listed at an elevation of 191 m (627 ft).[^1]

Firelookout.ca says the tower is not in its original home. It stands behind Station 10 of the Northern Bruce Peninsula Emergency Services, and the page says it has been there for many years.[^1]

An undated Ontario Forest Rangers page refers to a 2013 ministry decision and says the old tower's remains sit on the fire department grounds in Tobermory.[^2] Firelookout.ca instead describes a standing tower there, so the two sources differ on its state.[^1][^2]

Today the tower is on fire department grounds.[^1][^2] Neither source says whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]
