Three Tree Butte sits in the St. Joe National Forest, about eight miles north of Harvard.[^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts dates the site to the 1930s, when a tree-top crow's nest and a tent camp were there.[^1] The same writers say it was likely manned only during spells of high fire danger.[^1]

A 2022 post by Idaho Fire Lookouts says the last staffing came almost 80 years before the visit.[^4] All three lookout listings put the abandonment in the 1940s.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com marks the crow's nest as still there but gives no date for that.[^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts visited in early April 2022.[^4] Its report says the platform had collapsed, though some planks remained nailed to one tree.[^1] The old phone line, a single strand of #9 galvanized wire, was still fastened to the trees by the platform.[^1]

The writers had intended to drive up a forest service road, but a four-foot snow berm closed it off, so they parked by the road and walked.[^4] A University of Idaho hiking guide says Trail 26 climbs about two miles to the butte.[^5]

No source we read describes the site's condition after the 2022 visit, so we cannot say what remains today or whether visitors can reach it freely.
