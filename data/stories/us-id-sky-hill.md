Sky Hill's lookout was a crow's nest, set up in 1934 [^1][^2][^3][^4]. It stood about three miles north of Harrison [^1], in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest [^1][^2][^3]. It was abandoned a year later, in 1935 [^1][^2][^3][^4]. All four sources agree on those two dates [^1][^2][^3][^4].

Idaho Fire Lookouts classes the site as a hike-in, abandoned lookout [^2]. A hiking guide on WillhiteWeb combed the ridge for signs of a campsite or an observation tree and came up empty [^4]. No source describes a footing or structure left on the summit today.

The climb from the pass on Skygazer Lane covers about 1.5 miles and gains roughly 1,000 feet [^4]. The route begins on private timberland, where the owners have agreed to non-motorized public access [^4]. The summit itself is on an isolated patch of National Forest land [^4].
