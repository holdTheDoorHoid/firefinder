Betts Mountain is a summit in Meriwether County, Georgia, and it appears on the Woodbury quadrangle map.[^1] A summit directory lists the peak at about 392 meters, or 1,286 feet.[^1]

The Georgia tower survey lists a site called betts mtn in Meriwether County, at 32.92225, -84.57507, and numbers it GA082.[^2] Its table gives an elevation of 1285 with no unit stated; if that figure is in feet, it is one foot below the directory's figure.[^1][^2] The survey says that for towers numbered 73 and above, the names are its own, so the name betts mtn comes from the survey.[^2] The Forest Lookouts index also lists Betts Mountain in Meriwether County.[^3]

No source I found describes the site today, so the public record does not say whether a tower stands on the summit or whether the spot is open to visitors.
