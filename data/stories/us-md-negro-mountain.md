Negro Mountain is a ridge in the Allegheny Mountains, roughly 30 miles long, running from Deep Creek Lake in Maryland north into Pennsylvania.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Negro Mountain fire tower in Garrett County, and records it as gone.[^2] The sources I could read give no build date, no staffing record and no date for its removal.

The name has a history of its own. A version in Thomas Scharf's 1882 History of Western Maryland, which the Garrett County Historical Society later reprinted, tells of a servant named Nemesis who was killed and buried on the mountain while Colonel Thomas Cresap pursued attackers who had raided a settlement.[^3] Later research by the historian Champ Zumbrun, as reported on the same page, dates the event to 1756 and indicates that Nemesis was a free Black man. It also finds that the name Nemesis did not appear in writing until more than 120 years after the event, and that the name Negro Mountain appears on maps from 1822.[^3]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
