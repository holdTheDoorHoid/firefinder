The Mineral Springs lookout was a 73-foot tower in Adams County, Ohio, about a mile from the community of Mineral Springs.[^1][^2] Mineral Springs is an unincorporated community, and it was named for a nearby mineral spa.[^4] In mid-August 1934 the Chillicothe Gazette reported that the tower would open within a few days and that the public was invited to inspect the new towers.[^2]

By late October 1934 the same paper named Sherman Nevil, a local warden, as the towerman.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association registered the site on September 3, 2014, as US 1568, OH 14, and gives its elevation as 1,200 feet (366 meters).[^1] Its Ohio list also gives the name Peach Mountain, and it now marks the tower as gone.[^3]

None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, still stands at the site, or whether the spot is public land or open to visitors.[^1][^3]
