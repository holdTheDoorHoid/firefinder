Sunset Peak carried a fire lookout on the Lolo National Forest, about eight miles southwest of St. Regis in Mineral County, Montana.[^1]

Firelookout.com dates a 20-foot pole tower of the L-4 type to 1934, and gives the site an elevation of 5,744 feet.[^1] The same page says the tower was destroyed in 1959.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list places the site at 47.22550 north and 115.22413 west, and marks the tower as gone.[^2] Its structure type, a tower, matches the pole tower that firelookout.com describes.[^1][^2]

That list also has a separate Sunset Peak in Missoula County, so check the county before using the name.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Sunset Peak Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/sunsetpeak.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
