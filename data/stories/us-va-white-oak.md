The White Oak Mountain lookout was a Virginia Department of Forestry tower in Pittsylvania County.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register places the site at 1,043 feet, or 318 meters.[^1]

We found no build date and no design description for the tower in the sources we could read. The register says the tower was still in active use through at least 1975.[^1] We also found no record of who staffed it or which fires it watched.

After that, the register says the tower was converted into a communications tower, and it dates the removal to some point after 1975.[^1] The register does not mention a move.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list marks the White Oak Mountain tower as gone.[^2]

That same list places a standing tower at Hawthorne, in Montgomery County, under a heading for relocated and replica lookouts, and names White Oak Mountain as its origin.[^2] The list gives no date for a move and does not say who moved the tower or how. The Hawthorne claim therefore rests on that single list entry.

The register describes a conversion to communications use, while the association simply marks the site as gone. Neither source says what, if anything, remains at the White Oak Mountain site today.

We also found no source describing public access to either place, or the condition of the Hawthorne tower. Anyone who wants to see it should ask the landowner first, and should not climb a tower without permission.

[^1]: White Oak Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/va/white-oak-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Virginia, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
