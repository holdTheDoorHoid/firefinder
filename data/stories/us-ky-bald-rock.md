The Bald Rock lookout stood in Laurel County, Kentucky, inside the Daniel Boone National Forest, at about 1,299 feet.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says it was built in the 1930s and that the U.S. Forest Service administered it.[^1] The Forest Service's own picnic-area page gives a closer span: the tower stood on the site from 1936 to 1970.[^3]

The sources disagree about when it came down.[^1][^3] The register says it was removed in the mid-1970s,[^1] while the Forest Service says 1970.[^3] A 1950 survey description, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, places a survey disk near the lookout tower.[^2] Part of the tower and its cabin served briefly as a press box at South Laurel High School, and those parts were later taken away.[^1]

Today the Forest Service runs the site as the Bald Rock Picnic Area, off KY 192 north of Laurel River Lake.[^1][^3] It has two large picnic sites, two small ones, and volleyball and horseshoes.[^3] Nothing I read restricts entry to the picnic area.[^3] No source I read describes any remains of the tower on the ground today, so I cannot say whether footings or other marks are still there.[^1][^2][^3]
