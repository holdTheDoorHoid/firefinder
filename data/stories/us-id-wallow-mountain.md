Wallow Mountain Lookout stands in Clearwater County, Idaho, on the Nez Perce-Clearwater National Forests, about nine miles east of the Canyon Ranger Station.[^1][^2] Sources disagree on its elevation: the register gives 5,890 feet, while the Forest Service list and two other sites give 5,981 feet.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

The site has had several structures. The first, from the 1920s, was a D-6 cupola cabin.[^2] In 1932 it was replaced with an L-4 cab on a log base, and a 10-foot L-6 patrol tower followed in 1934.[^2] The current tower, a 10-foot treated timber tower with an R-6 flat cab, was built in 1968.[^1][^2]

The tower joined the Recreation Rental program in 1995.[^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists two known staff members, Darcy Aldrich-Pederson in 1974 and Diana Andrews in 1977.[^3] The rental arrangement has since lapsed.[^2][^3] Forest Fire Lookout says the tower cannot be rented at present, and Idaho Fire Lookouts says it can still be staffed in emergencies.[^2][^3] A Forest Service list of Clearwater lookouts, however, still marks it as a rental.[^4]

The National Historic Lookout Register listed the tower on December 5, 2020, as US 1468, ID 160, after a nomination by Brad Eells.[^1] A lookout list at firelookout.org shows it as standing.[^5] Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site "Hike-In Emergency," which suggests a walk-in visit.[^3] No source describes a current road, gate or permit.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5] Anyone planning a trip should check with the Nez Perce-Clearwater National Forests first.
