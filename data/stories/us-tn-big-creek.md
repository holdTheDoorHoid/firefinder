The Big Creek lookout is recorded in Sullivan County, Tennessee, at an elevation of 1,873 feet in the state's lookout tower catalog.[^2] TopoQuest, which places it on the USGS Holston Valley map, gives 1,879 feet, so the two sources disagree by six feet.[^3] Both list the tower as historical.[^2][^3]

Between 1933 and 1941, the Civilian Conservation Corps put up 98 lookout towers across Tennessee.[^1] The catalog also notes that many have since been taken down and that most of the sites are privately owned.[^1]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the Big Creek site today, or whether the land is open to visitors. The catalog recommends against climbing them, since the steps are frequently missing or rotten.[^1]
