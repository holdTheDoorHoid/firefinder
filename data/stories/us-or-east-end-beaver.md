The East End (Beaver) lookout stood in Baker County, Oregon, inside the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest, and the U.S. Forest Service administered the site.[^1] One lookout history site places it about 12 miles south of Baker City.[^2] A 1946 survey description puts the summit about 13 miles southeast of town, as the crow flies.[^3]

Two pole platform towers went up on the site in 1938, about half a mile apart.[^1][^2]

The towers were abandoned in the 1940s.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says they are gone.[^1] However, 1946 survey descriptions of the marks mention a Forest Service observation tower near them. They do not say whether it was one of the 1938 towers.[^3]

Today the sources offer little. None we read describes what, if anything, remains at the summit, such as footings or a foundation, and none says whether the public may visit.[^1][^2][^3] Anyone planning a trip should check with the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest first.
