Oscura Peak is the highest point of the Oscura Mountains, which lie in Socorro and Lincoln counties in New Mexico, at 8,625 feet.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's New Mexico list records a tower on the peak and marks it as gone.[^1]

A separate listing on the Forest Lookouts site gives the agency as "State."[^3] Wikipedia, however, says the mountains sit within the White Sands Missile Range, which limits public access.[^2] The two sources point in different directions on who manages the site, and this story cannot settle that.

The Oscura Mountains also have an Air Force Research Laboratory site on North Oscura Peak.[^2] Public access is restricted, and no source found describes a route to the summit.[^2]

No source found describes what, if anything, remains on Oscura Peak today.
