The Erin lookout is in Houston County, Tennessee, at an elevation of 820 feet.[^1] The Tennessee lookout list marks it as standing and gives it the code 6-20.[^1]

A reader's comment on a Land Between the Lakes area website describes summer work for the Tennessee Division of Forestry from 1975 to 1976, at the Division's towers in Stewart and Houston counties.[^2] The writer says the crews painted the Erin Fire Tower along with several other towers, kept the grounds clear, and also fought fires.[^2] That is one person's memory, not an official record, and we found no staffing records for the tower.

The 2014 National Register form lists one tower in Houston County, Towerview, among the extant towers, and it does not list Erin.[^3] The Tennessee list, revised in January 2026, and the 2014 form therefore disagree about whether Erin still stands. We could not settle that from the sources.[^1][^3]

The National Historic Lookout Register's list of Tennessee lookouts has 76 entries, and Erin is not among them.[^4]

We found no build date, design, or height for the tower, and no record of fires it watched.[^1][^3] We also found nothing about public access. Please do not climb the tower unless its owner or the managing agency says it is open to visitors.
