The Helmet (3 Forks) lookout stood in Flathead County, Montana, 19 miles southeast of Essex, on the Flathead National Forest.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page gives its land-survey location as 27N-13W-17.[^2]

Firelookout.com dates the site to 1937, when a 10-foot L-4 tower went up a quarter mile north of the summit.[^1] A Sanders County history says the L-4 cab was, by a wide margin, the favorite live-in lookout design.[^4] It describes that design in general as a 14-by-14-foot wood-frame cab that could sit on the ground or atop a tower.[^4]

Firelookout.com says the tower was abandoned in 1953 and is now gone.[^1] The statewide list also marks it as gone.[^3]

The sources we read do not describe what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3] Check with the Flathead National Forest before you go.
