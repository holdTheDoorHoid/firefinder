Foley Peak is a lookout site in Tillamook County, Oregon, at 2,275 feet.[^2] Fire Lookout dates a crow's nest there to the 1930s.[^2] The Oregon lookouts history says the Aircraft Warning Service erected a 12x29 cabin and a 12x16 woodshed at the lookout in 1943, at a cost of $922.85.[^1]

During the Second World War, the Aircraft Warning Service, a civilian body linked to the Army Ground Observer Corps, watched the sky for hostile planes.[^3] The Oregon lookouts history gives May 24, 1943 as the activation date and names the Portland Filter Center.[^1]

Fire Lookout lists the site as abandoned, with ruins.[^2] The two sources also name different agencies: the Oregon lookouts history gives the Oregon Department of Forestry, and Fire Lookout gives the Northwest Oregon FPA.[^1][^2]

A Best Maps list of Oregon lookouts that remain at least partly standing includes Foley Peak. That conflicts with the ruins description, and the list gives no detail.[^4]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, beyond the ruins note, or whether visitors can get there.[^2]
