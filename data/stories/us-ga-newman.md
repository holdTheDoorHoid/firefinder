The only source found for the Newman tower is a compiled list of Georgia towers. It places the tower in Coweta County at 33.35161, -84.79136.[^1] Newman is number 168 on that list, and the page says that names for towers numbered 73 and above are the author's own creation, so the name may not be a historic one.[^1] The page says the Georgia Forestry Commission supplied coordinates for 118 towers, and that an older Commission list covered 203 towers.[^1] Newman's entry carries a BLL number and a gfc number. The page links the first to the coordinates the Commission supplied and the second to the older list.[^1] The page's map was revised on 22 March 2016.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 29 registered Georgia towers, and Newman is not one of them.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists two Georgia entries, neither of them Newman.[^3]

No source found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.
