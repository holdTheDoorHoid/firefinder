Fulch is one of the Georgia lookout towers on the tnlandforms.us list, whose map was revised on 22 March 2016.[^1] The entry places the tower in Berrien County at an elevation of 218 feet (66.5 m), with the coordinates 31.14647, -83.21963.[^1]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors. Check who owns the land before you go.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
