Willis Mountain is a lone peak, a monadnock, that rises out of the Piedmont country of Buckingham County, Virginia.[^2] Its ridge runs about two miles with steep, rocky sides, and the summit reaches 1,159 feet.[^2] U.S. Highway 15 runs along the mountain's western side.[^2]

The mountain sits on a large body of kyanite quartzite that has been worked for decades.[^2] Wikipedia says the Kyanite Mining Corporation started working the southern stretch of the main ridge in 1957.[^2] A 2012 blog post says the mountain has lost elevation at the same time as its kyanite.[^3]

Wikipedia, which gives no date, places a fire lookout tower on the mountain's highest point.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Willis Mountain tower in Buckingham County and marks it as gone.[^1] The two sources disagree about whether a tower stands there now.

No source describes the summit today, so the tower's status remains unconfirmed. None says whether the public can reach the site.
