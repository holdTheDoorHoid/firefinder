The Woodland Lookout Tower is in Talbot County, Georgia.[^1] The tnlandforms.us Georgia tower list places it at 32.82041, -84.50437 and gives an elevation of 966, with no unit stated. Read as feet, that is about 294 m.[^1]

In 2006, a Savannah newspaper report drawn from a Forestry Commission spreadsheet counted 137 of the state's 144 surviving towers as unstaffed.[^2] Tower staffing has been cut since the late 1980s, and aircraft patrols have largely taken over.[^2] The article also said towers were being auctioned off, and that some had gone for scrap metal or to hunting clubs and other groups.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 29 registered Georgia towers, and Woodland is not among them.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists two Georgia sites, neither of them Woodland.[^4]

Readers should not confuse this tower with a registered Woodland Lookout Tower in Chickasaw County, Mississippi (US 1676, MS 39).[^5]

None of the sources checked describes what, if anything, remains at the Woodland site today, or whether the public can reach it.
