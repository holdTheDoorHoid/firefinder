Firelookout.ca records the Cypress Fire Tower in Maple Creek No. 111, Saskatchewan, at an elevation of 1144 m (3753 ft).[^1]

The Saskatchewan provincial park in the Cypress Hills was established in 1931, and it was extended into Alberta in 1951.[^2] The interprovincial park was created in 1989 to join the two parks on either side of the border.[^2] The Saskatchewan side has two blocks. Centre Block offers well-developed tourist services all year, while West Block is a wilderness area with primitive camping.[^2]

A visitor comment on the Firelookout.ca record says the tower site was originally formed in 1931, the same year the park was established.[^1][^2] The same comment places the tower in the Center Block rather than the West Block, and describes a steel structure with no copula.[^1] It also says something was removed in the early 2000s, and that the province now uses thermal cameras on its towers.[^1]

No official source we found describes the tower site today, so the comment's claims are unconfirmed. The park's Centre Block has tourist facilities, but no source says whether the tower site itself is open to visitors.[^2]
