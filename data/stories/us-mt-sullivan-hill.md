Sullivan Hill is a lookout site in Cascade County, about 36 miles southwest of Great Falls, at an elevation of 5,500 feet.[^1] The firelookout.com page describes a cabin from the 1930s, and says it was abandoned and is likely gone.[^1] It gives only the decade for the cabin, so the exact year it was built is not known.[^1]

The firelookout.org Montana list classes the site as a ground site rather than a tower, and gives its coordinates as 47.28955, -112.00857.[^3] A Forest Lookouts page places it under the land description 18N-3W-29.[^2]

Both the firelookout.com page and the Forest Lookouts page describe it as a private co-operator site.[^1][^2] The firelookout.org list marks it as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
