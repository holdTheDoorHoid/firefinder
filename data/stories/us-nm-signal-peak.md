Signal Peak Lookout stands on Signal Peak in the Gila National Forest, in Grant County, New Mexico, at about 9,000 feet.[^1][^3] The register puts the 30-foot Aermotor MC-24 steel tower up in 1932.[^1]

The register says the original cab was replaced in 1966. The replacement followed a Forest Service standard plan in the CL-100 to CL-106 series.[^1] It also says the supporting buildings went up in the 1960s.[^1]

Mark Gutzman nominated the lookout. The register recorded it on May 22, 2022, as US 1587, NM 45.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's 2020 New Mexico report lists it among the Gila lookouts as a new register nomination.[^2]

The association's New Mexico list shows the tower as standing.[^4] The sources I read do not say who has staffed it or what fires it has watched.

The Forest Service describes the Signal Peak Trail as a high-elevation hike through mixed conifer forest, leading up to a lookout tower.[^3] The trailhead is on New Mexico Highway 15, about 16.5 miles north of Silver City.[^3] The Forest Service page does not say whether the tower itself is open to climbing, so check with the Gila National Forest before going up it.[^3]
