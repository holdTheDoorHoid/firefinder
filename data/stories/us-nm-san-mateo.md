The San Mateo Lookout is a fire tower in Socorro County, New Mexico, on the Magdalena Ranger District of the Cibola National Forest.[^1] It was erected in 1934 and is a 59-foot-3-inch Aermotor LX-25 tower with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab.[^1][^2] The register calls it the oldest surviving detection facility on the Magdalena district and one of the few remaining U.S. examples of the platform-style, ladder-and-stairs design.[^1]

The sources we read do not name who staffed the tower or what fires it watched.

The register and the 2020 state report both describe it as on standby and say it is reached by a trail of about five miles.[^1][^2] The 2020 report also says the tower was not evaluated for the National Register in the 1988 thematic nomination and should be evaluated and nominated if eligible.[^2]

The state index still lists it as standing.[^3] We found no current staffing or public access notice, so anyone thinking about the hike should check with the Magdalena Ranger District first.
