The Bearskin lookout stood in Cook County, in the Superior National Forest, near Bearskin Lake.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page locates it some 22 miles north-northwest of Grand Marais.[^1]

The county tower list gives the tower the number 111, lists its date as 1922, and describes it as around 90 feet tall.[^2] The same list gives two other names for it: West Bearskin and Palisade.[^2]

A June 22, 1922 item from the Cook County News-Herald, quoted on the Palisade page, reports that five patrolmen headed to Clearwater Lake to start work at the Palisade station.[^3] Survey notes from 1934 describe a 90-foot steel fire lookout tower, and a 1958 note repeats that description.[^1][^2]

The county list gives the owner as the Minnesota Forest Service, while the Forest Lookouts page lists the Superior National Forest.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone, with no date.[^4]

The latest survey description we found dates from 1958.[^2] No source we read says what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^2][^4]
