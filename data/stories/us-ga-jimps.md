Jimps is a fire tower site in Bulloch County, Georgia, that appears on a personal list of the state's towers. The list places it at 32.37396, -81.8499 and gives its county as Bulloch.[^1] Its row cites a datasheet, CK0142, and no GFC or BLL code.[^1]

The list says it draws on the USGS name database for towers 1 to 72, and that the author made the names for towers 73 and above.[^1] Its list number, GA360, places it in the second group, so its name is the author's own label for the site.[^1]

The page credits GFC with coordinates for 118 towers, and notes that an earlier GFC list gave locations for 203 towers.[^1] The map was last revised in March 2016.[^1]

Jimps does not appear among the 29 lookouts that the National Historic Lookout Register lists for Georgia,[^2] nor among the two former sites in the Former Fire Lookout Sites register.[^3] No source we checked describes what, if anything, remains at the Jimps site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]

[^3]: Georgia, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ga/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
