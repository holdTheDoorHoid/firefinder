The Deputy lookout was a Forest Service station in Pope County, on a 100-foot steel tower about two and a half miles northeast of Brownfield.[^1]

A description from about 1942, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, said the cab offered a fine outlook.[^1]

In August 1972 the Southern Illinoisan ran a notice soliciting bids to buy this tower and seven other steel lookout towers in the Shawnee National Forest.[^1]

The tower is now listed as removed on the Forest Lookouts page, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association list shows it as gone.[^1][^2]

The tower sat within the Shawnee National Forest, which the 1972 notice names.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1]
