The Wildlife Lookout is not a fire lookout. It is a bird and observation tower in the Auburn Environmental Park, a wetland park that sits off Western Avenue in Auburn, Washington.[^1][^2][^3]

The city opened the tower in June 2009. The local paper reported the ribbon-cutting after a ground-breaking in December.[^1] Wood, steel and recycled materials went into the tower, which has three platforms at different levels. A trail and a 30-foot ramp reach the lower one. A state grant from the Recreation and Conservation Office helped pay for it. The Rainier Audubon Society helped decide where it would go.[^1]

The 2009 article says visitors can watch birds and other wildlife in the wetland beneath the tower.[^1] A 2013 article describes the tower as having three platforms and dating from 2009. It adds that monthly visits have kept rising as word of the new facilities spreads.[^3]

We found no sign that the tower was ever used to watch for fires, so it does not belong with the fire lookouts. Visitors should follow the park's own signs and rules.
