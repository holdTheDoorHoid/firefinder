The Tuscan Buttes lookout was in Tehama County, California, at about 40.262 N and 122.093 W.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list gives its type as "2-StoryCab" (a two-story cab tower) and marks it as gone.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page for Tehama County files the lookout under the California Department of Forestry and gives 1945 as its only date.[^2] The peakbagging table lists "Tuscan Buttes (LO site)" with an elevation of 1,872 feet.[^3] Its key uses "LO site" for a spot where the building has been taken away, and says no trace may be left and the present status could be unclear.[^3]

The Buttes also appear in local news. A 2022 Red Bluff Daily News article runs an August 25, 1999 photo; its caption credits a PG&E gas line with halting the fire, with the Buttes visible behind it.[^4] That caption does not mention the lookout. We found no source that describes what remains at the lookout site, or whether the ground is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
