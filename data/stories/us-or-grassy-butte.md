Grassy Butte is in the Umatilla National Forest in Grant County, Oregon, about 13 miles northwest of Monument.[^1][^2]

Records from 1931 describe a tree tower, or crow's nest, at the site.[^2][^3] A Heppner Gazette-Times item from November 1931, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, reports that a tree tower went up at Grassy Butte.[^3] A cabin followed in 1941.[^1][^2]

The only lookout named in these sources is Victor Johnson, listed as lookout personnel in 1934.[^2]

Albert Arnst took panorama photos at Grassy Butte on July 20, 1935.[^3] The register's copy of that panorama is credited to the National Archives Pacific Northwest Region.[^1] The register added the site on June 23, 2011.[^1]

The register notes that the 1941 cabin no longer stands.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page lists the lookout as removed, without a date.[^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the ground today, or whether the site is open to visitors. Anyone planning a visit should check with the Umatilla National Forest first.
