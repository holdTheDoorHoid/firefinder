Cain's Ridge Lookout stands in Fayette County, Alabama, and is administered by the Alabama Forestry Commission.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as a 100-foot steel tower. The register notes that some windows and step boards are missing, yet it calls the overall condition good.[^1]

I could not find when the tower went up, who designed it, or who built it. The register's details list the location, the agency and the registration date, but no build date.[^1] A regional lookout site lists only the county and the agency.[^2]

The tower was registered on May 27, 2016, with Andrew Zerbe as nominator.[^1] One Alabama preservationist who writes about the state's towers says many of the survivors have deteriorated badly, pose a real danger, and may not legally be climbed.[^3] The register does not describe public access, so treat this tower as off limits to climbing and enjoy it from the ground.[^1]
