Manzanita Mountain, about 11 miles southwest of Grants Pass in Josephine County, carried a 30-foot wooden live-in lookout tower run by the Oregon Department of Forestry.[^1][^2] The register and firelookout.com date the tower to 1961.[^1][^2] Rex's page quotes the Southwest Oregon District's annual reports, which record materials bought in 1959 and construction costs in 1960, so the sources differ by a year.[^3]

The register and firelookout.com say the tower was last staffed in 2006.[^1][^2] Rex's page instead says staffing ended in 2007, when the lookout was declared unsafe.[^3] The annual reports also record upkeep, including painting inside and out in 1961 and a new metal roof in 1989.[^3]

The register says the tower was condemned and burned to the ground in 2010.[^1] Rex's page says District personnel burned it that spring, and firelookout.com also gives 2010 as the year it burned.[^2][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
