Smithland, a community in eastern Marion County, lies about twelve miles northeast of Jefferson along State Highway 49. The place was named for John Frank Smith, who settled there in 1842, when the area still lay within Cass County.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list includes a Smithland tower at 32.83425, -94.17305, and marks it as gone.[^2] A Texas tower list last revised in January 2011 gives a Smithland entry at the same point, tagged "TFS." The page names a Texas Forest Service ("TFS") list of 91 towers among its sources.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
