Firelookout.ca lists a Fire Tower Mountain Fire Tower at Blind River, Ontario.[^1] Its entry gives no tower type, height or year built.[^1]

The name also belongs to a hill. Mapcarta places a peak called Fire Tower Mountain in Blind River, Algoma District, at 339 m (1,112 ft), and the registry coordinates sit close to it.[^2][^1]

No source found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit.

[^1]: Fire Tower Mountain Fire Tower, Firelookout.ca, https://firelookout.ca/lookouts/?slug=fire-tower-mountain-fire-tower (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Fire Tower Mountain Map, Mapcarta, https://mapcarta.com/24251734 (accessed 2026-10-20).
