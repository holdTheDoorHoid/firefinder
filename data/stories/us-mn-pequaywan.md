In 1935 the Pequaywan Lookout Tower was still in use.[^1] A Civilian Conservation Corps newsletter, The Sullivanite, reported on November 16 that two men were still standing fire watch there.[^1] The writer ribbed them for drawing an easy assignment.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's statewide list places a tower called Pequaywan in Saint Louis County, at 47.19073 north and 91.87289 west, and marks it gone.[^2] A Forest Lookouts page titled Pequaywan Lake gives the county as St. Louis County and carries the same 1935 newsletter item.[^1]

Pequaywan Township, which shares the tower's name, sits inside the Cloquet Valley State Forest.[^3]

Today, no source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site or whether the site is open to the public.[^2]
