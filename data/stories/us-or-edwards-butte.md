Edwards Butte is in Tillamook County, Oregon, and the Oregon Department of Forestry administered the lookout.[^1][^2] The sources disagree on where it sits. One places it nine miles southeast of Tillamook,[^2] while a 1940 issue of The Forest Log, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, puts it nine miles west of Camp Trask.[^3]

The idea goes back to 1936.[^3] A newspaper report from that year, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, said the lookout was planned for the southwest portion of the area burned in 1933.[^3] A 1937 annual report said plans were under consideration to finish the lookout during 1939.[^3]

The tower went up in 1940.[^1][^2] The Forest Log that October described a crew under foreman A.F. Koster building it, with lumber packed in over a five-mile trail and a cabin for the crew to use.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register calls it a 40-foot pole L-4 tower.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page calls it a timber tower with a 14-by-14 cab.[^3] The same page gives an activation date of March 9, 1942, and names the Portland Filter Center in the same entry.[^3]

The sources disagree about when the tower came down. The register says it was removed in 1956.[^1] Firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page gives 1960 as the year it was removed.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the butte today, and none says whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3] Anyone curious should check with the Oregon Department of Forestry before going.
