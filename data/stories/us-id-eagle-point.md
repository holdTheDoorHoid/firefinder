Eagle Point stands in Clearwater County, Idaho, about four miles southeast of the Canyon Ranger Station.[^2] The first structure on the site was a two-story log cabin, built in 1935.[^1][^2] In 1955 a 41-foot treated timber tower with an L-4 cab took its place.[^2][^3]

The current cab is an R-6 flat top, built in 1973 on an open timber tower that stands 41 feet tall.[^1][^2] A 1959 Coast and Geodetic Survey description, quoted on a lookout-history site, says the Forest Service owns the tower and gives its height as 41 feet.[^4]

In the 1980s the lookout stopped being staffed every season and moved to emergency use.[^3][^2] The register says it is still occasionally staffed during emergencies.[^1]

The sources disagree on elevation. The register gives 5,548 feet (1,691 m), while firelookout.com gives 5,709 feet.[^1][^2] The register added Eagle Point in December 2020 as US 1467, ID 159.[^1]

I could not find a source that says whether visitors may go up the tower, so check with the Forest Service before planning a visit.
