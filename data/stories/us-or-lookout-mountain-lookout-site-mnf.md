Lookout Mountain rises to 8,029 feet in the Malheur National Forest, about 18 miles southwest of Unity in Baker County.[^1][^2] The register says a 20-foot pole L-4 tower went up there in 1931, and firelookout.com gives the same year and tower type.[^1][^2]

The tower was gone by the 1950s. The register says it was removed, while firelookout.com says it was destroyed. Both put that in the 1950s.[^1][^2] The Rex's Fire Lookout Page marks the site as removed and gives its survey location as 15S-35E-10.[^3]

The Oregon Hikers field guide calls the summit a former lookout site, set between the Strawberry Mountain and Monument Rock wilderness areas.[^4] The guide lists a 7.4-mile route to the top, with about 1,750 feet of gain, that runs on a road that has long been abandoned.[^4] That listing suggests the summit is open to walkers, and none of the sources cited here mentions a closure.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today.
