Round Pass sits in Lake County, Oregon, on the Fremont National Forest.[^2][^3] An eight-sided lookout tower shown at the 1916 Paisley fair was later moved to the site, according to the Oregon lookouts page.[^3] A house went up in 1922.[^1][^3] Sources disagree on what came after.[^1][^2][^3] The FFLOS register says it was replaced in 1930 by a 14-by-14-foot L-4 cab, and firelookout.com gives a 14x14 cab for that year. The Oregon lookouts page describes a 12-by-12 house and mentions no 1930 change.[^1][^2][^3]

John Donnelly held the lookout post during the 1921 and 1922 seasons.[^3] In 1949 the Herald and News reported that Dr. and Mrs. Abbott would be stationed there that summer.[^3]

In August 1929 the Round Pass lookout spotted a fire on Wildcat Creek, under Deadhorse rim.[^3] Putting it out took 3 1/2 hours, and the fire was 1 1/4 acres.[^3]

In 1958 the lookout duties moved to Morgan Butte, and the house went with them on a lowboy trailer.[^1][^3] By February 1959 an OMNI range station was in service on the lookout's old site.[^3] The Oregon lookouts page ends its history there, so no source here describes the Round Pass site today.[^3]

The house's new location is Morgan Butte, where a fire lookout now stands in the Fremont-Winema National Forest.[^4] A trail listing says that lookout was built in 1958, modified in 1980, and is staffed in summer.[^4] It also says the lookout is closed to the public, though the nearby camping spot is scenic.[^4] The 1958 date matches the year of the move, but no source says the two are the same building.[^4]
