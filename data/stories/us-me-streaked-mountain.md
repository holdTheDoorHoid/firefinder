Streaked Mountain is a 1,770-foot peak in Oxford County, and the sources place its tower near Hebron, Paris and Buckfield.[^1][^2][^3] In 1950 the state's forest service put a lookout on the high northwest point, to take over from an older tower on Bear Mountain in Hartford.[^2][^3] The 1950 tower was 34 feet tall.[^2][^3] A 1950 newspaper report said it would go into operation the following spring, so the exact start date is not settled by our sources.[^2]

Watchmen staffed the tower for long shifts. Neil Wilson began in 1978 and spent more than two fire seasons there, sitting in the tower nine hours a day.[^3] The summit also had a mobile home for the watchmen.[^3] While Wilson was on duty at the tower, a P-3 Orion plane blew up in the air over Poland.[^3] We found no named fires that the tower spotted in the sources we read.

In 1987 the original tower was replaced.[^3] The Sun Journal describes that work both as a rebuild and as a tower moved from Speckled Mountain in Stoneham.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association still lists the tower as standing.[^1]

A 2021 Sun Journal article says the tower is no longer accessible to hikers. It names only two Maine towers still used to spot fires, Ossipee Hill in Waterboro and Mount Hope in Sanford, and Streaked is not one of them.[^3] Treat the tower as closed, and do not climb it. Check with the Maine Forest Service before planning any trip to the mountain.
