Arrow Wood Point is a former lookout site on Maury Mountain in Crook County, Oregon, inside the Ochoco National Forest.[^1][^2][^3] The U.S. Forest Service administers the land.[^1] Two listings give a 1940 date for a frame cabin here and mention no tower.[^1][^2]

Before that cabin, the Oregon Lookouts history files two 1934 newspaper items under this name.[^3] The July 26 item reports that a crew had finished a five-and-a-half-mile telephone line from Maury Ranger Station to the top of Maury Mountain, where an emergency lookout station had been set up. That new station would be used when visibility was poor and the fire danger high.[^3] That station came six years before the 1940 cabin, and the page does not say whether it stood on this exact point.[^1][^2][^3]

An August 23, 1934 notice says that Hadaway Coahran, Jr., would spend the rest of the summer with his father, who worked at the Maury Mountain lookout station.[^3]

The cabin went to West Maury Lookout in 1954, and both Arrow Wood listings now call it gone.[^1][^2] The register's West Maury page describes a 14-by-14-foot cabin built there in 1954 and removed in 1970.[^4] The firelookout.com page for West Maury says it was destroyed that year instead, so the sources disagree on how it ended.[^5] Neither West Maury page names Arrow Wood, but the dates line up.[^4][^5]

Today no source describes what remains at the Arrow Wood Point site, such as footings or a foundation, and none says whether the spot is open to visitors or how to reach it.[^1][^2] Anyone thinking of visiting should check access with the Ochoco National Forest before going.
