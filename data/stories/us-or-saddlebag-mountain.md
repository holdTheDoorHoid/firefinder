Saddlebag Mountain is a former lookout site in Lincoln County, Oregon, about 21 miles west-northwest of Dallas.[^2] The Oregon Department of Forestry administered the lookout.[^1][^2][^3]

Sources disagree on when the first tower went up. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register and firelookout.com both date it to 1942 and describe a 30-foot wooden L-5 tower.[^1][^2] The Lincoln FPA annual reports instead record a 40-foot cedar pole tower with a 10-by-10 cabin, built in 1953, with a total cost of $1180.22.[^3]

In 1952 the annual report put the lookout on a part-time schedule and tied that to poor visibility.[^3]

The reports list a lightning arrestor in 1954, shutters made and placed in 1956, and roof and shutter repairs in 1959 and 1960.[^3]

The cab was replaced in 1964 at a cost of $1019.55.[^3] The lookout was dismantled in 1969 after several years of disuse. The report also said that wind damage and vandalism had made repairs too expensive.[^3] Other sources give 1971 instead.[^1][^2] The register says it was removed then, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed.[^1][^2]

No source we checked describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
