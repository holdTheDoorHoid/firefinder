Happy Valley Lookout sits on high ground in the Rincon Mountains, inside Saguaro National Park, east of Tucson, Arizona.[^1][^2] According to the Park Service, few fire outposts of this kind remain in the national parks.[^2]

A structure has stood on the site since 1904, according to the register, but the cabin there now is newer.[^1] The Park Service dates the current building to 1968, while the register lists the same cabin as built in 1969.[^2][^1] The cabin doubled as living quarters for fire fighters, who were usually young married couples.[^2]

Aerial patrols took over the lookout job in the late 1970s.[^2] After that, park rangers and trail crews used the building, but it later fell into disrepair.[^2] The register says the cabin's last staffing date is unknown.[^1]

The register added the cabin on May 23, 2022, as US 1597, AZ 79.[^1] In 2024, with help from Western National Parks, the park evaluated the building's history, and architectural historians found it eligible for the National Register of Historic Places.[^2] Park staff are now stabilizing the building and working on plans to restore it.[^2]

The Park Service says the windows are currently boarded up, and the building sits inside the Saguaro Wilderness.[^2][^1] Neither source says the cabin is open to visitors, so treat it as closed unless the park says otherwise. Do not enter the boarded building while the park is stabilizing it.
