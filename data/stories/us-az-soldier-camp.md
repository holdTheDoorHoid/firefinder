Soldier Camp was a lookout station in the Catalina Mountains, on the Coronado National Forest.[^1]

A June 1921 newspaper report, reprinted by the Forest Lookouts site, shows the station was already in use by then.[^1] The paper described a small forest fire in the Canada del Oro district that had been burning since Wednesday night.[^1] Ranger B.G. Mettler received the report at the Soldier Camp lookout station around 11:30 on Wednesday night.[^1] Forest Ranger Smith went to the scene with two men and reported on Thursday afternoon that they had the fire well in hand.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Soldier Camp site as gone, with an unknown type.[^2]

Today the name belongs to a populated place on the Catalina Highway in Pima County.[^3] No source we read describes what remains at the lookout site. A Forest Service page for the Soldier Trail describes an old prison camp at the end of the route from the Catalina Highway, but it never mentions a lookout, so it does not link that camp to the lookout site.[^4]
