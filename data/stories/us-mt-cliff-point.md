Cliff Point is a former fire lookout in Lincoln County, Montana, about 15 miles southwest of Eureka, in the Kootenai National Forest.[^1] The site listing gives its elevation as 4,530 feet.[^1]

The listing dates the tower to the 1930s and marks it as gone.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list also classes the site as a tower with the status gone, and gives its coordinates as 48.75705 north and 115.32430 west.[^2] The Forest Lookouts list of Lincoln County towers gives the land description as township 35 North, range 29 West, section 25.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and we found nothing about public access.[^1][^2][^3] Anyone thinking of visiting the spot should check with the Kootenai National Forest first.
