Walker Ford was a lookout tower site in Union County, Tennessee, and the list gives its elevation as 1,964 feet.[^1]

The list marks the tower as moved, and its remark reads "moved to Maynardville."[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register describes a Maynardville Fire Tower, 100 feet tall on Hinds Ridge, with views over Maynardville and Norris Lake.[^2] The register says the Tennessee Division of Forestry administers it, and that it was registered on September 28, 2015.[^2] It gives the tower's elevation as 1,716 feet.[^2]

The list also has a separate, standing entry for Maynardville at 1,722 feet.[^3] The two figures differ by six feet. The list names only a town, so it is likely, but not confirmed, that the Hinds Ridge tower is the one that left Walker Ford.[^1][^2][^3]

No source reached describes the Walker Ford site today. None says whether the Hinds Ridge site is open to the public.
