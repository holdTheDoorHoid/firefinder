Curly Bear Lookout stood in Glacier National Park, about five miles south of Saint Mary, in Glacier County, Montana.[^1] It sat on a mid-slope hillside half a mile north of the summit, at about 6,500 feet.[^1]

The lookout was a two-story frame cab, built in 1934.[^2] The page also records panorama photos taken on July 26, 1935.[^2]

Spring rains washed out the trail to the lookout.[^2] In September 1948 a trail camp was being set up in the St. Mary valley to rebuild the trail, according to a Hungry Horse News item quoted on the Forest Lookouts page.[^2]

The same page says the National Park Service burned the lookout in April 1963 because it had no further use.[^2] The firelookout.com page also dates its loss to 1963.[^1] The Huckleberry Hiker blog lists it as destroyed in 1963.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association list gives a different pair of coordinates. They do not fit the firelookout.com description of the site five miles south of Saint Mary, so we leave them out.[^1][^4]

No source we read describes what remains of the lookout today, such as footings or a foundation. It lies inside Glacier National Park,[^1] but none of the pages we read say whether the spot is open to visitors.
