Hoodoo Mountain, in Bonner County, Idaho, began its lookout history in 1923, when a cupola cabin went up on the summit.[^1][^2][^3][^4] In the 1930s a tower with an L-4 cab was built.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The lookout was abandoned in 1935.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts reports that the spot now hosts electronics, with no trace of the original lookout left.[^2] Two listings also tie the site to the Pend Oreille Timber Protection Association.[^1][^2]

The summit sits at 5,119 feet, and WillhiteWeb reports that tree growth has narrowed much of the view from it.[^4]

Getting to the summit depends on a gate.[^4] WillhiteWeb says you can drive to the top when the gate is open, though the last miles need higher clearance.[^4] Whether the public may use that road is not stated on these pages.[^3][^4]
