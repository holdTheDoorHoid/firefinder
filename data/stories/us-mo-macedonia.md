Macedonia is a U.S. Forest Service lookout on the Clark National Forest, in Ripley County, Missouri. It sits about 9½ miles north-northwest of Doniphan.[^1][^2]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the station in 1956.[^2] The survey describes a treated timber tower, about 90 feet high, with a small house at its top, near the west center of Section 6, Township 24 North, Range 2 East.[^2]

The lookout page gives different figures.[^1] It gives the tower's height as 100 feet, built of yellow pine timber, and it cites another source that puts a wooden tower at 75 feet, with no other structures in Section 7.[^1] Its header gives the location code 24N-2E-8.[^1] The sources disagree on height and section, so these figures should be treated with care.[^1][^2]

No source describes the site today.[^1][^2] The sources do not say whether the tower still stands, whether any part of it remains, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
