Foreman is a fire lookout in Martin County, and the state's lookout directory lists it under the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources.[^2] The Civilian Conservation Corps began erecting a 99-foot 9-inch steel tower there in April 1936, and the Emergency Conservation Works program covered the cost.[^1] Men from the corps' Camp P-67 finished the tower that August.[^1] Earlier, in July, the same camp had built a frame house with five rooms, measuring 30 by 36 feet, for the tower's lookout man.[^1]

A combined tool room and garage was finished at the tower in June 1938.[^1] The dates in the directory entry come from North Carolina's Department of Conservation and Development biennial reports for 1934 to 1936 and for 1938.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
