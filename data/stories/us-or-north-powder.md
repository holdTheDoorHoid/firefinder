The North Powder lookout stood in the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest, in Baker County, Oregon, about 21 miles northwest of Baker City.[^1][^2] The site sits at 8,897 feet (2,712 meters).[^1]

In 1939 a cupola was built on a warehouse roof at the site.[^1] A 19-foot pole platform followed in 1942.[^1][^2]

A photo caption on the register page dates a panoramic view of the North Powder Lookout to 1936, three years before the cupola went up.[^1]

Both sources date its abandonment to 1963.[^1][^2] The register lists it as gone.[^1] The site was added to the register on November 20, 2019, and the U.S. Forest Service administers it.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, is left at the site today.
