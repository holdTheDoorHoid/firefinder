Mount Hope is a hilltop near Sanford in York County, Maine.[^1] The Maine Forest Service erected a 60-foot steel Aermotor tower with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab there in 1962.[^1][^2] It replaced a wooden tower on nearby Ridley Hill, where the state had lost its lease.[^1][^2]

The steel tower had an earlier home.[^3] A Lewiston Sun Journal table dates the tower at Sabattus Mountain in Lovell to 1939, and says it was disassembled in 1962 and moved to Mount Hope.[^3][^2] The sources disagree on the hilltop's elevation: the register gives 688 feet (210 metres), while the marker gives 600 feet.[^1][^2]

Volunteers have kept the lookout staffed.[^1] The register names the Maine Forest Service and the Sanford Fire Department as cooperators, and says the tower is staffed each year by volunteers.[^1] A 2000 article on York County's volunteer lookouts said the tower "continues to be staffed by volunteers."[^4] A historical marker also honours Ranger Anna Woodward of Cape Neddick, who served in the lookout for more than two decades.[^2]

A 2021 Sun Journal table counted Mount Hope as one of two standing, active towers in Maine.[^3] I found no published visiting arrangements for the tower, and the sources I read record no notable fires at this site.
