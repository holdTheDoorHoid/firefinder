Turner Peak sits seven miles east of Avery, in Shoshone County, Idaho, on the St. Joe National Forest.[^1][^2] Its listed elevation is 5,825 feet.[^1][^2]

The lookout went up in 1934. It was a 10-foot pole tower of the L-4 type.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts names Dan Zobel as the staffer for 1934, citing a book.[^2] It also names George Terrien for 1939 and 1940, citing the Former Forest Fire Lookout Registry.[^2]

Both sources give 1969 as the year the tower was destroyed.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list also marks the tower as gone.[^3]

A post dated October 25, 2023 describes four concrete footings and a concrete step at the site, and says little else is there.[^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as drive-up.[^2] None of these sources says whether the public may enter the site.
