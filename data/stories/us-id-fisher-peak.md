Fisher Peak Lookout stood on the Kaniksu National Forest in Boundary County, at 7,580 feet.[^1] In 1929, two newspapers reported plans for a new station on Fisher Peak, one of three lookouts the Forest Service was considering that year.[^2]

The sources disagree on when the lookout's cab went up. Firelookout.com dates an L-4 cab, set on four-foot concrete piers, to 1930.[^1] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page dates an L-4 cab on the northernmost point to 1937, although the same page lists a staffer in 1931.[^3]

Named staff include Lloyd Hydorn in 1931, John Dolan in 1932 and 1933, Chester Huffman in 1934, and Howard Monks Jr. in 1935.[^1][^3] The Idaho page adds that Randall Bearse assisted Monks that year and came from a Smith Creek CCC camp.[^3] David Pace is listed for 1936.[^1][^3]

Both pages say the lookout was gone by 1964, and firelookout.com calls it abandoned.[^1][^3] Both also place a crow's nest two miles southeast, on Farnham Peak.[^1][^3]

Today, Fisher Peak Trail #27 reaches a saddle below the summit.[^4] The Forest Service says the trail was made to reach the former lookout, which is long gone, though the views remain.[^4] The trail is about seven miles long and is listed for mid-June to November, subject to weather. A short half-mile spur climbs from the saddle to the summit.[^4] Parking is limited to turnouts near the trailhead, about 5.5 miles up Trout Creek Road.[^4]
