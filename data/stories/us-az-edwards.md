Edwards was a fire lookout tower in Graham County, Arizona, on the San Carlos Apache Reservation.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Arizona list gives its position as 33.47314, -109.94733 and marks it as gone.[^2] Mapcarta gives the site an elevation of 6,411 feet, or 1,954 metres.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. Because it lies within the San Carlos Apache Reservation, treat it as tribal land and ask the tribe for permission before going there.[^1]
