Boehls Butte Lookout stood about 10 miles northeast of Elk River in Clearwater County, Idaho, at an elevation of 3,610 feet.[^1] Firelookout.com dates it to 1934, when it was a 40-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1] The Idaho Fire Lookouts site gives the same 1934 build and tower type.[^2]

An aerial photograph in the Archives West collection carries the caption "Beohls Butte Lookout," spelled that way in the catalogue, and is dated 1936.[^4]

The Forest Lookouts site quotes a 1953 annual report from the Potlatch Timber Protective Association, which says the tower was fully rebuilt that year.[^3]

The tower was destroyed in 1972.[^1][^2]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, and none says whether the public may visit it today. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page tags the site as a drive-up site.[^2]
