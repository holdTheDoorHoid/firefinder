Dollar Watch Mountain sits in the central Pasayten, on the Okanogan National Forest.[^1][^4] The site was used as a tent camp during the 1920s.[^4] A lookout's diary from 1923, quoted on WillhiteWeb, records a fire report that was checked and found to be nothing.[^4]

A lookout house was built in 1930, and its materials were carried in across more than 15 miles.[^4][^3] The FFLOS register and firelookout.com call it an L-4 cab built that year,[^1][^2] WillhiteWeb calls it a cupola lookout house,[^4] and the Washington Lookouts page names it an "Aladdin" lookout house.[^3] Firelookout.com lists Cliff Burgett as known lookout personnel in 1929, a year before its build date.[^2]

The cab came down in 1954.[^1][^2][^4] The FFLOS register says it was removed, while firelookout.com and WillhiteWeb say it was destroyed.[^1][^2][^4]

Today the site lies in the Pasayten Wilderness, roughly 14 miles on foot from Billy Goat Trailhead.[^4] According to WillhiteWeb, an ordinary car can handle the gravel road to the trailhead, but the trail to the old lookout probably has no signs.[^4] A WillhiteWeb photo caption notes remains of the lookout, though no large pieces were found.[^4]
