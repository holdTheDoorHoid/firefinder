Rollins Mountain is in Penobscot County, Maine.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine list gives the site a tower and marks that tower gone.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the summit is open to visitors.

[^1]: Maine Fire Towers, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/me/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
