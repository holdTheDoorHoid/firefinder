Lolo Summit's lookout stood on the Lolo National Forest in Missoula County, Montana, about two miles northeast of Lolo Pass, at an elevation of 6,360 feet.[^1] Forest Lookouts lists its legal location as 10N-23W-6.[^2]

Firelookout.com describes a 20-foot platform tower built in 1917.[^1] The pass itself is listed at 5,233 feet, which puts the lookout roughly 1,100 feet above it.[^1][^3] Lolo Pass sits in the Bitterroot Range, and US 12 crosses it.[^3] The pass lies in both Idaho County, Idaho, and Missoula County, Montana.[^3]

Firelookout.com says the platform was abandoned by 1930.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks it as gone.[^4]

No source we read describes the site today, including whether any part of the platform remains or whether visitors can reach it.
