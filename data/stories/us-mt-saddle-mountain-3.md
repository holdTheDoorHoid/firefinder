Saddle Mountain stands in Ravalli County, Montana, about two miles northwest of Lost Trail Pass, inside the Bitterroot National Forest.[^1][^3] The firelookout.com page gives its elevation as 8,482 feet.[^1]

The site was first set up as a camp in 1939.[^1] The pole L-4 tower that followed was built in 1941, and firelookout.com describes it as a 10' pole L-4 tower.[^1]

The page lists known lookout personnel for 1945, 1958 and 1961.[^1]

The tower was torn down in 1972.[^1] The Forest Lookouts entry for Saddle Mountain marks it "Removed,"[^3] and the firelookout.org list shows the tower as gone.[^2]

The three sources agree that the tower is gone. None of them describes what, if anything, remains on the summit now, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
