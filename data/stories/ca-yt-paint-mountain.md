Paint Mountain, near Haines Junction in the Yukon, is the site of a fire-lookout tower.[^1] The hiking guide notes a helipad, the lookout tower and several communication towers at the summit.[^1]

Canada Lookouts logs a 2012 change from primary to emergency lookout, with air patrol slated to take over the primary duties.[^3]

Trina Moyles, who once worked as a fire tower lookout in northwestern Alberta, wrote that several Yukon towers have been shut down in recent years, including the one atop Paint Mountain, while five remain actively staffed.[^2] The article says would-be lookouts apply with Yukon Wildfire Management.[^2]

The hiking guide describes a route of 4 km (about 13,100 ft) with 714 m (2,343 ft) of climbing.[^1] It starts at the end of Pine Lake Road, in a residential area where visitors are asked to park with care.[^1] The guide says the buildings and communication equipment on the summit are private property and asks walkers to respect them.[^1] The route also crosses Champagne and Aishihik First Nations settlement land, and the guide asks walkers to respect their rules there.[^1]
