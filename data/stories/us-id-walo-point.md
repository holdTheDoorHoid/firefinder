Walo Point is in Shoshone County, in the St. Joe National Forest.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives its elevation as 5,900 feet.[^3]

A camp is recorded there in 1929.[^2] In 1935 an L-4 tower was set up atop the northernmost end of the ridge.[^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts also lists a patrol point on Attention Peak.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts names Boyd Hanna among the known staff for 1930, with a reference to Bert Russell's book Hardship & Happy Times.[^3]

Both sites give 1952 as the year the tower was destroyed.[^2][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the tower as gone.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site a hike-in.[^3]
