The Buttermilk Butte lookout was a cab set on the ground rather than a tower.[^1][^3] The L-4 cab was built on the butte in 1934, about 10 miles southwest of Twisp in the Okanogan National Forest.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookout Association lists the original butte as a ground site, now gone.[^3]

In 1951 a private buyer took the cab, and it ended up at a ranch four miles up the Twisp River.[^1] A separate relocation list gives the same move as a ranch up the Twisp River.[^4] The association's list shows the standing cab under the name Reynaud.[^3] None of the sources ties that name to the ranch, so the two may or may not be the same place.

The firelookout.com page says the cab is still there.[^2] Since it went to a private party, the sources describe no public access.[^1][^2]
