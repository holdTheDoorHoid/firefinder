Mormon Peak is in the Lolo National Forest, about 4 miles southwest of Lolo, in Missoula County, Montana.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 6,017 feet.[^1]

The lookout went up in 1938.[^1] That year a lookout house built to a 1931 plan was set on a pole tower, according to a Montana State Historic Preservation Office form on fire lookouts.[^3] Firelookout.com describes the tower as a 30-foot pole tower.[^1]

The only lookout worker the firelookout.com page names is Mrs. Robert Haugen, listed for 1962.[^1]

The tower was destroyed in 1980, according to firelookout.com, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as gone.[^1][^2]

None of the cited sources describes what, if anything, remains on the peak today. A Gaia GPS listing says a moderate one-way trail in the forest goes by the peak, so hikers can pass it on that route.[^4] The cited sources do not say whether the old lookout site itself is open to visitors.
