The Georgia tower list places a Pulaski Lookout Tower in Pulaski County, at 32.25544, -83.48017 and an elevation of 302 feet (92 m).[^1] It also gives the site the numbers BLL-64710 and gfc-335.[^1]

A 2013 chapter report from FFLA says the author understood that two Georgia towers would be auctioned for removal, one in Richmond County and another in Pulaski County.[^3] The author was the group's Georgia and South Carolina area representative.[^3] The report does not name the Pulaski tower or say where in the county it stood, and it does not confirm that a sale took place.[^3]

OpenStreetMap, a volunteer-edited map, carries a point named Pulaski Lookout Tower and tags it as a lattice observation tower.[^2] We could not find a source that describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
