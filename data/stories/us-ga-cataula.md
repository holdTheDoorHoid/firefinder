The Georgia lookout tower list at tnlandforms.us gives a tower called Cataula in Harris County, with an elevation of 710 and no unit given (read here as feet, about 216 m), and places it at latitude 32.66749 and longitude -84.87290.[^1] The list says its names for towers numbered 73 and higher are the compiler's own creations, so this name may not be the one the tower carried.[^1] The list's map was revised on 22 March 2016.[^1]

Since the late 1980s, Georgia has steadily trimmed its fire-tower personnel and turned to aerial patrols, which officials consider cheaper and more effective.[^2] A 2006 newspaper report, citing a forestry commission spreadsheet, found that 137 of the state's 144 surviving towers were no longer staffed.[^2] That article does not mention this tower.[^2]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
