The Oberlin Lookout Tower is in Allen Parish, Louisiana.[^1] The Forest Lookouts website names the Louisiana Forestry Commission as its agency.[^1] The same site's state list shows two Oberlin entries, marked "Site 1" and "Site 2," both in Allen Parish.[^2]

In 1915 Louisiana set aside money for fire protection, enabling it to hire people to staff lookouts as watchmen or patrol officers.[^4] Statewide, the count of fire towers reached 39 by 1943 and 56 by 1950.[^4]

A NOAA weather station named "OBERLIN FIRE TOWER" keeps a daily record that starts on 1 May 1952 and runs to 16 October 2026.[^3] That record gives the station's coordinates and elevation.[^3] It does not describe the lookout's construction or history.[^3]

No source we read says whether the tower still stands or whether visitors can reach the site.
