Trout Peak sits about 13 miles east-northeast of Sandpoint, in Bonner County, Idaho, on land in the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^3] The earliest record we found is from 1931, when the peak was listed as a fire finder site, with no tower mentioned.[^1][^3]

Later evidence is less clear. The Idaho Fire Lookouts team dates a tower or cabin here to 1942, but with a question mark, and says a 1942 map shows a "Lookout Station."[^2] Two benchmarks dating to 1947 led the same writers to guess that a larger tower went up about then.[^2]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page lists two known staff for 1949, Bob Kurth and Arnold Krause.[^2]

The site is listed as abandoned, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's list shows the tower as gone.[^1][^2][^4]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts team visited in September 2025 and labels the site a hike-in.[^2] The trailhead they had planned to use was roped off and closed when they reached it. A slow-moving fire was a few miles away. They took a nearby trail instead.[^2] That trail was longer than they expected, and a large section was overgrown.[^2] At the top they found cement footings, lumber on the south side of the peak, and a rock arrow next to the lookout foundation.[^2]

No source we read says whether the site is open to the public today. Check with the Kaniksu National Forest before you go, since closures and trail conditions change.
