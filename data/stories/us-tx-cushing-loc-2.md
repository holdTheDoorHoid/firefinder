Cushing was a Texas Forest Service fire lookout in Nacogdoches County.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists two Cushing sites, Loc 1 and Loc 2, and marks both as gone.[^2] This story covers Loc 2, the more southerly of the two.[^2]

A map database lists a Cushing Lookout Tower at latitude 31.7674, and says the city of Cushing lies four miles northwest of it.[^3] That point matches the index position for Loc 2.[^2][^3] The same entry gives an elevation of 696 feet.[^3]

No source I read describes what remains at the site today. The map entry is a database listing with no account of whether a structure still stands, and nothing I read says whether the spot is open to the public.[^3]
