Wolf Lake was a fire lookout tower in Becker County, Minnesota, in township 139 North, range 37 West, section 16.[^1] According to the Forest Lookouts list, the steel Aermotor tower went up there in 1929.[^1]

Wikipedia's Becker County article names Wolf Lake as the site of a U.S. Forest Service lookout tower.[^2]

The same list says the tower came down in 1999, following a sale the year before.[^1] The association's Minnesota list marks it gone.[^3]

No source we read describes the site today, so we cannot say what remains or whether the spot is open to visitors. Because the tower is listed as gone, there is no tower to climb.[^3]
