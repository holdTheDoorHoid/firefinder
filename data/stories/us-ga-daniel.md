The Georgia Forestry Commission's history says the first fire observation tower went up on the Ware Hotel in Waycross, possibly as a result of a 1928 forestry fair in that city.[^1]

A Georgia lookout tower list places a tower named "daniel" in Bryan County, at about 31.9 degrees north and 81.4 degrees west.[^2] The list gives it the number 134, taken from an older Georgia Forestry Commission list that described the locations of 203 towers.[^2] The name is a label rather than a record, since the list says names for towers numbered 73 and above are its compiler's own, and this tower is numbered 94.[^2]

By 2006, a forestry commission spreadsheet showed that 137 of the state's 144 still-existing towers were no longer staffed.[^3] Of those, 119 were listed as antenna towers, which are usually unoccupied. Commission staff still climb them now and then in fire season, while aircraft patrols have mostly taken over the work of tower operators.[^3]

Across the state, towers were being disposed of through auctions, scrap sales and sales to hunting clubs and similar groups.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
