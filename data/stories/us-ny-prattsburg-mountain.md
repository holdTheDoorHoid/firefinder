Prattsburg Mountain, in Steuben County, once carried an 80-foot Aermotor LS-40 fire lookout tower.[^1]

Bill Starr's list dates the tower to 1950.[^1] The pictorial history links that year's work to a Capital Construction Fund granted by the Legislature, and says ten new fire towers went up in 1950.[^2]

According to Starr's notes, the tower was in operation for three months a year: April, May and October.[^1] When fire danger was extremely high, the season stretched into June and November.[^1] At other times it ran the entire fire season.[^1]

The Starr list and CNY Hiking both mark the tower as removed.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list also shows it as gone.[^4]

No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
