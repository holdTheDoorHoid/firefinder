Bear Hill is a summit in Allenstown, in Merrimack County, New Hampshire.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of New Hampshire towers marks the Bear Hill tower as gone.[^3]

A hiker who went up on Sunday, March 22, 2020 found the foundations of the former fire tower just below the summit, along with plenty of anthills.[^4] The hiker had parked in the lot at the park gates on Podunk Road, walked along the road to the Bear Hill Trail, and from there found the summit 1.2 miles on. The park gates were still closed when the hiker arrived.[^4] The account describes no standing tower, only the foundations.[^4]

That March 2020 visit is the most recent account we read. No source describes the summit since then.[^4]
