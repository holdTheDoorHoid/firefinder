The Conservation Commission erected a 60-foot Aermotor LS40 fire tower on Number Seven Hill in 1922, and it became operational in 1923.[^1] Starr's list gives 1923 as the opening year, and the chapter's January 2023 newsletter calls 1923 the tower's first year of operation.[^3][^2] The hill sits about five miles southeast of Averill Park, in Rensselaer County.[^1][^3]

An observer's cabin went up on the hill in 1923, one of several new cabins built that year.[^2] The tower closed at the end of the 1970 season, as aerial detection came in.[^1][^2] It was later removed.[^1][^2]

A New York chapter post on Facebook reports that little is left of the Seven Hill tower site near Stephentown.[^4] No source I read says who owns the hilltop now, or whether the public may go there.
