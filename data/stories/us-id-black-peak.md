Black Peak stands at 6,258 feet, nine miles north-northeast of the Red Ives Ranger Station.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its location as Shoshone County, Idaho, and Mineral County, Montana.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts places it in the St. Joe National Forest, while Firelookout.com lists the St. Joe and Lolo National Forests together.[^1][^2]

The earliest record is a camp in the 1930s.[^1] Both sites put the patrol point on Berge Peak, three miles to the northwest.[^1][^2]

Both sites list it as abandoned, and Firelookout.com dates that to the 1930s.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site a hike-in.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts also covers a different Black Peak, on the Kootenai National Forest at 6,548 feet.[^3] Its history lists a cabin with a stone basement in 1929 and says it was destroyed in 1968.[^3] Keep the two apart when you search.
