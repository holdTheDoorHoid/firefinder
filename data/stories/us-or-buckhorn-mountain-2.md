A Buckhorn Mountain lookout in Douglas County appears on the Oregon Forest Lookouts list, at section 4 of township 32 South, range 5 West.[^1] The list says a lookout was established on the site while that section was still part of the Umpqua National Forest.[^1]

After a land adjustment, the site came under state control, and the list names the Oregon Department of Forestry as the agency now involved.[^1] The list does not describe what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

A different Buckhorn Mountain Lookout, in Wallowa County, is listed on the National Historic Lookout Register. It is a separate site and not the Douglas County lookout described here.[^2]
