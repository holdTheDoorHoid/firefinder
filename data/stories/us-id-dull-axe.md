Dull Axe Lookout stood in Clearwater County, about 26 miles northeast of Orofino.[^1] The Clearwater Timber Protective Association is the agency listed for it.[^3]

Before the tower, the site had other lookout structures. Firelookout.com dates a crow's nest there to 1915, a pole platform to 1924 and a log cabin to 1927.[^1] In 1934 a 40-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab went up, and a 60-foot pole tower with an L-6 cab stood half a mile north.[^1][^2]

The sources differ on what happened in 1957. Firelookout.com and the Idaho Fire Lookouts site both list the lookout as destroyed that year.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts site says the 40-foot tower was removed that year and cites an annual report from the Clearwater association.[^3]

The agency changed later on. The Clearwater and Potlatch timber protective associations were formed separately in 1905, and they merged in 1966 into the Clearwater-Potlatch Timber Protective Association.[^4] The Idaho Fire Lookouts site labels the lookout a drive-up one, though it is gone.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the tower as gone.[^5]

We found no source that describes what remains of the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
