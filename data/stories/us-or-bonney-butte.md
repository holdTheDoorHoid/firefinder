The Bonney Butte lookout site is in the Mount Hood National Forest, about nine miles southeast of Mount Hood.[^2] The U.S. Forest Service administered it.[^1]

The sources disagree on the county.[^1][^2][^3] The register places the site in Wasco County, while firelookout.com and the Forest Lookouts page say Hood River County.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Service set up a tent with a firefinder and a telephone there in 1925.[^1] The register and firelookout.com both date an L-4 cab on the site to 1933, and the register places it on the south end of the ridge below the summit.[^1][^2]

A Forest Lookouts item dated October 1932 describes a new standard cabin already built at Bonney Butte, so the cabin date is not settled.[^3]

The lookout was staffed for 74 days in 1941, and Dufur Ranger Station, its reporting station, was connected by telephone.[^3] In 1945 members of the Trails Club of Oregon staffed the station.[^3]

The lookout was deactivated in 1964.[^3][^4] In November 1967 a Forest Service employee set fire to the building to dispose of it.[^3] The register says the lookout was gone by 1967.[^1]

A hiking guide says a panel at the site tells the lookout's history.[^4] A trail guide refers to the remnants of the old lookout and says it was removed in the 1960s.[^5]

Hikers can reach the site on foot.[^4][^5] The trail guide describes a half-mile hike from the trailhead parking area to the summit.[^5] It also says the road up the butte is closed to motor vehicles from August 15 to November 1 to protect migrating raptors, which puts early October inside the closure.[^5] Oregon Hikers marks its hikes to the site "Closed" without saying why, so check current closures before a visit.[^4]

The register listed the site as US 576, OR 17, in August 2010.[^1]
