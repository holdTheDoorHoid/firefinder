Frye Knob, also called Frye Mountain, stands at about 4,495 feet in the Nantahala National Forest in Swain County.[^1][^2] The tower commanded a view of the Bryson City valley.[^1]

The register dates the tower to the 1930s.[^1] It describes the tower as built like a two-story house, with living quarters below and the fire lookout upstairs.[^1] The U.S. Forest Service administered it.[^1]

A panorama taken from the Frye Mountain fire lookout station on October 30, 1935, survives in a 1935-1936 fire lookout station survey by Lester Moe.[^3] It shows that a lookout station was on the mountain by then.[^3]

The Forest Lookouts site marks the tower as removed, without giving a date.[^2] A register photo from 2013, credited to Peter Barr, is captioned as a former lookout site.[^1] The register added the site on September 12, 2013, on the nomination of Bill Randolph.[^1]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, is left at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
