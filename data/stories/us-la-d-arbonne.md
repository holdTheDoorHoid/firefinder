The D'Arbonne Fire Tower was in Union Parish, Louisiana, about 10 miles north of Ruston.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page gives the location as 20N-1W-Sec.26 and names the Louisiana Forewstry Commission, spelled that way on the page.[^1]

In June 1979 local amateur radio operators planned their annual Field Day exercise at the tower. The American Radio Relay League sponsored the event, and members of the public were welcome.[^1]

A weather station named "D ARBONNE FIRE DNGR TOWER" has records in the Iowa Environmental Mesonet from February 1952 to August 1953.[^2] The name suggests the tower was in use by then. The page does not describe the tower itself.[^2]

No source I could reach describes the tower or its site today, including whether a structure remains or the site is open to the public.
