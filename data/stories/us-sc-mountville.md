Mountville was a fire lookout run by the South Carolina Forestry Commission in Laurens County.[^1][^2] The lookout-history site lists it as removed.[^1]

The tower was part of a statewide system that the commission's own wildfire history dates to the 1930s.[^3] A commission spokesperson said the towers were first built in the 1930s, when land clearing and weak farming methods had made rural wildfires more likely.[^4] Across the system, the towers had a small cab at the top, and operators climbed up into it in dry spells to watch for smoke.[^4]

The commission shut its final lookout tower on October 1, 1993.[^3] The lookout-history page gives only the name, county, agency and the word "Removed," so no source I found describes what, if anything, remains on the Mountville site or whether it is open to visitors.[^1]
