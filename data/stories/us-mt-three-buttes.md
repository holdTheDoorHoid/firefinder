The Three Buttes are flat-topped landforms northwest of Lodge Pole in Blaine County, Montana, at an elevation of 3,848 feet (1,173 m).[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a lookout here as a ground structure rather than a tower, and marks it gone.[^2]

One video's title suggests a fire lookout on the Three Buttes may date to the 1930s.[^3] A second video's title puts a lookout on the summit and says it burned in the 1980s.[^4] That title gives only the decade.

A 2021 hiking blog describes a winter climb to the top of Middle Butte, the tallest of the Three Buttes, and places the hike on the Fort Belknap Reservation.[^5] Check access before you go; no source I could read states the access rules.

No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout on the summit today.
