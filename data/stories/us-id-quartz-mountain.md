Quartz Mountain Crow's Nest was a 60-foot crow's nest on Quartz Mountain in the Kaniksu National Forest, about 10 miles north of Priest River in Bonner County.[^1][^2] Pages date it to about 1937.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts describes the nest as set in a tree.[^2] Firelookout.com says it was still there in the 1980s and marks it abandoned.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts reports that the crows-nest tree has since fallen, but it does not know when that happened.[^2]

The most recent description covers what is left of the tree. Idaho Fire Lookouts found a standing trunk and a fallen trunk, both still studded with boot pegs.[^2] The same post also reports finding older bottles tucked into the brush below the site.[^2] The post lists the site as drive-up.[^2] No source says whether the site is closed or whether any permit applies.
