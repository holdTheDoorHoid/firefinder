The Powell Mountain lookout stood on the flat top of the mountain in Garland County, inside the Ouachita National Forest.[^1][^2] A Coast and Geodetic Survey description from 1916 places the summit station close to the south edge of a small clearing.[^2]

The Central US Lookouts page dates a 50-foot steel tower to 1924.[^1] A survey recovery note from 1935 says the Forest Service had built a 60-foot steel lookout tower, centered over the station.[^2] The sources do not say whether these describe the same structure, so the height is uncertain.[^1][^2]

The page marks the tower as removed, without a date.[^1] A 1956 survey note says the Forest Service tower had been torn down, and that the concrete footings of the tower remained.[^2] A 1972 note mentions a concrete footing from the old lookout still in place.[^2]

The most recent recovery note in the survey record is dated 1989. It puts the station in a small clearing where a lookout tower and building once stood, and lists the land as owned by the U.S. government.[^2] No source describes the clearing in more detail, and none says whether the public can visit.[^1][^2] Check with the Ouachita National Forest before you go.

[^2]: Station EJ1941 POWELL, datasheet with station description and recovery notes, National Geodetic Survey, https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=EJ1941 (accessed 2026-10-10).
