Whitney Hill's lookout tower went up in the summer of 1929, part of Maine's Forestry District system in Aroostook County. A local newspaper reported that April that a new station would be built on Whitney Hill in Macwahoc Plantation, within the Mattawamkeag District: a 75-foot steel tower.[^1]

Beyond that, little about its early decades turns up in the sources found for this entry. The state Forest Commissioner's biennial report for 1967–68 records that a wood storage shed was built at the Whitney Hill tower that year, which suggests the station was still in service, or at least being maintained, nearly four decades after it was raised.[^1]

What happened to the tower after that — whether it kept watching for smoke into more recent decades, was retired, or still stands on the hill today — is not documented in the sources available here. Nothing was found tying it to a hiking trail or public access, so its current condition and reachability remain unknown.

[^1]: Whitney Hill, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/whitney-hill.html (accessed 2026-10-04).
