The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists two towers under the name Wallaceton in the City of Chesapeake, and both are marked as gone.[^1] The entry Loc 1 is listed at latitude 36.58743 and longitude -76.38678. The other, Loc 2, is listed at 36.59963 and -76.38082.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts Virginia page spells the name WALLACETOWN.[^2]

The name also belongs to Wallaceton, a building in Chesapeake that started life as a store.[^3] The store was meant for workers at the Wallace Company, a lumber firm, and for the people of Wallaceton, a community beside the Dismal Swamp Canal that is now inside Chesapeake's limits.[^3] Do not confuse that building with either lookout.

No source read describes what, if anything, remains at either site today, or whether either site is open to visitors.
