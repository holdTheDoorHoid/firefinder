The Forest Lookouts list includes a site called Flashman in McCurtain County.[^1] The map database TopoQuest places a Flashman Lookout Tower in that county at about 1,745 feet above sea level, on the USGS 1:24,000 map for Clebit, Oklahoma.[^2]

Oklahoma's state forestry history gives the wider setting. It says that towers went up across southeastern Oklahoma during the 1920s, and that the agency's protection zone once held 23 of those towers.[^3] After the observers retired, aircraft overhead took over their watch.[^3] That history describes the program in general, not this tower.[^3]

The sources give no build date, staffing record or condition for this tower. Today, I found no source saying whether the tower still stands, what remains of it, or whether the public may visit.

[^3]: History of the Oklahoma State Forestry Division, Oklahoma Department of Agriculture, https://ag.ok.gov/oklahoma-state-forestry-history/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
