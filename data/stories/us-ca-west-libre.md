The Angeles National Forest put up West Libre Lookout in 1936.[^1] The cabin followed California Region 5 Plan BC-301 and sat on a 20-foot enclosed timber tower.[^1] A photo page on peakbagging.com shows the tower as it probably looked in the 1950s, and says the tower dates from 1936.[^2] The Angeles Adventures site gives the lookout's dates as 1936 to the 1960s.[^3]

The register says Forest Service personnel purposely burned the tower in 1969.[^1] Peakbagging.com tells it differently: the tower was demolished at some point before 1980, and that page does not know when.[^2] Both accounts put the end before 1980, but they disagree on how it ended.[^1][^2] The register's 1969 date and method are used here, with that disagreement noted.[^1][^2]

Peakbagging.com says nothing is left at the site now.[^2] The site lies at about 5,112 feet (1,558 metres) in Los Angeles County, but we found no source on whether visitors may go there.[^1]
