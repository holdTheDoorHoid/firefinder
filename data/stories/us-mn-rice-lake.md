Rice Lake was a fire lookout in Aitkin County, Minnesota, about two miles west of the refuge office at Rice Lake National Wildlife Refuge.[^1] A report on Aitkin County's fire towers places the refuge about six miles south of McGregor, off Highway 65.[^1] The refuge's own page gives the distance as about five miles, so the two sources differ.[^2]

The report says the Civilian Conservation Corps built the lookout in 1940, and that it had an observation cabin.[^1] Its date table, however, lists the tower as about 1947, so the report gives two dates.[^1] The table gives the height as 100 feet (30 m) and the type as a stairway tower.[^1] The report describes it as mostly wood, built from 8-by-8 fir timbers.[^1]

The table names the United States Fish and Wildlife Service as owner.[^1] The report says the tower was dismantled in 1981 because it had deteriorated, and that a local citizen bought it for $1.00.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list marks it as gone.[^3]

Today the refuge is open daily from sunrise to sunset, and visiting is free.[^2] The refuge was created in 1935 to safeguard habitat for waterfowl.[^2] The refuge's page does not describe the tower site, and no source we found says what remains there now.[^2]
