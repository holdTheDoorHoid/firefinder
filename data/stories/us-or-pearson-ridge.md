The Pearson Ridge lookout stood in the Umatilla National Forest, in Umatilla County, Oregon.[^1]

It was a 93-foot wooden tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab.[^1][^2] The register and the Forest Lookouts site both date it to 1935.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com gives 1934 instead, and says the tower replaced an earlier crow's nest.[^2]

Firelookout.com lists James O'Donel as the lookout in 1946.[^2] Staffing was hard to fill in 1953.[^3] A district letter quoted on the Forest Lookouts site said three men had been hired and let go, and at least six more had been interviewed.[^3] The letter gave the main reason applicants turned the job down as the tower's height, which it put at 104 feet.[^3] The register and firelookout.com both give 93 feet.[^1][^2] A note in the forest files records that the stairway was rebuilt in 1958.[^3]

The register says the tower was removed in the 1970s, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed then.[^1][^2] Neither source gives a year.[^1][^2] The site was entered in the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register on August 21, 2011.[^1]

The register notes that the Pearson Guard Station, one mile north, was still there at the time of its entry.[^1] No source we read describes what remains at the lookout site, or whether the site is open to visitors.
