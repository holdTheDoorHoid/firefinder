Little Baldy lies in Shoshone County, Idaho, within the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1][^3] The Weebly list gives its location as 50N-5E-8, meaning township 50 north, range 5 east, section 8.[^3]

A camp is listed there in 1920, and a log cabin with a cupola in 1922.[^1][^2][^3]

In 1940 a 20-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab went up on the west point.[^1][^2][^3]

Mr. and Mrs. Joe Witt are the lookouts named for 1946.[^1][^2]

All three sources record the tower as destroyed in 1970.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list also marks it as gone.[^4]

No source describes what remains of the camp, the cabin or the tower on the site today.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page calls the site hike-in, which means it is reached on foot, but no source says whether the ground is open to visitors.[^2]
