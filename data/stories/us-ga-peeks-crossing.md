Tom Dunigan's list of Georgia lookout towers places a tower called "peeks crossing" in Coweta County, at an elevation of 949 feet (289 m).[^1] The list gives no build date, builder, height or design for it.[^1] Its notes column is empty, while many other Georgia entries on the same list carry a note or code there.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register says it has 29 lookouts registered in Georgia, and none of the names on its Georgia list matches this tower.[^2]

A similar name, Peek's Crossing, belonged to one of several railroad whistle stops in Coweta County, where passengers flagged trains down at small stations, according to the Newnan-Coweta History Center.[^3]

Statewide, the Georgia Forestry Commission started moving away from staffed lookouts toward aerial patrols in the late 1980s, and many of the towers were later scrapped and sold.[^4] No source we found links this tower to that change.

We found no source that describes what, if anything, is left on the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
