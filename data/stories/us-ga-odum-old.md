Odum old is the name the Georgia lookout list gives to a tower in Wayne County, at about 31.66 degrees north and 82.02 degrees west.[^1] The list tags it with a survey datasheet number, BS1567.[^1]

The list's heading says its fire towers were found on 1:100,000 USGS topographic maps and on NGS data sheets.[^1]

A second entry, named "odum," sits about 3 km north-north-west of it and carries the Georgia Forestry Commission number 807. The distance is worked out from the two listed coordinates.[^1]

A map site lists an "Old Odum Water Tower" about 0.6 km west of the listed spot and describes it as a water tower, so the two should not be mixed up. The distance is worked out from the two sets of coordinates.[^1][^3]

Odum is a town in Wayne County, Georgia, and its population was 463 at the 2020 census.[^2]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything remains there or whether the spot is open to visitors.
