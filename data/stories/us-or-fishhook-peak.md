Fishhook Peak stands in the Siskiyou National Forest in Curry County, about 30 miles west-northwest of Grants Pass.[^2][^1] The lookout went up in 1934 as an L-5 ground cab, a small cabin set on the summit rather than on a tower.[^1][^3]

A May 1940 Siskiyou National Forest guard-placement plan said the lookout would watch a wide area of high-hazard burns.[^3] In 1941 the station was manned for 100 days and sent its reports by radio to the Agness Ranger Station.[^3]

The sources do not agree on how the cabin ended. The register says it was "later burned down and is gone."[^1] The firelookout.com page calls it "Abandoned. Gone."[^2] The Oregon lookout list says "Removed by burning."[^3] A trail guide says it was "supposedly burned-down in the 1960s," and that pieces of its timbers still rest on the summit.[^5]

The site was added to the Former Fire Lookout Sites register on November 13, 2019.[^1] Fish Hook Trail #1180 runs along the peak's crest and ends at Fish Hook Camp.[^4] The Forest Service warns that the trail crosses the Biscuit Fire area, which burned in 2002, and that dead trees from that fire are more likely to fall.[^4] According to the trail guide, the old lookout site is a brief off-trail walk along the ridge from a point about 0.8 miles past the trailhead.[^5] The same guide says the forest camp just south of the peak has left no trace.[^5] The trail is on national forest land, and no source says the site is closed or private.[^4]
