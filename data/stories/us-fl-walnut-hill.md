Walnut Hill is a lookout listed in Escambia County, Florida, in a directory of eastern U.S. forest lookouts.[^1] The TopoQuest map site has a feature named "Walnut Hill Lookout Tower" at about 30.897 N, 87.552 W.[^2] The two sources do not say whether that feature is the same lookout as the directory entry.[^1][^2]

The cited pages do not say what, if anything, remains at the site or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Walnut Hill, FOREST LOOKOUTS, easternuslookouts.weebly.com, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/walnut-hill.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Walnut Hill (USGS 24K map features), TopoQuest, https://topoquest.com/map/usgs-24k/florida/walnut-hill/47351 (accessed 2026-10-21).
