Big Lookout Mountain stands in Baker County, Oregon, about 30 miles southeast of Baker City, on land run by the Bureau of Land Management (BLM).[^1][^3]

Firelookout.com dates the site's first development, a cabin, to the 1930s.[^3] The Forest Lookouts history starts later, in 1957, when the BLM was authorized to place a temporary lookout on the mountain.[^2] The NHLR says those temporary lookouts stayed until the permanent structure was built.[^1]

A June 1958 newspaper item said the BLM would staff the new station from July 1.[^2] In 1962 the agency was considering an access road into the area, along with a proposed station that would include a power line.[^2]

The present cab was built in 1963.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The NHLR describes a BLM design cabin similar to a Forest Service R6 Flat Top, set on a 10-foot block.[^1] Firelookout.com describes a 10-foot concrete base with a wooden live-in cab.[^3] The sources differ on the earlier history. Firelookout.com dates a cabin on the site to the 1930s,[^3] while the NHLR and Forest Lookouts start with the 1957 temporary lookout.[^1][^2]

A 1975 newspaper item said two members of the summer BLM fire crew took turns manning the tower.[^2] A visitor's photo caption dated July 2010 says the lookout had been staffed by two people, but it gives no staffing dates.[^4]

The NHLR registered the site on June 22, 2022, with Brad Eells as nominator.[^1] Firelookout.com says the cab is available for emergencies.[^3] I found no published rules for visiting. Because it is BLM land and the cab is available for emergencies,[^1][^3] check with the Vale District office of the BLM before you go, and do not enter the cab without permission.
