The Forest Fire Lookout Association says the tower now standing at Rhudy, in Craig County, came from Long Mountain, in Campbell County.[^1][^2][^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says the Long Mountain tower was built about 1934 and was administered by the Virginia Department of Game and Inland Fisheries.[^1]

The register does not describe the tower's design or height, and it does not name the people who staffed it.[^1] It says the tower stayed in use until at least 1972, and that it was then converted into a communications tower for private and state use.[^1] The register lists the tower as removed after 1972.[^1]

The register entry for the Long Mountain site was made on March 24, 2020, with John McDonald as nominator.[^1]

The move is the weakest part of the record. The association's Virginia list shows a standing tower at Rhudy, described as relocated from Long Mountain. It gives no date for the move, does not say who moved the structure, and files the entry under "Relocated / Replica Lookouts".[^2][^3] The same list marks the Long Mountain (Rustburg) site as gone.[^2] The register does not mention a move at all, and it describes a conversion instead.[^1] We could not confirm the move from a second source.

We found no information about public access, visits or rentals at Rhudy. Anyone planning a trip should ask the landowner or the managing agency first, and should stay off private or closed land.
