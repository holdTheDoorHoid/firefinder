The Yellow River Fire Tower stands in Yellow River State Forest in Allamakee County, Iowa, near Harpers Ferry.[^1][^3] It is a 100-foot Aermotor LS40 tower with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab.[^1][^4] The register says it is the state's sole fire lookout.[^1]

The U.S. Forest Service owned the tower first.[^4][^1] The Iowa Department of Natural Resources acquired it in 1950 and rebuilt it on its current site in 1962.[^4][^1] Iowa's Department of Natural Resources and a 2020 local newspaper article instead give 1963 as the year it was built, so the completion date is disputed.[^2][^5]

The register recorded the tower on July 1, 1995.[^1] The tower joined the National Register of Historic Places in 2021.[^4]

Wikipedia calls the tower in poor condition: most of its window sashes have been knocked out, the glass is gone, and the wooden stairs and cab have rotted, mostly from vandalism.[^4] Wikipedia also says the tower is now fenced off and shut to the public, for those reasons and for safety.[^4] In 2020 a friends group was exploring whether the tower could be restored and made open to the public.[^5]

The state forest itself offers numerous outdoor recreation, including camping, hiking and fishing.[^2]
