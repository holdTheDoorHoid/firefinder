The Kisatchie Lookout stands in the Kisatchie National Forest in Natchitoches Parish, Louisiana.[^1] The U.S. Forest Service built it in 1933, and the register page carries a 1934 Forest Service photo of the tower.[^1] The register describes a steel Aermotor LX-25 tower 99 feet 9 inches tall, and says the original climbing ladder was swapped for stairs in 1952.[^1]

The register says the Forest Service used the tower for fire control until 1982, when state overflights took over that job.[^1] The sources I checked do not name the people who staffed it or any fire it helped to spot.

Another source tells a different story. It says the national forest put up an 82-foot steel structure with a 7-by-7 cab in 1951 and 1952.[^2] The register's 1933 date and 99-foot height do not match that account, and I could not settle which one is right.[^1][^2]

Wikipedia's list of Louisiana lookouts describes Kisatchie as still standing and in active use, with no date attached.[^3] The register listed the tower on February 2, 2011, as US 901, LA 5, and names the Kisatchie Ranger District among its cooperators.[^1]

I found no source saying whether the cab is open to visitors or whether the public may climb it. Anyone planning a visit should check with the Kisatchie Ranger District first and stay off the structure unless access is clearly posted.
