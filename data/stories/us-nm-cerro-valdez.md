Cerro Valdez was a ground lookout site in Rio Arriba County, New Mexico, on the Santa Fe National Forest.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a ground site rather than a tower, and as gone.[^1]

The site had an L-4 lookout and its own log cabin.[^2] In the fall of 1949, both were moved to the Encino site, according to a 2020 chapter report.[^2] That report discusses the moved structures as part of the Encino complex. The complex was evaluated for the 1988 thematic National Register nomination and found not eligible, because the L-4 and cabins had been moved from Cerro Valdez and the lookout was not yet 50 years old on site.[^2]

In 2020, the same report says, Encino was the only lookout on the Coyote Ranger District and was staffed.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the Cerro Valdez site today, or whether that site is open to visitors. We found no source on whether the Encino site is open to the public either.
