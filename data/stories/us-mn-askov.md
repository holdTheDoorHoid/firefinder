A 100-foot Aermotor stairway tower was moved to this site in 1951, where the state list numbers it tower 107.[^1][^2] The county list calls it "the relocated first tower at the Finland site."[^1]

A 1952 Coast and Geodetic Survey description places the spot about seven miles northeast of Askov.[^3] It calls the structure about 80 feet high, with four steel legs and a lookout cabin on top.[^3] The county and state lists both give 100 feet, which differs from the survey's figure.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^4] The county list says it was removed and that the site is now private property.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains there. Since the land is private, visitors should stay off it.
