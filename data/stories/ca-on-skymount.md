Skymount was a steel fire tower. Firelookout.ca describes it as a guyed steel tower with an octagonal cupola, and places it in Algonquin Provincial Park, in the Parry Sound District.[^1]

According to a note on that page, the coverage the tower gave had not been fully considered when it was planned and built, and it added little to the coverage other towers in the area already provided.[^1] The same note cites an Algonquin Park tourist pamphlet and says the tower was torn down in the 1950s.[^1] The tower is missing from the Department of Lands and Forests Tower Detection System Map of 1963.[^1]

A hiking guide dated August 2025 says the Track and Tower Trail takes its name in part from the old Skymount fire tower site.[^2] The same guide describes a lookout over Cache Lake as the site of a fire tower that stood until the 1970s. That does not match the 1950s date, and the guide does not say whether it and the Skymount tower are the same.[^2]

Today the trail runs roughly through the centre of Algonquin's Highway 60 corridor.[^2] It remains open year-round, though winter conditions can be icy.[^2] The site's position is also uncertain. Firelookout.ca calls its coordinates approximate and says the real spot may lie 2 km farther south.[^1] No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the old site today.
