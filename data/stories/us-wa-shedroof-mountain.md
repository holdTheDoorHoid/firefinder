Shedroof Mountain stands at 6,764 feet (2,062 m) in Pend Oreille County, Washington, on the Kaniksu and Colville national forests.[^1] A camp was established at the site in 1915.[^1][^3] A log cabin was added just south of the summit in 1918, and a cupola cabin was built on the summit in 1926.[^1][^3]

In 1938 a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab replaced the cupola.[^1][^3] The register and WillhiteWeb say the tower was removed in the 1950s.[^1][^3] Rex's Fire Lookout Page says it was destroyed in that decade.[^2]

Willhite visited in 2022 and photographed what is left on the summit: four footings from the 1938 tower, a footing from the 1926 cupola cabin, and a summit benchmark.[^3]

Willhite's directions start in Metaline Falls.[^3] Drive north on Highway 31 for 2.1 miles to Sullivan Lake Road.[^3] Turn right and drive 4.9 miles to Forest Road 22, then take Road 2220 for 13 miles to its end.[^3] The hike from the north trailhead is four miles with about 1,100 feet of climb, and Willhite says the trail runs the whole way.[^3] The first mile follows what was once a road.[^3]
