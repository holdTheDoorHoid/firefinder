Twin Pines was a lookout nine miles south of Hood River, in Hood River County, Oregon.[^2][^1] The register and firelookout.com both name the Oregon Department of Forestry as the agency.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts lists the site under the Mt Hood National Forest instead, so the sources disagree about who ran it.[^3]

In 1931 a tent camp equipped with a phone occupied the site.[^1] The register dates the replacement of that camp, a wooden tower with a log cabin, to 1934.[^1] firelookout.com gives 1933 instead, for a 20-foot wooden L-4 tower with a log cabin.[^2] A photo captioned 1933 and a panorama dated November 10, 1933 suggest a lookout stood on the site by then, which fits firelookout.com's date better than the register's.[^1][^3]

The register says it was no longer there by 1945.[^1] firelookout.com dates its removal to the 1950s.[^2] A photo on the register page is captioned 1944, which places the lookout on the site that year.[^1] Forest Lookouts marks it as removed, with no date.[^3]

No source I read describes what remains on the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
