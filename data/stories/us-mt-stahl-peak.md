Stahl Peak Lookout is a cupola cabin on a high outcrop in the Ten Lakes area, within the Kootenai National Forest.[^2][^3] It sits on the Rexford-Fortine (Ksanka) District, outside Fortine, Montana.[^4] The newsletter places it among the many lookouts put up while staffing was at its peak.[^4]

Its cabin kit was packed 16 miles up Graves Creek to the summit in the fall of 1926 by two local wranglers.[^4] It took three trips with 18 mules to move everything.[^4] Snow fell before the job was finished, so the 2024 newsletter gives spring 1927 as the completion date.[^4] The register, FireLookout.com and the association's 2026 page all give 1926 as the build year.[^1][^2][^3]

The lookout was an active fire-detection post into the 1960s, and sources also describe emergency use into that decade.[^1][^3] The association's 2026 page says it now operates as an emergency shelter close to the Pacific Northwest Trail.[^3] It is not staffed and is not part of the recreation rental program.[^4] Visitors reach it by hiking a couple of miles off the trail.[^4]

The lookout had fallen to the bottom of the maintenance list before Legacy Restoration Funds, from the Great American Outdoors Act, came through.[^4] The association's initial size-up found a long list of repairs, including new shutters, several new windows, a full re-glazing, soffit and fascia work, repainting, and a thorough clean with rodent removal.[^4] In 2024 the lookout received $1,700 of Legacy funds for maintenance.[^4]

Volunteers spent five days cleaning out the lookout and cupola.[^4] They painted the exterior, replaced two cupola windows that had been repaired offsite, repaired shutters and worked toward rodent-proofing.[^4]

The association's 2026 project page calls the pre-project assessment "this year's" work, but the Forest Service file it links is labelled 2023, so the year is unclear.[^3] The planned work covers all windows and shutters, plus the south wall, which has settled because its foundation is loose-stacked rock.[^3] A major rehab was tentatively planned for 2026.[^4] The sources I read do not confirm whether it went ahead.
