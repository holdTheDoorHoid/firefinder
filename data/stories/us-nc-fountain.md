Fountain is a fire lookout in Edgecombe County.[^1] A 1976 survey description places it on U.S. Route 258, about 1.5 miles north of the point where that road meets N.C. Route 222 in the town of Fountain, and calls it a Forest Service lookout tower.[^2]

The survey mark described in the note sits on a steel plate over the concrete footing at one of the tower's legs.[^2] The mark was recovered in good condition in 1985, and again as described in 2004.[^2] A 2005 note said the tower looked run down, though its concrete footing and steel plate were in good condition.[^2]

In March 2018 the state opened bids on surplus property.[^1] The same directory entry says the tower is on state-owned land.[^1]

No source says whether the tower was sold or taken down, or what stands at the site today. Public access to the site is not described.
