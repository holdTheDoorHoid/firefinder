The Missouri Conservation Commission bought four acres in Camden County in 1939 to put up a fire tower.[^1] The Commission's 1942 annual report lists one 68-foot steel tower among that year's construction.[^1]

The tower stood right beside Highway 5, a dozen or so miles south of Gravois Mills, overlooking a curving lake.[^1] A 1951 newspaper article described it as a lookout the Commission used for forest fires, and said it was open to visitors at the time.[^1]

The site also had a tower residence.[^1] The annual reports show it re-roofed in fiscal 1965-66 and remodeled in fiscal 1971-72.[^1]

In fiscal 1973-74 the fire tower was relocated, and a privy was built at the site.[^1] In January 2008 the tower was sold to a private party, dismantled, and moved to the buyer's farm, where the record says it would be erected.[^1]

No source describes what remains of the four-acre site today.[^1] The tower's last recorded location is the buyer's farm, and none of the sources we read confirms it was put up there.[^1]

[^1]: Hurricane Deck, Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/hurricane-deck.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
