Burtonsville's fire tower began its working life on Muirkirk Road [^3]. The Department of Forestry erected a 60-foot forest fire lookout at 7813 Muirkirk Road in 1926 [^3]. In 1932 the structure was moved and set up again along Columbia Pike (US Route 29) in Burtonsville [^3].

Fire detection at the site ended by the mid-1970s [^3]. The University of Maryland owned the tower and used it for communications [^1]. Over time the structure deteriorated, and the university no longer needed it for that work [^1].

Because the tower was judged eligible for the National Register, the Maryland Historical Trust required mitigation before it could be removed [^1]. The tower was demolished in 2014 [^1][^3].

The sources disagree on the county. The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the tower in Montgomery County [^2], while the Forest Lookouts website gives Prince George's County [^4].

A scrambled passage in the DocsLib copy may say that no physical remains are left at the site, but it is unclear which tower it describes [^3]. No source checked says whether the site is open to visitors.
