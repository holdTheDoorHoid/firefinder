Tarver was a town in Echols County, Georgia, and is now extinct.[^1] In 1887 the state legislature gave it town status, and its municipal charter was repealed in 1995.[^1] East of Statenville, the site is beside Georgia State Route 94, and New Barnes Road and Will Rewis Road cross the highway there.[^1] A published list titled "Georgia lookout towers" places a lookout tower at 30.70072, -82.92390.[^2] The list's map was last revised in March 2016.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site today or whether it is open to the public.
