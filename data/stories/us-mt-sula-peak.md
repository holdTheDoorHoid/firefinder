Sula Peak Lookout stands on Sula Peak in Ravalli County, Montana, about 14 miles southeast of Darby, at 6,191 feet.[^1] The Bitterroot National Forest manages it.[^1]

The first lookout went up in 1929.[^1][^2] A Missoula newspaper reported in June 1929 that construction of the new lookout was nearly complete and that a Hamilton man would staff it over the summer.[^2] The early structure had a gable-roofed L-4 ground cab.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page dates a 10-foot tower of treated timber, carrying an L-4 cab, to 1934.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com dates a similar tower to 1957, with a question mark.[^1][^2] The legs were enclosed in concrete blocks in 1959, and a February 1960 newspaper item says the base was finished that fall.[^1][^2]

Firelookout.com says staffing ran through 1996, with another season in 1999, and it lists known lookout personnel from 1931 to 2002.[^1]

A wildfire destroyed the lookout in 2000.[^1][^2] A new building went up in 2001.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com describes it as a two-story octagonal lookout, and the Forest Lookouts page calls it a unique design.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Sula Peak as standing, with a two-story cab.[^3]

We found no access information for the site. The Bitterroot's history page offers stays in historic lookouts but does not name Sula Peak.[^4] Anyone planning a visit should check with the Bitterroot National Forest first.
