The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Crooked River Point as a ground lookout that is gone.[^1] It lies in the Payette National Forest in Adams County, Idaho, about 15 miles northwest of Council, at 6,929 feet.[^2][^3][^4] Forest Lookouts lists it under the name Crooked River Peak.[^4]

Firelookout.com, Idaho Fire Lookouts and Forest Lookouts all date the cabin to the 1930s.[^2][^3][^4] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both list Roy Bethel as known staff in 1942.[^2][^3]

The sources disagree on how the cabin ended. Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts call it abandoned.[^2][^3] Forest Lookouts says it was removed.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list says ground gone.[^1] None of the four sources dates the change.

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may go there. Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as hike-in, but none of the pages gives a route.[^3]
