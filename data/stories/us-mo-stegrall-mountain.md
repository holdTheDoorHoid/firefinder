Stegall Mountain sits in Carter County, Missouri.[^1] A June 1946 newspaper reported that a lookout tower had been finished at Stegall Mountain the previous month.[^1] A 1963 federal survey description puts the four-legged steel tower at about 70 feet, describes a cabin at its top, and lists the state as the owner.[^1]

The earliest ownership record we found is a 1947-48 state manual, which places the towersite under the Missouri Conservation Commission, within Deer Run State Forest.[^1] The Missouri Department of Conservation bought land that is now Peck Ranch Conservation Area in 1945 to help restore wild turkeys.[^2]

We found no lookout named in the state reports we read. A 1962-64 biennial report records that supervision of the tower passed from Deer Run to Eminence.[^1] The residence at the site was remodeled in fiscal 1965-66 and again in fiscal 1975-76.[^1] We found no notable fire tied to this tower in the sources we read.

The department's current page describes hiking up to the fire tower, so it appears to still stand.[^2] Its access now runs through Peck Ranch.[^2] A 1.5-mile gravel road crosses the natural area and dead-ends at a parking lot.[^2] From the lot, the page says, a hike up the tower road leads to the fire tower, with views over glades and Ozark hills.[^2] We found no statement on whether climbing the tower is allowed, so ask the department before going up.[^2]
