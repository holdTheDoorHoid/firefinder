A Tennessee Landforms table of lookout towers calls this one Brimstone 16. It places the tower in Scott County at an elevation of 2,914 feet, and marks it as removed.[^1]

OpenStreetMap, the online map, has a point named Lookout Tower Number Sixteen about 66 meters from that Brimstone 16 waypoint.[^2][^3] The OpenStreetMap point is tagged as an observation tower and gives an elevation of 879 meters.[^2] The table's 2,914 feet is about 888 meters, so the two sources disagree on the elevation, and we cannot say which is right.[^1][^3]

None of the sources describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. We found nothing on whether the public can reach it, so anyone interested should check who owns the land before going.

[^1]: Tennessee lookout towers, Tennessee Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Node: Lookout Tower Number Sixteen (356895222), OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/356895222 (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Tennessee lookout tower waypoints (towers.gpx), Tennessee Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/towers.gpx (accessed 2026-10-10).
