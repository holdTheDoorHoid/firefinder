The Texas Forest Service owned a fire lookout tower at Beckville, in Panola County.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list has two Beckville entries, Loc 1 and Loc 2, and both are marked as towers that are gone.[^2] Neither source says which entry matches this tower.

A Coast and Geodetic Survey station about three and a half miles airline north of Beckville was described in 1958.[^1] The survey placed it on a timbered hill, the highest in the area.[^1] It noted a grey, glass-enclosed cab on a four-legged structure, with an overall height of about 140 feet (roughly 43 m).[^1]

On January 15, 1993, a notice carried by the Tyler Morning Telegraph, as quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, invited sealed bids to buy, dismantle and remove Texas Forest Service lookout towers.[^1] The Beckville Fire Lookout Tower in Panola County was on the list.[^1] A notice of sale does not prove that the tower came down. The association's list marks both Beckville entries as gone.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
