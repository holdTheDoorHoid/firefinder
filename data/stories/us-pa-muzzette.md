According to the register of former lookouts, the Muzzette Tower, a steel Aermotor, was built in 1935 by the U.S. Forest Service.[^1] The register lists the site in Forest County at US 393 and PA 222, puts it at 1,781 feet (543 m), and spells the name Muzzette. OpenStreetMap uses Muzette.[^1][^2]

A 1936 map of the Marienville area labels a Muzette Fire Tower, one of several fire lookouts it shows.[^3]

The register says the tower was removed before 1995.[^1] OpenStreetMap labels its point "Muzette Lookout Tower (likely gone)" and gives the elevation as 539 meters, a few meters below the register's figure.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
