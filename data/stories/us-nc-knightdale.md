The Civilian Conservation Corps erected a 120-foot steel tower at Knightdale in 1938, according to the lookout history.[^1] The lookout history describes the tower as having an inside stairway.[^1] The tower site had been acquired from the trustees of Meredith College, on the east side of Hester Street.[^2] The Wake County forestry history calls it an International-Stacey steel tower.[^2]

In February 1940 a CCC camp completed a four-room frame house at the tower.[^1]

In 1971 the house and site were sold.[^2] The next year the tower was taken down and stored in Clayton.[^2] It was later moved to South Mills in Pasquotank County.[^2]

The forestry history does not say whether the tower still stands at South Mills.[^2] No source describes what remains on the Knightdale site today.[^2]

[^1]: Knightdale, Eastern US Lookouts (North Carolina lookouts, quoting Department of Conservation and Development biennial reports), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/knightdale.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Wake County forestry history, Michael Legeros, https://www.legeros.com/ralwake/wake/history/forestry.shtml (accessed 2026-10-21).
