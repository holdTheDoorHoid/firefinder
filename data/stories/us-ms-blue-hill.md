In 1950 the Coast and Geodetic Survey recorded the Blue Hill Lookout Tower as a survey station, and it measured from the peak of the roof on the observation platform.[^2] The station lay about 11 miles northeast of Booneville, in Prentiss County, on land of the Mississippi State Forest Service.[^2] The 1950 record gives the tower as steel, on four legs, 110 feet (33.5 m) tall, with a glass-enclosed viewing platform at the top.[^2]

A state highway department crew found the station in good condition in 1962.[^2] The National Geodetic Survey visited in 1970 and found the station recovered as the 1950 description had it.[^2]

The most recent recovery note in the datasheet dates from March 1979.[^2] A highway department crew reported the tower standing and in good condition, its glass-enclosed cabin on top of the 110-foot steel tower.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts list for Prentiss County marks the Blue Hill tower as removed, but it gives no date.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors. Ask the landowner before going near the site.
