Turnbull Lookout stood inside the Turnbull National Wildlife Refuge, about four miles south-southwest of Cheney in Spokane County.[^1][^2] The register lists U.S. Fish & Wildlife as its administrator.[^1] The tower went up in 1938. Sources describe it as 99 feet tall with an L-6 cab.[^1][^2][^3]

WillhiteWeb says a 1952 USGS description gave the tower a height of 108.8 feet, which does not match the 99-foot figure.[^3] A refuge photo dated September 5, 1952 was taken from the fire tower, looking out over Lower Turnbull Lake, so the tower was still standing that year.[^4]

By 1971 the tower was in ruins on the ground.[^1][^2][^3] Parts of the cab were reused in a museum cab in Ray Kresek's yard.[^3]

WillhiteWeb says hiking is banned on this part of the refuge outside hunting season, when hunters with special permits may enter.[^3] Gate signs forbid entry.[^3] Its photos show footings and timbers from the tower on the ground.[^3] We found no other source that describes the site today.
