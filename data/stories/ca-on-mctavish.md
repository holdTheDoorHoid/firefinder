The McTavish fire tower is in McTavish Township, in the Thunder Bay District of Ontario.[^1] The Canada Lookouts list dates it to 1950, when a steel tower of about 30.5 m (100 ft) with an octagon cab was built.[^1] It names the Ontario Department of Lands and Forests as the agency.[^1]

Mine history dates the discovery of the Amethyst Mine Panorama to 1949, when a Department of Lands and Forests crew was building a forest access road north of Loon Lake.[^2] The road was intended to haul in what was needed to build the McTavish tower and its towerman's cabin.[^2]

The same history says the tower is visible from the mine site, and places the towerman's cabin on the shore of Elbow Lake, near the mine's west end.[^2]

The Canada Lookouts list gives the tower's latest status as standing in 2018.[^1] None of the three sources says whether visitors can reach the tower itself.[^1][^2][^3] The firelookout.ca register gives the location as Shuniah instead.[^3]
