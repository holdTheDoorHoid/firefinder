The Firelookout.ca index places the Alex Graham Fire Lookout in Cariboo, British Columbia.[^1] The lookout shares its name with Mount Alex Graham, a peak that the BC Geographical Names office places east of Tigulhdzin-chi Creek.[^2]

The peak was labelled Anahim Mountain on a 1921 map. In 1934 it was renamed Mount Alex Graham so that it would not be mixed up with Anahim Peak.[^2] Major D.M. MacKay proposed the name in honour of Alex Graham, an early settler who pre-empted land near the junction of Alexis Creek and the Chilcotin River in 1892. He died in early 1934.[^2]

The lookout burned down in 2017.[^1]

A post in a BC fire-lookout Facebook group carries the title "Remains of the Alex Graham Fire Lookout, August 2021."[^3] Only that title could be read, so it is the only source we have for what was left on the site in 2021.

No source describes the site today, and none says whether the public may visit it.
