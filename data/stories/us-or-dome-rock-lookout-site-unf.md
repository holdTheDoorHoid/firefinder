Dome Rock is a summit in Lane County, Oregon, about 21 miles south of Oakridge, at roughly 5,740 feet.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com dates a lookout cab on the north point to 1936.[^2] Rex's Fire Lookout Page says a lookout house was built around 1936, and that a 14-by-14 L-4 ground house was constructed in 1948.[^3] The register dates its north-point cab to 1948, so the sources disagree about the first date.[^1] The register also carries photographs captioned 1942, which suggests a lookout was standing there before the 1948 date.[^1]

The register and firelookout.com place the site in the Umpqua National Forest, while Rex's page names the Willamette National Forest.[^1][^2][^3] A 1967 inspection survey, quoted on Rex's page, said almost nobody visited the lookout, and that it was used in extreme conditions but not routinely.[^3]

Rex's page quotes a note to the supervisor's office from the fire control officer, which says Dome Rock was destroyed on September 17, 1968.[^3] Firelookout.com also says it was gone in 1968.[^2] The register gives a later date and says the cab was gone by 1969.[^1]

A 1953 Coast and Geodetic Survey station description, also quoted on Rex's page, placed the survey station at the top of a wooden lookout house about 100 feet below the peak's highest point.[^3] It said the truck road ended at Big Pine Opening, and that the Dome Rock trail ran about five miles south from there to the summit.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the route is open to visitors.
