Hot Springs Lookout stood in Lemhi County, Idaho, about 24 miles northwest of Salmon, on Salmon National Forest land.[^1][^2][^3] The sources date its first building, a log cabin, only to the 1930s.[^1][^2][^3] By 1940 the site had an L-4 cab.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Lookouts page quotes a Recorder Herald item from August 25, 1949.[^3] It describes afternoon winds that had gotten out of hand, and says the lookout was then missing a window and two shutters.[^3] The lookout was abandoned in 1950.[^1][^2]

It burned in 2000.[^1][^2][^3] Forest Lookouts says it burned in a wildfire.[^3]

Today the sources describe no remains. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^2] No source we read says what, if anything, is left at the site now, or whether visitors may go there.
