The Forest Lookouts guide lists Big Creek Patrol as a patrol lookout on the Nez Perce National Forest, in Idaho County. It places the lookout two miles southwest of Burpee Mountain.[^1]

Two other sources describe a 1930 log cabin patrol lookout that was abandoned in 1940, but they file it under Burpee Mountain rather than under this name. The Forest Service list of Nez Perce lookouts gives the cabin a date of about 1930 and says it was two miles southwest, atop Big Creek Point.[^2] Rex Kamstra's Forest Fire Lookout page gives the same dates and places the patrol lookout in the same spot.[^3]

The sources do not clearly agree on where the patrol lookout stood, and it is not clear whether they describe one structure or two.

No source we found describes what remains at the patrol site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors. An Idaho lookout index lists the Burpee Mountain entry as gone.[^4]
