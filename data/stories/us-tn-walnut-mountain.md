The Walnut Mountain lookout tower stands in Campbell County, Tennessee, at an elevation of about 2,832 feet.[^1] OpenStreetMap gives the tower point an elevation of 855 meters, a little lower than the Landforms figure, so the two sources differ slightly.[^3]

We did not find when the Walnut Mountain tower was built, who built it, or who has staffed it. The sources we read also say nothing about it being moved, abandoned, or rented out.

A National Register document about Tennessee Division of Forestry fire towers includes Walnut Mountain in its list of extant Tennessee fire towers as of March 26, 2014.[^2] The same document gives the Division's peak as 208 towers in the late 1960s, and says 142 were still standing in March 2014.[^2]

In November 2023 a woods fire on Walnut Mountain destroyed a van and an outbuilding, according to local news.[^4] We found no report that the tower itself was involved.

Tennessee Landforms lists the tower as standing, but the listing has no date of its own; the page carries a revision stamp dated 8 January 2026.[^1] We found nothing on its current condition or whether the public may visit. Please check with the Division of Forestry or the landowner before going near it, and do not climb it unless it is open and safe.
