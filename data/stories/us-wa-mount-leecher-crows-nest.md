Mount Leecher's crow's nest is a tree-top lookout in Okanogan County, about 1,000 feet to the south of the summit tower.[^1][^2] Sources differ on the date: the National Historic Lookout Register gives 1920, firelookout.com dates it to as early as 1918, and TrailChick and WillhiteWeb say 1918.[^3][^4][^1][^2] WillhiteWeb suggests it was probably a patrol post for the summit lookout.[^2]

The summit tower came later: the structure that stands there now dates from 1954, and it was restored in 2009.[^5]

In 2014 the Carlton Complex fire burned trees around the crow's nest, but the nest itself came through.[^1][^2] I found no sources that describe who staffed the crow's nest.

It still stands more than a century later.[^2] WillhiteWeb places the tree a few hundred feet from the road and suggests parking at the gate when it is closed.[^2] TrailChick says the summit drive could once be driven when the gate was open, but the gate was kept closed as of 2019, which makes the trip a road walk of just over a mile to the main lookout.[^1]
