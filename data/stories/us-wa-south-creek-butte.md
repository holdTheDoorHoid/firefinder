The South Creek Butte lookout had an L-5 cab built in 1934.[^1][^3] The site is in Okanogan County, Washington.[^1] It stood in the Okanogan National Forest, about 23 miles west-northwest of Twisp, at 7,670 feet, or 2,338 metres.[^1][^2] Willhite's site has photographs captioned 1934 looking southwest, southeast and north, plus one captioned 1946.[^3]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register listed the site on November 19, 2012, on the nomination of Ray Kresek, under the number US 1467, WA 367.[^1]

Abandonment came in 1952, six years after the 1946 photograph.[^1][^3] Firelookout reports that the cab is no longer there.[^2]

The register lists the U.S. Forest Service as administrator.[^1] Willhite's page says more will be added after a visit.[^3] No source describes what remains at the site today, and none says whether the public may visit.
