Sheetiron Mountain rises to about 6,500 feet in the Mendocino National Forest, in Lake County.[^1][^2] The register dates the lookout to about 1915, and its 1915 photograph comes from the Upper Lake Ranger District collection.[^1]

The register describes a 7-by-7-foot observation cabin on a 70-foot open steel tower.[^1] A residence cabin was also built, and a BC-201 Fireman-Lookout cabin was added in 1935.[^1]

A California Ranger item from August 1941, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, describes the lookout's water supply. Spring water ran through a 500-foot pipe toward the lookout's kitchen, and a salvaged gas engine helped push it uphill into a 500-gallon storage tank. The item opens with Ranger John Monday.[^2]

The register says only the foundation of the original cabin remains today.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list shows the tower as gone.[^3] None of the sources we read say whether the site is open to visitors or how to reach it.
