Dyer Ridge is a site in Uintah County, Utah, at 40.73745, -109.57388.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it under "Unknown/Undocumented Sites" rather than among its lookouts.[^1] The association says a site moves to its lookout lists if evidence shows it was used as a regular wildland fire lookout, and on the copy I read, Dyer Ridge is still on the unknown list.[^1]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.

[^1]: Utah Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/ut/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
