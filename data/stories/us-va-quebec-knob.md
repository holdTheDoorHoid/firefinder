Quebec Knob is a former tower site in Smyth County, Virginia.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists its tower as gone.[^1] The Forest Lookouts list of Virginia lookouts files the Quebec Knob entry under "Smyth County - National Forest".[^2] No source read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Virginia, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Virginia - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/virginia.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
