A Georgia list of lookout towers gives a site called Jacks Creek Mountain in Walton County.[^1] It places the site at 989 feet (301 m) above sea level, at 33.82333 north and 83.62943 west. The list also gives the site the state number gfc-020.[^1]

Forest Lookouts lists a Walton County tower under a second name, Pipeline (Turkey Mtn), and files it under the Georgia Forestry Commission.[^2] The two lists may describe one site, since both place a tower in Walton County and the tnlandforms note also contains the word pipeline.[^1][^2]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at this site today, or whether the public can reach it. Check with the Georgia Forestry Commission before you go, and do not climb any tower that is closed or private.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Pipeline (Turkey Mtn), Forest Lookouts (easternuslookouts.weebly.com), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/pipeline-turkey-mtn.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
