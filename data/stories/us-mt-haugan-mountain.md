The Haugan lookout stood on Haugan Mountain in Mineral County, Montana, about 17 miles northwest of St. Regis.[^1] Rex's Fire Lookout Page places it in the Lolo National Forest and dates a 30-foot pole platform tower to 1916 and an L-4 tower to 1937.[^1]

Newspaper reports from July 1924, reprinted on the Forest Lookouts page, describe a spreading fire that destroyed the Haugan station and left the crew with none of their equipment saved.[^2] Forest officials believed a careless smoker on the middle fork of Big Creek started the blaze.[^2]

The two accounts do not line up. Rex's page does not mention a 1924 fire and says the tower was destroyed in 1955.[^1] The newspaper reports place the loss in 1924.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts page marks the site as removed, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list shows the tower as gone.[^2][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the ground is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
