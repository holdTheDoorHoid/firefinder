The earliest record for Suncrest Point is a fire watch camp rather than a tower.[^3] A 1949 Lincoln FPA annual report, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, describes a two-man fire watch camp in the Upper Siletz District, in an area of logging slash that was a high fire hazard.[^3] The page ties this camp to Suncrest, although the 1949 report does not name the point.[^3] The 1950 report says the camp had a 20-foot lookout tower, used for observation and equipped with a fire finder and telephone service on the forest line.[^3]

The reports list the site as a lookout station in use in 1951, say it was manned throughout the 1952 fire season, and say it was manned in 1953.[^3] Those are the last staffing entries the page gives.[^3]

The register tells a different later history, dating a trailer on the site to 1957 and saying it was gone in the 1960s.[^1] The firelookout.com listing gives the same two dates.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page marks the site "Removed" without a date.[^3]

No source we read describes the site today.[^1][^2][^3] The register lists the Oregon Department of Forestry as the administering agency.[^1] Firelookout.com places the site about 14 miles southeast of Lincoln City.[^2] We found nothing that says what remains there or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
