The Salem fire tower in Taylor County is listed among the Florida lookouts on an enthusiast site, but the sources I could read say little about it.[^1] I could not find when it was built, who built it, or what it was made of.

The earliest dated event I found is from October 1958.[^1] As the lookout site quotes from The Evening Independent, about 60 fires, apparently set by arsonists, broke out in Taylor County on a Saturday.[^1] A state forestry spokesman said the telephone line from the Salem tower had been cut.[^1] An estimate put the burning area at 500 to 1,000 acres, and the spokesman said that no fire was close to a dwelling.[^1]

In September 2005, bids were taken on the sale of the tower and its 10-acre parcel.[^1] The winning bid was $106,750.[^1] I could not find the buyer or any later record of the sale.

A Florida lookout report covering 2015 and early 2016 mentions two Salem sites. One, Salem #268, is still standing and the other, Salem #318, has been removed.[^2] The writer raises, without answering, whether the two were one tower that had been moved.[^2] The excerpt I read does not name a county for either site, so I could not tie either one to this Taylor County tower.[^2]

I found no access information or post-2005 status for the tower in these sources. The lookout record marks it as standing, but I could not confirm that from any dated source.

[^1]: Salem - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts site, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/salem1.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Florida lookout report (cr-fl-2015), firelookout.org, https://firelookout.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/cr-fl-2015.pdf (accessed 2026-10-09).
