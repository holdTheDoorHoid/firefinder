The John Spears tower stood in Crawford County, Indiana, on Hoosier National Forest land near what is now Patoka Lake.[^1][^2] The Forest Service's list places it near Taswell.[^3]

The Register says Indiana's DOF erected the tower in or around 1936 and later transferred it to the U.S. Forest Service.[^2] Indiana Fire Towers cites a 1936 county map that shows the tower.[^1]

The sources do not agree on ownership. The Forest Service's own list files the John Spears Tower under Indiana's Department of Natural Resources.[^3] Indiana Fire Towers suggests it may have been both state and federal property at different times, and cites a 1960 Outdoor Indiana article that lists it among the state's towers.[^1] A Forest Service web page reportedly shows the tower still standing in 1973.[^1]

In about 1987 the Forest Service sold the tower, which let it put up a newer, taller one on the site.[^2] The buyer took the tower apart and put it in storage.[^2] A later private owner, who wanted to rebuild it in Indiana, died before he could re-erect it.[^2] The owner's son then restored it with OFS Brands, an Indiana furniture company, and re-erected it on the firm's Cool Springs Campus.[^2]

Today the restored tower stands at the Cool Springs Campus near Velpen, Indiana.[^2] The Register says a visit to the campus or the tower requires an invitation, so it is not open to the public.[^2]

At the original site the tower is gone.[^1] Two lists mark it as removed or gone.[^4][^5] Indiana Fire Towers notes that Google Maps shows a communication tower on the site.[^1]
