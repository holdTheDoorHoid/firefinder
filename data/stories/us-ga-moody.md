A lookout tower is listed simply as "moody" in Troup County, Georgia, on a Georgia tower list from tnlandforms.us, at 33.09999, -85.08370.[^1] The list gives the code gfc-417.[^1] That list was revised on 22 March 2016, so it may predate later changes at the site.[^1]

In the late 1980s the Georgia Forestry Commission began replacing its tower operators with air patrols, and many towers across the state were scrapped and sold.[^2] The same post says Georgia's towers are at risk.[^2]

No source I could read describes the site today, or whether the public can visit it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Fire Tower, Clay County, Vanishing Georgia, https://vanishinggeorgia.com/2018/02/24/fire-tower-clay-county/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
