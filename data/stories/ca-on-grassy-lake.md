The Grassy Lake Fire Tower is listed on the Firelookout.ca register with a location in Greater Sudbury, Ontario, at coordinates of 47.74542, -81.19862.[^1] The register also warns against confusing it with the Grassy Lake Fire Tower in Saskatchewan, which shares the name.[^1]

Most of Ontario's fire towers went up between the 1920s and the 1950s. The towers came under Ontario's former Department of Lands and Forests, a body that became the Ministry of Natural Resources in 1972.[^2]

Through the 1960s the towers saw less and less use, as spotting from aircraft and fire-fighting crews took on their role. The government then decided it did not want lawsuits over people hurt climbing unmanned towers, and many towers were destroyed.[^2]

No source I read describes the site today, including whether any structure remains there or whether the public can reach it.
