The Marengo Lookout stands in Ashland County, Wisconsin, on National Forest System land in the Penokee Mountains, at an elevation of 1,420 feet (433 m).[^1] The register says no fewer than three towers have stood on that point.[^1]

The first was a 100-foot Wisconsin standard tower, built in 1939 and destroyed in September 1941 by a fall windstorm.[^1] The register says the steel legs were broken away from their footings in the wreckage.[^1] Photographs suggest a replacement stood by about 1949. Citing Geodetic Survey notes, the register describes it as a 65-foot steel tower with an enclosed cab.[^1] That tower served until the late 1980s, when a local resident told the register that vegetation had grown too tall for it.[^1]

The present 100-foot stair-type tower went up in 1987, probably an Aermotor MC-39.[^1] The register suggests it was likely surplus from elsewhere in the state, taken down and rebuilt here to replace the shorter tower, though it does not confirm this. A visitor's comment on the page says it may have come from Park Falls, and I found no other source for that.[^1]

The register says the tower was owned and operated by the state, and that it saw active service until 2015, when Wisconsin decommissioned its lookout network.[^1] A WJFW feature says that year the 72 towers still in use were decommissioned.[^2] I found no source recording anyone staffing it after that.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^3] A visitor report on the register says the road is heavily rutted on a small stretch.[^1] I found no source on whether the public may visit or climb the tower, so check with the DNR or the forest before going.
