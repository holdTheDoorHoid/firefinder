Salt Creek appears in the Firetowers of Saskatchewan survey, which places it at about 54.72 N, 108.51 W.[^1] Canada's Fire Lookouts lists the name in its Saskatchewan index.[^2]

The survey entry gives 1968 in its notes, with no further detail.[^1]

In April 2013 Wildfire Today reported that the Saskatchewan Ministry of Environment was shifting its lookouts from human observers to cameras.[^3] The same article said about 38 seasonal positions would be lost, and that the government put the cost of installing the equipment at $1.5 million.[^3] A union release reported a March announcement that observers would come off 42 towers before the 2014 fire season.[^4] None of these 2013 reports names Salt Creek among the affected towers.

Today the Saskatchewan Public Safety Agency says the province has 42 wildfire detection towers, each with a camera.[^5] No source describes the Salt Creek site today, or says whether the public can reach it.
