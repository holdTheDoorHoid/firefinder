Deadwood is a steel fire lookout in Alberta.[^1] The provincial register lists the tower at 30 m (98 ft) tall, on a site 648 m (2,126 ft) above sea level, on map sheet 84C/11, and gives 2017 as the year it was constructed.[^1]

An Alberta lookout towers map dated 2009 labels a site DEADWOOD, eight years before that construction year.[^2] Alberta Views reports that, as settlement spread, some lookout towers were removed or relocated and others were built.[^3]

Alberta says each lookout site has an observer on duty, typically from April to October.[^4]

The register lists Deadwood as active, with a weather station on site and an access mode of "drive".[^1] No source describes the site now beyond that entry, so do not assume it is open to the public.[^1]
