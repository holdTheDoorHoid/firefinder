Granite Mountain is a lookout site on Kaniksu National Forest land, about four miles north of Nordman in Bonner County, Idaho.[^1] Rex Kamstra's firelookout.com page puts its elevation at 4,780 feet.[^1]

A cabin came first, in 1932.[^1][^3] In 1934 a 40-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab was built, and a firefinder stood about a quarter mile south.[^1][^2][^3] Kemnow's page calls that firefinder a patrol firefinder, and it attaches the name Kresek to each date it lists.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^2] The tower was destroyed in the 1950s. The sources give only the decade, not a single year.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the tower as gone.[^4] None of the pages I checked describes what remains at the site today or whether it is open to visitors.
