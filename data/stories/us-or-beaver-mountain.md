Beaver Mountain sits in the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest in Union County, Oregon.[^1][^3] The register gives its elevation as 6,080 feet, while firelookout.com gives 6,040 feet.[^1][^2]

A 30-foot pole platform went up on the site in 1920.[^1] Later, in 1936, a 30-foot pole tower with an L-5 cab was built there.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the 1936 tower a 7-by-7-foot cab.[^2]

The register places the end of the lookout in the 1940s, and firelookout.com lists it as abandoned in that decade.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts also marks the site as removed.[^3]

The site was registered on November 18, 2019, on the nomination of Tammy McLeod, and the register lists the U.S. Forest Service as administrator.[^1] The register does not describe what remains at the site today, and it says nothing about public access.[^1]
