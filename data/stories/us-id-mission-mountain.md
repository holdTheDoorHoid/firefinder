Mission Mountain is a summit inside McCroskey State Park, about 10 miles north-northwest of Potlatch.[^1][^2] A crow's nest went up on top of the mountain in 1920.[^2][^4] Several sources list a tower there in the 1930s.[^1][^2][^4] WillhiteWeb is less sure and says there may have been one.[^3]

Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both list that tower as gone.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list also marks the site as a gone tower.[^6]

What is left of the crow's nest is still in the tree.[^3] WillhiteWeb reports the tree still standing on the summit, with a few boards and some wire dangling from it as of 2024.[^3] Old nails mark the path a ladder used to take up the trunk, and a phone line still runs down from the nest.[^3] A September 15, 2025 Idaho Fire Lookouts post says a contact confirmed the remains were still there, and its writers made the trip to see for themselves.[^2] The post calls it remarkable that the remains have lasted nearly a century in the treetop.[^2]

The summit now has picnic tables and a restroom.[^3] A gravel road climbs to the top, and any car can use it.[^3] Winter closes the park road, which is otherwise open for recreation.[^3] McCroskey State Park is a public recreation area.[^5] WillhiteWeb classes the summit as a drive-up site.[^3] We do not suggest climbing the tree to look at the remains.
