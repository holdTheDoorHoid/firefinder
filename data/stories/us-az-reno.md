Reno Lookout is on the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest in Greenlee County, Arizona, at 9,094 feet.[^1] The register's Built field gives 1965.[^1] Its description says an original 48-foot Aermotor built on the forest in 1931 has since been replaced by a taller tower.[^1] The register also describes the new tower as 54 feet tall, with a CL-100 cab and a catwalk.[^1]

A separate Arizona lookout history also dates the replacement to 1965 and gives the new tower's height as 54 feet.[^2] The two accounts differ on the first tower. The register calls it a 48-foot Aermotor.[^1] The Arizona history calls it an Aermotor model LX-25 and gives no separate date for that earlier tower.[^2]

The sources read do not say who staffed the lookout or when.

In June 2011 a post on the Arizona history page reported a fire that had started a few days earlier a couple of miles from the lookout, the Wallow Fire.[^2] A second post, dated June 2, said the tower was safe after a burnout was done around it.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Arizona list shows Reno as standing, although the list carries no date.[^3]

The sources read do not say whether the lookout is open to visitors or available to rent. The register names the U.S. Forest Service as its administrator.[^1] Anyone planning a visit should check with the forest first, and should stay away from any structure that is closed.
