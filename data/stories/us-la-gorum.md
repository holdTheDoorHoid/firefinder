Gorum is an unincorporated community on Louisiana Highway 119, about 24 miles (39 km) south-southeast of Natchitoches.[^1] The Central US Lookouts index places the Gorum lookout in Natchitoches Parish.[^2] OpenStreetMap marks a tower of that name at 31.4175, -92.8999, tagged as a lattice observation tower at an elevation of 111 m (364 ft).[^3]

A weather station called Gorum Fire Tower also appears in the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive, with records running from 1 November 1953 to 22 February 2012.[^4] That station point lies a few kilometres from the OpenStreetMap marker and reads lower, at 93.6 m, so the two records may not mark the same spot.[^4][^3]

Louisiana put up its first steel fire tower near Bogalusa in 1922.[^5] By 1950 the state had fifty-six fire towers.[^5] Most surviving towers are inactive, and a number are held in reserve.[^5]

No source describes what stands at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
