Amberson Ridge is a ridge in Perry County, Pennsylvania, at about 2,020 feet above sea level, placed at 40.2398 north and 77.6050 west.[^2] A second lookout-history site also files the old tower site under Perry County.[^3] The register and the topographic listing both use the name Amberson Ridge.[^1][^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register calls this the Amberson Ridge Tower Site, gives its registry number as US 172, PA 1, and records its registration date as September 16, 2008.[^1] The register dates the tower's use to the early 1900s and says it no longer stands.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors. Check who owns the ground before you go looking for it.
