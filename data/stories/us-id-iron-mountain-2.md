Iron Mountain once held a fire lookout about 11 miles north-northwest of Elk City, in Idaho County on the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1][^4] The site sits at 6,815 feet.[^1][^2]

The Forest Service dates the mountain's cupola-topped log cabin to 1919.[^5] Other sources date the cabin to 1923, so the build year is disputed.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists two staff entries.[^2] Adolph Weholt is named for 1907, which comes before every build date in these sources.[^2][^5] Mr. and Mrs. M.W. McLaughlin are named for 1924, drawn from a Caldwell Tribune item.[^2]

The cabin was abandoned in 1942.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association now lists the site as gone.[^6]

No source found describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today. Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site drive-up.[^2] Ask the Nez Perce National Forest about current access before you plan a trip.
