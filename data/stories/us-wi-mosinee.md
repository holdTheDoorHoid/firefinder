Mosinee Lookout is a 100-foot tower on Ranger Street in the city of Mosinee, next to a former Wisconsin DNR ranger station.[^1] The register says the tower has an inside corner ladder and was built in 1951.[^1] A 1958 photograph in the register shows the tower beside the ranger station.[^1]

The sources do not agree on the dates. A City Pages article dates the tower to the 1940s and says it was built for the DNR to watch for forest fires.[^2] The same article says the ranger station went up in the 1950s and served as a fire station until the mid-1990s.[^2] The register says the tower was staffed on and off during the spring fire season until 2015.[^1] The City Pages article says the tower was decommissioned in the early 2000s.[^2] The register's 2015 end date is close to the state's December 2015 announcement that its 72 fire towers would leave service in 2016.[^1][^4] The early-2000s date does not fit that timing, so it is the weaker of the two.[^2][^4]

The ranger station has a new role. City Pages reports that the city has owned the station for years and leases it for $1 to the local historical society, which is getting its own building there.[^2] The article says the building still needs considerable work before it is ready for the public.[^2]

According to the article, few fire towers remain in the state and this site has one of them, but the article does not say whether the tower can be climbed.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^3] I found no fire record and no public access note for this tower in the sources I read, so do not assume it is open to climb.
