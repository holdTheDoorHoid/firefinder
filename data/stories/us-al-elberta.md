The Elberta lookout tower stood in Baldwin County, Alabama.[^1] The Forest Lookouts directory gives its land survey code as 7S-5E-19.[^1]

The directory's only entry on the tower says that in June 2017 it was taken down and moved to a private location, where it was to be erected again.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the original site in Baldwin County. Nothing we read confirms that the tower has been put back up at its new home, and because that location is private, visitors should not expect to see it.[^1]

[^1]: Elberta, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/elberta.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
