Lutsey Point is a former lookout site on the Ochoco National Forest in Crook County, Oregon, about 31 miles east of Prineville.[^1][^2]

An August 1933 Central Oregonian item reported plans to hire Alvin Grimes, with crews from the Maury CCC camp, to build a tower and a dwelling for the lookout.[^3] Forest Lookouts says work was finished in 1934 on a 40-foot tower topped by an L-4 hip-roof cab, and the register's panorama photo is dated October 15, 1934.[^3][^1] The register, firelookout.com and a 2026 blog give 1935 as the build year, so the sources disagree.[^1][^2][^4]

The tower was removed in 1955.[^1][^4] Firelookout.com uses the word destroyed for the same year.[^2]

A May 2026 hiker's blog found four concrete footings from the old lookout on the broad summit. The northwest footing had been pulled out at some point.[^4] The writer found the summit a calm, remote place that is pleasant to visit.[^4]

The writer parked beside Road 4225 and walked about 1.8 miles up Road 200, an abandoned road, to reach the summit.[^4] Cut trees had been left across the road, and a logging operation was active about a third of a mile up.[^4] The writer describes the route as closed, so check with the Ochoco National Forest before you go.[^4]
