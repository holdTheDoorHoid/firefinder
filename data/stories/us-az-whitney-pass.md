Whitney Pass Lookout is a cabin in Mojave County, Arizona, at about 4,400 feet of elevation.[^1] The Bureau of Land Management administers it through its Arizona Strip District.[^1] The Register describes it as a limited-view cabin that looks into Arizona from just inside the Nevada border.[^1]

The Register says the cabin dates to 1962. Wade Luther nominated it, and the Register recorded it on May 15, 2022, as US 1573, AZ 80.[^1]

The Register calls the site primitive and lists no water, electricity or propane there.[^1] Peakbagging's Arizona table assigns the structure code A, meaning it is in good shape and could be staffed. That describes the building, not who staffs it.[^2]

The sources read do not say who has staffed the cabin or what fires it watched. The Register also says much about the structure and its role is still unknown.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a standing ground lookout under the same register number.[^3] Neither source mentions a closure, rebuild, or move.[^1][^3]

The sources disagree on the map position. The Association's index gives a longitude of -108.048, which would place the cabin outside Arizona.[^3] The Register gives -114.048, near the Nevada border, and this story follows the Register.[^1][^3]

The sources do not say whether the public may visit or go inside.[^1][^2] Check with the Bureau of Land Management before any trip.
