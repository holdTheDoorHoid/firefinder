Kathio State Park's observation tower did not start out as a park amenity. It began life as a fire lookout tower standing near the town of Isle, Minnesota.[^1][^3]

In 1981 the 100-foot tower was taken down at its original site and moved to Mille Lacs Kathio State Park, where it still stands today.[^1][^2][^3] It was rebuilt on one of the park's highest hills, part of the Mille Lacs Moraine — the ridge of glacial debris that dams up Mille Lacs Lake — so climbers start partway up before they even reach the stairs.[^1][^3]

The tower is open to visitors, who climb a long run of stairs — steep and narrow near the top — to a viewing platform.[^1] From there the view takes in Mille Lacs Lake and the landscape around it.[^2][^3] Nothing in these sources says when the tower was built, who built it, or when its fire-watching days near Isle ended before the 1981 move, so those details are unknown.

Today the tower is one of the park's "top stops," reached on foot and open for anyone to climb; it is on Minnesota Department of Natural Resources land, not private or restricted ground.[^3]
