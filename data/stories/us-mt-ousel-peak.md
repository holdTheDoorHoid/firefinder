Ousel Peak is in the Great Bear Wilderness.[^2] A Montana lookout list files the site under the Flathead National Forest.[^3]

The lookout went up in 1931, and its cab was an L-4 type.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list puts the site in its "Ground" category rather than with the towers.[^4]

The cab was destroyed in 1957.[^1][^2] A SummitPost page on the peak says nothing of the lookout is left.[^2] A county list also records panorama photos taken at the site on September 18, 1935.[^3]

Nothing of the lookout is described as standing today, so a visit now means reaching the summit itself.[^2] The trailhead is reached from Highway 2, about 6.5 miles east of West Glacier.[^2] The round-trip hike is about 8 miles and gains roughly 3,910 feet.[^2] The trail in the wilderness allows hikers and riders on horseback, but no motor vehicles.[^2]
