Twin Mound Lookout sits in Clark County, Wisconsin, and the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources is listed as its administrator.[^1] The Register dates the tower to the early 1930s. It describes an 82-foot Wisconsin standard design with a ladder protected by an external cage.[^1]

The earliest dated mention I could check is a May 1932 newspaper item, quoted on a Forest Lookouts page, that lists "Twin Mounds" in district 10 among the towers already completed.[^3] The Forest Lookouts page files that item under Clark County, which fits this site.[^3] The Register also points to 1934 National Geodetic Survey notes that place a fire tower on the site by then.[^1]

According to the Register, the site had part-time staffing during the spring fire season until 2015, when the state ended its lookout network.[^1] A WJFW feature says Wisconsin took its 72 remaining towers out of service in 2015.[^4] A Door County Pulse article says the DNR had settled on decommissioning all 72 towers and was looking for new owners. Towers left unsold would be torn down.[^5]

The Register lists the tower as US 1267, WI 48, registered on February 4, 2018, at an elevation of 1,305 feet (398 m).[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's statewide list still shows the tower as standing.[^2]

I found no readable source on whether the tower is open to visitors, has been sold or rented since 2015, or can be climbed. Before planning a visit, ask the DNR, and do not climb the tower without permission.
