Pine Hill is in El Dorado County, on ground now inside the Pine Hill Preserve.[^1][^3] In early 1935 CCC crews were building a road up to the summit and a lookout station there.[^2] The Register says the CCC built the lookout, a BC-301 cab on an L-701 tower.[^1] The Register and Peakbagging.com both give 1936 as the build year.[^1][^3]

In 1937 La Verne Campbell staffed the Pine Hill lookout for the summer.[^2] A year later a local paper reported that the Oswald family was staffing the lookout for the summer.[^2] Sources differ on the later work. The Register dates major tower work to 1972, while a Forest Lookouts timeline records a remodel in 1971.[^1][^2]

In 1977 the state forestry agency, CDF, said it intended to sell off the site as surplus land.[^1] In 1979 the state Department of Fish and Wildlife took over the property. Together with county and BLM land, it became the Pine Hill Preserve.[^1][^3] The Register says the lookout has stopped working as an active fire-detection post. CalFire and other agencies use it for radio repeaters, and large radio towers dwarf it.[^1] The Register also says its form and design are intact.[^1]

A Peakbagging.com page with a photo dated December 19, 2004 calls the lookout only in fair condition, run by CalFire, with staffing on an as-needed basis.[^3] The BLM says the Preserve parcels it manages are open to the public all year for low-impact recreation, on foot only. Its page does not address the lookout compound itself.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the enclosed tower as standing.[^5] Check with CAL FIRE or the land managers before going.
