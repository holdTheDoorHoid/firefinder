Raspberry Butte stands at 6,374 feet in the Clearwater National Forest, about 10 miles southeast of Kelly Creek Ranger Station in Clearwater County.[^1][^3] A 50-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab went up there in 1934.[^1][^2][^3] The forest's own list gives the same tower and the same 1934 date, but it draws partly on Rex Kamstra's firelookout.com site, so it is not a fully separate check.[^1][^3] Half a mile west, a patrol point carried a taller 75-foot pole tower with an L-6 cab.[^1][^2] The lookout was abandoned in 1945, a date that three sources agree on.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association now lists the site as a tower that is gone.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts calls the site hike-in, so a visit means walking in.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the summit, or whether the public may go there.[^1][^2][^3]
