Uncha is listed as a fire lookout site in the Bulkley-Nechako area of British Columbia, at 1,389 m (4,557 ft) above sea level, with coordinates of 53.996 and 125.601.[^1] The sources I could reach give no build date, design or staffing for a lookout there.[^1] No source describes the site today, so whether a tower ever stood on it, or whether the site is open to the public, is not known from this research.[^1]

[^1]: Uncha, Canada's Fire Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/uncha.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
