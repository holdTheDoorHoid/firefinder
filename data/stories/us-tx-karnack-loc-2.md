Karnack's tower is in Harrison County, Texas, and the Forest Lookouts list gives the Texas Forest Service as the agency.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list has two Karnack sites.[^2] Loc 1 is listed as gone.[^2] Loc 2, the one in our record, is listed as standing at 32.62116, -94.18207, which places Loc 1 to the north of it.[^2]

A Texas travel site gives some background. It says the Forest Service started putting up metal lookout towers at well-chosen spots in the forests, before the Texas Forest Service began using airplanes to spot fires.[^3] It adds that a few of the old towers survive, though they see little use.[^3]

We could not find when Loc 2 was built, what its design is, who watched from it, or whether visitors may go near it. The Forest Lookouts Karnack page has only a header, and no other page we could open tells the story. Ask the Texas Forest Service about the tower's history and whether the public may approach it.
