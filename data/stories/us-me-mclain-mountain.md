The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine list places McLain Mountain in Washington County. It records the structure type as unknown and the status as gone.[^1]

Maine's overview of its fire towers says the state ran more than 100 of them, each watched by a lone observer, and that the network ended after a budget crisis in 1991.[^2]

No source I could find describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Maine lookouts list, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/me/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Fire Towers, Maine Atlas Project, Office of the Maine Secretary of State, https://www.maine.gov/sos/maineatlas/explore-the-atlas/fire-towers (accessed 2026-10-10).
