View Point Lookout stood in the Ochoco National Forest in Crook County, Oregon, about 23 miles northeast of Prineville, at 6,181 feet.[^1][^2]

Sources disagree on when it went up. The Central Oregonian reported in April 1931 a plan for a 20-foot wooden tower at View Point.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives 1940 for the crow's nest lookout.[^1] The same register carries a panoramic photograph of the View Point Lookout dated August 23, 1936, which suggests a lookout was already standing by then.[^1] Firelookout.com dates its crow's nest to the 1930s.[^2]

The register says the lookout is now gone.[^1] Firelookout.com lists it as abandoned.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page marks it as removed.[^3]

None of the sources we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
