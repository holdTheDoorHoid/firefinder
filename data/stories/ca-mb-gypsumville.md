Gypsumville is a community on the north bank of Lake St. Martin, in Manitoba's Interlake Region.[^1] The Canada Lookouts site dates a steel tower here to 1947 and cites the Manitoba Department of Mines and Natural Resources annual report for that date.[^2] A newspaper item of 9 August 1948, credited on the same page to the Lundar Interlake Observer and The Winnipeg Tribune, describes a 24 m (80 ft) steel fire lookout tower being erected at Gypsumville under the supervision of Forest Ranger Emil Campbell. The item adds that the tower would offer a very good outlook over the surrounding land and help with fire control.[^3] The two dates disagree. The annual report summary puts a steel tower in 1947, while the August 1948 item still describes erection as under way, so the sources do not settle when the tower was finished.[^2][^3] Nothing found describes what remains at the site today or whether visitors can reach it.[^2]
