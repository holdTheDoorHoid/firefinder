The Wisdom Ranger Station lookout is a platform on top of the ranger station warehouse in Beaverhead County, Montana, within the Beaverhead National Forest.[^1][^2] Its description in the National Historic Lookout Register dates the platform to the 1930s, and the Register gives 1930 as its build year.[^1]

The Register listed the lookout as US 1567, MT 113 on April 14, 2022, and gives its elevation as 5,675 feet, or 1,730 meters.[^1] The Register's description says the platform is still there. A firelookout.com page on the same lookout uses the same wording.[^1][^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list reads differently: it marks the platform as abandoned.[^3] The 2022 Register entry is the newest account we found, so whether the platform still stands today is not confirmed.[^1]
