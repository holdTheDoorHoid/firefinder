Rocky Knob, in Caldwell County, North Carolina, is listed with the U.S. Forest Service as the agency for its tower.[^1] A tnlandforms.us list of North Carolina lookout towers ties the site to survey station FZ2013.[^3]

In 1971 the North Carolina Geodetic Survey set a station on the knob, about 3.3 miles south-southeast of Blowing Rock.[^2] Its description places the station near the northeast corner of a fire tower.[^2]

A 2010 recovery note for the station says the fire tower is gone.[^2]

The survey station was recovered in 2010 in good condition.[^2] Its marker is set in a concrete cylinder whose top is level with the ground.[^2] The 2010 note states that a four-wheel-drive vehicle is required.[^2] No source we read describes what else remains at the site, or whether the public may visit it.
