Clippings dated from 1942 to 1998 on a lookout history site place the White City fire tower in Gulf County.[^1] The oldest, a 1942 item from The Star, counted four aircraft spotting posts in the county, one of them at the White City tower. Each post sent daily reports to a district control center in Thomasville, Georgia.[^1]

Other clippings use a different name for what appears to be the same place. A 1947 item places the St. Joe tower close to White City, and a 1949 item also uses the St. Joe name, so the two names probably refer to one tower.[^1]

In October 1973 a Florida Department of Forestry worker who was based at the White City tower died in a highway crash about four miles west of Wewahitchka.[^1]

In mid-May 1998, thieves got into a shop area at the site and carried off tools and electronics worth roughly $10,000. The report also says that buildings on the tower property were broken into.[^1]

White City is an unincorporated community on State Road 71, about 7 miles northeast of Port St. Joe.[^2] We found no source describing what remains of the tower or its grounds today, or whether the public may visit the site.
