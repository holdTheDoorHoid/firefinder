In 1938 the state's biennial report recorded that a 120-foot (36.6 m) steel tower at East Lake had been completed.[^1] Civilian Conservation Corps crews from Camp P-67 finished the work.[^1] The site's page lists the lookout under the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources.[^1] The tnlandforms list of North Carolina towers places East Lake in Dare County.[^2]

A 1939 entry in the same series mentions a two-room frame house at the East Lake tower.[^1] Camp BF-2 completed it as a cooperative project.[^1] That entry gives the house's completion date as June 15, 1929, ten years before the entry's own date of June 15, 1939, so the two dates in it do not agree. The sources do not say which is right.[^1]

No source I found describes the site today, including whether anything of the tower remains or whether the public may visit it.
