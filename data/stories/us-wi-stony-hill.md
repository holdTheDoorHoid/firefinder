Stony Hill was a fire lookout in Douglas County, Wisconsin, at about 46.534 N, 91.593 W.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list marks its tower as gone.[^1] A compiled list of Wisconsin lookouts also places a Stony Hill tower in Douglas County.[^2]

The state once had a large lookout network, with 119 towers at its peak, and many surviving towers were built in the 1930s by Civilian Conservation Corps workers.[^3] Statewide, 72 towers still in service were taken out of use in 2015.[^3] An NHLR entry for the Camp Douglas lookout gives 2015 as the year the state's network ended.[^4] Wikipedia gives 2016 as the year the state chose to end operations at its final 72 active towers, so the sources differ on the year.[^5]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the Stony Hill site today, or whether the public may visit it.
