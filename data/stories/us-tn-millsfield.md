The Forest Lookouts website places the Millsfield tower in Dyer County. It describes the tower as steel and dates its construction to 1966.[^1] A National Register multiple-property form about the Tennessee Division of Forestry lists a Millsfield tower in Dyer County among the division's towers.[^2] It credits the division with building 208 fire lookout towers, roughly 1930 to 1970.[^2]

A June 1999 trip report on the County High Points site says its author climbed the Millsfield fire lookout tower to the second landing, so the tower was still standing then.[^3]

The Forest Lookouts page dates the tower's removal to 2015 and gives a communications tower as the reason.[^1] The Tennessee Landforms table marks the Millsfield site as removed.[^4] The page does not say whether the communications tower stands on the same spot.

No source we read describes the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
