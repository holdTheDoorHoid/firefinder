The Birch Lake tower stood in Cass County, and the Minnesota Forest Service owned it.[^1] It was an 81-foot Aermotor ladder tower.[^1][^2] Birch Lake Township takes its name from that lake, which borders the village of Hackensack.[^3]

The lists give two build years. The date column says 1927, while the note on the same entry says the Aermotor tower was erected in 1928.[^1][^2] Either way, it was sold and removed in 1981.[^1][^2]

A Minnesota tower history says the spot became a landfill and later a transfer station.[^3] The writer visited the former tower site, now in a gravel pit, and elsewhere on the same page calls it a sand pit.[^3] A photograph on the same page shows the landfill, which is now closed.[^3] The writer's visit was made with a transfer-station staff member's permission.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^4]
