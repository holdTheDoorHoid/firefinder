The map places a tower called "Lookout Tower" in Quincy Township, Houghton, Michigan.[^1][^2] OpenStreetMap, a map that people edit together, marks it as an observation tower.[^1] It gives no builder, date or height.[^1]

We could not confirm that this was ever a fire lookout. The National Historic Lookout Register lists seven Michigan lookouts, and none is named for Quincy Township, Ruby Marsh or Lake Annie.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists two Michigan sites, Bliss and Paulding, and neither is named for those places.[^4] The sources we read do not say who built this tower, when, or what it watched.

A map listing places the tower near Ruby Marsh and Lake Annie.[^2] We found nothing about its condition, its owner, or whether the public may visit. Until a record turns up, treat it as an unidentified structure, and ask the landowner before going near it.
