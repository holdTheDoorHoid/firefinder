The lookout site stood atop Mill Mountain in Bath County, Virginia, at 3,085 feet.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register places it in the Rich Hole Wilderness of the George Washington National Forest and lists the U.S. Forest Service as its administrator.[^1] Krissy Reynolds nominated the site, and the register listed it on January 15, 2018.[^1]

A Forest Lookouts page for Virginia lists the White Rock on Mill Mountain under Bath County and the George Washington National Forest, and marks it removed.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list shows the tower as gone.[^3]

The register's only description of the site is that it was atop Mill Mountain.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains there today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
