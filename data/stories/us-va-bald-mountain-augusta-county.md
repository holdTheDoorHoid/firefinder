Bald Mountain is in Augusta County, Virginia, on land the register places in the George Washington National Forest.[^1] A 1932 newspaper item, quoted by Forest Lookouts, put the planned tower on the county line between Nelson and Augusta counties.[^2]

In September 1932 The News Leader reported that two new lookout towers would be built, one of them on Bald Mountain.[^2] The paper said the planned structure would be steel, fifty feet high, with a 7-by-7 observatory on top and living quarters at ground level.[^2] The register gives the height as 60 feet and places a lookout residence cabin below the tower.[^1] These sources disagree, and we cannot tell from them which height was built.

A 1957 Coast and Geodetic Survey description places a survey mark at the apex of the tower's roof, so a tower stood there by then.[^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site on July 30, 2018, on the nomination of Kristin Reynolds.[^1] Forest Lookouts marks the tower as removed, but gives no date.[^2]

The register says the site is administered by the U.S. Forest Service.[^1] No source we read says what remains on the peak today, or whether visitors can reach the site.
