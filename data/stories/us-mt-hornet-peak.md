Hornet Peak Lookout sits on the summit of Hornet Mountain in the North Fork area of Montana's Flathead National Forest, about 45 miles north of Columbia Falls.[^2] It was built by Forest Service crews between 1922 and 1923, in the years after the catastrophic fires of 1910 pushed the agency to build out a chain of lookouts across the North Fork for early fire detection.[^2] Seasonal employees put it up for $719.38, using logs and other materials found on site or packed in by trail.[^2] The result is a D-1 style log cupola cabin, a single 14-by-14-foot room built of saddle-notched logs 8 to 10 inches thick, with built-in storage, a wood stove, two bed frames, and a hand-carved chair inside.[^2] Nearby, white-painted rocks spelling out "Hornet L.O." in letters about four feet tall still mark the site.[^2]

The lookout was staffed every summer from 1923 until roughly 1946, part of the North Fork's fire-watch network.[^2] At some point after that it fell out of use and sat abandoned for a number of years before the Forest Service restored it.[^1] A wildfire swept through the surrounding country in 2003, scorching the landscape around the tower, though the lookout itself survived and the area has since begun growing back in wildflowers and bear grass.[^3]

Hornet Peak was added to the National Register of Historic Places on August 19, 1983, and later entered on the National Historic Lookout Register in 2016.[^2][^1] Today it's one of the Flathead National Forest's rental cabins, bookable through Recreation.gov from mid-June through October for about $40 a night.[^2][^3] The two-story structure offers simple ground-level living space plus a small loft, with a propane stove, propane lantern, two cots, and a wood stove for heat, but no running water — guests pack in everything they need, including their own water and bedding.[^3] The access road, Hornet Road 9805, is described as narrow with few pullouts, so visitors are advised to drive carefully and check with the Glacier View Ranger District on conditions before heading up.[^3]
