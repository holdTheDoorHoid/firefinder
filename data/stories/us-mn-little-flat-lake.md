The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service built a wood lookout in 1938 at Tamarac National Wildlife Refuge in Becker County. It was on the south side of Little Flat Lake, roughly three miles from refuge headquarters.[^1][^2] The refuge itself was established that same year under Executive Order 7902 (May 31, 1938).[^3] The county list's entry for the wood tower reads "Stair."[^1]

The wood tower was removed in 1954/55, when a steel tower was erected.[^1][^2] That steel tower was about 100 feet tall and had been moved from North Dakota. The 1964 survey notes call it the Tamarac Wildlife Refuge Fire Tower.[^1][^2] It was removed in the mid-1980s.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Little Flat Lake as gone.[^4]

None of the sources read describes what, if anything, is left of either tower today.[^1][^2] The Fish and Wildlife Service page presents the refuge as a place for today's visitors, but no source says whether the old tower spot can be reached.[^3]
