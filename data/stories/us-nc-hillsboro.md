A lookout-history site lists a tower called Hillsborough in Orange County. It says the State Forest Service erected an 80-foot tower there in 1947, and it credits that date to the state's 12th biennial report of 1948.[^1]

Trail guides describe a fire tower on the summit of Occoneechee Mountain. It is probably the same lookout, though the guides name no builder or date for it.[^2][^3]

A surplus-sale note from 2018, quoted on the same page, says an auction of surplus state property had opened. It adds that the tower stands on leased land and that the landowner had asked for it to be removed.[^1]

Trail guides describe the retired tower on the summit as still there, fenced off and closed to the public.[^2][^3]

The tower is closed, so it should not be climbed. Hikers can reach the summit on foot, leaving from the designated lot for Occoneechee Mountain State Natural Area, reached via Virginia Cates Road.[^2][^3]
