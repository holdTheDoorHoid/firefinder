Calusa Peak sits in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, about 10 miles southwest of Kellogg in Shoshone County, Idaho, at an elevation of 4,700 feet.[^1][^2]

The only structure the sources name there is a crow's nest, dated to the 1930s.[^1][^2]

The Idaho site files the lookout under its St. Joe – Clearwater region, though it names the Coeur d'Alene National Forest as the land it stands on.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list places the peak at roughly 47.42 degrees north and 116.26 degrees west.[^3]

One county list spells the name "Calusa Point" and gives the same 1930s crow's nest, which may mean it is the same lookout.[^4]

Sources list the site as abandoned.[^1][^2][^3] The Idaho site also labels it a hike-in location.[^2]

None of the sources describes what, if anything, remains on the peak today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
