The Georgia tower lists include a lookout called Brunswick in Glynn County.[^1][^2] The tower list gives its coordinates as 31.19222, -81.50529 and a GFC number of 849, and the same page links to the Georgia Forestry Commission.[^1] The Forest Lookouts index gives only the name and the county.[^2] The name matches the city of Brunswick, which Wikipedia says serves as the seat of Glynn County government.[^3] The page carries a Google map revision stamp dated 22 March 2016.[^1]

No source I could reach describes the site today, or says whether the tower still stands or is open to the public. Do not climb a tower unless its access is confirmed.
