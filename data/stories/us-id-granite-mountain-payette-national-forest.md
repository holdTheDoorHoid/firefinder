Granite Mountain Lookout sits in Adams County, Idaho, within the Payette National Forest, about 13 miles north-northwest of McCall.[^1][^3] The site began as a camp in 1913.[^1][^3] A 10-foot pole tower with a 12-by-12-foot cab went up in 1916, and the present 20-foot treated timber L-4 tower was built in 1955.[^1][^2][^3]

Sources give different build years, because some count the camp and first tower and others count the tower standing now.[^1][^2][^3] The 1955 date is the one for the present tower, and the register, the Idaho site and firelookout.com all give it.[^1][^2][^3]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts staff list begins with a 1956 entry and runs through 2026.[^2] The register says staffing happens every fire season, and firelookout.com says every summer.[^1][^3] A July 18, 2025 visit described on the Idaho site found the tower staffed, with a helicopter resupply visit under way.[^2] A Forest Service recreation guide, however, calls the lookout unmanned.[^4] It is the only agency statement gathered here that says so.[^4]

The register added the lookout on September 29, 2020, as US 1433.[^1][^2]

The lookout is reached on foot, as the Idaho site describes it: hike-in and staffed.[^2] Trail #165 gains nearly 2,000 feet over a bit more than two and a half miles from a Goose Lake Road trailhead about 12.5 miles north of Highway 55, and it is non-motorized.[^5] Before a trip, check with the Forest Service: the trail page lists a New Meadows office as temporarily closed, while the recreation guide names the McCall Ranger District.[^5][^4]
