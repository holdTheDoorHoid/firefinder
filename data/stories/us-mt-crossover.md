Crossover Mountain, in Flathead County, Montana, had a Forest Service lookout on the Flathead National Forest, about eight miles north of the Spotted Bear Ranger Station.[^1][^2] The listing gives an elevation of 6,624 feet.[^1]

The listing dates the cab to 1937 and labels it an L-4 cab.[^1] A 1940 Coast and Geodetic Survey description already describes the lookout house, so the building was standing by then.[^2]

The 1940 description places the house on a sharp pointed peak, about two miles east of the South Fork of the Flathead River and about 15 miles northeast of Swan Lake.[^2] The two sources differ slightly on direction, with one saying north and the other north-northwest.[^1][^2]

The listing says the lookout was destroyed in the 1950s.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as gone.[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
