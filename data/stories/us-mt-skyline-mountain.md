Skyline Mountain sits about 16 miles northwest of Libby, Montana, in the Kootenai National Forest and Lincoln County.[^1] The firelookout.com page gives its elevation as 6,250 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list puts the site at 48.57841 north and 115.76650 west.[^2]

The page dates a cabin at the site to the 1920s and lists an L-4 cab for 1935.[^1] It does not say whether the L-4 cab replaced the earlier cabin.[^1] The same list gives the site's type as ground and its status as gone.[^2]

Neither source says when the lookout was removed.[^1][^2]

Today, no source we read describes what remains at the site, and none says whether the public may visit it.[^1] The sources we found describe the site in only a few lines.[^1]

[^1]: Skyline Mtn. Fire Lookout Cabin, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/skylinemtn.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
