The Union Lookout Tower stands in Union County, Mississippi, at about 619 feet above sea level.[^1] The register says the site was once thought to be the Center Lookout Tower, but the Forestry Commission's own signage confirmed the Union name.[^1] The Forest Lookouts index still files it under the Center name, as "Center (Union)", and OpenStreetMap calls it the Center Lookout Tower.[^2][^3]

The register describes a 100-foot Aermotor tower.[^1] Aermotor built its towers from galvanized steel.[^4] The register gives no build date.[^1] The register says the site includes accommodations for the County Forester, and it lists a main residence, a quonset hut, a secondary shed and a mechanics bay on the property.[^1]

The tower was registered on June 23, 2022, with Jason Johns as nominator.[^1] The register's entry gives no site visit date.[^1] I found no staffing record, fire history or report of notable fires for this tower.

The register lists the Mississippi Forestry Commission as the administering agency.[^1] I found no record of the tower being abandoned, moved or rebuilt. The register does not say whether it is open to visitors.[^1] I found no recent condition report. Check with the Forestry Commission before planning a visit.
