The Sandtown Lookout Tower carries the name of Sandtown, an unincorporated community in Neshoba County, Mississippi.[^1][^2] Sandtown sits along Mississippi Highway 482, roughly 4.7 miles (7.6 km) southwest of Bogue Chitto.[^2] Its name traces back to a Choctaw village that once occupied the same ground, known in Choctaw as Shinuk Kaha.[^2]

OpenStreetMap records the tower as a lattice observation tower, with its map point about 190 m (623 ft) above sea level.[^1]

No source describes what stands at the site today, and none says whether the site is open to the public. Do not climb or enter the tower without first confirming that it is open to visitors.
