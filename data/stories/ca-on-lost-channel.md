Lost Channel, in Ontario's Parry Sound District, is now a ghost town.[^1] The Pakesley Lumber Co. resumed milling hardwood there, and the Key Valley Railway carried mostly lumber from the mill out to Pakesley.[^1] The mill shut down in 1933.[^1]

The Canada Lookouts index dates the tower's erection to 1931. It was a light steel tower, 24.4 m (80 ft) tall, with an octagon cab.[^2] An Ottawa Journal item of 6 September 1933, reproduced on the index, says fires were spotted that day from the Lost Channel tower, across the northern part of the district.[^2]

No source found describes what remains of the tower, or whether its site is open to visitors.[^2] A ghost-town page says the old townsite's remains are hidden on the north side of the cove, and that the old bunkhouse still serves as a summer lodge, mainly for hunting and fishing.[^3]
