Cube Iron Mountain is in Sanders County, Montana, in the Lolo National Forest.[^2][^3]

firelookout.com dates the lookout to 1935 and describes it as an L-4 tower.[^1] A June 1935 item in the Sanders County Independent-Ledger reported that Cube Iron Peak had replaced Mount Headley as a lookout station.[^2] The same item said some Cabinet peaks had stopped serving as lookouts, a change tied to visual observation mapping of the forest.[^2]

An August 1936 item in the same paper names Halvor Ekern as the lookout on Cube Iron, and notes that three people hiked up to visit him.[^2]

The sources do not agree on how the tower ended. firelookout.com says it was destroyed in 1959.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page says it was removed and gives no date.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list shows a tower that is gone.[^3]

A hiking guide describes a trail route up to the summit of Cube Iron Mountain, using Trails 459 and 450 for 8.5 miles round trip.[^4] The guide says the summit looks west toward the American Selkirks and east toward the Cabinet Mountain Wilderness.[^4] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout today.
