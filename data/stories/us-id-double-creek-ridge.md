The Double Creek Ridge lookout went up in 1934 as an L-4 cab, set on a 20-foot tower.[^1][^2] The Nez Perce National Forest's list of its lookouts places the tower about 10 miles northeast of the Moose Creek Ranger Station.[^4] The same list gives its legal location as township 33 north, range 13 east, section 2.[^4]

The Bitterroot National Forest administered the site first.[^1] In 1957 the administration passed to the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1] The lookout was destroyed in about 1951.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Forest Lookouts credits that date to Ray Kresek, and the Nez Perce list draws on Kresek's book as well.[^1][^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the site as a tower that is gone.[^5]

None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ridge is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
