A steel fire tower stood on Scrub Oak Mountain, in the Lackawanna State Forest in Lackawanna County.[^1][^2] In July 1921 the state's forestry department announced twenty-two more steel fire towers for that summer, and one of them was to go on Scrub Oak Mountain.[^2] The register dates the tower to 1921 and calls it a steel Aermotor tower.[^1]

A 1959 Coast and Geodetic Survey station description, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, calls the Scranton Scrub Oak tower a four-legged steel structure. Its cab was glass-enclosed, painted orange, and topped with a black conical roof. The survey put the overall height at about 64 feet.[^2]

A November 1921 newspaper item said 813 people had registered at the Scrub Oak tower the previous month, more than at any other tower.[^2]

The register says the tower was removed in 1989.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains on the mountain today, or whether the site is open to the public.
