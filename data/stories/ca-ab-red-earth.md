Alberta's wildfire register lists the Red Earth fire tower as a steel tower, 30 m (98 ft) tall and standing at 627 m (2,058 ft) above sea level.[^1] The register gives 2015 as its construction year.[^1] It is marked active and reached by road, and it has no weather station.[^1]

The province has 127 lookout sites, and each is staffed by an observer, generally from April to October.[^2]

No source describes the site as it stands today, and none describes public visits. Treat it as a working fire-watch tower, not a place to visit.
