Slate Lick is a standing fire tower in Mineral County, West Virginia.[^1] Mapcarta labels the same spot the Slaty Lick Knob Lookout Tower, with coordinates almost identical to the Association's point, so we treat the two names as one tower.[^2]

The tower sits near Champwood, a community on Patterson Creek in Mineral County that is not incorporated.[^2] Mapcarta puts its elevation at 1,680 feet, or 512 metres.[^2]

We did not find a build date, a design, a builder, a staffing history, or a record of any fire the tower watched. Apart from the map and the Association's list, the sources we could read say nothing specific about this tower.

The state shut down its fire-tower program in 1990, after decades of operation.[^3] Fewer than a dozen still stand, and none is operating as a lookout.[^3] Several of the state's towers have been restored, and some are available for overnight rental.[^3] Those statements cover the state as a whole, not this tower in particular.

We found no access terms or rental listing for Slate Lick. Anyone interested should check with the land manager before going, and should not climb a tower that is closed or private.
