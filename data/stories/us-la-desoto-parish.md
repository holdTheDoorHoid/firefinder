The register calls it the Desoto Parish Lookout Site.[^1] It was a tower of at least 100 feet in the Grove Hill community of De Soto Parish, Louisiana.[^1] The listed elevation is 312 feet (95 m), and the register places it at about 31.97 N, 93.58 W.[^1]

The land around the tower was later taken over for a lignite coal mine.[^1] It was demolished at some point in the 1990s or 2000s.[^1] The register lists Dolet Hills Mining Venture as the body that administers the site.[^1]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site as US 2082, LA 5 on May 22, 2022, on the nomination of Matthew Nicholls.[^1]

Nothing in the register describes what, if anything, remains at the site now. We found no source saying whether the public can visit.

[^1]: Desoto Parish Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/la/desoto-parish-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
