Homeville (Sussex Courthouse) is a fire tower listed in Sussex County, Virginia, at 36.91977, -77.28013.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association marks it as standing.[^1] A photo caption in the Wikipedia article on Homeville says the community is at the intersection of Routes 35 and 40.[^3] The tower's name includes "Sussex Courthouse," but none of the sources I read explains why.[^1]

I found no build date, height, owner, staffing history, or named fire for this tower. The Forest Lookouts index gives only the county, and the register's Virginia page does not list it.[^2][^4]

Because the only status I have is one undated directory entry, treat "standing" as unconfirmed. Check with Sussex County officials or the landowner before you visit, and do not climb the tower or walk onto its land without permission.[^1]
