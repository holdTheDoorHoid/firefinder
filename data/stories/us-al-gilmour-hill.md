The Gilmour Hill fire tower stood in Barbour County, Alabama.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the site as US 1749, AL 20, and says the tower was found on an Alabama Forestry Commission list, which also administers the site.[^1] Andrew Zerbe nominated it, and the register dated the entry September 15, 2019.[^1]

The register says the site's location was found on a topographic map, and that only the stands remain.[^1] TopoQuest, a map site, calls the point Gilmore Hill Lookout Tower and places it at nearly the same coordinates as the register.[^1][^2] It gives the elevation as 501 feet, where the register gives 551 feet, so the two sources disagree on that figure.[^1][^2]

No source describes the site today beyond the register's note about the stands. None says whether the public can visit it.

[^1]: Gilmour Hill Fire Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/al/gilmour-hill-fire-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Gilmore Hill Lookout Tower, TopoQuest, https://www.topoquest.com/place-detail.php?id=164332 (accessed 2026-10-10).
