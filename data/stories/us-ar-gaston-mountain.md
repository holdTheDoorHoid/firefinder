Gaston Mountain's lookout stood in the Ouachita National Forest, about nine miles west of Mount Ida in Montgomery County, Arkansas.[^1] It was a four-legged steel tower, 70 feet (21 m) high, with a 45-foot (14 m) cabin at the top.[^1] A 1956 Coast and Geodetic Survey station description already describes the tower, so it was standing by then.[^1]

An ARGenWeb listing of the lookouts in the Mena and Oden ranger districts places Gaston Mountain among the towers that once stood in Montgomery County.[^2] The same listing says the Ouachita National Forest once had 43 lookouts and today has only six.[^2]

The tower is marked as removed, and the listing calls it long gone.[^2] Only Rich Mountain, Bee Mountain and Tall Peak are named as still standing.[^2]

Since it is listed as removed, there is no standing lookout here.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.

[^1]: Gaston Mountain - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts (reproducing National Geodetic Survey datasheet PID EJ2045), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/gaston-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Lookouts of the Mena & Oden Ranger Districts, Arkansas, ARGenWeb, https://www.argenweb.net/montgomery/lookouts.htm (accessed 2026-10-21).
