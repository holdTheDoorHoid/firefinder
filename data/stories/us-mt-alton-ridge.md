Alton Ridge was a fire lookout in Glacier National Park, in Glacier County, Montana.[^1] It stood about half a mile southeast of the Many Glacier Hotel.[^1] The firelookout.com page gives its elevation as 5,148 feet and describes the structure as a wooden platform tower.[^1]

The page's headline gives the years 1934 to 1937 without saying what they mark.[^1] A National Register nomination for Numa Ridge, another Glacier lookout, says it was one of several similar buildings put up in the park between the late 1920s and the early 1940s.[^2] The same nomination also describes a planned lookout network that let the park and neighboring national forest land keep watch on each other.[^2]

Alton Ridge also appears in the National Park Service's list of 1930s panoramic lookout photographs.[^3] The list records infrared and panoramic frames taken at Alton Ridge on 26 August 1935, by L. Moe.[^3]

The firelookout.com page marks the tower as gone.[^1] Today, no source I could read describes what, if anything, remains on the ridge.
