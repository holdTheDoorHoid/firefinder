McGary Butte, in Latah County about eight miles west of Elk River, got a 50-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab in 1935.[^1][^2][^3] The operator is named two ways. The Forest Lookouts page gives the Potlatch Timber Protective Association,[^3] while firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts list only the initials CTPA.[^1][^2]

In July 1939 the lookout reported a fire on a bridge along the Three Bear main line.[^3] A story from The Family Tree, a Potlatch Forests newspaper and reprinted on the Forest Lookouts page, says the fire destroyed seven bents, the framed supports of the bridge, and burned for over six hours.[^3] On July 21 the bridge was complete, and the first log train crossed it again.[^3]

The tower received extensive repairs in 1960, according to the Potlatch Timber Protective Association's annual report.[^3] It was destroyed in 1970.[^1][^2][^3] The state index lists the site as gone.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at McGary Butte today, or whether the site can be reached.
