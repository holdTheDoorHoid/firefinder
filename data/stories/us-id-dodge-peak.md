Dodge Peak is in Boundary County, Idaho, on the Kaniksu National Forest, about 14 miles southwest of Bonners Ferry.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 5,027 feet.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under its Northern Idaho region.[^2]

The lookout was a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, dated to 1933.[^1][^2] Both pages also describe a separate 35-foot patrol tower, with a log cabin on top, on Green Mountain 1.5 miles to the south-southwest.[^1][^2]

Both sources give 1955 as the year the lookout was destroyed.[^1][^2]

No source describes what remains on the peak today. Idaho Fire Lookouts files Dodge Peak as a hike-in site, so a visitor would need to walk in, but the page does not say whether the public can reach the summit now.[^2]
