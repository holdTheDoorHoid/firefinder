The Five Points lookout stood in Jefferson County, Indiana, on IN-7 northwest of Madison.[^2] The Forest Service lists it among the Indiana Department of Natural Resources fire towers, with Lancaster as the nearest town.[^1]

Indiana began building lookout towers in 1930, and by 1952 it had 33, almost all in the southern part of the state.[^1] The Indiana Fire Towers author notes that a 1936 county map shows no fire tower at the site, which the author reads as suggesting the tower was not there yet that year.[^2]

The tower is no longer standing.[^2] The author visited in June 2024 and found the footers, near a National Geodetic Survey mark said to be about ten meters from the old tower.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Indiana list marks the tower as gone.[^3]

As of June 2024, only the footers remained.[^2] No source we read says whether the site is public land or who owns it.
