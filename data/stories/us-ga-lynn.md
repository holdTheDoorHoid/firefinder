The Georgia lookout list has an entry named lynn in Decatur County, at 31.01517, -84.6155, with an elevation of 151 and no unit given.[^1] The entry carries no agency or registry code.[^1] Its compiler says names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own, and this tower is numbered 195.[^1]

The same list gives Decatur County a second tower, called West Bainbridge, at 30.91725, -84.59683, further south. It carries an NHLR code.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register has a Decatur County Fire Tower entry, which says the Georgia Forestry Commission put it up in 1953 and that it is sited at a ranger station.[^2] The sources do not show whether lynn is the same structure as either of these towers, so this story treats it as separate.

No source describes what remains at the lynn site today.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Decatur County Fire Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, https://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ga/decatur-county-fire-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
