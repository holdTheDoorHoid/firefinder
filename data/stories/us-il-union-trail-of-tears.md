Union Lookout, also called Trail of Tears Lookout, is a steel fire tower in Trail of Tears State Forest in Union County, Illinois.[^1][^4] The Civilian Conservation Corps built it in the 1930s as part of a network of lookouts that reached into Shawnee National Forest.[^2] The dates differ: the register says 1936 to 1938, Wikipedia says circa 1934, and Clio says 1934.[^1][^2][^4]

The name recalls 1839, when about 10,000 Cherokee people were forced from North Carolina to Oklahoma.[^1] The register notes that floating ice on the Mississippi River kept the group camped near the site in January before they crossed.[^1]

The tower stayed in use through the 1960s, when aircraft had mostly replaced towers for spotting fires.[^2] The sources read here do not name any lookouts or describe a notable fire seen from the tower.[^1][^2]

The register lists the tower as US 81 and IL 2, with a registration date of March 12, 1994.[^1] The National Register of Historic Places added it on February 5, 2003, and Wikipedia says it is the forest's last surviving lookout.[^2] Sources disagree on height: the register gives 80 feet with a 7-by-7-foot cab, and Clio gives 100 feet.[^1][^4] Agency names differ too. The register names the Illinois Department of Conservation, while Wikidata names the Illinois Department of Natural Resources.[^1][^3]

The register's visit report from July 2017 found most of the wooden steps missing or rotten, paint failing, and no access to the top.[^1] Clio, citing a park map, says the tower appears to be closed.[^4] The tower sits beside South Forest Road. Given that report, do not climb it.[^1]
