Larson Point was a log cabin on Clearwater National Forest land, about 11 miles west of the Kelly Creek Ranger Station in Clearwater County, Idaho.[^1][^3] Its elevation is listed as 5,133 feet.[^1][^3] The Forest Service list places it in Section 8, Township 39 North, Range 8 East.[^3]

The Forest Service list gives 1930 as the year the cabin was built. The two websites give only the 1930s, so 1930 is the most precise date available.[^3][^1][^2]

By 1960 the cabin was gone. All three sources record that year.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list files the site as a ground site, not a tower.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it as a hike-in site, and no source describes a closure.[^2]
