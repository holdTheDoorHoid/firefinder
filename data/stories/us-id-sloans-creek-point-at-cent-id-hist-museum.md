Sloans Point Lookout went up on the Payette National Forest in 1933, with a log cabin and a 72-foot Aermotor tower.[^1][^3] The register says wagons brought its materials in.[^1]

The register says the tower was staffed into the 1970s, while the historical marker gives 1934 to 1970 as its staffed years.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts says it was no longer staffed by the 1970s.[^3] I found no source describing a notable fire at this site.

The cabin residence came down in 1994, and Idaho Fire Lookouts says volunteers removed it.[^1][^3] In 2000 the tower was dismantled and moved to McCall.[^1][^3] The register says volunteers reassembled the cab and 10 feet of it at the Central Idaho Historical Museum.[^1]

Today the cab and 10 feet of tower are on the museum grounds, at 1001 State St. in McCall.[^1][^2][^4] A McCall travel guide says the museum is open June through August, Wednesday to Saturday, and that its exhibits include the cab and tower.[^4] Richard Holm, Jr. and Lawrence Kingsbury nominated the lookout, and the register listed it on January 2, 2008, as US 715, ID 48.[^1] The sources also disagree on elevation: Idaho Fire Lookouts gives 6,280 feet, while the register gives 5,021 feet for its coordinates.[^3][^1]
