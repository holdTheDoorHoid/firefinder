Bryantsville, also called Georgia, stood on Hoosier National Forest property west of Mitchell in Lawrence County, Indiana.[^1] Forest Service records describe a tower reportedly 100 feet tall, with a latrine on site, and say it was likely the same tower as the Georgia Tower.[^2] The same records say it may later have been leased to the state and manned by Indiana Department of Natural Resources staff.[^2] A 1949 topographic map shows the lookout tower, though not the radio tower.[^1]

Clarisse Carroll, a lookout at the Georgia Tower, described its construction and the lookout's work from 1950 to 1970.[^2] Her account describes four angle-iron legs set in deep concrete, and a seven-by-seven-foot cab with glass sides and a metal roof.[^2] A 2025 report says the farm where she and her husband lived stood close to the Georgia Tower.[^3]

Indiana Fire Towers reports that the tower was dismantled in 1972, citing a 2013 Outdoor Indiana article.[^1] The FFLA list marks the tower as gone.[^4]

A June 2024 visit by the Indiana Fire Towers author found the footers.[^1] The gravel road that begins where the pavement ends is said to lead to the old lookout site and a radio tower.[^1] A photo on the Indiana Fire Towers page is captioned as the radio tower, taken from the southeast.[^1] Beyond the footers, the sources do not say what remains at the lookout site today, or whether the radio tower still stands.[^1]
