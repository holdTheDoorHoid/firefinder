The Aycock Fire Tower stands in Wayne County, North Carolina.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register added it on May 21, 2026, as US 1820, NC 30.[^1] A separate tower list names it "Wayne (Fremont)" and gives coordinates close to the register's.[^2]

I could not find a construction date, builder or design for the tower in the sources I could read. The register says the North Carolina Forest Service once owned it and that it is now out of service.[^1] It gives the elevation as 135 feet (41 m).[^1] It lists the State of North Carolina as the administering body.[^1]

According to the register, the bottom stairs have been taken off.[^1] The register describes no public access.[^1] Treat it as a structure to look at from the ground, and do not try to climb it.

[^1]: Aycock Fire Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/nc/aycock-fire-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Wayne (Fremont), North Carolina fire tower list, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
