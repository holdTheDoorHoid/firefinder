Beaver Peak Lookout stood in Shoshone County, Idaho, inside the St. Joe National Forest.[^1][^2] Sources list the site as a camp in 1926, and an L-4 cab was added in 1935.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the peak's elevation as 5,655 feet.[^2]

The lookout was destroyed in 1953.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list has two Beaver Peak entries in Shoshone County. One, at 47.05694, -115.42511, is recorded as a ground lookout that is gone.[^4] The other, at 47.98720, -116.17042, is recorded as a tower that is gone.[^4] None of the first three sources gives coordinates for this lookout, so check the coordinates before you go looking for either site.[^1][^2][^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts classes the site as a hike-in.[^2]
