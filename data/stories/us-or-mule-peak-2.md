Mule Peak is in Klamath and Douglas counties, Oregon, on the Deschutes and Umpqua national forests.[^1] The firelookout.com listing places it 18 miles southwest of Crescent.[^2] The register gives its elevation as 6,860 feet, or 2,091 meters.[^1]

The cab on the peak was built in the 1930s.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register recorded the site on June 7, 2011, on the nomination of Ray Kresek.[^1]

Both lists now describe the cab as gone.[^1][^2] No source we read describes what remains at the site today, and none says whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]

Oregon has a second Mule Peak, in Union County on the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest. A wooden ground house there, which may date to 1924, is staffed from time to time in summer.[^3] The two sites are different places, so check the county before you plan a trip.[^1][^3]
