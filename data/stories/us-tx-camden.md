Camden sits in east central Polk County.[^3] The W. T. Carter and Brother Lumber Company developed the site in 1898.[^3]

The survey station sat about 2.5 miles south of the village of Camden, on a prominent, thickly wooded hill.[^1] The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the station in 1935, when a lookout tower stood over it.[^1] The survey mark sat directly beneath a bolt at the peak of the lookout house's roof.[^1] The survey also recorded distances to the anchor bolts set in the concrete footings.[^1]

A 1949 station recovery note reports that the tower had come down, leaving only concrete footings.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list marks the Camden tower as gone.[^2]

The 1949 note is the latest description I found.[^1] No source describes the site today, and none says whether it is open to the public.
