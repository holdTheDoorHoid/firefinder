Blackwater is a lookout that Canada Lookouts lists under the Central Kootenay district of British Columbia.[^1] The listing gives its position as 51.583 north and 117.383 west, and marks it as removed.[^1] Its elevation is printed as 1684 with no unit; read as metres, that is 1,684 m (5,525 ft).[^1]

Do not confuse it with the Blackwater lookout in the Northwest Territories, which Canada Lookouts lists on a separate page.[^2] That page reproduces a notice dated June 2, 1982, inviting sealed tenders to put up two steel lookout towers at Blackwater, N.W.T.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the British Columbia site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
