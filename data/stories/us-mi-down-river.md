A federal survey mark in Crawford County, Michigan, marks the site of the Down River lookout, about seven miles northeast of Grayling.[^1] The National Geodetic Survey classes the mark as a lookout tower and gives its adjusted NAD 83 position as 44 42 31.61 N, 84 35 38.23 W.[^2] The survey point was the center of the tower's base.[^1]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey first observed the station in 1932.[^2] When it described the station in 1949, it recorded a steel tower on four legs, about 125 feet (38.1 m) tall, finished in black and white, with a closed cab at the top.[^1] The mark was in good condition that year.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
