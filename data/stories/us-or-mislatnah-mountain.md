Mislatnah Mountain, also called Mislatnah Peak, is in Curry County, Oregon, on the Siskiyou National Forest, about 21 miles northeast of Brookings.[^1][^2][^4]

The site sits at 3,124 feet, or 952 meters.[^1]

In 1934 a cab of the L-5 design was built on the site.[^1][^2][^3] Sarlin took panorama photos from the lookout on April 6, 1934, and the register credits those panoramas to the National Archives' Pacific Northwest Region.[^1][^3][^4]

The lookout was given up in 1954 and no longer exists.[^1][^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register recorded the site on November 18, 2019.[^1]

None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
