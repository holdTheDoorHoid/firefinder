The Leesville tower stands in Lexington County, on the road the register gives as US 984 and SC 25.[^1] The register counts it among the roughly 140 towers the Forestry Commission built in South Carolina between the mid-1930s and 1993, but it does not give a build year for this one.[^1][^2]

The register identifies it as a Bethlehem Steel tower, topped by a steel cab that is 7 by 7 feet. The tower itself is 80 feet tall and made of galvanized steel.[^1] The register also counts it as one of only two towers still in their original spot in Lexington County.[^1] The Gaston entry counts three towers in the county that the Commission still owns. That is a different count, not a contradiction.[^3]

When the tower was registered in 2013, the register described it as being in poor condition.[^1] Radio gear appears to be housed in a small shed at the base, and the tower carries several antennas.[^1] I found no source that names an operator or a fire this tower watched.

The register believes the Forestry Commission administers the tower, and it does not say whether the public may visit.[^1] Given its condition and the antennas mounted on it, please do not climb it. Check with the Forestry Commission before going near it.
