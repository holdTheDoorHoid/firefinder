Crowell Ridge sits in Pend Oreille County, in the Colville National Forest.[^1] The ridge lies in the Salmo-Priest Wilderness.[^3]

A gable-roofed L-4 cab went up in 1929.[^1][^2][^3] Alec Muffelbonn was the lookout that year, according to WillhiteWeb.[^3] There was also a patrol lookout about three miles north-northeast, on Gypsy Peak.[^1]

In 1935 Bill Nelson was the summer lookout.[^3] A Newport Miner report dated August 22, 1935, quoted on Forest Lookouts, says Nelson cut a forefinger with an axe and spent a couple of days in the hospital.[^4]

The register lists the summit at 6,835 feet, while WillhiteWeb gives 6,895 feet.[^1][^3]

The register notes that ruins may survive at the site.[^1][^2] WillhiteWeb says the structure was not burned, so a considerable amount remains, though images over the years document its slow decline.[^3] Its photo captions list a bed frame, a phone line in a tree near the summit, and a roof on the hillside in 2025.[^3]

The site is reached on foot.[^3] WillhiteWeb gives a hike of 4.2 miles one way with 1,400 feet of gain, and says the summit is a short side trip from the Crowell Ridge Trail.[^3]
