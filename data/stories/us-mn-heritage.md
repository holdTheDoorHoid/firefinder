The Heritage Lookout is a stairway tower in Koochiching County, Minnesota, on the Nett Lake Indian Reservation.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register says it is one of three towers on that reservation.[^1] The register gives no build year, builder or tower height.[^1]

From the top, the register notes, Nett Lake itself is visible. Gheen Hill, site of another lookout, is about 14.2 miles to the southeast and can be seen with binoculars.[^1] The register does not say whether the tower was staffed or used to spot fires.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^2] The National Historic Lookout Register added it on January 8, 2021, at the nomination of Jacob Hufnagle.[^1]

The register says the Bois Forte Band of Chippewa administers the tower and gives no visiting rules.[^1] Anyone interested in seeing it should ask the Band before going.
