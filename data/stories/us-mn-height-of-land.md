The Height of Land tower is a steel fire lookout in Clearwater County, Minnesota, on forest land in the White Earth State Forest.[^1][^2] The Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC) built it in the mid-1930s. The two lists we read do not agree on the details. The Central US lookouts list gives 1934 and a height of 80 feet, with an exterior ladder.[^1] The Clearwater County list gives 1935, a height of 86 feet, an International Derrick design and a square cab.[^2] We could not settle which figures are right.

We found no names of anyone who watched from the tower, and no particular fire it was used on.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing, with no register number beside it.[^3] We found no report that it was moved, burned or taken down.

A visitor who wrote about the summit for P600 parked on a small road and then hurried on foot to the summit area, where the tower was standing.[^4] The writer said a forest track appeared to reach the summit, but its ladder starts high above the ground, so the writer did not consider climbing practical.[^4] We found no published rule on public access. Treat the tower as something to see from the ground, and check with the Minnesota DNR before going near it.
