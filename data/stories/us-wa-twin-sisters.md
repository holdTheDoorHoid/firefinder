Twin Sisters is a summit in Ferry County, about 12 miles southwest of Orient, within the Colville National Forest.[^2] A lookout tower went up there in 1966.[^1][^2][^3] It stood 30 feet tall, was built of treated timber, and had an R-6 flat cab.[^1][^2][^3]

All three sources say the tower was gone by 1975.[^1][^2][^3] A visit on October 1, 2011 reported that the footings and the views are still there.[^1]

Today the old lookout is reached by a Forest Service jeep trail.[^4] A branch off Route #109 runs roughly 0.97 miles north to a parking area below the peak.[^4] The Forest Service says the old lookout stood at the top, and it asks drivers not to go all the way up because many nails remain on the ground.[^4] Visitors are asked to get out below the top and hike the rest of the way.[^4] The route is open from April 1 to November 30.[^4] The agency says contractors clear the trail only periodically, so it may not be fully passable.[^4] The agency describes the site as a strong place for views and photographs.[^4] A 2011 visit report gives two options: the jeep trail, or walking an old stock driveway.[^1] Willhite says today's trails also ran over the summit in the 1930s.[^3]
