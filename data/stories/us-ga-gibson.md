The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers, whose map was revised on 22 March 2016, names one tower Gibson and places it in Glascock County.[^1] The entry gives the coordinates 33.23500, -82.61861 and an elevation of 484 feet (147.5 m), and it carries the tag gfc-621.[^1] Glascock County's seat of government is the city of Gibson.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors. Check who owns the land before you go.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Gibson, Georgia, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gibson,_Georgia (accessed 2026-10-21).
