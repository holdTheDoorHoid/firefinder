The state forest service's county list dates a stairway tower on this hill to 1934 and numbers it tower 9.[^1][^2][^3] The list also gives the alias "Camp/Section 11 lookout."[^2] It places the tower south of Highway 2 in Fond du Lac State Forest and carries the note "CCC: S-79."[^2]

The sources disagree about its height.[^2][^3] A 1952 Coast and Geodetic Survey description of the same hill calls the steel structure about 70 feet high, with a lookout cage on top.[^3] The state list gives 100 feet.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list marks the tower as gone.[^4] The county and state lists say the site is now an "ARMER site."[^1][^2] No source describes what stands there today, or whether the public can get to the spot.
