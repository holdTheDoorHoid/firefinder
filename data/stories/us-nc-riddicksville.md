Riddicksville is one of the North Carolina lookout towers in the tnlandforms.us list, in Hertford County, at coordinates 36.52666 north and 76.94 west.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page for North Carolina also lists Riddicksville under Hertford County, with no further detail.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
