The register calls this the Red Banks/Mahon Lookout Tower.[^1] It sits in Marshall County, Mississippi, the county where the community of Red Banks is found.[^1][^3] It is a 100-foot Aermotor tower near the busy I-22 interstate in north Mississippi.[^1] The register gives no build date.[^1]

The register says the site once held a house and workshop for the Marshall County forester.[^1] We found no list of towermen and no record of fires this tower watched.[^1]

When the register's author visited, the house on the site appeared to be under renovation by a private individual.[^1] The register describes the tower as standing, but gives no date for that visit.[^1] A post in a Facebook group about Holly Springs, Mississippi, asks for old photos of the Red Banks fire lookout towers from before someone tore one down.[^2] If the post means this tower, it conflicts with the register's description.[^1][^2] We could not settle the question from the sources we could read.[^1][^2]

The register names the Mississippi Forestry Commission as the agency in charge, but it does not say whether the public may visit.[^1] We found no information about access or rental.[^1] Please do not climb or enter the tower or the house without permission from whoever controls the site.[^1]
