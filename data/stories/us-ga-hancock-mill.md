The Georgia lookout tower list places a tower called hancock mill in Richmond County, at coordinates 33.26243, -82.00773.[^1] Its elevation column reads 361, and the entry does not state a unit.[^1] The list's compiler says the names of towers numbered 73 and up are their own invention, so this name may not match a historical one.[^1] The entry also carries the notes prescott and gfc-028.[^1]

A 2013 chapter report from the Forest Fire Lookout Association says its writer had heard, secondhand, that two Georgia towers were set to go to auction for removal: one in Richmond County near Augusta and one in Pulaski County.[^2] The writer suggested that anyone interested reach out to the Georgia Forestry Commission's property division.[^2] The report does not name either tower.[^2] The list includes other towers in Richmond County, such as one listed as tobacoo rd, so the report cannot be tied to this site on the evidence found.[^1][^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
