Huckleberry Butte is listed at 3,729 feet, about seven miles northeast of Orofino, in Clearwater County, Idaho.[^1] The county inventory codes the site 37N-3E-19 and lists it under the Clearwater Timber Protective Association.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association page gives the agency as CTPA.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under its St. Joe-Clearwater region.[^2]

The earliest entry, dated 1923, is a pole platform tower.[^1][^2] A log cabin is dated 1930.[^1][^2] By 1934 the site had a 60-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2]

Both lookout-history pages list the same dates, ending with the tower's destruction in 1964.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as "Drive-Up," but no source we read describes what remains there today or whether the site is open to visitors.[^2]
