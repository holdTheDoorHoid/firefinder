Clemson was a South Carolina Forestry Commission fire lookout in Pickens County.[^1] The commission's history dates its first fire tower to August 1930, near Camden, and says most sites covered 10 acres and held a tower plus a year-round home for the operator.[^2] A Post and Courier item says the network grew to more than 130 towers.[^3]

Statewide, the commission shut down its fire tower network on October 1, 1993.[^2] A Post and Courier item dates the end of the lookout network to 1994, so the two accounts differ by a year.[^3]

A Post and Courier report says only 30 towers are still standing across the state. The copy read has no date, so that count may be out of date.[^4] No source I read describes what is left at the Clemson site, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1]
