Sunny's Lookout Lighthouse is not a fire lookout. It is a ride at Morey's Piers, at 3501 Boardwalk in Wildwood, New Jersey.[^1]

Morey's describes it as an interactive family ride.[^1] Riders haul on a rope to climb toward the top of a 35-foot lighthouse.[^1] Letting go lets the seat travel back down.[^1] The park lists the ride type as a drop tower.[^1]

Coasterpedia credits the German firm Heege Freizeittechnik with building the structure, and it gives 2005 as the year the ride opened.[^2]

It landed in our records because of its map entry. OpenStreetMap tags the structure as a man-made tower, a tourist attraction, and an amusement ride.[^3] We found no link between this ride and forest fire detection. It should come off the fire lookout list.
