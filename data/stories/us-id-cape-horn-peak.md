Cape Horn Peak lies in Bonner County, Idaho, about two miles northeast of Bayview.[^1] The lookout pages list it under the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1] The peak's listed elevation is 4,519 feet.[^1]

A crow's nest is recorded on the peak around 1930.[^1][^2] By 1935 the site held a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2][^3]

The tower was destroyed in 1952.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks it as gone.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the peak today, or whether visitors may go there. One lookout page tags the site as hike-in.[^3]
