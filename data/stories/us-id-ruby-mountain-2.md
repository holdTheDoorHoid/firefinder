Ruby Mountain Lookout stood on Ruby Mountain in the Kaniksu National Forest, about six miles southeast of Eastport in Boundary County, Idaho.[^1][^2] The tower was a 30-foot pole tower with an L-6 cab, built in 1935.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com also describes a frame cabin at the site.[^2]

Two staff names are recorded, but the dates sit awkwardly. Hollice Aldridge is listed for 1933 and 1934, citing a Bonners Ferry Herald article from June 26, 1934.[^1][^2] Both years come before the 1935 tower, and neither source explains why.[^1][^2] Lloyd Hughest is listed as staffing the lookout in 1946, citing Fire Lookouts of the Northwest.[^1]

Both sources say the lookout was abandoned in the 1950s.[^1][^2]

Today, the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list records the tower as gone.[^3] The firelookout.com page says the frame cabin is still there, though it gives no date for that statement.[^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in.[^1] We found no source that describes its condition or access in more detail, so anyone planning a visit should check with the Kaniksu National Forest first.
