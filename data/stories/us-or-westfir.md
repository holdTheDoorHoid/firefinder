The Westfir lookout was a non-standard L-4 cab on the Willamette National Forest in Lane County, at an elevation of 1,360 feet.[^1] The register gives its coordinates as 43.751421 north and 122.504413 west.[^1] Firelookout.com places the cab one mile west of Oakridge.[^2]

Both sources date the cab to the 1930s.[^1][^2] They also put its end in the 1950s, but they describe the ending differently. The register says the cab was removed, while FireLookout.com says it was destroyed.[^1][^2]

The register added the site on May 16, 2011.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks it as gone and records its type as ground.[^3]

None of the cited sources describes what, if anything, remains at the site now. Visitors should ask the Willamette National Forest about the site before going.
