Hominy Peak had a fire lookout in Teton County, Wyoming, about 12 miles southwest of the South Entrance to Yellowstone.[^1][^3] Both lookout history pages name the Targhee National Forest.[^1][^2] The directory classes the site as a ground site rather than a tower and marks it gone.[^3] A second lookout page says it was removed.[^2]

A Komoot listing places the summit in a protected area, the Jedediah Smith Wilderness, and asks visitors to check local regulations.[^4] A trail description gives the peak's elevation as 8,344 feet, while Komoot lists the summit at 2,540 metres.[^5][^4] A trail guide also places the Jedediah Smith Wilderness within the Targhee National Forest, with Hominy Peak visible to the southwest from the trail.[^5]

The sources reviewed do not say what remains at the summit today or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
