Ray Mountain lies in the Ochoco National Forest, about 15 miles north-northeast of Prineville, in Crook County, Oregon.[^1][^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says the lookout was built in the 1930s.[^1] firelookout.com gives the same decade and marks the exact year as unknown.[^2]

The register entry, which names Ray Kresek as nominator, is dated June 25, 2011. It gives the elevation as 5,440 feet, or 1,658 meters.[^1]

Both listings mark the lookout as gone.[^1][^2] An Oregon lookouts history page for the mountain gives the national forest and a township, range and section, and little else.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.[^1][^2][^3]
