Penrose Peak is a summit in the Lolo National Forest, about 12 miles west of Plains in Sanders County.[^1] A ready-cut lookout house was set up on the peak in 1930.[^2] In July the Sanders County Independent-Ledger reported that the house was being packed up to the summit.[^2] By late September the paper said four ready-cut houses had been installed, including those at Big Hole and Penrose Peaks in the Plains district.[^2] The listing calls the Penrose cab an L-4 from 1930.[^1]

In August 1932 the Penrose lookout was sent to a fire on the C. C. Divide and fell about five feet off a windfall on the way. He was brought to Plains and sent to Thornton Hospital in Missoula, and replacements were sent out.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Penrose lookout as gone.[^1][^3] Neither the listing nor the newspaper items give a date for its removal or say what became of the cab.[^1][^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the peak is open to visitors.
