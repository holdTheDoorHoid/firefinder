Tunnel Mountain sits in Columbia County, Oregon, and the Forest Lookouts site lists it there.[^1] Its only dated lookout record we found is a note from The Forest Log, dated March 1935.[^1] The note says Camp Boyington, which had been established in late fall 1933, had set up a second lookout on the mountain.[^1] The Oregon Department of Forestry is named as the agency.[^1] By one climbing guide's count, seven Columbia County peaks have held fire lookout towers.[^2]

No source we read says how long the lookout stood, when or how it came down, or what remains on the peak. We found no description of the site today.

[^2]: Long Mountain, SummitPost, https://summitpost.org/long-mountain/752205 (accessed 2026-10-21).
