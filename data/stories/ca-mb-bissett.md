Bissett sits on Rice Lake in southeastern Manitoba. From Winnipeg, it lies two and a half hours to the northeast, along Provincial Road 304, and it serves as a gateway to Nopiming Provincial Park.[^1] The Canada Lookouts site cites a Manitoba Department of Mines and Natural Resources annual report for the lookout here, dating the replacement of its condemned wooden tower by a steel one to 1947.[^2] The page files the lookout under the Eastern Forest District.[^2] Nothing found describes what remains at the site today or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]
