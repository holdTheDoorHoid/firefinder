Wesley Mountain sits in the town of Wesley, in Washington County.[^1][^4] The 1910 Report of the Forest Commissioner lists a lookout station there.[^1] A 1918 Maine Forest Service map calls the site Wesley Hill, also known as Wesley Mt. or Day Hill.[^4] By 1920 an inventory recorded a 55-foot wood tower.[^1]

The 2011 tower survey in the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine chapter report recorded one cab removal at Wesley. Four towers were lost that year, and Wesley was not among them.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Wesley Mountain as gone.[^3] Its key, though, marks a tower that keeps its structure but has lost its cab as standing with an asterisk. Wesley carries no asterisk, so the list and the 2011 survey do not agree.[^2][^3]

The same list has a separate Day Hill entry, about 2 km away by the listed coordinates, marked standing with an asterisk.[^3] The 1918 map treats Day Hill as another name for this hill. We could not tell whether the two records describe one tower or two.[^4]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, stands at Wesley Mountain today, or whether visitors can reach it.
