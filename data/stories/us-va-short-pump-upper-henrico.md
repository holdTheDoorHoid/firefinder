Henrico County says two fire towers went up in the county in the early 1930s. The Meadows tower was one of them. Its sister, the Upper Henrico Tower in the West End, was torn down in the late 1980s.[^1]

The association's list uses the name Short Pump (Upper Henrico) for a Henrico County tower and marks it gone.[^3] That tower appears to be the county's Upper Henrico Tower, though no source says so outright.[^1][^3]

The Henrico County Historical Society says Mason Wade was once the attendant of the Short Pump tower, and that he went up to the top twice a day to watch for forest fires.[^2] The society also says another fire tower served the Short Pump area. It resembled the pictured tower and has been preserved in Goochland County, but the society does not say it was the Short Pump tower.[^2]

The association's list gives a different ending. It places a standing tower at Gillies Creek in Henrico County under its heading for relocated and replica lookouts, and says it was relocated from Short Pump.[^3] The county says the Upper Henrico Tower was demolished, while the association lists a standing tower relocated from Short Pump. The sources do not settle which account is right.[^1][^3]

No source we could read describes the Short Pump site today, or the tower the association lists at Gillies Creek.
