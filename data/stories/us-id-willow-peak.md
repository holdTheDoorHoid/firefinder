Willow Peak is a patrol lookout in Benewah County, Idaho, within the St. Joe National Forest.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page gives its survey location as 43N-1W-26.[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts' history of Sunset Mountain lists a patrol point two miles northeast, atop Willow Peak, though the page does not say whether that is the same summit described here.[^3]

No source describes what remains on Willow Peak today, or whether the public can visit it.[^1][^2]
