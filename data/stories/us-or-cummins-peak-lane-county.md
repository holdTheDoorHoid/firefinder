The Former Fire Lookout Sites register places the Cummins Peak lookout in Lane County, inside the Siuslaw National Forest.[^1] A 1923 newspaper item, quoted by the Forest Lookouts site, put the mountain in Lincoln County, not far from Waldport.[^2] The sources disagree on the county.[^1][^2]

A Siuslaw National Forest examiner said in 1923 that the peak was an ideal spot for a permanent lookout station.[^2] A new lookout house was going up in 1925, and a January 1926 newspaper item says one had been built at a cost of $924.[^2] The register dates the first lookout, a D-6 cupola, to the 1920s.[^1]

Staff were posted on the peak in the mid-1920s and again in the early 1930s.[^2] A 1924 newspaper item describes one man who spent a summer on the peak with only a rough log roof for shelter.[^2] In 1930 the Forest Lookouts site names Ed Weise as the lookout man, and in 1932 Therone Farris and Verus Dahlin shared the season.[^2]

The register says the cupola was replaced by an L-4 cab in 1932, and that the cab went onto a short tower in 1943.[^1] Firelookout.com dates the L-4 cab to 1934.[^3] For 1954 the sources differ. The register says a 41-foot treated timber tower was built that year.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site cites a memo that put the cost of moving the Blodgett Peak lookout to this site at an estimated $5,200.[^2] Firelookout.com says the tower was moved from Blodgett Peak.[^3]

The Forest Lookouts site says the lookout was activated on March 7, 1942, for the Eugene Filter Center.[^2] Its last staffed year was 1971.[^2] In 1972 it served as an emergency post and was sold for salvage.[^2] The register says it was removed in 1975, while the Forest Lookouts site gives c.1975.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may go there. Anyone planning a visit should check with the Siuslaw National Forest first.
