Squawman Lookout stood on Squawman Peak, at 7,033 feet in the Selkirk Mountains of Boundary County, Idaho.[^1][^3] The Priest Lake Timber Protective Association is the agency listed for it.[^1][^2][^3]

The lookout was an L-4 cab, built in 1934.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Some sources call the summit The Wigwams.[^2] The USGS quadrangle map for the area is labeled The Wigwams (1996), and at least one visitor report uses that name for the site.[^3][^4]

The cab was destroyed in 1972.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Forest Lookouts lists the site as removed, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks it gone.[^3][^5]

Visitors describe footings still at the lookout site on the summit ridge.[^2][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts reports one footing dated 1934, with other lettering in the cement that can no longer be read.[^2] A 1964 Coast and Geodetic Survey description calls the structure a 20-foot-high observation house.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in.[^2] One trip report describes a hike of 1.5 miles each way with the gate open, and says a higher-clearance vehicle is best on the road.[^4] The same visitor found a gate about a mile short of the trailhead shut, with nothing posted to say why.[^4] With the gate closed, the walk is 1.2 miles longer each way.[^4] Check whether the gate is open before you set out. The sources do not say whether the road is open to the public.
