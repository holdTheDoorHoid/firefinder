The Texas Forest Service ran a lookout at Buna, and the Central US Lookouts directory files it under Newton County.[^1] A Texas tower list compiled from USGS maps and a Texas Forest Service list gives a "Buna Lookout Tower" at the same point.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list marks the tower as gone.[^3]

Lists put the tower in Newton County, but the Texas State Historical Association places the town of Buna in south central Jasper County.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Farm Roads 253 and 1004 meet at Buna, which also sits on U.S. Highway 96 and State Highway 62, about thirty-six miles north of Beaumont.[^4] The place began as a mill site that the Beaumont Lumber Company called Carrolla. It was later renamed Buna, and a post office opened there in 1893.[^4]

No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the land is open to visitors.
