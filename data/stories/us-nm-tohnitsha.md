Tohnitsha Lookout stands on the Navajo Reservation in San Juan County, New Mexico, at 8,973 feet.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register gives its build year as 1936.[^1] The register's description places it in the International Derrick group. That maker was less common than Aermotor, but the register says it built a number of fire towers across the Southwest in the 1930s and early 1940s.[^1] The tower is 82.9 feet high and has a 7-by-7-foot metal cab.[^1]

The register recorded the lookout on July 20, 2002, under the number US 434, NM 9.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's New Mexico list shows it as a standing tower under the same number.[^2]

The sources I reached do not describe who staffed the tower, what fires touched it, or what repairs or changes it has seen since 1936. I could not find a fuller history.

Because it sits on the reservation, please check with the Navajo Nation before planning any visit. I found no current statement about access.[^1]
