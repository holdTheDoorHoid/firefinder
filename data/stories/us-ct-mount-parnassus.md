The Mount Parnassus lookout stood in East Haddam, Middlesex County, Connecticut.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes it as a 75-foot steel structure.[^2] The Forest Lookouts site lists the tower as removed, with no date.[^1]

Today, we found no source that describes what remains of the lookout at the site.[^1][^2] A 2015 state siting report does describe an existing 120-foot monopole at 194 Mount Parnassus Road in East Haddam, which the state wanted to replace with a 120-foot lattice tower.[^3] The report is about upgrading public safety telecommunications systems, and it does not mention a fire lookout, so we cannot say whether that monopole stands on the old lookout site.[^3] A map site, TopoZone, still shows an "East Haddam Fire Tower" point in the same area, but it gives no status for it.[^4]
