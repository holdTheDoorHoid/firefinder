Cultus Mountain is a volcanic cone of about 6,750 feet in the eastern Cascades, south of Elk Lake.[^3] A lake of the same name lies nearby, and the peak looks out over the southern part of the Deschutes National Forest.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register places the site in Deschutes County, Oregon.[^1]

The sources disagree about the first tower. The register and FireLookout.com date a 9-by-9 tower to 1925.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts and the Deschutes County Fire Lookouts blog list no 1925 tower.[^3][^4] They describe a telephone line planned in 1933 to make the peak an emergency lookout point, then a 20-foot round tower with a 10-by-10 L-5 cab built in 1935.[^3][^4] Forest Lookouts gives the cost of that structure as $947.24.[^3]

In 1957 the Deschutes National Forest announced plans for a lookout house on a high peak, and a helicopter might be needed to haul in materials.[^3] A 1958 call for bids for a tower and lookout house said the successful bidder would have to remove the old lookout buildings.[^3] The register says the L-5 tower was replaced that year by a 20-foot treated timber tower with an R-6 cab.[^1][^4]

The lookout's end is also recorded two ways. The register and FireLookout.com say it was abandoned in 1963 and is now gone.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts and the Deschutes County Fire Lookouts blog give 1968 as the removal year.[^3][^4] Forest Lookouts says the lookout was sold as surplus, stripped, and the rest burned.[^3] We could not reconcile those dates, so both are given.

The register and FireLookout.com both call the lookout gone.[^1][^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, is on the mountain today or whether visitors can reach it.
