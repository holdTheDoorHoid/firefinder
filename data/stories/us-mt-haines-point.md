Haines Point's lookout was a 20-foot pole tower of the L-4 type, dated to 1935.[^1] firelookout.com places it about 12 miles west-northwest of Thompson Falls in Sanders County, Montana, at an elevation of 5,106 feet.[^1]

The site lay in the Kootenai National Forest, and the local history gives its survey location as 22N-31W-16.[^1][^2]

All three sources use the name Haines Point, and all three record the tower as removed or gone, so a visitor should not expect a standing structure.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list also classes it as a tower rather than a ground structure.[^3]

No source consulted describes what remains at the site today, whether a footing or other trace survives, or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
