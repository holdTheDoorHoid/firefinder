Jack Pine Mountain is a Superior National Forest site in Lake County, Minnesota.[^4] The county survey list says the first lookout there was a wood structure built near Baird Station, which rangers came to call Jack Pine, and that wood lookouts of this kind dated to the 1910s.[^1] A steel ladder tower followed at some point.[^1]

A Forest Fire Lookout Association post from July 2022 includes a photo of the earlier and later Jack Pine lookouts, with a caption dating it to 1924.[^2]

The firelookout.org listing marks the tower gone.[^3] The sources read do not describe what, if anything, remains on the mountain now. The county list puts the mountain east of Highway 1 on Forest Road 377.[^1]
