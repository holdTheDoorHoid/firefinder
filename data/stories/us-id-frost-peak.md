Frost (Point) Peak rises to 5,832 feet about 11 miles southwest of Kellogg in Kootenai County.[^1] A crow's nest platform on Latour Baldy, one mile south, was used from 1930.[^1][^2][^3] A 30-foot pole L-4 tower went up on the summit in 1934.[^1][^2][^3]

Known staff lists run from 1944 to 1975. Bill Weeks is the first name listed, for 1944, and Brian Shiplett appears for 1973 to 1975.[^2][^1] Mark Grorud, who staffed the peak in 1972, remembered a 40-foot wooden tower, which does not match the 30-foot figure given elsewhere.[^2]

A pop-up cupola trailer served as the lookout from 1959 until 1962.[^1] The treated-timber tower that replaced the L-4 is dated 1961 or 1962, depending on the source.[^3][^2][^1] Forest Lookouts gives it an R-5 cab, while the other two sites call it an R-6.[^3][^2][^1]

The Idaho Department of Lands took over operations in 1966.[^1] The treated-timber tower was gone by 1988, and Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it as confirmed gone that year.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts marks the site as drive-up and gone.[^2] Its visit report, dated October 2, 2025, found a few scraps and what the writers believe are two outhouse pits, with some wire and cans but no significant remnants.[^2] No source says whether the site is open to the public today.
