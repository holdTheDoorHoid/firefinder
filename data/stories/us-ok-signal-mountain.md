Signal Mountain is a lookout in Pushmataha County, Oklahoma.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site lists it under Oklahoma by name and county.[^4] Mapcarta, a map site, places a tower of the same name in Pushmataha County and describes it as a tower.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists five registered lookouts in Oklahoma: Bear Mountain Lookout, Beaver Fire Tower, Carnasaw Lookout, Carter Mountain Lookout and Jadie Tower.[^3] Signal Mountain is not among them.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit. Ask the land manager before going, and do not climb a tower that is closed or private.
