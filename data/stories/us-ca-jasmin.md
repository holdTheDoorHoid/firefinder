Jasmin is a lookout site in Kern County. The name also appears as Jasmine.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page gives the Kern County Fire Department as the agency.[^1]

In August 1938 the Bakersfield Californian reported that Gus Richardson, who had been an assistant at the Kern County fire station in McFarland, was picked to serve as lookout at the Jasmine station.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list marks the site as gone and shows its location as undetermined.[^2]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public.
