Molino is a Lincoln County, Tennessee, lookout site at about 978 feet above sea level.[^1] Tom Dunigan's statewide list gives the tower the number 5-14, a district-tower number from a 1976 list, and marks it removed.[^1] The table shows the former location of towers as well as standing ones, so the entry marks where the tower stood.[^1] The list draws most of its data from USGS topographic maps and county maps.[^1]

The Tennessee Division of Forestry was responsible for building 208 fire lookout towers, from about 1930 to 1970, according to its National Register listing.[^2] That listing covers the division's towers from 1933 to 1975.[^2]

No source I found describes the Molino site today. Dunigan's page notes that most tower locations in the state are now private property.[^1]

[^1]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Tennessee Division of Forestry Fire Lookout Towers, 1933 to 1975 (National Register multiple property listing), National Park Service NPGallery, https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/GetAsset/NRHP/64501237_text (accessed 2026-10-21).
