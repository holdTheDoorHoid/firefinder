Sugarloaf is a former lookout site in Tuolumne County, California, at about 37.898 N and 120.146 W.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list gives its type as "Encl. Tower" (an enclosed tower) and marks it as gone.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page for Tuolumne County includes Sugarloaf in its list of county lookouts.[^2] The peakbagging table of California lookouts gives the site an elevation of 3,881 feet and labels it "Sugarloaf (LO site)."[^3]

In that table's key, "LO site" means the lookout building has been taken away. The key also says that no trace of the structure may remain, and that the site's present status could be unclear.[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, stands at the site now, or whether the ground is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
