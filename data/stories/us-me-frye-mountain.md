The Frye Mountain lookout stood in Waldo County, Maine, near the town of Montville.[^1] It was a tower that had been taken apart and put back up on the mountain.[^2] In the fall of 1931 the state erected five new steel lookout stations, and it also re-erected a dismantled tower on Frye Mountain.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page quotes the forest commissioner's report for that year, but it gives no height or design details for the tower.[^2]

Wikipedia's article on the wildlife area says the tower was manned from 1931 to 1991 by observers trained to detect fires.[^4] We found no names of watchmen and no record of a particular fire the tower reported.

The sources disagree on when the tower was moved. Forest Lookouts says it was removed around 2002, then moved to Swan Island in 2005 and reassembled there.[^2] The firetower.org register says it was disassembled and moved in 2002, and the Wikipedia article gives the same move year.[^1][^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine list marks the Frye Mountain site as having no tower, and it shows the relocated tower as standing.[^3]

The original hilltop is now part of the Gene Letourneau Wildlife Management Area, which was renamed in 1985.[^4] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains on the summit, such as footings.

Swan Island, at the head of Merrymeeting Bay, is a state wildlife management area that welcomes anglers, bird-watchers, campers and hikers, among others.[^5] The state page also describes a pond for fishing and a tower for watching wildlife. It does not say that either one is the former Frye lookout, so we could not confirm that link.[^5] We found no source on whether the relocated tower can be visited or climbed, so check with the state before going near it.
