A tower named The Fire Lookout Tower appears on OpenStreetMap, the free map that volunteers build, at a spot in southwest Florida.[^1] The map entry describes a lattice-style observation tower with a start date of 2024, and it is tagged as private.[^1]

I could find no other record of the structure: no owner, no photo, no staff list and no news report. Florida's older manned fire towers are a separate story. A 2010 news report about Lake County said the state Division of Forestry was phasing its towers out, partly because residents now report fires by cell phone and more airplanes are in use.[^2] Nothing I found ties this structure to that program.

Because the tower is tagged private, treat it as off limits. Do not visit or climb it without the owner's permission.[^1]
