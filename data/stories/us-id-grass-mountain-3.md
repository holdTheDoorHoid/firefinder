Grass Mountain sits in Idaho County, in the Payette National Forest, at 6,262 feet.[^1][^2]

A tent camp is listed on the site in the 1920s.[^3] By 1934 the site had an L-4 cab, with a patrol point about three miles north at 6,090 feet.[^1][^2][^3] The site lies about 12 miles east of Chamberlain Guard Station.[^2]

The lookout was destroyed in 1978.[^1][^2][^3] The three pages agree on those dates. Today none of the sources I checked describes what remains on the mountain, or whether the ground is open to visitors. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page labels the site as hike-in.[^1]

Another Grass Mountain, a lookout in the Kaniksu National Forest in northern Idaho, was destroyed in 1955 and is listed separately.[^4]
