The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia towers includes a tower in Wilkes County, numbered GA420 and named "wilkes" in the list.[^1] It gives the elevation as 605, which we read as feet, about 184 m.[^1] The name is the list author's own label, since the list says names for towers numbered 73 and above are its creation.[^1]

The list's only note on this tower reads "Ron 1953 topo".[^1] The list does not say what that note means.[^1]

The list's map shows towers from the USGS name database (numbers 1 to 72) and towers found on 1:100,000 USGS topo maps and NGS data sheets. The list also says the Georgia Forestry Commission supplied coordinates for 118 towers, and that an older GFC list gives locations for 203 towers.[^1]

We found no source that describes the site today, so what remains on the hill, if anything, is unknown. Whether the site is open to the public is also unknown.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
