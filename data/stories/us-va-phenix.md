The Phenix Fire Tower stands in Charlotte County, Virginia, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as standing.[^1][^3] The association marks it with an asterisk, which it uses for towers whose structure and support remain but whose cab is no longer in place.[^1] So the structure is still there, but the cab that once sat at the top is gone.

Only one of the sources has anything about the tower's past. The National Historic Lookout Register's entry for the Crafton Gate tower says the Phenix Fire Tower, like Crafton Gate, began as a lookout tied to Charlotte County's timber business.[^2] That entry gives no date for Phenix, so its construction date, builder and height remain unknown.

TopoQuest says the tower appears on a USGS 1:24,000 topographic map and gives its elevation as 564 feet.[^3] The sources I read do not describe public access or say whether the tower can be climbed. Until its owner has said it is open, treat the tower as off limits.

[^2]: Crafton Gate Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/va/crafton-gate-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^3]: Phenix Lookout Tower, VA, TopoQuest, https://topoquest.com/place/virginia/tower/phenix-lookout-tower/1491215 (accessed 2026-10-09).
