The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list places a Capron tower in Southampton County, Virginia, and marks that site as gone.[^1] A 2010 map of the Capron area, shown on the Pastmaps site, marks the Capron Lookout Tower near Kindred Road.[^3]

The same list has a separate entry for a tower relocated from Capron to the Southampton Ag & Forestry Center, which it shows as standing.[^1][^2] It gives the new point as 36.71223, -77.05640.[^1]

The list's Ag & Forestry Center may be the Southampton Agriculture & Forestry Museum at Heritage Village, 26135 Heritage Lane, in Courtland.[^4] The museum page we read does not mention a tower, so the match is not confirmed.[^4]

No source we read describes what remains at the original Capron site today, or whether that land is open to visitors. Ask the museum before you go, and do not climb any tower without permission from its owner.
