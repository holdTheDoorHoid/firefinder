The Hanson Fire Tower stands on Bonney Hill.[^1] Wikipedia lists Bonney Hill as the town's highest point, at 152 feet.[^3] The register places the tower in the Town of Hanson, though its description says it is near Plymouth.[^1]

The register describes the first tower here as a 48-foot steel structure.[^1] It attributes the move to rapid tree growth: in 1920 that tower went to Pelham, and a 68-foot tower replaced it.[^1] A State Forester report for 1920, dated January 1921, describes the same move but gives the tower as 50 feet tall.[^2] The 1921 date probably comes from that report, so the sources most likely describe one move, with the height in dispute.[^2]

The present tower is a 70-foot steel structure with a 10-by-10-foot cab, built in 1969.[^1] The register records its registration on December 15, 2004.[^1] The Massachusetts Bureau of Forest Fire Control administers it.[^1]

I found no current access, staffing or condition information for this tower in the sources I read. Check with the town or the state before going.
