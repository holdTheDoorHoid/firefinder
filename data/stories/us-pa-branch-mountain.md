The Branch Mountain Tower Site is in Union County, Pennsylvania.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it as US 219, PA 48.[^1] Henry Isenberg nominated it, and the register gives September 16, 2008 as its registration date.[^1]

The register calls it a "bald tower" and says it was used around 1917.[^1] It also says the tower is no longer standing.[^1]

TopoQuest, a topographic map site, describes Branch Mountain as a summit in Union County at 2,135 feet.[^2] It places the summit on the USGS 1:24K topographic map for Hartleton, Pennsylvania.[^2] TopoQuest describes the mountain itself, not a tower on it.[^2]

Today, we found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the Branch Mountain site. We also found nothing that says whether the summit is public land or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Branch Mountain Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/pa/branch-mountain-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Branch Mountain, TopoQuest, https://topoquest.com/place-detail.php?id=1170153 (accessed 2026-10-10).
