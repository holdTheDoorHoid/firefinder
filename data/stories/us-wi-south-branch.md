South Branch was a fire lookout on the Menominee Indian Reservation, in Menominee County, Wisconsin.[^1] A U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey description dated 1934 places a survey station at the center of the South Branch fire lookout tower.[^1] That description places the tower about 13 miles southwest of Mountain and about 7 miles west-northwest of Hayes, and describes it as a square steel frame topped by a closed cabin.[^1]

Its status is recorded as removed on the Central US Lookouts page, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a tower that is gone.[^1][^2] The only description of what remains is a geocaching recovery note from 2003, which reports only footings.[^1] The National Geodetic Survey datasheet for the station's survey mark, QM0634, carries condition code XX, and the listing's key defines code X as a surface mark reported destroyed.[^3]

No source describes the site after that 2003 note, so its present state is unverified.
