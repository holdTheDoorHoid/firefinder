The Maxton fire tower stood on Drummond Island in Chippewa County, Michigan, in the Lake Superior State Forest.[^1] It was a 100-foot tower near Maxton, about five miles north of the island's existing tower.[^1]

In June 1936, newspapers reported that the tower was being erected with the help of CCC crews.[^1] One item described it as a stairway ladder type and another as a steel tower, and both gave the height as 100 feet.[^1] Plans also included a cabin for the towerman, five miles of telephone line and a mile of truck trail.[^1]

A September 1937 newspaper item described a boy not quite two and a half years old climbing the 100-foot Maxton tower without help.[^1] That item shows the tower was standing by then.[^1]

A topographic place-name database still lists a Maxton Lookout Tower in Chippewa County, at 662 feet above sea level.[^2] The entry does not say whether a tower still stands.[^2]

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach the tower.[^1][^2]
