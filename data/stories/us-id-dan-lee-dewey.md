Dan Lee (Dewey) was a lookout on the Clearwater National Forest, six miles southeast of Pierce in Clearwater County, Idaho.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the lookout in its St. Joe - Clearwater group.[^2] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 5,005 feet.[^1] The structure was an L-4 cab, dated to 1941.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts calls the 1941 structure an L-4 tower instead, so the sources disagree on its form.[^1][^2][^3]

All three sources date its destruction to 1952.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Service list was compiled in June 2016 and draws on Forest Service records, among other material.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today. Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site drive-up, but it does not say whether anything is left there or whether the public can visit it now.[^2]
