Little Mountain is a rocky hill just south of L'Anse in Baraga County, Michigan.[^1][^3] In January 1921 a state district forest fire warden from Marquette went to look at it, near a camp called Knauf's, as a possible site for a fire tower.[^1] The plan called for a steel tower 65 feet high, three miles south of L'Anse, that would give a watcher a wide view across the country.[^1]

The notice then said the tower would be built on Froberg Hill in Baraga Township.[^1] A separate page on the same site quotes a May 1921 item from the L'Anse Sentinel. It describes a seventy-foot fire tower being put up on Froberg Hill by the public domain commission.[^2] The two notices differ on height, 65 feet against 70, and the January notice first looked at Little Mountain before naming Froberg Hill.[^1][^2] Neither says the tower was finished.[^1][^2]

Today the hill is a hiking spot.[^3] A trip report gives the route: about three miles south of L'Anse on US-41, then a westward turn onto Golf Course Road, which ends at a dirt lot.[^3] The marked trail takes about ten minutes of climbing to reach a rocky clearing on top.[^3] The writer noticed scattered conifers and many dead trunks, and did not mention a tower on top.[^3] No source here says whether anything of a tower remains.[^1][^2][^3]
