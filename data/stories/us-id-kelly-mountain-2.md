Kelly Mountain Lookout stood on Kelly Mountain in Idaho County, about ten miles east of Riggins, at an elevation of 4,737 feet.[^1][^2][^3] The site lay within the Nez Perce National Forest.[^2]

The site was first used in 1930, when a 100-foot tree platform and a log cabin stood there.[^1][^2] In 1938 a 40-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab went up.[^1][^2]

Pete Pomeroy set his book *The Mallory Burn* at the lookout.[^1][^2] The book was later made into a Wonderful World of Disney television special titled *Fire on Kelly Mountain*.[^1][^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1958.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as drive-up.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or says whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

A different Kelly Mountain, in Kootenai County, appears on the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list at separate coordinates, so the two should not be confused.[^4]
