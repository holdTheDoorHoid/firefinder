The Cooks Mountain lookout went up in 1931: a 10-foot pole tower topped with an L-4 cab, on a peak in Pend Oreille County.[^1][^2] FireLookout.com places it about six miles northeast of Usk.[^3]

The sources do not agree on how the tower ended, and none gives a date.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The register says it is not clear when the tower was removed.[^1] FireLookout.com and Willhite say it was destroyed, with no date given.[^3][^2] A lookout history site lists it as removed.[^4]

Willhite, who hiked up the north ridge, found footings on the very top.[^2] He notes there is no gate on the north approach from road NF-5015, and says the road is in fair shape to the saddle north of the peak.[^2] He lists a hiking distance of 0.4 miles and an elevation gain of 650 feet.[^2] The site is on Forest Service land.[^2] Willhite's page describes footings on the summit.[^2] None of the four pages describes a standing tower.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
