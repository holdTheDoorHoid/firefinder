State Line Lookout stood in Shoshone County, Idaho, on the St. Joe National Forest, about 20 miles east of Avery.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 6,250 feet.[^1]

It was an L-4 cab on a spur about three-quarters of a mile south-southeast of Ward Peak, and it dates to the 1930s.[^1][^2] Neither page gives an exact year for that.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com credits its photograph to Alvin Friedland.[^1]

Both Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts say the cab was destroyed in the 1960s.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it with the type "Ground" and the status "Gone."[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left at the site, or whether it is open to visitors. Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the lookout "Hike-In Destroyed."[^2]
