Clark Peak is a forested summit of about 9,000 feet in the Pinaleño Mountains, southwest of Safford in Graham County, Arizona.[^1][^3] It sits in the Coronado National Forest, and a fire lookout once stood at the summit.[^1]

The Arizona lookout table at Peakbagging.com lists the summit as "Clark Peak (LO site)" on a 1972 Webb Peak map.[^2] In that table's key, the label can mean the building has been taken down, possibly leaving no evidence, or that the site's status is unknown.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^3]

The Mountain Field Guide notes that newer LiDAR readings put the summit at about 9,010.9 feet, while older maps give about 9,006.[^1]

The guide names Forest Road 803 and then Clark Peak Trail #301 as the approach, with a brief summit spur at the end.[^1] It also says some traces of the lookout's past survive near the top.[^1] No source I read describes what those traces are or how much is left.
