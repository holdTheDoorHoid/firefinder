The Nickerson lookout is an 86-foot steel tower in Pine County, Minnesota.[^1] The Central US Lookouts site dates its erection to 1935.[^1] A photo caption on Wikimedia Commons gives the same year and places the tower at the Nickerson Forestry Station.[^2]

A 1945 item from the Askov American, quoted on the Central US Lookouts page, describes the tower as standing on the highest ground between the Twin Cities and Duluth.[^1] That same item says ten women were taken on for lookout work that season because labor was short.[^1] One of them, Ada Noeldner of Bruno, accepted the post while she awaited the homecoming of her husband Harold, then serving in Germany with the Ninth Army.[^1] Her shifts ran eight hours a day, spent scanning far-off country for anything suspicious.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list shows the tower as standing.[^3] Wikidata gives its elevation as 1,201 feet, about 366 meters.[^4]

The sources I could reach do not describe current access, opening hours, staffing, or any restoration or rental. Check with the managing agency before planning a visit, and do not climb a tower that is posted as closed.
