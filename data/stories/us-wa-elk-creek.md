Elk Creek is a former fire lookout on the Quinault Indian Reservation, in Jefferson County, Washington, about a mile northeast of Queets.[^1][^2] The Quinault Tribe administers the site.[^1] The lookout was a steel Aermotor tower, 60 feet high, with a metal cab measuring 7 by 7 feet, plus a rectangular wooden ground cabin beside it.[^1][^3] The Register and WillhiteWeb date it to the 1940s.[^1][^3] Fire Lookout gives only "the 1940," which may mean the decade or the single year 1940.[^2]

We found no source that names a lookout or a fire the tower watched. A WillhiteWeb photo caption shows the lookout and cabin in 1955.[^3]

The tower and cabin were abandoned in 1962.[^1][^2][^3] The Register says both are now removed, but its own photographs from July 2000 show the tower knocked over.[^1] Fire Lookout says ruins are still present.[^2] WillhiteWeb, which mentions logging around 2016, says the tower is still on the ground and the small cabin looks to be gone.[^3]

The site has good gravel road access, but it is on tribal land.[^3] WillhiteWeb says you should have the Quinault Tribe's permission, and that the access roads carry no-trespassing signs.[^3] Please do not go onto the site without that permission. WillhiteWeb also expects new growth to screen the structure from view later.[^3]
