Lookout Mountain stands in El Dorado County, in the Eldorado National Forest.[^1] The fire lookout index lists it as a tower and marks it gone.[^2]

The earliest record we found is from May 1913. The Mountain Democrat reported that the first fire guard in the El Dorado National Forest had started work on the mountain, while men were fixing telephone lines for the fire season.[^1]

In June 1915 the same paper said O. R. Murdock of Georgetown would be the season's lookout, and that he had already checked in on the mountain.[^1]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go up there.[^2]

[^1]: Lookout Mountain, Forest Lookouts, https://californialookouts.weebly.com/lookout-mountain1.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: California fire lookouts index, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/ca/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
