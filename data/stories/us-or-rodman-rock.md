Rodman Rock is a summit in Lake County, Oregon, on the Fremont National Forest.[^1] The register gives its elevation as 8,196 feet, while firelookout.com lists 6,754 feet.[^1][^2]

A lookout went up on the summit in 1933: an L-4 cab on a 16-foot trestle-type tower.[^1][^2][^3] Newspaper items name Mrs. Earl Small as the lookout assigned there in 1943, and a July 1961 item says Dave Ebert had held the post the previous summer.[^3]

In 1967 the Silver Lake district asked to dispose of the building. Its survey of unused lookouts rated the condition poor and advised burning it once the late-autumn rains and snow arrived.[^3]

A May 1968 letter from the Fremont forest to the regional office reported that Rodman Rock had been burned.[^3] The register says the tower was removed that year, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed in 1968.[^1][^2]

The register includes a 2007 photograph of the site, but its caption does not describe what is there.[^1] No source describes what remains on the summit today. A 1953 survey note says forest roads from Silver Lake reached within a five-minute walk of the survey station on the summit, but no source says whether that is still possible.[^3]
