The Hilda fire tower is in Jackson County, Mississippi, and it is listed under the Mississippi Forest Service.[^1]

In May 1940 the tower was caught up in the early tests of the Aircraft Warning Service.[^1] A Chronicle-Star report, reprinted on a lookout-history site, says the first test message went out from Pascagoula after efforts to link the Hilda tower to the central telephone service at Wiggins failed.[^1]

The tower was also named as a seedling pickup point.[^1] In December 1961 an area forester announced that tree seedlings would reach Jackson County, and landowners could collect them at the Hilda Fire Tower, four miles north of Gautier, between 1 and 4 p.m.[^1]

No source I could read describes what remains at the site today.
