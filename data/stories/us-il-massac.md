Massac was a timber tower with a live-in cab, completed in 1940 in the Shawnee National Forest.[^1][^2] Towers for the Massac, Atwood Ridge and Trigg sites were bought in the second half of fiscal 1938, for construction in fiscal 1939.[^3] Bob Frakes, a lookout historian, says the cab was large enough for someone to stay overnight.[^2]

Sources disagree on the county. The Forest Lookouts page heads its entry Massac County, while the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site in Pope County.[^1][^4]

The article says almost all Illinois towers were taken down or sold in the early 1970s.[^2] Its passage on disassembled towers names the 1940 Massac and Atwood towers.[^2] Frakes says three Illinois towers still stand, and Massac is not one of them.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Massac site as gone.[^4] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the ground is open to visitors today.
