Benham Spur was a lookout in Harlan County, Kentucky, and the Forest Lookouts listing marks the tower as removed.[^1]

The lookout shares its name with Benham Spur, a ridge near the town of Benham.[^1][^2] A town history page that quotes the Kentucky Atlas and Gazetteer says the ridge, which is part of Black Mountain, gave the town its name. The same quoted account dates the town to 1911 and credits the Wisconsin Steel Corporation, which was part of International Harvester, with building it.[^2] The town sits in Harlan County, which is also the county the lookout list gives for the tower.[^1][^2]

We found no source that describes the site today, what might stand there, or whether visitors can reach it.
