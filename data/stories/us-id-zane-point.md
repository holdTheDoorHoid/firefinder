Zane Point is a crow's nest on the St. Joe National Forest, in Benewah County, Idaho, about 13 miles east of St. Maries.[^1] It is listed at 3,000 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places it at 47.30290, -116.28170.[^3]

The sources date the crow's nest only to the 1930s.[^1][^2] It was abandoned as a lookout, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list gives the same status.[^2][^3] That list gives the structure type as crowsnest.[^3]

Firelookout.com says the crow's nest is still there, though that note is undated.[^1] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page and the Forest Fire Lookout Association list give its status only as abandoned.[^2][^3] We found no recent description of the site itself.

Idaho Fire Lookouts classes it as hike-in, so reaching it means walking in.[^2]
