Hornet Point Lookout stood in the Clearwater National Forest in Clearwater County, Idaho, about 10 miles north of the Kelly Creek ranger station.[^1] The Forest Service list places it in Section 28, Township 41 North.[^3] The site sits at 5,974 feet.[^1]

The lookout was a pole L-4 tower, built in 1934.[^1][^3] The Forest Service list draws on Ray Kresek's book, Fire Lookouts of the Northwest, and on Forest Service records.[^3]

Firelookout.com says the tower was destroyed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association index lists it as gone, and Idaho Fire Lookouts gives its outcome as destroyed.[^4][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under St. Joe - Clearwater and tags it Drive-Up.[^2] No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site now.
