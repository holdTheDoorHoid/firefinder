A Georgia tower list places a site called "doogan mtn" in Murray County, with an elevation of 2018.[^1] The list gives no unit, which we read as feet.[^1]

A mountain-bike photo, dated May 13, 2018 and located near Chatsworth, carries a title that says the fire tower is long gone.[^2]

The Murray County tower on the National Historic Lookout Register is at a different site. It is on Fort Mountain State Park, about 23 km south of the list's Doogan point.[^3][^1] According to the register, the frame is still there, and the cab is gone.[^3]

No source we read describes the Doogan site today beyond that 2018 title, and none says whether visitors may go there.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, Tom Dunigan, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: The view from Doogan Lookout - sadly the fire tower is long gone, MTB Project, https://www.mtbproject.com/photo/7026083/the-view-from-doogan-lookout-sadly-the-fire-tower-is-long-gone (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Murray Fire Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ga/murray-fire-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
