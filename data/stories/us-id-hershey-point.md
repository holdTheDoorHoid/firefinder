Hershey Point sits in the Payette National Forest, in Idaho County, Idaho.[^1][^5] It is about 12 miles southeast of Riggins.[^2]

The first lookout here was an L-4 ground cabin, built around 1932.[^1][^2][^3] In 1966 the site got a newer structure, a Region 6 flat-top cab on a 10-foot base.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com describes that cab as live-in and says it sits on a 10-foot concrete base.[^2] The sources do not say what happened to the 1932 cabin.

Idaho Fire Lookouts keeps a list of known staff that starts in 1956.[^3] Its July 17, 2025 post tags the site as Hike-In Staffed.[^3] Firelookout.com says the cab is staffed in the summer.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register records the lookout as registered on November 29, 2020.[^1] A 2024 McCall Life magazine feature describes a 10-by-10-foot lookout and a hike that climbs 1,600 feet to reach it.[^4]

Because the site is tagged as hike-in, it is reached on foot rather than by road.[^3] I could not find a rental listing or visitor rules for it. Check with the Payette National Forest before going, since it is a working, staffed site.
