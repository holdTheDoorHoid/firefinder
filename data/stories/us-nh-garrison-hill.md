Garrison Hill Park is north of downtown Dover, New Hampshire.[^1] Its tower is an observation tower, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it that way.[^4] Whether the tower ever served as a fire lookout is still debated.[^3]

In 1880 Joseph Ham and Harrison Haley bought the hilltop and developed it as a public park.[^1] A 65-foot wooden observatory stood there, and visitors could look at Mount Washington through a telescope.[^1] The sources differ on whether Haley built the observatory or bought it.[^1][^2]

In 1888 the city took ownership and kept the site open as a park.[^1] The wooden observatory burned in 1911.[^1][^2] In 1913 Abby Sawyer had a steel tower built to honor her husband.[^2] The park was added to the National Register of Historic Places on September 11, 1987.[^1]

The steel tower came down in 1990 for safety reasons.[^1][^2][^3] Volunteers built the present tower, which Wikipedia dates to 1993.[^1][^3] It is a 76-foot iron observatory painted green.[^1]

Today the road ends at a parking area near the summit, and a footpath leads from there to the tower.[^1][^3] From the deck, the view runs over the trees of Dover.[^3] According to the NH Family Hikes guide, the park still does what it was set up to do.[^3]
