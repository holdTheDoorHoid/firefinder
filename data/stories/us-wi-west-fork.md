The West Fork Lookout Tower stands near Round Lake in Sawyer County, Wisconsin, on national forest land.[^1][^2] The state's property record gives 1936 as its year built and describes a steel-framed observation and fire tower that is 100 feet tall.[^2] The Forest Lookouts site gives the same year and height, and says the steel tower went up that year.[^1]

The state record also lists other buildings on the site, though it does not name them, and says the structure was surveyed in 1985 and again in 2022.[^2] We found no record of who staffed the tower or which fires it watched, and no record of whether it is still used as a lookout.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Wisconsin towers marks West Fork as standing.[^3] The state record says it has not been demolished.[^2] We found no statement about public access, climbing or rental. Anyone planning a visit should check with the national forest first, and should not climb the tower unless it is confirmed open.
