Bateman Mountain's lookout stood on the east edge of Bateman Butte in Douglas County, Oregon, at about 2,320 feet.[^1][^2][^3] The sources disagree on distance: one puts it 21 miles northwest of Roseburg, and a 1953 survey note says about 22 miles.[^2][^3]

A 1932 Oregon Department of Forestry annual report recommended a lookout house there.[^3] A February 1934 newspaper item said the Wolf Creek project had finished 11 of the 15 miles to the site.[^3] In August 1934 a forest supervisor's memo to the regional forester described the house as a ground-level structure resting on rocks, and recorded the job as done.[^3] The register dates the 14-by-14-foot cab to 1933, while Firelookout dates its L-4 cab to 1934, so the build year is unsettled.[^1][^2]

Forrest Emmitt was stationed there in 1936.[^3] A 1937 item says his wife packed supplies to him every two weeks by pack mule, over a twelve-mile trip.[^3] Peggy Meacham was moved to the post from Mt. Scott in 1950, and Leta Petheram took the summer post in 1960.[^3] A 1943 letter says the station was linked by about 20 miles of telephone line to Roseburg, via the Baughman Lookout line, and that the post was reached by five miles of trail.[^3] An Army AWS station there was active from May 10 to October 16, 1943.[^3]

The 1965 entry says the lookout was not staffed that year, was ended after the fire season, and that its house was dismantled.[^3] The register says the cab was removed in 1965. Firelookout says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
