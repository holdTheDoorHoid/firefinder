The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers places a tower called "ringgold" in Catoosa County, at about 34.919 N, 85.098 W.[^1] The list says that names for towers 73 and above are the author's own creation, and this entry, GA091, falls in that range. The name is a label on the list, not a record of an old lookout name.[^1]

Two codes sit beside the entry. The first, BLL-41143, marks a tower whose coordinates the Georgia Forestry Commission supplied; the list counts 118 such towers. The second, gfc-708, is the tower's number on an older Commission list that described 203 towers.[^1]

The list gives no build date, design, height, status or access for the tower. It says nothing about what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public.[^1]

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
