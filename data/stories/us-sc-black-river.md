Black River is listed as a South Carolina Forestry Commission lookout in Sumter County.[^1] The commission's first fire tower went up near Camden in August 1930.[^2] The network later grew past 130 towers, and for 65 years the towers were used to watch for wildfires.[^3]

Most tower sites covered ten acres and held the tower and a year-round house for the operator.[^2] From the 1960s onward, most of the state's operators were women, and their husbands often held firefighting jobs.[^2]

The commission closed its fire tower system on October 1, 1993.[^2] A Post and Courier article says the system was decommissioned in 1994.[^3] Another Post and Courier article says more than 100 towers around the state closed as the commission moved into a new era.[^4] No source I found names Black River in those closures.

The same article reports that only 30 towers remain standing statewide, and that some are used for radio antennas and other communication equipment.[^4]

No source I found gives a build date for the Black River tower, describes what remains at the site today, or says whether the public may visit it.
