Paulina Peak is the high point on the rim of the Newberry Caldera, in the Deschutes National Forest in Deschutes County, Oregon.[^1][^4]

The Forest Lookouts site says the Forest Service erected a 12-by-18-foot cabin on the summit in the summer of 1915.[^2] The register says the lookout cab that came later was preceded by a cabin dated 1917, while firelookout.com dates the earlier cabin to 1915.[^1][^3] The cab most sources describe is an L-4 design.[^1][^2][^3] The register and firelookout.com date it to 1932, while Forest Lookouts describes a 14-by-14-foot hip-roof ground cabin dated 1931.[^1][^2][^3]

The summit was staffed from the early years.[^2] A Bend newspaper item quoted on the Forest Lookouts site reports that Chas. McNeff took over from G.T. Parker in September 1914.[^2] In 1918 a newspaper item named Mrs. Cora Leland of La Pine as the first woman lookout in Oregon, and possibly the first in the country.[^2] Virginia Barry took up the post in 1920.[^2] Sylvia Andrews is listed as lookout for 1952 and 1953,[^3] and a 1954 item names her again.[^2]

In 1964 a new R-6 cab with a flat roof was built on a short tower.[^2] The register and firelookout.com say the L-4 cab was removed or destroyed in 1968.[^1][^3] Forest Lookouts records that in 1969 the structure was recommended for surplus status, with a suggestion that the building be sold so it could be taken away.[^2] The Oregon Hikers guide gives both 1917 to 1968 and 1914 to 1969 in different passages.[^4]

Today the hike starts at Paulina Lake, and the summit area can be driven to in spring and fall.[^4] The guide describes a parking area just below the summit, where restrooms and information signs sit near the overlook.[^4] In summer, cars cannot go all the way up to the summit, and the guide mentions a shuttle from the visitor center.[^4] The guide adds that the summit road dates from 1958, when a U.S. Air Force radar station was set up there, and that the station has since been taken away.[^4] No source we read says whether any part of the lookout remains.
