Tohatchi Lookout stands at 8,827 feet in McKinley County, New Mexico, on the Navajo Indian Reservation.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register says it was built in 1928 and is the oldest lookout on the reservation.[^1] Its tower is a 62-foot Aermotor with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab.[^1] The register notes three active lookouts on the New Mexico side of the reservation.[^1]

The register names the Navajo Indian Reservation as administrator and the Bureau of Indian Affairs as a cooperator.[^1] The site was registered on July 20, 2002, with Dave Lorenz as nominator.[^1] A Forest Lookouts page places it in the Navajo Indian Agency area, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association list shows the tower as standing.[^2][^3]

Beyond the register entry, the public record on staffing and visits is thin. Anyone planning a visit should ask the Bureau of Indian Affairs first, since the register lists it as a cooperator.[^1]
