Jasper Cliff is a former fire lookout site in Lee County, Virginia, at 3,271 feet (997 meters), within the George Washington and Jefferson National Forests.[^1]

A Kingsport Times-News item dated March 19, 1950, reported that preliminary work was starting on a fire tower there.[^2] It added that a 30-foot steel structure was to take the place of a shack the Forest Service had used for a while.[^2]

According to the register, the Forest Service put up a steel tower 30 feet tall, with a 14-by-14-foot wooden cab, in 1950.[^1] That tower took the place of an earlier cabin, which the register dates to 1938 and says was made from salvaged windows and scrap lumber from CCC buildings.[^1]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register listed the site on April 10, 2011, as US 922, VA 8, on the nomination of Ron Stafford.[^1] The tower is no longer there.[^1][^2] No source we read describes what remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it today.[^1][^2]
