Lookout Mountain is a summit on the Keating Summit topographic quadrangle in Potter County.[^1] The sources disagree about when a tower first stood on it. A 1917 patrol summary lists the spot in the Austin Forest as bare, with nothing built on it.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register, by contrast, says the bald tower was used around 1917.[^3]

In April 1920 the Potter Enterprise reported a forest fire burning near Keating Summit, spotted from the Lookout Mountain tower over twenty-five miles off.[^2] The same report said Gifford Pinchot, the state's new chief forester, cited the sighting as an example of why many more towers were needed for fire protection on state forests.[^2]

The register says the tower is no longer standing.[^3] The site was entered in the Former Fire Lookout Sites register on September 16, 2008.[^3]

Today, no source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the summit, or whether it is open to visitors. A summit listing does place Lookout Mountain on the Keating Summit quadrangle.[^1]
