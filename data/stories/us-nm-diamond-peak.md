The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower at Diamond Peak in Sierra County, New Mexico, at latitude 33.22938 and longitude -107.8446.[^1] The list marks it as gone.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site places Diamond Peak in the Gila National Forest.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the peak, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: New Mexico Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Diamond Peak, Forest Lookouts, https://westlookouts.weebly.com/diamond-peak.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
