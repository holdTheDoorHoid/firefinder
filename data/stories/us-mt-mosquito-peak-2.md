Mosquito Peak is in Missoula County, in the Lolo National Forest.[^1] Firelookout.com calls the 1935 structure a crow's nest, places it 12 miles north of Missoula, and says it was abandoned in 1941.[^1]

In August 1935 The Missoulian, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, reported that the Forest Service was readying a strategic point for the height of fire season.[^2] George Foley was sent to the new Mosquito Peak lookout station in the Rattlesnake valley, 16 miles north of Missoula, the paper said.[^2] His beat ran between Mosquito Peak and Mount Stuart.[^2] The paper also says a stopgap lookout had been set up there.[^2]

The two sources disagree on the distance: 12 miles on firelookout.com, 16 miles in the 1935 newspaper.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks the site as an abandoned crow's nest.[^3] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.
