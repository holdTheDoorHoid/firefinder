Cuba Mesa is a former lookout site in Sandoval County, New Mexico, at 36.03261, -107.00620.[^1] The association's New Mexico table mixes lookout sites with non-lookout structures. Its entry gives the type as a trailer and the status as gone.[^1]

No source I checked describes what remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to the public. Anyone planning a visit should check with the land manager first.

[^1]: New Mexico Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
