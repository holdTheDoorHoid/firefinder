Badger Butte was a fire lookout in Grant County, Oregon, in the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest.[^1][^2][^4] The site lies about 10 miles northeast of Granite, at an elevation of 6,985 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists its coordinates as 44.93077, -118.30602, and the Forest Lookouts page gives its legal location as 7S-36E-28.[^2][^4]

The lookout directory dates it only to the 1930s.[^1] It lists the lookout as abandoned.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list gives its type as unknown and its status as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the butte today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

A different Badger Butte, in the Mount Hood region of Hood River County, Oregon, has its own lookout record. It is not the Grant County site.[^3]
