Angel Butte Lookout is in Clearwater County, Idaho, about 12 miles northeast of Orofino, at an elevation of 3,810 feet.[^1]

The Clearwater Timber Protective Association set the location up in the 1920s, according to the Idaho Fire Lookouts site.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association also dates it to the 1920s, but says its history is unknown and that the site was abandoned.[^1]

Neither source says what the site held.[^1][^2] The Idaho page asks whether it was a camp, a lookout or a cabin.[^2]

The association that set it up was one of two separate bodies, formed independently in 1905, that operated apart until they merged in 1966.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the site as gone and gives its type as unknown.[^4] The Idaho site calls it a hike-in.[^2] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains on the site today.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
