Quadna Fire Tower stands on Quadna Mountain near Hill City, in Aitkin County, Minnesota.[^1][^5] The county survey, which lists the owner as Mn MFS, calls it Tower 42.[^1] The survey describes it as an 81-foot Aermotor tower of ladder construction, put up in 1929.[^1] We found no conflicting build date.

Quadna Mountain was once a downhill ski area.[^4] A lodge on the mountain opened in 1964 and was described as a premier ski lodge.[^3] The ski area is no longer operating.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^2] A tower-history page places the tower on top of an old ski hill, where it is used as a mast for communication antennas.[^4] The sources we read name no one who staffed the tower and no fire it watched.

Today the Quadna Mountain Townhouse Association describes the mountain as a townhouse property with 93 units.[^5] The sources do not say who owns the tower now or whether visitors are allowed. According to the county survey, reaching the tower means a fair hike on an access road.[^1] Before you go, ask the townhouse association or Aitkin County, and do not climb the tower.
