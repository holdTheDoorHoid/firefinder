Warland Ranger Station Point is a former lookout site in the Kootenai National Forest, in Lincoln County, Montana.[^1][^3] Its name refers to the Warland Ranger Station.[^1] Firelookout.com places the site about six miles north of Libby Dam, at an elevation of 2,800 feet.[^1]

The only dated entry we found is from 1940. Firelookout.com says material for a 60-foot pole tower was on site that year, but the tower was never built.[^1] The same entry notes a 6-by-6-foot frame cab that was still there.[^1] The page calls the site abandoned.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the point as a ground lookout and marks it gone.[^2] The two sources use different words for its status.[^1][^2]

Forest Lookouts lists the point under the Kootenai National Forest and gives no further detail.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
