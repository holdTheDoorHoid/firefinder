Keller, a small community in the southern part of Bryan County, lies east of Richmond Hill.[^1] A Georgia tower list places a Keller lookout in the county at 31.84227, -81.25398, and gives 18 in its elevation column, with no unit stated.[^2]

The entry carries three labels. BLL-64696 points to coordinates the Georgia Forestry Commission supplied, according to the list's legend.[^2] The legend also describes an older GFC list of 203 towers, and this entry's gfc-135 is a number from that list.[^2] The usgs tag matches the legend's note that the map shows towers listed by the USGS name database.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the Keller lookout site today, and none says whether the spot is open to visitors.
