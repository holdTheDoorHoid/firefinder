Cottonwood Butte Lookout was a state fire lookout on a summit about 20 miles northwest of Grangeville, in Idaho County. The Idaho Department of Lands is listed as its agency.[^1][^2]

A frame cabin built in 1952 was meant for radio relay and fire detection in the Craig Mountain Fire District, which was headquartered at Craigmont.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

A Lewiston couple was to run the station, according to a 1952 newspaper report.[^3] Willhite lists them as the first lookouts in the cabin.[^4] The sources agree the lookout was still staffed in 1989.[^1][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts has no documented staffing history for the site.[^2]

By 1955 a Highway Department radio station also stood on the summit, and a newspaper said the Air Force would begin a communications installation there that August.[^3] Willhite says the Air Force put one in that year.[^4] A new metal roof went on the lookout in 1966.[^3][^4]

The lookout was gone by 1994.[^1][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists that end as "Gone / Destroyed," while firelookout.com only says gone.[^1][^2] The lookout index marks the site as a ground site that is gone.[^5]

Idaho Fire Lookouts describes the site as drive-up, and Willhite describes a road to the top.[^2][^4] Willhite also says the views from the top are limited.[^4] No source describes what remains at the summit today.[^4]
