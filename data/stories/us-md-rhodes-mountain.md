Rhodes Mountain, in Cecil County, Maryland, rises about 252 feet above sea level.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Rhodes Mountain tower as gone.[^2] The Forest Lookouts site has a Rhodes page that gives the county, but its text does not describe the tower's history.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, and none says whether it is open to visitors.
