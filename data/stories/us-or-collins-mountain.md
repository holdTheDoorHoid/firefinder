Collins Mountain rises to 3,669 feet in the Umpqua National Forest in Douglas County, Oregon, about 24 miles east of Canyonville.[^1][^2]

The site was used for fire watch well before a cab stood on the summit.[^3] The Oregon Lookouts page says the site was first used in 1914 as an emergency lookout equipped with a compass, and that a lookout was posted in a 50-foot tree around 1925.[^3] The register and firelookout.com instead date a tree platform to 1931 and say it survived into the 1980s.[^1][^2]

A 1932 administrative site record, quoted on the Oregon Lookouts page, described a planned 14-by-14 glass-ribbed house for the lookout. It also said the public could reach the tract only by horse or on foot, six miles from the closest road.[^3] An L-4 lookout house went up in 1933.[^1][^2][^3]

A September 1941 newspaper item, also quoted there, said Chester Morgan had staffed the Collins Butte lookout for the season just ended.[^3]

The sources disagree on what came next. The Oregon Lookouts page dates a new tower and cab to about 1954. The register and firelookout.com date a wooden tower to 1970.[^1][^2][^3] The register and firelookout.com both say the lookout was used for emergencies in the 1970s.[^1][^2]

The register says the lookout is now gone.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, and none records whether the site is open to visitors.
