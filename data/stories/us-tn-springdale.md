The Springdale Lookout stands in Claiborne County, Tennessee.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register lists it as US 991, TN 12. It was registered on March 14, 2014, with Ron Stafford as the nominator.[^1] The Register puts the site at 2,283 feet and names the Tennessee Division of Forestry as the administering agency.[^1] A Tennessee Landforms list places a Springdale tower in Claiborne County at 2,300 feet and marks it as standing.[^2] The two elevations differ by about 17 feet, and none of the sources we read explains the gap.

None of the sources we read gives a build year or describes the tower's design. A National Register study of Tennessee Division of Forestry towers says most of the division's towers went up between the late 1940s and about 1960.[^3] That is a statewide pattern, not a date for this tower.

The Register page has no visit report and no staffing record.[^1] We cannot say who, if anyone, has staffed the tower or which fires it watched.

We found no current condition report, visit notes or public access rule for the tower. Anyone who wants to see it should check with the Division of Forestry first, and should not climb the tower without permission.
