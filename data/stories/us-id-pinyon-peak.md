Pinyon Peak Lookout is a ground cabin on a high summit in the Challis National Forest, about 34 miles west-northwest of Challis.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register dates it to 1930 and classes it as an L-4 groundhouse with a hip roof and a catwalk.[^1] Two other sites call it an R-4 cab, and a third calls it a Region 5 model 4A ground house, so the exact cab type is still in question.[^2][^3][^4]

In 1935 a newspaper item, quoted by the Forest Lookouts site, reported that a new road was being completed toward the station, with only half a mile still to build.[^4] The same site quotes a 2006 notice seeking volunteers to paint and tidy up the historic lookout.[^4] In August 2012 the lookout was evacuated as the Halstead fire advanced.[^4]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site lists the tower as staffed, with named lookouts running from 1985 through 2023.[^3] The Fire Lookout site says summer staffing is possible.[^2]

The register says the lookout has stayed in active service since it was built, and it was added to the register on February 12, 2004.[^1] The Forest Lookout Association's Idaho list still marks it as standing.[^5]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site calls it drive-up.[^3] I found no closure notice for it, but I also found no current visitor information or road report. The 1935 account describes the last four to six miles of the road as a shelf cut into the rock with dynamite, so check conditions with the Forest Service before you drive up.[^4]
