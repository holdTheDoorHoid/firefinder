The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower at Millston in Jackson County, Wisconsin, at 44.16192 north and 90.65797 west, and marks it as gone.[^1] Millston is a town in Jackson County, and the census-designated place of Millston is located within it.[^2]

Wisconsin's fire tower network reached 119 staffed towers at its peak.[^3] The state later moved to retire the network, and replacement costs and modern technology were among the reasons.[^3] The DNR's timeline gives 2016 as the retirement year, while WJFW says 72 towers went out of service in 2015, so the sources differ on the year.[^4][^5]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Millston site today.
