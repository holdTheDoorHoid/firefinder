Harvey Point is a lookout site in Granite County, on the Lolo National Forest.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com lists a 20-foot pole L-4 tower there, built in 1934.[^1] The L-4 was a prefabricated 14-by-14-foot lookout house, built from 1929 on.[^4]

In 1949 the cab was moved to the northeast quarter of section 25, township 10 north, range 5 west, where it became a maintenance cabin.[^1] The Forest Lookouts Granite County list places the Harvey Point entry at township 10 north, range 14 west, section 17.[^2] The two township and range figures appear to describe the tower site and the cab's later site, but the sources do not say so outright.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Harvey Point as a gone tower.[^3]

Sources do not say whether the moved maintenance cabin still stands, and none describes what remains at the tower site. No source describes the site today, so a visitor should not count on finding anything there or on access.
