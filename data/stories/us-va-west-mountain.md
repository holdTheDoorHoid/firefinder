The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list places West Mountain in Rockingham County, at 38.79652, -78.9297.[^1] Its county list gives the type as a tower with a question mark after it, and marks the site as gone.[^2]

Across Virginia, the state forestry office began putting up steel lookout towers in 1928.[^3] A 1991 survey of the department's own properties recorded that it owned a fire tower in each of Virginia's counties.[^3] No source describes what remains at the West Mountain site today, or whether it is open to the public.
