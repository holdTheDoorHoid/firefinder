The Portage Lake tower was a Forest Service lookout in Crawford County, Michigan.[^1] It stood 120 feet (36.6 m) tall and had a pyramidal roof. The survey point used for the tower was the apex of that roof.[^1]

The National Geodetic Survey datasheet lists a first observation by the Coast and Geodetic Survey in 1932 and a condition report in 1949.[^1] The 1949 description says the mark was recovered in good condition. It places the tower 3 feet (0.9 m) east of an extension of a north-south telephone line.[^1]

The Michigan Fire Towers history page says aerial surveillance and population expansion brought about the decline of the state's towers in 1962.[^3]

Today a trail guide describes the Portage Lake Fire Tower Trail as a dirt road, and says vehicles of every size can use it.[^2] It names a transmission station alongside the lookout tower as the trail's main focus, and says the route passes the remains of the tower before it turns onto Annis Pit Road.[^2] No other source I found describes what remains at the tower site today, or who owns the land.
