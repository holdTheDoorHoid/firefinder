The Currituck Fire Tower is a 100-foot Aermotor LS-40 in Currituck County, North Carolina.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register dates construction to the 1940s or 1950s, and active use ran until the 1980s.[^1] The register gives no exact build year, so the decade is as close as the register gets.[^1]

As of 2022, the register says, the tower structure was still standing, but its cab had been removed.[^1] The register lists the tower as privately owned.[^1] It was added to the register as US 1703 on December 25, 2022.[^1]

Because the tower is privately owned, anyone wanting to see it should ask the owner first. Please do not climb it without permission.

[^1]: Currituck Fire Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/nc/currituck-fire-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
