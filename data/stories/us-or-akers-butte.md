Akers Butte rises about 10 miles north-northwest of Wallowa in Wallowa County.[^2] A lookout was already in use there in 1930, when Harvey McDonald served as fire warden at the summit.[^3]

In April 1933 a field report said the site needed a cabin and a tower.[^3] By July, a field report said, a man named Rice had raised a 38-foot tower and built a cabin.[^3] The register dates the build to 1933 and gives the tower as 38 feet.[^1] An October 1933 report describes a 40-foot pole tower and a 14-by-18 frame cabin as completed, so sources disagree on the height.[^3]

A windstorm in early March 1940 leaned the lookout and warped its walls, a June 1940 report says.[^3] The register and firelookout.com both say it was abandoned in the 1940s and is gone.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it.
