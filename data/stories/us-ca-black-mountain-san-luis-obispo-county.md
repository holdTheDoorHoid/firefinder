Black Mountain sits in the Los Padres National Forest, north of Pozo in San Luis Obispo County.[^1][^2]

A lookout stood on the peak before the present one.[^1] A photo caption on the register page names a "Previous BC-301 Cabin with AWS Residence."[^1] A 1954 newspaper item reported that Mrs. C. C. Yates was serving as fire ranger at the Forest Service lookout station there, on duty since May 28 of that year.[^2]

The present cabin was built in 1964.[^1] It was a CL-100 cab on an 11-foot block tower.[^1] According to the register, the cabin went to the California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection and was taken to Smith Mountain in Monterey County, where it still stands.[^1] The Smith Mountain register entry gives the move as 1976, when CalFire placed the cab on a 10-foot block tower there.[^3]

The clippings page says the cab was removed and its base was used to house equipment. It gives no date for this.[^2] A photo caption that mentions 2009 marks the 11-foot block tower as "Now Removed."[^1] No source I found describes the summit as it is now beyond these notes.
