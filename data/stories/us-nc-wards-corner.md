The Civilian Conservation Corps put up a 120-foot (37 m) steel tower at Wards Corner, in Pender County, in April 1936.[^1] Emergency Conservation Works money covered the $2,088.00 cost.[^1] Members of Camp P-68 finished the tower on November 16, 1936.[^1] The state's Department of Conservation and Development reported the work in its biennial report for 1938.[^1]

CCC Camp P-68 also supplied the labor for a five-room frame house, 30 by 36 feet, completed in May 1937 as living quarters for the lookout.[^1]

A 2021 list of North Carolina lookout towers still carries the name Wards Corner for a site in Pender County.[^2] No source I found says whether the tower still stands, what remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it.
