Box Canyon Lookout is a crow's nest on the Payette National Forest, about 8 miles southeast of Riggins, in Idaho County.[^1][^2]

The site sits at 6,707 feet.[^1] Both lookout-history pages date the crow's nest to the 1930s and list it as abandoned in that decade.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list also marks it as abandoned.[^3]

Rex Kamstra's page, on the Forest Fire Lookout Association site, is the exception.[^1][^4] It says the crow's nest is "Still there," but it gives no date for that remark.[^1] The other two listings call the site abandoned and say nothing about a standing crow's nest.[^2][^3]

The University of Idaho's Keeping Watch collection gives the same coordinates and sends readers to Kamstra's page for more detail.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site as hike-in.[^2] No source we read says what stands on the site today, or whether visitors may go there.[^1][^2]
