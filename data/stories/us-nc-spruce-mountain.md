Spruce Mountain sits in Haywood County, inside Great Smoky Mountains National Park.[^2] The name comes from the red spruce that grow on it.[^1] A 2019 trip report says a fire tower and a ranger's cabin went up on the summit four years after the park was created, and that power was connected at the same time.[^1]

The tower is gone.[^1] What remains are foundation blocks, overgrown with blackberry brambles.[^1] Until 2000, when it was closed, the Polls Gap Trail ran from near Balsam Mountain Campground and crossed the Spruce Mountain Trail.[^1]

A 2019 trip report describes reaching the site on foot.[^1] The Spruce Mountain Trail covers 1.2 miles in and out, beginning on one-lane Balsam Mountain Road and finishing at Campsite 42, in an open clearing at the mountain's base.[^1] The only recent description found is that trip report, so conditions may have changed since 2019.[^1]
