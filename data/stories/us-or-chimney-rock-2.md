Chimney Rock is a lookout point six miles southeast of Sweet Home, in Linn County, Oregon.[^1][^2]

The Linn County Fire Patrol built the lookout in April and May 1952, and it was completed in early June.[^1] A state study had identified the lower Green Mountain and Calapooya area as needing a lookout.[^1] The new tower took over the duties of the older Chandler Mountain lookout, which sits about 1.5 miles east and was developed with a 10-by-10-foot cabin in 1915.[^3][^2]

The materials were cut at the Sweet Home headquarters and hauled to the site once a road of about half a mile, connecting existing logging roads, was finished.[^1] The annual report describes a standard Forest Service cab, 14 by 14 feet with a catwalk, on a 9-foot tower, and an enclosed tower section meant for a garage, wood shed and tool storage.[^1] L.G. Knight, a carpenter, drew the plans, and the cost was $3,133.[^1] A firelookout.com page instead calls the cab a two-story L-4 cab, so the two descriptions do not match.[^2]

The lookout was staffed from about June 15, 1952.[^1]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites record, filed under the Chandler Mountain name, says the 1952 cab was removed in 1970.[^4] Firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page for Chimney Rock lists it only as Removed, with no date.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list marks the same Chandler Mountain record as gone.[^5]

Today, no source checked describes what, if anything, remains at the point, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^4][^5]
