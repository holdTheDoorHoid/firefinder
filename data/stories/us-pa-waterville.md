The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the Waterville lookout in Lycoming County as a steel I-S tower, dated to 1933, with removal in 1975.[^1] The register gives its elevation as 1,929 feet (588 m) and names Henry Isenberg as the nominator. The entry was registered on September 16, 2008.[^1]

The earlier record does not line up neatly with those dates. A 1912-13 Department of Forestry report mentions an observatory on private land about two miles from Waterville, but the excerpt we read does not say whether it is the same site.[^2] A December 1916 item in the Wellsboro Agitator says a watcher worked day and night from a lookout tower on a mountainside at Waterville.[^2] The item credits him with helping locate fires by spotting where smoke rose.[^2]

So a lookout tower was standing at Waterville by 1916, well before the register's 1933 date.[^2] The sources we read do not say whether the 1933 steel tower replaced it or stood on the same spot.[^1][^2]

A TopoQuest gazetteer entry titled Waterville Lookout Tower sits at the register's coordinates, on the USGS Jersey Mills map.[^1][^3] That is a map label, and it does not show whether anything stands there today.[^3] No source we read describes what remains at the site now, or whether the ground is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
