Chita (Loc 2) was a Texas lookout site in Trinity County.[^1] A Texas tower table says the tower there came from Chita (Loc 1), and gives 1970 as its date.[^2] The Loc 1 entry gives 1970 as its removal year and says the tower moved to Chita Loc 2.[^2]

Before the move, the tower stood at Loc 1. A 1942 Coast and Geodetic Survey description of that station, PID BL2168, identifies the tower as a Texas Forest Service lookout made entirely of steel, with a closed cabin on its upper level, and puts its height at roughly 125 feet.[^3] The table gives the Loc 1 tower a height of 100, with no unit printed, which does not match the survey's figure.[^2][^3]

The table gives 1981 as the removal year for Chita (Loc 2) and says the tower was sold that year.[^2] The Texas lookout list marks the Chita (Loc 2) site as gone, at coordinates 31.01442, -95.23913.[^1] A forest lookout list gives the Chita tower's operator as the Texas Forest Service.[^4]

No source describes what remains at the Chita (Loc 2) site today, and none says whether the public can reach it.
