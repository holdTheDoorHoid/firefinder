Castle Peak Lookout stood on Castle Peak in Madera County, California, within the Sierra National Forest.[^1] A September 1913 item in the Fresno Morning Republican, quoted on a lookout history page, places a lookout on 'Castle Peak or Oat Mountain,' a phrase that does not say whether the two names refer to one place.[^2]

The register gives 1930 as the build year.[^1] It describes the tower as a California Region 5 Plan 4AR design, 20 feet tall, with an H-brace open steel frame.[^1] The 1913 item came seventeen years before that build date, and neither source says whether the 1930 tower replaced an earlier station.[^1][^2]

The register gives no removal date.[^1] It cites a 1986 survey by Thornton that raised the possibility the structure was moved to or from Oat Mountain, though it adds that no evidence supports this.[^1] The register describes six tower piers as the only remains on site.[^1] The site was registered on February 1, 2020.[^1]

No source we read describes the site today.
