Basset is a steel fire lookout in Alberta, 30 m (98 ft) tall on a site 755.0 m (2,477 ft) above sea level.[^1] Alberta's wildfire database gives 2011 as its year of construction.[^1]

Alberta's lookouts are part of how fires are spotted.[^3] The province says the sites are chosen for a view clear enough to spot and report wildfires.[^3] A history of Alberta's lookouts sorts them into two types: summit lookouts, and towers on high boreal hills that put the observer above whatever could block the view.[^4]

Alberta reports 127 wildfire lookout sites, and at each one an observer keeps watch through the day, usually from April to October.[^2] The database lists Basset as active, with a weather station on site, and codes its access as "fly", which usually means it is reached by air rather than by road.[^1]
