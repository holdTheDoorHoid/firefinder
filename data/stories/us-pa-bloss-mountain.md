The Bloss Mountain tower began on Maple Hill.[^1][^3] The register for that earlier site dates the steel Aermotor tower to 1921 and says it was relocated to Bloss Mountain in 1939.[^1] The Bloss Mountain register gives 1939 as the build year and says the tower was moved from Maple Hill.[^2]

A 1939 newspaper gave the height as 80 feet and said a stone cabin was going up nearby for the observer, who would staff the tower for four or five months each year.[^3] In 1949 a Wellsboro paper listed the towermen for Tioga County's five fire towers, naming Glen Campbell at Bloss Tower.[^3]

The register says the tower was removed in 1986.[^2] It lists the site under former fire lookout sites.[^2] OpenStreetMap still has a point called Bloss Lookout Tower at the spot, but a map point does not show whether anything stands there.[^4] No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
