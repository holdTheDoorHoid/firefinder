The Forest Lookouts site lists a tower at Brownfield in Laclede County, inside the Mark Twain National Forest and given as 34N-13W-20.[^1] That page is the only tower-specific source read, and it gives only the location.[^1]

Brownfield, in the northeast of Laclede County, is not an incorporated town.[^2] It sits just over a mile southeast of the Laclede-Pulaski county line, about a mile from the Gasconade River, inside the national forest.[^2] A post office operated there from 1930 to 1955, named for Riley Brownfield, an early postmaster.[^2] A 1954 USGS topographic map, as a map seller describes it, shows a Brownfield school and two churches nearby.[^3]

No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the tower site today, or whether the site is open to the public.
