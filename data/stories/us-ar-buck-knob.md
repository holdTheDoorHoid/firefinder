The Buck Knob lookout stood on the summit of the peak that carries its name, in Scott County, inside the Ouachita National Forest.[^1] A federal survey description places it about 16 miles south-southeast of Waldron.[^1] The tower was a four-legged steel frame with a wooden cabin at the top, roughly 50 feet high.[^1]

An ARGenWeb caption links a photo of the tower to a 1933 to 1934 Civilian Conservation Corps pictorial review.[^2] The Encyclopedia of Arkansas credits the Civilian Conservation Corps with 3,100 towers built during the Depression, many of them sited in the Ouachita and Ozark-St. Francis forests.[^5]

That survey description was written in 1956 and still describes the tower as standing on the summit.[^1] Lookout listings now mark it as removed.[^1][^2]

Buck Knob now hosts a communications site on the Mena Ranger District.[^3] A Forest Service project titled Buck Knob Tower Special Use 2016 concerns an application to add communications equipment there, and its decision is dated September 6, 2016.[^3] A later Forest Service project covers a permit renewal for Verizon at an existing communications tower on Buck Knob Mountain, with a decision signed September 15, 2020.[^4] No source here describes a lookout cab or footings on the summit now, or says whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
