Hehe Butte, which a 1936 forestry report spells He He Butte, is a lookout site in Wasco County, Oregon, on the Warm Springs Indian Reservation, about 25 miles southwest of Maupin.[^1][^2][^3]

Work on a tower there started in the spring of 1936, according to a forestry report of that year that Oregon Lookouts quotes.[^3] The report describes an 80-foot wooden tower and says it will be in service later in that fire season.[^3]

The register and firelookout.com date the tower to 1938 instead.[^1][^2] Both describe a 90-foot, three-legged treated timber tower with a 6-by-6-foot cab and a cabin.[^1][^2] The sources do not reconcile the year or the height.[^1][^2][^3]

The register lists the site as registered on August 25, 2010.[^1] Both the register and firelookout.com say the tower is gone, and no source gives a date or reason for its removal.[^1][^2][^3]

The land is tribal, and the register names the Warm Springs Indian Reservation as administrator.[^1] No source describes public access, so anyone wanting to visit should ask the reservation first.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains of the tower or cabin today.[^1][^2]
