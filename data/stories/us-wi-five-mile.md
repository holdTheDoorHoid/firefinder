Five Mile Lookout is in Washburn County, Wisconsin, at 1,113 feet.[^1] The register lists the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources as the agency that administers it.[^1]

The register dates the tower to August 1932.[^1] The register gives it as an 84-foot tower of the Wisconsin standard design, with a protected ladder on the outside.[^1] The register listed it on August 28, 2017, on the nomination of Tyler Bormann.[^1]

The name is shared with the Five Mile Tower Fire, a wildfire that started on the afternoon of April 30, 1977, and burned over 13,000 acres across Washburn and Douglas counties.[^3]

The register describes the tower in the present tense.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list marks the same tower as gone.[^2] The two sources disagree on whether it still stands.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes the site today or whether visitors may go there.[^1][^2]
