John Kerr Peak lies about 17 miles to the northeast of Reserve, in Catron County, on the Reserve Ranger District of the Gila National Forest.[^1][^3] A fire lookout once stood on its summit, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^2][^3]

A hiker's trip report from 2019 found the summit still marked by its past.[^2] The writer counted four braces that once supported a fire lookout on the summit, and found steps leading nowhere on the north side.[^2] On a shorter peak nearby, the writer passed steps that they judged once led to a cabin, plus the footing of a shed.[^2] A fire ring had been placed in the summit area.[^2]

The old 4WD track to the summit is easy to spot, with little signage.[^2] The writer found a rough spot that needed high clearance.[^2] A lightning-caused fire burned 30 acres on the peak in May 2017, and the sources we read do not connect that fire to the lookout.[^1] The Continental Divide Trail runs to the east of that burn.[^1] No source we read describes the summit after 2019.
