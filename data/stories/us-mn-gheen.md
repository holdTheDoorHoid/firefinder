The Gheen Lookout stands on Gheen Hill in St. Louis County, Minnesota, and the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources administers it.[^1] An earlier wooden tower was put up on the same site in 1921.[^1] The current 100-foot stairway tower was built by the Civilian Conservation Corps in 1934.[^1][^2]

Most of Minnesota's forestry fire lookouts were built during the Civilian Conservation Corps era. A history of the state's towers says the ones built to guard state and national parks were operated by CCC workers, and most were later turned over to the DNR.[^2]

The sources read do not say who staffed the Gheen tower or what fires it watched.

A 2026 photo caption gives the elevation as 1,503 feet, while the Register gives 1,476 feet (450 meters).[^3][^1] The Register notes communications equipment on the tower as of 2020, and several other communications towers share the hill.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^4] The Register registered it on January 8, 2021, after Jacob Hufnagle nominated it. The sources do not say whether the public can visit it. Do not climb it without the DNR's permission.
