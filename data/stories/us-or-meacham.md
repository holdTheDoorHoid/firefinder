The Meacham lookout stood in the Umatilla National Forest in Umatilla County, Oregon, and the Forest Service administered it.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register numbers it US 1127 and dates its construction to 1937.[^1] It describes the tower as a 20-foot L-4 pole tower.[^1]

The register states plainly that the tower is now gone.[^1] The firelookout.org listing for Oregon marks the tower gone under the same register number.[^2] The Oregon forest-lookout index places the site at 1S-37E-17 and marks it removed.[^3] The register entry itself dates from August 20, 2011, with Ray Kresek named as nominator.[^1]

The register's description is the fullest entry I found, and it ties the 1937 build to the tower's present absence.[^1] No source I found describes remains at the site, its view, or whether the ground is open to the public.
