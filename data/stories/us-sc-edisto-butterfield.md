The Edisto Tower was built around 1933 by the Civilian Conservation Corps, according to the National Historic Lookout Register.[^1] The Register describes a 110-foot Bethlehem Steel tower with a 7-by-7-foot steel cab and pivot-out windows.[^1] A Facebook post from the Butterfield Plantation gives the same 1933 date and the Edisto name.[^3]

The South Carolina Forestry Commission first called it the Edisto Tower.[^1] The Register says it first stood about 12 miles east-southeast of "Bamburg," which looks like a typo for Bamberg.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page lists the tower under Bamberg County and the Forestry Commission, and records only a 2005 sale.[^2] That page names no one who staffed the tower and no fire it watched.[^2]

The Forestry Commission closed its fire tower system on October 1, 1993.[^4] In October 2005 the tower was bought from the Forestry Commission and moved to private property.[^1][^2] The Butterfield Plantation post says the tower was taken out of service, moved and set up again at Butterfield in 2005.[^3]

Today the tower stands at Butterfield Plantation in Allendale County.[^1][^3] The Register gives the site an elevation of 160 feet, or 49 meters.[^1] According to the Facebook post, the view on a clear day takes in Augusta, Columbia and the Butterfield Fish Pond.[^3] The Register says the tower was moved to private property, and we found no public access information, so please ask the owners before going near it.[^1] The Register added the tower on April 19, 2012, as US 941, SC 10, on the nomination of Michael T. Finch Jr., MD.[^1]
