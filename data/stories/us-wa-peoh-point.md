Peoh Point sits about three miles south of Cle Elum in Kittitas County, on what the register calls a 2000-foot cliff.[^1][^2] A proposed lookout there got three miles of access road in 1950.[^3][^4]

The register, firelookout.com and Willhite say an L-4 cab was built on the point in 1951.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts page gives a different account for that year, describing a 14-by-14-foot ground house built as a replacement lookout.[^4]

In 1974 the Washington Department of Natural Resources put a live-in cab on a 10-foot concrete block base.[^1][^3] The register notes that the cab was briefly seen in the television series Northern Exposure, which was filmed in nearby Roslyn.[^1]

DNR removed the lookout in 1996.[^1][^4] One listing says it was ordered destroyed that year.[^2] Forest Lookouts says the remaining portion was turned into a communications building.[^4] Willhite says a small portion of the lookout is now part of the communications structure.[^3]

Today the point holds communication buildings, and the views are still there.[^3] A 2022 trip report says the road ends at a yellow gate, about a half-mile walk from the point and its communications area.[^5] Willhite says the gate may be locked, and if it is, the walk is under a mile.[^3]
