North Mountain was a fire lookout site in Clackamas County, Oregon, inside the Mount Hood National Forest.[^1] The first structure was a 20-foot wooden tower with an L-4 cab, built in 1933.[^1][^2] The register lists the site at 4,064 feet (1,239 meters) under number US 1760, OR 505.[^1]

Both sources place emergency use of the lookout in the 1960s.[^1][^2]

In 1965 the wooden tower was replaced by a 41-foot treated timber tower with an R-6 cab.[^1][^2] Both sources now list the lookout as gone.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association added the site to its register on September 18, 2019.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, is left at the site today.
