The Henderson fire tower is in Vance County, North Carolina.[^1] A North Carolina lookout-tower list places it at 36.357 N, 78.371 W, and that list was compiled partly from 1:100,000 USGS topographic maps.[^1]

A report in The Robesonian, dated December 3, 1952 and quoted by a lookout-history site, says a 100-foot (30.5 m) tower had been built a mile north of Henderson.[^2] The article date means the tower was built no later than December 1952.[^2] The same site credits the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources with the lookout.[^2]

No source I could read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Henderson - FOREST LOOKOUTS, easternuslookouts.weebly.com, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/henderson.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
