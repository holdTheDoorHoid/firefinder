Gunsight Mountain's lookout was a cab dated to 1939, which firelookout.com calls an L-4 cab.[^1] It stood in the Flathead National Forest about ten miles northeast of the Spotted Bear Ranger Station, in Flathead County, Montana, at an elevation of 7,180 feet.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list records its type as ground rather than tower.[^3] The local history gives its survey location as 26N-13W-31.[^2]

The cab was abandoned in 1951, and the listing now records the site as gone.[^1][^3] All three sources use the name Gunsight Mountain and place the site in Flathead County.[^1][^2][^3] Anyone heading to the site should not expect a standing cab, since firelookout.com and the state index both mark it as gone.[^1][^3]

No source consulted describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
