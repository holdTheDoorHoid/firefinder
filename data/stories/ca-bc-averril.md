The firelookout.ca index lists the Averril Fire Lookout in the Fraser-Fort George area, at 54.415, -122.425, and gives its year built as N/A.[^1]

BC's geographical names register places Mount Averil on the south side of upper Angusmac Creek, north-east of Summit Lake and Prince George.[^2] Whether it is the lookout's peak is not confirmed by a source I could read.

Two Facebook group posts ask about the Mount Averil fire lookout.[^3][^4]

No source I could read describes what remains at the site today.
