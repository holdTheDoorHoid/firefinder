Buck Ridge is a tower site in Johnson County, Tennessee, at 4,297 feet (1,310 m).[^1] The Tennessee lookout list gives its code as FZ2143 and tags it "cnf". The same list writes the Cherokee National Forest as CNF, so the tag most likely refers to that forest.[^1] The list marks the tower removed.[^1]

The list says many Tennessee towers have come down and most former sites are now privately owned.[^1] It also warns visitors against climbing the towers.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the Buck Ridge site, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Tennessee lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
