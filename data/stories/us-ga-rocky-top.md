A Georgia lookout list on tnlandforms.us includes a tower called rocky top, in Union County, at coordinates 34.98208, -84.00395, with a value of 3051 in its elevation column.[^1] The list does not state a unit for that column, and its note says names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own, so the name may not be a historical one.[^1]

Sources differ on the summit's height. Wikipedia describes Rocky Top as a summit in Georgia, files it under mountains of Union County, gives its elevation as 3,071 feet (936 m), and says the name reflects its appearance.[^2] A tower-site listing for Rocky Top near Ivylog gives a ground elevation of 3075.0 feet (937.3 m).[^3]

Today, that listing describes a radio tower site at Rocky Top.[^3] It says the site is not owned by the company that runs the listing, and that anyone wanting space on it should contact the site owner.[^3] No source says whether the summit is open to visitors.
