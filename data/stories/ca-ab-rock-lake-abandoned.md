OpenStreetMap, a free map created by its users, lists a tower called Rock Lake Fire Lookout, and the name ends with "(abandoned)".[^1] The map tags the structure as an observation tower.[^1] Mapcarta places it near Daybreak Peak, with Rock Lake Provincial Park about 9 km east of it.[^2]

Peakery, a site that logs peak climbs, has a summit under the same name at 2,085 m (6,840 ft).[^3] Its climbing log shows climbers at that summit in July 2014 and in August 2020, and the most-climbed route it lists starts at the Willmore Wilderness Staging Area.[^3] The match is by name only, so the summit may not be the same place as the mapped tower.

Alberta's boreal fire towers came in several forms. Some were narrower structures, up to 18.3 m (60 ft) high, with a small cupola on top, built of timber or steel.[^4]

No source we read gives this site's build date or construction material, describes what remains there today, or says whether the public may go there.
