A November 1950 list of observation towers in Hillsborough County, printed by the Tampa Tribune, named a "Hurrah tower" at Hurrah Church, seven miles south of Pinecrest. Its towerman was Kenneth Pace.[^1] Wikipedia's article on Picnic says that community is also known as Hurrah, and that it lies between Plant City and Lithia.[^3] Mapcarta places the tower near the hamlet of Fort Lonesome, puts the community of Lillibridge four miles northeast of it, and lists Hurrah Church about 620 feet east of it.[^2] The tower was sold at auction in 2008.[^1] No source we found describes the site today.
