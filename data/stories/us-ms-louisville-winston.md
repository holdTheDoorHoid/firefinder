The Louisville (Winston) Lookout Tower stands on the edge of the Tombigbee National Forest in Winston County, Mississippi.[^1]

The Register describes it as a 100-foot Aermotor LS-40 and gives its elevation as 637 feet (194 meters).[^1]

We did not find a build date, a builder, or any record of who staffed the tower. The Register lists the build date as unknown and names the Mississippi Forestry Commission as its administrator.[^1]

The tower was registered on July 9, 2023, as US 1744, MS 61, after a site visit on July 8, 2023.[^1] The visit description uses the present tense, which suggests the tower was still standing then.[^1]

The Register also notes that the Forestry Commission still has active offices at the same location.[^1] It does not say whether the public may visit or climb the tower, and we found no other source on that. Because a working agency office sits on the site, treat access as restricted and ask the Forestry Commission before going near it.[^1]

[^1]: Louisville (Winston) Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ms/louisville-winston-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
