Black Spring Lookout sits on the Northern Cheyenne Indian Reservation in Rosebud County, Montana.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register lists the Northern Cheyenne Agency of the Bureau of Indian Affairs as its administrator, and the Register accepted the tower on April 12, 2022.[^2] A lookout site places it about five miles northwest of Lame Deer.[^1]

We found no record of when it was built. A survey description written by the US Geological Survey in 1957 takes the apex of the tower roof as a survey mark, so the tower stood by then.[^3] That description places the tower about two miles north and six miles west of Lame Deer, a different spot from the five miles on the lookout site. We have not settled which is right.[^1][^3]

The Register's description says only "No information at this time." It gives the elevation as 3,685 feet, while the lookout site gives 3,692 feet.[^2][^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^4]

None of the sources we read say who staffed the lookout or what fires it watched, and none describe its condition in recent years. Because it sits on tribal land, anyone wanting to see it should ask the Northern Cheyenne Tribe first, and should not climb a structure that is closed.
