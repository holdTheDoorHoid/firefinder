Sugar Pine Mountain sits in Humboldt County, California, in the Hoopa area.[^1][^2] The register lists its location as the Hoopa Indian Agency and says the Bureau of Indian Affairs administered it.[^1] The lookout was a 7x7 observation-only cabin on an 80-foot, three-legged timber tower, and the register dates it to the 1930s.[^1] The only photo the register knows of dates from 1937.[^1] A residence cabin also stood on the site, and the US Indian Service is listed as a cooperator.[^1]

The register says the lookout was removed before 1986, but gives no exact date.[^1]

Today a county radio site occupies the mountain, according to a Humboldt County staff report.[^2] The county holds a license from the Hoopa Valley Tribe for that communications site, and the report says it supports emergency radio in the Hoopa area.[^2] The report gives no coordinates and once calls the site Sugar Hill Mountain, so we cannot be sure it is the same summit, or whether that equipment stands on the old lookout footprint.[^2] We found no source describing what, if anything, remains of the lookout itself. Anyone interested should ask the Hoopa Valley Tribe before going up.

[^1]: Sugar Pine Mountain Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ca/sugar-pine-mountain-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
