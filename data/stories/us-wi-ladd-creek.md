The Ladd Creek fire tower stood in Rusk County, in section 18 of township 36 north, range 3 west.[^1] In January 1983 the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources asked for sealed bids on the steel tower, which a notice reprinted by Central US Lookouts described as about 100 feet tall.[^1] Bids were to be received at the DNR's Park Falls area headquarters.[^1]

Across the state, lookouts were staffed from May into September for many decades, and in 2015, 72 towers that were still in use were taken out of service.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association now lists the Ladd Creek tower as gone, placing it near 45.60 north and 90.80 west.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Ladd Creek site today, and none says whether the site is open to visitors.
