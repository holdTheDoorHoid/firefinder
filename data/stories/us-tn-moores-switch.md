The Moores Switch fire tower is in Hickman County, Tennessee.[^1] The Tennessee Landforms list of lookout towers gives its elevation as 798 feet and marks it as standing.[^1] The same list numbers it 7-9, a district-and-tower number taken from a state list made in 1976.[^1]

The sources say little about its beginnings. A National Register survey of the state Division of Forestry's towers names Moore's Switch among the fire lookouts built of wood. It says steel towers came to replace Moore's Switch and the Cobel tower, both in Hickman County, sometime in the 1940s or 1950s. The survey cites an undated Division of Forestry record for that date.[^2] It gives no build year and no builder.

The same survey lists a Moores Switch tower among the Division of Forestry towers still standing as of March 26, 2014. That is the newest dated record I found.[^2]

The sources do not say whether the tower standing today is the original wooden tower, the steel replacement, or a different structure. Tennessee Landforms says many of the towers have come down, and that most sites are now on private land. It also advises against climbing the towers, because the steps are often missing or decayed.[^1] I found nothing on whether this one is open to visitors. Ask the landowner before going to look, and do not climb it.

[^1]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, Tennessee Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Tennessee Division of Forestry Fire Lookout Towers, 1933 to 1975, National Register multiple property document, NPGallery (National Park Service), https://npgallery.nps.gov/pdfhost/docs/NRHP/Text/64501237.pdf (accessed 2026-10-09).
