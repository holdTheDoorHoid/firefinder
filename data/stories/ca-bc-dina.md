Dina Fire Lookout sits above Nadina Lake, in the Bulkley-Nechako region of British Columbia.[^1][^4] The trail to it is 3.3 km long, and the Houston Hikers guide puts the round trip at three to three and a half hours, with its trailhead along the Old Tahtsa (Washout) FSR.[^1]

The Houston Hikers pages give the distance from Houston three different ways: 60 km or more in the trail header, about 110 km in the directions, and 124 km on a separate trail map.[^1][^3]

A Houston Hikers trail map, whose file name dates it to April 2012, says the lookout is no longer manned.[^3] The guide, which shows no date, lists a lookout tower on the site, along with an outhouse, room for tenting, a spring cot, and a propane stove and fridge.[^1] It presents the site as either a moderate day hike or a spot for camping, and it mentions no closure or permit requirement.[^1]

The two coordinate sets for the lookout are about 2.4 km apart.[^4][^2] Firelookout.ca gives 53.86954, -126.97775, while the web index gives 53.863 and -126.943, with an elevation of 1,304 m (4,278 ft).[^4][^2]
