Kiamichi Mountain is a fire lookout in Le Flore County, Oklahoma.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site lists it as Kiamachi Mountain, a spelling that a 1934 newspaper quote also uses for the national forest.[^1][^2] A 2009 Forest Service project title uses the modern spelling, Kiamichi.[^3]

A travel writer says the steel lookout it describes fits the 1930s, when many Civilian Conservation Corps men ended up in the Ouachita National Forest, but adds that exact construction details are hard to pin down.[^4]

The same article says locals and trail riders know it as the Honobia Fire Tower, and that it sits in the Kiamichi Mountains, inside the Ouachita National Forest.[^4] A tourism listing calls a tower near Whitesboro the Kiamichi Lookout Tower, on County Road 254.[^5] The names differ, and the sources I read do not tie them to the Forest Lookouts record.[^1][^4][^5]

The travel article also says the old steel frame is still standing above the canopy.[^4] It describes a rugged 4x4 trail with creek crossings, no paved road and no parking lot at the tower, and says the tower is technically open at all hours.[^4] Near the tower, the remains of an old Forest Service homesite can still be found.[^4]

A 2009 Forest Service project also describes a Corps of Engineers proposal to replace a guyed tower on Kiamichi Mountain with a self-supporting tower of the same height.[^3] The project record does not say whether that tower is the lookout.[^3]
