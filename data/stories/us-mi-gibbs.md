The Gibbs City Fire Tower is in Iron County, Michigan, within the Ottawa National Forest.[^1]

The Civilian Conservation Corps, through its Company 3611, put the tower up in 1939-40.[^1] A forest lookouts site gives 1940 as the year it was completed.[^2] The structure stands 100 feet tall, is an Aermotor design, and has a metal cab measuring 7 by 7 feet.[^1] The register lists the site's elevation as 1,683 feet (513 m).[^1]

The register says the tower stayed in service until 1970, when air detection took over its role.[^1] Its entry, dated May 30, 1996, describes it as the last surviving fire tower on the Iron River district of the forest.[^1]

The U.S. Forest Service administers the site.[^1] The sources I found do not describe what remains there today, or whether visitors can reach it.
