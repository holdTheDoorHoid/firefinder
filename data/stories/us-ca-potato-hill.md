Potato Hill, in Lake County within the Mendocino National Forest, got a lookout in 1935.[^1] Its cabin followed a California Region 5 plan, numbered BC-301, and sat on an enclosed timber tower.[^1] A photograph of the lookout from 1935 is credited to the Upper Lake Ranger District collection.[^1] The Forest Service administered the site.[^1] The register gives the site's elevation as 4,417 feet, or 1,346 meters, and lists it as US 1903, CA 98.[^1]

The site joined the Former Fire Lookout Sites register on February 1, 2020, on the nomination of Brad Eells.[^1] The register gives no removal date, and only the foundation is left.[^1]

No source describes the site now, so this record cannot say what the foundation looks like today or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Potato Hill Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ca/potato-hill-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
