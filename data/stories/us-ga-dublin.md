The Georgia lookout tower list places a tower at Dublin, in Laurens County, at coordinates 32.49391, -82.93161. Its row is coded GA198.[^1] The row files the tower under GFC number 508.[^1] The page's map is stamped as revised on March 22, 2016, so the list may not reflect later changes.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts website also has a page for Dublin. The text we could read there gives only the name and the county (Laurens County, Georgia), and no history.[^2]

We found no source that describes the site today, whether a tower still stands there, or whether visitors can reach it.
