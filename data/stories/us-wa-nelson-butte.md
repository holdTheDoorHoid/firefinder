Nelson Butte sits in Chelan County, Washington, about 24 miles northwest of Chelan, inside the Wenatchee National Forest.[^4][^1] The spot began as a lookout camp.[^2] In September 1931 a Wenatchee Daily World item, quoted by Washington Lookouts, reported that the lookout on the butte had spotted a fire near the slide across the lake.[^3] Men went out and quickly got the blaze in hand.[^3]

A 1933 item, also quoted there, describes a Chelan camp making its way toward the summit, with most of a road to Safety Harbor Creek already built.[^3] WillhiteWeb says the tower went up in 1934, by which time a road reached the site.[^2] The structure was a 20-foot pole L-4 tower, built in 1934.[^4][^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it as US 1417 and registered it on October 28, 2012.[^4]

The tower was abandoned in 1953 and is now gone.[^1][^4] WillhiteWeb calls the 1953 change "abandoned or destroyed" and does not say which.[^2]

None of the sources describes what, if anything, is still standing on the summit. WillhiteWeb's route notes, which carry no date, describe a climb from the north ridge along an old fire break that is still open but slowly regrowing.[^2] The page lists gravel roads, a place to pull over and park, and a hike of under half a mile.[^2] No source says whether the site is open to the public, so check before you go.
