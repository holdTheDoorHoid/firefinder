Cruiser Mountain is in the Flathead National Forest, in Flathead County, Montana.[^1][^2] A firelookout.com page places it about 18 miles east-northeast of the Spotted Bear Ranger Station.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts listing gives its legal location as 26N-12W-22, which uses the township, range and section format.[^2]

The firelookout.com page dates the lookout only to the 1930s, says its history is unknown, and marks it abandoned.[^1] That single line does not make clear whether the decade refers to building or to abandonment.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana county list gives its type as unknown and its status as gone.[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout on the mountain today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
