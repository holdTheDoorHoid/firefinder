The National Historic Lookout Register describes the Buckeye Lookout as an 80-foot International Stacy tower whose ladder runs up a corner leg, and dates its construction to November 1933.[^1] Central US Lookouts gives a different year, 1935, when it says a steel tower went up at the site.[^2] The register gives the more specific date, but the sources do not resolve the gap.[^1][^2]

The tower stands in Florence County at about 1,505 feet above sea level.[^1] The Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources administered it, and the register says it was manned regularly through the spring fire season until 2015.[^1][^2]

Statewide, the DNR announced plans in December 2015 to stop using its lookout towers.[^3] An assessment earlier that year found that the 72 towers, still in use to varying degrees, cost more to keep up than they were worth for fire detection.[^3] Many of the towers sat on easements across private land, and in those cases the owners might take ownership of them.[^3] The sources do not say whether Buckeye was one of those cases.[^1][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association still lists the tower as standing, and the register added it on June 9, 2017.[^4][^1] The sources do not describe public access, climbing days or rentals, so anyone curious should check with the Wisconsin DNR before planning a visit.[^1]
