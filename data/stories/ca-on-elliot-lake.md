Elliot Lake's Fire Tower Lookout stands on Fire Tower Road, which the Explore Elliot Lake tourism site calls the highest point in the immediate Elliot Lake area.[^1]

Sources disagree on how old the structure is.[^1][^2][^3] Explore Elliot Lake dates the tower to 1954 and calls it original.[^1] The Firelookout register and BlogTO both describe it as a replica of a fire ranger's lookout station.[^2][^3] A history site that lists Elliot Lake among nine tourist towers says most of those are newer towers built to replace older ones, but it does not say which, so it does not settle this tower's age.[^4]

Explore Elliot Lake says a recreation of the cupola that once sat atop the 30.5 m (100 ft) tower stands near the parking lot and offers photo opportunities.[^1] The Ranger's Heritage Centre, a cabin from the 1950s, stands nearby; BlogTO says it was built to house the tower watchman and his wife.[^3]

The city announced in May 2026 that the lookout was open for the summer season, and noted that the bathrooms would stay closed until a later date.[^5] From the observation deck, visitors can see former mine sites that have returned to nature, and on clear days, Manitoulin Island across the North Channel of Lake Huron.[^1] Trails, picnic tables and a Wifi hotspot are listed among the amenities.[^1] The site is open seasonally, so check before you go.[^1]
