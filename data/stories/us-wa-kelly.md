The Kelly lookout site is in Grays Harbor County, Washington, at about 880 feet.[^1] Willhite calls it Kelly Hill and says observers used it at some point in the 1930s.[^2] The register dates the cabin to 1930 and describes it as 10 by 12 feet.[^1] Willhite gives the size as either 10 by 12 or 14 by 14 feet, depending on the account.[^2]

Willhite says that by 1940 the site had been abandoned and the structure was left in place.[^2] Irene Potter visited in 2000 and found some walls still standing.[^2] Timber sales around 2008 cleared trees at the site, and a sale across the road in 2016 opened some big views.[^2]

Willhite says only shingles and boards are left, and duff is building up over them.[^2] A Forest Lookouts listing describes the remains as decaying.[^3]

The register names the Washington Division of Forestry and DNR as administrator.[^1] Willhite reports that in 2018 the land belonged to Green Diamond and was open to hiking, biking and horses.[^2] The register and Willhite do not agree on who controls the land, and no current source settles it.

To get there, start at Highway 12 at Brady and drive north on Middle Satsop Road, Matlock-Brady Road, Boundary Road and Kelly Road. About a mile up Kelly Road, take the wide gravel road on the left.[^2] Park at the gate and hike up the road signed 6850. Willhite gives the hike as three-quarters of a mile with 350 feet of climbing.[^2] Check current access before you go. Willhite's account gives no date, and describes 2018 ownership, so conditions may have changed.[^2]
