Grubstake stood in the Kootenai National Forest, about 12 miles north-northeast of Yaak in Lincoln County, Montana, at 5,777 feet.[^1] A separate Lincoln County list places it in the same forest,[^3] and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list records it as a tower that is gone.[^2]

The firelookout.com guide dates it to 1934 and describes a 40-foot pole tower with an 8-by-8-foot cab.[^1] The same listing names a log cabin with the tower.[^1] The site lies about three-quarters of a mile south of Wood Mountain.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list gives its coordinates as 48.97303, -115.56160.[^2]

The guide says the lookout was destroyed in 1958.[^1] No source consulted describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
