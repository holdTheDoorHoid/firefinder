The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register, which calls the site Thorodin Tower Site, lists a 40-foot steel tower built in 1941 in Roosevelt National Forest.[^1] A 2009 Boulder Daily Camera trail column places the tower on Starr Peak, the northeastern summit of Thorodin Mountain.[^4][^3]

A May 1943 newspaper item, reprinted on a lookout history site, says Forest Ranger Art Randall had to go to the summit and load snow into a tank, since snow was the lookout's only source of drinking water that summer.[^2]

Sources disagree about when the tower came down. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register and a SummitPost guide, which cites the National Historic Lookout Register, give 1996.[^1][^3] The Daily Camera column says the tower stood from 1941 to 1976.[^4]

As of May 2010, a SummitPost guide found a large rooftop antenna structure built by American Tower where the tower had stood.[^3] No source reviewed describes the site since then. The same guide said neither end of the Boiling Gulch Trail had a public trailhead, and that land at the trail ends was private.[^3]
