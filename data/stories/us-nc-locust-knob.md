The tnlandforms.us list of the state's lookout towers places Locust Knob in Mitchell County, North Carolina, at about 36.03 N, 82.20 W.[^1]

A June 2008 blog post that summarizes Peter J. Barr's guidebook on North Carolina's lookout towers gives the year the tower was erected as 1974.[^2] The same post reports that, in Barr's account, the tower was still standing but not open to the public.[^2]

No source we read describes the site today, so we cannot say whether the tower still stands or whether access has changed since 2008.

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Fire Towers, Toe River History (blog post, June 2, 2008), http://toeriver.blogspot.com/2008/06/fire-towers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
