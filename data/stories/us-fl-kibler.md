Kibler appears on the Forest Lookouts website as a lookout in Manatee County, Florida, under the heading KIBLER. The site's address and page title spell the name Kibbler.[^1] A Florida tower list places a tower called Kibler at 27.47417 north and 82.23694 west, in Manatee County.[^2]

No source we could read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]
