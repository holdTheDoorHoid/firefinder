The Berwind tower stood in McDowell County, West Virginia, and the state's forestry agency ran it.[^1][^2] The earliest record we found is a March 1950 notice that reappointed observers for the designated towers. Berwind was on that list, with William Thompson of Berwind named as its observer.[^2]

A 1957 notice said the observers would start work once hunting season began, and it named Mrs. Edwin Hall for the Berwind tower.[^2] A 1960 item said every tower in District Four was manned.[^2] The same item mentions a Frank Fowler of Berwind.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of West Virginia towers marks the Berwind tower as gone.[^3] The former site was registered on January 26, 2017, on the nomination of Brian M. Powell, and the register lists the West Virginia Division of Forestry as its administrator.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
