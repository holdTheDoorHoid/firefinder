West Peak Lookout stands on the Coronado National Forest in Graham County, Arizona, near the community of Bonita, at 8,628 feet.[^1][^2] The register names the Safford Ranger District as a cooperator.[^1] The Civilian Conservation Corps built it in 1933, and the register describes a 46-foot Aermotor LX-25 tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab.[^1]

The lookout was listed on the National Register of Historic Places on January 28, 1988, as part of a multiple-property listing of Southwestern forest fire lookouts.[^2][^3] The National Historic Lookout Register took it on May 15, 1997, nominated by Dave Lorenz, the Arizona registrar.[^1]

The Arizona lookout list shows the tower as standing.[^5] I could not find a visit report, a staffing record or a fire history for West Peak in the sources I was able to read, so its current use and condition are unknown.[^1] The nevadahiking.org listing advises checking current access before going, and I could not confirm any public visiting arrangement.[^4]
