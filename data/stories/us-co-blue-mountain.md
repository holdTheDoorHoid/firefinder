The Blue Mountain Tower Site is a former fire lookout in the San Juan National Forest, in Archuleta County, Colorado.[^1] The register gives its elevation as 8,895 feet.[^1] The U.S. Forest Service administers the site.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site on September 18, 2009.[^1] A photo on the register page is dated May 1926.[^1]

The register says the tower has been taken down.[^1] A Colorado list of lookouts marks the site as removed under the same register number, US 533, CO 5.[^1][^2]

The register gives the site's coordinates as 37.194500 north and 106.859750 west.[^1] The Colorado list gives slightly different coordinates, 37.19556 north and 106.86056 west.[^2] No source I found describes what, if anything, remains on the ground, or whether visitors can reach the site.

[^1]: Blue Mountain Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites register, Forest Fire Lookout Association (firetower.org), http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/co/blue-mountain-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Colorado lookout list, firelookout.org, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/co/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
