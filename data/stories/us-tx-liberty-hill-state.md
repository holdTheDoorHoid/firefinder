The Liberty Hill (State) lookout was a Texas Forest Service tower in Polk County.[^1] It is a separate site from the Liberty Hill tower in San Jacinto County, which is listed under the Sam Houston National Forest.[^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list gives this tower's coordinates and marks it as gone.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
