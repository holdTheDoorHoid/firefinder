Owl Mountain is in Jackson County, in the Routt National Forest.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the site's elevation as 10,944 feet (3,336 metres) and describes a 40-foot steel tower there that went up in 1954 and came down sometime in the 1970s.[^1] The register gives only the decade for the removal.[^1] The site was recorded on September 18, 2009.[^1]

The western lookouts site, the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Colorado list and the register all mark the tower as removed.[^1][^2][^3]

Two road listings describe a forest road up the mountain.[^4][^5] A 4x4 guide describes Forest Road 791 as a gentle four-wheel-drive route up the south flank of Owl Mountain that reaches a communication tower, and says heavy snow shuts it.[^4] The Colorado Trail Explorer lists the road as unpaved, about 5.7 miles long, and open to hiking and highway vehicles.[^5] No source describes whether any footings or other remains of the lookout stand near that communication tower today.
