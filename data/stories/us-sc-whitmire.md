The Whitmire lookout stands in the Sumter National Forest in Union County, South Carolina.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register says it was built around 1935 as a 100-foot Aermotor LS-40 tower with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab and a low hip roof.[^1]

The Forest Service kept the tower in use as a lookout and dispatch center until the 1980s.[^1] The register does not name anyone who worked there or any fire it watched, and we did not find another source that does.[^1]

The register added the tower in January 2013 under the numbers US 963 and SC 17.[^1] It gives the elevation as 481 feet, or 147 meters, says the U.S. Forest Service administers it, and names the Enoree Ranger District as a cooperator.[^1]

We found no record of the tower being moved or torn down. A HikingWNC listing describes it as a roadside stop of about a tenth of a mile round trip, with the upper deck closed to visitors and no view from the tower.[^2] That listing has no date in our copy, so it may not show the tower's condition today.

The listing describes only a roadside visit, with no access to the tower.[^2] Please do not climb the tower while its upper deck is closed.
