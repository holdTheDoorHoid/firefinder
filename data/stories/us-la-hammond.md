The Central US Lookouts index lists a Hammond Lookout Tower in Tangipahoa Parish.[^1] OpenStreetMap carries a point with that name at 30.5354593, -90.4820561, tagged as a lattice observation tower at an elevation of 15 m (49 ft).[^2] The changeset comment for the point's version 3, however, reads "Add/modify communication mast.", so the marker may describe a communications mast rather than a fire lookout.[^2]

Louisiana put up its first steel fire tower near Bogalusa in 1922.[^3] By 1950 the state had fifty-six fire towers.[^3] Most surviving towers are inactive, and a number are held in reserve.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public.
