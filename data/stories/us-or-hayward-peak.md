Hayward Peak sits in Josephine County, Oregon, inside the Siskiyou National Forest. The Oregon list on Weebly calls it Hayward Mountain.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places it at 42.35556, -123.93426.[^3] The Oregon list gives its legal location as 37S-10W-18, and a Siskiyou list gives T37S, R10W, SW18.[^2][^4]

Firelookout.com lists a tower there in 1941, and a Siskiyou National Forest lookout list gives 1941 for the site as well.[^1][^4]

The Oregon list records 64 days of staffing in 1941 and names the Redwood Ranger Station as the lookout's reporting station.[^2]

Firelookout.com says the lookout was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1] The Oregon list marks it as removed, without giving a date.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Oregon lookouts marks the tower as gone.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the public can reach it.
