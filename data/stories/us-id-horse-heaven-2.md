Horse Heaven, in Lemhi County, Idaho, was a cabin rather than a tower.[^1][^3] Sources date the cabin on the west point to about 1934.[^1][^2][^3] The site is in the Salmon National Forest, 32 miles west-northwest of Salmon.[^1]

A 1934 item from the Recorder Herald, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page for this site, says Ivan Decker was on his way to his lookout at Horse Heaven.[^3] The item does not name a county.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists Ivan Decker, sourced to the Recorder Herald, at a different Horse Heaven, whose history matches the Idaho County tower described below.[^4][^6]

Sources agree the cabin was destroyed in the 1950s.[^1][^2][^3] None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the public may visit it. Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site a hike-in.[^2]

Do not confuse this site with the Horse Heaven in Idaho County.[^4][^5] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes that Idaho County site as a log cabin from 1929, with a 30-foot pole tower built in 1931 and removed in 1950.[^4]
