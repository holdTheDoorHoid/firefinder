Antelope Mountain Lookout sits at 6,456 feet on Antelope Mountain in the Malheur National Forest, about 40 miles northeast of Burns in Grant County, Oregon.[^1][^3] Sources do not agree on when the first lookout went up. A 1961 Forest Service inspection note, quoted on Forest Lookouts, calls it an old-type lookout built in 1928. An archive file records a 20-foot tower with an L-4 cab in 1929. The register, firelookout.com and Cheryl Hill's visit notes all give 1930.[^2][^1][^3][^4]

The lookout in place today is a different cab. In 1974 a Forest Service contract moved a flat-top R-6 cab with a catwalk from Lake Butte, about 45 air miles away, onto a tower on this summit. The 1974 contract called for removing the old tower and house here.[^2][^1] The 1974 contract allowed the contractor to choose how far to dismantle the cab, and a visitor in 2022 described it being moved whole by truck.[^2][^4]

The lookout is a 30-foot tower by most accounts, though the register says 31 feet.[^1][^3][^4] The register lists the site as US 913, OR 117, and says it was registered on September 4, 2011.[^1] Firelookout.com says it is staffed every summer.[^3] When Cheryl Hill visited in October 2022, the cab was already shut up for winter.[^4]

A gravel road, Road 544, runs to the summit.[^4] The available sources do not say whether visitors may go up into the cab, so check with the Prairie City Ranger District before planning a visit.[^1]

The register's "built" field gives 1963. None of the other sources read supports that date, and the register's own description puts the first lookout in 1930.[^1]
