The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower called Vidor (Terry) in Orange County, Texas, and marks it as gone.[^1] Its listed position is 30.12368, -93.94210, and the association's county table carries the same entry.[^2]

The first word of the name matches Vidor, a community in western Orange County about six miles east of Beaumont.[^3] The community takes its name from the lumberman Charles Shelton Vidor.[^3] No source checked ties the tower to that town.

No source describes the site now, so it is not known what, if anything, remains there or whether the ground is open to visitors.
