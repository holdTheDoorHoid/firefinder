The Central Plains Lookout stands in Fluvanna County, Virginia, and is privately owned.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register says it was built in the 1930s.[^1] The Register gives no builder, design, or reason for its construction.[^1]

The Register says the present owner maintained the tower until the 1970s.[^1] Starting in 1974, the family that bought the tower staffed it until use ended.[^1] The Register does not name any fire the tower watched.[^1]

According to the Register, the bottom stairs came off in the 1980s.[^1] The Register gives no date for the tower's last use.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list still marks it as standing.[^2]

The Register registered the tower on January 23, 2017.[^1]

Today the owner's wishes shape what is public. The Register withholds the coordinates at the owner's request.[^1] We found no public access, so visitors should treat it as private property and not climb it.

[^1]: Central Plains Lookout, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/va/central-plains-lookout/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Virginia Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
