Newport Hill stood in Bonner County, Idaho, about four miles north-northeast of Oldtown, in the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1] In August 1936 the Newport Miner printed a Forest Service ranger's report on the construction of a 45-foot steel lookout tower with a 7 by 7 foot observatory on the summit.[^3] The ranger said the view was good, though the tower could not be seen from the town of Newport.[^3]

A crow's nest and a tent cabin were added in 1938.[^1][^2] Later listings describe a 40-foot tower of Aermotor steel, built in 1959, with living quarters in a cabin.[^1][^2] A history of the Binarch Mountain lookout, also in Bonner County, says that lookout's Aermotor tower was dismantled and moved to Newport Hill in 1959.[^4] That history gives the tower a height of 80 feet, which does not match the 40 feet listed for Newport Hill, so the sources do not agree on the structure.[^4][^1][^2]

The listings say the tower and cabin have since come down.[^1][^2] The firelookout.com header reads "R.I.P. 1959-?" and gives no end date.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site drive-up and marks it removed.[^2]

No source described for this story says what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
