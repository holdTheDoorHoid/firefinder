A statewide list of Georgia lookout towers gives a tower called nelsons bluff in McIntosh County, at about 31.49 degrees north and 81.21 degrees west.[^1] Its entry ends with the note "topo 1954".[^1]

The same list says that names for towers numbered 73 and above are its own creation. This tower is numbered 384, so, by the list's own account, the name nelsons bluff is one the list made up.[^1]

A mapping site describes a cliff called Nelsons Bluff in McIntosh County, close to the hamlet of Raccoon Bluff.[^2] The same page lists Raccoon Bluff, a hamlet two miles southwest, among nearby places.[^2] Both the list and the mapping site place the name in McIntosh County.[^1][^2]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything remains there or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers (GA tower list with coordinates, elevation and GFC number), tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Nelsons Bluff Map - Cliff - McIntosh, Georgia, USA, Mapcarta, https://mapcarta.com/20769614 (accessed 2026-10-21).
