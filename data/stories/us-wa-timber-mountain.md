Timber Mountain sits in the Colville National Forest in Pend Oreille County, Washington, about 15 miles south of Ione.[^1][^2]

The first lookout here was a 50-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, developed about 1932.[^2][^3][^4] The present 41-foot treated timber tower, with an R-6 flattop live-in cab and catwalk, replaced it in 1959.[^1][^3][^4] Firelookout.com lists known lookout staff by name, from Roger Sherman in 1951 and 1952 to Dorothy Brennan in 1971.[^2] The Register and TrailChick say the tower was staffed until 1981. After that it was abandoned and fell into poor shape.[^1][^4]

Restoration began in 1995, when the Pend Oreille Anthropological Society and the Forest Service started work as a Passport in Time project.[^1][^3] Will Hite says lightning protection and a new roof went on in the summer of 2007, in preparation for a rental program.[^3] The Eastern Washington chapter's 2017 report describes a one-week Passport in Time session that replaced the plywood subfloor and painted the interior and exterior, with Forest Fire Lookout Association members volunteering.[^5] Will Hite gives five days of 2017 work, which included replacing the subfloor and painting the cab.[^3] Will Hite notes that stolen copper lightning protection still needed replacing.[^3]

Sources say the tower was used off and on for brief periods in fire season. Will Hite adds that it has also served as a vantage point after thunderstorms.[^3] The Register's September 2018 visit reports call the tower in good condition and say it needs no paint.[^1]

Today the access road is gated.[^3][^4] Will Hite says the gate stays locked all year, so the walk in is about 2.1 miles.[^3] TrailChick lists a 4.5-mile round trip with 1,100 feet of gain.[^4] The cab is locked.[^4] TrailChick says the catwalk was open on its 2018 visit, but that is not a standing permission, so check with the Colville National Forest before going up.[^4] The 2017 chapter report described plans to return the lookout to volunteer service and into the rental program.[^5] We found no current listing that shows it can be rented.
