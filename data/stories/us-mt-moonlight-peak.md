Moonlight Peak is a lookout site in the Lewis and Clark National Forest, in Lewis and Clark County, Montana.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page gives its legal location as 25N-11W-33.[^1]

The page quotes two notices from The Choteau Acantha. A June 23, 1938 notice assigned a lookout fireman to Moonlight Peak.[^1] A June 13, 1940 notice listed Moonlight among the stations where men would be employed on fire-prevention work that summer. That notice gives only the short name.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of standing Montana lookouts does not include Moonlight Peak.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the peak today, or whether the public may visit it.

[^2]: Montana Lookouts (Standing), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/mt-st/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
