The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register describes Big Shiney as a steel Aermotor tower built in 1928.[^1] For many years the Lackawanna Forestry District ran the tower, which stood in Springbrook Township.[^2] The register spells the name Big Shiney, while the newspapers of the time wrote Big Shinney and Big Shinny.[^1][^2][^3]

A windstorm blew the tower down in November 1950.[^1][^3] In early April 1951 the district took it off its official list of fire towers and said a replacement would go up near Dupont.[^2] That replacement was built on Tark Hill, on Suscon Road about five miles from Dupont.[^3] The Tark Hill tower was reported open in April 1952, with James Reap overseeing its observation post.[^3]

The Tark Hill tower was later taken down.[^3] A 1960 newspaper report says the Federal Aviation Administration wanted the Tark Hill site for a VORTAC facility, so that tower was dismantled, and a new 90-foot (27 m) steel tower was going up south of Dupont.[^3]

The register places the old site at US 204 and PA 33.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains there today.
