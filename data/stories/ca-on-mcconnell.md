The province's tower data places the McConnell Fire Tower at 46.893809, -80.647704, with a location accuracy of within 50 metres (164 ft).[^1] An online list of Ontario towers that were still standing places a McConnell Tower at Dewdney Lake, on the Chiniguchi River, in its Mid-Northern Ontario section.[^2] The two entries are matched here by name and area only.

The list says that, as of 2013, the Ontario Ministry of Natural Resources (MNR) had resolved to take down many of these towers, and it notes that the ministry no longer maintains them.[^2] It warns that climbing one is hazardous and asks visitors not to climb.[^2]

A 2014 forum thread gives conflicting answers. In July, a visitor who could not see the tower from Dewdney Lake asked whether it was still up.[^3] One reply said it would still be standing unless something had knocked it over during the winter or spring, while another poster believed it was no longer standing.[^3] Posters said the best view was from the middle and southern sections of the lake, and that it was visible only briefly while paddling north.[^3]

Later posts report its removal. A poster who flew over the area on 22 July wrote that it was gone, and another said in August that it had been taken out by helicopter the previous autumn.[^3] These are informal accounts, and no official source I found confirms either one.

No source I found describes what remains at the site today.
