Eagle Rock Mountain was a fire lookout tower in Botetourt County, Virginia.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a tower and marks it gone, at 37.63221, -79.83051.[^1][^2]

A topographic map site shows a tower feature nearby under a different name, Eagle Mountain Lookout, at 37.6340193, -79.8294904.[^3] The site gives the feature type as Tower and the elevation as 1,880 feet (573 meters). It shows the feature on the Eagle Rock USGS topo map.[^3] The two points lie close together, so the map feature may be the same site as the association's tower.[^1][^3]

The association's "gone" mark is the only word on the tower's condition. No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at either point, or says whether the land is open to visitors, so find out who owns the mountain before going onto it.
