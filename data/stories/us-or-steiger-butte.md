Steiger Butte is a lookout site in Klamath County, about three miles southeast of the Klamath Indian Agency.[^1] A 1925 listing calls it an emergency lookout.[^1] A 1927 annual forestry report lists the butte at 4,700 feet, with two main lookouts and a secondary one. The report says the butte was used only when smoke or haze blocked the main lookouts' view of the reservation's west side.[^1]

A Klamath County lookout blog lists the Klamath Indian Agency as the managing agency and dates construction only to the 1920s. It does not describe the structures.[^2] The blog says the butte was used on smoky days and stopped being used once Agency Butte was set up.[^2] Its Agency Butte entry dates the first lookout there, a crow's-nest platform in a tree, to 1930.[^2]

No source describes the butte today, including whether anything remains on it or whether visitors can reach it.
