Bald Mountain lies in Centre County, Pennsylvania.[^1] In 1908 a state forestry report called for lookout stations on several vantage points.[^1] It named a point on Bald Mountain Knob, along the road from Milroy to Potter's Bank, as especially desirable because the Bell and United telephone lines ran close by.[^1] The report proposed a fifty-foot tower there.[^1]

In 1914 a tree observation post went up at the site, with its viewing point 35 feet up.[^1] The register calls it a tree tower built that year, and says it was still in use around 1917.[^2] That was lower than the fifty-foot tower the 1908 report had proposed.[^1]

The register says the tower is no longer standing.[^2] The lookout history lists it as removed, with no date.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. No source we found says whether the site is open to the public.
