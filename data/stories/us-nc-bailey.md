A statewide list of North Carolina lookout towers places a fire tower named Bailey in Nash County, at about 35.85 north and 78.11 west.[^1] The list says it draws on the NC FFLA site and on USGS topographic maps.[^1] It also notes that a few observation towers and Coast Guard lookouts may be included, so a listing alone does not confirm a fire lookout.[^1] The North Carolina Forest Service's District 5 office serves Nash County.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public.

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: N.C. Forest Service - Contacts, N.C. Forest Service, https://ncforestservice.gov/Contacts/nash.htm (accessed 2026-10-21).
