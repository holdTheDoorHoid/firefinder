Maple Lake Lookout stood at about 7,554 feet in Idaho County.[^2][^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts site and firelookout.com both describe a two-story cabin topped by a cupola, built in 1925.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page calls it a D-6 cupola cabin from that year.[^3]

The sources do not agree on the forest. Forest Lookouts names only the Clearwater National Forest, while the other two pages name the Clearwater and Nez Perce forests together.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com places the lookout 14 miles south of the Powell Ranger Station.[^2]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site does not yet have a documented staffing history for the lookout.[^1]

Both the Idaho Fire Lookouts page and firelookout.com mark the lookout as gone.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page labels the lookout hike-in, but it also marks the lookout gone, so that label does not tell us whether the site can be visited now.[^1] Because two sources place the site in the Clearwater and Nez Perce forests, ask the Forest Service before going to look.[^1][^2]
