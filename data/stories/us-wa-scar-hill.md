Scar Hill is about 9 miles southeast of Aberdeen, in Grays Harbor County.[^2][^1] The land is Weyerhaeuser property.[^1][^2][^3]

According to the register, a tower on the property went up in the 1930s, was abandoned in the 1940s, and no longer stands.[^1] firelookout.com gives the same two decades.[^2] WillhiteWeb's visit notes are less sure. They say the tower is only "said to be" from the 1930s.[^3] The site is not in the Kresek Inventory or the Fish/Spring book, though it appears on firelookout.com with an unknown source.[^3] The page also says the lookout's exact spot on the summit is unknown.[^3]

When WillhiteWeb visited in February 2021, the summit held communications equipment: a benchmark area with three communication structures, a sign for the DNR Scar Hill Radio Site, and a tower on the south side of the summit.[^3] We found no source describing any remains of the lookout.

Access requires a permit. The most direct route from the highway is posted No Trespassing, even for permit holders, because of an active quarry. The recreational access point for permit holders is a gate to the north.[^3]
