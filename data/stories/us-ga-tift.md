A Georgia lookout tower list places a tower named tift in Tift County, at 31.48933, -83.51951, with 400 in its elevation column and no unit given.[^1] The list gives this tower two numbers, BLL-41139 and gfc-517.[^1]

A photograph on Wikimedia Commons is titled as a Georgia Forestry Commission tower on US 41 North in Tifton.[^2] The file credits Michael Rivera and is dated 3 May 2014.[^2] Its recorded camera position, 31.488847, -83.518972, sits close to the list's coordinates.[^2][^1] The photograph is released under a Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 license.[^2]

Statewide, the Georgia Forestry Commission shifted from staffed lookouts to aerial patrols during the late 1980s.[^3]

The 2014 photograph is the most recent source found, and it is linked to this tower only by camera position.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site today.
