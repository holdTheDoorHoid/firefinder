Joaquin was a lookout tower in Sandoval County, New Mexico, and the State built it rather than the Forest.[^1][^2] The history says State Forestry built and staffed the lookout in 1962, and that the work was probably finished later that year.[^1] Interior stairs climbed the steel tower, which was braced by many guy wires, to a small plywood cab fitted with a trapdoor.[^1] A small Quonset hut housed the living quarters.[^1] The history turns up no photographs of the lookout.[^1]

Forest inspectors visited in May 1963, when the tower had no cab and the lookout had no staff.[^1] Staffing in 1963 and 1964 fell to the State Fire Control Aid, and in 1966 Frank Gonzales was on duty there for State Forestry.[^1] Gonzales had a single radio, and he hauled water from Joaquin Canyon by jeep.[^1] Acreage on the San Diego Grant came to the Forest in stages during 1964 and 1965, and the Forest paid for the lookout's staffing once its portion was in hand.[^1] The Forest received fire reports from the lookout in 1965 and 1966.[^1]

According to the history, Joaquin was the one new lookout built in the effort to close detection gaps on the grant lands, and it saw only a few years of use.[^1] The Forest judged the structure unsafe and inadequate.[^1] A new steel tower was proposed to replace it, but it was never built.[^1] The anticipated 1968 program of work called for a steel tower 54 feet tall with a 14-foot-square cab.[^1] The Forest may first have meant to build it with Accelerated Public Works money, but the program closed before 1968, and the history says that probably derailed the project.[^1] The state structure came down in the late 1960s.[^1]

The history describes a concrete footing with a 1962 inscription, and it says that by 2014 the site had been reduced to foundations.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^2] No source we read describes the site after 2014 or says whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Fire Lookout History of the Santa Fe National Forest, npshistory.com (prepared for the Santa Fe National Forest Fire Management Program), https://npshistory.com/publications/usfs/region/3/santa-fe/fire-lookout-history.pdf (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: New Mexico Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
