The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower called Negrofoot in Hanover County. Its table also lists the same tower as relocated to Field Day of the Past in Goochland County.[^1]

Negro Foot is an unincorporated community in Hanover County, Virginia.[^2] A 1990 historic-resources survey of Hanover County lists a tavern on the Negrofoot property and calls Negrofoot an important stagecoach stop at a crossroads linked to Route 33.[^3]

Field Day of the Past traces its start to 1992, when a steam and gas engine show was first held in Goochland County.[^4] The show's Who We Are page says it has since moved its operations to Mount Airy Farm on Church Road, in Dinwiddie County.[^4]

The association's table gives the Goochland entry as relocated from Negrofoot, yet it also marks the tower as gone.[^1]

No source we could read describes what stands at the Goochland site today, or whether the tower moved when the show did. Anyone planning a visit should ask Field Day of the Past first.
