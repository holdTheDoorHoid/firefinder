A statewide list of Georgia lookout towers includes an entry named "butts new," in Butts County.[^1] The county is in west-central Georgia, and Jackson is its seat.[^2] The list gives the tower an elevation of 643 feet (196 m) and a set of coordinates. Its reference code is printed as "gfc-405?", with a question mark, so the number is not settled.[^1]

The same county has a separate entry called "Butts Lookout Tower," listed at 740 feet (226 m). By our calculation from the two sets of coordinates, the two points are about 5.7 km (3.5 miles) apart.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register says 29 lookouts are registered in Georgia, and none of its names includes "Butts."[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists two former Georgia sites, and neither has "Butts" in its name.[^4]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
