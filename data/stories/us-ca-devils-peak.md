Devil's Peak was a fire lookout station on the Georgetown district of the Eldorado National Forest.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places it in El Dorado County, where it is listed as a platform that is now gone.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page puts the peak under its Placer County heading, so the two sources disagree on the county.[^1][^2]

A 1962 article in the Mountain Democrat and Placerville Times, reprinted on the Forest Lookouts page, recalls Leland Hill's early years there.[^1] Over the two summers he was posted there, he put up the station and a barn.[^1] Mule trains hauled in the flooring and windows for its single room.[^1]

His only lookout was a tall sugar pine beside his cabin, with metal pole steps driven into the trunk until he could climb to about 50 feet.[^1]

The index lists the site as gone.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]
