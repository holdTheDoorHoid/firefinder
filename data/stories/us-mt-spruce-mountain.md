Spruce Mountain lies in Flathead County, Montana, about 27 miles southeast of Hungry Horse, in the Flathead National Forest, at an elevation of 6,180 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a tower that is now gone.[^2]

The firelookout.com page describes a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab in 1931, set one mile east-southeast of the prominent summit, and records that the lookout was abandoned in 1965.[^1] The Sanders County Ledger says L-4 cabs could stand on the ground or on pole or timber towers as tall as 100 feet.[^3]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
