Little Snowball Lookout went up in 1934 on a narrow ridge just north of Little Snowball Mountain, near the Blue Ridge Parkway's Craggy Gardens.[^1][^2][^5] The Big Ivy Civilian Conservation Corps camp built it, a 21-foot steel tower carrying a 14-by-14-foot live-in cab, to watch a fire-prone stretch of the Great Craggy Mountains.[^1][^2]

Only two people staffed the tower over its working life.[^2] Mote Allen, a former CCC worker who had helped build it, was the first lookout.[^3] When he was too old to enlist in World War II, the Forest Service hired him as fire warden.[^3] Cling Fabe Webb took over the tower in 1944. After Mote's retirement he served as its dispatcher for two decades, and the Forest Service eventually retired the tower.[^3] None of the sources we read names a fire the tower spotted.

The Forest Service decommissioned the tower in 1980, after aircraft had taken over spotting fires, and planned to demolish it.[^2][^5] Lloyd Allen, who had grown up in Big Ivy below the tower, bought it for $300 and took it apart by hand.[^2][^5] The sources differ on the timing. Get Up and Go Camping has him spending the year after the sale on the job.[^2] Big Ivy says he removed it around 1985 and kept the pieces at his house for twenty years.[^3] The Register says volunteers dismantled it and carried it out in the 1980s.[^1]

The tower was reassembled at Big Ivy Historical Park in Barnardsville, in the community it protected.[^3][^4] Get Up and Go Camping dates the re-erection to 2006, while Big Ivy dates the dedication to 2007, after a year of volunteer work.[^2][^3]

Today the restored tower stands at Big Ivy Historical Park, and it is an easy visit with no hike.[^2][^4] Visiting hours are hard to pin down, so phone before you drive out.[^2] The sources we read do not say whether visitors may climb it, so ask before you try.[^2] The original mountain site is a separate trip: a walk of more than seven miles there and back from Beetree Gap, where a small clearing holds four concrete blocks that once held up the tower.[^5]
