Martin Point never had a permanent lookout building.[^1] The site sits about 15 miles west of Missoula in Missoula County, at an elevation of 6,034 feet.[^1] It is listed under the Lolo National Forest.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list records it as a trailer site at 46.95827, -114.48847 and marks it gone.[^2]

Its lookout was a pop-up trailer, one of a small Forest Service fleet.[^1] Forest Service Region 1 came up with the trailer as a solution to temporary lookout needs, and the USFS built six in 1959. They have been used at several sites where a permanent structure was not necessary.[^1]

The mobile lookouts also came with a furnace, a fridge, an oven, propane-powered lights, hot running water and a shower.[^1] The page says most permanent lookouts envied those luxuries.[^1]

In the 1970s the trailers were pressed into service as control centers for aircraft at small airstrips when fires broke out.[^1] The page says every one has since been taken out of service.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the Martin Point site today or whether the public can reach it.
