A tower is listed as "pine mtn" in Harris County, Georgia, on a list of Georgia lookout towers kept by the tnlandforms.us site.[^1] The list puts it at 32.77367, -84.90277 and gives an elevation of 1,166, with no unit.[^1] Read as feet, that is 355 m.[^1] The list's map was last revised on 22 March 2016.[^1]

The same list has a "Harris County Lookout Tower" a few hundred metres away, and a different "pine mtn" in Polk County, so the name alone does not pick out this site.[^1] The list also says names for towers numbered 73 and above are its author's own creation, so "pine mtn" may not be a historical name.[^1] The sources do not say whether the two Harris County entries are one structure.[^1]

An October 29, 1955 item from The Columbus Ledger, reprinted on the Eastern US Lookouts site, describes Harris County as one of many Georgia counties whose forestry units are linked to the Georgia Forestry Commission.[^2] It names two of the unit's towers, Harris Tower and William Mountain Tower, along with their operators.[^2] The item does not say where either tower stood, so it cannot be tied to this site.[^2]

No source describes this site today. Whether a tower, footings or nothing remains, and whether the ground is open to visitors, is not recorded in the sources I found.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Harris County - FOREST LOOKOUTS (reprints an October 29, 1955 item from The Columbus Ledger), Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/harris-county.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
