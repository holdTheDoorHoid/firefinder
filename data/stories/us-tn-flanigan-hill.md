The Flanigan Hill lookout stands in Maury County, Tennessee, at an elevation of 1,165 feet.[^1] The Tennessee lookout list gives it the code 7-4 and marks it as standing.[^1]

The 2014 National Register form lists Flanigan Hill in Maury County among the extant towers as of March 26, 2014.[^2] The Tennessee list was last revised in January 2026 and still shows the tower as standing.[^1] The same form says the state's Division of Forestry was responsible for 208 fire lookout towers built between about 1930 and 1970.[^2]

We found no build date, builder, design, or height for this tower, and nothing about who staffed it or what fires it watched.[^1][^2] We also found no rental history.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register's list of Tennessee lookouts has 76 entries, and Flanigan Hill is not one of them.[^3]

We found nothing about public access or ownership. Please do not climb the tower unless its owner or the managing agency says it is open to visitors.
