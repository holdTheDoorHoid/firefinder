The Simmons Ridge site, recorded as a camp, sat about nine miles east-northeast of Elk City, in Idaho County, within the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Service list gives a camp there from 1938, abandoned in 1942.[^3] Firelookout.com gives the same dates, describing a camp in 1938 that was abandoned in 1942.[^2] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page tags the site as a hike-in, and as abandoned.[^1]

The Forest Service list was compiled in April 2010 by Cindy L. Schacher, an archaeological technician, from Ray Kresek's book on northwest lookouts and other miscellaneous sources.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list also includes a Simmons Ridge camp in Idaho County, abandoned, with coordinates about 11 km east of this record's map point. The sources read do not say whether it is the same camp.[^4]

No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the site now.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Service list's note says highlighted rows mark lookouts still in existence, but the plain text does not show which rows are highlighted, so this site's status stays unverified.[^3]
