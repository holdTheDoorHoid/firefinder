Hoffman Hill is in Washington County, Oregon, in the Oregon Department of Forestry's Tillamook FPA.[^1][^2]

The two lookout pages give slightly different distances from Forest Grove. Firelookout.com says 15 miles northwest,[^1] while the Forest Lookouts page says 16 miles north.[^2]

In 1931 a temporary platform was raised in the crowns of three trees, roughly 50 feet up.[^1][^2]

A 1935 Coast and Geodetic Survey station description, reprinted on the Forest Lookouts page, puts a survey mark 25 yards northwest of the lookout house.[^2]

A July 1938 Eugene Register-Guard item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, describes a warden on the lookout. When a conflagration threatened the peak, his wife and small son were cut off there.[^2] Forest Grove wardens rushed a crew of CCC firefighters, a pumper and 35 civilians to help.[^2]

The sources differ on how the lookout ended. Firelookout.com says it was abandoned in the 1950s.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page says only that it was removed, with no date.[^2] The FFLA list shows the site as a platform that is gone.[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, is left at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
