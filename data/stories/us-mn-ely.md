The Ely lookout stood just south of the town of Ely, in Saint Louis County, Minnesota. A side road and a short gravel road, reached from County Route 21, led to it.[^1]

A survey station description from 1934 puts a 90-foot steel tower on the site, and the Forest Service maintained it, the description says.[^1][^2] The survey disk was set in bedrock at the center of the tower's base.[^1] A 1959 recovery note found the marker in good shape.[^1]

In June 1964 the lookout was dismantled and moved to another location.[^1][^2] The sources we read do not say where it went or whether it still stands.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Ely site as gone.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the original site today, or whether the public may visit it.[^1][^3]
