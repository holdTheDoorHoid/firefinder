Sherwin Point was a temporary trailer lookout in Latah County, Idaho, about seven miles north of Bovill.[^1][^2] The lookout is listed under the Potlatch Timber Protective Association.[^1][^3] The Forest Lookouts page dates the trailer to about 1956 and credits that date to a source it names as Kresek.[^1] The firelookout.com page gives its elevation as 4,074 feet.[^2]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page says it does not yet have records of the lookout's staffing.[^3]

The lookout is listed as gone.[^4] None of the pages says when the trailer left the site or what became of it.[^1][^2][^3]

Today, no source describes what, if anything, remains at the site.[^1][^2][^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page labels the spot drive-up, but it does not say whether the public may visit.[^3]
