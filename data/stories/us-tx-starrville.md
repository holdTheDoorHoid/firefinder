Starrville, in northern Smith County, sits about a mile northeast of Starrville Mountain and roughly three miles east of Winona.[^1] A Methodist minister, Joshua Starr, bought the 640-acre Hall Survey in 1852 and began offering lots for sale in the town.[^1] The post office was renamed Starrville in 1857.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list includes a Starrville tower at 32.45357, -95.13280, and marks it as gone.[^2] A Texas tower list last revised in January 2011 gives a Starrville entry at the same point, tagged "TFS." The page names a Texas Forest Service ("TFS") list of 91 towers among its sources.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
