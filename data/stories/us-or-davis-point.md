The Forest Lookouts site lists Davis Point under the Oregon Department of Forestry.[^1] The site's only dated note, from a March 1986 visit, says the three-legged open platform tower had fallen over the edge of the point.[^1] The page describes the structure as temporary.[^1]

A map site lists Davis Point as a peak in Clatsop, Oregon, at 1,900 feet (579 m).[^2] A list of standing Oregon lookouts, which counts 156 of them as of July 2026, does not include Davis Point.[^3] That list also warns that its information may be out of date.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains on the point today, or whether the site is open to the public.

[^2]: Davis Point Map - Peak - Clatsop, Oregon, United States, Mapcarta, https://mapcarta.com/23790202 (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Oregon's Standing Lookouts - Every Lookout in Oregon, Every Lookout in Oregon (cherylhill.net), https://cherylhill.net/firelookouts/oregons-standing-lookouts/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
