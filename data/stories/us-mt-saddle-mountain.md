Saddle Mountain is in Lake County, about 11 miles south of St. Ignatius, at an elevation of 5,331 feet, on the Flathead Indian Reservation.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site files it under Lake County and the Flathead Indian Agency, at township 16 North, range 20 West, section 6.[^2]

The record starts in 1936 with an 18-foot steel tower and a frame cabin.[^1] A July 1959 Char-Koosta item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, reports that Joe Gradipe had resumed his post on Saddle Mountain, in the Arlee District.[^2]

The cabin was destroyed by vandals in 1963, and the tower was gone by 1969.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site lists the lookout as removed.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks the site gone, at 47.16948, -114.16940.[^3]

The firelookout.com entry says nothing about what remains at the site or whether the ground is open to visitors.[^1] No other source I found describes the site now, so anyone thinking of going should ask the land managers first.
