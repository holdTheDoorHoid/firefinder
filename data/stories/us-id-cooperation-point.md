The Cooperation Point lookout stood about eight miles southwest of Powell Ranger Station, in Idaho County on the Clearwater National Forest, at an elevation of 6,785 feet.[^1]

The name may point to shared work. A place-name note in the Clearwater forest history says the Lolo Forest built a lookout on the point to watch for fires in the Warm Springs area, working with the Clearwater.[^2] That note calls the peak "Cooperation Mountain," so the match with this point is likely but not certain.

The tower was a pole structure with an L-4 cab, dated to 1934.[^1][^3]

Four lookout pages and lists give 1950 as the year it was destroyed.[^1][^3][^4][^5] One Idaho page tags the site "Abandoned" in its header, but the history on the same page also says destroyed in 1950.[^3]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains on the point today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
