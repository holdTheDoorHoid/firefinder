Peterson Point sits at 6,194 feet (1,888 m) in the Ochoco National Forest, in Wheeler County, Oregon, about 8 miles south-southeast of Mitchell.[^1][^2]

In August 1928 a new telephone line was completed to near Peterson Point, set to end at a secondary observation point, according to a Central Oregonian item quoted on the Weebly page.[^3] A March 1929 item from the same paper announced that a lookout tower would be built there for smoky weather and after electrical storms.[^3]

The register dates a crow's nest and tent cabin to 1930. The Weebly page names Dick Helms as the emergency fireman posted there that year.[^3][^1] In 1941 they were replaced by a 66-foot wooden L-6 tower and cabin.[^1][^2]

The register says the tower and cabin were taken down in the 1950s, while firelookout.com says they were destroyed in that decade.[^1][^2]

No source read describes what, if anything, remains on the point today.[^1][^2][^3]
