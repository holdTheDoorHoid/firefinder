High Place is listed as a fire lookout in Ozark County, Missouri.[^1] The page's entry gives the legal location as 24N-11W-34, meaning township 24 North, range 11 West, section 34, and names the Mark Twain National Forest as the agency.[^1]

For context, a 2022 Missouri Conservationist article on the state's fire towers says that in 1966 the state conservation department operated 102 towers, and the U.S. Forest Service operated 43 in Missouri.[^2] The same article says most of Missouri's towers stood 100 feet high and came from Aermotor, a Chicago company.[^2]

No source I could read describes the site today, so I cannot say whether a tower still stands or whether visitors can reach it. Check with the Mark Twain National Forest before you go, and do not climb any tower without permission.

[^1]: High Place - Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/high-place.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Shooting the Smoke, Missouri Conservationist, Missouri Department of Conservation, https://mdc.mo.gov/magazines/missouri-conservationist/2022-07/shooting-smoke (accessed 2026-10-21).
