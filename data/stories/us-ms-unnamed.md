That is the only description we found of where it stands.[^1]

The Mississippi Secretary of State's public lands list, dated July 29, 2020, includes a "Lawrence County Tower" under the state Forestry Commission.[^2] It is the only entry we found with a Lawrence County name.[^2] The list gives no coordinates, build date or status for that entry, so we could not confirm that it is this point.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register does not have a Lawrence County entry that we could find.[^3] Its Mississippi list counts 65 lookouts in the state.[^3] So the record's origin and history remain open.

We found no staffing or fire history for this site. We do not know whether a structure stands at the point today, whether it is open to visitors, or who looks after it. Anyone curious should ask the state Forestry Commission before going to the site, and nobody should climb a structure without permission.
