Gant Lookout stood on Gant Mountain in Lemhi County, Idaho, about 23 miles west-northwest of Salmon.[^1] The site is listed at an elevation of 8,276 feet, and Forest Lookouts places it in the Salmon National Forest.[^1][^2][^3]

The lookout was a two-story frame cabin on the northernmost point of the mountain. It is dated to 1934.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists one known staff member, Joseph O. Gjertson, for 1939, citing a former Forest Fire Lookout Registry.[^2]

In July 1951 the Forest Service announced plans to dispose of a two-mile spur telephone line that ran to the lookout. Forest Lookouts quotes the notice from The Recorder Herald.[^3] The notice concerns the telephone line, not the cabin.[^3]

Two sites give 1970 as the year the cabin was gone.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts marks the lookout as removed, without a date.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts classes the site as a hike-in, though the cabin is gone.[^2] None of the three listings describes what, if anything, remains at the site today or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3] Check with the Forest Service before you go.
