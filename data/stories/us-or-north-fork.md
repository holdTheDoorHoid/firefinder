North Fork Station is a former lookout site in Clackamas County, Oregon, on Oregon Department of Forestry district headquarters property.[^1][^3] The register dates the first lookout there to 1937, a 100-foot twin tree crow's nest, and says a steel tower replaced it in 1940.[^1][^3] A June 1920 newspaper item quoted on the Forest Lookouts site shows the station already had a ranger cabin by then, and that a crew was running a telephone line to it.[^2]

The sources do not agree on when the steel tower went up. The register and firelookout.com put it in 1940.[^1][^3] A district annual report quoted on the Forest Lookouts page says a 140-foot tower was erected at North Fork Station in 1957. It also says the platforms and railings were finished in 1958, and that an electric manlift was installed in place of a safety rail.[^2] We could not reconcile the two dates.

A 1959 Oregonian item quoted on the same page describes a 145-foot steel tower erected near Molalla for the Clackamas-Marion Forest Fire Patrol, with an electric lift instead of stairs.[^2] A 1961 item says a fire patrol association of forest landowners maintained that tower.[^2] The page does not say whether it is the same structure as the 140-foot tower at North Fork.[^2]

No source we read names anyone who worked the North Fork tower. The 1959 item also says lookouts travel up and down the Molalla tower by electric lift.[^2]

The register and firelookout.com both list the tower as gone, and neither gives a removal date.[^1][^3] We found no source describing what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the grounds are open to visitors. Anyone interested should ask the Oregon Department of Forestry before going.
