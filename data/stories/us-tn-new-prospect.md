The New Prospect Lookout Tower stands in Lawrence County, Tennessee.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register says the Tennessee Division of Forestry administers it and calls the site an active forestry site.[^1] The Register describes an 80-foot Aermotor LS-40 tower.[^1]

The Register gives the elevation as 1,009 feet (308 meters).[^1] A statewide tower inventory gives 1004 feet, about five feet lower, and lists the tower as standing.[^2]

Neither source says when the tower was built or who built it, and I found no other source that does. The Register's only dates are a site visit on November 26, 2022 and the tower's registration on August 9, 2023.[^1]

The sources do not say whether the public may visit or climb the tower. Because the Register describes an active forestry site, ask the Division of Forestry before going, and do not climb the tower.

[^1]: New Prospect Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register (nhlr.org), http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/tn/new-prospect-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-08).
[^2]: Tennessee lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-08).
