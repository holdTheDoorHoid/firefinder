Brown Mountain rises to 7,311 feet, about 30 miles east of Medford in Klamath County, Oregon.[^2][^1] A 1945 newspaper gave its height as 6,700 feet.[^3] Two site listings place it in the Winema National Forest, while a 1945 newspaper report put it in the Rogue River National Forest district.[^1][^2][^3]

In July 1945 a fire lookout was posted on the crest for the first time.[^3] The site record gives 1945 as the build year and says the materials were parachuted in, because the mountain is a jumble of basalt boulders.[^1] No pack trail climbed the mountain, so lumber, a stove, a radio, fire-finding instruments and a tent were dropped by plane.[^3]

Eugene Pyeatt of Klamath Falls was selected to staff the station.[^3] A newspaper said he would live in a tent through the worst of the fire season, and that radio would be the only link to camp.[^3]

The records disagree about the start. Firelookout.com lists a cabin in 1929 that it says was destroyed in the 1960s.[^2] The 1945 newspaper accounts describe that year's post as the first lookout on the crest, which does not fit an earlier cabin there.[^3]

The site record says the structure is now gone.[^1] Another listing marks it simply as removed.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the public can reach it, so the present state of the site is unknown.
