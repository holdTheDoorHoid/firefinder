The Cromwell lookout, also called the Skunk Island tower, stands in Carlton County.[^1] A Forest Lookouts history page dates a 100-foot Aermotor tower there to 1949.[^1] A federal survey description written in 1952 by the Coast and Geodetic Survey gives a steel frame with four legs, about 80 feet tall, capped by an enclosed viewing deck. It places the tower about three and three-quarter miles northeast of Cromwell.[^1]

The two heights disagree, and I could not settle which is right. The 1949 entry gives 100 feet, while the 1952 description says about 80 feet.[^1]

I found no record of who staffed the tower, when it was staffed, or any notable fire it was used for.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^2] Wikidata places it within the Fond du Lac State Forest and links a Wikimedia Commons photo of it.[^3]

I found no current statement about public access or climbing. Check with the land manager before any visit, and do not climb the tower without permission.
