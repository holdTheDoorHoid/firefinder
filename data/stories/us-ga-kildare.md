Kildare is on the Georgia lookout tower list kept by tnlandforms.us. The list places it in Effingham County at an elevation of 126 (the list gives no unit; read as feet, about 38 m), at 32.52872, -81.45686. It is also tagged "gfc-126" on the list.[^1]

Effingham was one of Georgia's original counties, formed on February 5, 1777, out of the colonial parishes of St. Matthew and St. Phillip. Springfield is its county seat, and the town was established in 1799.[^2]

No source I found describes the Kildare site today, including whether a tower or any footing remains, or whether the land is open to visitors.
