Mount Harriman, in Orange County, carried a platform fire tower, according to the Forest Fire Lookout Association's New York county list, which marks the tower gone.[^1]

The same list gives a different tower, on Jackie Jones Mountain in Rockland County, a separate entry with its own coordinates, marked standing.[^1] A hike write-up places that tower in Harriman State Park.[^4] The sources disagree on whether it can be visited. The chapter's homepage says the Jackie Jones towers are closed pending inspections.[^2] The chapter's tower list calls the Jackie Jones tower open to the public, and says it was restored in 2019.[^3] A hiking post from November 2021 reported the tower fenced off and closed.[^4] Treat it as closed, and do not climb it.

No source read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit of Mount Harriman today, or whether that site is open to visitors.
