A tower called Long Lane Branch stood in Newberry County, South Carolina, according to a fire lookout history site, which lists its owner as National Forest.[^1] The same entry marks it as removed.[^1] OpenStreetMap maps a "Long Lane Lookout Tower" at 34.39458, -81.59177, which appears to be the same site.[^2]

Across the state, the Forestry Commission wound down its tower network in 1993, together with several other state and federal agencies.[^3] A Columbia magazine piece says aircraft, satellites and cellular phones had made the network obsolete for reporting forest fires, and that towers taken out of service have been up for auction since 2003.[^3]

Today, the OpenStreetMap entry still carries the name "Long Lane Lookout Tower" at that spot, but no source says whether a tower stands there now or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
