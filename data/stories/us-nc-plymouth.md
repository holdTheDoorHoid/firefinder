Plymouth is a lookout tower in Washington County, North Carolina.[^2] The town of the same name is the seat of Washington County, according to Wikipedia.[^3] The Forest Lookouts page for the state gives the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources as its agency.[^1] It says the Civilian Conservation Corps started raising a steel tower 99 feet 9 inches tall there in April 1936, and that the Emergency Conservation Works program covered $1,770.21 of the cost.[^1] The same entry adds that a crew from Camp P-67 of the CCC completed the work on August 13, 1936.[^1] The tnlandforms.us list gives the tower's coordinates as 35.86999, -76.74083.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today or whether the site is open to the public.
