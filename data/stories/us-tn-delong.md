DeLong is listed as a fire lookout tower in Hamilton County, at about 1,065 feet (325 m), by Tennessee Landforms.[^1] A 2014 National Register form lists it among the Tennessee Division of Forestry towers that were extant that year.[^2]

The Tennessee Division of Forestry (TDF) is credited in the form with 208 lookout towers built around 1930 to 1970. The form adds that many of them are gone or awaiting demolition.[^2] It also says surviving records of the towers' construction are scarce, which is why the exact number and locations of the 1930s towers are unknown.[^2]

The sources disagree on the tower's present state. Tennessee Landforms marks DeLong as historical, which may mean it is no longer standing, while the 2014 form counts it as extant.[^1][^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.

[^1]: Towers list, Tennessee Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Tennessee Division of Forestry Fire Lookout Towers, 1933 to 1975, National Register multiple property documentation form, National Park Service, https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/GetAsset/NRHP/64501237_text (accessed 2026-10-21).
