German Ridge's fire tower stood in the German Ridge area of Perry County, in the Hoosier National Forest.[^2] The Forest Service's list gives the tower as 80 feet tall, of steel, with picnic tables.[^1]

The tower appears on a 1953 topographic map of the Rome quadrangle.[^2] A 1997 National Geographic trail map of the forest shows it too, but later editions of that trail map leave it off.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's 2018 Indiana Chapter annual report includes a 2019 work plan in which its writer still hopes to interview the widow of the German Ridge towerman.[^3]

The tower is no longer standing.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Indiana list marks it as gone.[^4]

On a June 2024 visit the author found one base footer, still visible under the gravel in a parking area at the end of a forest road.[^2] A photo caption on the Indiana Fire Towers page places a German Ridge sign at the parking spot where the tower stood.[^2] The lot is a trailhead parking area on national forest land, and the forest road was in decent shape when the author visited.[^2]
