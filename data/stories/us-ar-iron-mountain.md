Iron Mountain Tower stands on the highest point of a mountain about six miles east of Mountain View, in Stone County.[^1] A Coast and Geodetic Survey description from 1953 already describes a four-legged steel tower, with a steel and wooden stairway climbing to a glass-enclosed cabin at the top.[^1] The Register lists the build date as unknown.[^2] It gives the site's elevation as 1,560 feet and says the Arkansas Forestry Commission administers the tower.[^2]

The sources do not say who staffed the tower or what fires it watched.

The Register's entry is dated April 10, 2023.[^2] A visitor's September 2020 visit says the tower sits outside Mountain View and is no longer maintained.[^2] No later check turned up, so the tower's standing status rests on that 2020 visit.

The sources do not say whether the public can go up it. The Forestry Commission administers the tower, and the Register says it is no longer maintained, so check with the Commission before going, and do not climb an unmaintained steel tower.[^2]
