Round Mountain's lookout began as a state station in Township 11, Range 8 of Aroostook County. The state built it in 1909.[^1] In 1914 a temporary wooden lookout tower there was repaired.[^1] In 1915 a new lookout station went up, the summit was cleared of trees, and the telephone line and trail were put in good order.[^1] A 48-foot steel tower was erected in 1918, and a 1920 inventory still lists it at that height.[^1]

A 1919 Maine Forest Service panoramic map, centred on the tower, also places it in Township 11, Range 8.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the steel tower as standing, with an asterisk showing that its cab is no longer there.[^3] The sources do not say when the cab came down, and I found no current information on public access or ownership. Please do not climb this tower until the Maine Forest Service has confirmed that it is open to visitors.
