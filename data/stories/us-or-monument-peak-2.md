Monument Peak sits in Malheur County, Oregon, on land the Bureau of Land Management manages.[^1] A 1965 survey note places it about 11 miles southeast of Juntura, while a 1963 newspaper item puts it at 17 miles from Juntura.[^1]

The earliest record is from 1958, when firelookout.com lists a fire finder and a trailer used as living quarters on the peak.[^2] The Rex's Fire Lookout Page dates a wood-frame cabin, 15 feet 6 inches square and set on a concrete slab, to about 1965.[^1]

An August 1958 newspaper item reported two temporary lookouts that fire season, one of them on Monument Peak east of Juntura.[^1] Firelookout.com names Mr. and Mrs. Lloyd Edmunson as lookout personnel for 1958 and 1959.[^2] In the summer of 1963, Clara Powell and Altha Hill were working the station for the BLM, according to the Record-Courier.[^1]

Firelookout.com lists the lookout as gone.[^2] The Rex page says it was removed, but it gives no date.[^1]

A 1965 federal survey recovery note on the Rex page places a survey marker in a rock outcrop under a 6.8-foot cairn, on the southern and highest of the Jones Buttes, about 30 feet northeast of the Monument Peak lookout.[^1] No source says whether that marker, any footings or anything else still stands there, or whether the summit is open to visitors today.[^1][^2]
