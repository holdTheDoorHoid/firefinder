Birch Hill's lookout stood on Chippewa National Forest land in Itasca County.[^1][^2] A 1959 survey described it as steel, with four legs and an observation room on top, and put its overall height at 110 feet.[^1][^2] The statewide A-Z list's height column reads 100 feet, so the sources disagree on the height.[^2]

The A-Z list gives the removal as May 1972.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page gives a different date for the sale. It says bids for the sale of the tower were opened on June 8, 1972, and that airplane patrols had by then proven better than the tower.[^1] The two dates do not agree.

As of September 2023, the A-Z list says tower footings remain at the site.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list records the tower as gone.[^3] No source I read says whether the footings can be reached or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Birch Hill, Forest Lookouts (includes a 1959 National Geodetic Survey station description and a 1972 note), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/birch-hill1.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Minnesota Fire Lookout Towers A-Z list, PDF, Wikimedia Commons, https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/9f/A-Z_List-Towers.pdf (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Minnesota Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
