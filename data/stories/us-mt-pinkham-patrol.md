Pinkham Patrol was a Kootenai National Forest lookout in Lincoln County, Montana, about nine miles south of Eureka, at an elevation of 5,463 feet.[^1] The Montana list places it at 48.74602 north and 115.04694 west.[^2]

Firelookout.com dates a camp and a 72-foot observation tower here to 1924, and says the site was abandoned in 1948.[^1]

That summer, entry to federal lands in the Pinkham Creek drainage required a permit, from about the first of August until the first heavy rain in September.[^3] A patrolman's memoir by Sam Billings says that after the closure ended, some crews were assigned to erect a new 72-foot tower on Pinkham Ridge, and that the work stopped after the first heavy snows.[^3] The listing and the memoir may describe the same tower, or two different ones.[^1][^3]

The Montana list marks the tower gone.[^2] Neither listing says what remains at the site today or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]
