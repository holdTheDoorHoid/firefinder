The Chandler Lookout is a 100-foot tower in Henderson County, Texas, that the National Historic Lookout Register dates to 1970.[^1]

It was put up as part of the state's push to grow its fire tower network.[^1] A 1970 Texas Forest News article says the Texas Forest Service, which operates the towers, had four of the 100-foot towers up and the rest on the way.[^2] Two of the new towers were planned for Henderson County, and the article places one about four miles northwest of Chandler.[^2]

The Register lists Macon Carver among the first lookouts employed at the tower, with work at the site by September 1970.[^1] Its entry is dated March 17, 2019, and includes a drone photo and video from that March.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list shows the tower as standing.[^3] The available sources do not say whether the public can visit or climb it, and they name no fires it watched. Please check with the owner before any visit, and do not climb it without permission.
