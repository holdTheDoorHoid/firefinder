Andesite was a 40-foot pole tower in Madison County, Montana, raised in 1952 about 16 miles east-southeast of Ennis.[^1] It stood about a quarter mile east of a ridgetop, at an elevation of 8,500 feet.[^1]

The listing calls it a private co-op site, and the Forest Lookouts page gives the cooperator as private.[^1][^2]

The listing dates its abandonment to 1957, though it puts a question mark after the year.[^1] A Forest Lookouts page marks the tower as removed but gives no date.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list also marks it as gone.[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. Both listings describe the site as private, so anyone curious should ask the landowner before going to look.[^1][^2]
