A Georgia list of fire lookout towers includes one called Lakeland, in Lanier County. The list, on a page marked as revised on 22 March 2016, places the site at 31.05295, -83.06906, with an elevation of 183 (the list gives no unit).[^1] Its entry carries three codes: BLL-65152, usgs and gfc-814.[^1]

In the late 1980s the state forestry agency started moving away from staffed towers and toward air patrols.[^2] The same source adds that many of the towers were disposed of by scrapping and sale.[^2]

Today, no source we could reach describes what remains at the Lakeland site, or whether the public can get to it. Check with the Georgia Forestry Commission before going to look.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Georgia Firehouses & Towers, Vanishing Georgia, https://vanishinggeorgia.com/tag/georgia-firehouses-towers/page/2/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
