Bearwallow Lookout stands on Bearwallow Ridge, on the line between Tazewell and Buchanan counties in southwestern Virginia.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register says it was one of many towers on ridges overlooking the route of today's Coal Heritage Trail.[^1]

The sources disagree about when it went up. A Harrisonburg Daily News item dated October 28, 1929, which Forest Lookouts quotes, says a 62-foot steel tower had already been built on the ridge.[^2] The Register gives 1933 as the build year and describes a 60-foot Aermotor tower.[^1] I found nothing that explains the gap, so both dates are given as the sources state them. The height is 60 or 62 feet depending on the source.[^1][^2] The sources I read do not say why this ridge was chosen.

I found no staffing history or named fires for the tower in the sources I read.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^3] The Register names Arthur Stiltner as its administrator.[^1] It recorded the tower on January 23, 2017, as US 1171, VA 37.[^1] The Register also says access to the tower is limited.[^1] The sources I read say nothing about its use after that, so its condition today is unverified. Do not climb the tower, and ask before any visit.
