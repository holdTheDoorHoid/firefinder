The Georgia lookout tower list places a tower called "bonneyman" in Pierce County, at 31.2575 degrees north and 82.29722 degrees west, and 133 feet (40.5 m) above sea level.[^1] The marker's datasheet gives a height of 132.94 feet (40.521 m), which agrees.[^2] The page says the list was prompted by a gap: the FFLA site had no Georgia entries in 2010.[^1] Its names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own, so "bonneyman" is the compiler's label.[^1]

The list's note on the entry reads "datasheet BS0174", which points to a survey marker of that number from the National Geodetic Survey (NGS).[^1][^2] The marker's datasheet gives the same position as the list, to the second, but says that position was scaled from a map, with an estimated accuracy of plus or minus 6 seconds, so the exact spot is uncertain.[^2]

The datasheet places the marker about 4.9 miles northeast of Waycross, along U.S. Highway 82.[^2] When the Coast and Geodetic Survey described it in 1961, the marker lay near the southwest edge of a concrete base that supported the northwest leg of a Forest Service fire tower.[^2] So a tower stood here by 1961.

The 1978 recovery note says that tower had been dismantled.[^2] It mentions the concrete foundation that had held one of its legs, with the marker 1.5 feet southwest of that foundation's center.[^2]

The datasheet still lists the marker under current survey control, but its last recorded condition is from 1978, when it was noted as good.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site now, or whether it is open to the public.
