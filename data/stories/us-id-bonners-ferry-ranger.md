Bonners Ferry Ranger Station Lookout was a 40-foot steel tower that stood at the ranger station, about a mile southwest of Bonners Ferry in Boundary County, Idaho.[^1][^2] The sources place the site in the Kaniksu National Forest, at an elevation of 1,880 feet.[^1][^2][^4] The association's list classes it as a tower and gives its coordinates as 48.67327, -116.33885.[^3]

The tower dates from the 1930s.[^1][^2]

It was gone by 1960.[^1][^2][^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page lists the site as drive-up and removed. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Idaho lookouts marks the tower as gone.[^2][^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the ranger station today. None says whether the grounds are open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page is dated September 16, 2025.[^2]
