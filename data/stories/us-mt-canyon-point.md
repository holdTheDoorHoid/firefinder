Canyon Point is a former fire lookout in Flathead County, Montana, on the Flathead National Forest, about 18 miles southeast of Hungry Horse.[^1] The Forest Lookouts listing places it at township 29 North, range 17 West, section 35.[^2] Both lookout pages agree that it sits in Flathead County on the Flathead National Forest.[^1][^2]

The firelookout.com page dates an L-4 cab at the site to 1932, on the mid-slope between Canyon Creek and Clorinda Creek.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list calls it a ground lookout and gives its coordinates as 48.23092 north, 113.74179 west.[^3]

According to the same firelookout.com page, the cab was destroyed in 1960.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list now shows the lookout as gone.[^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
