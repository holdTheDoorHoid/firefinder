Devil's Nose is a former lookout site in Calaveras County, California.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts files it under the Stanislaus National Forest and marks it as removed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it with the type "Ground" and the status "Gone," at 38.46280, -120.42042.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^2]: California Lookouts (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/ca/ca-co/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
