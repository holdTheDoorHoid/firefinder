The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers places a tower named Wheeler in Wheeler County, at 32.15871 north and 82.74333 west.[^1] Its entry carries the codes BLL-64713 and gfc-511, and the list's header is stamped as revised on March 22, 2016, so the entry may be older than today.[^1]

Across Georgia, the Georgia Forestry Commission began shifting from staffed towers to air patrols in the late 1980s, and many of the towers were scrapped and sold.[^2]

No source here describes the Wheeler site now, so this story cannot say whether a tower stands there, what remains, or whether the spot is open to visitors. Until access is confirmed, do not assume a tower at this spot is open to climb.[^1]
