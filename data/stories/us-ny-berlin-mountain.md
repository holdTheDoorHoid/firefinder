The Taconic Mountains include Berlin Mountain, which rises 2,818 feet.[^1] It lies in Berlin, Rensselaer County, New York, with the state line just east of the summit.[^1]

Massachusetts put up a 50-foot steel lookout on the mountain around 1915.[^1] Two such towers were built to replace the Mount Greylock tower, which had poor visibility, and this was one of them.[^1] For about three years Vermont, New York and Massachusetts shared the operating costs, and Massachusetts used this tower and two others, on Osceola and Mount Everett, to report fires to the nearest New York fire warden.[^1]

In 1922 New York added observation stations on two other Rensselaer County hills.[^1] In late 1923 Massachusetts moved the tower to Brodie Mountain in Hancock, in Berkshire County.[^1][^2][^3] Only the footings are left on Berlin Mountain.[^1]

At Brodie Mountain, a 1952 notice said a tower there would return to service.[^3] A May 1954 newspaper item said the tower had been closed since before World War II, and that the state wanted to reopen it but did not own the right of way.[^3] A 50-foot tower was built there in 1967, and a new tubular steel tower was erected in 2009.[^3]

A 2026 hiking guide says the Hancock tower, which stands on East Mountain, has recently gone out of service and now mainly carries radio and microwave gear.[^4] The guide adds that public access is not officially designated and that the route runs across several private parcels.[^4] It also reports that the cabin beside the former fire tower survives.[^4] The guide's account, a tower that has kept watch since 1923, does not clearly match the 2009 tubular steel tower in the Forest Lookouts entry, so these sources do not settle which structure stands there now.[^3][^4]

Several hiking trails approach the summit of Berlin Mountain from the east.[^1] Protected conservation land covers much of the upper slopes and summit, and the 37-mile Taconic Crest Trail runs across the mountain.[^1]
