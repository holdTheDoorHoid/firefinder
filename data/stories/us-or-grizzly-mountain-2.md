Grizzly Mountain stands at about 5,072 feet (1,546 m) in Klamath County, Oregon, on the Parker Mountain topographic map.[^3] The Klamath Forest Protective Association (KFPA) is the managing agency the sources name for the site.[^2] The KFPA's 23rd annual report, cited for 1930, describes a rough road made to reach the summit.[^1]

The Klamath County Fire Lookouts blog dates the construction of the lookout to 1931 and lists its structures as unknown.[^2] A June 3, 1931 Evening Herald item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, says the peak, which the paper called Grizzly Peak, had a staffed lookout in what it called the Jenny Creek section.[^1]

The blog adds that staffing lasted a single season and that the site came before Parker Mountain.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site today.

Other lookouts share the name. The National Historic Lookout Register's Grizzly Mountain Lookout is in Curry County, Oregon, and is a different site.[^4]
