A fire tower once stood on the rocky summit of Buffalo Mountain in Floyd County, Virginia, near the Blue Ridge Parkway.[^1]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site on July 30, 2018, with Kristin Reynolds as nominator.[^1] It lists the Virginia Department of Forestry as administrator.[^1]

The register puts the removal before 1987, most likely in 1986, around the time the area joined the Buffalo Mountain Natural Area Preserve.[^1]

Anyone can visit the preserve, which takes in the mountain's summit and slopes.[^2] Visitors find a 10-car gravel parking area and three hiking trails, and they must stay on marked trails.[^3]

Wikipedia describes a trail of about one mile to the summit.[^2] A January 2026 WVTF report gives the trail as two miles.[^4] It also says the state, working with The Conservation Fund, has added 975 acres, bringing the preserve to more than 2,100 acres.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the tower on the summit today.
