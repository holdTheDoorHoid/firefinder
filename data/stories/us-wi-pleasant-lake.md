The Pleasant Lake fire tower stood in Iron County, at the survey location 44N-1E-34.[^1] A 1950 map sheet of the Butternut area, based on field work done in 1940, names the Pleasant Lake Fire Tower and says fire prevention in the area is focused on it and the Butternut tower.[^2]

In a Wisconsin Public Radio report, DNR officials said the state would no longer use its 72 fire lookout towers, most of them built in the 1930s. Instead, the DNR planned to watch for fires more often from airplanes.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Pleasant Lake tower as gone, with its point at 46.24421 north and 90.35445 west.[^4] No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the land is open to visitors.
