The Tennessee lookout list gives a tower called Arnold AFB, in Franklin County, with an elevation of 1,112 feet (339 m), and marks it removed.[^1] Wikipedia places Arnold Air Force Base in Coffee and Franklin counties, Tennessee, next to the city of Tullahoma.[^2] The base is named after General Henry "Hap" Arnold.[^2] The sources checked do not say whether the tower relates to the base. No source describes what remains at the site now, or whether it is open to the public.
