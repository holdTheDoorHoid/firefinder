Arrowwood National Wildlife Refuge, in Stutsman and Foster counties, North Dakota, was established on September 4, 1935, and the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service manages it.[^1] A steel lookout tower was erected on the refuge in 1936, according to a compiled list of North Dakota lookouts, and the list records it as demolished.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association numbers this tower 2 and places it at 47.18580 north and 98.82932 west, and lists it as removed.[^3] The same list records a first steel tower on the refuge, also erected in 1936 and also demolished.[^2] The refuge drew 14,500 visitors in 2004.[^1] The refuge runs for 16 miles beside the James River and mixes wetlands, forest and prairie.[^1] No source describes what remains at this tower site today, or whether the spot is open to the public.
