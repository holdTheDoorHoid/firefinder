Middleton is a Hardeman County, Tennessee, lookout site whose tower has been moved. The site sits at about 630 feet above sea level.[^1] Tom Dunigan's statewide list gives the tower the number 8-26.[^1] The list's numbers are state forest tower numbers in district-tower form, taken from a 1976 list of towers.[^1] The table shows the former location of towers as well as standing ones, so the entry marks where this tower stood.[^1]

The Tennessee Division of Forestry was responsible for building 208 fire lookout towers, the state's National Register listing says, between about 1930 and 1970.[^2] That listing covers the division's towers from 1933 to 1975.[^2]

Dunigan's list marks Middleton as moved.[^1] No source I found describes the original site today, and none says where the tower stands now.

[^1]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Tennessee Division of Forestry Fire Lookout Towers, 1933 to 1975 (National Register multiple property listing), National Park Service NPGallery, https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/GetAsset/NRHP/64501237_text (accessed 2026-10-21).
