Blondy Pass lies in Hot Springs County, about 30 miles west of Thermopolis.[^1] The firelookout.com listing marks the lookout as gone and places the site on the Wind River Indian Reservation.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wyoming inventory files Blondy Pass under a heading that mixes lookout sites with non-lookout structures, with the type unknown and the status gone.[^2] The same section says these sites will be shifted onto the lookout listings if documentation shows they were used as regular fire lookouts.[^2]

A 2017 notice from the Thermopolis Independent Record announced an outing to Blonde Pass, to be hosted by a historical society.[^3] According to the notice, the old stage road over the pass lies on the Wind River Indian Reservation and is shut to the public.[^3] Two named people helped secure permission to use the byway.[^3] The notice spells the name differently and does not mention a lookout, so it may or may not describe this same place.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains of a lookout at the site today. Public access to the road is restricted, though the 2017 notice says permission was secured to use it.[^3]
