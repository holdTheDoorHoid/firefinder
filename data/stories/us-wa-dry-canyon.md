Dry Canyon Lookout stood in Pend Oreille County, on Colville National Forest land.[^1] The Register gives the elevation as 3,770 feet, while Willhite lists 3,778 feet for the lookout.[^1][^3]

The lookout was built in 1937 as a 10-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2][^3]

The Register says the tower was removed in the 1950s, while firelookout.com and Willhite both say it was destroyed in that decade.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts page also lists the site as removed.[^4]

Willhite is the most detailed source we found, but his text does not describe what remains on the summit.[^3] His two photos captioned "Summit in 2024" have no description.[^3]

To reach the site, go to Ione, cross the Ione Bridge, and take Sullivan Lake Road about four miles to the signed Dry Canyon Road.[^3] At a three-way junction, take the rightmost road, which is much rougher than the other two.[^3] Willhite advises high clearance and good tires for that road.[^3] The road ends at a wide grassy spot.[^3] From there, the site is about a quarter mile on foot. The old road, which is no longer drivable, fades out about halfway, and the rest is bushwhacking through mostly easy open terrain.[^3] Willhite notes that the road ends somewhere other than where the map shows it.[^3] Check current access before you go.
