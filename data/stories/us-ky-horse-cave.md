Horse Cave Lookout Tower is mapped in Hart County, Kentucky, close to the hamlet of Bear Wallow, which lies about four miles southeast of it.[^2] OpenStreetMap places it at 37.1776 north, 85.9205 west, and describes the structure as an observation tower of lattice construction, 280 metres (919 ft) above sea level.[^1][^2] Mapcarta also lists the city of Horse Cave and Cave City as nearby.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts index for Kentucky lists a Horse Cave tower in Hart County.[^3]

Today the only description of the site in these sources is the map entry, which still shows a tower at the spot.[^1] No source describes what stands there now, or says whether visitors can reach it.
