Lincoln State Park Fire Tower stands in Lincoln State Park in Spencer County, Indiana, and Mark A's site notes it is the only tower in that county.[^1][^2] The Indiana Department of Natural Resources administers the tower.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register, which registered the tower on January 2, 2026, says it is a 120-foot Aermotor design that Civilian Conservation Corps crews built in 1935.[^1] Mark A is less sure of that date. A photo at the park's nature center has a handwritten note dating it to about 1937, and a 1936 county map leaves the tower off, which he reads as a sign it was not yet built.[^2]

A structural survey that Mark A quotes says the CCC built many fire towers on Indiana state lands, many since torn down, and calls this one typical of that work.[^2]

Today the register lists the tower as open to the public as of 2025.[^1] Mark A describes it as standing and climbable up into the cab, and says the climb is fun with a great view.[^2]
