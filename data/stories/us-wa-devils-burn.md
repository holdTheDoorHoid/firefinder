Devils Burn is a former lookout site in Cowlitz County, about four miles north of Woodland.[^1][^2]

The register names the Department of Natural Resources as administrator. Firelookout.com names the Washington Division of Forestry and DNR, and Forest Lookouts names the State Division of Forestry.[^1][^2][^4]

The register dates a tower on the site to 1949, and firelookout.com gives the same year.[^1][^2] Willhite describes something different: a ground cabin beside a 15-foot stump, at a site he calls temporary.[^3]

The site sits on a low point above the Cascade and Elk Creek drainages, inside the Weyerhaeuser St. Helens Tree Farm.[^3] Willhite located it in October 2020, using a 1942 state list of lookouts and 1960 and 1975 Metsker maps.[^3]

The register reports that the tower was eventually abandoned and no longer stands.[^1] Firelookout.com also lists it as abandoned.[^2]

Willhite's October 2020 visit is the most recent description we found. He reports a ground cabin and a 15-foot stump with artifacts beside it.[^3]

Access is by permit. Willhite says a Weyerhaeuser permit is required, and with a motorized permit the hike is about two minutes.[^3]

For now, Willhite treats Devils Burn and the Cascade Creek site as separate places.[^3] An inventory he cites puts the Cascade Creek lookout exactly one mile away, over the same territory.[^3]
