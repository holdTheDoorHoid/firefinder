Wausau lies in Washington County, in the Florida Panhandle.[^1] Wikipedia describes it as the Possum Capital of the World.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site lists a tower at Wausau in Washington County.[^2] Its one dated entry is for fall 2008, when the tower was auctioned for $100 and taken down.[^2]

The state agriculture department's page on surplus fire towers describes how they are sold. When the Florida Forest Service decides a tower is no longer needed, it is sold as surplus, and the highest bidder wins.[^3] The winning bidder is expected to take the tower apart and move it to its new location.[^3] The page gives no date for these rules and does not mention the 2008 sale, so it does not show how that sale was run.[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the Wausau site today, or whether the public can visit it.
