The Town Hill Fire Hill is a standing lookout in Marion County, Alabama, and the Alabama Forestry Commission is listed as its administrator.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register registered it on March 5, 2023, on a nomination by Jason Johns.[^1]

The Register places the lookout next to I-22, a heavily traveled interstate, and says it has a diagonal staircase inside.[^1] A community map database records the same site as a lattice tower.[^3] The sources read say nothing about when the tower was built, who designed it, or whether it ever had a lookout on duty.

The Register gives the elevation as 9,137 feet (2,785 meters). The map database gives 220 meters for the same spot.[^1][^3] Those figures differ by more than ten times, so at least one is wrong. Until someone checks a survey, treat neither as settled.

The sources do not say whether the public may visit the tower or whether it has been restored or closed. Since it stands beside a busy interstate, check with the Alabama Forestry Commission before going.
