Bunker Hill's lookout is a steel training tower at the headquarters of the Coos Forest Protective Association, about half a mile southeast of Coos Bay.[^1][^2] The first lookout on the hill was not a stand-alone tower. In 1937, men from the Walker CCC camp built a 7-by-7-foot lookout cab on top of a water tank at the Coos Fire Patrol headquarters.[^3][^4] The NHLR and Firelookout both tie the water-tower platform to about 1939, so the sources disagree on the date, and the 1937 construction notes are the earlier record.[^1][^2][^3]

The Coos Fire Patrol used the tower until the 1950s.[^4] In December 1941 an air raid lookout station opened there on a 24-hour basis, manned almost entirely by women.[^3] In 1943 a man named Donaldson returned to his work as the lookout at Bunker Hill after military service.[^3] A later account says the tower's wartime air raid role was brief, because observers on the ground did most of the watching.[^4]

Once the Bay Park-Millington Water District connected to the Coos Bay and North Bend water system, the tank was no longer needed, and the combined tower was dismantled in 1965 with its materials salvaged.[^3][^4] Accounts don't agree on its height, which ranges from 47 to 70 feet.[^3]

A new lookout went up in 1996 for training: a 12-by-12-foot cab on a 40-foot steel tower donated by the U.S. Coast Guard.[^1][^2][^3] A 1996 Register-Guard article said the tower would not be regularly staffed.[^3]

The NHLR registered the lookout on March 1, 2003 as US 473, and says it stays on the National Lookout Register until it is 50 years old.[^1] Lookout staff train on it and use it in emergencies, and it is an interpretive site for visitors.[^1][^2][^4] The 2026 directory lists it as unmanned.[^5] A visitor's post dated December 31, 2021 found the buildings shut for the holiday, but the grounds still reachable, with marked visitor parking just past the gate and a view of the lookout from the lot.[^4] Treat it as a working headquarters site: view it from the visitor lot, and check with the association before going onto the grounds.
