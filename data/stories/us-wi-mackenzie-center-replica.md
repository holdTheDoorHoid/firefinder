The MacKenzie Center sits on a 285-acre property near Poynette in Columbia County, about 25 miles north of Madison.[^1] Its 80-foot (24 m) fire tower, the Wisconsin DNR says, was relocated there from the Wisconsin State Fairgrounds in West Allis.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association files the tower under its relocated and replica lookouts, listing it as a replica from the State Fair and as standing.[^3] The DNR describes it as a relocated fire tower, and the two accounts do not match on whether it is a replica.[^2][^3]

The DNR says the tower is not in active use, but it still gives visitors a sense of an early fire-detection method that dates to the U.S. Forest Service's start in 1905.[^2]

Today the grounds and trails are open from dawn to dusk all year, and no vehicle sticker is needed to enter.[^1] The observation platform, 40 feet (12 m) above the ground, is the highest point the public can reach, and the DNR asks visitors to take care on the climb.[^2]
