The Little Soldier Lookout stood on Little Soldier Mountain in Custer County, Idaho.[^1] It was built in 1934 as a ground-mounted L-4 cab.[^2][^3] The National Historic Lookout Register describes a hip-roofed groundhouse about 14 feet by 14 feet, with a catwalk.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Our sources name a few people who worked there. In July 1957 a fire spotter at the lookout was flown out by helicopter after an accidental injury, according to a newspaper item quoted on a lookout history site.[^4] A June 1958 item described a summer worker who would travel three days by horse and pack mule to the tower. We found no later report that he arrived.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists Cole Nicole LeFavour for 1984 to 1987 and Christa Everett for 1988 to 1989.[^3]

In August 1988 a fire jumped the Salmon River and moved toward the mountain. The lookout was evacuated.[^4] The Register listed the lookout on February 12, 2004. It noted that the cab spent several years on emergency status before returning to active use.[^1] A windstorm severely damaged the cab in 2010, and parts were salvaged the next year.[^2][^3]

A regional list marks the lookout as gone.[^5] We found no source that says what, if anything, is left at the site.

The Register describes the main trail as a steep ten-mile climb from the Indian Creek Ranger Station, on foot or horseback, with no road access.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as a hike-in.[^3] The site sits within the Frank Church River of No Return Wilderness.[^2]
