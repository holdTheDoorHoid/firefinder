Roots Knob is a lookout site in the Payette National Forest, in Idaho County.[^1][^3] Records give it a 1934 tower: an L-4 cab on a 10-foot pole.[^1][^2][^3] The University of Idaho's photo archive titles it Root Knob Lookout, while the firelookout.com and Forest Lookouts pages use Roots Knob.[^4][^1][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts also has a photo of the landing field at Root Ranger Station, taken from the lookout.[^2]

A 1952 photograph in that archive shows a ranger, Val Simpson, on the lookout's steps.[^4] Its caption calls the lookout abandoned, says the structure beneath it is badly deteriorated, and says it was to be destroyed.[^4]

Two sources date its destruction to 1959.[^1][^2] The third lists it only as removed, with no date.[^3] The accounts differ on how it ended.

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
