Haystack Mountain's lookout site sits at 4,615 feet in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, about 20 miles north of Wallace in Shoshone County.[^1][^2] A Forest Lookouts list for Shoshone County gives its survey location as 51N-4E-21.[^3]

A crow's nest went up on Haystack Mountain in 1932.[^1][^2][^3] The sources also record a second crow's nest about 1.25 miles to the north.[^1][^2] A Forest Fire Lookout Association list of Idaho lookouts gives the entry as a crow's nest, listed as abandoned.[^4]

All three sources give 1947 as the year the lookout was abandoned.[^1][^2][^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^1] Another Haystack Mountain, a lookout in Lemhi County on the Salmon National Forest, is a separate site.[^5]
