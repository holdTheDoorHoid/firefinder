Gartin Knob is a standing fire tower in Lincoln County, West Virginia, near the hamlet of Atenville.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as Gartin (Mann) Knob, while a visitor's blog post calls the same tower the Manns Knob Fire Tower.[^1][^3]

The blog says the tower went up in the 1930s.[^3] We found no more precise build date, and no record of its design, its builder, or the people who staffed it. The blog also describes a small house next to the tower.[^3] The Forest Lookouts directory names the West Virginia Division of Forestry as the agency for the tower.[^4]

The sources disagree on its height above sea level. The blog gives 1,439 feet, while Mapcarta gives 1,411 feet, or 430 metres.[^3][^2]

We did not find any notable fire tied to the tower in the sources we could read. The Association still shows the tower as standing, but its list gives no date for that status.[^1]

The state shut down its fire-tower program in 1990, after decades of operation.[^5] Fewer than a dozen still stand, and none is operating as a lookout.[^5] Several of the state's towers have been restored, and some are available for overnight rental.[^5] Those statements cover the state as a whole, not this tower in particular.

For Gartin Knob itself, the sources we checked give no public access or rental terms. Anyone interested should confirm access with the Division of Forestry before going, and should not climb a tower that is closed or private.[^4]
