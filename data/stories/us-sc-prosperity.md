The Prosperity Lookout Tower is listed in Newberry County, South Carolina, about two miles south of the town of Prosperity.[^2] Mapcarta describes the site only as a tower.[^2]

A Newberry Sun feature from October 1972 gave a telephone line for the Prosperity Tower.[^1] It told landowners, as part of complying with the statewide Burning Notification Law, to phone the closest tower to say they planned to burn.[^1] When fire weather was critical, the article recommended postponing burns until rain came.[^1]

The state's Forestry Commission network grew past 130 towers, and it spent 65 years spotting wildfires.[^4] The Commission shut down its fire tower system on October 1, 1993.[^3]

No source I could reach describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can get near it.
