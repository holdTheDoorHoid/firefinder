Osborne Lookout stands on Archuleta Mesa, on the Jicarilla Reservation in Rio Arriba County, New Mexico, at 8,913 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as Osborn (Arachal).[^2] The register does not explain either name.

Construction began in late summer 1959, and the register calls Osborne the fourth lookout on the reservation.[^1] The Bureau of Indian Affairs designed it as a ground-level house with a cupola rather than a tower.[^1] It was completed and staffed in 1960.[^1] That year fires burned more than 1,000 acres on and near the reservation, and the register says the new lookout played an important role that year.[^1]

Neither source says when, or whether, the lookout stopped being used. The register says the Bureau of Indian Affairs administers it, and the association lists it as standing.[^1][^2]

The lookout is on tribal land,[^1] and I found no public visiting rules. Do not go up to or into the lookout. Ask the Jicarilla Apache Nation or the BIA first.

[^2]: New Mexico Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
