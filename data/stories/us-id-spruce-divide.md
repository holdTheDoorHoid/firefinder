Spruce Divide is a former lookout site on the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1][^3] It sits about five miles east of Lakeview.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts names the Idaho Panhandle National Forest for the same place.[^2]

Sources also disagree on the county. Firelookout.com puts the site in Shoshone County, while Forest Lookouts lists it under Bonner County.[^1][^3]

The lookout was a crow's nest, built in 1931.[^1][^3] The Idaho lookout list also types it as a crow's nest.[^4]

Firelookout.com says it was abandoned, and the Idaho lookout list marks it abandoned too.[^1][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it as destroyed or removed, with no date.[^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site hike-in, and its writers climbed from a parked car.[^2] Their visit post, dated November 9, 2024, found the western hilltop bare and wrote that any crow's nest there had disappeared long ago.[^2] On the forest road on the way out, they saw trees and posts with #9 wire and/or old insulator loops on them.[^2] They also climbed the hilltop to the east, which had more trees, but did not think it was the site.[^2]

No source we read says more about the site since that visit, or whether it is open to visitors. Check with the Coeur d'Alene National Forest before you go.
