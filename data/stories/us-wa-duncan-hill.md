Duncan Hill sits about 30 miles northwest of Entiat, in Wenatchee National Forest and Chelan County.[^1] A cabin was placed on the hill in 1923, and an L-4 cab went up on its southerly summit in 1935 to replace it.[^2][^3]

Willhite says panoramic images were taken from the roof in 1934, 17 feet above the ground.[^3] The Washington Lookouts page dates the panorama photos to September 6, 1934.[^4]

Firelookout.com names H. Johnson as known lookout personnel in 1933.[^1]

The registry says the cab was removed in 1967.[^2] Firelookout.com and Willhite say it was destroyed that year.[^1][^3] The sources agree on the year but not on how the cab ended.[^1][^2][^3]

Willhite says a 2015 fire damaged the area, though some pockets of forest survived.[^3] Willhite lists two well-maintained trails, a six-mile one-way hike with 3,000 feet of gain.[^3] To reach the lower trailhead, drive about 34 miles up the Entiat River Road, then turn onto FR-5608 for six miles. Willhite describes the forest road as in good shape, passable for most cars.[^3]

The only item on Willhite's page about possible remains is a photo captioned "Built up lookout platform," with no date given.[^3] No source describes the summit beyond that.
