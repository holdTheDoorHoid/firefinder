The Perry Mountain Lookout Tower stands in the Talladega National Forest in Chilton County, Alabama, and the U.S. Forest Service administers it.[^1] The Forest Service built it in 1937 as a 100-foot Aermotor tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab.[^1][^2] The register gives its elevation as 669 feet, or 204 meters.[^1]

I found no source that names the people who staffed the tower or the fires it watched. Forest Lookouts lists the county as Perry rather than Chilton.[^2] The register itself says Chilton County, so this account follows the register.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists June 23, 2009 as the date it registered the tower, after a nomination by Thomas Kaufmann.[^1] The register names the Oakmulgee Ranger District as a cooperator.[^1] OpenStreetMap maps a "Perry Mountain Fire Tower" at about the same spot, tagged as a lattice observation tower.[^1][^3]

The register describes the tower in the present tense.[^1] I found no condition report, no record of removal or relocation, and no later account of its state.

Nothing I found says whether the tower is open to visitors. Before going, ask the Oakmulgee Ranger District, and do not climb the tower on the strength of this account.[^1]
