The Nugget Ridge lookout was a log cabin with a crow's nest, built in 1922 in Shoshone County, Idaho.[^1][^3][^4] A county lookout list places it in the St. Joe National Forest.[^4] Its patrol point was 1.5 miles east, on top of Hardpan Point.[^1][^2]

The firelookout.com entry calls the lookout destroyed but gives no year for that.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the site as Abandoned.[^3]

A visit described on the Idaho Fire Lookouts site came more than a century after the cabin went up, and it found no remains of the cabin.[^2] At the Hardpan patrol point the visitors found almost nothing, apart from one metal spike still standing among the plants.[^2] No source we read says whether the site is open to visitors.
