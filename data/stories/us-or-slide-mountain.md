Slide Mountain is in Lake County, in the Fremont National Forest.[^2] A forest history quoting a September 1925 item says the telephone line to the peak was completed on August 20, and that its completion went with the erection of a lookout there.[^3] The register describes a 20-foot platform from the 1920s that stood before the steel tower.[^1]

In 1929 a 47-foot all-steel Aermotor tower with a cab went up, at a cost of $1,022.09.[^2] The register and the Oregon lookout history give the cab as 7 by 7 feet, while firelookout.com gives 5 by 5.[^1][^2][^4] A one-room cabin of 14 by 16 feet was built for living quarters in 1930.[^2] A 16-by-18-foot garage followed in 1933; at a later date it was dismantled and moved to Paisley.[^2]

A 1942 memo put the tower at 48 feet, one foot more than the 1929 figure.[^2] In July 1943 a Bend newspaper reported that John Strang Jr. had been serving as the Forest Service lookout on the peak.[^2] By 1956 every improvement except the tower had been removed.[^2]

A May 1968 letter from the Fremont National Forest says the steel towers on Slide Mountain and Shoestring had been offered to the Army the previous fall. The Army would not take them, so the Forest Service planned to push them over, unbolt the steel, and try to sell the scrap metal.[^2] In October 1968 a memo said both towers had been sold for scrap but were not yet removed.[^2] The register and firelookout.com both say the cab and cabin were gone by 1973.[^1][^4]

No source I read describes what remains on the summit now, or whether the site is open to visitors.
