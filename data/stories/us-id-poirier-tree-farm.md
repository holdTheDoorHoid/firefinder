The Poirier Tree Farm Lookout is a two-story frame cabin, 14 by 14 feet, on private land about a mile southwest of Blanchard in Bonner County.[^1][^2] A 2022 writeup dates it to the 1930s.[^4]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site says the cabin served as a lookout over the tree farm for years.[^3] It also says it does not yet have a documented staffing history for this lookout.[^3]

Firelookout.com describes the cabin as abandoned for several years.[^2] The register says that as of 2017 the structure was in poor shape and at risk of collapse.[^1] The Idaho Fire Lookouts site adds that the cabin has been repeatedly vandalized, and that gunfire aimed at a corner post brought one corner down.[^3] The Forest Lookout Association's Idaho list still shows it as standing.[^5]

The cabin sits on private land, and signs at the access points say visitors must get the family's permission before a visit.[^3] A 2022 writeup said walk-in hiking was allowed then, about 1.5 miles one way, with many signs asking people to keep motor vehicles out.[^4] The same writeup's photo captions say the lower floor looked unsafe to stand on, so I would not encourage anyone to climb in or enter the building.[^4]

The lookout was added to the register on December 7, 2020, as US 1470, ID 162.[^1] The 2022 writeup also reported that the 3,000-acre tree farm and ranch was for sale at about 22 million dollars. I found no later report on that sale.[^4]
