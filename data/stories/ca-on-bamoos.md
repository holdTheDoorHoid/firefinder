The Bamoos Fire Tower stood in the Thunder Bay District of Ontario, north of Marathon.[^1][^2] A post in a Facebook group about Ontario fire towers calls it the Bamoos Lake tower and says the poster was unsure of its official name then.[^2]

The Firelookout.ca entry says the tower was dismantled in 1979. Its writer learned of this only afterwards, so the date is a secondhand one.[^1] The same entry notes that the tower still appears on some topographic maps.[^1]

In October 2025, a visitor working in the area of the Little Pic River flew over Bamoos Mountain, took a few photographs, and saw that the old tower no longer stands.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. None says whether the public may visit it.
