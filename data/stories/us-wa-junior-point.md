Junior Point sits in the Wenatchee National Forest in Chelan County, about 21 miles northwest of Chelan.[^1][^2]

A shelter cabin went up in 1931 just west of the summit.[^1][^4] A 1931 Wenatchee Daily World item, quoted by Washington Lookouts, reports that the Forest Service had just finished a look-out house at Junior Peak.[^3] An L-4 cab was built on the summit in 1933.[^1][^4]

Willhiteweb, citing Kresek, says the site once served pack trains heading toward Pyramid Lookout and, during AWS, was a halfway house for suppliers on snowshoes bound for Big Hill.[^4]

Sources disagree on how the cab ended. The register says it was removed in 1968, while firelookout.com and Willhiteweb say it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2][^4] Washington Lookouts also lists the site as removed.[^3]

No source describes footings, a foundation or other remains at the summit today. Willhiteweb now presents the site as a viewpoint for adventurous visitors, and a wooded campsite just below the summit has a picnic table and fire ring.[^4] The road in is gravel, and Willhiteweb says it suits vehicles with good tires and higher clearance than an average car.[^4] Its page labels the site drive-up.[^4]
