Currier Mountain, once known as Pine Peak, lies in Coos County, west of Mount Jefferson and east of Cherry Mountain.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts listing places the site in the White Mountain National Forest.[^3]

The Forest Lookouts timeline starts the station in 1916.[^3] A 2009 forum post says the tower ran from about 1916 to 1934.[^1] As its writer understood it, the tower was built to watch over logging slash on the northern slopes of the Presidential Range.[^1] Service ended in 1934, and the tower was taken down sometime in the 1940s.[^3][^4]

As of 2009, a forum post says no maintained trails led to the mountain.[^1] A 2009 trip report reached the summit by hiking a snowmobile trail to the base of the north-facing slope, then bushwhacking about 30 minutes.[^1] A 2015 blog visit found no sign of the old tower except what the author presumed was communication wire.[^4] No source describes footings or other remains of the tower.
