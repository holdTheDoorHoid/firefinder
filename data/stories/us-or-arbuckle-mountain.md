The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register places Arbuckle Mountain in Morrow County, within the Umatilla National Forest.[^1] Firelookout.com places it roughly 20 miles southeast of Heppner.[^3] In August 1922 a Heppner paper described a new lookout station: a platform in a large fir tree, ninety feet up and held by a steel crow's nest frame.[^2] In October 1927 a Heppner paper reported that work on a new lookout tower was finished.[^2]

The register says a 24-foot pole platform followed in 1930.[^1] Rex's page says a 24-foot pole platform was in use in the 1930s, and a January 1931 item said the tower had been upgraded and a cabin put up for the lookout.[^2]

The later wooden tower took shape between 1946 and 1948.[^1][^2] The register and firelookout.com give a 67-foot wooden L-4 tower built in 1946.[^1][^3] A Heppner paper said in September 1946 that the foundation was nearly complete.[^2] A January 1948 Ukiah district report, however, describes a new 14-by-14-foot lookout station on an 83-foot tower, with the house to be finished that spring.[^2] The sources disagree on the height and on when the tower was finished.[^1][^2][^3]

Firelookout.com lists Homer Landers in 1922, Frank Farnsworth in 1927, Kate Smith in 1945 and Calvin Webb in 1949, among others.[^3]

The register and firelookout.com say the lookout was last manned in 1974 and taken down in 1976.[^1][^3] Rex's page also gives 1976 for the removal, and records a repair in January 1956.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the road up it is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
