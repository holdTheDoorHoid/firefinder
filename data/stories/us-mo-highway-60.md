A 1963 federal survey description places the Highway 60 lookout about six miles southeast of Ellsinore in Carter County, one mile south of the junction of U.S. Highway 60 and County Road B.[^1]

The survey describes a steel tower on four legs that narrows as it rises, with a tiny observing room at the top.[^1] The overall height is given as roughly 70 feet (21 m).[^1]

The survey also places the tower on state-owned property.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page files it under the Mark Twain National Forest, and these sources do not settle which is right.[^1]

The page lists the tower as removed.[^1] The survey also gives driving directions from the south edge of Ellsinore.[^1] No source I could read says what, if anything, remains at the site today or whether it can be reached now.

[^1]: Highway 60 - Forest Lookouts (quotes a 1963 Coast and Geodetic Survey station description), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/highway-60.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
