The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas index lists a tower called Rocky Hill (Ewing) in Angelina County, at about 31.36 north and 94.55 west, and marks it gone.[^1] The entry's name adds the place name Ewing in parentheses.[^1]

Edward Cavallerano's book *Fire in the Piney Woods*, published by Texas A&M University Press, covers where Texas lookout towers stood and how they were designed.[^2] Bob Bowman, writing in his East Texas column, says towers such as the one at Lufkin were built by the Civilian Conservation Corps in 1936.[^3] Both are general accounts of Texas towers, not records for this site.[^2][^3]

Today the index lists the Rocky Hill site as gone. No source we read describes what, if anything, remains there, or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1]

[^2]: Fire in the Piney Woods: Texas Lookout Towers, the Forest Service, and the Civilian Conservation Corps, Texas A&M University Press, https://muse.jhu.edu/book/137054 (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Bob Bowman's East Texas Fire Lookout Towers, Texas Escapes, https://texasescapes.com/BobBowman/Fire-lookout-towers.htm (accessed 2026-10-21).
