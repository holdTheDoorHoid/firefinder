Bald Mountain stands at an elevation of 9,936 feet in Fremont County, Wyoming, about 12 miles southwest of Wind River.[^1] Rex Kamstra's firelookout.com page places the peak on the Wind River Indian Reservation.[^1] The reservation was created by an 1868 treaty with the Eastern Shoshone Tribe, and today the Shoshone and Northern Arapaho tribes share the land.[^2] By area, it ranks as the seventh largest reservation in the country.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association files the peak under lookout sites and non-lookout structures, lists its structure type as unknown, and marks it gone.[^3] Kamstra's page agrees that the site is gone.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may go there. Because it lies on reservation land, visitors should not assume they have access.
