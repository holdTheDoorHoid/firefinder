Ballentine was a South Carolina Forestry Commission fire lookout in Richland County.[^1]

The Civilian Conservation Corps finished the tower here in 1939.[^1] The corps also finished the tower-unit buildings at Ballentine and at other sites in 1939–40.[^1] Each tower unit had a four-room dwelling, barn, chicken house, smokehouse and latrine.[^1]

According to the commission's history, most tower sites covered 10 acres and included the tower and a year-round house for the operator.[^2]

Across South Carolina, the commission says women staffed most towers from the 1960s on, and their husbands often worked as firefighters.[^2]

The commission ended its fire tower network on October 1, 1993.[^2] The lookout-history site lists Ballentine as removed.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains at the Ballentine site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
