Bus Point's lookout was a crow's nest high in a large tree, in the Mount Hood National Forest in Wasco County, Oregon.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com's page title calls it a tower, but the page's own text and the Forest Lookouts page both describe a nest in a tree.[^1][^2]

The nest sat at the 95-foot level of the tree.[^2] Firelookout.com gives the site an elevation of 3,333 feet.[^1]

Firelookout.com says the lookout was established in 1933.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page holds panorama photographs dated November 7, 1933.[^2] That date falls within a Forest Service survey that photographed lookouts across Oregon and Washington from 1933 to 1935.[^2][^4]

Firelookout.com describes the lookout as abandoned but still there, and a 2013 post also calls it abandoned.[^1][^3] The author went out on July 20, 2013 to look for the remains of the old nest and could not find it.[^3] The author also reports that a geocacher who found the cache hidden on that visit located the nest, though the post gives no date for that find.[^3]

We found no source that describes the site in detail today. The Firelookout.com note that it is still there is undated, and the author's own 2013 visit was inconclusive. No dated source confirms that the nest survives, so we list it as standing, with that caveat.[^1][^3] The nest is high in a tree, so please do not try to climb to it.
