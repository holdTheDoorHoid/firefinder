The Pewitt tower stood in Williamson County, Tennessee.[^1] Its Tennessee Landforms waypoint sits at 978 feet (298 m) above sea level.[^1] The list numbers it 7-2, a state forest tower number from a 1976 list, which suggests it belonged to the Division of Forestry's network.[^1][^2]

A National Register form on the division's towers credits the agency with constructing 208 fire lookout towers, put up around 1930 to 1970.[^3] Taken together, the towers embodied the division's fire detection system from 1933 to 1975.[^3]

Tennessee Landforms marks the Pewitt tower as moved.[^1]

The Landforms list adds that many towers are gone and most of their sites are now privately owned.[^2] No source describes the Pewitt site today, or the tower's new location.
