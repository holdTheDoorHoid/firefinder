A firelookout.ca listing gives the Clancy Fire Tower's location as Basin Depot, Ontario, and places it in Algonquin Park.[^1]

Wikipedia says the Pembroke Forest Fire District was established in 1922 by the province's Department of Lands and Forests, which has since become the MNR.[^2] Its headquarters were in Pembroke, and the district served as the hub for 15 fire tower lookouts, including those in Algonquin Park.[^2] The article lists Clancy among those 15 towers.[^2]

In that system, a towerman took degree bearings from his own tower and radioed the information back to headquarters.[^2] The listing also says a towerman lived at Bridgedam, citing a book by Roderick MacKay.[^1]

The tower appears on the Department of Lands and Forests Tower Detection System Map of 1963. It also appears on Pembroke topographic map 31F, in its 1977 third edition and its 1996 fourth edition.[^1]

Wikipedia also says that all of the district's towers were to be retired once aerial firefighting came into use in the 1970s.[^2]

No source says what, if anything, remains at the Clancy site today, or whether visitors may go there.[^1][^2]
