The Asbury site is in Dale County, Alabama, and the Alabama Forestry Commission administers it, according to the register.[^1] The Eastern U.S. lookouts site files it under Dale County too.[^2]

The register added the site on September 15, 2019. Its entry says the name was found on an Alabama Forestry Commission list and that the site was located on a topographic map.[^1] The entry's only description is brief: the stands are all that remain.[^1]

A 1819 News feature gives the most common explanation for why Alabama's lookouts were neglected: aircraft could cover far more ground when spotting fires than a 100-foot tower.[^3] The same article says the state still keeps roughly one in three of its former lookouts.[^3]

The register does not describe anything else at the site, and no source says whether the land is open to visitors. Nothing in the record suggests a climbable tower remains.
