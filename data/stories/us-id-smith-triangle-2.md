Smith Triangle was a lookout in Boundary County, Idaho, seven miles southwest of Porthill, in the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^2] Its elevation is listed as 5,922 feet.[^1][^2]

The sources disagree on what was built. firelookout.com describes a 20-foot L-4 tower dated 1934.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts describes a small cabin dated 1937, and the statewide list gives the type as "Ground" rather than "Tower."[^2][^3] No source explains the difference, so each version is given as its source states it.[^1][^2][^3]

Both sites list Howard Green for 1934 and Ed Johnson for 1935 and 1936.[^1][^2] Both also say the lookout was destroyed in the 1950s.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in and destroyed.[^2] No source describes what remains there, such as footings or a foundation, and none says whether the public may visit.[^1][^2][^3]
