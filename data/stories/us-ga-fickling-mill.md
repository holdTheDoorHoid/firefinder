The Fickling Mill lookout tower is listed in Taylor County, Georgia, at 32.629722, -84.187770, with an elevation of 589, which we take to be feet (179.5 m) because the list states no unit.[^1] A second Georgia lookout list also names a tower Fickling Mill, in Taylor County.[^2] The name is shared with an unincorporated community in the same county, which takes its name from a Dr. Fickling, a settler from the early days who also fought in the Civil War.[^3]

An early mill there may have been started by the Fickling family.[^4] In 1910 the Fickling family sold the property to two new owners, who built a dam and a set of water-powered mills for grinding grain, ginning cotton, and sawing and planing lumber.[^4] A 2017 blog post describes the pond and dam at the mill site, but none of the sources we read say whether a tower still stands there.[^5]
