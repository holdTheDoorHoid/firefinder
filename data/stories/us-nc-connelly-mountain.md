Connelly Mountain is a summit in Swain County, North Carolina, shown on the Whittier topographic quadrangle.[^2] A MountainZone summit page puts its elevation at roughly 3,976 feet (1,212 m) above sea level.[^2]

A list of North Carolina lookout towers compiled by tnlandforms.us includes a tower on Connelly Mountain, at 35.38121, -83.36430.[^1]

No source we read describes what stands on the summit today, or whether the public may visit it.
