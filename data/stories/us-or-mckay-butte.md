McKay Butte rises about 8 miles northeast of LaPine, in Deschutes County, Oregon.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the summit an elevation of 5,253 feet.[^1]

The page dates a tree platform with a tent cabin to 1933.[^1] The same page says it was abandoned in 1936.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list also marks the site as abandoned rather than gone.[^3]

The sources disagree about who managed the land. Firelookout.com places the butte in the Deschutes National Forest.[^1] A Forest Lookouts listing gives the agency as "State" and the legal location as 21S-11E-26.[^2]

That listing also dates a set of panorama photos to June 25, 1938.[^2] That is two years after the 1936 abandonment, and the two accounts do not reconcile the dates.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3] A 2026 directory of Oregon's standing lookouts does not include the butte.[^4]
