Blue Joe is a hike-in lookout on the Kaniksu National Forest, about 17 miles west of Porthill in Boundary County.[^1][^2] Its listed elevation is 5,650 feet.[^1]

The sources are thin. Idaho Fire Lookouts gives its history as a crow's nest from the 1930s, and both sites list the lookout as abandoned.[^2][^1] The only named person is Robert Campbell, listed as known staff for 1936.[^1][^2]

None of the sources gives a build date or a removal date. Beyond the label "crow's nest," none describes the structure, and none says it was taken down. No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.

[^2]: Blue Joe, Idaho Fire Lookouts, https://www.idahofirelookouts.com/northern-idaho/blue-joe/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
