The Crane lookout sat on the summit of Crane Mountain in Iron County, Missouri, roughly 8 miles south of Ironton.[^1] The Coast and Geodetic Survey described it in 1938 as a 100-foot (30 m) steel tower with four sides and an enclosed observation cab at the top, and it took its survey point from the center of the Forest Service tower.[^1]

A 1956 recovery note gives the height as 110 feet (33.5 m) and describes a house on top measuring 8 feet square, so the two survey notes disagree on the height.[^1] The lookout index files the site under the Clark National Forest.[^1] That forest was established in 1939 and absorbed by the Mark Twain National Forest in 1976.[^2]

The index marks the tower as removed.[^1] A trail-app listing for the old tower road, Forest Service Road 2192, places the removal in the 1970 to 2000 era and says the old foundation piers can be found just east of the trail.[^3]

The app describes the road as a 0.9-mile trail in the Fredericktown sector of Mark Twain National Forest.[^3] No agency source we found describes what remains at the tower site now.
