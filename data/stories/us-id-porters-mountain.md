Porters Mountain sits about seven miles south of Elk City, in Idaho County, on the Nez Perce National Forest. It is listed at 6,411 feet.[^1][^2]

The site began as a crow's nest, which the Forest Service list dates to 1922 with a plus-or-minus margin.[^1][^4] A cabin followed in 1930.[^1][^2]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page names one watcher, J.B. Jenkins, in 1915.[^2] The Forest Lookouts site quotes the Idaho County Free Press with the spelling "Jinkins," so the two sites spell the name differently.[^3]

The site was abandoned in 1950 and is now gone.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a ground-level site.[^5]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page classes it as hike-in and gives no trail or access rules.[^2] No source says whether visitors may go there now.
