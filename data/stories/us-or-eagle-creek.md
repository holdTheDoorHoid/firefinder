The Eagle Creek lookout was an L-4 cab set on the ground in Clackamas County, Oregon.[^1] The Forest Service built it in 1937, about an eighth of a mile east of a minor summit and below it.[^1] The spot lies about 11 miles east of Estacada, in the Mount Hood National Forest.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register puts the site at 2,600 feet, or 792 meters.[^1]

A panorama photo of the lookout is captioned March 20, 1934, three years before the 1937 build date.[^1][^3] The photo date and the build date do not line up.[^1][^3]

A February 10, 1954 note, quoting a 1953 Estacada District historical report, says the tower was condemned and destroyed that year. It adds that after the loss, the district had one full-time lookout left.[^3]

The sources date its end differently. The 1953 report says it was destroyed that year, while the register and firelookout.com say only that it was gone by 1957.[^1][^2][^3] The register added the site on August 18, 2010, on the nomination of Ray Kresek.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, or whether the land is open to visitors. The register gives its coordinates as N 45.307360°, W 122.123750°.[^1]
