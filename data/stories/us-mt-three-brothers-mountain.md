Three Brothers Mountain is in Jefferson County, Montana.[^1] The Forest Lookouts listing for the site gives "Private" and "Removed" under its name.[^1]

The same listing groups it with other Jefferson County sites, Shingle Butte and Strawberry Butte among them.[^1]

Not to be confused with the Three Brothers lookout site in Chelan County, Washington. That is a separate place, where an L-4 cab built in 1934 was removed in 1966.[^2]

No source found describes what, if anything, remains on the Montana site today. The listing's "Private" label does not say whether the site can be visited, so do not assume it is open.[^1]
