Emigrant Butte rises in the Ochoco National Forest, about 24 miles northwest of Burns, in Harney County, Oregon.[^1][^2]

The earliest recorded lookout here was a house.[^3] Ranger E.W. Donnelly built one in 1921, and the register dates a house with a cupola to that year.[^3][^1] In September 1922 a newer house went up, a box-type building with a glassed-in cupola for the fire finder.[^3] Thomas Ewing, of Brothers, was in charge of the station that summer.[^3]

The post changed hands during the 1929 season.[^3] Al Frey was the lookout until he took a winter job, and Lester Williams took over for the rest of the season.[^3] Jack Choate came over from Dry Mountain that autumn when Williams left.[^3]

In October 1933 W.H. Lohuis was building a lookout tower and house there, one of several NRA projects.[^3] The register says a 20-foot L-4 tower replaced the house that year.[^1]

In 1939 the Forest Service asked for approval to build a combined garage, woodshed and storage building at the lookout.[^3]

The register and FireLookout.com both say the tower was gone by the 1940s.[^1][^2] The register has a photo of the site captioned 2008.[^1] No source we read describes what is there now or whether the public can visit.
