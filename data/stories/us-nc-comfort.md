The Comfort tower is in Onslow County, North Carolina, at about 34.97 north and 77.50 west.[^3] The state's Division of Forest Resources lists it under both Jones and Onslow counties.[^1]

The Civilian Conservation Corps erected a 99-foot 9-inch (30 m) steel tower here in 1936. Emergency Conservation Works funding covered the $2,001.33 cost.[^1]

OpenStreetMap maps a "Comfort Lookout Tower" at the site as an observation tower with an elevation of 20 metres.[^2] No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or who can visit it.[^1][^2]
