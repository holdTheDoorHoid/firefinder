Thorn Spring Butte is a former lookout site in Grant County, Oregon.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com describes an L-4 cab there from the 1930s, which it says was abandoned.[^2] It places the butte in the Umatilla National Forest, about 8 miles west of Monument, at an elevation of 4,086 feet (1,245 m).[^2]

The Oregon Lookouts list is much shorter.[^1] It gives the butte's survey location as 8S-26E-35, names only a National Forest as the agency, and marks the lookout as removed.[^1] The two sources word the cab's fate differently: removed on one list, abandoned on the other.[^1][^2]

The same Grant County list also includes Thorn Creek Butte, a separate site.[^1]

No source I found describes what, if anything, is left on the butte today, or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Thorn Spring Butte, Forest Lookouts (Oregon Lookouts), https://oregonlookouts.weebly.com/thorn-spring-butte.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Thorn Spring Butte Fire Lookout Cabin, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/or/thornspringbutte.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
