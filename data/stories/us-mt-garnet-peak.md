Garnet Peak is a former Forest Service lookout in the Flathead National Forest, in Flathead County, Montana.[^1][^2]

The firelookout.com record dates an L-4 cab on the peak to 1936.[^1] A Coast and Geodetic Survey description from 1940 calls the structure a Forest Service lookout house and places it 12 miles northwest of Big Prairie Ranger Station.[^2] The firelookout.com record uses a different landmark, 24 miles south of Spotted Bear Ranger Station.[^1] The two accounts do not share a reference point, so a map check is worth doing before anyone heads out.

The cab was abandoned in 1956 and is now listed as gone.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a ground lookout.[^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it.

[^2]: Garnet Peak - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts (Montana), https://montanalookouts.weebly.com/garnet-peak.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
