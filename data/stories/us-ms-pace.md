The Pace Lookout Tower is in Bolivar County, Mississippi.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes an Aermotor tower, 110 feet tall, whose stairs run straight up the inside, with 17 landings and 18 flights.[^1] It gives no build date and names the Mississippi Forestry Commission as the administering agency.[^1]

The register lists the tower as US 1654, MS 20.[^1] It was registered on July 10, 2022, on the nomination of Jason Johns.[^1] The site visit on July 8, 2022 found that the first flight of stairs had been removed, and it noted that the Forestry Commission had vacated the site.[^1]

Records of its history are thin. A Mississippi Secretary of State list of public lands includes the entry "Bolivar Fire Tower (Sold 8/11/2018)."[^2] The list does not tie that entry to this tower, so we cannot say whether they are the same structure.[^2]

We found no later report on the tower's condition or access. Visitors should ask the Forestry Commission before going near the site, and should not climb it.
