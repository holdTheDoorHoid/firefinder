The Georgia tower list gives this site the name "nevils," places it in Bulloch County, and lists its elevation as 164, which is about 50 m if read as feet.[^1] The list's author says the names for towers numbered 73 and above are the author's own, so "nevils" is a label and does not show what the tower was called locally.[^1] The list draws on USGS topographic maps and National Geodetic Survey datasheets, and this entry cites one of those datasheets.[^1]

That datasheet is for survey mark CK0191, which the Coast and Geodetic Survey set in 1955.[^2] It places the mark about 0.9 mile south of Nevils and 353 feet southeast of the east leg of a "Forest Service lookout tower."[^2] The tower was therefore standing in 1955, the earliest date we found for it.[^2] The description does not say which forest service ran the tower.[^2]

A 1989 recovery note by the US Power Squadron says the survey mark was not found.[^2] That note concerns the mark, not the tower.[^2]

We found no source that describes the site today, so this account cannot say whether any structure remains there or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Datasheet for PID CK0191 (benchmark M 211), NOAA National Geodetic Survey, https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=CK0191 (accessed 2026-10-21).
