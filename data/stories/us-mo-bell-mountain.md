Bell Mountain rises to 1,702 feet in Iron County, Missouri, inside the Bell Mountain Wilderness of the Mark Twain National Forest.[^2] A lookout-history website lists two lookouts, Bell Mountain #1 and #2, and marks both as removed.[^1] The Forest Service reports that concrete pilings from a former fire tower remain on the summit and that no tower stands there now.[^2]

Hikers reach the summit on foot. Trailheads on the north and southwest edges of the wilderness lead to loops that cross the summit and slope.[^2] Dispersed camping is allowed, and the Forest Service notes there are no toilets, structures or treated water.[^2]

A social media post from the account Missouri Hidden Gems describes a rusted steel tower still standing, built by Civilian Conservation Corps workers in the 1930s.[^3] That post conflicts with the Forest Service page and the lookout-history record, which both say the tower is gone, so this story follows the agency page.[^1][^2]
