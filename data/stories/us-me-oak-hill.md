The Oak Hill fire tower stands in Township 8, Range 5 (T8 R5 WELS) of Aroostook County, Maine.[^1][^2]

According to Maine Memory, the Maine Forest Department built a steel tower in 1924. It is 75 feet tall, and the site sits at an elevation of 1,096 feet.[^1]

Two 1924 Daily Kennebec Journal items, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, agree. In April 1924 the state bought steel for a 75-foot tower on Oak Hill, and by July 22 a construction crew had put it up.[^2]

A Maine Memory slideshow gives 1925 instead.[^3] We follow the 1924 date, which the photo record and both newspaper items support.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^4] The Forest Lookouts page quotes only those two 1924 items, and it does not describe the cab or name anyone who staffed the tower.[^2]

We found no source on who owns the site now or whether visitors may climb it. Please ask before going.
