Marshfield is the county seat of Webster County, Missouri.[^2] The Missouri Department of Conservation's annual report for fiscal 1967–68 logs the erection of a fire tower at Marshfield.[^1]

No source describes the Marshfield site today.[^1] The sources do not say whether a tower still stands there, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1]
