Mount Ord's 1936 lookout was an Aermotor MC 39 tower.[^1][^2] The mountain sits in the Tonto National Forest in Gila County, Arizona.[^1] The tower served the mountain for close to fifty years.[^2] In 1983 the Forest Service built a taller, 102-foot tower to take the old one's place. It has a 14-by-14-foot metal cab and catwalk, and it still stands on the summit.[^1][^2]

The 1936 tower now sits at the Rim Country Museum in Payson, Arizona.[^1] The register gives no date for the move.[^1] A tourist listing for the museum calls the tower one of its exhibits, and says admission covers all of the attractions.[^3] That listing is undated, so check the museum's current hours and displays before you go.

The Arizona fire lookout list shows the museum tower as standing, but it does not say when anyone last checked it.[^4]

Anyone who wants to see the old tower should visit the museum, where it is a display. The sources do not suggest it can be climbed, so treat it as a museum piece.
