Attean Mountain sits above the upper Moose River valley in Somerset County, in Attean Township.[^1][^2]

A September 1905 Daily Kennebec Journal item describes a "second observatory" on the summit. It names Sullivan Newton of Jackman as the chief fire warden for that district and says the observer there has warned the main office of several blazes.[^2]

In December 1905 a report says some landowners who worked with the State Forestry Department set up three lookout telephone stations, one of them on Mount Attean.[^2]

An item that the Forest Lookouts page dates to August 1907, though the page also tags it 1905, says twelve fires were reported from the station.[^2] By 1910 the watchmen there had camp kits, and the telephone line from Jackman to the summit was in good repair.[^2]

A new lookout station went up in 1914, with a mile and a quarter of telephone line. In 1916 the tower got matching board lining and exterior paint.[^2]

The 1920 inventory lists a 12-foot wooden tower. That spring, on May 7, the Attean station was the first of the season to open.[^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says the tower was removed before 1932.[^1] A Maine Atlas caption, though, dates a photo of "the old fire tower on Sally Mountain at Attean" to 1932 and says the tower came down sometime in the 1930s. The same caption dates a wood tower on a 2,221-foot mountain to 1908, a date the other accounts do not mention.[^3] Because the caption uses a different mountain name, we cannot be sure it describes this site.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine list marks the tower as gone and gives coordinates for the mountain.[^4] We found no source that describes what remains on the summit now, or whether the summit is open to visitors.
