Burrville is a fire tower in Morgan County, Tennessee, at an elevation of about 1,640 feet. The Tennessee Landforms list marks it as standing.[^1]

The documentation I could read is thin. A multiple-property nomination for the state's Division of Forestry fire lookout towers, dated June 2014 and covering 1933 to 1975, includes a table that lists a Burrville tower in Morgan County.[^2]

I found no build date, no builder and no list of staff for Burrville, so its early years are not documented in these sources.[^1][^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 76 Tennessee lookouts, and its list has no entry for Burrville.[^3]

Access is unknown. Nothing I read says whether the tower can be visited or who owns the land, so treat it as off limits unless its owner or the Division of Forestry says otherwise.[^1]

[^1]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, Tennessee Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Tennessee Division of Forestry Fire Lookout Towers, 1933 to 1975, National Park Service NPGallery, https://npgallery.nps.gov/pdfhost/docs/NRHP/Text/64501237.pdf (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^3]: Tennessee, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/tn/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
