The East Bay Fire Tower is in Franklin County, Florida.[^1] OpenStreetMap describes it as a lattice tower of the observation type, lists its site at 7 feet (2 m) above sea level, and links it to federal place-name record 281948 in the Geographic Names Information System.[^2] Its edit note calls the structure a communication mast, but that is a mapper's label and does not show what the tower is used for now.[^2]

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.

[^1]: East Bay - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/east-bay.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: East Bay Fire Tower, OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/358695098 (accessed 2026-10-21).
