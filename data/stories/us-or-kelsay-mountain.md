Kelsay Mountain rises in the Umpqua National Forest, 30 miles southeast of Oakridge in Douglas County, Oregon.[^1][^2] Its lookout was a 22-foot tower with a 14-by-14 L-4 cab.[^1][^3] Sources disagree on the year: the Oregon lookouts page dates the cab to 1934, while the register and firelookout.com date the tower to 1935.[^1][^2][^3]

In August 1935 the Umpqua supervisor, V.V. Harpham, publicly praised lookout Charles Neal for saving the Lemalo Falls canyon on the North Umpqua River from a fire.[^3] Neal spotted the smoke, sent an alarm and ran most of the way to the blaze. He trenched the fire and kept it to roughly a quarter acre until workers from the Diamond Lake CCC camp reached it.[^3] The fire had been started by unregistered campers.[^3] A 12-by-20-foot pole and shake garage followed in 1938 for $56.[^3]

The register says the tower was removed in 1965, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2]

A 2007 photograph of the site is on the register, but its caption does not describe what remains there.[^1] No source we read describes the summit today or says how to reach it.
