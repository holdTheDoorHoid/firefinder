The Barrens lookout was a Tennessee Division of Forestry tower in Coffee County.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page files it under the name AEDC (The Barrens) and places it in the Division's District 5.[^1] Tennessee Landforms gives its elevation as 1,093 feet, about 333 meters, and its number as 5-8, marked removed.[^2] The National Register form for the state's towers lists The Barrens in Coffee County as well.[^3]

The Forest Lookouts name points to AEDC, the Air Force center near Manchester and Tullahoma, which a Wikipedia article says sits on much of the former Camp Forrest.[^4] The only description of the tower we found is in a 2017 email quoted on the Forest Lookouts page. Its writer describes an 80-foot tower with a cab, which they removed from Arnold Air Force Base.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page dates the removal to about 2006.[^1] The writer, who owns a crane service in Manchester, says the tower was handed over to them to take down and dispose of.[^1] The writer had planned to put it up on a farm, but the promoters of the Bonnaroo music festival, also in Manchester, asked to have it on their festival grounds instead.[^1] A year after it went up there, at their request, the cab came off and a cuckoo clock took its place. In 2014 a large disco ball replaced the clock.[^1]

The 2017 email is the latest account we found, and it does not say whether the tower still stands on the festival grounds.[^1] Tennessee Landforms also has a separate record called Arnold AFB, in Franklin County, at an elevation of 1,112 feet and marked removed. The sources we read do not show whether it is the same structure as The Barrens tower.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the AEDC site or the festival grounds, or whether the public can visit either place.
