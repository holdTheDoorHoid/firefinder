The Bayfield lookout appears in our sources through one item from October 1934. The Oshkosh Daily Northwestern reported on October 23, 1934 that a new fire tower had gone up near Bayfield.[^1]

The item says the Apostle Islands lie northeast of Bayfield, and that protecting them had been impossible until then for lack of equipment.[^1] The state conservation department said the new tower and a power boat suited to centrifugal pumps had remedied that situation, and that forest protection of the islands would be extended next spring.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list marks the Bayfield tower as gone.[^2]

We found no source that describes the site today, so we cannot say what remains there or whether it is open to visitors.
