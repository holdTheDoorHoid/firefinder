Hemlock Mountain sat about 15 miles northwest of Kellogg, in Kootenai County, on the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1][^3]

The lookout site is listed at 4,932 feet.[^1]

The lookout went up in 1934 as a 30-foot pole tower with an L-6 cab.[^3][^1] A cabin for living quarters went up alongside it.[^1][^2]

The L-6 was a pre-cut frame cab, 8 feet by 8 feet, and smaller than the L-4.[^4] Forest Service style notes say the cab sat on tall wooden towers.[^4]

The tower was destroyed in 1955.[^3][^2] The FFLA list records it as gone.[^5]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as hike-in.[^2] I found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
