A steel fire tower once stood on Johnson Knob, near Whittaker in Kanawha County.[^1][^2] An October 1929 newspaper report described it as 90 feet tall, with a log cabin for living quarters.[^1]

The Southern West Virginia Forest Fire Protective Association paid for the tower. The state game, fish and forestry commission supervised the construction, and the tower was then given to that commission.[^1]

The registry names the West Virginia Game & Fish Commission as the agency that administers the site.[^2] A lookout history page lists the West Virginia Division of Forestry in its header instead, so the sources do not agree on which state agency is responsible.[^1]

The registry says the tower has been removed.[^2] The county list of fire towers marks it as gone.[^3] No source I found describes what, if anything, remains on the knob or whether visitors can reach it.

Do not confuse this site with the tower at Whittaker Station. That structure is the former Gauley Mountain tower, moved in October 2009 to Cass Scenic Railroad State Park, where it is accessible by train from Cass.[^4]
