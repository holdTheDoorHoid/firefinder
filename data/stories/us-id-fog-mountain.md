Fog Mountain is in Idaho County, in the Nez Perce National Forest, about 14 miles west-southwest of the Moose Creek Ranger Station.[^1]

The earliest lookout on record was a log cabin with a cupola, built in 1924.[^1][^3] An L-4 cab replaced it in 1935.[^1][^3] The Forest Service says the L-4 design was constructed between 1929 and 1953, and it describes the model as a pre-cut frame lookout measuring 14 by 14 feet.[^5]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists Mr. LG 'Jud' Morris as a staffer from 1939 to 1941, citing the Former Forest Fire Lookout Registry.[^2]

Firelookout.com dates the abandonment to 1950 and says the lookout is no longer on the site.[^1] Forest Lookouts and Idaho Fire Lookouts both give 1950 for abandonment.[^2][^3] The banner on the firelookout.com page gives 1924 to 1935 instead, which conflicts with its own text. That end year matches the L-4 replacement, which may explain it, but the page does not say so.[^1]

The Idaho lookout list at firelookout.org marks the site as gone.[^4] No source we read describes what remains of the lookout today.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site "Drive-Up Abandoned / Destroyed."[^2] Forest Service road 319 leads to the Fog Mountain Trailhead, which needs a four-wheel drive vehicle unless visitors hike or ride in on horseback.[^6] That trailhead page does not describe the lookout site itself. From the trailhead, the Fog Mountain Trail climbs the mountain's steep flanks.[^6]
