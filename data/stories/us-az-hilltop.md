The Hilltop Lookout stands on the San Carlos Reservation in Gila County, Arizona.[^1] The Bureau of Indian Affairs built it in 1930.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register calls it an early model L-4 with expanded catwalks, and places it within the fire management system of the San Carlos Agency.[^1]

The sources I read name no staff, no fires and no later changes to the lookout.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists its type as Ground and its status as Standing.[^2] It was added to the National Historic Lookout Register on April 1, 2005, as number US 632.[^1] The register gives its elevation as 6,606 feet.[^1]

Today, the sources do not say whether the lookout is staffed or open to visitors, and I could not read a recent trip report. Ask the reservation before you go, and do not enter a structure that is closed or private.

[^1]: Hilltop Lookout, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/az/hilltop-lookout/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Arizona Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/az/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
