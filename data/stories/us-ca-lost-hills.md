In October 1938 two people were stationed at the Lost Hills lookout station in Kern County.[^1] An item from the Bakersfield Californian, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, describes one of them, Fred Harmon, as a county forestry employee, and names the other as Butler Seibert.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page names the Kern County Fire Department as the agency.[^1] The 1938 item instead refers to the county forestry department, and the sources do not say whether the two are the same body.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list also includes Lost Hills.[^2] It lists the type as unknown, the status as gone, and the location as undetermined.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2]
