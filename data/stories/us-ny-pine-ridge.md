The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a standing open tower called Pine Ridge in Clinton County.[^1] OpenStreetMap marks a tower at the same spot as the Flat Rock Fire Tower and tags it as a lattice observation tower.[^2] We found no build date, builder or height for it.

The names point to the Altona Flat Rock, which an Adirondack Almanack article describes as a place of high cliffs and rock ridges.[^3] The same article carries a photo credit for an "Altona Flat Rock Fire Tower" from 2005.[^3] It also says a game warden often watched conditions from a 20-foot fire tower on the central Flat Rock.[^3] The article does not say whether that structure is the tower the FFLA lists today.[^3]

The article describes a fire that began on the Rock in August 1957 and was the worst fire the state had seen in over ten years.[^3] A separate blaze scorched 40 acres of the Rock in 1965.[^3] Since then, Miner Institute has run a permit system that controls who can enter the area.[^3]

The FFLA's April 2022 newsletter calls the Pine Ridge tower near Altona a private tower.[^4] The FFLA list still shows it as standing.[^1] We found no staffing, visiting or owner information, so anyone curious should leave it alone unless the owner gives permission.
