Solomon Mountain is in Boundary County, Idaho, on the Kaniksu National Forest, about 11 miles northeast of Bonners Ferry.[^1][^3][^4]

The lookout was a 75-foot pole L-6 tower with a cabin, built in 1935.[^1][^2][^3]

The lookout list names workers on the tower from 1935 to 1951.[^1] Edward Glen and his wife were the first name on it, followed by Jonas Parshall in 1938 and Loretta Bell in 1944.[^1][^2][^5] Harry Wonenburg (1946), Harry Hofstetter (1947) and George Brabb (1949) came next, and Tom Coble and his wife were the last named, in 1950 and 1951.[^1][^2]

Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both say the tower was destroyed in 1958.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts instead says it was removed, and gives no date.[^3] The sources disagree on this point.

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site drive-up.[^2] No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the road to it is open to visitors. Check with the Kaniksu National Forest before you drive out.
