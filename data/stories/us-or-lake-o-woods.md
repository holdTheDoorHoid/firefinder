Lake O' Woods started as a crow's nest in a tree in the 1920s.[^2] The register places it about three miles north of the Rogue River.[^2] An L-4 cab was added in 1933.[^1][^2] The site is in Curry County, Oregon.[^1] The Forest Service puts it about 21 miles northeast of Gold Beach, while firelookout.com puts it 21 miles southeast of Port Orford.[^4][^1]

The cabin that stands now came from somewhere else. Its steel flat-topped cab was first erected on Barklow Mountain in 1955, where it replaced an L-5 cab built there in 1933.[^3][^2] The register spells the mountain "Barkalow."[^2] In 1974 the cab was flown by helicopter to this site and placed on a short steel tower with a catwalk.[^4][^1][^2]

The Forest Service records fire-season staffing from 1974 through 1996.[^4] Firelookout.com says it was last staffed in 1998, so the two sources disagree.[^1] They also differ on the tower: the Forest Service describes an eight-foot tower, while the register gives a 10-foot steel I-beam tower.[^4][^2]

The Forest Service and the register both call it Lake of the Woods Lookout.[^4][^2] The register lists it as US 494.[^2]

Today it is a one-room cabin for one to four people, rented through Recreation.gov or by calling 1-877-444-6777.[^4] The Forest Service lists the site as open.[^4] Guests provide their own sleeping gear and cooking equipment.[^4] The windows give 360-degree views that take in the Pacific Ocean and the Kalmiopsis and Wild Rogue Wildernesses.[^4]
