Green Creek Point sat in Idaho County.[^3] The statewide list gives its position as 45.96225 north and 115.87808 west.[^4] Its tower was a 100-foot steel structure, which both lookout histories describe as an Aermotor, and it was built in 1931.[^1][^2][^3] The tower stood on the Nez Perce National Forest, according to a compiled list of Idaho County lookouts.[^3]

A patrol point lay 1.5 miles east, at Wall Peak.[^1][^2] The Idaho Fire Lookouts site files the lookout under its Selway region.[^2] The firelookout.com page gives the lookout's run as 1931 into the 1950s.[^1]

Both lookout histories say the tower was gone by the 1950s.[^1][^2]

The state list also marks the tower as gone.[^4] No source I found says what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
