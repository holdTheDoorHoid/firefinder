Old Summit is a former tower site in Graham County, Arizona, at 33.41336 north and 109.93736 west.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Arizona list marks the tower as gone.[^1] The Forest Lookouts Arizona index places the site on the San Carlos Apache Reservation.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Arizona Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/az/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Arizona - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts, https://westlookouts.weebly.com/arizona.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
