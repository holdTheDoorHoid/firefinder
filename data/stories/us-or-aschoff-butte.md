Aschoff Butte is a former lookout site in Clackamas County, Oregon, in the Mount Hood National Forest, about 15 miles west-northwest of Mount Hood.[^1][^2][^3]

According to the Former Fire Lookout Sites register, the Forest Service put up a tree crow's nest and a tent here around 1925.[^1] Firelookout.com lists a crow's nest and camp for the same period.[^2]

By about 1935 both sources describe a treated timber tower, but they disagree on its height.[^1][^2] The register gives 110 feet, while firelookout.com gives 82 feet and calls it an L-6 tower.[^1][^2] The Oregon Lookouts page also lists a "Panorama photos" entry dated June 20, 1934, about a year before the tower's date.[^3]

Both sources say the tower was gone by 1960.[^1][^2]

Today the sources do not describe what, if anything, remains on the butte, such as footings or a foundation, and they do not say whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2] Anyone planning a trip should check access with the Mount Hood National Forest first.
