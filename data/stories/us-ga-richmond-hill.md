A list of Georgia lookout towers on tnlandforms.us places a tower called "richmond hill" in Bryan County.[^1] The list is a Google map that its header says was revised in 2016.[^1] Its entry gives coordinates and an elevation of 18, with no unit stated, and no register number.[^1] Treat the entry as a lead rather than a confirmed record, since it is a single line in an informal list.

No source describes what remains at the site today.
