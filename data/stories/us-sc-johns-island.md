The Johns Island lookout was a South Carolina Forestry Commission tower in Charleston County.[^1] According to a lookout history site that quotes the state commission's report for 1939 and 1940, the Civilian Conservation Corps did all of the fire tower construction at no cost to the state, and a new tower went up on the Johns Island site.[^1] In the 1956–57 report year, the old wooden tower there was replaced by a new steel structure.[^1]

The commission sold the tower at auction in February 2011 for $2,415, including taxes and fees, and removal was required within 45 days.[^1] A blog post suggests the commission is selling several towers to the highest bidder, and it names the Johns Island Tower among them.[^2] Bidding starts at $500, and the purchaser is responsible for removing the tower.[^2] The commission shut down its fire tower system on October 1, 1993, and the Johns Island tower was one of its towers.[^1][^3]

Today, no source says whether the tower was removed, what remains at the Johns Island site, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1]
