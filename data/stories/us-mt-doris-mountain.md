Doris Mountain is about six miles south of Hungry Horse, in Flathead County, Montana, on the Flathead National Forest.[^1] Its elevation is 7,437 feet.[^1]

Firelookout.com describes an L-4 cab built in 1931 and destroyed in 1950.[^1] A Daily Inter Lake report dated August 4, 1930, reprinted on the Forest Lookouts Montana page, says a forestry student was stationed on Doris lookout at the time.[^2] The two dates do not line up, and the sources we read do not explain the gap.[^1][^2]

That report names Kendall Rutherford, 19 years old, a forestry student of Wyoming who was stationed on the lookout. It says he died by drowning while swimming in an unnamed lake in the South Fork district, on the afternoon before the report ran.[^2]

Firelookout.com does not give a cause for the 1950 destruction.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list classes the site as a ground lookout and marks it gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains on the mountain today or whether visitors may go there. Check with the Flathead National Forest before planning a trip.
