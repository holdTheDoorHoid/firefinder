In May 1930 the state's Department of Conservation and Development announced a new fire lookout for the western part of Columbus County.[^1] The tower would probably go about two miles east of Chadbourn.[^1] The plan called for a 100-foot steel tower with an inside stairway, an enclosed cabin at the top, and a range of vision of about 15 miles in all directions.[^1]

By August 1931 the tower was finished.[^1] It stood about one mile east of Chadbourn on State Highway 20 and was the 22nd unit in a proposed state system of 125 towers.[^1] C.R. Bailey of Chadbourn gave the state the site.[^1] A lookout history site lists the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources as the agency.[^1]

No source I could open says whether the tower still stands or what remains at the site today.
