Gyp Point is a peak in Marion County, Oregon, and PeakVisor files it under Mount Hood National Forest.[^2] The app gives the summit's position as 44.861681 N, 121.95851 W, and its elevation as 5,062 feet (1,543 m).[^2]

The Forest Lookouts page has one line of history for the site: in 1932 it served as a supplementary lookout point.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach the summit.
