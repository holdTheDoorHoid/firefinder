Hilltop was a lookout in Lincoln County, Oregon, about 16 miles southeast of Newport, on the divide between Big Elk Creek and Drift Creek.[^1][^2][^3] The register describes it as a 40-foot pole tower, administered by the Lincoln FPA.[^1]

The build date is disputed. The register and firelookout.com both give 1935.[^1][^2] A newspaper quoted on Rex's page reported in October 1934 that a lookout house and tower on that divide had just been completed, and that the tower was 40 feet high.[^3] That newspaper also names the supervisor of the Siuslaw National Forest, while the register lists the Lincoln FPA as administrator.[^1][^3] Panoramic photographs were taken at the site in March 1934 and again in September 1936.[^3]

The register says the tower was removed in 1950. Firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2] Rex's page lists the site as removed but gives no year.[^3]

The register recorded the site on November 10, 2010, and its page carries a 2010 photograph of the site.[^1] None of the sources we read describes what remains there now, or whether the public can reach it.
