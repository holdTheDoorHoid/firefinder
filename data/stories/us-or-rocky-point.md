Rocky Point is a former lookout site on the Oregon North Coast, in Lincoln County, about 12 miles east of Lincoln City.[^1][^2] The register gives its elevation as 2,890 feet and lists it under the register numbers US 732 and OR 141, with coordinates of N 44° 58.480' W 123° 47.506'.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the same elevation and calls the structure a cabin.[^2]

Both listings date the cabin to the 1920s and say it had disappeared by the 1950s.[^1][^2] The register names Lincoln FPA as the administering body, and lists Ray Kresek as the nominator.[^1] Its description of the site is a single sentence, and the entry is dated November 15, 2010.[^1]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains of the site today.[^1][^2]
