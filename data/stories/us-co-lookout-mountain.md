Lookout Mountain Tower Site is on Lookout Mountain in Saguache County, inside the Rio Grande National Forest.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists its elevation as 11,516 feet (3,510 metres), gives its registry numbers as US 537 and CO 9, and recorded it on September 18, 2009. Its entry says the tower no longer stands.[^1]

The sources disagree on what kind of structure stood there. The register and a western lookouts website both speak of a tower, while the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Colorado list calls it a platform.[^1][^2][^3] All three list it as removed.[^1][^2][^3]

No source I could reach describes what, if anything, is left on the summit today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^3]: Colorado Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/co/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
