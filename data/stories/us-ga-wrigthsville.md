A list of Georgia lookout towers on tnlandforms.us includes an entry labelled "wrigthsville" in Johnson County. It is number GA243 on the list and carries the tags "BLL-41091" and "gfc-624".[^1] The page gives a "google map revised" date of 22 March 2016.[^1]

The label is most likely a misspelling of Wrightsville, which Wikipedia describes as the county seat of Johnson County.[^2] The list's coordinates place the tower about 1.6 mi (2.5 km) south of the point Wikipedia gives for the town.[^1][^2] Wikipedia gives the town's population as 2,195 at the 2010 census, and places it in the Dublin Micropolitan Statistical Area.[^2]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at this site today, or whether it is open to the public.

[^2]: Wrightsville, Georgia, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrightsville,_Georgia (accessed 2026-10-21).
