Red Mountain, in Trinity County, California, had a lookout tower on its summit.[^1][^2] A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description from 1961 calls it a four-legged steel structure that supported a cabin and stood 50 feet high.[^1] The station sits on the highest point of the mountain, about six miles south and four miles west of Wildwood, and 10½ miles south and 1⅓ miles east of Peanut.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site lists Trinity National Forest as the agency and marks the tower as removed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list shows it as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today. Because the page names Trinity National Forest as the agency, its office is the logical place to ask before anyone visits.[^1]
