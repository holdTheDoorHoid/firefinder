Poison Peak is a Salmon National Forest lookout site in Lemhi County, Idaho.[^1]

The earliest record I found is a newspaper item from August 1935.[^1] It says a new lookout had been set up at Poison Peak, behind the Edson ranch, and the paper understood that Lynn Thomas was in charge.[^1]

A 1982 overview of the forest's lookouts by Bing Young says 1929 was among the worst fire seasons the forest has recorded, the Wilson Creek Fire above all.[^2] It says that year's fires, including the Wilson Creek Fire, pushed the forest to improve detection, and that this, along with Depression-era labor, made a large lookout building effort possible.[^2] The same overview says 1935 appears to have been the peak, when 34 lookouts stood across the forest.[^2]

No source I found describes the site today, so I cannot say what remains there or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Poison Peak, Forest Lookouts (Ron Kemnow), https://idaholookouts.weebly.com/poison-peak.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: An Overview of the Lookouts in the Salmon National Forest, Bing Young (1982), IDAHO: A Climbing Guide, https://www.idahoaclimbingguide.com/an-overview-of-the-lookouts-on-the-salmon-national-forest-by-bing-young-1982/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
