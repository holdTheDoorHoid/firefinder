Nipple Mountain had a 40-foot pole tower with an L-6 cab and an accompanying cabin for living quarters, built in 1931.[^1] It sat in the Nez Perce National Forest, 11 miles southwest of Elk City, in Idaho County, at 7,098 feet.[^1][^2]

The lookout was destroyed in 1968.[^1][^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page gives the same destruction year and lists the site as a hike-in.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list also marks the tower as gone.[^4] Photographs on the Idaho page are courtesy of The Fire Lookout Museum.[^3]

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit. Anyone planning a trip should ask the Nez Perce National Forest about access first.
