The Midway Lookout Tower stands in Yazoo County, Mississippi, according to the National Historic Lookout Register.[^1] The register gives the registration number US 1741, MS 58, and says the Mississippi Forestry Commission administers the tower.[^1] It was registered on July 9, 2023, with Jason Johns as the nominator.[^1]

Wikipedia lists the community of Midway, in Yazoo County, as unincorporated. It places the community on Mississippi Highway 433, roughly 12.6 miles east-northeast of Yazoo City.[^2] The sources I read do not say how the tower relates to that community beyond the shared name.

The register describes the tower as a 100-foot Aermotor LS-40 and gives its elevation as 398 feet (121 m).[^1] OpenStreetMap lists the tower as a lattice observation tower but gives its elevation as 110 metres.[^3] The two sources disagree on elevation, and this story uses the register's figure.

The register lists the build date as unknown.[^1] I found no other account of when the tower went up, who put it there, or whether it has been moved, and nothing about its fires or staffing.

The register notes a site visit on July 2, 2023.[^1] I could not tell whether the public can reach the tower, and I found no later report of its condition. Please check with the Mississippi Forestry Commission before planning a trip, and do not climb the tower without permission.
