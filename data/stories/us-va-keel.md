The Virginia lookout list at firelookout.org includes a tower called Keel in Dickenson County. It gives the site as 37.13550 north, 82.45827 west, and marks the tower as gone.[^1]

Keel should not be confused with the Birch Knob tower. The same list places a Birch Knob tower on Pine Mountain about 11 km to the north, also marked as gone.[^1] A Virginia Association of Counties page about visiting Dickenson County describes an older 54-foot fire observation tower on Birch Knob that was in operation from 1964 to 1978 and was dismantled in 1980.[^2] That account concerns Birch Knob, not Keel.

No source describes the Keel site today, so it is not known whether footings or other remains survive, or whether the spot is open to visitors.[^1]

[^1]: Virginia Lookouts, firelookout.org, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Visit Dickenson County and the Birch Knob Tower, Virginia Association of Counties, https://www.vaco.org/county-connections/visit-dickenson-county-birch-knob-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
