Prouty Peak sits at 6,263 feet in the Colville National Forest, in Pend Oreille County, Washington, about 12 miles east-northeast of Metaline Falls.[^1][^2] A Forest Lookouts page gives the forest as the Kaniksu National Forest instead.[^4]

In 1930 a 40-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab was built on the peak.[^1][^2][^3] A tent cabin and a patrol tower stood half a mile northeast.[^1][^2][^3]

Willhite reports that Jimmie Monroe was struck by lightning at the site in 1933.[^3]

Sources disagree on how the lookout ended. The register gives 1935 as the year it was destroyed.[^1] Willhite and the Forest Lookouts page both say that after the 1935 fire season, the legs were sawed off and the tower was shoved over a cliff.[^3][^4] Firelookout.com instead dates its abandonment to the 1940s and lists the lookout as gone.[^2]

The register added the site on April 17, 2020.[^1] None of the sources used for this story describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, so the story does not describe any remains or view.
