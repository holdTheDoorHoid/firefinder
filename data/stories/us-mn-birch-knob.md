The Birch Knob lookout stood in St. Louis County, roughly 15 miles northwest of Virginia, Minnesota, on ground the U.S. Forest Service maintained within the Superior National Forest.[^1] A federal survey description written by the Forest Service in 1936 already describes a steel lookout tower at the spot, shorter than 100 feet.[^1] The same record places a Forest Service bronze survey disc in concrete at the tower's base.[^1]

A compiled list of Minnesota towers reads as showing the tower still standing in 1972 and removed in 2007.[^2] The entry is brief, so the 2007 date rests on that one line.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list also marks the tower as gone.[^3]

We found no source that describes the site today or says whether the public can reach it.
