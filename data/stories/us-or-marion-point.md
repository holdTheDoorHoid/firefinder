Marion Point is a lookout site in Union County, Oregon, about 13 miles south-southwest of La Grande, on the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest.[^1] Its listed elevation is 6,535 feet.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page gives its location as 5S-37E-32.[^2] Oregon's index gives its coordinates as 45.09072, -118.21830.[^3]

One listing dates the lookout to the 1930s, with no exact year given.[^1] A July 4, 1940 item from The Record-Courier, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, said a man named Neal Taylor would be placed at Marion Point as soon as a house could be built for him.[^2]

The listing calls the lookout abandoned, and Oregon's index shows the site as gone.[^1][^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
