The Georgia lookout tower list on tnlandforms.us gives an entry numbered GA242 at a point labelled "china hill" in Telfair County.[^1] The entry gives coordinates, a county and an elevation, but no details of the structure itself.[^1] The name is the compiler's own label, because the list says names for towers numbered 73 and above are its creation, and GA242 falls in that range.[^1]

Telfair County is home to China Hill, an unincorporated community.[^2] The community had a post office under the name Chinahill from 1876 until 1904, and took its name from a stand of chinaberry trees near its first townsite.[^2]

A separate archaeology study covers a proposed road realignment that branches off US 441/SR 31 in the county. It calls the Telfair County Lookout/Fire Tower National Register eligible and says the realignment aims to minimize impacts to it.[^3] The list also has a Telfair Lookout Tower entry, GA054, about 10 miles (16 km) from this point by coordinates.[^1] No source says whether the study's tower is GA054, this point, or a third structure.[^1][^3]

No source found says what, if anything, stands at the China Hill point today, or whether it is open to visitors.
