The Lyndoch fire tower is described as steel.[^1] The firelookout.ca register gives its address as Tweed, Ontario, and lists coordinates of 45.34472, -77.13028.[^1]

A map note on the same register page says the tower no longer appears on the fourth edition of Pembroke topographic map 31F. It does appear on the third edition, from 1977.[^1]

Across Ontario, most lookout towers went up between the 1920s and the 1950s, when forest fire protection was at its height.[^2] Ontario's Department of Lands and Forests oversaw them, and in 1972 it was renamed the Ministry of Natural Resources.[^2] Through the 1960s the towers saw less use, as aircraft spotting and fire-fighting crews took on more of the work.[^2]

No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at the Lyndoch site today.
