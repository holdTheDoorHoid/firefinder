Heavens Peak #1 was a two-room frame cabin, 15 by 17 feet, built in 1945 on the mid-slope of the Glacier Wall, about two miles east of Heavens Peak.[^1] The site lies within Glacier National Park, in Flathead County, Montana, at 4,700 feet, about six miles west of Logan Pass.[^1]

The standing Heavens Peak lookout shares the name. It dates from 1945 and was staffed until 1953.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the two as separate entries, and marks this one as a ground site that is gone.[^2] The association's coordinates put the site about four miles from the standing lookout's listed position, which does not match firelookout.com's two-mile figure,[^1] so the location is unresolved.[^2]

The firelookout.com guide says the cabin was destroyed in 1963.[^1] No source consulted describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
