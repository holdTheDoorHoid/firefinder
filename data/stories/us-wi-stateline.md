The Stateline tower stood in Vilas County, in northern Wisconsin, according to a Wisconsin lookout history site.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register places the site at 46.24313, -89.63456 and lists the tower as gone.[^1]

OpenStreetMap still carries a point named Stateline Lookout Tower at the spot, but a map marker does not show whether a structure stands.[^3] No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the ground is open to visitors, so do not plan a climb there.

[^1]: Wisconsin Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Wisconsin - Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/wisconsin.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Stateline Lookout Tower, node 353896447, OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/353896447 (accessed 2026-10-10).
