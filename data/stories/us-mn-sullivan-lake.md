The Sullivan Lake fire tower stood on a hill on the lake's south shore, in eastern Morrison County, Minnesota.[^2] The owner was the Minnesota Forest Service. That agency is now the Forestry Division of the state DNR.[^2] The tower went up in 1937. It was 90 feet tall, built of steel, and had an exterior ladder.[^1][^2]

In September 1937, Civilian Conservation Corps crews were running telephone wire from the Garrison fire towers to the Sullivan Lake tower, according to a Brainerd Daily Dispatch item quoted on the Forest Lookouts site.[^1]

A 1960 survey note describes a steel frame on four legs, with an observation room up top, and gives the overall height as 100 feet.[^2]

Sources disagree on height. The Forest Lookouts page and the county survey both describe a 90-foot ladder tower, but the survey's overall figure is 100 feet.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^3]

The three sources we read do not describe what, if anything, remains on the hill today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
