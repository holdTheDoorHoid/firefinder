The Lucky Strike Lookout stood about 12 miles northeast of Ukiah, in the Umatilla National Forest, Umatilla County, Oregon.[^2] The register describes a 67-foot wooden L-4 tower with a ground cabin, built in 1936.[^1] A panorama photo in the same register is captioned July 25, 1935, a year before that build date, so the build year is not settled.[^1]

Firelookout.com names Mr. and Mrs. Clarence Huston as lookout personnel in 1936, and Keith Hutchinson in 1937.[^2]

The cabin left the site before the tower did.[^3] A Ukiah Ranger District annual report, quoted by Forest Lookouts, says that by January 1951 the Lucky Strike house had been set on a concrete base at Tower Mtn., and a garage from the site had been moved to the district guard residence.[^3] SummitPost says the ground cabin at Tower Mountain burned in the 1940s and was replaced by this one.[^5]

A January 1956 entry in the same annual report says the lookout was dropped from the detection system and the tower was taken down.[^3] The register and firelookout.com date the tower's removal to 1957.[^1][^2]

The cabin now serves as the living quarters at Tower Mountain, where a separate tower erected in 1935 stands at 92 feet.[^4] Oregon Hikers says visitors need a lookout on site before they can go inside the cabin.[^4] No source we read describes the Lucky Strike site itself today.
