Free Use Point lies about 12 miles south of Grangeville, in Idaho County, Idaho, on the Nez Perce National Forest, at 5,003 feet.[^1][^2]

The two sources disagree on the build year.[^1][^2] firelookout.com dates the 20-foot pole tower to 1931.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts dates its L-4 cab to 1934.[^2] Both give 1953 as the year the lookout was destroyed.[^1][^2] The photo on the firelookout.com page is credited to the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1]

No source I could find describes what remains at the point today. A recreation listing places the point inside the Nez Perce-Clearwater National Forests, classes it as a rest area, and mentions no tower.[^3]
