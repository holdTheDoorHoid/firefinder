Tuscarora is a steel fire lookout tower in Craven County, in eastern North Carolina.[^1][^2] In 1975 the National Geodetic Survey recorded it as a 90-foot (27 m) steel tower on four legs, owned by the North Carolina Forest Service.[^1]

The survey station at the site is named "Tuscarora Lot", and its record codes it as a lookout tower.[^1] The description places the tower about 10.5 miles northeast of Trenton and 12.5 miles west-northwest of New Bern.[^1] A 2003 recovery note by a US Power Squadron team says the station was recovered in good condition.[^1]

A list of North Carolina fire towers also names Tuscarora in Craven County, with the same survey number.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
