Ruddy Hill lies in the Mount Hood National Forest in Marion County, Oregon, about seven miles north of Mount Jefferson.[^1][^2]

The U.S. Forest Service put up a lookout there in the 1920s, a firefinder platform with a cabin.[^1] The register calls it a platform lookout, while firelookout.com calls it a platform tower with a cabin.[^1][^2]

Both sources say the platform and cabin were gone by 1942. Neither gives a more exact build date than the 1920s.[^1][^2]

The register gives the site an elevation of 5,943 feet, or 1,811 meters, and places it in Marion County.[^1] The site was added to the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register on September 20, 2010, with Ray Kresek as the nominator.[^1] Neither source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
