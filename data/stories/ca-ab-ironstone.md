Ironstone Lookout sits on the higher of Willoughby Ridge's two summits, the southern one, in the Crowsnest Pass of south-west Alberta.[^1][^2] One account describes it as a fire lookout under active management.[^1] The route starts at the York Creek staging area south of Coleman, and a 2022 hike report gives the round trip as 12 km with 550 m (1,804 ft) of ascent.[^3]

Sarah's Bench sits beside the trail.[^3] It is named for Sarah Thompson, a former lookout observer.[^2][^3] When Bob Spirko climbed up in June 2015, an observer named Steve met the group at the lookout, and it was his fifth season on the job.[^2][^3] Spirko's route reached a gate at the last fork on the road.[^2]

Across Alberta, the province says it runs 127 lookout sites for spotting wildfire, each with an observer on watch, generally from April to October.[^4]

In June 2022 another hiker reported that no one appeared to be on duty at the lookout that day.[^3] That writer noticed an antenna rising from the watch tower and kept clear of the buildings.[^3] That is the most recent account we read. We found no source on whether the public may go into the buildings, or on whether the lookout is staffed now.
