Capitol Hill sits in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, six miles north-northwest of Wallace in Shoshone County, Idaho, at an elevation of 5,318 feet.[^1] A camp was located on the site in 1930.[^1][^2] A 40-foot pole L-4 tower went up in 1936.[^1][^2]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page names Jay Murray for 1937 and Mr. and Mrs. Clyde Peacock for 1946.[^2] Firelookout.com names only the Peacocks, also for 1946.[^1]

Both sites give 1960 as the year the tower was destroyed.[^1][^2]

A post on the Idaho Fire Lookouts site, dated July 3, 2024, describes a visit that found the tower's footings and two benchmarks.[^2] The writers say nothing else seems to remain beyond those, and that trees have grown up so thickly around the site that its view has been lost.[^2]

Neither cited page describes the site after that visit.[^1][^2] The writers call the drive up an easy one and say they met other visitors on the roads.[^2] The cited pages do not say whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2]
