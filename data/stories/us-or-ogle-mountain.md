Forest Lookouts lists Ogle Mountain under Clackamas County, Oregon, and names the Clackamas-Marion FPA as its agency.[^1]

A February 1916 Molalla Pioneer item, quoted on the same page, listed five sites in the district as lookout camps: High Camp, Highland Butte, Table Rock, Ogle Mountain and Mountain Home Rock.[^1]

A hiker's trip report says the route climbed directly to the summit, past four more mine openings.[^2] Those openings can be dangerous, so keep well clear of them. No source describes what remains of a lookout at the site today, or whether the summit is open to visitors.
