The Elk City lookout stood about one mile east of Elk City, Idaho, in Idaho County, on the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1][^4] The Forest Service list places it in Section 25, Township 29 North, Range 8 East.[^4] Its listed elevation is 4,549 feet.[^1][^2]

An L-4 tower went up around 1930.[^1][^3][^4] The Idaho County listing also records an L-4 cab.[^3]

Four sources date the abandonment to 1942.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list marks the tower as gone.[^5]

The Forest Service list was compiled in April 2010 from Ray Kresek's Fire Lookouts of the Northwest and other sources.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under its Selway region and marks it hike-in.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site today.
