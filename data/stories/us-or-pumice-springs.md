Pumice Springs was a tree lookout on the Deschutes National Forest in Deschutes County, Oregon, at about 5,030 feet (1,533 meters).[^1] The register dates it to about 1930 and calls it tree-based.[^1] Firelookout.com describes an open cab on top of an 80-foot tree.[^2]

A 2020 Wildfire Today article, based on drone photographs by Steven Stenkamp, says the site was also known as the Sand Springs lookout.[^3] The photographer dated it to 1930 and said it was in service for under ten years.[^3] He described the climb as a ladder for about 20 feet, then 2-by-4s, then branches, and finally another section of ladder near the platform.[^3] His drone data put the platform at 81 feet.[^3]

Firelookout.com says the lookout was abandoned in the 1940s and was still there in 1997, which is the last date it gives.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as abandoned, not gone.[^4] Firelookout.com also says its precise location had not yet been determined, although the register gives coordinates.[^1][^2]

The 2020 drone photographs show what remains of the tree, and the article says a section of ladder is still there.[^3] A trip report on The Roaming Civic blog describes two hours of searching near Sand Springs Campground, where the writers could not find the tree.[^5] We found no source that says whether the site is open to visitors.
