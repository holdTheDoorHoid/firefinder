The Lonedell fire tower was a Missouri Conservation Commission lookout in Franklin County.[^1] A 1965 newspaper report places it two miles east of Lonedell.[^1] The state's annual report for 1949-50 lists 40 acres obtained for the tower site.[^1]

Vandals struck the tower in 1963, 1964 and 1965, according to items from the Sullivan Tri-County News that the page quotes.[^1] Around August 6, 1963, someone broke the hasp off the door and threw the fire finder out of a window, causing about $25 in damage.[^1] In June 1964 the paper reported that a power meter had been smashed and windows broken.[^1] The vandals had not been traced.[^1]

In August 1965 a young man broke in and smashed the tower's windows, a stool and a fire-finding device.[^1] He was later fined and sentenced.[^1]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today.
