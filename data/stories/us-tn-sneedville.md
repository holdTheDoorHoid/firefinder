The Sneedville Lookout Tower stands in Hancock County, Tennessee.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register lists it as US 1088, TN 24 and gives its registration date as January 3, 2015.[^1] The Register puts the site at 2,559 feet.[^1] A Tennessee Landforms list gives the Sneedville tower 2,572 feet.[^2] The two sources differ by about 13 feet.

No source we read gives the year the tower was built, why it was put on that spot, or what its design is. The Register's page has no build date.[^1] A National Register study of Tennessee Division of Forestry towers says most of the division's towers went up between the late 1940s and about 1960.[^3] That is a statewide pattern, not a record for this tower.

The only visit report we found is by Jeff Flanary, who also nominated the tower.[^1] It is dated May 4, 2008, and it answers "Staffed? No," so the tower was not staffed at that time.[^1] The sources we read name no fires the tower watched.

The 2008 report says most of the glass was gone from the cab, the cab floor was gone, and the first set of stairs had been removed.[^1] It also says the steel looked good, the cab needed paint, and a fence encircles the tower. Overall, the report rates the tower in good condition.[^1] Landforms lists the tower as standing.[^2]

The report notes a small weather station and another tower next to the fire tower, with a dish and several antennas on that other tower.[^1] The newest visit record we found is from 2008, so today's condition and access are not known from these sources. The Register's form records that no directions to the tower are signed and no Register sign is posted.[^1] Anyone planning a visit should ask before going, and should not climb the tower unless it is open to visitors.
