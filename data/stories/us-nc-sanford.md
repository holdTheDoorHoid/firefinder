A list of North Carolina lookout towers includes one called Sanford, with coordinates of 35.45333, -79.19055.[^1]

The North Carolina Forest Service runs its Lee County office at 1400 Fire Tower Road in Sanford.[^2] The sources do not tie that road to the lookout.

No source describes what remains at the site today.

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: N.C. Forest Service Contacts - Lee County, NC Agriculture, https://www.ncagr.gov/divisions/nc-forest-service/contacts/lee (accessed 2026-10-21).
