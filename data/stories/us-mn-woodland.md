Woodland was a fire lookout in Kanabec County, Minnesota.[^1] The Central US Lookouts page for Minnesota gives its land reference as 42N-23W-17 and says a 100-foot (30.5 m) Aermotor tower was built there in 1955.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list marks the tower as gone.[^2]

A visitor's log on the kjackson.us tower site describes the place as the site of the former tower.[^3] The visitor first took a set of radio antennas nearby for the lookout, and a GPS receiver then led around a corner to the spot.[^3] The log's photo caption says the site lies next to a road and a house.[^3]

No source we found says whether the site is open to the public.
