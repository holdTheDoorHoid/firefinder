Brenton Point is the southern tip of Aquidneck Island, in the city of Newport, Rhode Island.[^1][^2] The state's park history says the point's ocean outlook and rugged ground made it a favored spot for early settlement.[^1] William Brenton, a colonial governor, gave the point its name.[^1]

The state made the grounds a state park in 1976, and the Rhode Island Department of Environmental Management's parks division administers it.[^2]

The tower itself is thinly documented. The only trace I found is a volunteer-made map entry, which places a feature called the Park Police fire tower at Brenton Point and tags it as an observation tower.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Rhode Island list covers lookout structures, and none of its entries carries that name.[^4]

Nothing I could read gives a build date, a builder, a staff history or a photograph for this tower. No source I read says whether it still stands.[^3]

Access is not documented. Brenton Point is a recreation area, but nothing I found says whether the tower can be reached, so look at it from the park grounds and ask park staff before going near it.[^2]
