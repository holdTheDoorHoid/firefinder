The ZX Ranch lookout was a shared job, at least by 1929.[^2] In that year the Fremont National Forest and the Klamath Indian Agency ran it as a cooperative post.[^2] A year later the Klamath Agency wrote that it would not pay its share of the lookout and smoke-chaser's wages that season, pointing to a road crew in the Sycan country and a man posted on Yamsay.[^2] The Fremont forest supervisor replied that the forest would take care of the station itself.[^2]

The tower still standing there was built in 1933: a 20-foot pole tower with a 6-by-6 cab.[^1][^3][^4] The sources do not say whether it replaced the 1929 station.[^1][^2]

The firelookout.com listing calls the tower abandoned, but gives no date for that status.[^3] The register lists the site as private land with no public access, and a 2008 photograph on its page shows the lookout.[^1] A 2026 statewide list of standing lookouts shows it as unmanned and not open to the public.[^4]

Today the ZX Ranch is private land, so the tower is not a place for visitors.[^1][^4]
