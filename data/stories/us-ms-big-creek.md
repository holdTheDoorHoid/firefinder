The Forest Lookouts site files the Big Creek tower under Wayne County and the De Soto National Forest.[^1] The national forest covers parts of ten Mississippi counties, and Wayne is second only to Perry in land area.[^2] The site lists the tower as removed and gives no further detail.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors. Ask the national forest's office before going near the site.
