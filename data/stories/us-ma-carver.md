The Carver Fire Tower stands on Bare Hill in Plymouth County, Massachusetts, and overlooks a section of the Myles Standish State Forest.[^1] The Massachusetts Bureau of Forest Fire Control administers it, and the register dates the present tower to 1988.[^1]

The register describes an 80-foot tubular steel tower with a slant-window cab and no door onto the catwalk. The register says it is the biggest and tallest lookout in Massachusetts, and counts it as the third tower watching over this part of the forest.[^1]

The site had older towers, and two sources disagree on their dates.[^3][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says a 38-foot tower from Chelmsford was moved to the site in 1918, and a new 68-foot iron tower was built in 1930.[^3] A Forest Fire Lookout Association post says the Chelmsford tower arrived in 1919, and that the tower built in 1932 served only to protect the state forest's own lands.[^2] Both accounts say the older tower closed at the end of the 1986 fire season, and both say it was dismantled and moved to Minnesota in 2002.[^3][^2]

The register's visit report from March 24, 2014 says the tower has gone several years without staff. It also records damaged backup solar panels and says the tower needs paint.[^1]

That 2014 report is the most recent we could read, so the tower's present condition is unconfirmed.[^1] The register notes no NHLR sign at the site.[^1] We found no public visiting arrangement, so ask the Bureau before going near it.
