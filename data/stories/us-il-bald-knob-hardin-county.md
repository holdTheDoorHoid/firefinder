The register lists a Bald Knob Lookout Site in Hardin County, Illinois, inside the Shawnee National Forest.[^1] It gives the elevation as 511 feet, or 156 meters, and says the U.S. Forest Service administers the site.[^1]

The register dates the tower's removal to a time before 1984.[^1] The entry was registered on January 8, 2021, on the nomination of David Kissane.[^1]

Other listings do not match this record. The Forest Lookouts page for Bald Knob describes a Union County site in the Shawnee National Forest.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Illinois list places the Union County Bald Knob at 37.55182 and -89.34614, and marks it gone.[^2] The register gives this Hardin County site as 37.558460 north and 88.335120 west.[^1] That is nearly the same latitude, but about a degree of longitude farther east.

The same association's Hardin County list names Hicks and Melcher Hill, both platforms, and Peters Creek, a tower. None is called Bald Knob, and all three are marked gone.[^3]

We could not tell whether the register describes a separate Hardin County tower or the Union County Bald Knob with a wrong location. No source we read describes what remains at the Hardin County location today, or whether the public can reach it.
