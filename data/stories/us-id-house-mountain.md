House Mountain stands in Elmore County, Idaho, about 38 miles east-southeast of Boise, in the Boise National Forest.[^1][^2] Its elevation is 7,700 feet.[^1][^2]

In 1931 there was a cabin at the lookout site.[^1][^2][^3] In 1968 a two-story cab was built.[^1][^2] The sources differ on construction. The firelookout.com page describes a two-story concrete frame, Idaho Fire Lookouts describes a frame cab on a concrete base, and Forest Lookouts describes a cab atop a cinder-block first story.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists a couple, Mr. and Mrs. Glen Baldwin, as staff in 1932 and again in 1938, citing The Idaho Statesman.[^2] It lists Burt Search for 1968.[^1][^2]

In August 1935 the road to the lookout was closed during hunting season, according to an Idaho Statesman item that Forest Lookouts quotes.[^3]

The lookout was gone by 1990.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts marks it only as removed.[^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit, or whether the road and summit are open to visitors today.
