Red Mountain's lookout was an L-4 cab on a stone base, built in 1934 at 8,722 feet in the Boise National Forest.[^1][^2][^3] It stood 15 miles northeast of Lowman, in Boise County.[^1][^3]

The Rex's page says the cab was last staffed in 1967.[^3] The firelookout.com record has a note for 1968 that reads as an emergency, but it does not say what happened.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts has no documented staffing history for the lookout.[^2]

Sources disagree on when the cab left the mountain. The Rex's page says it was removed but gives no year.[^3] A presentation by a Boise National Forest archaeologist lists Red Mountain as removed in 1983.[^4] The firelookout.com record calls the site gone.[^1]

A SummitPost climbers' page says one of its views shows the lookout's former foundation at the top, and the page gives no date.[^5] No other source describes what is on the summit now.

The Forest Service trailhead page shows the status "Site Open", but it lists operating hours as Memorial Day to Labor Day, a period that had ended by October 10, 2026. The summit trail is trail 143.[^5][^6] A notice on that page reports a road and trail closure in the Crooked Fire area, but does not say whether it reaches the Red Mountain trail. For site-specific questions, the page points readers to the Lowman Ranger District.[^6]
