Dead Horse Rim sits in Lake County, Oregon, in the Fremont-Winema National Forest, about 18 miles northeast of Bly.[^1][^2][^4]

In July and August of 1911, Forest Guard Lester E. Elder worked the point as a patrol lookout. That August, a trail was marked up to the rim from Deadhorse Lake.[^1]

Firelookout.com dates a crow's nest at this site to 1913.[^2] A June 1915 note said Dead Horse Rim and Drakes Peak were to receive fire-finding instruments.[^1] The sources do not say whether the 1911 patrol point and the 1913 crow's nest were the same structure.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list places the site under the name Dead Horse Lake, as a crow's nest.[^3] Firelookout.com says the crow's nest was still there in 1993 and marks it abandoned.[^2]

Today, no source checked describes what, if anything, remains on the rim.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Dead Horse Rim Trail #139, a Forest Service trail, crosses the rim on its way to Forest Road 34. No other improved access reaches the Dead Horse and Augur Creek Roadless Areas.[^4]
