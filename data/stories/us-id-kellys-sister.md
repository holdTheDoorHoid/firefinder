Kellys Sister was a fire lookout in the Clearwater National Forest, in Clearwater County, Idaho.[^1][^2][^3] It stood about 18 miles east of Kelly Creek Ranger Station, at an elevation of 5,822 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Service's list of Clearwater lookouts, compiled in June 2016, gives the build year as 1936.[^1] The lookout was a 90-foot steel tower with a log cabin.[^1][^2][^3] A forest fire burned that cabin the same year.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Service list mentions only the cabin's loss.[^1] The lookout-history sites describe the whole lookout as gone.[^2][^3][^4] Its listed coordinates are 46.71996, -114.87510.[^4]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, such as footings or a foundation. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the spot as a hike-in site.[^3] We found nothing on whether visitors are allowed there, so check with the Forest Service before you go.
