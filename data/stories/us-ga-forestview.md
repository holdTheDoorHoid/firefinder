A Georgia lookout tower index lists a lookout called Forestview, in Camden County, at 30.92509, -81.59276.[^1] The index gives its elevation as 27 with no unit; read as feet, that is about 8.2 m.[^1]

Camden County lies in the southeastern corner of Georgia.[^2] It is among the state's first counties, created on February 5, 1777.[^2] Its county seat is Woodbine, and its largest city is Kingsland.[^2] According to the 2020 census, its population was 54,768.[^2] The county is also part of a combined statistical area that spans the Florida–Georgia line.[^2]

No source describes the Forestview site today, so it is not known whether a tower or any remains stand there, or whether the public can reach the spot.
