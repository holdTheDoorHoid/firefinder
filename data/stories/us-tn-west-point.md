The Tennessee Division of Forestry is the agency the Register lists for the West Point Lookout Tower in Lawrence County.[^1] A News-Democrat item dated October 7, 1955, reprinted on a lookout-history site, says the state had bought twelve 80-foot steel towers and that one was to go up at this place by early winter.[^2] I found no source that confirms when it was actually built.

The Register describes the tower as an 80-foot Aeromotor LS-40, a design the Division used widely until the mid-1970s.[^1][^3] The Register records a site visit on November 25, 2023, and its visit note describes the tower as tucked back in the woods, a short walk from the road.[^1] The Register gives December 16, 2023 as the date it registered the tower, with registry numbers US 1769, TN 71.[^1]

The sources disagree about whether it still stands.[^1][^4] The Register lists the tower with its 2023 visit and records no removal.[^1] Tennessee Landforms, however, lists the Westpoint tower as removed, with no year given.[^4] I could not settle which account is current.

I found no sources on who staffed the tower or which fires it watched. I also found no current rule on whether the public may visit or climb it. Check with the Division of Forestry before going, and do not climb any tower without permission.
