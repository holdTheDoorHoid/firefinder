The Julian lookout tower stood in Wayne County, Missouri, within the Mark Twain National Forest [^1]. A 1963 survey record names the U.S. Forest Service as both owner and operator of the tower [^1].

A Greenville Sun report from April 9, 1936, quoted on the Central US Lookouts page, says Company 726 of the CCC arrived at Greenville Camp F-5 on November 2, 1935 [^1]. The same report credits the crew with building the Julian Lookout Tower [^1]. It adds that the job was made harder by rough roads and severe cold weather [^1].

A 1963 Coast and Geodetic Survey station record describes a steel frame topped by an enclosed observation room [^1]. The same record puts its height at about 100 feet [^1].

The Central US Lookouts page now lists the tower as removed [^1].

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Julian - Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/julian.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
