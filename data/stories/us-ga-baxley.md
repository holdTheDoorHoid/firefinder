In December 1933 a steel tower 100 feet high was being erected on the old Baxley-Hazelhurst road, according to a Macon Telegraph item quoted on the Forest Lookouts website.[^1] The crew was Civilian Conservation Corps camp Alabax P-62.[^1] The item describes the tower as still being erected, not finished.[^1]

The item said a telephone line would tie the tower to Camp Alabax in Baxley, so that a fire could be reported as soon as it was spotted. It gave the tower's intended coverage as a twelve-mile radius.[^1]

The tnlandforms.us list has a site called baxley in Appling County and gives its elevation as 196. It carries two codes, BLL-64701 and gfc-801.[^2] Both lists use the name Baxley for an Appling County tower, but neither says they are the same structure.[^1][^2]

No source we found describes the Baxley site today, so we cannot say whether any part of the tower remains or whether the ground is open to visitors.
