Weitas Butte Lookout sits at 5,967 feet on the Clearwater National Forest, just three miles north of Rocky Ridge Lake along the historic Lolo Motorway.[^2][^3] Its first lookout went up in 1931, a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab on top.[^1][^3][^4] A garage was added in 1935.[^2][^4] In 1953 the pole tower was replaced by the timber structure visitors see today, a 53-foot tower with a 14-by-14-foot L-4 cab.[^1][^2]

The site was first known by a name that the Forest Service, when preparing maps, judged unfit for print, so it became Weitas.[^4] Sources give different distances from Pierce: Firelookout.com says 16 miles east, while the Forest Service directions say 38 miles east, off Forest Roads 500 and 557.[^2][^3]

The tower was one of the first lookouts placed in the rental system in 1983, and it could sleep up to four guests.[^1][^3] The National Historic Lookout Register added it on April 6, 2009.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts has no documented staffing history for the tower.[^4]

Rentals ended because of safety concerns, and the tower is now on standby.[^4] Firelookout.com also lists it as on standby.[^2] The Forest Service page, last updated February 19, 2025, lists the site as closed and not available for rental.[^3] The National Historic Lookout Register entry says rentals were expected to return in 2015.[^1]

Today the tower is a drive-up site on standby.[^4] Check with the Forest Service before you plan a trip.
