Tyson Peak Lookout stood on Tyson Peak in Benewah County, in the St. Joe National Forest, at 4,745 feet.[^1][^2]

The site had a camp in 1934, and a tower with an L-4 cab is listed for 1939.[^1][^3][^4]

Sources disagree on when the tower came down. Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts date its destruction to 1957.[^3][^1] Forest Lookouts quotes a National Geodetic Survey note that the tower had already been removed before the Survey's 1956 visit.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^2]

Forest Lookouts also reproduces the Survey's directions to its station mark. They say the road reaches a series of bad washouts, that only four-wheel-drive vehicles can go beyond them, and that a trail of about one mile leads to the top.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts calls the site a hike-in.[^1]

The Survey described a station tablet cemented in bedrock about seven inches lower than the ground, directly under the tower's center.[^4] No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
