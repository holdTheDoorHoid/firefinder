The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower under the name Keenan in Montgomery County. It gives the position as 30.32972, -95.64365, and marks the tower as gone.[^1]

A community with the same name lies about 10½ miles west of Conroe, in western Montgomery County, along the Atchison, Topeka and Santa Fe Railway and Farm Road 2854.[^2] According to the Texas State Historical Association, the community dates to about 1906 and was named for W. S. Keenan, a passenger agent for the railroad.[^2] Timber was also shipped from the community.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.

[^1]: Texas Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Keenan, TX, Texas State Historical Association, https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/keenan-tx (accessed 2026-10-21).
