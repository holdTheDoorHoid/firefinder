Alberta's wildfire lookout dataset gives 1982 as the year the Ram site was constructed, and it lists the type as a cabin.[^1] The site sits on Ram Mountain, reached by a remote hike about an hour's drive from Nordegg.[^2]

Two trip reports describe the ridge-top site. One mentions a small firewatch structure at the high point, with solar panels and grounding nearby.[^3] The other describes a helicopter landing pad at the lookout's high point.[^4]

Alberta says each of its 127 wildfire lookout sites is staffed by a lookout observer, generally from April to October, and that its wildfire staff, with Agriculture and Forestry, have adopted BearSmart recommendations for many sites.[^5] The hiking guide says an attendant lives on site.[^2]

The Alberta dataset lists the site as active.[^1]

The hiking guide rates the route moderate. It covers 19.1 km return, with about 1,055 m of total elevation gain counting ascents and descents, and takes an estimated 6.5 to 9 hours.[^2] The fenced lookout area needs permission to enter.[^2] The guide says not to cross the fence if the attendant cannot be asked.[^2] A November 2023 trip report found the small firewatch structure shut and secure for the winter.[^3]
