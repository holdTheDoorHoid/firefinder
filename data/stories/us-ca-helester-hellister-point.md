Helester Point, also spelled Hellister Point, was a lookout in the Tahoe National Forest in Placer County, California.[^1] The register dates it to 1938, when a California Region 5 Plan BC-301 cabin was set atop a 20-foot open steel tower with K-bracing.[^1] The U.S. Forest Service administered it.[^1]

Its ending is not clear. In 1981 the General Services Administration marked the building as surplus, and the register does not say when it was removed or where it went.[^1] The register's removed field gives circa 1982, but the text does not confirm that date.[^1] A 1986 survey, which the register calls the Thornton survey, showed the tower still standing and the cabin gone.[^1]

Today, the register describes no remains at the site and does not say where the tower stands now.[^1] It also does not say whether the site is open to visitors.[^1]
