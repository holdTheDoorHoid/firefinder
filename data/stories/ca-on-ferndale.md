The Ferndale tower is listed on Ferndale Road at Port Carling, in the Muskoka District of Ontario, at an elevation of 270 m (886 ft).[^1]

Sources date it to the middle of the 20th century, and one says the 1960s.[^2][^3] It was built as a lookout for the Ferndale Resort, not as a government fire tower.[^2][^3]

Sources disagree about its material. Firelookout.ca calls it a steel tower and says it appears on a 1996 Huntsville topographic map.[^1] The Ominous page calls it the wooden tower.[^3]

The tower is best known from the Red Green Show, where the character Ranger Gord watched for fires from it.[^3] A GeoCities list of Ontario towers still standing includes it, with a note that it is not an official tower.[^4]

A 2009 post on the Ominous page says the wooden tower still stands, with its bottom boarded over to keep people from climbing.[^3] A comment on the same page says the land is private, and that the tower may have been fixed up or rebuilt, though the commenter was not sure.[^3] No source describes its condition today or whether visitors can go onto the land.
