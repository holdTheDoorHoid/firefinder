Candlewood Mountain stands in Litchfield County.[^1] The page for it on the Forest Lookouts website gives only the name, the town of New Milford and the county.[^1] Mapping sites call the structure the Candlewood Lookout Tower.[^2][^3]

TopoZone gives its coordinates as 41.5675943°N, -73.4501224°W and an approximate elevation of 814 feet (248 m).[^3] OpenStreetMap records an elevation of about 794 feet (242 m) and tags the structure as an observation tower.[^2] ExpertGPS files a tower of the same name under Sherman, while the Forest Lookouts page gives New Milford and TopoZone places it on a New Milford map, so the sources disagree on the town.[^1][^3][^4]

No source I found describes the site today.
