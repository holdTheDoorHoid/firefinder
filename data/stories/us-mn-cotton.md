The Cotton lookout sits roughly two miles west of Cotton village in Saint Louis County.[^1] A National Geodetic Survey station description, reproduced on a local lookouts site, gives a four-legged steel frame roughly 100 feet tall, capped by an enclosed viewing deck.[^1] The Civilian Conservation Corps erected it in 1937, according to that same site and a 2011 photo caption on Wikimedia Commons.[^1][^2]

I found no record of who staffed the tower, when its fire watch ended, or any notable fire it was credited with. The Minnesota lookout list still marks the tower as standing.[^3]

The Commons caption also says the tower and its forestry station were sold at auction a few years before the photo was uploaded in August 2011.[^2] It names no buyer and no sale date. Wikidata lists the tower as located in the Cloquet Valley State Forest,[^4] which sits oddly with a sale, so the land ownership needs checking.

I found no current statement about public access or climbing. Until the owner says otherwise, treat the tower as closed to climbing.
