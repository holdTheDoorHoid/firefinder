Union Hill is listed as a Texas Forest Service lookout in Cass County.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas table gives the tower's coordinates and marks it as gone.[^1]

A National Geodetic Survey datasheet, reproduced on a Forest Lookouts page, calls the site the Union Hill Lookout Tower and places it on the USGS Sardis quadrangle.[^3] The datasheet has no description of the structure, so apart from the association's listing, none of the sources here says what the site is like today.[^1][^3]

A Tennessee Landforms table of Texas towers also places the tower in Cass County.[^4]

Before going, check with the landowner or the Texas Forest Service, and do not climb any structure you find.
