A Georgia lookout list on tnlandforms.us includes a tower listed as color, in Clinch County. Its listed position is 30.77257, -82.61438. The list gives its elevation as 123 with no unit; read as feet, that is 37.5 m.[^1] The page says it was revised on 22 March 2016.[^1]
