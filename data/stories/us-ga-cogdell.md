Cogdell is a census-designated place in Clinch County, Georgia, where the 2020 census counted 23 people.[^1] The place covers 0.89 square miles (2.31 km²), all of it land, and its postal code, 31634, is listed with Homerville.[^1]

An online list of Georgia lookout towers places a tower at Cogdell, at 182 ft (55.5 m), and tags the entry gfc-821.[^2] The list gives the point as 31.16460 north and 82.71997 west.[^2] The community's Wikipedia infobox gives its elevation as 186 ft, a few feet above the list's figure.[^1]

No source we found describes the site today, so what stands at the listed point, if anything, is not known.
