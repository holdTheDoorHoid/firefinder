Orphan Point was a lookout on the St. Joe National Forest in Shoshone County, Idaho.[^1][^3][^4] The county list gives the code 43N-4E-27 for its place in the forest.[^3]

In 1939 the site is listed as a camp. In 1942 it had a tower with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2][^3]

All three lookout listings say the tower was destroyed in 1954.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the site as gone, and it records the type as tower.[^4] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page gives its elevation as 6,250 feet.[^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site as hike-in, meaning visitors reach it on foot.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or says whether it is open to visitors.
