The Homosassa Fire Tower is in Citrus County, Florida. The register places it on US 19, at a former state forestry site.[^1] The register gives its elevation as 22 feet (7 meters).[^1]

The sources I could reach do not say when the tower was built or who designed it.[^1][^2] The register gives no construction year, and the regional lookout list gives only the tower's name and county.[^1][^2] Florida's lookout network was already growing in the 1930s, when a history of the Florida Division of Forestry counted 22 lookout towers by 1932. That history does not name Homosassa.[^3]

I found no record of who staffed the tower or which fires it watched.[^1][^2]

The register added the tower on July 24, 2016, as US 1138, FL 21, and describes it in the present tense.[^1] The same entry calls its site former state forestry land, so the sources do not settle whether the tower still stands there. Treat that present-tense description as unconfirmed.[^1]

Today the register names the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services as administrator.[^1] I found nothing on public access, climbing or rental for this tower, so anyone planning a visit should check with that agency first.[^1]
