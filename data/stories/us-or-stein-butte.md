Stein Butte rises to about 4,400 feet in the Rogue River National Forest, in Jackson County, Oregon.[^1] Panorama photographs were taken there in May 1934.[^3] A 10-by-10 L-5 lookout cabin went up in 1936.[^2][^3] The register places it on a minor point about a mile west-northwest of the summit.[^1]

A 1937 entry in the Oregon Lookouts history records ten dollars spent on water development.[^3] The cabin came to an end in 1968.[^1][^2][^3] The register says it was removed, while firelookout.com and Oregon Lookouts say it was destroyed.[^1][^2][^3]

Oregon Hikers describes a large marker on the summit that marks the cabin's life from 1936 to 1968, and says a rectangular foundation remains.[^4] That puts the marker on the summit rather than on the point the register names, so the sources disagree about where the cabin stood.[^1][^4] Visitors can reach the summit on the Stein Butte Trail, and Oregon Hikers says the summit has views of the Siskiyou peaks and Applegate Lake.[^4] There is no tower to climb.[^1][^2]
