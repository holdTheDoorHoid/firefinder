The McMinn Ridge fire tower stands in McMinn County, Tennessee, at an elevation of 1,296 feet according to the Tennessee Landforms table.[^1] The table gives its number as 3-3, a district and tower number from the state's 1976 list of towers. It also prints the code "FC1817" beside the name. The page says that six-character codes in its Info column are NGS datasheet PIDs, which can be looked up with an NGS tool.[^1]

The Tennessee Division of Forestry (TDF) built 208 fire lookout towers, with construction running from about 1930 to about 1970.[^2] The sources do not say when this tower went up, what design it used, or how tall it is.[^1][^2]

None of the sources describe staffing or fires at this tower.[^1][^2]

The 2014 National Register document lists McMinn Ridge among the Tennessee towers that were still standing on March 26, 2014.[^2] The Landforms table also shows it as standing, on a page last revised in January 2026.[^1] The 2014 list is more than a decade old, and the Landforms table gives no check date for individual towers. No newer record turned up, so its condition today is unknown. The Tennessee index of the National Historic Lookout Register, which counts 76 registered lookouts in the state, does not name it.[^3]

The sources say nothing about whether the tower can be climbed. The 2014 document says in general terms that surviving towers are often in poor repair and that visitors are often cautioned about the risks of going up them.[^2] Do not climb this tower. Check with the Tennessee Division of Forestry before going to see it.
