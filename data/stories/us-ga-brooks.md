A Georgia lookout tower list places a tower called Brooks in Brooks County, at 30.86824 and -83.56523.[^1] The same list, whose page records a Google map revision dated 22 March 2016, also names three other towers in the county: Catledge Hill Lookout Tower, Kennedy Hill and Quitman.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, and the only source for this tower is that list of names and positions.[^1]

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
