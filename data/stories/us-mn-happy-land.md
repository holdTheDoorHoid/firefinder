Happy Land is a former fire lookout site in Koochiching County, a little over three miles from Littlefork.[^1] The tower did not start out here.[^1] The Minnesota Forest Service erected it at Littlefork in 1934 as tower number 90, a 100-foot stairway lookout, and the county inventory suggests a nearby Civilian Conservation Corps camp may have built it.[^1] In 1953 it was moved to the Happy Land site.[^1]

The county inventory spells the name Happyland.[^1] It credits the name to rail workers who came up out of a bog, found fewer mosquitoes and called the spot a happy land, citing Warren Upham's Minnesota place-name encyclopedia.[^1]

The inventory says the site is destroyed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list marks the tower as gone.[^2]

No source we checked describes what, if anything, remains at Happy Land or at Littlefork today.
