Birdnest Lookout is in Northampton County, Virginia.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register gives its elevation as 33 feet (10 meters) and names the Virginia Department of Forestry as administrator.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as standing.[^2]

The sources I read say nothing about when the tower was built or how tall it is. The register's 2009 visit report calls it a metal "aerometer" tower. That probably means an Aermotor, though the report does not say so.[^1] I found no staffing history or named fires for it.

The register holds two visit reports, from 2009 and 2017.[^1] In 2009 the visitor found the tower in "okay condition but looked rusty," and thought it was not staffed at the time.[^1] The 2017 visit found it "Appears unstaffed," with an overgrown perimeter fence and a large cell tower beside it.[^1] That report marks the tower as not in good condition and says it needs paint.[^1] It also describes the tower as "Located directly adjacent to highway, easily visible."[^1]

The register recorded the tower on January 3, 2015, as US 1092, VA 22.[^1]

The sources do not say whether the public may go up the tower or onto the site, and the newest report is from 2017, so its condition today is unknown. Do not climb the tower or enter the fenced grounds. Ask the Virginia Department of Forestry first, since the register names it as administrator.[^1]

[^1]: Birdnest Lookout, National Historic Lookout Register (nhlr.org), http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/va/birdnest-lookout/ (accessed 2026-10-08).
[^2]: Virginia, Forest Fire Lookout Association (firelookout.org), https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-08).
