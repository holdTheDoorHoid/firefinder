Prater Mountain is in Bonner County, Idaho, about nine miles north-northeast of Priest River, at 4,594 feet.[^1][^2] The sources name the Priest Lake TPA as the agency for the site.[^1][^2][^3]

In 1934 a 10-foot pole lookout with an L-4 cab went up on the southwest summit.[^1][^2][^3] A 62-foot pole L-6 patrol tower was built about a quarter mile to the east that same year.[^1][^2]

By 1960 neither tower remained.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts records the 1960 entry as destruction.[^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page files the site under "Destroyed/Removed," and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a tower that is gone.[^2][^4]

None of the sources describes what, if anything, remains on the summit now. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page tags the site "Hike-In," the only access note we found, and it does not say whether the land is open to visitors today.[^2]
