The Broad Ridge tower site is in Tioga County, Pennsylvania.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives US 224 and PA 53 as its road references.[^1] The register describes a tree tower put up in 1916 and in use until about 1917. It no longer stands.[^1] A history of Pennsylvania forest lookouts also dates a structure to 1916, describing a tree observation post set at 40 feet above the ground.[^2] If they describe the same structure, the two sources agree on its start date, but one calls it a tower and the other an observation post.[^1][^2] The 40-foot figure is the only height either source gives.[^2]

The register entry was nominated by Henry Isenberg and added on September 16, 2008.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the land is open to visitors.

[^2]: Pennsylvania (A-F) - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/pennsylvania-a-f.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
