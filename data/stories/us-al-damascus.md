The Damascus Fire Tower Site lies in Coffee County, Alabama, at an elevation of 411 feet (125 m).[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it as US 1752, AL 23, and places it at about 31.35 degrees north and 86.00 degrees west.[^1] The Alabama Forestry Commission is the agency the register lists as administering the site.[^1]

Andrew Zerbe nominated the site, and the register records its registration date as September 15, 2019.[^1]

According to the register, only the stands are left, and they sit next to a water tower.[^1] It does not describe the stands any further.[^1] No source I read says whether the public can visit the site, so anyone planning a trip should ask the Alabama Forestry Commission first.

[^1]: Damascus Fire Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/al/damascus-fire-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
