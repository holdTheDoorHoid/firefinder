McClure Mountain is a prominent high point near Twisp.[^4] A frame cabin once stood on the summit, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites register dates it to 1930 and lists it as gone.[^1] FireLookout.com gives the same year and the same verdict.[^2] WillhiteWeb reports the 1930 date from Ray Kresek, but says it has no maps or images of the lookout.[^3]

TrailChick dates the summit cabin only to the 1930s and notes that little is documented about the site.[^4] The register also holds a panorama of the lookout dated August 8, 1934, credited to the National Archives Pacific Northwest Region.[^1] The site was added to the register on November 17, 2012.[^1]

The summit now holds many communication towers.[^3] WillhiteWeb says the many previous towers make it hard to tell where the cabin stood, and its best guess is a rocky knob at the far east point.[^3] A road winds up the southeast shoulder to the top, but it crosses private land and isn't open to the public.[^4] TrailChick says the road mostly serves upkeep of radio and communication gear on the summit.[^4]

Hikers can still reach the top on foot over National Forest land.[^4] TrailChick describes a ridge route that starts from Lookout Mountain Road.[^4] WillhiteWeb describes a good motorcycle trail that runs all the way up, reached through a seasonal forest road gate.[^3] The author thinks the gate is usually open by May but cannot confirm it.[^3]
