The San Juan Lookout stood in Orange County, just east of the Forest Service's San Juan Fire Station on Highway 74.[^1][^2]

The register says the tower was up and running by 1922. It was a 7-foot open timber tower with an 8-by-8-foot wooden cab that had a hip roof, and each window had its own propped-up shutter.[^1] The San Diego chapter dates it to around 1930 and describes the same tower, with the same cab.[^2] The two sources disagree on the date.[^1][^2]

The register notes that the site may have served in emergency staffing.[^1]

Both sources say the tower is now gone.[^1][^2] The register gives its removal date as unknown.[^1]

Both sources say a trail from the fire station still reaches the site.[^1][^2] Neither describes what, if anything, remains on the ground today, or whether the public may use that trail.

[^1]: San Juan Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ca/san-juan-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: San Juan, FFLA-SDRC, https://www.ffla-sandiego.org/fire-lookout/southern-california-towers/san-juan/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
