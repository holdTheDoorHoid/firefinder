Torrens is a cabin lookout rather than a tall tower.[^1] The provincial wildfire data gives 1962 as its construction year and lists it as a cabin 5 m (16 ft) high, at an elevation of about 1,815 m (5,953 ft).[^1] The Canada Lookouts list for Alberta carries an undated photograph of the site from the Alberta ESRD online collection.[^2]

According to the province, each wildfire lookout site has an observer on duty, mainly between April and October.[^3] The province also says the sites are remote and that staff have to plan for many possibilities, including an inquisitive bear.[^3]

The provincial data lists Torrens as active and as a weather station.[^1] It gives the access mode as "drive" and does not say whether the public may visit.[^1] Treat it as a working fire-watch site, and do not climb or enter the structure.
