The Elysian Fields lookout stood in Harrison County, Texas, about four miles northwest of the community of Elysian Fields.[^1] A federal survey description written in 1958 calls it a glass-enclosed cab on a four-legged steel frame, painted gray, with an overall height of 100 feet.[^1] The survey page's header names the Texas Forest Service, but it does not say who owned or ran the tower.[^1]

A 1963 survey note says a new tower was planned a few feet west of the present one, to be built before long.[^1] It does not say whether that happened.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the original Elysian Fields tower as gone.[^2] It lists a relocated Elysian Fields tower at Winston Ranch, in Nacogdoches County, as standing.[^2][^3] The association gives no date for the move and does not say who moved the tower or why. It also does not say which tower was moved, the 1958 structure or the one planned in 1963.[^2]

A TopoQuest map record still lists an Elysian Fields Lookout Tower at N32.421821° W94.225192°, close to the association's coordinates for the original site, but it does not say whether anything stands there.[^2][^4] The association's listing gives no condition or visiting information for the Winston Ranch tower.[^2] Ask the property owner before going near it, and do not climb it.
