The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list includes a tower called Grassy Hill, in Franklin County, and marks it as gone.[^1] The hill lies on the western side of Rocky Mount and is a notable local landmark.[^2] Today it is a state natural area preserve run by the Department of Conservation and Recreation.[^2] The preserve was dedicated in 1999 to safeguard rare plants and natural communities.[^3]

Some trees carry fire scars, which suggest that burning may have been an important factor in keeping the hill open.[^2] Wildfire suppression in the twentieth century let the grassy woods close into forest.[^2]

The preserve has five miles of hiking trails, and visitors must stay on the marked trails.[^4][^2] Deer hunting closes the West Loop Trail and the land west of the Connector Trail from September 15, 2026, to January 3, 2027.[^2] The whole preserve also shuts on some dates, including October 9 to 12 and December 31 to January 3, 2027.[^2]

No source read describes what, if anything, remains of a tower on the hilltop today.
