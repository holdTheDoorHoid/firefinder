On August 22, 1936, the Freeport Journal-Standard reported that work was underway on a 100-foot fire lookout tower for the forestry service, two miles north of Pinckneyville, in Perry County. That height is about 30 meters.[^1] The paper added that a person at the top could see 13 miles in every direction.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site files the tower under the spelling Pinckneyrville, and the 1936 newspaper item is the one it quotes.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Oak Grove (Pinckneyville) tower as gone.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it can be visited.
