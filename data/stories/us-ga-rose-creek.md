A Georgia lookout list on tnlandforms.us includes a tower called rose creek, in Camden County, at coordinates 30.89015, -81.78514, with a value of 19 in its elevation column.[^1] The list does not state a unit for that column, and its note says names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own, so the name may not be a historical one.[^1]

The Eastern U.S. Lookouts directory has a Rose Creek page that gives Columbia County, not Camden.[^2] Because it does not match this entry's county, it is not used here.

No source describes what remains at the site today.
