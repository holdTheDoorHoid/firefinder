The Kern County Fire Department is believed to have built the Tollgate Lookout around 1940.[^1][^2] Most lookouts were put up by federal or state agencies, so this one stands out.[^2] The register describes a 12-by-12 foot cabin on a 20-foot enclosed timber tower.[^1] A 1936 newspaper notice had planned two lookout stations, one of them at Tollgate, and a June 1940 item listed a "Toll gate lookout" among new items at $750.[^3]

The tower sits near the upper end of Tollgate Canyon, in the hills north of Highway 58 that lie between Tehachapi and Keene.[^2] In August 1954 a 50-acre fire on the Carl Arnold Ranch was under control in under three hours, and the fire chief credited the prompt smoke report from the Tollgate lookout.[^3] The register says staffing continued through at least 1965.[^1] According to the Tehachapi News feature, the lookout was probably manned in summer fire seasons, roughly April to September, and sometimes into the 1970s.[^1][^2]

For decades the land around it was the Loop Ranch. The Nature Conservancy now holds the land, as part of its Frank and Joan Randall Preserve.[^2][^1] Sources differ on the preserve's size: the Tehachapi News feature gives 81,000 acres, and the Conservancy's page says over 70,000.[^2][^4] The register lists the lookout as registered on May 27, 2022.[^1]

A team of lookout preservationists visited on June 26, 2022, with permission, to record and evaluate the building.[^1][^2] The feature describes the old building as still standing, weathered on its siding, with most of its windows blown out.[^2]

The Conservancy's page lists limited access to the preserve.[^4] Please do not climb or enter the lookout. Ask the Conservancy before you go near it.
