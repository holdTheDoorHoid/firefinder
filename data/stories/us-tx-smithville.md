The Forest Lookout Association lists a standing tower at Smithville, in Bastrop County, Texas, at about 30.01 degrees north and 97.21 degrees west.[^1] Smithville, a city close to the Colorado River, had 3,922 residents at the 2020 census.[^2]

The sources we read do not say when the Smithville tower was built, who put it up, or what it was made of. The Association's list is the only record we found for it, and it gives only a name, county, location and status.[^1] We also found no account of who staffed the tower or of any fire it helped to watch.

The same list shows a separate tower at Bastrop as gone, so the two should not be confused.[^1]

In a May 2026 report, Community Impact said the Texas A&M Forest Service had broken ground on April 29 for a regional office on a 300-acre property in Smithville, about 13 miles south of its current office.[^3] Officials describe Bastrop County as among the areas of Texas most exposed to wildfire.[^3] The article about the project does not mention the lookout tower.[^3]

The Association's list shows the Smithville tower as standing, with no note on its condition or owner.[^1] We found no public access or rental information for it. Please do not climb it unless the owner has given you permission.
