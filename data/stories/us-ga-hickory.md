The Georgia lookout list places a tower named Hickory in Habersham County, at 2,382 feet (726 m).[^1] The list says its names for towers numbered 73 and above are its own, and this one is number 366, so the name is the list's label.[^1]

The U.S. National Geodetic Survey described its station HICKORY in 1975. The station is about one mile west of Tallulah Falls, at the top of a mountain that the directions reach by a woods road.[^2] Its description places the survey disk 15 feet west of one of four concrete footings for a lookout tower, so the summit had tower footings by 1975.[^2]

A 1993 recovery note says the mark was found as described, but no source describes what remains of the tower or its footings today.[^2] By then the mountain land had been divided into subdivision lots, and the mark appeared to fall on one of them.[^2] The summit appears to be private land, so the site is not known to be open to visitors.[^2]

[^2]: Station HICKORY, data sheet ED1433, National Geodetic Survey, https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=ED1433 (accessed 2026-10-21).
