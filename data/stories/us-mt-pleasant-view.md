The Pleasant View lookout in Lincoln County, Montana, was an L-6-type cab erected in 1934 on the Kootenai National Forest.[^1][^2][^3]

Forest Lookouts lists it under the Kootenai National Forest at 34N-32W-19.[^2] The Montana index places it at 48.69669 north and 115.77929 west and classes it as a ground site.[^2][^3]

The firelookout.com page says the cab was abandoned in 1942, eight years after it was built.[^1]

The Montana index marks the site as gone.[^3]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
