The statewide list of Georgia lookout towers has one entry named "airport," in Bulloch County. The list gives its elevation as 175 feet (53 m).[^1] The Forest Lookouts site has a page under the same name and county.[^2]

By our calculation from the two coordinate pairs, the list's point sits about 0.6 km (0.4 mile) from the published coordinates of the Statesboro–Bulloch County Airport.[^1][^3] Separately, the state forestry commission's air unit began in Statesboro in May 1958, working from a hangar at the airport.[^4]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
