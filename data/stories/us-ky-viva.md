The Kentucky State Division of Forestry operated a lookout tower at the Viva site in Laurel County.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says the tower was built in 1937, and the site sits at 1,319 feet, or 402 meters.[^1]

Viva was a coal mining camp, and the register says it was established in the early 1900s.[^1] A University of Kentucky Coal Camp Project record lists "Operated 1900-1933" under its post office address and status field, and names Wildcat Coal Co. as the company.[^2] The same record gives a begin year of 1903 and an end year of 1925, which do not match that range.[^2]

Surface mining continued in the area into the 1970s.[^1] According to the register, many of the mine sites qualified for federal and state abandoned mine land money and were reclaimed with it.[^1]

The register says the tower was removed in the late 1970s.[^1] It also says the ridge is no longer open to vehicles and that the former tower site could not be accessed.[^1] The register adds that online aerial and satellite photos did not show any remains of the site.[^1] We found no description of a public route to the site.

The site was added to the register on January 2, 2026, on the nomination of Robert Wittenback.[^1]

[^2]: Viva, Coal Camp Project, University of Kentucky Appalachian Projects, https://appalachianprojects.as.uky.edu/node/4802 (accessed 2026-10-10).
