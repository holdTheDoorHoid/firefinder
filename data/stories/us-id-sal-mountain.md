Sal Mountain Lookout stands in Lemhi County, Idaho, about 12 miles south of Salmon, within the Salmon-Challis National Forest.[^1][^2] The register and firelookout.com both date the lookout to 1926. The register describes a 10-by-10 wood-frame cab at ground level, while firelookout.com describes a 10-by-10 wooden cab on a rock crib.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives a two-story log cab on a rock crib, so the sources disagree on whether the cab sat at ground level or had two stories.[^1][^3]

Newspaper items quoted on a forest-lookout history page show radio equipment and lookout staff in the 1930s.[^4] A June 1934 item reports a radio receiving and sending unit installed at Sal Mountain.[^4] Items from 1935 and 1936 say Bill McNamer left for the lookout and Harold Harris was placed on it. They speak of "LaSal mountain," which the page files under Sal Mountain, but no source we read confirms that LaSal mountain is this site.[^4] The sources do not say who built the cab or who staffed it first.[^1][^2]

The register says no one has staffed it since the 1940s, and that it still stands.[^1] Firelookout.com is terser: it lists the site as abandoned in the 1940s and still standing.[^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts also dates the abandonment to the 1940s.[^3] The register listed the lookout on September 30, 2020, with Brad Eells as nominator.[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^3] No source we read says whether visitors may go into the cab or what condition it is in now. Check with the Salmon-Challis National Forest before a trip, and stay out of any historic structure that is closed or not clearly open to visitors.
