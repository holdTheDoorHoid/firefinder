A fire tower once stood on South Mound, a hill in Clark County, Wisconsin.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as Tioga (South Mound) and marks it gone.[^1] OpenStreetMap has a point named Lookout Tower close to the listed spot.[^2]

The climbing site SummitPost describes South Mound as privately owned and says few climbers are allowed to hike its summit.[^3] Its listed coordinates sit roughly 300 meters from the lookout coordinates, so it is probably the same hill.[^1][^3]

We found no date for when the tower was built or when it came down.[^1][^3] No source we read describes a standing tower on the hill today. Because the land is private, a visit would need permission first.[^3]

[^1]: Wisconsin Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Node: Lookout Tower (353894182), OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/353894182 (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: South Mound, Wisconsin, SummitPost, https://www.summitpost.org/south-mound-wisconsin/1068147 (accessed 2026-10-10).
