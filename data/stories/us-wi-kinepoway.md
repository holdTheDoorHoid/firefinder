The Kinepoway Lookout stands on private property inside the Menominee Indian Reservation in Menominee County.[^1] Coast and Geodetic Survey notes put the tower in place by 1934, but its build date is not known exactly.[^1] The register credits the Wisconsin Conservation Commission with building it.[^1] A Central US Lookouts listing names the Wisconsin Department of Conservation as the agency instead.[^2]

The tower sits at about 1,296 feet above sea level.[^1] Spring fire-season staffing was intermittent until 2015, when the Wisconsin DNR shut down its lookout network.[^1]

Management has since passed from the state DNR to Menominee Tribal Enterprises.[^1] The register says fire protection on the reservation is handled cooperatively by the DNR and the Tribe, and that it is unknown whether the Tribe staffs this tower.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^3] It was added to the National Historic Lookout Register on March 14, 2019.[^1] Because the site is private land, any visit would need the landowner's permission, and the sources describe no public access or climbing.[^1]
