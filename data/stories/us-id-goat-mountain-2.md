This Goat Mountain lookout sat in Shoshone County, about 20 miles east of Clarkia, Idaho.[^2][^3] It stood on St. Joe National Forest land at 6,155 feet.[^1] It is a different site from the Goat Mountain in Boundary County.[^4]

The first structure, dated 1916, was a crow's nest with a cabin.[^1][^2] A 50-foot pole tower followed in 1934.[^1][^2] The two pages word the 1934 tower differently, one as a tower with an L-4 cab and the other as an L-4 tower, but both give 50 feet.[^1][^2]

A patrol point on Blackdome lay about 1.5 miles to the southeast.[^1][^2]

The lookout was destroyed in 1961.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list marks it as gone.[^3]

None of the pages describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the lookout as Drive-Up, but that label does not say whether the site is open to the public.[^1]
