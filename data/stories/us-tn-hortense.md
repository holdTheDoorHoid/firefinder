Hortense was a lookout tower about five miles (8 km) north of Dickson, in Dickson County, Tennessee.[^1][^2] The state's list gives the site an elevation of 924 feet (282 m).[^1]

A Coast and Geodetic Survey station on top of the tower was described in 1953.[^2] According to the 1953 description, the structure stood on four legs, rose about 40 feet (12 m), and carried a wooden observation platform on top.[^2] The survey's data sheet codes the station marker as a lookout tower and names the station "Sylvia St Forest Lot HR 32."[^2]

The state's list marks the tower as removed.[^1] It links the site to survey point GC2564, the same station the survey described in 1953.[^1][^2] The list was last revised in January 2026.[^1]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains on the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
