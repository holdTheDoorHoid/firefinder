The Forest Lookouts site lists a lookout site called Black Butte in Meagher County, on the Lewis and Clark National Forest.[^1][^2] The one dated item on the Black Butte page is from 1938.[^1] A notice of July 1 that year, printed in The Independent Record and quoted on the site, named Bill Musgrave as the person assigned to the Black Butte lookout.[^1] Its wording is in the future tense, so it reports a planned assignment.[^1]

Do not confuse this site with the Black Butte lookout in Lincoln County, which the Montana lookout list records separately as a standing tower.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at this Black Butte site today, or whether the public can reach it.
