Kent Lookout Tower stands in Langlade County, at about 45.24 degrees north and 88.88 degrees west.[^1] OpenStreetMap, which maps it as an observation tower, records its elevation as 565 metres.[^2]

I could not find a build date, builder or design for Kent in the sources I checked, so its early history is unknown. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list shows it as standing, and its row carries no register number.[^1] I also found no record of who staffed it, whether it was damaged or moved, or whether it is rented.

The wider story matters here. A WXPR news report from December 2015 said the DNR had announced it would remove the towers it once used to watch for wildfires.[^3] The sources do not say whether Kent is one of the DNR's towers or who owns it now. A 2019 chapter report from the Wisconsin chapter of the Forest Fire Lookout Association said that, as of the fall of 2019, 37 towers had passed to private landowners, 10 had been sold and taken down, 4 had been sold and were awaiting removal, and 56 were still to be dismantled.[^4]

Today the tower appears on the association's list as standing.[^1] None of these sources describe public access. Check with the landowner or land manager before going near it, and do not climb it if it is closed or posted.
