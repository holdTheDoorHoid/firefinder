Huntsville is listed as a fire tower in McDowell County, North Carolina.[^1] A state-wide list of North Carolina fire towers places it at 35.61, -81.93638 and gives its National Geodetic Survey point number as FA3533.[^1] The list cautions that a few of its entries may be observation towers or Coast Guard lookouts.[^1]

The Eastern US Lookouts guide lists a tower it calls Huntersville Mountain. Its entry gives McDowell County and the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources, and says the Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC) erected a 52-foot (15.8 m) tower there in 1940.[^2] The two sources spell the name differently and do not say whether they describe the same tower.[^1][^2]

No source describes the site today.

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: North Carolina Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/north-carolina.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
