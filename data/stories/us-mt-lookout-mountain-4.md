The Fire Lookout Tower listing places Lookout Mountain in Park County, 22 miles east-northeast of Gardiner.[^1] Rex's Fire Lookout Page puts it in the Gallatin National Forest and gives its location code as 8S-12E-34.[^2]

The listing gives 1955 and describes a 20-foot R-6 tower of treated timber.[^1] It gives the elevation as 9,870 feet.[^1] It also carries an "Emergency 1969" entry, which it does not explain.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the tower at 45.09569, -110.22535 and lists it as gone.[^3] Montana has other lookouts with this name, including one in Ravalli County, so the county and coordinates are needed to tell them apart.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
