Simmons Mountain in Patrick County, Virginia, once held a fire tower.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register puts the site at 1,922 feet, or 586 meters, and dates the tower to the 1960s.[^1] Its description says the tower was standing at least until around 1980.[^1]

The register says a lumber company bought the site and removed the tower.[^1] Its Removed field gives only the 1980s, so the exact year is not known.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list also marks the tower as gone.[^2]

The site was added to the register on January 8, 2021.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the site can be visited.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Simmons Mountain Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/va/simmons-mountain-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Additional Virginia Information, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
