The Eastern US Lookouts site lists a Copeland lookout in Collier County, Florida.[^1] The lookout shares its name with Copeland, a community in eastern Collier County outside any incorporated town, where State Road 29 meets Janes Memorial Scenic Drive.[^1][^2] Big Cypress National Preserve lies east of the community, and Fakahatchee Strand Preserve State Park is also to the east.[^2] No source I could read describes what stands at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Copeland - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/copeland.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Copeland, Florida, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copeland,_Florida (accessed 2026-10-21).
