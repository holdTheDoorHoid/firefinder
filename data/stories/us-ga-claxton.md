A Georgia lookout list places a tower called "claxton" in Evans County.[^1] The list gives its elevation as 152 with no unit; if that figure is in feet, the site sits about 46.3 m above sea level.[^1] The same row carries two register numbers, BLL-64697 and gfc-114.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today or whether the public can reach it.[^1]
