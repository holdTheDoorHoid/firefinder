The Victoria tower is at Tube Lake, near the community of Walford, in Sables-Spanish Rivers, Ontario.[^1][^2] The Canada Lookouts index lists the Victoria tower at Tube Lake, Walford.[^2] Wikipedia mentions an old Walford fire tower, which stands north of the village by Tube Lake, so the two sources appear to describe the same structure.[^3]

Within its township, Walford is the furthest-west community, and the township lies along Highway 17.[^3]

Firelookout.ca says the cab was intact at an earlier date but was lost later, with no specifics given.[^1] If the Wikipedia tower is this one, it still stands without its cab.[^1][^3] No source found describes whether visitors can reach the site.
