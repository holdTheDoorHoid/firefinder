The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower called Bennett – first in Douglas County, Wisconsin, at 46.42314 and -91.87874. It is marked as gone.[^1] A second Bennett tower, listed as Bennett (Lake Minnesuing), is marked as standing.[^1]

A map point called Bennett Lookout Tower is listed at 1,312 feet (about 400 m) above sea level, and TopoQuest notes that it appears on the USGS Lyman Lake 1:24,000 sheet.[^2] By our own calculation from the published coordinates, the two points are about 16 metres apart, so they probably mark the same spot.[^1][^2]

No source describes what remains at the site, or whether the land is open to visitors. Ask the landowner or local forest managers before going there, and do not climb any tower you find without permission.

[^1]: Wisconsin Lookouts (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/wi-co/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Bennett Lookout Tower, TopoQuest, https://www.topoquest.com/place-detail.php?id=1581174 (accessed 2026-10-10).
