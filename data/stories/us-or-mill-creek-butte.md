Mill Creek Butte, in Hood River County's Mount Hood region, had a 9-by-9-foot cab built in 1937, according to the register and firelookout.com.[^1][^2] The register says a 41-foot treated timber L-4 tower replaced that cab in 1948.[^1][^2] The register also lists the U.S. Forest Service as the administering agency.[^1]

Rex's notes name an emergency lookout, Bob Rush, in 1934, three years before the register's first cab. His other duties included truck driving.[^3] Rex's notes also quote a 1948 Hood River District report, logged January 6, 1949, that says a contractor constructed the tower. The register dates the tower to 1948.[^3][^1]

Tower personnel painted the lookout during the summer of 1963. In 1966 two shutters were installed to replace earlier ones that had been torn away in a break-in.[^3]

The register and firelookout.com say the site was gone by 1972, and Rex's notes record the lookout removed that year.[^1][^2][^3]

None of the sources I read describes what remains at the site, or whether the spot can be visited today.
