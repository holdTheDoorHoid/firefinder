The Round Mountain tower stood on the mountain of that name in Bland County, Virginia, near Bastian.[^1] A 1935 newspaper item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, described it as a 100-foot steel fire tower on the summit.[^1] A road built by the Civilian Conservation Corps climbed to it from U.S. Route 21.[^1]

In April 1930 the lookout in the tower reported two mountain fires near Bland Courthouse, and both were brought under control.[^1] The item said the fires were threatening for a time, but the area burned was comparatively small.[^1] By 1935 a watchman was on duty each spring and summer.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site marks the tower as removed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list shows it as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes the site today, so we cannot say what, if anything, remains on the mountain or whether visitors can reach the top.[^1][^2]
