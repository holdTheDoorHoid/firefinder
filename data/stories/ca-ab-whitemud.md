Alberta's wildfire register lists the Whitemud lookout as a steel tower, 30 m (98 ft) tall and about 835 m (2,740 ft) above sea level. It dates the present structure to 2019 and gives its map sheet as 84D/08.[^1]

The Canada Lookouts page for Whitemud places the lookout in the Peace River Division.[^2]

The register lists the tower as active and shows a weather station on site. It codes the access as fly, which suggests the site is reached by air rather than by road.[^1]

Alberta calls its fire lookouts integral to wildfire detection and sites them where visibility is good.[^3]

No source I found describes the site on the ground today, or says whether the public may visit. Anyone planning a visit should check with the managing agency first.
