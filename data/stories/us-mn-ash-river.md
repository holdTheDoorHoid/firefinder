The Ash River Lookout stands in Kabetogama State Forest in Saint Louis County, and the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources administers it.[^1] The Register describes an 81-foot Pittsburgh-Des Moines tower of the windmill style, with an octagonal cab sitting where a windmill would normally be mounted.[^1] The Register says the tower is among very few of its type in the United States.[^1]

The site had earlier towers.[^1][^3] The St. Louis County list records a first steel ladder tower there in 1923 and an 80-foot ladder tower in 1924.[^3] The Register mentions only the 1924 tower, which it says was removed in 1935, and it notes concrete anchors from that tower still on the site.[^1] The county list says the 1924 tower was replaced in 1935 and later erected at the Elephant Lake site in 1940.[^3] Both accounts put the change in 1935, so they fit together.[^1][^3]

The current tower dates to 1935.[^1][^3] The county list calls it the third lookout at the site and marks it as standing.[^3] The heights do not match across sources. The Register and the county list give 81 feet, while a Forest Lookouts page gives 80 feet and reproduces a 1948 survey description of the site.[^1][^2][^3]

The most recent details we found are the Register's registration on October 5, 2018, and its August 2018 photograph.[^1] We did not find a current source that says whether visitors may climb the tower. Please check with the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources before you go, and do not climb it unless it is open to the public.
