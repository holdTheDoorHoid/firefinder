The Forest Lookouts page gives the Klamath National Forest as the agency.[^1] It cites a June 5, 1932 entry in the Fox Diaries, in which the writer and two companions record completing a run of outpost wire to Doe Peak, which the entry calls an emergency lookout point.[^1]

PeakVisor places the peak in Jackson County, in the Siskiyou Mountains.[^2] It gives the peak's height as 5,226 feet (1,593 m), and says the summit can be reached by trail.[^2]

A list of standing Oregon lookouts, which counts 156 of them as of July 2026, does not include Doe Peak.[^3] That list warns that its information may be out of date.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains of a lookout on the peak today.
