The MMR Fire Tower is in Land Information Ontario's fire-tower layer, which gives its purpose as fire detection.[^1] The point is recorded to within 10 metres (33 ft), on the south side of the Pakashkan road, south of Pakashkan Lake.[^1] That lake lies about 50 kilometres north of Upsala.[^2]

The record says no power is available at the tower.[^1] It was marked verified in March 2009.[^1] A general comment in the record says that no picture was taken of the tower and that it has not been on NRVIS.[^1]

No source I could read describes the tower today, or whether visitors can reach the site.
