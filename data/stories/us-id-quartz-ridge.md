Quartz Ridge Lookout stood at the south end of Quartz Ridge in the Nez Perce National Forest, about 12 miles southeast of Grangeville in Idaho County.[^1][^2][^4] It was a 100-foot steel Aermotor tower with an L-4 cab for living quarters, built in 1934.[^1][^2][^3] One page gives the tower's model as Aermotor MC-40.[^3]

A January 1936 item, quoted on a lookout history page, opens with a report that a Forest Service man had been hit by lightning and survived. It then names Al Moore as the lookout on duty when lightning hit the tower.[^3]

Sources agree the tower was gone by 1969.[^1][^2][^5] Rex's page lists it as removed.[^3]

A federal survey record for the site lists a disk stamped QUARTZ RG 1951, set in rock that the description places under the steel tower.[^4] The record's last condition note is from 1962 and rates the disk good. It does not say whether the disk can still be found.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as drive-up but labels the entry destroyed, without giving a cause.[^2] The site lies on national forest land.[^1]
