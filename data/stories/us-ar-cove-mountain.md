The Cove Mountain lookout sat in Perry County, inside the Ouachita National Forest.[^1] The Civilian Conservation Corps erected it in 1933.[^1] During the Great Depression, the corps put up 3,100 towers, and many of them stood in the Ouachita and Ozark-St. Francis national forests.[^2] That 1933 date sits inside the Great Depression, the period the encyclopedia ties to that work.[^1][^2]

The structure was a four-legged steel frame with a sheet-metal house on top, and its overall height was 70 feet.[^1] A National Geodetic Survey description dated 1956 still describes it, so the tower was standing that year.[^1]

The site records the lookout as removed in 1972.[^1] The encyclopedia credits aerial surveillance, and later satellites, with making lookouts less necessary.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it is open to visitors.
