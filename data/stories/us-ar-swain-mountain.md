The Swain Mountain lookout stood at the summit of Swain Mountain, about eight miles south of Eureka Springs in Madison County.[^1] A Madison County Journal item dated May 30, 1940, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, said the tower was nearing completion and was being built by the Huntsville CCC camp.[^1] The same item said a telephone line connecting the tower had been built earlier, and placed the tower about one mile from the Madison-Carroll county line, near Highway 23.[^1]

A 1940 station description by the Coast and Geodetic Survey gives the tower as wooden and about 100 feet high, and says the survey sighted on the enclosed cabin at the top.[^1] The same description calls it a lookout tower of the Arkansas Forest Service.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts listing marks the tower as removed.[^1] No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Swain Mountain, Forest Lookouts (centraluslookouts.weebly.com), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/swain-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
