The Paulding lookout stood on Ottawa National Forest land in Ontonagon County, Michigan, at an elevation of about 490 meters (1,609 feet).[^1]

The register gives the build date as circa 1938 and says the exact date is unknown. A survey first marked the site in 1939, and that survey recorded a ground spike bearing the date 1938.[^1]

A November 1971 newspaper item, quoted on a lookout history page, reported that the Ottawa National Forest had put out bids to remove ten fire towers, citing an announcement from its supervisor.[^2] The same quote mentions a tower "located on Paulding," but it does not clearly match this lookout, and we found no record that links the two.[^2]

A later survey, cited on the register, says the tower was gone by 1975.[^1] The register lists Kenneth McDonough as the nominator and gives a registration date of January 1, 2026.[^1]

The register page includes a photo captioned "Pauling Reference Mark #1," dated September 2024.[^1] We found no source describing the site's access, or any standing structure there.

[^2]: Conners, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/conners.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
