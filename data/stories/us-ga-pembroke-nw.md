A tower called "pembroke nw" appears on tnlandforms.us's list of Georgia lookout towers. The list places it in Bryan County at 32.155556, -81.636944, and gives its elevation as 106 without saying whether that is feet or metres.[^1] Its only note is "Ron," and the page credits Gary Weber and Ron Stafford with supplying a large number of tower locations.[^1] Bryan County has a separate entry named "pembroke," at 32.10203, -81.62653, so the two should not be mixed up.[^1]

The page's map was revised on 22 March 2016.[^1] No source I could read describes what remains at the site today, so its current state is unknown. Anyone planning a visit should first check who owns the land and whether the site is open to the public.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
