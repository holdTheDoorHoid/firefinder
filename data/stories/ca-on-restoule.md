Restoule Provincial Park, in Parry Sound District, Ontario, is managed by Ontario Parks.[^2] Its fire tower is listed on Firelookout.ca as the Restoule Fire Tower.[^3] The province's management plan for the park, which carries a 2002 copyright line, calls it the Stormy Lake fire tower.[^1]

The plan dates the tower to 1954 and puts its height above 30 m (about 98 ft).[^1] Wikipedia gives its height as 30 m (100 ft).[^2] The plan treats it as a relic of the period when lookouts were posted in towers to spot forest fires.[^1] The plan notes that this detection method was eventually replaced by aircraft patrols.[^1]

The tower still overlooks Stormy Lake, and Wikipedia says many people hike the marked trail to it.[^2] Ontario Parks promotes the Fire Tower Trail for its view of Stormy Lake.[^4] Discovery Routes places the trailhead at a picnic area just west of the park road, and calls the climb to the top of the Stormy Lake Bluffs sometimes challenging, a trail for experienced hikers.[^5]

According to the plan, opening it as a public viewing tower would require extensive upgrading.[^1] Do not climb it. No source describes it as open to climbers.
