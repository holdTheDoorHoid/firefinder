The register lists a steel tower dating to 1921 on Round Mountain, in Huntingdon County.[^1] A November 1921 Mount Union newspaper item places the tower near Marklesburg and reports that a group of more than 100 college students had recently climbed it.[^2]

In April 1923 the Huntingdon Daily News reported that two men, Clyde Hecter and Chas. Ritenour, would be in charge of the Round Knob fire tower for the months ahead.[^2]

The register says the tower was relocated in 1928 and given the name Rock Hill.[^1]

No source we could read describes what, if anything, remains on Round Mountain today, or where the Rock Hill tower stands now. No source we found says whether either place is open to the public.
