The Kildare lookout was a Texas Forest Service tower in Cass County.[^1] The Handbook of Texas locates the Kildare community eight miles southeast of Linden, in the southeastern part of Cass County.[^2] Settlement began in the early 1870s, built up around big sawmills and a stop on the Texas and Pacific Railway, and lumbering remained a local focus.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list gives the tower's coordinates and marks it as gone.[^3] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the grounds are open to visitors.
