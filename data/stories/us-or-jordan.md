A standard 14-by-14-foot ground-house lookout was erected on a site near Jordan, in Linn County, in 1949.[^1][^2] A district report from that year says an old portable building from the Thomas Creek site was moved to the Jordan site and redesigned as a garage and tool room.[^2] The register describes the cab as an L-4 design.[^1]

Forest Lookouts says the Jordan lookout was dismantled in 1969, and that its materials went to the Green Peter site.[^2][^5] The register and firelookout.com both say the L-4 cab itself was moved to Green Peter Lookout in 1970.[^1][^3]

The cab's new home, Green Peter Lookout, is listed under the Oregon Department of Forestry's South Cascade District.[^4] The National Historic Lookout Register says the lookout was rebuilt in 2004, and that the station is no longer manned.[^4]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the Jordan site today, or whether the public may visit it.
