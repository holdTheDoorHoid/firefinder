The Guy lookout is in Faulkner County, Arkansas.[^1] The clearest description we found is in Michael Murphy's article on the county's fire towers. It speaks of a steel fire tower "currently standing near Guy" and says that tower was erected in the early 1960s.[^2]

Murphy's article is about the county's fire towers of the 1930s, and he judges that the towers in that forestry unit were probably built of wood.[^2] He notes that Tower 18-1 stood on Buffalo Hump, and that the Buffalo Hump location is not the same site as the steel tower near Guy.[^2]

The Central US Lookouts page for Guy gives only the county and a township, range and section code, so we cannot confirm that it is the tower Murphy describes. It seems likely, but no source says so outright.[^1][^2]

Murphy describes the tower as standing when he wrote. No source we read describes the site today, so whether a tower still stands there, and whether visitors can reach it, is not recorded here.[^2]

[^1]: Guy - Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/guy.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: The Rise and Fall of Fire Lookout Towers in Faulkner County, Michael Murphy, arkansas-ccc.com, https://arkansas-ccc.com/bibliography/murphy_2002.pdf (accessed 2026-10-21).
