The Deer Park Fire Tower stands in Deer Park, an unincorporated community in Osceola County, Florida.[^1][^3] The community sits off US 192, south of County Road 419.[^1] Its roots are in timber: George Washington Hopkins, from Michigan, started buying land in eastern Osceola and Brevard counties in 1895, and the community was set up to house workers at a logging and mill operation.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register describes the tower as an Aermotor LS-40 and says the Florida Department of Agriculture & Consumer Services administers it.[^3] The register gives its elevation as 47 feet (14 m).[^3] The tower was registered on July 24, 2016 as US 1137, FL 20, with Robert Spear as nominator.[^3]

I could not find a build year, height, staffing history or fire record for this tower in the sources I read. The Eastern lookouts page lists only its name and county.[^4] A Wikimedia Commons photo of the state's Deer Park Forestry Site is licensed for reuse, but its description does not confirm that the tower appears in it.[^5]

No public access information turned up, so check with the state, which administers the tower, before you visit.[^3]
