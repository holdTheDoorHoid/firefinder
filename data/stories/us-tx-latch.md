A fire lookout tower went up in the Latch area of southwest Upshur County in 1960.[^1] The Texas Forest Service's page for it lists the tower in Upshur County.[^1]

In November the Gilmer Mirror reported that a tower on Latch Mountain, on Joe Schuler's place, was near completion and would soon be in service.[^1] Two weeks later the paper described a tower on Chamberlain Mountain, on O.V. Schuler land, that was meant mainly to help a tower at Pine Mills spot fires in Wood County.[^1] The reports do not say whether these were one tower or two.[^1]

A newly formed fire protection district in Wood County had been started with money from an oil company and a paper company.[^1]

The tower had staff.[^1] In December 1966 the Gilmer Mirror named Max Blackstone crew leader of the Latch fire lookout tower, replacing Jerry Barton, who had resigned.[^1] The report said Blackstone had worked as a crewman for the Texas Forestry Service for the past 18 months.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Latch tower as standing.[^2] A post in a Latch community Facebook group, which I could not date, asks people who climb the fire lookout tower on Latch Mountain to photograph names carved in its wood, if it is safe.[^3] I could not confirm its condition, and I found no recent photo.

The 1960 reports tie the site to land owned by Joe Schuler and O.V. Schuler, and I found no current public access information.[^1] Treat the site as private and do not climb the tower.
