Tungston Mountain is in Boundary County, Idaho, about seven miles southwest of Eastport, on the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1] Firelookout.com gives 1937 as the date and lists the type as unknown.[^1]

A separate entry for nearby Bethlehem Mountain describes a 20-foot pole L-4 tower dated to 1937, on the southwest point of Tungston Mountain, and says it was destroyed in the 1950s.[^3] The sources do not say whether that is the same structure as the one listed for Tungston Mountain.

Both firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts say the lookout was abandoned in the 1940s, though neither gives a single year.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^4] No source we read describes the site today, so we cannot say what, if anything, is still standing or in place. Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site a hike-in, so visitors must walk in.[^2] We found nothing on whether the site is open to the public.
