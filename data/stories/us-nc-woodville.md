The Woodville lookout stood in Bertie County, North Carolina.[^1][^2] The North Carolina Division of Forest Resources erected an 80-foot tower there in 1928.[^1] The tower was later removed.[^1] A statewide list of North Carolina towers gives the site's coordinates as 36.11111 north and 77.18166 west.[^2] That list was last revised in June 2021 and still carries the Woodville entry, so being on it does not show that a tower stands there.[^2]

No source we read describes the site today, including whether it is open to visitors or what, if anything, stands there now. Ask locally before going, since we could not confirm who owns the land.

[^2]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
