A compiled list of Georgia lookout towers places a tower called "dry pond" in Jackson County, at about 34.19 degrees north and 83.56 degrees west.[^1] The list's compiler says that names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own, so "dry pond" is a label the compiler gave the site.[^1]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything remains there or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers (GA tower list with coordinates, elevation and GFC number), tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
