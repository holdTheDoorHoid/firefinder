Bunchgrass, also written Bunch Grass, is a lookout site in Shasta County.[^1][^3]

Peakbagging places the site in the Shasta-Trinity National Forest and says the lookout was built in 1934. Surveyors who visited in 1948 found it still there.[^2]

On 9 October 1947 the Weekly Adin Argus reported that the Soldier Mountain and Bunch Grass stations had been discontinued because of weather conditions.[^1] The two accounts fit together: the station was discontinued in 1947, and the lookout was still standing when surveyors visited in 1948.[^1][^2]

A photo dated July 17, 2012 has a caption saying only the corner footings are left. The caption's author had no information about when the building was removed.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list marks the tower as gone.[^3]

We found no later description of the site, so we cannot say whether anything remains now or whether visitors can reach it.
