A second entry on the same list, "wrightsboro GFC", sits in Columbia County and is tagged "gfc-021" and "BLL-50809".[^1] It is numbered GA270 on the list, the entry after the first Wrightsboro entry, GA269.[^1] Its coordinates put it about 1,430 ft (436 m) from the other Wrightsboro entry, and the list does not say whether the two are one tower or two.[^1] The page gives a "google map revised" date of 22 March 2016.[^1]

The name begins with Wrightsboro, an unincorporated community in McDuffie County about 18 mi (29 km) to the west-northwest.[^1][^2] Wikipedia says Quakers made the first lasting settlement there, in the 1760s.[^2]
