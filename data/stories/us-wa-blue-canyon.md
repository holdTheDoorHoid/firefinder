The Blue Canyon lookout was a tower in Whatcom County, Washington, in Section 23 of Township 37 North, Range 4 West.[^2][^3][^4] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says it was built in 1941 and that the tower is now gone.[^1] Willhite notes that the Kresek inventory also lists a tower there in 1941.[^4] The register lists the lookout as US 1227, WA 128, registered January 26, 2012.[^1]

The register gives the site an elevation of 2,880 feet, or 878 meters.[^1][^2] Willhite lists the elevation as unknown. He says the exact spot inside Section 23 still needs to be found, and he sees no evidence that the lookout stood on the hill near the Snub Benchmark, though he believes others have placed it there.[^4]

The Washington Division of Forestry/DNR administered the site.[^1] Willhite's documents say there was a telephone line five miles to the town of Wickersham, and they describe the lookout as a state lookout.[^4] We found no source that names anyone who staffed it.

A 1942 inventory, as Willhite reports it, says the improvements had been cleared off.[^4] The Former Fire Lookout Sites page and firelookout.com both mark the tower as gone.[^1][^2]

Willhite's page says the hiking distance has not been measured yet.[^4] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site, and none that says whether it is open to visitors.
