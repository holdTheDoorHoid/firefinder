Providence was a lookout tower in Polk County, Florida, and the Forest Lookouts site dates its erection to 1963.[^1]

In 1990 the tower was taken apart into three sections and moved about two miles north. There it was put back up and named the Green Swamp tower.[^1] A second Forest Lookouts page, for Green Swamp, describes the same move and says the pieces were reassembled at that spot.[^2] In April 2005 an Associated Press article put the number of Florida's surviving fire towers at 160, and noted that many were no longer in use.[^3]

The Providence site is where the tower stood until 1990.[^1] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains there now. For the Green Swamp location, we found no source that says whether the tower still stands or whether the public can visit it.
