Pot Mountain's lookout was an L-4 tower in the Flathead National Forest, 16 miles east of the Spotted Bear ranger station in Flathead County, Montana.[^1] The firelookout.com page gives its elevation as 7,674 feet and dates the tower to 1937.[^1] Working with the U.S. Forest Service, the Forest Fire Lookout Association completed a national inventory that found more than 8,000 lookouts in 49 states at one time.[^3]

The page says the tower was abandoned in 1949 and that the site is now ruins.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks the tower gone and places it at 47.93110, -113.17933.[^2] The page's word "ruins" suggests something is still on the ground, while the list says the tower is gone.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what the ruins consist of today. Anyone thinking of visiting should ask the Flathead National Forest about access before going, and should not expect a standing tower.
