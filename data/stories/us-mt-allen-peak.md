The Allen Peak lookout house stood on a bare east-west ridge along the boundary of the Kootenai and Cabinet national forests, in Lincoln County, Montana.[^1][^2] It lay about 31 miles south-southeast of Libby.[^1]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the lookout house in 1940, placing the site 11 miles northeast of Trout Creek and 8 miles south-southwest of Raven Ranger Station.[^2]

Firelookout.com dates an L-4 cab to 1934.[^1] A federal survey record for the station has two entries under the same name. One carries the code for a surface mark reported destroyed; the other carries a different code, so the entries alone do not show what happened to the house.[^3]

Firelookout.com lists the lookout as gone, and the Forest Lookouts page says it was removed.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's state list agrees.[^4]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

A trail listing for Allen Peak gives its length as 1.5 miles, within the Kootenai National Forest, and says it can be reached on foot, by bicycle or by horse.[^5] The listing does not say whether the trail reaches the lookout site.
