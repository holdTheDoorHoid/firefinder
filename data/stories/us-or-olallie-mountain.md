The Olallie Mountain lookout stood on the summit of Olallie Mountain in Lane County, Oregon, within the Willamette National Forest.[^1][^2][^5] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as a 14-by-14-foot L-4 ground house that the Forest Service built, and dates the work to 1930.[^1]

The sources do not agree on the year.[^1][^3][^4] Firelookout.com gives 1932 for an L-4 cab.[^3] A September 1931 Morning Oregonian item, quoted on Rex's Fire Lookout Page, reported that a cabin on the mountain had only just been finished.[^4] We could not settle which date is right, so the record keeps all three.

A lookout-fireman station was set up around 1924, years before the cabin dates above.[^4] A July 1931 Eugene Guard item names Paul Deuber as the forest lookout there, and a 1933 Corvallis Gazette-Times item mentions a summer lookout fireman on the mountain.[^4] The register says the house had not been staffed for many years.[^1] Firelookout.com says it was last used for emergencies in the 1970s.[^3]

The site also has a place in Oregon's lookout history. The register notes that the Oregon Lookout Association was started there in 1991.[^1]

The lookout burned in 2019.[^1][^3] The Forest Service dates the fire to autumn 2019 and says the cause is still under investigation.[^5] The register notes that restoration work was under way when the fire came.[^1] The register also lists a "Removed" date of 2019, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register listed the site on January 8, 2021.[^1][^2]

We found no source describing a standing building or what, if anything, is left on the summit. The Forest Service's Olallie Mountain Trail #4100 runs to the top of the mountain from Olallie Trail #3529, which starts in the Three Sisters Wilderness, and the agency asks visitors to contact the local ranger station for current trail conditions.[^5] We could not confirm the trail's status beyond that.
