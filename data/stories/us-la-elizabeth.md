The Elizabeth Lookout Tower is in Allen Parish, Louisiana.[^1] The Central US Lookouts catalogue lists it under the Louisiana Forestry Commission.[^1] OpenStreetMap, a volunteer map, tags the structure as a lattice observation tower.[^2]

A Wikipedia list of the state's towers says funds from 1915 let the state hire lookout watchmen or patrolmen, and it gives 39 towers built by 1943 and 56 by 1950.[^3] The same list reports that many towers survive, with most now idle and a number kept on standby.[^3]

A business directory lists an Elizabeth Fire Tower at 2495 Turner Road, under fire departments.[^4] The directory does not say whether that is this structure.[^4]

No source describes what stands at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.
