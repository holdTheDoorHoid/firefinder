Timmonstown was a lookout tower in Worcester County, Maryland, at about 38.35 north and 75.30 west.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone.[^1]

Maryland's first fire tower went up in Garrett County in 1915.[^3] Dozens were in operation by the 1930s and 1940s, and the Civilian Conservation Corps built many of them.[^3] Many were dismantled in the mid-20th century, but some still exist as part of the state's communications network.[^3]

No source read describes what remains at the Timmonstown site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
