Short Creek sits in the Frank Church River of No Return Wilderness in Valley County, Idaho, on Salmon-Challis National Forest land.[^1][^2][^5] Three sources date a first log cabin on the site to 1930, and firelookout.com says it is still there.[^2][^3][^4] An August 1932 item, quoted by Forest Lookouts from The Recorder Herald, reports that Perry Black set out for Short Creek to work on a lookout house.[^4] All four sources date the L-4 cab to 1934.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

The Forest Lookouts page quotes a July 1951 item naming Frank Hutchison of Salmon as the lookout's staffer.[^4] A June 1963 item on the same page reports that Gregory Mayne of Los Angeles was slated to staff the lookout.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts has no documented staffing history for the site.[^3]

The register, firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts all say the L-4 cab was abandoned in the 1950s.[^1][^2][^3] The 1963 item does not fit that date, so the sources disagree.[^4] The register added the site on September 26, 2011, and calls it an orphan wilderness lookout that could use a friend.[^1]

Access is on foot. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in and abandoned.[^3] A Forest Service page describes a 1.0-mile spur trail that begins where the Bernard Trail and Paradise Creek Trail meet and ends at the lookout.[^5] The same page places the trail inside the wilderness and says phone coverage is usually absent there and help is not easy to reach.[^5] No source describes public entry into the cabin, so check with the Salmon-Challis National Forest about its condition before you go.[^3][^5]
