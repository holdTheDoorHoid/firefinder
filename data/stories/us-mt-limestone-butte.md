Limestone Butte is in Stillwater County, Montana, about 18 miles west of Fishtail, on the Custer National Forest.[^1] The firelookout.com page lists a crow's nest on the butte in 1938, and says the lookout was abandoned in 1950.[^1] It gives the elevation as 7,506 feet.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list also places the site in Stillwater County and marks it abandoned.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts page for the butte also reproduces a 1940 Coast and Geodetic Survey description. That description places a Limestone lookout in the Flathead National Forest, about 13 miles from Spotted Bear Ranger Station.[^3] It may describe a different lookout, so the location above rests on the Stillwater sources instead.

No source we read describes what remains on the butte today, or whether the public can reach it. Anyone planning a visit should check with the Custer National Forest first.
