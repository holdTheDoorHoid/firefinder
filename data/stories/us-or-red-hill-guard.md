Red Hill Guard Station stood in the Mt. Hood National Forest, in Hood River County, at about 4,500 feet.[^1] Firelookout.com places it about 6 miles north of Mount Hood.[^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register describes a 12-by-14-foot frame cabin, built in the late 1920s, with a firefinder platform on its roof and used mainly as a guard station.[^1] The register also says the fireman's work covered trail upkeep and patrols on Mount Hood's north slope, and that the cabin served as a lookout around lightning storms, during them and afterward.[^1] Firelookout.com dates the platform atop the frame cabin to the 1930s.[^2]

Firelookout.com lists emergency use in the 1960s.[^2] The register says the cabin had disappeared by the 1970s.[^1] Firelookout.com also marks it as gone,[^2] and the Oregon Lookouts page marks it as removed.[^3]

One of the register's photo captions reads "Remains of cabin foundation," but the captions carry no dates.[^1] Beyond that, we found no source that describes the site now or whether visitors may go there.
