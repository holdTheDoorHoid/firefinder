The Bass Lake lookout in Mahnomen County is also called the Twin Lakes lookout tower.[^2] A 1964 Coast and Geodetic Survey description places the survey station on a wooded hill and says it sits on land the State of Minnesota owns.[^1] The same description puts the station near the site of an abandoned lookout tower.[^1] The county list's note for the 1964 survey also records an abandoned tower site.[^2]

The county tower list gives the owner as the Minnesota Forest Service, and says that agency has since become part of the state Department of Natural Resources.[^2]

The latest condition note we found is from 2006, when the county list said a downed tower remained at the site.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^3] No source we read says whether the site is open to visitors today.[^1][^2][^3]
