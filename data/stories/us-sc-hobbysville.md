The Forest Lookouts site lists a lookout tower at Hobbysville, in Spartanburg County, under the South Carolina Forestry Commission.[^1] Photo captions spell the name Hobbyville.[^2] A February 2009 caption calls it the Hobbyville Lookout Tower and places it near I-26, southeast of Woodruff.[^2]

The photo was taken on 17 February 2009 and shows the tower from a distance.[^2]

The Post and Courier says South Carolina moved away from fire towers in the early 1990s. It also links the closure of more than 100 towers around the state to 1993.[^3]

No source we found describes what stands at the Hobbysville site now, or whether visitors can reach it. The Forest Lookouts entry gives only the county, the agency and a satellite link.[^1]
