Lenox Mountain has carried a fire lookout since 1915, when a 48-foot tower went up on the summit.[^1] The register says the towns of Lenox, Richmond and Pittsfield donated $200 toward its purchase, and that reaching it took two miles of telephone line.[^1]

In 1944 a Pittsfield newspaper reported a new state employee "stationed at the Lenox fire tower."[^2] In 1952 a state crew began building a power line up the mountain to tie the tower into the state fire radio network, and that year the state acquired the ground beneath it.[^1][^2]

The register says the tower was temporarily closed in 1963 because of its poor condition.[^1] A new 75-foot tower with a 10-by-10 cab went up in 1966 and was named in memory of Oscar R. Hutchinson, a late Lenox fire chief.[^1][^2] In November 1969 a newspaper reported that the state had taken down the old 1915 tower the month before, and that the Hutchinson tower sat within 100 feet of the old site.[^2]

In 1970 high winds blew the 1966 tower over.[^1] A North Adams paper described the fallen tower as 80 feet tall, where the register gives 75.[^2] The register dates the present 68-foot tower to autumn 1970.[^1] A state notice of bids from March 1971 calls for "Construction of a Fire Tower on Lenox Mountain in Richmond, Massachusetts." The sources do not say whether that notice covers the same work.[^2]

The register dates the last staffing to the 1988 fire season.[^1] A fire in winter 1995 destroyed the cab and stairs.[^1] As of 2015 the register says the frame remained and was being used to host communications equipment.[^1] A Former Fire Lookout Sites entry registered the site in May 2008 and lists the U.S. Forest Service as administrator, which conflicts with the register's Massachusetts Bureau of Fire Control.[^3]

A 2026 hiking guide dates the end of lookout use to 1998 and says the tower is now fenced off for communications gear. Its 1998 date differs from the register's 1988.[^4] The guide says the tower is closed to climbers, but a vantage point sits just beyond the summit.[^4]
