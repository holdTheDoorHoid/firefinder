Imperial is one of 127 wildfire lookout sites that Alberta says are spread across its boreal forest and along the Rocky Mountains.[^1] Canada Lookouts files it under the Whitecourt Forest.[^2] A provincial lookout towers map from 2009 already marks a site named Imperial.[^3]

Alberta's wildfire lookout layer lists the structure as steel, with a tower height of 30.0 and no unit given, and gives 2012 as its year of construction.[^4] The same layer marks the lookout as active and records its access mode as drive.[^4]

Alberta says observers are stationed at each lookout site, generally from April to October, to spot wildfires. It also says boreal lookout sites are typically far from neighbouring communities.[^1]

No source we read describes the tower or the site as it stands today, or any public visiting arrangement. Treat Imperial as a working fire lookout, not a visitor site.
