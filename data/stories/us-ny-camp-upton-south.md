The Camp Upton South Tower Site is in Suffolk County, New York, about two and a half miles northeast of Yaphank, on what is now the Brookhaven National Laboratory campus.[^1][^4]

The register says the wooden tower was built sometime after 1932, after the steel tower on the north site had gone up.[^1] It describes the wooden tower as operating alongside the steel primary tower.[^1] The Long Island fire tower list says the steel tower was erected in 1932, and that the state also used a wooden tower for fire observation, on the southern of two sites marked on the USGS map.[^2]

The register says the wooden tower closed in 1953 and was taken down in 1956, the same time the steel tower came down.[^1] The Long Island list says both towers closed in 1953 and were taken down in 1955 or 1956 by Brookhaven National Laboratory, then the owner of the land.[^2] The register's page for the north site gives 1955 for the steel tower, so the sources disagree on the year.[^3]

According to the register, nothing is left on the site today.[^1]

The laboratory's history says the area it occupies was once Camp Upton, and that the laboratory was born in January 1947.[^4] We found no source that says whether the old tower site is open to visitors.
