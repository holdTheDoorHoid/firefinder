The Rindt tower stands in Taylor County, just southwest of Westboro. The Forest Fire Lookout Association still lists it as standing.[^1][^2]

The Star News of Medford, in an article reprinted at the end of 2015, says the tower was built before 1940.[^1] The same article describes the county's three towers. It says the Perkinstown and Rib River towers were staffed 15 to 20 days each spring, and that two men, the last fire spotters in Taylor County, have done the spotting in recent years. It does not describe anyone staffing the Rindt tower, and we found no record of fires it spotted.[^1]

The article gives two accounts of who controls the Rindt tower. It says the DNR's Prentice station oversees it. Later it says the Perkinstown and Rindt towers sit in sections of the Chequamegon National Forest and are under federal control.[^1] A Forest Lookouts page for Wisconsin also places the Rindt tower in the Chequamegon National Forest.[^4] We could not settle the question from other sources.

In a press release dated December 16, 2015, the DNR said it planned to close the state's remaining fire towers, pointing to safety concerns at some towers and to newer technology.[^1][^3] The article also says the DNR had started reaching out to private landowners and was laying out what could happen to the towers and their sites.[^1]

We found no report after 2015 that says what became of the Rindt tower. The listing still says standing, but it gives no date for when it was checked.[^2] We also found nothing about public access or rental. Please do not climb it unless the owner or land manager confirms it is open to visitors.
