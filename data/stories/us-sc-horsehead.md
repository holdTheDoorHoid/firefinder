The Horsehead Lookout is a 60-foot steel tower on the Cape Romain National Wildlife Refuge, by the Atlantic Intracoastal Waterway in Charleston County, South Carolina.[^1] The U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service administers the site.[^1] A second listing files it under the name Horsehead Creek.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register gives 1930 as the build year, and its description says the tower was built in the 1930s.[^1] Ron Stafford nominated it, and the Register registered it on April 7, 2011, as US 906, SC 9.[^1]

The Register describes the tower as abandoned. Years of rust preceded the collapse of two of its legs around June 14, 2014.[^1] The sources read do not say whether the tower has since been removed or repaired, or whether the public can reach it. The site is inside a national wildlife refuge and the tower is abandoned, so please do not climb it, and check with the refuge before any visit.[^1]
