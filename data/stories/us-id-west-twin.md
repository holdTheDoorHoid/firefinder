West Twin was a tower lookout in Latah County, Idaho, six miles north-northeast of Moscow, at 4,533 feet.[^1][^2] Lookout lists give its build period only as the 1930s.[^1][^2][^3] The Potlatch Timber Protective Association is the agency listed for it, and the Forest Lookouts page gives its location as 40N-5W-13.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the tower as gone.[^4] Both lookout pages instead describe it as abandoned.[^1][^2]

We found no source that describes the site today. Idaho Fire Lookouts marks the site drive-up, but it does not name the landowner or say whether visitors are welcome.[^2] Since none of these sources names who controls the land, ask before you go.
