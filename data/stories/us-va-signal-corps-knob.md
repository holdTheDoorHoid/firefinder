Signal Corps Knob is a summit in Augusta County, Virginia, on the McDowell USGS topographic quadrangle. Its elevation is roughly 3,894 feet (1,187 meters).[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list records a tower on the knob as gone.[^2]

No source I found describes what remains on the summit today, or whether the summit is open to visitors.

[^1]: Signal Corps Knob, Augusta County VA (McDowell Area), MountainZone, https://www.mountainzone.com/mountains/virginia/augusta-va/summits/signal-corps-knob/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Virginia Fire Towers (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/va-co/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
