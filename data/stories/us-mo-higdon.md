A 1938 federal survey description refers to a Forest Service fire tower in Madison County, 50 feet (15 m) tall and built of steel.[^1] The survey station named Higdon sat about 0.3 mile southwest of that tower.[^1] The survey puts the station about 8 miles northeast of Fredericktown, which gives a rough sense of the area.[^1]

The survey's designation for the mark is "Higdon Fire Tower," yet the description puts the station 0.3 mile away from the tower.[^1] The sources do not show whether the two names belong to the same structure.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page files the tower under a "National Forest" heading without naming the forest.[^1] The page lists the tower as removed.[^1]

No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.

[^1]: Higdon, Forest Lookouts (quotes a 1938 Coast and Geodetic Survey station description), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/higdon.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
