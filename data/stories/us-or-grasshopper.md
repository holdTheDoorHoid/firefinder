Grasshopper Mountain lies in Lane County, about 17 miles northeast of Oakridge.[^2] A lookout camp was started in 1919, and an L-5 cab was built in 1933.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts describes the 1933 work as a 10x10 L-5 ground house.[^3]

People staffed the lookout in the late 1930s and early 1940s. In 1938 Gordon Stanley was sent to spend the summer there as a lookout fireman for the Forest Service, and a 1941 entry says the lookout was staffed for 85 days.[^3]

The structure was gone by 1968, according to the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register and Forest Lookouts.[^1][^3] Oregon Hikers tells a different story. It says the last lookout on the summit was erected in 1958 and decommissioned in the 1970s, so the sources disagree about the final years.[^4]

The sources also disagree about the forest. The register places the site in the Willamette National Forest, while Oregon Hikers places it in the Umpqua National Forest.[^1][^4]

Today, Oregon Hikers describes four concrete footings at the summit, which sits inside the Rogue-Umpqua Divide Wilderness.[^4] Its field guide lists a 7.3-mile hike with 2,075 feet of gain to the former lookout site.[^4] The register and Forest Lookouts pages say nothing about the site today.[^1][^3]
