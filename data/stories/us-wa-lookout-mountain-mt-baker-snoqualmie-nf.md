Lookout Mountain sits in the Mount Baker-Snoqualmie National Forest, five miles east of Marblemount in Skagit County, Washington.[^1] The Forest Service describes a hike of 3.5 miles through timber, then 1.2 miles across meadows, on the Lookout Mountain-Monogram Lake Trail.[^2]

The first lookout was a cathedral-style cupola erected in 1928, according to the register.[^3] The Forest Service says the structure it replaced dated to 1929.[^2] Dates for the current tower vary. One site gives 1961,[^1] the register and the Forest Service give 1962,[^3][^2] and the register's own description and a trail guide give 1967.[^3][^4]

The register describes a live-in 15-by-15-foot R-6 cab.[^3] Its 2008 visit report recorded the lookout as not staffed.[^3]

Today the lookout is open to the public year round, though road and trail conditions can limit access.[^2] Overnight stays are first come, first served, and volunteers maintain the building.[^4] The Forest Service warns that the catwalks can collapse, so it limits them to two people per side, and a recreation pass is required for each vehicle at the trailhead.[^2]

One trip guide puts the climb at just over 4.5 miles and calls the upper slope a major avalanche hazard, urging extreme caution on winter attempts.[^4] Check current Forest Service conditions before you go.[^2]
