The Jay Cooke lookout was a 100-foot tower completed in 1937.[^1] A May 1937 item from the Big Lake Breeze, credited to CCC Company 1760 in Cloquet and reprinted on a Forest Lookouts page, says the tower was finished and now covered an area that lookouts had not previously watched.[^1] The county survey list names CCC Company 1760 as the probable builder and describes an Aermotor stairway tower, number 101.[^2]

A 1952 survey note in the county list gives the height as roughly 115 feet, notes a steel, four-legged frame, and marks the tower removed.[^2] The 1937 item and the county's own entry say 100 feet, which conflicts with the 1952 figure.[^1][^2]

The firelookout.org listing marks the tower gone.[^3] No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the tower site today.

The county list places the tower alongside what is now Jay Cooke Road.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page files it under Jay Cooke State Park, which Wikipedia describes as a Minnesota state park that protects the lower reaches of the Saint Louis River.[^1][^4]
