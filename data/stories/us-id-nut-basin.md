The Nut Basin lookout stood about 10 miles northeast of Riggins, in Idaho County, on the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1] A fire lookout was in use there as early as 1915.[^6] A July 8, 1915 item in the Idaho County Free Press named Wm. Brown as the lookout man, as quoted on Rex's Fire Lookout Page.[^3]

The cabin went up in 1927 as an elevated cupola cabin.[^1][^2][^5][^6] A Forest Service history page places it among four D-6 frame lookouts on the forest that were topped with a cupola.[^4] The Idaho list calls it a two-story cabin.[^7]

It was destroyed in 1957.[^1][^3][^5][^6]

The newest description found is a June 2006 SummitPost photo caption, which says only remnants of the lookout are left, without describing them.[^5] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site as drive-up.[^2] The climbing guide describes a four-wheel-drive road to the summit, reached from Nut Basin Road, and it does not mention a permit or closure.[^6]
