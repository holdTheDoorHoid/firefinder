The Georgia list of lookout towers places a Butts Lookout Tower in Butts County at 33.293 N, 84.009 W, at 740 feet (226 m) above sea level.[^1] A topographic-map site lists a tower of the same name in Butts County, with an approximate elevation of 741 feet (226 m).[^2] The two elevations differ by one foot.[^1][^2]

The Eastern US Lookouts site has a page for Butts Mountain, which appears to cover the same site. It gives the county as Butts and the agency as the Pisgah National Forest.[^3] That agency label looks wrong, because the Pisgah National Forest lies entirely within North Carolina.[^4]

The National Historic Lookout Register's Georgia index lists 29 registered lookouts, and none of them is named Butts.[^5]

No source describes the site today, so I cannot say whether a tower or any remains stand there, or whether the site is open to visitors. Anyone planning a visit should first find out who owns the land, and should not climb any tower that is closed or posted.
