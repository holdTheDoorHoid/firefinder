The Forest Lookouts page lists this Blue Mountain under the Kern County Fire Department.[^1] In June 1936 a newspaper reported that two lookout stations would be erected, one in the Blue Mountain area on the Tulare-Kern county line.[^1] By September 1940 a Bakersfield paper mentioned the new lookout station on Blue Mountain.[^1]

In late July 1953 a fire called the Blue Mountain fire was first reported at 2:45 p.m. on a Sunday, by E.R. Preston at the lookout station.[^1] By 5:30 p.m. the flames had spread enough that Preston had to leave his station.[^1] About 50 ranchers helped fight the fire, which was reported under control after covering 1,280 acres of oaks, grass and brush.[^1]

The FFLA list records the Blue Mountain enclosed tower as gone.[^2] These sources do not say when the tower came down, and no source describes what remains at the site today or whether the public can reach it.
