The Texas Forest Service completed a fire lookout tower at East Mountain, in Upshur County, Texas, by May 1962, when a Gilmer Mirror report announced it.[^1] The 87-foot steel girder structure stood on land the agency leased from a Longview landowner. The new tower was to work alongside the agency's towers at Hallsville, Gilmont, Latch and Meadows.[^1]

Crewleader Leon B. Patrick of Longview was responsible for running the tower, with crewman Bennie Wood of Diana.[^1]

The tower also drew trouble. In January 1968 a jury found two Longview boys not guilty of breaking into it the previous October.[^1] In February 1972 the newspaper reported that thieves had broken in twice recently and taken a short-wave radio, binoculars and a coffee pot, among other things.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas index lists the tower as gone.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]
