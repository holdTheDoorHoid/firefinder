A fire tower in Calloway County stood 85 feet tall, according to a November 1960 newspaper report that a forest-lookout history site reproduces.[^1] The site lists the clipping under Calloway County and Murray.[^1]

In 1960 the tower had an operator on duty. Mrs. Ruth Fergerson was running it when shots were fired at it, and she dropped to the floor when the shooting started.[^1] Six rifle shots went through the tower's glass windows, and two more hit the outside, the paper said.[^1]

Four Calloway County youths were held in the county jail on charges of firing eight shots at the tower. The report places it six miles east of the town it came from.[^1] Sheriff Cohen Stubblefield made the arrests roughly thirty minutes later, and none of the boys reportedly made a statement.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 17 lookouts in Kentucky, and this tower is not one of them.[^2] No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
