Grassy Knob's site sits at 4,376 feet (1,334 meters) in Greenbrier County, West Virginia, and a fire lookout once stood there.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register of former sites lists the tower as US 1668, WV 59. It gives a registration date of January 26, 2017, with Brian M. Powell as the nominator.[^1] The register names the West Virginia Division of Forestry as the agency that administered the site.[^1]

The Division of Forestry's article about the state's towers says the agency relied on temporary seasonal employees to staff them.[^3] A deputy state fire manager quoted in the article says each tower would be staffed during fire season or other periods of high danger, and that observers would go up each morning and stay until dark.[^3] That account describes the state's towers in general rather than this one.

The register now lists the tower as gone.[^2] No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
