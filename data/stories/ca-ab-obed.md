Obed is a steel fire lookout tower, 30 m (98 ft) tall, standing at 1,605 m (5,266 ft) above sea level, according to Alberta Wildfire's lookout layer.[^1] The layer gives 1973 as the year the present structure was built.[^1] Alberta's towers moved from wood to steel after steel came into use in the 1920s.[^2] For over a century, Alberta's seasonal lookout observers have been responsible for detecting fires, identifying them and passing on their locations.[^2]

A lookout observer who spent his first season at Obed in 1978 later recalled the tower as a drive-in site near Hinton.[^3] Alberta reports that each of its 127 wildfire lookout sites has a staff member on watch daily, usually from April through October.[^4]

The layer still lists Obed as active and marks it as a weather station.[^1] The layer codes its access mode as drive.[^1] No source I found says the public may visit. Alberta describes the sites as remote, and staff there have to plan for many contingencies, including a curious bear.[^4] Treat Obed as an active, staffed lookout, not a place to visit.
