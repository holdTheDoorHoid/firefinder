Big Hill is a point in the Payette National Forest, about 30 miles southeast of Chamberlain G.S.[^1] Its listed elevation is 8,094 feet.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page gives its survey code as 21N-14E-20, which we read as section 20, township 21 north, range 14 east.[^3]

The lookout here was a 15-foot platform. Listings date it to the 1930s, and they give only the decade.[^1][^3]

The Forest Lookouts page dates its abandonment to 1942, and it credits both dates to a source it names only as Kresek.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under its West Central region and lists it as abandoned and hike-in.[^2]

Firelookout.com says the platform was still there in 2000.[^1] We found no source that says whether it still stands, or that describes the site today.[^1][^2][^3]
