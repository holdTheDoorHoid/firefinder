Cinnamon Mountain Lookout stands on the Gallatin National Forest in Gallatin County, Montana, about 32 miles north-northwest of West Yellowstone.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register says the Forest Service constructed the present tower in 1958, and it names the Hebgen Lake Ranger District as a cooperator.[^2]

The site had a tent lookout before that. The Register describes a "rag camp" lookout there in the 1930s, and Forest Lookouts describes a camp from those years.[^2][^3] The current structure is a 10-foot timber tower with a flat-topped R-6 cab. The Register also mentions a catwalk.[^2] The Register calls the timber wood, while the other two sources say it is treated timber. All three agree on the height and the cab.[^1][^2][^3]

firelookout.com says the lookout was last used for emergencies in the late 1960s.[^1] The Register says it was rehabilitated in 1999 and registered on October 31, 2002, as US 460, MT 36.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^4] The Register gives the elevation as 9,097 feet, while firelookout.com gives 9,235 feet. Our record uses the Register figure.[^2][^1]

We found no source that says whether the cab is open to visitors. Ask the Forest Service, starting with the Hebgen Lake Ranger District, before planning a visit, and do not climb or enter a closed structure.
