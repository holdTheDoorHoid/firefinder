West Hill was a fire lookout site in Tioga County, Pennsylvania.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says the first lookout there was a tree tower, built before 1913.[^1] The two sources we read disagree about the tower that followed. The Forest Lookouts site says a wood tower of 30 to 35 feet was erected in 1915.[^2] The register says a wooden tower replaced the tree tower in 1917, and that this tower was still used around that time and is no longer standing.[^1] We cannot settle which date is right.

The register added the site to its list on September 16, 2008.[^1]

No source we read describes the site today, including whether any part of a tower remains or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: West Hill Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/pa/west-hill-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: West Hill, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/west-hill.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
