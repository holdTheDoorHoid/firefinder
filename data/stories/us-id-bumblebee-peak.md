Bumblebee Peak sits about 12 miles northwest of Kellogg in Shoshone County, Idaho, on the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, at an elevation of 4,746 feet.[^1] The site was first developed with a platform tower in 1931.[^1][^2][^3] A 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab was built there in 1933.[^1][^2][^3]

A second tower, 30 feet tall with a treated timber R-6 flat cab, is dated 1959 by two sources.[^2][^3] The firelookout.com page puts the new tower in 1960 instead.[^1]

Staff listings begin with Mick Clumper, who is listed for 1931 to 1933 and again in 1935.[^2] From 1951 to 1957 most of the names are married couples, starting with Mr. & Mrs. Kenneth Davis.[^2] The last name listed is Kim Park, in 1972.[^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1975.[^3][^4] Firelookout.com says it was removed that year instead.[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts' visit notes say the footings they found probably came from the most recent tower, plus one older footing that is badly crumbled.[^2] They could not find the privy, but several benchmarks are on the nearby point.[^2] The site is listed as a drive-up location, and the sources we read name no closure or permission rule.[^2]
