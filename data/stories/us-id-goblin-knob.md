Goblin Knob Lookout stood in Boundary County, Idaho, about nine miles east-northeast of Nordman, at an elevation of 6,606 feet.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts lists the Priest Lake Timber Protective Association as the agency.[^3]

The cab was a gabled L-4 design, built in 1931.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists two known staff members: Juell Turley in 1933, and Lawerence Olsen in 1938, spelled that way in the source.[^2]

The cab was destroyed in 1972.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts marks the site as gone in the same year.[^3] It lists the site under Boundary County, with the code 61N-3W-5.[^3] Rex's page and the Idaho Fire Lookouts site both credit the Fire Lookout Museum for the lookout's photographs.[^1][^2]

None of the three listings we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
