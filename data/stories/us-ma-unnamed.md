OpenStreetMap marks this spot as a fire lookout, and its tags set access to no.[^1] The closest match we found is the Norwell Fire Tower in the Town of Norwell, Plymouth County, registered as US 619, MA 30.[^2] The register's printed longitude matches this spot to within about 50 metres.[^1][^2] Its printed latitude is garbled, so the match rests on longitude alone and should be checked before the record is renamed.[^2]

The register says the Bureau of Fire Control built the current tower in 1956, at the Navy's request, to replace an iron tower on Prospect Hill just to the northeast.[^2] The register gives that earlier tower a height of 48 feet and a build year of 1916.[^2] The Forest Lookouts site gives 40 feet for the 1916 tower on Prospect Hill in Hingham.[^3] Both sources describe the 1956 replacement as a 70-foot steel tower with a 10-by-10 cab.[^2][^3]

The register dates its listing to December 15, 2004, with Henry Isenberg as nominator.[^2] It names the Massachusetts Bureau of Forest Fire Control as administrator.[^2] The Forest Lookouts site has a separate Judges Hill entry in Norwell. That may be the current site, but we have not confirmed it.[^3]

We found no dated record of the tower's condition in the sources we read. OpenStreetMap still lists the structure, with access set to no, so the public should not expect to visit.[^1] Please do not climb it. If you want to see the tower, ask the state forest fire agency first.
