Mount Irvine stands northwest of Limestone in Cattaraugus County, in the Southern Tier of New York.[^1] In 1928 the state Conservation Department's Division of State Parks put a 60-foot Aermotor LS40 steel lookout tower on the highest point between the headwaters of Rice Brook and South Carrollton Creek, inside what is now Allegany State Park.[^1][^2][^3]

The park kept hold of the tower. In 1931 the state moved jurisdiction over several park towers to Forest Fire Control, but Mount Irvine was not one of them. The park staffed it only when fire danger was high.[^1][^2]

The tower did not stay on the mountain. The park moved it to Science Hill, near the Pennsylvania border, in 1931.[^1][^2] The Southern Tier list gives 1932 for that move in its Science Hill entry, so the sources disagree by a year.[^2] The tower stood on Science Hill until 1978.[^1][^2]

The takedown date is also disputed. Wikipedia and the Southern Tier list both put it in 1978, and the Southern Tier list says park personnel did the work.[^1][^2] The Allegany State Park article dates the takedown to the early 1970s, around the time the park's fire towers were abandoned for aerial surveillance in 1971.[^3] A class from Ellicottville BOCES reassembled the tower near their school, and the park article says that saved it.[^1][^2][^3]

We could not find a source that describes the Mount Irvine summit today. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list marks the Mount Irvine tower as gone, and it marks the Science Hill tower as gone too.[^4] No source we read says whether the reassembled tower still stands at Ellicottville, so a visitor should not expect to see it on the mountain.
