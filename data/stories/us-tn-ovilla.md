Ovilla was a lookout tower in Wayne County, Tennessee.[^1][^2] A Tennessee lookout inventory gives its elevation as 1,057 feet and marks the tower as removed.[^1] The same inventory lists two other Wayne County towers, Hardins Creek and McGlamerys, as standing.[^1]

The inventory's own note says its research into Tennessee's lookout history is still in progress.[^1] The inventory also reports that many towers have come down, and that most tower sites in the state are now privately owned.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the Ovilla site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.

[^1]: Tennessee lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Tennessee forest lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/tennessee.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
