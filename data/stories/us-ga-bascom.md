Georgia's lookout list places a tower named Bascom in Screven County, at 32.85659, -81.63722, at 229 feet (69.8 m) above sea level.[^1] A directory of Georgia forest lookouts also lists a Bascom entry in Screven County, with no other detail.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists a different tower in the county, the Sylvania Lookout Tower, at 32.764960, -81.649550.[^3] The Bascom entry lies about 10 km north of that point, so the two are separate locations.[^1][^3]

No source found describes the Bascom site today, so whether anything still stands there and whether the public can visit are unknown.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Georgia - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/georgia.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Sylvania Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, https://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ga/sylvania-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
