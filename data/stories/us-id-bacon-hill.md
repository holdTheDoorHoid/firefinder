Bacon Hill is a lookout site in Adams County, Idaho, on land that the Bureau of Land Management manages.[^1] The Forest Lookouts guide files it in its Adams County group, which also includes Council Mountain.[^1] The guide credits Ray Kresek of the Fire Lookout Museum, whose inventory of Northwest lookouts it says helped fill many gaps in its own information.[^2] Through that inventory, the guide records a camp on the hill in 1942 and an abandonment in 1950.[^1][^2]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains on the hill today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1]
