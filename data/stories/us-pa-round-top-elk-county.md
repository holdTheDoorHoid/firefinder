The U.S. Forest Service built a wooden fire lookout tower on Round Top, in Elk County, in 1940.[^1] The register puts the site at about 553 metres (1,815 feet), at 41 degrees 27.854 minutes north and 78 degrees 49.002 minutes west.[^1] Henry Isenberg nominated the site, and it was added to the Former Fire Lookout Sites register in September 2008.[^1] The register says the tower no longer stands, so the entry marks a place rather than a tower you can climb.[^1]

A map database still holds a tower-type point named Round Top Fire Tower in Highland Township, Elk County.[^2] A map point does not show what is on the ground, though, and no source we read describes what remains at the site or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Round Top Tower Site (Elk County), Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/pa/round-top-tower-site-elk-county/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Round Top Fire Tower Map - Highland Township, Pennsylvania, USA, Mapcarta, https://mapcarta.com/22764014 (accessed 2026-10-10).
