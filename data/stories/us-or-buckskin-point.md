Buckskin Point is a ground site in Clackamas County, Oregon, about 21 miles south of Mount Hood.[^2][^4][^1]

The U.S. Forest Service erected a 10-by-10-foot cab in 1933, on a minor point about 1.5 miles west of Wests Butte.[^1][^2]

The Forest Lookouts page lists panorama photographs dated October 12, 1933.[^3] A 1946 map of the Mount Hood National Forest shows the site as an emergency lookout.[^3]

The register gives an August 17, 2010 registration date and names Ray Kresek as the nominator.[^1] The register page also carries a 1954 photograph of the lookout.[^1]

According to the register, the lookout was no longer standing by 1968, and firelookout.com gives the same year.[^1][^2]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the point today, or whether the public may visit it.
