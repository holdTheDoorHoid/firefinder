A list of Nova Scotia's fire towers, last updated September 18, 2009, places a tower at Valleyview Provincial Park and gives its talk group as DNR Annapolis.[^1] The list also marks the site as an IMRS site, a term it does not explain.[^1] A Canada Lookouts page headed Valley Provincial Park gives the name as Valleyview Provincial Park and places the tower in the Annapolis area.[^2]

Tourism Nova Scotia puts the park 5 km (3 mi) north of Bridgetown.[^3] Nova Scotia Parks gives the address as 960 Hampton Mountain Road, Bridgetown, and lists the 2026 operating season as June 5 to October 12, 2026.[^4] The park page describes a Look Off with a panoramic view of the Annapolis Valley.[^4]

The Department of Natural Resources operated the province's towers.[^5] A CBC News report, carried by Yahoo, says they were taken out of service in 2015, later dismantled, and replaced by aerial surveillance.[^5] A 2012 provincial news release had planned to replace 19 towers with aerial detection the following year and to keep 14 in remote areas.[^6] That plan points to 2013 rather than 2015, so the sources do not agree on the end date.[^5][^6]

No source I found describes what remains at the Valleyview tower site today, or ties the park's Look Off to the former tower.[^1][^4]
