The lookout on Richmond Hill in Dighton, Massachusetts, began operating in 1912.[^1][^4] The Forest Fire Lookout site describes a 38-foot steel tower with an 8-by-8-foot cab and a ladder, and says a quarter mile of telephone line ran out to it.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register and the Forest Lookouts page both describe the first tower as 28 feet tall, so the sources disagree on its height.[^2][^4]

The tower was staffed until 1915, and the Forest Fire site says it was discontinued that September.[^1]

The tower did not stay on Richmond Hill. The Forest Fire site says the tower went to Rehoboth in 1915.[^1] The Register and the Forest Lookouts page date the move to 1916 and place the structure on Great Meadow Hill.[^2][^4] The Forest Fire site's Rehoboth page also dates the tower's first operation there to 1916, after its move from Dighton.[^3]

In 1931 a new 68-foot tower replaced the older one on Great Meadow Hill.[^3] In 1955 the Army asked for the 1931 tower to be moved, and it was rebuilt on Long Hill.[^2] The Forest Fire site says the Army ordered the tower removed, citing national security.[^3]

Today the tower on Long Hill is the 1931 structure, not the 1912 tower from Richmond Hill.[^2][^3] No source we read describes what remains on Richmond Hill today, or whether the site is open to the public.
