Carpenter Mountain is in the Willamette National Forest, about seven miles north of McKenzie Bridge in Linn County.[^1] A government lookout was on the summit by 1914, when it found a fire ten miles northeast of Belknap Springs.[^2] Accounts of the early buildings disagree. The NHLR calls the first one an open-sided fire shelter from 1917, and the Forest Lookouts page lists a cupola-style house for that year.[^3][^2] Firelookout describes a rag camp and shelter, and says lightning destroyed the site in 1921.[^1]

The present cab is an L-4 design, which Cherylhill describes as a ground cabin.[^4] The NHLR says the CCC built it in 1935, while the Forest Service dates it to 1934.[^3][^5]

Early reports show the lookout spotting fires. In June 1918 it saw a fire about a quarter-mile wide near the southern boundary of Linn County, and in 1920 lightning struck the telephone wire to the station.[^2] A 1939 newspaper item named Alvin Blankenship as the fireman for that season and said it was most important to have a lookout stationed there.[^2] In 1986 lightning hit the cabin again. A ranger report recorded 14 windows blown out and the roof lifted off its frame, and the cabin was left under repair.[^2]

Firelookout says the cabin saw little use after the 1960s and fell into disrepair.[^1] It was restored in the 1990s, and the Forest Service lists a 1995 renovation.[^1][^5] The NHLR adds that the cabin is also listed on the National Register of Historic Places.[^3]

Today the Forest Service says the cabin is manned in fire season and cannot be rented by the public.[^5] The 2026 directory lists it as staffed.[^6] The Forest Service's directions end by turning left on Forest Road 350, driving about five miles north to the Carpenter Mountain Trailhead, and climbing Trail 3302.[^5] The Forest Service rates it a one-mile, moderate trail.[^5] Cherylhill's 2014 visit describes parking at a trailhead about a mile away and hiking up.[^4] For current conditions, the Forest Service asks visitors to contact the McKenzie River Ranger Station, so call before you go.[^5]
