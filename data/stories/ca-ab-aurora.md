The Aurora fire lookout is a steel tower in Alberta. Alberta's wildfire register lists it at 30 m (98 ft) tall, about 1,331 m (4,366 ft) above sea level, with 2014 as its construction year.[^1]

Alberta says its lookouts are placed where good visibility makes it easier to spot and report wildfires.[^2] After 1912, Alberta used two kinds of lookout: summit sites with low buildings set on the ground or on short stilts, and towers on high forested hills that raised the observer above obstacles to sight.[^3]

The register codes the site's access as "fly", which suggests it is reached by air rather than by road. It is listed as active and has a weather station.[^1]

The register is the only current record I found for this site, and no source describes the tower on the ground today.
