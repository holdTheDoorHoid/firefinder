The Pasquia Hills sit in east-central Saskatchewan, close to the Manitoba boundary [^2]. The Firetowers of Saskatchewan listing includes a Pasquia Hills tower site at 53 degrees, 22.1 minutes north and 102 degrees, 32.3667 minutes west, and it leaves the height and year of construction blank [^1]. The Canada Lookouts index of Saskatchewan towers carries the same name [^4].

The listing names one towerman for the site, Einer Wicker, with dates unknown but before 1960 [^1]. Wicker had filed on a homestead in the Torch River district, then joined the Canadian Army in 1939 and spent the next seven years in it [^3]. After his discharge he joined the Saskatchewan Department of Natural Resources, and he served as towerman at Pasquia Hills, English Cabin and Thunder Mountain fire towers until he retired [^3].

No source I could read describes what, if anything, stands at the Pasquia Hills site today, or whether the public can visit it.
