The Boulder Lake fire lookout stood in Oconto County, in the Nicolet National Forest.[^1] The Coast and Geodetic Survey set a concrete survey monument near it in 1934 and described the spot by reference to the tower and to a lookout keeper's house, so the tower was standing by then.[^2] That 1934 description also places the monument on land owned by the U.S. Forest Service.[^2]

In August 1938, fifteen Mystery Week campers from the Gardner Dam Camps made an eight-mile jaunt to the lookout, as a lookout history page quotes The Post-Crescent.[^1] The tower was a 100-foot spire. Climbers went up its ladder in groups of three, each scout on a safety belt.[^1]

Two 1990 recovery notes on the same datasheet mention a concrete pier of the old fire tower at the site and place the survey station close to the spot where the old Boulder Lake lookout tower stood.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^3]

No source I found describes what stands on the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3] The 1990 National Geodetic Survey note names the Wisconsin Department of Transportation as the owner of the land, while the 1934 description named the Forest Service. The notes do not explain the change.[^2] A 2003 check rates the survey monument as in good condition.[^2]
