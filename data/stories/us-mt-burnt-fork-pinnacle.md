Burnt Fork Pinnacle is a peak in Missoula County, in the Lolo National Forest, standing a little over 6,600 feet high.[^1][^3] An L-4 style cabin served as its lookout.[^1][^2] A 2011 blog dates the cabin to about 1932, and Rex Kamstra's list gives the same year.[^1][^2]

The blog says the cabin was destroyed in 1950, and Kamstra's entry also points to 1950 and lists the cabin as gone.[^1][^2] Ron Kemnow's listing says it was removed and gives no date.[^3] The sources disagree on how it left the peak.[^1][^2][^3] The Montana lookout list marks it as gone.[^5]

The peak is reached on foot.[^4] Trail 418 runs three miles from the Burnt Fork Trailhead to the summit, and the Forest Service says horses are allowed.[^4] There is no potable water at the trailhead.[^4] The 2011 blog says the trail climbs to the old lookout site.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains there today.
