Cat Mountain lies south of Cranberry Lake in St. Lawrence County.[^1][^2] In October 1909 state fire superintendents decided to add a lookout there, to go with the six Adirondack stations and three in the Catskills.[^1]

A lookout was set up in 1910 on top of a 37-foot tower that cost $698.96.[^1] By 1917 a 47-foot steel tower had been erected there.[^1] The sources do not say whether it replaced the first.[^1]

Ed Akey of Kanakena was expected back on the mountain in 1950.[^1] Harry E. Watson, a fire ranger at the tower, died in 1967 after eleven years as a fire observer at Cranberry Lake.[^1]

The state list now marks the tower as gone.[^2] We found no date for its removal, and no source describes the site today, including what remains on the mountain or whether the land is open to visitors.
