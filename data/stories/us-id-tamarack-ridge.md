Built in 1933, Tamarack Ridge Lookout was a 40-foot pole tower carrying an L-4 cab on the north point.[^1][^2] The site lies in Clearwater County, Idaho, within the Clearwater National Forest, at an elevation of 4,697 feet.[^1][^2] The records also list three patrol points.[^1][^2]

Two lists give its survey location as section 5, township 37 North, range 7 East.[^4][^5] A 2016 Forest Service list of Clearwater lookouts, built from a regional book and agency records, gives the same 1933 and ruins dates.[^4]

By 1940 the lookout was abandoned and in ruins.[^1][^2][^4]

Today the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the tower as gone.[^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page describes the site as drive-up ruins.[^1] No source describes what is left of the ruins, or whether there are any rules for visitors.
