Pine Knob is a summit in Lee County, Virginia, that the Forest Lookouts page places on Stone Mountain.[^1] The page lists the Virginia Department of Forestry as the agency.[^1]

A December 1940 item from a Civilian Conservation Corps company, quoted on the same page, says a work crew had attempted to build a fire tower on the peak.[^1] The crew carried heavy timber up the mountain for 1.6 miles, and the item says they did it without an accident.[^1] It does not say whether the tower was finished.[^1]

A Wikipedia article on the Stone Mountain Wilderness mentions an old tower site on Stone Mountain, with good views across the Powell River to Powell Mountain.[^3] That article does not name Pine Knob, so it is unclear whether it describes the same place.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Pine Knob tower as gone.[^2] None of the sources checked gives a date for its removal, describes what remains on the peak today, or says whether the peak is open to visitors.
