East Gold Hill had a 50-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, built in 1935 in Latah County on the St. Joe National Forest.[^1][^2][^3]

The tower stood from 1935 to 1965.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both say it was destroyed in 1965.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com's heading for the site reads R.I.P. 1935-1965.[^1]

Rex's page gives the same year but a different ending. It says the tower was taken away in the autumn, once fire season was over.[^3] The three pages agree on the year but not on how the tower ended.[^1][^2][^3]

Photographs on Firelookout.com are courtesy of the Fire Lookout Museum and Michael DeMarco.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or says whether it is open to the public, so check with the St. Joe National Forest before going.[^1][^2][^3]
