The Saddle Mountain guard station stood on Saddle Mountain in Lane County, in the Siuslaw National Forest.[^1][^2] The register lists the site's elevation as 2,297 feet (700 m).[^2] In June 1912 a fire destroyed the rangers' cabin on the mountain.[^1] A July 1913 item quoted on the Forest Lookouts page said two men were at work on a replacement cabin.[^1]

Lookouts were posted there in the fire seasons of the 1920s and 1930s.[^1] The page records Mr. and Mrs. Wm. Louney in 1924.[^1] It names a patrolman called Lynch in 1928 and 1929, as R. E. Lynch in one item and Robert Lynch in the other.[^1] Riley Thompson was on the mountain in 1931 and 1932, and in 1933 Cecil Thompson was paid $85 per month for the work.[^1] A 1934 item about Mrs. Allen T. Osborne says her husband was posted at the lookout for the fire season.[^1]

A 1934 panorama of the lookout is captioned 4-18-34 on the register page and credited to the National Archives Pacific Northwest Region.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page lists panorama photos under April 12, 1934, and does not say whether they are the same photographs.[^1]

The register dates a pole L-4 tower on the site to 1937 and says it no longer stands.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
