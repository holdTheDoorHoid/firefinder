Gold Peak is a lookout site on Kaniksu National Forest land, about three miles northwest of Upper Priest Lake in Bonner County, Idaho.[^1] Rex Kamstra's page lists its elevation at 4,534 feet.[^1]

The first facility, in the 1920s, was a tent camp with a 20-foot platform.[^1][^2][^3] In 1934 a 40-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab replaced it.[^1][^3] Ron Kemnow's Forest Lookouts page also lists a 40-foot platform patrol tower about three-quarters of a mile northeast, in the same 1934 entry.[^3]

Kamstra's page and Kemnow's page both give 1966 as the year the tower was destroyed.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the lookout under drive-up.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the tower as gone.[^4] None of the pages I checked describes what remains at the site today or whether the ground is open to visitors.
