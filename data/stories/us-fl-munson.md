Munson has its own page on the Eastern US Lookouts site, which gives Santa Rosa County as its county.[^1] A TopoQuest map gazetteer places a Munson Lookout Tower point at about 30.8638 N, 86.8764 W.[^2]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^2]: Bear Creek, FL, TopoQuest, https://topoquest.com/place/florida/stream/bear-creek/278305 (accessed 2026-10-21).
