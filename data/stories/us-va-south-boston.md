The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list includes a tower at South Boston in Halifax County, Virginia, and marks it gone. It is placed at 36.67284, -78.86059.[^1]

The state's historic register entry for the South Boston Historic District says the town began as a rail stop in 1854 and that by the early 1900s it ranked second in the nation as a bright-leaf tobacco market.[^2]

None of the sources checked describes what, if anything, remains at the tower site, or whether the site is open to visitors. Do not assume a tower stands there.

[^1]: Virginia Fire Towers (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/va-co/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: South Boston Historic District, Virginia Department of Historic Resources, https://www.dhr.virginia.gov/historic-registers/130-0006/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
