Dairy Point is on the Fremont National Forest in Lake County, Oregon, at 5,652 feet.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site on May 20, 2011.[^1]

The register says a lookout cab was built on the site in 1931 and was gone by 1950.[^1][^2] The Lake County list dates the site to the 1930s and describes its structures as unknown.[^3] It calls Dairy Point an emergency lookout that worked alongside the Dairy Creek Guard Station.[^3]

The register page includes a panorama photo taken on May 14, 1933, credited to the National Archives' Pacific Northwest Region.[^1] It also has a 2005 photo of the site, credited to Ron Kemnow.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it. The register lists the U.S. Forest Service as the manager.[^1] Anyone planning a trip should check with the Fremont National Forest before going.
