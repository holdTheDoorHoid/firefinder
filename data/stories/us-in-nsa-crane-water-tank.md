The tower at Crane now sits inside a water tank on the Naval Weapons Station Crane in Martin County, Indiana.[^1] The Register's description says the area became a naval ammunition facility when World War II began.[^1]

The sources disagree about where the tower came from. A 1946 station description for a survey mark calls the water tower one "built over and around an old Forest Service lookout tower."[^2] The same Indiana Fire Towers page quotes a Forest Service page saying the Navy's Burns City depot ran four towers during the war.[^2] The sources I read do not settle which account is right, and none gives a build year for the tower inside the tank.[^2]

The sources I read do not say who staffed this tower or what fires it watched.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as standing, but with its cab no longer in place.[^3] After a February 2025 visit, Indiana Fire Towers described this tower as one that is now part of a water tank tower.[^2] A caption on that page also says a 1978 topographic map shows a water tank with a lookout tower.[^2]

The Register added the tower on August 2, 2026, with Jason Johns as nominator.[^1] The Navy administers the site.[^1] Indiana Fire Towers says it sits on restricted federal Naval property with no public access, and that it is not possible to visit or climb these towers.[^2] Anyone planning a trip should know the site has no public access.[^2]
