Dun Brook Mountain, which the FFLA list also spells Dunn Brook, lies in the Adirondacks in the town of Indian Lake, Hamilton County, east-northeast of Blue Mountain Lake.[^1][^2][^4] The Conservation Commission put a wooden tower on the mountain in May 1911.[^1]

The lookout was not always manned.[^2] The Eastern lookouts site quotes the Conservation Commission's annual reports, which say the station was not staffed in 1913 or in 1914.[^2] A 1919 entry on the same site says the lookout station was abandoned.[^2]

The sources do not agree on when the lookout ended. CNY Hiking lists the tower as removed in 1919.[^3] Wikipedia says the tower stopped fire watching in 1920.[^1] The FFLA list calls it a platform tower and marks it gone.[^4]

We found no source that describes the site today. None of the sources we read says what, if anything, is left on the summit, and none says who controls access or whether a visitor can reach the top.
