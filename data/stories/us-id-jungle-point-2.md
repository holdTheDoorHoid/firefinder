Idaho County has two lookouts called Jungle Point.[^2][^4] This story follows the one on the Clearwater National Forest, about 14 miles east-northeast of Kooskia, at 4,000 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

Its design was an L-4 tower set half a mile southeast of the summit.[^1][^2] Sources date it to the 1930s.[^1][^2][^3] The Clearwater National Forest list places it in section 15, township 33 North.[^3]

All three sources list the lookout as abandoned, and two of them say it is gone.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^3]

The other Jungle Point is a camp on the Nez Perce National Forest, about 13 miles south-southeast of Grangeville.[^4] Firelookout.com dates that camp to 1930 and says it was abandoned in 1942.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the camp under its Selway region page, not this tower.[^5]
