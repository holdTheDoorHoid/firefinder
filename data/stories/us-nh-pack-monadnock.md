The Pack Monadnock Fire Tower stands in Miller State Park in Hillsborough County, New Hampshire.[^1][^2] The Register describes it as a 41-foot International Derrick tower built in 1939.[^1] After the 1938 hurricane caused extensive damage to forests across the state, the state built five more lookouts, including one in Miller Park.[^2]

The first cab was 7 feet square and made of metal, the Register says.[^1] In 1940 a 10-by-10-foot wooden cab replaced it, and a ground cabin for the watchman was built that year.[^2] In February 1960 lightning destroyed the cab.[^2] The Register says the cab was burned and then repaired, while Historic Structures says it was destroyed and a new one was built that season.[^1][^2] Historic Structures adds that the new cab had no steel above the tower, and that watchmen were to clear out of the station during electrical storms.[^2] The watchman at the time had been operating the station since 1941.[^2]

The state took the tower out of service in 1981 and removed the cab, leaving the steel frame in place.[^2] In 2002 the Department of Environmental Services offered funds to rebuild the cab in return for permission to put an air quality monitoring facility on the site.[^2] A cab built at a state facility and airlifted to the summit went up in the fall of 2004.[^2] The Register says it was placed in service in 2005.[^1]

Today the tower is an active state fire lookout.[^2] The Register says the state staffs it on class 3 fire-danger days and higher, while Wikipedia describes it as staffed seasonally. Those two descriptions are not quite the same.[^1][^4] The Register also notes that the observation deck can get cramped with hikers, and Wikipedia says a paved road runs to the top.[^1][^4]

Visitors do climb it. A state parks history says visitors can still climb the tower, though the same document gives a 27-foot height for the 1939 tower and says its original cabin no longer exists.[^3] I have used the 41-foot height and the 2004 cab, because the Register and Historic Structures both describe those.[^1][^2]
