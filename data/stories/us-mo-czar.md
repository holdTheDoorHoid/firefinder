The Czar Lookout Tower is on a ridge about a mile northeast of Route Y, near the community of Czar in southeastern Crawford County.[^2] The Central US Lookouts page places the tower in the Mark Twain National Forest.[^1]

In February 1934, The Salem Post and The Democrat-Bulletin reported 43 1/2 miles of telephone line under construction.[^1] The line was to be extended to Czar once the lookout tower was set up at or near the community, north of Viburnum.[^1] That same year, Civilian Conservation Corps enrollees erected a 100-foot (30 m) steel tower here.[^1]

A May 1935 newspaper item reported a lookout cabin for the Czar lookouts: two rooms of modern living quarters, 24 by 12 feet, with a garage of the same size.[^1]

A 1956 Coast and Geodetic Survey station description calls the tower a four-legged steel structure, with a 10-foot-square steel and glass house on top, and says it is about 113 feet (34 m) high.[^1] The 1934 report gave 100 feet, so the two accounts differ on height.[^1]

Wikipedia describes the tower in the present tense, but no source I could read confirms its condition or whether visitors can reach it today.[^2]
