Saddle Mountain lookout stands in Catron County, New Mexico, on the Gila National Forest.[^1] Its first tower went up in 1933.[^2][^3] That tower was an Aermotor MC-24 with a wooden cab, according to the Register's own description.[^1]

Sources disagree on the build year. The Register's "Built" field says 1966.[^1] That is the year the cab was replaced with a steel cab from the Forest Service's CL-100 series.[^1][^2][^3] The 1933 date and the MC-24 design describe the first tower, and the 1966 date describes the newer cab.[^2][^3]

I found no staffing history or named fires in the sources I read. One later item is an October 2013 vandalism investigation at the tower. The Forest's law enforcement section started it, and the sources give no details.[^2]

The Register accepted the lookout on January 2, 2006, as US 653, NM 18, with Dave Lorenz as nominator.[^1] In 1988 a thematic National Register review found the lookout not eligible for the National Register, because its original design, construction, materials and workmanship had lost their integrity.[^3] The 2020 chapter report says it should be looked at again, now that it is 54 years old.[^3]

Today the Forest Fire Lookout Association's New Mexico list shows the lookout standing.[^4] I found nothing on current staffing or whether the public can go up it, so please check with the Gila National Forest before planning a visit.
