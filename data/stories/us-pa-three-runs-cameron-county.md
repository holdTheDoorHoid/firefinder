The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes a steel Blaw-Knox tower at Three Runs in Cameron County, built in 1923.[^1] The register gives the site an elevation of 2,206 feet (672 metres).[^1]

A trail that leads to the site is still called the Three Runs Tower Trail, in the Quehanna Wild Area.[^3] The wild area covers parts of Elk and Moshannon State Forests.[^4] Both the register and a hiking account say the tower was removed in 1979.[^1][^2]

Neither the tower nor its cabin is still there.[^3] The site is now a parking area that serves part of the Quehanna Trail.[^3] From there, a hike of about half a mile reaches the Three Runs Vista.[^3]

The road in starts as an easy gravel road and turns rocky and dirt before its east end, where it becomes a high-clearance four-wheel-drive track.[^3] The trail listing asks drivers to respect the private properties the road passes through.[^3]
