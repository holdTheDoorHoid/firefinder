Eaglehead was a Minnesota Forest Service lookout in Pine County, numbered tower 2 in the county list.[^2] The list dates it to 1928 and gives its height as 81 feet, for an Aermotor ladder tower.[^2] A 1952 survey described a four-legged steel structure with an enclosed observation platform at the top and put its height at about 100 feet, which does not match the 81 feet in the county table.[^2]

The county list says the tower was removed.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as Tower Gone, without a date, and places it in Pine County at 46.12867 north and 92.60553 west.[^1]

No source we read describes the site now, including whether anything stands there or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Minnesota Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Pine County Minnesota Fire Lookout Towers, Wikimedia Commons, https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/64/Pine_County_Towers.pdf (accessed 2026-10-10).
