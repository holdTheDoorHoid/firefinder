Candler is listed as a lookout site in Marion County, Florida, where the listing gives a steel tower 100 feet (30.5 m) tall.[^1] Candler is an unincorporated community in Florida.[^2] The site is on neither of Florida's lookout registers.[^3][^4] The National Historic Lookout Register counts 31 Florida lookouts, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register counts six former ones.[^3][^4]

No source describes what remains at the Candler site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
