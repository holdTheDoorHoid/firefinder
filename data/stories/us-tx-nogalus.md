The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list gives a Nogalus tower in Houston County, at 31.26806, -95.12944, and marks it as gone.[^1] A map listing carries the name Nogalus Lookout Tower for a tower in Houston County.[^2]

A separate lookout directory files Nogalus under Trinity County instead, and gives the name and county with nothing more about the tower.[^3] The register and the map listing both say Houston.[^1][^2] This story follows them, but the sources do not settle the question.

No source found describes what remains at the Nogalus site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1]

The 2025 book Fire in the Piney Woods describes the location and design of Texas lookout towers, and is a place to look for a fuller record.[^4]
