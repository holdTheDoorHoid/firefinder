The Lee tower stood on Cedar Mountain, northwest of Leetonia in Tioga County.[^1][^2] The register describes it as a steel Aermotor tower built in 1928.[^1] An October 1939 report from the Wellsboro Agitator, quoted on a regional lookout history site, called it the 80-foot Lee Tower at Leetonia and listed it among the other towers in District 16.[^2]

In April 1949 the Wellsboro Gazette named Raymond Cole as the towerman on Lee Tower, which it placed on Cedar Mountain north west of Leetonia.[^2]

The tower was removed in 1986.[^1] The site was entered in the Former Fire Lookout Sites register on September 16, 2008.[^1]

Today, no source I read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain at this spot, or whether the ground is open to the public. Plan on a site visit only after you have checked who owns the land.
