Munger Mountain stands about 10 miles south of Jackson Hole, in Teton County, at 8,387 feet.[^1] The lookout was a fire lookout cabin on the Bridger-Teton National Forest.[^1]

In 1936 a telephone line was under construction to the summit lookout.[^2] In August 1938 a local paper mentioned Phil Korell at the Forest Service lookout there.[^2] In June 1945 a local newspaper reported that Mrs. Burgess of Riverton was posted to the Munger Mountain lookout.[^2] In July 1954, after two weeks of hot, dry weather, the Teton Forest judged it advisable to staff the Munger Mountain lookout, along with Monument Ridge.[^2]

In the mid-1970s the Forest Service judged some lookouts a possible liability and a fire risk, then torched the Munger and Deer Creek lookouts.[^3] The lookout is listed as gone.[^1]

Recreation in the Fall Creek/Munger Mountain area includes hiking, horseback riding, mountain biking and OHV use.[^4] No source describes what remains on the summit today.
