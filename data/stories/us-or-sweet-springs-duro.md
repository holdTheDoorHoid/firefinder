Sweet Springs Mountain, also known as Duro, was a lookout site in Marion County that had a cabin.[^1][^3] According to the Former Fire Lookout Sites register, the cabin dates from 1940 and no longer stands.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the same 1940 date and also says the cabin is gone.[^2]

The register places the site in the Mount Hood region, and it lists the elevation as 4,078 feet, or 1,243 meters.[^1] Firelookout.com puts the site about 20 miles northwest of Detroit.[^2]

The Clackamas-Marion Fire Patrol Association administered the site.[^1][^3] The Oregon Forest Lookouts page names the same association and marks the site as removed.[^3]

The site was registered on September 20, 2010, on the nomination of Ray Kresek, and it carries the numbers US 650, OR 82.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the cabin at the site today.
