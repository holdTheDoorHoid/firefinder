Paulina Butte sits in the Ochoco National Forest, in Crook County, Oregon.[^1] The first lookout here was a tree platform about 100 feet up, with a cabin on the ground for living quarters, put up in the 1920s.[^2] In 1933 a 75-foot Aermotor steel tower with a 7x7 steel cab took its place, and living quarters were in a cabin near the base.[^1][^2]

Newspaper items from the 1930s and 1950s name a few of the people who worked the station.[^2] In 1936 the Central Oregonian reported that Fisher Logan and his wife had moved up for the fire season, and in 1937 that Logan was the Forest Service fire lookout there.[^2] In 1952 the Herald and News said Joan Loosley, a home economics student at Oregon State College, would spend the summer as lookout.[^2]

The register and firelookout.com both say the tower was abandoned in 1964 and removed in 1968.[^1][^3] The Forest Lookouts site also lists the 1968 removal.[^2]

A visitor's post on the Oregon Hikers forum, dated April 28, 2013, places the site 42 miles east of Prineville.[^4] The writer saw footings from the steel tower and the remains of the old tree lookout.[^4] No other source we read describes the site today, and none says whether it is open to visitors or how to reach it.
