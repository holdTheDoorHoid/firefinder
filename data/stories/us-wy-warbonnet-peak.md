The Warbonnet Peak lookout stood on Warbonnet Peak in Converse County, Wyoming, about 28 miles southwest of Douglas.[^1] Firelookout.com places the peak in the Medicine Bow National Forest and gives its elevation as 9,414 feet.[^1] The Wyoming list on firelookout.org places the peak at 42.43484 north, 105.79392 west, files the lookout under an unknown type, and marks it gone.[^2]

No source read describes a lookout structure on the summit today. A climbing guide says the summit and its sides lie on a patented mining claim, and that the claim's owners are absent landlords tied to Monarch Warbonnet Mining.[^3] The same guide says the mining operation is abandoned and finds little evidence of occupation.[^3] A photo caption on the climbing page refers to a "Summit BM" on the peak.[^3] Because the guide describes a patented claim with absent owners, check who controls access before going up.
