The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine list has two entries that carry the name Peaked Mountain, and the two sites are not the same.[^1] This Washington County entry is separate from the Penobscot County lookout listed as Chick Hill (Peaked Mountain).[^1] The association places the Washington County tower at 44.88795 north, 67.91016 west, and marks it gone.[^1] That same Tower Gone label appears for about 80 Maine sites on the list.[^1] The Penobscot entry carries the register number US 98, while this one has no register number on that list.[^1]

A Forest Lookouts list of Maine towers files a Peaked Mountain under Washington County in the Maine Forestry District.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the summit today, or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

[^2]: MAINE - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/maine.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
