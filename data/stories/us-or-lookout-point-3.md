Lookout Point was a fire lookout on the Fremont National Forest, about 22 miles northeast of Chemult in Klamath County, Oregon.[^1][^2] The listing gives its elevation as 6,294 feet.[^1]

A listing dates the lookout only to the 1930s, and says it was abandoned.[^1] A national list of Oregon lookouts marks it as gone, with no date.[^2]

A separate directory of Oregon lookouts lists Lookout Point in Lane, Multnomah and Yamhill counties, but none in Klamath County. The Yamhill entry is marked removed.[^3] Anyone searching by name should check the county.

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
