Grapeland, Texas, lies in Houston County.[^2] It sits at the junction of U.S. Highway 287 and Farm Road 227, roughly twelve miles north of Crockett.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower at Grapeland as standing, at latitude 31.46928 and longitude -95.48055.[^1] That listing is undated and gives no builder, design, height or owner.[^1]

We found no source that says who built the Grapeland tower or when, which agency ran it, or whether anyone staffed it or watched fires from it. The Texas State Historical Association entry does not mention a lookout tower.[^3] Our web searches turned up no other reference to a lookout at Grapeland.

Today the listing is the only public record we found that the tower survives. We could not confirm its condition, its owner, or whether the public may visit. Do not climb it without the owner's permission.
