The Vernon Lookout Site is in Lamar County, Alabama.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register dates the tower to the early 1940s and says it was removed around 2000.[^1]

The register gives two heights for the tower.[^1] The state list calls it a 100-foot tower, while a local forestry office told the register it was 120 feet.[^1] The photograph on the register page is courtesy of Andrew Zerbe, who also nominated the site when it was added to the register on November 27, 2016, as US 1632, AL 9.[^1]

The register places the tower roughly where a water tank now stands.[^1] To make room for the tank, the tower's foundations were dug up, but the register says remains can be found at the end of the lot beside it.[^1] The lot is administered by Lamar County, so anyone planning a visit should ask the county about access first.[^1] No other source we read describes the site today.

[^1]: Vernon Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/al/vernon-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
