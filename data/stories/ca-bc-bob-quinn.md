Canada Lookouts lists a Bob Quinn lookout in the Kitimat-Stikine area at latitude 56.956 and longitude 130.215, with an elevation of 1,569 m (5,148 ft).[^1] The Wikipedia article on Bob Quinn Lake gives that lake's position as 56.97222°N 130.24722°W, a few kilometres from those coordinates.[^2]

No source I read says what remains at the site today.
