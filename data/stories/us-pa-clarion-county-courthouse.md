Construction on the Clarion County Courthouse ran from 1883 to 1885, after a fire destroyed the second courthouse on the site in September 1882.[^2] The two earlier courthouses on that spot were both brick buildings, and fire destroyed each one.[^2] The present building is a variation of the Queen Anne style, and the county's history gives the height from the ground to the top of the tower figure as 213 feet (65 m).[^3]

In October 1920 a newspaper reported that the courthouse tower would be used as a lookout post for forest fires that autumn, run by Forester Zerby, and that county officials had granted permission.[^1] No later source describes the lookout.

The National Register of Historic Places listed the courthouse in 1979.[^2][^5] A renovation that started in the summer of 2001 ended up costing almost $1.9 million and five months more than planned, once serious deterioration turned up in the clock tower.[^3]

In April 2024 the county was getting ready to clear the courthouse out for a major renovation.[^4] According to a historical marker, the Lady of Justice still stands on the tower's spire.[^5] No source describes whether the tower itself can be visited.
