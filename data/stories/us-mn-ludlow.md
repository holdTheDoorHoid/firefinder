Ludlow was a forest service lookout in Beltrami County, on Ludlow Island.[^1][^2] A Moorhead newspaper placed the island ten miles north of Red Lake Falls.[^1]

Survey notes from 1934 describe a 70-foot fire tower at the site, but the county list gives no build date for it.[^2][^3]

In September 1931 fires burned near Ludlow.[^1] The Moorhead paper reported that more than 25 settlers had been burned out of their homes, and that the forestry service tower and station on the island were completely destroyed.[^1] The county list is less certain. By the time of a 1954 survey, it says, the 70-foot tower had been taken down, and it adds that wildfire may have destroyed it.[^2][^3]

The accounts do not fit together neatly. A 1934 survey describes a 70-foot tower three years after the newspaper said the tower was destroyed. Either a tower was rebuilt or the newspaper overstated the loss, and the sources do not say which.[^1][^2]

The county list calls a later ladder tower the second at the site, Fire Tower 86, with a height of 81 feet. It gives no date for that tower.[^2] A 1966 survey gave its height as about 85 feet and described a steel frame with an observation room on top.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association marks the Ludlow tower as gone, with no date.[^4] The 1966 survey is the latest description we found. It recorded footings and octagonal cab remnants at the site.[^2] No source describes the site today or says whether visitors may go there.
