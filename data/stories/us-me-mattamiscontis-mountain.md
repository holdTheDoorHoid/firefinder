The Mattamiscontis Mountain lookout stood on a 1,400-foot mountain in Penobscot County, Maine.[^1] A wooden tower went up there in 1914.[^1] In 1917 the wooden tower was replaced by a 48-foot steel tower, and the watchman that year was John Stinchfield.[^1] A 1919 panoramic map from the Maine Forestry Service, centered on the tower, places it in Township 3, Range 9.[^2]

The site stopped working as a lookout in 1968.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site on September 15, 2008, on the nomination of Henry Isenberg.[^3] Its page records that the tower is gone.[^3]

The register's state list gives coordinates for the site, but the site's own page says none are available, so treat those numbers as rough.[^3][^4]

The register does not say what, if anything, remains at the site today, and we found no other source that does. We also could not confirm whether the public can reach it.[^3]
