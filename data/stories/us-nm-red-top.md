Red Top is in Sandoval County, on the Cuba Ranger District of the Santa Fe National Forest.[^1][^2] The lookout there began as a 14-foot-square, wood-frame house of the L-4 type, built in 1933 according to a 1989 Forest Service survey.[^2]

The survey says a trailer apparently replaced the house sometime in the 1960s, and it does not know when the trailer was placed.[^2] In 1989 the survey called that trailer the only trailer lookout in use on the Santa Fe National Forest, and it did not recommend the trailer for National Register eligibility.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's New Mexico list marks the site as gone.[^1] It codes the site as a ground site and does not mention the trailer that the 1989 survey describes there.[^1][^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Forest Fire Lookouts - New Mexico (by Ron Kemnow), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Lookouts in the Southwestern Region, USDA Forest Service Southwestern Region, Cultural Resources Management Report No. 8 (1989), https://archive.org/download/CAT10400086/CAT10400086.pdf (accessed 2026-10-10).
