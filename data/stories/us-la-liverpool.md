The Liverpool Fire Tower is in St. Helena Parish, Louisiana.[^1][^2] The Louisiana list gives its location as Greensburg, the parish seat, and places the tower at about 30.926 degrees north and 90.729 degrees west.[^1][^2]

Louisiana's first steel fire tower dates from 1922 and was put up near Bogalusa. By 1943 the state had 39 fire towers.[^1] The sources we read do not say when the Liverpool tower was built, who built it, what design it has or who staffed it.

The list describes the tower as still standing and in active use.[^1] It also notes that the tower can be seen on Google Street View.[^1]

We found no public access information for this tower. Because the list calls it active, please do not climb it, and check with local officials before you plan a visit.
