Pecan City sits in Dougherty County, Georgia, as an unincorporated settlement, and its name reflects its setting in pecan country.[^1] A compiled list of Georgia lookout towers, kept on tnlandforms.us, places a Pecan City tower at 31.50877, -84.05174 and gives its elevation as 255, with no unit stated.[^2] A second Georgia list, the Eastern US Lookouts page, also has a Pecan City entry in Dougherty County.[^3]

That page's entry for Pretoria, another Dougherty County name, describes a tower "recently erected" in March 1931 by the Pine Island Timber Protective Organization near Albany, Georgia. The entry gives its height as 60 feet and places it on a hill 20 feet high.[^3] The page's own note adds that this may be the tower from Pecan City, so the two may be the same structure.[^3]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything remains or whether the public can reach it.

The National Historic Lookout Register and the Former Fire Lookout Sites register do not list a Pecan City entry.[^4][^5]
