The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers places a tower named Ellaville in Schley County, at 32.24916, -84.33286, with an elevation of 581 (the list shows no unit).[^1] The list gives it GFC number 323, a code that points to an older Georgia Forestry Commission list covering 203 towers.[^1] It also carries the reference BLL-41111.[^1] The compiler says names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own creation, so "Ellaville" is a label rather than a documented name for the tower.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public. The Forestry Commission's county contact page lists no office for Schley County and points readers to Sumter County instead.[^2] Check with the Forestry Commission before visiting, and do not climb a tower whose access you have not confirmed.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers (GA tower list with coordinates, elevation and GFC number), tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: County Contacts, Georgia Forestry Commission, https://gatrees.org/gfc-contacts-2/county-contacts/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
