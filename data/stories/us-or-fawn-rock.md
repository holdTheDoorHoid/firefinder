Fawn Rock was a Forest Service lookout site in the Willamette National Forest, in Lane County, Oregon, about 13 miles southwest of McKenzie Bridge.[^1][^2] Its elevation is about 3,045 feet.[^1][^2]

The shelter went up in 1930.[^1][^2] The register describes a roofed shelter with open sides and a firefinder on top.[^1][^2] A separate Oregon lookout history calls the instrument an alidade, and says the shelter was used as an emergency lookout.[^3]

The sources agree that the lookout ended in 1970, but they differ on how. The register and the Oregon history say it was removed, while firelookout.com says it was destroyed.[^1][^2][^3] The register added the site to its list of former lookouts on April 25, 2011.[^1]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. Nothing we read says whether the site is open to visitors, so check with the Willamette National Forest before you plan a trip.
