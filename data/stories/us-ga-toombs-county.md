Tom Dunigan's index of Georgia fire towers lists a Toombs County Lookout Tower in Toombs County, at 32.15602, -82.30735.[^1] The index gives its register numbers as BLL-64704 and gfc-512.[^1] It explains that the Georgia Forestry Commission provided coordinates for 118 towers under BLL numbers, and that an older commission list described 203 towers under gfc numbers.[^1] The index says its first 72 towers are listed by the USGS name database. This tower is number 57 in that run, so the index did not write its name.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
