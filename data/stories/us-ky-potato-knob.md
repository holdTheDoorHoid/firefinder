Potato Knob is a summit in Carter County, on the Tygarts Valley topographic quadrangle. Its approximate elevation is 1,102 feet (336 m).[^2]

The Forest Lookouts compilation of eastern U.S. lookouts has a page for Potato Knob under Carter County, but the page gives only the name and the county.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains at the summit today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^2]: Potato Knob in Carter County KY (Tygarts Valley Area), MountainZone, https://www.mountainzone.com/mountains/kentucky/carter-ky/summits/potato-knob-6/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
