The Narraguinnep tower stood on Narraguinnep Mountain in Dolores County, in the San Juan National Forest.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the site's elevation as 9,031 feet (2,753 metres). It files the tower as US 538, CO 10, recorded the site on September 18, 2009, and says the tower no longer stands.[^1]

A western lookouts website files the same peak under the name Narraguinnep Mountain and marks the tower as removed.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Colorado list also marks it as removed.[^3]

No source I could reach describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
