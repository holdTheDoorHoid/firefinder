Sheep Mountain sits in the Clearwater National Forest, about 35 miles northeast of Orofino in Clearwater County, Idaho.[^1][^2] Its elevation is 5,708 feet.[^1][^2] The site began with a camp in 1914, and a cabin with an elevated platform followed in 1917.[^2][^3]

In 1930 an L-6 tower with a log cabin for living quarters replaced that cabin.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts credits Everete Van Arsdale with 22 years on the lookout, though it gives no exact dates for that service.[^1] The page cites the book Fire Lookouts of the Northwest for that figure.[^1]

By 1950 the place had fallen into ruin, a date the Forest Service list also gives.[^2][^3]

Today Idaho Fire Lookouts describes the place as drive-up ruins.[^1] None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, still stands on the summit, or whether the site is open to visitors.
