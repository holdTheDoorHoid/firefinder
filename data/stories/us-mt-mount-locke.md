Mount Locke sits about nine miles northeast of Fortine, in Flathead and Lincoln counties.[^1] Forest Lookouts records a 1929 lookout there, a 20-foot platform tower with a log cabin.[^1] The same page gives the peak an elevation of 7205 feet.[^1]

The same record says the lookout burned in a forest fire.[^1]

Visit Montana describes the Krinklehorn-Locke Mountain Trail, a 2.0-mile National Forest trail, and says it leads to the historic site of Locke Lookout.[^2] The trail begins at Trail 358 and connects the Krinklehorn Trail with the Whitefish Divide Trail.[^2] That listing calls the place a historic site and does not say whether anything remains.[^2] Forest Lookouts places the lookout in the Flathead National Forest, while the trail listing points to the Kootenai National Forest map.[^1][^2]
