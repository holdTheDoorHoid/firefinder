The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list places a Geneva lookout in Sabine County, at 31.49366 north and 93.91890 west, and gives its structure type as unknown.[^1] The list marks the lookout as gone.[^1]

The association's expanded Texas inventory gives 1934 as the date and about 1938 as the removal year. It lists the manufacturer and height as unknown.[^2]

Today the list marks Geneva as gone. No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site.[^1]

[^1]: Texas Lookouts (By County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/tx-co/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Texas lookout inventory, expanded list (PDF), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/TX-expanded.pdf (accessed 2026-10-21).
