Alida Hill is in Clearwater County, about two miles northeast of Alida.[^1] A 1938 federal survey description places the station at a former fire-tower site on state-owned land.[^1] The survey station sits on the highest point of a northeast-southwest ridge, and its survey mark projects about six inches.[^1] The 1938 directions end with a trail about a quarter mile long that leads from the road to the ridge top.[^1]

The tower was already gone by then.[^1] A Clearwater County list, quoting 1938 survey notes, says the tower was dismantled, the state lease was canceled, and the site is now county forest land.[^2][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list marks Alida Hill as gone.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
