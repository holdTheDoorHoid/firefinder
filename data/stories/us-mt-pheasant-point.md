Pheasant Point was a crow's nest on the Kootenai National Forest in Lincoln County, Montana.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com places it 12 miles south-southeast of Troy, at an elevation of 3,930 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list gives its coordinates as 48.29973 north and 115.82259 west.[^2]

The listing dates the crow's nest to 1935 and places it two miles northeast of Bull Lake.[^1] Early in the twentieth century, homesteaders moved into the Bull Lake area.[^3] By the early 1920s the Bull Lake area had turned into a favored getaway for people living in Troy and Libby.[^3]

Both listings mark the lookout as Abandoned.[^1][^2] Neither source describes what remains at the site today or whether the public can reach it, so check with the Kootenai National Forest before you set out.[^1][^2]
