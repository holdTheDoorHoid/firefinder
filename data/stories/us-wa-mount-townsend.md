A lookout cab went up on the summit of Mount Townsend in 1933.[^1][^3] It was an L-4 cab. HistoryLink describes that plan as gabled and shingled, with thick shutters and no cupola.[^1][^4] WillhiteWeb places it on the summit's lower north end.[^3]

WillhiteWeb says W.B. Osborne took panoramic photographs there on August 27, 1935.[^3]

A USGS field party recorded the cab as abandoned in 1955.[^3] The sources agree on 1962 but not on how the cab ended: the register says it was removed that year,[^1] while firelookout.com and WillhiteWeb say it was destroyed.[^2][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the summit today. The peak lies in Olympic National Forest.[^2] WillhiteWeb calls it one of the more popular hiking spots, with several easy trails to the top.[^3] That guide gives the route as 4 miles one way.[^3] We did not confirm current trail or road conditions.
