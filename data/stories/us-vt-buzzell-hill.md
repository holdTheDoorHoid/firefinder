A newspaper notice dated August 26, 1942, quoted by the Eastern U.S. lookout directory, says a fire tower was under construction on Buzzell Mountain.[^1] The notice ran in the Caledonian Record and called the site Buzzell Mountain.[^1]

The directories list the site as Buzzell Hill, and they disagree about the county. The Forest Fire Lookout Association places it in Essex County, at coordinates 44.57805, -71.76928.[^2] The Eastern U.S. lookout directory lists it in Caledonia County.[^1]

Both directories mark the tower as gone or removed.[^1][^2] Statewide, Northern Woodlands reports that Vermont was the first state to shut down all of its fire towers, and that its final working tower closed after summer 1985.[^3]

No source describes what remains on the hill today, and none says whether the hill is open to visitors.
