The Coney Peak lookout stood in the Lolo National Forest, six miles northwest of Hot Springs in Sanders County, at about 6,100 feet.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com describes a 30-foot L-4 tower there, dated to 1934.[^1]

Firelookout.com dates the tower's destruction to 1952.[^1] Forest Lookouts (Montana) lists the site under Lolo National Forest and gives its status as "Removed," with no date.[^2] The two pages differ on how the tower ended, and only one of them gives a year.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the site at 47.68268, -114.76832, files it among its Sanders County entries, and lists the tower as gone.[^3]

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today or whether the site is open to the public.
