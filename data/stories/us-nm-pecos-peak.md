Pecos Peak was a lookout tower in San Miguel County, New Mexico, at about 35.57 N, 105.63 W.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone, with no register number.[^1][^2] Barillas, also in San Miguel County, is listed as a standing tower.[^2]

No source we checked describes what remains at the Pecos Peak site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: New Mexico Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: New Mexico Lookouts (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/nm-co/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
