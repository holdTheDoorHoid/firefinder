Skookum Mountain is a former fire lookout site in Wasco County, Oregon, at about 44.91 N, 121.19 W.[^2] Do not confuse it with Skookum Butte, a different lookout site in Klamath County.[^2]

Firelookout.com, the only history page we found, has a single 1930s entry with no details and marks the site abandoned.[^1] A statewide index lists the structure type as unknown and the site as gone.[^2] We found no source that describes what remains here today, or whether the mountain is open to visitors.

[^1]: Skookum Mtn. Fire Lookout, Firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/or/skookummtn.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Lookouts of Oregon (list), Firelookout.org, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/or/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
