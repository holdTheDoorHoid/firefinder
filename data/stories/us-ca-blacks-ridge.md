Blacks Ridge Lookout stands in Lassen County, in the Lassen National Forest, which the U.S. Forest Service administers.[^1][^2]

The Register says the tower was built in 1934. It describes an Aermotor, a 45-foot X-brace open steel tower topped by a 7-by-7-foot cab meant for observation only.[^1] In 1938 a California Region 5 Plan BC-201 Fireman Lookout cabin was built as a residence for the lookout operator, and the Register's 2008 photo is captioned as the cabin and tower together.[^1]

Our second source is thin. The Forest Lookouts page gives the forest and a location code, 34N-7E-3, but no history.[^2]

The Register recorded the lookout on November 27, 2019.[^1] The sources read do not say whether it is staffed, rented, or open to visitors, and we found no later record of its condition.[^1][^2] Check with the Lassen National Forest before any trip, and do not climb the tower without first confirming its status.
