The tower at Agnew Lake, Espanola, was erected in 1931 as a guyed steel tower 24 m (80 ft) tall, according to the Canada Lookouts website, which files the lookout under the name Baldwin.[^1]

In 1966 a self-supporting steel tower, 30.5 m (100 ft) high, took the original's place.[^1]

A list of still-standing Ontario towers names the Baldwin Tower at Agnew Lake, Espanola.[^2] The same list says that, as of 2013, the Ontario Ministry of Natural Resources had decided to take down many of the towers that were still standing.[^2] It also says the ministry no longer maintains them and asks people not to climb them.[^2]

No source I could read describes the Baldwin site today, so this story cannot say what remains there or what a visitor would find.
