Camel's Hump is a former lookout site in Nevada County, on the Tahoe National Forest, at 3,548 feet.[^1]

The register's description dates construction to 1938 and gives the design as California Region 5 Plan BC-301, on an eight-foot enclosed timber tower.[^1] Its build-date field says circa 1939, so the two entries differ by a year.[^1]

In October 1950 the Nevada State Journal reported that Dave Medford was posted at the lookout for the U.S. Forest Service.[^2]

The register gives no removal date, but says some ruins remain at the site.[^1] A photograph on that page, captioned 1963, shows the lookout and is credited to the Forest Service.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the lookout as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes those ruins in their current state, or says whether the site is open to visitors.[^1]
