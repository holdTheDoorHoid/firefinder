Sunset Peak was a fire lookout in Missoula County, Montana, 14 miles northwest of Seeley Lake.[^1] A statewide list of Montana lookouts gives its coordinates as 47.31803, -113.70171 and marks the tower as gone.[^2]

The listing dates the lookout to 1944 and describes a 20-foot L-4 tower.[^1] The L-4 was a 14-by-14-foot frame cab that served as living quarters and could sit on top of a tall fire tower.[^3] The same listing places the site in the Lolo/Flathead National Forests and records the tower as gone by 1955.[^1]

Another Sunset Peak lookout stood in Mineral County, 8 miles southwest of St. Regis.[^4] That tower is listed as a 20-foot pole L-4 tower dated 1934 and destroyed in 1959, so the two are separate sites.[^4]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
