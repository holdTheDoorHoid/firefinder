Pirate Hill is in Washington County, Maine.[^1] Maine State Archives records place its fire tower in Township 11, Range 3.[^1][^2]

The Maine Forestry Service produced two 360-degree panoramic maps centered on the Pirate Hill fire tower, one dated 1925 and the other 1929.[^1][^2] The 1925 map is already centered on the tower, so the tower stood by then.[^1] Each map measures 30 by 30 inches.[^1][^2] Both maps are filed in the State Archives' collection titled Fire Tower Maps, Maine Forest Service.[^1][^2]

The association's Maine list marks the tower as gone and places it at 45.55547, -67.54315.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains on the hill today, or whether the site is open to the public. Check with the landowner before visiting.
