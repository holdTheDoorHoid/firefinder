Glassy Mountain lies near Pickens, South Carolina, and is an isolated monadnock whose summit stands 1,703 feet (519 m) high.[^2] A lookout-history website lists a South Carolina Forestry Commission fire tower on it, in Pickens County.[^1]

In 1990, the previous owners sold it to the South Carolina Heritage Trust Program, which made it a heritage preserve.[^2] Statewide, the commission shut down its fire tower network on October 1, 1993.[^3]

A student newspaper article from October 2023 places an abandoned fire tower close to the summit parking.[^4] The same article describes a trailhead just past the turn to Glassy Mountain Baptist Church, with just under a mile of trail to the top.[^4]

Wikipedia also says the summit is easy to reach, since a small road serving transmitter towers goes up to it.[^2] No source I read says whether the tower may be climbed, so this story does not suggest it.[^4]
