Agatha Creek Lookout stood on a ridgetop in Shoshone County, Idaho, in the St. Joe National Forest.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places it about 12 miles west-northwest of Avery, at an elevation of 3,900 feet.[^1]

It was a log cabin rather than a tower.[^1][^4] The cabin went up in 1925 on the ridge between Agatha and Black Prince Creeks.[^1][^2][^3] Its patrol point lay two miles north, atop Drive Point.[^1][^2]

The cabin was abandoned in the 1930s.[^1][^2][^3] The association's list of Idaho lookouts classes the site as a ground site and marks it gone.[^4]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains on the ridge today.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page calls the site a hike-in, but it does not say whether the land is open to visitors.[^2]
