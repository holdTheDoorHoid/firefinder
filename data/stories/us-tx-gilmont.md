The Texas Forest Service fire lookout tower at Gilmont was about five miles northeast of Gilmer, in Upshur County.[^1]

In September 1960 the Gilmer Mirror reported that a new radio relay system was being installed at the tower. It was meant to link Gilmont with the towers at Hallsville and Negley and with the Forest Service district headquarters at Linden.[^1] Quick radio contact let crews pin down a fire from its smoke and get equipment moving fast.[^1] Crew leader Carl Stewart said the system would be ready by January.[^1]

In September 1965 the same paper reported that two fires had been spotted on August 17 in the northeast of the county.[^1] A later report by Stewart, then a Texas Forest Service crew leader, suggested malicious mischief might have caused them.[^1] The clipping also called him the person in charge of the Gilmont tower.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas index lists the tower as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
