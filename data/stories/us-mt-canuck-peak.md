Canuck Peak is a former fire lookout in Lincoln County, Montana, about 15 miles northwest of Yaak, in the Kootenai National Forest.[^1] The site listing gives its elevation as 6,934 feet.[^1]

The listing, which is titled Canuck Mountain, dates a 30-foot pole tower and a log cabin on the peak to 1921, and says the lookout was abandoned.[^1] A second firelookout.com page, under the name Canuck Peak, repeats those details.[^2] The Forest Lookouts list of Lincoln County towers gives the land description as township 37 North, range 34 West, section 34.[^4]

The same listing says the log cabin is still there, in ruins.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list, though, gives the status as gone.[^3] The two sources disagree about whether anything is left.

No source we read describes the ruins in any detail today, or says whether the public can reach the site.[^1][^3]
