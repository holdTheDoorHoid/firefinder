The Oak Park Lookout Tower was a fire tower in Emanuel County, Georgia.[^1] A statewide list of lookouts gives its elevation as 237 feet and tags it gfc-111.[^1] A separate Georgia lookout list on the Eastern US Lookouts site files the Oak Park entry under Emanuel County and the Georgia Forestry Commission.[^3]

The tower did not stay at Oak Park. The statewide list has a separate Flat Creek Lodge entry, at its own coordinates, marked "moved from oak park."[^1] A 2007 Georgia Trend article places the tower at that hunting and fishing lodge. It places the lodge in a 2,000-acre pine forest about nine miles from downtown Swainsboro, and it calls the tower "a decommissioned fire tower from Oak Park in Emanuel County."[^2]

The lodge's website now presents it as a guided quail-hunting property, and says it "offers professionally guided hunts."[^4] No source I found describes the tower at the lodge today, so it is not known whether it still stands there or whether visitors can see it. No source describes the original Oak Park site now, either.

The lodge takes bookings, so call ahead before any trip, and do not climb or approach the tower without the owners' permission.[^4]
