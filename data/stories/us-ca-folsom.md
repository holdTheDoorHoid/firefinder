Folsom Lookout's cabin sat atop a 30-foot enclosed timber tower, in Calaveras County, California, within the Stanislaus National Forest.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes the cabin as a California Region 5 Plan BC-301 design.[^1]

The register dates the build to 1934.[^1] It gives the site's elevation as 4,990 feet, or 1,521 meters, but the same page also gives 4,855 feet for the Calaveras Ranger District.[^1] We did not reconcile the two figures.[^1]

The register says the lookout was most likely a joint effort with the State of California.[^1] The photo captions in the register do not all agree with its build date.[^1] One image is captioned 1920, which is earlier than the 1934 build date, and a second is captioned 1937.[^1]

The register added the site on December 29, 2019, with Brad Eells as nominator, under the numbers US 1880, CA 75.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the enclosed tower as gone.[^3] The register leaves the removal date unknown and says nothing is left at the site.[^1]

No source we read says whether the site can be visited.[^1] The register names the Stanislaus National Forest as administrator, so ask there before planning any trip.[^1]
