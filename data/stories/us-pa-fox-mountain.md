The Forest Lookouts site lists a 20-foot wood tower built on Fox Mountain in 1916, and a 60-foot Aermotor erected there in 1920.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register places the site in Potter County and describes a steel Aermotor tower built in 1916.[^1] The two sources disagree on what was built that year.

The register says the Aermotor tower was removed in 1987.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page also gives 1987 as the removal year, but it does not say which tower that was.[^2]

Fox Mountain has no fire tower now.[^3] A hiking write-up says the summit clearing may mark where the old tower stood.[^3] The same write-up describes a gated forest road from East Cowley Road, a 2.5-mile ascent, then a 0.7-mile spur to the summit.[^3] The writer also reports that logging was in progress on the day of the hike.[^3] The sources we read do not say whether the gate is open to the public.

[^2]: Fox Mountain, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/fox-mountain1.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Fox Mountain Fire Tower - Austin, PA, MyHikes, https://myhikes.org/trails/fox-mountain-fire-tower (accessed 2026-10-10).
