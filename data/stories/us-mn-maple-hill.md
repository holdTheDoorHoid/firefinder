Maple Hill was a fire lookout in St. Louis County, Minnesota.[^1][^2] A St. Louis County table of fire lookout towers dates it to 1929, gives its owner as the code "Mn MFS," and classes it as a ladder tower.[^1] The table's height column reads 85, but a 1977 survey note calls the tower about 60 feet tall, so the two figures disagree.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list marks the tower as gone.[^3] According to the county table, a communication tower now occupies the site.[^1] The Central US Lookouts page gives its location as 57N-21W-36, a land-survey reference.[^2]

Neither the county table nor the Forest Fire Lookout Association describes a lookout still standing on the hill.[^1][^3]
