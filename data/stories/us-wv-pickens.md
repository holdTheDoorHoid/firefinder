The Former Fire Lookout Sites record calls this the Pickens Tower Site, registry number US 1645, WV 36, in Webster County, West Virginia.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's West Virginia list gives the other name Holly River (Pickens) for the same site.[^2] The record names the West Virginia Conservation Commission as administrator and puts the site at 2,761 feet (842 metres) above sea level.[^1]

For context, the West Virginia Encyclopedia says roughly 100 lookout towers, managed by federal and state forestry agencies, once stood across the state, and that the state's forest fire towers stopped operating in 1990.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the Holly River (Pickens) site as gone.[^2]

No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public.[^2]
