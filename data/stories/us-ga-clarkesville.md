A Georgia lookout list places a Clarkesville Lookout Tower in Habersham County.[^1] The list gives its elevation as 1,474 with no unit; if that figure is in feet, the site sits about 449 m above sea level.[^1] A second Georgia lookout list also names a Clarkesville lookout in Habersham County, with no other detail.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: GEORGIA - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/georgia.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
