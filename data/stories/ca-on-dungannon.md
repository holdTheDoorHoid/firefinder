The Dungannon fire tower site is near Bancroft in Ontario, at an elevation of 477 m (1,565 ft).[^1]

The Tweed Fire District's tower notes list a Dungannon tower among those built in 1931. It was a light steel tower of 24.4 m (80 ft), moved to the site from Faraday.[^2] The same notes list a Tudor tower, also a 24.4 m (80 ft) light steel tower, that was moved from Dungannon, among towers built in 1938.[^2]

A Department of Lands and Forests tower detection map from 1963 instead shows a 30.5 m (100 ft) steel tower at this location, according to the Firelookout.ca record.[^1] The Tweed notes name no replacement for the Dungannon site, so the two sources do not match.

The Firelookout.ca record says the site appears on a 1977 topographic map, and that the current (1996) mapsheet shows a communications tower there.[^1] No source confirms what stands at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1]

[^1]: Dungannon Fire Tower, Firelookout.ca (Open Canada Fire Lookout Index), https://firelookout.ca/lookouts/?slug=dungannon-fire-tower (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Ontario Fire Towers, tower history notes for the Tweed and Lindsay fire districts, GeoCities archive, https://www.geocities.ws/ontarioftl/oldtowers2.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
