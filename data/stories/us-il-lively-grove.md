The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Illinois list places a tower called Lively Grove in Washington County.[^2] The name also belongs to an unincorporated community on Illinois Route 153 that lies within Johannisburg and Lively Grove townships.[^3]

The sources say little about the tower itself. A Southern Illinoisan notice dated September 12, 1975, sought bids to tear the tower down.[^1]

A May 2021 Illinois Country Living feature says nearly all of the state's towers were removed or sold in the early 1970s.[^4] That puts this 1975 demolition notice somewhat later than the general pattern the article describes.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the spot can be visited.
