B.X. Mountain sits in the North Okanagan of British Columbia.[^2] A 1913 provincial report places the lookout about 29 km (18 mi) out of Vernon.[^1] A Daily Colonist report from October 1913 gives the telephone line from Vernon to the lookout as about 31 km (19 mi).[^1] The two figures differ, and the sources do not say why.[^1]

In 1913 a lookout station was established on the mountain, and the Forest Branch built the telephone lines that linked it to Vernon.[^1] The mountain sits on the watershed of the Vernon City, Armstrong City and Coldstream Municipal domestic supply systems, and from the site the station overlooked the White Valley irrigation reservoir and watershed.[^1]

A 1914 list of new work completed in the Vernon District names a "B.X. Look-out Cabin."[^1] In 1924 the lookout already there was significantly upgraded.[^1] In 1926 the old lookout gave way to a new standard structure costing $1,719.[^1]

Today the firelookout.ca record gives no structure height or build year for the tower.[^2] No source I found describes the site now, or says whether the public can reach it.
