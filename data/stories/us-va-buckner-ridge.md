Buckner Ridge was a fire lookout tower in Scott County, Virginia.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a tower that is gone.[^2] Mapcarta still carries a point named Buckner Ridge Lookout Tower in Scott County, and places the community of Fort Blackmore about 2.5 miles south of it.[^3] A map point is not evidence that a structure stands there.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains on the ridge today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]

[^2]: Virginia lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Buckner Ridge Lookout Tower, Mapcarta, https://mapcarta.com/21845052 (accessed 2026-10-10).
