The Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources administers the Squirrel Hill lookout, an 84-foot tower of the state's standard design near Minocqua Winter Park in Oneida County, with an outside ladder the register describes as protected.[^1] The register gives its construction as September 1939 and lists the site at 1,730 feet.[^1] Wikipedia calls Squirrel Hill the highest point in Minocqua, at 1,735 feet.[^4]

The sources disagree about the start date. A December 1934 item in the Oshkosh Daily Northwestern describes two young deer hunters who had been lost since Friday and were found after a night in an unused fire tower at Squirrel Hill, west of Minocqua.[^2] If that tower is this one, either the 1939 date is wrong or an earlier tower stood on the hill before the 1939 structure.[^2] The sources I checked do not settle which.

The register accepted the tower on June 9, 2017, on a nomination by Tyler Bormann.[^1] A Wisconsin lookout list still shows it as standing.[^3]

A 2015 Associated Press report said dozens of the state's forest-fire towers, most dating to the 1930s and 1940s, were being decommissioned. The part of that article I could read does not name this tower.[^5]

Today I found no source about staffing, notable fires, or whether the public may climb or rent the tower. Check with the Wisconsin DNR before any visit, and do not go up a tower that is closed to the public.
