Hardscrabble Knob is a former lookout site in Augusta County, Virginia, in the George Washington National Forest.[^1] The U.S. Forest Service built and used the tower there.[^1] The site sits at 4,290 feet (1,308 m).[^1]

The design is unusual, and the register cannot settle it. It gives two possibilities, an Aeromotor windmill tower or a CAA aircraft beacon tower, and it calls the 6-by-6-foot cab unique.[^1] The register also says that, as far as it knows, no other fire tower has this design.[^1]

The site was registered on June 9, 2015, on the nomination of Renick Proffitt.[^1] The Forest Lookouts list labels the site "Ruins."[^2]

Today only the remains of the tower are on the ground, and a lookout cabin nearby is still partly standing.[^1] No source we read describes whether the public may visit the site.
