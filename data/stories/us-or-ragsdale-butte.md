Ragsdale Butte is in Jackson County, Oregon, at 1,447 meters (4,748 feet).[^1]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says a rag camp went up on the north summit in 1933 and was replaced in 1939 by an L-4 cab.[^1][^2] A September 1933 Roseburg paper said the CCC was to build a 10-by-10 lookout before snow fell.[^3] The register calls the 1933 structure a rag camp, and firelookout.com calls it a camp.[^1][^2]

In May 1934 the Medford Mail Tribune reported that the Rogue River National Forest's road superintendent had a road crew at work building the road to what it called Ragsdale Peak lookout.[^3]

In May 1941 the Medford Mail Tribune reported that Bob Mooney had been given the job of lookout man at the Ragsdale Butte station and would start within days.[^3]

The sources name different agencies.[^1][^3] The register lists the Oregon Department of Forestry as administrator.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page heads its entry "Umpqua National Forest," and the 1934 road item refers to the Rogue River National Forest.[^3]

The register says the L-4 cab was removed around 1962.[^1] The firelookout.com page says it was destroyed in 1962.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page says the Forest Service burned the lookout in 1963.[^3] The sources disagree on the year and on whether it was taken down or burned.[^1][^2][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left at the site today, and none says whether the public can visit. The register includes a 2007 photo labelled as the lookout site, with no further description.[^1] Ask the Oregon Department of Forestry before going.
