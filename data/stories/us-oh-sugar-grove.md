A March 1937 newspaper item, copied on the Forest Lookouts page, says the Sugar Grove tower was built the year before at 100 feet and completed a chain of 19 towers guarding southern Ohio's woods.[^1] In 2015 it stood on Fire Tower Road in Sugar Grove.[^3]

The National Historic Lookout Register calls it a 110-foot high steel tower.[^2] The 1937 and 1958 items both say 100 feet, so the height is in dispute.[^1]

A towerman was put in charge when the tower went into service.[^1] A 1939 item says a fire tower watchman there sighted smoke in Blue Valley, and a 1941 item says the watchman reported a forest blaze near Enterprise that burned over 100 acres.[^1] A 1963 item, filed under Sugar Grove, describes a watchwoman in a 7-by-7-foot room atop a fire tower in Berne Township.[^1] The 2017 report places this tower in Berne Township, so the 1963 item probably concerns it.[^4]

The tower's title changed hands in 1977. A newspaper item from that year says the tower had outlived its use, that its title passed from one state agency to another, and that the college now holds it.[^1]

In 2014 the register said efforts to move and restore the tower were ongoing.[^2] A 2015 Ohio chapter report said a plan to move it to Charles Alley Memorial Park stalled after the Lancaster parks superintendent left.[^3]

In 2017 fundraising began for a plan to take the tower apart by section and rebuild it at Alley Park.[^4] A 2017 newspaper item quoted on the Forest Lookouts page says the steel was rusting and some stairs were missing or rotten.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Ohio list still shows it as standing.[^5] I found no source from after 2017 confirming whether the move happened, so its current location is unconfirmed. When last described, the tower stood on private land, so do not visit or climb it without the owner's permission.[^4]
