Monture Hill Lookout, also called Montour Mountain, stands in Powell County, Montana, at about 4,600 feet.[^1][^2][^3] The present structure is a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2]

The first lookout on the site had an open-air cab on a 30-foot pole, and both the Register and firelookout.com date it to about 1935.[^1][^2] The replacement is where the sources part ways. The Register gives 1945 and firelookout.com gives 1941.[^1][^2] A Helena Independent item from April 1945, quoted by Forest Lookouts, describes a new tower under construction, 30 feet high with 14-by-14-foot living quarters.[^3] That item says the new tower would bring the total to five in a section covering about a million acres.[^3] It also places the tower seven miles southwest of Ovando, while firelookout.com gives the location as 16 miles southeast of Seeley Lake.[^2][^3]

The Forest Lookouts page also quotes a July 1921 item that does not name the mountain. It describes a Missoula National Forest fire assistant inspecting a lookout site, with a telephone line to the summit to be built next.[^3]

The builder is named differently in each source. The Register says the Blackfoot-Clearwater Timber Protective Association, firelookout.com says the Blackfeet Fire Protective Association, and the 1945 item says the Blackfoot Forest Protective association.[^1][^2][^3]

Both the Register and firelookout.com say the tower sat unused for many years.[^1][^2] The sources read name no fire it watched. The Register now describes the tower as abandoned, on private property with no public access.[^1]

The Register accepted the tower on April 13, 2022, as US 1553, MT 110, after a nomination by Kyle Stetler.[^1] Because the land is private and the Register reports no public access, visitors should not plan to go up it.[^1]
