The Lake Pierce tower stands in Polk County, Florida. The National Historic Lookout Register also knows it as the Hatchinea-Catfish Creek Fire Tower.[^1] It is a 100-foot Aermotor LS-40 with 15 landings.[^1]

The Florida Division of Forestry built the tower in 1939.[^1] The register's description says it "may still be operated as fire danger requires," but the entry gives no staffing history for the tower.[^1]

The register also records a prescribed burn in the area around the tower in the winter of 2008 to 2009.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register listed the tower on July 24, 2016, as US 1074, FL 14, with Robert Spear as nominator.[^1] The register names the Florida Department of Agriculture & Consumer Services as the agency that administers it.[^1]

Nothing reviewed says whether the public may enter the site or climb the tower.[^1] Until the agency confirms access, treat the tower as closed to climbing, and do not try to reach the top.

[^1]: Lake Pierce (Hatchinea-Catfish Creek) Fire Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/fl/lake-pierce-hatchinea-catfish-creek-fire-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-08).
