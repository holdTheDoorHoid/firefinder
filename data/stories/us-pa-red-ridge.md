The Red Ridge Tower Site is in Lycoming County, Pennsylvania.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it as US 442, PA 271.[^1] The register dates a wooden tower at the site to 1916, and says it has since come down.[^1]

The site was added to the register on September 16, 2008, on the nomination of Henry Isenberg.[^1] The register gives no coordinates for it.[^1]

The register lists the next entry as Red Ridge Point Tower Site (US 443).[^1] That is a separate numbered listing, so do not assume the two are the same place.[^1]

The register does not describe what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1] It says nothing about public access, so check with the land manager before going to look.[^1]

[^1]: Red Ridge Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/pa/red-ridge-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
