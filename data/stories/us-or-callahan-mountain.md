Callahan Mountain is in Douglas County, Oregon, about 16 miles east of Canyonville, on the Umpqua National Forest, at 2,914 feet.[^1] Forest Lookouts cites Forest Service files for a one-room, shake-sided lookout dwelling built there in 1922 for $34.07.[^2] Firelookout.com dates a 110-foot crow's nest at the site to 1930.[^1] Forest Lookouts later calls the structure a lookout tree.[^2]

In March 1934 the Roseburg News-Review reported that the Forest Service had sent an observer to Callahan during a spell of dry weather and low humidity.[^2] The paper placed the lookout about three miles from Tiller.[^2]

Firelookout.com says the lookout was abandoned in the 1950s and that the cabin half a mile away was destroyed in 1965.[^1] Forest Lookouts reports that in 2007 the lookout tree was still standing, although most of the improvements had disappeared.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Callahan Mountain as abandoned, not gone.[^3] No source we read describes the site after 2007, so what stands there now is not known. Do not climb the tree, and check with the Umpqua National Forest before you go.
