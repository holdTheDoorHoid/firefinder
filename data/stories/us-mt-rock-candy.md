The Rock Candy Lookout Site is in Lincoln County, Montana, in the Kootenai National Forest.[^1] The Forest Service administers it, and the register puts the site at 7,211 feet, or 2,198 meters.[^1] Firelookout.com places it about 12 miles west-northwest of Yaak.[^2]

According to the register, a log cabin put up in the 1920s was destroyed by a forest fire in 1931.[^1][^2] A 10-foot pole L-4 tower went up on the site in 1933.[^1][^2] By 1975 the tower had fallen into ruin.[^1][^2]

Rod Fosback nominated the site, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register recorded it on August 16, 2011.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a gone tower.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
