Spokane Mountain sits on the Spokane Indian Reservation in Stevens County, Washington, at about 3,870 feet.[^1][^3] The Bureau of Indian Affairs is listed as the administrator of the site.[^1] A D-6 cupola cabin went up on the mountain in 1934, and the register calls it one of a small number of surviving examples of its style.[^1][^2]

The cabin stopped being used in 1964.[^1] That year a steel tower went up on Wellpinit Mountain, and Willhite's trip report says the Wellpinit location made a better viewpoint and already had utilities.[^1][^2][^5]

The cabin burned in a forest fire in 2015.[^2][^3] A Forest Lookouts page also dates the burning to 2015.[^4] Willhite and TrailChick call the fire the Carpenter Road Fire, and TrailChick puts its size at 64,000 acres.[^2][^5] The register lists 2015 under "Removed," but its description says the lookout burned that year.[^1]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains of the cabin today. The trip report we read used photographs taken about a month before the fire, and it gives drive-up directions from West End Road on a dirt road.[^2] The sources do not say whether visitors may go up now. The land is administered by the Bureau of Indian Affairs, so check with the reservation before going.[^1]
