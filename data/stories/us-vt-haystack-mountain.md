In the spring of 1920, the Vermont Timberland Owners' Association, in cooperation with the Forestry Department, put up a tower and a cabin on Haystack Mountain in Wilmington, according to a history of Vermont forestry that the Forest Lookouts site quotes.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site in Windham County as an open tower, and marks it as gone.[^2]

A 1924 newspaper item puts the mountain at about 3,700 feet, while Wikipedia gives 3,445 feet, so the sources disagree on its height.[^1][^3]

Forest Lookouts marks the tower as removed, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone.[^1][^2]

Most of the mountain lies inside the Green Mountain National Forest, and walkers can reach the peak by public trails that start at the Chimney Hill development.[^3] No source read describes what, if anything, remains of the 1920 tower or cabin on the summit.[^1][^2][^3]
