The Whitehouse tower stood in Lucas County, in northwest Ohio, on land the Former Fire Lookout Sites register places in the Maumee State Forest.[^1][^2] The register lists the Ohio Department of Natural Resources as administrator and gives the elevation as 685 feet (209 meters). It entered the site on September 3, 2014, as US 1579, OH 25.[^1]

The state forest lies about three miles west of Whitehouse.[^2] It preserves some of the oak openings habitat that once covered much of northwest Ohio and southeast Michigan.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list also gives the site the alternate name White House (Oak Openings).[^3]

That list marks the tower as gone.[^3]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^3]
