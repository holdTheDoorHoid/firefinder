The National Historic Lookout Register lists the Brooks Tower in Conecuh County, Alabama, and says the Alabama Forestry Commission administers it.[^1] The Register says the commission replaced an original 100-foot wooden tower in 1962 with an Aermotor MC-39 tower that has a 7-by-7-foot cab.[^1] The height of the new tower is garbled in that same sentence, so this story does not take a height from it.[^1]

TopoQuest, a map and place-name site, also lists a Brooks Lookout Tower in Conecuh County.[^2] It gives the elevation as 380 feet above sea level, while the Register gives 375 feet (114 metres).[^1][^2] I could not tell which figure is right, so both are shown.

The Register names Thomas Kaufmann as the nominator and records the registration on January 3, 2011.[^1] Neither source says who staffed the tower, which fires it watched, or whether it was closed, moved or rebuilt after 1962.[^1][^2]

The Register page includes a drone video from another site, but neither source says what condition the tower is in today, or whether the public may visit or climb it.[^1][^2] Anyone planning a visit should check with the Alabama Forestry Commission first, and should not climb the tower without its permission.
