Sheldon Mountain rises a few miles north of Libby, in Lincoln County, Montana, within the Kootenai National Forest.[^1] The Fire Lookout site lists a camp in 1914, a D-6 cupola cabin in the 1920s, and an 8-by-8-foot frame cab in 1933.[^1]

The lookout was already described in print in 1930.[^2] A July 1930 item in The Western News, quoted on a Montana lookout site, calls it "the lookout on Sheldon mountain" and gives its elevation as 4,500 feet and its distance from Libby as four and a half miles.[^2] The Fire Lookout site gives the distance as three miles north of town and the elevation as 4,476 feet.[^1] We could not settle the distance from these sources.

The Fire Lookout site says the cab was abandoned in 1942.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks the site as gone.[^3]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
