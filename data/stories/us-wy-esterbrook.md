Esterbrook's fire lookout was a wooden tower in the Medicine Bow National Forest, about 20 miles south of Douglas, Wyoming.[^1] A June 1940 Casper Star-Tribune item, as quoted on a lookout history page, said a Civilian Conservation Corps camp would move to Esterbrook that summer and that its work would include finishing a lookout tower a mile east of the ranger station.[^2] The forest lookout listing describes the tower as a 30-foot wooden L-4 type.[^1]

The listing names Don March as lookout personnel from 1946 through 1953.[^1]

In August 1952, lightning briefly damaged the tower's radio and cut communications until a repair was made.[^2] The same newspaper report said the Laramie Peak division had five fires in four days.[^2]

The tower is now listed as gone, and one lookout history page says it was removed.[^2][^3] The sources reviewed do not say what, if anything, remains on Esterbrook Hill today or whether the hill is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
