Plumer Tower is in Cornplanter Township, Venango County, on State Game Lands No. 253.[^1][^2] A Pennsylvania Game Commission map describes that tract as about 665 acres.[^3] Mapcarta places the tower near Rouseville and Petroleum Center.[^1]

The storymap is the only source I could read that covers its history. It gives 1935 as the build year, credits the McClintock-Marshall Construction Company as builder, and lists the height as 80 feet.[^2] I found no second source for those figures.

A Pastmaps page for Rouseville names the tower among the landmarks in a modern survey that covers the area from Rouseville to Seneca.[^4] The National Historic Lookout Register lists 35 Pennsylvania lookouts, and Plumer is not one of them.[^5]

The storymap uses the present tense for the tower's height, which suggests it still stands.[^2] I found no recent inspection or visit report.

I found no staffing history. The storymap places the tower on game land.[^2] I could not find any access or climbing rule for it. Ask the Game Commission before you plan a visit, and do not climb the tower unless it is clearly open to the public.
