The Holly Springs lookout was in Ouachita County, Arkansas, about 2.5 miles south of the community of Holly Springs.[^1] A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description from 1941 calls it a lookout tower of the Arkansas Forestry Commission.[^1]

The Encyclopedia of Arkansas notes that the commission was created by Act 234 in 1931, and that it also built a good many towers.[^2] By 1941, when the survey described the site, the tower was already standing.[^1] The description gives an all-steel tower with an enclosed cabin at the top, about 110 feet (33.5 m) high.[^1]

At its peak, the system had 122 known fire towers in Arkansas.[^2] By 1994, forty-eight were still standing, and whether any were still in use is unknown.[^2]

No source we read describes the Holly Springs site today. We cannot say whether the tower still stands, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1]
