The Forest Lookouts page lists the Camp Colby tower as an auxiliary lookout in Piscataquis County, under the Maine Forestry District.[^1] A 1944 state panoramic map of the Camp Colby site places the tower in Township 6, Range 7.[^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register and the Maine lookout list both place it in Piscataquis County.[^3][^4] The 1944 map record gives Penobscot County instead, so the sources disagree.[^2]

The register dates the collapse to between 1989 and 1999 and says the tower was then removed.[^3] The Maine lookout list marks the site as gone.[^4]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
