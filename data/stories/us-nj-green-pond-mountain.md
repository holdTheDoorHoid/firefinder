The Green Pond Mountain Lookout is a 60-foot steel tower on Picatinny Arsenal, the U.S. Army site in Morris County, New Jersey.[^1][^2] The Army built it in 1942 as a sentry post with fire-detection duties, overlooking the fuse and explosive assembly area.[^1][^2] Of New Jersey's fire towers, only this one has a catwalk around its cab.[^1][^2] Wikipedia places it in Jefferson Township and warns readers not to confuse it with a separate grey metal tower, visible from Route 15.[^3]

When it was in use, a lookout in the cab passed each fire's location and size to local dispatch.[^2] A backfire set at the tower stopped a forest fire in April 1994.[^1] A February 2023 Register visit report marks the tower as not staffed.[^1] The Register listed the tower on June 4, 1994.[^1]

Accounts of the 1994 cab work differ. The Register says restoration was complete and that the New Jersey chapter of the Forest Fire Lookout Association had taken on rebuilding the cab.[^1] The Army article says the Register's volunteers restored the cab that year, and that the tower has since fallen back into disrepair.[^2]

By 2020 the tower had been abandoned for use as a radio site and was slated for demolition.[^1] The state forest service had expressed interest in taking it over.[^1] The Army article adds that fire department leaders hoped to save it for its historic value.[^2]

In September 2020 a lantern was lit inside the cab for the first time in nearly 30 years, as part of the annual Lighting of the Fire Towers.[^2] The Army said the Labor Day weekend lighting was planned as a yearly event.[^2] A September 2023 Register visit report said the cab had deteriorated further and was unlikely to last beyond five years at that pace.[^1]

The site is closed to public visitors.[^1] The Register's 2020 visit report says public access has been closed since 2001, and the site is run by Picatinny Arsenal.[^1] Radio antennas are attached to the tower, and a 2020 visit report noted a cellular antenna pole within 50 feet of it.[^1] The newest source found is from 2023, so its current condition is unconfirmed.[^1] Anyone curious should ask Picatinny before going.
