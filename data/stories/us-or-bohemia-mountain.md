Bohemia Mountain, in southeastern Lane County, Oregon, is listed as an early patrol lookout site in the Umpqua National Forest.[^1][^2] A July 1921 newspaper item, reprinted on the Weebly page, describes a former Forest Service ranger who had been stationed on the mountain and who planned to return to his old grounds for the first time in 16 years.[^1]

The summit stands at 5,994 feet (1,827 m).[^3] The Forest Service ranks it as the highest spot in the Cottage Grove District.[^4] No source I read describes a tower or cabin on the summit today.

Today a trail reaches the top, and the Forest Service describes it as a steep climb to a rocky summit.[^4] The round trip is about 1.6 miles, and directions start in Cottage Grove, where drivers go east on Road 2400 toward Dorena Lake.[^2] On a clear day, the Forest Service says, the view reaches Cascade peaks between Mount Hood and Mount Shasta.[^4]

A separate lookout stands at the summit of nearby Fairview Peak, and it is sometimes possible to drive up to it.[^2] The register says the present 53-foot tower there was built in 1972.[^5]
