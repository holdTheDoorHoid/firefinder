Nelson Mountain is a wooded hill about 3.5 miles northwest of Albemarle, in Stanly County, North Carolina.[^1][^2] The Coast and Geodetic Survey set a survey mark on the hill in 1948.[^1] Its description says the hill was owned by L.C. Burleson.[^1]

By 1967 a fire tower stood on the hill.[^1] The survey recorded the mark 3.9 feet from the tower's northwest leg.[^1] The mark was also about 92 feet south of a microwave tower, measured from outside a locked fence.[^1]

In 1972 the Concord Telephone Company had put up a relay tower about 300 feet northwest of the mark.[^1] The company owned the land by then.[^1] A 1974 note puts a 310-foot telephone microwave tower about 75 feet from the mark.[^1]

A 2009 survey note found the microwave tower gone.[^1] It also found the lookout gone, with its old leg supports still there.[^1] The last note that clearly describes the lookout standing is from 1967.[^1]

A 2014 recovery note by a geocaching group says the station and its reference marks are on private property.[^1] Permission for access was not granted.[^1] The latest entry does not describe what stands on the hill now.[^1] Visitors should stay off the land unless the owner gives permission.
