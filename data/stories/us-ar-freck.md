The Freck fire tower was in Marion County, Arkansas.[^1] A 1954 Coast and Geodetic Survey description places its survey station about six miles south and half a mile west of Yellville.[^1] That description calls the tower a four-legged wooden structure about 120 feet high, with an observation cabin on top.[^1]

Newspaper notices, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page from The Mountaineer Echo, show the tower was staffed in the 1940s.[^1] A notice from April 1943 reports a daughter born at the tower to Mr. and Mrs. S. A. Keene.[^1] An October 1946 notice places Lester E. Delano at the fire tower south of Yellville.[^1] In February 1947 the paper reported that Harry Elmer Smith had taken over at the tower from Delano, who had spent several months there.[^1]

The 1954 survey description is the latest dated account of the tower this record has found.[^1] The February 1947 notice is the latest report of who staffed the tower.[^1] No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether the tower or any footings remain, or whether the public can visit. Anyone heading there should first check who manages the land.

[^1]: Freck, Forest Lookouts (quoting The Mountaineer Echo, 1943 to 1947, and reproducing the National Geodetic Survey description for PID GF0707), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/freck.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
