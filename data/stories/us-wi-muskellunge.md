The Muskellunge Lookout Tower stands in Vilas County, Wisconsin, on Plum Firetower Road, according to the state's historic property record.[^1] OpenStreetMap lists the site at 522 metres above sea level.[^5]

The Wisconsin Historical Society gives 1934 as the year the tower was built, and names the International Stacey Company in its architect field.[^1] A Forest Lookouts list adds an earlier entry, drawn from a 1916 Biennial Report, that describes a 55-foot steel structure as a main lookout.[^3] The sources do not show whether that earlier structure stood where the 1934 tower does now, so the two dates stay side by side.[^1][^3]

According to the state record, the Department of Natural Resources staffed the tower on an as-needed basis through the spring fire season, with 2015 as the last year listed.[^1] WJFW reports that 72 aging towers still in service statewide were decommissioned in 2015.[^4] Neither source names this tower in that closure, so the 2015 end of staffing rests on the state record alone.[^1][^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^2] The state's survey, dated 2017, says the tower is not on the National or State Register of Historic Places.[^1]

I found no source that describes current access or says whether visitors may go up. This story does not encourage climbing the tower. Before you plan a trip, check with the Department of Natural Resources, which the state record names as the agency that staffed it.[^1]
