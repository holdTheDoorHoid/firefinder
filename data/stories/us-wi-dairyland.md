Dairyland Lookout is a 100-foot tower in Douglas County, Wisconsin.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register gives its design as USFS-style, with an inside corner ladder, and credits Bethlehem Steel as the manufacturer.[^1] The tower went up in August 1937 for the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources.[^1]

The register gives Tyler Bormann as the nominator and records the tower's registration on August 28, 2017.[^1] The sources read do not say who staffed the lookout, when it stopped being used, or whether any fires or smokes were reported from it.

The register says the tower and its land changed hands in a state land parcel sale and are now privately held.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's 2021 Wisconsin report tracked the DNR's divestment of its towers.[^3] As of November 2021 it counted 39 towers as conveyed to the private landowners where they stand.[^3] That count also includes towers that reverted to the Menominee Tribe, the U.S. Forest Service and the Department of Military Affairs, and it covers the whole state, not this tower alone.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^2] The sources read do not document public access, and the register places the property in private hands, so treat the tower as private.[^1] Please do not climb it, and ask the owner before any visit.
