The Jack Mountain lookout stood in Idaho County, in the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1][^3] A University of Idaho photo record, matched to this lookout by its title, puts it about 14 miles south-southeast of Elk City.[^4]

The sources disagree about the first tower. Rex Kamstra's firelookout.com page and Idaho Fire Lookouts both describe a wooden tower, 60 feet tall with a 7-by-7-foot cab, built in 1931.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts county list gives the 1931 tower as a 90-foot steel tower instead.[^3] All three agree that a 100-foot steel tower replaced it in 1953.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists a known staffer, Merril Hogaboam, for 1937 to 1939, and cites a book on the Lochsa by Louis F Harting.[^2] Both sites say the lookout was last staffed in 1969.[^1][^2]

Both sites say it was removed in 1972.[^1][^2] A University of Idaho photo dated 1951, a date marked approximate, shows what was left of the tower lying on the ground.[^4] A second photo in the same collection describes the tower as leaning over.[^5] That date comes before the 1953 replacement the other sites describe, so the timeline is unsettled, and these sources do not resolve it.

No source we read describes what remains at the site today or whether the public may visit it.
