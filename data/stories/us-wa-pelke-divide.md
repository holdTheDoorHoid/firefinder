A log cabin and two crow's nest platforms were built on Pelke Divide in 1934, on the Kaniksu National Forest in Pend Oreille County.[^1][^2][^3] The two platforms stood a quarter mile apart.[^1] The register says the lookout was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1][^2]

Where the lookout stood is less certain. The register's coordinates lie north and east of the ones in the Forest Association's list.[^1][^4] Willhite, working from old maps, places the lookout at a spot reached by road, but notes that the ridge is long and has many potential sites.[^3]

The author found a new forest on the ridge with no trees left from the 1930s, which points to the whole ridge having burned, along with any trace of the cabin and platforms.[^3]

According to Willhite, a road gets you to the spot from the saddle parking area with little brush in the way.[^3] No source I found describes a marker or any remains on the ground now.
