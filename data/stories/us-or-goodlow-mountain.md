Goodlow Mountain is in Klamath County, Oregon.[^1] Forest Lookouts files it under the Fremont National Forest and dates its lookout use to about 1944.[^1] The page calls it a secondary, short-lived lookout point and says the lookout sat in his vehicle. It mentions no tower or cabin.[^1]

The Forest Service research page names the forest as the Fremont-Winema National Forest and places a Goodlow Mountain Research Natural Area on its Bly Ranger District.[^2] That protected area was set up to represent ponderosa pine forest in southern Oregon, and its current restoration work includes prescribed fire and control of three invasive species.[^2]

The research page does not say whether the protected area includes the summit. No source we found describes what, if anything, stands on the summit today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
