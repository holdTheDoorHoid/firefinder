Moose Mound is in Granite County, Montana, about 21 miles southwest of Phillipsburg, on the Deerlodge National Forest.[^1][^3] Forest Lookouts gives its legal location as 4N-16W.[^3]

Firelookout.com dates the site to the 1930s, when it had a patrol tower and a camp.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists its type as a platform, with coordinates of 46.07110 north and 113.63320 west.[^2]

Firelookout.com says the site was abandoned in the 1940s, after its 1930s use as a patrol site.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as Abandoned.[^2] Forest Lookouts lists it as Removed, but its page does not say what was removed.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
