Bald Knob lies in Somerset County, inside Forbes State Forest.[^1] The lookout register describes its tower as a steel tower built before 1924.[^2] A history of Forbes State Forest instead says the Pondfield and Bald Knob fire towers were both constructed in 1925, so the sources disagree on when the tower went up.[^3] Forbes's first known fire tower was Sugar Loaf, erected in 1921.[^4]

An earlier 1909 state report described a railroad observatory near Laurel Summit that could be used as a fire lookout, and the lookout history files that description under Bald Knob.[^1] A 1913 line on the same page mentions one 50-foot fire tower, without naming the site.[^1]

The register says the tower was removed in 1933.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. The state's forest history reports heavy recreational use of Forbes, but no source says whether the tower site itself is open to visitors.[^4]
