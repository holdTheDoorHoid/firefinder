Forest Lookouts files this tower as Beersheba (Dykes Mtn), in Grundy County, under the Tennessee Division of Forestry's District 3.[^1] The page reprints the same October 7, 1955 News-Democrat notice as the Center page. According to it, the state had bought twelve steel towers, 80 feet each, and one was to go up at this site before early winter, for roughly $36,000.[^1] That is a plan, not a record of construction. The page also shows a bare "Removed" line beneath the notice.[^1]

Tennessee Landforms lists the Dykes Mountain tower as removed too.[^2] The 2014 list of extant Tennessee fire towers names a single Grundy County tower, Altamont, and nothing called Dykes Mountain or Beersheba.[^3]

No source I found describes the site today, so I cannot say what, if anything, remains on the mountain.
