Birchwood's fire tower was a Wisconsin Conservation Department lookout in Rusk County.[^1] In May 1932 a newspaper item, quoted by a lookout history site, reported a fire lane under construction southeast of town, while the tower the state conservation commission had authorized had not yet been started.[^1] The tower was erected in 1952. It was a 110-foot steel tower of ladder-type design.[^1]

In January 1983 the Department of Natural Resources invited sealed bids for the tower, which the notice put at about 100 feet tall.[^1] That is shorter than the 110 feet recorded when it went up, so the two figures disagree.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list now marks the tower as gone.[^2]

The association gives the site as 45.59402, -91.53159.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains there, or whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2]
