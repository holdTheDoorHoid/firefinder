The Windy Mountain Fire Lookout stands in the Cariboo Regional District of British Columbia.[^1] Mapcarta gives its latitude as 51.66222° north, and lists Windy Mountain and Mount Heger as the highlights of the area, with Mount Heger Peak about 5 km east of the tower.[^1]

OpenStreetMap, a volunteer-built map, carries the tower under the same name.[^2] Mapcarta suggests two recreation sites to explore nearby, Windy Lake and White Lake, each about 7 km northeast of the tower. It says its page draws on OpenStreetMap, Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons.[^1]

Nothing I could open confirms what remains at the summit today, or whether the public may reach it.

[^2]: Windy Mountain Fire Lookout, OpenStreetMap way 475561977, OpenStreetMap contributors, https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/475561977 (accessed 2026-10-20).
