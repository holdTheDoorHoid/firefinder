Wood Butte sits in Wallowa County, in the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest, on the Powwatka Ridge road.[^1][^3] A note quoted on the Oregon Lookouts page, from April 1927, says J.W. Wood, a per diem lookout, had used a rise near his home as a lookout site, and that before he died he asked that half an acre there be transferred to the Forest Service.[^3]

The date of the tower is disputed. The register and firelookout.com both give 1935 for a 40-foot pole L-4 tower.[^1][^2] The Oregon Lookouts page records a new tower in 1932, and under 1936 it notes that building "this tower" took 699 man-hours of effort.[^3] This story uses 1935, the date the register and firelookout.com both give.

Firelookout.com lists the people who staffed it: Ray Knudson in 1936, Harold Lamb in 1941, and Marvin Wiggans in 1942 and 1943, with others through 1963.[^2]

In 1953 a note on the Oregon Lookouts page calls the lookout not satisfactory and says it should be abandoned, with a lookout placed in section 30 instead.[^3] By 1966 the lookout was staffed only when lightning storms or extreme fire weather called for it.[^3] That August vandals broke two windows while the tower stood empty, a report in the Wallowa County Chieftain said.[^3]

The register records the tower as removed in 1966.[^1] Firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^2]

No source I read describes what, if anything, stands at the site now, and I found none that says whether visitors can reach the point. The register's latest picture is a 2009 photo, and its caption does not say what can still be seen.[^1]
