Vermillion Peak sits in Sanders County, Montana, in the Kootenai National Forest.[^2][^3] Firelookout.com says a pole L-4 tower went up on the peak in 1935 and was destroyed in 1963.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page quotes a September 1936 newspaper item about the work. The item says a crew was hauling gear to a new lookout site near the upper end of Graves Creek, with Ben Cox as foreman.[^2] That is a year later than the date firelookout.com gives, so the two sources disagree about when the tower was built.[^1][^2]

In June 1946, Forest Service packers reached the station and found it wrecked. A bear had climbed the tower and got inside the cabin on top of it.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts page gives 1963 as the year it was destroyed, citing a source it names as Kresek.[^2] The Montana list marks the tower gone.[^3] No source describes what remains at the site today, and none says whether the peak is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3]
