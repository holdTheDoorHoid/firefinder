Resica is a fire lookout in Monroe County, Pennsylvania.[^1] Its page heads the site Resica Pine Mountain.[^1]

The chief forest fire warden's report for 1915 says that Patrolman Primrose constructed a lookout at Resica that year.[^1] Because an employee put it up under a co-operative agreement, the lookout ended up belonging to the Pocono Protective Fire Association.[^1] The same report, as the page explains, calls both this lookout and one on Mt. Pocono rustic wooden structures.[^1] The association was organized in 1902 to build wooden fire towers that would watch over its members' lands, and Abandoned Online credits it as the first association of its type in the country.[^2]

No source describes the site now, so what remains there and whether it is open to the public are not known.
