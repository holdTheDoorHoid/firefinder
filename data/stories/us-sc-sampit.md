The Forest Lookouts history site lists a Sampit tower in Georgetown County under the South Carolina Forestry Commission.[^1]

The Commission's first fire tower went up in August 1930 near Camden.[^3] The network later grew past 130 towers, and it spent 65 years spotting wildfires.[^2] The Commission brought its fire tower system to an end on October 1, 1993.[^3] The Post and Courier dates the decommissioning of the system to 1994.[^2]

No source I could reach describes the Sampit site today, so it is unclear whether anything remains there or whether visitors can get near it.
