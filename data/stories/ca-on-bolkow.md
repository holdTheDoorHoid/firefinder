Bolkow Tower is named in two lists of Ontario fire towers, and both place it at Chapleau.[^1][^2] Most of Ontario's fire towers went up between the 1920s and the 1950s, when the Department of Lands and Forests had jurisdiction over them; that department became the Ministry of Natural Resources in 1972.[^3] Through the 1960s, aerial spotting and firefighting crews took on much of the work, and the towers saw less use; the fan site says the government later had many of them destroyed.[^3]

The fan list is headed as a list of towers still standing, and it says the MNR no longer maintains them, so visitors are asked not to climb them.[^2] No source describes what remains at the Bolkow site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
