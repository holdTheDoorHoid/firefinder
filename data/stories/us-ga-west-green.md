The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers places a tower named West Green in Coffee County, at 31.626111 north and 82.734444 west.[^1] The list's header carries a revision stamp of March 22, 2016, so its entries may be older than today.[^1]

West Green is an unincorporated community along U.S. Route 221, northeast of Douglas and southwest of Lehigh, near the Coffee and Jeff Davis county line.[^2]

Across Georgia, the Georgia Forestry Commission began shifting from staffed towers to air patrols in the late 1980s, and many of the towers were scrapped and sold.[^3]

No source here describes the West Green site now, so this story cannot say whether a tower stands there, what remains, or whether the spot is open to visitors. Until access is confirmed, do not assume a tower at this spot is open to climb.[^1]
