Hedgehog Mountain sits in the southwestern part of Township 15, Range 6, in Aroostook County.[^2][^3] The forestry department erected a 24-foot steel tower on the mountain in 1914.[^2] A 12-by-14-foot house went up at the foot of the mountain, beside a spring, where the watchman lived and fire-fighting tools were kept.[^2] A trail was cleared from the camp to the lookout station in 1915.[^2]

In 1917 the chief warden for the Fish River territory, J.M. Brown, reported that the station had been fixed up, painted and was in good order.[^2] A 1920 inventory still listed a 24-foot steel tower.[^2] The Forest Lookouts entry records the tower as removed.[^2]

The register lists the site as US 110, ME 36. It was registered on September 15, 2008, on the nomination of Henry Isenberg, and it says the tower is no longer standing.[^1]

Today the summit is reached by a hiking trail. The Hedgehog Mountain Land Trust is listed as the trail manager, and the trailhead is at a rest area on the west side of ME-11, about 12.5 miles north of Portage.[^4] The trail is steep, about three-quarters of a mile to the summit, where hikers can sit at a picnic table.[^4] A hiking blog calls the trail unmarked and describes passing the remains of an old forest service cabin and the anchor points of a dismantled fire tower.[^5] That blog is the only source we found on what remains at the site, so check with the land trust before assuming the anchor points are still visible.[^5]
