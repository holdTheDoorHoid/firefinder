Edra is a steel fire lookout in Alberta.[^1] The provincial register gives its tower as 30 m (98 ft) tall, at an elevation of 802 m (2,631 ft), and places it at 57.85 N, 113.25 W on map sheet 84H/14.[^1]

The register gives 2016 as the year the present structure was built.[^1] A 2009 Alberta lookout towers map already labels a lookout Edra, though, so the name was in use before this tower went up.[^2]

Alberta says its 127 wildfire lookout sites sit across the boreal forest and along the Rocky Mountains.[^3] Every site has a lookout observer on watch, usually from April to October, and the province describes the sites as isolated.[^3] The same page links its wildfire staff to Agriculture and Forestry, and says they have taken up BearSmart recommendations at many of their sites.[^3]

The register lists Edra as active, with a weather station on site and access by air.[^1] No source found describes the site on the ground today, or sets out who may visit it. Treat it as a working station and check with the agency before going near it.
