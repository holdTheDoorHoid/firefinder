Blackstone was a fire lookout tower in Providence County, Rhode Island.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's index of Rhode Island towers lists it at 41.91909, -71.48684 and marks it as gone.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public.

[^1]: Rhode Island Fire Towers, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/ri/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
