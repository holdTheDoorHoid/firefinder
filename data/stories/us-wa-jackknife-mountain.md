Jackknife Mountain is a former lookout site on the Colville National Forest, in Ferry County, Washington.[^1] It sits about 12 miles northwest of Kettle Falls.[^2] The Register lists the U.S. Forest Service as administrator.[^1]

Sources date the first cabin and tower two ways. A May 1924 item quoted on Forest Lookouts says Ranger Foote built a cabin and a tower there the previous fall.[^3] That tower stood 50 feet tall, with a 14-foot base and a six-foot platform at its top.[^3] WillhiteWeb dates the work to fall 1923.[^4] The Register says a camp was set up on the site in 1924.[^1]

A February 1928 item reports that Mr. Hougland added an enclosure to the tower the previous fall.[^3] Randolph Riley was stationed on the lookout during the summer of 1929.[^3][^4] Panoramic photos were taken in 1934, and WillhiteWeb says there was some kind of ground structure below.[^1][^4]

WillhiteWeb says concrete was poured for footings in 1939, and a 45-foot wooden tower with an L-4 cab went up in 1940.[^4] The Register also gives a 45-foot wooden L-4 tower for 1940.[^1]

WillhiteWeb says the AWS used the site in 1942. The Forest Lookouts page gives the service dates as August 27, 1942 to July 1, 1943.[^4][^3]

In 1944 Helen Gilbert was the lookout until illness sent her to a hospital in Colville in August. WillhiteWeb says Ollie Mae Gilbert took over her duties that week.[^4] An October 1944 Colville Examiner item says women manned fire lookout points that year and names Ollie Mae and Helen Gilbert at Jackknife.[^3] In 1945 the lookout was Beverly Nixon and Roberts Reed, and in 1948 Betty and Donald Frances.[^4][^2]

The Register and WillhiteWeb say the 1940 tower was removed in 1952.[^1][^4] Fire Lookout says it was destroyed that year.[^2]

The Register posts a 2012 site photo credited to Ron Kemnow, but its text does not describe what the photo shows.[^1] WillhiteWeb credits a Ron Kemnow photo too, without a description.[^4] No source I read says what stands on the mountain now, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
