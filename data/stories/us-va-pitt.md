Pitt was a lookout in Page County, Virginia, on the George Washington National Forest.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a ground lookout rather than a tower, and as gone.[^2]

A hiking-trail listing describes a loop of about 3.5 kilometres called Pitt Springs Lookout via Massanutten South Trail, which starts and ends in Shenandoah, Page County.[^3] No source found says whether that trail reaches the lookout site, and none describes what remains there today or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^2]: Virginia Fire Towers (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/va-co/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
