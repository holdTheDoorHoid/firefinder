The Forest Lookouts page for Magnolia names the Texas Forest Service as the agency.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas index lists a Magnolia tower in Montgomery County as gone.[^2] No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Magnolia, Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/magnolia.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Texas Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
