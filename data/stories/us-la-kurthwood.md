The Kurthwood Fire Tower is in Vernon Parish, Louisiana.[^1] OpenStreetMap, a volunteer map, marks a tower at the spot and tags it as a lattice observation tower.[^2] The Central US Lookouts page for the tower has no history. It gives the parish and a National Geodetic Survey station record that has no description.[^1]

Wikipedia gives one account of the community's name.[^3] In that one report, Nona, a sawmill town, became Kurthwood when Joseph H. Kurth set up a mill there around 1920.[^3]

A site that reproduces a USGS topographic sheet of 2003 describes that map as including the Kurthwood Fire Tower.[^4]

No source describes what stands at the tower site now, or whether the public can visit it.
