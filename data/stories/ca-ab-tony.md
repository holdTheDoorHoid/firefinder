A Canada Lookouts page for Tony Creek, under a Whitecourt Division heading, has a single dated line: a tower was erected there in 1963.[^1] The provincial register lists a lookout called TONY, 1,006.9 m (3,303 ft) above sea level, with a steel tower 30 m (98 ft) tall that it dates to 2005.[^2] The 1963 entry and the register match by name only, so the earlier tower may or may not have stood on this same site.[^1][^2]

The register lists the tower as active, with access mode "fly" rather than road access.[^2] Alberta reports that each of its 127 wildfire lookout sites has a lookout observer on watch, usually from April to October.[^3] No source describes the site itself today or any public visits, so treat it as a working fire tower, not a place to visit.
