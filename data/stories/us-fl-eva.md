In 2010 the Eva Lookout tower stood in south Lake County, off State Road 33 near Lake Erie Road.[^1] That July the Orlando Sentinel reported that state forestry officials planned to offer the tower for sale by bid that year.[^1] State officials told the paper the state could save money by selling its old towers.[^1] The paper added that after Eva was sold, Lake County would have just one tower left standing, at Paisley on the southern edge of the Ocala National Forest.[^1]

An April 2005 Associated Press report said the state still used all four of its Lake County towers on high-danger days, including times when lightning kept planes grounded.[^2]
