The Winter lookout was a Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources tower in Sawyer County, 92 feet tall.[^1] In 2006 the tower, which no longer had a cab, was sold.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list marks the Winter tower as gone.[^2]

The DNR planned to take 72 of its fire towers out of service in 2016, including five in Sawyer County.[^3] The article names those five, Hayward, Sand Lake, Pipestone, Swayne and Connors Lake, and does not include a tower called Winter.[^3]

The same article reports a recent structural assessment of the DNR's towers, most of them built in the 1930s and 1940s, which found them unsafe.[^3] The article puts the cost of replacing them at $25 million and calls that prohibitive.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Winter site today.[^2] The article reports a Winter Station firefighter saying a tower east of the village of Winter had been dismantled. It does not name that tower, so the remark cannot be tied to this site.[^3]
