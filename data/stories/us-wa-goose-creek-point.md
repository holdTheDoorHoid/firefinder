Goose Creek Point is a former fire lookout site in Pend Oreille County, inside the Kaniksu National Forest. The way in runs through the town of Usk.[^1][^3]

The lookout was built in 1938 as a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab on top.[^1][^2][^3] It was removed in the 1950s.[^1][^2][^3] The site was entered on the Former Fire Lookout Sites register on December 14, 2012.[^1]

Getting there means leaving Usk on Kings Lake Road, crossing the Pend Oreille River, and following FR50 up to a ridge.[^3] A visitor's account on the WillhiteWeb page is undated. It reports many trees down along the route, so the advice was to drive the spur road as far as it goes and then walk.[^3] The walk from the main gravel road is about three-quarters of a mile.[^3]

At the top, the same account noted what may be a foundation for a garage. The view to the south was still open, while the rest was blocked by trees.[^3]

No other source describes what remains at the site today.
