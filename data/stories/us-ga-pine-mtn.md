The tower list kept by tnlandforms.us places a tower called Pine Mtn in Polk County, at 1,062 ft (324 m) elevation, and gives it a Georgia Forestry Commission reference number.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site lists the Polk County tower under the Georgia Forestry Commission.[^2]

In September 2006 the Commission sold the tower at auction for $61.00. The sale required that it be removed within 30 days.[^2] The same site lists at least six other Georgia towers sold at auction that month, such as one in Washington County for $700.00.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, stands on the mountain today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Georgia lookout list, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/georgia.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
