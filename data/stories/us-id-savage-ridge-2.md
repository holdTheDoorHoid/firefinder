The Savage Ridge lookout site sits at 8,100 feet in the Payette National Forest, about nine miles north-northwest of Yellow Pine in Valley County, Idaho.[^1][^2] Its coordinates are 45.08165, -115.58329.[^3]

Two platform towers were built on the west side of Savage Point in the 1930s.[^1][^2]

Both pages and the state list describe the site as abandoned.[^1][^2][^4] The firelookout.com page also says the platforms are still there. That note has no date, and we found no newer source that checks it.[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site hike-in and gives no trail details.[^2] We found no source that says whether the site is open to visitors, so check with the Payette National Forest before you go.

Idaho has a second lookout with the Savage Ridge name, a Clearwater National Forest log cabin in Idaho County that was destroyed in 1972. This story covers only the Valley County site.[^5]
