The Hartsville lookout is in Trousdale County, Tennessee, at about 1,031 feet.[^1] Its state number is 4-26, a district and tower pair.[^1] A Forest Lookouts index places it under Trousdale County and the Division of Forestry's district four.[^2]

We could not find when this tower was built, what it was made of, or who staffed it. A table of towers still standing, dated March 26, 2014, lists Hartsville in Trousdale County.[^3] Tennessee Landforms also marks it as standing.[^1]

Searches for Hartsville often turn up something else. The Tennessee Valley Authority planned to demolish a 500-foot cooling tower left over from an unfinished nuclear plant project in Hartsville, in mid-September 2025.[^4] That structure was not a lookout, and nothing we read links it to this tower.

We found no visitor information for this tower. Tennessee Landforms advises people not to climb the state's old towers, since steps are often removed or rotten.[^1] Please do not climb it, and stay off the grounds unless whoever owns the site has given permission.
