Otter Butte's lookout was a 30-foot tower with an L-4 cab, built at its west point.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Forest Lookouts dates it to about 1934, and the Forest Service list gives the same year as an approximate date.[^3][^4]

The site lies in the Nez Perce National Forest, roughly 14 miles west-southwest of the Moose Creek ranger station.[^1][^3] Forest Lookouts gives its land location as 31N-10E-11.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives its elevation as 6,088 feet.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places it at 46.03313 north, 115.18173 west.[^5]

The tower was destroyed in 1955.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the tower as gone.[^5] None of the sources we read describe what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site hike-in, so it is reached on foot.[^2]
