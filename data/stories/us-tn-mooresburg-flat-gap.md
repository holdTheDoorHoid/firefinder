The tower now called Mooresburg went up in 1937 under the name Flat Gap, and the register records that the name was later changed.[^1] The Tennessee Division of Forestry administers it, in Hawkins County, at an elevation of 2,585 feet.[^1][^2]

The register's visit report, dated April 20, 2008 and written by Jeff Flanary, found the cab rusted, the roof in need of repair, and most of the glass still intact. The stairs appeared acceptable even though the lowest run had been removed, and there was no fence.[^1] The site also holds two other towers, and antennas cover this tower itself along with a small solar panel.[^1] The tower was registered on March 14, 2014, with Ron Stafford as nominator.[^1]

We found no condition report newer than the 2008 visit, so the tower's present state is unconfirmed. Anyone planning a visit should check with the Tennessee Division of Forestry first.
