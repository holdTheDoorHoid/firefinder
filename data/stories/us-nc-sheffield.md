A North Carolina lookout-tower list on tnlandforms.us places a tower named Sheffield in Davie County, at 35.99222, -80.69333.[^1] The list starts from 208 North Carolina fire towers credited to the "NC FFLA site" and to Gary Weber, plus a few more found by looking at 1:100,000 USGS topographic maps and NGS.[^1] It warns that it "may include a few observation towers and Coast Guard lookouts."[^1]

Sheffield is a community in western Davie County, in Clarksville Township, bordering Calahaln Township.[^2] Wikipedia gives the community's coordinates as 35°57′59″N 80°40′48″W.[^2] The listed point lies about 3 km north-west of those coordinates, so the site may not sit in the village itself.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the listed point, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1] For this entry the list gives a name, a county and coordinates, and the list was last revised in June 2021.[^1]
