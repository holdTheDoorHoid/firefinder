Boiler Ridge is a lookout site in Clatsop County, Oregon, about 17 miles east-southeast of Seaside.[^1][^2] The site stands at 1,573 feet, or 479 meters.[^1]

A platform and a camp went up at the site in 1932, and a live-in tower followed in 1940.[^1][^2]

The Clatsop FPA administered the site.[^1] The Oregon Lookouts page places it under the Oregon Department of Forestry, with the Clatsop FPA.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site on September 22, 2010, as US 672, OR 104, after a nomination by Ray Kresek.[^1]

The register says the lookout was gone by the 1950s.[^1] firelookout.com repeats that the tower was gone in the 1950s.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list marks the site as "Tower Gone."[^4]

We found no source describing what, if anything, remains at the site, and none saying whether the public can reach it.
