Pritchard Peak lies about 13 miles north of Wallace, in Shoshone County, Idaho, within the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1] The records describe a camp with a crow's nest, a lookout perch in a tree, set up there in 1939.[^1][^2][^3] The two pages that describe the site give different heights: 4,424 feet on one and 4,400 feet on the other.[^1][^2]

The camp was abandoned in 1941.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Idaho lookouts also marks the site as an abandoned crow's nest.[^4]

An Idaho Fire Lookouts article dated September 16, 2024, describes a visit to the site.[^2] The access road is still on the map, but the writer found it overgrown.[^2] The top has several flat spots, but the writer found no remnants of the camp.[^2] No tree had a full set of identifying markers, such as boards, wire or spikes.[^2] The only object found was a piece of braided cable, which the writer guessed came from a logging choker.[^2] The page tags the site as hike-in.[^2] No source says whether the land is open to visitors today.
