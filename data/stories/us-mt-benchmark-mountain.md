Benchmark Mountain is in the Flathead National Forest, about eight miles west of Polebridge, at 7,099 feet.[^1] The firelookout.com listing dates an L-4 cab on the peak to 1931.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the type as ground rather than tower.[^3]

Forest Lookouts lists the site in Flathead County, under the Flathead National Forest, and notes that panorama photographs were taken there on September 9, 1935.[^2]

The firelookout.com listing says the cab was destroyed in 1957.[^1] Forest Lookouts marks it removed, without a date.[^2] Both listings, and the association's list, show it as gone, but they disagree on how it ended: one says destroyed, the other removed.[^1][^2][^3]

We found no source describing what remains on the mountain today, or whether the public may visit.[^1][^2]
