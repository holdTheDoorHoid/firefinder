A compiled list of Georgia lookout towers, kept on tnlandforms.us, places a Peacock Creek tower in Liberty County at 31.78469, -81.43968.[^1] The list gives its elevation as 17, with no unit stated, and carries two codes for it, BLL-64698 and gfc-127.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register's Georgia list has 29 towers, and this one is not among them.[^2]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything remains or whether the public can reach it.
