Shitike Butte Lookout stands on the Warm Springs Indian Reservation in Jefferson County, Oregon, and the Confederated Tribes of Warm Springs administer it.[^1] The Register gives 1955 as the build year and describes a 90-foot Aermotor tower with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab.[^1] The sources do not agree on that date.[^1][^2][^3] A forestry report for fiscal 1936, as quoted on a history page, says two lookouts were completed in the fall of 1935, one of them an 80-foot steel tower on Shitike Butte.[^3] FireLookout.com and Cherylhill also give 1935.[^2][^4]

FireLookout.com says the tower is staffed in summer and lists a known lookout for 1950.[^2] Cherylhill's directory, dated July 2026, lists it as staffed.[^4]

In August 2007 a history page quoting The Oregonian reported on the Biddle Pass Fire, one of three started by lightning on July 12.[^3] According to that article, the lookout tower was the only structure threatened.[^3] A 1987 renovation gave the tower new windows, stairs, walls, ceilings and paneling, and the living quarters were remodeled.[^3]

The Register, which registered the tower in 2006, says it had watched over the pine forests for 51 years and was still in active service.[^1] The Register also says access is limited by tribal regulations.[^1] Cherylhill lists the site as not accessible to the public.[^4] None of these sources describes a public route to the lookout, so anyone interested should ask the Tribes before planning a visit.[^1][^4]
