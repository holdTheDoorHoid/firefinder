In the Forest Fire Lookout Association's register, the Twin Creek Tower Site sits in Shawnee State Forest in Adams County, Ohio, at 1,286 feet (392 m) of elevation.[^1] The register lists the Ohio Department of Natural Resources as the administering agency.[^1] The site was added to the register on September 3, 2014.[^1]

As transcribed on the Eastern U.S. Lookouts site, a 1934 newspaper item gives the tower's height as 60 feet and places it at the head of lower Twin Creek. It names John Kennedy as towerman and John E. Price as the substitute.[^3] The transcription dates the item to October 27, 1934 and credits the Chillicothe Gazette as its source.[^3]

Ohio's fire-tower era later wound down. A historical marker in Waverly dates the discontinuation of fire-tower use to 1978 and credits changing land use and improved air detection of forest fires.[^4] It notes that Green Ridge Tower in Pike State Forest was the last tower manned.[^4]

The association's Ohio list marks the Twin Creek tower as gone.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or what access visitors have.
