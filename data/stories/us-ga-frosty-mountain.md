Frosty Mountain is a summit in Dawson County, Georgia, inside the Chattahoochee National Forest, and a Forest Service fire tower once stood on it.[^1][^3] Sources differ on the name. WillhiteWeb says Frosty appears to be a sub-peak on Amicalola Mountain, while SummitPost says the summit of Amicalola Mountain is called Frosty Mountain.[^2][^3]

The earliest date we found for the tower is 1966. That year a U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) team visited the summit, reported a fire tower there, and placed a benchmark marked FROSTY MTN FT.[^2]

When the USGS returned in 1975, it found the benchmark destroyed and made no mention of the lookout.[^2] Forest Lookouts lists the tower as removed but gives no date.[^1]

A SummitPost photo posted in July 2008 has a caption reporting that only concrete footings are still on the site, which is now overgrown.[^3] That is the most recent description we found of what is on the summit.[^3] The Approach Trail to Springer Mountain goes over the summit, so the site can be reached on foot.[^2]
