The Davidson lookout was in Hampton County, South Carolina, and the South Carolina Forestry Commission administered it.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site files a state forestry report under Davidson. The report covers July 1, 1946 to June 30, 1947, and says two lookout towers were built that year, one of them in Hampton County near Early Branch.[^2] We found no other source that ties the Davidson name to that tower.[^2]

The register gives the site an elevation of 85 feet. It says the tower was registered on April 7, 2011, and that the tower has been removed. It gives no date for the removal.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, stands at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2] Anyone curious about the site should check with the South Carolina Forestry Commission before going to look for it.

[^2]: Davidson, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/davidson.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
