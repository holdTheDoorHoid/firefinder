Gay Knob is in Pocahontas County, West Virginia, at an elevation of 4,522 feet (1,378 m), according to the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register.[^1] The register entered the site on January 26, 2017, on a nomination by Brian M. Powell.[^1]

A TopoQuest listing that uses the USGS Edray map names a "Gay Knob Fire Tower (historical)" at about 4,524 feet.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's state list marks the site as unconfirmed, with its type unknown.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is on the knob today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1]
