The Atherton Hill tower stood in Windham, in Cumberland County, Maine.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it as US 82, ME 8.[^1]

The tower was taken down and sent to Connecticut in 2000.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site records only the removal, dated 2000.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine list now marks the tower as gone.[^3]

We found no source that describes the Windham hilltop today, so we cannot say whether anything remains there. No source we read names the Connecticut town or owner that received the tower, so we cannot say whether it still stands or whether visitors may see it.
