Vada, an unincorporated place in northern Texas County, lies along Route 32 roughly three miles northeast of Success.[^1]

The lookout there was a U.S. Forest Service tower in the Mark Twain National Forest.[^2] Survey notes place it about 200 yards east of the highway, roughly a fifth of a mile northeast of a survey station named VADA.[^2] According to that description, the structure has four steel legs, stands about 90 feet (27 m) tall, and has a glazed lookout room on top.[^2]

Missouri fire towers stood between 60 and 120 feet (18 to 37 m) high, and most were steel.[^3] In 1966 the U.S. Forest Service operated 43 fire towers in Missouri.[^3]

The Central US Lookouts page lists the tower as removed.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
