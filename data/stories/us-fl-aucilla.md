The Eastern US Lookouts site lists a forest lookout called Aucilla in Madison County.[^1] Its listing also carries the code T1S R6E.[^1] The same listing records an auction sale of the tower in 2008.[^1]

A U.S. Geological Survey monitoring location is listed in Madison County under the name Aucilla Fire Tower, Adc.[^2] No source describes what stands at the site today.

[^1]: Aucilla - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/aucilla.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Aucilla Fire Tower, Adc. - USGS Water Data for the Nation, U.S. Geological Survey, https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/USGS-302529083415701/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
