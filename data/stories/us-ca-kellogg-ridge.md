Kellogg Ridge was a fire lookout tower in Plumas County, California, at about 39.742 north and 121.233 west.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a tower and marks it gone.[^1]

The association files Kellogg Ridge under the heading Unknown/Undocumented, with entries drawn from a book by Thornton, *Fixed Point Fire Detection: The Lookouts*.[^2] Its list gives no register number for the tower.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: California Lookouts (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/ca/ca-co/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Additional California Information, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/?p=490 (accessed 2026-10-10).
