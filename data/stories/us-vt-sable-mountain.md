Sable Mountain, in Essex County, is listed by the Forest Fire Lookout Association as a Crowsnest-type lookout that is gone.[^1] Its coordinates on that list are 44.92258 north and 71.68622 west.[^1] The Eastern US Lookouts page for the mountain lists it under Essex County and marks it "Removed".[^2] A separate register of former lookout sites has no Vermont entries.[^3] Round Mountain, also in Essex County, is listed the same way.[^1]

Other Essex County entries on the county list show a mix: Gore Mountain and Stone Mountain are towers marked gone, while Monadnock Mountain's tower is listed as standing.[^4]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
