Grassy Knob was a secondary fire lookout in Jackson County, Illinois, listed under the Shawnee National Forest.[^1] Its page describes a 40-foot (12 m) telephone-pole tower equipped with a crow's nest and a ladder.[^1]

A 2018 year-end report from the FFLA's Illinois chapter calls Trigg Tower the last of at least sixteen towers in the Shawnee National Forest.[^2]

The report counts four Illinois towers that, as far as its authors know, still stand but are not in active service. Grassy Knob is not among them.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
