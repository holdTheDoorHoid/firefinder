Spurger is an unincorporated community in Tyler County, with the Neches River running through it.[^1] Settlers are known to have been in the area by 1834. A school opened there in 1859, and a post office was open by 1881.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas index marks a Spurger tower as gone and places it at 30.68863 north, 94.22235 west.[^2] The Forest Lookouts site files that tower under Tyler County.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site, or whether the land is open to the public.
