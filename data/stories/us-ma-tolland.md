Lair Mountain in Tolland got its first fire tower by moving one over from Becket Mountain, though the sources give different dates.[^1][^2][^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says the first tower, a 40-foot Aermotor, was built in 1915 and was the former Becket Mountain tower.[^1] A 1916 state report, quoted on a lookout history site, says a 30-foot tower that had been in use on Becket for three years was taken down, moved to Lair's Hill, and extended by 20 feet.[^2] A Becket Mountain history gives 1913 for the original build and 1916 for the move, and it also reports an account of a 1914 build and a rebuild on Lair in 1915.[^3]

In 1934 a new 60-foot Aermotor tower was built by CCC crews from camp 64 in Chester, using ECW funds.[^1] A new road and a power line followed in 1958, and two-way radios came in 1961.[^1]

The tower was sold on eBay in 2003.[^1] A 2005 survey by New England FFLA members found that only the footings were left.[^1] No later source we read describes the summit or those footings.[^1][^3]
