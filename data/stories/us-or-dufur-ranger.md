Rex's Fire Lookout Page credits carpenter foreman Don Bodley with building a lookout tower at the Dufur Ranger Station in September 1933, using Civilian Conservation Corps crews from a side camp of Friend Camp.[^1] It was a 20-foot pole tower set in the Forest Service compound in the town of Dufur.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register dates the tower to 1934 and says the Oregon Forestry Department built it at the Dufur headquarters compound.[^3] Rex's page and firelookout.com give 1933, and firelookout.com places the tower in the Forest Service compound, so we could not settle the year or the agency from these pages.[^1][^2][^3]

Robert Cooper took panorama photographs there on June 30, 1934.[^1] Rex's Fire Lookout Page lists the tower as removed but gives no date, and the register says it is now gone.[^1][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the tower.[^1][^2][^3] Wikipedia calls the ranger station a Forest Service facility, and says its buildings include a main office, two warehouses and three bunkhouse units.[^4] The sources do not say whether the old tower spot is open to visitors, so ask at the station before going.
