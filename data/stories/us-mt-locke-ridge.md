Locke Ridge is a former lookout site in the Flathead National Forest, about eight miles northeast of Fortine.[^1] The firelookout.com page dates the lookout to 1938 and describes it as a pole tower with a cabin. It gives the elevation as 7,206 feet.[^1] The page places the lookout in Flathead and Lincoln counties.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list records the tower as gone, at 48.80984 north and 114.73258 west.[^2] The firelookout.com page also lists it as gone.[^1]

A Lincoln County list also names Locke Ridge under the Flathead National Forest, without further detail.[^3]

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today or whether visitors may go there. Check with the Flathead National Forest before making the trip.
