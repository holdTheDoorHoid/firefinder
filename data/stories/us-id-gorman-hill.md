Gorman Hill is a former lookout site in Clearwater County, Idaho, within the Clearwater National Forest.[^1][^2] A 40-foot pole L-4 tower was built there in 1933.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The L-4 was a 14-by-14-foot frame cab that could be set atop a tall tower.[^5]

Harold Coffman nominated the site, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites register listed it on January 20, 2011.[^1] The register gives its elevation as 5,348 feet (1,630 meters).[^1]

The tower was abandoned in 1955.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The Forest Service list places the site in section 22, township 39 North.[^3]

Firelookout.com places the site about 10 miles east of the Kelly Creek ranger station.[^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts labels it drive-up.[^4]

The register says the tower is now gone. No source we read describes what, if anything, is left on the peak today.[^1]
