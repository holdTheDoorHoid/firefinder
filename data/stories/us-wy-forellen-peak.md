Forellen Peak is a 9,777-foot summit in the Teton Range, in Grand Teton National Park, Wyoming.[^1] It sits in the range's northern section and rises more than 2,500 feet above Owl Creek.[^1] The name is German, from a word meaning trout.[^1] Wikipedia describes it as less dramatic than the range's southern sections.[^1]

A directory of forest lookouts places the peak in Teton County, under the Teton National Forest.[^2] That listing does not match the park placement above, and neither source explains the difference. The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it among its unknown or undocumented Wyoming sites, with only a name, county and coordinates.[^3]

The route from the north is a hike with no technical sections, but it covers many miles off trail.[^1] Wikipedia names the Berry Creek trail as the best access.[^1] None of the sources read describes a lookout structure or any remains on the summit today.
