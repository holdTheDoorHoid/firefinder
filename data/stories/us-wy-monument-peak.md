Monument Peak Lookout stood on Monument Peak, about 22 miles northeast of Dubois in Fremont County, Wyoming.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the lookout's elevation as 11,384 feet and places the peak on the Wind River Indian Reservation.[^1] The Wyoming list on firelookout.org gives the peak's position as 43.66973 north, 109.19820 west, and marks the lookout as a ground lookout that is gone.[^2]

Both inventory sites list the lookout as gone.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com's page titles it a fire lookout cabin.[^1] Do not confuse it with Monument Ridge in Sublette County, which the same Wyoming list gives as standing.[^2]

Nothing in the sources read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today.[^1][^2] Because the peak sits on the Wind River Indian Reservation, check access rules with the reservation before going.
