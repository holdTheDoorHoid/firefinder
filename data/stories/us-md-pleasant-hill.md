In 1935 the state's forestry department bought a ten-acre tract near Pleasant Hill in Cecil County so it could put a fire lookout there, and a newspaper reported that the tower was about to go up.[^1] The sources disagree on the seller. The 1935 newspaper says the land came from the duPont interest, while a later DNR report names Stockford Farms, Inc. and gives a deed date of 29 June 1935.[^1][^3]

In August 1935 a crew of relief workers was building a fire observation tower near Pleasant Hill, supervised by District Fire Warden John M. Bayles.[^1] The paper said the tower would be 100 feet tall, with an outlook over the countryside around it.[^1] A September notice tied the tower to clearing ten acres as a public park.[^1] The DNR report, however, says the tower was erected in the late 1930s, so the exact date of construction is unclear.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Pleasant Hill tower as gone.[^2] The DNR report, dated April 2007 and covering towers DNR managed as of December 2006, found the tower removed. Only the concrete footings of the original tower remained, the site was unimproved, and off-road vehicle trails heavily crossed it, with some dumping.[^3]

The DNR report recommended that the site be considered for a future communications facility. If that was not feasible, it recommended considering whether to declare the tract surplus and sell it.[^3] The sources I read do not say what was done. The report gives the route in: north on Union Church Road from I-95, with the property on the right at the end of a deeded right-of-way.[^3] No source I read describes the site today, or says whether the public may enter it.
