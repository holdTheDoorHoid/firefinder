The 1938 Waskish lookout was a steel fire tower in Beltrami County, Minnesota. The tower that now stands near Bemidji as Cloverleaf began there.[^2][^3]

The county report lists the owner as "Mn MFS," which we read as the Minnesota Forest Service, and dates the tower to 1938. It gives the height as 99 feet 9 inches.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page gives 100 feet.[^2] We treat the height as about 100 feet, or roughly 30 meters. The 1938 tower replaced an earlier Waskish tower, and a 1954 survey note says that earlier tower had already been relocated elsewhere.[^1]

A 1966 survey note describes the 1938 tower as a self-supported, four-legged steel structure topped by an observation room, made by Bethlehem Steel Co.[^1]

The sources we read do not name anyone who staffed the tower or any fire it watched.

By 1966 the tower had been sold, and the same survey note says it stood near Bemidji as the Cloverleaf tower. Its footings remained at the original site.[^1] According to the county report, Cloverleaf is the second Waskish tower, moved from its original site and now privately owned, and it sits southeast of Bemidji.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Cloverleaf as standing and lists the original Waskish site as gone.[^3]

The county report also says the tower is privately owned and not accessible to the public, so ask the owner before going near it.[^1] We found no public access arrangement.
