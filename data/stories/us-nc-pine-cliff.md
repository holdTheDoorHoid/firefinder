Pine Cliff is a lookout tower in Craven County, North Carolina.[^1] The tnlandforms.us list of North Carolina lookout towers gives its coordinates as 34.91333, -76.80583.[^1] The Forest Lookouts index for North Carolina also lists a Pine Cliff entry under Craven County.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the tower today, or whether the public may visit the site.

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: NORTH CAROLINA - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/north-carolina.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
