The Bethel lookout tower stood in Sevier County, Arkansas, about three and a half miles north and half a mile east of Lockesburg.[^1] The Coast and Geodetic Survey described it in 1951 as a four-legged steel structure about 115 feet (35 m) tall, with an enclosed platform at the top.[^1] It sat about two and a half miles northwest of the Lockesburg to Provo road.[^1]

The National Geodetic Survey station list places the tower at about 34 degrees 1 minute 16 seconds north and 94 degrees 9 minutes 47 seconds west.[^2]

No source we found describes the site today, so it is not known whether the steel tower still stands or whether the site is open to visitors. Check who owns the land before going near it.

Arkansas once had 122 known fire towers. In 1994, forty-eight were still standing, and whether any of them were still operating is not known.[^3] The people who staffed these towers often lived beside them, and their job was to spot smoke.[^3]
