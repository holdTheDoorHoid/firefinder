South Beaver was a fire lookout on a knoll near Waldport, in Lincoln County, Oregon.[^1][^3] A May 1934 newspaper item, quoting the supervisor of the Siuslaw National Forest, said a lookout house was planned on the hill near Waldport, not far from the coast, and a local history dates its construction to that year.[^3][^4]

The sources do not agree on the details. The FFLOS register describes a pole L-4 tower from the 1930s.[^1] The weebly history quotes a November 1936 report that the tower was framed and raised, and a 1942 inspector's letter that describes a 60-foot log tower with a 14-by-14 cabin, built about 1937.[^3]

The post was activated on March 9, 1942, under the Eugene Filter Center.[^3] A 1942 letter from an inspector says the post was visited on May 5, when a middle-aged man and his wife were living in the cabin.[^3]

In 1953 the lookout was moved or taken apart, and the sources differ on where it went. The FFLOS register says the cab was moved to Stodt Lookout.[^1] The Osbornes history says the tower was relocated to Stodt Mountain.[^4] The weebly history says the structure was dismantled and its salvaged parts used to build the Stodt Mountain lookout.[^3] The Stodt Mountain register tells a different story: it dates its tower to 1954, says the cab, cables, bolts and stairs came from other Lincoln FPA towers, and says that tower was abandoned in 1972.[^5]

The FFLOS register and firelookout.com both list the South Beaver site as gone.[^1][^2] I found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, or whether visitors may go there.
