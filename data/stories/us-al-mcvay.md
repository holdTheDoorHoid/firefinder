The McVay site is in Clarke County, Alabama, and the Alabama Forestry Commission administers it, according to the register.[^1] The Eastern U.S. lookouts site also lists the McVay lookout in Clarke County.[^2]

The register added the site on September 15, 2019.[^1] Its description is short. The stands are all that remain, and the register also notes one building from when the tower was used as an antenna site, which it calls badly vandalized.[^1]

A 1819 News feature says that when a lookout stopped being staffed, the structure remained standing.[^3] Those towers were eventually fitted with antennas for communications, and many still carry them.[^3]

No source describes the site since the register entry, and none says whether the land is open to visitors. Nothing in the record suggests a climbable tower remains.
