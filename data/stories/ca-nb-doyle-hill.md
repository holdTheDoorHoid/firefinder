Doyle Hill was a fire lookout site in Albert County, New Brunswick, about 2.4 km (1.5 mi) north of Teahan Corner.[^1]

A new lookout tower was built there in 1941, according to the Department of Lands and Mines annual report. It was a 30.5 m (100 ft) galvanized steel tower with an octagonal observer's cabin walled in glass, telephone lines, and living quarters for the observer.[^1] Local licensees and timber-land owners helped with the build.[^1]

A later annual report from the same department, covering the year ending 31 March 1964 (the Canada Lookouts page files it under 1963), describes the Doyle Hill tower as abandoned.[^1] In that report, the living quarters were moved to New Ireland Road for game protection.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Doyle Hill site today. It is not known whether the hill is open to visitors.

[^1]: Doyle Hill, Canada's Fire Lookouts (Ron Kemnow), https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/doyle-hill.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
