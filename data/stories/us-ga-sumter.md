A Georgia tower list places the Sumter Lookout Tower in Sumter County at 32.09600, -84.22852, with an elevation of 463 feet (141 m).[^1][^3] A map site gives the same elevation and lists Jimmy Carter Regional Airport about two and a half miles east of the tower.[^3] The same list entry carries two reference numbers, BLL-41120 and gfc-324.[^1] OpenStreetMap tags the structure as an observation tower with lattice construction.[^2] The list's Google map was last revised on March 22, 2016.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
