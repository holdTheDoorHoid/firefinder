Dad Peak is a former lookout site in Sanders County, Montana, inside the Kootenai National Forest.[^1] A July 1930 item from the Sanders County Independent-Ledger, reprinted on a Montana lookout page, said a new station was being established there and that a telephone line would run to it from the Bull River Ranger Station.[^1] Lookout listings date the cabin to 1931. The 1930 item describes the station as still being set up, so I could not reconcile the two dates.[^2][^3]

The cabin was an L-4 ground house with a gable roof, set on a rock foundation.[^2] It stood about half a mile south of the summit.[^3]

In the 1930s it was one of nine lookouts on the Noxon Ranger District that were staffed on a regular basis.[^2]

The cabin was abandoned in the 1940s.[^3] Heavy snow brought it down in 1971.[^3][^1] The register dates the collapse to the winter of 1971–72, which runs into 1972 rather than ending in 1971.[^2]

Today, the register files Dad Peak under former fire lookout sites.[^2] None of the pages I read describes what remains on the ground there now.[^1][^2][^3]
