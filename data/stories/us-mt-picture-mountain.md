Picture Mountain's lookout stood in Flathead County, Montana, on the Flathead National Forest, about 12 miles south of the Spotted Bear Ranger Station.[^1] Its listing gives the elevation as 7,729 feet.[^1] The page title calls it a cabin.[^1] Forest Lookouts places it at township 23 north, range 15 west, section 12.[^2]

The earliest date the listing gives is 1928, when the lookout was a frame cupola cabin.[^1] In 1939 it received an L-4 cab.[^1]

The listing says it was abandoned in 1950 and is now gone.[^1] Forest Lookouts marks it as removed.[^2] The Montana lookout list classes it as a ground site, not a tower, and marks it gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether anyone can visit it. Check with the Flathead National Forest before planning a trip.
