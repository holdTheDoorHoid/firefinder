The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers records a Beaver Lookout Tower in Hancock County, at 33.37070 north and 82.92015 west, with an elevation figure of 656.[^1] The list says towers numbered 1 to 72, which includes this one, take their names from a USGS name database. It also says towers were discovered on 1:100,000 USGS topographic maps, and that GFC provided coordinates for some of them.[^1]

TopoZone places a Beaver Lookout Tower on the Sparta USGS topographic map, with a feature type of Tower and an elevation of about 656 feet (200 meters).[^2] The Forest Lookouts page on Georgia towers also has a BEAVER entry under Hancock County, with no further detail.[^3]

No source we found describes what remains at this site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
