Lake Mountain sits in Powell County, Montana, about 24 miles east of Seeley Lake.[^1] Firelookout.com lists the Lolo and Helena National Forests together, while Montanalookouts names only the Helena National Forest.[^1][^2]

Firelookout.com dates the lookout, a log cupola cabin, to 1922.[^1] The same page says it was destroyed in 1958.[^1] Montanalookouts lists the site as removed, with no date.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks it as ground gone.[^3]

A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description from 1960, quoted on the Montanalookouts page, places the survey marker on the north one of three small hills atop the mountain.[^2] The description adds that the south hill is the highest and holds what is left of a burned lookout cabin.[^2] It also says there is a good helicopter landing on the site of the burned cabin.[^2]

We found no source describing the site since 1960, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
