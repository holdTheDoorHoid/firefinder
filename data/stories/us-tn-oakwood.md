The Oakwood tower is in Montgomery County, Tennessee, at about 729 feet above sea level.[^1] The Tennessee Landforms site lists it as standing.[^1]

The site's remarks name Fort Campbell as the location.[^1] Fort Campbell is a United States Army installation,[^2] so the tower is probably inside a military post, and public access is likely limited.

The Tennessee Landforms page gives the site code MTT002 and a map location, but no history of the tower.[^1] We found no build date, builder, staffing history, or fire record for it in the sources we read. The National Historic Lookout Register lists 76 Tennessee lookouts, and none is called Oakwood.[^3] Nothing we read confirms the tower's condition beyond the index entry.[^1]

Anyone curious should ask the post before going near the site, and should not climb the tower.
