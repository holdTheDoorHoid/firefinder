Ark Fire Tower stands in the settlement of Ark, in Gloucester County, Virginia, about three miles northwest of Gloucester.[^4] It is a four-legged steel Aermotor tower with a glass-enclosed cab on top.[^3][^4]

The earliest record cited here comes from October 7, 1933. A Daily Press report, quoted on a Weebly lookout page, said a fire watch tower was under construction near the Ark post office. The report described a new fire-protection system combined with fire-lane cutting, and said Civilian Conservation Corps crews from a camp near West Point were cutting those lanes through the local forest.[^3] The Weebly page links that report to this tower; no other source does. A 1942 Coast and Geodetic Survey description refers to the Ark Lookout Tower, so the tower was in place by then.[^4]

The sources disagree on the height.[^3][^4] The Weebly page calls it a 100-foot Aermotor tower, while the 1942 survey gives an overall height of about 125 feet.[^3][^4] They also differ on the agency.[^3][^4] The Weebly page lists the Virginia Department of Forestry, and the survey says the Virginia Forest Service operated the tower. The sources do not explain the difference in name.[^3][^4]

In August 2015 the 100-foot Aermotor tower was offered at a government auction, listed in scrap condition, according to the Weebly page.[^3] The sources do not say whether it sold.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^2] The Register, which registered it on March 24, 2020, says it was still standing as of 2019 but disused as a fire tower, and that it carries two radio antennas.[^1]

A visit report dated April 19, 2023 found the frame and cab intact, but the glass was not intact, and some upper steps and landings were missing and sagging.[^1] The same report says the tower is not staffed.[^1]

The 2023 report says a chain-link fence surrounds the tower, and that neither an NHLR sign nor directions to the tower are posted.[^1] It also judges the steps unfit for regular climbing.[^1] No source describes public access or rental, so visitors should not climb the tower.[^1][^2][^3]
