Boulder Baldy is a peak in the Big Belt Mountains.[^2] The Forest Lookouts index files the site under Broadwater / Meagher County.[^1] PeakVisor gives the peak an elevation of 8,944 feet (2,726 m) and places it in Helena National Forest.[^2]

The one early mention we found is a July 1936 item from The Independent Record, quoted by the Forest Lookouts index.[^1] The item mentions a transportable shortwave unit at the Boulder Baldy station, roughly 20 miles northeast of Townsend.[^1]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^2]: Boulder Baldy (2,726 m) - Big Belt Mountains, USA, PeakVisor, https://peakvisor.com/peak/boulder-baldy.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
