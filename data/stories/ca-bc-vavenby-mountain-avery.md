The Vavenby Mountain fire lookout sits in Area B, the Thompson Headwaters, of the Thompson-Nicola Regional District, at an elevation of 1,866 m (6,122 ft).[^1] Firelookout.ca gives 1956 as its build year and says it may also be known as the Avery lookout.[^1] Canada Lookouts, which lists it under Avery (Vavenby), gives the same construction year.[^2]

A trail guide to British Columbia's historic lookouts gives the name Vavenby Mountain Lookout. It says more than 300 lookouts once stood across the province, and that under a hundred still stand today.[^3] The guide adds that most such sites were taken down or left to decay as wildfire detection improved.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the Vavenby site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
