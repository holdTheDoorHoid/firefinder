The Roseau River fire tower is in Roseau County. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list shows it as standing at the coordinates the list gives.[^1] We found no build date, builder, height or design for it in any source we read.

A 2008 state list of vacant or underused buildings includes Forestry fire towers.[^2] One fire tower on that list has "River" as its only location name. It is shown as built in 1928 and scheduled for disposal, with an estimated value of $100.[^2] The list gives no county or coordinates for that tower, so we have not matched it to the Roseau River tower, and it may be a different structure.

The Roseau River Wildlife Management Area is built around a 27-mile dike system, according to the state Department of Natural Resources.[^3] The agency's page for the area does not mention a fire tower.[^3]

We found no record of who staffed the tower or of any fire it helped spot. We also found no public access or visiting information, so check with the land manager before going.
