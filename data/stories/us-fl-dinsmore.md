A Florida Times-Union item from July 10, 1933, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page for Dinsmore, describes an 80-foot wooden fire lookout tower under construction near Dinsmore, and the page files it under Duval County.[^1]

The statement came from the Tallahassee office of State Forester Harry Lee Baker.[^1]

The tower was being erected with a novel wooden joint coupler, a device patented in Germany.[^1] The item says it would be the first structure in this country built with that device.[^1]

The article adds that, according to the state forester, such couplers had been used on the European continent since the World War.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: DINSMORE - FOREST LOOKOUTS (quoting The Florida Times-Union, July 10, 1933), Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/dinsmore.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
