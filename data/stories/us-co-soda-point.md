Soda Point Lookout sits on the Ute Mountain Indian Reservation in Montezuma County, Colorado.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register lists the Bureau of Indian Affairs as administering it, with the Ute Mountain Ute Agency as a cooperator, and dates the lookout to 1945.[^1] The site was registered on March 16, 2010.[^1]

The register describes an open cab with no roof, set atop a 22-foot steel tower fitted with the sort of climbing steps once common on utility poles.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Colorado list gives the same county and register number, calls the structure a crowsnest, and marks it as standing.[^2] No second source I checked confirms the 1945 build year, and the material I could reach says nothing about staffing, fires or any later changes.

Because the lookout sits on the Ute Mountain Indian Reservation, check access with the Ute Mountain Ute Agency, which the register names as a cooperator, before planning a visit.[^1]

[^2]: Colorado Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/co/ (accessed 2026-10-08).
