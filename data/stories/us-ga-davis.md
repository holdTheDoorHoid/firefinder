The Georgia lookout list on tnlandforms.us gives a tower called davis in Charlton County, at 30.81497 north and 82.10918 west.[^1] The Georgia index on the Eastern US Lookouts site also lists a Davis tower in Charlton County.[^2] Its row also includes the codes BLL-65257 and gfc-831.[^1] The list says the Georgia Forestry Commission supplied coordinates for the towers marked BLL, and that an older Forestry Commission list described the locations of the gfc-numbered towers.[^1] This row is number 209, and the author says names from 73 up were invented by the author, so the name may be a label rather than what the tower was called.[^1] Do not confuse this with the Davis Memorial tower in Meriwether County, which has its own entry and coordinates.[^1][^2] No source I found describes the site today, so I cannot say whether a tower stands there or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1]

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Georgia - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/georgia.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
