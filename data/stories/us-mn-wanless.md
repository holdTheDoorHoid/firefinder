Wanless was a fire lookout tower in Lake County, Minnesota, on Superior National Forest land.[^1] The county list describes it as a 100-foot ladder tower that the U.S. Forest Service erected.[^2] The same entry quotes 1934 survey notes that describe a 112-foot steel tower, so its height and design are given two different ways.[^2]

A USGS topographic map of the Cramer area, dated 1955 and published in 1957, names the Wanless Lookout Tower among its features.[^3] That places the tower on the landscape in the mid-1950s.[^3] The Pastmaps description of the map counts the tower among the strategic high points that kept watch over the timberland before aircraft took on that job.[^3]

A 1986 note in the county list says the tower is now dismantled.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Wanless tower as gone.[^4] No source read describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^4]
