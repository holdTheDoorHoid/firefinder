Berwick is a fire lookout tower in Amite County, Mississippi.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page for it gives a 2023 status of standing, based on Google Earth imagery.[^1]

No source I found describes the site today beyond that 2023 view, so I cannot say whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Berwick - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts (easternuslookouts.weebly.com), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/berwick.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
