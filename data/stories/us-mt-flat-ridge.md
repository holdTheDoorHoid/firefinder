Flat Ridge is a former lookout in Mineral County, Montana, about two miles north-northeast of Superior, on the Lolo National Forest.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 4,806 feet.[^1] That page is a short entry, and it credits its photo of the site to the Fire Lookout Museum.[^1]

Firelookout.com describes a platform tower 30 feet tall, put up around 1927 and given up in 1941.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks the site as a gone tower.[^2]

The 1927 date is approximate. The page header reads "R.I.P. 1927-?", which leaves the end date open, even though the text gives 1941 as the year the tower was abandoned.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains on the ridge today. Nothing we read says whether the site is open to the public, so check with the Lolo National Forest before visiting.

[^1]: Flat Ridge Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com (Rex Kamstra), https://www.firelookout.com/mt/flatridge.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
