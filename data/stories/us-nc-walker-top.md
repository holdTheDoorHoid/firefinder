Walker Top Lookout stands on Burkemont Mountain in Burke County, on private land within South Mountains State Park.[^1][^2] In 1951 the North Carolina Forest Service put up the Aermotor tower near Carthage.[^1] The 2016 photo caption ranks it first among western North Carolina fire towers, standing 100 feet high.[^2]

The tower reached its current spot in 1990, when it was relocated from Moore County.[^1][^2] A 2016 photo caption says the Forest Service manned it until the late 1990s.[^2] The sources we read give no later staffing details.

The Register registered the tower in 2008 after a nomination by Peter J. Barr.[^1] Kenneth Suttles and the state park appear as its cooperators.[^1] A 2016 Flickr caption says the photographer can see the tower from home.[^2]

The Register places the tower on private land inside the state park.[^1] Please do not go up it or onto the tract without the owner's permission. Another site list gives a slightly different longitude for the tower, about 1.5 km east of the Register's point by our calculation, so the exact spot is not settled.[^3]
