Squaw Hump is a summit in Benewah County, Idaho, at 2,850 feet, about 18 miles southwest of St. Maries.[^1] The Coeur d'Alene Timber Protective Association is the listed agency.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts page gives its location code as 44N-4W-28.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list puts the summit at 47.12607 north, 116.84311 west.[^4]

The lookout there was a crow's nest.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives 1930 as the year.[^2] The other two sites say the 1930s.[^1][^3]

Three of the sources list the lookout as abandoned.[^1][^2][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts classes the site as a hike-in.[^2]

None of the sources we read describes the site today or says whether anything of the crow's nest still stands. Anyone planning a visit should check current conditions and the land manager's information first.
