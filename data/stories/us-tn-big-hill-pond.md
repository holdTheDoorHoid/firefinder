The Big Hill Pond Lookout Tower stands inside Big Hill Pond State Park in McNairy County, Tennessee, at 601 feet (183 meters).[^1] The Register describes it as a 70-foot Aermotor observation tower.[^1]

The Register lists the build date as unknown.[^1] A National Register study of Tennessee Division of Forestry fire lookout towers lists a Big Hill Pond tower in McNairy County among the towers still standing on March 26, 2014.[^2] We found no record of who built it, who staffed it, or any fire it watched.

A Tennessee lookout-tower list also includes a Big Hill Pond tower in McNairy County at 601 feet.[^3] The Register shows a registration date of December 30, 2022, with Jason Johns as nominator. It names Tennessee State Parks as the administrator.[^1]

Visitors can climb it. Tennessee Magazine says the tower can be reached along the Turkey Call and Tuscumbia trails.[^4] The Tennessee Hiking Club puts the route at 4.3 miles and says the stairs pay off with a view over the Tuscumbia River bottomlands.[^5] Both guides describe it as open to climb, but conditions can change, so check with the park before you go.
