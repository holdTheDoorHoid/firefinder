Columbia Hill is in Nevada County, California, on the Tahoe National Forest.[^1]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register says the hill's first structure was finished before the 1911 fire season.[^1] A Morning Union item from October 1911, quoted by the Forest Lookouts site, reports that Harry Lillie had come back from Columbia Hill, where he had manned the lookout through the fire season.[^2]

In 1936 the original structure gave way to a California Region 5 Plan BC-301 cabin on a 16-foot enclosed timber tower.[^1] The register's build year, 1936, matches that replacement cabin.[^1]

The register lists the removal date as unknown.[^1] No source we read says what, if anything, remains of the lookout today, or whether the public may visit the site.[^1]
