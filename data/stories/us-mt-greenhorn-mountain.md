Greenhorn Mountain is about an hour northwest of Helena, in the Helena National Forest.[^1] Ron Kemnow's listing files the site under the code 11N-6W-23 in that forest.[^2] The U.S. Geological Survey's geologic-unit entry for Greenhorn Mountain says the unit makes up the highest peak between Mullan Pass and Marysville, in section 23 of Township 11 North, Range 6 West.[^3]

The only dated item about a lookout in these sources is a July 26, 1939 newspaper notice, quoted on Kemnow's page. It lists Greenhorn Mountain near Marysville among points where a need had been shown, without saying what the need was for.[^2] No source found says that a lookout was built there, who staffed it, or what became of it.[^2]

Today the Continental Divide Trail passes the foot of the mountain on section 337. Wild Montana's 2026 volunteer trail crew was set to improve the tread of that section.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the lookout site, or whether the summit is open to visitors.[^2][^3]
