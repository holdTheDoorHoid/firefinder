Paget Lookout is an old fire lookout on the south slope of Paget Peak, in Yoho National Park, British Columbia.[^1][^2] A travel blog gives 1944 as the year it was built.[^3] Wikipedia says it was built after two hard fire seasons in the late 1930s and early 1940s.[^2]

The two accounts also differ on rank. The blog ranks it as the oldest of the national parks' remaining fire lookouts.[^3] Wikipedia says it was among the earliest put up in the park.[^2]

The blog says the lookout was staffed from May to September, and that by the 1970s fires were being detected by air patrols and satellite remote sensing.[^3] Wikipedia says the lookout was used until the late 1970s.[^2]

Today it is a disused lookout that visitors reach on foot.[^2] Parks Canada lists the route as a day hike of 3.5 km one way, with 525 m (1,722 ft) of climbing and an estimated three hours return.[^1] The trailhead is at the Sherbrooke Lake parking lot, on the north side of the Trans-Canada Highway, and Parks Canada says it can be reached only from Highway 1 westbound.[^1]
