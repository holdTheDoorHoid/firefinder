The Bear (Castle) Mountain lookout stood in Lane County, Oregon, about 8 miles southwest of Lowell.[^1][^2] In late 1933 a road was being pushed up the mountain, and a field report said that, if possible, a nearby camp should stay where it was to finish the road and put up the tower and lookout house.[^3] The tower went up in 1934, built by members of the Wendling CCC camp.[^1][^3] The sources disagree on its height. Two describe a 70-foot wooden tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab, while the Forest Lookouts page gives 86 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

In 1937 a trail about half a mile long was cut from the lookout down to a spring. Before that, the lookout man had to get water from a CCC road about three and a half miles away.[^3] Forest Lookouts files the site as Castle Rock (Bear Mountain), and newspapers of the time called the station Castle Rock too.[^3] Lookouts were posted here at least from 1935 to 1942.[^3] In August 1935 the lookout, Charles Kingsley, said fire seemed to be burning across the hills.[^3] In September 1940 Forest Guard Bob Wagner radioed that a 700-acre fire, four days old, was under control.[^3] In September 1942 Ronald Byers was transferred to Castle Rock from the Mt. Nebo post.[^3]

In 1955 the tower was cut down to 45 feet and a live-in cab was added.[^1][^2] In 1956 the observation cab was found unsafe to use, and it was finished that fall with materials that cost $450.[^3] In 1968 someone stole the copper wire that protected the tower from lightning. A district court convicted the thief and put him on probation.[^3]

Sources disagree on when the tower came down. Forest Lookouts says the lookout was removed in 1971.[^3] The other two put its abandonment in 1972.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site on November 18, 2010, with Ray Kresek as nominator.[^1]

The tower itself is gone.[^1][^2] The site is administered by the Eastern Lane FPA.[^1][^2] No source describes what, if anything, is left on the mountain now, or whether the land is open to visitors. Ask the Eastern Lane FPA about access before going.
