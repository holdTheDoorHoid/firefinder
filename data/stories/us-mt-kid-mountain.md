The Kid Mountain lookout was a cabin rather than a tower.[^1] Firelookout.com describes it as an L-4 cab that stood in the Bob Marshall Wilderness, built in 1930 to replace an earlier camp.[^1] Montanalookouts lists the Flathead National Forest as the agency.[^2]

Firelookout.com says the cabin was still there in 1956 but has since gone.[^1] Montanalookouts lists the site as removed, but gives no date.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks the cabin as ground gone.[^3]

A 2021 Flathead Beacon feature on the forest's historic buildings says its lookout network, which once had an important role, has been almost entirely taken apart.[^4] That article does not mention Kid Mountain by name.[^4]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, is left at the cabin site today, or whether the public can visit it.[^1][^2]
