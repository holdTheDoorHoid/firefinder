The Georgia lookout tower list at tnlandforms.us gives a lookout named Reidsville in Tattnall County, at 205 feet (62 m) elevation.[^1] The list does not say whether that entry is the same tower the newspaper describes. A local newspaper says a historic fire tower may have gone up in the late 1930s on Reidsville's western outskirts, and that it spent roughly 80 years in a network of lookouts covering the middle of Tattnall County.[^2] A photo caption places the tower at the Georgia Forestry Commission in Reidsville, where crews were taking it apart.[^2] The paper says this was the tower's third move: crews took it down and relocated it to a new site on Old River Road.[^2] No other source we found describes the site today, so we cannot say whether the tower is standing or open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers list, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Historic fire tower taken down at Tattnall County Forestry Commission, The Tattnall Journal, https://tattnalljournal.net/local-news/historic-fire-tower-taken-down-tattnall-county-forestry-commission/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
