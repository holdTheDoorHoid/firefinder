Hawk Hills is a steel tower, 30 m (98 ft) tall, at 730 m (2,395 ft) elevation, according to Alberta Wildfire's lookout layer.[^1] The layer dates the present structure to 2012.[^1]

The site also has an older entry. A Canada Lookouts web index gives 1949 for the completion of a lookout ladder project at Hawk Hills, citing the Department of Lands and Forests annual report for the year ended 31 March 1950.[^3] The same index files Hawk Hills under the Peace River Division.[^4] Across Alberta, many working lookouts have been rebuilt where their predecessors stood, a RETROactive history says.[^5]

The layer marks Hawk Hills as active and lists its access mode as "drive".[^1] Alberta says each of its lookout sites is staffed by an observer, generally from April to October.[^2] No source I found says the public may visit Hawk Hills, so treat it as a working lookout.
