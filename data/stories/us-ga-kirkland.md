A tower named "kirkland" is listed in Atkinson County on the tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers, with GFC number 819 and a second identifier, BLL-65209.[^1] The list's coordinates sit close to the community's own coordinates on Wikipedia.[^1][^2]

The community of Kirkland lies in Atkinson County, Georgia, and is unincorporated.[^2] Before Atkinson County came into being in 1917, the community was in Coffee County.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register counts 29 registered Georgia lookouts, and its Georgia page does not name Kirkland.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register lists two former Georgia sites and does not name Kirkland either.[^4]

No source describes the site today, including whether it is public.
