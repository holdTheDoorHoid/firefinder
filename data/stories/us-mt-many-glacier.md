The Many Glacier lookout stood on Swiftcurrent Ridge in Glacier County, Montana, about two miles west of Babb Junction.[^1] The page gives its elevation as 5,900 feet and places it on the Blackfeet Indian Reservation.[^1]

In the 1930s a frame cabin stood at the east end of the ridge. It was later abandoned and is now gone.[^1] By the 1940s the site had a 60-foot steel tower.[^1]

In 1959 that steel tower was moved to a new site two miles south-southwest of Saint Mary, where it became the Red Eagle Lookout.[^1][^2] WillhiteWeb gives 1960 for the same move, so the year is disputed.[^3]

The Red Eagle tower was gone in 1986, and WillhiteWeb says it was destroyed that year.[^2][^3] Neither account says any part of it survives.[^2][^3] The Red Eagle page counts it among the lookouts of Glacier National Park.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the Swiftcurrent Ridge site today, or whether it is open to visitors. Because the listing places it on the Blackfeet Indian Reservation, check access with the tribe before going near it.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Many Glacier tower as gone.[^4]
