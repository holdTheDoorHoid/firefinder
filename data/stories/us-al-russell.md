The Russell Lookout Site is in Walker County, Alabama, with an elevation of 671 feet (205 meters).[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register files it among its former lookout sites, lists it as US 1628, AL 7, and gives a registration date of November 27, 2016.[^1]

The register judges that the Civilian Conservation Corps probably built the lookout for the State of Alabama.[^1] The register also estimates that the lookout came down before 1970.[^1]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. The register lists the land as administered by a private landowner, so treat the site as private property and ask the owner before going near it.[^1]

[^1]: Russell Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/al/russell-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
