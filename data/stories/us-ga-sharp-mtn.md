A Georgia lookout tower list on the tnlandforms.us website includes a site called sharp mtn, in Pickens County.[^1] The list gives its elevation as 2,353 without naming a unit. If that figure is in feet, the peak would stand about 717 metres above sea level.[^1]

Wikipedia names Sharp Mountain among the notable peaks of Pickens County, alongside Sharp Top Mountain. It also says the county sits in the Blue Ridge Mountains.[^2] Neither source says whether that Sharp Mountain is the same peak as the list entry.

No source describes the site today, so it is not known what, if anything, remains there, or whether visitors may go up.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Pickens County, Georgia, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pickens_County,_Georgia (accessed 2026-10-21).
