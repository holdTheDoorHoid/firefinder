Bunker Point was a ground lookout on the Flathead National Forest, in Flathead County, Montana.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com lists an L-4 cab for 1939 and says the cab was abandoned in 1948.[^1] The Forest Lookouts Montana page gives the land description as 24N-15W-22.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the site at 47.82535, -113.49872.[^3]

The Forest Lookouts page marks the site as removed, but gives no date.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a ground lookout that is gone.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^2]: Bunker Point, Forest Lookouts (montanalookouts.weebly.com), https://montanalookouts.weebly.com/bunker-point.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association (firelookout.org), https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
