The Canada Lookouts register lists a Bridge River lookout under the heading Squamish-Lillooet, in British Columbia.[^1] The entry gives a position of 50.778 N, 122.817 W and an elevation of 1972. The page prints no unit for that figure; read as metres, it is 1,972 m (6,470 ft). Nothing else appears in the entry.[^1]

No source I could read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Bridge River lookout entry, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/bridge-river.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
