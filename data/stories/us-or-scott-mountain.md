Scott Mountain, in the Willamette National Forest about 18 miles west of Sisters, got a lookout cabin in 1929.[^1][^2] The register and firelookout.com both call it a cathedral cabin.[^1][^2] A June 1929 Eugene paper had described the planned building differently, as a "fire chaser house" of the tower variety.[^3]

Supplies went up by pack mule.[^3] A 1929 item said the material for one such building came to 60 to 70 pack-mule loads.[^3] In late August the Eugene Guard reported that the station was nearly completed.[^3]

The sources differ on when the cabin came down. The Rex page has a 1959 entry saying the lookout was removed,[^3] the register gives 1960,[^1] and firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^2] In August 1960 a group hiking up to the lookout found that it had burned.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the site is open to visitors. Check with the Willamette National Forest before planning a trip.
