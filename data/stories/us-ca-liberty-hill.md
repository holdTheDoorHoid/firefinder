Liberty Hill is in Tuolumne County, on the Stanislaus National Forest.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list calls the lookout a tower and marks it gone.[^2] It also gives the site a number on the association's Former Fire Lookout Sites register.[^2] That register is sponsored by the association itself.[^3]

A peakbagging table lists the site at 7,540 feet and labels the lookout "LO site."[^4] The table's key defines the label to cover a site where the building was taken away and may leave no trace, or where its status is unknown.[^4] The same table lists a survey disk near the lookout, named Liberty Hill, that was first placed in 1972.[^4]

No source describes the site today.
