Horse Valley Peak lies in Iron County, in the Dixie National Forest.[^1] A Utah lookout list says a lookout was likely on its summit, southeast of Parowan, but the same list places the peak among sites its author has not visited.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Utah list is more cautious. It files the peak under "Unknown/Undocumented Sites," and it says a site will be moved to its lookout lists if evidence shows regular fire-lookout use.[^2] The two sources differ: one calls a lookout likely, and the other files the site as undocumented.[^3][^2]

The Forest Service lists a Horse Valley Peak Loop for Off-Highway Vehicles.[^4] The route follows the Smith Canyon road for several miles, and its off-road section becomes a tough stretch that the Forest Service says only experienced riders should take.[^4] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today.
