The Paret Lookout Tower is in Perry County, Mississippi, on the De Soto National Forest.[^1] The U.S. Forest Service administers it.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register describes a steel Aermotor tower, 100 feet tall, with a metal cab that measures 7 by 7 feet.[^1] According to the register, a Civilian Conservation Corps unit whose members came from New Jersey built it in 1934.[^1] We found no second source for that date or that crew, so treat it as the register's account.

The register was filed on July 24, 2003.[^1] The nominator was Judith Henry, district ranger of the De Soto Ranger District.[^1] Only a few of the roughly 50 state and federal fire towers in southern Mississippi are still left, and the register counts this one among them.[^1] The view from the tower takes in a 16,000-acre piece of national forest that the National Guard uses as a shooting range.[^1]

That 2003 entry is the most recent description we found. A WDAM report dated February 22, 2026 says a wildfire in the De Soto National Forest was near Paret Tower Road and had forced evacuations in south Perry County.[^2] The report does not say whether the fire reached the tower.[^2]

We found no information on public access, so ask the Forest Service before going near the site. Do not climb the tower.
