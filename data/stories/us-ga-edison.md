Edison is a town in northwestern Calhoun County, Georgia.[^1] The state legislature chartered it in 1902 as the "Town of Edison," and it takes its name from the inventor Thomas Edison.[^1]

The Tennessee Landforms list places a site called edison at about 31.561° N, 84.729° W, in Calhoun County, and gives two register codes, BLL-41129 and gfc-201.[^2] The map on the page was last revised on March 22, 2016.[^2]

Calhoun County was formed from portions of Early and Baker counties on February 20, 1854, and Morgan is its seat.[^3]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
