Cornwall Lookout stands at the top of Cornwall Hills, in Cornwall Hills Provincial Park, about 15 km southwest of Cache Creek.[^4] It sits at 2,032 metres (6,668 feet).[^1]

A 2016 article cites records of a fire lookout on the site as far back as the 1890s.[^2] The register and that article date the current building to the late 1950s.[^1][^2] Yet a photograph taken in August 1955, shown in a 2019 article, already shows a one-storey building. That building was later added to, taking it to two storeys, and the original still forms the base.[^3]

The lookout was staffed every summer until the early 1990s. After that it was used only if a fire risk existed in the area.[^2] As of 1999 it was the only active forest service lookout in the Kamloops fire district. It was last used during the 2003 wildfire in the Cornwall Hills.[^2] A 2016 visit report records it as not staffed.[^1]

In 2015 BC Parks announced plans to dismantle the lookout unless a group took on its upkeep and liability.[^1][^2] The Four Wheel Drive Association of BC took over management.[^2][^3] Sources differ on the year: a 2019 article says 2015, while a 2016 article says the paperwork was signed a couple of weeks before publication.[^3][^2] BC Parks says a partnership agreement with the association was in place by 2016.[^4]

In August 2019, volunteers working with the association replaced the stairs, two posts and a beam, and repainted most of the upper exterior.[^3]

Today the park has no camping or day-use facilities, and it is not regularly serviced or patrolled.[^4] Off-road vehicle travel is prohibited.[^4] BC Parks warns that the road near the forestry recreation site can be impassable in rainy periods, and that visitors must hike the last couple of kilometres where the road ends in a meadow.[^4] A 2016 visit report found the tower in good condition, but it noted no sign directing visitors to it.[^1]
