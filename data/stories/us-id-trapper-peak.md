A fire lookout cabin once stood on Trapper Peak in Cassia County, Idaho.[^3] The listings place it within the Sawtooth National Forest.[^1][^2]

The climbing guide places the peak in the South Hills.[^4]

Sources date the cabin only to the 1930s.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts page gives the same decade and cites Kresek for it.[^3]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page says it has no documented staffing history for the lookout.[^2]

Two listings mark the cabin as gone.[^1][^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today.[^1][^2][^3]

A climbing guide says roads of mixed quality reach the base of the peak, and the summit is about 0.4 miles of climbing from the parking spot.[^4]
