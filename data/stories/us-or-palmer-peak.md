Palmer Peak lies in Multnomah County, Oregon, about 20 miles northwest of Mount Hood, in the Mount Hood National Forest.[^1]

In 1913 the Forest Service set up a tent and alidade platform on the peak.[^2][^3] Panorama photos were taken from the peak on July 10, 1930.[^3] A panoramic view from 1933 is credited to the National Archives Pacific Northwest Region.[^2] A 1946 record lists the lookout as an emergency lookout.[^3]

Firetower.org and firelookout.com both describe the 1913 platform as gone.[^2][^1] The Oregon lookout history site lists the lookout as removed.[^3] The register lists the site as US 634, with a registration date of September 19, 2010.[^2] The sources describe the lookout as gone, and none we read says what remains on the peak today or whether it is open to visitors.[^1]
