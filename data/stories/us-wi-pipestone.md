The Pipestone Lookout Tower stands in Sawyer County, Wisconsin.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list shows it as a standing tower.[^2] We found no source that gives the year it was first built, its height, or who built it.

The one dated detail we found is from September 1936. A Forest Lookouts entry says a crew from the Winter Side Camp had started building a new truck trail to the tower's new location.[^1] The entry credits The Voice of 1610, which it ties to CCC Camp Connors Lake.[^1] It does not say whether the tower itself was moved or when it reached that spot.[^1]

The sources we read do not name anyone who staffed the tower or any fire it watched. We found no current information about its condition or public access, so ask the local forest office before going near it.

[^1]: Pipestone, Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/pipestone.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Wisconsin Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
