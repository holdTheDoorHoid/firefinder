The Delta Lookout Tower stood in Bayfield County, Wisconsin, at 46.43229 north and 91.32354 west.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone.[^1]

A 1971 USGS topographic map, as summarized on a map reseller's page, places the lookout in the Lake Delta area of the Chequamegon National Forest and says the tower was used to watch for fires.[^2]

OpenStreetMap still carries a tower node with the same name, tagged as an observation tower and given an elevation of 412 meters.[^3] A map node records where someone mapped a tower, not whether one stands there now.[^3]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]

[^1]: Wisconsin Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: 1971 Map of Delta, Drummond, WI, Pastmaps, https://pastmaps.com/map/delta-bayfield-county-wi-usgs-topo-1971-p1989 (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Node: Delta Lookout Tower (353893422), OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/353893422 (accessed 2026-10-10).
