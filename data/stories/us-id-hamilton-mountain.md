Hamilton Mountain is in Kootenai County, Idaho.[^1] It sits on the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, about eight miles southeast of Lakeview.[^2][^3]

Around 1923 a cupola cabin went up on the northernmost point of the peak.[^2][^3][^4] Most sources call it a log cabin, though one says log and frame.[^2][^3][^4] A patrol platform stood half a mile south.[^2][^3][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts dates the patrol platform to 1926, reading what it thinks is that year in carvings in the cement footings.[^4] The writers rate the evidence as pretty strong, though they are unsure what some of the other carvings say.[^4] The other sites date only the cabin, to about 1923.[^2][^3]

The cabin was destroyed in 1950, and the lookout is now listed as gone.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

When Idaho Fire Lookouts visited, they found little at the cabin site beyond a benchmark and a location post.[^4] At the patrol platform they found cement footings with writing in them.[^4] The site is labeled a hike-in.[^4] No source we read describes the site after that visit, and none says whether the land is open to visitors, so check with the Coeur d'Alene National Forest before you go.
