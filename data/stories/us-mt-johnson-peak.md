Johnson Peak is a former lookout in Flathead County, about 16 miles west of Whitefish, on the Flathead National Forest.[^1][^2] The peak stands at 6,009 feet.[^1] FireLookout.com says the lookout was a 90-foot steel Aermotor tower with an L-4 cab, built in 1934.[^1]

FireLookout.com names Donald Hook as lookout personnel in 1957 and James G. Christianson in 1958.[^1] A 1966 Forest Service list of the lookouts occupied during that year's fire season includes one called "Johnson." The list gives no full name, so it may refer to this peak but the sources do not confirm it.[^4]

The tower was gone by 1984, and heavy snow destroyed its L-4 cab over the winter of 2011 to 2012, according to FireLookout.com.[^1] The page's heading lists 1984 and 2011 as the end dates.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list also marks Johnson Peak as gone.[^3]

FireLookout.com says a wooden patrol tower stands about 1.5 miles northeast of the peak, but the page does not say when it saw the tower there.[^1] No source I read describes what remains on the peak itself, or whether the public can reach the site.[^1][^2][^3]
