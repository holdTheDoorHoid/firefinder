The entry places the Richards Fire Tower in Killaloe, Hagarty and Richards, eastern Ontario, on Lot 2, Concession VII of Richards Township.[^1] It gives the elevation as 341 m (1,119 ft).[^1] The entry describes a light steel tower with guy wires and an octagonal cupola, and gives its total cost as $875.37.[^1]

The tower stopped being manned in 1974.[^1] Its towerman's quarters were at Jack's Lake, along the old Highway 62, and have since been demolished.[^1] The entry says the tower appears on a 1963 Department of Lands and Forests tower detection map.[^1]

The entry says the tower was sold to a private firm for scrap metal value, with no date given.[^1] It also says a 1996 topographic map, the third edition of Pembroke sheet 31F, still shows the tower.[^1]

Apart from the demolished quarters, the entry does not describe the site today. It does not say what, if anything, remains of the tower, or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1]
