Tenas is one of the British Columbia lookouts listed on the Canada Lookouts website.[^1] The entry gives its position as 50.097 N, 119.625 W, and its elevation as 1905, with no unit stated.[^1]

The page also labels the site Bulkley - Nechako. That label does not fit the coordinates given, so the region is unconfirmed.[^1]

No source found describes what, if anything, stands at the site today.

[^1]: Tenas, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/tenas.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
