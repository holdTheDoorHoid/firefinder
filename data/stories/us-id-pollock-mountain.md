Pollock Mountain Lookout is a metal ground cabin on a summit in the Payette National Forest, in Adams County, Idaho.[^1][^3][^4] The register dates the cabin to 1930 and names the Forest Service as the administering agency.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site's timeline begins with a camp in 1911, before the cabin appears.[^4]

The register says the lookout is usually staffed during fire season.[^1] The Fire Lookout site says it is staffed in summer and names a lookout from 1942.[^2] The Idaho Fire Lookouts site lists it as staffed annually, with names running through 2025.[^3]

The register added the lookout on September 29, 2020, as US 1437, ID 129.[^1] The Forest Lookout Association's Idaho list still shows it as standing.[^6]

The Forest Service's Pollock Trailhead page, last updated May 28, 2026, shows no featured alerts and no closure for the trailhead itself.[^5] A closure line on that page applies only to the New Meadows Ranger District office hours, which the page lists as closed until further notice.[^5] The page gives the trailhead season as June through October, depending on snow levels.[^5]

The page directs drivers about seven miles along Smokey Boulder Road, Forest Service Road 074, to the trailhead, and mentions an active fire lookout at the summit.[^5] The Idaho Fire Lookouts site calls the tower hike-in.[^3] Check with the New Meadows Ranger District before any trip.[^5]
