The Forest Fire Lookout Association index lists a Moose River tower in Lewis County, New York, at 43.59567 north and 75.20009 west. It calls the tower a standing windmill tower.[^1] That is nearly all we found.

The index gives no builder, no build year, no list of observers and no fire record, and it says nothing about who owns the land or whether the public can reach it.[^1]

Do not mix this tower up with Moose River Mountain. That is a separate tower in Herkimer County, which the index shows as gone and the Adirondack Architectural Heritage list places among towers removed.[^1][^2]

The index says this Lewis County tower stands.[^1] We found no recent report confirming it. Anyone who wants to see it should first find out who owns the land and whether visitors are allowed.

[^1]: New York Fire Towers, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/ny/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Regional List, Adirondack Architectural Heritage, https://aarch.org/preserve/fire-towers/regional-list/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
