Archer Point Lookout was a 20-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, about 14 miles south of Moose Creek Ranger Station, at 5,061 feet.[^1] A Forest Service list dates it to about 1933.[^4] The Forest Lookouts site gives 1934.[^3]

The site was administered by the Bitterroot National Forest until 1956.[^3] In that year administration moved to the Nez Perce National Forest, according to the Forest Lookouts site.[^3] Firelookout.com and the Forest Service's Nez Perce list both place the site in Nez Perce.[^1][^5]

The lookout was destroyed in 1957.[^1][^3][^4][^5]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site files the site as hike-in and destroyed.[^2] The Forest Service includes Archer Point in its list of Selway-Bitterroot Wilderness lookouts.[^4] No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
