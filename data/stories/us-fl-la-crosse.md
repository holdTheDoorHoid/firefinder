Mapcarta lists the La Crosse Forestry Site as a tower in Alachua County, Florida, and gives LaCrosse Lookout Tower as another name for it.[^1] Mapcarta gives its elevation as 135 feet (41 metres).[^1] The town of La Crosse lies in Alachua County, about 15 miles north of Gainesville.[^1]

A Florida tower list gives the same name, La Crosse Forestry Site, with coordinates of 29.85833 north and 82.40778 west.[^2] The Forest Lookouts website lists the tower under the heading LA CROSSE, Alachua County.[^3]

No source we could read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
