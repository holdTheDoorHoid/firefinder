Mosquito Ridge is a lookout site in Placer County, California, in the Tahoe National Forest.[^1][^2]

In June 1937 the Nevada State Journal reported that Henry G. Bucknell, a Civilian Conservation Corps enrollee from Camp Bradley, would be assigned to the Mosquito Ridge lookout.[^1] The article placed him under the supervision of District Ranger Charles Beardsley of the Foresthill District.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a tower with the status Gone.[^2] Peakbagging lists Mosquito Ridge under its LO code, which covers a building that has been torn down, possibly leaving no trace, or a site whose status is unclear.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the ridge today, and we found nothing on whether the public can visit. Check with the Tahoe National Forest before going.
