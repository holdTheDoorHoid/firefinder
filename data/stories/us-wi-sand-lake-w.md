The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Sand Lake tower in Menominee County, Wisconsin, as standing, at 44.88083 north, 88.55528 west.[^1] Its list gives two towers the Sand Lake name and tells them apart with letters: (W) for this Menominee County tower and (P) for the one in Sawyer County.[^1]

A second list, from the Forest Lookouts site, also places a Sand Lake tower in Menominee County, but it gives only the name and county.[^2]

We found no build date, builder, fire history or owner for this tower. Statewide changes to the fire towers may have affected it, but we found no source that says so. WJFW reports that Wisconsin's 72 towers were decommissioned in 2015.[^3] The Sawyer County Record reports a DNR plan to take towers, mostly in northern Wisconsin, out of service in 2016.[^4] Neither source names a Menominee County tower.[^3][^4]

The association still lists the tower as standing.[^1] We found no recent photos, condition report or access rules for it. Before going near it, find out who manages the site and whether visitors are allowed.
