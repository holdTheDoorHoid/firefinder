Cedar Ridge is a lookout site in the Kaniksu National Forest, in Boundary County, Idaho. The listing puts it 18 miles north of Nordman, at an elevation of 5,295 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Lookouts page gives the code 64N-5W-12.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts and firelookout.com both name the Kaniksu National Forest as its agency.[^2][^3]

Lookout histories date its crow's nest only to the 1930s.[^2][^3]

Sources list the site as abandoned, but none gives a year for that. The statewide list at firelookout.org tags it as a crow's nest and gives its position as 48.90755, -116.92900.[^2][^3][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as drive-up.[^2] None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, remains there today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
