Black Peak is a peak in Sanders County, Montana, about 11 miles southwest of Trout Creek.[^1] The Idaho Fire Lookouts site says it straddles the Montana-Idaho border.[^2] The Fire Lookout page gives its elevation as 6,548 feet.[^1]

A cabin with a stone basement was built on the peak around 1929.[^1][^3] A 1940 Coast and Geodetic Survey description places the lookout house at the highest spot on the peak and calls the forest the Cabinet National Forest.[^3] The Fire Lookout and Forest Lookouts pages both name the Kootenai National Forest.[^1][^3]

The sources disagree about how it ended. The Fire Lookout and Idaho Fire Lookouts pages both say it was destroyed in 1968.[^1][^2] The Montana Forest Lookouts list says it was removed before 1966.[^3]

The Idaho site lists it as a hike-in lookout, marked destroyed.[^2] No source we read describes what remains at the site now, or whether the public can reach it.
