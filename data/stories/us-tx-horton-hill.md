The Horton lookout is in Jasper County, Texas, about seven miles north of Jasper.[^1] Nearby logs were loaded at a railroad switch at Horton, set up after the Gulf, Beaumont and Great Northern Railway was built through northern Jasper County between 1901 and 1903.[^2]

The earliest lookout date we found comes from a survey. A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description, written in 1935, places a survey mark about 40 yards east of State Highway 8.[^1] The mark sits beneath a bolt at the peak of the roof of what the description calls "the lookout house," so that lookout house was already on the site in 1935.[^1] The description gives no height and names no builder.

The Texas State Historical Association says the Horton lookout tower, three miles to the south, marked the area in the mid-1980s.[^2] The entry does not say three miles from what, and the sources do not say whether this is the tower the 1935 survey describes.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as Horton Hill, standing.[^3] The association marks a tower with an asterisk when its framework remains but its cab is no longer in place, and Horton Hill carries that mark.[^3] We found no date for when the cab came off, and no account of who staffed the tower or which fires it watched.

We found no statement about public access, so please do not climb the tower. Before visiting, check with the landowner or the Texas Forest Service.[^4]
