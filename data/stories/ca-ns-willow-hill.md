Willow Hill was a tower on the hill in Ardoise, Hants, Nova Scotia, that Nova Scotia's Department of Natural Resources listed under its DNR Bible Hill entry.[^1][^2]

An unofficial list of the province's towers, drawn from reports by scanner listeners and last updated in September 2009, gives its location as on the hill in Ardoise and says the tower "might be closed as of 2009."[^1] The list gives that closure date only as what area listeners reported.[^1]

The same list describes 33 seasonal towers across the province, with many more in the past.[^1]

The province decommissioned its lookout towers in 2015 and then dismantled them, CBC News reports, in favour of aerial surveillance.[^3] That article does not name Willow Hill, and its 2015 date refers to the towers as a group, so the sources do not settle when this tower ended.[^1][^3]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors. Do not assume a tower still stands there.
