Flat Lake sits in Becker County, inside the Tamarac National Wildlife Refuge.[^1][^2] An earlier lookout there was a wooden tower that the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service built in 1938.[^1] It came down once the steel tower went up.[^1]

The steel tower was officially called the Tamarac Wildlife Refuge Fire Tower.[^1] The county report dates it to 1954 and 1955, and says it was moved to Flat Lake from North Dakota.[^1] It stood about 100 feet tall on four steel legs, with a cabin of small, green-painted metal at the top.[^1]

The report says the steel tower was removed in the mid-1980s, without giving an exact year.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Flat Lake as gone.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.[^1][^2]
