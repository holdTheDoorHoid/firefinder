The Sharon fire lookout stands on Moose Hill in Norfolk County.[^1] The tower there today is a 75-foot steel tower with a 10-by-10-foot cab, built in 1966.[^1] The Register says the tower it replaced had been raised in 1917, and that this earlier tower had itself stood in for a 1912 tower on Bluff Head, half a mile south.[^1]

Other sources give other dates for the Moose Hill predecessor. A lookout history page says a new 40-foot tower with stairs went up at Moose Hill in 1916, and that several nearby towns contributed toward its purchase price.[^2] A separate former-site record, which calls the older hill Bluff Hill, says that 1912 tower stood on private land with the landowner's consent. It was staffed until 1916, when it came down once the fire season ended.[^3]

In 2015 a state crew worked on nine Massachusetts towers, and the Sharon tower had its inside floor and an inside wall replaced.[^4] I did not find a source that says who staffed the standing tower or what fires it watched.

Mass Audubon describes its Moose Hill sanctuary as the oldest and largest it runs, with woods, meadows, wetlands and hiking trails.[^5] The sources do not say whether the tower stands inside that sanctuary or whether visitors may climb it. Check with Mass Audubon before any visit, and do not climb the tower unless it is clearly open to the public.
