Vanderburg Mountain sits in Sanders County, Montana, on the Flathead Indian Reservation.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com dates its tower to 1935 and describes it as a 50-foot pole tower of the L-4 type.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page lists the site under the Flathead Indian Agency and gives its location as township 18 North, range 24 West, section 13.[^2] In July 1959, an item cited to the Char-Koosta reported that Louie Parker was on the Vanderburg tower in the Flathead Agency District.[^2]

Firelookout.com says the tower was destroyed in 1961, after the July 1959 item.[^1] The Montana list files it as a tower and marks it gone.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today, and none says whether the mountain is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3]
