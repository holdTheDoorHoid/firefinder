Round Oak Lookout Tower is listed in Jones County, Georgia, at about 33.12 N, 83.63 W.[^1] The 2016 tower list counts it among its first 72 entries, which the list says were taken from the USGS name database.[^1] TopoZone's Jones County tower list pairs the name with the Dames Ferry USGS map.[^2]

The list gives the tower two Georgia Forestry Commission references. BLL-41099 points to a set of coordinates the agency supplied, and gfc-608 points to an older agency list of tower locations.[^1] A separate Georgia list of forest lookouts also has a Round Oak entry, listed under the label "Jones County - State".[^3]

Another point in the same tower list, named Round Oak Old, sits about 2 km (roughly 6,400 feet) southeast of this one. That distance is calculated from the two listed coordinates, not stated by the list.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether a tower still stands there.
