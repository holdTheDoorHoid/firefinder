A November 1950 list of observation towers in Hillsborough County, printed by the Tampa Tribune, named a "State Park tower" on Road 301 at Hillsborough State Park. The towerwoman was Dorothy Griffin.[^1] The park, which Wikipedia calls Hillsborough River State Park, lies in the northeast corner of the county near Zephyrhills and is one of the eight original Florida State Parks, created in 1938.[^2] The Civilian Conservation Corps originally built the park, and some of those early structures still stand.[^2] Damage from Hurricane Milton forced the park to close in October 2024; it reopened on February 24, 2025.[^2] It covers 3,383 acres and has more than seven miles of trails.[^2] No source we found describes the lookout site today.
