The Copeland tower was a 40-foot pole tower in the yard of an old ranger station in the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^2] The station sits about 14 miles north of Bonners Ferry, in Boundary County, Idaho, at about 1,800 feet of elevation.[^1] Both sites date the tower to 1928.[^1][^2]

Neither page names anyone who staffed the tower or any fire it watched.[^1][^2]

Both sites say the tower was abandoned in the 1940s, and neither says whether the pole still stands.[^1][^2] A Boundary County history blog lists Copeland among the ranger stations once scattered across the county.[^3]

A statewide lookout list marks the tower as standing, which conflicts with the abandonment reports, so its present condition is unknown.[^4]

We found no recent visit report for the site. Idaho Fire Lookouts tags it as drive-up, but the tower stood in the yard of an old station, so check with the forest before going to look for it.[^2]
