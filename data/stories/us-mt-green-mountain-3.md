Green Mountain in Sanders County, Montana, stands about eight miles north of Trout Creek, in the Kootenai National Forest.[^1] Firelookout.com says the site was developed as a camp in 1933, and that its 50-foot pole L-4 tower was built in 1936.[^1]

The lookout had staff in the 1950s. Sandpoint News-Bulletin items, quoted on a local history page, show Dean Henderson on the mountain in the summer of 1956 and stationed there again in 1957, when he came down after 73 days.[^2] In 1958 a crew began repairing the telephone line to the lookout.[^2] Firelookout.com lists Charley Madsen as lookout personnel in 1959.[^1]

The tower's end is recorded two ways. Firelookout.com says it was destroyed in 1966.[^1] The local history page lists the site only as "Removed" and gives no date.[^2] A Forest Fire Lookout Association list marks the site as gone.[^3]

No source I found describes what, if anything, is left on the mountain, or whether the site is open to visitors.
