Dobry Point was a tree lookout.[^1][^3] Around 1934 a crow's nest with a tent cabin was erected there, in the Mount Hood region of Wasco County, Oregon, at about 4,000 feet.[^1][^2]

The Forest Lookouts page puts the nest at the crown of a pine about 120 feet tall. A second tree was felled against that pine to act as a ladder.[^3]

The page gives the lookout's 1936 telephone ring as two short, one long and three short. It also labels the post a supplemental lookout.[^3]

The sources differ on whether a tent cabin stood there. The register and firelookout.com mention one, but the Forest Lookouts page does not mention a tent cabin.[^1][^2][^3]

The register lists the site as now gone and says it was registered on August 17, 2010, on the nomination of Ray Kresek.[^1] firelookout.org lists it as abandoned.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, or whether it is open to visitors. The register says the U.S. Forest Service administers the site.[^1]
