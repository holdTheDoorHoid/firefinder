The Turnpike lookout stood in Pope County, Arkansas.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page names the Ozark National Forest as its agency and labels the site "Turnpike Freeman Springs."[^1]

A 1954 Coast and Geodetic Survey station description locates the station about 20 miles north of Dover and seven miles south of Lurton, a tenth of a mile east of State Highway 7.[^1] It describes the lookout as a four-legged tower about 85 feet high, carrying a house about six feet by six feet.[^1] The surveyors also made a traverse connection to the tower from triangulation station Freeman R.M. 3, about 53 feet away.[^1]

The listing marks the lookout as removed.[^1] No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Turnpike, Forest Lookouts (centraluslookouts.weebly.com), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/turnpike.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
