The Wood Hump lookout was an L-4 cab on a pole tower in the Bitterroot National Forest, built in 1940.[^1][^2][^3] The sources disagree on the height of the pole.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts give 15 feet, while Rex's Fire Lookout Page gives 20 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

The sources name one person who staffed the lookout: Lavon Andrews, listed for 1961.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts also asks readers to add what they know about the staff and the dates.[^2]

The tower was abandoned in 1967.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com says it was still in place in 1974 but has since departed.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the lookout as gone, and so does the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list.[^2][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the elevation as 7,224 feet and marks the lookout as hike-in.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
