Black Mountain Lookout stands on the Clearwater National Forest in Clearwater County, Idaho, at about 7,077 feet.[^1][^2]

According to the register, the site dates back to the 1930s.[^1] Kamstra's page and Idaho Fire Lookouts date an L-4 cab on the site to 1932.[^2][^3] A 10-foot concrete base was built in 1952 under that cab.[^2][^3] In 1972 an R-6 flat cab took the place of the L-4 cab on that same base.[^2][^3] The register gives 1972 as the build year for the present structure, describes it as a 14-by-14-foot modified R-6 flattop cab with catwalk, and calls it the site's third structure.[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts places older patrol towers about one mile southwest and two miles south.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists known staff starting in 1963.[^3] Kamstra's page says summer staffing continues.[^2] The same site reports that the tower is currently staffed, in a September 2025 article.[^3]

The register added the lookout on April 6, 2009.[^1] It lists the tower as in active service under the U.S. Forest Service, with the North Fork Ranger District as cooperator.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts describes the visit as hike-in.[^3] The pages read do not describe visiting rules or trail conditions, so anyone planning a hike should check with the North Fork Ranger District first.
