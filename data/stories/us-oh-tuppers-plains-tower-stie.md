The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register places the former Tuppers Plains tower in Meigs County, Ohio, at an elevation of 920 feet (280 m).[^1] The community of Tuppers Plains sits on the line between Olive and Orange townships and had 438 residents at the 2020 census.[^3] The register describes the structure as a 97-foot tower and dates its entry to September 3, 2014.[^1] It is filed as US 1574, OH 20, and it spells the name "Tuppers Plains Tower Stie," so searches may need both spellings.[^1]

The association's Ohio list marks the tower as gone, so there is no standing tower to climb.[^2] Nothing in the sources describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.
