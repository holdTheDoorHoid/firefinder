Woodward, in Centre County, Pennsylvania, had a wooden fire observation tower.[^1] A 1919 item in the forestry publication *Forest Leaves*, quoted on a lookout history page, reported that a modern wooden tower had gone up near Woodward, linked by telephone to the foresters' headquarters and the rangers.[^1] An earlier state report, from 1918-19, already mentions "the Woodward tower" when it lists telephone rights of way, so the tower may have stood before 1919.[^1]

In June 1920, the Lewisburg Journal reported that the forester planned to build a stairway that would let people admire the views across Union and Centre counties from its summit.[^1] That item describes a plan, not a finished stairway.[^1]

The lookout history page marks the tower as removed.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public.

[^1]: Woodward, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/woodward.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
