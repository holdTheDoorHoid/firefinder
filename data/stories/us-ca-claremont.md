Claremont Mountain is a summit south of Quincy, in Plumas County, on the Plumas National Forest.[^1][^2]

The earliest plan for a lookout on the mountain that we found appeared in October 1907.[^1] A Plumas newspaper said a telephone line would reach the summit the next season, along with a summer ranger camp and a lookout station for the fire season.[^1] A November 1907 item said the line would be built from Quincy in the spring and a fire lookout established during the dry months.[^1]

By May 1909 the forest said the station on Claremont Mountain would be maintained through the summer season.[^1] A November 1909 report credited the station with helping to locate fires that year.[^1] The same report counted 85 fires that forest officers had put out or helped to put out on or next to the Plumas Forest.[^1]

In June 1911 the paper said that Claremont and Mount Hough, another lookout peak, would both be maintained, each with a telephone link to headquarters.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Claremont site as a ground station that is gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the summit is open to visitors.
