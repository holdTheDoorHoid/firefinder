The Glen Lookout Tower stands in Alcorn County, in the northeastern part of the state.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as a 100-foot Aermotor tower and gives its elevation as 602 feet (183 meters).[^1]

The register does not say when the tower went up. It lists the build date as unknown.[^1] Forest Lookouts shows the tower only under Alcorn County, with no other detail.[^2] We found no record of who staffed the tower or of any fire it watched.

The Mississippi Forestry Commission administers the site, according to the register.[^1] The register spells the agency's name with a typo, "Commision." The commission's own website uses the correct spelling.[^1][^3]

The site has a house and a few outbuildings, and the register says the property seems to be out of use.[^1] Jason Johns nominated the tower, and it was registered on June 23, 2022, as US 1617, MS 13.[^1] We found no public access information. Anyone curious should ask the Forestry Commission before going near the site.
