Ball Mountain rises to about 6,567 feet in Tehama County, and the register places its lookout site in the Mendocino National Forest.[^1] The register says the lookout was built for that forest in 1935, as a Region 5 Plan BC-301 cabin on a 20-foot enclosed timber tower.[^1]

A 1936 newspaper notice, quoted on a lookout history page, tells a different story.[^2] The notice says the government had used the spot for years, had bought it from Pickering Lumber, and planned a new station on it.[^2] The 1935 date and the 1936 notice do not agree, and no other source we found settles which is right.[^1][^2]

The sources also differ on the forest.[^1][^2] A lookout history page that carries the 1936 notice heads the site as part of the Shasta National Forest.[^2] The register names the Mendocino.[^1]

The register gives the removal date as unknown, and says only the foundation remains at the site.[^1] No source we found says whether the public can reach it today.[^1]

[^2]: Ball Mountain - FOREST LOOKOUTS, quoting a 1936 newspaper notice, Forest Lookouts (California), https://californialookouts.weebly.com/ball-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
