Across Tennessee, the Civilian Conservation Corps erected 98 lookout towers between 1933 and 1941.[^1] A personal inventory of Tennessee lookout towers, published at tnlandforms.us, lists Skinner Mountain as a tower in Fentress County, at an elevation of 1,759 feet (536 m).[^1] Its district-and-tower number, 4-3, comes from a 1976 list of state forest towers.[^1]

The inventory marks the tower as removed.[^1] Its author gave some towers unofficial names where the USGS maps and the state forestry list had none.[^1]

A separate tower called Pickett, also in Fentress County, is listed as standing at 1,720 feet (524 m).[^1] The inventory lists it apart from Skinner Mountain.[^1]

It also says many towers have since been taken down, and that most former sites are now privately owned.[^1] No source I found describes what, if anything, remains on Skinner Mountain today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Tennessee lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
