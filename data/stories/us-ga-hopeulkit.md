A web list of Georgia lookout towers includes an entry named hopeulkit, at 32.521213, -81.86047 in Bulloch County.[^1] The list says names for towers from number 73 onward are its author's own, so the name is a label, and its spelling differs from that of the community Hopeulikit, which is in the same county.[^1][^2]

Hopeulikit is an unincorporated community in Bulloch County, about eight miles northwest of Statesboro.[^2] Its unusual name is said to derive from a dance hall of the 1920s.[^2]

The entry's only source note is "topo".[^1] The list's notes say some towers were found on 1:100,000-scale USGS maps and NGS data sheets, and this entry carries no Georgia Forestry Commission number.[^1]

No source I found calls it a fire lookout, and none describes the site today. What, if anything, stands there, and whether the public may visit, is unknown.
