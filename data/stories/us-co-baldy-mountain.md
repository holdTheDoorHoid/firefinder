Baldy Mountain stands in La Plata County, Colorado, on the San Juan National Forest, at 9,805 feet.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says a tower was built there in the 1920s.[^1]

A San Juan National Forest handbook whose fire records begin in 1909 says that other lookouts were planned for Baldy Mountain, to be built later.[^4] If that means this peak, it sits badly with a tower the register dates to the 1920s. The handbook does not say which Baldy Mountain it means, so we cannot tell whether it describes this one.[^4]

The register says the tower was later removed.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site and the Forest Fire Lookout Association list both mark it as removed.[^2][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit, and none says whether the public can reach it. The register lists the U.S. Forest Service as the administering agency.[^1]
