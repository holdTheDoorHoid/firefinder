Mount Hagen, also spelled Hagan, is in the Willamette National Forest, about 12 miles west of McKenzie Bridge in Lane County, Oregon.[^1][^2][^3] The register lists the U.S. Forest Service as the administrator.[^1] The first lookout, dated to 1933, combined a tree platform with an L-5 cab set on the ground for living quarters.[^1][^2] A September 1933 item from the Eugene Guard, quoted by the Forest Lookouts history, says a crew was then building a trail to the peak and a lookout house on it.[^3]

In 1953, eight miles of road replaced the old walk-in trail to the lookout.[^3] The cab was replaced in 1955 by a tower.[^1][^2][^3] The sources disagree on its height. The register and firelookout.com give 25 feet, while the Forest Lookouts history gives 24 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

The register says the tower was gone by 1979. The Forest Lookouts history says it was dismantled and salvaged that year.[^1][^3] The firelookout.com heading gives the lookout's dates as 1933 to 1955, but the same page says the tower was gone by 1979.[^2]

The register added the site on May 7, 2011, as US 960, OR 318.[^1] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
