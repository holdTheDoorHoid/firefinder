Bon Wier, an unincorporated place in eastern Newton County, lies along U.S. Route 190.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site lists Bon Wier, in Newton County, as a Texas Forest Service lookout.[^2] A separate Texas tower list places a Bon Wier Lookout Tower at 30.79833, -93.70806, in Newton County.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list gives the same point and marks the tower as gone.[^4]

No source we read describes the site today.
