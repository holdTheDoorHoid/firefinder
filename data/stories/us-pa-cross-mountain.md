The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes Cross Mountain's fire tower as a steel I-S tower and gives 1934 as its build year.[^1]

A March 1935 Gettysburg Times item, quoted by the Forest Lookouts history, announced an 80-foot fire tower on Cross Mountain that would soon be erected by the Pennsylvania department of forests. The two sources therefore disagree on the year.[^2] The item said the tower would provide fire detection for the western part of Washington County, Maryland, as well as Franklin County.[^2]

The register says the tower was removed in 1962.[^1]

Today the mountain carries a different structure. A Cold War site history describes the Cannonball facility atop Cross Mountain, near Shimpstown, as a reinforced concrete microwave tower about 103 feet tall, one of the Presidential Emergency Facilities sites.[^3] The facility sits on private property, and the public cannot reach it.[^3] The same history says the tower was abandoned in 1977, that its dishes and communications equipment are gone, and that its door has been stolen.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains of the fire tower, so it is unclear whether any trace of it survives.[^1][^2]
