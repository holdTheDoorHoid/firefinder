Deer Mountain, in Oxford County, got a 36-foot steel tower in 1926.[^1] A crew that had been rebuilding the Saddleback tower finished the erection in September that year.[^1]

A 1935 Lewiston newspaper item gives the tower's height as 39 feet, so the sources do not agree on its height.[^1]

In 1950 the tower was taken apart and carried off the mountain.[^2] A Lewiston Sun Journal list of Maine towers gives the same year, and places the mountain in unorganized territory T4, R2.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as US 102, ME 28.[^2] It was registered on September 15, 2008, on the nomination of Henry Isenberg.[^2] The register gives no coordinates for it.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on Deer Mountain today, or whether the summit is open to visitors.
