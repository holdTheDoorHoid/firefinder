Sherwood Mountain sits on the Spokane Indian Reservation in Stevens County, about two miles southeast of Wellpinit.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register lists the reservation as the administrator.[^1]

The register and Firelookout both date the lookout to 1936, when an 83-foot steel Aermotor tower was built with an L-4 cab for living quarters.[^1][^2] WillhiteWeb says the early record is unclear.[^4] It suggests the 1936 station may have been the L-4 cab alone, while some accounts say the steel tower went up that year and the cab housed the living quarters.[^4]

The register and Firelookout say the tower and cab were abandoned in the 1950s.[^1][^2] WillhiteWeb reports the steel tower still standing in 1979.[^4] The tower was gone by 1990.[^1][^2][^4] Forest Lookouts lists the site as removed.[^3]

The site entered the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register on December 17, 2012, as US 1526, WA 426, with Ray Kresek as nominator.[^1]

The only description of the site today is WillhiteWeb's visit.[^4] The author found that trees on the site have grown tall enough to block the views.[^4] The author saw what seemed to be the foundation of an L-4 cab and metal pieces near the ground surface, but no footings from a tower.[^4]

From Wellpinit, a dirt road climbs to the summit once the pavement ends, and the author says only one spot would have been hard for a standard vehicle.[^4] None of the sources says whether visitors need permission from the tribe to go up, or whether the site is open to the public.
