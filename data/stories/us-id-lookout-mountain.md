The Lookout Mountain site is about 14 miles southwest of Avery, in Shoshone County, Idaho, at an elevation of 6,757 feet.[^1][^2] One source places it in the St. Joe National Forest and the other in the St. Joe / Clearwater National Forest.[^1][^2]

The lookout went up in 1934 as a 10-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2] Its staff list is short.[^2] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page names three people: Adams in 1925, Charles Cartwright in 1967, and Wayne Koski in 1969.[^2] It credits the 1925 name to a Forest Service photo collection and the 1967 name to the Kellogg Evening News.[^2] The 1925 entry is older than the 1934 build year on the same page, and the page does not explain the gap.[^2]

The lookout was abandoned in 1970.[^1][^2] The Idaho lookout index now lists the tower as gone.[^3]

A visitor's post dated August 14, 2025 describes the hike in as a moderate trip of about three miles, on a trail that motorcycles also use.[^2] The writer reported scattered tower scrap, including hinges and window glass, plus cans and bottle pieces.[^2] The writer also noticed what may be a rock structure, possibly used for cold storage.[^2] No source we read describes a standing tower there today. Anyone planning a trip should check with the national forest about access before going.
