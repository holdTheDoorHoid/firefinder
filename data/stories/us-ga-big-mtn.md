A gazetteer page that cites a Geographic Names Information System report describes Big Mountain as a summit in Oglethorpe County, Georgia.[^1] That report carries no ownership details, so the page cannot say whether the land is public or private.[^1]

The Georgia tower survey lists a site called big mtn in Oglethorpe County, at 33.80440, -83.03184, and numbers it GA087.[^2] The survey says that for towers numbered 73 and above, the names are its own, so the name big mtn comes from the survey.[^2] The Forest Lookouts index also lists Big Mountain in Oglethorpe County.[^3]

No source I found describes the summit today, so the public record does not say whether a tower stands there or whether the spot is open to visitors.

[^2]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Georgia - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/georgia.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
