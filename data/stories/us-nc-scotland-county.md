A list of North Carolina lookout towers includes a tower called Scotland County, with the county given as Scotland. It gives the coordinates as 34.94527, -79.44611.[^1]

The North Carolina Forest Service keeps a Scotland County office on US 15-501, about ten miles north of Laurinburg.[^2] The county's web page for that office shows a photograph captioned "Fire Tower" alongside pictures of the office entrance and a brush truck.[^3] The caption does not say where that tower stands, or whether it is the one on the list.

No source describes what remains at the site today.
