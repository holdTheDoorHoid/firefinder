The Seven Mile Point lookout site sits at 4,851 feet in the Clearwater National Forest, about 15 miles northeast of Pierce in Shoshone County, Idaho.[^1][^2]

A 20-foot L-4 tower was built there in 1941.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts says Warren Yahr staffed the lookout in 1945 and mentions his book Smokechaser.[^2] Yahr's own page on the same website lists Bear Butte for 1945 to 1948 and does not mention Seven Mile Point, so the 1945 posting is not settled.[^3]

The book, Smokechaser, was published in 1995, and its catalog record gives subject headings for fire lookout stations in the Clearwater National Forest.[^4]

The site was destroyed in 1962.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site drive-up.[^2] No source we read describes what remains there today or whether the land is open to visitors, so check with the Clearwater National Forest before you go.
