Banner, an unincorporated place in Calhoun County, Mississippi, lies along Mississippi Highway 9W about 7 miles (11 km) north-northwest of Bruce.[^3]

A federal survey datasheet for the site is titled "Banner Fire Tower." It codes the marker as a lookout tower and says the Coast and Geodetic Survey first observed the station in 1967.[^2]

The datasheet places the station at about 34°07′07″ N, 89°23′06″ W, in NAD 83.[^2]

The Eastern US Lookouts page for Banner marks the lookout as removed.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today.[^1][^2]
