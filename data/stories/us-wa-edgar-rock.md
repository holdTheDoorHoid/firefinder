Edgar Rock is a rocky outcrop in the Wenatchee National Forest, in Yakima County, Washington.[^1][^2] A Forest Service lookout once stood on top of it, a ground cab above the Cliffdell summer homes.[^1][^2] The Register dates the cab to 1933, and WillhiteWeb says it was built around that year.[^1][^2] A WillhiteWeb photo is captioned as the lookout in 1942.[^2] The sources give the summit's elevation as 3,600 feet in one place and 3,680 feet in another.[^1][^2]

We found no source that names anyone who staffed the lookout, or any fire it watched.

The Register says the cab was removed in 1951.[^1] WillhiteWeb says it was destroyed around that year, and Fire Lookout says it was destroyed in the 1950s.[^2][^3] Forest Lookouts lists it only as removed.[^4]

WillhiteWeb reports that fasteners and leftover pieces from the lookout remain on the summit, and that the rock still commands the views a lookout would have had.[^2] The site is reached by a trail about two miles long that climbs 1,300 feet.[^2] From Highway 410, the directions turn onto Jefferson Road and then Lost Creek Road, where the trailhead has parking for two or three vehicles.[^2] No source we read gives a recent condition report, so check with the Naches Ranger District before you go.
