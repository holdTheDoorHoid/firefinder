The Virginia lookout list at firelookout.org includes a tower called Joplin in Prince William County. It gives the site as 38.56061 north, 77.38453 west, and marks the tower as gone.[^1]

The name matches Joplin, an unincorporated community and former town in Prince William County.[^2] Wikipedia gives the community's coordinates as 38°33′39″N 77°23′6″W, so the two points sit roughly 50 metres apart.[^1][^2]

The town's land was taken to form the Chopawamsic Recreational Demonstration Area.[^2] The National Park Service's history of the park says site selection for that project began in the summer of 1934 near Joplin.[^3] The former town is now part of Prince William Forest Park, which sits beside Marine Corps Base Quantico.[^2]

No source describes the Joplin tower site today, so it is not known whether footings or other remains survive, or whether the spot is open to visitors.[^1]
