Divide Mountain sits on the Blackfeet Indian Reservation, about five miles south of Saint Mary in Glacier County, Montana.[^1][^3] The register gives its location as Glacier National Park.[^2]

A forestry report for the reservation says a 14-by-14 ready-cut lookout house went up on the east slope of the mountain in June 1931.[^1] It sat on a stone foundation.[^1] Cables ran from each corner to anchors set in the rock, and a telephone connected it to the Park Service system.[^1]

A windstorm in November 1931 demolished the building, and only the floor and one wall survived.[^1] In 1932 the agency said it lacked the money to rebuild.[^1] A replacement was finished by fiscal 1934, and the register describes it as an octagon intended to help it resist the high winds there.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com dates its octagonal cab to about 1932, and its header gives 1938 as a start year.[^3] The sources disagree on the date: the 1934 report and the register point to 1934, while firelookout.com says about 1932.[^1][^2][^3]

The 1932 report planned to pay an observer from July 25 until the high fire hazard ended, which it expected to be about September 10.[^1] Because the spot was so exposed, the observer was to live about half a mile away, in a tent the agency would supply.[^1] The 1934 report planned a lookout to be stationed there, with a telephone connection to the Park Service lines.[^1]

Firelookout.com says the cab was abandoned for many years before it collapsed over the winter of 2021–22.[^3] Firetower.org says that as of 2022 the foundation remains, while wind has destroyed the structure itself.[^2]

An NPS article on the panoramic photo project, last updated August 12, 2025, names Divide Lookout as one of the few that remain from the period of Lester Moe's panoramic photo work.[^4] That clashes with the firetower.org and firelookout.com pages, which say the structure is gone, and I could not reconcile the two.[^2][^3] The register lists the site as administered by the National Park Service, with the Blackfeet Nation as a cooperator, and records its registration on December 25, 2022.[^2] The register describes only the foundation, as of 2022.[^2]
