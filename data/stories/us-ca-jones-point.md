Jones Point is in the Stanislaus National Forest, in Tuolumne County, California.[^1] A lookout was built here in 1936.[^1] The register describes a ground cabin built to the California Region 5 BC-301 plan, set on concrete piers in the Groveland Ranger District.[^1] The register offers few details about this lookout.[^1]

The register has a 1964 photograph of the lookout from the Stanislaus National Forest archive, a 1974 photo, and a 2011 photo.[^1] It places the lookout's disappearance between the 1974 photo and the 1986 Thornton Survey, so the exact year is unknown.[^1]

The register's text does not describe what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1] We found no other source on the site and nothing on public access, so check with the Stanislaus National Forest before visiting.

[^1]: Jones Point Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ca/jones-point-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
