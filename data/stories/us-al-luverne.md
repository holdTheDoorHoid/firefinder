Luverne Lookout stood in Crenshaw County, Alabama, and the Alabama Forestry Commission administered it.[^1][^2] The Register says the commission built it in 1948 as a 100-foot MC-39 Aermotor with a 7-by-7-foot cab.[^1] It also says the tower was once the county's last fire tower.[^1]

The sources disagree about when it came down. A Forest Lookouts page says that in March 2013 the tower was taken down and sent to New York, to be restored and set up somewhere new.[^3] The Register says it was removed before April 2019.[^1][^2]

The Register registered the tower on June 23, 2009, and the former-site listing followed on March 25, 2020.[^1][^2] A photo on the former-site listing shows the tower's foundation in January 2024.[^2] Since the Register says the tower is gone, there is no standing tower to climb.[^1] The sources say nothing about who staffed it, what fires it watched, or whether the public may visit the site.
