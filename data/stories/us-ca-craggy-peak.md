Craggy Peak is in Siskiyou County, near Callahan, on the Klamath National Forest.[^1][^2] The lookout site register puts the earliest possible build date at 1915.[^1] It describes a ground cabin with an odd window layout, which may follow a California Region 5 Plan 4A design.[^1]

Records quoted on the Forest Lookouts page show the lookout already in place by June 1915, when a Shasta Forest supervisor was on his way to inspect it.[^2] That October, Mr. Corey and his wife were still at the lookout.[^2] He reported a fire near Granite Creek. A U.S. ranger and a state firewarden reached it quickly and extinguished it.[^2] In October 1921, a Mr. Friday was filling the fire warden post at the lookout station.[^2]

The register gives the removal date as unknown.[^1] Only a foundation is left.[^1] We found no source that describes the site today, or says whether it is open to the public, so check with the Klamath National Forest before you go.

[^2]: Craggy Peak - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts, https://californialookouts.weebly.com/craggy-peak.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
