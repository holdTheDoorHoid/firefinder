The Parting Springs Tower Site was a West Virginia Division of Forestry lookout in Randolph County.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register places it at 4,004 feet, or 1,220 meters, and lists it as US 1689 and WV 80.[^1]

We found no build date, design, height or staffing history for the tower in the sources we could read. The register page gives its registration and location details, and little else.[^1] The register entry was made on January 26, 2017, on the nomination of Brian M. Powell.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's West Virginia list marks the Parting Springs tower as gone.[^2] The same list places a standing tower at Godwin, also in Randolph County, and describes it as relocated from Parting Springs.[^2] The list marks the Godwin location as private, and gives no owner, build date or account of the move. The Godwin claim therefore rests on that one list.

Today, the sources do not describe what remains at the Parting Springs site. Godwin is marked private,[^2] so visitors should ask the owner before going near it, and should not climb the tower.

[^1]: Parting Springs Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/wv/parting-springs-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: West Virginia, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wv/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
