The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list gives a Peach Tree Village tower in Tyler County, at 30.97220, -94.60355, and marks it as gone.[^1]

The register's name points to Peach Tree Village, a ghost town two miles north of Chester, on the site of an Alabama Indian settlement called Ta-Ku-La.[^1][^2][^3] A ghost-town guide gives the village's coordinates as 30°56’41″N, 94°36’47″W.[^4] On those figures alone, the register's point lies about 3 km north-northeast of the village.[^1][^4]

The Tyler County heritage collection says Camp Ta-Ku-La, a Christian camp, now maintains the village's chapel and the Kirby home.[^5] That describes the village buildings, not the tower.

No source found describes what remains of the tower site, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1]

The 2025 book Fire in the Piney Woods describes the location and design of Texas lookout towers, and is a place to look for a fuller record.[^6]
