The Department of Lands and Mines report for 1937 names a Doaktown Lookout Station in Northumberland County, New Brunswick.[^1] That year part of the telephone line from Doaktown to the station was rebuilt in a more satisfactory location, using warden labour where possible.[^1]

In 1948 about 9.7 km (6 mi) of metallic circuit telephone line on poles was erected to connect the Doaktown tower to the Doaktown exchange, by way of the New Betts Mills Brook Settlement Road and the Grand Lake Road.[^1] The report expected the new line to give the tower good telephone service, and the old line, which badly needed repair, was taken down.[^1] The two entries come from the 77th and 112th annual reports of the Department of Lands and Mines, and neither says outright that they describe the same lookout.[^1]

No source describes the site today, including whether the tower still stands or whether the public can reach it.
