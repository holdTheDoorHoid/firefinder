Petway is a fire tower in Cheatham County, Tennessee. The Tennessee Landforms list gives its elevation as 761 feet and marks it as standing. It is numbered 6-10, a district-and-tower number taken from a state list made in 1976.[^1]

I could not find a build date, a builder, or a design for this tower. The Division of Forestry survey lists a Petway tower in Cheatham County among the towers standing as of March 26, 2014.[^2] The survey says records for the 1930s towers are scarce. It also says the Civilian Conservation Corps is reported to have built 98 lookout houses and towers in Tennessee during that decade. That figure covers the whole state, not this tower.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts page for Petway gives only the name and the county.[^3]

The sources do not settle who runs the tower. A fire department page for Station 3, on Petway Road, says the station sits in the Cheatham Wildlife Management Area "near the TWRA fire tower."[^4] That page does not name the tower, so it may not mean this one. The 2014 survey counts Petway with the state Division of Forestry towers.[^2]

The wildlife area is reached from State Route 250, also called Petway Road.[^5] I found no route to the tower itself. Tennessee Landforms advises against climbing lookout towers, because the steps are often missing or decayed.[^1] Check access with the wildlife agency before you go, and do not climb the tower.
