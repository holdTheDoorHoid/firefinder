A tower called Vaughn is on the Georgia lookout list kept on the tnlandforms.us site.[^1] The list places it in Columbia County and tags it with National Geodetic Survey (NGS) datasheet DF1298.[^1] The list's towers were found on USGS topographic maps and in NGS survey datasheets.[^1] That datasheet describes a survey station named VAUGHN, about nine miles west of central Augusta.[^2]

The U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey marked the station in 1932 and described it in 1934.[^2] The description places it on the property of W.F. Vaughn, and the disk is stamped VAUGHN 1932.[^2]

The only fire-tower mention in the datasheet comes from a 1955 recovery note.[^2] It places the station's disk an estimated quarter mile northeast of a fire-lookout tower.[^2] The list's point for the tower lies about 0.3 km (0.2 mi) southwest of the surveyed station, which fits that note.[^1][^2] It is not certain, though, that the note and the list describe the same structure.

A 1980 recovery note by the Georgia Department of Transportation found the station mark in a ditch close to the road.[^2] The same note mentions a steel survey tower used during work on a reference mark.[^2] That was a different thing from a fire lookout.

No source we found says whether a fire tower still stands at this spot, or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: NGS Data Sheet, PID DF1298 (VAUGHN), National Geodetic Survey, NOAA, https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=DF1298 (accessed 2026-10-21).
