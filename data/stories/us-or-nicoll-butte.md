Nicoll Butte is in Harney County, Oregon, about 37 miles west-northwest of Burns.[^2][^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it under the Ochoco National Forest.[^1]

The lookout was built in 1952.[^1][^2] The register and firelookout.com both describe a 40-foot wooden L-4 tower.[^1][^2] A July 1952 inspection report rated the workmanship on the new 40-foot wood tower as excellent.[^3] It also flagged two split diagonal braces in the lower panel for replacement, and one missing bolt.[^3]

The register added the site on June 24, 2011, on the nomination of Ray Kresek.[^1]

Both the register and firelookout.com date the end to 1970.[^1][^2] The register says the tower was removed, and firelookout.com says destroyed.[^1][^2] The Oregon Lookouts page lists it as removed without a date.[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, or whether it is open to visitors. The tower is listed as removed, so there is no tower to climb.[^1][^2][^3]
