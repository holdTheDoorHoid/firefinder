Martins Knob is listed in Harris County, Georgia, at 32.77319, -84.80354, with a listed elevation of 1,220 (no unit is given; read as feet, about 372 m).[^1] A Forest Lookouts index of Georgia towers also has a Martins Knob entry in Harris County.[^2] OpenStreetMap, a map that volunteers edit, names the structure Martins Knob Lookout Tower and describes it as a lattice-construction tower of the observation type.[^3]

The Georgia list also has a separate Harris County Lookout Tower about 9.5 km (6 miles) to the west, at 32.77180, -84.90521. The two are different entries, so the names should not be mixed up.[^1]

No source describes the site today, and none says whether it is open to the public, so check its current status before planning any visit.
