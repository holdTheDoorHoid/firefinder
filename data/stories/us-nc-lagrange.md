LaGrange is listed as a lookout tower in Lenoir County, North Carolina, on tnlandforms.us's list of North Carolina lookout towers. The list places it at 35.295 N, 77.78056 W. Beside the entry, the list gives the note "fire tower rd? gw".[^1]

The tower shares its name with LaGrange, a railroad town in western Lenoir County on North Carolina's Coastal Plain.[^2] No source we read ties the tower to the town.

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the tower today, or whether the public may visit the site.

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: LaGrange Historic District, National Register of Historic Places Registration Form, North Carolina State Historic Preservation Office, https://files.nc.gov/historic-preservation/nr/LR1275.pdf (accessed 2026-10-21).
