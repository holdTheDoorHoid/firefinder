Ironpoint sits in Perry County, Ohio, on land of the Wayne National Forest.[^1] The national forest is publicly owned and spans more than 250,000 acres of hill country in southeastern Ohio.[^2] The Forest Service administers the site, and the register gives its elevation as 1,020 feet (311 m).[^1]

Ohio's forestry division dates the state's fire towers to the 1920s and ties them to the fight against wildland fires.[^3] The Civilian Conservation Corps built 11 steel-frame towers in southeast Ohio in 1933 and 1934, then eight more before its 1942 disbanding.[^3] None of the sources says whether Ironpoint was one of these towers.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Ironpoint tower as gone.[^4] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site on September 3, 2014.[^1] A regional Ohio list also files it under the name Kehota (Ironpoint).[^5]

No source describes what remains at the site today or whether the spot can be visited, so check with the Forest Service before going.
