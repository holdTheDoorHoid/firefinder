The Loch Erne Fire Tower is listed in the Firelookout.ca registry with a location in Thunder Bay, Ontario.[^1]

An undated description on the same listing says the Ministry of Natural Resources cleared a road that runs across patented claims, leading to an abandoned fire tower south of the property.[^1] The same description also mentions roads for truck haulage and rubber-tire tractors that start at the Inco road, roughly 4.8 km (three miles) to the east.[^1]

The listing calls the tower abandoned.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the public may visit. The route described crosses patented claims, so check the land status before going. Nothing in these sources suggests the tower is open to climbers.

[^1]: Loch Erne Fire Tower, Firelookout.ca, https://firelookout.ca/lookouts/?slug=loch-erne-fire-tower (accessed 2026-10-20).
