A website called Forest Lookouts lists a Bull Shoals Mountain lookout in Arkansas and gives its county as "Marian County."[^1] That looks like a misspelling of Marion County, where the town of Bull Shoals sits.[^2] The listing gives only the name and county.[^1] No source gives a construction date, height, owner, staffing or description, and nothing else in the sources checked confirms that a tower stood on the mountain.[^1]

The Encyclopedia of Arkansas describes a tourist tower on a Bull Mountain linked to Bull Shoals, the Top O' the Ozarks Tower, which the entry says was built from used offshore drilling platforms.[^2] The same entry credits Roy Danuser with founding several attractions that became closely tied to the town.[^2] It does not describe the tower as a forestry lookout.[^2] Nothing in the sources checked ties that tower to the lookout on the list.[^1]

No source describes the site today.[^1] Whether anything remains on the mountain and whether the public can visit are both unknown.[^1]

[^1]: Bull Shoals Mountain, Forest Lookouts (Arkansas listing), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/bull-shoals-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Bull Shoals (Marion County), Encyclopedia of Arkansas, https://encyclopediaofarkansas.net/entries/bull-shoals-marion-county-931/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
