The museum at Nutimik Lake is in Whiteshell Provincial Park.[^3] The Canada Lookouts page for a Nutimik Lake lookout says only that it has no information at present.[^1]

Forest fire protection was one purpose of the Whiteshell's roads.[^2] Before roads, crews were moved by plane, canoe or train, and each mode had drawbacks.[^2] Roads greatly reduced the time crews needed to reach a reported fire.[^2] The same history says towers were put at strategic spots across the forest reserve, and that the government also set up telephone communication.[^2] This history does not name the Nutimik lookout, so its place in that network is not on record here.

The original museum at the lake closed in 2017 because of structural problems, and a replacement was planned.[^3] The replacement opened in August 2025.[^3]

A geocache listing carries the name Nutimik Fire Tower, but the copy we read gave no description of what stands there now.[^4] No source we found describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout or whether the site is open to visitors.
