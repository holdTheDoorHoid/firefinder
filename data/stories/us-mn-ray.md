Ray is an unincorporated community southeast of International Falls, where US 53 meets MN 217.[^1] The county report lists two fire towers at Ray, and both rows name the Minnesota Forest Service as owner.[^2]

The first, tower 91, appears on a 1939 tower list. The report says it is unclear why that list gives the number, and it calls the tower apparently an earlier one.[^2]

The second, tower 190, was a stairway tower 100 feet tall, put up in 1936, and a 1960s list gives it that number.[^2] A 1967 survey note describes a 110-foot tower with a metal cabin on four legs, but the report does not say which Ray tower the note covers.[^2]

The report marks the 1936 tower as removed. The site lies a short distance off Highway 53, to the northwest of Ray.[^2] The Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list also marks Ray as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
