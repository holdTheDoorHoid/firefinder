Van Camp is a flat-topped point in Idaho County, about 24 miles east-northeast of Kooskia, in the Clearwater National Forest.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association gives its elevation as 4,377 feet.[^1][^2]

Toward the close of the 1925 season, the Middlefork District assigned two firemen, Earl Whitney and Russell Stadtman, to keep watch from the point through the end of summer.[^3] Forest Lookouts quotes a 1981 account saying the pair pitched a tent camp near a water supply, about three-eighths of a mile away, and lacked a mapboard or lookout map.[^3]

Forest Lookouts describes a 1927 photo of a lookout tree whose observation platform sat roughly 50 feet up.[^3]

Three sources date the cab and platform to 1930, but they disagree on the platform.[^1][^2][^4] The association describes a 30-foot tree platform with an L-4 cab living quarters.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the same 1930 date and a 30-foot tree platform.[^2] The Forest Service's 2016 list describes a 50-foot spar pole platform with an L-4 cab, also dated 1930.[^4] The 1927 photo predates the 1930 date, and the sources do not explain that gap.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

The lookout was abandoned in the 1950s.[^1][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives 1950 as the year.[^2] A patrol point stood on Smokey Peak, 2.5 miles to the northeast.[^1][^2]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today. Idaho Fire Lookouts marks the site as drive-up.[^2] The association's list marks it gone.[^5]
