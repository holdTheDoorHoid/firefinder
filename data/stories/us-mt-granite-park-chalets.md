The Great Northern Railway built Granite Park Chalet in Glacier National Park, and the National Park Service says it was built in 1914 and 1915. Nearby sources supplied the timber and stone used to build it.[^1] Wikipedia's infobox gives 1913 as the build year, and its text says 1914.[^2]

In 1930, a National Park Service report, as quoted by a Montana lookout index, said two new lookouts had been installed that year, one of them at Granite Park Chalets.[^3] The chalets' porter operated that lookout, as a courtesy from the Glacier Park Hotel Co.[^3]

A Montana lookout site provisionally reads the chalet as a fire lookout location for some period.[^4] No source found describes a separate lookout tower there.

The chalet was added to the National Register of Historic Places on June 27, 1983, and it is a National Historic Landmark.[^2][^1]

Today the chalet still stands and offers lodging, managed by Belton Chalets, Inc.[^1] Access is by trail only, with no vehicle access.[^1][^2]
