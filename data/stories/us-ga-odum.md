A Georgia lookout list on tnlandforms.us includes a tower named odum, placed in Wayne County.[^1] It gives the position as 31.69104, -82.02991 and an elevation of 149, with no unit stated.[^1] The entry carries the code gfc-807. The page's notes say these gfc numbers come from an older GFC list that described 203 towers.[^1]

The same list has a second entry, named odum old, also in Wayne County, at 31.66397, -82.02253 and an elevation of 151.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 29 registered lookouts in Georgia, and neither Odum entry is among them.[^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register lists two former lookout sites in Georgia, Lafayette and Yonah Mountain, so neither Odum entry is recorded there as a former lookout.[^3]

No source I could read describes what remains at either site today, or whether the public can reach them.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Georgia, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/GA (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Georgia, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://www.firetower.org/lookouts/us/ga/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
