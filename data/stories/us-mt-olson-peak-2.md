Firelookout.com calls Olson Peak a fire lookout cabin, and says its L-4 cab dated from the 1930s and is now gone.[^1] The site lies roughly 26 miles southwest of Augusta, in Lewis & Clark County, Montana, at 8,881 feet.[^1]

The forest is listed two ways. Firelookout.com names both the Lolo and Helena National Forests for the site, while Forest Lookouts names only the Helena National Forest, at township 17 north, range 9 west, section 21.[^1][^2]

No source checked describes what, if anything, remains of the cab or the summit today, or whether the public can visit.
