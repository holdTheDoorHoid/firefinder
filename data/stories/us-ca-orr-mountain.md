Orr Mountain Lookout stands on the summit of Orr Mountain, in the Goosenest Ranger District of the Klamath National Forest, in Siskiyou County.[^1][^2] The register gives 1934 as the build year and describes a steel fire tower.[^1] A photo page suggests the Civilian Conservation Corps may have built it.[^3]

A 1971 National Geodetic Survey note describes a steel tower with four legs, crowned by a cabin measuring 12 by 12 feet.[^2] In 1937 a Bakersfield newspaper, quoted on the forest lookouts page, reported that Lloyd Darley, the lookout there, had spotted a fire 14 miles away that went out after three minutes.[^2]

The register is inconsistent about staffing. Its description says someone staffs the tower every fire season, but its staffing field says only sometimes.[^1] The schedule it lists is Fridays and Saturdays from May through October, covered by one named person.[^1] The register added the lookout on April 16, 2014.[^1]

Today the picture is mixed. A photo page from July 2010 says the lookout had staff on duty when photographed and has since been turned into a rental.[^3] The register also lists it as available for rental.[^1] The latest visit report I found in the register is dated October 2, 2019.[^1] I could not confirm how to book a stay, so contact the Goosenest Ranger District before planning a visit.
