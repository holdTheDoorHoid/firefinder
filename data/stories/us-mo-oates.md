Oates was a steel lookout tower in Reynolds County, Missouri, inside the Mark Twain National Forest.[^1] The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the station in 1956 under the designation Oates Lookout Tower.[^1] It sat on a high, timbered ridge about 15 miles southeast of Howes Mill and 10 miles south-southeast of Bixby, measured in a straight line.[^1]

The survey gives the tower a height of about 85 feet (26 m), with an 8-foot (2.4 m) square house on top.[^1] It lists the Oates USGS quadrangle, dated 1981, for the station.[^1] The page credits an April 16, 2016 photo to Bob Frakes.[^1]

The Central US Lookouts site lists the lookout as removed.[^1] The page does not describe what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1]
