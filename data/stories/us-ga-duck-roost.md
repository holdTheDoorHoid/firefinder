Duck Roost is listed among Georgia lookout towers in Washington County, at about 33.109° N, 82.684° W.[^1] The Tennessee Landforms list gives that location, the county, an elevation and a register code, gfc-622. The map on the page was last revised on March 22, 2016.[^1]

Washington County was formed on February 25, 1784, and Sandersville is its seat.[^2]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
