Faught Ridge was a lookout tower in Navajo County, about 13 miles south of Show Low.[^1][^3] A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description from 1958 calls it the Faught Ridge Indian Service Lookout Tower. The description gives the cabin as 8 feet by 8 feet and galvanized steel, supported on four legs, with an overall height of 120 feet.[^1]

The same survey notes also mention a small frame house used as living quarters, close to the tower.[^1]

TopoQuest places the lookout locale at N34.065323°, W110.075663°, at an elevation of 6,971 feet.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Arizona list marks the tower as gone.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, is left at the site now. The land lies within the Fort Apache Indian Reservation.[^1] Treat it as tribal land and ask the tribe for permission before going there.
