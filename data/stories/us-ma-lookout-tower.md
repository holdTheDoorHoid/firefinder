OpenStreetMap lists a tower here called Lookout Tower. It is tagged as an observation tower rather than a fire lookout, and its source note mentions Sandy Point.[^1]

Mapcarta places the tower in Essex, Massachusetts, close to a peak called Bar Head and a pond called Stage Island Pool.[^3] It says Sandy Point State Reservation lies about 1,600 feet southwest of the tower.[^3] Mapcarta describes that reservation as a state park in Ipswich, at the southern end of Plum Island.[^3]

A current copy of the state resource inventory that the OpenStreetMap note cites describes an observation tower at a wildlife refuge. We could not confirm that it is the same structure.[^2]

We found no build date, no builder and no history for this tower in the sources we read, and no register entry for it. Its visitor status is unknown. Please ask the land manager before visiting, and do not climb any tower you find on this site.
