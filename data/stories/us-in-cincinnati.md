The Cincinnati Fire Tower stands on private land in Greene County, southwest of Bloomington, Indiana.[^2] The Indiana Fire Towers site also calls it Little Cincinnati, and says it is the only tower in its county.[^2]

The register says the tower was erected before 1940 on land the State of Indiana leased, and it relies on Dean Alger's book, Indiana's Historic Fire Towers, for its history.[^1] The Indiana Fire Towers site is less sure about that state link: its author doubts the tower ever belonged to state-owned land.[^2] That site also reads a September 1939 magazine article as implying the tower was already standing then.[^2] The sources I read do not say who built it.

What remains is in poor shape. According to the register, the cab is still on top but has lost its roof and floor.[^1] The wooden steps and the planks on the landings have rotted, the page says.[^1] The tower was registered on August 2, 2026, and the register calls it in complete disrepair.[^1] It also warns that climbing is unsafe and that a visit without the landowner's consent is illegal.[^1]

The Indiana Fire Towers author visited in June 2024. Because the tower is not on public property, the author could not get right up to it, but could see it from a few directions.[^2] That visit found the cab in horrible condition and the tower not climbable.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^3]
