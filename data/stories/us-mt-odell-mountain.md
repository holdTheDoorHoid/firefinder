The Odell Mountain lookout stood on Odell Mountain in Beaverhead County, nine miles east-southeast of Wisdom, on the Beaverhead National Forest. The site sits at 9,405 feet.[^1][^2]

A camp was there by 1916.[^2] Firelookout.com lists a camp that year, and a Dillon paper of August 1916 mentions a visit to the Odell Lookout station.[^2][^3]

Firelookout.com describes a wooden L-4 tower in the 1930s.[^2] A June 1934 Dillon item about new telephone construction reports that another line was being strung from the O'Dell lookout to Wisdom.[^3]

Dillon newspaper items from 1936 to 1963 report people posted there in several summers.[^3] John Bailey was appointed in 1936.[^3] Henry Tiel of Roundup was the lookout in 1944, and in 1953 Pete Haldron, a forestry student at Syracuse University, held the post.[^2][^3] In 1963 Forest Service lookout men were installed at Bender and O'Dell lookouts.[^3]

In September 1954 a Dillon paper said work on the lookout had started a year earlier. The plan was to heighten it by about 30 feet for better visibility and to add a cabin.[^3] Firelookout.com instead describes an 18-foot steel live-in dated 1954.[^2] The two accounts do not match. In 1957 a Beaverhead Forest radio technician installed an F.M. repeater on the lookout to improve radio contact between Wise River, Wisdom and Jackson.[^3]

Firelookout.com says the lookout was destroyed in 1976.[^2]

A Forest Service decision signed June 23, 2016 covers rerouting and rebuilding about half a mile of non-motorized Trail #3010 to the Odell Mountain Lookout.[^4] We found no source that describes what stands at the summit today.
