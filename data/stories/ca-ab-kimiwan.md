An online index of Canadian lookouts says the Kimiwan lookout in Alberta opened for service in 1976.[^1]

Alberta's government publishes a map of its 127 wildfire lookout sites, which sit in the boreal forest and along the Rockies.[^2] According to the province, each site has a lookout observer on duty, usually from April through October, who keeps watch for wildfire throughout the day.[^3] In the boreal forest, steel was introduced for lookout towers in the 1920s and gradually displaced wood.[^4]

The Kimiwan entry lists one later event: a new steel tower, completed in January 2012.[^1]

We found no source that describes the Kimiwan site today, so we cannot say what stands there or whether the public may visit. Do not climb or approach the tower.
