Sand Mountain sits at the south end of Wytheville, Virginia, and can be seen from anywhere in town.[^1] A fire lookout once stood on it, in Wythe County.[^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register records the site as US 699, VA 4, with a registration date of October 30, 2010.[^2] The Town of Wytheville administers the site.[^2]

The register gives two accounts of the tower's fate, and they do not agree. One says the lookout was converted into a tower carrying directional microwave antennas. Its cab and stairs were removed, and its steel frame still stands.[^2] The other says the lookout was removed and replaced by a Century Link tower, which is not the original.[^2] The register does not say which account is right.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list marks the site as "Tower Gone."[^3]

A microwave license record lists a site called SAND MTN, at coordinates close to the register's,[^2][^4] with a 107.6-foot lattice tower. That record has no date, so it cannot settle which account is right.[^4]

The register describes the site as fenced and on town property, with the access road closed to motor vehicles.[^2] It also says the site is open as a hiking trail, with parking about two miles below the tower.[^2] One of the register's photos is captioned as a foundation where the stairs were mounted.[^2]

The town has also developed the 1,800-acre Crystal Springs Recreation Area, which lets hikers and mountain bikers explore the mountain's flanks and summit.[^1]
