Blackjack Peak rises in Shoshone County, Idaho, 18 miles east of Avery, on the St. Joe National Forest.[^1] The peak's elevation is listed as 6,015 feet.[^1][^2]

A tower with an L-4 cab went up in 1934, on the peak's southeast point.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the same year and the same L-4 design.[^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts says it has no documented staffing history for the tower, and none of the three sources names a lookout.[^1][^2][^3]

The tower was abandoned in the 1940s. The sources give no more exact date.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the tower as gone.[^4]

Today, none of the sources describes what, if anything, remains on the summit. Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under "Drive-Up," but it does not say whether the public may visit the peak now. Check with the St. Joe National Forest before you go.[^2]
