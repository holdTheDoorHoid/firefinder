Newspaper clippings on a Florida lookout history site place a fire tower at Wetappo in Gulf County.[^1]

In April 1942 The Star said the county had four aircraft spotting posts, one of them at the Wetappo tower. Each post sent daily reports to a district control center in Thomasville, Georgia.[^1]

In May 1950 the Panama City News Herald described a session at the tower. Boys from the Port St. Joe 4-H club saw a demonstration of fire-protection methods and gear, and two of them became interested enough to make a color map of Gulf County's forest land ownership for the county agent.[^1]

No source we found describes what remains at the Wetappo site today, or whether the public may visit it.

[^1]: Wetappo, FOREST LOOKOUTS (clippings from The Star, April 3, 1942, and Panama City News Herald, May 7, 1950), Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/wetappo.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
