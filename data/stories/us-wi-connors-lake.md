Connors Lake Lookout is a 100-foot Aermotor tower in Sawyer County, Wisconsin.[^1] A state record places it in the Flambeau River State Forest.[^2] The register says it was built in 1934 and has a stairway in the style the US Forest Service uses.[^1]

In September 1936 a CCC camp newsletter said the Connors Lake tower had helped the camp reach fires sooner. It also said work on a tower cabin would start soon, with its lumber and materials already on hand.[^3] A 1991 state survey of the Park Falls Ranger Station tower cabin says it was built during the CCC program and is in good condition.[^2] The same record says the fire tower was moved from its original site at Connors Lake to the area headquarters between January and July 1973, when it was not in use. Because the record is about the cabin, it is unclear whether the tower itself or only the cabin left the site.[^2]

A 2015 article in the Sawyer County Record quoted a Winter Station firefighter, who said the Connors Lake tower had sat unused for five years.[^4] The same article said the state planned to take 72 towers out of service in 2016, and it counted Connors Lake among Sawyer County's five.[^4] A recent structural assessment had found the towers unsafe, and replacing them was judged too costly.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list still shows the tower as standing.[^5]

The register recorded the tower on June 28, 2017.[^1] The sources I found describe no public access, rental or visitor program for it. Because the state judged its towers unsafe, I would view it from the ground rather than climb.[^4]
