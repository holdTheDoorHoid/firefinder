Alberta's wildfire register lists a steel tower at Round Hill.[^1] It is 30 m (98 ft) tall and stands at an elevation of 693.7 m (2,276 ft), at about 55.30 N, 111.995 W.[^1] The register gives 2013 as the year it was built.[^1]

Alberta says it places lookouts where visibility is good enough to spot fires and report them.[^2] The province has 127 such sites, spread across its boreal forest and the Rockies.[^3] Round Hill is listed among the lookouts in the provincial register.[^1] Each has an observer on duty who keeps watch for wildfire all day, generally from April to October.[^3] The observer's main job is catching smoke and fire early and reporting what they see, lightning included.[^4]

The register lists no weather station at Round Hill.[^1] It shows the tower as active and reached by road.[^1] No source we found describes visits to the site, so treat it as an operating fire-watch station rather than a place to visit or climb.
