Garrett Mountain is in Lake County, California.[^1] The Forest Lookouts county index lists an enclosed tower on the mountain and marks it gone.[^2]

The earliest dated mention we found is from June 1944.[^1] A Ukiah Republican Press item, reprinted on the Forest Lookouts page, places Mr. and Mrs. B.M. Bucknell at the Garrett Mt. Lookout Station on a Saturday afternoon. Two deer were feeding nearby, and then a large panther appeared in the clearing. Men from a ranch a few miles away came up with their varmint dogs, but the dogs failed to pick up the animal's scent.[^1] The clipping adds that Mrs. Bucknell regretted not having her camera loaded, since she could have photographed both the deer and the cat.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains on the mountain today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
