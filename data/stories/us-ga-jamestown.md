A lookout tower is listed in Chattahoochee County, Georgia, at 32.2756° N, 84.8607° W, with an elevation of 332 and no unit stated; read as feet, that is about 101 m.[^1] The Georgia tower list gives it the name "jamestown". This tower is entry 389, and the compiler says names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own invention, so the name is a label rather than an official one.[^1]

Jamestown is also the name of an extinct town in the same county.[^2] A post office there operated from 1849 until 1914, and the community took its name from a railroad agent, John A. James.[^2] The tower's listed point lies a short distance west of the coordinates Wikipedia gives for the former town.[^1][^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
