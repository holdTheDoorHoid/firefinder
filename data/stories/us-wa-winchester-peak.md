Winchester Peak is in Pend Oreille County, five miles west of Cusick, at 3,960 feet.[^1][^2] The first record is a crow's nest from 1940.[^1][^2] WillhiteWeb calls it a temporary site and says no maps it found show it.[^2]

Both sites say the crow's nest was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1][^2] Firelookout.org's list also marks the site as abandoned.[^3]

The sources disagree on the national forest. Firelookout.com lists the Colville National Forest, while the Forest Lookouts site lists the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^4] Forest Lookouts gives the land location as 33N-43E-30, and Firelookout.org's list places the lookout at 48.32826, -117.41575.[^3][^4]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors. Check with the forest before you go.
