Buffards Mountain rises in Buckingham County, Virginia.[^1] TopoQuest places its summit at about 711 feet above sea level, on the USGS Dillwyn map sheet.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page spells the name Buffords, while TopoQuest and the Forest Fire Lookout Association spell it Buffards.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list shows a tower on the mountain and marks it as standing.[^3]

The Forest Lookouts page gives only the name and county. It has no construction date, builder, height, cab design, lookout names, or fire history.[^1]

None of the three sources says when the tower was built, who ran it, or what became of it.[^1][^2][^3]

No source names an owner or sets out access rules.[^1][^2][^3] Do not climb the tower or go onto the land without the owner's permission.
