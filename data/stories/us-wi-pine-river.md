The Pine River Lookout Tower stood in Forest County.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site lists it as Pine River (Alvin), in the Chequamegon-Nicolet National Forest.[^1] OpenStreetMap records its elevation as 527 metres and tags the structure as an observation tower.[^2]

Pastmaps lists a 1970 USGS map of Alvin, in a print from 1982, with the Pine River Lookout Tower among its landmarks.[^3] A Pastmaps description of a 1972 print of that same map says researchers can trace the tower on it.[^4]

Across the state, the Conservation Department set up an engineering division in October 1931 to design new towers. It began replacing the old windmill-type towers in 1932.[^5] Wisconsin retired its lookout towers in 2016 and stepped up aerial detection to back up citizen reports.[^5]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Pine River tower as gone, with coordinates 45.94926, -88.82656 and no National Historic Lookout Register (NHLR) or Former Fire Lookout Sites Register (FFLOS) number.[^6]

Nothing I read says what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
