By late July 1931 the Ouachita National Forest had put up a fire lookout on Wolf Pinnacle, in Polk County, Arkansas.[^1] A newspaper report from July 31, 1931, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, describes a 50-foot steel tower on the summit, in Posey Hollow.[^1] The planned user was a fire guard due on duty that autumn.[^1]

A 1952 Coast and Geodetic Survey description gives a different height. It describes a steel structure on four legs, with a cabin shut in at the top, roughly 90 feet high and about 10 miles northeast of Mena.[^1] The sources do not explain the gap, so both heights are given here.

The Forest Lookouts page now marks the tower as removed, with no date.[^1] A history of the Mena and Oden ranger districts says eleven fire towers once stood in those districts, and it lists Wolf Pinnacle among them.[^2] The same page says the Tall Peak fire towers remain standing.[^2]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, is left on the summit today, or whether visitors can reach it. The land is part of the Ouachita National Forest.[^1]
