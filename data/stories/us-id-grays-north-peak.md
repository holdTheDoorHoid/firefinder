Grays (North) Peak is an 8,806-foot lookout site in Valley County, Idaho, about 22 miles east-southeast of Yellow Pine, on the Boise National Forest.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list classes it as a ground site rather than a tower, and places it at 44.82987, -115.06650.[^4]

The Forest Lookouts site gives the cabin's date as 1935, citing Kresek, and firelookout.com gives the same year.[^3][^1] The cabin was abandoned in the 1950s.[^1][^2]

The sources are not in full agreement on the status. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the peak as abandoned, while the firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts pages call it gone.[^4][^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it as a hike-in site.[^2] The pages read do not say what, if anything, remains on the peak today or whether visitors may go there.
