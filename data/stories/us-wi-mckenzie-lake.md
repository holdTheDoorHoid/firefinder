McKenzie Lake Lookout Tower stands in Washburn County, Wisconsin, on a county forest unit that planners call the McKenzie Unit.[^1] The Namekagon River runs along the unit's east and north sides.[^1] OpenStreetMap places the tower at 372 metres above sea level.[^4]

The 2021 county forest plan says that earlier plans describe the site's first tower as a windmill that was converted into a tower, built in 1922 by P.A. Mcdonald.[^1] In 1934 a steel structure took its place.[^1] The plan does not say how long the windmill tower stood, or who designed the steel one.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing, and its county list shows the same.[^2][^3] The plan does not say who staffed the tower or when its lookout watch ended.[^1]

The plan describes a busy recreation area.[^1] Much of that use comes from the many seasonal properties around the nearby lakes. The Casey Loop ATV and snowmobile trail passes through the unit and circles all of it, and Snowmobile Trail 8 runs across its northernmost fringe.[^1] No source I found says whether the tower is open to visitors. This story does not encourage climbing it. Check with Washburn County before you plan a trip.
