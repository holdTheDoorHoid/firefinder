Dickenson County, Virginia, is home to Skeetrock, an unincorporated community in the Appalachian mountains near the Kentucky border. Clintwood is the county seat.[^1] Wikipedia dates a post office at Skeetrock from 1901 to 1958, and says the name comes from locals who skated on the creek bed when it was slippery.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Skeetrock lookout in Dickenson / Pike (KY), with the type unknown and the status gone.[^2] Its listed point lies northeast of the community's coordinates.[^1][^2]

No source we checked describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^2]
