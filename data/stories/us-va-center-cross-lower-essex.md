The Center Cross lookout is listed in Essex County, Virginia.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list gives the tower's name as Center Cross (Lower Essex). It places the tower at 37.78837 north, 76.78082 west, and marks it as standing.[^2]

The same list has a second Essex County tower, Caret (Upper Essex), also marked as standing.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts page gives only the name and county. It has no construction date, builder, height, cab design, lookout names, fire history, or owner.[^1]

None of the sources I read says when the tower was built, who ran it, or what has happened to it since the list was compiled.[^1][^2]

The sources do not say who owns the tower or what the access rules are.[^1][^2] Do not climb a tower you find here, and check with the landowner before going near it.

[^1]: Center Cross, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/center-cross.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Virginia Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
