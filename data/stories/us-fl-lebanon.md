A tower called the Lebanon Lookout Tower appears on a USGS topographic map of the Lebanon Station area in western Levy County, Florida.[^1] The map is dated 1955 and was published in 1956, and it lists the tower among the local landmarks.[^1]

The map places the tower near a quarry. It describes Lebanon Station as a small focal point near the quarry and the tower, with the Atlantic Coast Line railroad running southeast toward the station.[^1] The land around is called Gulf Hammock, a country of dense sloughs and creeks where timber was once moved by river and rail.[^1]

The Eastern US Lookouts listing for the tower places it in Levy County and gives no further notes.[^2] No source I found describes what remains at the site today.

[^2]: Lebanon - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/lebanon1.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
