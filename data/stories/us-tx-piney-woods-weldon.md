The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list places Piney Woods (Weldon) in Walker County, at 31.03083 north and 95.46667 west, and marks it as gone.[^1]

A Forest Lookouts page titled Piney Woods (W) gives Walker County and the Texas Forest Service for a tower of that name, but the sources do not say whether it is the same tower as the Weldon entry.[^2]

Edward Cavallerano's 2025 book, Fire in the Piney Woods, begins with the Piney Woods and the conditions that made fire prevention and detection necessary, and it finishes with the location and design of Texas lookout towers.[^3]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^2]: Piney Woods (W) - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts (centraluslookouts.weebly.com), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/piney-woods-w.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
