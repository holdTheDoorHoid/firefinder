Bald Mountain sits in the Jefferson National Forest in Craig County, Virginia, and its tower overlooked New Castle to the south.[^1] The listing dates the tower to 1955, one of two towers the county built that year, with the other on Potts Mountain.[^1]

An older account from a Civilian Conservation Corps company describes a tower on the same mountain. Excavation began in September, and the structure was not finished until April 20. The entry is dated April 1940.[^2] That tower was 40 feet tall with a wood cab. Its lookout was to live there through the spring and fall fire seasons, watching 40,000 acres in Craig, Alleghany and Botetourt counties.[^2] The 1940 and 1955 dates do not match, and no source says whether one tower replaced the other.[^1][^2]

The listing says the tower was removed sometime around 2000 to 2002.[^1] A second list of former lookouts marks the site as gone.[^3]

No source describes the summit today, so it is unclear what remains there or whether the public can reach the site.[^1]
