The Coeur d'Alene Timber Protective Association set up a camp on Marble Mountain in 1920.[^1][^2] The summit sits at 6,300 feet, about 13 miles southwest of Avery.[^2] The Forest Service took over the site in the spring of 1923.[^1][^3] The association's annual report said the agency fixed the station up into a modern one, though the association felt it had gained little from the change.[^3]

The L-4 tower went up in 1939.[^1][^2][^3] The site also has a sad chapter. A 19-year-old lookout, Clair Cory, was killed by lightning while staffing the mountain.[^1] The Idaho lookout site's staff list places him there in 1927 or 1928.[^1] A December 1928 newspaper report, quoted by an Idaho lookout list, spells the name Corey and says he was on Marble Mountain when he was struck.[^3]

The tower is listed as gone.[^2][^4] An August 2025 trip report says a hiking party reached the peak on foot because a large stretch of road was still under deep snow.[^1] They searched for the tower's footings and tie-downs and found none.[^1] They did find the ruins of a cabin, along with a scant amount of other ruins at the site.[^1] The sources do not say whether the site is open to visitors now.
