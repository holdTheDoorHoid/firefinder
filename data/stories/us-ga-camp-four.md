A Georgia lookout tower list places a tower called camp four in Clinch County, at 31.00921, -82.94030.[^1] Its elevation column reads 195, but the list does not say whether that figure is in feet or metres.[^1]

The list's Google map is marked as revised on 22 March 2016.[^1] The list also says that names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own creations. Camp four is numbered 129, so its name may not be a historical one.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
