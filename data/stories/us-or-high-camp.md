High Camp is a site in Clackamas County, Oregon, at about 4,407 feet, roughly 18 miles south of Estacada.[^1][^2] The register lists the Oregon Department of Forestry as administrator.[^1]

The site's history starts in 1914. The earliest structure was a tree crow's nest from that year.[^1] In August 1914 the Morning Oregonian reported that men from the Clackamas-Marion Fire Patrol Association, working from the High Camp lookout station, had spotted a fire that was 26 miles from a settlement.[^3] That season eight miles of telephone line were built to the lookout, and a 1915 State Forester report said the permanent lookout at High Camp gave excellent results.[^3]

In August 1919 a patrol airplane located a fire, and a message it dropped fell within a few feet of the tower. Ed Woodworth was in charge of the lookout then.[^3] Woodworth's family stayed with him at the station in several 1920s summers, and he was in charge again in 1928.[^3] A 1939 district report says a 1.7-mile extension of a logging road gave the lookout road access.[^3]

The new tower went up in 1951.[^1][^2] The district's 1951 report lists a 14-by-14 cab atop a 100-foot tower, and its 1952 report describes a wood tower with living quarters in the cab.[^3] The two registers give 90 feet instead, with an L-4 cab and a ground cabin for living quarters.[^1][^2] A 1957 U.S. Geological Survey description of the station lists it as not occupied.[^3] Firelookout.com says the lookout was staffed into the 1980s before it was condemned.[^2]

The Oregon chapter of the Forest Fire Lookout Association salvaged parts from the cab.[^2] Rot ruled out saving the superstructure, which came down in 2001.[^1][^2]

The register gives September 17, 2019 as the registration date.[^1] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at High Camp today, or whether visitors can reach it.
