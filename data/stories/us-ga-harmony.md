The Georgia lookout tower list places a tower called harmony in Chattahoochee County, at coordinates 32.382413, -84.867449.[^1] Its elevation column reads 463, and the entry does not state a unit.[^1] The list's compiler says the names of towers numbered 73 and up are their own invention, so this name may not match a historical one.[^1] The only other note on the entry is "aerial imagery".[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
