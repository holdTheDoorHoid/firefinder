The Culpeper Lookout overlooks the Germanna Bridge in Culpeper County, Virginia.[^1] A 1955 newspaper report placed the steel tower one mile west of Richardsville, where it replaced a wooden lookout of the same height that the Civilian Conservation Corps had built around 1935.[^2]

The report, dated September 29, 1955, describes a new 110-foot tower with a view over parts of five counties.[^2] It said that, if completed on schedule, the work would have taken four days.[^2] The Register gives 1955 as the build year and says the tower was built by 12 men.[^1] The two accounts differ on who those men were. The Register calls them foresters and game wardens, while the newspaper says they were chief forest wardens or foresters.[^1][^2]

Before the new tower went up, the Richardsville station had been manned for the past five years during the fire season by Milton Brooks, and it kept the Rappahannock Valley under surveillance.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^3] Its current administrator is a private owner, and the Register adds that the owner has asked for the coordinates to stay unlisted.[^1]

The sources read do not say what condition the tower is in today, whether anyone still staffs it, or whether the public may reach it. Because it is privately owned and its location is withheld, please do not try to climb it or search for it on private land.[^1]
