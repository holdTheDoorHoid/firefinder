Moose Peak is a former fire lookout site in the Kootenai National Forest, about 13 miles east-northeast of Trout Creek, in Sanders County, Montana.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 6,010 feet.[^1]

The lookout was an L-4 tower, built in 1934.[^1] Firelookout.com marks it gone in 1955, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks the site as gone too.[^1][^2]

A hiking listing names a Moose Peak Trail in Sanders County.[^3] None of the cited sources describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the old lookout site is open to visitors.
