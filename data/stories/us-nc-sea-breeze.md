The statewide list of North Carolina lookout towers includes a tower named Sea Breeze, in New Hanover County, at 34.06361 north and 77.89667 west.[^1] The list notes that a few of its entries may be observation towers or Coast Guard lookouts, so this entry alone does not confirm a fire lookout.[^1] The entry gives only a name, coordinates, a county and a short code, and nothing more.[^1]

Sea Breeze is a census-designated place (CDP) in New Hanover County, with a population of 2,245 at the 2020 census.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public.
