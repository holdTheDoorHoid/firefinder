Allan Mountain is a lookout site in Lemhi County, Idaho, filed under the Salmon National Forest.[^1] The Forest Service now calls that forest the Salmon-Challis National Forest.[^2]

The entry comes from an Idaho lookout website. Its index spells the name Alan Mountain, while the entry heading uses Allan Mountain.[^1] A 1924 Salmon Forest lookout report, cited by the entry, describes daily visits to the site during fire season.[^1] The entry says the lookout had a camp at Axe Park, but it does not explain how that place relates to the mountain.[^1] An August 1924 Salmon Herald item reports that a son was posted at the Axe Park lookout while his mother and sister camped with him.[^1]

The Forest Service says that since 1908 it has put up, kept up and staffed as many as 50 lookout locations around Salmon and Challis.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains on Allan Mountain today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
