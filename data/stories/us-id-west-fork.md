West Fork Lookout stands on West Fork Mountain in Boundary County, Idaho, within the Idaho Panhandle National Forests, at 6,337 feet.[^1][^4] It was built in 1934 with a 35-foot Aermotor steel tower, a 7-by-7 cab and a frame residence cabin.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the first construction as 1934 or 1935, so the sources differ by a year on the start date.[^1][^2][^3]

The earliest named lookout in the sources is L.L. Hydron, cited by Idaho Fire Lookouts from a 1935 Bonners Ferry Herald item.[^3] Burt Callahan is named for 1936.[^3][^2]

The lookout was abandoned in the 1950s, and the register says it has not been staffed since then.[^1][^3] Forest Fire Lookout says the ground cabin is long gone, and the register says only the tower remains.[^1][^2]

The steel tower still stands, but its lower stairs are gone and the frame shows wear and damage.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts judges it unsafe to climb or explore.[^3] The Forest Service describes Trail #347 as about 3.4 miles long, starting from the junction with Smith Creek-Red Top Trail #21, and open from mid-June through November, weather permitting.[^4] The register listed the lookout on December 2, 2020, as US 1465, ID 157.[^1] A lookout list at firelookout.org shows it as standing.[^5]
