Fatman Mountain's lookout stood in Sanders County, on the Kootenai National Forest.[^2][^3] firelookout.com describes it as a log cabin with a crow's nest, dated to 1934.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list files the structure as a crowsnest and marks it abandoned.[^3]

The earliest staffing record in our sources is a June 1931 notice from the Sanders County Independent-Ledger, as quoted on the Montana lookout page, naming Ralph Flemming as the person staffing Fatman again that year.[^2] That is three years before the 1934 date on firelookout.com, so the sources disagree about when the lookout began.[^1][^2]

firelookout.com says the cabin was destroyed in 1948.[^1] The Montana page marks the site as removed,[^2] and the association's list calls it abandoned.[^3] The three accounts do not agree on how it ended.[^1][^2][^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it. Given the destroyed, removed and abandoned records, a visitor should not expect a standing lookout here.[^1][^2][^3]

[^2]: Fatman Mountain, Forest Lookouts, https://montanalookouts.weebly.com/fatman-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Montana lookout list, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
