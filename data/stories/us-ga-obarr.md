Tom Dunigan's list of Georgia lookout towers names an "O'Barr Lookout Tower" in Dodge County, with an elevation of 346 ft (105.5 m).[^1] A business directory lists a "Dodge County Lookout Tower" at 126 Jim Harp Road, in or near Eastman.[^2] The directory does not say whether that is the same structure as the list entry.

Today, no source we could reach confirms that the O'Barr tower still stands or says whether visitors may go there, so its condition and public access are not known.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: DODGE COUNTY LOOKOUT TOWER in Eastman, GA, Original YP local business directory, https://www.originalyp.com/eastman-ga/dodge-county-lookout-tower/government-offices-county/se0118-19-1562538-001 (accessed 2026-10-21).
