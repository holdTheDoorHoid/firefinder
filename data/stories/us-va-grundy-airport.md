The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list includes a tower called Grundy Airport, in Buchanan County, and marks it as gone.[^1] The name matches Grundy Municipal Airport, a public-use airfield three nautical miles southwest of Grundy's downtown.[^2] The listed point is about 185 metres from the airport's coordinates on Wikipedia, so the two appear to refer to the same place.[^1][^2]

The Town of Grundy owned the field, and a municipal commission ran it.[^2]

Sources disagree on when the field opened.[^2][^3] Wikipedia says it was completed in 1969, on land the United Coal Company gave to the town.[^2] An airfield history site instead places its establishment somewhere between 1961 and 1963, and its earliest map depiction is a 1963 USGS topographic map.[^3] That map shows a single unpaved runway and no buildings.[^3]

Wikipedia says the airport shut for good on October 31, 2019.[^2]

No source read describes what, if anything, remains of a tower at the airfield today, or whether the grounds are open to visitors.
