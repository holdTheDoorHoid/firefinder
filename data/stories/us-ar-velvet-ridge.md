The Velvet Ridge lookout is in White County.[^1] According to the National Historic Lookout Register, the tower is still standing on a small parcel the Arkansas Forestry Commission owns.[^2] The register also says the parcel was once much bigger and included living quarters for the area forester.[^2]

The register gives no build date.[^2] The Arkansas Forestry Commission was created by Act 234 in 1931 and built many fire towers.[^3] I found no source that says it built this one.

A diagonal stair climbs the inside of the tower, with treads made of metal. The register gives the site elevation as 701 feet (214 m).[^2]

The register notes that the cab has lost its floor, and it records a site visit on September 29, 2020.[^2]

I found no account of the tower's staffing, its fires, or its later history, and I could not confirm whether the public may visit the parcel. Do not climb a tower whose cab has no floor, and stay off the land unless you have permission.
