Bermont, in Charlotte County, Florida, was a sawmill town that started around the state's 1920s land boom. Wikipedia describes it as a ghost town.[^1]

The Bermont Lookout Tower carries the same name. A listing of eastern U.S. forest lookouts places it in Charlotte County, at township 40 South, range 26 East, a land-survey grid reference.[^2] Map records tag it as a lattice observation tower.[^3]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 31 Florida lookouts, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists six former sites. Neither has an entry for Bermont.[^4][^5]

The National Historic Lookout Register also lists a separate Charlotte County tower, the Punta Gorda Lookout Tower. It is a 100-foot Aermotor at a Florida Forest Service station in Punta Gorda.[^6]

No source describes the site today, so whether the tower still stands and whether the public can reach it are unknown.
