Dixon is listed among the British Columbia lookouts on the Canada Lookouts website.[^1] The entry places it in Bulkley-Nechako, at 55.182 north and 125.547 west, and gives an elevation of 1732 with no unit.[^1]

No source found describes the site today, so nothing is known about what remains there or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Dixon, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/dixon.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
