Pine Mountain was a fire lookout in Alcorn County, Mississippi.[^1] According to the Eastern US lookouts list, the site received a 100-foot (30.5 m) Aermotor tower with a 7 x 7 cab in 1949.[^1] Aermotor, a maker of wind-powered water pumps, also built galvanized steel lookout towers under the Aermotor Corporation name.[^2] Its "7 x 7" model had a steel cab measuring 7 by 7 feet.[^2] The model was made in sizes from 35 feet (11 m) to 175 feet (53 m) tall, and hundreds were in service in the southeastern United States.[^2]

The same list records that the tower was taken down in 1998.[^1] Today, no source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether visitors can reach it.
