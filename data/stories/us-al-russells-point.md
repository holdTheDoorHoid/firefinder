Russell's Point was a 100-foot steel fire lookout in Jackson County, Alabama, near Stevenson.[^1] A July 1, 1942 item from the Alabama Journal, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, reported that the tower had been completed. The item credited the Alabama Conservation Department's division of forestry with building it, and said Forester Brooks Toler had four more under way.[^1]

The page header names the Alabama Forestry Commission as the agency, which differs from the body the 1942 item credits.[^1]

No source we read describes the site today, so this story cannot say whether the tower still stands or whether the public can see it.

[^1]: Russell's Point, Forest Lookouts (easternuslookouts.weebly.com), quoting the Alabama Journal, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/russells-point.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
