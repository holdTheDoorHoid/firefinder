West Royce Mountain lies in Coos County, New Hampshire, in the Beans Purchase.[^2][^3]

A notice in The Portsmouth Herald from August 1939, which the Forest Lookouts site quotes, listed towers that were either being built or only planned, and West Royce was among the sites named.[^1] The notice also names Camp Peabody of Green's Grant alongside the site, and gives the mountain's figure as 3,215.[^1] A hiking-history list gives the height as 3,210 feet, so the two figures differ slightly.[^1][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's New Hampshire directory marks the West Royce tower as gone.[^2] The NH Family Hikes history page places West Royce Mountain under a heading for peaks that once had a fire tower, which agrees with that status.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the public may go up.[^1]
