The Carr Pond Mountain tower stood in Aroostook County, Maine.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page files it under the Maine Forestry District.[^2] Two state panoramic maps, from 1928 and 1952, both place the tower in Township 13, Range 8.[^3][^4]

The register dates the collapse to between 1998 and 2003 and says the tower was then removed.[^1] The Maine lookout list marks it as gone.[^5]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
