The Colt Mountain lookout was a 20-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, dated to 1934.[^1][^2][^3] It stood on the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, about 16 miles northeast of Coeur d'Alene, at an elevation of 4,206 feet.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com places the site in Kootenai County.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page also gives the code 52N-1W-28 for the site.[^3]

Firelookout.com marks the tower as gone in 1960.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the same loss year and labels the site as destroyed or removed.[^2]

Photos of the tower on firelookout.com and on the Idaho Fire Lookouts page are credited to the Forest Service.[^1][^2] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page files the site as hike-in, but no source we checked describes a route or what, if anything, remains on the mountain today.[^2]
