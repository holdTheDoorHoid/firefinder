Biggerstaff Mountain stands in Rutherford County, North Carolina.[^2] The lookout page names the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources as its agency.[^1][^2] A statewide tower list places the mountain at 35.5 north and 81.82083 west.[^3]

An earlier wooden tower stood on the mountain.[^1] In 1934 it was replaced by a steel tower 47 feet 9 inches tall, about 14.6 meters, built with ECW funding.[^1] The page describes the steel tower as a replacement, so the wooden one came first.[^1] The page cites the Department of Conservation and Development's 5th biennial report, covering 1932 to 1934, for this detail.[^1]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2][^3] Check with the Division of Forest Resources before any trip, and do not plan a climb.
