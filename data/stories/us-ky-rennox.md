The Rennox Lookout sits on Dividing Ridge, at the point where Metcalfe, Adair and Cumberland counties meet in Kentucky.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register lists it as US 1592, KY 13, administered by the Kentucky Division of Forestry.[^1] It was registered on May 22, 2022, and the register puts the site at 1,114 feet, or 340 meters, above sea level.[^1]

The sources we read do not say when the tower was built, how tall it is, what it is made of, or who watched from it.[^1][^2] We found no account of its staffing or of any fire it spotted.[^1][^2] Those parts of its history remain open.

A Columbia Magazine photo caption from July 2025 says the tower still stands, with its legs spread across all three counties, two of them in Metcalfe.[^2] The caption spells the name Renox, while the register uses Rennox.[^1][^2]

We found nothing about public access.[^1] Please ask the Kentucky Division of Forestry before visiting, and do not climb the tower.
