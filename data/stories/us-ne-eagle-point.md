Eagle Point was a lookout tower in Cherry County, Nebraska, in the Samuel R. McKelvie National Forest.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower at 42.72315, -101.07007 and marks it gone.[^2]

The forest sits in the Sandhills.[^3] The Forest Service places it south of Nenzel, covers 116,000 acres, and says it represents the Nebraska Sandhills prairie ecosystem.[^3]

The Forest Lookouts site marks the lookout as removed.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it. Anyone interested should ask the Forest Service before going to look for it.

[^2]: Nebraska Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/ne/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Samuel R. McKelvie National Forest, USDA Forest Service, https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/nebraska/recreation/samuel-r-mckelvie-national-forest-0 (accessed 2026-10-10).
