The Blue Mountain lookout is listed in Le Flore County, Oklahoma, inside the Ouachita National Forest.[^1] The national forest reached Le Flore County in 1930, the expansion that President Calvin Coolidge had proposed.[^2] Today, in the Oklahoma section of the forest, the Winding Stair Mountain National Recreation Area and six other designated areas give visitors a broad range of things to do.[^2] The listing marks the tower as removed.[^1] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.
