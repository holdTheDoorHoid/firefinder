Eakin Point is a lookout site in Idaho County, in the Bitterroot National Forest, at about 8,170 feet.[^1][^2] It lies 18 miles south-southwest of the Magruder Ranger Station.[^1]

Sources date a log cabin and a platform tower to the 1930s, and two of them place the tower one mile south of the summit.[^1][^2][^3] A 1973 draft Bitterroot National Forest history by O.F. Schumaker, quoted by Forest Lookouts, disagrees. The draft says the point never had a building, that it was a tent camp throughout, and that it was abandoned before 1940.[^3]

Three pages date the abandonment to the 1960s, which the draft's before-1940 date contradicts.[^1][^2][^3] A statewide list marks the platform as gone and gives coordinates for it.[^4] The other two pages describe the location by township and by distance from a ranger station, not by coordinates, so the exact spot is unsettled.[^1][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts calls the site a hike-in.[^2] Beyond the statewide list's note that the platform is gone, no source we read describes what, if anything, is still on the point, or whether it is open to visitors today.
