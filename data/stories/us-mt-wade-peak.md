Wade Peak is a former lookout about ten miles southwest of Superior, in Mineral County, Montana, on the Lolo National Forest.[^1] An April 1934 Missoulian item said materials for ten new lookout towers had been ordered, and one of the planned sites was Wade Peak in the Superior district.[^2] Firelookout.com says the tower was built that year. It was a 30-foot pole L-4 tower.[^1]

In September 1935 a Missoulian item, quoted on Forest Lookouts, reported that most lookout men had come back from their posts, and named Bert Smallwood as a lookout man from Wade Peak.[^2]

Firelookout.com marks the tower as gone by 1960.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, is left on the summit today, or whether the site is open to the public. Anyone thinking of going up should check with the Lolo National Forest first.
