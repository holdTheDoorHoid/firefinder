Tedoc Mountain is a former lookout site in Tehama County, California, at about 40.248 N and 122.896 W.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list gives its type as Ground and marks it as gone.[^1] The same list gives it no registry number.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page for Tehama County includes Tedoc Mountain in its list. The entry carries a photo credited to Ron Kemnow and dated September 2009, and nothing else. It is the only dated item we found for the site.[^2] The same list also includes Tuscan Buttes, which we cover separately.[^2]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, stands at the site now, or whether the ground is open to visitors. Anyone planning to go should ask the landowner or land manager about access first.[^1][^2]

[^1]: California Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/ca/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Tehama County, Forest Lookouts, https://californialookouts.weebly.com/tehama-county.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
