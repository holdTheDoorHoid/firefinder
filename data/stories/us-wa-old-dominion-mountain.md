Old Dominion Mountain sits in Stevens County, about 7 miles east-northeast of Colville, in the Colville National Forest.[^1][^2] In July 1937 the Colville Examiner, as quoted by WillhiteWeb, reported that a 20-foot tower was planned, topped by a 14-by-14-foot structure.[^3] The register and WillhiteWeb both record the first tower actually built in 1938 as a 15-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, so the plan and the build differed.[^1][^3]

A list of known lookouts runs from 1938 to 1958 and starts with Ray Scott.[^2] In 1941 Bob Wimmer was the lookout, and he retired from the Forest Service that season.[^3][^4] In July 1947 a Colville Examiner item says James Linne was manning the lookout, with two inches of fresh snow on the ground that morning.[^3]

In 1963 a 20-foot treated timber tower with an R-6 flat cab replaced the first one.[^1][^3] The tower suffered from vandals, worst after it was abandoned in the late 1970s.[^1] In 1983 the tower was sold to a local farmer, who propped a water tank on its legs and attached the cab to his house.[^1] A second listing says the lookout was sold and removed that year.[^4]

Nothing in these sources places a tower or cab on the summit today.[^1][^2][^3][^4] WillhiteWeb lists the top as a drive-up spot with a parking area, a short trail to the top, and a summit benchmark at 5,773 feet.[^3]

Willhite's directions start at the east end of Colville, on Hwy 20.[^3] They take the Colville-Aladdin road north to a right turn on Northport Road, follow it to Threeforks, and turn right onto the S Fork Mill Creek Road. In a few miles, look for a wide Forest Road on the south side.[^3] An older survey description gives a different approach, from the Old Dominion Mine Road, so check a current map before you go.[^4] The summit is on Colville National Forest land, and no source describes a closure or a permit requirement.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
