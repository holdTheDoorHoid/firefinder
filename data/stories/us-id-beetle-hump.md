Beetle Hump Lookout was a platform tower on the St. Joe National Forest, in Shoshone County, Idaho.[^1][^3] It sat 19 miles east of Avery at an elevation of 6,183 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list places the site at 47.23569, -115.40442.[^4] Forest Lookouts lists its location as 45N-8E-23, a township, range and section reference.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the tower under the St. Joe - Clearwater region.[^2]

Sources date the platform to the 1930s.[^1][^2][^3] They record it as abandoned, but none gives a year for that.[^1][^2][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts classes the site as a hike-in.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains of the platform at the site.[^1][^2][^3]
