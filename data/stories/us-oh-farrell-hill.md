The Farrell Hill fire tower was planned on Farrell Hill at California Hollow, between Bourneville and Bainbridge in Ross County, Ohio.[^1] In December 1935 an associate state forester announced that P.T.C. Brown, Jr., a real estate dealer, had donated an acre at the top of the hill for the planned tower.[^1]

A January 1936 notice anticipated that the tower would be erected early that year.[^1] By September 1937, though, construction had started on what was planned as a 73-foot field tower, which suggests the early-1936 forecast did not hold.[^1] CCC crews from Camp Pike were to extend the California Hollow road a mile so cars could reach the site.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page files the tower under Ross County and the Ohio Dept of Natural Resources.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Ohio towers now marks the Farrell Hill tower as gone.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register entered the site on September 3, 2014, as US 1557, OH 3, at an elevation of 1,320 feet, or 402 meters.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains on the hilltop today, or whether visitors may go up there.[^1][^3]
