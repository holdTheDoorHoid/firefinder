OpenStreetMap, a map created by volunteers, lists a tower named Okolona Lookout Tower.[^1] The map entry tags it as a lattice tower with observation as its type, and gives an elevation of 125 metres.[^1] The Encyclopedia of Arkansas places Okolona, a small town, in the southwest of Clark County, close to the Little Missouri River.[^2]

No source describes what stands at this spot today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
