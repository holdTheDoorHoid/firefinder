Rock Cut's fire tower belonged to the Minnesota Forest Service.[^1] It stood north of Craigville in Koochiching County, beside an old logging railroad grade.[^1][^2] The county list gives it the number 119 and a height of 94 feet (29 m).[^1]

A 1959 survey note describes a four-legged steel tower with a room for observers at the top, and puts its overall height at 125 feet (38 m).[^2] The two heights disagree.[^1][^2]

The county list marks the tower as removed, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone.[^1][^3]

A power line now runs along the old logging grade.[^2] Apart from the power line, no source describes the site now or says whether it is open to visitors.
