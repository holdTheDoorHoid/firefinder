The Westwood Tower Site is on Grossman Camp Hill in Norfolk County, Massachusetts.[^1] The register gives its elevation as 245 feet, or 75 meters.[^1]

The register describes a 40-foot steel tower that went into service in 1930 and remained in use until 1955.[^1] It also calls the tower municipal, which suggests the town rather than the state owned it.[^1]

The register says the tower is still standing.[^1] Our own records list the site as gone, and we found no second source to settle the question. A Red Knights motorcycle-club sheet lists the site among the Massachusetts fire towers, as Westwood - Grossman Camp Hill.[^2]

We found no source that describes what remains of the tower today or says whether the public may visit. Check access before going, and do not climb any tower that is closed or on private land.

[^2]: Fire Tower Quest, Red Knights Massachusetts Chapter 2, http://www.rk-mass2.org/FireTower1.pdf (accessed 2026-10-10).
