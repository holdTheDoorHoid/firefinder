Kingston Springs, a town of 2,824 residents at the 2020 census, sits in Cheatham County, Tennessee.[^3] A Tennessee Landforms table of lookout towers lists a tower called Kingston Springs at an elevation of 827 feet and marks it as removed.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts website files the tower under Tennessee, in Cheatham County, and gives no other details.[^2] Its page has a heading for the tower and its county, with no further text beneath it.[^2]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, is left on the site today, or whether the public can reach it. Anyone curious about the spot should first find out who owns the land.
