Bonnet Top was a lookout tower in the Kootenai National Forest, about ten miles north of Yaak in Lincoln County, Montana.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 5,544 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list gives its coordinates as 48.97894, -115.70148.[^2] The Montana Lookouts county list places it under the Kootenai National Forest and gives a survey location of 37N-32W-13, which reads as township 37 north, range 32 west, section 13.[^3]

Firelookout.com places a 50-foot pole L-4 tower there in 1933.[^1] The same page marks it gone in 1958.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list likewise shows the tower as gone.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, is left on the site today.[^1] Firelookout.com names the Kootenai National Forest for the site, so ask the forest about access before setting out.[^1]
