Big Soldier was a lookout site in the Challis National Forest, in Custer County, Idaho.[^2] The register says it lies within the River of No Return Wilderness, which Forest Lookouts and Rex's page call the Frank Church River of No Return Wilderness.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Lookouts history says the site was set up as a patrol and emergency lookout in 1927.[^2] The register says a tent camp was in use in 1934 and that the lookout was constructed that year as a groundhouse with a catwalk.[^1] Forest Lookouts and Rex's page instead date an L-4 cab to 1937.[^2][^3] The sources disagree on the year and on the design, and we have not settled which is right.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

A June 1946 item in the Post-Register, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, reported that a married couple had gone to the lookout for summer work.[^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts says it has no documented staffing history for the site.[^4]

The Forest Lookouts page says the lookout was abandoned around 1973.[^2] The register, which listed the site in November 2008, described the groundhouse as inactive for many years.[^1]

Rex's page says the cab burned in a 2018 forest fire.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it as destroyed in fire that year.[^4] We did not find an independent report naming this lookout in that fire, so the 2018 loss rests on these two sites.[^3][^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^3][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts calls it a hike-in site.[^4] Nothing in the sources shows a structure still standing there.[^3][^4]
