White Goat Lookout stood in the Challis National Forest, about 17 miles north-northwest of Challis, Idaho, at 9,421 feet.[^1][^2] The site sat on the edge of the Frank Church River of No Return Wilderness.[^1]

Both pages date the building to 1940 and describe it as a flat-roofed cabin.[^1][^2] The firelookout.com page calls it a log cabin.[^1] A third listing gives only the 1940s.[^3]

The plan was to put an L-4 cab on top of the cabin, but that cab was never completed.[^1][^2] Both pages say the cabin was destroyed in 2000.[^1][^2]

The cabin is gone, and none of the three pages describes what, if anything, remains of it at the site today.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts classes the site as a hike-in, and the pages read do not say whether it is open to visitors.[^2]
