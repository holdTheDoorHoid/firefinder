Raeford is a tower in Hoke County, North Carolina, that the Forest Lookouts page lists under the state's Division of Forest Resources.[^1] The tnlandforms list numbers it NCT140 and gives Hoke as its county.[^2]

The two sources cited here do not describe what remains at the site today.[^1][^2]
