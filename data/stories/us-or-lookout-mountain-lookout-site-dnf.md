Lookout Mountain rises to 6,223 feet in the Deschutes National Forest, about 13 miles northwest of LaPine.[^1][^2] The Forest Service administers the site, and the register says a cabin was built there in the 1930s.[^1][^2] A Rex's Fire Lookout Page entry gives the 1920s, but with question marks, so that earlier date is uncertain.[^3]

The register says the cabin is now gone, and firelookout.com and a statewide lookout list both mark it gone.[^1][^2][^4] The register recorded the site on May 11, 2011.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today. None says whether the site is open to visitors.
