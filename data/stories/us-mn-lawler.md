The Lawler fire tower stood along County 13, east of the hamlet of Lawler in Aitkin County.[^1][^2] The Minnesota A-Z list of fire lookouts says little is known about it beyond its name and where it stood.[^1]

A USGS topographic map portrayed for 1969 and published in 1972 labels a Lawler Lookout Tower.[^3]

The village has a fire history of its own. On October 12, 1918, a train's smokestack threw sparks that ignited a fire just west of town. Carried by the wind, it reached Moose Lake and Cloquet, and everything in the village burned except Charlie Spicola's store and the Lawler Hotel.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^2]

Today a modern communication tower stands on the old lookout site. A kjackson.us page on Lawler says the new tower took the place of the old fire lookout, and one of its captions says the structure houses communications equipment.[^4] Another caption shows a road leading into the site.[^4] None of the sources read says whether the public can visit the site.[^4]
