Shady Grove is a Houston County tower that the Forest Lookouts Texas list credits to the Texas Forest Service.[^1] A February 22, 1953 Grand Prairie Daily News item, quoted on the site's page for the tower, places it about 10 miles southeast of Crockett.[^2] That item says a Houston County deer-restocking project began early in 1952, when the first load of deer was released at the tower. More shipments had been planned for the winter of 1952, but trapping results fell sharply. The item blames a mild winter that left deer enough natural food to skip the bait traps.[^2]

A 1952 Forest Service photo record, described as Deer Release - Shady Grove/Firetower, labels the site as part of the Davy Crockett National Forest.[^3] The sources do not say who ran the tower, so the Texas Forest Service credit is unconfirmed. I also did not find when the tower was built, or how tall it is.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^4] TopoQuest places it in Houston County, at about 31.21 N and 95.35 W.[^5] I found no visit, access or rental information. Treat its status as unverified, and do not climb it without permission from the land manager.
