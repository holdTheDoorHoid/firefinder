Forest Lookouts lists a lookout called Banks in Bradley County.[^1] Mapcarta places Banks Lookout Tower in Clay Township, and says the town of Banks lies about three and a half miles southwest of it.[^2]

In earlier days the town of Banks was linked with railroads and the timber business.[^3] In 1883 a company run by James E. Lindsay of Davenport, Iowa, acquired property where the town would later take shape.[^3] For reasons now forgotten, the post office changed its name to Banks in 1906, and the town was incorporated the next year.[^3]

No source describes the site now, including whether a tower stands there or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1]
