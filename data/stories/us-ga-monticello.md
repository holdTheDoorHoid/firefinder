The Monticello Lookout Tower is listed as a fire tower in Jasper County, Georgia, under the Georgia Forestry Commission.[^2] A Georgia tower list on tnlandforms.us places it at 33.31901, -83.72434, and gives it the codes BLL-41100 and gfc-605.[^1] That list was revised on 22 March 2016, so it may predate later changes at the site.[^1]

The Commission's county contact page gives Jasper County's location as "Firetower on Hwy 212, Northwest of Monticello."[^3] In the late 1980s the Commission began replacing its tower operators with air patrols, and many towers across the state were scrapped and sold.[^4]

No source I could read describes the site today, or whether the public can visit it.
