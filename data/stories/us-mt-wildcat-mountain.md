A lookout cab went up on Wildcat Mountain in 1932, about 11 miles southeast of Hungry Horse in Flathead County, Montana.[^1]

firelookout.com describes it as an L-4 cab and gives its elevation as 6,600 feet.[^1] The page names the Flathead National Forest as the agency.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list types the structure as ground-level rather than a tower, and gives its coordinates as 48.24418, -113.92563.[^2]

The page says the cab was destroyed in 1956.[^1] The association's list also marks the site as gone.[^2]

Beyond that status, no source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Wildcat Mtn. Fire Lookout Cabin, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/wildcatmtn.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
