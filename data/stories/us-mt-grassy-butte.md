The Grassy Butte lookout was a cabin in Toole County, Montana, about 31 miles northeast of Shelby.[^1] Its elevation is listed as 4,429 feet.[^1] The firelookout.com page dates the cabin only to the 1930s and says it was abandoned.[^1]

Both the firelookout.com page and the Forest Lookouts page describe the land as private cooperator land.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page places the site in township 35 north, range 3 east, section 22.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a ground site rather than a tower, and marks it gone.[^3]

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3] Because the land is listed as private, please ask the owner before going near the site.[^1][^2]
