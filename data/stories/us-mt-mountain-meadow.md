Mountain Meadow is a lookout site in Flathead County, Montana, on the Flathead National Forest, about 11 miles southwest of Whitefish.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 5,310 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list gives the type as crowsnest and places the site at 48.33975 north, 114.55289 west.[^2]

Firelookout.com dates the crow's nest and a camp at the site to 1929.[^1]

The site was abandoned in 1938.[^1]

The sources do not settle what is left. Firelookout.com says the crow's nest is "still there," but the remark carries no date.[^1] The association's list gives the status as Abandoned.[^2]

The only pages we found are short listings, and the "still there" remark has no date. So we cannot tell whether any of the crow's nest is on the ground today, or whether the public may visit the site.
