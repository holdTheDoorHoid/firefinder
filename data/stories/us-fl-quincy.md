The Eastern US Lookouts site has a page for a Quincy lookout and gives its county as Gadsden.[^1] A Florida tower list, revised in February 2016, names a Quincy Lookout Tower in Gadsden County.[^2] Topo Zone's list of Gadsden County towers uses the same name.[^3]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]

[^2]: Florida lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/fltowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Map of Towers in Gadsden County, Florida, Topo Zone, https://www.topozone.com/florida/gadsden-fl/tower/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
