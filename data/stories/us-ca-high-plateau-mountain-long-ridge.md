High Plateau Mountain is a plateau-like ridge in the Siskiyou National Forest, in Del Norte County.[^1] A 1941 survey description places it about 18 miles northeast of Crescent City and about five miles north of Gasquet.[^1]

A forest history from 1939, as quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, dates a lookout cabin on the mountain to 1935.[^1] The same 1941 survey description refers to the top of a tower, which suggests a tower stood at the summit by then.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list gives the site the name High Plateau Mountain (Long Ridge), classes it as a tower, and marks it gone.[^2]

We found no source that describes what remains at the summit now, or whether the site is open to the public.
