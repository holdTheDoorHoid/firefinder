Cold Spring was a fire lookout tower in Monroe County, Tennessee.[^1] The Tennessee Landforms table lists it at 3,155 feet (about 962 m) and marks it removed.[^1] A map site gives its elevation as 3,127 feet (953 m), 28 feet lower than the table.[^2]

The table row carries the tag "cnf". The page abbreviates the Cherokee National Forest as CNF and credits that forest with 18 fire towers.[^1] The row also gives FC1693, a six-character code that the page identifies as an NGS datasheet ID, one that can be looked up with an NGS tool.[^1]

The same page credits the Civilian Conservation Corps with putting up 98 of the state's lookout towers between 1933 and 1941.[^1] It adds that many towers have since been taken down, and that most former tower sites are now privately owned.[^1]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the Cold Spring site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Tennessee lookout towers, Tennessee Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Cold Spring Lookout Tower Map - Monroe, Tennessee, United States, Mapcarta, https://mapcarta.com/21574816 (accessed 2026-10-21).
