The Ben Draper Lookout stands in Cass County on the Land o' Lakes State Forest, on a tract donated by Ben Draper, an early settler in the area.[^1][^2] The Civilian Conservation Corps and the state's forestry agency built it in 1937.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register describes a 110-foot Aermotor structure with a metal cab measuring 7 feet by 7 feet.[^1] A second history site also gives 110 feet, but the Forest Lookouts site says 100 feet, so the height is not settled.[^2][^3]

The tower was staffed regularly during fire season until 1990.[^2] A history site says the tower is now used on an emergency basis and also carries a radio repeater, but it gives no date for either.[^2]

The register entry, dated January 19, 1996, said an information kiosk would be set up to interpret the historic site.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association still lists the tower as standing.[^4]

Today the sources do not say whether the public may visit or climb it.[^1][^2] The register names the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources as the administering agency, so anyone who wants to see the tower should ask the DNR first.[^1]
