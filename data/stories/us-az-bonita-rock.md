Bonita Rock is a former lookout site on the Fort Apache Indian Reservation in Apache County, Arizona.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page names the reservation as the land the site sits on and marks the lookout as removed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a ground lookout rather than a tower, and marks it gone.[^2]

The Peakbagging table of Arizona lookout sites spells the name Bonito Rock.[^3] Its coordinates match the association's entry, so I treated the two listings as one place.[^3][^2] The table gives an elevation of 8,780 feet, interpolated from contour lines rather than read from a nearby survey marker.[^3] In this table, the label LO site means the building was taken away and there may be nothing left to find, or that its status is unknown.[^3]

Today, no source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site.[^3] The site lies on reservation land.[^1] None of the three sources I read says whether the public may visit, so anyone thinking of going should ask for permission first.

[^2]: Arizona Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/az/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Arizona Fire Lookouts, Table 1, Peakbagging, http://www.peakbagging.com/Peak%20Lists/AZ_Lookout1.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
