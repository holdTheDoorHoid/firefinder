The Dix Hill tower went up in 1924 in the Town of Huntington, in western Suffolk County on Long Island.[^1][^2] The state Conservation Commission put it up, using money from a nearby landowner.[^2][^3] It was a 60-foot Aermotor LS-40 tower.[^1][^3]

A March 1933 report described tests in late 1932, in which a semiportable radio set at the Dix Hill tower kept two-way contact over 50 miles with a state airplane.[^2]

A June 8, 1945 notice from the Long Island State Park Commission proposed relocating the Dix Hills tower to High Hill, where more height would cover more ground.[^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says the tower was closed in 1948 and removed in 1949, and it does not mention a move.[^1] The NYSFFLA newsletter says it was in use through the 1948 season.[^3]

Today the site is on privately owned land that has been developed.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. Because the land is private, visitors should not assume they can go there.
