The Willisville fire tower stood on Willisville Mountain, in the Unorganized North Sudbury District of Ontario.[^1] The Ontario Department of Lands and Forests built it in the 1920s.[^1] Wikipedia gives its height as 24 m (80 ft).[^1] Nearby, the community of Willisville lies on the Charlton-Cranberry-Frood Lake system within the La Cloche Mountains.[^1]

Ontario's tower layer lists a point named Burke Mountain Fire Tower, and a lookout index files the Willisville tower under the name Burke Mountain.[^2][^3]

Fire ranger John (Jack) Burke started a visitors' journal at the tower on 12 July 1927. A blog post about Camp Manitou says that may be when the tower opened.[^4]

Wikipedia says Burke ran the tower in its early years.[^1] It also recounts that, while he was scanning for fires from the cupola, he saw a herd of elephants at the foot of the mountain trail. The herd had got out of a circus truck that had broken down close by.[^1] The article also records a visit by the boxer Joe Louis and the singer Bing Crosby, who signed the tower's guestbook.[^1]

A photograph caption places forest ranger Ernie Rogers in the tower cupola in May 1971.[^5]

A lookout index lists the Willisville tower, under the Burke Mountain name, as removed.[^3]

Today no source describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain, and none says whether the site is open to the public.
