The Catoosa fire tower stands on Pilot Knob in Morgan County, inside the Catoosa Wildlife Management Area.[^1][^2] The register calls it an 80-foot fire tower and names the Tennessee Division of Forestry as its administrator.[^1] The sources we read give no year for its construction, no names of its lookouts and no record of the fires it watched, so those parts of its history are still unknown.

The register added the tower on March 20, 2016, as US 1124, TN 28, on a nomination by Christopher Covert.[^1] A Tennessee Landforms list of towers also marks the Catoosa tower as standing.[^3]

The wildlife area itself is administered by the Tennessee Wildlife Resources Agency.[^2] It closes to entry between sunset and sunrise, and some of its interior roads are gated and open seasonally.[^2][^4] A hunting guide's page for the area calls the Pilot Knob tower the high point of the area.[^4] It does not say whether the public may climb the tower.[^4] Check the area's current rules before planning a visit.
