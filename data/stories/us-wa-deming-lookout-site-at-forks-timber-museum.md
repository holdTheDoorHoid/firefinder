The Deming lookout stood on Deming Mountain in Whatcom County, about two miles north of Deming.[^1][^2][^3] The first lookout there was an 80-foot pole tower, first used in 1929.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page instead describes the earlier structure as a small tower built of native materials, so the two accounts do not fully agree.[^3]

In 1947 a 75-foot steel tower replaced it.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page says the new tower had an 8-by-8 cab, and that the lookout's living quarters came from an abandoned Aircraft Warning Service building.[^3]

The site also had an Aircraft Warning Service station.[^3] The Forest Lookouts page reports that Station Tare 1-0 was deactivated on October 16, 1943, after which the state kept the site in use as a fire lookout station.[^3]

The register says a 40-foot wooden DNR tower went up in 1965 and came down in 1985.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page gives the replacement years as 1964 to 1966.[^3]

The lookout was removed in 1985, and its cab went to the museum at Forks, Washington.[^1][^3] Sources disagree about which cab reached Forks.[^3][^5] WillhiteWeb dates the cab to 1965, while the Forest Lookouts page links an 8-by-8 cab to the 1947 steel tower, and the sources do not settle it.[^3][^5]

Today the cab is on display outdoors at the Forks Timber Museum.[^1][^6] The museum notes that few fire lookout towers remain and describes its tower as a good opportunity to see a vintage one closely.[^6] WillhiteWeb says the tower came from near Bellingham, was hauled in pieces on a lowboy and reassembled, and that its interior furnishings came from a former lookout at Sekiu, just north of Forks.[^5]

The register calls the display the Forks Museum, while WillhiteWeb and the Forest Fire Lookout Association list use Forks Timber Museum.[^1][^4][^5] The Association's Washington list marks the Forks lookout as standing and the original Deming site as gone.[^4]

None of these pages says whether the tower can be climbed, so view the display at the museum and check with the museum before visiting.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
