Teepee Point Lookout sits in Ravalli County, Montana, about 21 miles southeast of Darby, at 6,625 feet.[^1] The Bitterroot National Forest manages it.[^1]

The current lookout went up in 1984 to replace McCart Lookout.[^1][^2] It is a two-story gabled cab, and the Forest Lookouts page calls its plan a modified R-6 design.[^1][^2] The same page says McCart's lookout duties passed to this site.[^2]

The lookout is manned every summer.[^1] Firelookout.com lists known lookout personnel for 1989, 1998 and 2007.[^1] We found no fire history or closure for the site in the pages we read.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the lookout as standing, with a two-story cab.[^3]

We found no access information for the site and no rental listing for it. Anyone planning a visit should check with the Bitterroot National Forest first.
