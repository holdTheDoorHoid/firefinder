Tallow Butte is a former lookout site in the Umpqua National Forest, in Douglas County.[^2][^3]

The earliest structure in the sources dates from 1960.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com describes an 8-by-8-foot enclosed platform with a tent cabin.[^1] The Oregon page records an alidade, a sighting instrument for taking bearings, mounted on a tin-roofed platform in the same year.[^2] The two accounts differ on what the structure was, though both put a lookout on the site in 1960.[^1][^2]

A 1962 newspaper item quoted on the Oregon page describes a second-year fire watcher at the site.[^2]

The accounts diverge after that. Firelookout.com says an R-6 cab was added in 1964, and it notes an "Emergency" entry for 1970 without explaining it.[^1] The Oregon page says a lookout house was built in 1965.[^2] We could not reconcile the 1964 and 1965 dates.

The Oregon page says the site was removed in 1976.[^2] Firelookout.com marks it gone on the same line as the 1970 "Emergency" note, and gives no removal date.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon index lists Tallow Butte as a ground site that is gone.[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains on the site today.

[^3]: Oregon Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/or/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
