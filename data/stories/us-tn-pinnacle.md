Pinnacle is a fire tower on Buffalo Mountain, above Unicoi, Tennessee, in the Cherokee National Forest.[^1][^3] The register gives 1930 as the build year and describes a 14-by-14-foot live-in wooden cab on a 40-foot steel tower.[^1] A trail wiki says the Civil Conservation Corps built it, but it gives no date.[^2]

The tower was staffed into the 1980s.[^1] The register says it was last staffed then, and the wiki says service ran until 1989.[^1][^2] A 2008 visit recorded in the register noted several problems. The first flight of stairs had been taken down, the roof had at least one big hole, and most of the glass was gone, though the steel looked sound.[^1]

Restoration followed.[^1] The register credits the Friends of Pinnacle Tower with the work, helped by a grant from the Forest Fire Lookout Association.[^1] The Forest Service says that in 2011 the cab was replaced with a covered platform that matched the old catwalk and cab in size, shape and layout.[^3] The wiki says a joint partnership restored the 40-foot observation platform at a cost of $100,000, and that the trail dates from 2010.[^2]

Today the Forest Service lists the tower as open year-round.[^4] The trailhead at the foot of Buffalo Mountain is reached from Interstate 26 at Exit 32 in Unicoi.[^3] The hiking and biking trail leads to the rehabilitated tower, where the platform looks out over high peaks such as Unaka Mountain and Roan Mountain.[^3] The wiki notes that there are no fees or permits, and that horse and motorbike riding are not allowed.[^2] A forest road also reaches the tower from Dry Creek Road, but it is high-clearance, closed from mid-January to mid-March, and ends at a gate about half a mile below the tower.[^4]
