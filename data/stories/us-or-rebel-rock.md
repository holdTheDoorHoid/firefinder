Rebel Rock was a lookout site in a saddle about a mile southwest of the summit, in the Three Sisters Wilderness.[^3] The site lies in the Willamette National Forest in Lane County.[^1]

An L-4 ground cab went up there in 1955.[^1][^3][^4] A September 1967 memo quoted on the Weebly page counts Rebel Rock as one of two unused lookouts on its district and says it was in very good condition.[^4]

A 1949 Eugene Guard item, quoted on the same page, mentions Rebel Rock lookout in connection with slides shown at a Forest Service banquet, which would predate the 1955 build date.[^4] None of the pages read explains that earlier reference.[^4]

The 2017 Oregon chapter report says Rebel Rock was lost in the Rebel Fire.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register says the lookout burned in the Rebel Fire in 2017, and firelookout.com says the cab had stood abandoned since the 1960s before the fire.[^1][^3]

The Weebly page dates the loss of the structure to September 2017, when a wildfire destroyed it.[^4] None of the pages read describes what, if anything, remains at the saddle today, or whether visitors can reach it, so this story does not guess.[^1]
