Whitehawk Lookout stands on the Boise National Forest in Valley County, Idaho, at 8,384 feet.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as an L-4 groundhouse with a catwalk.[^1] Most sources date its construction to 1933 and place the cab about a mile south of the summit.[^2][^3] One forest lookout site gives 1934 for the same standard L-4 house, so the build year is not fully settled.[^4]

Firelookout.com says the cab is staffed in the summer.[^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists three known staff members: Franklin A. Schoeffler in 1940, Donna Kreiensieck in 1976, and Mr. and Mrs. Ronald G. Harbison in 1986.[^3] The same site asks readers to share more names and dates, which suggests the list is incomplete.[^3]

None of the pages read describes the lookout as abandoned, moved or burned.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The Register says it remains active and gives September 24, 2011, as its registration date.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts instead gives 2020 as the year it was added to the Register, so the two dates disagree.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels Whitehawk as a drive-up site that is staffed.[^3] The pages read do not say whether visitors may go into the cab or climb anything, and none of them mentions a rental.[^2][^3] Because the cab is staffed in summer, expect a working lookout and check with the Boise National Forest before going.[^2]
