The Elstie tower was a steel Aermotor lookout in Gallitzin Township, Cambria County.[^1][^2] The register dates it to 1930.[^1] In September 1930 the Indiana Gazette reported that foundation work had just started on a tower planned at 80 feet, close to the Blair County boundary.[^2]

The forestry department had authorized the tower as the ninth in its district.[^2] Foresters had expected the costly late-summer fire season to rule out more towers, so the approval came as good news.[^2]

An April 1939 Altoona Mirror item said Dysart resident D. Angelis had been picked as observer at the tower near Coupon. It also said he had served for years as a fire warden.[^2]

The register lists the tower as removed in 1981.[^1] It was added to the register on September 16, 2008.[^1]

No source we read describes what stands at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors. A map database still has a point called Elstie Fire Tower in Gallitzin Township. Its entry gives the type as tower and no status.[^3]
