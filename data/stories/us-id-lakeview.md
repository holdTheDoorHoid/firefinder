Lakeview Lookout stood in the Kaniksu National Forest, about a mile east of Lakeview in Bonner County, Idaho.[^1] The site sits at about 3,950 feet.[^1][^2]

The camp came first, in 1924, and a 20-foot platform followed in 1928.[^1][^2][^3] In 1931 a 40-foot pole structure topped by an L-4 cab was built.[^1][^2][^3] That structure was destroyed in 1956.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts page credits these dates to Kresek, so agreement among the pages may not be independent.[^3]

Lakeview is not the same place as Lakeview (Wabaningo), another Bonner County lookout farther north.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists this one as gone.[^4] The Idaho Fire Lookouts listing calls it a hike-in site.[^2]

None of the pages I read says what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors. Check with the Kaniksu National Forest before you go.
