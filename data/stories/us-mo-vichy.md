The Vichy lookout was built on a towersite in Maries County, Missouri.[^1] Sources disagree about how much land was bought and when.[^1][^2] A Missouri Department of Conservation annual report for 1945–46 says 240 acres were acquired for a towersite.[^1] The department's Spring Creek Gap Conservation Area page dates the first purchase to 1948: a 280-acre parcel it calls the Vichy Towersite.[^2]

In October 1948, a Bland Courier item quoted on the lookout history page reported that a wooden tower was planned north of Vichy, near Highway 63.[^1] During the 1950s the lookout was used as a Ground Observer Corps post.[^1] Its wooden cab was completely remodeled in fiscal 1958–59, and a new fire tower went up there in fiscal 1967–68.[^1]

The 1948 purchase is now part of Spring Creek Gap Conservation Area, which the department describes as an extremely scenic area of forests, woodlands, steep topography, dolomite glades and old fields.[^2] No source I found describes what remains of the tower or its site today.[^2]
