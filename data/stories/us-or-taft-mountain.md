Taft Mountain is a former lookout site in the Umpqua National Forest, in Douglas County.[^1][^3] In July 1932 a Roseburg newspaper reported plans for nine new lookout houses, one of them at Taft Mountain, near an old cabin on the Little River-North Umpqua divide.[^1]

The lookout went up in 1933.[^1][^2] The Oregon Forest Lookouts page describes a 20-foot round timber tower with an L-4 cab.[^1] Firelookout.com describes a 30-foot pole tower with a 12-by-12-foot cab.[^2] Both sources give 1933, but they disagree on the height and the form of the tower.[^1][^2] The 20-foot figure in our record matches only the first account.

Panorama photographs were taken in August 1933 and again in March 1934, and a woodshed was built in 1936.[^1] In September 1937 a Roseburg paper noted that a man had spent the previous two weeks at the lookout and then returned to school.[^1]

Firelookout.com lists the tower as abandoned under its 1933 entry, with no other date.[^2] The Oregon page and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon index both mark it as gone, though neither gives a removal date.[^1][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it.
