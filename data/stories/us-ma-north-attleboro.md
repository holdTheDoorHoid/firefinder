The North Attleboro fire tower is a steel lookout that the Massachusetts Bureau of Forest Fire Control put up in 1972 [^1]. According to the register, it was the most recent lookout the Bureau established, meant to fill an unwatched gap in its statewide detection network [^1]. OpenStreetMap lists the start date as 1 April 1972 [^2].

The register lists a 75-foot steel tower, with a wooden cab measuring 10 by 10 feet and an observation deck beneath it [^1]. It gives the elevation as 374 feet, or 114 meters [^1].

The lookout sits in the Town of North Attleboro, in Bristol County, and the register files it as US 618, MA 29 [^1]. It was entered in the National Historic Lookout Register on December 15, 2004, on the nomination of Henry Isenberg [^1].

The register entry covers the build only. It does not describe staffing, fires or what happened to the tower in later years, and it does not say whether the public may visit [^1].
