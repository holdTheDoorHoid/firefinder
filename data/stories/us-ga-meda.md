A Georgia tower list kept on tnlandforms.us places a lookout labelled "meda" in Putnam County, at 33.27555, -83.36611.[^1] The page's author says names for towers numbered 73 and above are the author's own creation, so this label is not a name taken from a historical source.[^1] It matches Meda, which RoadsideThoughts puts in Putnam County.[^2]

The entry has no BLL or gfc tag.[^1] The page says the Georgia Forestry Commission (GFC) supplied coordinates for 118 towers under the BLL tags, and that an earlier GFC list covered 203 towers under the gfc numbering.[^1]

The page's map was last revised in March 2016.[^1] No source found describes what remains at the site today, so it is unknown whether a tower still stands at those coordinates.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Meda (Putnam County, GA) - Genealogy, RoadsideThoughts, https://roadsidethoughts.com/ga/meda-xx-putnam-genealogy.htm (accessed 2026-10-21).
