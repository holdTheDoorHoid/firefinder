The Summertown Lookout Tower stands in Lewis County, Tennessee, close to a paved road, and drivers on the highway can pick it out among the pines.[^1] A 2006 visit report describes it as a 100-foot steel tower with a diagonal stair.[^1] The register does not record when it was built, and I did not find a build date or a builder in the other sources I checked.[^1][^2]

That 2006 visitor found the tower unstaffed and judged that it had gone unused for many years.[^1] The two bottom landings had been taken off to block climbing, and several boards were gone from the landings.[^1] The 2006 visit also found a locked Tennessee Department of Forestry shed on the site.[^1]

The same visitor thought the site might be private land, and saw a fence across the access road plus no-trespassing signs.[^1] Given those signs and the poor condition described in 2006, the safe course is to keep off the tower.

The register recorded the lookout on January 3, 2015, as US 1089, TN 25.[^1] The Tennessee Landforms tower list also marks it as standing.[^2] I did not find a more recent visit report or any rental or public visiting arrangement in these sources.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Summertown Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/tn/summertown-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-08).
[^2]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, Tennessee Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-08).
