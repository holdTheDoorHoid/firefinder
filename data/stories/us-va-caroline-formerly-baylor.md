A lookout in Caroline County was once called the Baylor Lookout, after the Baylors, a leading family in Bowling Green [^1]. The register now lists it as the Caroline Tower Site, formerly the Baylor Lookout Site, and says the Virginia Department of Forestry administered it [^1].

The tower was removed around 2001 or 2002 [^1]. The Forest Lookouts site also marks it as removed [^2]. The site was added to the former lookout register on July 30, 2018 [^1].

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today. The register mentions only the Baylor family cemetery, which it places across from where the tower stood [^1]. It does not say whether the land is open to visitors [^1], so ask before going, and treat the cemetery with respect.

[^1]: Caroline Tower Site (formerly Baylor Lookout Site), Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/va/caroline-tower-site-formerly-baylor-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Caroline (Baylor), Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/caroline-baylor.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
