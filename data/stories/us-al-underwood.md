The Alabama Forestry Commission built the Underwood lookout in 1938, according to the register.[^1] The register places it at East Brewton, in Escambia County, Alabama.[^1] The crew stopped at 60 feet, where the sloped legs were 14 feet square at the top, rather than build to 90 feet, where a 7-by-7-foot cab would be mounted.[^1]

Each of the four sides of the 14-by-14-foot metal cab is made from two 7-foot-wide pieces.[^1] The register says that, out of more than 900 listings, none has a cab like this one.[^1]

We found no record of who staffed the lookout or of any fires it helped with.

The register added the tower on January 27, 2010, on a nomination by Madeline Hildreth.[^1] It does not describe the tower's condition after that.[^1] We could not find a newer report on whether it still stands.

Today we could not find any information on public access or rental. A 1819 News opinion column estimates there are probably under 75 historic lookouts still standing in Alabama, and says many are unsafe and that climbing them is illegal.[^2] Please do not climb this tower, and check with the Forestry Commission before visiting any lookout site.
