Flat Top Mountain rises to 4,498 feet above sea level in Watauga County.[^1] Flat Top Manor is in Moses H. Cone Memorial Park, beside the Blue Ridge Parkway at milepost 294.[^2] A Parkway photograph credited to the National Park Service shows an observation tower on the mountain at the Cone estate, with Ben Cone standing in front of it in a white suit.[^3]

The photo caption calls that tower an observation tower. A trail guide describes a 40-foot fire tower atop Flat Top Mountain, but neither source says whether it is the same structure, so the sources do not settle the tower's purpose.[^3][^4]

The trail guide says the hike starts at Moses Cone Manor, off the Parkway at milepost 294, and climbs a broad gravel carriage road, switchbacking through woods to the fire tower.[^4] The same guide says that on clear days, Mt. Mitchell and Grandfather Mountain are visible from the summit.[^4]
