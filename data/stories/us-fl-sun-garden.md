A lookout tower named Sun Garden is listed for Clay County, Florida.[^1] Mapcarta, a map site built on OpenStreetMap and GeoNames data, spells the name Sungarden, places the tower in Clay, and gives its elevation as 105 feet (32 m).[^2] Among the nearest hamlets it lists are Leno, about six miles northeast, and West Tocoi, about seven miles east.[^2] Because both spellings are in use, a map search should try each.[^1][^2]

No source I could open describes the site today. None says whether a structure remains, or whether the public can reach the spot.

[^1]: Sun Garden - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/sun-garden.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Sungarden Lookout Tower Map - Clay, Florida, Mapcarta, https://mapcarta.com/20694388 (accessed 2026-10-21).
