Thomas Hill's lookout was a 20-foot (6 m) pole platform in Idaho.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts says the site acted as a patrol point serving Linfor Hill.[^1] The platform dates from 1934 and was given up in 1950.[^1]

Linfor Hill's own tower, a 30-foot (9 m) pole L-4, also went up in 1934.[^2] Relative to that tower, the Thomas Hill patrol point lies roughly one mile to the southeast.[^2]

The platform has since fallen over.[^1] Its cement footings, pole legs and railings survive, and the writer finds it surprising that the platform endures at all.[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in, and places it in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest at 4,450 feet (1,356 m).[^1] No source here describes whether the public may visit it today.[^1]
