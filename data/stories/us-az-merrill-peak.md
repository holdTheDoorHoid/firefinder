Merrill Peak is a former lookout site in Graham County, on the Coronado National Forest.[^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists its tower as gone, at coordinates 32.70306 north and 109.96042 west.[^1]

The Arizona peak list calls it Merrill Peak (LO site) and gives a spot elevation of 9,288 feet.[^3] That figure is a spot height printed on the map, not a survey benchmark.[^3] The site falls on the Webb Peak map sheet, in Township 8 South, Range 23 East, Section 26.[^3] The list uses the LO site label where the building has been removed or its status is unknown, and it adds that the site may show no evidence of the building.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the summit today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
