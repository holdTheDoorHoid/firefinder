The Florence Lookout Tower stood in Florence County, Wisconsin.[^1] Mapcarta lists it in Florence, Wisconsin, as a tower also known as the Rainbow Fire Tower, at an elevation of 1,578 feet, or 481 meters.[^2] Mapcarta says the page draws on OpenStreetMap, GeoNames, Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons.[^2]

WJFW describes how the state's towers worked. When smoke was sighted, the lookout used binoculars and a compass table to pinpoint it, and the azimuth and distance were passed to a dispatch center.[^3] By WJFW's account, 72 towers were still in service when they were decommissioned in 2015.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the coordinates as 45.96237, -88.49942, and it marks the tower as gone.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site or whether the public can reach it. Anyone curious about the spot should ask the local landowner before going.
