The Lantana Fire Tower stands in Cumberland County, at an elevation of about 2,037 feet.[^1] The earliest date we found is 1936.[^2] A topographic map of Crossville from that year labels the Lantana Lookout Tower, so the tower was in place by then.[^2]

We found no builder and no build date. The National Register form on the Division of Forestry towers says that many of the Division's 1930s towers were built with help from the Civilian Conservation Corps, which it credits with building 98 fire lookout towers in Tennessee.[^3] We found nothing that ties Lantana to that program. The same form lists a Lantana tower in Cumberland County, with no further detail.[^3]

We found no staffing history and no named fire for this tower. OpenStreetMap maps it as a tower named Lantana Fire Tower, of the observation type.[^4] Tennessee Landforms lists it as standing.[^1]

Tennessee Landforms warns against climbing, since the steps are frequently missing or decayed.[^1] It also notes that many towers have been taken down and that most sites are privately owned.[^1] We found no public visiting arrangement for Lantana, so please do not climb it. Ask the landowner before going near the site.
