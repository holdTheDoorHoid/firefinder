The Waterloo Tower Site is a former fire lookout in Athens County, Ohio.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists 30 former lookout sites in Ohio, and Waterloo is one of them.[^5] The register gives its elevation as 1,000 feet (305 meters).[^1]

The register recorded the site on September 3, 2014, and gives its record number as US 1577, OH 23.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Ohio list marks the tower as gone.[^3] Forest Lookouts lists it under Athens County, with the Ohio Department of Natural Resources as its agency.[^2]

For context, an Ohio historical marker says 39 fire towers were erected in the state. The first went up in 1922 on Copperhead Hill, in Shawnee State Forest.[^4]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
