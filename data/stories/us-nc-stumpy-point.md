In December 1939 the Civilian Conservation Corps built a lookout tower near Stumpy Point in Dare County, North Carolina.[^1] A 1940 state report, as quoted on a lookout list, gives the erection date as December 21, 1939.[^1] The wooden tower, treated with creosote, stood 120 feet (37 m) tall and had a catwalk and an interior staircase.[^1] A CCC side camp of enrollees, living in house trailers, was established to build it.[^1]

In 1953 a 120-foot (37 m) Aermotor steel tower replaced the wooden one.[^1]

In the spring of 2009 a private party offered the steel tower for sale. It had been taken down and loaded into a 40-foot (12 m) shipping container.[^1]

In 2012 the lower half was re-erected at the Homestake Ranch in northeastern Wyoming, and a wood-frame cab was added to it.[^1][^2] The upper half was planned for a landowner's property in Minnesota.[^1][^2]

According to the Homestake page, the lower half was erected on the same ranch as the Homestake lookout.[^2] That page says the land is owned by the Moskee Land Corporation and that access is by permission only.[^2] No source found describes what, if anything, remains at the original site near Stumpy Point.
