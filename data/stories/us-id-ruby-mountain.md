Ruby Mountain stands about 6 miles southeast of Eastport, in Boundary County, Idaho, on the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1] Its lookout was a 30-foot pole tower topped by an L-6 cab, built in 1935.[^1][^2]

The earliest named lookout is Hollice Aldridge, whom the Idaho Fire Lookouts page dates to 1933 and 1934.[^1][^2] The same page lists Lloyd Hughest as staffing the tower in 1946, citing the book *Fire Lookouts of the Northwest*.[^2]

The tower was abandoned in the 1950s.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association index lists the tower as gone.[^3] The firelookout.com page also says a frame cabin was still there, but no source gives a date for that.[^1]

A 2025 post from Boundary County historic sites describes a Ruby Cabin that overlooks Spruce Lake.[^4] The post calls it the first cabin used for fire detection in the county, and says it has survived several fire towers on the same site.[^4] The post does not name Ruby Mountain, so it is not certain that this is the same cabin as the frame cabin on the mountain.[^1][^4] Planned work would secure the existing outhouse to the mountainside.[^4]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page tags the site as hike-in. No source says whether the site is open to visitors today.[^2]
