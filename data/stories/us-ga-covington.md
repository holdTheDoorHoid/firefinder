The Georgia lookout list gives a lookout called "covington" in Newton County, at 33.54656, -83.91216, with an elevation of 885 feet (269.7 m).[^1] The list also carries two codes for it, BLL-50789 and gfc-411.[^1]

A RepeaterBook listing for the WA4ASI amateur radio repeater names its site as the Newton County Forestry Fire Tower, at Covington.[^2] The listing was reviewed on January 14, 2026, and it marks the repeater as on the air.[^2]

The listing's text gives no coordinates, so the link between it and the list entry rests on the county and the name.[^2] According to that listing, the site hosts a radio repeater on a forestry fire tower.[^2] No source describes the tower's condition, or whether visitors can reach the site.
