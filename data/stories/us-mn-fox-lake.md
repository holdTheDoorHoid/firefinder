The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower at Fox Lake in Beltrami County, at about 47.84 north and 95.04 west, and marks it as gone.[^1] I found no other source that names a tower at the lake.[^1][^2]

The Beltrami County tower report is a list of the county's towers, in a table with columns for GPS position, owner, date, height and type.[^2] Its list has no entry for Fox Lake.[^2] The report does mention a lookout symbol on a 1936 county map at a site it names only by township, range and section, and says such finds need further study.[^2] The sources I found therefore do not say when a tower at Fox Lake was built, who ran it, how tall it was or what it looked like.[^1][^2]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Minnesota Lookouts (By County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/mn-co/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Beltrami County Fire Lookout Towers (report), Wikimedia Commons, https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8f/Beltrami_County_Towers_Report_-_corrected.pdf (accessed 2026-10-10).
