Squeezer Creek is in Lake County, Montana, about 15 miles south of Swan Lake, at an elevation of 3,680 feet.[^1] Swan Lake sits to the east of both Flathead Lake and Bigfork.[^3] Firelookout.com names the Montana DNRC as the agency.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list places the site at 47.72600 north and 113.78070 west.[^2] The lookout was an 80-foot crow's nest, which firelookout.com dates to the 1920s.[^1] The page says the crow's nest was still in place in 2004.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list records the crow's nest as abandoned.[^2] The newest date firelookout.com gives is 2004.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2]
