The Aniwa tower stood in Marathon County. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list places it at 45.03104, -89.26429 and marks it as gone.[^1] A regional lookout site gives the same county and the land-survey code 30N-10E-35.[^2] OpenStreetMap has a point named Aniwa Lookout Tower at nearly those coordinates.[^3]

A December 2015 WXPR report says the fire towers in service at the time were built in the 1930s and 1940s. The same report says the DNR had recently announced that the towers used to spot out-of-control fires were being removed.[^4]

Across the state, a 2019 chapter report counted 10 towers sold and removed, with 4 sold and awaiting removal and 56 left to dismantle.[^5]

The list marks the Aniwa tower as gone, and no source describes what remains at the site or whether it is open to the public.[^1]
