The Burnt Knob lookout stood in Idaho County, in the Payette National Forest, about 7 miles north-northwest of Chamberlain Guard Station.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives its elevation as 7,339 feet.[^1][^3]

Sources agree that a tower with an L-4 cab went up in 1933.[^1][^2][^3] Forest Lookouts and Idaho Fire Lookouts both describe a 15-foot tower.[^2][^3] Firelookout.com describes a 10-foot pole tower instead.[^1] The sources disagree on its height.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists one known staffer, Elmer Purdyman, for 1915, citing the Idaho County Free Press.[^3] The page's history starts with the 1933 tower and does not link that 1915 entry to it.[^3]

The tower was destroyed in 1960.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

Two structures share the Burnt Knob name.[^1][^4] The one on the Nez Perce/Bitterroot National Forests is a log cabin built in 1930.[^4] Keeping Watch gives coordinates for its Burnt Knob and links to that cabin's page, so check any map pin against the Chamberlain Guard Station description before using it.[^5][^1]
