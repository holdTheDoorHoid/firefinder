Log (Baldy) Mountain is in Valley County, Idaho, about 24 miles east-southeast of McCall, on the Boise and Payette National Forests.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives its elevation as 9,179 feet.[^2]

The lookout was a log cabin. The sources date it only to the 1930s, and Forest Lookouts attributes that date to a source it names only as Kresek.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts says its records cannot yet document who staffed the lookout.[^2]

Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both describe the cabin as abandoned.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the lookout as gone, but none of the pages we read says when or how the cabin disappeared.[^4][^1][^2][^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^2]
