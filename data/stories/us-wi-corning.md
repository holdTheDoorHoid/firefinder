A newspaper item dated January 16, 1940 reports that Fromm Brothers, of Hamburg, had put up a fire tower in the town of Corning, Lincoln County, on land the firm owned about 12 miles (19 km) west of Merrill.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site quotes the item, which says the builders used the steel framework of a 150-foot (46 m) radio tower that had been dismantled near Janesville.[^1]

The same item says the state conservation department maintained fire towers in Corning, Rock Falls and Harrison, and that the Fromm tower brought the county's total to five.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin county list shows a tower called Corning, in Lincoln County, as gone.[^2] The same list has a separate Fromm entry for Lincoln County, also marked gone, at different coordinates, so the sources do not show whether the two names are one tower or two.[^2]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
