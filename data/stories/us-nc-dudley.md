The Dudley Lookout Tower sits in Wayne County's Coastal Plain, on the side of the state near Goldsboro.[^2] The blog notes that it is not a mountain tower, which it treats as unusual.[^2] The register gives the site an elevation of 174 feet (53 m).[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register added the tower on May 25, 2026, as US 1821, NC 31.[^1] Its entry gives 1935 as the year it was built and lists the N.C. Forest Service as administrator.[^1] The 2026 blog post says the construction date and builder are not publicly documented, so the 1935 date comes from the register alone and I could not confirm it elsewhere.[^2]

The register says the Forest Service sold the tower at auction, along with several others, to a private buyer in April 2018.[^1] It also says the tower is no longer in operation, and that its lower stairs have been removed to prevent access for safety.[^1]

Treat the tower as private property, given the 2018 sale the register records.[^1] The blog says ownership and access are not documented publicly, and it advises against crossing fences or entering private property to reach it.[^2] The register describes no public access, and the blog says not to treat the coordinates as a destination, so do not try to climb it.[^1][^2]
