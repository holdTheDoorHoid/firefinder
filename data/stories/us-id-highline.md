Highline Lookout stood in the Nez Perce National Forest, about 12 miles southwest of the Moose Creek Ranger Station in Idaho County, at an elevation of 7,260 feet.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts dates the first staffing of the spot to 1914, nearly two decades before the tower was built.[^3]

The lookout went up in 1932 as a 35-foot platform tower topped with a log cabin.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts describe an "L-5" cabin on a log crib, while Forest Lookouts calls it a two-story log cabin, so the sources do not agree on the cabin's form.[^1][^2][^3]

Firelookout.com says the cabin and tower are no longer there, and Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the lookout as gone.[^1][^2][^4]

No source describes the site today beyond the cabin and tower being gone. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the spot as a hike-in, but none of the sources says whether the site is open to the public.[^2]
