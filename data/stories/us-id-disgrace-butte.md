The Disgrace Butte lookout was an L-4 tower on a 30-foot pole, put up in 1935 in the Nez Perce National Forest, about 14 miles northeast of Elk City.[^1][^2] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page gives the site's elevation as 6,589 feet.[^2] Both pages say the tower may have taken the place of the Vermillion Peak lookout, about two miles northeast, but neither gives further detail.[^1][^2] The FFLA's Idaho list has a separate Vermillion Peak entry, typed Ground and also marked gone.[^3]

The page's Known Staff list names Gail Renshaw for 1949 and Edwin Morris for 1952, and the Morris entry cites a former lookout register.[^2]

Firelookout.com reports the tower standing in 1959 and gone by 1963.[^1] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page also lists it as gone in 1963.[^2] The FFLA list records it as a tower that is gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left at the site today. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page labels it hike-in, so the site is reached on foot.[^2]
