The Majette lookout tower was a forest fire tower in Bay County, Florida, east of Bayou George.[^1]

A January 1932 newspaper item reported that two 30-foot observation towers had been built to spot and report fires. One stood at Old Majette, about five miles east of Bayou George in Bay County, and the other at Smith's Still in Calhoun County.[^1] A telephone system of about fifty miles was to link the towers to turpentine operators across the protected territory.[^1]

In September 1940 a newspaper item said improvements to the Majette lookout tower site were well along that year.[^1] A 1945 USGS topographic map labels the Majette Fire Tower and describes it as a primary landmark for forest management, with California Swamp and Green Bay Swamp among the tracts it overlooks.[^2]

A January 1948 item gives a different picture: a tower three miles east of Bayou George, 85 feet high and of steel.[^1] The 1932 and 1948 accounts disagree on both height and distance, and the sources do not show whether they describe the same tower.[^1]

The lookout page says the tower was sold at auction in 2008.[^1] No source I found describes the site today, including whether any part of the tower remains or whether visitors can reach it.

[^2]: 1945 Map of North Of Allanton, Panama City, FL (USGS topographic map, published 1945), Pastmaps, https://pastmaps.com/map/north-of-allanton-bay-county-fl-usgs-topo-1945 (accessed 2026-10-21).
