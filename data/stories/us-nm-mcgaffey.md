McGaffey Lookout stands in McKinley County, New Mexico, on the Mount Taylor Ranger District of the Cibola National Forest.[^1][^2]

Its first structure was a wood tower from the 1920s.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register also gives a 59-foot Aermotor MC-39 tower, dated 1934.[^1][^3]

The present tower dates from 1965 and is 54 feet tall, with a 14-by-14-foot CL-100 metal cab, a catwalk and a corrugated roof.[^1][^3] The 2020 report also notes a tool storage shed and a vault toilet at the site.[^2]

In 1943 the Gallup Independent announced that Mrs. Richard Clawson would serve as lookout during May, June and July. The paper believed it was the first time a woman had done that job on the Cibola.[^3] In 1947 the same paper said the lookout was manned by Garland Willcoxsin.[^3]

The Register, the 2020 report and the Forest Lookouts page all date the present tower to 1965.[^1][^2][^3] A hiker's blog gives 1959 and says the lookout is manned during high fire danger.[^4]

In 1988 the lookout was judged not eligible for the National Register, with its age given as the reason.[^2] The 2020 report says a later review found it eligible for nomination.[^2]

A lookout listing shows the tower as standing.[^5] One hiker reported the landing was closed on their visit, so check before going up.[^4] The sources do not say whether the public may climb it.
