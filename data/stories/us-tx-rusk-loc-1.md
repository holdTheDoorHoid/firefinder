The Forest Lookouts page for Rusk files a Texas Forest Service tower in Cherokee County under the heading RUSK #9.[^2] A June 1939 item from the Rusk Cherokeean, quoted on that page, calls it Tower No. 9 and places it 1 1/2 miles northwest of Rusk.[^2]

Items from 1946 say that patrolman J. Layton Jones handled fires from the Rusk lookout tower, three of them in January and fourteen of the 34 forest fires recorded in March.[^2]

In January 1993 the Tyler Morning Telegraph ran a notice seeking bids on Texas Forest Service fire towers, including the Rusk Fire Lookout Tower in Cherokee County. Bids were due by 9:30 a.m. on February 23, 1993.[^2] The notice announces a planned sale; it does not show that the Rusk tower was sold or removed.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas index lists two Rusk sites in Cherokee County, Loc 1 at 31.81696 north and 95.16485 west, and Loc 2 at 31.80444 north and 95.11667 west. It marks both as gone.[^1] The index does not say which of the two matches the 1939 tower.[^1]

Today the index lists both sites as gone. No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at either one, or whether either is open to visitors.[^1]
