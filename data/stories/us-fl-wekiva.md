A wire report from Tallahassee, carried by the Fort Walton Beach Playground Daily News and dated March 17, 1981, described a forest fire that was moving quickly. The blaze began on a Sunday afternoon in Levy County and was still uncontrolled the following day.[^1] The report said no private homes were in danger, but the Division of Forestry lookout tower at Wekiva was threatened.[^1]

The flames came within a few feet of the lookout's state-owned home before volunteer firefighters stopped them.[^1] The lookout's furniture was taken out of the house as a safety step.[^1] The report refers to the tower's lookout and to a state-owned home for that lookout, which points to a staffed tower in 1981.[^1]

A U.S. Geological Survey record still carries the name "Wekiva Fire Tower, ADC." It describes an atmosphere monitoring site.[^2] The record says nothing about the tower's condition. We found no source describing what remains at the lookout site today, or whether the public may visit it.

[^2]: Wekiva Fire Tower, Adc. (USGS site 291945082384301), U.S. Geological Survey Water Data for the Nation, https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/291945082384301/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
