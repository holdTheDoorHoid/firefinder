The Humboldt tower stood in Luzerne County, Pennsylvania, according to the register.[^1] The register describes it as a steel Aermotor tower, built in 1921.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page holds most of what we found about its early years. An April 1923 newspaper item reports the death of a fourteen-year-old boy who had been battling a blaze near the tower.[^2] That item places the fire near Shellton, in Schuylkill County, which does not match the Luzerne County listing.[^2] The sources do not settle the point.[^1][^2]

In 1924 state foresters were trying to identify whoever was keeping dogs in the tower. Twice, dogs had been found in its cabin along with food and water.[^2]

In 1937 a newspaper reported that Peter Polega, who had received an amateur radio license, was posted to the Humboldt tower as a lookout for forest fires.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts page says the tower was removed in 1966.[^2] The register says it was removed before 2005, which fits that date.[^1] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Humboldt Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/pa/humboldt-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Humboldt, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/humboldt.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
