The Yonah Mountain Fire Tower stood on the summit of Yonah Mountain, in White County, Georgia, inside the Chattahoochee National Forest.[^1][^2]

A White County Public Safety post on Instagram says the U.S. Forest Service built the tower around 1925.[^3] The post adds that the tower watched over the county for decades, and that observers up there could see smoke from miles off and warn crews about fires without delay.[^3]

According to the same post, the tower stayed in use until the 1980s, when better detection methods reduced the need for such lookouts.[^3] The register describes the tower as one that once stood on the summit.[^1]

The register lists the U.S. Forest Service as the agency, and gives the registration date as January 16, 2016, on a nomination by Richard Beck.[^1]

The register does not describe what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1]
