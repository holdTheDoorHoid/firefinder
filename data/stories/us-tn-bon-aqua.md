The Bon Aqua Lookout Tower Site is in Hickman County, Tennessee.[^1] The register gives its elevation as 963 feet, while the Tennessee tower list gives 994 feet.[^1][^3] Forest Lookouts lists the Tennessee Division of Forestry as the agency behind it.[^2]

A visit report dated February 26, 2006 describes a 60-foot steel tower with diagonal stairs, just off Highway 100 on Tower Drive.[^1] The visitor noted that the town's water towers stand nearby.[^1]

The sources do not agree on whether the tower still stands. Forest Lookouts marks it as removed, but gives no date.[^2] The Tennessee tower list marks it as standing, followed by a question mark.[^3]

In 2006 the visitor found the tower on private land with no-trespassing signs. A home sat on the tower site, and a fence surrounded the base.[^1] The visitor did not get a close look, because the owner was not around.[^1] The most recent visit report we found is from 2006, so we cannot say what remains at the site today.[^1] The Tennessee list says most former tower locations are now private property, and it advises against climbing, because steps are often removed or rotten.[^3]
