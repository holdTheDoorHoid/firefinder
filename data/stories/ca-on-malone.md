The Malone tower stands in the Petawawa Research Forest, near Chalk River, Ontario.[^1][^2] The research station on that land dates from 1918, when military authorities asked the Forestry Branch of the federal Department of the Interior to look after the forest on the Petawawa Military Reserve.[^3] The station also pioneered systematic study in several fields, fire research among them.[^3]

Firelookout.ca describes Malone as one of Ontario's two active fire lookouts, and places it in the research forest.[^1] An independent Ontario fire-tower site says only two towers are still manned in the province, and both are in that forest, east of Algonquin Park.[^4] That site carries no date, so its claim that they are manned may be out of date.[^4]

The forest still serves research: the Canadian Forest Service, provincial governments and industry all use it as a site for scientific work.[^3] No source found describes whether visitors can reach the tower or walk to it, so check with the forest before planning a trip.
