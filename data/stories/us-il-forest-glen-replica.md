The 72-foot observation tower stands in Forest Glen Preserve, a 1,800-acre preserve in Vermilion County that was founded in 1968.[^4][^6] The Vermilion County Conservation District governs the preserve.[^3]

Most sources give the tower's height as 72 feet.[^3][^5][^6] A 2021 Illinois Country Living article gives 74 feet.[^2]

The article quotes Amy Steeples, who has worked at the site for 25 years, saying the tower was built in 1969 solely to observe the Vermilion River valley.[^2] The same article calls it a fire tower, complete with a cab, but says it was never used to prevent fires.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Illinois list places it under "Relocated / Replica Lookouts," as Forest Glen (Replica – Public Observation), with the status standing.[^1] The magazine's account does not call it a copy of an older lookout, so the two sources disagree about what kind of structure this is.[^1][^2]

In a May 2026 release, the district describes it as a 72-foot observation tower and tells visitors to be ready for 99 steps to the top.[^5] The district announced a Tower at Twilight hike on Saturday, June 27, 2026, scheduled to meet at the tower parking lot, east of Pine Knoll Pavilion, before a short gravel walk to the tower.[^5] The preserve's address is 20301 E 900 North Road, Westville.[^5] The 2021 magazine says the tower remains open.[^2] Wikipedia says the tower draws many climbers every year.[^3]
