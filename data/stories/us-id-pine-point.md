Pine Point is a spot on a ridge in Shoshone County, Idaho, about six miles southwest of Kellogg, in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1] It sits at an elevation of 4,374 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the site as gone.[^4]

The lookout here was a cabin rather than a tower, set at the northwest end of the ridge.[^1] The firelookout.com and Forest Lookouts pages both date the cabin to 1947.[^1][^3]

A 1944 entry names John McKay among the known lookout personnel, and the Idaho Fire Lookouts page cites a news article for it.[^1][^2] That is three years before the 1947 cabin date, so either the 1944 entry belonged to an earlier station or one of the dates is wrong. The sources do not say which.

The cabin was abandoned in the 1950s.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under drive-up.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, or whether it is open to visitors.
