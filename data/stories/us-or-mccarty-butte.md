McCarty Butte, in Lake County, got a small ground cabin around 1939.[^1][^2][^3] Forest Lookouts gives that date as approximate.[^3] The register and firelookout.com both give 1939.[^1][^2]

The house was a non-standard design, 12 by 12 feet, built of wood on a rock foundation. A 4-by-4 outhouse was the only other building.[^1][^3] It held an Osborne #6 firefinder, and the observer's water came from Smoke Creek Spring.[^3]

In June 1943 Jake Kooyman was named the butte's sentinel and lookout man.[^3] The house was painted in 1948, and in 1956 the station carried the air marking number F-4.[^3]

The register and firelookout.com both say the cabin was gone by the 1960s.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts marks it as removed, without a date.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
