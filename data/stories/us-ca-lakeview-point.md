Lakeview Point sits at 3,774 feet in Siskiyou County, on land the Klamath National Forest administered.[^1] The register gives 1937 as the build year, but the same entry says the tower described in a 1986 survey was never built.[^1]

A Klamath National Forest chronology, quoted on a lookout history site, records a May 13, 1939 announcement that a temporary lookout would be installed at Lakeview Point, west of the Copco road.[^2] The plan was to house it in a tent with radio contact.[^2]

The register's only photo shows a small observation-only ground cabin that was reportedly built in 1954.[^1]

The register says the Forest Service gave up the site in 1976 and sold the land.[^1]

According to the register, a small gazebo built by the landowner stands at the site, and the lookout platform's remains lie nearby.[^1] Neither source says whether visitors may enter.[^1][^2]
