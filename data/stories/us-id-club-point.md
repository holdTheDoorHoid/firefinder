Club Point Lookout stood in the St. Joe National Forest, in Shoshone County, Idaho.[^1][^2][^3] In 1935 an L-4 cab was placed atop the site's easternmost point.[^2][^3][^4] The site sits at an elevation of 6,236 feet.[^3]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page files the site as a hike-in, so visitors had to walk in.[^3] The lookout was abandoned in 1953, and the two Club Point pages agree on both the 1935 and 1953 dates.[^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list classes it as a ground site rather than a tower, and marks it gone.[^1]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to the public. Anyone planning a trip should check with the St. Joe National Forest first.
