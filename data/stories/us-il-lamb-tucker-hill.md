Lamb, which its page also titles "Lamb (Tucker Hill?)", was a secondary fire lookout in Hardin County, Illinois, within the Shawnee National Forest.[^1] The page dates the pole to about 1938. It is a 40-foot (12 m) pole fitted with a ladder and crow's nest, raised as a secondary lookout site.[^1] The question mark after "Tucker Hill" suggests the name of the hill is not certain.[^1]

The FFLA's Illinois chapter, in its 2018 year-end report, counts four Illinois towers that, as far as the chapter knows, still stand but are not in active service. Lamb is not one of them.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
