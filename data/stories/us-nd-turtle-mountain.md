The Turtle Mountain tower began on the J. Clark Salyer National Wildlife Refuge, where it stood as a 100-foot steel tower.[^1] When the U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service no longer needed it, the tower went to the North Dakota Forest Service in 1957.[^1] After that transfer, it was reconstructed in the Turtle Mountains of Rolette County to detect fires.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register listed it on July 1, 1991.[^1]

The register says the tower now serves mainly as a radio relay site.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's North Dakota list, though, marks the Turtle Mountain tower as removed.[^2] The two sources disagree about whether the steel tower still stands, and the sources I read do not settle it.[^1][^2]

No source I read says whether the public can reach the site, or describes the ground there now.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Turtle Mountain Tower (US 20, ND 1), National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/nd/turtle-mountain-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: North Dakota Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association (firelookout.org), https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nd/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
