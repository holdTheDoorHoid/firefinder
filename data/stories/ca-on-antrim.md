The Antrim Fire Tower stands on Onaping Lake Road in Unorganized North Sudbury, Sudbury District, Ontario.[^1] The listing describes the design as heavy-gauge steel with an octagonal cupola.[^1] Its towerman's quarters were at Onaping Lake, in McDonald Bay.[^1] The listing gives the elevation as 513 m (1683 ft).[^1]

The tower appears on a Department of Lands and Forests Tower Detection System map from 1963, and on the 1997 Sudbury topographic map 41-I.[^1]

Today the tower is used as a communications tower.[^1] No source describes what else remains at the site, and none says whether the public may visit, so do not plan to climb it.
