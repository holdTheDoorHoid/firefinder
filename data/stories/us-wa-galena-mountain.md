Galena Mountain got an L-4 cab in 1931. The register gives that date.[^1] Eric Willhite's page says little is known about the station. It attributes to Kresek the claim that the station was established by 1931 with that cab.[^2]

In 1942 and 1943 it was an Aircraft Warning Service post.[^3] Willhite's photo captions give the dates as August 29, 1942 to October 7, 1943, and he writes that sleeping quarters for the observers were added with Aircraft Warning Service funds.[^2][^3]

The cab's fate is less clear. The register and the Weebly page both say it was moved to Maloney Ridge in 1941.[^1][^3] Willhite says the move was reported in 1950, and he is not sure whether the structure was helicoptered out or dismantled and reassembled there.[^2] The Maloney Ridge register describes its own lookout: an L-4 cab built in 1941, an AWS cabin in 1942, a new L-4 cab in 1953, and removal in 1969. It does not say whether the 1941 cab came from Galena Mountain.[^4]

The register says the Galena Mountain lookout is now gone.[^1] Willhite's March 2018 visit found no road to the site. He describes a climb of about 80 vertical feet from the Index-Galena Road, and a photo caption dates a washout on that road to 2007-2008.[^2] His access note reads "Paved (when access is re-established)."[^2] His photo captions label three footings at the lookout site.[^2] The Maloney Ridge register says that lookout was removed in 1969 and does not describe what, if anything, remains there.[^4]
