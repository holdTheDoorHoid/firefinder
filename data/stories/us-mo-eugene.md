In November 1950 a newspaper reported that work had started on a Conservation Commission lookout tower known as the Eugene tower.[^1] It was to stand on Highway 54 at the Miller and Cole county line, and it was to be 100 feet high and in operation by 1951.[^1] Four acres had been acquired for the towersite in 1949-50.[^1]

The same item said Tom Farris of Olean had been hired as the forest towerman.[^1] The tower was to be under the supervision of the Lake Ozark fire protection district, based at Camdenton.[^1]

In fiscal 1971-72 the tower was moved because of a highway relocation project. The next year a new parking lot was built at the tower.[^1] The page does not say where the tower was moved to, or whether it still stands there.[^1]

The page files the tower under Miller County.[^1] But Wikipedia's entry for the community of Eugene lists it in Cole County, so the county line location is worth checking.[^2] No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1]
