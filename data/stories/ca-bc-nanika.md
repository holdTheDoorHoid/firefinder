Nanika is a lookout site in the Bulkley-Nechako area of British Columbia.[^1] The Canada Lookouts listing gives its position as 54.137 N, 127.495 W, with an elevation of 1,750 m (5,741 ft).[^1]

A Facebook post in the BC Forest Fire Lookouts group carries the line "1978 Nanika Lookout, via Houston." The line gives a year but no other date.[^3]

The Houston Hikers page describes Nanika Mountain as standing at 1,889 m (6,198 ft), at the northern end of the Morice Range and overlooking the north end of Morice Lake.[^2] The Canada Lookouts listing and the Houston Hikers page both use the name Nanika, and the sources do not say whether they describe the same place.[^1][^2]

The page's directions give the route as about 80.9 km from Houston, but its details list the distance as 60 km or more.[^2] The route runs along the Morice River Road and then the Morice-West FSR, a forest service road.[^2] The page labels it a backcountry route suited only to experienced walkers, with no trail signs and no cell coverage.[^2] It advises carrying a map, a GPS and emergency supplies.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the lookout site today.
