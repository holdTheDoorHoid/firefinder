Cedar Mountain is a summit in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, in Kootenai County, Idaho, at 4,764 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

A crow's nest is dated to about 1929, and a 20-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab to about 1934.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists one name from the 1940s, Beb Lemons, cited to a letter to Ray.[^2]

A December 1950 item from the Post-Register, reprinted on the Forest Lookouts page, reported that radio gear for the state forestry department was due soon. It included a mobile relay unit for a site on Cedar Mountain.[^1]

The tower was destroyed in 1959. Idaho Fire Lookouts, firelookout.com and the Forest Lookouts page all record that year.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts page also carries a separate "Removed" line, with no date attached.[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^2] None of the sources we read describes what remains at the summit now, or says whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
