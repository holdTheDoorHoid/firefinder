Flag Point sits on the east side of Mount Hood, in Wasco County, Oregon, within the Mt. Hood National Forest.[^1] A lookout first went up on the summit in 1924, a 6-by-6-foot cabin on a 40-foot pole tower.[^1][^6] In 1932 the first structure was replaced with an L-4 cab on a 30-foot pole.[^1] The present tower, with an R-6 flat cab, was built in 1973.[^1][^3][^6] Sources disagree on its height. Most say 41 feet, but the recreation.gov listing and one 2026 news report say 40.[^1][^3][^4][^5][^6]

The forest staffed the lookout every summer as part of its fire-watch network, and it was available to rent in the off-season.[^2][^6]

Lightning started the Grasshopper Fire, which was first seen east of Grasshopper Point on July 23.[^3] Crews spent three days clearing vegetation around the structure, cutting trees where they could, and applying fire retardant to it.[^2] The Forest Service said the lookout was destroyed overnight, and another report puts the loss in the early hours of August 1.[^2][^4] The summer staffer was safely evacuated.[^3]

The lookout is gone.[^2][^6] The sources we read do not describe what, if anything, remains on the summit.[^2][^3][^4] A recreation.gov listing still describes the lookout, even though the fire destroyed it.[^2][^5] The forest reminds visitors to respect all closures and fire restrictions, so check its current notices before any trip there.[^2]
