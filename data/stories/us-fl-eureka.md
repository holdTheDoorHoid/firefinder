The Eureka Fire Tower Site lies in the Ocala National Forest, in Marion County, Florida.[^1][^2] The community of Eureka is an unincorporated place in the county, about 3.5 miles east of Fort McCoy.[^3]

The U.S. Forest Service administered the site.[^1] The register dates the tower's removal to the 1940s or 1950s.[^1] Going by the footings, the register thinks the tower was probably short and perhaps built of wood.[^1] Robert Spear nominated the site, and the register recorded it on July 24, 2016.[^1]

Footings from the tower are still there.[^1] Poles set into the ground around the perimeter appear to mark the site's edge, and a witness post sits near Forest Road 9.[^1] We found no source saying whether visitors may enter the site or how to reach it on foot, so check with the Forest Service before going.
