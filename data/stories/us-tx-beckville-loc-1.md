The Texas Forest Service ran a fire lookout tower in Panola County, near Beckville. The Forest Lookouts site calls it Beckville #51.[^1] A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description written in 1958 places the tower about 3.5 miles airline north of the town, on the highest timbered hill nearby. It describes a grey, four-legged tower topped by a glass-enclosed cab, and gives its overall height as about 140 feet.[^1]

In a notice dated January 15, 1993, the agency asked for sealed bids to buy, take down and remove its fire lookout towers. The Beckville Fire Lookout Tower was on the list.[^1] The notice offers the tower for sale. It does not say when the tower was taken down.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list has two Beckville entries, Loc 1 and Loc 2, and marks both as gone.[^2] Neither point is tied to this tower in any source we read.

No source we read describes the site today, including whether anything remains on the hill.

[^1]: Beckville, Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/beckville.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Texas Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
