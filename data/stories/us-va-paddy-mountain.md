The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Paddy Mountain tower under Shenandoah County, Virginia, and Hardy County, West Virginia, at 38.97389, -78.59222, with its status as gone.[^1] The same list puts a question mark after the word tower, so the structure type is not certain.[^1]

MountainZone places a Paddy Mountain summit in Frederick County, Virginia, at an approximate elevation of 2,986 feet (910 m).[^2] The two sources disagree on the county, and their coordinates are about 4.4 km apart, so it is unclear whether they describe the same spot.[^1][^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the tower site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Virginia Fire Towers (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/va-co/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Paddy Mountain in Frederick County VA (Wardensville Area), MountainZone, https://www.mountainzone.com/mountains/virginia/frederick-va/summits/paddy-mountain-4/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
