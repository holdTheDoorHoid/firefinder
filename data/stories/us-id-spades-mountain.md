Spades Mountain, in Kootenai County, Idaho, began as a camp lookout in 1923.[^1][^4] A 50-foot pole tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab went up in 1926, and a log cabin for living quarters was added in 1928.[^1] A 54-foot steel tower was built in 1941.[^1] In 1963 that tower was moved to Long Mountain, and a 67-foot treated timber tower with an R-6 flat cab took its place.[^1][^2] Another local list dates the 67-foot tower to 1952 and calls the 1941 tower an Aermotor.[^4]

The staff list on the Fire Lookout Tower page runs from 1946 to 1972, and many of the names are couples.[^3] In October 1945, a newspaper item quoted on the Forest Lookouts page described two 19-year-old women, Mabel Rose and Marjorie Olson, as the staff.[^4] The Register says the tower has not been staffed since the 1970s.[^1]

Its easy access and closeness to Coeur d'Alene made it a popular hangout, and it was frequently vandalized.[^5] The tower was removed in July 2015.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association said the lookout was taken down under a demolition contract, that the group was not told in advance, and that no materials were salvaged.[^6]

The Register says the ground house remains.[^1] A January 2022 article said a local photographer had helped lead the charge to save the tower and possibly rebuild one on the site for the rental program, but that no further progress had been made.[^5] Because the tower is gone, check with the Idaho Panhandle National Forests before going to the site.[^1]
