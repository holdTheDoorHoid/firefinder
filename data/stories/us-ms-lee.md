The Mississippi Forestry Commission administered a fire lookout tower on the highest point in Lee County.[^1] The register describes the tower in the past tense and says concrete footings from it remain on the site, so the tower itself appears to be gone.[^1]

The commission still owns a 0.1-acre parcel there, and a radio tower stands on it.[^1] The register gives the site an elevation of 519 feet (158 m) and lists it as US 2104, MS 3.[^1]

The site was added to the Former Fire Lookout Sites register on January 1, 2026, on the nomination of Doug Sprouse.[^1] The register does not say whether the parcel is open to visitors.

[^1]: Lee Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ms/lee-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
