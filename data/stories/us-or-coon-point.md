Coon Point is a cape in Klamath County, about 4,179 ft (1,274 m) above sea level.[^2] Forest Lookouts lists a Coon Point lookout in Rogue River National Forest, and gives its location as 36S-6E-19.[^1] The site page dates the entry to the 1950s and calls the place "an emergency lookout point."[^1] The TopoQuest gazetteer places the cape on the Pelican Bay USGS map.[^2]

Today, no source describes what remains at the site, or whether it is open to visitors.
