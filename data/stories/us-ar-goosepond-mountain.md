Goosepond Mountain is in Saline County, Arkansas, about 14 miles northeast of Hot Springs.[^1][^3]

The Arkansas Forestry Commission dates from 1931 and also erected many more towers.[^4]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the lookout tower on Goosepond Mountain in 1940.[^1] The survey recorded it as a lookout run by the Arkansas Forestry Commission, standing on the summit.[^1] The tower was steel and 40 feet (12.2 m) high.[^1]

The survey tied the tower to a station called Goosepond, which lies about 30 feet to its west-northwest.[^1] The federal listing gives the tower's name as Goosepond Lookout Tower.[^2]

Nothing I could read says whether the tower is still standing. No source describes the summit today, or says whether the public may reach the tower.
