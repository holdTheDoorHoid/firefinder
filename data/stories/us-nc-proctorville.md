A North Carolina lookout-tower list places a Proctorville tower in Robeson County, at 34.496 N, 79.032 W.[^1]

The lookout-history site says the CCC put up a 99-foot 9-inch (30.4 m) steel tower in 1934.[^2] It credits the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources with the lookout.[^2]

In April 1954 a report in The Robesonian, quoted on the same site, said county forest ranger Braddie Pait and other Forest Service officials had closed the grounds to everyone except people accompanied by a State Forest Service representative.[^2] According to Pait, the grounds had been left unfit for legitimate visitors, and the report apparently blames young boys for damage to official signs and posts.[^2]

No source I could read describes what remains at the site today, whether the tower still stands, or whether the grounds are open now.
