The Wilson Lookout Tower stood in East Feliciana Parish, Louisiana, near the village of Wilson.[^1][^2] The village is part of the Baton Rouge metropolitan statistical area.[^2] Wikipedia's list of the state's fire towers includes the tower under that name.[^1]

Fire-protection money was directed in 1915, making it possible to hire lookout watchmen or patrolmen.[^1] The statewide total was thirty-nine fire towers by 1943 and fifty-six by 1950.[^1]

The list marks the Wilson tower as torn down.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public.
