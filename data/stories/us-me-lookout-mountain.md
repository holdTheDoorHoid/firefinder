The site is listed in the town of Lee, in Penobscot County.[^1] The association's Maine list gives Burke Hill or Cobb Hill as other names for it.[^2]

Maine set up lookouts to catch forest fires quickly, because its lumber and papermaking businesses relied on the state's trees. Most of those lookouts are no longer in use.[^3]

The former-lookout register says the tower was removed during the 1930s.[^1] The association's Maine list classes the site as ground rather than tower, so the sources disagree about what stood there.[^2]

The site entered the register on September 15, 2008, nominated by Henry Isenberg.[^1] The association's Maine list marks it as gone.[^2] No source read describes what, if anything, remains on the hilltop, or whether the public can reach it.
