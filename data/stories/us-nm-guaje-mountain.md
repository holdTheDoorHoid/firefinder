Guaje Mountain is a lookout site in Los Alamos County, New Mexico, at about 35.92 N, 106.28 W.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists its type as Ground and marks it gone, with no register number.[^1][^2] The same county list shows Bandelier, a two-story cab in Los Alamos County, as standing.[^2]

No source we checked describes what remains at the Guaje Mountain site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^2]: New Mexico Lookouts (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/nm-co/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
