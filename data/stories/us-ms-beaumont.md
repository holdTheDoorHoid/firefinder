Beaumont was a fire tower in Perry County, Mississippi.[^1] A 1943 recovery note by the U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey describes it as a standard U.S. Forestry Service lookout.[^1] According to the note, the tower is a four-legged steel structure with an enclosed cabin on top, roughly 110 feet (33.5 m) tall.[^1] The survey station sat on a prominent hill, and surveyors measured 74.21 feet (22.6 m) from it to the tower's center.[^1] They sighted on the center of the tower's top.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site marks the tower as removed, with no date given.[^1] Research found no source describing what remains at the site today or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Beaumont - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/beaumont.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
