A list of Georgia fire towers that tnlandforms.us published, last revised in March 2016, places a tower labelled "tobacoo rd" (list number GA285) in Richmond County, at 33.38024, -82.0878.[^1] The list gives its elevation as 462 with no unit; read as feet, that is about 141 m above sea level.[^1] The list files the tower under two numbers: BLL-50819, one of 118 towers whose coordinates the Georgia Forestry Commission (GFC) supplied, and gfc-026, from an older GFC list.[^1] The page says its author made up the names for towers numbered 73 and above, and GA285 falls in that range, so "tobacoo rd" is a label rather than a documented name.[^1]

Georgia has kept reducing the staff on its fire towers since the late 1980s.[^2] A Georgia Forestry Commission report from 1987 counted 93 towers with full-time staff and 73 with seasonal staff.[^2] A 2006 article, drawing on a forestry commission spreadsheet, reported that 137 of the state's 144 surviving towers were unstaffed, and that 119 of those were recorded as antenna towers.[^2]

No source describes what remains at this site today, or whether the public can reach it.
