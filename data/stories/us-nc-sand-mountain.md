Sand Mountain is listed as a fire tower in Caldwell County, North Carolina, at 35.98333, -81.66055.[^1] The list's footer counts 208 North Carolina fire towers, and it warns that some entries may be observation towers or Coast Guard lookouts.[^1] A history page for the state's lookouts lists a Sand Mountain in Caldwell County under the U.S. Forest Service.[^2]

No source in this set describes what stands at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
