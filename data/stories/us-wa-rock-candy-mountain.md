Rock Candy Mountain is about 8 miles west of Tumwater, in Thurston County.[^1][^2] The Washington Division of Forestry/DNR is listed as the administrator.[^2]

The register describes a tower and cabin built in the 1930s.[^2][^1] The willhiteweb.com account places the build after 1934. It says photos dated October 1934 were taken four feet above ground, so no structure was there yet.[^3]

The same account cites a 1942 state lookout inventory that calls the lookout a groundhouse. That description does not match the tower in the register.[^3][^2]

The register and firelookout.com both list the tower and cabin as gone.[^2][^1] A listing on washingtonlookouts.weebly.com marks it removed.[^5]

A 2022 hike report describes the summit as the site of the former fire lookout, with views to the west and north.[^4] It does not describe any remains of the lookout, and none of the other sources we read does.[^4]

Visitors can reach the site on foot by trail in Capitol State Forest.[^4] The trails are shared with bicycles, and motorized bikes are allowed from May through November.[^4] A gate sometimes blocks the road. When it is open, the willhiteweb.com account says a car can drive 2.5 miles to the summit.[^3]
