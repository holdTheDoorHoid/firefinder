Remer – first was one of two fire lookouts southwest of the town of Remer in Cass County, Minnesota. A report on Cass County's fire towers says the two sites lay about two miles apart, off Highway 200.[^1]

The report describes the first as likely an 81-foot (25 m) ladder tower, standing about three and a half miles west of town.[^1] A 1934 survey note in the same entry gives the height as 94 feet (29 m), so the figures disagree.[^1] The owner was the Minnesota Forest Service, now the forestry division of the state Department of Natural Resources.[^1]

According to the report, the first lookout was likely moved to a site it calls Blueberry when the second Remer tower went up.[^1] That second tower, erected in 1938, was a 100-foot (30 m) Aermotor stairway tower.[^1] The report says it later became a primary Forest Service lookout, which made the Lima lookout to the southeast unnecessary.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list marks Remer – first as gone.[^2] No source we found describes what remains at the original site today.[^1]
