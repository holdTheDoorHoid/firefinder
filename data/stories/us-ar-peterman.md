Peterman was a lookout tower in Pope County, in the Ozark National Forest.[^1] The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the station in 1954 as a steel tower with four legs, about 110 feet high, carrying an observation cabin.[^1] The survey gave the station's position by its distance and direction from the towns of Appleton and Hector.[^1] It also ran a traverse from a triangulation station named Peterman to the center of the tower's base.[^1]

For context, the Encyclopedia of Arkansas credits the Civilian Conservation Corps with putting up 3,100 towers in the Depression years, many of them in the Ouachita and Ozark-St. Francis national forests.[^2]

The Central US Lookouts page marks the tower as removed.[^1]

A National Geodetic Survey list of marks it does not publish gives the survey mark at the tower site a code meaning its surface was reported destroyed.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, or whether visitors can reach it.
