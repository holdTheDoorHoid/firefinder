The Parting Springs lookout stood in Randolph County, West Virginia, at an elevation of about 4,004 feet, or 1,220 meters.[^1] The West Virginia Division of Forestry administered the site.[^1] The sources we read do not say when the tower went up, who built it, or how tall it was.

West Virginia's first fire towers went up in 1916.[^3] The state's network of them closed in 1990, after decades of service.[^3] We found no observer named at Parting Springs and no fire that its tower watched.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the original Parting Springs site as having no tower now.[^2] The same list places a relocated tower at Godwin, also in Randolph County. It is listed as standing, and its location is marked private instead of given as coordinates.[^2] We found no date for that move and no reason given for it.

The former Parting Springs site was registered as a lookout site on January 26, 2017, on the nomination of Brian M. Powell.[^1]

We found no public access to the Godwin tower.[^2] Please ask the owner before going near it.
