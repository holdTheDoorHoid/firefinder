Hopeful Lookout Tower is a tower in Mitchell County, Georgia, near the unincorporated community of Hopeful.[^1][^2] A web list of Georgia lookout towers places it at 31.14768, -84.40102 and numbers it 27.[^1] The list says towers 1 to 72 are the ones listed in the U.S. Geological Survey's name database.[^1] The list's notes credit USGS topographic maps and Georgia Forestry Commission data, among other sources.[^1]

The area was first settled around 1819, but the name Hopeful came into use only in the late 1800s.[^3] A photo blog says the community is named for Hopeful Baptist Church, which it places in the middle of town.[^3] Wikipedia gives another account, saying the name reflects the hopeful outlook of the first settlers.[^2]

No source I found describes the site today, so what, if anything, remains there, and whether the public may visit, is unknown.
