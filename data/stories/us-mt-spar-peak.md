Spar Peak lies in Lincoln County, Montana, about 18 miles south-southwest of Troy, in the Kootenai National Forest, at an elevation of 6,585 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a ground lookout that is now gone.[^2]

The firelookout.com page records an L-4 cab here in 1930 and says it was destroyed in 1959.[^1]

Hiking trail No. 324 now climbs to the summit in the Cabinet Mountains.[^3] The trail guide gives an eight-mile round trip with about 2,800 feet of climbing, rates it hard, and gives driving directions to the trailhead from MT-56.[^3]

The trail guide reports traces of an old lookout tower on the summit.[^3] Firelookout.com describes an L-4 cab, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a ground lookout, so the sources do not clearly agree on what stood there.[^1][^2][^3] No source I found says how much of the structure survives.
