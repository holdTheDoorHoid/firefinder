The Cooper Hill lookout, listed as West Ridge, stood in Washington County, Maine.[^4] Maine Memory dates a wooden fire watch tower on the mountain to 1913, with a rebuild in 1925.[^1] A Forest Lookouts page titled Cooper Mountain #37 gives the same dates, so the two names most likely refer to one lookout.[^2]

A photographic print from about 1919 shows Forest Service watchmen Allen and Oscar Sadler at their post on Cooper Hill.[^1]

A steel tower was put up in 1937. Maine Memory says it used steel from the Naval Radio Tower in Bar Harbor, and the Forest Lookouts page describes an unconventional design built from salvaged parts.[^1][^2]

By 2008 the tower stood among a large number of commercial radio antennas, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine chapter listed it among planned removals for 2009, saying it had to come down to make room for new radio towers.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine list marks the tower as gone.[^4] No source we read gives the year it came down, or describes what remains at the site today.
