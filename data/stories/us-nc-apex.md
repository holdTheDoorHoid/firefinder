The Civilian Conservation Corps erected a fire tower near Apex in Wake County, North Carolina, on July 12, 1940.[^1][^2] The tower was 100 feet (30 m) tall, built of creosote-treated timber, with a catwalk and an interior staircase.[^1]

The first site was at the Oak Grove Primitive Baptist Church crossroads, about four miles east of Apex.[^2] In 1951 a lease was filed for a new site on Swift Creek Church-Apex Road, about 2.5 miles southeast of Apex, where a steel Aermotor tower of about 99 feet (30 m) was erected.[^2]

The history gives 1981 as the last year the tower was manned.[^2] In 1986 the site reverted to its owner and the tower was sold.[^2] The history does not say where the tower went after the sale.[^2] No source we read describes what remains at either site today, or whether either is open to the public.

[^1]: Apex, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/apex.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: North Carolina Forestry Service History, Legeros, https://legeros.com/ralwake/wake/history/forestry.shtml (accessed 2026-10-21).
