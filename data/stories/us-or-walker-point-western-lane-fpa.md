This Walker Point sits about 24 miles east-northeast of Florence, in Lane County.[^1][^2] The Western Lane FPA administers it.[^1]

The register and firelookout.com say a 35-foot pole tower with a 6-by-6-foot cab went up in 1942.[^1][^2] The Rex page gives that first tower as 55 feet, so the sources disagree on its height.[^3] The register and firelookout.com say a 30-foot live-in tower replaced it in 1952.[^1][^2] The Rex page instead quotes a 1951 state annual report describing a 30-foot tower topped by a 14-by-14 lookout house.[^3]

The Columbus Day storm of October 12, 1962 destroyed that tower.[^1][^2][^3] A 1963 state report, quoted on the Rex page, says that year's work was a rebuild of a lookout it calls Walker Peak Lookout, lost in the 1962 wind storm. We could not connect that name to this site.[^3] The register and firelookout.com date the replacement, an enclosed three-story cab, to 1963.[^1][^2][^3]

The register says the three-story cab was removed in 1969.[^1] The Rex page says the same,[^3] and firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^2]

The Rex page says that in 2014 a site unused for years went back into service for the Forest Watch camera detection system.[^3] That page does not say what equipment is there now. Apart from the 2014 item, no source we read describes the site today, and none says whether the public can reach it. The register names the Western Lane FPA as administrator, so ask that office about access before you plan a trip.[^1]
