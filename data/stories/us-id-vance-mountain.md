Vance Mountain is a former lookout site in Idaho County, Idaho, on the Bitterroot National Forest.[^1][^2] Some listings call it Vance Point instead.[^2][^3]

Its elevation is listed as 6,800 feet (about 2,073 m).[^2] The site was used in the 1930s.[^1][^2][^3]

The sources differ a little on where the camp stood. Forest Lookouts places the detection gear on the western point and the tent camp lower, beneath the summit.[^1] The firelookout.com page and the Forest Service's Selway-Bitterroot list both put the camp on the westernmost point, about 16 miles north-northeast of the Magruder Ranger Station.[^2][^4]

The Forest Service list and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's list both record the site as abandoned.[^4][^5]

No source describes what, if anything, remains of the camp, or whether the site is open to visitors. One Idaho listing tags it "Hike-In".[^3]
