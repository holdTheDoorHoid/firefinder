The Scare Ridge lookout stood about 20 miles east-northeast of Reedsport, in Douglas County, Oregon.[^2][^1]

The register dates the lookout to 1948.[^1] It gives a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab on top, administered by the Western Lane FPA.[^1][^2] A history page quotes a 1948 annual report of the Oregon Department of Forestry, which says a 14 by 14 foot cabin was built on a 30-foot tower.[^3] The same report says a lumber company's division laid two miles of protective road linking the lookout to the Smith River road system, and it gives $1365.20 for materials and $624.49 for labor.[^3]

The register says the lookout was staffed into the 1970s.[^1]

In August 1951 a fire near the Umpqua River threatened the station, and crews backfired to save it.[^3] The history page quotes one newspaper calling the blaze "the Umpqua river fire near Scottsburg" and another calling it "the Vincent Creek fire."[^3] A 1966 item says a teenager was evacuated from the lookout as a precaution. The lookout sat near the northwest edge of a fire that night.[^3]

The history page gives 1973 as the removal year and says the tower came through two forest fires.[^3] The register says only that the lookout was removed at some later point.[^1]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.
