Crane Hill was a 30-foot tower with an L-4 cab in the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1][^2] The Idaho County list places it in Township 31 North, Range 5 East, Section 2, and gives the same design.[^3] The lookout dates to about 1933.[^1][^2][^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page gives the site's elevation as 3,823 feet and files it under its Selway region.[^2]

It was destroyed in 1954, according to the three lookout sites we read.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks it gone as a tower, at 46.05773 north and 115.8027 west.[^4]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page tags the lookout as drive-up.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
