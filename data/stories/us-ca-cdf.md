The OpenStreetMap map database calls this structure the CDF Lookout Tower. The name suggests a link to the California Department of Forestry, but the sources we read do not say who built it, when it went up, or what it was used to watch.[^1][^2]

A notice of exemption for the Ukiah Forest Fire Station, posted on December 27, 1999, describes a project to build a standard two-engine CDF station and to demolish the buildings already on the site.[^2] The notice says the existing structures are more than forty years old and too small for new, larger fire engines.[^2] It does not mention a lookout, so we have not linked it to this tower.

The OpenStreetMap record notes that there are holes in parts of the balcony and describes the condition as poor.[^1] The same note says it was repainted somewhat recently.[^1]

We could not find its build date, staffing, fire history or present status in the sources we searched. We also found nothing about public access, so please do not climb it.

[^2]: Ukiah Forest Fire Station, CEQAnet, https://ceqanet.lci.ca.gov/1999128256 (accessed 2026-10-09).
