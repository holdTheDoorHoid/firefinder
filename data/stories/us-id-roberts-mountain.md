Roberts Mountain sits northeast of Lake Pend Oreille, where the Bitterroots reach their northern tip.[^4] Firelookout.com places the lookout 17 miles southeast of Bonners Ferry in Bonner County, Idaho.[^1]

In 1928 the summit was fitted with a platform tower and a cabin for living quarters.[^1][^3] In 1933 a 60-foot pole tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab was built.[^1][^2] Rex's Fire Lookout Page describes that tower as having an L-6 cab.[^3]

The sources disagree on the national forest. Firelookout.com gives the Kootenai National Forest, while Idaho Fire Lookouts and Rex's page give the Kaniksu.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts has no documented staffing history for the lookout.[^2]

All three lookout sources give 1958 as the year it was destroyed.[^1][^2][^3] The climbing guide puts a lookout on the summit between 1928 and 1958.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site a hike-in.[^2] The approach starts in Troy, Montana, and runs 12 miles west on Callahan Creek Road (FS-427) to the FST-407 trailhead. The route then runs more than 8 miles round trip, with about 3,200 feet of climbing.[^4]

No source read describes what remains on the summit today. The climbing guide describes a route to the peak, not the lookout site.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
