The grassy summit of Buck Bald lies inside the Cherokee National Forest, in the Coker Creek area near Tellico Plains.[^1] The Tennessee lookout list places it at 2,348 feet (716 m) in Polk County and marks its tower removed.[^2] A Forest Service page describes it as the site of a former fire tower, reached by Forest Roads 311 and 198 off Highway 68, and recommends a picnic there with a wide view over the countryside.[^3] The visitors' page says the summit once held a Forest Service fire tower.[^1]

The visitors' page describes a stone marker reading "C-15" in big lettering, which it says was placed to mark the tower.[^1] A cabin rental company's page calls the marker concrete and says planes used it to pin down their position.[^4] The two pages disagree about the material.[^1][^4]

Today the drive from Highway 68 changes from rough pavement to Forest Service gravel and ends at a compact parking spot, with steps climbing to the grassy top.[^1] The summit has a single picnic table, and cell reception may be missing there.[^1] Walkers can leave a car in a small lot on Highway 68 and hike a 4-mile round trip instead.[^1]
