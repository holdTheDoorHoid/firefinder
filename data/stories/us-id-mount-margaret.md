Mount Margaret sits in Latah County, Idaho, and is part of the St. Joe National Forest.[^1][^3][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives its elevation as 3,765 feet.[^2]

A tower with an L-4 cab went up in 1936, on the west point of the mountain.[^1][^2][^3]

All three sites say the lookout was destroyed in 1952. None of them gives a cause or describes how it was lost.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Idaho sites marks the site as gone.[^4]

The sources we read do not describe what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the site is open to visitors. Idaho Fire Lookouts calls the lookout a hike-in, so a visit would mean walking in.[^2]
