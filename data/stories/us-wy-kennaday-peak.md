Kennaday Peak Lookout sits on Kennaday Peak, east of Saratoga in Carbon County, Wyoming, inside the Medicine Bow National Forest.[^1][^2][^4] The Civilian Conservation Corps raised the peak's first structure in the 1930s, and it was one of six built in that area.[^1][^3] That first cab, an L-4, was later replaced.[^2] The current tower, a flat-top R-6 cab measuring 15 feet square with a catwalk, dates to 1964 and stands on a 10-foot concrete block base.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

The register listed the lookout on September 1, 1997, on the nomination of the Medicine Bow-Routt forest supervisor.[^1] Firelookout.com names Wilma Perue as a lookout staffer from 1969 to 1971.[^2]

The tower still stands, and it is now an overnight rental.[^3][^4] The Forest Service lists the season as mid-July to mid-September, at $80 a night, with room for four in two bunk beds.[^3] Guests get a propane heater, solar lights and a propane cookstove.[^3] Indoor plumbing is absent.[^3] A vault toilet stands outside, and visitors must supply their own water.[^3]

The drive is gravel.[^4] The Carbon County guide describes about six miles on Brush Creek Road from Wyoming Highway 130, then a left turn onto Forest Road 215 for another 6.5 miles to the summit.[^4] The county guide recommends high clearance and says four-wheel drive is not needed, while the Forest Service says a 4WD may be needed.[^3][^4]
