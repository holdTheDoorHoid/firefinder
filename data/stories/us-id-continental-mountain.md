Continental Mountain, in Boundary County, Idaho, carried a fire lookout built about 1938. It was a 50-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, on a peak about 10 miles north of Upper Priest Lake in the Kaniksu National Forest, at an elevation of 6,677 feet.[^1][^2]

The tower no longer stands.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts says no date for its removal or destruction has been recorded.[^1] Rex Kamstra's listing describes the tower as one that has since departed, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks it as gone.[^2][^4]

No source describes what, if anything, is left on the peak today, and none says whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site "Hike-In," which describes how it is reached rather than whether it is open.[^1] Ron Kemnow's Forest Lookouts page gives only the forest and the legal location, township 65 North, range 5 West, section 36.[^3] Check with the Kaniksu National Forest before planning a trip to the peak.
