Bald Ridge, which the Central US Lookouts site lists as Baldridge, was a fire tower in Pulaski County, Missouri.[^1] The site files it under the Mark Twain National Forest.[^1] A June 1937 notice quoted there says it was one of the first fire towers built in that area, and that it was a "Crow's Nest," a lookout built atop a tall tree.[^1]

A 1947 Coast and Geodetic Survey description, also quoted on the page, gives a different picture. It describes a steel structure with a closed-in wooden platform at the top, about ten miles southeast of Fort Leonard Wood and about ten miles northeast of Roby.[^1]

The same 1937 notice says the tower had been abandoned, with its territory to be divided between the Texas and Roby towers.[^1] The two accounts do not agree, and the page does not explain the gap.[^1] Today the page lists the tower as removed.[^1]

No source read describes what remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Baldridge - Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/baldridge.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
