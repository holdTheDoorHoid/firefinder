The Bullards Creek Lookout Tower is listed in Jeff Davis County, Georgia, at 31.92352, -82.46874. The list gives its elevation as 211 with no unit; read as feet, that is about 64 m above sea level.[^1] The statewide list of towers numbers it 11.[^1] It says towers 1 to 72 were listed from the USGS name database, and this tower falls in that range.[^1] Its code, gfc-527, uses the numbering of an older Georgia Forestry Commission list, which the statewide list says described the locations of 203 towers.[^1]

A Forestry Commission district supervisor told a newspaper in 2006 that the state's fire tower count had reached a high of over 200 in the 1960s.[^2] A commission spreadsheet from that year counted 137 of the state's 144 surviving towers as unstaffed.[^2] At that point the towers were being auctioned, with some going for scrap and others to hunting clubs or similar groups.[^2]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public.
