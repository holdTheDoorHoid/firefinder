The Wilkie Lake fire tower is in Algoma District, Ontario.[^1] Canada Lookouts files it under Algoma District, Wilkie Lake and Elliot Lake.[^2]

A trip report of a visit on October 9, 2022 says the writer stood at the tower's base.[^3] The writer did not climb it, because its lower ladder section was missing.[^3] The only way to the higher ladder was to stand on an old sighting table, which the writer did not think looked stable.[^3] Please do not climb it.

The approach the report describes starts at a boat launch inside Mississagi Provincial Park, paddles across Flack Lake, follows the Old Baldy Loop trail, and ends in an off-trail bushwhack.[^3]

No source says whether the tower site is public land or open to visitors, or whether the tower lies inside the park.[^1][^2][^3]
