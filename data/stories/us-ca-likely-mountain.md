Likely Mountain stands at about 7,377 feet in Lassen County, and the California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection's Lassen-Modoc Unit administers it.[^1] The lookout dates to 1966.[^1] CDF architect Mike Plesha proposed reworking the long-standing BC-3 cab, and officials asked for a reduced-scale prototype to be tested first.[^1] The 64-square-foot prototype led to a full-size cab at Sawmill Peak in 1969.[^1] Its 38-foot steel tower has three legs and was sourced from Thompson Peak in Plumas National Forest.[^1]

The Register says a trailer, called the Nomad, has served as the residence there since 1967.[^1] As of 2015, the Register says the tower was staffed intermittently during predicted lightning events.[^1] A 2008 CAL FIRE list named Likely Mountain among the lookouts that might be staffed that fire season.[^2] The sources read do not name a fire the lookout spotted.

The Register listed the lookout on October 20, 2019, as US 1321, CA 144.[^1] CAL FIRE's March 2022 lookout fact sheet still lists it under the Lassen-Modoc Unit.[^3] No source read says it was abandoned, burned, moved or restored.

Today the sources do not describe public access. A 2008 CAL FIRE fact sheet says the agency does not rent its lookouts.[^2] Because the lookout is staffed only at times, please do not climb it or enter the grounds.[^1] Check with CAL FIRE's Lassen-Modoc Unit before planning any trip.[^1]
