The Apex Mountain fire lookout is listed in the Okanagan-Similkameen area of British Columbia.[^1] The firelookout.ca index gives its year built and structure height as N/A.[^1]

A YouTube video is titled as a visit to the lookout in 1959, travelling there by Willys truck.[^2] A post in the BC Forest Fire Lookouts Facebook group is titled as the Apex Mountain lookout in 1988, with one of the poster's old ham radio friends in the doorway.[^3]

No source I could read describes the site today, so whether any of the tower remains is unknown.
