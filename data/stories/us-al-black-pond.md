Black Pond Lookout Tower stands in Winston County, Alabama, within the Bankhead National Forest, and the U.S. Forest Service administers it.[^1][^2]

The Register says Civilian Conservation Corps workers built the tower between 1936 and 1938.[^1] Its build field reads "circa 1936-1938," so the single year of 1936 in our record is the start of that range rather than a firm date.[^1]

It is a 100-foot steel tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab.[^1] The Register also notes that the tower's wood has been replaced in recent years, but gives no date for that work.[^1]

The Register says the tower is fenced off. The access road crosses private land, and the Register asks visitors to get the nearby landowner's permission before going.[^1] Please do not climb it. An opinion column by Thomas Kaufmann says many of the state's remaining towers are in poor shape, very unsafe, and illegal to climb.[^3]

The Register gives November 27, 2016 as the date the tower was registered, under US 1159, AL 71.[^1] The sources I could read give no staffing history and no record of fires it watched.
