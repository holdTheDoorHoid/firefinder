The Coulson Fire Tower is listed in the North Cochrane District of Ontario.[^1] The firelookout.ca listing says the tower was in unusually good condition in 2016.[^1] The same listing says the former observer's cabin is thought to survive at the mountain's trailhead.[^1] The listing gives the position as 48.63805, -80.33022.[^1]

No source describes the site after 2016, so the sources do not confirm whether the tower still stands. The listing gives no access details, so do not assume the trailhead is open to visitors.

[^1]: Coulson Fire Tower, Firelookout.ca, https://firelookout.ca/lookouts/?slug=coulson-fire-tower (accessed 2026-10-20).
