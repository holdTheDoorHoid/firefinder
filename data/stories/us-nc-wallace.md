A list of North Carolina fire towers includes a tower at Wallace in Duplin County. The list gives its coordinates and county, but no survey mark.[^1]

On May 19, 1955, The Wallace Enterprise reported a drive to raise money for an observation tower in Wallace, tied to Observer Corps activities.[^2] Dr. H. W. Colwell, the county's civil defense director, announced the campaign.[^2] The tower was to stand on top of the old pump house at the new town hall site, and it was to be equipped with a telephone.[^2]

The article describes a civil-defense observation tower, so it may not be the structure the fire-tower list names.[^2] The coordinates TopoQuest gives for the WLSE-AM radio tower in Wallace put it about 2,000 feet (600 m) from the list's point.[^1][^3]

No source describes what, if anything, stands at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
