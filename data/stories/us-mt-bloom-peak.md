The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register places Bloom Peak in the Kootenai National Forest, in Sanders County, Montana, at 5,863 feet (1,787 meters).[^1] Firelookout.com puts the site nine miles north of Murray, in Shoshone County, Idaho, and Sanders County, Montana, on the Coeur d'Alene and Kootenai forests, at the same elevation.[^2] The Idaho Fire Lookouts site gives 4,932 feet and the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^3] The sources do not agree on the state, the forest, or the elevation, and we could not settle them.

The lookout went up in 1932.[^1][^2][^3] The register says the structure "may have been an L-4 cab."[^1] Firelookout.com calls it an L-4 cab.[^2] The Idaho site describes a 30-foot L-4 tower.[^3]

We found no documented staffing history. The Idaho site says, "We don't yet have a documented staffing history for this lookout."[^3]

The lookout was abandoned in 1943.[^1][^2][^3] The register says the structure was removed after 1943.[^1]

The register says "A flat gravel spot and a bit of concrete remain at the site."[^1] The Idaho site, which reports a visit in 2022, describes "Rock foundation where the tower once stood."[^3] The register entry was made on January 8, 2021, and was nominated by Michael Wharton.[^1]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site lists the site as drive-up.[^3] We found no current Forest Service statement about access, so check with the national forest before going. There is no lookout structure to climb.[^1][^3]
