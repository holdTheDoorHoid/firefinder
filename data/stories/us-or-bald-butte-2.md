Bald Butte sits above Oakridge in Lane County, Oregon.[^1] In June 1917 a government trail crew under Assistant Ranger J. C. Kuhns was working a path from the Salt Creek bridge up to the summit, where, a newspaper reported, a lookout station was to be established.[^1]

Oakridge was one of several ranger districts set up in the Willamette National Forest, according to that forest's history.[^2]

In the days before August 3, 1917, a lookout named Gus Ryker was posted on the butte.[^1] He spotted smoke coming from a house roof in the valley below, phoned the house, and then called Oakridge.[^1] A crew from Oakridge drove to the fire by automobile and, with a bucket brigade, put the flames out.[^1]

No source describes the butte today, including whether any trace of the lookout remains or whether the summit is open to visitors.
