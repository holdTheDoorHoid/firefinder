Buck Knob was a fire lookout tower in Wise County, Virginia.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page quotes a Coast and Geodetic Survey station description from 1963. It places the station about 6 miles northwest of Wise and 5½ miles southwest of Pound. The survey point is the top of a glass cabin's roof, carried on four steel legs, and the survey gives an overall height of about 50 feet.[^1]

In 1950 the tower had a tender, who kept groceries in the tower building.[^1] That December the Wise County forest warden reported that the building had been broken into a few nights earlier, and that thieves carried off carbide and groceries. It was the second break-in.[^1]

Earlier that year the same warden, Owen C. Carter, had broken a leg while repairing a telephone line to the lookout. For the past two months he had been running fire fighting from bed.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Buck Knob tower as gone.[^2] The latest dated item on the Forest Lookouts page is the 1963 survey description, and the page gives no removal date.[^1]

A TopoZone map page still lists a point called Buck Knob Lookout in Wise County. A map label does not show whether a structure stands there.[^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the knob now.[^1][^2][^3] The 1963 directions to the survey station ran through a pipe gate, and the sources do not say who owns the land or whether visitors may go there.[^1]
