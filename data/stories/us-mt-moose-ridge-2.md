Moose Ridge is a former fire lookout in the Lewis and Clark National Forest, about 37 miles west-southwest of Choteau, Montana.[^1][^2] The site is listed at 6,912 feet.[^1]

Work on the lookout started in the summer of 1940.[^2] A newspaper item from that June, quoted on a Montana lookout history page, said five CCC men would begin on it the next week under a foreman named Paul Hazel.[^2] The lookout was to sit on the ridge between Rock and Moose creeks.[^2] By January 1941 the lookout house was 95 percent complete, and a telephone line down to the Gates Park-Spotted Bear line still had to be built.[^2] The same June item also calls the post the Lake View lookout.[^2]

Firelookout.com lists the site as a camp in the 1940s and says it was abandoned in the 1950s.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association also lists it as a camp that has been abandoned.[^3]

None of the sources here describes what, if anything, remains on the ridge today. One page about the lookout also carries the single word "Removed," with no date or explanation.[^2]
