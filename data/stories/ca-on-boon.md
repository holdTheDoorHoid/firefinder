Boon Tower appears on a fan site's list of still-standing Ontario towers, at Bull Lake, north of Massey.[^1] The Canada Lookouts index gives its place as Bull Lake and Massey.[^2]

Most of Ontario's fire towers went up between the 1920s and the 1950s, when the Department of Lands and Forests had jurisdiction over them; that department became the Ministry of Natural Resources in 1972.[^3] The towers saw less use in the 1960s, when aerial spotting and firefighting crews took on more of the work; the fan site says the government later had many of them destroyed.[^3]

Boon has no asterisk on the list. The list's key says an asterisk marks a tower whose cupola was removed and which now serves as a communications tower, so the list does not record that change for Boon.[^1] The same list says the MNR no longer maintains these towers and asks visitors not to climb them.[^1] No source describes what remains at the Boon site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
