Firelookout.ca places the Sphene Lake Fire Tower at Rainy River, at 48.98227, -93.29433.[^1] Its record gives no tower type, height or year built.[^1] The lake lies in the Rainy River District of northwestern Ontario, near Fort Frances, and the Northernlite camp once stood on it.[^2]

A 2015 exploration film on a camp history site carries the title "ATV Ride Abandoned 1924 Sphene Fire Tower and Buildings."[^2] The page offers that title as its only date for the tower.[^2]

A fan-run list of Ontario towers that still stand names a Sphene Lake Tower near Rainy Lake.[^3] The entry gives only a name and a location, so the list does not confirm that it is the tower described above.[^3] The list is undated.[^3] It also reports that the Ontario Ministry of Natural Resources decided in 2013 to take down many of the towers it lists, and it asks visitors not to climb them, because the ministry no longer maintains them.[^3]

Guests once reached the camp by boat across Rainy Lake and then by a controlled-entry portage road, or by floatplane.[^2] Rainy River First Nations bought the property in 2012, and the buildings are now abandoned.[^2]

Today, no source we found describes the tower site or says whether the public can visit it.
