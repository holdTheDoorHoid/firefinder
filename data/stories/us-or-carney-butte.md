Carney Butte is about 10 miles north of Ukiah, in Umatilla County.[^3] The Oregon Department of Forestry put up a 30-foot steel live-in lookout tower there.[^1][^2]

The sources disagree on the year.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives 1958. The Forest Lookouts page says the new lookout was nearly finished by the end of 1952, and that its cab was sealed and painted the following year.[^1][^2]

The only lookout staff we found named is Carol Wallace, listed for 1954.[^3]

In 1977 the tower was moved to the Umatilla Army Depot to serve as a guard tower.[^1][^2] Nothing we read describes what, if anything, remains on the butte itself.[^1]

The depot opened in 1941 and stored and supplied munitions for the Army until the early 1990s.[^5] Army Corps of Engineers staff did fieldwork at the former Umatilla Chemical Depot in November 2025, as the Corps works to clear a 416-acre area for Oregon National Guard training.[^4] The land had been identified in 2023 as potentially containing unexploded ordnance.[^4]

We found no source that says whether the tower still stands at the depot, or whether the public can reach it there.[^1][^2]
