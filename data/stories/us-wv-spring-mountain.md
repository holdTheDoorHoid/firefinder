The Spring Mountain tower stood on the border between Boone and Logan counties, at the headwaters of Pond Fork of Little Coal River.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register places the site in Logan County at 2,670 feet (814 m). It names the West Virginia Division of Forestry as administrator and records the registration on January 26, 2017, as US 1637, WV 28.[^1]

By October 1926 the tower was already in place. A newspaper item from that month says it had only a forest telephone line connection, and that a line from the Blair station to the Madison central office was planned.[^2]

In 1953 the tower's observer was Cody Allen, who worked the District 5 lookout.[^2] One Friday night that October, a generator exploded and a fire broke out at the tower. Allen was hurt.[^2] He had an injured shoulder and slight burns, and he fell about 20 feet from a landing while getting out.[^2] Two newspaper items disagree about the generator.[^2] One calls it electric, and the other says gasoline.[^2] The radio and generator came through, but the force of the explosion shattered the observation room's windows.[^2] A district forester said the explosion and fire had left the tower completely out of service, with repairs set to start the same day or the next.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts page labels the site "Removed," and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's state list marks it as gone.[^2][^3]

The three sources we read describe the site but say nothing about what, if anything, remains there, or whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3]
