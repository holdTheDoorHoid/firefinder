The Fairview Lookout Tower is in Itawamba County, Mississippi, at an elevation of 550 feet (168 m).[^1] The Register gives its coordinates as N 34.360333° W 088.316618° and numbers it US 1683, MS 43.[^1] The next entry in its list is Dumas Lookout Tower (US 1684).[^1] The Register lists it as a 100ft International Derrick, administered by the Mississippi Forestry Commission.[^1]

The Register lists the build date as unknown.[^1] It says nothing about who staffed the tower or about any fire history at the site.[^1]

Jason Johns nominated the tower after a site visit on July 17, 2022, and it was registered on July 18, 2022.[^1] That visit is the most recent record found, so the tower's current condition and public access remain unconfirmed.[^1]

[^1]: Fairview Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ms/fairview-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-08).
