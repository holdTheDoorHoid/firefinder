Slaven Knob Lookout Tower stands in Scott County, at about 1,509 feet.[^1] Tennessee Landforms lists it as standing, with the code GB2625 and no date.[^1] A 2014 list of Tennessee fire towers still standing that March also includes Slaven Knob.[^2] Neither source says what condition the tower is in now.

We found no record of when the tower was built or who built it. A local encyclopedia entry about the Black Creek tower names Smith Knob and Black Creek as the only Scott County towers the state forestry agency still runs.[^3] The same entry places Slaven Knob at Foster Crossroads and says it still exists.[^3] By that account, Slaven Knob is not one of the two the agency still runs.[^3] The entry does not say who owns the tower now or whether anyone uses it.

Tennessee Landforms advises against climbing its towers, since steps are frequently missing or decayed.[^1] We found no public visiting arrangement for Slaven Knob, so please do not climb it. Ask the Division of Forestry before going near the site.
