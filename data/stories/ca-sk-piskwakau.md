The Piskwakau tower site appears in the Firetowers of Saskatchewan listing, which gives its position as 54 degrees, 30.9375 minutes north and 103 degrees, 29.375 minutes west [^1]. For this site the listing leaves its height, year of construction and names of towermen blank [^1].

The Canada Lookouts index of Saskatchewan towers gives two similar names, Piskwaka and Pushwakau [^2]. The sources I read do not say whether they name one tower or two.

The province's early towers appear in the forestry reports that Canada Lookouts quotes [^2]. The 1914 report lists one 24.4 m (80 ft) lookout tower at Nisbet, and the 1915 report says a tower had been ordered for the Beaver Hills Forest Reserve [^2].

No source I could read describes what, if anything, stands at the Piskwakau site today, or whether the public can visit it.

[^1]: Firetowers of Saskatchewan, websiteservice.ca, https://www.websiteservice.ca/firetowers_page4.htm (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Saskatchewan, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/saskatchewan.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
