Twin Butte was a lookout in Linn County, Oregon, inside the Willamette National Forest.[^1][^2] The register says the U.S. Forest Service administered it.[^1]

Sources do not agree on when the first lookout went up.[^1][^4] The Oregon Websites and Watersheds Project says it was established in 1920.[^4] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register dates a camp equipped with an alidade to 1923.[^1] A 1920s item quoted by Forest Lookouts mentions a fire-finder set on a stump.[^3]

The register says an L-4 cab replaced the early camp in 1932.[^1] Likewise, firelookout.com lists an L-4 cab in 1932, after a 1923 camp.[^2] Forest Lookouts instead dates a house of R-1 type, with a gable roof, to about 1931.[^3]

A July 1939 newspaper item, quoted by Forest Lookouts, named Alvin Sorseth as the lookout fireman.[^3] The item put the station 13 miles by trail, and six by road, from the ranger station.[^3] That item gave the elevation as 5,942 feet, while the register gives 5,312 feet (1,619 m).[^3][^1]

Later items name Leo Welch in 1941 and John Miller in 1947.[^3] In 1947 a bear got in through the door and took much of the stored food, so a new supply had to be ordered in.[^3]

The register and the Oregon Websites and Watersheds Project both say the lookout was removed in 1986.[^1][^4] firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year, and Forest Lookouts lists it as removed in 1986.[^2][^3]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
