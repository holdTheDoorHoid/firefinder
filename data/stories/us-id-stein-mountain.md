The site had a log-cabin lookout from 1917, and the lookout standing there now was built in 1958 in Lemhi County, Idaho.[^1][^2] The register describes the present structure as a 15-by-15-foot R-6 cab with a catwalk and a carport, and says it replaced the 1917 cabin.[^1] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both add a step in between: an L-4 cab on a log crib, which they date to the 1930s.[^2][^3] The register does not mention that 1930s cab.[^1] Firelookout.com also says the 1958 R-6 cab sits on a 10-foot concrete base.[^2]

Firelookout.com says the present cab is staffed in the summer, but gives no year for that.[^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists Mr. and Mrs. Gene Mullikin as known staff for 2001 and 2002, and names no one else.[^3]

None of the three sites describes a fire at the lookout.[^1][^2][^3] All three treat the 1958 cab as the one on the mountain today.[^1][^2][^3] The register recorded the lookout on September 25, 2011.[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the lookout as Drive-Up.[^3] The Forest Service describes a 2.6-mile Stein Mountain trail that begins at Trail #6134 and ends at the lookout, so the sources describe the approach differently.[^4] The sources do not say whether visitors can enter the cab.[^1][^2][^3] The register's rental page says rentable towers are marked on its listing pages, and the detail page for Stein Mountain does not mention rental.[^5][^1]
