The statewide list of Georgia lookout towers has an "allentown" tower in Laurens County, at 414 feet (126 m) elevation.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site has a page under the same name, also listed for Laurens County.[^2]

The city spans four counties, Bleckley, Laurens, Twiggs and Wilkinson, and is mostly within Wilkinson.[^3] By our calculation, the list's point sits about 1.7 km south-east of the city's published coordinates.[^1][^3]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
