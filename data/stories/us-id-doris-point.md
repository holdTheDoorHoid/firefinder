Doris Point Lookout stood on the Clearwater National Forest in Clearwater County, Idaho, 19 miles east-northeast of Pierce.[^1] The Forest Service list places it in Section 11, Township 37 North, Range 8 East, and calls the site Doris Point (Butte).[^3]

Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both date the structure to 1934 as an L-4 cab.[^1][^2] The Forest Service list also describes an L-4 cab.[^3] The list marks 4,753 feet as the elevation of Doris Butte, so that figure may not describe the lookout itself.[^3]

All three sources date its destruction to 1962.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the lookout in its St. Joe - Clearwater group and labels it hike-in.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.
