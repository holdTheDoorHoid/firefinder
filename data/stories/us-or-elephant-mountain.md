The Elephant Mountain lookout stood in Douglas County, Oregon, on the Umpqua National Forest.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives its design as a 20-foot pole L-4 tower, built in 1933.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the same year and tower, and places it 34 miles south-southeast of Oakridge.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts history tells a different start. It describes a 20-foot round pole tower with an open platform around 1928, and a 14-by-14-foot L-4 lookout house on a 20-foot timber tower in 1932.[^3] The sources disagree on when the lookout was built, and we could not settle which account is right.[^1][^2][^3]

Lester Moe and Robert Cooper made panorama photos on August 6, 1933, and James Rittenhouse made one on August 19, 1936.[^3] In July 1934 a Roseburg newspaper item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, reported an allotment of $6,000 for a stub road to the lookout in the Diamond Lake district.[^3]

The register says the tower was removed in 1963, while firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page gives 1962 for its removal.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register listed the site on December 10, 2010, on the nomination of Ray Kresek.[^1]

The register page carries a photo of the lookout site captioned with the year 2007.[^1] No source we read describes what remains at the site today. Both sources record the tower as removed or destroyed, so neither reports a tower still standing.[^1][^2]
