The Roundtop Mountain site is in the St. Joe National Forest, at 6,003 feet.[^2] It lies in Shoshone County.[^4]

The site opened in 1922 with a pole tower 50 feet tall, a cab 7 feet square, and a ground cabin for living quarters.[^1][^3] Around 1927 a 45-foot steel windmill tower, with a 6-foot-square cab, replaced the first one.[^1][^3] In 1952 a third tower went up: 53 feet of treated timber, topped by an L-4 cab.[^1][^2][^3] The firelookout.com page marks two runs of years, 1927 to 1952 and 1952 to 1979, and Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the last tower as 53 feet tall.[^1][^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1979.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as drive-up.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association marks it as a tower that is gone.[^4]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.[^1]
