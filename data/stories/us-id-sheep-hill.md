Sheep Hill sits at 5,676 feet in the Clearwater National Forest.[^1][^2] Its lookout was a 30-foot tower with an L-4 cab, built in 1933.[^1][^2] It appears on the Forest's list of its lookouts, compiled in June 2016.[^3] That list draws on Forest Service records and Ray Kresek's book, Fire Lookouts of the Northwest.[^3]

The lookout listings all mark the tower as gone.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page tags the site as hike-in, and no source says whether it is open to visitors.[^2]

Another lookout also called Sheep Hill is a different structure. It sits in the Nez Perce National Forest at 8,405 feet, is actively staffed, and was added to the National Historic Lookout Register.[^5]
