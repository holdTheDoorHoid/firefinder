Deer Creek Point is a site in the Bitterroot National Forest, in Ravalli County, Montana, at 8,367 feet, about 18 miles south-southwest of the West Fork ranger station.[^1] Firelookout.com places the site only by that distance and direction from the station, with no road or trail named, so it is a starting point rather than a map reference.[^1] Firelookout.com lists a fire lookout cabin here from 1941.[^1] The same page says the cabin was abandoned in 1950 and is now gone.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page for the site gives the location code 3S-23W-15 and marks the site as removed.[^2] No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it can be visited.[^1][^2]
