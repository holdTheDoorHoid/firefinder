The Shawnee Mountain tower stood on the mountain of that name in McDowell County, West Virginia, at about 849 meters (2,786 feet).[^1] The West Virginia Division of Forestry administered it, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list now marks it as gone.[^1][^3]

A Charleston Gazette item from October 1926 says the tower had a telephone link to the Bluefield Telephone Company's Northfork exchange.[^2]

In November 1939 a Steubenville Herald Star item reported a widespread search for a fire tower watchman who had been unaccounted for 36 hours as forest fires broke out anew. The watchman, William Thompson, could not be reached at his station atop the mountain, and the item said state police had been called in to help.[^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the site as US 1677, registered on January 26, 2017, on the nomination of Brian M. Powell.[^1]

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.
