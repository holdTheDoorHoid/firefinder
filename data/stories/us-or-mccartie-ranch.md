The McCartie Ranch lookout stood in Lake County, Oregon, about 9 miles north of Bly, on the Fremont National Forest.[^1][^2] The site is roughly 1,680 meters up.[^1]

Both sources say an L-4 tower went up on the site in the 1940s, with a patrol crow's nest half a mile to the west.[^1][^2] The register's Built field gives only the decade, so no single build year is confirmed.[^1] A year of 1940 would go further than the sources do.[^1]

Both sources say the lookout was abandoned in the 1950s.[^1][^2] The register adds that it is gone.[^1]

A lookout history page for Oregon quotes 1943 newspaper reports.[^3] A June item says two camps of about 30 high school boys were situated at Penny and Rodeo Springs.[^3] An August item says the boys at the Rodeo Springs camp built a lookout tower on Rodeo Butte.[^3] A 1941 item places Rodeo Butte near Bly.[^3] The sources do not say whether that tower is the same one as McCartie Ranch.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]
