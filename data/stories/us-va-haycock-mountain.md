Haycock Mountain Lookout stands in Floyd County, Virginia, and the Register lists its owner as a private party rather than a public agency.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association also files it under the name Haycock Knob (Pocket Ridge).[^2]

The Register describes an 80-foot Aermotor tower built in the 1940s.[^1] Its standout feature is the watchman's cabin, which the Register says still stands whole.[^1] Few other Virginia towers, the Register notes, keep their cabin this complete.[^1]

The sources I read record no staffing, named fires or other history for the tower.[^1][^2][^3]

The Association lists the tower as standing.[^2] The Register registered it on January 23, 2017, as US 1166 and VA 32.[^1] A Forest Lookouts index of Virginia towers also lists a Haycock Mountain in Floyd County.[^3]

The Register does not list the tower's coordinates, because the owner asked it not to, so this story omits them.[^1] No source describes public access or a rental.[^1][^2][^3] Because the land is privately owned, do not climb the tower or walk onto the property without the owner's permission.[^1]
