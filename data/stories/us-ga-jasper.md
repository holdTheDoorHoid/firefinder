Jasper Lookout Tower is listed in Jasper County, Georgia, at 33.3971° N, 83.7710° W.[^1][^2] The list and TopoQuest give the same coordinates to four decimal places.[^1][^2] The Georgia tower list takes its names for entries 1 to 72 from the USGS name database, and this tower is entry 28, so its name is a USGS name.[^1] TopoZone's list of towers in Jasper County gives the same name.[^3]

The list gives the tower's elevation as 840, and TopoQuest gives 836 feet above sea level. The two figures are close, and the list does not say what unit it uses.[^1][^2] TopoQuest says the tower is shown on the USGS 1:24,000 map for Stewart, Georgia.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
