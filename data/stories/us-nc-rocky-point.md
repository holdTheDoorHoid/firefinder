The North Carolina lookout towers list gives Rocky Point its own entry in Pender County.[^1] A waymark listing is titled Rocky Point Fire Tower.[^2]

The name Bannerman belongs to a separate entry on the same list, farther north.[^1] The Bannerman page dates an 80-foot steel tower near Bannerman's Bridge to 1927 and credits Cooper River Timber Company with building it, working with the State Division of Forest Resources.[^3] Bidding opened in March 2018 in an auction of surplus state property, and the page says the tower sits on state land.[^3] That history belongs to the Bannerman tower, not to this one.[^1][^3]

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^2]: Rocky Point Fire Tower, Waymarking.com, https://www.waymarking.com/waymarks/wm199E_Rocky_Point_Fire_Tower (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Bannerman - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/bannerman.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
