The Payette National Forest built the Rocky Point Lookout in 1937, according to the National Historic Lookout Register.[^1] The register describes a one-of-a-kind log structure with dovetailed corners, built entirely by hand from logs gathered at the site.[^1] Another listing gives 1936 as the build year.[^2]

The register says the lookout went inactive in 1945.[^1] Its windows came out in the 1960s, and the historic lookout was left to the weather.[^1] Firelookout.com says it was abandoned in 1952,[^2] while the Idaho Fire Lookouts site places the abandonment in the 1960s.[^3] The sources do not agree on when that happened.

The lookout burned in a forest fire in the summer of 2015.[^1][^2][^3]

The site lies inside the Frank Church River of No Return Wilderness, about nine miles north-northeast of Chamberlain G.S. in Idaho County.[^2] The Idaho Fire Lookouts site lists it as a hike-in site.[^3] None of the sources describes what, if anything, is left at the site today.
