The tower's official name is Sydere Tower, and a Facebook group about Ontario fire towers also calls it the Departure Lake Tower.[^1] The Departure Lake area lies in Haggart Township, Cochrane District, with Ontario Highway 11 running between Strickland and Smooth Rock Falls.[^2] Canadian census data places the community within Unorganized North Cochrane District.[^2]

Departure Lake itself lies 2.8 kilometres south of the community; a picnic area sits where North Access Road ends.[^2] The Poplar Rapids River crosses beneath Highway 11 and the Ontario Northland Railway just east of the community.[^2]

No source we could read describes what stands at the tower site today, or whether the public can reach it.
