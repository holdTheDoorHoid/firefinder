Swamp Mountain sits about 17 miles east-southeast of Sweet Home, in Linn County, Oregon.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says a skid-mounted house with a cupola was built here in 1949, and that a two-story L-4 cab replaced it in 1959.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page says Santiam Lumber Company built the 1959 structure as a 14 x 14 lookout house atop an enclosed garage.[^3] The two descriptions appear to match, although the page does not use the register's term.[^1][^3]

The Forest Lookouts page quotes Albany Democrat-Herald reports from the 1950s.[^3] In October 1952 the paper reported that the cooperative lookout on Swamp Mountain was the only one not removed.[^3] In October 1954 it reported that the lookout still open had shut for the season, citing Bill Shumate, the Linn County Fire Patrol dispatcher.[^3]

The sources differ on when the lookout ended. The Forest Lookouts page says it was dismantled in 1969, and its usable material went to the Green Peter lookout site.[^3] The register says the cab was removed in 1970, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2] We could not reconcile the dates, so both are given here.

No source we read describes what remains on the mountain today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
