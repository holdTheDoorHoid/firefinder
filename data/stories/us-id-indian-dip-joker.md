Indian Dip, also listed as Joker Point, lies in Shoshone County, Idaho, on the St. Joe National Forest, at an elevation of 5,710 feet.[^1][^2] It is about 19 miles west-southwest of the Red Ives Ranger Station.[^1]

The site began as a camp around 1927.[^1][^2][^3] In 1931 an L-4 cab went up atop the north summit.[^1][^2][^3] The lookout was destroyed in 1957.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list gives the kind as "Ground" and the status as "Gone."[^4]

A patrol point 2.5 miles north, atop Brush Hill, carried a 15-foot L-6 tower.[^1][^2] The firelookout.com page labels that patrol tower "Ruins."[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the entry "Drive-Up Destroyed."[^2] We found no source describing what, if anything, remains at the Dip site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
