Baldy Hughes was a Pinetree Line radar station. The NBC Group's 1997 history places it about 31 km (19 miles) southwest of Prince George, British Columbia, while Wikipedia gives 35.9 km (22.3 miles) south-southwest.[^1][^2]

The station was named after Hughes, an early pioneer often known as "Baldy", who supplied fresh horses to a stage line on the old Cariboo Wagon Road. Its operations site was on a mountain close by that also carries his name.[^1]

Construction began in 1952.[^1][^2] The two accounts differ on when the US Air Force unit moved in. The NBC Group says the 918th AC&W Squadron arrived in 1955, once construction was finished, while Wikipedia says operations started in June 1953.[^1][^2]

The RCAF took over on 1 March 1963, and the station closed in April 1988.[^1][^2]

Wikipedia says the former station now houses the Baldy Hughes Addiction Treatment Centre.[^2] No source I read describes the site beyond that, or says whether the grounds are open to visitors.
