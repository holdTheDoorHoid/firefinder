Hicks Hill lies in the Bulkley-Nechako area of British Columbia.[^2] BC Geographical Names locates the hill west of where Tchesinkut Creek meets the Endako River, between Fraser Lake and Burns Lake.[^1]

The same record marks the hill as the location of the Hicks Hill (wildfire) Lookout, and says the place name was adopted on 19 January 1961.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public.

[^2]: Hicks Hill Fire Lookout, Canada Lookouts (Ron Kemnow), https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/hicks-hill.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
