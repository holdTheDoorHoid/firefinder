The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower at Melfa, in Accomack County, Virginia, and marks it as standing.[^1] The association gives its location as 37.64133 north and 75.76971 west, and its page of standing Virginia towers repeats the same line.[^1][^2]

The listing says nothing more. It gives no design, height, builder, build date or register number.[^1][^2] The sources do not say whether the tower was ever staffed, whether it saw any fires, or what has happened to it since it was listed.[^1][^2]

Because the record is this thin, most of the tower's history is still open. Before going to look for it, a visitor should find out who owns the site and whether it is open to the public.

[^1]: Virginia - Forest Fire Lookout Association, firelookout.org, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Virginia Fire Towers (Standing) - Forest Fire Lookout Association, firelookout.org, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/va-st/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
