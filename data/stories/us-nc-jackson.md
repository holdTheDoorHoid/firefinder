The National Geodetic Survey keeps a survey mark for the Jackson Lookout Tower, listed under the number FX4177.[^1] Coast and Geodetic Survey staff first observed and described the tower in 1959.[^1] The station lies about 4.0 miles northeast of Jackson, two miles west of Edwards Crossroads, on the north side of a blacktop road in Northampton County, North Carolina.[^1]

The tower is a four-legged steel frame that holds up a square metal cabin.[^1] The survey gives its overall height as 110 feet (about 33.5 m), and places the survey point at the top center of the cabin.[^1]

A list of North Carolina lookout towers on the tnlandforms.us website carries the Jackson tower under the same survey mark, in Northampton County.[^2]

No source describes the site today, so this entry cannot say whether the tower still stands, what remains of it, or whether the land is open to visitors.
