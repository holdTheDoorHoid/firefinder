The Quitman Lookout Tower is listed in Quitman County, Georgia, at 31.91099, -85.03271, with an elevation listed as 499 (unit not stated).[^1] A directory of Georgia forest lookouts files it under the Georgia Forestry Commission.[^2] The list gives the tower two reference numbers, BLL-41117 and gfc-313.[^1] Its map was last revised in March 2016, so its details may be out of date.[^1]

In the late 1980s, the Georgia Forestry Commission began replacing tower staff with air patrols, and many towers were scrapped or sold.[^3]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today.
