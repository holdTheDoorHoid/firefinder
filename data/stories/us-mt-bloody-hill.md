Bloody Hill is in Teton County, on the Lewis and Clark National Forest, at location 26N-10W-3 (township 26 north, range 10 west, section 3).[^1][^2] The Teton County index lists Bloody Hill with four other lookouts: Beartop, Bennie Hill, Mount Wright and Wrong Ridge.[^2] The site's one dated entry is from 1938.[^1] A June 23, 1938 item from The Choteau Acantha, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, covered assignments for an area running from Birch Creek to the North Fork of the Sun River, inclusive.[^1] It named Al Gordon of Glacier Park as the person assigned to the Bloody Hill lookout.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the hill today, or whether the public can reach it.
