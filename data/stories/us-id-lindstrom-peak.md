An L-4 tower was built on Lindstrom Peak in 1935.[^1][^2][^3] It stood in the St. Joe National Forest, four miles south of St. Maries, at 4,695 feet.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page describes it as a tower with an L-4 cab and credits the detail to Kresek.[^3]

The sources disagree on the county. The Forest Lookouts page lists the peak under Benewah County.[^3] Firelookout.com gives Shoshone County, Idaho, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list also places the peak in Shoshone County.[^1][^4]

Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both note a patrol tower about a mile to the southeast.[^1][^2] The tower was destroyed in 1965, the same year on all three pages.[^1][^2][^3]

No source I read describes what, if anything, is left at the site today. Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site Drive-Up, but no source says whether the public may visit it.[^2]
