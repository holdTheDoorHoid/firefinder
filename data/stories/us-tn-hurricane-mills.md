A Tennessee tower list gives a tower at Hurricane Mills, in Humphreys County, an elevation of 712 feet and marks it as standing.[^1] The same list gives the code FD1633 for it, which we could not match to any other record.[^1]

A 2014 National Register study of the Division of Forestry's fire towers has no Humphreys County entry in its list of towers still standing on March 26, 2014.[^2] The two sources disagree, and we could not settle which is right, or whether the structure on the tower list is the one the study missed.

We found no record of when a Hurricane Mills tower was built, who staffed it, or any fire it watched. We also found no public information on access or ownership, so do not climb or enter any structure at the site without permission.

[^1]: Tennessee lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Tennessee Division of Forestry Fire Lookout Towers multiple property document, National Park Service, https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/8986b1c9-543a-4d56-9482-166cde732e78 (accessed 2026-10-09).
