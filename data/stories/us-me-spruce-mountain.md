Spruce Mountain is a former fire lookout site in Oxford County, Maine, at latitude 44.4077 and longitude -70.58437.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association records its structure type as unknown and its status as gone.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Maine Fire Towers (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/me/me-co/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
