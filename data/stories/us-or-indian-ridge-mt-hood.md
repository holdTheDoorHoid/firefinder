Indian Ridge lies about 20 miles southwest of Mount Hood, in Clackamas County, Oregon, and the U.S. Forest Service administered the lookout site.[^1][^2]

The first structure was an open-sided shelter with a windowless cupola on the roof, which the register dates to 1923.[^1][^2] In 1932 a ground cabin went up. The register calls it an L-5 cabin,[^1] while the Forest Fire Lookout Association calls it an L-4 cabin built that year.[^2]

Forest Lookouts records a painting job at the lookout in 1956. In 1958 linoleum went down on the floor, and aerial markings were painted on the lookout.[^3]

The lookout was deactivated in 1964.[^3] The sources disagree about its end. The Forest Fire Lookout Association says the cabin was destroyed in 1969,[^2] and the register says it was removed by 1970.[^1] Forest Lookouts lists the site simply as removed.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today.
