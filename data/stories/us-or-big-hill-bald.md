Big Hill Bald is a lookout site in the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest, about 20 miles southeast of Union, Oregon, at an elevation of 4,686 feet.[^1]

Firelookout.com lists 1936 beside the word "unknown," so the build date is unclear.[^1] The same page says the lookout was abandoned in 1940.[^1] Forest Lookouts lists it as destroyed, with no date given.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks it gone.[^3]

The sources disagree on the county. Firelookout.com and the association's list place it in Wallowa County.[^1][^3] A Forest Lookouts page for Union County files Big Hill under the same forest with the code 6S-42E-20, while the Big Hill page gives 6S-42E-29.[^4][^2]

We found no source describing what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
