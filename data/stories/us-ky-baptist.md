Baptist is a lookout in Wolfe County, Kentucky, listed on the Eastern US Lookouts site.[^1] The site gives its status as standing, based on Google Earth imagery dated April 4, 2023.[^1]

The name matches Baptist, an unincorporated community in Wolfe County.[^2] Wikipedia dates the community's settlement to the early 1800s and traces its name to the Stillwater Baptist Church, organized in 1837.[^2] A post office under that name operated from 1917 to 1974.[^2]

The 2023 imagery is the only status check the sources give. No source describes the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
