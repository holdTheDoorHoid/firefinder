McDuffie Lookout stands in McDuffie County, Georgia, and the Georgia Forestry Commission administers it.[^1] The register describes it as an 82-foot Aermotor LS-40 galvanized tower with a steel cab, though it hedges, saying the tower appears to be that model.[^1]

The register gives no build year.[^1] I found no source saying when the tower went up or who first used it.

The register notes two radio antennas and suggests the tower may serve as a repeater station.[^1] It also places the tower beside an active Georgia Forestry Commission office.[^1]

The register lists the elevation at 519 feet (158 meters), while a Georgia towers list gives 512 feet.[^1][^2] The register accepted the lookout on June 9, 2015, as US 1069, GA 18, on a nomination by Michael T. Finch, Jr.[^1]

Neither source says who staffed the tower, which fires it watched, or whether the public can visit it. Because it stands beside a working office, do not assume you can go up it. Ask the Georgia Forestry Commission first.

[^1]: McDuffie Lookout, National Historic Lookout Register (nhlr.org), http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ga/mcduffie-lookout/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
