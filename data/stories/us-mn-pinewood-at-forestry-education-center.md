The Pinewood Fire Tower now stands on the Beltrami County Fairgrounds in Bemidji, Minnesota, beside the Forestry Education Center.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list shows no tower left at the original Pinewood site and lists the relocated tower as standing.[^1]

We did not find when the tower was built, who made it, or who staffed it at Pinewood. The sources we read name no fire it watched.

Dave Quam moved the tower in 2004, and private donations paid for the move.[^2] The Pioneer identifies him as a member of the Forest Fire Lookout Association for many years who also looks after the tower. It says he saved the tower from being torn down more than ten years ago.[^3]

According to the Pioneer, the platform stands 80 feet above the ground, and the roof reaches 90 feet.[^3] Visitors can climb during the county fair, from about 11 a.m. until dusk on each day the fair runs, with no more than four people on the tower at once.[^3] The Pioneer also says the tower and the Forest Education Center are open by appointment from May 1 to Oct. 1.[^3]

In 2019, Quam reported more than 1,000 climbers on each fair date.[^2] In the same report he said he was then 80 or older and was looking for a successor as superintendent of the display.[^2]
