Timber Ridge is a former lookout site on the Kaniksu National Forest in Boundary County, Idaho.[^1] It sits about 14 miles southeast of Bonners Ferry, at 6,395 feet.[^1] Rex's Fire Lookout Page also gives the name Timber Mountain and the location 60N-3E-20.[^3][^5]

The lookout was a three-tree platform, dated to the 1930s.[^1][^2] The sources give only the decade.[^1][^2]

Both sources say the site was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com adds that the platform was still there in the 1980s.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list still shows the site as a platform with abandoned status.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts describes the site as a hike-in.[^2] No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may go there.
