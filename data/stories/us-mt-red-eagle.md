The Red Eagle tower was a 60-foot steel tower in Glacier National Park's lookout system.[^1] Firelookout.com says it was moved from Swiftcurrent Ridge in 1959 and was gone in 1986.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts list places Red Eagle Mountain in Glacier National Park at 34N-14W-9, and marks the tower as removed, without a date.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association index lists the tower as gone.[^5]

The sources do not clearly identify where the tower came from. The Forest Lookouts list has a separate Swiftcurrent Mountain site in the park, at 35N-17W-13, where a lookout was assigned in July 1947.[^3] We could not tell from these pages whether that is the ridge firelookout.com names.

Glacier's 1936 Swiftcurrent fire lookout is a different structure. Its National Register nomination, which we could not date, describes a wood-frame cab on a 10-foot tower, in good condition and unaltered in its original location.[^4] That does not match the 60-foot steel tower described for Red Eagle.

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the Red Eagle tower at its site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
