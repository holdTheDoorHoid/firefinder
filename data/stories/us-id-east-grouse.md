East Grouse stood in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, about three miles north of Mullan in Shoshone County, Idaho.[^1] Its listed elevation is 6,081 feet.[^1][^2]

The Civilian Conservation Corps built it in 1935. Company 531, working from Camp F-132 at Big Creek, finished a round tower of native timber, listed at 20 feet and fitted with an L-4 cab.[^3] The other two sources describe the same 20-foot L-4 tower.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists one known staff member, Herbert Vance, for 1937.[^2]

Firelookout.com gives the end as 1950 and calls the tower gone.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it as removed that year, and the Shoshone County listing says it was gone around 1950.[^2][^3]

The site is marked hike-in, so visitors reach it on foot.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
