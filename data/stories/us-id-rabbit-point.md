Rabbit Point, on the Nez Perce National Forest, carried a log cupola cabin at the south end of its ridge.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Firelookout.com, Idaho Fire Lookouts and the Forest Service list all give 1930 as the build year.[^1][^2][^3]

A Forest Lookouts list of Idaho County towers gives a camp at the site in the 1930s.[^4]

Firelookout.com says the cabin was removed about 1950, and Idaho Fire Lookouts also lists it as removed.[^1][^2] The Forest Service list instead says it was destroyed in 1950.[^3] The sources agree on the rough date but not on how the cabin ended. The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as ground and gone.[^5]

Firelookout.com says the site borders the Frank Church River of No Return Wilderness, and Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it as a hike-in site.[^1][^2] No source describes what remains on the ridge today. A Forest Service history page counts 20 log cabin lookouts with cupolas on the Nez Perce forest and says only one remains.[^6]
