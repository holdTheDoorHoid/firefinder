Jacks Point Lookout Tower, also known as Jacks Point Lookout, is mapped in Leslie County, Kentucky, close to the hamlet of Avawam.[^2] OpenStreetMap places it at 37.2253 north, 83.3248 west, and describes it as an observation tower, 473 metres (1,552 ft) above sea level.[^1][^2] Wooton, a community in Leslie County, sits about 3.5 miles south of the tower.[^2] Mapcarta also places Yerkes, a coal town in Perry County, about four miles north of it.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts index for Kentucky lists a Jacks Point tower in Leslie County.[^3]

Today the only description of the site in these sources is the map entry.[^1] No source describes what stands there now, or says whether visitors can reach it.
