The Hiles tower stood in the Town of Hiles, in Forest County.[^1][^3] The Forest Lookouts page names the Chequamegon-Nicolet National Forest as its agency and gives the location code 37N-12E-12.[^1] The unincorporated community of Hiles lies about 1½ miles west of the tower.[^3] TopoQuest puts the site at 1,797 feet above sea level, on a USGS 1:24K map named Argonne.[^4] The Wisconsin lookout list gives its position as 45.70629 north, 88.94321 west, and marks it as gone.[^2] Wisconsin's fire towers of that period went up in the 1930s and 1940s, and in 2015 the DNR announced that the towers were being removed.[^5]

No source describes what remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it.
