Winberry Mountain is in Lane County, Oregon, and the Forest Lookouts page lists it under the Willamette National Forest.[^1] The only dated records we found are two newspaper items from July 1920, one of them marked as being for the 1919 season.[^1]

The Sunday Oregonian item names Nell Southworth, a university student, as having passed her summer break on "Winbury Butte," which it places in the Cascade national forest in Oregon.[^1] The Morning Register names Clara Holbrook among the women working as lookouts "on Winberry mountain."[^1] The two items spell the name differently, and the sources we read do not show whether they describe the same peak.

We found no source that says when a lookout was built on the peak, what it looked like, how long it was staffed, or what happened to it. No source describes the site today.

[^1]: Winberry Mountain, Forest Lookouts, https://oregonlookouts.weebly.com/winberry-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
