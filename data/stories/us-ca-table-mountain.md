Table Mountain sits in Plumas County, within the Plumas National Forest, at 39.77158 north and 121.07690 west.[^1][^2] The California list classes it as a ground site rather than a tower.[^2]

The earliest dated item we found is a September 1924 notice from the Feather River Bulletin, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page. It mentions a "Table Mountain lookout station" and a group taken there for a Saturday outing, so a lookout station was already in place by 1924.[^1]

A June 1931 item in the same paper says the lookout on a site called Table Rock was abandoned when the Pilot Peak station was established.[^1] That name is not Table Mountain, so the item may describe this site or another one.

The California list marks the site as gone.[^2] The peakbagging table lists it as Table Mountain (LO site), a label its key uses where the building has been removed or its status may be unknown.[^3]

The key adds that little may survive at such sites. We found no source that describes what is on the ground now, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
