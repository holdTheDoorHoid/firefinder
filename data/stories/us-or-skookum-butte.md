Skookum Butte rises to about 6,070 feet in Klamath County, Oregon.[^1][^2]

Sources disagree on the forest. The register and firelookout.com name the Winema National Forest,[^1][^2] while the Forest Lookouts page places the site on the Fremont National Forest.[^3]

The lookout began as a one-room cabin built in 1932.[^4] A November 1932 newspaper said the station would be occupied by the Johnson Meadow fireman and lookout, and that a steel tower would be erected the next year.[^3]

The steel tower went up in 1933 and is listed at 60 feet.[^1][^2][^4] A June 1933 newspaper described a 50-foot tower under construction,[^3] and a 1953 Coast and Geodetic Survey description gives the structure about 135 feet high.[^3] We could not reconcile those figures, so treat the height as uncertain.

Crews had a hard time building it that year. Meadows flooded, trucks could not cross them, and the date the tower was hoped to be ready slipped from June 20 to July 1.[^4]

The tower was gone by 1965.[^1][^2][^4] The Forest Lookouts page marks the site as removed.[^3]

Photos from a 2022 visit show four footings at the tower site, a step, and a road near the summit that has been abandoned.[^4] No source we read describes any standing structure there now.

Roads run almost to the top. The last quarter mile is an abandoned road. Fallen trees could block access before that stretch, and wet roads could be impassable.[^4] None of the sources we read mentions a closure or a gate.
