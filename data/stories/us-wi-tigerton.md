The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower at Tigerton, in Shawano County, Wisconsin, at 44.71549 north and 89.10503 west, and marks it gone.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page for Tigerton names the county and little else.[^2]

Wisconsin's Department of Natural Resources has decided to take all 72 of its fire towers out of service, and towers that go unsold will be torn down, according to Door County Pulse.[^3] The sources we read do not tie the Tigerton tower to that decision.

The association's list gives no date for the tower's removal and no reason for it.[^1] Nothing we read describes what, if anything, stands on the site today, and we found no information on public access.[^1][^2] Before going to the spot, check who owns the land.
