The Forest Lookouts page for the Meta tower, in Osage County, is a short run of dated notes from the Missouri Conservation Commission's records.[^1] In fiscal year 1950–51 the state acquired a 20.42-acre towersite and put in an entrance road to the tower.[^1] During the 1950s the tower was used as a Ground Observer Corps observation post.[^1] A 1962 entry also logs the building of an entrance road to Meta Tower.[^1] The page places the site at location code 41N-11W-4 and credits its notes to the commission's annual and biennial reports.[^1]

The latest dated entry on that page is 1962. I could find no other source that describes the site today, so I cannot say whether the tower still stands or whether visitors may go there.[^1]

[^1]: Meta - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts (centraluslookouts.weebly.com), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/meta.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
