A Saskatchewan tower listing describes Elk House as a light steel tower 24.4 m (80 ft) tall, at the legal land location LSD 13-8-50-22-W2.[^1] The same listing notes a new cupola went on the tower in 1957.[^1]

Several towermen are recorded at Elk House on a towermen list kept on the same website.[^2] Sherry Reidt's service is given as 1986 to 2001, with Elk House among six towers she worked.[^2] Sheldon Murray is listed at Elk House from 1990 to 1999.[^2] Travis French was full time at Elk House in 2000 and 2001.[^2] Joan Pelech's entry, which also includes Elk House, runs from 2000 to 2002.[^2] Hilda Switzer Lundgren's entry covers seven towers, including Elk House, with service years of 1957 and 1970 to 1994.[^2]

The same list says the last tower was manned after mid-August 2001 and that all towers were condemned, with six to be rebuilt. That line does not name Elk House, so it is unclear whether it covers this tower.[^2]

A register page for North Cabin, another Saskatchewan tower, reports that as of 2013 the province had switched to cameras to spot fires.[^3] No source we found says whether the Elk House tower still stands or whether the site is open to visitors. Check with the managing agency before any visit.
