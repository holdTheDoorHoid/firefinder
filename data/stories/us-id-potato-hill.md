Potato Hill sits about a mile north of Deary in Latah County, Idaho, at 4,017 feet.[^1][^2] Locals call it Spud Hill.[^4]

A Twin Falls News item from April 1939, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, reports a district ranger's statement that the chamber of commerce in Dreary (the item's spelling of Deary) had donated ten acres on the summit for a forest lookout station. The item adds that the money was raised through subscriptions.[^3]

The tower was a 15-foot pole L-4 tower, built in 1939.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts site also quotes a 1921 Potlatch TPA annual report that mentions another mile of line built between the town and Potato Hill, where a lookout point was set up.[^3] The other pages date the tower to 1939, so the two may not describe the same structure.[^1][^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1964.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Today a cell tower occupies the site, according to onX Maps.[^4] The route is Potato Hill Road, a two-mile out-and-back that starts on Main Street in Deary and is gated at the trailhead.[^4] It crosses private property, Bennet Lumber land and state parcels, and onX Maps advises visitors to mind property boundaries.[^4] We found no source saying whether the summit itself is open to the public.
