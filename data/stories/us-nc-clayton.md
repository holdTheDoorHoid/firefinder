Clayton is a town in Johnston County, North Carolina.[^4] A North Carolina lookout listing names a Clayton tower in Johnston County.[^1] A tower list pairs the Clayton site with survey mark EZ2569.[^3]

The U.S. Geological Survey set that mark in 1960, in the concrete footing of a tower.[^2] When North Carolina's geodetic survey described the mark in 1979, it put the mark in the footing of a tower it called abandoned, roughly half a mile east of the SR 1720 junction near Thanksgiving Church.[^2] The description places the mark about 6 miles north-northeast of Wilsons Mills, and it stands 0.8 feet above the ground.[^2]

Recovery notes say the mark was in good condition in 1991, and it was recovered as described in 2006.[^2]

No source I read names the tower or describes the site today.
