A 1963 clipping from the Hobbs Daily News-Sun, reproduced on a lookout history site, places Turkey Tower above Cimarron and quotes State Forester Ray Bell.[^1]

Shortly before a July 2, 1963 newspaper report, a lookout at Turkey Tower reportedly spotted smoke from a fire in the Vernejo Park area, roughly 20 to 25 miles northwest of the tower.[^1] The newspaper gave two estimates of the burned ground. A forestry pilot put it at about 3,000 acres, while a timber company manager put the loss at about 700 acres.[^1] The clipping also reports that the Forestry Department had no fire protection agreement for the W.S. Gorley Ranch land.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Turkey as gone, in Colfax County.[^2]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.

[^2]: New Mexico Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
