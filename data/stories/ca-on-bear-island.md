Firelookout.ca describes the Bear Island Fire Tower as a steel tower, with an elevation of 348 m (1,142 ft) recorded for the site.[^1] The listing gives no structure height or build year.[^1] Bear Island sits in Lake Temagami, in northeastern Ontario.[^2]

The only build date I found is a social media post from the firelookout.ca account, which says "I believe this tower was erected in 1937."[^3] The wording is tentative, so treat 1937 as unconfirmed. The same post says the tower has been removed, but gives no date for that.[^3]

The Temagami First Nation is based on the island, and a First Nation reserve covers it.[^2] Anyone thinking of going should treat it as First Nation land.

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it can be visited.
