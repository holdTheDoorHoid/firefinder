The Fairfield lookout was a firefinder set on top of a grain elevator in the town of Fairfield, Camas County, Idaho.[^1][^2] The two lookout write-ups name the Sawtooth National Forest as the agency.[^1][^2] Both lookout sites give its elevation as 5,065 feet.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page gives the land as 1S-14E-9 in Camas County.[^3]

In August 1941, Camas County's commissioners authorized a lookout on top of the Fairfield elevator to fight wheat fires on the Camas prairie.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts dates the firefinder to 1942, the year after that approval.[^2] Both lookout sites say the firefinder was staffed during lightning storms.[^1][^2]

The lookout is listed as abandoned.[^1][^2][^4] No source we read says whether the elevator or the firefinder still stands, or whether the site is open to the public. Idaho Fire Lookouts describes the lookout as drive-up.[^2]
