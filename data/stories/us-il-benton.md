A National Geodetic Survey station record, described by the Coast and Geodetic Survey in 1960, places the Benton tower about three miles north of Benton in Franklin County. The station sat about 0.1 mile west of State Highway 37 and about 100 yards northwest of the main office building at the Southern Illinois Forestry headquarters.[^1] The record calls it a four-legged steel forestry tower, 100 feet tall, with an enclosed observation cabin at the top.[^1]

In September 1975 the Southern Illinoisan carried a notice inviting bids to demolish the lookout.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site marks the tower as removed, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone.[^1][^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors. Bob Frakes, a historian of fire lookouts, says three Illinois towers still remain; the article does not mention the Benton tower as standing.[^3]
