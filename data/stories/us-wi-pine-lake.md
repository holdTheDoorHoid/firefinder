In 1941 the Conservation department was building a fire tower on the Wimple property, along the old Wimple road in Iron County, Wisconsin.[^1] A February 17, 1941 item from the Rhinelander Daily News, quoted on the Central US Lookouts page, said the concrete foundation was finished and that the planned height was 73 feet.[^1] The page's location line reads 44N-3E-28, a land-survey style township, range and section code; the Conservation department is named only in the 1941 item.[^1]

The Central US Lookouts page ties that 1941 item to this site, and no other source checked confirms the tie.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list records the Pine Lake tower as gone.[^2] OpenStreetMap places the tower node at 526 metres of elevation.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
