Switchback Mountain Lookout stood at 3,537 feet in Skamania County, Washington, in the Gifford Pinchot National Forest, about 17 miles west-southwest of Mount Adams.[^1][^2]

The lookout was built sometime in the 1930s.[^2] Firelookout.com describes a 20-foot wooden L-5 tower.[^1] Based on images, Willhite says the cab appears to sit on a 20-foot pole tower, with stairs added and numerous pole supports.[^2] His notes also place the panoramic photos on the roof at 20 feet plus 15 feet for the cab, so the total height is unclear.[^2]

Aerial images from the 1930s show the road already in place.[^2] Photographs on Willhite's page are dated October 19, 1935, and look north, southwest and southeast.[^2]

Firelookout.com and its index list the tower as gone.[^1][^3] Willhite says the date of its destruction is unknown.[^2]

The old road is abandoned, and a berm at its start blocks vehicles.[^2] Forest Road 8851 is paved from the Mosquito Lake area to the Little Niagara Falls junction, with gravel and some potholes for the final few miles.[^2] The walk to the site is about one mile with a 250-foot climb, and the old road ends at the top of a steep slope down to the Lewis River.[^2]

Willhite's 2019 visit found the old road easy to walk.[^2] He photographed three of seven footings, two more footings, and phone line insulators left on trees along the abandoned section.[^2] Willhite's photo captions say the views over the Lewis River valley are gone.[^2] The sources describe the walk on national forest land and mention no closure.[^1][^2]
