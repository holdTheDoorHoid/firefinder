The High Knob lookout stands in Wise County, Virginia, on the Clinch Ranger District of the Jefferson National Forest.[^1]

The Civilian Conservation Corps built the original lookout in 1938 and 1939. It was a wooden ground house with a hip roof.[^1]

In 1978 and 1979 the Job Corps built a three-story, eight-sided tower, with a small visitor center on its second floor.[^1]

The register says arson destroyed that tower early on October 31, 2007.[^1] Forest Lookouts describes the same night as vandalism.[^2] Local communities and state and federal politicians then raised money for a replacement.[^1]

Ground was broken on August 28, 2012, for a new stone, concrete and steel structure.[^2] According to Forest Lookouts, the Forest Service expected to finish it by late summer 2013.[^2] The register says the replacement opened in 2014.[^1]

WCYB said the new tower is a bit taller than the original, but the register's visit report from October 18, 2015 says it is slightly lower.[^3][^1] The same report says the tower is not staffed.[^1] WCYB reported an ADA-accessible ramp, and a 2015 visit report describes the top as an open, round deck built around a frame.[^3][^1]

The Forest Service lists the High Knob Recreation Area as open from late May to late September.[^4]
