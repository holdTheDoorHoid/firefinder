The Texas Forest Service ran a lookout tower in Tyler County that carried the Barnum name and was numbered 16 in the survey records.[^1][^3] The earliest dated record that names the tower is from 1949.[^1] That year the Coast and Geodetic Survey described a marker stamped BARNUM 1949, set 90 feet east of the tower and 5.5 miles north of the town of Chester.[^1] The survey's directions ran along woods roads and through a locked gate, with the key to be obtained from a large house on the route.[^1] The Texas State Historical Association places a community named Barnum in northeast Polk County, while the lookout records put this tower in Tyler County.[^4][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone, and the Central US Lookouts site marks it removed.[^2][^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains of the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
