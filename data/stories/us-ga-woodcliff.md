Woodcliff is a fire tower in Screven County, Georgia.[^1] The tnlandforms.us Georgia tower list gives its position as 32.72902, -81.74300 and an elevation of 300, with no unit stated. Read as feet, that is about 91 m.[^1] The list also tags it gfc-108, a number from an older Georgia Forestry Commission list that described the locations of 203 towers.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 29 registered Georgia towers, and Woodcliff is not among them.[^2]

Across the state, tower staffing has been cut since the late 1980s, and aircraft patrols now do much of the watching.[^3] In 2006 a Savannah newspaper, citing a Forestry Commission spreadsheet, counted 137 of Georgia's 144 surviving towers as unstaffed.[^3] The same article also said towers were being auctioned off, and that some had gone for scrap metal or to hunting clubs and other groups.[^3]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists only two Georgia sites, and Woodcliff is not one of them.[^4]

None of the sources checked describes what, if anything, remains at the Woodcliff site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
