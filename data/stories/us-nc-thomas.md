In 1929 a steel tower 93 feet high was put up four miles west of Ahoskie, in Hertford County.[^1] The North Carolina Division of Forest Resources is named as the agency.[^1] The lookout history page cites the state's third biennial report, which covered 1928 to 1930, for that date.[^1] The page gives only feet; converted, the height is about 28.3 meters.[^1] The page files the tower under Ahoskie, while the state's tower index lists it as Thomas.[^1][^2]

The page marks the tower as removed, with no date given.[^1]

Today, no source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1]

[^1]: Ahoskie, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/ahoskie.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: North Carolina Towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
