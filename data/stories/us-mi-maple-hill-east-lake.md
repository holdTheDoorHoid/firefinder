Maple Hill (East Lake) is a fire tower site in Mackinac County, Michigan, in the Hiawatha National Forest.[^1] The Forest Lookouts listing gives its location code as 43N-4W-3.[^1]

The listing quotes a 1966 Evening News item, which says the Maple Hill tower and two other Mackinac County towers were constructed in the late 1930s.[^1]

The same 1966 item reports that aerial fire detection was replacing three Mackinac County towers: Moran, Maple Hill and Daggett.[^1] Plans at the time were to keep the Moran and Maple Hill towers for emergency detection. The plan did not name Daggett as a tower to keep.[^1]

We found no source that describes what remains at the Maple Hill site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1]

[^1]: Maple Hill (East Lake), Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/maple-hill.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
