The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list places the Monico tower in Oneida County, at 45.59452 north and 89.22787 west, and marks it as gone.[^1] A Central US Lookouts page for Monico also gives the county as Oneida County.[^2]

Statewide, Wisconsin resolved in 2016 to shut down its remaining 72 functioning fire lookout towers.[^3]

We found no source that describes the site today, so we cannot say what remains there or whether it is open to visitors.
