Short Mountain stands in Cannon County, about twelve miles east of Woodbury.[^1][^2] A Cannon County history page carries a 1961 caption saying a tower was going up on the mountain.[^1] According to the caption, the tower formed part of a countywide scheme intended to guard woodland against fire.[^1] A lookout directory adds that the steel tower was finished there by year's end.[^2] The two sources agree on 1961, so we take that as the year the tower went up.

We did not find who planned or ran the tower, or a model name for its steel frame. Tennessee Landforms lists Short Mountain as standing at 2,092 feet.[^3] The 2014 list of Tennessee fire towers still standing that March includes Short Mountain in Cannon County.[^4] We found no record of the tower being moved, damaged or taken down.

Tennessee Landforms advises against climbing its towers, since steps are frequently missing or decayed.[^3] We found no public visiting arrangement for Short Mountain, so please keep off the tower. Check locally before going near the site.
