Heliograph Lookout stands in Graham County on the Coronado National Forest.[^1] Its National Register record, filed under the name Heliograph Lookout Complex, gives 1933 as the significant year and lists the Civilian Conservation Corps in its architects field.[^2] The National Historic Lookout Register dates the Civilian Conservation Corps structures at the site to 1934, so the two sources differ by a year.[^1][^2]

The tower is a 100-foot Aermotor MC-39 with a 7-by-7-foot steel cab, and the register calls it the tallest tower on the Coronado.[^1] A log cabin ground house stands at the site as well.[^1] The complex is listed on the National Register as part of a multiple listing for National Forest fire lookouts in the Southwestern Region.[^2] The National Historic Lookout Register added the lookout to its own list on January 31, 2003.[^1]

I found no record of a fire, a move or a closure in the sources I read, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association still lists the tower as standing.[^3] The register says it is still in active service.[^1] It names the Safford Ranger District as a cooperator, but none of these sources say whether the tower is staffed or open to visitors.[^1]

Check with the Safford Ranger District before you plan a visit. Do not climb the tower unless staff invite you up.
