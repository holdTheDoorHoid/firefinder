Jones Mountain, in Union County, Pennsylvania, once carried a lookout tower.[^1] The register calls it a bald tower.[^1] It says the tower was used around 1917.[^1]

The tower is no longer standing.[^1] A Bureau of Forestry blog post notes that many of the state's older forest fire observation stations have since stopped standing upright.[^2] The register gives its registry numbers as US 334 and PA 163.[^1] The site was entered in the register on September 16, 2008, with Henry Isenberg as the nominator.[^1]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. No source we found says whether the site is open to the public.

[^1]: Jones Mountain Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/pa/jones-mountain-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Fire towers: tried and true technology, Forest Fridays, Pennsylvania Bureau of Forestry, https://weconservepa.org/blog/fire-towers-tried-and-true-technology/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
