Canada Lookouts places the Ben Nevis lookout at Pushkin Lake, in Ben Nevis Township, which Wikipedia describes as an unincorporated township in the Unorganized North part of Cochrane District.[^1][^2] The same record quotes the 1949 report of the Department of Lands and Forests.[^1] That report records a frame cabin measuring 22 by 18 (no unit given), erected for a towerman in Ben Nevis Township.[^1] The quote does not mention a tower, so it is not clear that the cabin relates to this lookout.

An Ontario list of towers still standing gives the location as Pushkin Lake, Kirkland Lake.[^3] That Kirkland Lake reference differs from the township sources, so this story follows the township. The same list says the Ontario MNR had decided, as of 2013, to remove many of these standing towers.[^3] It also says the MNR no longer maintains them and asks visitors not to climb.[^3]

A post in a northern Ontario group describes an abandoned fire tower west of Pushkin Lake, about 30 km north of Larder Lake.[^4] No date for the post could be read, and no source read confirms what remains at the site today.[^4]
