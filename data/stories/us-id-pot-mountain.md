The Pot Mountain lookout site is in Clearwater County, Idaho, on the Clearwater National Forest.[^1] The first lookout there was a 12-by-12-foot cabin of whipsawed lumber, built in 1919 with a map board on its roof.[^1][^2][^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says the board was reached by ladder.[^1]

A 1914 newspaper item, quoted on a lookout history site, said a telephone line would be extended toward Pot Mountain.[^4] The item also said the peak might rank among the finest lookout spots in the Clearwater country.[^4]

In 1934 a hip-roofed L-4 cab replaced that first cabin, about 500 feet to the south.[^1][^2][^3] The register says two lookouts, Bob Elliott and Turk Oliver, spent their free hours during a single summer blasting and winching rock by hand for the base, which measured 14 feet square. The register does not date that work.[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists Elliott and Oliver as known staff in 1925.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts calls the lookout abandoned, and a regional list marks it gone.[^2][^3][^5] None of these gives a date for when that happened.

The register describes the site's history and coordinates but says nothing about what remains there. We found no source that describes the site today.[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as a hike-in.[^3] Firelookout.com places it seven miles west of Kelly Creek R.S.[^2] We found no closure notice and no route description.
