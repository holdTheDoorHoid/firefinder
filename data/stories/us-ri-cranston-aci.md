The tower started out on Wickaboxet Rock in Kent County, Rhode Island.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives its height as 65 feet (19.8 m), says it is made of steel, and dates its first use to 1926.[^1]

The register says the tower was taken out of fire service in 1973, then moved to the Adult Correction Institution in Cranston, where it serves as a guard tower.[^1] The original site entered the register on September 8, 2008.[^1]

Today the record is unclear. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Rhode Island list marks the Cranston entry as gone, and it marks the Wickaboxet Hill entry as gone too.[^2] The list and the 2008 register text do not agree.[^1][^2] No source we read says whether the tower still stands at the institution.[^1][^2]

Its last recorded use was as a guard tower at a correctional institution.[^1] No source we read describes public access to the tower.[^1]

[^1]: Wickaboxet Rock Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://www.firetower.org/lookouts/us/ri/wickaboxet-rock-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Rhode Island Fire Towers, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/ri/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
