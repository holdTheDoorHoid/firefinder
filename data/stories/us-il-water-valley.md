Water Valley was a fire lookout in Union County, Illinois, on the Shawnee National Forest.[^1][^2]

A 1942 description calls it a Forest Service station near Giant City State Park, with a steel tower 100 feet tall and an excellent view. It notes that visitors were welcome.[^1]

On August 18, 1972, an advertisement in the Southern Illinoisan invited bids to buy this tower and seven other steel towers on the Shawnee National Forest.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Illinois list now marks the tower as gone.[^2] The Forest Service says the Trigg Lookout Tower is the sole survivor of the 16 fire lookouts built on the Shawnee National Forest.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left at the site, or whether it is open to visitors today.
