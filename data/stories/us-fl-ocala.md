The register puts the Ocala Fire Tower Site in the northern part of the Ocala National Forest, along FR 9 North.[^1] The register says the former tower was made of wood and stood at most 40 feet high.[^1] The register gives the site an elevation of 150 feet, or 46 meters.[^1]

The register places the site in Alachua County, Florida.[^1] Wikipedia says the forest lies in parts of Marion, Lake and Putnam counties, so the two sources disagree about the county.[^2]

The site was registered on January 23, 2017, on the nomination of Bob Spear. The register says the U.S. Forest Service administers it.[^1]

The register says no footings remain. It also notes that possible scraps of the tower's wood are still on the ground there.[^1] No source we read describes how to reach the spot or whether visitors are welcome there.

[^1]: Ocala Fire Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/fl/ocala-fire-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Ocala National Forest, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocala_National_Forest (accessed 2026-10-10).
