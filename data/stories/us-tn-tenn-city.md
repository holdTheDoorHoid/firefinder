Tenn City is a lookout tower in Dickson County, Tennessee.[^1] The Tennessee Landforms list of towers marks it as standing and gives the site an elevation of 880 feet.[^1]

The sources we checked do not say when the tower was built, what design it uses, or how tall it is.[^1][^2]

A National Register document about the state Division of Forestry's towers includes Tenn City in its list of fire towers still standing as of March 26, 2014.[^2] The same document says the Division built 208 fire lookout towers between about 1930 and 1970, and that 142 of them were still standing in March 2014.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register counts 76 registered lookouts in Tennessee.[^3] Tenn City is not among the names on its Tennessee page.[^3]

Staffing is also a gap. The National Register document, dated 2014, says aircraft and satellite imagery play a bigger part in spotting fires, and that only a handful of the state's towers are still in active service.[^2] We found no record that Tenn City was ever staffed.[^1][^2]

Today the sources we checked say nothing about who owns the tower, whether it can be visited, or whether anyone maintains it.[^1][^2] The document says many of the old towers have been removed or are awaiting demolition. It adds that surviving towers are often in disrepair, and that visitors are commonly cautioned about the risks of climbing them.[^2] Treat the tower as closed to climbing unless its owner or land manager says otherwise.

[^3]: Lookouts in Tennessee, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/tn/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
