The Forest Lookouts site files this steel lookout under the name Micanopy, in Alachua County, Florida.[^1] The town lies south of Gainesville, and Wikipedia says it has been continuously inhabited longer than any other community in Florida's interior.[^2] Its motto, according to the same article, is "The Town that Time Forgot."[^2]

The tower was erected in 1953.[^1] In an October 1996 auction, the winning bid was $557, from a field of four bidders.[^1]

The tower came down in March 1997. A crane lowered the top 60 feet, readying that section for transport to a new home.[^1]

We found no source that names that new home, says whether the tower was put back up, describes what remains at the site today, or says whether the public can visit it.
