The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the Moss Hill lookout tower in 1949.[^1] The station sat on the crest of a timbered ridge, about two miles northeast of Zavalla and about 12 miles southeast of Huntington.[^1]

The tower had four steel legs and a cabin enclosed in glass on top.[^1] The survey gives its overall height as about 110 feet, which converts to roughly 33.5 meters.[^1] Surveyors sighted on the center of its base.[^1]

A Texas lookout list places Moss Hill under the Angelina National Forest in Angelina County.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Moss Hill (Zavalla) site as gone.[^3]

No source describes the site now, so it is not known what, if anything, remains on the ridge or whether the land is open to visitors.
