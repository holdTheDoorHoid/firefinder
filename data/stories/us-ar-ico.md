The National Historic Lookout Register calls this site the Ico Lookout Tower, and it added the tower to the Register on April 11, 2023.[^1] Central US Lookouts files a Grant County site under the name Cane Creek.[^2] I have treated the two names as one tower, though neither page says so outright.

No construction date is on record. The Register lists the build year as unknown.[^1] Its visitor calls the tower an Aermotor design and says it may stand on private property.[^1] The Encyclopedia of Arkansas notes that the most popular lookout type outside the West was built by the Aeromotor Company. These were steel towers with four legs.[^3]

The Register says the Arkansas Forestry Commission administers the tower.[^1] The Commission was established by Act 234 in 1931.[^3]

The sources I read do not say who watched the tower or what fires it spotted.

The Register's description is in the present tense and records a site visit dated 1/22/22, so the tower appeared to be standing then. I found no later check.[^1] The Register gives the site's elevation as 400 ft (122 m).[^1] Because the land may be private, confirm access with the Commission or the landowner before going, and do not climb it.
