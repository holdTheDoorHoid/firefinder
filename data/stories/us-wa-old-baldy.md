Old Baldy is a 7,844-foot summit in the Okanogan National Forest, about nine miles west-northwest of Conconully.[^1][^2]

A Forest Service bulletin from April 1934, quoted on two of the pages, said a fireman-lookout had occupied the summit for a six-week period in each of the two years before.[^3][^4] It also says radio set No. 73 was used in 1932.[^4] The L-4 cab itself went up in the summer of 1934, a date the register also gives.[^3][^1]

In 1950 a survey party sighted from the peak of the roof.[^3] A 1950 station description calls the cab a typical Forest Service lookout house.[^4]

The register says the cab was removed in 1953.[^1] Two other pages say it was destroyed that year.[^2][^3] In 1956 a USGS crew went to the summit to place a benchmark, and the lookout went unmentioned.[^3]

Willhite visited in 2022.[^3] His photographs show a stovepipe on the summit, an old woodpile and a rock wall that is still standing.[^3]

The route starts at a pass, where an early trail quickly fades.[^3] After that it is an easy off-trail hike of about a mile one way, with 1,500 feet of climbing.[^3] The summit lies in national forest land, and no source describes a closure or a permit requirement.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
