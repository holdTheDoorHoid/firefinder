A North Carolina lookout list kept on the tnlandforms.us site includes a Reynolds Mountain in Buncombe County.[^1] Its row gives the position 35.65138, -82.56777 and an unexplained code, FB3163. The list was last revised in June 2021.[^1] It is the only source found that names a tower on this summit, and it gives no build date, staffing or history for the site.[^1]

Other sources describe the mountain only as a summit. Mapcarta places it in Reems Creek Township, near the village of Woodfin, at 3,005 feet.[^2] TopoZone lists it on the Weaverville USGS topographic map, with the same approximate height.[^3]

None of the three sources gives the summit's present condition, so we cannot say whether any footings or foundation remain, or whether visitors can reach the top.[^1][^2][^3]

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Reynolds Mountain, Mapcarta, https://mapcarta.com/21321138 (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Reynolds Mountain Topo Map NC, Buncombe County (Weaverville Area), TopoZone, https://www.topozone.com/north-carolina/buncombe-nc/summit/reynolds-mountain-8/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
