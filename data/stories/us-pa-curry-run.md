The Curry Run fire tower is a modern, freestanding observation tower, according to its OpenStreetMap record.[^1] "Freestanding" here means the structure stands on its own rather than being part of a building.

That record also says the tower is not accessible to the public.[^1] Please do not try to climb it.

The record gives no build year, builder or owner, so the tower's age is unknown.[^1] The only clue is the word "modern" in its description.[^1] I could not find any other published account of the tower, and its name may be a local one. The land manager or the local forestry office would be the best place to ask for its history and to learn whether public visits are ever allowed.

[^1]: Curry Run Fire Tower, OpenStreetMap node 12646745352, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/12646745352 (accessed 2026-10-09).
