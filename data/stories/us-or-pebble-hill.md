Pebble Hill is a lookout site in Curry County, on the Siskiyou National Forest about 25 miles southeast of Port Orford.[^2] The name is not settled. The Forest Lookouts site calls it Pebble Hill, while firelookout.com calls what is probably the same place Pebble Point.[^1][^2]

The earliest date in the sources is 1920. The Forest Lookouts site says the place then served as a patrolman station, staffed by C.E. Criteser.[^1] The same page quotes a July 1920 item from the Siskiyou Bulletin.[^1] It said work had started on a Pebble Hill Trail, led by Walter Fry and crew, and that the grade never topped fifteen percent.[^1]

Firelookout.com dates the site to the 1930s, leaves its type unknown and calls it abandoned.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Pebble Point site as gone. Its coordinates sit about half a kilometre from the Pebble Hill point, so the two names probably refer to one place.[^3]

Today no source describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it.
