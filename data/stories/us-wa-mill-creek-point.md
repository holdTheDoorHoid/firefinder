The Mill Creek Point lookout stood on a 4,938-foot summit in the Kaniksu National Forest, in Pend Oreille County, about 13 miles northeast of Usk.[^1][^2]

A crow's nest, dated to 1937, was the earliest structure on the summit.[^1][^3] In 1940 a 50-foot pole tower, braced with cables, carried an L-4 cab.[^1][^2][^3] Galena Point, roughly a mile southeast, held a patrol crow's nest that was part of the lookout.[^1][^3]

By 1950 the tower had been phased out.[^1][^3] FireLookout.com's header runs the site's life from 1940 into the 1950s.[^2] Forest Lookouts marks the site as removed, without a date.[^4]

Willhite visited in 2023 and found the spur road to the summit blocked by fallen trees.[^3] Remnants on the summit include some footings, plus bolts and anchors.[^3] He lists the hike at half a mile each way with 300 feet of gain, and says a vehicle with some clearance is best.[^3] No source says whether the site is open to visitors.
