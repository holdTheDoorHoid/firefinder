Air Line is listed as a lookout tower in Hart County, Georgia, on a site that catalogues eastern US lookouts.[^1] A list of Georgia lookout towers places it at about 34.36 degrees north and 83.03 degrees west, with the GFC number 920.[^2] The list says its GFC numbers come from an older Georgia Forestry Commission list that described 203 towers.[^2] The list also says names for towers numbered 73 and above are its compiler's own, so "air line" may be a label rather than the tower's official name.[^2]

The name is shared with an unincorporated community in Hart County, which had a post office from 1856 to 1907.[^3]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything remains there or whether visitors can reach it.
