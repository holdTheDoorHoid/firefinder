Withers is a fire lookout tower in Clinch County, Georgia.[^1] The tnlandforms.us Georgia tower list gives its position as 30.85685, -82.89803 and an elevation of 163, with no unit stated. Read as feet, that is about 50 m.[^1]

Georgia has been cutting back on tower staffing since the late 1980s, with aircraft patrols doing much of the watching instead.[^2] A 2006 Savannah newspaper article, citing a Forestry Commission spreadsheet, counted 137 of Georgia's 144 surviving towers as unstaffed.[^2] The same article also said towers were being auctioned off, and that some had gone for scrap metal or to hunting clubs and other groups.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 29 registered Georgia towers, and Withers is not among them.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists two Georgia sites, and Withers is not one of them.[^4]

None of the sources checked describes what, if anything, remains at the Withers site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
