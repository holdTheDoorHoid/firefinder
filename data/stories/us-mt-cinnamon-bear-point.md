Cinnamon Bear Point is a lookout site on the Lolo National Forest in Granite County, about 15 miles east-northeast of Stevensville.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com lists its elevation as 7,046 feet.[^1]

A July 12, 1935 Missoulian item, as quoted by Forest Lookouts, reports the placement of two new lookouts in the Rock Creek district: Hogback and Cinnamon Bear.[^3] The report says both lookouts were manned, with the watchers living in tents and working from towers set up in trees.[^3]

Firelookout.com's entry mentions a 1930s camp about two miles from its summit and marks the site abandoned.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the type as a platform and the status as abandoned.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.
