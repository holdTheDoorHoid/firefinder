Taft Summit lies seven miles east-northeast of Lookout Pass, in Sanders County, Montana, at an elevation of 6,280 feet.[^1] The site is on the Lolo National Forest.[^1][^3]

The first tower there was a 35-foot pole platform, built in 1909.[^1] The 1910 fire, which firelookout.com calls the Great Idaho Fire, burned about three million acres across northern Idaho and western Montana.[^1][^2] Two of the tower's legs burned in that fire, and the tower fell into the Montana Power Company lines beside it.[^1]

An L-4 tower went up there in the 1930s and was destroyed during the 1950s.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts Montana page marks the site as removed and gives no date.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^4]

Firelookout.com says a crow's nest about half a mile east is still there, though the page does not date that remark.[^1] I found no source describing the summit site today or whether the public can reach it.
