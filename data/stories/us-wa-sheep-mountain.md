Sheep Mountain sits in the Colville National Forest, about eight miles south-southwest of Republic in Ferry County.[^1][^2]

In July 1933 a disassembled lookout station was unloaded from cars for erection on the mountain.[^4] By October the tower had been raised, and work was under way on a 14-by-14-foot house on top of it.[^4] The register dates the lookout to 1934.[^1] In June 1934, a four-man crew led by Glen Campbell was finishing the station and fitting lightning protection.[^3][^4]

The lookout was a pole tower 45 feet tall, topped by an L-4 cab.[^1][^2][^4] It came down in 1964.[^1][^2][^4] Sources disagree on how.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The register says it was removed, and the Forest Lookouts page also lists it as removed, while the Firelookout and WillhiteWeb pages say it was destroyed.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

The site was entered in the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register on November 19, 2012, as US 1464, WA 364, with Ray Kresek as nominator.[^1]

None of the sources I read describes what, if anything, is left on the mountain today, and none says whether visitors can reach it. The register still lists the U.S. Forest Service as administrator and gives the elevation as 3,919 feet (1,195 m).[^1]
