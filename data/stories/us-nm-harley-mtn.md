Harley Mountain has a fire lookout tower on a high ridge about nine miles east of Mescalero, New Mexico, between the Whitetail and Elk Canyon drainages.[^1] The earliest record I found is a 1936 survey station description from the Coast and Geodetic Survey, and it already calls the structure a fire lookout tower.[^1] I could not find a build date, a builder, or a design in the sources I could reach, and the state list has no build-date column either.[^2]

The survey mark is recorded at about 8,671 feet.[^1] A U.S. Geological Survey note from 1963 describes the station mark as a fire lookout tower and rates its condition as good.[^1] Those survey notes are the only dated records I found. I found nothing on staffing, notable fires, or later changes to the structure.

The state association's list shows the tower as standing in Otero County, with no register number.[^2] That list has no staffing, access, or date columns, so it does not say who owns the tower or whether the public can reach it.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page files the tower under the Mescalero Indian Agency. That label shows how the site groups it, not who manages the ground it stands on.[^3]

I could not find access or current-condition information. Treat the tower as unknown ground, and do not climb it without the landowner's permission.
