The Georgia Forestry Commission completed a fire tower in Seminole County in 1952.[^1] The register calls it a later-generation Aermotor design, 100 feet (30.5 m) tall, with a metal cab 7 by 7 feet (about 2.1 m) across.[^1] It was last staffed in 1988, and the National Historic Lookout Register gives January 2, 2008 as its registration date.[^1]

The register places it on US 717 and GA 12, and gives its elevation as 142 feet (43 m).[^1] The Georgia lookout list puts the tower about 3.9 km from the register's coordinates, a distance we worked out ourselves from the two coordinate pairs.[^2][^1] The list gives its elevation as 140, with no unit stated.[^2] The two sources therefore disagree about where the tower stands.[^2][^1]

The register says the tower remains in service for emergency use.[^1] We found no source that says whether the public may visit or climb it.

[^1]: Seminole County Fire Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ga/seminole-county-fire-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
