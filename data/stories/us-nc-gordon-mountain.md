A TN Landforms list of North Carolina lookout towers includes a Gordon Mountain tower in Anson County, at coordinates 34.91417, -80.19611.[^1] PeakVisor, a peak-listing site, says Gordon Mountain is the highest of Anson County's five named mountains, at 202 meters.[^2]

Another Gordon Mountain, a summit in Transylvania County at 2,952 feet, is a different peak.[^3]

No source describes the site today, including whether a tower stands there or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, TN Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Anson County mountains, PeakVisor, https://peakvisor.com/adm/anson-county.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Gordon Mountain, North Carolina, TopoQuest, https://topoquest.com/place-detail.php?id=1020476 (accessed 2026-10-21).
