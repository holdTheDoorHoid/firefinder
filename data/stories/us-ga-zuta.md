A list of Georgia lookout towers includes an entry for Zuta, in Glynn County, at 31.32072, -81.58550.[^1] The list gives an elevation of 22 with no unit; if the figure is feet, that is about 7 m. It also tags the entry gfc-850.[^1] The list was last revised in March 2016.[^1]

Zuta is an unincorporated community in Glynn County, Georgia.[^2] Its name comes from Zuta Branch, a waterway in the Cowpen Swamp on the Altamaha River floodplain.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the Zuta site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
