The Cow Knob tower stood on the top of Cow Knob in the Shenandoah Mountains, on the Virginia and West Virginia state line.[^1][^2] The register places it in Rockingham County, in the George Washington National Forest.[^1]

The register dates the tower to 1932.[^1] It stood 45 feet tall in steel, with a 14-by-14-foot wooden cab, and could be seen from U.S. 33.[^1] A 1956 Coast and Geodetic Survey station description gives its height as about 45 feet, with a four-legged steel frame and a 14-foot-square wooden house on top.[^2]

The register lists the tower as removed before 1993.[^1] The register also says it was sold at auction to a local buyer from the Rockingham County area, who hoped one day to rebuild it.[^1] The register listed the site in July 2018.[^1]

The two pages we read describe the tower and its removal, but not what remains at the site or whether the ground is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
