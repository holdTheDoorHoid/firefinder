The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list places a fire tower at Halls Lake in Florence County, at about 45.82 north and 88.30 west, and marks it as gone.[^1]

No other source found describes the site, so it is not known what, if anything, remains there today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1]

[^1]: Wisconsin Lookouts (Alphabetical List), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
