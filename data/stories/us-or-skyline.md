The Portland Parks Bureau developed the Skyline lookout in 1958 to watch Forest Park.[^1][^2] It stood off Northwest Skyline Boulevard near the park's northern edge.[^1] The tower was a modified steel power-line tower, and the sources disagree about who gave the steel.[^1] A 1958 newspaper item names Pacific Light and Power Co., while a photo caption from the same period credits Portland General Electric Co.[^1]

The same 1958 article said junior foresters, drawn from high schools and colleges, had worked lookout posts on the Willamette's northeast side for two years and would staff this tower the next year.[^1]

Firelookout.com lists the tower as abandoned and gives no date for it.[^2] The same site says the exact location has not been determined.[^2] Today no source describes what remains of the tower, and none confirms whether anyone can reach the spot.[^2][^3] A WillhiteWeb page about the Skyline site gives a best guess at a spot near the top powerline crossing above Skyline Drive and says it is still unconfirmed.[^3]
