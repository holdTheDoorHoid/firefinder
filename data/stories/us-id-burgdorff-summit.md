Burgdorff Summit rises about six miles northeast of Warren, in Idaho County.[^1] Both lookout sites name the Payette National Forest as its forest.[^1][^2] Its elevation is 8,080 feet.[^1][^2] Some sources spell the name Burgdorf, with one f.[^2]

Records for the 1920s describe a crow's nest and a camp on the summit.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list places the summit at 45.31747 north and 115.593 west, and labels it a crow's nest site.[^4]

An Idaho County index files the summit under the Payette National Forest.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts places it in its West Central region.[^2] Both lookout pages call the site abandoned and describe ruins.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the summit as a hike-in.[^2]
