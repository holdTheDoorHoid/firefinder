Firelookout.ca lists a Deacon Fire Tower at Brent, Ontario.[^1] Brent sits on Cedar Lake in northern Algonquin Provincial Park and lies in geographic Deacon Township, in the unorganized southern part of Nipissing District.[^2] It began as a railway station on the Canadian Northern Railway's transcontinental main line, and lumber companies operated there in the late 19th and early 20th centuries.[^2]

Firelookout.ca places the tower on a 1963 Department of Lands and Forests map of its tower detection system.[^1]

A photo page on mcelroy.ca, with a photo dated September 2011, calls it the Brent fire tower. It says the remains sit on the ground beside the Pan Lake to Ghost Lake portage in Algonquin Park.[^3] Both pages name Brent, so this story treats them as one tower, though no source explicitly ties the Deacon name to the mcelroy.ca tower.[^1][^3]

Wikipedia describes Brent today as a camping area with some park facilities and a gateway to the park's interior.[^2] No source I could read describes a marker or any standing structure at the tower site.[^1][^3]
