Lookout Mountain stood in Hood River County, about nine miles east-northeast of Mount Hood, in the Mount Hood National Forest.[^2][^3]

A lookout was set up here in 1911, with an alidade (a sighting instrument) on the summit and a log cabin in a meadow below the top.[^1][^3]

In 1914 a log cabin went up on the peak, along with a 10-foot enclosed platform.[^1][^2] The sources differ on what came next. The register says a 10-foot platform followed in 1930, while firelookout.com describes a 10-foot live-in tower.[^1][^2] In 1940 a 10-foot treated timber L-4 tower replaced it.[^1][^2]

In June 1922 an Oregon Daily Journal item said A.E. Florer of Hood River had signed on as the summer lookout.[^3] In 1933 a CCC crew from Friend Camp built a garage at the site.[^3]

The register dates abandonment to 1966 and says the structure was later removed.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its history as "R.I.P. 1914-1930; 1940-?", so it does not supply an end date for the later years.[^2]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, is left on the mountain now, or whether the public can reach it.
