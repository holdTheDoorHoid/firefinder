Consolidated was a lookout tower in Forest County, Wisconsin, near 45.65930 north and 88.73492 west.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.

[^1]: Wisconsin Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Consolidated, Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/consolidated.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
