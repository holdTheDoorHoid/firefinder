Trinity Lookout sits on Trinity Mountain in the Boise National Forest, about 40 miles east of Boise in Elmore County, Idaho.[^1][^3] Sources put the summit between 9,434 and 9,451 feet.[^1][^3]

A lookout has stood on the mountain since 1922, when a 12-by-12 stone cabin with a metal roof went up.[^1][^2] That cabin was later replaced by an L-4 cab on a 10-foot concrete base.[^1][^2]

Sources disagree on when that cab went up. The register, Rex's Fire Lookout Page and Forest Lookouts all date it to 1956.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives 1922 for the current structure, and Keeping Watch records the tower as built in 1922.[^4][^5] This story uses 1956 for the cab and 1922 for the first cabin on the site.

Staffing goes back a long way. A January 1945 newspaper item, quoted by Forest Lookouts, says the Pine district used four women as lookouts, one of them on Trinity Mountain.[^2] In December 1955 a ground party was organized in Boise to reach men who had been in a crash somewhere in rough, snowy country. An Idaho State Journal item quoted by the same site suggests they passed the night in the lookout station.[^2]

The cab is still standing and in use. The register added the lookout on September 27, 2020, and Idaho Fire Lookouts says it has a fire-detection watch right now.[^1][^4]

Keeping Watch says the lookout has seasonal staffing, and the register says it is usually manned during fire season.[^5][^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it as drive-up.[^4] Because it is a working lookout, do not climb up without permission. Ask the Boise National Forest before you plan a visit.
