The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower at Bastrop, in Bastrop County, Texas, at coordinates 30.17398, -97.24602.[^1] Its Texas list marks the tower gone.[^1]

No source found describes what, if anything, remains at the Bastrop tower site today, or whether the public can reach it. Visitors should not assume a tower is still standing there.

[^1]: Texas Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
