Wahoo (Squaw) Peak lies in Idaho County, about 13 miles east-northeast of the Moose Creek Ranger Station.[^1][^2] Most sources date the lookout there to 1934, when a 30-foot pole observation tower and a cabin were built.[^2][^3][^4][^5]

Not every source agrees on the start. The Forest Lookouts site also lists a log cabin with a cupola in 1925, citing an undated inventory of lookouts on the Moose Creek Ranger District.[^1] That same page says management of the site passed from the Bitterroot to the Nez Perce National Forest in 1957.[^1]

Sources agree that the tower was destroyed in the 1940s.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5] Spokane Historical holds a photograph captioned "Squaw Peak Lookout in 1960," which places that lookout in the Bitterroot National Forest.[^6] That does not fit this site, which the Forest Lookouts page says moved its administration out of the Bitterroot in 1957, so we have not counted the photo as evidence that a tower stood after the 1940s.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the tower as gone.[^7] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page calls the site a hike-in, and a Forest Service list titled "Selway-Bitterroot Wilderness Lookouts" includes it.[^4][^5] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it is open to the public.
