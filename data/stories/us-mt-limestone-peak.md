Limestone Peak had a lookout on the Flathead National Forest, in Flathead County, Montana.[^1][^2][^3]

A cabin went up there in the 1930s, about half a mile below the summit on the north spur.[^1] It was a ground-level cabin rather than a tower, according to the Forest Fire Lookout Association's list.[^3]

The firelookout.com page describes the cabin as abandoned.[^1] The Association's list marks the lookout as gone and places it at 47.88387, -113.25233.[^3]

The Forest Lookouts page gives its legal location as 25N-13W-35.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^3]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
