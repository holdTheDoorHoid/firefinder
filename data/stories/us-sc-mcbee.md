McBee was a fire lookout run by the South Carolina Forestry Commission, in Chesterfield County.[^1] A state report covering July 1944 to June 1945 records that the McBee tower got an overhaul and a coat of paint that year.[^1]

The state ran a system of lookout towers from the 1930s on.[^2] By the 1980s the system was mostly a communications network, and the towers were slowly taken out of use.[^2] The commission shut its final forest fire lookout in 1993.[^2]

OpenStreetMap maps a point named McBee Lookout Tower at the site, but its map data does not show whether a structure stands there now.[^3] No source we read describes what remains at the site today, and we found nothing on whether it is open to visitors.
