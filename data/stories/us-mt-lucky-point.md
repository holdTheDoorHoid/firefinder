Lucky Point is a former lookout site in the Kootenai National Forest, about nine miles west-southwest of Yaak in Lincoln County, Montana.[^1] The firelookout.com page dates it to 1934 and describes a 10' pole L-4 tower built that year. It lists the site at 5,179 feet.[^1]

The page's heading reads "R.I.P. 1934-?" and notes that the tower has been down for an unstated number of years.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list also gives the tower as gone, at 48.77900 north and 115.88546 west.[^2]

A Lincoln County list names Lucky Point under the Kootenai National Forest, with a township and range and no other detail.[^3]

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today or whether it is open to visitors. Check with the Kootenai National Forest before going.
