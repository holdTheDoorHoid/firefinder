Coffin Butte is in the Umpqua National Forest in Douglas County, Oregon, about 29 miles west of Crater Lake.[^1][^2]

The register and Firelookout.com say a 40-foot pole L-4 tower went up in 1934.[^1][^2] The Oregon lookouts page dates the L-4 lookout house to 1937, so the build year is disputed.[^3]

Staffing records are scattered. A September 1938 item from the Roseburg News-Review described John Hutchinson as the Forest Service lookout at Coffin Butte.[^3] In November of that year, the same paper said he had spent several days posted at the station because of a forest fire in the area.[^3] A July 1939 item from the Oregon Statesman reported that a lookout there had been hurt chopping wood and was carried out from his remote post by packhorse.[^3] In July 1952 the News-Review reported that Elinor Sherk was driven to Tiller to start work as the Coffin Butte fire lookout.[^3]

By 1963 the lookout had deteriorated so far that its disposal was called for, and two men were given the job of destroying it.[^3] The register says it was removed in 1963, and Firelookout.com says it was destroyed.[^1][^2] The register added the site on December 9, 2010.[^1]

We found no source describing what remains at the site today, such as footings or a foundation, and none says whether the site is open to the public. A directory of Oregon lookouts that are still standing does not list Coffin Butte.[^4]
