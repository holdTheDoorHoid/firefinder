The Sable Hill lookout site sits at 6,084 feet in the Nez Perce National Forest, about 12 miles east of Elk City in Idaho County.[^1][^2]

In 1933 an L-4 cab was set on a 50-foot tower there.[^1][^2][^3] The L-4 was a 14-by-14-foot frame cab, and the National Historic Lookout Register says cabs of that size could go on top of tall fire towers.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists one known staffer, Spence A. Dewey, for 1944, citing a former forest fire lookout registry.[^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1958, a date all three sources we read give.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site hike-in but gives no trail details.[^2] No source we read describes what remains on the hill today or whether the land is open to visitors, so check with the Nez Perce National Forest before you go.
