A lookout directory places the Rosedale tower in Gadsden County, Florida.[^1] A map of the Chattahoochee, Florida area lists a "Rosedale Fire Tower" among its named places.[^2]

The directory's entry is short. Its only dated item is a bid opening on September 14, 2005 for the sale of the tower, which produced a winning bid of $1,011.[^1]

When the Florida Forest Service decides a fire tower is no longer needed, it is sold off as state surplus, and the winning bidder is expected to take it apart and haul it to a new location.[^3] The directory does not say whether this tower went that way or where it ended up.

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it. Confirm access before going.
