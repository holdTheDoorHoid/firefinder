Rocky Point is a crow's nest lookout in Latah County, Idaho.[^2][^4] Its listing names the Potlatch Timber Protective Association, or PTPA.[^1][^2]

The crow's nest dates from the 1930s.[^1] Forest Lookouts gives the location as 41N-4W-31 and lists the name Kresek beside the crow's nest.[^2]

The firelookout.com page says the crow's nest was still there in the 1980s and calls the site abandoned.[^1] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page also lists a crow's nest, and the firelookout.org index marks the site abandoned.[^3][^4]

The index gives the site's coordinates as 46.84568, -116.87758.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit.
