Town Hill, in Berkshire County, once carried a 30-foot wooden fire tower that was first operated in 1915.[^1] The register numbers the site US 48 and MA 48, and gives its elevation as 1,798 feet (548 meters).[^1] Building the tower also meant laying 1.5 miles of phone line to the site.[^1]

The tower was staffed until 1917.[^1] The register says it no longer stands.[^1] In the fall of 2003, a group of Forest Fire Lookout Association members spent several hours looking for what was left of it, and the only thing they found was an old, rusted phone line.[^1] The site was added to the register on September 3, 2008.[^1]

No source describes the summit since that 2003 search, or says whether the hilltop is open to visitors.

[^1]: Sandisfield Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ma/sandisfield-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
