Lamb Butte lies in Lane County, about eight miles southeast of McKenzie Bridge, within the Willamette National Forest.[^1]

A firefinder went up on the butte in 1930.[^1][^2] A lookout cab followed in 1933.[^1][^2][^3] The sources disagree on the design. The register and firelookout.com call the 1933 cab an L-6, while a lookout history site calls it an L-5 ground house, 10 by 10.[^1][^2][^3] The house sat on top of the butte.[^3]

A September 1934 Eugene Register-Guard item, quoted by the history site, reports that a strong wind blew the shutters off the house.[^3]

The register and firelookout.com say the cab was gone by 1959.[^1][^2] The history site says the lookout was removed in the 1950s.[^3]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains on the butte today, or whether the summit is open to visitors.
