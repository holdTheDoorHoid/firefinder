A Georgia tower list kept on tnlandforms.us places a lookout labelled "mcgregor" in Montgomery County, at 32.19343, -82.51625.[^1] The page's author says names for towers numbered 73 and above are the author's own creation, so this label is not a name taken from a historical source.[^1] It matches McGregor, a Montgomery County place that Wikipedia classes as unincorporated.[^2]

The entry carries three tags, BLL-64709, usgs and gfc-509.[^1] The page says the Georgia Forestry Commission (GFC) supplied coordinates for 118 towers under the BLL tags, and that an earlier GFC list covered 203 towers under the gfc numbering.[^1]

The page's map was last revised in March 2016.[^1] No source found describes what remains at the site today, so it is unknown whether a tower still stands there.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: McGregor, Georgia, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGregor,_Georgia (accessed 2026-10-21).
