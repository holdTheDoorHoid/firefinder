Ridley Hill, in Springvale in York County, Maine, once carried a wooden fire lookout tower.[^1][^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register describes that predecessor as a 60-foot wooden tower of the western type, with living quarters in the cab and a catwalk.[^3]

The state no longer held its lease on the hill, and with the wooden tower in poor condition, it was decided to build a replacement.[^1] That replacement was a steel tower about three miles south, on Mount Hope Hill, which the Maine Forest Service erected in 1962.[^1][^3]

On July 9, 1949, the Ridley Hill lookout reported a sighting of fire on the east side of Sanford.[^4] The Sanford Department traced it to a resident who had been burning trash without a permit.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine list marks the Ridley Hill tower as gone.[^2] No source I could read describes what remains at Ridley Hill today, or whether the hill is open to visitors.
