Flat Rock is a former tree lookout in the Umpqua National Forest, in Douglas County, Oregon.[^1][^2] The Oregon lookouts history says it was established in 1930, with the platform at the 18-foot level.[^1] Fire Lookout calls it an 18-foot tree platform from the 1930s.[^2] Robert Reinhart took panorama photos on July 15, 1938, according to the same history.[^1]

Fire Lookout lists the platform as abandoned and says it was still there in the 1980s.[^2] The Forest Service says the tree has been retired from lookout duty, yet its original platform, now incorporated into the tree, remains visible.[^3]

Today the site is reached by Flat Rock Trail #1526, a Forest Service recreation trail that starts on the Yellow Jacket Loop Trail #1522.[^3] The directions reach Forest Road 2700-495, which leads to Hemlock Lake Campground.[^3] Visitors park near the picnic area, then hike the Yellow Jacket Loop Trail until they reach the trailhead sign at milepost 1.25.[^3] The approach is long: past the end of the pavement, the Forest Service directions add 12 miles of gravel road.[^3] After a steep climb, the summit offers views of Hemlock Lake, Mount Thielsen and Mount Bailey.[^3]
