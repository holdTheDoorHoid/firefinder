Gopher Ridge is a tower in St. Johns County, Florida.[^1][^3] The Forest Lookouts site has a page for it under that county, and TopoZone lists it by name among the county's towers.[^1][^2] Its map entry places the tower at 29.6661° N, 81.4145° W, in the Spuds USGS map area, about 10 m (33 ft) above sea level.[^3] No source we could read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^2]: St. Johns County tower listing, TopoZone, https://www.topozone.com/florida/st-johns-fl/tower/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Gopher Ridge Tower Topo Map FL, St. Johns County (Spuds Area), TopoZone, https://www.topozone.com/florida/st-johns-fl/tower/gopher-ridge-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
