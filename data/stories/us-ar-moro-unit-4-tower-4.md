The Arkansas Forestry Commission's Unit 4, Tower 4 stood on state-owned land, just west of and beside State Highway 97, about two and a half miles southeast of Kingsland in Cleveland County, Arkansas.[^1] Local people called it the Moro Lookout Tower.[^1] The structure was all steel, and a fully enclosed cabin sat at its summit.[^1] A list of Arkansas lookouts files it under Cleveland County and the Forestry Commission.[^2]

A federal survey description dated 1942 already describes the tower, so it was standing by then.[^1] The survey tied its station to a triangulation mark called Moro, which lies about 41 feet (12.4 m) east of the tower's centre.[^1]

No source we read describes the site today, or says whether any part of the tower still stands.
