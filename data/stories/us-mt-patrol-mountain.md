Patrol Mountain sits in the Lewis and Clark National Forest, about 23 miles west of Augusta, Montana.[^2] A camp went up at the lookout site in 1923, and a log cabin was built there in 1925.[^1][^2] The lookout that stands today is an R-6 flat cab, a 1962 U.S. Forest Service building.[^1][^3]

A 1924 newspaper item reported new trails built to the Patrol Mountain lookout, and a 1928 notice names a person who worked as a lookout there during the summer.[^4] None of the sources I could open describes a particular fire the lookout helped spot.

The cab is manned during the summer.[^1] A 2022 magazine profile described a veteran lookout who had worked the post for 26 fire seasons.[^3] The National Historic Lookout Register added the site on April 12, 2022, as US 1540, MT 103.[^1]

Getting there takes effort. Each fragile piece went up by mule on a 6-mile trail that winds from the valley.[^3] None of the sources I could open says whether visitors may go inside the cab, so check with the Forest Service before planning a trip.
