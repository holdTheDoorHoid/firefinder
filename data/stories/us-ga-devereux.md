The Georgia Forestry Commission's older tower list gives the Devereux tower the number 614, and it places the tower in Hancock County at an elevation of 615 feet.[^1] The second number on the record, BLL-41092, belongs to a group of 118 towers whose coordinates the Commission provided.[^1]

Devereux is an unincorporated settlement in Hancock County, Georgia.[^2] The tower list places the tower at 33.20237 north and 83.07919 west.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 29 lookouts in Georgia, and Devereux is not among them.[^3]

Statewide, the Commission moved away from staffed towers in the late 1980s and relied on aerial patrols instead. Many towers were sold off as scrap.[^4]

No source we found describes the site today, including whether any part of the tower remains or whether the land is open to visitors.
