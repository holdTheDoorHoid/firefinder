The Meriwether lookout is listed in Meriwether County, Georgia, at 33.03013, -84.75687, with a listed elevation of 961 (no unit is given; read as feet, about 293 m).[^1] The same list gives it two codes, BLL-63472 and gfc-419.[^1] A Forest Lookouts index files the tower under the Georgia Forestry Commission.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts page for the site carries only its name and county, so no source here describes the site today or says whether a visitor can reach it.[^3]
