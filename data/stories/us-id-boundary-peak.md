Boundary Peak Lookout stood on a peak in Kootenai County, Idaho, inside the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1][^2][^3] The tower there was built in 1931. It was a 20-foot pole structure with an L-4 cab, a design all three detailed listings describe.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the peak as a hike-in site at an elevation of 4,467 feet.[^1]

Every detailed listing gives 1962 as the year the tower was destroyed.[^1][^2][^3] The Fire Lookout index at firelookout.org still marks the tower as gone, so there is no standing lookout left to climb.[^4] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]

A different Boundary Peak, in Idaho County, is also listed as gone, so check the county before planning a trip.[^4]
