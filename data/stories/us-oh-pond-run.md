The Pond Run Tower stood in Shawnee State Forest in Scioto County, Ohio, at 1,160 feet (354 meters).[^1] The register says it was 87 feet tall, which is about 26.5 meters.[^1]

On October 27, 1934, the Chillicothe Gazette reported that the tower was then being built on the Shawnee State Forest.[^2] A Scioto Historical page says enrollees from Camp Scioto built it.[^3] The page also says the camp, on Pond Run, was home to Company 552 of the Civilian Conservation Corps.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Ohio list marks the tower as gone.[^4] The Scioto Historical page refers to the tower in the past tense, placing it above McBride Lake.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site now, or whether the spot is open to visitors.
