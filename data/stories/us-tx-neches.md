The Forest Lookout Association lists a standing tower at Neches, in Anderson County, Texas, at about 31.87 degrees north and 95.50 degrees west.[^1] The community lies in eastern Anderson County, about nine miles northeast of Palestine, where U.S. Highway 79 meets two farm roads beside the Union Pacific tracks.[^2]

We could not find when the tower went up, who built it, or what it was made of. The Association's list gives only a name, county, coordinates and status.[^1] For context, Bob Bowman's East Texas lookout page dates the Lufkin tower and similar towers to Civilian Conservation Corps work in 1936, and says they stayed in use until they were phased out.[^3] We have no source that ties the Neches tower to that program, so its history should be treated as unknown for now.

We also found no record of who staffed the Neches tower or of any fire it helped to watch.[^1] Edward Cavallerano's 2025 book, Fire in the Piney Woods, covers where Texas lookout towers stand and how they were designed.[^4] We read only its publisher summary, so the full text may hold more about Neches.[^4]

The Association's list shows the tower as still standing, but it says nothing about its condition, its owner, or whether visitors are allowed.[^1] We found no rental information either. Please do not climb a tower unless the owner has given you permission.
