The Berland Fire Tower appears in OpenStreetMap, a free map that people add to, as an observation tower of lattice construction at about 54.09 N, 117.40 W.[^1]

A review of the 2015 fire season puts the number of lookouts that Agriculture and Forestry maintains across the province at 127.[^2] The review says lookouts keep ongoing watch over visible areas for wildfire, and that they also serve as locations for crew staging, communications and weather monitoring.[^2]

Staffing is described two ways. The 2015 review says most lookouts are staffed through the core of the fire season, with the rest staffed only during high hazard and risk.[^2] A government page on bears says each site has a lookout observer working roughly from April to October.[^3] We could not tell which description covers this tower.[^2][^3]

We found no source that describes what stands at the Berland site today, or whether the public may visit.
