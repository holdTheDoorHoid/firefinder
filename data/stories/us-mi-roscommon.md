The Roscommon Lookout Tower is the name the National Geodetic Survey (NGS) gives a survey mark in Crawford County, Michigan.[^1][^2] The NGS datasheet classes the mark as a lookout-tower marker and gives its position on the NAD 83 datum as 44 31 39.16679 N, 84 29 15.32604 W.[^1] The datasheet ties the mark to the Eldorado USGS quadrangle, dated 2017.[^1]

The earliest record is a survey observation at the station in 1932.[^1] In 1949 the Coast and Geodetic Survey recovered the mark in good condition. It noted that the observation point sat at the center of the top of the lookout tower.[^1]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^2]: Roscommon - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts (easternuslookouts.weebly.com), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/roscommon.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
