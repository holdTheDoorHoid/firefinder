Canoe Creek is listed as a lookout in Escambia County, Florida.[^1] It is not on either of Florida's lookout registers.[^2][^3] The National Historic Lookout Register counts 31 Florida lookouts, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register counts six former ones.[^2][^3]

The U.S. Geological Survey lists a groundwater well named "CANOE CRK TOWERSITE" in Escambia County.[^4] The name may refer to a tower site, but the survey's entry lists the site only as a well.[^4]

No source describes what remains at the Canoe Creek site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
