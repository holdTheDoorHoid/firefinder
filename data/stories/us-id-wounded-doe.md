Sources describe Wounded Doe as a camp rather than a tower, on Wounded Doe Ridge in the Clearwater National Forest, Idaho County.[^4][^2][^3] The Forest Service list places it 19 miles southwest of Powell Ranger Station.[^4][^2] Sources disagree about when it began. The Forest Service list and firelookout.com both give 1933 for the camp.[^4][^2] Louis Hartig's 1981 history, as quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, says Ranger Ralph Hand set up a tent camp on the point in 1923, with Jess Mosher holding the lookout.[^3]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site lists Jess Mosher under 1922 and Keith McGee under 1939.[^1] Hartig dates the first manning, with Mosher on the lookout, to 1923.[^3] In 1939, Ranger Hans Roeffler set up another tent camp, with McGee as lookout.[^3] The radio link was unreliable because of equipment faults and static, so McGee and Daland Broker, on Rhoda Point, used mirror flashes instead, which Hartig says worked only imperfectly.[^3]

Hartig says nobody staffed the point after 1939, and it was eventually taken off the fire plan.[^3] The Forest Service list and firelookout.com both mark it abandoned in 1939.[^4][^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page gives no elevation and tags the site as a hike-in.[^1] None of the sources says whether the ground is open to visitors today.
