The Atkins tower is a galvanized Aermotor LS-40 that stands 110 feet tall, with a steel cab about seven feet square.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register says it was built near 1936. I found no exact date in any other source.[^1]

The South Carolina Forestry Commission is named as the agency for the tower.[^2] The site once had a tower keeper's house, which stood until about 2010, and the register now describes the site as abandoned.[^1] The register also places the tower at what seems to be its original site.[^1]

The tower overlooks farmland in the Pee Dee District of Lee County.[^1] The register's photographs are captioned October 2013, and I found no later report on its condition.[^1] Statewide, South Carolina ended fire observation from its lookouts in 1993, but I found no record of this tower's last year in use.[^3]

The sources I checked say nothing about public access. Check with the Forestry Commission before going to the site, and do not climb the tower.
