A Georgia lookout tower list places a tower named tennille in Washington County, at 32.94926, -82.84706.[^1] It gives 457 in its elevation column but does not say what unit that is.[^1] The list says its towers come from the USGS name database and from 1:100,000 USGS topographic maps.[^1] The list says it was motivated by a gap: no Georgia towers were listed at the FFLA site in 2010.[^1]

The list credits the Georgia Forestry Commission, abbreviated GFC, with coordinates for 118 towers under one numbering, and an older GFC list with locations for 203 towers under another.[^1] This tower carries both kinds of number, BLL-41090 and gfc-626.[^1]

Statewide, the Georgia Forestry Commission shifted from staffed lookouts to aerial patrols during the late 1980s.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today.
