Finley Butte rises about four miles southeast of La Pine in Deschutes County, on the Deschutes National Forest.[^1][^2] A 60-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab went up there in 1932, according to the register and two other lookout histories.[^1][^2][^3]

Panoramic photographs were taken from the top in 1934 and again in 1938.[^1][^3] In 1936 the Shevlin-Hixson Company had a man stationed on the butte for the last week of May.[^4][^3]

The Forest Lookouts page marks the tower as removed, and the register says it is now gone.[^4][^1] With no tower left, the summit view runs in only one direction, north.[^3]

The summit today has a shed and communications equipment, and a rock pit lies below the hill.[^3] A visitor in 2022 reached the top from La Pine along the Finley Butte Road, turning toward a signed quarry about three miles in and then climbing the hill.[^3] That visitor also noticed an old gate near the top that seemed damaged and had been open for several years.[^3]
