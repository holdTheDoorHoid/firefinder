Center Mountain is in Valley County, Idaho, inside the Payette National Forest, at an elevation of 9,323 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

The earliest use we found was a camp with a fire finder in the 1920s.[^1] A University of Idaho photograph dated 1922 shows a lookout shelter on the mountain and names Fred Gardner as its lookout.[^4] A log cabin followed in 1934, and the Forest Lookouts site dates it to about that year.[^1][^3]

The lookout was destroyed in 1952.[^1][^2][^3] A climbing guide says the site was used as a lookout and then soon abandoned, owing to its remoteness.[^5] That sits uneasily with a cabin built in 1934 and destroyed in 1952, and the guide gives no date for the abandonment.[^1][^3][^5]

The peak lies in the Frank Church–River of No Return Wilderness, east-southeast of Edwardsburg.[^5] The guide describes access by the Cougar Basin Trail and says that, as of 2020, the Center Mountain Trail had mostly vanished.[^5] The three lookout pages we read do not describe what, if anything, remains of the lookout today.[^1][^2][^3] Anyone heading there should plan a remote wilderness hike, not a visit to a standing tower.[^1][^2][^5]
