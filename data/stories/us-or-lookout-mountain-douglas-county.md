Lookout Mountain stood in Douglas County on the Umpqua National Forest, about 30 miles east of Roseburg.[^2]

In 1924 a D-6 cupola cabin, built on the ground, was put up here.[^1][^2] A 30-foot timber tower with an L-4 cab followed in 1949.[^1][^2][^3]

A News-Review item dated November 26, 1936 reported that Harry Willis, a veteran lookout in the North Umpqua district, had been sent back to his station for additional duty.[^3]

The register and firelookout.com agree on the 1966 abandonment and the 1983 end, though one says removed and the other says destroyed.[^1][^2] firelookout.com also dates the destruction of the cupola cabin to 1955.[^2]

A letter tied to a July 5, 1972 inspection says the lookout had been used as an emergency lookout for about four days a season.[^3] It estimated $1,000 to $1,500 in repairs and recommended abandoning the structure and disposing of it.[^3] That letter sits poorly with the 1966 abandonment date, since it describes recent use.[^1][^3]

The register lists the site as removed.[^1] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the public can visit.
