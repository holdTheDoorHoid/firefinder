Poison Rock was a fire lookout on Estel Ridge in Mendocino County, inside the Mendocino National Forest.[^1][^2] Peakbagging gives the site an elevation of 5,559 feet and says the lookout was built in 1935.[^2]

In July 1937 the Ukiah Republican Press reported that a Mr. Long was posted at the lookout.[^1] A September 1938 item from the same paper said a heavy shutter fell while the station was being painted.[^1]

According to a survey datasheet quoted by Peakbagging, the lookout had been abandoned by 1964 and was slated for removal.[^2] A survey benchmark placed in 1942 is about 150 feet west of the foundation, on a rock.[^2]

Peakbagging's photo page reports that only the foundation is left.[^2] Its table marks the entry as a lookout site, a term it uses where the building has been removed or its status is unknown.[^4]

The firelookout.org list marks Poison Rock as gone, with no register number.[^3] That matches Peakbagging's photo page.[^2][^3]

We found no source that describes how to reach the foundation or whether visitors are welcome there, so check with the Mendocino National Forest before you go.
