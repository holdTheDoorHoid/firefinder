Deer Mountain is a steel fire lookout in Alberta.[^1] The provincial register lists the tower at 30 m (98 ft) tall, on a site 1,161 m (3,809 ft) above sea level, on map sheet 83J/14. It gives 2016 as the year the tower was constructed.[^1]

Alberta runs 127 wildfire lookout sites, located for good visibility so fires can be spotted and reported.[^2] Each site has a lookout observer on duty, typically from April to October.[^3]

The register lists Deer Mountain as active. Its access mode is recorded as "drive", and it records no weather station on site.[^1] No source describes the site now beyond that entry, so visitors should not assume the public can climb the tower.[^1]
