The Virginia lookout list at firelookout.org includes a tower called Kelly Knob in Craig County. It gives the site as 37.35592 north, 80.44091 west, and marks the tower as gone.[^1]

The name matches Kelly's Knob, a rocky overlook on the Appalachian Trail in Jefferson National Forest.[^2] A trail guide gives directions along State Route 601, also called Clover Hollow Road, to a trailhead for the day hike.[^2] Along the way, a sign and blue trail markers lead to "the lookout," but the guide does not say whether that is a structure or the overlook itself.[^2] At the top, the guide describes rocky outlooks and a firepit, and does not mention a tower.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains of a tower on the knob today, so it is not known whether any footings survive.[^1] The guide presents the walk as a short outing for families or groups on a national forest trail.[^2]

[^1]: Virginia Lookouts, firelookout.org, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Kelly's Knob via the Appalachian Trail, Craig County, Virginia, The Outbound, https://THEOUTBOUND.COM/virginia/hiking/hike-to-kelly-s-knob-via-the-appalachian-trail (accessed 2026-10-21).
