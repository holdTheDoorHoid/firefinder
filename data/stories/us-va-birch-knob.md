The site sits on the highest summit of Pine Mountain, on the border between Kentucky and Virginia, in Dickenson County.[^1][^3] A 54-foot fire tower stood there and was in operation from 1964 to 1978.[^1] It was dismantled in 1980, but its piers are still visible on the knob.[^1][^3]

In December 2003 the United States Forest Service completed a steel observation deck after four years of construction.[^1][^3] It went up at about the same spot as the old tower.[^1]

Today the deck is reached by 183 steps from the parking area, and the view takes in Kentucky, West Virginia, Virginia, North Carolina and Tennessee.[^1]

The Forest Service lists the site as open from late March to late December, for day use only from sunrise to sunset, with no fee.[^2] A gravel road, Birch Knob Road (FS 616), runs to the tower.[^2]
