The Umpqua National Forest site lies in Douglas County, at 3,362 feet, on the northwest edge of a long summit ridge.[^1]

A September 14, 1933 Roseburg Chieftain item said the Civilian Conservation Corps planned to build a 10-by-10 lookout ahead of the snowfall.[^3] The Oregon page describes the cabin as a 10-by-10 Aladdin L-5 erected on a 10-foot round fir timber tower and anchored by cables.[^3]

The register and firelookout.com both date the tower to 1933 and call it a 10-foot pole L-4 tower, so the sources differ on the design.[^1][^2] The register says it was removed in 1965, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2] The Oregon page says only that it was removed.[^3]

firelookout.org's county list marks the site as gone.[^4] I found no source that describes what remains at the summit today, or whether the public can reach it.
