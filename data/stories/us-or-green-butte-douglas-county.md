Green Butte stands in the Umpqua National Forest, about 19 miles east-southeast of Canyonville in Douglas County.[^2][^1]

In July 1932 the Roseburg News-Review reported plans for nine new lookout houses, one of them on Green Butte between Elk Creek and Cow Creek.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register dates the tower to 1933, when it was a 20-foot wooden L-4 design.[^1][^2] The Oregon Lookouts page dates a set of panorama photographs by William Birchall and Lester Moe to August 14, 1933.[^3]

In 1941 the Forest Service stationed Bob Harris at the lookout, and once the fire season ended he and his new wife moved to Pinehurst.[^3] In July 1945 the News-Review named Sarah Neiderheiser as the lookout for the Green Butte station at Tiller.[^3]

The register says the tower was removed in 1964.[^1] Firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year, so the sources differ on whether it was taken down or lost.[^2] The Oregon Lookouts page lists it as removed, without a date.[^3]

No source in this research describes what, if anything, is left at the site today.[^1][^2][^3] The register includes a 2007 photograph captioned as the Green Butte Lookout site, but this research did not inspect the image, so it cannot say what the site looks like now.[^1] None of the three sources gives access or closure information, so anyone planning a visit should check current access with the Umpqua National Forest first.[^1][^2][^3]
