A November 1945 Index-Journal item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, described a plan for an extensive fire protection system in Greenwood County, South Carolina. The plan included lookout towers more than one hundred feet high, one of them near Kinard's church in the Epworth community.[^1] The sources we read do not confirm when the Epworth tower was finished.

Greenwood County provided both sites for the towers.[^1] The terms said the property would revert to Greenwood County if the towers were ever abandoned.[^1] A magazine article reports that the South Carolina Forestry Commission officially ended its tower network in 1993. The article does not mention Greenwood County or the Epworth tower.[^2] It says the network had become obsolete once planes, satellites and cell phones could be used to report fires.[^2]

In June 1984 a fire warden and his wife lived next to the Epworth Fire Tower, and she was the tower operator.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site marks the tower as removed, but it gives no date for that.[^1] We found no source that describes the Epworth site now, who owns the land, or whether visitors can reach it. Ask the county or the Forestry Commission before going there.
