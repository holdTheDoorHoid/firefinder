Forest Lookouts lists a tower in Calhoun County under the South Carolina Forestry Commission.[^1]

The Forestry Commission's first fire tower went up near Camden in August 1930.[^2] Across the system, when a smoke was spotted, two towers each took a bearing on it, and the fire was placed where the two readings crossed on a map.[^2] The Post and Courier says the network eventually reached more than 130 towers and served 65 years in wildfire detection.[^3]

The agency says it closed its fire tower system on October 1, 1993.[^2] That date covers the whole network, not any one tower.[^2] A Post and Courier history says the agency closed the last of its lookout towers in 1993, and gives 1994 as the year the system was decommissioned.[^3]

No source we read says whether the Calhoun tower still stands, or describes the site today. A Post and Courier article says only 30 of the state's towers remain standing.[^4] Check with the Forestry Commission or the landowner before any visit.
