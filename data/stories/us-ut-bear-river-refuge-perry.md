In August 1934 the refuge reported two new steel observation towers, one at the Perry entrance and one at the Headquarters site.[^1] The Perry Entrance page on Forest Lookouts refers its 1934 and 1935 entries back to the Headquarters site.[^2]

The 1935 entry says the 40-foot tower at Headquarters was dismantled and rebuilt at the North End station.[^1] The Utah Fire Lookouts page by WillhiteWeb identifies that station as the Perry Entrance.[^3] The sources do not explain how a tower at the Perry end in 1934 fits with a tower moved there in 1935.[^1][^3]

Forest Lookouts lists the Perry tower as removed.[^2] Captions on the WillhiteWeb page describe a fallen tower at the Perry Entrance around 1990, and a separate photo of its base is dated July 1985.[^3] The two sites agree the tower is gone but differ on how it ended.[^2][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Utah list also marks the Perry tower as gone.[^4]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the Perry entrance today. The refuge is located in Brigham City, Utah. Its 12-mile auto tour route is open from sunrise to sunset, and its visitor center sits about a quarter mile from Interstate 15 at exit 363.[^5]
