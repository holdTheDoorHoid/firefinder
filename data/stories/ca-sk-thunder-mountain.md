Construction of the Thunder Mountain tower was under way in May 1931.[^1] A report credited to the Baldur Gazette of Manitoba, and quoted on Canada Lookouts, said work was now going ahead on four lookout towers fitted for radio, Thunder Mountain among them.[^1]

In 1951 the original wooden tower was taken down and replaced with steel, according to a Department of Natural Resources annual report for 1951-52, as quoted on the same page.[^1]

The towermen list on websiteservice.ca gives Philip Bird for 1949 to 1970 at Thunder Mountain.[^3] It says George Roberts took over the tower from him, until 1981.[^3] Bill (Zac) Macleod is listed there for 1993 to 2002, after Sam Roberts.[^3] The tower list also names Einer Wicker at Thunder Mountain, with dates unknown and before 1960.[^2] A story about him by Marvin Torwalt, on the same site, says he served as towerman there on several occasions over his working life.[^4]

No source we found describes what remains at the Thunder Mountain site today, or whether the public can reach it.
