Boston Mountain Lookout stood about 17 miles southeast of Elk City, in Idaho County, on the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1][^2] The site is listed at 7,648 feet.[^1]

The first structure was a crow's nest from the 1920s.[^1] In 1931 a 60-foot pole tower with an L-6 design and a log cabin was built.[^1][^2]

The lookout was destroyed in 1958.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the tower as gone.[^3] One page complicates the picture: it says the older crow's nest was still there in the 1980s, yet it also dates the destruction to 1958. It does not say which structure the 1958 loss refers to.[^1]

No source we read says what, if anything, stands on the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under hike-in.[^2]
