The National Geodetic Survey's Paisley Fire Tower station lies about half a mile north of the community of Paisley, in Lake County.[^1] Paisley lies at the southern fringe of the Ocala National Forest.[^2]

A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description from 1968, reproduced on a National Geodetic Survey data sheet, describes the tower as standing on four steel legs, with a metal lookout house at the top. It gives the height as 115 feet (35 m) and says the U.S. Forest Service owned the tower.[^1]

A TopoZone map listing shows a tower feature at the site's coordinates, at an approximate elevation of 108 feet (33 m). That listing carries no date and does not say whether the tower stands today.[^3]

No source I read describes the site today, or says whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^3]
