A lookout structure has stood at the Wisconsin Dells site since 1934, according to a 1958 Wisconsin Conservation Commission bulletin that the National Historic Lookout Register quotes.[^1] That bulletin approved a steel tower costing $6,140 in 1958 to replace a wooden one 24 years old, and the Register gives 1958 as the build year.[^1]

The Register says the tower was crewed on and off during spring fire season until 2015, when Wisconsin shut down its lookout network.[^1] That year the state retired the 72 towers still working.[^5] The sources read say nothing about which fires this tower watched or who worked it.

The DNR has also chosen to retire all 72 towers, is seeking new owners for them, and says unsold towers will be torn down.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists this tower as standing.[^3]

The county is disputed. The Register places the tower in Columbia County, while the Central US Lookouts site and the Association's list say Adams County.[^1][^2][^3] The Association also calls the site Dells (Blue Mound).[^3]

Nothing in the sources says the public may visit or climb the tower, and the land around it is private.[^1] Please leave the tower alone and check with the DNR before planning any trip to the site.
