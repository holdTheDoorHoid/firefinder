Canada Lookouts lists a British Columbia lookout called Pendleton, in the Bulkley-Nechako area, at 54.456 N, 125.355 W.[^1] Beyond the region, its entry gives only coordinates and an elevation.[^1]

A map site, CartoGiraffe, lists a track named Pendleton Lookout in Area B (Broman Lake/Sheraton).[^2] The track is oriented north-south, ends at Augier Road, and has a cul-de-sac at its far end.[^2] The same map lists Pendleton Bay and the Babine Lake Campground among nearby features.[^2]

No source I found describes what remains on the ground at this site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Pendleton, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/pendleton.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Pendleton Lookout, Area B (Broman Lake/Sheraton) map, CartoGiraffe, https://www.cartogiraffe.com/canada/british+columbia/regional+district+of+bulkley-nechako/area+b+%28broman+lake+sheraton%29/pendleton+lookout/ (accessed 2026-10-20).
