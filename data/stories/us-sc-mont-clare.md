Mont Clare was a fire tower in Darlington County, run by the South Carolina Forestry Commission.[^1]

A state report for July 1940 to June 1941 says a side camp put up a complete tower unit at Mont Clare.[^1] The wooden tower that stood there before 1958 was 80 feet tall.[^1] A 1958 Florence Morning News report, quoted by the lookout-history site, says the Civilian Conservation Corps had erected it nearly twenty years earlier.[^1]

The same 1958 newspaper item names Ted Bailey as the Mont Clare towerman, a role he had filled for four years.[^1]

In 1957 and 1958 a steel tower replaced the old wooden one, and the newspaper says the new tower is 100 feet tall.[^1] According to the newspaper, a tractor operator brought the old tower down with a heavy steel cable attached to a diesel-powered unit.[^1] The commission shut its final forest fire lookout in 1993.[^3]

OpenStreetMap maps a point named Mont Clare Lookout Tower and tags its construction as lattice, but the map data does not say whether a tower stands there now.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site today, and we found nothing on whether it is open to visitors.
