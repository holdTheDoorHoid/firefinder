The Wappaweeka tower was built in 1965.[^1] The Department of Natural Resources annual report for 1965-66, as quoted on Canada Lookouts, counts it among three towers that year put up at new sites.[^1] The tower list on websiteservice.ca gives it the name "WAPPAWEEKA (CLARKE)" and a height of 27 m (90 ft), of heavy steel.[^2] The same list also has a separate CLARKE entry, at different coordinates, so the two appear to be separate sites.[^2]

The towermen list gives Jim Settee for 1943 to 1963, with seven towers that included Wappawekka Hills.[^3] His own account says he started as a towerman at Kingsmere in 1943, later relieved George Roberts at Wapaweeka Hills, and finished at Round Hill in 1965.[^4]

No source we found describes what remains at the Wappaweeka site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
