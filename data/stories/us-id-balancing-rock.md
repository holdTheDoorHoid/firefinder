Balancing Rock Lookout is a cabin rather than a tower.[^2] It sits in the Clearwater National Forest in Clearwater County, Idaho, at about 4,116 feet (1,255 meters).[^1][^2]

The L-6 was a small observation cabin on an 8-by-8 wood frame, typically set up as a backup, emergency or patrol site.[^1] This one was built in 1933 on a log crib, and sources describe it as believed to be Idaho's only surviving L-6.[^1][^2][^3]

Few details of its history are on record, and none of the sources names anyone who staffed it.[^2][^3] The cabin was abandoned in the 1940s.[^2][^3][^5]

Volunteers restored it in 2013 and 2014.[^4][^5] Jerry Branning, a Forest Fire Lookout Association member, managed the project.[^3][^4]

The Idaho site describes it as the sole standing L-6 in the state and lists it as a hike-in site.[^3] Its listing also asks visitors to keep the location quiet, so this story leaves out directions. The sources I could read do not describe current access or permit rules, so check with the Clearwater National Forest before going.
