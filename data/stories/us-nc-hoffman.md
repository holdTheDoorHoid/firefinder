A lookout-history site lists a tower at Hoffman, in Richmond County. It says the U.S. Resettlement Administration put up a 100-foot steel tower there between 1936 and 1938, and that the tower was slated to pass to the state at some later point.[^1][^3]

The Resettlement Administration was a federal body of the New Deal, set up in 1935. The Farm Security Administration took over from it in September 1937.[^2]

The entry credits the state's Department of Conservation and Development and its 7th biennial report of 1938.[^1]

No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at the Hoffman site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^3]
