The Wareham tower was a 40-foot wooden lookout, and it was fully enclosed.[^1] The register says it was the only one of its kind in the state.[^1] It was first operated in 1930, and the local fire company ran it.[^1] The site is on Tempest Knob in Plymouth County, and the register numbers it US 28, MA 28.[^1] It was entered in the register on September 3, 2008.[^1] A Massachusetts fire tower pin list also names Tempest Knob for the Wareham tower.[^2]

The register does not describe what, if anything, remains on Tempest Knob today.[^1] We found no source that says whether the public may visit the site.[^1][^2]
