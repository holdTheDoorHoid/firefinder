The Squaw Valley Lookout site is about 21 miles north of Newport, in Pend Oreille County, Washington.[^1] Firelookout.com lists the Kaniksu National Forest as its agency and gives the site an elevation of 2,920 feet.[^1]

Both sources describe an 80-foot crow's nest from the 1930s.[^1][^2] It was abandoned in 1940.[^1][^2] Both sources say it was still there in the 1980s, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association index lists its status as abandoned.[^1][^2][^3]

No source says whether the crow's nest still stands. The map location comes from an inventory rather than from the author's own knowledge, and the author has not yet visited the site.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
