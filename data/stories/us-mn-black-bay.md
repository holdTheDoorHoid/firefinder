The Black Bay lookout stands near the eastern end of Minnesota Highway 11, about 11 miles east of International Falls, on a narrow strip of land between Rainy Lake and Black Bay.[^2][^3] The Civilian Conservation Corps built it in 1939 as a 100-foot stairway tower.[^1][^3] The county inventory adds that the tower is also known as the Rainy Lake lookout.[^3]

The sources do not agree on the height. The register and the inventory both give 100 feet.[^1][^3] A 1967 survey note, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page and in the inventory, describes a steel tower with an observation cabin and an overall height of 90 feet.[^2][^3]

Ownership is also unclear on paper. The register lists the Minnesota Department of Transportation as administrator.[^1] The inventory's owner column reads Mn MFS, the Minnesota Forest Service, which it says is now the DNR's Forestry Division.[^3] I found no source that gives the date the tower or its land changed hands.[^1][^3]

In 2004 a station recovery note found the tower inside a fenced area topped with barbed wire.[^2] The register says MnDOT once planned a site improvement to open the tower to the public in 2008, and that, as of the register entry, the plan had not been carried out.[^1]

The register entry is dated August 1, 2019. The Forest Fire Lookout Association still lists the tower as standing.[^1][^4]

I found no current source on access, and nothing I read shows the tower is open to visitors. Treat it as closed to the public unless MnDOT or the DNR says otherwise.[^1][^2]
