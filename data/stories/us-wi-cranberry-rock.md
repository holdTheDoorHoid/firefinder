Cranberry Rock Lookout is a 100-foot tower in Juneau County, Wisconsin, at about 1,037 feet of elevation.[^1] The register describes a Forest Service-style tower with an inside corner ladder, made by Bethlehem Steel, and dates it to 1932.[^1]

In December 1931 The Daily Tribune reported that four watch towers in the 11th district of the Wisconsin conservation commission's forest fire protection branch were nearing completion, and it named Cranberry Rock as one of the sites.[^2] By 1968 the Conservation Department's Nekoosa district had a watch tower on Cranberry Rock. That May, the Nekoosa council let its civil defense director ask the district for permission to use the tower during severe weather warnings.[^2] A 1977 newspaper notice sought someone for tower duty at Cranberry Rock, with inquiries to the Nekoosa ranger station.[^2]

In December 2015 the DNR announced that all 72 of its fire towers would close in the spring.[^3] I found no source that names Cranberry Rock among them, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list still shows the tower as standing.[^4]

The register recorded the tower on August 16, 2017.[^1] The sources I found describe no public access, rental or visitor program. The DNR has said its towers are no longer structurally safe for personnel, so I would view this one from the ground.[^3]
