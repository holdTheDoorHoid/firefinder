The Long Mountain tower site lies in Campbell County, Virginia.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says a lookout tower was built there about 1934, and that the Virginia Department of Game and Inland Fisheries administered it. The register gives the site's elevation as 1,406 feet (429 m).[^1]

The register says the tower stayed in service through at least 1972, after which it was converted into a communications tower used by private and state communications systems.[^1] The register's removal note reads "after 1972," but it does not say what was taken down.[^1] We found no source that names the tower's staff or describes any fire it watched.

The site was registered on March 24, 2020, on the nomination of John McDonald.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list, which calls the site Long Mountain (Rustburg), marks it as gone.[^2] The same list places a standing tower called Rhudy, in Craig County, under relocated lookouts, and names Long Mountain as its origin.[^2] The association gives no date and no details for that move, so treat it as its claim alone.

We found no source describing what, if anything, remains at the Campbell County site, or whether the land is open to visitors. The Craig County tower's access is not described in the sources we read, so ask whoever manages it before you go.

[^2]: Virginia Fire Lookout Towers, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
