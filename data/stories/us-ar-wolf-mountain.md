Wolf Mountain Lookout is a lookout tower in Miller County, Arkansas, listed on the National Historic Lookout Register as US 1796, AR 27.[^1] The register added it on January 2, 2026, and that is the only firm date we found for the tower.[^1]

We could not find when the tower was built, what it was made of, or who staffed it. The register entry gives the county and a site elevation of 457 feet (139 meters), and little else.[^1] The Central US Lookouts website has a page for Wolf Mountain that, in the text we could read, gives only the name and the county.[^2]

Our search did not turn up any account of notable fires, staffing, or later changes to the structure. The newest image we found is a photograph from December 2024, credited to Austin Ardwin on the register page.[^1] That suggests the tower was still standing then, but we could not confirm its present condition, whether it is open to the public, or who manages it. Anyone interested should not climb or enter a tower without the owner's permission.
