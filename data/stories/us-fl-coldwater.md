The Eastern US Lookouts site lists a Coldwater lookout in Santa Rosa County, Florida.[^1] A map page based on OpenStreetMap data calls it the Coldwater Lookout Tower and places it at about 30.736 degrees north and 86.950 degrees west, recorded as a tower.[^2] That page also places the community of Allentown six miles west of the tower.[^2] No source I could read describes what stands at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Coldwater - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/coldwater.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Coldwater Lookout Tower Map, Mapcarta, https://mapcarta.com/20648686 (accessed 2026-10-21).
