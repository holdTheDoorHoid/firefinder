Cahaba Lookout Tower stands in Perry County, Alabama, on the Talladega National Forest.[^1][^2] The U.S. Forest Service administers it, and the register lists the Oakmulgee Ranger District as a cooperator.[^1] It is a 100-foot Aermotor MC-39 tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab, and Thomas Kaufmann's article calls that model the most common steel tower in Alabama's forests.[^1][^3]

Sources disagree on the build year. The register gives 1936, yet its own description says the Forest Service built the tower in 1935.[^1]

The register was entered on June 23, 2009, with Thomas Kaufmann as nominator.[^1] The sources read do not say who staffed the tower, when it stopped serving as a lookout, or whether the cab is open to visitors. Across Alabama, Kaufmann writes, many surviving towers are in poor shape and dangerous, and climbing them is against the law.[^3] Check with the Talladega National Forest before any visit, and do not climb a tower that is closed.
