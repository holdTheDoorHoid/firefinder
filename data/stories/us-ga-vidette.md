Vidette is a small city in western Burke County, Georgia, where State Routes 24 and 305 meet.[^2] It had 103 residents in 2020 and belongs to the Augusta metro area.[^2]

A fire tower under the name Vidette is on the Georgia lookout list kept on the tnlandforms.us site.[^1] The list puts it in Burke County at 33.05416, -82.16251. Its elevation is given as 331 with no unit; read as feet, that is 101 m.[^1] Its only tag is "datasheet", with no survey number.[^1] The list says its towers were found on USGS topographic maps and in National Geodetic Survey datasheets.[^1] The tower's listed point lies about 8 km (5 mi) east of the coordinates Wikipedia gives for the city.[^1][^2]

The list page was last revised in March 2016.[^1]

No other source we found describes this tower, what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
