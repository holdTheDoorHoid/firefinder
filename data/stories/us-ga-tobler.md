Tom Dunigan's index of Georgia fire towers lists a tower called Tobler in Upson County, at 32.79321, -84.25082.[^1] Tobler is entry 182 in the index, and the index says names for entries past 72 are its author's own creation, so the name is the author's label.[^1]

Roadside Thoughts, a gazetteer site, reports that a post office named Tobler operated from 1885 until 1905, somewhere in Upson County.[^2] Its sources do not say where the post office stood.[^2] Nothing here ties that post office to the tower.

No source I found describes what remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Tobler (Upson County, GA), Roadside Thoughts, https://roadsidethoughts.com/ga/tobler-xx-upson-profile.htm (accessed 2026-10-21).
