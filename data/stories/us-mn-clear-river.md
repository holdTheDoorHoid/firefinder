The Clear River fire tower is in Roseau County, Minnesota, on Beltrami Island inside Beltrami State Forest.[^1] A survey station description reproduced on the Forest Lookouts site places it 12 miles due south of Warroad.[^1] The same page credits the Minnesota Forest Service and dates the tower to 1934, describing a steel tower 100 feet high with ladder access.[^1] That height is about 30.5 meters.[^1]

The pages I read do not say why the tower was placed there, or who did the construction work.[^1] They also say nothing about who staffed it or about any notable fires.[^1]

The directory of Minnesota lookouts still lists the tower as standing.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page says something different. Its survey note says the tower will soon come down and be replaced on or close to the same site. The note gives no date, and I found no later record showing whether that happened.[^1] The two sources do not settle what stands today.

I found no access rules or climbing information for this tower. It sits on state forest land, so check with the land managers before visiting, and do not climb a tower that is closed or off limits.[^1]

[^1]: Clear River, Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/clear-river.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Minnesota Lookouts, Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
