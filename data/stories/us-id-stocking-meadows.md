Stocking Meadows Lookout stood in Clearwater County, Idaho, about 17 miles east-southeast of Clarkia, at an elevation of 4,590 feet.[^1] The Clearwater Timber Protective Association is listed as its agency.[^2]

The earliest date we found is 1913.[^2] That year an association annual report gave a cost of $143.95 for four and a half miles of work running to the place "known as Stocking Meadows Lookout."[^2] Two lookout websites date the first structure to 1914, a crow's nest with a log cabin, so the sources do not agree on when the lookout began.[^1][^2][^3]

Forest Lookouts lists a 45-foot platform in 1920.[^2] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts date a 50-foot tower with an L-4 cab to 1934, while Forest Lookouts says it went up in the 1930s with Civilian Conservation Corps labor.[^1][^2][^3]

The only staff entry we found is for 1925, and it names only Brad, in a photo from the Forest Fire Lookout Museum collection.[^3]

Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both say the tower was destroyed in 1972.[^1][^3] Forest Lookouts lists it as "Removed," so the sources do not agree on how it ended.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list also marks it as gone.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site.[^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page tags it "Drive-Up," but it gives no current condition for the site.[^3]
