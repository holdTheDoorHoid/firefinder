Norton Hill is in Lincoln County, Oregon, and the Oregon Department of Forestry ran the lookout.[^2][^3]

The first lookout there went up in 1939.[^3] It was a platform on 20-foot fir poles with a tent floor, and its total cost was $95.[^3] A Lincoln County fire patrol report for 1942 describes a cabin, 18 by 20 feet, built on the hill.[^3]

The tower itself is dated two ways.[^1][^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says a 54-foot steel tower with a 7 by 7 cab was built in 1942.[^1] Rex's Fire Lookout Page quotes a 1954 entry saying the lookout was under construction late that season, and a separate undated entry gives the cost of a 54-foot steel tower.[^3] The sources do not agree, and we could not settle the date.

Rex's page records the lookout as activated on September 10, 1942, with the Eugene Filter Center listed beside that date.[^3]

Its later history is disputed too. The register says the tower was moved to Euchre Lookout in 1965.[^1] Rex's page says the lookout was dismantled and salvaged in 1967.[^3] The Euchre Mountain page does record a tower built there in 1965 for $1067.58, but it does not mention Norton Hill.[^4] A January 1971 newspaper item, quoted on the same Euchre page, says high winds apparently overpowered the tower atop Euchre Mountain.[^4] We found no source saying whether any part of the Norton Hill tower still stands, on Euchre Mountain or anywhere else.[^1][^4]

The register says the lookout "is now gone."[^1] No source we read describes what remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register listed the site on November 11, 2010, as US 724, OR 135.[^1]
