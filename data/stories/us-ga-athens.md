Georgia's lookout list places a tower named Athens in Clarke County, at 33.92556, -83.37028, at 729 feet (222.2 m) above sea level.[^1] A directory of Georgia forest lookouts also lists an Athens tower in Clarke County, with no other detail.[^2]

The same list has a second entry in that county, named uga. It sits about 3 km south of the Athens point, at 33.89688, -83.36460, and its elevation is 723 feet.[^1] The two entries may describe one tower or two.[^1]

The list's map was revised on 22 March 2016.[^1]

No source found describes the site today, so what remains there and whether the public can visit are unknown.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Georgia - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/georgia.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
