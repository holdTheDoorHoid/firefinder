Bone Spring is in Wallowa County, Oregon, on the Umatilla National Forest, about 20 miles east of Milton-Freewater.[^1][^2] The site sits at 5,867 feet (1,788 m).[^1]

The register dates the first use of the site to 1930, when a cabin was built, and gives 1932 for a 48-foot pole tower.[^1] A 1931 newspaper item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, describes a tower about 70 feet high under construction at the Bone Springs ranger station, with a cabin on top.[^3] That height does not match the register's 48-foot tower, and the sources do not reconcile the two.[^1][^3]

In 1949 the register says an 87-foot L-4 tower of treated timber took the pole tower's place.[^1] A Walla Walla Union-Bulletin item from July 31, 1949 describes the new towers as "over 80 feet" and says the old tower was condemned and then pulled down.[^3]

In August 1953 the same paper reported two Forest Service workers serving as lookouts at Bone Springs.[^3] In May 1973 the site was reduced to stand-by status.[^3]

The register lists the site as US 1110, OR 466, registered in July 2011.[^1] The register says the tower was gone by 1987, and firelookout.com dates the second tower to 1949 to 1987.[^1][^2]

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^3]
