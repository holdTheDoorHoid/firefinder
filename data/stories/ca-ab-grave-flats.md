Grave Flats is a lookout site in the Edson Forest of Alberta.[^1] A telephone spur line 5.6 km (3.5 mi) long was built to the Grave Flats lookout (L.O.), to end a loop in the Brazeau Forestry telephone system.[^1] The entry is headed 1945 and cites the Department of Lands and Mines annual report for the year ending 31 March 1946.[^1]

The Alberta lookout layer gives 1974 as the year of construction for the building listed at the site.[^2] The layer records it as a cabin, not a tower, at 5.0 m (16 ft) tall and 2,076 m (6,811 ft) above sea level.[^2]

The layer lists the site as active, with a weather station, and gives its access mode as drive.[^2] OpenStreetMap, a volunteer-edited map, tags the access as private.[^3] No source read describes the site's condition today, or states a rule on public visits.
