The Thomasville Lookout stands in Clarke County, Alabama, and the Alabama Forestry Commission administers it.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register entered the tower on May 22, 2022, on a nomination by Jackson E. Knight.[^1] The register gives its elevation as 377 feet, or 115 meters.[^1]

The register has little to say about this tower.[^1] I found no record of when it was built, who staffed it, or which fires it watched.

The blog Jackson's Ramblings places the lookout beside Al 5, north of Thomasville, and labels it Al 146.[^3] The register gives AL 78 for the same tower, so this account follows the register, under the national number US 1594.[^1]

The register says the tower sits behind a gate with no-trespassing notices.[^1] I found no later report on its condition and no arrangement for visitors. Do not go past that gate, and do not climb the tower on the strength of this account.[^1]
