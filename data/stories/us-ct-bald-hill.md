Bald Hill, in the town of Union in Tolland County, Connecticut, held a fire tower.[^1][^2] The register gives the steel tower a height of 60 feet and says it began operating in 1913.[^1] The site sits at 1,286 feet, or 392 meters, according to the register.[^1]

The register was filed on September 9, 2008, on the nomination of Henry Isenberg. It lists the place under the name Bald Hill Tower Site.[^1]

The entry appears in the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, but it gives no removal date or condition.[^1] No source we read says what remains on the hill today, or whether visitors can reach it.

[^2]: Bald Hill, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/bald-hill.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
