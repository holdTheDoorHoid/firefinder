Two Oregon lookout sites share the name High Rock, so this story covers only the Linn County site.[^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places it at 44.68452, -122.43446.[^3] Firelookout.com and the Oregon list both put it in the Willamette National Forest.[^1][^2]

Firelookout.com gives the lookout's date only as the 1930s and says it was abandoned.[^1] According to the Oregon list on Weebly, Cooper & Arnst made panorama photographs at the site on June 10, 1934.[^2]

The Oregon list marks the lookout as removed but gives no date.[^2] The Association's list marks the site as gone.[^3] The same list also includes a separate High Rock in Clackamas County, in the Mount Hood region, which is not covered here.[^3][^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Linn County site today, and none says whether the public can reach it.
