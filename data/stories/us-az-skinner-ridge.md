The Skinner Ridge lookout stood on the Kaibab National Forest in Coconino County, Arizona.[^1][^2]

The tower was a steel Aermotor MC-39 model.[^1] The 2020 New Mexico year-end report gives its height as 59 feet, as it stands at Encino.[^1] The report gives the erection year as 1929 or 1933.[^1] It was still standing on the ridge in 1941, according to a document by Barbara Zinn that the report cites.[^1]

The tower did not stay on the ridge.[^1] The Santa Fe National Forest obtained it in 1948, and it was erected at Encino, New Mexico, in July 1950.[^1][^3] Encino is on the Coyote Ranger District of the Santa Fe National Forest.[^1] As of 2020, Encino was the district's only lookout and had staff on duty.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Skinner Ridge site as gone.[^2] The Forest Service signed a decision for a wireless tower project in July 2016, and that project includes a communications tower on top of Skinner Ridge.[^4] The project page lists it as completed, but it does not say whether the ridge tower was built.[^4] A separate 2013 proposal for a 125-foot communications tower on the same ridge was later cancelled.[^5]
