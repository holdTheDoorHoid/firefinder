Nasukoin Mountain rises in the Flathead National Forest, about 13 miles west of Polebridge in Flathead County.[^1] Its elevation is 8,086 feet.[^1] The site is recorded as a camp in 1929, and an L-4 cab followed in 1931.[^1]

The cabin was abandoned in 1956, and the lookout website lists the site as gone.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Montana lookouts also marks it Gone, as a ground-level structure.[^2]

Today a National Forest trail runs from the Red Meadow Creek roads 115 and 589 to Nasukoin Peak, a 5.8-mile route rated high difficulty.[^3] None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, remains of the cabin site.
