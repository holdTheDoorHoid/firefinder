The Canaan Mountain lookout stood on a rocky outcrop southwest of Davis, in Tucker County.[^2][^3] It was the first fire lookout the Monongahela National Forest built, and it went up in 1924.[^2] Logging had stripped the mountain by then, which left the site with a wide view.[^2][^3]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites entry calls it a fire tower, but the Forest Service and the Mon Forest Towns guide both describe a cabin rather than a steel tower.[^1][^2][^3] Built on a large rock, it was a wood-framed cabin where the lookout lived, with a porch running around its sides.[^2][^3]

The cabin stayed in service for nearly two decades.[^3] In 1943 the Forest Service built Bearden Knob Tower two miles away to replace it, and the lookout was no longer needed for fire detection.[^2][^3][^4] The cabin was dismantled in 1946.[^1][^2][^3]

Today the site is a rocky outcrop along Canaan Loop Road, just outside Davis.[^3] A small loop pulloff lets drivers stop beside the rock, which sits right at the edge of the road.[^3] There is no formal trail, though the spot links up with Fire Trail 2.[^3] Regrowth now mostly blocks the view from the rock, and according to the guide, the outcrop is all that survives.[^3] A nearby Forest Service sign cautions visitors about wildfire risk.[^3]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites entry has photos of footings taken in September 2008, while the 2026 guide describes only the outcrop, so the two accounts differ on what is left.[^1][^3]
