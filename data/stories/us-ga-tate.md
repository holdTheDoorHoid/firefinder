The Tate Lookout Tower is named on a list of Georgia lookout towers published at tnlandforms.us. The list places it in Pickens County, at 34.40843, -84.35437.[^1] The page says the list was prompted by a gap: the FFLA website listed no Georgia towers in 2010.[^1] It also credits the Georgia Forestry Commission with coordinates for 118 towers, and says its map was revised on 22 March 2016.[^1]

No source describes the site today. Nothing in the sources says whether a tower still stands there, or whether the public can reach the spot. Visitors should not assume the place is open or that a tower can be seen, and should ask the local county or the Georgia Forestry Commission before making a trip.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
