Elk Summit in Idaho County was a lookout site about 13 miles south-southeast of the Powell Ranger Station.[^1][^3] The Clearwater National Forest list records a cupola cabin there, dated to 1924.[^1][^2][^3] That list was compiled in June 2016 from Forest Service records and a book by Ray Kresek.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts also tags the site as a hike-in.[^2] The Clearwater list gives no elevation and no photo for it.[^3]

Every list checked marks the cabin as gone.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The state list places the site at 46.32866 north, 114.66782 west.[^4] No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today.[^1][^2][^3]

Do not mix this site up with a second Elk Summit on the state list. That one sits at different coordinates and is recorded as a standing tower.[^4]
