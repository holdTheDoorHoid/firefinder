Stuart Lake's fire tower dates from the late 1950s.[^1] A Department of Natural Resources annual report for the year ending 31 March 1958, as quoted on Canada Lookouts, records that a steel tower and a towerman's cabin were put up at Stuart Lake.[^1] The tower list on websiteservice.ca places the lookout at 54 degrees 13 minutes 47.2 seconds north, 104 degrees 53 minutes 25.9 seconds west, and gives its height as 24 m (80 ft), of heavy steel.[^3]

The towermen list on websiteservice.ca names Sandra Muench as the Stuart Lake towerman for 1998 to 2002.[^2] Its entry then notes that the tower "went to aerial detection".[^2]

No source we found describes what remains at the Stuart Lake site today, so whether a tower still stands there is unknown.
