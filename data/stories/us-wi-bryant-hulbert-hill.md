In July 1928 the Stevens Point Daily Journal reported that erection had started on a second state fire observation tower in Langlade County, on Hulbert Hill about two and a half miles north of Bryant.[^2] The report gave a planned height of 73 feet (22 m) and said the site would offer a broad view over the middle of the county.[^2] In 1928 the county's first state tower stood on Nine Mile Hill, toward its east end.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's inventory of Wisconsin lookouts lists the site as a tower under the name Bryant (Hulbert Hill), and marks it as gone.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains on Hulbert Hill today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
