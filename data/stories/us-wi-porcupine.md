The Porcupine Fire Tower stood in Oneida County.[^1] A map of the Cassian area, drawn from a 1939 field examination, shows it near Crescent Flats, in the southeast part of the sheet.[^2]

In a Wisconsin Public Radio report, DNR officials said the state would no longer use its 72 fire lookout towers, most of them built in the 1930s. Instead, they planned to watch for fires more often from airplanes. One official estimated that replacing the state's towers would probably cost about $25 million.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list shows the tower as gone, with its point at 45.57069 north and 89.52891 west.[^4] No source read describes the site today, including whether anything remains there or whether the public may visit.
