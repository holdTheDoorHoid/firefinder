The Jenckes Hill tower stood in Lincoln, in Providence County, at about 430 feet (131 meters).[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes it as a 45-foot steel tower with a 16-by-16-foot cab, first operated in 1936.[^1] The register says a survey it names the National Geographic Survey called the tower the "Lonsdale" tower.[^1] It also says the flat roof was painted blue.[^1]

The register gives the tower's removal as around 1941, according to state records.[^1] A 1968 USGS survey note, however, records the landowner saying the tower would be torn down within two years, which implies it was still standing that year.[^1] The register itself calls the two accounts in direct conflict.[^1]

The register describes the site as lying in the middle of a new neighborhood, roughly between Countryside and Morgan roads.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association marks the Jenckes Hill tower as gone.[^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the ground, or whether the land is open to the public.[^1][^2]
