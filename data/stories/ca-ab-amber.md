Alberta's wildfire register dates the Amber tower to 2013.[^1] It is a steel tower, 30 m (98 ft) tall, standing at 611.0 m (2,005 ft) above sea level.[^1] The register lists it as active, with a weather station on site.[^1]

Across the province, Alberta says each lookout site is staffed by a lookout observer, generally from April to October.[^2]

The register codes the tower's access as "fly". No source here explains how that works on the ground, and none describes public visits, so treat the Amber tower as a working fire-watch site rather than a place to visit.[^1]
