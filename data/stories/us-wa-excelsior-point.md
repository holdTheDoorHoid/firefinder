Excelsior Point is a bump on Damfino Ridge in Whatcom County, Washington, inside the Mt. Baker-Snoqualmie National Forest.[^1][^3][^4] A fire lookout, an L-5 cab, was built on the point in 1935.[^1][^2][^4] Panoramic photographs were taken from its roof on September 29, 1935.[^3][^4] The panoramas are credited to the National Archives' Pacific Northwest region.[^1]

The pages we read do not name anyone who staffed the lookout or any fire it watched.

The lookout was abandoned in 1968.[^1][^2][^4] The register now says the site is gone, and the Forest Lookouts list marks it as removed.[^1][^3]

WillhiteWeb says that little if anything exists at the site today.[^4] The same page adds that the 1935 panoramas still match the view today.[^4]

You reach the point on foot. WillhiteWeb says the trailhead beside the Mt. Baker Highway is plowed, so the point can be reached year-round.[^4] To find the trailhead, continue past Glacier on the Mt. Baker Highway. It is on your left, a short way after the Nooksack Falls signs.[^4] From there, follow the trail to the ridge.[^4] The sources we read name no closure.
