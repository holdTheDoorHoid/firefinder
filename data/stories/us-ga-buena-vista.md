Buena Vista is listed as a fire tower site in Chattahoochee County, Georgia.[^1] A statewide list of Georgia towers places it at 32.422272, -84.716886 and numbers it 409. The list gives its elevation as 523 with no unit; read as feet, that is about 159 m above sea level.[^1] The same list says that names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own creation, so "buena vista" is a label from that list.[^1] Its entry carries the note "aerial imagery."[^1]

A Forestry Commission district supervisor told a newspaper in 2006 that the state's fire tower count had reached a high of over 200 in the 1960s.[^2] That same year a commission spreadsheet counted 137 of the state's 144 surviving towers as unstaffed.[^2] At that point the towers were being auctioned, with some going for scrap and others to hunting clubs or similar groups.[^2]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public.
