Stahl Peak, also called Stahl Mountain, is a lookout site at 3,716 feet in Lewis County, Washington.[^1][^2][^3]

An L-4 cab was built on the site in 1933.[^1][^2][^3] A February 1934 item reports that a CCC crew under Foreman McCray completed a telephone line to Stahl Mountain on January 15.[^3][^4] Panorama photos were taken from the site on July 26, 1934.[^4]

The sources do not agree on the national forest. The register lists the Mount Baker-Snoqualmie National Forests, firelookout.com says Gifford Pinchot, and the Forest Lookouts list says Snoqualmie.[^1][^2][^4]

The cab was gone by 1956.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts list marks the lookout simply as removed.[^4]

WillhiteWeb's photos show the site of the former lookout cab, with two anchor bolts and a third anchor at the summit.[^3] The register includes a September 2001 photo captioned "Remnant September 2001."[^1]

From Highway 7, take the turnoff for FR 74, then follow FR 7409 for seven miles.[^3] WillhiteWeb calls the road uneven at times and says most vehicles can manage it carefully when conditions are dry.[^3] The trail runs 0.75 miles one way, has no marker, and can be tough to locate at the start.[^3]
