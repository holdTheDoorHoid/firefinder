The Georgia lookout list places a tower named Hilyer in Troup County, at 804 feet (245 m).[^1] The list gives its position as 32.99 degrees north and 85.00 degrees west.[^1] The list says its names for towers numbered 73 and above are its own, and this one is number 146, so the name is the list's label.[^1]

A genealogy guide places Hilyer in Troup County.[^2]

No source describes the site today, so whether a tower or footing remains, and whether the land is open to the public, is not known.[^1]
