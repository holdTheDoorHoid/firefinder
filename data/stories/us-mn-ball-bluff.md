The Ball Bluff Lookout Tower stands in Aitkin County, Minnesota, inside the Savanna State Forest.[^3][^4] Sources disagree about how the first tower came to be, and we cannot tell which tower stands today.

The Forest Lookouts site credits the Civilian Conservation Corps with putting up a 100-foot International Derrick tower, fitted with an inside ladder, in 1934.[^1] A local history of the Ball Bluff area tells it differently.[^3] In that account the state built a fire tower on Ball Bluff hill in 1934, and it stood 60 feet tall.[^3] In 1936 a replacement was built and the first tower was removed.[^3]

A 1980 survey description by the Minnesota Department of Transportation calls the tower a steel structure on four legs, 100 feet high, with a metal room for observers at the top.[^1] Wikidata gives the same 100-foot height.[^4] We have used that height for the standing tower.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^2] We found no record of who staffed it or of any fire it watched. The sources we read do not describe public access, climbing rules, or rental, so we cannot say whether visitors may go up. Check with the land manager before going near it, and do not climb the tower unless it is open to the public.
