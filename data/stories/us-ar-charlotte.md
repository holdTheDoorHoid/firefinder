Charlotte was a fire lookout in Independence County, Arkansas. The Forest Lookouts website lists a lookout under that name in the county.[^1]

The tower no longer stands at Charlotte. The National Historic Lookout Register says the tower was moved from Charlotte to the Bar ōf Ranch, outside Clinton, Arkansas.[^2] The register describes it as a 100-foot MC-39 tower, which is about 30.5 m.[^2]

The register says the ranch is best known for its chuckwagon races, which draw roughly 20,000 people each year.[^2] It also places the tower on private property and does not describe any public access.[^2] Ask the owner before any visit, and do not climb the tower without permission.

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the original Charlotte site today.

[^1]: Charlotte, Forest Lookouts (Arkansas), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/charlotte.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Bar ōf Ranch Tower (Relocated), National Historic Lookout Register (nhlr.org), http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ar/bar-%C5%8Df-ranch-tower-relocated/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
