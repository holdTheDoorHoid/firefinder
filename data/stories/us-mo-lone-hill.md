The Central US Lookouts page lists Lone Hill as a Missouri Conservation Commission lookout in Butler County. It gives the survey location as 24N-5E-18.[^1]

A steel tower 100 feet (30.5 m) high went up at Lone Hill in 1951.[^1]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today.

[^1]: Lone Hill, Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/lone-hill.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
