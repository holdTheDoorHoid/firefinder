The Sand Springs tower stood in Preston County, West Virginia.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives its elevation as 2,560 feet (780 meters) and says the State of West Virginia administered it.[^1] The register added the site on September 16, 2008, on the nomination of Henry Isenberg, and lists it as US 168, WV 18.[^1]

The register page carries a 1942 postcard credited to Ron Kemnow.[^1]

The register says the tower was taken down.[^1] A caption credits Brian Powell with a September 2008 photo showing a foundation at the site.[^1] West Virginia closed its forest fire towers in 1990, after decades in use.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list also marks the Preston County site as gone.[^3]

No source we found describes the site today or says whether it is open to the public. The register gives its coordinates as N 39.695288°, W 79.762753°.[^1]
