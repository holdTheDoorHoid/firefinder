Red Hill Lookout stood on a 7,751-foot mountaintop in the Kaibab National Forest, Coconino County, Arizona.[^1] Before the 1958 cab, the register says, a wooden tower was used for detection there in the 1920s.[^1]

In 1958 the lookout was replaced with a metal Cl-100 cab, 14 feet square.[^1] The cab sat on a 10-foot concrete block base and had a flat corrugated roof.[^1] The register notes that it was often used early in the season, when snow kept people from reaching other lookouts.[^1] It was added to the register on March 15, 2006, nominated by Dave Lorenz.[^1]

The Williams Ranger District began scoping a project to demolish Red Hill and Round Mountain in December 2019.[^2] Its project page says both towers were targeted for removal over safety concerns about their buildings.[^2] A decision was signed on March 5, 2020.[^2] The page now lists the project as completed.[^2] Its only implementation date is a May 2020 estimate, and it does not say when the towers came down.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list shows the tower as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains on the mountaintop today, or how a visitor would reach it.
