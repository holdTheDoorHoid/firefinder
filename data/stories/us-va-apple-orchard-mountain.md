The Apple Orchard Mountain tower stood in Bedford County, Virginia.[^1][^2] The mountain is the highest point in each of Bedford and Botetourt counties, and the highest spot on Virginia's stretch of the Blue Ridge Parkway.[^4]

A lookout worked the tower in 1933.[^2] An August 1933 newspaper item, quoted from the Idaho Evening Times on the Forest Lookouts page, tells how the lookout, William Dawson, treated a boy bitten by a rattlesnake. The boy was on his way to the tower.[^2] Dawson put on a tourniquet and phoned a second camp asking for a suction pump.[^2]

The Forest Service administered the tower, and the register says it was removed in 1957-1958.[^1] The register also says the tower was relocated to Purgatory Mountain in neighboring Botetourt County at that time.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list marks the Purgatory Mountain entry as gone, so the moved tower is not listed as standing there.[^3]

Wikipedia describes the summit as open and mentions an FAA radar nearby.[^4] None of the sources read describe what, if anything, remains of the lookout on the summit. Check with the national forest before going.
