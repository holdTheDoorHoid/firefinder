Tower Mountain Lookout stands in the Umatilla National Forest, about 18 miles east-southeast of Ukiah in Umatilla County, Oregon.[^2] The site was originally called Lookout Mountain, but it was renamed Tower Mountain in 1925 to avoid mix-ups with a nearby Lookout Mountain.[^2]

A 60-foot pole tower with a 6-by-6-foot cab, dated to 1914, was the site's first structure.[^2] FireLookout.com says a 92-foot Aermotor steel tower replaced it in 1934.[^2] The National Historic Lookout Register instead says the Forest Service built the 92-foot tower in 1929.[^1] The two dates conflict, and I did not find a Forest Service record that settles them.

The register says a CCC-built cabin came to the site from the old Lucky Strike Lookout in the late 1940s.[^1] FireLookout.com says the original ground cabin burned in the late 1940s and was replaced by the Lucky Strike cabin.[^2]

The register lists the lookout as registered on December 5, 1995, as US 142, OR 20.[^1] It names the U.S. Forest Service as administrator, with the North Fork John Day Ranger District as cooperator.[^1]

FireLookout.com says the lookout gets staff each summer, but its page gives no date for that.[^2] The register says the tower remains in service, and one visit report there describes the site as generally in good shape.[^1] The sources I read do not give public visiting rules, so check with the Forest Service before you go, and do not climb the tower without permission.
