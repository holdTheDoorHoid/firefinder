The Forest Lookouts site lists a Pierce tower in Polk County, Florida.[^1] The only dated fact its entry gives is a 2011 auction that brought $505.[^1]

The sale fits a wider pattern. A 2010 WFTV report, carried by Firehouse, said Florida was selling off its manned fire towers. It put the age of some of them at about 70 years, and said upkeep could run to tens of thousands of dollars a year.[^2] The report gave one example: a recently dismantled tower at Leesburg, which a Georgia man won at auction for about $600 and then paid to take down.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains of the Pierce site today, or whether the public may visit it.

[^2]: Fla. Fire Towers Being Sold Off, Firehouse (report by WFTV, 2010), https://www.firehouse.com/community-risk/news/10465752/fla-fire-towers-being-sold-off (accessed 2026-10-21).
