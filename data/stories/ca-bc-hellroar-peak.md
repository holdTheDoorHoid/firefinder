Hellroar Peak is listed among the British Columbia lookouts on the Canada Lookouts website, in the Thompson-Nicola area.[^1] Its one dated entry, for 1930, quotes the British Columbia Forest Service annual report for that year.[^1] That entry says a short-term lookout was developed on the peak in the Kamloops District, and that $173 was spent on improving it.[^1]

The federal geographical names database lists a Hellroar Mountain at 52.2188889 north and 119.1555555 west.[^2] That name differs from the lookout's, and the sources do not say whether the two are the same summit.[^1][^2]

No source found describes what remains on the peak today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Hellroar Peak, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/hellroar-peak.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Hellroar Mountain, Canadian Geographical Names Database, Natural Resources Canada, https://geogratis.gc.ca/services/geoname/en/features/0caaef01849c20c3cb95e6fbd54831d4 (accessed 2026-10-20).
