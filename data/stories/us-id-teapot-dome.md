The Teapot Dome lookout stood in Valley County on the Payette National Forest, about 18 miles east of McCall, at an elevation of 5,518 feet.[^1][^2] An L-4 cab went up there in 1930.[^1][^2][^3] The L-4 was a standard 14-by-14-foot lookout building that Region 1 designed in 1929. It could be made from dimensional lumber or from logs.[^4]

In the 1950s an annual Guard School used the site.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts says it has not yet documented who staffed the lookout.[^2]

In 1948 the cab was taken apart and moved to Miners Peak, also on the Payette National Forest.[^1][^3][^7] The register and Firelookout.com both say that move started the Miners Peak site.[^5][^6]

That cab was later replaced. Idaho Fire Lookouts says a flat cab took its place in 1974 and dates the two-story log cab that followed to 1983.[^7] The register and Firelookout.com both give 1986 for the two-story cab, so the sources disagree on the date.[^5][^6]

Today, Idaho Fire Lookouts says the Miners Peak tower is staffed for fire detection, and it lists the site as a hike-in one.[^7]

Nothing I read describes what remains of the Teapot Dome site today. None of the pages says whether any part of the 1930 cab survives in the present Miners Peak tower.
