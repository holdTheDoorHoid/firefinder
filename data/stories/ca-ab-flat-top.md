A weather station named Flat Top Lookout at Slave Lake, Alberta, is listed at latitude 55.14639 and longitude -114.82028.[^1] Its record gives the elevation as about 1,035 m (3,396 ft).[^1] That figure is the station's elevation, not a tower height.[^1]

The Iowa Environmental Mesonet, which keeps the record, shows the station's data archive beginning on 28 October 2011. The archive page gives no end date.[^1]

OpenStreetMap, the community-edited map, has a separate point called Flattop Fire Tower at 55.1456422 and -114.8154140, tagged as an observation tower.[^2] The two points are not identical, so the sources do not show whether they mark the same structure.

No source we read describes the lookout's buildings or its condition today. None says whether the public may visit it.
