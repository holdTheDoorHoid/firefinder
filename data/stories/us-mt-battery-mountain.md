Battery Mountain once carried a fire lookout on the Flathead National Forest, about 15 miles east of Bigfork in Flathead County, Montana.[^1] The site sits at roughly 6,900 feet.[^1] The tower dates to 1932 and is recorded as a 30-foot pole L-4 design.[^1]

The two lookout pages that cover the tower disagree on how it ended.[^1][^2] One says it was destroyed in 1957.[^1] The other lists it only as removed and gives no year.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks it as gone.[^3] Both accounts point to a tower that no longer stands.[^1][^2]

The site is listed as gone,[^3] but the sources we checked do not describe what, if anything, remains there or whether the spot is open to visitors.
