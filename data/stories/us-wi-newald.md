The Central US Lookouts page for Newald lists the tower in Florence County, with the Nicolet National Forest.[^1] The same site's statewide list also places it in Florence County and a National Forest, without naming which.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list gives the point as 45.73199, -88.56283 and marks the tower gone.[^3]

OpenStreetMap has a node named Newald Lookout Tower, tagged as an observation tower.[^4]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
