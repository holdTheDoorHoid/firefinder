The Eastern U.S. Lookouts directory has a page for a Yeehaw lookout and places it in Indian River County, Florida.[^1] The page holds only the name and county.

The directory's county does not match Osceola County, where Wikipedia places Yeehaw Junction, a community whose name begins with Yeehaw.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today.

[^1]: Yeehaw - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Eastern U.S. Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/yeehaw.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Yeehaw Junction, Florida, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeehaw_Junction,_Florida (accessed 2026-10-21).
