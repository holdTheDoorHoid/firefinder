The legislature set up two county seats for Panola County, Mississippi, and Batesville, a city, is one of them.[^2]

The Eastern US Lookouts list includes a Batesville lookout in Panola County and marks it as removed.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today.[^1][^2]
