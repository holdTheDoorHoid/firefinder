Little White Mountain is a mountain near the city of Kelowna, in the central Okanagan Valley. Its elevation is 2,171 m (7,123 ft).[^1]

A lookout was in use on the mountain by 1913.[^2] That year the Daily Colonist, as quoted by Canada Lookouts, reported a telephone line about 19,300 m (about 63,300 ft) long from Kelowna to Little White Mountain Lookout, which the item gives as 12 miles.[^2] Forest Branch reports, quoted by the same archive, record $14.00 spent on maintenance of the lookout in 1921 and a substantial improvement in 1924.[^2]

A Summer/Fall 2006 newsletter from the Kelowna volunteer group F.O.S.S. says vandals burned the old Ministry of Forests lookout tower, which stood on the summit, last winter (2005-06).[^3] Between August 18 and 20, 2006, F.O.S.S. volunteers helped BC Parks staff clean up the site.[^3]

The Crawford Trail, which the newsletter says extends almost 20,000 m (about 65,600 ft), runs to the top of the mountain, mostly through Myra-Bellevue Provincial Park.[^3] By 2006, according to the same newsletter, BC Parks had removed part of Little White Road to keep motorized traffic out and built a footpath for permitted uses.[^3] No source I found describes the summit as it is today, or whether any part of the tower remains.
