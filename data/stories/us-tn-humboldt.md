Humboldt was a fire lookout in Gibson County, Tennessee, at an elevation of 479 feet (146 m).[^1] The state's list gives it the tower number 9-4.[^1] The list says the table's numbers are state forest tower numbers in district-tower form, taken from a 1976 list of towers.[^1]

A National Register form on the Division of Forestry's lookout towers says the agency built 208 fire lookout towers, roughly between 1930 and 1970.[^2]

The state's list marks the Humboldt tower as removed.[^1] The list adds that many towers have come down and that most of those sites are now privately owned.[^1] The list was last revised in January 2026.[^1]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at this site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Tennessee Division of Forestry Fire Lookout Towers, 1933 to 1975, National Register of Historic Places multiple property documentation form, National Park Service, https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/GetAsset/NRHP/64501237_text (accessed 2026-10-21).
