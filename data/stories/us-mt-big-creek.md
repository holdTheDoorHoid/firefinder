Big Creek Lookout stood in the Flathead National Forest, about 12 miles north of Whitefish in Flathead County, Montana, at an elevation of 6,430 feet.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com dates it to 1933 and describes a 20-foot pole L-4 tower.[^1] Forest Lookouts gives its location code as 33N-21W-35, filed under Flathead County.[^2]

Firelookout.com says the tower was destroyed in 1957.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site marks it "Removed," and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list reads "Tower Gone."[^2][^3] The two accounts word the ending differently: destroyed in one, removed in the other.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list places the site at 48.58032 north, 114.27495 west.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains on the site, and none says whether the ground is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
