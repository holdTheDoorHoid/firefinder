The Snaggy Mountain tower stood on the Maryland and West Virginia line in Preston County.[^1] In January 1925 a Cumberland Evening Times item reported that a 60-foot steel tower would replace a wooden lookout on the Garrett County side of the mountain. West Virginia would pay for the replacement, and Maryland would maintain the tower and staff it with three deputy wardens.[^2]

By July 1925 a Charleston Gazette item said the tower was in operation and would be run jointly by the two states' forest officials. It said the tower could protect forest up to 15 miles away in West Virginia and up to ten miles away in Maryland.[^2]

A Charleston Gazette item from October 1926 says the two states owned the tower jointly.[^2] The register says Maryland owned and maintained it, and names the Maryland Department of Natural Resources as the administering agency. It gives the site an elevation of 947 meters.[^1]

The register says the tower was destroyed by a fire in the 1980s, and it gives the same decade as the removal date.[^1]

Today the land is part of Alpine Lake Resort.[^1] The register says the resort is open to the public, but the neighborhood that holds the tower site is limited to residents.[^1] One register photo is captioned "Tower foundation."[^1] We found no source that describes anything else that remains.
