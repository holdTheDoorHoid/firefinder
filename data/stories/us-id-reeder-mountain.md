Reeder Mountain rises about three miles northwest of Nordman, in Bonner County, Idaho, within the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com lists its elevation as 4,729 feet.[^1]

Rex's Fire Lookout Page dates the lookout to the 1930s, when it was a crow's nest.[^2] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts give the same decade without a year.[^1][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts has no documented staffing history for the site.[^3]

Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both list it as abandoned, but neither dates that.[^1][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site "Drive-Up," which suggests a car can reach it.[^4] No source read describes what, if anything, remains there today, so check with the Kaniksu National Forest before you go.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
