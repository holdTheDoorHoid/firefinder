East Leake was a lookout in Goochland County, Virginia.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's lists give two East Leake points there, East Leake (Loc 1) and East Leake (Gum Spring), and both are marked as gone.[^1][^2] We could not find a build date, a builder or a design for either point in the sources we could read.

A tower now listed as Rassawek is in Goochland County and is listed as standing.[^1][^2] The list describes it as relocated from East Leake, but it does not say when the move happened, why it happened, or which East Leake point it came from.[^1][^2] The listed point for Rassawek is about 16.5 km from the East Leake (Loc 1) point.[^1][^2]

The name Rassawek also belongs to an archaeological site.[^3] Wikipedia describes that site as being in Fluvanna County, Virginia, where the James and Rivanna rivers meet, and says it was once a village that served as the Monacans' capital.[^3] The lookout list places its tower in Goochland County and does not explain the name.[^1][^2] The Wikipedia coordinates for the site are about 3.8 km from the listed Rassawek point.[^1][^2][^3]

We have no information about access to the tower, so do not climb it.
