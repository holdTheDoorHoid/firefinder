Firelookout.com dates the site's earliest use to 1921, when a camp lookout was there, and Forest Lookouts lists a camp for that year.[^1][^3] The cupola cabin the sources describe dates from the 1920s.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives its elevation as 6,210 feet and files it as a hike-in site.[^2]

A fire warden's report from January 1927, quoted by Forest Lookouts, places a smoke chaser, a lookout and two deputy wardens in the cabin that year.[^3] A September 1928 Northern Idaho News item, also quoted there, mentions a Caribou mountain lookout arriving on a Saturday night.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts has no staffing history on record for this lookout.[^2]

The sources give 1952 as the year the cabin was abandoned.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com says heavy snow brought the cabin down in 1999, a year it marks with a question mark.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts places the collapse in the 1990s.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as gone.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left at the site today, or whether the public can visit it. Anyone planning a visit should check locally first.
