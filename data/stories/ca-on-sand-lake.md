Firelookout.ca lists a Sand Lake fire tower in Unorganized Centre Parry Sound, Parry Sound District, at an elevation of 174 m (571 ft), and describes it as a steel tower.[^1] A separate Ontario lookout index lists a Sand Lake tower in Algoma District and describes it as a light steel structure with an eight-sided cab.[^2] The two entries disagree on the district, and the sources do not show whether they describe the same tower.[^1][^2]

Across Ontario, the towers were overseen by the Department of Lands and Forests, a body that became the Ministry of Natural Resources in 1972.[^3] Through the 1960s the towers saw less and less use for aerial fire spotting, as fire-fighting crews took over that work.[^3]

No source describes this site today, including whether any of the tower remains or whether the land is open to visitors.
