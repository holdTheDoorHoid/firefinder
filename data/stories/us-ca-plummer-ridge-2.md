The Plummer Ridge lookout stood in the Eldorado National Forest, in El Dorado County.[^1]

The only dated record I found is from June 1929. The Mountain Democrat reported that William Littlefield, of Plymouth, a fire guard on the forest staff at Plummer Lookout, had married Alma Allen, also of Plymouth, at Reno on Memorial Day. The couple were posted to the lookout for the summer.[^1]

The Forest Lookout Association's California list marks the site as abandoned, and classes it as a tree lookout rather than a tower.[^2] An OpenStreetMap point for the site carries a tag, building:destroyed, set to yes.[^3] A peak list calls the site an LO site, a label it uses for sites whose building is gone or whose status is unknown.[^4]

I found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it. The sources also disagree on where the site is. The map point and the list's coordinates are about six kilometres apart, so the exact spot is uncertain.[^2][^3]
