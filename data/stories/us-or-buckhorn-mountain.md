Buckhorn Mountain Lookout is an L-4 ground cabin on the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest in Wallowa County, Oregon, at about 5,300 feet.[^1][^2][^5] The cab was not built on this spot.[^1] In the fall of 1952 it was lowered from the tower at Fairchild Point and moved here as the new lookout for that end of the Chesnimus District, according to a 1954 history of the forest as quoted by Forest Lookouts.[^3][^4] The register gives the date as "Relocated 1952" and says the original construction date is unknown.[^1]

The cab sits just below the summit, on the southeast side of the ridge.[^2] It rests on short stone piers and has a catwalk.[^1]

A list of known lookout personnel on firelookout.com runs from 1955 to 1971, and the page says the cab was last staffed in the 1970s.[^2] A 2023 visit described on Every Lookout in Oregon says it has not been staffed since then.[^5] No fire at the site appears in the sources reviewed.[^1][^2][^3][^5]

The register's 2022 visit report found the door open, the shutters missing, and glass on the floor, and could not say how many windows were broken.[^1] Every Lookout in Oregon lists its status as abandoned.[^5] No source reviewed describes a restoration or a rental.[^1][^2][^3][^5]

The site is now called Buckhorn Overlook. It is a tourist stop with a view of the Imnaha River, reached by road, with a parking area at the overlook.[^5] The same 2023 account says it could find no information that the point is officially called Buckhorn Mountain.[^5] That visit found the lookout and its garage still standing, but slowly crumbling.[^5]
