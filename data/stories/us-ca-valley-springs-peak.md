The Forest Fire Lookout Association describes the Valley Springs Peak lookout in Calaveras County as a two-story cab, and its California list marks the site as gone.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page for the peak files it under the California Department of Forestry.[^2]

A notice of exemption for a project titled Valley Springs Peak Fire Lookout Removal of Improvements was received and posted on February 19, 1993.[^3] The notice names the agency as the California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection, or CAL FIRE.[^3] The project description calls for dismantling and removing improvements on the site before it is relinquished.[^3] The notice describes what was to be done. It does not report that the work was finished.

A peakbagging.com table lists a Valley Springs Peak lookout site and names Cal Fire in its row.[^4] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains on the peak today or whether visitors may go there.
