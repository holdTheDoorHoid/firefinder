Elk Mountain sits in the Bridger-Teton National Forest in Lincoln County, Wyoming.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site says the station went into service in 1941.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a "Ground" type that is standing.[^2]

The sources do not agree on what the structure is. A firelookout.com page calls it a cabin.[^3] A 2007 item quoted on the Forest Lookouts site refers to a lookout tower on the mountain.[^1] We report both descriptions and could not settle the question from the pages we read.

In August 2007 the Middle Fire burned 2,900 acres about ten miles southeast of Alpine Junction.[^1] According to the same item, firefighters took a precautionary step to protect the Elk Mountain lookout.[^1]

The Buckrail article, citing the National Park Service, says the Bridger-Teton abandoned all of its fire lookout towers after the early 1970s, and that none on the forest remain manned.[^4] That article speaks of towers rather than cabins, so it may not settle what happened to this site. We found no record of who staffed Elk Mountain or when its watches ended.

We found no statement about whether the public can reach the lookout today or whether it can be rented. It sits inside a national forest, so check with the Bridger-Teton before visiting. Do not climb or enter any lookout structure unless the Forest Service confirms it is open.
