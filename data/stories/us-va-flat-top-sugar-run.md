Flat Top, also called Sugar Run, stood at the upper end of Sugar Run in Giles County, where Pearis, Sugar Run and Flat Top Mountains come together.[^1] The site is in the Jefferson National Forest, south of Pearisburg.[^1]

The tower was already standing in 1934, when a U.S. Geological Survey party set a benchmark on the summit about 90 yards from it.[^1] The survey returned in 1952 and found the lookout still there.[^1] In 1964 the tower was still standing, but the survey's measurements suggested it might have been moved a short distance.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^2]

A side trail of about five minutes climbs from the Appalachian Trail to the top.[^1] The site lists the detour as 0.2 mile with a 40 foot gain.[^1] The trail skirts beneath the summit, and a visitor's account describes communication towers up there.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout at the site today.[^1][^2]
