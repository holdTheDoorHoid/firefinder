Equinox Mountain, the highest peak of the Taconic Range, rises nearly 3,000 feet above its eastern footings in Manchester, in Bennington County.[^3][^2]

The mountain's fire-watch history is brief. The Vermont state forester's fourth annual report says arrangements had been made for a lookout station on the mountain in the summer of 1912, equipped with wireless.[^1] Under the plan, reports would travel by wireless to a base station, and an operator there would phone the warden for the town concerned.[^1] The report called the scheme largely experimental and government-funded, and hoped it would prove cheaper than running telephone lines through forest country.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page for the mountain ends with the status word "Removed," and the Vermont lookout list marks the tower as gone.[^1][^2]

Today a privately operated toll road reaches a summit observation building, passing near towers used for broadcasting and other purposes.[^3] We found no source that describes a fire lookout on the summit now.
