Desolation Butte has had a fire lookout since 1923, and possibly since 1918.[^1][^3] The register's description of that first building is a 7x7 cabin raised on a steel tower 50 feet tall.[^2]

The lookout that stands now went up in 1961 to replace it.[^2] Its cab follows the Forest Service R-6 flat-top design and sits atop an open timber tower 67 feet high.[^2][^1]

Firelookout.com and a trail guide both say someone staffs the tower each summer.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com also lists known lookout personnel going back to 1916.[^1]

The register gives the summit as 7,034 feet and lists the lookout as registered on June 22, 2022.[^2]

The road to the top is Lookout Road, also numbered Forest Road 10103, and the trail guide says a high-clearance vehicle can make the trip.[^3] Camping near the tower requires asking the staffed lookout first.[^3] A 2023 trip report notes that reaching the butte is not a quick trip.[^4]

The lookout is listed as active and standing.[^4] It sits in the Umatilla National Forest.[^3]
