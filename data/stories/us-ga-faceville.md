The Georgia Forestry Commission put up the Decatur County Fire Tower in 1953.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register places it among the final Aermotor towers of their generation.[^1] The register says the tower sits at a ranger station, is 100 feet tall, and has a 7-by-7-foot metal cab.[^1]

The tower had staff until the 1990s.[^1] The register gives a date registered of January 2, 2008.[^1] The same page says the tower now serves communications.[^1]

A separate Georgia tower list on tnlandforms.us gives the name "faceville" to a tower in Decatur County, and links it to an older Georgia Forestry Commission number, 221.[^2] Faceville is an unincorporated community roughly 18 km (11 miles) south-southwest of Bainbridge.[^3]

The two sources do not agree on the exact spot. The register's coordinates lie north-east of the list's point. The register gives the elevation as 100 feet (30 m). The list gives 293, a figure its page does not label; read as feet, that is 89 m.[^1][^2]

No source describes the site today beyond the register's note about communications use.[^1] None says whether the public can visit the ranger station, so do not assume access, and do not climb the tower.
