The Smith Lookout Tower is a 100-foot tower in Smith County, Mississippi, administered by the Mississippi Forestry Commission.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as 100 feet tall, says its make is unknown, and asks readers with information to get in touch.[^1] OpenStreetMap records its construction as lattice.[^2]

The register numbers the tower US 1819, MS 65.[^1] It was added on May 19, 2026.[^1]

No construction date, staffing history or fire record appears in the sources available for this entry, so the tower's early years are not recorded here.[^1][^2]

The register gives no access rules or visiting details, but it names the Mississippi Forestry Commission as the administering agency.[^1] Ask that agency before anyone visits the site, and do not climb the structure without permission.
