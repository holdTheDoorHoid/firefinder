The River aux Sables Fire Tower is listed in Algoma District, Ontario.[^1] A directory of Ontario lookouts spells the name "RIVER AUX SAUBLE" and places it in Algoma District.[^2]

The River aux Sables runs through Algoma and Sudbury Districts. It begins at Lac aux Sables in Algoma District and drains into the Spanish River close to Massey.[^3]

Lookout towers began going up in Ontario in the 1920s, and by 1962 the province had 316 steel towers in service.[^4]

The listing says it is unsure whether the tower still stands.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.
