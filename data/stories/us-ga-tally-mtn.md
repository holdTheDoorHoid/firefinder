The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers has an entry named tally mtn in Haralson County, at 33.71888, -85.26084.[^1] Wikipedia describes a summit of the same name in Haralson County, 1,522 feet (464 m) high, located southeast of Tallapoosa, at nearly the same coordinates.[^2] Georgia has a second Tally Mountain, in Pickens County, so the name alone does not settle which peak a source means.[^2]

No source describes the site today.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Tally Mountain (Georgia), Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tally_Mountain_(Georgia) (accessed 2026-10-21).
