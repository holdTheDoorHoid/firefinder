Sand Mountain is a lookout site in Boundary County, Idaho, within the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts site groups Idaho lookouts by county, and its county index gives this site's survey location as 60N-1E-32.[^1][^2]

The Forest Lookouts entry is headed Sand Mountain, but the one dated item it quotes refers to a tower it calls the Sand Peak lookout.[^1] The two names may describe the same place, though no source here says so outright.[^1]

That item is dated August 1, 1929, and the page credits it to the Pend Oreille Review.[^1] It describes the Sand Peak tower as prepared for building.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.[^1][^2]
