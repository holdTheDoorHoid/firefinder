A Georgia lookout tower list on tnlandforms.us includes an entry called "horseleg mtn" in Floyd County.[^1] The list's map is marked as revised on 22 March 2016.[^1] The page says that names for towers numbered 73 and above were made up for the list, so this name is a label rather than a confirmed one.[^1] The entry is number 76 on the list.[^1]

The list also carries two other sets of numbers.[^1] The page says that the Georgia Forestry Commission (GFC) provided coordinates for 118 towers under BLL numbers, and that an older GFC list covered 203 towers under gfc numbers.[^1] This entry carries BLL-41096 and gfc-715.[^1]

Wikipedia places Horseleg Mountain in Floyd County and gives its elevation as 1,322 feet (403 m).[^2] The list gives 1,507 for this entry, so the two sources disagree on height.[^1][^2] Wikipedia traces the name to the peak's supposed resemblance to a horse's leg.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the public may visit it. Check who owns the land before going, and keep out of any closed or private area.
