The Mamers tower is in Harnett County, North Carolina.[^1] The Civilian Conservation Corps erected it in September 1934.[^1] From 1933 to 1938 the corps assisted in building 52 new fire towers across North Carolina.[^2]

The tower was a 72-foot 9-inch (22.2 m) steel tower with a two-room tower house.[^1] The Emergency Conservation Works program paid the $990.63 cost, and the state contributed nothing.[^1]

The listing pairs the tower with the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources.[^1] A Forest History Society summary dates the state's first fire tower to 1926 and places it in Harnett County.[^2] That summary does not name the site, so it cannot be tied to Mamers.[^2]

No source I could read says whether the Mamers tower still stands, what remains of it at the site, or whether the public can reach that site.
