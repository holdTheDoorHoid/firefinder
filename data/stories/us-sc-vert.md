Vert Tower is a lookout in Berkeley County, South Carolina, listed on the National Historic Lookout Register as US 1790 and SC 36.[^1] The register gives its elevation as 76 feet, or 23 meters, and lists its coordinates.[^1]

The register accepted the tower on January 2, 2026, on the nomination of Dan Curry, who also supplied the photograph shown on the page.[^1] The register counts 37 lookouts in South Carolina.[^2]

The page does not say when the tower was built, what type of lookout it is, who built or owns it, or what condition it is in.[^1] We did not find those details in the other sources we checked.

For context, the South Carolina Forestry Commission built its first fire tower in 1930 and closed its tower system on October 1, 1993.[^3] Nothing we read connects Vert Tower to that network, so this story does not treat it as one of those towers.

The register gives no visiting arrangements. Do not climb the tower, and find out who owns the land before going near it.[^1]
