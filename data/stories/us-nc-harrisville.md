Harrisville is one of the unincorporated communities in Montgomery County, North Carolina.[^1] A list of North Carolina lookout towers names a lookout tower called Harrisville in that county and gives its coordinates.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts listing for North Carolina gives the North Carolina Forest Service as the agency for the Montgomery County tower.[^3] It says the state erected a 99-foot 9-inch (30.4 m) tower at the site in 1944, and it cites the state's Department of Conservation and Development report for that year.[^3]

The same listing places Buck Mountain, a separate Montgomery County tower, in the Uwharrie National Forest.[^3]

The National Historic Lookout Register does not list a Harrisville tower.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the Harrisville site today, or whether the public may visit it.
