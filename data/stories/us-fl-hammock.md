The Forest Lookouts website lists a lookout called Hammock in Walton County, Florida.[^1] The same index shows two other entries with Hammock in their names: one in Highlands County, and Cooks Hammock in Lafayette County. They are separate lookouts, so visitors should not confuse them with this one.[^2]

No source found describes the site today. It is not known whether a tower or any other structure remains there, and no source says whether the land is open to the public.

Visitors should check access with the land manager first, and should not climb any tower without permission.
