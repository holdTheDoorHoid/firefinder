Craft Point is a former lookout in the Malheur National Forest, about 20 miles northeast of Burns in Harney County, Oregon.[^1][^2]

The register says it was built in 1930 as a 15-foot platform observation tower.[^1] It was abandoned in the 1940s, and a 2021 visit report says the site was never upgraded to a more substantial structure.[^1][^3] That same report says the platform may have been used in emergencies, but little has been written about it.[^3]

The register added Craft Point on December 25, 2022.[^1] It was still standing as of May 30, 2021, the date the register gives and the date of the visit report.[^1][^3] The report's history section says it is in poor shape but still up.[^3] The firelookout.com entry, which does not date its status, reads "Still there."[^2]

The visit report also notes a closure. It says the area was closed as of July 14, 2024, because of the Falls and Telephone fires.[^3] We found no later notice about whether the area reopened, so check the forest's closure status before any trip. The 2021 visitor parked near where the road was blocked off by larger rocks, then walked to the summit.[^3]
