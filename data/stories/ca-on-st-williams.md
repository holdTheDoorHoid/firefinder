Firelookout.ca lists a St. Williams Fire Tower at Long Point Bay, Ontario, at 42.66939, -80.23802.[^1] The record says the Department of Lands and Forests Tower Detection System Map (Ontario Resources Atlas, 1963) confirms that the tower existed.[^1] It also calls the tower the southernmost fire lookout in Ontario.[^1]

The record gives no tower type, height or year built.[^1] Most Ontario towers were built in the 1920s to 1950s. They fell under the Department of Lands and Forests, which was succeeded by the Ministry of Natural Resources in 1972.[^2]

A fan-run list of towers still standing does not name one at St. Williams.[^3] The list is undated, but it mentions a 2013 ministry decision to take down many of the towers it lists.[^3]

Today, no source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the public may visit it.
