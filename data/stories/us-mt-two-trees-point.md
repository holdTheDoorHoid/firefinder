Two Trees Point is about 18 miles north-northeast of Thompson Falls, in Sanders County, Montana.[^1] Its lookout stood at about 6,040 feet.[^1] The Fire Lookout page places it in the Lolo National Forest.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page gives both names, Cabinet National Forest and then Lolo National Forest.[^2]

The sources do not agree on when the cabin went up. The Fire Lookout page says a log cupola cabin was built on the east summit in 1933.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page gives 1928 for a log cabin with a cupola.[^2] A November 1933 item from the Sanders County Independent-Ledger, quoted on that same page, describes a spur off the new road that linked to the Two Trees Lookout, which suggests a lookout was already in place that year.[^2]

The cabin was destroyed in 1962.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a ground site, with the lookout gone.[^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
