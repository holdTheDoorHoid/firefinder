The Forest Lookouts page places the Kelleter tower in Washington County, on the Meramec State Forest.[^1] The Missouri Conservation Commission acquired 8.91 acres for a fire tower in 1940.[^1] The next year a 66-foot (about 20 m) wood tower went up on the towersite.[^1] A Forest Service bulletin quoted on that page says CCC Camp S-78 built it for the Commission.[^1]

A Franklin County Tribune report dated October 31, 1941, put the new tower on Pea Ridge, east of Sullivan.[^1] The bulletin gives the dedication as Sunday, October 26, and says the tower was named for Mrs. Pauline J. Kelleter, mother of a Clark National Forest supervisor.[^1] A Tribune item of November 1, 1946 reports that a "new" Kelleter tower on Pea Ridge was officially dedicated.[^1] The sources I read do not explain that second dedication.[^1]

Ed Christopher was the tower man in 1965, when a bobcat chased a woman into her home near the tower.[^1] He killed it with a shotgun after a short hunt.[^1] A 2022 Missouri Conservationist feature says he spent about 38 years as a towerman, often working Kelleter with his wife, Wilma.[^2] In the early days he took a bearing on a fire from Kelleter, drove to the Lone Hill or Onyx Hill tower for a second, and went out to fight it himself.[^2]

Upkeep followed. The residence gained a bedroom in fiscal 1956-57, and the 1965-66 work included two bedrooms and a bath.[^1] In fiscal 1969-70 the Department decided to keep the old wooden tower as a historical type and reworked its structure with pressure-treated wood.[^1] The same page dates the old wooden tower to the 1930s.[^1] That does not match the 1941 dates above, and I have not resolved the difference.[^1]

The last entry I found is a fiscal 1972-73 roof on the tower residence.[^1] No source I found describes the site today. I cannot say whether the wooden tower still stands, whether anything remains of it, or whether the public may visit.[^1] The Missouri Department of Conservation's Pea Ridge Conservation Area is in Washington County, but I found no source that links it to the old tower site.[^3]
