Cadotte is a steel fire lookout in Alberta. The provincial wildfire lookout register lists the tower at 30 m (98 ft) tall, on a site 733 m (2,405 ft) above sea level.[^1]

The register gives 2014 as the year the tower was constructed.[^1] Builders began using steel for towers in the 1920s, gradually moving away from wood.[^2] Alberta says its lookouts are either mountaintop cabins or steel towers on high ground across the boreal forest, placed where a clear view helps spot fires early.[^3]

Today the register lists Cadotte as active. Its access mode is recorded as "fly", which means the tower is reached by air, and the register notes a weather station on site.[^1] The same record marks the site location as not verified.[^1] On safety grounds, Alberta bars the public from climbing lookout towers, and it reserves lookout dwellings for private use.[^3] The register says nothing about the tower's condition or surroundings today.[^1] No other source found describes this lookout.
