Five Canyon Lookout Tower is in Otero County, New Mexico.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a standing tower.[^2] Its name is written two ways in the sources. The Forest Lookouts site files the page under "Five Mile," while the page heading itself reads "FIVE CANYON."[^1]

We could not find when the tower was built, who built it, or why. The sources we read give no height, design details or fire history. The Forest Lookouts page places the tower under the Mescalero Apache Indian Agency.[^1] OpenStreetMap, which says its entry was added from USGS topographic maps, tags the structure as an observation tower.[^3] That tag does not say whether anyone used the tower to watch for fires.

No staffing record turned up for this tower, so we cannot say whether anyone ever watched fires from it.

Today the tower is listed as standing.[^2] We found no information about public access, visiting rules or rental. Because the Forest Lookouts page ties the tower to the Mescalero Apache Indian Agency, land rules here may differ from those on a national forest tower. Ask the local owners or managers before going near it.
