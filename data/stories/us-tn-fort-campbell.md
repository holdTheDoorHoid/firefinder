Fort Campbell was a fire lookout tower in Stewart County, Tennessee.[^1] The Tennessee Landforms list gives it the code STT004 and an elevation of 561 feet (about 171 m).[^1] Its name comes from that list. The list's author says some names were assigned to towers that had no name on the USGS maps or on the state forestry office's list, and I could not tell whether this one is among them.[^1]

Between 1933 and 1941 the Civilian Conservation Corps erected 98 lookout towers across Tennessee.[^1] The Tennessee Landforms list marks this tower as removed.[^1] A National Register study of Tennessee's towers says many of the Division of Forestry's former lookouts are gone or slated for demolition. Their use had ended, or leases on private land had lapsed.[^2]

No source I found describes what remains at this site today, or whether it is open to visitors. The Tennessee Landforms list says most tower locations are now private property.[^1] No source I found says whether the site lies on the Army post that shares the name, so visitors should not assume the site is open to them.

[^2]: Tennessee Division of Forestry Fire Lookout Towers MPS, National Park Service, https://npgallery.nps.gov/pdfhost/docs/NRHP/Text/64501237.pdf (accessed 2026-10-21).
