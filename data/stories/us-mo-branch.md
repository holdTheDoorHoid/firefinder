The Branch towersite is in Camden County, Missouri. An annual report quoted on the Central US Lookouts page says 40 acres were acquired for the towersite in 1945-46.[^1] A June 1946 newspaper item lists the Branch tower as under construction or due that summer, and describes the type as a 60-foot creosoted pole structure.[^1]

A 1947-48 state manual lists the towersite as owned by the Missouri Conservation Commission and 40 acres in size.[^1] A department map last updated May 10, 2021, gives 39 acres.[^2] The sources disagree on the acreage, and we did not find an explanation.

In fiscal 1955-56 the wooden tower was replaced with a steel tower.[^1] A July 1958 item refers to a conservation officer of the Branch lookout tower.[^1] A fiscal 1969-70 report records a new roof on the towersite garage and a new tile floor in the tower.[^1]

A 2018 department plan for the Central Region towersites says to keep historical information on each fire tower. It also calls for annual maintenance of the radio towers at Branch and in the Camdenton Conservation Service Center area.[^3] The 2021 map's legend lists a Fire Tower, a parking lot and MDC Land.[^2]

No source we read describes the tower as it stands today, and we did not find a current status or any statement about visitors. Check with the department before you visit, and do not climb a tower that is closed or private.
