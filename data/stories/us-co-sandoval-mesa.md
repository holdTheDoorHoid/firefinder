Sandoval Mesa, in Archuleta County, held a fire lookout built in 1970.[^1][^2] The register places the site at 8,488 feet and describes a 50-foot metal tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab, ringed by a four-foot catwalk that it calls unusual for a cab that small.[^1]

Its stairs and landing layout set it apart from the standard fire tower models.[^1] The Southern Ute Indian Tribe owns and runs the tower, and the register says the public cannot visit it.[^1] It was added to the National Historic Lookout Register as US 871 on January 21, 2010.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Colorado list gives the same registry number, NHLR US 871, and now marks the tower as removed.[^2] No source reviewed describes what, if anything, stands at the site today.
