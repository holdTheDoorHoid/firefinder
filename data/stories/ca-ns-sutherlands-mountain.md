A list of Nova Scotia's fire towers on the Maritimes Scanning Site, last updated September 18, 2009, places a tower labelled Blue Mtn on Blue or Sutherlands Mountain in the southeast of the Pictou area.[^1] The list does not settle which of the two hills held the tower.[^1] It gives the talk group as DNR MacLellans Brook.[^1]

Sutherlands Mountain lies within the community of Blue Mountain and rises about 272 m (892 ft).[^2] A local community-maps page calls it one of the highest prominent uplands in the area.[^2] A Canada Lookouts page names the site Sutherlands Mountain (Blue Mtn) and puts it in the Pictou area.[^3]

The Department of Natural Resources ran the province's towers.[^4] A CBC News report, carried by Yahoo, says they were taken out of service in 2015, later dismantled, and replaced by aerial surveillance.[^4] Randall Freeman, a former tower operator, described overlapping views between neighbouring towers, so that one tower's view reached into the next tower's territory.[^4]

A 2012 provincial news release had planned to replace 19 towers with aerial detection the following year and to keep 14 in remote areas.[^5] That plan points to 2013, not the 2015 date CBC gives, so the sources do not agree on when the network ended.[^4][^5]

No source I found describes what remains of the tower today, or whether its site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
