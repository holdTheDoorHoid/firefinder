Mount Warner, a hill in Hampshire County, had a fire tower for only a short time.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says the tower went up in 1939, came down in 1940, and stood on concrete footings.[^1] Jack Kelley nominated the site, and the register added it in May 2016.[^1] The register's photo caption shows the footings as they looked in 2014.[^1]

Today the Trustees of Reservations lists a 156-acre reservation on Mount Warner Road in North Hadley.[^2] It is open every day from sunrise to sunset, and admission is free.[^2] The Trustees suggest the Salamander Loop, which passes through oak and beech before reaching evergreens higher up.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains of the tower at the site now, beyond that 2014 photograph.[^1] None says whether the old footings lie inside the reservation.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Mount Warner Fire Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ma/mount-warner-fire-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Mount Warner, North Hadley, MA, The Trustees of Reservations, https://thetrustees.org/place/mount-warner/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
