Cow Camp, also called Calico, was a tent camp in the Lolo National Forest, about 15 miles northwest of Plains in Sanders County.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts (Montana) describes it as a tent camp with an observation post atop a tree, and gives its location code as 22N-27W-14.[^2]

Firelookout.com dates the camp and its crow's nest to 1936.[^1] The camp was abandoned in the 1930s, and the page says it was still there in 2014.[^1] A 2016 forest fire damaged the tree that held the observation post.[^1]

The two sources describe the site differently. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's listing gives its status as Abandoned.[^3] Firelookout.com's entry, by contrast, records the fire damage in 2016.[^1]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today or whether it is open to the public.
