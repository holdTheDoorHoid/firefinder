Ward Peak sits on the Idaho and Montana border, about 20 miles east of Avery, Idaho, in the St. Joe and Lolo national forests.[^1] Its elevation is 7,312 feet.[^1]

A cabin was first built on the summit in 1914.[^1][^3] In 1929 an L-2 model with a shake cupola took its place.[^1][^2][^3]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page names one known staffer, Herb Uttley, under the year 1925, and credits a book by Bert Russell for that entry.[^2]

The cabin was destroyed in the 1950s.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the lookout as gone.[^4]

Today the summit is reached on foot, and Idaho Fire Lookouts marks the site as hike-in.[^2] Trail 250 starts from the state-line road (No. 391) and climbs to the top, a total of 1.3 miles.[^3] The route begins with the Little Joe Road out of St. Regis.[^3] The top still has a USGS survey marker, plus remains of the old lookout cabin.[^3] No source we read describes anything else on the summit now.
