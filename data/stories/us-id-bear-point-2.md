Bear Point Lookout stood in Idaho County, on the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1]

It dates to about 1930.[^1][^2] One account describes a cab on a 10-foot, round tower of native logs.[^1] A second pairs a 10-foot pole tower with a log cabin used as living space; both date to about 1930.[^2]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site lists Bert Nixon as known staff from the 1930s, and says he staffed the lookout for 35 years.[^3]

Bear Point did not last. The three sources that give a decade all put its destruction in the 1960s, though none gives a year.[^1][^2][^3] A Forest Service history includes a photograph captioned as the lookout in 1961, which places the lookout there that year.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Idaho sites marks the tower as gone.[^5]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none gives access rules for it. Anyone curious about the location should check with the Nez Perce National Forest before going.
