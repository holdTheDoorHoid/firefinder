Pothole Butte sits in Klamath County, Oregon, on the Winema National Forest, at 6,176 feet (1,882 meters).[^1] Firelookout.com places it six miles east of Crater Lake.[^2]

The register dates the site to the 1930s and describes a guard station at the foot of the butte. A motor trail ran to the summit, which let people keep watch in the worst fire weather.[^1] Firelookout.com describes a cab from the 1930s instead.[^2] The two descriptions do not match, so both are reported here.[^1][^2]

The register added Pothole Butte on June 7, 2011.[^1]

Both sources say the lookout is gone.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site with type Ground and status Gone.[^3] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none that says whether the public can reach it.
