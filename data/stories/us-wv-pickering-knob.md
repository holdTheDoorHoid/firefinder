Pickering Knob is a former fire tower site in Mingo County, West Virginia. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it under the numbers US 166 and WV 16, with a registration date of September 16, 2008.[^1] Henry Isenberg nominated the site.[^1] The register gives its coordinates as 37.780067 north and 82.108224 west, and its elevation as 1,950 feet, or 594 meters.[^1]

The register says the State of West Virginia administers the site, and its page states that the tower has been removed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list of West Virginia towers also marks the Pickering Knob tower as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]
