Cow Mountain is in Essex County, Vermont.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places it at 44.55550 north and 71.70221 west.[^2] By April 1915 the state already had a lookout there, according to a short item from the Albans Messenger that Forest Lookouts quotes.[^1] The Association's Vermont list gives the structure type as unknown and the status as abandoned.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the summit is open to visitors.

[^1]: Cow Mountain, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/cow-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Vermont Fire Towers, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/vt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
