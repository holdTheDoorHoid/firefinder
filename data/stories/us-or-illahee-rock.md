Illahee Rock sits in the Umpqua National Forest, about 35 miles east-northeast of Roseburg.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com says a camp was established there in 1922, and that the first structure, a D-6 cupola cabin, followed in 1925.[^2] Eugene Weekly describes the same 1925 cupola as one of two lookouts on the summit, the other a 1958 lookout atop a 30-foot tower.[^3] The register instead dates the older cupola to 1922 and the adjacent tower to 1955, and describes that tower as 20 feet high, with a live-in cab and catwalk.[^1] Firelookout.com gives 1956 for its 20-foot L-4 tower.[^2] The sources do not agree on when the tower went up or how tall it is.[^1][^2][^3]

The cupola has since been lowered.[^1][^2] The register says it was shortened some years ago, and firelookout.com says its roof was dropped down.[^1][^2] The register says the tower is still staffed each year.[^1] Firelookout.com says the tower is available for emergencies.[^2] I could not confirm current staffing.

The site is on the National Historic Lookout Register, where it was listed on December 30, 1994.[^1] Eugene Weekly calls the trail up Illahee Rock little known, and says it reaches two of the state's oldest fire lookouts.[^3] The hike is 0.9 miles and gains 550 feet.[^3] The trail begins on a steep, rocky road that the article says is too rough to drive.[^3]
