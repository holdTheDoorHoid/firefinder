The register places Budd Lake Fire Tower on the west shore of Budd Lake in Morris County, New Jersey.[^1] It says the site was called the "95 Mile Tree Lookout" as early as 1905, for its location at mile 95 of the old state turnpike.[^1]

The current tower dates to 1924.[^1] A May 1924 newspaper notice said the state Department of Conservation and Development would erect a sixty-foot steel lookout with telephone service on Turkey Hill, near Waterloo.[^2]

A 1925 clipping describes a lookout at the "Turkey Hill fire tower" on Waterloo Mountain.[^2] That does not match the register's west-shore location, and the sources do not show whether the tower moved or the clipping means a different structure.[^2][^1]

The register describes a 60-foot Aermotor steel tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab, and says no other lookout in the state has an Osborne fire finder.[^1] The New Jersey Forest Fire Service staffs it seasonally, according to a 2023 visit report.[^1] The same report noted recent steel work and a new paint job, and the register also dates a repainting to April 2023.[^1]

A June 2025 hiking guide places the tower in Allamuchy Mountain State Park and counts it among 21 New Jersey towers still used for fire detection.[^3] It says the top cabin is locked, but that visitors can climb most of the way up with no fence in the way.[^3] Its author believes the "No Trespassing" sign at the base of New Jersey fire towers is not enforced.[^3] The walk from the parking area is short.[^3] Because the register lists it as active, ask the Forest Fire Service before climbing.[^1]
