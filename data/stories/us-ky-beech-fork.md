Forest Lookouts lists a Beech Fork tower in Leslie County, Kentucky, and marks it as removed.[^1] OpenStreetMap maps a tower point named Beech Fork Lookout Tower at about 37.023 north, 83.405 west.[^2] The point is tagged as an observation tower with an elevation of 628 metres.[^2] A note on the point asks "where?", so the mapped spot may not be exact.[^2]

The sources do not describe what the site looks like today.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Beech Fork, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/beech-fork.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Beech Fork Lookout Tower, OpenStreetMap node 358614014, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/358614014 (accessed 2026-10-10).
