The Bolar Lookout Tower stands on Pipestem Knob inside Pipestem Resort State Park, which straddles the border between Mercer and Summers counties.[^1][^3][^4] A state marker says that reaching the top of the tower's steps puts visitors on the park's highest point, at 3,000 feet, facing east.[^5]

We did not find when the tower was built. The park's official opening was on Memorial Day 1970.[^1] The marker also says the Pipestem Knob Primitive Baptist Church stood close by and was relocated when the park went in.[^5]

Before the renaming, the tower was called the Pipestem Tower.[^2] In 2011 the park renamed it the Bolar Lookout Tower, in honor of Steve Bolar, its sixth superintendent. The dedication was set for Wednesday, October 19, 2011.[^2][^4]

Bolar spent 32 years in the state parks system, and he received the 2009 Distinguished West Virginian Award. He died in July 2009 after a terminal illness.[^3]

The sources we read do not name anyone who staffed the tower or any fire it watched.

The tower sits near the park entrance and is reached by the Pipestem Knob Trail. That trail is 0.2 miles one way, on pavement, and fairly steep.[^2] A trail guide describes it as full of switchbacks.[^4] We found no recent report on the tower's condition or hours, so check with the park before you go.
