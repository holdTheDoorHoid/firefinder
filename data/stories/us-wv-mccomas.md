The McComas tower stood in Mercer County.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list places it near 37.39 north and 81.30 west, and lists it as a tower that is gone.[^1] The register calls it the McComas Tower Site, number US 1638, WV 29, and gives its elevation as 2,593 feet (790 m).[^2] The site's registration is dated January 26, 2017.[^2]

It was one of West Virginia's fire towers.[^1] The e-WV encyclopedia dates the earliest of these towers in the state to 1916 and says the towers were closed in 1990.[^3] It also explains how a fire was located: an observer's bearings on a column of smoke from two or more towers were compared to pin down the blaze, a technique it calls triangulation.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the McComas site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
