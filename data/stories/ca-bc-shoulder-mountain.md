Canada Lookouts gives Shoulder Mountain's latitude as 54.25 and its longitude as 126.17, in the Bulkley-Nechako area.[^1] A travel directory for Babine Lake mentions a trail climbing to a forestry lookout on Shoulder Mountain, near Topley Landing, among the many trails along the lake's shorelines and forested areas.[^2] The same directory lists Shoulder Mountain among the places popular for snowmobiling in winter.[^2]

A June 1, 1977 item in the Lake District News, as quoted by Canada Lookouts, said the primary lookout site on Shoulder Mountain would be manned.[^1] The secondary site on Deesius Mountain, east of Babine Lake, would stay unstaffed unless a patrol crew manned it during extreme conditions.[^1]

The directory entry is undated.[^2] No source we found describes what remains on the mountain today, or whether visitors are allowed up.
