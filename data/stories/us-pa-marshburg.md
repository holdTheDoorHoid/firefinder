The Marshburg fire tower stood in McKean County, Pennsylvania.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association says the U.S. Forest Service built the steel Aermotor tower in 1935.[^1]

By April 1937 the tower had a regular lookout. A Kane Republican item, as transcribed by Forest Lookouts, named Frank Kiess to the post.[^2] In July 1939 the Allegheny National Forest sent a man to the tower, a Bradford Era item reported.[^2] The same item said no forest fires had been reported in McKean County, though several had burned in neighboring counties.[^2]

The register says the tower was removed before 1995.[^1] The site was registered in September 2008 on the nomination of Henry Isenberg, and the register lists no coordinates for it.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]
