The Forest Lookouts list of Minnesota towers says the Toivola tower went up in 1932; it was 85 feet tall, built of steel, with a ladder on the outside.[^1] The same list gives the site's land-survey location as township 54 north, range 20 west, section 12, in Saint Louis County.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association gives the coordinates as 47.16761 north, 92.81737 west.[^2]

The association's list classes the Toivola site as a tower and marks it gone.[^2]

We found no source that describes the site now, so we cannot say what remains there or whether the ground is open to visitors.

[^2]: Minnesota fire lookout list, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
