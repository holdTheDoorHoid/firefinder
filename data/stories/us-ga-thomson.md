A Georgia lookout tower list places a tower named thomson in McDuffie County, at 33.54951, -82.50947.[^1] Its elevation column reads 531, with no unit given.[^1]

A 2017 post on the Forgotten Georgia blog is headed "Old Fire Watch Tower in Thomson." It includes a short note signed by Tara Taylor: "My grandfather used to work in this watch tower."[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register describes a McDuffie County lookout that appears to be an 82-foot (25 m) Aermotor LS-40 with a steel cab, and says it sits next to an active Georgia Forestry Commission office.[^3] Its listed coordinates, 33.445348 and -82.542365, differ from the list's point for thomson.[^3][^1]

Statewide, the Georgia Forestry Commission shifted from staffed lookouts to aerial patrols during the late 1980s.[^4]

No source describes what remains at the site today.
