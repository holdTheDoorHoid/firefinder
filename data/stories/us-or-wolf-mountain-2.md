The Wolf Mountain lookout stood on a peak in the Willamette National Forest, in Lane County, Oregon.[^1] The peak is recorded at 6,461 feet, and firelookout.com places it about 12 miles southeast of Oakridge.[^1][^2]

The register says the site began as a camp in 1920, and that a cab and ground cabin went up in 1931.[^1] A newspaper that April had described a planned 70-foot wooden tower on Wolf Mountain.[^3] In 1940 a 65-foot treated timber L-4 tower replaced the early buildings.[^1][^3]

Roy Beemer was the fire lookout at Wolf Mountain point in 1931.[^3] That October a newspaper reported that two unidentified men had attacked him on a trail not far from his lookout.[^3]

A 65-foot tower on Wolf Mountain came down during the winter before June 1939.[^3] A June report said a turnbuckle on one guy wire had come unscrewed, which let the tower fall once the wind picked up.[^3] The Civilian Conservation Corps worked on a replacement that year, and the new 65-foot tower went up in 1940.[^3]

The register says the tower burned down in 1966, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2] The Oregon Lookouts page says it was removed in 1967.[^3] The sources do not agree on how the tower ended.

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left at the summit now. A trail listing for a Wolf Mountain at 6,480 feet in the Willamette National Forest says the peak can be reached by service road 2316526, and that roads close by gate in winter.[^4] The listing does not mention the lookout, so it is unclear whether it describes this site.
