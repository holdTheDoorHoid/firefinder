Boulder Butte is a former lookout site at 7,350 feet in Chelan County, on the Wenatchee National Forest.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com places it three miles northeast of Stehekin.[^2]

A Wenatchee newspaper reported in July 1930 that an emergency station on the butte had been manned while fires burned in the Methow.[^3] WillhiteWeb says an L-4 cab was in place by September 1934.[^4] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says the cab dates to the 1930s.[^1]

The register says John Wilbur Boehm staffed the tower in the 1930s and built a storm cellar under it.[^1] WillhiteWeb's text names Jim Berry as the lookout in 1938 and 1939, while he attended college. His children shared a few stories, including that he could see the mail boat come into Stehekin.[^4] Its photo captions name a lookout "Brockman" for those two years, so the sources disagree on who held the post.[^4] We found no fire reported by this lookout in the sources we read.

Sources disagree on how the cab ended. The register says it was removed in 1959.[^1] Firelookout.com and WillhiteWeb say it was destroyed that year.[^2][^4] The Forest Lookouts page gives its status as removed.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Washington list gives the site the type Ground and the status Gone.[^5] No source we read says what, if anything, remains on the summit. WillhiteWeb has photographs captioned 2022, but its text does not describe them.[^4] The storm cellar is named in the sources, but none says whether it survives.[^1][^4]

Getting there means a boat and a hike. The trail starts at Stehekin Landing and is not reachable by road, and hikers travel about 36 miles up the lake by boat.[^6][^4] Purple Pass is 8 miles from the trailhead, and Boulder Butte lies another half mile beyond it.[^6] WillhiteWeb gives the total hiking distance as 8 miles.[^4] We found no closure or permit rule for the trail.
