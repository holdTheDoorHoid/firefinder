The Sepulga Tower stands in Conecuh County, Alabama, near the Bowles community.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register lists it as US 1601 and AL 79, and gives its elevation as 480 feet, or 146 meters.[^1] The register describes the tower as approximately 120 feet tall.[^1]

The register is brief. It says that "Little is known about this tower," and it does not say who built the tower, when it went up, who staffed it, or which fires it watched.[^1] It lists the administrator as unknown.[^1] We found no other historical record that fills these gaps.

Jackson Knight nominated the tower, and the register accepted it on June 8, 2022.[^1] The register credits the photograph to Knight.[^1]

A short YouTube video is titled "Abandoned Sepulga Lookout Tower," which suggests the tower is no longer in use.[^2] We found nothing on its present condition or whether the public may visit. Anyone interested should ask whoever manages the land before going, and should not climb it.
