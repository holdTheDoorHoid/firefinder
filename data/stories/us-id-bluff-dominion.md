The Bluff, also listed as Bluff (Dominion), had an L-4 cab in 1935.[^1] It stood in the St. Joe National Forest, in Shoshone County, Idaho.[^1][^4]

A patrol tower stood two miles south, atop Dominion Point.[^1][^2] Two sources say the cab was destroyed in 1956.[^1][^2] A third lists the site as gone that year.[^3]

The Dominion name also belongs to a lookout called Dominion Peak, which sat on the state line according to a 1933 newspaper item quoted on the Shoshone County list. That site is listed in another township, so it is a separate lookout.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to the public.
