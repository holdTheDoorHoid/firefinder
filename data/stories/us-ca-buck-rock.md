Buck Rock Lookout sits on a granite dome in the Sequoia National Forest's Hume Lake Ranger District, south of Hume Lake and just north of Kings Canyon and Sequoia National Parks.[^1] A lookout has stood here since around 1912, making the site one of the earliest fire-detection points in the area.[^1] The Forest Service originally used a "4A" style 14-by-14-foot cab design from 1917 to 1923, when it was upgraded to the newer "4AR" model.[^2] The current cab at Buck Rock dates to that 1923 rebuild, and it is one of only three surviving examples of the older 4A style still standing anywhere.[^2] Reaching the cab means climbing a suspended flight of stairs bolted to the rock itself.[^2]

Because of its age, Buck Rock is now the oldest standing lookout cab in the Sequoia National Forest.[^1] Its position gives it some of the best views in the district, overlooking Kings Canyon and Sequoia National Parks as well as most of the Hume Lake area, and that combination of easy access and scenery has made it one of the most-visited lookout sites in the Giant Sequoia National Monument.[^1]

Unlike many fire lookouts that were decommissioned decades ago, Buck Rock has stayed in active service. It is staffed full-time during fire season and, notably, remains open to the public at the same time — visitors can climb the stairs and talk with the lookout on duty rather than just view the tower from below.[^1] The Buck Rock Foundation, a volunteer nonprofit, supports the lookout's historic preservation and runs public education about fire lookouts and fire management, and also operates a webcam showing current conditions at the site.[^3] The lookout was added to the National Historic Lookout Register in 1998.[^2]

Access today is by trail within the national forest, and the stairway climb itself requires a head for heights. Because the lookout is both a working fire station and a historic structure, visitors should check current conditions and any closures with the Hume Lake Ranger District or the Buck Rock Foundation before planning a climb.
