Skookum Point is a former lookout site in Sanders County, Montana, about 26 miles north-northeast of Thompson Falls.[^1] Firelookout.com lists the Lolo National Forest and the Montana DNRC as its agencies.[^1]

The page describes a log cabin dated to 1929.[^1] It says the cabin was abandoned in 1942 and was still there in the 1980s.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list places the site at 47.96793, -115.02054, classes it as a ground structure rather than a tower, and marks it as gone.[^2] The two pages do not conflict: the cabin was standing in the 1980s, and the list now records it as gone.[^1][^2]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Skookum Point Fire Lookout Cabin, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/skookumpoint.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
