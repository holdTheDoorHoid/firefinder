Mount Kempis Tower is listed as number 57 on an online list, at Wavell, Ontario.[^1] The Canada Lookouts Ontario index also lists Mount Kempis, at Wavell.[^2]

The list is titled Ontario Fire Towers Still Standing.[^1] Its author plans to keep it as it is, and says the MNR decided in 2013 to take many of these towers down.[^1]

The list adds that the MNR no longer maintains the towers. Because climbing one is dangerous, the ministry has taken the bottom 6 m (20 ft) of ladder off these towers, and the list asks people not to climb them.[^1]

No source I found describes the tower or its site today.

[^1]: Ontario Fire Towers Still Standing, Ontario Fire Towers site, https://forestrangers.github.io/ontariofiretowers/stillstanding2.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Ontario lookouts index, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/ontario.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
