Peggy Peak is a hike-in site in Shoshone County, on St. Joe National Forest land.[^1][^2][^4]

The earliest entry, from 1926, records an alidade and a cabin at Twin Lakes.[^1] A 1934 entry lists an L-4 cab.[^1] The pages do not make clear whether those two entries describe the same structure.

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site lists Red Dennis for 1933.[^2][^3] It also names Boyd Hanna, with no year shown; the page attributes both names to Hardship & Happy Times by Bert Russell.[^2] The site names three peaks, Angle, Mosquito and Fortune, as the lookout's patrol points.[^2]

The Idaho site lists the lookout as gone but does not have a date for its abandonment or destruction.[^2] The association's Idaho list also marks it gone.[^4]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains on the peak today. The Idaho site labels the lookout hike-in, so any visit would be on foot.[^2]
