Dry Diggins Lookout is in Idaho County, on the Nez Perce National Forest, at about 2,386 meters.[^1] The first lookout here was an L-4 cab built in 1932, about a mile southeast of the present site.[^1][^3] The wind took that cab in 1967.[^1][^4]

The present tower dates to 1968: an R-6 cab on a 10-foot concrete base.[^1][^2] The Idaho lookout list gives 1968 and 1969 for the work and credits the Cedar Flats Job Corps and a district crew.[^3] A separate history page describes a cinder-block first floor instead, so the base material is disputed.[^4] Its known-staff list runs from 1932 to 1980, with gaps.[^3]

The register added the lookout on February 23, 2013.[^1] Firelookout.com says the tower has been abandoned for decades, and that its roof blew off during the winter of 2020 to 2021.[^2]

Today the Idaho lookout list shows it as hike-in with emergency status, and the register says the cab is available for emergencies.[^3][^1] With the roof gone, the sources give no reason to climb it, and none of them describe public visits.
