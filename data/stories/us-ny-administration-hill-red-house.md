Allegany State Park, in Cattaraugus County, New York, once had a fire tower on the hill behind the park's Administration Building at Red House.[^1][^2]

The New York chapter of the Forest Fire Lookout Association says the park erected it in 1928, as one of a pair of 30-foot steel towers.[^1] The other stood on Bova Mountain's western flank, above the ski area.[^1]

A historical marker in Red House tells a different story about the park's 1928 towers. According to the marker, they stood on Mount Tuscarora and near Rice Brook, so the two accounts do not match.[^2]

The association's Southern Tier list marks the Red House tower as removed.[^1] Its New York index lists the site as gone.[^4] No source I found describes what remains on the hill today.

Guided climbs of a fire tower in the park run on Sundays and select holidays, starting from the Summit Ski Hut parking lot.[^3] Volunteer guides from the Friends of Allegany State Park meet people at the foot of the tower.[^3]
