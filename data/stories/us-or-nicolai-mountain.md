Nicolai Mountain is in Clatsop County, about 18 miles east-southeast of Astoria.[^4][^2] The Oregon Department of Forestry administered the lookout.[^2][^3]

The early history is disputed. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register and firelookout.com say a 70-foot crow's nest went up in 1939 and was replaced in 1951 by a 45-foot wooden live-in tower.[^1][^2] The Oregon Lookouts page starts later and has no 1939 entry.[^3] It records the site as activated on October 30, 1942, at the Portland Filter Center, and says a 14-by-14-foot lookout cabin was built on a tower in 1947.[^3]

A 1951 item in the Clatskanie Chief describes a family visit to the summit.[^3] The keepers, Mrs. Bridgewater and her daughter, lived in the room at the top, and a two-way radio linked them to the outside world.[^3]

The Oregon Lookouts page reports that a new 40-foot tower with a 14-by-14 cabin was completed in 1961, and that the lookout was abandoned in 1963.[^3] The registers give 1965 for abandonment.[^1][^2] A 1969 district report describes a concrete building put up to house a high-band radio repeater.[^3]

The registers say the tower was removed in 1994, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2] The Oregon Lookouts page lists it as removed but gives no date.[^3]

None of the pages we read says what remains on the summit today, including whether the 1969 radio building still stands, or whether the site is open to visitors.
