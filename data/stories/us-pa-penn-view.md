A new fire tower went up on Penn View Mountain in Burrell Township, Indiana County, in 1935.[^1][^3] The register describes it as a steel M-M tower built that year, and gives the site an elevation of 2,047 feet (624 m).[^1]

Notices from early 1935, quoted on the Eastern US Forest Lookouts site, describe an 80-foot fire tower that would replace an older beacon light tower, with the beacon moved to the top of the new one.[^2] Work was to resume with CCC camp labor once the weather eased, and the foundations had already been poured.[^2] A 2000 Gazette article, drawing on a county history, says workers from the CCC's Blue Hole Camp in Somerset County began putting up the tower in February 1935.[^3]

The register says the tower was removed in 1981.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the ground is open to the public. Check who owns the land before you go looking for it.
