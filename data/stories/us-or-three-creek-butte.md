Three Creek Butte stands about ten miles south of Sisters in Deschutes County, at 5,545 feet.[^2][^1]

The register says the Brooks-Scanlon Lumber Company built a six-by-six-foot frame cabin there in the 1940s. The cabin was left unused for years before it burned in 2002.[^1][^2]

A 1967 annual report to the Oregon Department of Forestry, quoted on the Oregon lookouts page, says Brooks Scanlon would provide a backup lookout structure on the butte. The same report says the district put someone in the lookout when fire danger was high or thunderstorms posed a risk.[^3]

The register and firelookout.com both say a vandal burned the cabin in 2002.[^1][^2] The Oregon lookouts page tells that fire differently. Over Memorial Day weekend, it says, an intoxicated person whose vehicle had broken down burned the abandoned lookout's remains and set several slash piles on fire to draw notice and prompt a rescue. That person was then rescued by law enforcement and booked on a charge of burning the lookout.[^3] The accounts differ on who set the fire.[^1][^3]

The register lists Cascade Timberlands LLC as the administrator.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the cabin's dates as the 1940s to 2002 and marks it R.I.P.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
