Hurricane Mountain is a rocky summit near Keene in Essex County, and its fire watch goes back more than a century.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register says the peak has hosted a fire tower since 1909, while the Friends of Hurricane Mountain date the first observers to 1910.[^1][^3] In 1919 a steel tower replaced the first wooden structure: a 35-foot Aermotor LS-40 with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab.[^1][^2]

For roughly 50 years, observers manned the summit through the warm months, from dawn until dusk, and lived in a small cabin just below it.[^3] The Friends group says the tower shut down in 1979, once the DEC began relying on air surveillance to spot fires.[^3] Many Forest Preserve towers were taken down one at a time, and Hurricane's, which sat on Primitive forestland, came next.[^4] The Friends of Hurricane Mountain asked local government and the Adirondack Park Agency to spare it.[^4] In 2010 the state designated half an acre at the tower's base as historic, and that spared the tower.[^4][^3] Restoration followed, and by September 2015 the tower was open to hikers again.[^5]

Later NHLR visits recorded the work in progress.[^1] A 2017 report notes that a state police helicopter flew in materials for the job, and a March 2018 visit found the tower's roof needing replacement and possibly missing part of its covering.[^1] The register's visit reports also disagree about staffing.[^1] The 2017 report marks the tower as staffed, though its comments describe volunteers and paid stewards serving as interpreters, while the 2007 and 2018 reports list it as unstaffed.[^1]

The Friends of Hurricane Mountain look after three trails to the summit, and a 2018 visit found all of them well signed.[^1] The 2018 visit also noted that the Elizabethtown and Crows Clearing trailheads are not plowed in winter.[^1] Each year a statewide event lights a lantern in the cab from about 9 to 9:30 p.m., so the tower can be seen from miles away.[^3] The most recent register visit is from March 2018, so conditions may have changed since.[^1]
