Earn Knob is a peak in Craig County, Virginia, on the Jefferson National Forest, and it rises to 3,608 feet.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register also calls it Sinking Creek Mountain Tower, and says it was a neighbor to towers on Potts Mountain and Butt Mountain.[^1]

The register dates the tower only to the 1930s, and says the U.S. Forest Service administered it.[^1] The county's own history ties a New Deal work camp from the Depression years to road building, telephone service and fire towers across Craig County.[^4] The history does not name this tower.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list marks the Earn Knob tower as gone.[^2]

Earn Knob is one of the prominent points on the long ridge of Sinking Creek Mountain.[^3] Wikipedia says the area has no official trails and can be reached by Earn Knob Road at its western end.[^3] No source we checked describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today or whether visitors can reach it.
