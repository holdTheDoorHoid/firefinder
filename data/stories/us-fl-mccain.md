The Forest Lookouts index lists a McCain lookout in Taylor County.[^1] Mapcarta, a map database, places the McCain Lookout Tower in Taylor, Florida, and types it simply as a tower.[^2] The map puts the tower near the village of Steinhatchee: Steinhatchee lies 2½ miles to the southwest of it, and Jena lies 2½ miles south of it.[^2] The same map gives the spot an elevation of 23 feet (7 m).[^2]

None of the sources checked describes the site today. It is not known whether a tower, footings or any other remains stand there, and none of the sources checked says whether the land is open to visitors. Until access is confirmed, do not climb or enter any structure at the site.

[^1]: McCain - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts (eastern US index), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/mccain.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: McCain Lookout Tower Map - Taylor, Florida, USA, Mapcarta, https://mapcarta.com/20673194 (accessed 2026-10-21).
