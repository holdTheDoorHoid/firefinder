Swamp Ridge was a lookout in the Kootenai National Forest, about 21 miles south of Eureka in Lincoln County, Montana.[^1]

Firelookout.com lists a cabin and a patrol tower under the single year 1929, so the page does not say which came first.[^1]

Rex's Fire Lookout Page lists the peak as Swamp Ridge (Mtn), in the Kootenai National Forest, with the survey code 33N-26W-32.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the site at 48.58403 north and 114.99870 west, and marks the tower as gone.[^2] Firelookout.com also marks it gone.[^1] The association's list calls the structure a tower, which fits the patrol tower that firelookout.com names.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
