Canfield Fire Tower 98 was a lookout run by the Arkansas State Forestry Commission in Lafayette County.[^1] The Encyclopedia of Arkansas reports that Act 234 of 1931 created the commission, which went on to put up many more lookouts.[^2] The network peaked from the 1930s to the 1970s, with more than 120 towers in use across Arkansas.[^2] A federal survey description dated 1950 places the station a mile and three quarters south of the village of Canfield, about 100 feet east of State Route 29.[^1] The survey also gives the total height as 130 feet and describes a cab enclosed in glass atop a four-legged steel tower.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts listing marks the tower as removed.[^1] No source here describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
