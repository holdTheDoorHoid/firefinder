Mathis Lookout stands in Putnam County, Georgia, on the Oconee National Forest.[^1][^2] The U.S. Forest Service built it in 1942, and the register lists the agency as its administrator, with the Oconee Ranger District as a cooperator.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register spells the county "Putman," but a Georgia towers list gives it as Putnam.[^1][^2]

The register describes a 110-foot Aermotor tower with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab, and calls it the tallest federal fire tower remaining in Georgia.[^1] It puts the site at 662 feet (202 meters). The Georgia list says 687 feet, so the two sources disagree on elevation.[^1][^2]

The register accepted the tower on July 26, 2007, as US 705, GA 8, on a nomination by John Mayer.[^1]

Neither source says who staffed the lookout or which fires it watched. I found no account of a closure, a move or a restoration, and no source saying whether visitors can climb it today. Check with the Oconee National Forest before any visit, and do not climb a tower that is closed or posted.

[^2]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
