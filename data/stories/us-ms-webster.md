The Webster Lookout Tower stands in Webster County, Mississippi, and the register lists the Mississippi Forestry Commission (MFC) as its administrator.[^1] The register describes a 100-foot Aermotor tower with a diagonal staircase, at an elevation of 559 feet (170 meters).[^1] It gives no build date. The entry lists the build year as unknown, and we found no other source that dates the tower.[^1]

Before technology took over, fire towers were the main way to pin down where a forest fire was burning.[^2] Observers in the cabins at the top of each tower kept watch for smoke. When two towers sighted the same blaze, they passed its position to officials.[^2] In 2020, an MFC outreach forester estimated that the agency began winding down its reliance on fire towers in the late 1980s, and said it still retains many of them across the state.[^2] That is general context for the state, not a record of this tower. The sources we read do not say who watched from this tower or which fires, if any, it spotted.

The register names Jason Johns as the nominator and records the tower's registration on July 21, 2022, as US 1688, MS 48.[^1] The entry records a site visit dated 2/5/22 and notes that the MFC has stopped using the site.[^1] The register does not say when the agency stopped using the tower, what happened to the site afterward, or whether it is open to visitors today. Ask the MFC before going out to the site, and do not climb the tower.
