The Porcupine Ridge lookout site is in Morrow County, Oregon, on the Umatilla National Forest, about 20 miles west of Ukiah.[^1][^2] The register gives its elevation as 5,126 feet.[^1]

A 60-foot pole observation tower went up there in 1941, according to the register and firelookout.com.[^1][^2] A panoramic photograph of the site is dated July 22, 1935, and its caption calls it the Porcupine Ridge Lookout.[^1][^3] That date comes before 1941, so the summit may have held an earlier lookout. The sources do not say whether the 1941 tower replaced one.

The site joined the Former Fire Lookout Sites register in June 2011, as US 1079, OR 437.[^1] Forest Lookouts marks the tower as removed.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list records it as gone.[^4] The register and firelookout.com both describe the remains as ruins.[^1][^2]

The U.S. Forest Service administers the site.[^1] None of the sources we read says what is left on the summit or whether visitors can reach it. Check with the Umatilla National Forest before you go.
