Sheephorn Mountain Lookout sits atop Sheephorn Mountain in Lemhi County, Idaho, about 22 miles south-southwest of Salmon, in the Salmon-Challis National Forest.[^2][^1] A lookout has stood on the mountain since 1933, when a 9-by-9-foot wooden cab went up.[^1][^5] The tower that stands now dates to 1965, when a Region 6 flat cab replaced that first cab.[^1][^2]

An October 1965 Recorder Herald item describes the new building as two stories, with a cinderblock lower section and a wood upper section. It says the new lookout is replacing one abandoned years earlier, and that forest crews began work on August 1, expecting to finish by November 1.[^5]

Staffing records are thin. A July 1935 Recorder Herald item describes a man who had come back from the Hat Creek fire and then left for the lookout at Sheephorn peak, to stay on duty through the rest of the summer.[^5] Idaho Fire Lookouts says it does not yet have a documented staffing history for this tower.[^3]

In March 1967 the Post-Register reported that the site would be withdrawn as an administrative site, to protect the lookout building that had been constructed there.[^5] The National Historic Lookout Register added Sheephorn Mountain on October 2, 2020.[^1]

The Forest Service lists Sheephorn among several historic lookouts that remain unstaffed, and it encourages visitors to hike to the peaks.[^4] Firelookout.com says the tower is on the Recreation Rental program, but the Forest Service page does not mention rentals and no current listing surfaced in search.[^2][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site drive-up with an "Emergency Status" tag and does not explain the tag.[^3] Check with the Salmon-Challis National Forest before planning a visit, since the rental and entry status remain unclear.
