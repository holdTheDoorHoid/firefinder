The Palmdale lookout tower is in Glades County, Florida, and the Forest Lookouts site lists the Florida Forest Service as its agency.[^1] The page gives no construction date, no builder, and no history beyond those two details.[^1]

The only height I found is a map tag. OpenStreetMap gives the tower a height of 60.96 metres, which is 200 feet, and tags it as a steel lattice observation tower.[^2] Treat that figure as unverified, since OpenStreetMap is built by volunteers and its tags do not say where the height came from.[^2]

I found no source stating whether the tower still stands, whether it is staffed, or whether the public may visit. A standing label in our records is not backed by any source I could read.

Anyone planning a visit should ask the Florida Forest Service first, and should not climb a tower without its permission.

[^1]: Palmdale - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/palmdale.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Palmdale Lookout Tower, OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/8303817021 (accessed 2026-10-09).
