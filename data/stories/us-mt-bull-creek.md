Bull Creek Lookout is a standing tower in Big Horn County, Montana.[^1][^3] The National Historic Lookout Register gives its build year as 1930 and lists the tower as administered by the Northern Cheyenne Agency of the Bureau of Indian Affairs.[^1] Firelookout.com places it on the Northern Cheyenne Indian Reservation.[^2]

The Register added the tower to its listings on April 12, 2022.[^1] Its description reads only "No information available at this time."[^1] In the sources I could reach, I found no design, height, staffing history, fires, or later changes for this tower.

The sources do not say whether the public may visit. Because the tower sits on the Northern Cheyenne Reservation, anyone interested should ask the Northern Cheyenne Tribe or the Northern Cheyenne Agency before going near it.[^2]
