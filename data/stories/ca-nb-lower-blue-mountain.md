Canada's Fire Lookouts lists a New Brunswick lookout called Lower Blue Mountain, in Victoria County.[^1]

PeakVisor, a peak-listing site, lists a peak of the same name in Lorne Parish, Victoria County. It stands 595 m (1,952 ft) above sea level in the Canadian Appalachians, at 47.067955 N, 67.224995 W, and has a trail to its summit.[^2] No source links the lookout to this peak.

No source describes the lookout site today. It is not known whether a tower or any remains stand there, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^2]: Lower Blue Mountain, PeakVisor, https://peakvisor.com/peak/lower-blue-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
