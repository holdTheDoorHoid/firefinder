The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower named Suffolk (Magnolia), in the City of Suffolk, and marks it gone.[^1]

A newspaper item dated April 10, 1929, quoted on a forest lookout history page for Suffolk, says an observer in the Suffolk forest fire lookout tower reported 16 fires on March 29.[^2] Some of those fires were in Isle of Wight County, which the item says had no organized forest fire protection.[^2] The page also names the Virginia Forestry Department alongside the tower, without saying what role it played.[^2]

A Magnolia page on the same site gives Orange County, not Suffolk, and is headed National Historic Lookout Register.[^3] The two entries may be different towers, or one of them may be wrong. The sources read here do not say which.[^3]

No source I read describes what remains at the site now.
