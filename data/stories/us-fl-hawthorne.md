The Hawthorne Fire Tower is in Alachua County, Florida, and the register places it at a state forestry site on State Road 20.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register gives its elevation as 172 feet (52 meters).[^1] A regional lookout list gives only the tower's name and county, so it adds nothing further.[^2]

The sources I could reach do not say when the tower was built or who designed it.[^1][^2] The register gives no construction year, and the other page I checked says nothing about either question.[^1][^2] The state's lookout network was already growing in the 1930s, when a history of the Florida Division of Forestry counted 22 lookout towers by 1932.[^3] I found nothing that links this tower to that early group.[^1][^3]

I found no record of who staffed the tower or which fires it watched.[^1][^2]

The register added the tower on July 24, 2016, as US 1140, FL 23.[^1] Its entry places the tower at that state forestry site, and I found no later record of changes to it.[^1]

Today the register's present-tense wording places the tower at the site, but it states no condition or check date.[^1] The sources do not say whether the public can climb or visit it.[^1] The register names the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services as administrator, so anyone planning a visit should ask that agency first.[^1]
