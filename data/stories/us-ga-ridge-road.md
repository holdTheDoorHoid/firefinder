The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers places a tower called "ridge road" in Hall County, at about 34.187 N, 83.899 W.[^1] The list says that names for towers 73 and above are the author's own creation, and this entry, GA247, falls in that range, so the name is a label rather than a record of an old lookout name.[^1] The row carries no Forestry Commission code.[^1]

Hall County also has a separate entry on the list at Lula.[^1] Gainesville Times reports that the Lula tower stands beside the Commission's office, is 100 feet tall and is one of a handful still in service across Georgia.[^2] Those details concern the Lula tower, not the Ridge Road entry.

The list gives no height, design, build year, status or access for Ridge Road.[^1] We found no other source describing what remains at the site today or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Dodging decades of demolition: How the Lula fire tower has survived, Gainesville Times, https://gainesvilletimes.com/life/dodging-decades-demolition-how-lula-fire-tower-has-survived (accessed 2026-10-21).
