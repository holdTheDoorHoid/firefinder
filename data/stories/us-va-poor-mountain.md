Poor Mountain's summit rises to 3,928 feet (1,197 m).[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a fire tower on the mountain in Montgomery County, marked as gone, at 37.16249, -80.19235.[^1] Its county list gives the same entry,[^2] and the Eastern US Lookouts page for Virginia also lists a Poor Mountain tower in Montgomery County.[^3]

By our calculation, those coordinates sit about 3.2 km southwest of the summit point Wikipedia gives, so the old tower may not have stood on the summit.[^1][^4] Wikipedia describes an antenna farm at the mountain's apex, with towers serving several radio and television stations.[^4]

The Poor Mountain Natural Area Preserve lies on the mountain as well.[^5] The preserve page says preserves are open from sunrise to sunset unless otherwise posted, and it describes a small parking area with 4 miles of hiking trails.[^5] It places the preserve in Roanoke County, while the lookout lists put the tower in Montgomery County.[^1][^5]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the lookout site today, or whether it can be reached.
