The Arkansas Forestry Commission dates from 1931 and also erected many more towers.[^4]

The Glenville tower carried the number 94.[^1] It is listed in Nevada County, with the Arkansas State Forestry Commission as its agency.[^1][^3] The federal survey's listing calls it Glenville Fire Twr 94 AFC.[^2]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey described it in 1950. The cab was enclosed in glass and sat on a four-legged steel frame, and the survey gave an overall height of 112 feet (34.1 m).[^1]

The site lay about nine and a half miles north of McNeil and six miles east-southeast of Willisville, roughly 120 feet south of a gravel road.[^1]

Nothing I could read says whether the tower is still standing. No source describes the site today, or says whether the public may visit it.
