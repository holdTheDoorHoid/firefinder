The Dumas Lookout Tower is a 100-foot Aermotor tower in Tippah County, Mississippi, at about 675 feet, or 206 meters.[^1] On its parallel staircase, the register counts 14 landings and 15 flights, and says the bottom flights are gone.[^1]

The register calls it an Aermotor.[^1] Aermotor also made fire lookout towers from galvanized steel.[^2] Wikipedia says hundreds of one 7-by-7 model were used in the southeastern United States.[^2] The sources we read do not say whether this tower is that model.[^1][^2]

The register gives no build date, and we found none in the sources we read.[^1] The register names the Mississippi Forestry Commission as administering the site, yet says the commission has stopped using it.[^1]

The register's writer visited on July 17, 2022.[^1] The tower was registered as US 1684, MS 45 the next day, on the nomination of Jason Johns.[^1]

The register does not say who owns the site today or whether visitors may enter.[^1] We found no account of the fires it watched or the people who staffed it.[^1] Anyone interested should confirm who controls the land before going there, and should not climb the tower.
