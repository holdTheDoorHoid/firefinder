Fir Timber Butte is a butte in Lake County, and its entry on the Forest Lookouts page places it in the Fremont National Forest.[^1] A 1910 entry on that page describes it as a patrol observation point used often in the early teens, before permanent staffed lookouts were established, and notes that it appears many times in the early diaries of the Paisley Ranger District.[^1]

A Lake County lookout list dates a patrol lookout on the butte to the 1910s.[^2] The two sources also disagree on the section: the Forest Lookouts page gives section 01, and the list gives Section 2, both in Township 34 South, Range 18 East.[^1][^2]

No source I found describes what remains at the butte today, or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Fir Timber Butte - Forest Lookouts, oregonlookouts.weebly.com, https://oregonlookouts.weebly.com/fir-timber-butte.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Lake County Fire Lookouts (blog), https://lakelookouts.blogspot.com/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
