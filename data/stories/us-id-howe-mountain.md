The Howe Mountain lookout stood about three miles north-northwest of Clark Fork, in the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1] The site is listed at 3,835 feet.[^2] The Forest Lookouts Idaho page gives the site code 56N-2E-21.[^3]

The tower dates to around 1934 and carried an L-4 cab.[^1][^2][^3]

A September 1937 item in the Northern Idaho News, quoted on one lookout history page, says that Floyd Hamilton was visiting home from the Howe Mountain lookout that week.[^3]

All three sources give 1961 as the year the tower was destroyed.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under its Northern Idaho listings and lists it as a hike-in, so visitors reach it on foot.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout today.
