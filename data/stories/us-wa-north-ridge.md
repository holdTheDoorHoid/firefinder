North Ridge Lookout stood on a ridge in Kittitas County, Washington, at 5,922 feet (1,805 meters).[^1][^2] A lookout-history site places it in the Wenatchee National Forest.[^2]

The tower went up in 1932.[^1][^2][^3] The pole tower was 40 feet high and carried an L-4 cab at the top.[^1][^2][^3] One lookout-history site lists A. Bernardi as known lookout personnel for 1933.[^2]

The tower was taken down in 1955.[^1][^2][^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register recorded the place on August 11, 2012.[^1]

The tower is gone, but footings remain.[^1][^3] The WillhiteWeb trip report says the path runs close beside them at the ridge's highest spot.[^3] The route runs up Forest Road 4510 to a gate, then walks 0.7 miles on the road beyond it and follows a trail for about 3 miles.[^3] Photos from that visit are labeled 2017.[^3] No source we read says the route is closed, and none confirms the current rules for visiting.
