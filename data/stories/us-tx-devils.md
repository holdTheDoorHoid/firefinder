The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower called Devils in Sabine County, Texas, and marks it as gone.[^1] It gives the coordinates as 31.26653 and -93.68867.[^1]

A Shelby County Today column by Neal Murphy recalls a further fire tower in Sabine County, sited south of Red Hills Lake beside Highway 87, and says that tower no longer stands.[^2] The column gives no name for it, so it cannot be matched to this listing.[^2]

As background, a Texas Escapes history of fire towers says the Forest Service started putting up metal lookout towers at key points in the woods.[^3]

No source I read describes what remains at the Devils site today, or whether it is open to the public.[^1]

[^2]: "The Fire Towers" by Neal Murphy, Shelby County Today, https://scttx.com/articles/fire-towers-neal-murphy (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Fire Lookout Towers, Texas Escapes, http://texasescapes.com/BobBowman/Fire-lookout-towers.htm (accessed 2026-10-21).
