Pores Knob is a fire tower in Wilkes County, North Carolina, and the tnlandforms list also calls it Pores Knob (Poore).[^2] The Forest Lookouts page for the state lists it under the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources.[^1]

A state biennial report covering 1928 to 1930, which the page quotes, dates the tower to 1929. The quoted entry describes a steel tower, 47 feet (14.3 m) tall, sited south of Moravian Falls.[^1]

The tnlandforms list gives the tower the NGS pid FZ2766.[^2]

The two sources cited here do not describe what remains at the site today.[^1][^2]
