Schley Mountain is a peak in Mineral County, Montana, about 15 miles north-northwest of Lolo Pass.[^1] The firelookout.com entry names the Lolo National Forest, gives an elevation of 7,324 feet, and dates an L-4 cab to 1938.[^1] A later note on the same entry reads "Emergency 1960s," and the page does not explain it.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks the lookout as gone.[^2]

A trail guide describes a hike from a parking lot that rises gently toward a rocky summit, and says an easy off-trail scramble leads to the true peak.[^3] The same guide describes the trail as mostly wooded, with views of the Great Burn and Mission Mountains.[^3] No source I could reach describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout today.
