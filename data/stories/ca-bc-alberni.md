Alberni is a former fire lookout in Alberni-Clayoquot, British Columbia.[^1][^2] The Canada Lookouts page for Alberni places the site at 49.283 N, 124.75 W.[^1]

A list of abandoned Canadian fire lookouts includes Alberni among the sites whose towers are gone, either destroyed or taken away.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the Alberni site today, or whether it can be visited.

[^1]: Alberni, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/alberni.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Abandoned Canadian Fire Lookouts, Life is Adventure (steveamie.com), https://steveamie.com/canadian-fire-lookouts/ (accessed 2026-10-20).
