Canada Lookouts lists Rennie among its Manitoba lookouts, but the page gives only the name.[^1]

The Manitoba Historical Society notes that fire towers were once in common use across the province and that most have since been taken down.[^2] No source I found describes the Rennie site today, so what remains there, and whether the public can reach it, is unknown.

[^1]: Rennie, Canada's Fire Lookouts (Ron Kemnow), https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/rennie.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Sasaginnigak Fire Tower (Sasaginnigak Lake, Atikaki Provincial Park), Historic Sites of Manitoba, Manitoba Historical Society, https://mhs.mb.ca/docs/sites/sasaginnigakfiretower.shtml (accessed 2026-10-20).
