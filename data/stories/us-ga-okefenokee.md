The Georgia lookout list includes an entry named "okefenokee" in Ware County, at about 31.06 degrees north and 82.28 degrees west.[^1] A note under the list also mentions wildlife observation towers in the swamp.[^1]

The entry lies about 0.5 km west of the coordinates Wikipedia gives for Okefenokee Swamp Park. That distance is worked out from the two positions.[^1][^2]

The park is 12 miles south of Waycross. The Okefenokee Association has operated it as a concession since 1946, and the Association's current lease is with the Georgia Forestry Commission.[^2]

Wikipedia dates boardwalks to 1940, and says they led to a 75-foot timber observation tower.[^2] In 1965 a 90-foot steel structure replaced the original tower, and the article says it became the swamp's tallest viewing point.[^2]

The list's heading covers fire towers, but none of these three sources describes a fire lookout at the park.[^1][^2][^3]

The park's own website reports the observation tower as shut for maintenance, and the copy read carries no date.[^3]
