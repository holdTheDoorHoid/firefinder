The Forest Lookouts website lists a Suwanee tower among its Florida entries, but its page gives only the name.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register has a Levy County tower under the title Cedar Key (Suwannee) Fire Tower.[^2] Its entry gives the tower as an 80-foot Aermotor LS-40, still standing and in good condition, and lists the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services as administrator.[^2] These two sources do not show that they describe the same lookout.[^1][^2]

That page gives no status or condition for it.[^1]

[^1]: Suwanee, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/suwanee.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Cedar Key (Suwannee) Fire Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, https://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/fl/cedar-key-suwannee-fire-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
