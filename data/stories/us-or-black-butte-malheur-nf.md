Black Butte is in Grant County, about eight miles north of Mount Vernon, inside the Malheur National Forest.[^2][^1] The National Historic Lookout Register says the first structure here went up in 1936, a 20-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1] Firelookout.com dates it to 1933 instead, with the same kind of tower and a patrol point half a mile east.[^2] We could not settle the date from these sources.

The original pole legs were replaced with treated timber in 1964, and the concrete piers were redone in 1994.[^1][^2] The register also mentions a garage, added at a date it does not record.[^1]

Firelookout.com says the tower was used in emergencies for some years.[^2] The register says it is staffed as needed.[^1] The forest's August 2023 notice names the towers it staffed that summer, and Black Butte is not among them.[^3] We found no source that records a particular fire at this lookout.[^1][^2]

Firelookout.com, an undated page, says the tower is currently considered unsafe to occupy.[^2] Because the page has no date, check its status before relying on it.

The register recorded the tower on January 3, 2013, with Howard Verschoor as the nominator.[^1] We found no source saying the public may enter or climb it. Check with the Prairie City Ranger District before any visit, and treat a tower that is deemed unsafe as off limits.
