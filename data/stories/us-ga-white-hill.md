The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers places a tower named White Hill in Screven County, at 32.67910 north and 81.57128 west, and tags it with the code gfc-107.[^1] The list's header is stamped as revised on March 22, 2016, so the entry may be older than today.[^1]

White Hill is a hamlet in Screven County. Sylvania, the county seat, sits about six miles northwest of it.[^2]

Across Georgia, the Georgia Forestry Commission began shifting from staffed towers to air patrols in the late 1980s, and many of the towers were scrapped and sold.[^3]

No source here describes the White Hill site now, so this story cannot say whether a tower stands there, what remains, or whether the spot is open to visitors. Until access is confirmed, do not assume a tower at this spot is open to climb.[^1]
