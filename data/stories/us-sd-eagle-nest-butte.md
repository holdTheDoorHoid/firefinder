Eagle Nest Butte is in Jackson County, South Dakota, about seven miles south of Wanblee, on the Pine Ridge Indian Reservation.[^1] The lookout that stood there is listed as gone.[^1][^3]

The South Dakota lookout list places the site at 43.4646 north and 101.6597 west.[^3] The lookout's own page gives its elevation as 3,410 feet.[^1] Mapcarta, which lists the butte as a peak, gives 3,396 feet, so the two sources differ by 14 feet.[^2]

No source describes what remains on the butte today, or whether visitors may go up. No source gives a visitor rule either, so treat access as unknown.
