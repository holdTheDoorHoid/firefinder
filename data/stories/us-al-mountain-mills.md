The Register places the Mountain Mills Lookout Tower in Colbert County, Alabama, and lists the Alabama Forestry Commission as its administrator.[^1] The Register dates it to 1942 and describes a 100-foot tower of the International Derrick type, whose cab is 7 by 7 feet.[^1] It describes the tower as the last fire tower the Civilian Conservation Corps completed in Alabama.[^1]

A July 1942 item from the Alabama Journal announced that the tower near Barton was complete, called it a 100-foot steel tower, and said four more were under construction.[^2] The excerpt credits the state conservation department's forestry division and does not mention the CCC.[^2] The Encyclopedia of Alabama says the CCC had built 61 lookout towers in the state by 1942, which puts the Mountain Mills tower within a wider program.[^3]

We found no record of who staffed the tower or how it was used over the years. The Register records its registration on December 6, 2009, on a nomination by Thomas Kaufmann.[^1] It does not say whether the public may visit, so check with the Alabama Forestry Commission before going to the site.[^1]
