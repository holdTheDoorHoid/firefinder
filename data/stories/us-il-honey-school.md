Honey School was a fire lookout in Alexander County, on the Jonesboro Ranger District of the Shawnee National Forest.[^1] Wikidata lists the site as inside the national forest.[^5] A 1942 description calls it a Forest Service lookout about three and a half miles east of Thebes, and says visitors were welcome.[^1]

A National Archives photograph dated June 1941 is captioned "Honey School Lookout tower and residence," so the site already had a house alongside the tower by then.[^2]

The Central Illinois lookout list marks the lookout as removed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Illinois list shows it as "Tower Gone."[^3]

A trail-map app describes a maintained gravel forest road, SFR 656, that runs to what it calls an "old fire tower plot."[^4] No source describes what remains of the tower site today, or whether visitors may go there now.
