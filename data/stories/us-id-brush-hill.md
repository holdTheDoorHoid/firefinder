Brush Hill is in the Clearwater National Forest, in Idaho County, about nine miles north of Lowell.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both give its elevation as 4,620 feet.[^1][^2] The Forest Service list places it in section 22, township 34 north, range 7 east.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts files it under St. Joe - Clearwater.[^2]

The site began as a camp in the 1920s, with two patrol trees according to firelookout.com and the Forest Service.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts describes two patrol points one mile east-south-east.[^2]

Both lookout sites now list the place as ruins.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts calls it drive-up ruins.[^2] No source describes what the ruins look like today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
