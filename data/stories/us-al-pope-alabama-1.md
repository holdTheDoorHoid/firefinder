The Pope (Alabama #1) lookout stood in Colbert County, Alabama, at an elevation the register gives as 785 feet, or 239 meters.[^1] According to the register, the tower stood on privately owned land within the Thomas Wildlife Management Area.[^1]

According to the register, pictures kept by the Colbert County Forestry Office indicate that the first tower was built as a 100-foot wooden structure with exterior stairs.[^1] It was later changed to have interior stairs.[^1]

In the 1950s a lightning strike set the tower on fire, and a steel tower of standard 100-foot height took its place.[^1] The Alabama Forestry Commission administered the site, and the register added it on April 24, 2017, as US 1697, AL 12.[^1]

The tower was removed around 2000, and the land was sold back to its original owner.[^1] Only the foundations are left, and thick brush makes them hard to locate.[^1]

The site is private land.[^1] Do not go onto it without the owner's permission.

[^1]: Pope (Alabama #1) Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/al/pope-alabama-1-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
