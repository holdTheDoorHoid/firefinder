Fails (Mossy Grove) was a Texas Forest Service lookout in Walker County, northwest of Huntsville.[^1][^2]

The Forest Lookouts page reproduces a National Geodetic Survey station description for survey mark PID BL1669.[^2] The Coast and Geodetic Survey described it in 1935.[^2] The description places the station about 15 miles southeast of Madisonville, on land owned by the Texas Forest Service.[^2] It sets the mark 49.2 feet north of the northeast leg of the lookout tower, so a lookout tower already stood on the site when the description was written.[^2] The page lists the station on the USGS Crabbs Prairie quadrangle, which is dated 1976.[^2]

The state list marks Fails (Mossy Grove) as gone.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Texas Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Fails (Mossy Grove), Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/fails-mossy-grove.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
