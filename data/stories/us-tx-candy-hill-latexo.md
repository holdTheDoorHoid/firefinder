The Texas lookout list calls this site Candy Hill (Latexo). It is in Houston County, and the list marks the tower as gone.[^1] A Texas tower table gives 1958 as its date, names Nashville Bridge as the manufacturer, and lists the height as 122, with no unit printed.[^2]

The same table records the tower as removed in 1964 and says it was moved to Grapeland.[^2] The Grapeland entry gives 1964 as the date, also names Nashville Bridge and a height of 122, and lists the tower as standing 3 miles south of Grapeland. It is marked in use.[^2] The Texas lookout list also shows Grapeland as standing.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the Candy Hill site today. None says whether the public can reach it. The table places the standing tower at Grapeland, not at Candy Hill.[^2]

[^1]: Texas Lookouts, alphabetical list, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: TX expanded, Texas lookout tower table, firelookout.org, https://firelookout.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/TX-expanded.pdf (accessed 2026-10-21).
