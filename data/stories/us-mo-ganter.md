The Ganter lookout site is in Camden County, Missouri.[^1] The Central US Lookouts page gives its location code as 37N-16W-36 and files the site under the Missouri Conservation Commission.[^1]

In fiscal 1948-49 the commission acquired an 80-acre towersite there, according to its annual report as quoted on that page.[^1] In 1952 a steel tower was erected on the site.[^1]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. Nor does any source say whether the public may visit it, so anyone planning a trip should check with the state's conservation agency first.

[^1]: Ganter, Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/ganter.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
