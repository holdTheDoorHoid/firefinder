Rocky Butte is a crow's nest lookout on the Mt. Hood National Forest in Wasco County, Oregon.[^1][^3] Fire Lookout.com gives its elevation as 4,796 feet, while Oregon Hikers gives 4,775 feet.[^1][^2]

Three sources date the crow's nest to 1929.[^1][^2][^3] It sat atop a 60-foot ponderosa pine and was reached by an access ladder.[^2][^3] A Roaming Civic Mt Hood tag page that covers these crow's nests is less definite. It says there is minimal information on when Rocky Butte was built, and that it pre-dates 1930.[^8]

The 1969 date on Fire Lookout.com's Rocky Butte page belongs to a different tower.[^1] That page places this site 1.5 miles east of Grasshopper Point, which it says was removed in 1969.[^1] Oregon Hikers says the Grasshopper Point tower was destroyed in 1967.[^4] Fire Lookout.com and the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register give about 1969 for that tower's end.[^5][^6]

Fire Lookout.com says the crow's nest has long been abandoned.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as abandoned.[^7]

A November 2021 visit turned up what is left of the ladder and guy wires on the summit.[^3] Oregon Hikers reports that guy cables remain on the tree, along with the top section of the access ladder and a few planks from the nest.[^2]

Oregon Hikers marks the trails here as closed.[^4] Its page for the Rocky Butte to Grasshopper Point hike carries a July 2026 notice that the trails are closed because of activity from the Grasshopper Fire.[^4] The same page warns that the route past the site is for experienced hikers only.[^4] That notice is from July 2026, so check the current Forest Service closure before going, and stay out of any closed area.
