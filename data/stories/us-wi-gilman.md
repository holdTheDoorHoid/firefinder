The Gilman Lookout Tower was in Taylor County, Wisconsin, on ground about 1,305 feet above sea level.[^2] TopoQuest places it on the USGS Huron map sheet.[^2] OpenStreetMap has a point under the same name.[^3]

The tower shares its name with the village of Gilman, a site the Stanley, Merrill and Phillips Railway's line reached, by crossing the Yellow River, in 1902 and 1903.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list marks the tower as gone and gives its coordinates as 45.04597, -90.88914.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
