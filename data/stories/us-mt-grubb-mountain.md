Grubb Mountain is in Flathead County, in the Flathead National Forest. It was a patrol lookout about two and a half miles south of Sanders Mountain.[^1][^2]

The earliest item in these sources is a July 7, 1930 report in The Daily Inter Lake, quoted on Kemnow's page. It reported a pack string heading into Grubb Mountain in Pleasant Valley to set up a lookout camp. The paper said that setting up the camp completed the lookout and smoke-chaser system in the Kalispell district.[^1]

Kemnow's entry gives 1948 as the year the lookout was abandoned, and credits that date to a source named Kresek.[^1] The Sanders Mountain page describes a 50-foot (15 m) metal tower and cabin dated 1934. It also says "Abandoned 1948. Gone." It does not say whether that refers to the Grubb patrol or to the Sanders tower.[^2]

No source found describes what remains on the mountain today, or whether the public can reach the site.[^1][^2]
