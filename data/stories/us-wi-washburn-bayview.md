The Washburn tower stands in Bayfield County, in northwestern Wisconsin. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's statewide list calls it Washburn (Bayview), places it at about 46.74 north and 91.02 west, and marks it as standing. The list gives no national register number.[^1]

OpenStreetMap, the open map, places the Washburn Lookout Tower within a few meters of the list's coordinates, lists its elevation as 410 meters, and tags it as an observation tower.[^2] The name suggests the city of Washburn, but the sources we read do not say how far the tower sits from the city or what "Bayview" refers to.

We could not find a build date, builder, height or staffing history for this tower in the sources searched. Searches on its name returned directory entries and records of other towers, so those details remain unknown here.

Wisconsin's wider record is clearer. In 2015 the Associated Press reported that dozens of Wisconsin fire lookouts, most of them raised in the 1930s and 1940s to watch for forest fires, were being removed from service.[^3] The Department of Natural Resources said the 72 towers still in use were not worth the expense of upkeep, repairs or replacement.[^3] The report does not name this tower, so it cannot be placed among those taken out of service.

The sources read give no access rules for the Washburn tower. A travel article names the Mountain Fire Lookout Tower as the state's sole remaining fire tower open to climbers.[^4] That suggests, though it does not state, that this tower is not open to visitors. Ask the landowner before going near the site, and do not climb a tower that is closed or private.
