Blalock Lookout Tower stands in Marion County, Alabama.[^1][^2] The Register's description calls it a standard 100-foot tower, erected for the Alabama Forestry Commission in the late 1940s.[^1] Its elevation is 857 feet (261 m).[^1]

The Register's "Administered by" field, however, names Charles Daniel Mullins rather than the forestry commission.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page lists the Alabama Forestry Commission under the county name.[^2] The sources I read do not explain the difference.

The Register says the site is now private property.[^1] The Register's undated description says the tower appears to be in excellent shape, with all of its steps present and little rust on the frame. It adds that some cab windows are missing, and that the writer was not allowed to climb the tower.[^1]

The Register gives November 21, 2016 as the date the tower was registered, under US 1143, AL 70.[^1] I found no staffing history, fire record or dated condition report. Anyone interested should ask the landowner first, and should not climb it.
