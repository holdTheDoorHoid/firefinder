Antioch is a fire tower in Tipton County, Tennessee.[^1][^2] The Tennessee Landforms list gives its elevation as 430 feet, about 131 metres, and marks the tower as standing.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site has an Antioch entry under Tipton County, but it is only a heading and gives no history.[^2] We found no account of when the tower went up, what it was made of, or who watched it, and our web searches turned up nothing more on this tower.

For context, a Kingsport paper reported in October 1955 that the state had bought twelve 80-foot steel lookout towers as part of a forest-fire protection effort.[^3] The places it named did not include Tipton County, so that list does not help date this tower.[^3]

Anyone planning a visit should first ask the landowner or the Tennessee Division of Forestry, because we could not confirm who owns the site or whether the public may go there.
