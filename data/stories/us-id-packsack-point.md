Packsack Point sits in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, at 4,494 feet, in Shoshone County.[^2][^4]

Forest Lookouts gives its land location as 52N-2E-12, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association places it at 47.87037 north, 116.15685 west.[^3][^4]

In the 1930s the site had a camp with a crow's nest.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list gives its type as a crow's nest.[^4] The lookout is listed as abandoned.[^1][^2][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site hike-in, so it is reached on foot.[^2] None of the sources we read describe what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
