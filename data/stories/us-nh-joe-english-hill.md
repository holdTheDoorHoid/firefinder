A New Hampshire family hiking list counts Joe English Hill among the mountains that once had a fire tower.[^1] The hill sits on the southern edge of New Boston.[^2] The New Boston Historical Society says that, generally speaking, the hill and the nearby pond were quiet New Boston landmarks until 1942, when the bombs began falling.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's New Hampshire list shows the site with its tower gone.[^3]

The historical society adds that the nearby pond may be closed to the public today, since it sits on the land of the New Boston Air Force Station.[^2] In 2020 the station was transferred to the United States Space Force and is now called the New Boston Space Force Station.[^2]

A 2020 hiking report describes firepits near the summit, plus a viewing spot with chairs for watching raptor migration, and says the trail starts up a private driveway.[^4] I could not find a source that describes what, if anything, remains of the tower, or whether the summit itself is open to the public. Check who owns the land, and look for any posted notices, before you go.
