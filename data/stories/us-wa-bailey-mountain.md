Bailey Mountain rises in the Okanogan National Forest, about 20 miles southeast of Tonasket.[^1] The lookout here was a crow's nest with a tent cabin, dated to the 1930s.[^1][^2] Willhite's page credits that description to the Kresek inventory, and it notes that the lookout may not have stood on the summit at all.[^2] The mountain has several high points, and the page asks where exactly the lookout was.[^2]

Firelookout.com and the Forest Fire Lookout Association both list the site as abandoned.[^1][^3]

In 1945 a USGS party visited the summit and set a benchmark named BAILEY.[^2] The page notes that the party did not mention a lookout.[^2]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. Willhite's page hopes a tree may still be standing, and notes that another visit is still ahead.[^2] No source says whether the summit is open to visitors.
