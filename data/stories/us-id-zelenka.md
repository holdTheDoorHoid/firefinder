Two lookout-history websites give only one date for Zelenka Lookout, 1925, and print "unknown" beside it.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list places the site in Clearwater County, at 46.579 north and 116.083 west, and records its structure type as unknown.[^3]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site files the lookout under its St. Joe - Clearwater region, labels it a hike-in and describes it as abandoned.[^2] The firelookout.com page also says abandoned.[^1] Of the three, only the Forest Fire Lookout Association's list calls it gone.[^3]

All three sources describe the lookout as abandoned or gone.[^1][^2][^3] None of them describes the site's condition today, and none says whether the public may visit it.
