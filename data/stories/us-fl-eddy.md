The Eddy Fire Tower stands on the John Bethea State Forest in Baker County, in northern Florida, and the Florida Forest Service owns it.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register gives its model as an Aermotor LS-40, 100 feet tall, with a cab measuring 7 by 7 feet.[^1] Forest Lookouts calls it a steel tower.[^2]

The tower was registered on August 8, 2013, as US 987, FL 4.[^1] The register gives the site elevation as 133 feet (41 meters).[^1]

The sources read do not say when the tower was built, who staffed it, or whether it ever watched for a particular fire.[^1][^2]

The forest around it has had a hard time.[^3] The Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services says catastrophic wildfires have affected more than 80 percent of the property since the state acquired it.[^3] That statement does not mention the tower, so the sources do not say what happened to it in those fires.[^3]

The sources do not say whether the public may visit or climb the tower today.[^1][^2] The register describes it in the present tense, but none of the three sources gives a date for its most recent check.[^1][^3]
