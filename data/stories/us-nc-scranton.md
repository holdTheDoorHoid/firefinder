The lookout site, which cites a 1946 state biennial report, says the State Forest Service erected a 120-foot tower at Scranton that year.[^1] In the 1950s the tower also had a second job: watching the sky for aircraft as part of the Ground Observer Corps.[^1]

A list of North Carolina lookout towers places the tower in Hyde County at 35.55055, -76.44.[^2] The North Carolina Forest Service's Hyde County office sits at the Scranton Tower, on Highway 264 about 11 miles east of Belhaven, and its mailing address is 155 Fire Tower Road.[^3] Sources do not describe the tower's condition.[^3] Its name on the office page suggests it still stands.[^3]

No source says whether the public can climb the tower. The site works as a state office, so visitors should not assume they can.[^3]
