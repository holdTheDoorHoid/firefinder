Bunker's Knob is a lookout site in Wayne County, Missouri, that the Central US Lookouts site files under the Mark Twain National Forest.[^1][^2] The page gives its location code as 27N-4E-8.[^1]

No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.[^1]

[^1]: Bunker's Knob - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/bunkers-knob.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Missouri - Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/missouri.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
