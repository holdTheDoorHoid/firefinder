The Etoile (Loc 1) lookout was a Texas Forest Service tower in Nacogdoches County.[^1][^2] The state list spells the county Nacagdoches, while the Forest Lookouts page spells it Nacogdoches.[^1][^2]

The Forest Lookouts page for Etoile does not describe a tower of its own. It reproduces a National Geodetic Survey station description, written in 1949, for a survey mark on the summit of a high hill.[^2] The mark sits 89.59 feet northwest of the center of Etoile Lookout Tower No. 6, and it is stamped with the name Etoile and the year 1949.[^2] The page does not clearly tie that tower to the Loc 1 site, so we have not assumed they are the same.[^2]

The state list marks Etoile (Loc 1) as gone. A second Etoile site, Loc 2, is listed as standing.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains of Etoile (Loc 1) today, or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Texas Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Etoile, Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/etoile.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
