Grandview Lookout stands in the Kaibab National Forest in Coconino County, Arizona, close to the Grand Canyon's South Rim.[^1][^2] The site was among the original tower sites chosen in 1909, when an early national monument at the Grand Canyon was being planned, and a platform tower stood there before the current one.[^1] The Forest Service built the present Aermotor tower in 1936.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes the tower as 81 feet, Wikipedia gives 80 feet, and both put the cab at about 7 feet square.[^1][^2] A historic lookout residence also stands on the site.[^1]

The sources I could read do not say who staffed the tower, how long it was manned, or whether it ever reported a notable fire.

The National Register of Historic Places listed the tower on January 28, 1988.[^2] It was registered with the National Historic Lookout Register on May 1, 2001, after a nomination by Dave Lorenz, who directs the AZ/NM FFLA.[^1] A lookout listing site shows it as standing.[^3] I found no record of it being moved or rebuilt.

The Forest Service administers the tower, with the Tusayan Ranger District as a cooperator.[^1] Wikipedia places it off Coconino Rim Road near Twin Lakes, and the Arizona Trail passes it.[^2] The sources I could read do not say whether the tower is open to climbers or available to rent, so check with the Tusayan Ranger District before going up.
