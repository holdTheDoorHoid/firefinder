Elk Peak sits in Coos County, about 18 miles northeast of North Bend.[^1][^2] The earliest dated mention in the sources we read is a May 1916 notice about 17 miles of new telephone line, planned to connect the Elk's Peak lookout station with the Gardiner headquarters.[^3] An August 1916 notice about a trail to the peak said a lookout point was to be established there, so the two notices do not agree on whether a station already stood.[^3] In April 1925 the Siuslaw News counted Elk's peak among the sites slated for a lookout that year.[^3]

In December 1925 the Coos Bay Times listed Elks Peak, north of Allegany, as one of the lookouts Coos FPA maintained.[^3] The register dates the tower on the peak to about 1941, and firelookout.com gives 1941.[^1][^2] Neither says whether that tower replaced the earlier station.[^1][^2]

The register says the tower was abandoned and no longer stands, and firelookout.com gives the same outcome.[^1][^2] Rex's page marks the site as removed but gives no date for that.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the peak today, and we found nothing on current access. The register lists Coos FPA as the administering agency, so ask that office before planning any trip.[^1]
