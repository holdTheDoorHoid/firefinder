South Fork Mountain is a former lookout site on the Kaniksu National Forest in Pend Oreille County, Washington, at 4,203 feet.[^1] The U.S. Forest Service administers the land.[^1] A 40-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab went up there in 1935.[^1][^3]

The tower had a short working life.[^1][^3] The tower and its cab were given up in 1936, a year after construction.[^1][^3] WillhiteWeb counts five other lookout sites within four miles, and places Orwig just 2.3 miles to the north.[^3]

A Washington lookout index lists the site as removed.[^4] Another listing simply calls the lookout gone.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites entry says the lookout has come down.[^1] The register added the site on April 20, 2020.[^1]

The sources we read do not describe what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
