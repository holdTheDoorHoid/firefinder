The register places Haystack Mountain Tower Site I in Aroostook County, on State Route 163 east of Ashland and across from a picnic area.[^1] It gives the site an elevation of 671 feet, or 205 meters.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page records a new camp for the lookout man on Haystack Mountain in 1915.[^2] In a letter dated November 15, 1916, the chief fire warden for the Aroostook and Big Machias River territory reported that the lookout station had been repaired and said a new lookout ought to be built the following year.[^2]

A Maine Memory postcard of about 1915 puts Haystack Mountain at Castle Hill, roughly ten miles west of Presque Isle, and gives the mountain a height of 1,142 feet.[^3] The register's second Haystack entry, Site II, is also listed at Castle Hill.[^4] The 1,142-foot figure is far above the 671 feet the register gives for Site I, and these sources do not settle which spot each entry describes.[^3][^1]

Both register entries say the tower is no longer standing.[^1][^4] The Site I entry was registered on September 15, 2008, on the nomination of Bob Spear.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.[^1]
