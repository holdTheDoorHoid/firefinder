Jericho Mountain sits in Clearwater County.[^3] The Potlatch Timber Protective Association appears in the lookout's records.[^3] A 1915 association report already refers to the Jericho lookout while describing a new trail.[^3] Two lookout sites date the first structure, a 55-foot tree platform, to 1920.[^1][^2] The 1915 mention is earlier, so the site may have been in use before that platform went up.[^3] In February 1921 The Timberman noted that two cabins had gone up, one of them at Jericho and the other at Elk Butte.[^3] By 1932 the site had a 70-foot pole L-6 tower.[^1][^2]

The only named lookout in the sources is Alvin Schuh, in 1917.[^2][^3] An Elk River Sentinel item dated August 3, 1917, as quoted on Forest Lookouts, has him at the station keeping watch for fires.[^3]

Repairs were made to the tower in 1954.[^3] By 1965 the site was listed as ruins.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts describes the site as a drive-up ruin.[^2] We found no source that describes what remains there now. Nobody we read says whether the land is open to visitors, so check access before going.
