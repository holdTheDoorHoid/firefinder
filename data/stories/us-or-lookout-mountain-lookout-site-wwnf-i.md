The Lookout Mountain site is in the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the site as US 1124 and gives the lookout as a frame cab from the 1930s that has since disappeared.[^1] The register gives the coordinates as N 45.292090°, W 117.443400°, at 8,831 feet, and records a registration date of August 20, 2011.[^1]

firelookout.com adds a caveat: the cab may never have been used, and the site may have been only a proposed one.[^2] The Oregon Forest Lookouts index lists the site under the legal description 3S-43E-21 as Removed.[^3]

No source says whether anything of the site, such as a foundation, remains today, or whether it is open to visitors.
