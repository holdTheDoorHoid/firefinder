Acorn Butte, in Valley County, Idaho, within the Payette National Forest, got its lookout in 1933, when a 14 x 14 L-4 cab went up as a lookout ground house.[^1][^2][^3] The site is listed at an elevation of 7,760 feet.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists Ronald Mitchell as a staffer in 1965, citing a 1966 Idaho Press item.[^2] The cab was last staffed in 1975.[^2][^3] Firelookout.com says it was last used for emergencies in the 1970s.[^1]

A forest fire destroyed the cab in August 2000, according to the Forest Lookouts list.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts and firelookout.com both date the loss to 2000.[^2][^1]

No source describes what remains on the butte now, or whether the site is open to visitors. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^2]
