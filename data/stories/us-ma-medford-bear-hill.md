The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes a 70-foot steel tower on Bear Hill, in Middlesex County, that was first operated in 1919 and was no longer used after 1926.[^1]

A 2011 blog visit describes a concrete tower on the same hill, standing beside a working water holder.[^2] The blog says a wooden observation tower stood on Bear Hill before it, and that the concrete tower replaced the wooden one in 1910, the year an electric trolley station opened at Sheepfold. It credits the Friends of the Fells for that history.[^2] The writer guessed that the concrete tower was built for leisure rather than for fire watch.[^2]

The register and the blog do not agree on dates or construction. The register's steel lookout starts in 1919, while the blog places a concrete tower on the hill in 1910. The sources do not say whether they describe the same structure.[^1][^2]

Bear Hill is tucked behind the north end of the Middlesex Fells, a reservation with more than 100 miles of trails for hiking, biking, skiing and riding.[^2][^3] In 2011 the writer reached the tower after a few minutes' uphill walk from a parking lot, and climbed its spiral stairs.[^2] The sources do not say whether the concrete tower is open to climbers today.[^2][^3]
