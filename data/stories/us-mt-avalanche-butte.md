Avalanche Butte is about 28 miles northeast of Helena, on the Helena National Forest in Broadwater County, at an elevation of 7,701 feet.[^1] Rex's Fire Lookout Page begins its entry with the 1930s and the single word "camp," then describes a 10-foot wooden tower and asks whether a cab was added later.[^1]

The first dated item is a 1936 newspaper notice, quoted on Ron Kemnow's Forest Lookouts page, which places a portable short-wave set at "Avalanche Mountain," 30 miles northeast of Townsend.[^2] That sentence does not mention a tower, and it measures from a different town, so the link to this lookout rests on Kemnow's filing of it under Avalanche Butte.[^2]

Rex's entry says the lookout was abandoned in the 1950s.[^1] Kemnow's page shows "Removed" beside "National Geodetic Survey," with no date and no explanation, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^2][^3]

A 1958 U.S. Geological Survey description, reproduced on Kemnow's page, places a survey marker on the butte's highest point, and it calls that spot the site of an abandoned Forest Service observation point. The survey station sits on the county line between Broadwater and Meagher counties.[^2] The description's directions date from 1958 and should not be read as current access.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, and none says whether the public can visit the site.[^1][^2][^3]
