Cory Peak is a mountain in the Klamath Mountains, about 7,746 feet high, and it is listed in both Siskiyou and Trinity counties.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site lists the Klamath National Forest as the agency for the site.[^2]

The earliest mention we found is from 1929. A Siskiyou Daily News notice from October 3, 1929, as quoted by Forest Lookouts, names Paul Friday as being from the Cory lookout. It says he took over as fire guard at the Trumble ranch from Claude Tovrea.[^2] The next summer the same paper reported that Carl Utz had brought his wife and their two children up to the lookout on Cory Peak, where he was to be stationed through the season.[^2]

We found no source that gives the year the lookout was built, what it looked like, or when it came down. The Fire Lookout Association lists the site as gone, with its type unknown.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit, or whether the peak is open to visitors. Check with the Klamath National Forest before you go.
