The Mount Maud lookout stands near Grand Portage in Cook County.[^1][^3] The county's tower survey lists two Maud entries. It says footings from a first tower remain on the site. It lists the standing tower as a stair tower and calls it likely a Minnesota Forest Service tower.[^2] The survey gives no build date or height for either entry, and we did not confirm a builder for either in the sources we checked.

The sources part ways on the current tower's cab. The Forest Fire Lookout Association marks Maud with an asterisk, which its key defines as a tower that keeps its structure but has lost its cab.[^1] The county survey describes the tower as standing with an open cab.[^2] We could not tell which account is more recent, so we report both.

A hiking blog describes a short, fairly steep trail that starts at the parking turnaround. It warns that several platform boards are missing above the halfway point.[^3] Comments on the same page disagree. One reader found all the boards intact, and another, who hiked there in August 2016, noticed recent repairs.[^3] The blog is not an official source. We did not find a statement about who owns the tower or whether the public may enter the site. Please check with the land manager before you go, and do not climb a tower that is closed or posted as off limits.
