The Alpine lookout is a Clark County fire tower listed under the Arkansas Forestry Commission.[^1] The tower is a 110-foot (33.5 m) steel structure with a 7 by 7 foot cab at the top.[^1]

Act 234 created the Forestry Commission in 1931, and the agency built many towers of its own.[^2] Across the state, towers were shut down as forests saw more human traffic and roads spread into them, and aerial surveillance reduced the need for them.[^2]

A blog post calls the Alpine tower abandoned, and a drone video of it carries the same word in its title.[^3][^4] No source we read describes the site today, including whether the tower still stands or who may visit it.
