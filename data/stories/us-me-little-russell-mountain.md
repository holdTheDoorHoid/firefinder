Little Russell Mountain in Somerset County, Maine, got a fire lookout tower in 1920.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site lists the site under the Maine Forestry District, and its entry says a 48-foot steel tower was erected there that year.[^1] The same entry adds that the tower led to the Mucalsea Mountain station being shut down.[^1]

A 1921 view taken from the Little Russell Mountain Forest Fire Station survives in a Maine map collection.[^3] The catalogue entry says the view covers the land around the lookout.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing. Its legend marks a tower with an asterisk when the structure remains but the cab is gone, and this one carries that mark.[^2]

We found no staffing history, no named watchmen and no record of fires the tower spotted. We also found no public access information, so check with the land manager before going near the site.
