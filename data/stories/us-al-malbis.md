The Malbis Fire Tower carries the name of Malbis, an unincorporated community in Baldwin County, Alabama.[^1] The Malbis Plantation, founded in 1906 by Jason Malbis, was described as covering hundreds of acres northeast of Daphne.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts website lists a Malbis lookout among its Alabama towers.[^3] A GPS tower directory files a Malbis Fire Tower under Daphne,[^4] and OpenStreetMap maps a tower of that name as an observation tower.[^5]

Do not confuse it with the Malbis Plantation water tower.[^2] That metal tower was on SR-181, and Daphne Utilities said the water tower had not been used in over 20 years.[^2] The article says it was rusted and would soon come down.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the lookout site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
