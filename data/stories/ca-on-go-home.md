The Go Home Fire Tower is recorded as a steel tower with a square cupola, in Georgian Bay Township, Muskoka District, Ontario.[^1] The Firelookout.ca register gives its site elevation as 206 m (676 ft).[^1] The same record says the tower appears on the Huntsville topographic map sheet 31E, third edition, from 1996.[^1]

Wikipedia's article on Go Home Lake says the tower once stood on the northwest side of the lake, with a view over Go Home Bay.[^2] It describes the tower as among the final staffed towers in Southern Ontario, and says it fell under the Parry Sound Fire District in the early 1970s.[^2] The Ontario fire-tower site says the towers were grouped into fire districts, and names Parry Sound among them.[^3]

The article describes the tower in the past tense.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site today. The article says the lake has about 430 cottages, most of them reached only by water, and two marinas.[^2]
