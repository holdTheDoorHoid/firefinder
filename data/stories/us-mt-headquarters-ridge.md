The Headquarters Ridge lookout stood two miles northeast of Agency, in Sanders County, Montana, at an elevation of 4,085 feet.[^1] Firelookout.com dates its 72-foot steel tower to the 1930s.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page files the site under the National Bison Range, which the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service ran.[^2]

A February 1934 report from The Missoulian, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, describes a crew of 25 men employed on the range with road construction and also erecting three 60-foot steel towers for lookout fire stations.[^2] That report names High Point, Wild Horse Mesa and Thumb-Nob as the sites, not Headquarters Ridge, so it does not settle how tall this tower was.[^2] Firelookout.com gives the height as 72 feet.[^1]

Firelookout.com lists the tower as gone.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page marks it as removed,[^2] and the statewide lookout list also records it as gone.[^3]

The range was formerly called the National Bison Range and is now the CSKT Bison Range.[^4] Management passed from the Fish and Wildlife Service to the Confederated Salish and Kootenai Tribes in 2022, and the range has a visitor center and two scenic roads.[^4] The sources we read do not describe what, if anything, remains of the lookout on the ridge, or whether that spot is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Ask the range before you go looking for it.
