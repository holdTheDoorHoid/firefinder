The Kahill tower stood in Bradford County, Pennsylvania.[^1] The register calls it a steel Blaw-Knox tower and gives the alternate names Kahill and Cahill.[^1] It says the tower was built in 1922 and removed in 1946.[^1]

A June 2021 post on paparksandforests.org says Blaw-Knox towers were erected in 1922 and 1923.[^2] That post is about a different tower, Little Flat, so it offers context for those dates rather than proof of the Kahill dates.[^2]

The site was entered in the register on September 16, 2008, with Henry Isenberg as the nominator.[^1]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. No source we found says whether the site is open to the public.
