A Canada Lookouts entry for the Wendigo Lake site quotes the 1949 report of the Department of Lands and Forests.[^2] It records a 7.6 m (25 ft) temporary wooden lookout erected in Bayle Township, which is the source's spelling.[^2] The record does not say whether that structure is the tower listed below.

A list of Ontario fire towers still standing names it as the Bayly Tower at Wendigo Lake, near Englehart.[^1] The same list notes that the Ontario MNR had decided, as of 2013, to remove many of these standing towers.[^1] It also says the MNR no longer maintains them and asks visitors not to climb.[^1]

A post in an Ontario fire tower Facebook group calls it an abandoned fire tower by Wendigo Lake, east of Englehart.[^3] No date for the post could be read, and no source read confirms what remains at the site today.[^3]
