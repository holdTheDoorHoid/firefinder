The fire tower on Peekaboo Mountain stands in Aroostook County, Maine, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as standing.[^1] By September 1950 a Portland Press Herald item described a newly built fire tower on Peek-a-Boo Mountain, near a planned forestry wardens' headquarters at Weston, about 30 miles north of Houlton.[^2] The state approved $600 for land and buildings for that headquarters.[^2] We did not find the tower's exact build year, height or design in the sources we read.

The association's state list does not carry the asterisk it uses for towers whose cabs are gone, so the cab appears to remain.[^1]

In 2008 the Aroostook Riders ATV Club, which now maintains the tower, replaced every stair and landing. Railing, grid work and paint were still to come that year. The association's 2008 Maine report also says hikers like the spot and that the view from the tower is good.[^3]

We found no fire history or staffing records for this tower in the sources we read. We also did not find its access route or any closure notice, so check locally before you go.
