The Widdifield Fire Tower stands on Tower Drive in North Bay, in the Nipissing District of northeastern Ontario, at an elevation of 390 m (1280 ft).[^1]

The first structure on the site was a 24 m (80 ft) steel tower topped with an octagonal cab.[^2] Its round light warned pilots approaching the North Bay airfield.[^3]

Mary Kennedy staffed the lookout in the 1940s.[^3][^4]

The original was later torn down. In 1959 a 30 m (100 ft) heavy steel tower took its place.[^3][^2]

The replacement then featured in the television series The Forest Rangers, during the winter of 1965.[^3]

Ontario's fire towers were overseen by the Department of Lands and Forests until it became the Ministry of Natural Resources in 1972.[^5]

Firelookout.ca reports that the structure is now a communications tower.[^1] No source found says whether the 1959 steel tower is the structure standing there today, gives its present height, or says whether the site is open to visitors.
