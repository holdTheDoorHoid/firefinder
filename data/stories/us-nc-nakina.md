The Nakina lookout stood in Columbus County, North Carolina, near the village of Nakina.[^1][^2]

The sources differ on its size and location.[^1][^2] A North Carolina lookout index quotes a state report for 1930 to 1932.[^1] That report puts a 100-foot steel tower, with an inside stairway and a glass-enclosed cab, two miles southeast of Nakina in 1931.[^1] A 1942 Coast and Geodetic Survey description puts a four-legged steel fire tower about 0.6 mile south of the village.[^2] It was about 110 feet tall, painted gray, and had an enclosed cabin on top.[^2] We could not reconcile the two accounts, so the height and the position stay unconfirmed.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes the site today, including whether the tower still stands or whether the public may visit.[^1][^2]

[^1]: North Carolina lookouts, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/north-carolina.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Nakina Fire Tower, NGS data sheet PID EB2269, National Geodetic Survey, https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=EB2269 (accessed 2026-10-21).
