A lookout index for Ontario lists a Secord tower in Sudbury District.[^1] Its entry gives one dated event, a 1937 installation of a UHF radio-telephone set at the tower, which the index attributes to a Department of Lands and Forests report.[^1]

Across Ontario, the towers were overseen by the Department of Lands and Forests, a body that became the Ministry of Natural Resources in 1972.[^2] Through the 1960s the towers saw less and less use for aerial fire spotting, as fire-fighting crews took over that work.[^2]

No source describes this site today, including whether anything remains or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: ONTARIO lookout index, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/ontario.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Ontario's Fire Tower Lookouts, forestrangers.github.io, https://forestrangers.github.io/ontariofiretowers/ (accessed 2026-10-20).
