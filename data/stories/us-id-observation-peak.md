The Observation Peak lookout stood in Boise County, about eight miles southwest of Stanley, at 9,151 feet.[^1] A cabin was there in the 1930s.[^1][^3]

Sources differ on the national forest.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com lists the Sawtooth National Forest.[^1] Rex's Fire Lookout Page, which credits its cabin dates to Kresek, lists the Boise National Forest.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list files the cabin as a ground structure and marks it gone.[^4] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both call it abandoned.[^1][^2]

None of the five sources cited here describes what, if anything, remains of the cabin today. The peak is reached on foot.[^5] Discover Sawtooth describes a 6.5-mile hike from the Grandjean Trailhead, a 13-mile round trip, and notes that the trail enters wilderness. A permit is required, it is free, and it can be obtained along the way.[^5]
