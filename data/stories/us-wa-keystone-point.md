Keystone Point is a summit in Chelan County, Washington, about three miles southwest of Entiat.[^1] A fire-lookout page for the site dates a crow's nest here to 1934 and says it was abandoned in the 1930s.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Washington list shows the type as "Crowsnest" and the status as "Abandoned."[^2]

A hiking guide doubts that a crow's nest was ever built. It says a 1935 photograph shows none, and that there was probably only a camp and a firefinder.[^3] The same guide rates the Columbia River views highly.[^3]

Today the summit carries communication equipment, which the guide says lessens the summit experience.[^3] No source we read describes any lookout remains on the summit.

For a visit, the guide describes two trails from a parking area, and one climbs steeply to the ridge, about 1,000 feet in half a mile.[^3] The guide adds that the lot is normally kept clear through the winter.[^3]
