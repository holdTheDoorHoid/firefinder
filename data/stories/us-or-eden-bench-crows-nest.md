The Eden Bench crow's nest sits in the Wenaha Wildlife Area in Wallowa County, Oregon, at 3,852 feet (1,174 meters).[^1] The Oregon Department of Fish and Wildlife administers the land.[^1]

The register describes an old, tree-based crow's nest that is still standing above the Grande Ronde River.[^1] The site was registered on May 22, 2022, on the nomination of Mark Avolio, who also supplied a 2021 photo.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon index lists the site as a crowsnest with the status Abandoned, which does not match the register's description.[^2]

The wildlife area is open daily, with a closure from January 1 to March 31 and some exceptions.[^3] The guide's directions include a steep, narrow gravel road, and it advises against using them with passenger cars.[^3] Eden Bench Road also crosses 1.5 miles of private land marked no trespassing.[^3]

The newest evidence of the structure itself that we found is the register's 2021 photo.[^1] We found no source that says whether the lookout itself can be reached.
