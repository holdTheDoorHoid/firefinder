Max Lake Lookout is a fire tower near Max Lake, a lake in Thunder Bay District, Ontario.[^1][^2]

Most of Ontario's fire towers were built between the 1920s and the 1950s, during the peak years of forest fire protection, and they fell under the Department of Lands and Forests.[^3] That department became the Ministry of Natural Resources in 1972.[^3] From the 1960s on, aerial spotting and firefighting crews took over, and the towers saw less use.[^3]

A visitor who reached this tower in August 2025 said they were unsure of its history.[^1]

The same visitor reached the top, which means the tower was still standing.[^1] The bottom section of the ladder had been detached and was roped on below the remaining ladder.[^1] Treat that as a reason to stay on the ground. The Firelookout.ca entry does not say who owns the site or whether the public may visit.[^1]
