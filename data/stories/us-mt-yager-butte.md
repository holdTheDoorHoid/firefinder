The Yager Butte lookout stood on Yager Butte in Powder River County, Montana, about 29 miles west of Broadus, inside the Custer National Forest.[^2] A cabin went up there in 1956, and the 53-foot treated timber tower followed in 1958.[^2][^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register describes a treated timber flat-cab tower, 53 feet tall, dated to 1958, and lists it as US 1157, MT 66, registered November 27, 2016.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks the tower on Yager Butte as gone.[^3] The same list gives a relocated Yager Butte tower, at a private site named Tarter, as standing.[^3]

The sources name different places for where the tower went.[^3][^4] A Flickr caption says it was moved to Diamond Butte in 1958, and puts Yager Butte about 20 miles north of Diamond Butte.[^4] Diamond Butte holds a Forest Service lookout, and the caption says it is staffed in the summer and open to overnight visitors the rest of the year.[^4] The sources we read do not show whether that tower and the one at Tarter are the same structure.[^3][^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the original Yager Butte site.[^3]
