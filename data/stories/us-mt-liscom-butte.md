Liscom Butte Lookout stood on Liscom Butte in Powder River County, Montana, about 33 miles northwest of Brodus.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list classes it as a ground site rather than a tower, and marks it gone.[^5]

Firelookout.com records a camp on the site in 1939 and an R-6 flat cab in 1958.[^1] Both firelookout.com and the Montana lookouts page list the site under Custer National Forest.[^1][^2]

The Montana lookouts page records a May 2009 award of a contract to tear the structure down.[^2] A 2009 Arizona chapter report from the Forest Fire Lookout Association says Liscom Butte was also removed under a demolition contract, and calls it one of only two lookouts lost in the area that year.[^3] Firelookout.com instead says it was destroyed around 2009.[^1] The wording differs, but the sources agree that the structure came down in 2009.

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today. A trail guide that draws on Forest Service trail data lists a one-mile trail named Liscom Butte Lookout (trail 4428) in the Custer Gallatin National Forest, and says mountain bikes are allowed there year-round.[^4] That listing says nothing about the summit itself.
