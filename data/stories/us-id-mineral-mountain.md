Mineral Mountain stands about 10 miles north of Potlatch, Idaho.[^1] The oldest tower the sources list there was a 40-foot wooden one, dated to 1935.[^1][^2] In 1966 a steel tower went up there.[^1][^3] The Forest Lookouts site, which cites a state forester's report, says the steel tower replaced an older wooden lookout of the 4-L type.[^3] Sources describe the steel tower as 52 feet tall and an Aermotor MC-40.[^1][^2][^3] WillhiteWeb adds that it had a ground cabin below.[^2]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site lists two known staff entries: Bert Watkins for 1961, and a staffer named only as Becker for 1970.[^4]

Firelookout.com lists the tower as gone in 1981, and the Idaho Fire Lookouts site lists it as removed or gone that year.[^1][^4] WillhiteWeb says both the wooden and the steel towers were gone by then.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list also marks the site as a gone tower.[^5]

Rocky slopes on Skyline Ridge, on Mineral Mountain, lie within McCroskey State Park, which Wikipedia describes as a public recreation area.[^6] The summit is now a picnic site, with footings still visible at the summit picnic table.[^2] WillhiteWeb says the road to the top seemed to be overgrowing as of 2024, though it still goes to the summit.[^2] WillhiteWeb also reports names dated 1940 on the footings, and says that conflicts with the recorded history.[^2]

To visit, park at the pass where the spur road leaves Skyline Drive. Any vehicle can reach that spot, and the summit is one mile of walking from there.[^2]
