The Pend Oreille County Historical Society & Museum in Newport, Washington, has a fire lookout replica among its exhibits.[^1][^2] One museum listing says the replica follows Forest Service blueprints.[^1] The museum sits next to the Big Wheel and gazebo in Centennial Plaza, and its exhibits also include a 1908 depot, a sawmill and a pioneer church.[^2]

The museum is at 402 S. Washington Ave., and its listing gives (509) 447-5388 as a contact number.[^1] The listing shows weekly hours, but they are undated, and no source I read describes the replica's condition today, so call ahead before you visit.[^1]
