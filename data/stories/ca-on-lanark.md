Firelookout.ca lists a Lanark Fire Tower at 45.1175, -76.37417, with an address given as Tweed, ON.[^1] The entry gives no elevation, tower type, height or year built.[^1] It says the tower appears on the fourth edition of Pembroke topographic map 31F, dated 1996, but not on the Tower Detection System Map.[^1]

The design was straightforward: a tall structure on a high point, a resident observer, and a way to report fires to crews on the ground.[^2] At the height of the system, Ontario alone counted 325 lookouts.[^2] In forested terrain, the towers were built of wood and later steel, each with a small platform where the lookout lived.[^2]

An online list of Ontario towers still standing reports that, as of 2013, the ministry had decided to remove many of them.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at this site today, or whether the public can reach it.
