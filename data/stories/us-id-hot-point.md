Hot Point Lookout stood in Idaho County, about 18 miles east-southeast of Kooskia, on Nez Perce National Forest land.[^1][^3] It sat about two and a half miles west of Fenn Ranger Station, at an elevation of about 3,900 feet.[^1][^2] The Forest Service list places it in Section 20, Township 32 North, Range 7 East.[^3]

Its tower went up in 1933.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Sources give the tower a height of 30 feet and an L-4 type cab.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The Forest Lookouts list for Idaho County gives the same 1933 start and dates the destruction to 1957.[^4] The lookout was destroyed in 1957, a date that all four sources give.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under its Selway region and lists it as hike-in.[^2] Today, no source we read describes what remains at the site, or whether visitors may go there.
