In Aitkin County, Minnesota, the White Elk lookout was a 100-foot Aermotor stairway tower; the county survey also calls it Fire Tower 41.[^1] The Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC) erected it in 1937, and the county survey lists its owner as the Minnesota Forest Service.[^1] The survey places it roughly 3.5 miles west of Highway 169, along County 68, with White Elk Lake close by.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list marks the tower as gone.[^2] A 2010 USGS topographic map, reproduced by Pastmaps, labels a White Elk Lookout Tower.[^3] The two records do not agree.

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
