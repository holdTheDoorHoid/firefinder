Weaver Creek is listed as a lookout tower in Santa Rosa County, Florida, filed under the Choctawhatchee National Forest on a site that catalogues eastern US lookouts.[^1] OpenStreetMap carries a tower of the same name and tags it as an observation tower.[^2]

An Eglin Air Force Base history of the area says that, in the Choctawhatchee National Forest's early years, the Forest Service built fire lines, lookout towers, roads and telephone lines.[^3] The same history includes a 1922 photo of the Niceville Ranger Station, whose caption mentions a lookout tower.[^3]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything remains there or whether visitors can reach it.
