The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a standing tower at the Martin National Wildlife Refuge in Somerset County, Maryland, under the name "Martin NWR – Kedges Straits."[^1] It files the entry in a separate group from its main list of lookouts, labelled "Non-Wildland Lookouts."[^1]

The refuge takes in the northern half of Smith Island, which sits in the Chesapeake Bay.[^2] The Fish and Wildlife Service says the refuge dates to 1954, when the late Glenn L. Martin gave 2,569 acres.[^2]

I could not find a build date, a builder, or a staffing history for a tower at this site in any source I could reach. The association's entry gives only a name, a county, coordinates, the type "Tower," and the status "Standing."[^1]

The refuge's Wikipedia article says peregrine falcons have nested there every year since a nesting tower went up in 1984.[^3] No source I could reach ties that structure to the association's entry, so I cannot say whether the two are the same.[^3][^1]

Today the Fish and Wildlife Service says the refuge is off-limits to visitors so its wetlands stay protected, though boats offer a view of much of it.[^2] Given that closure, do not plan a visit or a climb to any tower there.
