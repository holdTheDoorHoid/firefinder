The Goodman Lookout in Marinette County was built in October 1939, according to the National Historic Lookout Register, which lists the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources as the administering agency.[^1] The register describes a 110-foot Wisconsin standard tower of the protected ladder type, and it added the tower to its list in June 2017.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list now marks the Goodman tower as gone.[^2] In December 2015 the DNR announced it would retire all 72 of its fire towers, and Door County Pulse reported that unsold towers would be torn down.[^3][^4] Neither of those reports names the Goodman tower.[^3][^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]
