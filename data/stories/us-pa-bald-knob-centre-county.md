A wooden fire tower stood on Bald Knob in Centre County, Pennsylvania, and the register dates it to 1909.[^1] A lookout-history site quotes a 1908 to 1909 Department of Forestry report, which said the tower had been put up for observation, triangulation and public use.[^2] The register and the lookout-history site both give 1909.[^1][^2]

Both sources place the tower in Centre County.[^1][^2] The register gives the site the registry number US 184, PA 13 and records its registration on September 16, 2008.[^1] It also says the tower is no longer standing.[^1] The lookout-history site lists the tower as removed.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.

[^1]: Bald Knob Tower Site (Centre County), Former Fire Lookout Sites register, Forest Fire Lookout Association, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/pa/bald-knob-tower-site-centre-county/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Bald Knob - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Eastern US Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/bald-knob2.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
