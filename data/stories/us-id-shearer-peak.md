Shearer Peak rises to 6,226 feet in the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1][^2] A crow's nest was built on it in 1931.[^1][^2] By 1953 the site held an L-4 cab on a 53-foot tower of treated timber.[^1][^2] One history page also notes a patrol point about a mile to the northeast.[^1]

On August 28, 1950, the Ravalli Republican reported that Jack Harbaugh had returned after three months at the lookout.[^3] Leonard Weisbeck is listed among the known staff for 1956.[^1] Roy Abbott is listed for 1971 and 1972, and that listing credits a source titled Faces of the Selway Bitterroot Wilderness.[^2]

Both history sites date the tower's destruction to 1973.[^1][^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. One history page lists it as a hike-in site.[^2]
