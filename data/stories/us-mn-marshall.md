The Marshall Lookout Tower stands in Koochiching County, Minnesota, and the National Historic Lookout Register names the Bois Forte Indian Reservation as its administrator.[^1] Survey reports describe the tower as 136 feet tall.[^1] The register says that may make it the tallest tower in Minnesota.[^1] It is also sometimes called the Bob Marshall Tower, after a well-known forester and conservationist.[^1]

The register gives no build year and no builder.[^1] It says nothing about staffing or fire spotting.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^2] The National Historic Lookout Register added it on June 10, 2019, at the nomination of Jacob Hufnagle.[^1] The register says the wooden steps had recently been replaced as of 2019.[^1]

The register gives no visiting rules. Anyone hoping to climb or visit should ask the Bois Forte tribe first.[^1]
