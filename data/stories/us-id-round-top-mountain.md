Round Top Mountain Lookout stood on Round Top Mountain, about 3 miles northeast of Hope, in Bonner County, within the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^2] The site is listed at 6,149 feet.[^1]

Records date the tower to 1935. It was a 50-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, which Forest Lookouts describes as a round timber tower.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts has a photo captioned "Round Top circa 1955," from the Stan Meyers Collection.[^2]

All three sources give 1961 as the year it was destroyed.[^1][^2][^3] Forest Lookouts gives that date as approximate, c.1961.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in and marks the lookout destroyed.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
