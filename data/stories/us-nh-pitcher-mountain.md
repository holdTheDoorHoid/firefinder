Pitcher Mountain Lookout stands on the summit of Pitcher Mountain in Stoddard, Cheshire County, on a peak that the Monadnock-Sunapee Greenway crosses.[^1][^2][^3] Wikipedia gives the mountain's height as 2,153 feet, while the Register lists the tower's site elevation as 1,823 feet, so the figures disagree.[^2][^1]

The first lookout station, from 1915, was a wooden tower with an uncovered platform.[^1][^4] In 1925 a 25-foot steel tower with a wooden cab replaced it, according to the Register and a 2025 hiking blog.[^1][^5]

In April 1941 the Marlow-Stoddard fire reached the summit.[^5] The Register says the fire destroyed the tower and that the state rebuilt it right away.[^1] The hiking blog says the fire destroyed the wooden structures on top, and that the state rebuilt the room almost at once on the same steel base.[^5] The accounts differ on how much was lost, but both say the state rebuilt quickly.

In October 1950 a state forestry official said the tower was being wired for two-way radio, as part of a system linking Mount Kearsarge, Federal Hill and Warner Hill to Concord.[^4]

The Keene Sentinel reports that the state's towers are staffed whenever fire danger is Class 3 or higher.[^3] A 2016 Register visit note says the state staffs this tower in fire season,[^1] and the 2025 blog says state employees still man it on those days.[^5]

A 2016 Register visit found the tower in good shape but in need of paint, with a piece of plywood over one window.[^1] Forest Lookouts lists a February 2015 bid notice for cab replacement and repairs, which does not show whether the work was done.[^4]

The hiking blog quotes a trailhead kiosk that describes Andorra Forest as a privately owned property under a conservation easement.[^5] The parking lot is on the northeast side of NH 123 in Stoddard.[^5] The tower is a working state lookout. The blog's author climbed it in 2025 and admired the views, but the sources do not say whether the cab is open to visitors. We list access as restricted and do not encourage climbing.
