Mount Bigelow stands at 8,434 feet in the Santa Catalina Mountains of Pima County, on the Coronado National Forest.[^1][^3] The National Historic Lookout Register dates the present tower to 1958 and describes a 53-foot metal tower with an Army-design CL-100 cab.[^1] A 2013 summary of the register describes a 14-by-14-foot wood cabin on a CL 100-106 metal tower, which it also dates to 1958.[^2] The two descriptions disagree about the cab: the register says metal, and the 2013 summary says wood.[^1][^2]

Earlier history comes from newspapers. A lookout station on the mountain reported a forest fire of several acres on the east side of the Galiuro Mountains in June 1926.[^3] In January 1929 the Forest Service called for bids on a 48-foot steel tower to replace a condemned wooden structure.[^3] A 1937 item describes a 50-foot tower and log cabin and names the lookout as Charles Noll.[^3] A 1938 item calls Charles Nolles the "Hermit of Mount Bigelow" for his 17-year seasonal vigil on the mountain.[^3] The newspapers spell his name two ways.[^3] In 1940 a newspaper named Lloyd Basteen as ranger in charge of the lookout.[^3] In 1969 a Forest Service dispatcher said that at times the lookout could not see the Rincon Mountain next door.[^3] The sources I read do not say whether any of these early structures is the one standing today.

The 2013 summary says the lookout went inactive after several antennas, two of them television antennas, were built beside it, because radiation from the antennas was considered hazardous to rangers occupying the lookout.[^2] The Forest Service did not plan to use the lookout again, so removal of the wood cabin began on 23 April 2013 and was soon finished. The metal platform was left.[^2]

The register added Mount Bigelow on January 31, 2003.[^1] The summary includes a photo of the metal platform as it looked in 2014, after the cab was removed, but I found no later source on the tower's current state.[^2] I found no source saying the public may go up, and the summary describes antennas beside the tower, so please don't climb it.[^2]
