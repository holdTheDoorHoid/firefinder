Old Blue sits on a ridge in Douglas County, about 13 miles northwest of Roseburg, at roughly 2,536 feet.[^1][^3] The Douglas FPA administered the site.[^1] The first lookout stood just below the summit, an open platform built in the 1920s on top of three 100-foot trees, and the watchman climbed up using spurs.[^1] Cabin quarters came in the 1930s.[^1]

A 1938 recommendation, quoted by the Weebly summary, proposed moving the warden station to the top of Old Blue Mountain so it could serve as a fire lookout, with a telephone line to connect it.[^4] In 1955 a new 14-by-14-foot lookout house went up on a 50-foot treated tower, at a cost the page gives as $3,841.43.[^1][^3][^4] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register and firelookout.com describe the cab as an ODF live-in cab, while the 2007 Oregon report says flat cab; none of the pages read gives the years it was staffed.[^1][^2][^3]

The lookout was judged unsafe to occupy and removed in June 2007.[^1][^4] The Oregon chapter of the Forest Fire Lookout Association listed it among three Oregon lookouts lost that year.[^2] Sources disagree about how it ended: the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register and the Weebly summary say removed, while firelookout.com says destroyed.[^1][^3][^4] The Weebly summary adds that a camera detection system was to replace it, a plan rather than a confirmed installation.[^4]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register now lists the place as a former site.[^1] None of the pages read describes what, if anything, remains on the ridge today or whether visitors can reach it, so this story does not guess.
