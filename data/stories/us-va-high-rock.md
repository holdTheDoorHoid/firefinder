High Rock is a former fire lookout site in Bland County, Virginia, at an elevation of 3,762 feet (1,147 meters).[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register listed it on October 25, 2010, as US 698, VA 3, on the nomination of Dan East.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site also places it in Bland County and marks the tower as removed.[^2]

The register says the tower is gone. Only four concrete footers and the concrete steps the metal stairs were fixed to are left.[^1] A single 20-foot length of standard tower was attached to one of the footers, and no antenna is left on it.[^1] The register adds that guy wires still support it.[^1]

The register does not say whether the public can reach the site.[^1] The Forest Lookouts entry gives no further detail.[^2]
