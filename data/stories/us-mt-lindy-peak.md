Lindy Peak rises about six miles northwest of Libby, in the Kootenai National Forest and Lincoln County, Montana, at an elevation of 4,323 feet.[^1]

The crow's nest was a platform in a tree, and firelookout.com dates the present one to 1910.[^1] A Lincoln County list on a Forest Lookouts site dates the site's start, a tree observation post, to the 1920s, which is later than 1910 and has a question mark on the year.[^3] The sources disagree, and we could not settle which date is right.[^1][^3]

The site gained a frame cabin in 1931.[^1] The page dates the abandonment to 1942.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list also shows the site as abandoned, with the type listed as Crowsnest.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors may reach it.
