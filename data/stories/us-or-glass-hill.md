Glass Hill is a 5,391-foot summit in Union County, Oregon, where a timber lookout was built in 1936.[^1][^2][^4]

A CCC crew of ten from the Hilgard camp began construction that August, according to a 1936 newspaper item.[^3]

Sources disagree on the height. The register and firelookout.com give 100 feet, a 1936 paper says 90, and a construction record says 82.[^1][^2][^3] The cab is called an L-6 in the register and a 7-by-7-foot cab on firelookout.com.[^1][^2]

Gale Mills was stationed at the lookout in 1940, and firelookout.com names Jeanine Watkins as lookout personnel in 1954.[^3][^2] The tower, cabin and garage were painted in 1954.[^3] A 1970 state forestry report says plans for 1971 included replacing the lookout with a metal tower, but the sources do not say whether that happened.[^3]

The sale date is disputed. Rex's page quotes a 1977 state report that the lookout was sold to a private buyer for $4,176, while the register and firelookout.com put the sale at auction in 1984.[^3][^1][^2] The cab was removed at some point, and a 2003 note says the tower, without its cab, fell over.[^1][^3]

Firelookout.org lists the tower as standing with its cab gone, but CherylHill's list gives the status as unknown.[^4][^5] The site is privately owned, and firelookout.com says it has no public access.[^2] The register gives a date registered of November 15, 2019.[^1] Please stay off the site.
