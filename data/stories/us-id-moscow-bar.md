Moscow Bar Lookout stood in the Clearwater National Forest, in Clearwater County, Idaho, about 10 miles east-southeast of the Canyon Ranger Station.[^1] The site sits at 5,274 feet.[^1] The name goes back to a short placer-mining attempt by a group of Moscow, Idaho men, who soon found the work unprofitable.[^2]

An L-4 cab went up there, but the sources disagree on the year. Firelookout.com and a 2016 Clearwater National Forest list give 1934, while Idaho Fire Lookouts gives 1931.[^1][^3][^4] A 20-foot L-6 tower stood about two miles northwest, which Idaho Fire Lookouts calls the patrol point.[^1][^3][^4]

Sources agree the lookout was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1][^3][^4] We found no source that describes what remains at the site today or its current access. Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site "Drive-Up Abandoned,"[^3] and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the lookout as gone.[^5]
