In March 1911, a Routt County newspaper reported that the Forest Service's plans for that year included a lookout station on Bears Ears, along with a telephone and a trail.[^1]

A Colorado lookout index lists the site in Routt County, at 40.77742, -107.24936.[^2]

The name went to a ranger district early on. District #6, called the Bears Ears District, covered the land south of the Snake River Divide and west of California Park.[^3] A Forest Service history says that in 1918, two early districts were combined to form a new Bears Ears Ranger District.[^3] Later, the Bears Ears and Hahn's Peak districts were joined under one name, the Hahn's Peak/Bears Ears Ranger District, with headquarters in Steamboat Springs.[^3]

The sources found do not describe the lookout site today. It is unclear whether a station or other structure remains there, or whether the public can reach the spot.[^1][^2]
