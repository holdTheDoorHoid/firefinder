The Minnesota Forest Service put up a lookout at Rail Prairie, south of Pillager, in 1930.[^1][^2][^3] It was an 81-foot ladder tower.[^1][^2] A local paper described it as a steel tower on a high hill in Rail Prairie township, meant to protect that stretch of the county from fire.[^3]

In August 1930 Scott Mitchell of Pine River went on duty as the towerman there.[^3]

A newspaper account dated May 2, 1931 describes a fire that swept over the area around the tower.[^3] The lookout, John Devine, was stuck in the tower for three hours while flames burned around it.[^3] He shut the windows to keep the thick smoke from getting to him.[^3]

The tower did not stay at Rail Prairie. A tower list for Morrison County says it was moved to the Motley site in Cass County in 1954, and a note from 1956 says it no longer stood.[^1][^2] The tower also went by the name Rail Mountain lookout.[^1]

The site now lies inside Camp Ripley.[^4] A 1995 report describes Phase II testing at the Rail Prairie Forestry Lookout Tower Site, recorded as 21MO81.[^4] We found no source that describes what remains above ground, or whether visitors can reach the site, so ask before going.
