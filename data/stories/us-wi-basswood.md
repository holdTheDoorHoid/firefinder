Basswood Hill sits about 17 miles north-northwest of Antigo, in Langlade County.[^1] The survey station there stands on a low wooded hill.[^1] A National Geodetic Survey station record, copied on the Forest Lookouts page, says the Forest Service described the station in 1940. The record gives the design as a standard 100-foot Forest Service tower of structural steel, with an enclosed observation platform on top.[^1]

The same record says the tower had already been moved from the site it occupied in 1934.[^1] So the tower on Basswood Hill was not standing on that earlier site.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page lists the Wisconsin Department of Conservation as the agency.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list now marks the tower as gone.[^2]

The sources we read do not describe what, if anything, remains on Basswood Hill today, or whether the public can reach it. Anyone planning a trip should check who owns the land before going.

[^2]: Wisconsin Lookouts (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/wi-co/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
