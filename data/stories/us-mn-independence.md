Independence is a small settlement with a post office in New Independence Township, Saint Louis County.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower at Independence, at about 46.96 north and 92.49 west, and marks it gone.[^2] A second Minnesota lookout list also includes Independence in Saint Louis County, with no date or other detail.[^3]

No source we checked describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
