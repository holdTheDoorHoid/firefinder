Lost Creek Ridge had a lookout about six miles east of Coolin, in Bonner County, Idaho.[^1] Its summit is listed at 6,285 feet.[^1][^3]

The tower went up in 1932. It was a 15-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2][^3] The sources do not all agree. The Forest Lookouts page, which attributes its dates to Kresek, gives 1933 and a 20-foot pole tower.[^4] The tower was abandoned in 1940.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts list the land under the Priest Lake TPA, while the Forest Lookouts page calls it a National Forest site.[^1][^2][^4]

WillhiteWeb's photo captions describe four footings at the site, one with a memorial bolted on top.[^3] The final stretch is on foot from a parking area on the ridge.[^3] WillhiteWeb describes a route that starts on Lost Creek Road off the East Side Road and needs a higher-clearance vehicle, then covers about a mile each way with roughly 900 feet of gain.[^3]

The Firelookout.org Idaho list marks the tower as gone.[^5] None of the sources we read says whether the site is closed or needs permission to visit.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
