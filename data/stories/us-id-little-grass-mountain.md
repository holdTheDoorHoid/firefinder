Little Grass Lookout stood in the Kaniksu National Forest in Bonner County, Idaho, 10 miles north-northwest of Nordman, at 5,696 feet.[^1] All three sites we read say it was built in 1934 as a 50-foot pole tower of the L-4 type.[^1][^2][^3]

The three sites agree that the tower was destroyed in 1960.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts page marks it as removed.[^3] It lists the location code 62N-5W-5 and attributes its dates to a source named Kresek.[^3]

None of the sources we read describes what remains at the site today. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page files the site as "Hike-In," which suggests it is reached on foot, but it gives no route.[^2] We found no source that says whether the site is open to the public.
