Big Knob Tower stands on Clinch Mountain in Scott County, in far Southwest Virginia.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register says its view reaches into neighboring Tennessee as well as Virginia.[^1]

The earliest dated record of the tower in these sources is a May 26, 1958 report that the Division of Forestry planned a truck trail up to it.[^2] The trail was meant to ease upkeep of the tower and its telephone line, and to make it easier for the lookout man to reach the tower.[^2] The same report says Arlin Hilton of near Dungannon had served as lookout watchman for the last two fire seasons.[^2]

A 1964 Coast and Geodetic Survey recovery note at a survey mark on the site says a lookout tower had been built there.[^2] The note does not give a construction date.[^2] The years 1930 and 1933, stamped on the survey disk, belong to the survey mark. Nothing in the available records dates the tower from them.[^2] No record gives the tower's height or cab design.[^1][^2][^3]

The Register recorded the tower on April 27, 2017, as US 1192, VA 21.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as standing.[^3]

The Register says the tower is in private hands and not open to visitors.[^1] Do not climb it or try to get up to it, and ask the owner before any visit.
