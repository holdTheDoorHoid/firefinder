The Bear Hill tower is in the Middlesex Fells Reservation.[^3][^5] The Forest Lookouts page places it in Medford, in Middlesex County,[^2] while other sources call the town Stoneham.[^3][^4]

The sources do not agree on when the tower was built.[^1][^3][^4] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes a 70-foot steel tower that came into use in 1919 and was retired after 1926.[^1] A goxplr guide says the original tower was a wooden structure that the Appalachian Mountain Club put up in the late 1890s, and that a new steel tower was finished in late 1908.[^3] The 2011 blog, citing Friends of the Fells, says a wooden tower gave way to a reinforced concrete one in 1910, the year a trolley station opened at Sheepfold.[^4] We cannot tell from these whether the register's steel lookout and the concrete tower the blog describes are the same structure.[^1][^4]

In 2011 a blog writer found a concrete tower next to a working water holder and climbed its spiral stairs.[^4]

Today the tower is closed.[^3][^5] A Middlesex Fells trail guide reports that Friends of the Fells shows it as closed.[^5] The goxplr guide says the tower was shut to visitors in 2023 for safety reasons, and that a chain-link fence went up around the base and the entrance was blocked off.[^3] It adds that several trails still offer views of the tower.[^3] The trail still reaches the foot of the tower, but the tower itself is closed.[^5]
