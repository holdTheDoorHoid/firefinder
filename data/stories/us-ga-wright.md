Montgomery County sits in east central Georgia. The county was carved out of Washington County in 1793, and its county seat is Mount Vernon.[^1] It is named for Richard Montgomery, a Continental army brigadier general who died in the 1775 assault on Quebec.[^1]

A Georgia lookout tower list has an entry for a tower named Wright in Montgomery County, at 32.09581, -82.54431, with an elevation of 283. The list gives no unit for elevation; read as feet, that is about 86 meters.[^2] Its last field reads "datasheet CL2136."[^2]

Statewide, the Georgia Forestry Commission began shifting from staffed towers to aerial patrols in the late 1980s, and many towers were scrapped or sold.[^3]

No source we found describes what, if anything, stands at the Wright site today, or whether the public can reach it.
