The Mount Gilead lookout stood in Henderson County, Tennessee, and shares its name with the unincorporated community of Mount Gilead in the same county.[^1][^3] Tennessee Landforms lists the site at 663 feet (202 m), gives the tower the number 8-11, and records the survey code FE2297.[^1]

A federal survey datasheet for that code describes a steel tower on four legs, with a cab walled in glass, roughly 100 feet (30 m) overall.[^2] The Coast and Geodetic Survey wrote that description in 1959.[^2] The datasheet places the station about 4 miles southeast of Cedar Grove.[^2]

The datasheet's history line gives 1959 as the year the survey first observed the station.[^2] That is a survey date, not a construction date.[^2]

Landforms marks the tower as removed.[^1] It is not among the towers listed as standing on March 26, 2014, in the National Register document.[^4]

The datasheet's 1959 description includes road directions to the station, but no source describes what remains at the site today or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^4]
