Signal Hill sits about 12 miles west-northwest of Custer, at 6,483 feet, and firelookout.com places it in the Black Hills National Forest.[^2] A July 1939 item from the Rapid City Journal, quoted on Forest Lookouts, placed a 30-foot wooden tower there, roughly three miles northwest of Alkali Springs, in limestone terrain.[^1]

According to the item, the tower was staffed only during emergencies, because stations on Elk Mountain and Summit Ridge already covered the same ground.[^1]

Later sources treat the site as a former tower: a 2020 SDPB guide to Black Hills towers lists Signal Hill among the former tower sites, not the ones with a standing tower.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list and firelookout.com both mark the tower as gone.[^4][^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the hilltop today, and none says whether visitors can reach it. Anyone planning to go should confirm access before setting out.
