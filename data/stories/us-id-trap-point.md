Trap Point is in Clearwater County, Idaho, on the Clearwater National Forest.[^1][^2] The Forest Service list gives its elevation as 5,748 feet.[^1]

The Forest Service describes the lookout as a cabin with a crow's nest, dated to the 1930s.[^1] Firelookout.com gives much the same account: a crow's nest and cabin from the 1930s, later abandoned.[^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts gives a different account, on a page titled Long Creek Ridge (Trap Point). It lists a camp there in 1934 and says the site was abandoned in 1941.[^3] The Forest Service list treats Long Creek Ridge as a separate camp with those dates, and it gives Trap Point no abandonment year.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association also lists the two names at different coordinates.[^4] The sources do not agree on whether the two names belong to one lookout.[^1][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Trap Point as abandoned.[^4]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, and none says whether it is open to visitors. Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as a hike-in.[^3]
