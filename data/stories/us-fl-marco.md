The Florida Forest Service built a fire lookout tower at Marco, in Collier County, in 1964.[^1] Construction was under way that February, and about 80 feet of the tower had already gone up.[^1] By early April the tower was ready for use.[^1] It was 100 feet tall and stood on U.S. 41, about 16 miles southeast of Naples.[^1]

The agency planned three towers for the county, and this one was the first.[^1] The same clipping also places the tower just east of Naples, so its direction is given two ways.[^1]

On opening day, a Friday, the tower was to be open to visitors all day, and the first 100 people to climb it were to receive "Towernaut" certificates.[^1]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can still climb a tower there.
