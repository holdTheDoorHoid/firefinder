Bathtub Mountain sits in Shoshone County on the St. Joe National Forest, about 16 miles southeast of Avery. Its elevation is 6,219 feet.[^1]

A camp is recorded here in 1919, and a log cabin went up half a mile west in 1921.[^1]

In 1931 a 35-foot pole tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab went up.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts ties the tower to a patrol point roughly two miles northwest of Pineapple Peak.[^2]

In 1950, John M. Johnson was listed for the first part of the season before he moved to Snow Peak. Reed Elwell is also named, with no date.[^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1956.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site drive-up.[^2] No source we found describes what remains at the site today.
