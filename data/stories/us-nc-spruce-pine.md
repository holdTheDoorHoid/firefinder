A North Carolina lookout-tower list places a Spruce Pine tower in Mitchell County, at 35.880 N, 82.104 W.[^1]

A Facebook group post about Mitchell County says the Spruce Pine lookout and fire tower stood on Woody's Knob in Little Switzerland and dates its construction to 1958.[^2]

A biennial report of the Department of Conservation and Development, covering 1958 to 1960 and quoted by a lookout-history site, records a tower cabin constructed at the Spruce Pine tower.[^3] The same site credits the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources with the lookout.[^3]

The post dates the tower itself to 1958.[^2] The report gives only a 1958 to 1960 span for the cabin, which is consistent with that year but does not confirm it.[^3]

No source I could read describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
