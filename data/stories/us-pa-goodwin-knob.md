The Goodwin Knob tower stood in Fayette County, Pennsylvania, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it under the registry numbers US 302, PA 131.[^1] According to the register, the tower was set up on a building in 1922 and taken down in 1987.[^1]

The register records the site as registered on September 16, 2008, with Henry Isenberg as nominator.[^1]

The register gives no coordinates for the tower, so its exact position is not known from that record.[^1] PeakVisor, a peak-listing site, describes Goodwin Knob as a hill in the Allegheny Mountains, about 1,201 feet (366 meters) above sea level. That is the hill's elevation, not the tower's.[^2] The register does not say what, if anything, remains at the site today or whether it is open to the public, so do not assume access.[^1]
