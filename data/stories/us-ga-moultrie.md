The Moultrie lookout is listed in Colquitt County, Georgia, at 31.10399, -83.79805, with a listed elevation of 301 (no unit is given; read as feet, about 92 m).[^1] The same list adds the word sunset and the code gfc-228 to the entry.[^1]

A photograph from about 1955, held by the Georgia Archives, places the Colquitt Forestry Unit behind Sunset School, and its catalogue record notes a fire tower in the background.[^3] The photo does not name the tower. Its map pin sits about 10 km (6 miles) from the point the list gives, so it is unclear whether the photo shows this lookout.[^1][^3]

The Forest Lookouts page for the site gives only the name and the county. No source here describes the site today or says whether it is open to visitors.[^2]
