A compiled list of Georgia lookout towers places a tower called "douglas" in Coffee County, at about 31.49 degrees north and 82.81 degrees west.[^1] A Forest Lookouts page for Georgia lists a "DOUGLAS" entry under Coffee County and calls the tower the 94-foot Oil Derrick fire tower.[^2] That entry gives no coordinates, so the link to the compiled list rests on the shared name and county.

In 2011 the City of Douglas sold the tower at auction for $4,010.[^2] In 2012 the city sold it again, for $2,210, and the page suggests the 2011 sale fell through.[^2]

The compiled list marks this entry "no cab", without saying what the note means, and its Google map was revised in March 2016.[^1] The same list has a second tower called "douglas", in Douglas County, which is a different site.[^1]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything stands there or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers (GA tower list with coordinates, elevation and GFC number), tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Georgia - Forest Lookouts, easternuslookouts.weebly.com, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/georgia.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
