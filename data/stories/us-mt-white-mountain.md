White Mountain Lookout stands in the Lolo National Forest, about 11 miles north-northwest of Lolo Pass.[^1][^2] The first cab on the site was an L-4 built on a 40-foot wooden tower in 1934.[^1][^2][^4]

A replacement followed: a 41-foot timber tower topped by an L-4 cab with a catwalk. The sources disagree on its year. The NHLR description gives 1953, while firelookout.com and PNWSOTA give 1954.[^1][^2][^3]

No one has staffed it since the early 1970s, and it now holds communication equipment.[^2] Its register entry dates from September 24, 2011, when the NHLR said restoration was planned.[^1] Fundraising to refurbish the tower has begun, started by district staff and other interested people.[^2]

In 2005 KLP Inc. handled both the design and installation of lightning protection for the tower.[^4]

Sources also disagree on the county. NHLR says Mineral County, while firelookout.com says Missoula County.[^1][^2]

Today visitors walk about two miles through forest, partly along a road described as permanently closed.[^3]
