The Black Rock Lookout stands in Mohave County, Arizona.[^2][^3] The Bureau of Land Management administers it through its Arizona Strip District.[^1] The Register spells the county "Mojave," which is the name of a California county. Arizona's county is spelled Mohave.[^1][^3]

The Register gives the year built as 1981.[^1] It describes a unique two-story BLM lookout design, and the lookout inventory also lists a two-story cab.[^1][^2]

The sources do not describe who staffed the lookout or whether it reported any fires.[^1]

The Register added the tower on May 15, 2022, on a nomination by Wade Luther. Its page includes a 2021 photograph courtesy of Wade Luther.[^1] The inventory lists the lookout as standing.[^2]

The two sources give different coordinates for the site. The Register gives 36.792819 and -113.751968, while the inventory gives 36.77328 and -113.80833, so the exact location is not settled.[^1][^2]

The sources do not say whether the public can visit or climb the lookout. Check with the Arizona Strip District Office before going, and do not climb a tower that is closed or private.
