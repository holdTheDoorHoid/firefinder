Flynn Butte is in Latah County, Idaho, about 12 miles east-northeast of Potlatch, in the St. Joe National Forest.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the forest a longer name, St Joe Clearwater National Forest.[^2]

The earliest structure the sources list was a crow's nest, which one history dates to about 1918.[^3] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both list 1918 as well.[^1][^2]

A July 14, 1928 Daily Panidan item names Forest Ranger W. H. Douglas and Vladimir Kinman, who was working as a fire guard at the site.[^3]

Firelookout.com says the crow's nest was abandoned in the 1930s and was still there in the 1980s.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the same 1930s date for abandonment.[^2] The Idaho list at firelookout.org places the site in its "Lookout Sites / Non-lookout Structures" section, marked Abandoned.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts says a book by Ray Kresek reported the crow's nest was still there in 2019. Its own team went looking in 2022 and found it fairly easily. Telephone wire still hung near the top of a tree, and planks lay on the ground.[^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site Hike-In, but that 2022 visit is the most recent description we found. No source says whether visitors may go there now, so check with the forest before heading out.[^2]
