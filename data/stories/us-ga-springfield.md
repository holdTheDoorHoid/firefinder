A fire lookout tower is recorded at Springfield, in Effingham County, Georgia.[^1] A list of Georgia towers gives its coordinates as 32.35336, -81.34269, its elevation as 100 (no unit is given; read as feet, that is 30.5 m), and cross-references it as BLL-64692 and GFC number 125.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register lists 29 lookouts in Georgia, and Springfield is not among them.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists two Georgia sites, neither of them at Springfield.[^3]

None of the sources read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether it is open to the public. Do not assume a tower stands there. Check with the landowner or the managing agency before going.
