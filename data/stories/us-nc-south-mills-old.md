The North Carolina lookout-tower list names a second entry, "South Mills (old)", in Pasquotank County.[^1] Its coordinates sit a little north and west of the entry simply called "South Mills" on the same list.[^1]

Forest Lookouts has one South Mills page, headed "SOUTH MILLS (NEW)".[^2] That page describes a 99-foot, 9-inch tower the State Forest Service erected in 1944, and a March 22, 2018 auction of state surplus property, where bidding opened.[^2] The page does not name an older tower, and it does not say which of the two list entries it covers.[^2]

A separate history describes a 120-foot steel tower that was removed from Knightdale, in Wake County, in 1972, stored in Clayton, and later moved to South Mills, Pasquotank County.[^3][^4] That history does not say which South Mills site the tower went to.

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the old site today, or whether the public may visit it.
