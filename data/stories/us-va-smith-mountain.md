The Smith Mountain Fire Tower is in Bedford County, Virginia.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as an Aermotor MC-39 tower with its cab floor 59 feet and 4 inches up, and says its construction date is unknown.[^1]

Its bottom two flights of stairs have been taken off, and radio equipment has been fitted to it, adding about 15 to 20 feet to its height.[^1] The Register lists the elevation as 1,498 feet, names Appalachian Power as the administrator, and lists the Smith Mountain Cooperative Wildlife Management Area as a cooperator.[^1]

A Forest Lookouts page puts the Virginia Department of Forestry in its heading, which conflicts with the Register, and it gives no dates for either arrangement.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list still shows the tower as standing.[^3] The Register accepted the tower on January 2, 2026, on a nomination by Rocco Germani, who also supplied a February 2024 photograph.[^1]

The sources read do not say who staffed the tower, what fires it watched, or whether the public may climb it.[^1][^2] Because Appalachian Power is listed as the administrator, anyone interested in the tower should ask the company before going.[^1]
