The McGarry fire tower is an old lookout recorded at McGarry, Ontario.[^1] The Canada Lookouts list places it at Virginiatown.[^2]

A provincial management statement for the McGarry Township Forest Conservation Reserve gives the reserve's size as 1,434 ha and locates it south-east of Virginiatown.[^3] The same statement reports an old fire tower in the north of what it calls Part 2.[^3] The register's entry repeats that wording, which suggests it means this tower.[^1]

A fire-tower enthusiasts' group post on Facebook describes the McGarry Tower as abandoned, placing it to the south-east of Virginiatown.[^4] That is a community post, not an official record.
