The Gillham Lookout Tower is recorded in Sevier County, Arkansas, about a mile and a quarter north-northeast of Gillham, a place on U.S. Highway 71.[^1][^3]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the tower in 1952.[^1] The structure was made of wood and rose about 120 feet (36.6 m), with a small white-painted cabin on top.[^1] The surveyors took their point at the center of that cabin.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page lists the station data under a National Geodetic Survey heading.[^1]

The spot lies north of a gravel road and a short distance west of a graveyard.[^1] The National Geodetic Survey's index files the point under the name Gillham Lookout Tower.[^2]

Nothing I could read says whether the tower is still standing. No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
