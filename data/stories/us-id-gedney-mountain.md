Gedney Mountain sits in Idaho County, in the Nez Perce National Forest, at 7,326 feet.[^1] It was a cabin on the ground rather than a tower.[^1][^5] The Forest Service list places it about 17 miles west of the Moose Creek Ranger Station and gives 1934 as the year the cabin went up.[^3]

Sources agree that the cabin was built in 1934 and burned in a forest fire that same year.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Two of them say it was never replaced.[^1][^2] The Idaho County lookout page repeats the two 1934 entries, one for the cabin and one for the fire.[^4]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page lists the site as hike-in, with its status as burned.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site now.
