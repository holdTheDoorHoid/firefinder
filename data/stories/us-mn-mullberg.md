Mullberg was a Lake County lookout.[^2] According to the author of a kjackson.us page about it, it never appeared on post-World War II lists, though the Forest Fire Lookout Association's list includes it.[^1][^2] The author found its position on a 1920 map of the Superior National Forest, on a high point less than half a mile from a lake today named Malberg.[^1] A 1916 photograph shows a primitive wooden structure, which the page calls the Mullberg tower.[^1]

The page also suggests that a tower on Kelso Mountain, built in 1927, may have replaced it.[^1]

A July 2011 visit turned up four large bolts grouped on the ground and some G9 wire buried in the leaf litter. The author found no anchors, but thought the summer growth could have concealed them.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a platform and marks it gone. Its coordinates are 47.93913, -91.12256.[^2] The kjackson page calls the structure a tower.[^1]

The bolts and wire from 2011 are the only remains described in the sources checked.[^1] Those sources describe no visit since then and do not say whether visitors can reach the site.[^1][^2]

[^2]: Minnesota Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
