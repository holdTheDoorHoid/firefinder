Ant Hill Lookout stood on a ridge above Ant Creek in Idaho County, about 18 miles southeast of Pierce, at 4,439 feet.[^1][^3]

Two workers built trail #225 from Fish Creek to the site.[^3] Louis Hartig's 1981 history of the Lochsa Ranger District, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, dates that work to 1931.[^3] Other sources date the tower itself to 1932.[^1][^2][^4]

Hartig says two carpenters poured four concrete piers for a ten-foot tower, and that an L-4 lookout cabin was assembled on top of it.[^3] Use of the site ended after the 1933 season.[^3] Hartig says lookouts set up that year on Boundary Peak and Frenchman Butte already covered nearly the same view.[^3]

The empty tower stood with rotting timbers until 1946.[^3] Hartig says it was then condemned and burned in a forest-wide sweep of unneeded buildings.[^3] The Forest Lookouts summary gives a different reason, duplicated views, but also says it was condemned and burned.[^3] The firelookout.com page and a 2016 Forest Service list both record it as destroyed in 1946.[^1][^4]

The land is national forest, and the Idaho Fire Lookouts site files Ant Hill as a hike-in site.[^1][^2] No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
