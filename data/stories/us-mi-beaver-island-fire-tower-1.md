Beaver Island Fire Tower #1 is the name the Michigan Fire Towers directory gives a lookout on Beaver Island, in Charlevoix County.[^1] The directory places it at survey point QK0852 and lists it under the Manistee National Forest.[^1] The Beaver Island Archive calls the island's tower a state tower, and it uses a different point, QK0850, as a representative point for that tower.[^2] The archive marks the identity and geometry of the two points as probable, and places QK0852 about 1.3 km southeast of QK0850.[^2]

An October 1929 newspaper item reported that a boatload of fire-tower steel had been taken to the island, and that a county fire warden would supervise a tower near St. James.[^3] The next day's item said the Department of Conservation's Fire Fighting Division was converting a federal survey tower used for triangulation, near St. James, into a standard fire tower.[^3] Two sources name different papers for the first item: the archive gives the Gobles News, and the Forest Lookouts site gives the Suttons Bay Courier.[^2][^3]

A federal survey description of QK0852, written in 1931, gives directions that mention a fire-tower sign along the way.[^3] In 1934 a Charlevoix Courier item, as the archive quotes it, reported equipment brought to the island for "a new fire tower to be erected here."[^2]

A 1978 island history map labels a state fire tower with the years 1942 to 1964.[^2] The archive calls the tower's existence well established, but doubts the 1942 to 1964 range.[^2]

A 1956 survey recovery party recorded a lookout tower built right over the QK0852 point, and said the party had seen it.[^2] The Michigan page for Beaver Island #2, which lists the point QK0850, says USGS reported that a tower had been torn down by 1966.[^4] The federal listing for QK0850 calls its lookout tower destroyed.[^2] The sources do not show whether the 1956 tower and the tower reported torn down in 1966 were one structure.[^2][^4]

No source we read describes what stands at either point today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
