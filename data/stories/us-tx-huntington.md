The Coast and Geodetic Survey described this tower in 1949 as Huntington Lookout Tower No. 14 of the state forest service.[^2] The Central U.S. page lists the agency as the Texas Forest Service.[^2]

The tower stood on the east side of U.S. Highway 69, about half a mile south of the center of Huntington.[^2] Huntington lies in Angelina County, and U.S. Route 69 runs through the city.[^3]

The surveyors recorded a four-legged steel structure about 110 feet high, or roughly 33.5 meters, with a glass-enclosed lookout cab at the top.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone, at 31.27068 and -94.57426.[^1] The Central U.S. page marks it as removed.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to the public.
