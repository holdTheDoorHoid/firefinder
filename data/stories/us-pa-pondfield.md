The Pondfield Fire Tower stands on Chestnut Ridge, beside Skyline Drive, in Fayette County's Forbes State Forest.[^1][^3] It is 80 feet tall.[^2]

The Marshal McClintock Company built it in 1935, and Civilian Conservation Corps crews erected it.[^1][^2] TribLIVE gives 1937 as the year it went up.[^3] A ground cabin for the watchman sat beside the tower.[^1]

The register says watchman jobs were seasonal, paid poorly, and usually held by farmers and housewives.[^1] Before radio towers, someone manned the tower during heavy fire seasons.[^2] Sources disagree on when the staffing ended. An inspector told TribLIVE that someone was posted there until the 1960s.[^3] The register says it was staffed until the 1970s.[^1]

By 2016 the Herald-Standard reported that the general public had been kept out of the tower for several years. It had once been a stop on the forest's annual open house.[^2] The register says the tower is now used for educational purposes, and TribLIVE says the cabin and grounds are used for historical purposes.[^1][^3] A 2016 state plan to replace up to 25 fire towers did not include Pondfield.[^2]

A December 2024 visit report in the register found the stairs in rough but usable shape and the cab ceiling caving in slightly. It says the tower and cabin need major repairs.[^1] Radio transmitters are mounted on the cab, and the visitor said that, as far as they could tell, they were not in use.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register listed the tower as US 1765 on December 5, 2023.[^1]

TribLIVE also says the tower is locked when idle and off-limits to visitors.[^3] The register says the stairs should be replaced if it ever reopens to the public.[^1] Please do not climb the closed tower.
