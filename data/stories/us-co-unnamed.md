The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Colorado list places an unnamed site in Routt County at about 40.586 north and 106.754 west.[^1] The list files it under Unknown/Undocumented Sites, and the entry gives only that name, the county and the position.[^1] The list says such a site will be moved to its lookout lists if evidence shows it was actually used as a regular lookout for wildland fire.[^1]

No source describes what remains at this site today, and nothing we read says whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Colorado Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/co/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
