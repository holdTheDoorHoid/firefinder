The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower on Pine Mountain in Cook County, at 47.89505 north and 90.32694 west, and marks it gone.[^1][^2]

TopoQuest describes Pine Mountain as a summit in Cook County, with coordinates close to the association's point and an elevation of 2,188 feet above sea level.[^3]

A separate summit, Lima Mountain, also in Cook County, once hosted a fire tower, according to Wikipedia.[^4] The association lists a Lima Mountain tower as gone too, at different coordinates.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the Pine Mountain summit, or whether the summit is open to visitors.[^2]
