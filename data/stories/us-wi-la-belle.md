The La Belle Lookout Tower stands in Menominee County, Wisconsin, on the Menominee Indian Reservation.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts site gives its land location as 29N-16E, section 32, and offers little else about it.[^1]

The Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list shows the tower as standing. The list leaves its register column blank, so it carries no National Historic Lookout Register or Fire Lookout Survey number.[^2]

We could not find when the tower was built, who built it, how tall it is, or what design it uses. We also found no account of who staffed it or of any fire it watched. Any later move, closure or restoration is unrecorded in the sources we could read.

The tower sits on the reservation.[^1] We found no public access information, so visitors should ask the Menominee tribe for permission before going near it, and should not climb it without that permission.

[^1]: La Belle, Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/la-belle.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Wisconsin lookout structures list, Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
