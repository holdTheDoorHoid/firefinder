Poplar Ridge is a former fire tower site in Braxton County, West Virginia. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it as US 1663, WV 54, with a registration date of January 26, 2017.[^1] Brian M. Powell nominated the site.[^1] The register gives its coordinates as 38.623333 north and 80.608611 west, and its elevation as 1,729 feet, or 527 meters.[^1] It names the West Virginia Conservation Commission as the administering agency.[^1] In the register's numbering, the entry that follows it is the Cabell Tower Site (US 1664).[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list marks the Poplar Ridge tower as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]
