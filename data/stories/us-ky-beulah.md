A 1947 notice in the Dawson Springs Progress, quoted on Forest Lookouts, describes a fire tower at Beulah, in Hopkins County.[^1] It said the tower offered a strong vantage for catching fires on the east side of Caldwell County.[^1]

In October 1957 the same paper reported that a new Charleston Tower had taken over from an earlier wooden tower that once stood east of Beulah along Highway 70.[^1] The new tower was twenty feet taller.[^1] The notice does not name the Beulah tower.[^1] Read together with the 1947 item, the two probably describe the same older lookout, but no source says so outright.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the Beulah site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1]

[^1]: Beulah Heights, Forest Lookouts (quoting The Dawson Springs Progress, 1947 and 1957), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/beulah-heights.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
