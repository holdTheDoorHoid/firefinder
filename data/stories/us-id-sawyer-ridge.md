The Sawyer Ridge tower stood in the Nez Perce National Forest in Idaho County, about 25 miles northeast of Riggins.[^2] The Forest Service put up a 41-foot timber tower with a 14-by-14-foot L-4 hip-roof cab and catwalk in 1949.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists David Emmons as a staffer in 1972, with a note that he was transferred at the end of the season.[^3]

The Forest Service declared the tower surplus in 1987.[^1] Bruce and Nancy Dreher bought it and set it up at their home near the Salmon River and the Slate Creek community.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register says the tower was reconstructed and faithfully restored. Firelookout.com says Bruce re-erected only the top 28 feet, so the sources disagree on how much was rebuilt.[^1][^2] The Register listed the tower on February 24, 1996, as US 163, ID 13.[^1]

Nancy Dreher now uses the cab as an art studio.[^2] The Register calls the structure the Eyeful Tower.[^1] It sits on private property, so visitors should arrange tours in advance.[^1][^3] The firelookout.org list marks the original Sawyer Ridge site as having no tower.[^4]
