Pinnacle Butte's lookout stood in Fremont County, Wyoming, 20 miles northwest of Dubois, at an elevation of 11,516 feet.[^1] Forest Lookouts lists the Washakie National Forest and then the Shoshone National Forest, while firelookout.com names only the Shoshone.[^1][^2]

The staffing record is secondhand. Forest Lookouts pairs a Pinedale Roundup interview with Charlie Bayer, dated October 21, 1965, with a note that Mont Collom, an art and music teacher at Lander High School, was the fire observer on the Pinnacle Butte station.[^2] According to the same account, Collom staffed the lookout each summer until at least 1921, the year Bayer left the district.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wyoming list records the tower as gone.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
