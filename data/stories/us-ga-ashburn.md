A lookout tower called Ashburn is listed on the Georgia tower list kept by tnlandforms.us. The list puts it in Turner County at 31.71707 north and 83.60345 west, and gives its elevation as 392.[^1] It carries two codes, BLL-41135 and gfc-519, and the list was last revised in March 2016.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts website has a page for a tower called Adams, but that page places it in Irwin County, not Turner County.[^2] The tnlandforms list also puts its Adams tower in Irwin County, so the two names belong to separate sites.[^1]

No source we found describes the Ashburn site today, so we cannot say whether any part of the tower remains or whether the ground is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Adams - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts (easternuslookouts.weebly.com), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/adams.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
