Foy's Hill is in Cecil County, near Principio Furnace.[^1] The Whitaker Iron Company presented the steel tower, and a newspaper reported that the state Department of Forestry would supervise the fire station and pay its upkeep.[^1] The tower was erected in 1924.[^1] An August 1924 newspaper described a fifty-foot tower, but the state's report for fiscal 1930 gives 60 feet, and this history follows the state's figure.[^1] A newspaper also said the tower would be equipped with phone cables, binoculars and quarters for the lookout.[^1]

In December 1925 the tower was closed for the season, and a newspaper said the fall's fire losses had been small.[^1] In 1932 Harry M. Hartenstine, of Principio, was starting his ninth year as towerman, and a November 1932 report said no fires had been reported that season.[^1]

In February 1935 a newspaper reported that the tower would move to the Elk Neck district and be raised 40 feet, to 100 feet.[^1] The sources we read do not say whether that move happened.[^1] The Forest Lookout Association's Maryland list now marks the Foy's Hill tower as gone.[^2] The state's 2007 report describes a Black Hill tower in the Elk Neck State Forest, dated to probably the late 1940s, but it does not mention Foy's Hill.[^3]

No source we read describes the Foy's Hill site today.[^1][^2][^3] We cannot say what remains there or whether the ground is open to visitors.
