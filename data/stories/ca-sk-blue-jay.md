Blue Jay is listed as a fire tower in Moose Range No. 486, Saskatchewan.[^1][^2] The Firetowers of Saskatchewan list gives its location as 53-23-52.1 X 103-23-44.0, but leaves its height, year of construction, towermen and notes blank.[^1]

Firelookout.ca gives the same position in decimal degrees, 53.39781, -103.39556.[^2] A note on its record casts doubt on both whether the tower still stands and whether the coordinates are right.[^2] The Firetowers list says it is always seeking more information and corrections.[^1]

Neither source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Firetowers of Saskatchewan (tower list page), Website Service, https://www.websiteservice.ca/firetowers_page4.htm (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Blue Jay Fire Tower, Firelookout.ca, https://firelookout.ca/lookouts/?slug=blue-jay-fire-tower (accessed 2026-10-20).
