The Kimble Ridge fire tower stood in Lawrence County, Ohio, on land of the Wayne National Forest.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the site as US 1562, OH 8, entered on September 3, 2014, at an elevation of 1,009 feet, or 308 meters.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Ohio towers marks the Kimble site as gone.[^3] A trail guide to the Vesuvius Backpack Trail says the tower once stood on a high point roughly 400 feet above the lake, and that it has since been dismantled.[^4]

The guide, which is no longer being revised, describes only concrete footings at the spot, in open ground that is overgrown in summer.[^4] It places the site at the far eastern end of the Vesuvius Backpack Trail in Wayne National Forest, about 9.9 miles along the route.[^4]
