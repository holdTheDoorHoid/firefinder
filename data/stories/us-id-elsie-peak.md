Elsie Peak sits in the St. Joe National Forest, about nine miles south of Kellogg in Shoshone County, at an elevation of 5,257 feet.[^1][^2] The listings begin in 1921 with a crow's nest and a log cabin about 1.5 miles east of the peak.[^1][^2][^3] A tower followed in 1931.[^1][^2][^3] Both pages also mention a patrol tower 2 miles east on Early Ridge.[^1][^2]

The lookout was destroyed in 1954.[^1][^2][^3] Every list marks the tower as gone or destroyed, and none describes what, if anything, is left on the peak today.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The state list places it at 47.39770 north, 116.11280 west.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as a hike-in and files it under its St. Joe - Clearwater region.[^2] The Shoshone County list gives the location as 47N-3E-29.[^3]
