The Black Creek Fire Tower is listed at Bruce Mines, Ontario.[^1] Bruce Mines is a town in the Algoma District, on Lake Huron's north shore, along Highway 17.[^2]

The tower still stands, but its cab is gone.[^1] The Firelookout.ca entry does not say whether visitors can reach it.[^1]
