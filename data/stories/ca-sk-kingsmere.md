Kingsmere Fire Tower appears on two Saskatchewan lookout lists. The Firetowers of Saskatchewan list places it at about 54.05 N, 108.44 W.[^1] The Canada Lookouts Saskatchewan index names it too, with no further detail.[^2]

A towermen list credits Jim Settee with seven towers, Kingsmere among them, in a span from 1943 to 1963.[^3]

The Department of Natural Resources began putting up towers through the province's north roughly fifty miles apart, a gap chosen so that towermen could talk to one another by radio. That is the general pattern of the network, not a record of this site.[^4]

No source reached describes the Kingsmere site today, so it is not known whether a tower or any other structure remains there, or whether the spot is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
