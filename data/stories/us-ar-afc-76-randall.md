AFC 76 is labeled Randall on the Central US Lookouts page, which lists it as a Cleveland County tower run by the Arkansas Forestry Commission.[^1] The commission dates to Act 234 of 1931, and the Encyclopedia of Arkansas says it also put up a large number of towers of its own.[^2]

The page marks the tower as removed.[^1] No source describes the site today, so what remains there, if anything, and whether visitors can reach it are both unrecorded.

Statewide, the count fell a long way.[^2] At its height Arkansas had 122 known fire towers, and the encyclopedia reports that 48 were still standing in 1994, though how many were still in service is not known.[^2]

[^2]: Forest Fire Lookouts, Encyclopedia of Arkansas, https://encyclopediaofarkansas.net/entries/forest-fire-lookouts-7905/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
