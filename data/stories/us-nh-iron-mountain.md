Iron Mountain is a peak in Bartlett, in Carroll County, New Hampshire.[^1] A New Hampshire family hiking list says the mountain once had a fire tower and gives its elevation as 2,726 feet.[^2]

An August 1939 item credited to the Portsmouth Herald, reproduced on the Eastern US Lookouts site, said towers that were being built or planned would go at the sites it listed. The Iron entry gives 2,727 and places the site in Bartlett, with Camp Swift River of Albany listed beside it.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's New Hampshire list shows the tower as gone.[^3] A hiking list describes the site as no longer standing and adds a note about artifacts, without saying what they are.[^4] That list also gives the elevation as 2,716, so the three figures differ slightly, from 2,716 to 2,727.[^4][^2][^1]

I could not find a source that describes the summit today or says whether the public may go there. Check who owns the land before you go.
