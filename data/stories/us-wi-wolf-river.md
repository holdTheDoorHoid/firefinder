The Wolf River lookout in Forest County was built in November 1939, according to the National Historic Lookout Register, which describes a 79-foot International Stacy tower with its ladder set in an inside corner.[^1] The Register lists a registration date of June 9, 2017, and names Tyler Bormann as the nominator.[^1]

The Register says the tower was crewed on and off during spring fire season until 2015, when Wisconsin shut down its lookout network.[^1] For many decades the state manned its towers from May to September, then cut staffing back to the peak season between snowmelt and green-up.[^4] The sources read do not name any fire this tower spotted or the people who staffed it.

In 2015 Wisconsin shut down the 72 towers still working.[^4] The DNR has also chosen to retire all 72 towers, is seeking new owners for them, and says unsold towers will be torn down.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Wolf River tower as standing.[^2] The sources read do not say who owns it now, whether it has been sold, or whether the public may climb it. Anyone planning a visit should check with the Wisconsin DNR first, and should not climb a tower without permission.
