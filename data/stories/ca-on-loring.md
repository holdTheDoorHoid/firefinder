Firelookout.ca places the Loring Fire Tower on East Road in Parry Sound District, Ontario, at an elevation of 272 m (892 ft).[^1] Its record lists the tower type as steel.[^1] A 1963 Department of Lands and Forests map places a steel lookout about 24 m (80 ft) tall at the same spot.[^1]

Ontario's former Department of Lands and Forests, now the MNR, set up the Parry Sound Forest Fire District in 1922.[^2] The Wikipedia article on the district lists Loring among its fire towers.[^2] The article reports that by 1969 just four of the district's towers were still staffed: Ardbeg, Go Home, Stormy Lake and Boulter.[^2] Loring is not on that list.[^2]

A 1996 topographic map shows a communications tower at this location.[^1] The record offers an educated guess that this communications tower was once a fire tower, because old fire towers were often repurposed for telecommunications.[^1]

The record also says the site now falls within the North Bay district.[^1] No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether any part of a tower remains there. No source says whether the public may visit.
