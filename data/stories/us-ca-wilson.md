OpenStreetMap lists this point as the Wilson Tower and tags it as a fire lookout and an observation tower.[^1] Its edit note says the record was updated from a College of the Siskiyous campus map.[^1] We could not find a register entry, Forest Service page or news report for a Wilson Tower at this location, and the record gives no owner, height or date.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list has only one Wilson entry, Mount Wilson, which it places in Los Angeles County at 34.22449 north and 118.05864 west.[^2] That is a different site from the one in this record, and the list does not include a Wilson Tower near this point.[^1][^2]

We found no information about who built this tower, when, or whether it still stands. We also found nothing about public access, so please do not climb it.
