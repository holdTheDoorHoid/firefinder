The Argillite Lookout Tower is in Greenup County, Kentucky.[^1] The sources I could reach do not say when it was built, how tall it is, or what it is made of.

The Register says the owner's mother worked there as a lookout from 1966 to 1969.[^1]

The Register lists the tower as privately owned by David Baldridge, who also nominated it when it was registered on January 2, 2026.[^1] A Forest Lookouts page gives its listing as "Greenup County - Kentucky Division of Forestry."[^2] The sources do not say when ownership changed, or which listing is current.[^1][^2]

The owner reports that, as of 2025, he had recently worked on the tower, including replacing its flooring.[^1] The sources I could reach say nothing about public access. Please do not visit or climb it without the owner's permission.

[^1]: Argillite Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ky/argillite-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Argillite, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/argillite.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
