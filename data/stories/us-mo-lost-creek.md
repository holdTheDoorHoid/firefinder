Lost Creek Lookout Tower stood in Wayne County.[^1] A topo-map listing places a Lost Creek Lookout Tower on the Greenville SW map sheet.[^2] The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the tower in 1963.[^1] Its description gives a steel tower with four legs and an observing room closed in at the top, about 130 feet overall, and says the U.S. Forest Service owned and operated it.[^1] Directions from that survey started at the caution light on U.S. Highway 67 in Greenville and followed county and gravel roads for several miles.[^1]

A Missouri Conservationist article gives a range of 60 to 120 feet for the state's fire towers, and says most were 100 feet tall and made by the Aermotor Company of Chicago.[^3] By that measure, the 1963 figure of about 130 feet was taller than most.[^1][^3]

The lookout history page lists the tower as removed, with no date or method given.[^1] The research found no source that says what, if anything, remains at the site today or whether the public can visit it.
