A Georgia tower list calls this lookout greenwood and places it in Henry County.[^1] The other Georgia sources do not use that name for the Henry County tower, so the tie rests on the county and the list's GA109 code. The tower was a 100-foot (30 m) Aermotor, and the sources give two model numbers for it, LS-40 and MC-40.[^2][^3]

Beginning in the late 1980s, the Georgia Forestry Commission started phasing out its tower operators, relying on an air patrol system instead.[^4] Many of the towers ended up scrapped or sold.[^4]

The 2012 report says the tower had been bought from the Georgia Forestry Commission.[^3] In 2012 the tower was still located at an abandoned Georgia Forestry Commission headquarters site in Henry County, off Highway 42 south of McDonough.[^3] A Forest Fire Lookout Association restoration grant that year went toward restoring the tower and relocating it to the Reynolds Nature Preserve near Morrow.[^3]

The Reynolds Nature Preserve belongs to the Clayton County Parks and Recreation system.[^3] A tower list entry for Reynolds, in Clayton County, carries the note "GA109-relocated," which points to the greenwood entry.[^1] The Forest Lookouts index describes the move as still to come, and the 2012 report describes a plan, so neither source confirms that the move happened.[^2][^3] No source describes the old McDonough-area site today.
