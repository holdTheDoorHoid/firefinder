Sally Mountain lies near Attean in Somerset County, and it once carried a wooden fire lookout on its summit.[^1][^2][^4]

A fire on the mountain led to a wooden tower in 1908, and a watchman's cabin was built that same year.[^3] A panoramic map from the state's fire-tower series, centered on the tower, is dated 1919.[^4] That map's archive record gives Sagadahoc County, while the Maine Memory record and the Forest Fire Lookout list both put the mountain in Somerset County, so we follow them.[^4][^1][^6]

Maine Memory holds 1932 photographs of the old tower and of the watchman's cabin.[^1][^3] According to the Maine Atlas, the tower came down at some point in the 1930s.[^2] A Maine Memory caption suggests the cabin photograph was taken around the time the tower came down.[^3]

Today the old tower site, along the last stretch of trail to the summit, holds only four pieces of metal bolted into the rock, according to a trail guide.[^5] The mountain is a hike: the guide puts the summit about three miles away, credits the Forest Society of Maine with protecting the land, and says the summit has impressive views.[^5] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone, so there is no lookout to climb.[^6]
