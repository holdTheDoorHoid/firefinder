Mount Fillmore sits in Sierra County, inside Plumas National Forest, about eight miles northeast of La Porte and twelve miles north of Downieville.[^1] The peak takes its name from a naval officer.[^1]

By August 1916 a lookout station on the mountain had been abandoned.[^2] The Sacramento Union reported that Pilot Peak offered a far better view down the Middle Fork slope, and that a new fire outlook station was going up there.[^2] By 1917 the Forest Service was staffing Pilot Peak, and an Oroville paper named its lookout man as Samuel Grambois.[^2]

The Fire Lookout Association's California list marks Mount Fillmore as a ground lookout that is now gone.[^3] No source we found describes the summit today or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
