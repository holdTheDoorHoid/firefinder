The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Fisher Peak tower in its Virginia list and marks it gone. The list gives the point as 36.56005, -80.82324.[^1][^2] On its county page the association files the point under Carroll, with Surrey (NC) written beside it.[^2]

The Piedmont Land Conservancy spells the name Fishers Peak.[^3] Its page puts the peak in the northwest of Surry County, on the North Carolina–Virginia line near the Parkway, and gives its height as nearly 3,300 feet.[^3] The two sources probably describe the same mountain, though the spellings and county names differ.[^1][^3]

The conservancy says it has conserved over 1,800 acres of the area.[^3] It describes the land as completely forested.[^3]

No source we read says what remains at the tower site, or whether the public may go there. The conservancy's page does not mention a tower. Ask the Piedmont Land Conservancy before going onto the preserve.
