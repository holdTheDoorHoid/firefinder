The Big Hill lookout stood near Retreat, in the Southampton Township woodlands of Burlington County, New Jersey.[^1] A February 1932 Courier-Post item calls it the Big Hill Fire Tower near Vincentown.[^1]

The state forestry service operated the station only part of the year. In March 1924 a new watcher took a post there.[^1] In March 1930 the tower's season opened with Clifford Prickitt in charge.[^1]

In March 1940, William Hagerthey, 23, was one of 11 South Jersey men getting their lookout towers ready for fire season.[^1] The Courier-Post put him at the 60-foot (18 m) Retreat tower and described him as the youngest of the state's fire observers.[^1] In May 1947, James Lawton was to man the tower, and that item gives its height as 90 feet (27 m).[^1]

The heights do not agree. The 1940 item says 60 feet (18 m) and the 1947 item says 90 feet (27 m), and neither says whether the tower was raised or rebuilt in between.[^1]

One watchman's accident made the news. Peter Curlis fell from the observation platform on December 16, and the paper put the drop at 35 feet (11 m).[^1] A February 1932 item reports on his injuries and recovery.[^1]

No source I could read describes what remains at the site today.[^1]
