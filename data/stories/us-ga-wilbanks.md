Wilbanks Lookout Tower is recorded in Gwinnett County. A Georgia tower list on tnlandforms.us gives the site an elevation of 1,192 feet (363 m) and carries the note "topo 1964".[^1] The list says towers 1 to 72 come from the USGS name database, and the rest were found on 1:100,000-scale USGS topographic maps and in NGS data sheets.[^1] This tower is number 60, so the list does not explain its 1964 note; that note may date a map rather than construction.[^1]

A second Georgia list, from the Eastern US Lookouts site, also includes a Wilbanks tower under Gwinnett County, with no other detail.[^2] A Georgia Department of Transportation plan for State Route 20 in Gwinnett County shows a label for the Wilbanks Lookout Tower near milepost 4.0, so the name also appears on a road-project map.[^3]

No source I found describes the tower or its site today. Whether it still stands, and whether the public can visit, are unknown from these sources.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Georgia - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/georgia.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: PDF Transmittal SR 20 GWINNETT M004052, Georgia Department of Transportation, https://mydocs.dot.ga.gov/info/designplans20/DesignPlansLibrary/M004052_0000003_CST.PDF (accessed 2026-10-21).
