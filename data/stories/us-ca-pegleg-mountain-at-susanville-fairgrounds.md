A lookout from Peg Leg Mountain now stands at the Lassen County Fairgrounds in Susanville, California.[^1][^2] The lookout began on the mountain in 1913, when the Red River Lumber Company built it and packed the materials in by horse.[^1] The U.S. Forest Service took over its operations in the 1920s.[^1]

Cal Fire replaced the mountain lookout in 1984 with a new structure, prefabricated and assembled with prison inmate labor.[^2] Lassen News reports that the old lookout was taken apart, moved to Smokey Bear Park, which is located at the Lassen County Fairgrounds, and reassembled there.[^1]

The sources disagree about what reached the fairgrounds. The register says the 1920s lookout went there, and that a replica was on display by 2008.[^2] A Peakbagging photo caption says the building removed from the mountain is now at the fairgrounds.[^3] A 1984 notice quoted by the Forest Lookouts site had planned a replica, with much of the salvaged material going into it.[^4] A 1985 fair notice in the same account says the old lookout had been reassembled there as a working exhibit.[^4] I could not settle which account is right, so the building there may be the original, a replica, or a rebuild that uses both.

The tower was condemned several years before 2023 because it needed repairs.[^1][^5] Restoration began on June 16, 2023, with a new roof and rebuilt, reinforced and painted tower and cab.[^1] Cal Fire said the project was complete and that the lookout would reopen to the public on July 22, 2023, with a ribbon cutting that day.[^1] A NewsBreak report from that summer says the lookout had been closed for a long time because it was condemned, and that it has now been restored.[^5]

I found no report after 2023 on whether it is open today, so check with Cal Fire or the fairgrounds before you go. The mountain kept its own tower, built in 1984; the register says the Lassen National Forest was staffing that lookout as of 2016.[^2]
