Brasstown Bald is Georgia's highest mountain, in Union County within the Chattahoochee National Forest.[^1][^3]

A lookout structure was already on the summit when Asberry Smith started as a lookout in 1917, he recalled in a 1973 Atlanta Constitution interview. He later helped build a second, wooden tower.[^3] The sources do not agree on when the first tower went up.[^1][^2] Georgia Highpoint says the Civilian Conservation Corps erected the first observation tower in 1935.[^2] The Eastern US lookouts listing gives 1939 for a lookout tower.[^1] Smith remembered that the CCC built a rock tower, which the Forest Service later tore down and replaced with a steel one.[^3]

The Georgia ForestWatch newsletter from winter 2023 describes the present fire lookout as staffed only rarely, if at all.[^3]

In 1965 the lookout was replaced, and the old tower was moved to Southern Forest World in Ware County.[^1][^2] A Georgia tower list places a relocated Brasstown tower at that museum.[^4] Georgia Highpoint gives both 1965 and 1966 for the present observation tower, while the 2023 newsletter says the circular tower visitors see today was completed in 1965.[^2][^3]

Facilities at the visitor center include restrooms, a theater, observation decks, exhibits and a lookout tower.[^2] The center opens every day from Memorial Day through October, with weekend openings in the shoulder seasons.[^2] From the parking area you must walk a paved half-mile trail to the visitor center, and a shuttle bus runs from the lot while the center is open.[^2] The park entrance fee covers the bus ride, and climbing the tower carries a small fee.[^2][^1] From November through April, if the road is icy, the gate three miles down at Highway 180 is closed.[^2]
