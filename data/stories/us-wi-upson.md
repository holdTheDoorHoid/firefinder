The Upson lookout stood in Iron County in northern Wisconsin, at a spot the lookout-history site records as township 46 north, range 1 east, section 32.[^1] Pastmaps, a site that sells prints of old USGS maps, has two descriptions that name the tower.[^3][^4] One describes a sheet labelled 1940 and names the Upson Fire Tower.[^3] The other describes a 1956 sheet centred on Saxon and says the land climbs toward the Upson Lookout Tower.[^4] That description adds that the tower looks over a shift into denser woods, dotted with Weber Lake and Upson Lake.[^4]

OpenStreetMap tags the point as an observation tower and gives its elevation as 555 meters.[^5]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone and gives it no register number.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^2]
