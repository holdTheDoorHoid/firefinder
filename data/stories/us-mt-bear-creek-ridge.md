Bear Creek Ridge lies about 15 miles south of Libby, in Lincoln County, Montana, on Kootenai National Forest land. Its listed elevation is 4,950 feet.[^1]

The site is listed as a crow's nest.[^1]

The sources disagree on when it went up. Firelookout.com dates the crow's nest to 1934.[^1] The Lincoln County forest lookouts page gives 1936 instead, as the year a tree crow's nest was set up at the site.[^2]

Firelookout.com says the crow's nest was abandoned in 1941.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site among lookout sites and non-lookout structures, marked as an abandoned crow's nest.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or says whether it is open to the public.

[^1]: Bear Creek Ridge Fire Lookout, Firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/bearcreekridge.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Lincoln County - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts, https://montanalookouts.weebly.com/lincoln-county.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
