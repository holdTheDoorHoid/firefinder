Olinger Top appears to be the Jefferson National Forest lookout tower on Stone Mountain in Lee County, Virginia.[^1][^2]

In 1977 the Appalachia Independent reported that the tower was to be removed.[^1] A local history of the Olinger community says the removal ended one practical use of the name, but the name itself lived on.[^1] The Olinger Gap Trail, a 1.1-mile route linking the Keokee Loop Trail and the Stone Mountain Trail, still carries the name, according to the same history, which cites Virginia's Department of Wildlife Resources.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Olinger Top tower as gone, at 36.81205, -82.91691.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the tower site today, or whether the spot can be reached.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Olinger, Lee County: Olinger Gap, Stone Mountain, and a Community Kept in the Records, Appalachian Community Histories, https://appalachianhistorian.org/olinger-lee-county-olinger-gap-stone-mountain-and-a-community-kept-in-the-records/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Virginia, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
