Cienegita is a lookout tower in Otero County, New Mexico. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's New Mexico list records it as a tower that is standing, with coordinates near 33.30 north and 105.79 west.[^1] The row carries no national register number.[^1]

On the Forest Lookouts website, the page for the tower is headed by the location line "New Mexico - Mescalero Indian Agency." The page gives no county and no owner for the tower.[^2]

We could not find a build date, a builder, a design or a height for this tower in the sources we checked. We also found no record of who staffed it, what fires it watched, or whether it has been closed.

These sources do not say who owns or manages the site, or whether the public may go up. The heading names an agency, but we have not confirmed who runs the site today.[^2] Until the owner or manager is known, do not assume the site is open to visitors. We do not recommend climbing the tower.

[^1]: New Mexico lookouts list, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Cienegita, Forest Lookouts, http://westlookouts.weebly.com/cienegita.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
