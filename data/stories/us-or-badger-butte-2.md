Badger Butte is in Hood River County, Oregon, inside the Mt. Hood National Forest.[^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register gives the site an elevation of 5,981 feet and says it is administered by the U.S. Forest Service.[^1] The register says the lookout was constructed around 1933, but it does not describe the structure.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page describes a fir tree about 40 feet tall with a crow's nest, dated to the 1930s.[^3]

On November 6, 1933, Robert Snyder and Lester Moe took panoramic photographs at the site.[^3] The register credits those panoramas to the National Archives Pacific Northwest Region.[^1] In 1936 the station's assigned ring pattern was three short rings and one long.[^3]

The register and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's list both mark the lookout as gone.[^1][^4] Firelookout.com is brief: it lists 1933 and calls the site gone.[^2] A December 1979 Cultural Resource Inventory logged a piece of ladder, some wire, nails and an insulator, all still on the site.[^3]

The register and the Association's list do not agree on the location. The register's coordinates put the site well south of the point given in the list.[^1][^4] Firelookout.com places the lookout nine miles southeast of Mt. Hood.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, is on the site today. The register has a 2011 photograph of the site, credited to Howard Verschoor, and does not describe what it shows.[^1] No source says whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
