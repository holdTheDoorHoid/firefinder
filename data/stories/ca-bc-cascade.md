Cascade is a lookout in Kitimat-Stikine, British Columbia.[^1] The Canada Lookouts page for it gives a position of latitude 57.447 and longitude 130.334 (west), at an elevation of 1,360 m (4,462 ft).[^1]

A British Columbia guide to fire lookouts says the province's network held more than 300 lookouts at its height.[^2]

No source found describes the site today, so its condition and whether the public can reach it are unknown.
