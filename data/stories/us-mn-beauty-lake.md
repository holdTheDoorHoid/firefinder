The Beauty Lake lookout went up in 1934 as an 80-foot ladder tower in St. Louis County.[^1][^2] The county survey lists the Minnesota Forest Service as its owner and says the agency is now the DNR's forestry division.[^1] The statewide A-Z list of towers numbers this one Tower #26.[^2]

The tower came down in 1989. The county survey says it was removed that year, possibly because of vandalism.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list records the tower as gone and gives the site as 47.21585, -93.05676.[^3] A Forest Lookouts page places it in section 30 of township 55 north, range 21 west.[^4] No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
