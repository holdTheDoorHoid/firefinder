Thor Mountain was a cabin, not a tower.[^1][^3] The association's list files it as a ground site.[^3] The sources date the cabin to 1930 and place it about 13 miles southeast of Avery, in Shoshone County, Idaho, in the St. Joe National Forest.[^1][^2] The site sits at 6,384 feet.[^1][^2]

Both detail pages place a patrol point about a mile to the southwest, on Pinnacle Ridge. They do not say whether the cabin stood there or on the mountain itself.[^1][^2]

Both pages say the cabin was abandoned.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it as a hike-in site, and none of the pages says whether the land is open to the public.[^2]
