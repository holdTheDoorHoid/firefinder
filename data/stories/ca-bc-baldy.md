The Baldy Mountain fire lookout is listed near Clearwater, in the Thompson-Nicola area of British Columbia.[^1][^2]

As of 2026, a Rainlander guide places the lookout among towers whose structures are either confirmed standing or likely still standing but unmaintained.[^2] Wild Bluebell's region-by-region list of towers thought to survive includes it too.[^3]

The name Baldy is shared by several towers in British Columbia, so check the location before you plan a visit.[^4][^5] Baldy Hughes is a separate lookout in the Fraser-Fort George region.[^4] A 1925 provincial report records that a primary unit went up that year on Mount Baldy, near Penticton, during a programme of lookout construction.[^5]

No source I found describes the site today, or says whether visitors can reach it.
