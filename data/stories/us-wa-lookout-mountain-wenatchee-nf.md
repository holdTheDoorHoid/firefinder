Lookout Mountain, a 3,465-foot peak about eight miles east of Cle Elum, once carried a pole tower lookout built in the 1930s, according to the Former Fire Lookout Sites register.[^1][^2] The U.S. Forest Service administers the land, and the site entered the register on October 21, 2012, after a nomination by Ray Kresek.[^1] The register lists the pole tower as gone.[^1]

Willhite also notes that reports place a pole-tower lookout on this mountain in the 1930s.[^3] The register gives coordinates for the site, but Willhite says he has not determined the lookout's exact location.[^1][^3]

Willhite says trees cover the top, though some western escarpments offer views.[^3] Hart Road reaches some communication towers, and he adds that the road past them has stopped being a county road.[^3] He also flags possible landowner problems, land that is for sale, and trespass signs that may now be posted, so visitors should not assume the summit is open to them.[^3] None of the cited sources describes what remains of the tower itself today.
