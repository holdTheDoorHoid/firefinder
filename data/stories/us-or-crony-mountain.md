Crony Mountain, about eight miles south of Grants Pass in Josephine County, Oregon, once carried a fire lookout.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register puts the site at 3,464 feet, or 1,056 meters, and lists the Oregon Department of Forestry as its administrator.[^1] The register numbers the site US 785 and OR 194, and places it in southwest Oregon.[^1]

The register dates the lookout to the 1930s and does not identify its style.[^1] firelookout.com gives the same decade and marks the style as unknown.[^2]

Both sources say the lookout is gone.[^1][^2] The register added the site on December 9, 2010, on the nomination of Ray Kresek.[^1]

The register gives coordinates for the spot, N 42.330500°, W 123.393170°.[^1] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, or whether anyone may visit it.

[^1]: Crony Mountain Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/or/crony-mountain-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Crony Mtn. Fire Lookout, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/or/cronymtn.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
