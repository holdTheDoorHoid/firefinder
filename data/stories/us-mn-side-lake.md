The Side Lake Lookout Tower stands inside the Sturgeon River State Forest, in St. Louis County, Minnesota.[^1][^2] Wikidata classes it as a fire lookout tower.[^1]

The A-Z list of Minnesota lookouts gives its date as 1932 and says the CCC and the state built it.[^3] The Civilian Conservation Corps ran from 1933 to 1942, so a 1932 build by the CCC cannot be right as written, and the sources do not say which detail is wrong.[^4] Wikidata also gives 1932, but it cites no source for that date, so it does not confirm the date independently.[^1] The list describes a stairway tower with a square cab, made by a company it calls Pittsburg Des Moines, and says the tower stands at a forestry station.[^3]

The list gives the height as 100 feet. Wikidata gives the same figure, also without a source.[^3][^1]

The same entry includes a 1936 survey note describing an 80-foot steel tower at a station about 16 miles north of Chisholm.[^3] That figure does not match the 100 feet, and the sources do not explain the difference.

The list says the tower was "used occasionally," but we found no record of the lookouts who worked it or the fires it watched.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as standing.[^5]

We found no statement about public access. Do not climb the tower, and check with the managing agency before visiting.
