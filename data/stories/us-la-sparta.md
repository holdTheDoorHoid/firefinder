The Sparta fire tower stood in Winn Parish, in the Kisatchie National Forest, about five miles northwest of Calvin.[^1][^2] The lookouts site gives its location as township 12 north, range 5 west, section 13.[^1]

Staffing is documented for 1944.[^1] A report dated March 30, 1944, which the lookouts site quotes from The Town Talk, announced that Beatrice Collins, a 17-year-old from Calvin, had been appointed "Lookout A" for the tower.[^1] The report described her as the state's first woman or girl to win a federal appointment of this kind.[^1] It also said her father had been lookout on the Sparta tower for several years before he was promoted to agriculture aid with the U.S. Forestry Service.[^1]

The same item also covered the duties, which meant climbing 100 feet to the crow's nest and staying watchful for forest fires while on duty.[^1]

The lookouts site marks the tower as removed.[^1] No source describes the site today, or whether the ground is open to the public.
