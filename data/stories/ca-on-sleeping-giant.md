Ontario's fire-tower lists place a Sleeping Giant tower on the Sibley Peninsula, near Thunder Bay.[^1][^2] The peninsula holds Sleeping Giant Provincial Park, which was established in 1944 as Sibley Provincial Park and renamed in 1988.[^3] Most of the lower part of the peninsula falls within the park.[^3]

One of the lists says that, as of 2013, the Ontario Ministry of Natural Resources (MNR) had chosen to take down many towers that were still standing, and that the ministry no longer maintains them.[^1] The same list asks people not to climb the towers. It draws a line between going to the sites and climbing the structures.[^1]

The list that shows this tower as standing carries no date that we could find, and the year it mentions is 2013.[^1] No source found describes what remains on the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
