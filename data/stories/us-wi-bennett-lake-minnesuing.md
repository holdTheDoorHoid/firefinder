The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a standing tower called Bennett (Lake Minnesuing) in Douglas County, Wisconsin.[^1] The listing gives the county, coordinates, type and status, and nothing about the tower's history.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page for Wisconsin files the tower under Bennett in Douglas County and gives a land survey code, 46N-12-33, but no history either.[^3]

We could not find who built the tower, when it went up, or whether a fire agency ever staffed it. The sources we read do not give those details, so the record is thin.

The association also lists an earlier tower, Bennett – first, in Douglas County, and marks it as gone.[^2] That site sits at nearly the same coordinates as a TopoQuest map entry called Bennett Lookout Tower, which gives an elevation of 1,312 feet.[^2][^4] The names overlap, so the sources do not show whether the map entry describes the first tower, the standing one, or both.[^4]

We found no information about the tower's condition, owner or public access today. Anyone curious about it should ask the landowner or local forest managers before going near it, and should not climb a tower that is closed or private.
