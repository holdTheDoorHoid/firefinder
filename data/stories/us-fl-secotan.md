Secotan is a small community in Taylor County, Florida.[^1][^2] It lies about five miles north of Perry, beside the Atlantic Coast Line railroad.[^1][^2] In May 1926 a Tallahassee newspaper reported that the first station north of Perry had been dubbed "Secotan."[^1] By 1952 a lookout tower stood nearby.[^2] A USGS topographic map that portrays 1952, published in 1973, labels the tower "BM 48 Secotan Lookout Tower" and shows it near the community, which the map describes as anchored by the railway.[^2] The map's description calls the tower important to fire watching in the region's pine forests, and says this part of Taylor County was mainly devoted to commercial forestry and rail freight.[^2]

OpenStreetMap, the volunteer-built world map, still carries a point named Secotan Lookout Tower.[^3] That is a map entry, not a site report.[^3] No source describes what remains of the tower now, or whether the public can reach it.
