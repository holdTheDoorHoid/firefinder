Carrill Peak Lookout stood on the peak about eight miles south-southeast of Coeur d'Alene, in Kootenai County and the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1][^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the site at 47.54074, -116.68100.[^4] The site's elevation is listed as 4,098 feet.[^2]

Records give 1936 for the tower, which was 40 feet tall and had an L-4 cab, though one list marks that year as approximate.[^1][^2][^3] The Kemnow list gives the location as section 35 of township 49 North, range 3 West, and credits both dates to a source it names only as Kresek.[^3]

Sources put the tower's destruction in 1947, and one of the lists marks that date as approximate.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the structure as gone.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the peak. Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under drive-up, but none of the pages I read says whether the ground is open to visitors.[^2]
