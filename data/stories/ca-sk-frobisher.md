The Firetowers of Saskatchewan listing gives the Frobisher tower's position as 56-27-40.3 X 108-34-06.4.[^1] A Saskatchewan index on the Canada Lookouts site also lists Frobisher by name.[^2]

A towermen list gives Bobby Montgrand's service as 1996 to 2002, with Frobisher as his tower. The entry says he then went to aerial detection.[^3]

That list is a compilation. It credits Maurice Bourassa's efforts for its information and asks readers to send corrections.[^3]

The list also says the last tower was manned after mid-August 2001 and that all towers were condemned, with six to be rebuilt. That line does not name Frobisher, so it is unclear whether it covers this tower.[^3]

No source we found describes what remains at the Frobisher site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
