The listing calls Drury Peak a crow's nest.[^1] It is dated to the 1930s, and it was on the Lolo National Forest about seven miles north of St. Regis in Mineral County, Montana, at an elevation of 5,492 feet.[^1]

Both listings we read call the site abandoned.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list records it as a crow's nest with the status abandoned, at coordinates 47.40443, -115.10507.[^2] Neither listing says what, if anything, remains on the peak.[^1][^2]

Today, we found no source that describes the site or whether it is open to visitors. Ask the Lolo National Forest before going to look for it.

[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
