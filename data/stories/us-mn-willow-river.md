The county survey lists an 80-foot ladder fire tower at Willow River in Pine County, Minnesota, owned by the Minnesota Forest Service.[^1] The survey places the tower at the state tree nursery, where it is also called G.A. Nursery or General Andrews Nursery.[^1] In 1939 the state bought land for the nursery; four years later, the nursery site and adjoining land became part of a state forest.[^2]

The survey lists the tower as removed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association also marks it as gone.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public.

[^3]: Minnesota Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
