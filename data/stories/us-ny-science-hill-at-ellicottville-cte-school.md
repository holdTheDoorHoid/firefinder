The tower the Forest Lookouts list places at the Ellicottville CTE School began on Mount Irvine, inside Allegany State Park.[^1][^2] The Conservation Department's Division of State Parks built it there in 1928 as a 60-foot Aermotor LS40 steel tower.[^2] In 1932 the Park moved it to Science Hill, southwest of the Bradford entrance near the Pennsylvania border.[^1][^2] Like the Park's other towers, it had a lookout on duty only when fire danger was high.[^1]

The sources disagree on when it came down. The New York chapter's list and the EverybodyWiki article on the mountain say it stayed on Science Hill until 1978, when Park personnel took it down and an Ellicottville BOCES class reassembled it at the school.[^1][^2] A Hamilton College fire-tower page says a third park tower was dismantled and moved to the Ellicottville BOCES school in the early 1970s, citing a 2005 webpage from the Allegany State Park Fire Tower Restoration Committee.[^3] We could not settle the date from the sources we read.

The Forest Lookouts list now shows the tower standing at the Ellicottville CTE School, and it marks the Science Hill site as gone.[^4][^5] The school is probably the Ellicottville BOCES school that the other sources name, but no source says so outright. We found no source that describes public access to the school grounds, so ask the school before going near the tower, and do not climb it.[^4]
