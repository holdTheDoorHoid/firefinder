The Forest Lookouts site lists a fire tower at Benton, in Marshall County, Kentucky.[^1] Its only status note says the tower is standing, based on Google Earth imagery dated April 4, 2023.[^1]

That 2023 imagery note is the most recent information we found.[^1] No source describes what is at the site now, or whether the land is open to visitors, so check with the landowner or local forest office before planning a visit.[^1]
