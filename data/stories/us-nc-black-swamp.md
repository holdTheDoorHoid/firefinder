The Black Swamp fire tower is listed in Jones County, North Carolina, and the Forest Lookouts page files it under the Croatan National Forest.[^1] A statewide list of North Carolina towers gives its position as 34.87555 north and 77.20166 west, and the list's map was revised in June 2021. The same list notes that a few observation towers and Coast Guard lookouts may be mixed in.[^2] OpenStreetMap carries a tower of the same name, tagged as an observation tower, and gives its elevation as 7 metres (23 feet).[^3]

No source we read describes the site today, including whether a tower still stands there or whether the public may visit. Ask the Croatan National Forest before going, and do not climb any tower without permission.

[^2]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Black Swamp Fire Tower, OpenStreetMap node 357809750, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/357809750 (accessed 2026-10-21).
