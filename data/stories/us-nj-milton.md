The Milton Station Fire Tower stands on Morris County park land near Jefferson, within Mahlon Dickerson Reservation.[^1][^2][^3] A 2026 hiking guide places it atop Bowling Green Mountain.[^3] The Civilian Conservation Corps built it in 1937 under the Woodlands Protective Expansion Project.[^1] Its steel frame is an 80-foot International Derrick tower with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab.[^1][^2][^3] The register calls it a key facility and notes that every other Division A tower can be seen from it.[^1]

A 2016 visit report posted on the register page asked whether the tower was staffed and recorded the answer as no.[^1] None of the pages I read names a particular fire the tower reported.[^1][^3]

The register says the tower was rehabbed and given a new stainless steel roof, but it gives no date for the work.[^1] The same 2016 report describes a steel square with a lock and chain on a floor below the cabin, which it says was meant to block future intruders.[^1] The 2026 guide says the tower still plays an active role today, but no source I read says whether it is staffed today.[^3]

The guide says the park is open daily from sunrise to sunset, with trailhead parking at the Snow Bowl Area on Weldon Road.[^3] It also says visitors willing to climb are rewarded with views from the top, and that the observer may invite them up into the cab when the tower is staffed.[^3] No official source I could reach confirms that visitors may climb it, and the 2016 lock and chain suggests the cab may be closed off.[^1][^3] Do not climb it unless the county confirms it is open. Check with Morris County park staff first.
