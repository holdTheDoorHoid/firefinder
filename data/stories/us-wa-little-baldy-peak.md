Little Baldy Peak lies in Skamania County, Washington, within the Gifford Pinchot National Forest, roughly nine miles northwest of White Salmon.[^1][^2] The lookout there was an L-4 cab set on the south end of the ridge in 1933.[^1][^2][^3]

A road reached the site by August 1936, according to panoramic photographs that Willhite describes as taken from the cab's roof that month.[^3] Osmar Ladiges is recorded as the lookout in 1938.[^3] Willhite thinks the cab was probably out of use by 1957, when the DNR built Nester Peak Lookout nearby.[^3]

The sources do not say when the cab was taken down. The register and firelookout.com both list it as gone.[^1][^2] Willhite's page includes a photo labelled with the cab's footings, which is the only physical remnant the sources mention.[^3]

Today the route starts at Willard, runs east on Willard Road, and then climbs a forest road.[^3] Willhite says to park just below the summit at a switchback and then walk a short, overgrown trail south to the site, and lists the access as good for any vehicle.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's regional list gives the site type as ground, with the lookout gone.[^4] No source describes what else, if anything, stands there now.[^3][^4]
