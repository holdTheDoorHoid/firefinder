Deer Lake was a 100-foot stairway tower in Itasca County, Minnesota.[^1][^3] The Civilian Conservation Corps erected it in 1935.[^3] The tower was also called the Deer Lake Farm lookout, and the state forest service built a small farm near the site.[^3]

In April 1942, the Deer Lake Echo, a CCC camp newsletter, said enrollees from the camp had been stationed in the lookout to guard against fire.[^1]

The tower list gives its height as 100 feet, but its 1959 survey notes describe a four-legged steel frame with a viewing room at the top and give an overall height of 125 feet.[^3] The list's own figures disagree on the height.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it is open to the public.
