A Georgia list of lookout towers names a site called "run creek" in Long County, at 31.64487, -81.66327.[^1] The list numbers its entries from GA001 to GA427, and this site is GA212. The list's author says that names for towers 73 and above are the author's own creation, so the name may be the list's own label rather than an official one.[^1] Unlike many entries on the list, it has no register code after its name.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register counts 29 registered lookouts in Georgia, and this site is not one of them.[^2]

No source found describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1]

[^2]: Lookouts in Georgia, National Historic Lookout Register, https://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ga/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
