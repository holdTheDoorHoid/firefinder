Lookout Mountain is in Lane County, Oregon, about three miles north of McKenzie Bridge, inside the Willamette National Forest.[^1] One listing gives its elevation as 5,276 feet.[^1]

The lookout's history is brief. One listing dates it to the 1930s without giving a year.[^1] A second Forest Lookouts page says an alidade was established at the site around 1935.[^2] The first listing calls the site abandoned, and Oregon's index of lookouts shows it as gone.[^1][^3]

In August 2023 the Lookout Fire began with a lightning strike on Saturday, August 5, inside the H.J. Andrews Experimental Forest.[^5] The Upper McKenzie Community Center described that strike as hitting a Lookout Mountain about four miles northeast of McKenzie Bridge.[^4] The sources do not say whether that is the same peak as this lookout site, so we do not link the fire to the lookout.

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it. Ask the Willamette National Forest before you go looking for it.
