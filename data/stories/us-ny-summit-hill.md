The Allegany State Park Commission built a 60-foot Aermotor LS40 steel lookout on South Mountain in 1926.[^1][^2] It stands in Allegany State Park, about two miles south of Salamanca, in Cattaraugus County.[^1][^2] By the end of 1928 the park had five towers, and this one was one of three main towers with Mount Irvine and Mount Tuscarora.[^2] In 1931 the Bureau of Forest Fire Control took over its operation.[^2] None of the sources I read names a fire the tower spotted.[^1][^2]

Use ended when aerial detection arrived. Wikipedia says the tower ceased fire lookout work at the end of the 1970 season, because of aerial detection.[^2] The Southern Tier listing gives the same closing year.[^1]

Restoration came later. The Fire Tower Restoration Committee, which sits within the Allegany State Park Historical Society, dates from 1999.[^1] Restoration was completed in 2006, and the tower reopened with a ceremony on October 14, 2006.[^1][^2]

The sources disagree on access. The Southern Tier listing says a tall fence now surrounds the tower, and the park lets people in only on certain dates.[^1] The DEC says the top platform is closed to visitors, though the stairs can still be climbed, and that trees have grown up so there are no views from the top.[^3] FFLA newsletters have announced set open times: Saturdays from 11:00 to 1:30 in 2021, and 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. on June 20, 22 and 24, 2026.[^4][^5] Those June dates have passed, and the sources I read list no later open days. The state Office of Parks, Recreation, and Historic Preservation owns and manages the tower.[^3]

A 2020 newsletter notes that the trails around the summit are a mountain-biking area, with no horse trails near the top.[^6] Check with Allegany State Park before you go, and do not climb the tower while it is closed.
