The Theta lookout tower stands in Maury County, Tennessee, at an elevation of about 1,041 feet.[^3] The Forest Lookouts site lists it as a Tennessee Division of Forestry tower in the agency's District 7.[^1]

In October 1955 the state bought twelve steel lookout towers, each 80 feet tall, for roughly $36,000.[^1] The News-Democrat reported that one of them was to be put up at this site before early winter.[^1] We did not find a record confirming when the Theta tower was finished, so its build date rests on that plan.

A National Register document about the Division's fire towers says that most of them went up between the late 1940s and about 1960.[^2] The document gives no build date for Theta.[^2]

We found no names of anyone who staffed the Theta tower, and no record of a fire it helped spot.

The same National Register document lists Theta among the towers still standing as of March 26, 2014.[^2] Tennessee Landforms also lists it as standing, but that listing has no date of its own. The page carries a revision stamp dated 8 January 2026.[^3]

We found no public access information for Theta. The sources we read do not say whether the tower is open to visitors, closed, or privately owned. Please ask the Division of Forestry or the landowner before going near it, and do not climb it unless it is open and safe.
