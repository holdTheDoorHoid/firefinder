Pittsboro is a lookout tower in Chatham County, North Carolina.[^2] The town of the same name is the seat of Chatham County, according to Wikipedia.[^3] The Forest Lookouts page for the state gives the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources as its agency and credits the Civilian Conservation Corps with putting up a 100-foot tower there in 1940.[^1] The page cites the state Department of Conservation and Development's 9th Biennial Report (1942) for that entry.[^1] The tnlandforms.us list gives the tower's coordinates as 35.73527, -79.16027.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today or whether the site is open to the public.
