Mullinax Mountain is in Bartow County.[^2] The county lies in the northwest region of Georgia and has Cartersville as its county seat.[^3] A lookout-tower list gives a site called "mullinax mtn" in that county, with coordinates and an elevation, but no GFC number and no notes.[^1] The list's map was last revised in March 2016, so its entries may be dated.[^1]

A MyTopo list of summit features in Bartow County includes a Mullinax Mountain, so the name is attached to a named summit in the county.[^2] The two names match, which suggests that both lists describe the same summit.[^1][^2]

No source describes the site now, so I cannot say whether a tower, a foundation or any other trace remains there, or whether the summit is open to visitors.
