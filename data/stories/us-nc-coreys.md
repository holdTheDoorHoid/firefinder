A list of North Carolina lookout towers compiled by tnlandforms.us gives the name Coreys for a tower in Currituck County, at 36.53889, -75.96333.[^1] The federal geodetic survey has a station at those same coordinates, named Coreys Lookout Tower.[^2] Its listing carries the code XX, and NGS's legend gives X for a surface mark reported as destroyed; the code describes the survey marker, not the tower.[^2]

No source we read describes the site today, including what remains there or whether the public may visit it.
