The Telfair County Lookout/Fire Tower stands beside US 441/SR 31 in Telfair County, Georgia.[^1] A list of Georgia towers gives its site an elevation of 270 feet (82 m) and carries two reference numbers for it.[^2]

A 2007 archaeological report covers a road project at the tower. The survey was conducted by PBS&J for a proposed road that splits off the highway and arcs around the tower, with the aim of limiting harm from the widening of US 441/SR 31.[^1] The report calls the tower National Register eligible, which is different from being listed on the Register.[^1]

No source describes the site today, so it is not clear what remains there or whether the public can visit.
