The Fourche Pinnacle lookout was in Perry County, Arkansas, near a survey station on the highest point of Pinnacle Butte, about three miles northwest of Perryville.[^1] The survey record notes a station recovery in 1967 and places the lookout tower near the station.[^1] The Arkansas State Forestry Commission is listed as the agency for the lookout.[^2]

The lookout's page on the Forest Lookouts site is titled Fourche Mountain, but its heading and the state index both use Fourche Pinnacle.[^1][^2] The survey directions begin at the post office in Adona. They go east on State Highway 10 for 0.6 mile to a side road on the right, then south on a dirt road for 3.7 miles to the tower and station.[^1]

The 1967 station recovery is the latest dated account of the site this record has found.[^1] No source describes the tower or the site today, so it is not known whether the tower still stands, whether any remains are there, or whether visitors may reach it.

[^2]: Arkansas - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts (state index), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/arkansas.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
