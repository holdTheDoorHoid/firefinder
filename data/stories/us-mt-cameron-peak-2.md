Cameron Peak is in the Lolo National Forest, about 16 miles northwest of St. Regis, in Mineral County, Montana.[^1] The site sits at an elevation of 6,081 feet.[^1]

The lookout was an L-4 tower, built in 1936.[^1] The firelookout.com page says it was destroyed in 1962.[^1]

Sources do not agree on how the tower came down. Forest Lookouts' Montana page lists it as removed,[^2] and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana county list shows it as "Tower Gone."[^3] All three sources agree the tower is no longer standing.[^1][^2][^3]

We found no source that describes Cameron Peak today, including whether anything remains at the site or whether the summit is open to visitors.
