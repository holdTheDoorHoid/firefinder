Air Bellows is a lookout tower in Alleghany County, North Carolina.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page for it names the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources as the agency.[^1] The tnlandforms.us list of North Carolina lookout towers gives its coordinates and county.[^2]

The same name belongs to a gap in the Blue Ridge.[^3] NCpedia places Air Bellows Gap on the Alleghany-Wilkes county line, where an early road crossed the mountains.[^3] It also lists Airbellows, a former community in southern Alleghany County, which today serves as a Blue Ridge Parkway access point.[^3] Nothing we read ties the tower to either place.

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the tower today, or whether the public may visit the site.
