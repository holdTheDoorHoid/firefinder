A fire-tower listing on websiteservice.ca describes the Dore Lake tower as 24.4 m (80 ft) tall and made of heavy steel.[^1] It gives the legal land location as LSD 2-2-66-9-W3, and places the site at 54 degrees, 40 minutes, 34.8 seconds north and 107 degrees, 14 minutes, 52.7 seconds west.[^1]

A list of Saskatchewan towermen, also on websiteservice.ca, places Tom McBride at Dore Lake from 1949 to the mid-1960s.[^2] The same list places Henry Myers there from 1991 to 2001.[^2]

A 1946 photo caption on a jkcc.com page of Dore Lake memories names a "Dore Tower Cabin."[^3]

Canada Lookouts' Saskatchewan index lists both "Dore River" and "Dore Lake" as entries, and the sources do not say whether they name one tower.[^4]

No source we found describes the Dore Lake tower site today, so it is not known what remains there or whether the spot is open to visitors.
