Horseshoe Mountain stands in Sheridan County, Wyoming, on the Bighorn National Forest, about 18 miles west of Sheridan.[^1][^2] The lookout's listing gives the site's elevation as 7,932 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places it at 44.78621 north and 107.32247 west, and marks the lookout gone.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site now, or whether the ground is open to the public.

The Forest Service names three surviving historic lookouts on the Bighorn National Forest: Sheep Mountain, Black Mountain and James T. Saban. Horseshoe Mountain is not one of them.[^4][^5]
