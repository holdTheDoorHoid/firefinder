Honey Mountain's lookout stood about five miles south-southwest of Lakeview, in Bonner County, on the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, at 4,580 feet.[^1][^2]

The lookout was an L-4 cab, recorded for 1940.[^1][^2][^3]

Firelookout.com says the cab was gone by 1961.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives 1961 as both destroyed and removed, and Forest Lookouts lists it as removed without a year, so it is unclear how it ended.[^2][^3] The Idaho lookout list marks it as gone.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in. A post dated November 9, 2024 says its writers went up there and took photographs, but found no tie-downs, concrete piers, wires or trash, and said the spot showed no sign a lookout had ever stood there.[^2] No source says whether the site is open to the public.
