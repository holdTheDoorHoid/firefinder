Papoose Mountain's lookout began in 1930 as a pole platform.[^1][^2][^3] A pole platform is simply a platform raised on poles. A tower with an L-4 cab followed in 1938.[^1][^2][^3]

The site lies in the St. Joe National Forest, in Shoshone County.[^1][^4] Forest Lookouts gives its land location as 43N-7E-25.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places it at 47.04555 north, 115.51999 west.[^4] A patrol point lay two miles southeast, atop Badger Mountain.[^1]

The lookout is listed as abandoned.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list, however, marks the tower as gone.[^4] The sources disagree on whether the tower is gone or only abandoned.[^1][^2][^4]

None of the sources we read describe what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
