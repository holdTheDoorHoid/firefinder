The Park Falls tower was erected in 1934 in Butternut, Wisconsin, according to the state chapter of the Forest Fire Lookout Association.[^2] The National Historic Lookout Register gives no construction year.[^1] It describes a stairway in the style of the US Forest Service and says the tower appears to be a 100-foot Aermotor.[^1] The register does not know the year, so the 1934 date rests on the chapter report alone.[^1][^2]

The register lists the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources as administrator, and records the registration on June 28, 2017, as US 1218, WI 24.[^1]

The chapter's 2024 report says the tower was later moved to Park Falls.[^2] In 2018 the DNR sold it, and it was moved onto private property less than five miles from the Park Falls site.[^2][^3]

Today the tower is located on that private land, so the Park Falls site itself no longer holds it.[^2][^3] The sources describe the land as private, so this story does not suggest a visit.[^2][^3]
