The Way Lookout is in Orangeburg County, South Carolina, near Bouchetts Cross Roads.[^1][^2]

A transcription of the state forestry commission's report for July 1940 to June 1941 says three towers were built that year, and one of them was the Way Tower.[^2] The report for 1941 to 1942 says Civilian Conservation Corps workers finished the buildings and fence at the site.[^2] The report for 1957 to 1958 says the wooden tower at Way was swapped out for a steel one.[^2]

The register names Mildred Zeigler as a former lookout at the tower, but gives no dates for her time there.[^1] The register added the lookout on November 21, 2016, on the nomination of Terrie D. Johnson, and says it protected Orangeburg County.[^1]

The Forestry Commission ended its fire tower network on October 1, 1993.[^3] We found no date for when the Way tower stopped being used.[^2][^3]

We found no recent report on whether the tower still stands, who can visit it, or whether anyone maintains it.[^1] Until the Forestry Commission confirms access, please treat the site as off limits.[^1]
