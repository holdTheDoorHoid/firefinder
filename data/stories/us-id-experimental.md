The Experimental Lookout stood on Experimental Point in Bonner County, Idaho, at an elevation listed as 5,983 feet.[^1][^2][^6] Three lookout write-ups name the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^2][^3] A University of Idaho photo record places the point in the Priest River Experimental Forest instead, so the sources do not agree on which forest it lies in.[^6]

Sources date the lookout to about 1918, when it was a platform on a frame cabin; an enclosed cupola came later.[^1][^2][^3] A university photo taken in July 1918 shows lumber being packed in to build a lookout cabin.[^4] A second university photo, dated 1917, already calls the place Experiment Station Lookout.[^5] That 1917 date sits awkwardly beside the about-1918 start.[^1][^5]

A photo catalogued with a 1928 date shows a lookout on Experimental Point.[^6] The site was abandoned in 1932.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com also says the cabin was removed, with no separate date given.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left on Experimental Point today, or says whether the site is open to the public. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in.[^2]
