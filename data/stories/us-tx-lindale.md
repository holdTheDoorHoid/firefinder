The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower under the name Lindale in Smith County. It gives the position as 32.54598, -95.36681, and marks the tower as gone.[^1]

A community with the same name lies ten miles north of Tyler, in northwestern Smith County, along the Missouri Pacific Railroad and U.S. Highway 69.[^2] According to the Texas State Historical Association, the townsite had been settled by 1873, when a post office opened under the name Lyndale.[^2] The spelling became Lindale the following year. In 1875 the settlement gained a railroad station on the International-Great Northern Railroad.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.
