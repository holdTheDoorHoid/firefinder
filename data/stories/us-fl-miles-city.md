Miles City is an unincorporated community in Collier County, Florida, near the junction of Interstate 75's Alligator Alley section and State Road 29.[^2] It takes its name from Miles Collier (1914–1954), son of local landowner Barron Collier.[^2] The community lies within the Naples–Marco Island metropolitan statistical area.[^2]

Opened in the 1920s, Miles City housed dozens of families employed by the Roskey Packing Plant.[^2] It was also once a stop on the Atlantic Coast Line Railroad's Harrisburg–Everglades City branch.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts website has a page for a Miles City lookout, filed under Collier County.[^1] No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^2] Anyone planning a visit should confirm the site's access before going.
