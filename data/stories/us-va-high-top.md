The High Top Tower Site marks a former fire lookout on Allegheny Mountain, in Bath County, Virginia, on the border with West Virginia.[^1]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register places it at about 38.07 degrees north and 79.96 degrees west, at an elevation of 3,636 feet (1,108 meters).[^1]

The register added the site as US 1707, VA 14 on January 15, 2018, on a nomination by Brian Powell.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list gives the same number and marks the tower as gone, placing it at nearly the same spot.[^2]

No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^2] None says whether the public may reach it, so anyone planning a visit should check with the land manager first and should not climb any structure they find there.[^1][^2]

[^2]: Virginia, Forest Fire Lookout Association (firelookout.org), https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
