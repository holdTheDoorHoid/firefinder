The Forest Lookout Association lists a standing tower called Caret (Upper Essex) in Essex County, Virginia, at about 37.98 degrees north and 77.04 degrees west.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site lists a tower called Upper Essex in the same county, which appears to be the same site.[^2]

We could not find when this tower was built, who built it, or what it was made of. The Association's entry gives a name, county, coordinates and status, with no register number.[^1] The Forest Lookouts entry gives the name and county, with no history.[^2]

Essex County has a second tower on the Association's list, at Center Cross, which it calls Lower Essex. It is also listed as standing, at separate coordinates, so the two should not be mixed up.[^3]

We found no record of who staffed the Upper Essex tower or of any fire it watched.[^1] The list shows it as standing today, with no note on its condition or owner.[^1] We found no information on public access or rentals. Please ask the landowner before going near it, and do not climb a tower without permission.
