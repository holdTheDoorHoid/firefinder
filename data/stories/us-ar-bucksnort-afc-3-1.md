The federal survey calls this station Lookout Tower 3 1 AFC.[^2] It lies about three miles north of Thornton, in Dallas County.[^1]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey described it in 1942 as a standard-type steel lookout, gray and about 110 feet (33.5 m) tall, on a small knoll covered in pines.[^1] The page says it was known locally as the Bucksnort Fire Tower and was Unit 3, Tower 1 of the Arkansas Forestry Commission.[^1] The Encyclopedia of Arkansas says that commission was created by Act 234 in 1931.[^3]

The 1942 entry is the earliest dated record, so the tower existed by then.[^1]

The federal listing for the station has no published datasheet.[^2] Two of its entries carry codes: one for a surface mark reported destroyed, and one for no geodetic control.[^2] Those codes concern the survey marks and control, not necessarily the tower, so they do not show whether the tower still stands. No source I could read says what stands at the site today or whether the public may visit it.

Ask the agency before going, and do not climb a structure whose condition is unknown.
