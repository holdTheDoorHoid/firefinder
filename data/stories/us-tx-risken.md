Risken is a survey site on ranch land in Kenedy County, South Texas, about 7.5 miles southeast of Sarita.[^1][^2] The earliest description I found dates from 1950, when the Coast and Geodetic Survey wrote up the site as a survey station with a wood-frame lookout tower at its center.[^1][^2] In that description the tower stood 30 feet (9 m) high, with a metal pyramid-shaped roof, on the highest knoll in the area. It sat about 250 feet east of the southeast end of a pond and about 250 feet from a hunting lodge.[^1]

The National Geodetic Survey still keeps a datasheet for the site, and it still lists the marker as a lookout tower.[^2]

The land belonged to Carl and Merle Risken of Riviera, Texas, and the 1950 description says a gate key could be obtained from them.[^1] No source I found describes the tower after 1950, so whether it still stands, and in what state, is not known. Treat the land as private and do not enter without the owners' permission.

[^1]: Risken - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts (Central US Lookouts), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/risken.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Designation AH1527, Risken Lookout Tower, National Geodetic Survey, https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=AH1527 (accessed 2026-10-21).
