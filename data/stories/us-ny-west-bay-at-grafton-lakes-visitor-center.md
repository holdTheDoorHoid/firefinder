A tower stands in a children's playground at Grafton Lakes State Park in Rensselaer County, New York.[^1] A December 2017 newsletter from the New York chapter of the Forest Fire Lookout Association says it was erected on December 1, 2017, at the park's new Welcome Center.[^2] The register says Davana LLC built it that year using parts of the West Bay fire tower from Florida.[^1] The newsletter says the tower was first located in the Florida panhandle, at West Bay.[^2]

The Florida tower has its own record. A January 1948 item, quoted on a Forest Lookouts page, put the West Bay tower at 80 feet and described it as made of wood.[^3] The same page says it was sold at auction for $100 and removed in the fall of 2008.[^3] The register and the newsletter do not say who bought it or how its parts reached New York, so those steps remain unknown.[^1][^2]

The register says the tower has been adapted for small children, and hopes it will draw families to Dickinson Hill, the park's other fire tower.[^1] Dickinson Hill is an older, separate structure. A November 2025 news article says it was built in 1924, is the only one left in Rensselaer County, and was reopened for recreation in 2012.[^5]

The register entry is dated December 25, 2022, and names the state's Office of Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation as administrator.[^1] The sources I found do not say whether visitors may climb the playground tower. The Welcome Center it was erected at was designed with exhibition spaces, information, offices and public restrooms.[^4]
