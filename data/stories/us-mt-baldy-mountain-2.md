The Forest Service and Montana built a camp together on Baldy Mountain in 1917.[^1] In 1922, a newspaper item quoted on the Montana lookouts site reported that work had started on a lookout house near Plains.[^3]

The cabin with a cupola came next. The register and firelookout.com both date it to 1928,[^1][^2] but the Montana site dates a log-style cabin with a cupola to 1927.[^4]

A 20-foot L-4 tower followed in 1949, according to the register and firelookout.com.[^1][^2] The Montana site instead puts an L-4 house on a treated timber tower in 1957.[^4] The sources disagree on these dates.

A newspaper item from September 1936 said Maynard Johnson was stationed on the lookout.[^3]

The register says a 30-foot treated timber structure replaced the 20-foot one in 1979, and that the lookout was removed in 1990.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the same 1979 and 1990 dates.[^2] The Montana site says an older structure was removed in 1961, after sitting beside the newer tower for several years.[^4]

A photo log from July 2016 notes that concrete footings are still at the summit.[^5] It also suggests that a rock platform may have been the base of the 1928 cabin.[^5] The site is in the Lolo National Forest, about 11 miles north of Plains.[^2][^5] No source says whether the summit is open to the public today.
