Young is a lookout tower in Johnston County, North Carolina, listed on TopoQuest's map database at an elevation of 219 feet (67 m) [^1]. It appears on the USGS 1:24,000 topographic map for Dunn, North Carolina [^1].

The state's first steel fire towers date from 1928, and in the 1930s the Civilian Conservation Corps built a large number of towers in the mountains [^3]. Towers were usually staffed during fire season, which ran from October through May [^3].

A WRAL article about a vacant fire tower in north Raleigh, Wake County, says Johnston County also has several fire towers still standing, without naming them [^2]. No source describes what remains on the Young site today, or whether the public can reach it.

A Carolina Country review of Peter J. Barr's guide notes that lookout towers can be dangerous and that many are not routinely maintained [^4].
