Mount Muller is in Clallam County, Washington.[^1] Firelookout.com places its lookout three miles west-northwest of Lake Crescent, at 3,748 feet, and names the Washington Division of Forestry/DNR as the agency.[^1]

A 1917 Tacoma Times headline, as WillhiteWeb quotes it, announced that the state was installing a fire lookout station on Muller Mountain.[^2] In 1918 the Washington Forest Fire Association reported that it paid part of the salary of the lookout stationed on "Mt. Mueller," a spelling that differs from the modern name.[^2][^3]

The record gets murky here. WillhiteWeb places the Kloshe Nanitch lookout a few miles west of Mount Muller but, in the page's view, on the same mountain. Its author is quite sure the Muller and Solduc names all refer to Kloshe Nanitch, though they would be glad to be proven wrong.[^2] The Washington Lookouts index gives Muller the township and range 30N-10W-15, and Kloshe Nanitch 30N-11W-23.[^3][^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association keeps Muller as its own entry.[^5] Sources do not agree on whether this was a separate lookout.

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Muller location. If Muller is the Kloshe Nanitch site, WillhiteWeb reports that the Forest Service had the reconstructed cupola removed in 2013 and left the platform in place.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as gone.[^5] Firelookout.com marks it abandoned and gives its type as unknown.[^1]
