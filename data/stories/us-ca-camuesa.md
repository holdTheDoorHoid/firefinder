Camuesa Lookout was built in 1935 on Camuesa Peak in Santa Barbara County, California, within the Los Padres National Forest.[^1][^2] The register gives its design as a California Region 5 Plan BC-301 cabin, set on an open timber tower 10 feet tall.[^1]

In October 1936, during a lightning storm, a patrolman parked along Buckhorn Road, and three lookouts, Camuesa among them, reported a fire in his vicinity.[^3] The 'fire' was actually the tail light of the patrolman's own car, which glowed deep red with the emergency brake on.[^3]

The register lists the site as registered on April 16, 2020.[^1] It gives no removal date and says only the tower piers survive on site.[^1] A Peakbagging photo note says the lookout was torn down by 1953, according to a USGS datasheet, and that its writer doubts anything remains.[^2] The two accounts disagree.

A visitor's e-bike trip report on Hike Los Padres says riders could reach a turnaround roughly a quarter mile beyond the peak.[^4] No source we read describes the lookout site itself today.
