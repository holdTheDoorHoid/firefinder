The Dent Point lookout was a 1933 L-4 tower on the Nez Perce National Forest, about nine miles northeast of Elk City.[^1][^2] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page puts the site at 6,285 feet.[^2] Both pages also mention a patrol lookout about two miles west-northwest, on Green Ridge. The wording does not say whether that was this tower or a separate station.[^1][^2]

The tower was abandoned in 1950.[^1][^2] Both pages now list it as gone.[^1][^2] The FFLA's Idaho list records it the same way and gives its coordinates as 45.91632, -115.30606.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page labels it a hike-in site, so it is reached on foot rather than by road.[^2]
