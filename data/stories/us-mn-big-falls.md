The county survey list dates the Big Falls lookout to 1927 and names the Minnesota Forest Service as its owner.[^1][^2] The list gives the tower as 96 feet tall, of ladder type.[^1][^2]

A 1959 survey note gives a different picture. It describes the tower as steel, with four legs and a metal observation room on top, and gives an overall height of 120 feet.[^1][^2] The list says 96 feet and the survey says 120 feet overall. The sources do not reconcile the two figures.

In 1959 a National Geodetic Survey marker sat on the northeast edge of Big Falls, on Minnesota State Forest property, about 62 feet south of the tower's southwest leg.[^3]

The county list marks the tower as removed, but gives no date for that.[^1]

I found no source describing what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none saying whether the land is open to visitors. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list also records the tower as gone.[^4]
