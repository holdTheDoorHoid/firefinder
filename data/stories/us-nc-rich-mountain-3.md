A North Carolina lookout list kept on the tnlandforms.us site includes a Rich Mountain in Transylvania County.[^1] Its row gives the position 35.17666, -82.68166 and an unexplained code, FB3115. The list was last revised in June 2021.[^1] It is the only source found that names a tower on this summit.[^1]

Do not confuse it with the Rich Mountain Lookout in Madison County, which stands on the North Carolina and Tennessee line in Pisgah National Forest. That tower is listed on the National Historic Lookout Register.[^2]

The list gives nothing about this summit's condition, so we cannot say what, if anything, remains there or whether the public can reach it.[^1]

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Rich Mountain Lookout, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/nc/rich-mountain-lookout/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
