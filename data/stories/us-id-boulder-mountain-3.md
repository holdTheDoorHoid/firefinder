Boulder Mountain Lookout sat at an elevation of 8,377 feet, about 8 miles east-southeast of McCall in Valley County, on the Payette National Forest.[^1][^2]

Sources describe a 15-foot wooden tower with an L-4 cab, built in 1940.[^1][^2][^3]

The tower was destroyed in 1965, according to firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts list gives 1966 for the same loss.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^2] No source describes what remains of the tower at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
