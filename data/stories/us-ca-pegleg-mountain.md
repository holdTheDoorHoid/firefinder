Pegleg Mountain stands in the Lassen National Forest in Lassen County, at 7,112 feet.[^1][^4] The Red River Lumber Company built the first lookout on Peg Leg Mountain in 1913, carrying the materials in by pack horse.[^3] The Forest Service took over the station in the 1920s, and the register says it put up a new lookout then.[^1][^3]

A 2023 Lassen News article, drawing on a Cal Fire statement, says the old lookout, in service since 1913, was removed in 1984 when Cal Fire put up a new tower.[^2] The register adds that the new structure was prefabricated, and that prison inmates helped put it together.[^1]

Lassen News says the old lookout was taken apart, shipped to Smokey Bear Park at the Lassen County Fairgrounds, and rebuilt there.[^2] Peakbagging says the building removed from the site is now at the fairgrounds.[^4] The register, though, says the 1920s lookout went to the fairgrounds, and that a replica was built and on display by 2008.[^1] Sources disagree about which building went there.[^1][^2][^4]

Lassen News says the tower had been condemned several years earlier because it needed repairs.[^2] Lassen News reports that a restoration began on June 16, 2023, adding a new roof and rebuilding, strengthening and repainting the tower and cab.[^2] A ribbon cutting was set for July 22, 2023, the day the lookout was due to reopen to the public.[^2]

The mountain tower was staffed by the Lassen National Forest as of 2016, the register says.[^1] A Peakbagging photo caption from June 2016 quotes a Lassen National Forest employee as saying that Cal Fire ran the lookout, but did not staff it from 2005 to 2015.[^4] The register lists Cal Fire as owner of the mountain tower.[^1] I found no report on its condition after 2016, so its current status is unconfirmed.

The 2023 ceremony notice invited people to tour the recently restored lookout at the fairgrounds.[^2]
