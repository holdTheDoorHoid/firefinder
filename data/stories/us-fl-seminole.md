The Seminole lookout is listed in Seminole County, Florida, on the Forest Lookouts website.[^1] OpenStreetMap maps a point named Seminole Lookout Tower there and tags its construction as lattice.[^2] A 2015 report from the Florida chapter of the Forest Fire Lookout Association mentions several towers called Seminole, without giving their locations, so the name alone may not identify this site.[^3]

A July 2010 news report, carried by Firehouse, quotes a Forestry area supervisor saying the state's manned towers are being phased out, because cell phones let residents report blazes from nearly any spot.[^4] The same report also says Orange, Seminole and Brevard counties once had ten towers between them and now have two.[^4] That figure is for the three counties combined, so it does not give Seminole's share.[^4]

The point's latest edit note reads "Add/modify communication mast.", but no source I could read confirms whether a lookout or a mast stands on the spot now, or whether the public can visit.[^2]
