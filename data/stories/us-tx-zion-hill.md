The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Zion Hill lookout in Jasper County, Texas, at latitude 30.83719 and longitude -93.97072, and marks it as gone.[^1] A Texas tower list on tnlandforms.us gives the same coordinates for Zion Hill.[^2] A separate list of Texas lookouts shows "Jasper County - Texas Forest Service" beneath the name ZION.[^3]

The name matches Zion Hill Missionary Baptist Church, a Jasper congregation that, by local tradition and church records, was organized in 1852.[^4] A Texas Historical Commission marker in Jasper marks the church and cemetery.[^4] The marker's coordinates are less than 100 metres from the tower's listed position, but none of these sources connects the church with the tower.[^1][^4]

The association lists the tower as gone.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, stands at the listed spot now, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

If you visit the marker, keep to the public road and ask the church before walking its grounds.
