The San Augustine lookout was a metal fire tower that stood a short way west of the town of San Augustine, Texas.[^1] Neal Murphy, writing in Shelby County Today, locates it off Highway 21 west, on the road now called County Road 280, about 150 yards into the woods.[^1]

Murphy's best guess is that the U.S. Forest Service built it in the 1930s.[^1] The Central US Lookouts list names the Texas Forest Service as the agency instead, so the two sources disagree on which agency was involved.[^2]

Across East Texas, the Forest Service turned to metal towers after watching from trees proved rarely effective.[^3] Murphy recalls teenagers in the 1940s and 1950s trespassing to climb it, and admits the climbing was dangerous.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association marks the tower as gone.[^4] Murphy says a recent drive down CR 280 showed no sign that a fire tower had ever stood there, and that it was torn down years ago.[^1]

No source we read says who owns the land now, or whether the public can visit the spot.
