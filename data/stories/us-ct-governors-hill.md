The Governors Hill lookout was a steel tower in New Haven County, Connecticut.[^1][^2] A March 1929 item from The Forest Worker, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, reported a newly finished steel observation tower in Oxford, the tenth of its kind built for the state's forestry agency.[^1] The same page adds a note that the location may be wrong, so the Oxford tower and the Governors Hill site are not yet shown to be the same structure.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site lists the tower as removed, with no date given.[^1]

Today, we found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the Governors Hill site.[^1][^2] A hiking listing for Southford Falls State Park describes a retired fire tower at the end of a short spur, and it does not name Governors Hill.[^3] Check with the park before you go looking for it, since that listing and this record may not refer to the same tower.
