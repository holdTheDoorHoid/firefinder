North Fork Pinnacle is a prominent, timber-covered ridge in Perry County, in the Ouachita National Forest.[^1] A Forest Service lookout tower once stood on its summit.[^1] A 1940 Coast and Geodetic Survey description, reproduced on the Central US Lookouts page, gives the tower's height as roughly 115 feet and its build as timber.[^1]

The same page marks the tower as removed in 1965.[^1]

In June 2009, a visitor posting on the Underground Ozarks forum described the access road as dating from the 1960s, with the original 1930s road bed still visible.[^2] At the top, the poster reported remnants of the old facility, including a cistern, foundations, the tower base and other concrete debris, and called the summit neat and easy to reach.[^2] That account is seventeen years old. No newer source describes the summit, and none says whether the public can go there now. A National Geodetic Survey list of marks it does not publish gives the tower's survey mark a code meaning its surface was reported destroyed.[^3]
