The Eastern US Lookouts site lists a Miccosukee lookout in Leon County, Florida.[^1]

A map database, Mapcarta, spells the name Micosukee Lookout Tower and places a tower of that name in Leon, Florida, with an elevation of 223 feet (68 metres).[^2] It also puts the Miccosukee Methodist Church 1,300 feet southwest of that tower.[^2] The two listings may be the same site, but no source confirms it.

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it. Do not try to climb any tower you find there.

[^1]: Miccosukee, Forest Lookouts (Eastern US), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/miccosukee.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Micosukee Lookout Tower Map, Mapcarta, https://mapcarta.com/20674220 (accessed 2026-10-21).
