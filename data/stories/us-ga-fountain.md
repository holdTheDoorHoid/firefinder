The Georgia list of lookout towers has an entry named fountain in Macon County, at 32.28300 north and 84.15232 west.[^1] The list gives the elevation as 461, with no unit stated; read as feet, that is about 140 metres.[^1] It ties the entry to a federal survey mark, datasheet CM1651.[^1]

The National Geodetic Survey datasheet for that mark is designated FOUNTAIN and lies in Macon County.[^2] Converted from degrees, minutes and seconds, its published position matches the list's coordinates.[^1][^2] The disk is stamped FOUNTAIN 1974.[^2]

The list does not describe the entry further, so the link to an old lookout tower rests on the shared survey mark and matching coordinates.[^1][^2] The datasheet's 1974 description puts the station on the spot where an old lookout tower stood, between two of that tower's north footings.[^2] Its directions run east along State Route 26 from Ellaville, and the description says the station stood on property owned by a private individual at the time.[^2]

No source describes the site today, or whether any of the footings remain.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Survey mark CM1651 (FOUNTAIN), National Geodetic Survey, NOAA, https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=CM1651 (accessed 2026-10-21).
