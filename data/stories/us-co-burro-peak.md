The Burro Peak Tower Site is in the San Juan National Forest, in Montezuma County, Colorado, at an elevation of 11,620 feet.[^1] The Forest Service administers it.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site on September 18, 2009.[^1] A second lookout list uses the name Burro Mountain for the same site.[^3] A historic survey of Colorado's national forests says the Montezuma and San Juan forests were consolidated in 1947.[^4]

The register describes the site as a tower that has been taken down.[^1] The Colorado list records its type as unknown and marks it removed.[^2]

The register gives the site's coordinates as 37.485780 north and 108.105700 west.[^1] No source I found describes what remains at the site now, or whether it is open to visitors.
