Bova Mountain sits in Cattaraugus County, New York.[^1]

By the end of 1928, five towers were in operation in Allegany State Park, and one of them stood on Bova Mountain.[^2] The Summit tower in the same park was restored in 2006, and the Wikipedia article says it is open to the public whenever the park is open.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list marks the Bova Mountain tower as gone.[^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the Bova Mountain site, or whether the spot is open to visitors.[^1]
