The Ty Ty Lookout Tower is listed in the tnlandforms.us survey of Georgia lookout towers, in Tift County at 31.46157, -83.61683.[^1] The list gives an elevation of 371, with no unit stated, and gives no code for the tower.[^1]

No source I could reach describes the site today, so it is not known whether a structure still stands there or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers list, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
