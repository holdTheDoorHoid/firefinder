West Fork Point Lookout stood in Idaho County, in the Nez Perce National Forest, at 6,056 feet.[^1][^2] The sources disagree on its distance from Kooskia. Firelookout.com puts it 20 miles southeast of the town, while the Forest Service list says 22 miles.[^1][^3]

The lookout went up in 1933 as a 40-foot L-4 pole tower.[^1][^2][^3] It was abandoned in 1950.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association now lists the tower as gone.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts found one cement footing left behind at the site, the only trace it reports of the lookout.[^2] The site is filed under drive-up access.[^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts also found a benchmark and describes the location as gorgeous.[^2] Apart from the footing and the benchmark, no source describes the site today, and none says whether the spot is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
