The Baker Ranger Station lookout was a platform on the roof of a carpenter shop, at the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest station in Baker City, Baker County, Oregon.[^1][^3]

The sources disagree on the date. The register says the 24-foot platform was built in 1948.[^1] The firelookout.com page dates it to the 1930s, when a firefinder sat on the roof of the carpentry shop.[^3] Forest Lookouts records panorama photographs taken at the station on October 20, 1935.[^2]

The register has a photograph captioned as the 1963 lookout, so something stood on the roof at least by then.[^1]

Both the register and firelookout.com say the platform was gone by 1970.[^1][^3] Forest Lookouts marks it simply as removed, with no date.[^2]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, is left at the site today. The register lists the U.S. Forest Service as administrator.[^1] We also found nothing on whether the public may visit the station grounds, so ask the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest before going there.
