A North Carolina lookout-tower list names a tower Ellerbe in Richmond County, at 35.08611, -79.66444, and notes a survey benchmark near it, AG9765.[^1] The benchmark's description was written by the North Carolina Geodetic Survey in 1993.[^2] It places the mark about 5.6 miles east-northeast of Ellerbe, and its directions include 4.65 miles along Fire Tower Road, state road 1455.[^2] The same description says the Millstone Fire Tower is visible from the mark, on a grid azimuth of 167 degrees, 8 minutes, 44.71 seconds.[^2]

By our rough calculation, the listed coordinates sit about 600 metres from the benchmark, on a bearing of about 168 degrees, close to the 167-degree azimuth given for Millstone.[^1][^2] The two sources do not say whether the Ellerbe and Millstone towers are one structure, so this story leaves both names open.[^1][^2] No source describes what, if anything, stands on the site now.[^1]

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: NGS Data Sheet, PID AG9765 (RAINES), National Geodetic Survey, NOAA, https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=AG9765 (accessed 2026-10-21).
