Clarks Knob, also called Gobblers Knob, sits west of Shippensburg in Franklin County.[^1][^3] A steel state fire tower, dating to 1943, was brought here from Pine Mountain and removed in 1945.[^1][^2] That year AT&T built a replacement on the same spot for its communication equipment.[^1][^2]

The register gives 1945 as the build year, but the tower it describes is AT&T's replacement, not the state tower. So the 1945 date in our record should be read as the AT&T build date.[^1] The old fire cab was moved down to a lower level, where DCNR staff used it for monitoring fires as needed.[^1] The register says the Pennsylvania Department of Conservation and Natural Resources administers the tower.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register recorded the tower on September 3, 2014, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites register recorded it on September 16, 2008.[^1][^2]

I found no notable fires at this site in the sources, and no staffing record beyond the as-needed monitoring. A 2024 blog post says the tower still exists in some form and describes a long ascent to reach it.[^3] I found no public access rules or climbing guidance. Check with DCNR before you go, and do not climb the tower without permission.
