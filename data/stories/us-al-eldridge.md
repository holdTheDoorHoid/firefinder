The Eldridge lookout is in Walker County, Alabama, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it as administered by the State of Alabama.[^1] The register gives the site an elevation of 743 feet, or 226 meters, and it carries the registry number US 1625, AL 6.[^1] The register describes a 100-foot steel tower and says its lower part is still lying twisted on the ground.[^1]

Andrew Zerbe nominated the site, and the register recorded it on July 24, 2016.[^1] The page shows a photo of the remains credited to Zerbe.[^1]

For what is on the site today, the register's description of the twisted lower section is the only source we read.[^1] The register does not say who owns the land now or whether visitors are welcome, so ask the State of Alabama before going.[^1]

[^1]: Eldridge Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/al/eldridge-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
