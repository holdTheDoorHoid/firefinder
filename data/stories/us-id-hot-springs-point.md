Hot Springs Point stood in Idaho County, about seven miles west-southwest of Powell Ranger Station, on Clearwater National Forest land.[^1][^3][^4] The Forest Service list places it in Section 9, Township 36 North, Range 13 East.[^3] A cabin went up there in 1929.[^1][^2][^3][^4] firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts call it an L-4 cabin, while the Forest Service list gives only "Cabin."[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Service list gives the site an elevation of 5,535 feet.[^3]

The cabin was destroyed in 1956, a date all four sources give.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under its Selway region and lists it as hike-in.[^2] The Forest Lookouts list for Idaho County agrees on the 1929 and 1956 dates.[^4] Today, no source we read describes what remains at the site, or whether visitors may go there.
