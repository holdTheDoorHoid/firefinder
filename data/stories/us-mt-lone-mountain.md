Rex's Fire Lookout Page quotes a July 1915 Spokesman-Review item about R.G. Willson, a forest supervisor, who was supervising construction of an emergency telephone line to Lone Mountain.[^2] The item also said the peak was planned as a lookout station for two forests, the Madison and the Gallatin.[^2]

The Fire Lookout Cabin listing describes a D-6 cupola cabin dated to 1921, sitting at 11,166 feet, 14 miles east-southeast of Ennis in Madison County.[^1] The listing puts its abandonment at about 1930.[^1]

The sources also differ on the forest.[^1][^2] Rex's page gives one forest for the site, the Beaverhead National Forest,[^2] while the listing names the Beaverhead/Gallatin National Forests.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a Ground type and as Gone.[^3] Rex's page marks it "Removed" but gives no date.[^2] None of the three pages describes what remains at the site today or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
