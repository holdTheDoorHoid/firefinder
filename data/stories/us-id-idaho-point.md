Idaho Point is a point in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, in Shoshone County, at 6,505 feet. It lies about 14 miles east-northeast of the Magee Ranger Station.[^1][^2]

The lookout went up in 1934 as a 20-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2][^3] The L-4 was a 14-by-14 cabin design, and such cabins were produced until 1952.[^4] The only lookout named in these sources is James Melton, listed for 1959.[^1][^2] The tower was destroyed in 1971.[^1][^2][^3]

A post dated November 14, 2024, describes finding the remains of the footings, a broken wooden post that may have been a flag pole, and other debris on the rocky outcrop.[^2] The site lies about a quarter mile from Eighty Peak, and Idaho Fire Lookouts marks it as drive-up.[^2] No source says whether the site is open to the public.
