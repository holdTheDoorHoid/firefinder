Poe Mountain is a ridge-top site above the Little Wenatchee River in Chelan County, within the Wenatchee National Forest.[^1][^3][^5] An L-4 cab was built there in 1933.[^1][^2][^3]

A June 1933 item from The Leavenworth Echo, as quoted by Forest Lookouts, names Marshall Stenerson as the Poe Mountain lookout-fireman on the Lake Wenatchee Ranger District.[^4][^2]

Donald Reed Theoe was the lookout in 1957.[^3] Willhite describes a summer so wet that no fire broke out in the area, leaving Theoe with plenty of time to search for dry firewood.[^3] Ten people visited him that season, and one later married him.[^3]

The cab came down in 1970. The register says it was removed, while FireLookout.com and Willhite say it was destroyed.[^1][^2][^3]

Willhite's trip report includes photographs captioned as a footing, an eye bolt and an anchor rod.[^3]

Today the site lies within Glacier Peak Wilderness.[^5] The Forest Service describes the Poe Mountain Trail as steep and dry, leading from the Little Wenatchee Ford Trailhead to an old lookout site, and notes that wilderness regulations apply.[^5] Willhite lists the hike at 3 miles with 2,000 feet of gain.[^3]
