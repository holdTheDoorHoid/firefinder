The Agawam Fire Tower stands on Provin Mountain, in Hampden County, Massachusetts.[^2][^3] The current tower, a 70-foot steel structure with a 10-by-10-foot wooden cab, went up in 1959 to replace a 48-foot tower from 1920.[^1] The Mass. Bureau of Forest Fire Control administers it.[^1]

The state says early fire detection depends on trained Bureau observers at a network of 42 operating towers, but that page does not name this one, so it describes the program rather than this lookout.[^4] The register's visit report from June 5, 2016 says the tower was no longer staffed.[^1] We found no account of when staffing ended or of any fire it spotted.[^1][^2]

The two sources differ on condition. The 2016 visit report describes boards nailed across the hatch and cracked stairs.[^1] A 2024 write-up says the tower has been inactive for many years but is still in good shape.[^2] Both may be true for their dates. The register's height of 70 feet also differs from the write-up's 68.[^1][^2]

Today the tower stands on private land next to a working radio transmission site.[^2] The summit also holds the original studios and current transmitters of WWLP, which first signed on from there in March 1953.[^3] According to the register, walking up to the site counts as trespassing on private property.[^1] The write-up notes that the official New England Trail map passes the tower, and that the Forest Fire Lookout Association discourages visits without prior permission.[^2] Ask the landowner first, and do not climb it.
