Shirley Gap lookout stood on a spur west of the summit, in the Umpqua National Forest of Douglas County, Oregon, at 3,920 feet (1,195 meters).[^1][^2] The register dates the tower to the 1930s.[^1] It was registered on March 29, 2011, on the nomination of Ray Kresek.[^1]

firelookout.com places the site 26 miles northeast of Roseburg.[^3] The Forest Lookouts page lists it among the Douglas County sites and gives its survey location as 24S-2W-32.[^2]

Both the register and firelookout.com list the tower as gone, and neither gives a date for its removal.[^1][^3] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains on the spur today, or whether visitors may go there.[^1][^2][^3]
