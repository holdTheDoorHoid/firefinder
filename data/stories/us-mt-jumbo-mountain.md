Jumbo Mountain Lookout stands in the Bob Marshall Wilderness, on the Flathead National Forest's Spotted Bear Ranger District in Powell County, about 21 miles northeast of Seeley Lake.[^1][^2]

An L-4 cab from 1937 stood there before the current one.[^1][^2] A 1957 newspaper item, quoted on a Powell County history blog, says seven men were working on the new lookout that September, and that its materials were flown from Missoula to a landing field at Basin Creek and packed about five miles to the site.[^3] The blog gives the newspaper's date as both 1957 and 1954.[^3] The 1957 date fits the register, which says the current cab was built that year.[^1][^3]

That cab is an R-6 flat cab, built in 1957 to replace the L-4.[^1][^2] It was used for emergencies into the 1970s.[^1][^2]

Staffing is described two ways. The register says the lookout has been semi-regularly staffed since 2001.[^1] Firelookout.com names 2001 only as a year it was staffed, with no schedule given.[^2] Neither source gives a staffing schedule.[^1][^2]

The register records the lookout as US 1497 and gives its registration date as January 9, 2021.[^1] Its elevation is listed as 8,214 feet on the register and 8,284 feet on firelookout.com.[^1][^2]

The lookout sits inside a wilderness area, and nothing in the sources reviewed shows that the public may visit it, stay overnight, or rent it. Anyone planning a trip should check with the Spotted Bear Ranger District first.[^1][^2]
