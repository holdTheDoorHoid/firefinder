The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a site called Carracas (Cedar Rock) in Rio Arriba County, New Mexico.[^1] Its type is recorded as a trailer, and the list marks it as gone.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page places the site in the Carson National Forest.[^2]

A map-reprint site describes a USGS topographic map of the Bancos Mesa area that names a Cedar Rock Lookout among its landmarks.[^4] The site gives the map a 1995 date and says it was published in 1997.[^4] We have not seen the map itself.[^4]

The name Cedar Rock was also given to a lightning-caused fire on the Jicarilla Ranger District in 2023.[^3] The fire, estimated at four to five acres, sat north of Forest Road 218 (Carracas Mesa Road).[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the lookout site, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]
