The Parkin Fire Tower sits on a summit in Greater Sudbury, Ontario, north of the town of Capreol.[^1][^2] A directory of Ontario lookouts gives its location as Wanapitei Lake, Capreol.[^3]

Lookout towers began going up in Ontario in the 1920s, and by 1962 the province had 316 steel towers in service.[^4] Ontario's fire towers fell under the former Department of Lands and Forests, which became the Ministry of Natural Resources in 1972.[^4]

A hiking account from 2023 describes the route. From Capreol, turn right onto Portelance Road after 8.0 km on Regional Road 84. The road is a dirt road, and the writer warns that logging may be in progress.[^2] The hike to the summit is about 1.5 km and steep, with a climb of 174 m (570 ft). Parking is roadside only.[^2]

The first 5 km of the dirt road is used by residents of the Wahnapitae First Nation Reserve and by camp and cottage residents in the area.[^2] The writer advises giving logging trucks plenty of room.[^2]

The account, posted in June 2023, includes photographs of the tower and gives May 23, 2023 as the climb date.[^2] The writer says trees mostly block the summit view unless a visitor climbs the tower, and advises caution.[^2] The account lists a GPS-recorded height of 490 m (1608 ft) for the location.[^2] No source describes the site beyond that 2023 account.
