North Fork Mountain sits in Shasta County.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists its site as a Ground type and marks it gone.[^2] The peak table puts the site at 5,358 feet, within the Shasta-Trinity National Forest, and labels the site "North Fork Mtn (LO site)."[^3] Its key defines an LO site as a place where the building has been removed.[^3]

The only dated item on Ron Kemnow's page for the site is a September 1923 clipping from the Blue Lake Advocate.[^1] It reports that two men held a contract to pack in material for a lookout station at the head of Squaw Creek, which feeds the Pit River.[^1] The clipping does not name North Fork Mountain, so this story does not treat it as the site's history.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
