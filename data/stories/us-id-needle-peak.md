The L-4 tower on Needle Peak dates to 1935, and the peak sits about four miles southeast of Red Ives R.S. in Shoshone County, Idaho.[^1][^2]

The peak lies within the St. Joe National Forest, and firelookout.com gives its elevation as 6,643 feet.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists one name under known staff, Paddy Mcintyre, with the credit 'Up The Swiftwater', and gives no dates.[^2]

Both sources record the tower as destroyed in 1957.[^1][^2] The two pages agree on both dates, so they do not conflict here.[^1][^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, and none says whether it is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3] The association's list marks the tower gone.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site Drive-Up, which describes how it is reached, not whether it is open today.[^2]
