Mica Mountain rises about 20 miles east of Tucson, inside the Rincon Mountain District of Saguaro National Park.[^1] At 8,668 feet, it is the tallest peak in the Rincon Mountains.[^1] Its summit once held a fire lookout tower.[^2][^3]

A peakbagging page describes a 100-foot Aermotor tower with a 7-foot square cab on the summit, and dates it to 1938.[^3] A survey benchmark called Rincon Tower also sat at the summit.[^3] The page found one datasheet for that tower, dated 1946, which carries a different name.[^3]

By February 2015, a hiker's trip report found the summit offering only forest views, with the base of an old lookout tower still there.[^4] A March 29, 2019 photo on the peakbagging page shows the tower's footings, which the page says is all that is left.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^2]

Hikers reach the summit on foot.[^4] The 2015 trip report describes starting at a small pull-off on East Redington Road, with 5,500 feet of gain over 10.5 miles one way, and a summit register that the writer signed.[^4] The latest description I found is the 2019 photo, and no source describes the summit since.[^3]

Do not confuse it with Happy Valley Fire Lookout, a separate structure in Saguaro National Park.[^5] The park says the current Happy Valley lookout went up in 1968, and architectural historians judged it eligible for the National Register of Historic Places.[^5]
