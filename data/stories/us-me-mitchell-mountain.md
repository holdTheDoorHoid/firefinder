Mitchell Mountain is in Haynesville, in Aroostook County.[^1]

A Maine Memory photo record says the wooden fire observation tower on the mountain stood 36 feet tall and went up in 1918.[^1] The same record adds that a steel tower of identical height took its place in 1927.[^1] The record itself is a 1920 photographic print showing Jim Haynes, who kept watch for the Forest Service on the mountain that year.[^1]

A 1920 panoramic map centered on the tower is catalogued in Digital Maine's collection of fire tower maps made by the Maine Forest Service.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine list records the tower as gone.[^3] Maine's overview of its fire towers says the state ran more than 100 of them, each watched by a lone observer, and that the network ended after a budget crisis in 1991. It also says that, once the staffed system ended, some towers were taken apart or fell down unaided.[^4]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
