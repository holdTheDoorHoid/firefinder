The Ponemah fire tower stands in Beltrami County, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as standing at the coordinates it gives.[^1] Ponemah itself is a census-designated place inside the Lower Red Lake unorganized territory, and the 2020 census counted 599 residents there.[^2]

We found no build date, builder, height or design for the Ponemah tower in any source we read. The Association's list gives no build year for it either.[^1]

A 2008 state list of vacant or underused buildings covers Forestry fire towers, but it does not name a Ponemah tower.[^3] That list covers only buildings that were vacant or under-used in 2008, so its silence tells us little about who owns this tower.

We found no record of who staffed the tower or of any fire it helped spot. We also found no visiting information. The sources do not say who owns or manages the land it stands on, so check with the land manager before going.
