Mushroom Mountain is a lookout site in Lincoln County, Montana, on the Kootenai National Forest, about 11 miles northwest of Yaak.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 5,626 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list gives the type as crowsnest and places the site at 48.95217 north, 115.86198 west.[^2]

Firelookout.com dates the site's crow's nest to the 1920s.[^1]

The page says the lookout was abandoned, but it gives no date for that.[^1] The association's list gives the same status, Abandoned, and neither source calls the site gone.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes the site today. The only pages we found are short listings, so we cannot tell from them what, if anything, remains on the ground, or whether the public may visit the site.
