In late July 1935 the state announced that a tower on Shade Mountain in Juniata County would be completed within a week [^2]. The Department of Forests and Waters said the tower would be steel and eighty feet tall, on a site inside the Rothrock forest district [^2]. It was one of a group of new lookouts being built under the emergency conservation work program, and the state said it would fit into its statewide chain of observation stations [^2]. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives the build year as 1935 and calls the design a steel I-S tower [^1].

In October 1938 a newspaper item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, reported that Mr. and Mrs. D. C. Titzell of Port Royal had returned to the tower, where Mr. Titzell would be on duty for the fire season [^2].

The register lists the tower as removed in 1994 [^1].

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, and I could not find whether the public can reach the site.
