The Royalton lookout stood in Franklin County, near Royalton, and was a 100-foot tower.[^1][^2]

A June 1970 Southern Illinoisan report, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, said the tower had kept watch over the Shawnee National Forest near Royalton for nearly 25 years.[^1] The last fire watch by forest rangers from the tower came in 1964.[^1] The report also noted that the surrounding timber had largely been cleared and that airplanes had taken over spotting fires.[^1]

Afterward the tower was left up for sightseers, and a picnic area went in nearby.[^1] Vandals plagued it. On the weekend of May 31, 1970, they broke the hatch at the foot of the observation room and smashed 17 windows.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association now lists the Royalton tower as gone.[^2] Bob Frakes, an Illinois lookout historian quoted in Illinois Country Living, says nearly all of the state's towers were dismantled or sold off in the early 1970s.[^3] The most recent account of the tower dates from 1970, and no source describes the site today.[^1]
