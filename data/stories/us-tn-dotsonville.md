The Dotsonville lookout stands in Montgomery County, Tennessee, at an elevation of 647 feet.[^1] The Tennessee lookout list that includes it says its table marks the state Division of Forestry's 208 tower sites, counted around 1976, as district-tower numbers. Dotsonville appears in that table under the code 6-12.[^1]

The Division was responsible for building 208 fire lookout towers between about 1930 and 1970, according to a 2014 National Register form on the state's fire towers.[^2] We did not find a build date, a builder, or a design for Dotsonville in any source we read.[^1][^2]

That 2014 form lists Dotsonville among the towers still standing as of March 26, 2014.[^2] The Tennessee list was last revised in January 2026 and still marks the tower as standing.[^1] We found no record of its staffing or of any fire it watched.[^1][^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register's list of Tennessee lookouts has 76 entries, and Dotsonville is not one of them.[^3]

We found nothing about public access or ownership. Please do not climb the tower unless its owner or the managing agency says it is open to visitors.
