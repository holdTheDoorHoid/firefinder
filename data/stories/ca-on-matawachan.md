The Matawachan Fire Tower is recorded in Greater Madawaska, in eastern Ontario, at an elevation of 427 m (1,401 ft).[^1] Firelookout.ca describes the tower as steel and says it appears on the 1977 Pembroke topographic map, map 31F, third edition.[^1]

An Ontario fire tower history page places the tower in the Tweed Fire District, which was formed in 1922.[^2] The same page says six towers were built that year, Matawatchan among them, and gives this one as a light steel tower of 24.4 m (80 ft).[^2]

The name is spelled two ways. Firelookout.ca uses Matawachan, while the township's history and the fire tower history use Matawatchan.[^1][^2][^3] The township's own history says it had two communities, the village of Matawatchan and Camel Chute.[^3]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today.
