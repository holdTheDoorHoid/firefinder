The Burnt Mountain tower stood in Township 5, Range 10 in Piscataquis County, Maine.[^1][^2] In April 1924, steel was bought for several lookout stations as part of the state forestry department's preparations for the 1924 season; one was a 40-foot tower on Burnt Mountain.[^2] By July that year a construction crew had put the tower up.[^2] A state panoramic map dated 1925 has the tower at its center.[^3] A May 1935 item in the Lewiston Daily Sun, cited on the same Forest Lookouts page, still describes a 40-foot tower there.[^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register puts the removal between 2004 and 2005.[^1] The register gives September 15, 2008 as the date the site was registered.[^1]

Today the summit sits inside Baxter State Park.[^2] A hiking guide describes a one-way, 1.3-mile route that follows an old road to the former tower site, on a summit the guide says has lost its view.[^4] According to the guide, only the concrete footings remain.[^4] Just beyond the tower site, the path reaches a rock ledge that offers a view.[^4] The trailhead lies roughly 13.7 miles along the Park Tote Road from the Matagamon Gate.[^4]
