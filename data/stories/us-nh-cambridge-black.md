A fire lookout station went up on Black Mountain in Cambridge, Coos County, in 1910.[^1] That year the New Hampshire Timberland Owners Association paid for the tower, one of seven it funded.[^3] By 1922 the site held a replacement wooden tower with a cabin, according to a forestry report covering 1921 and 1922 that the Forest Lookouts page quotes.[^1]

The sources do not agree on the tower's form.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list calls it an open tower and marks it gone.[^2] The 1922 report describes a cabin, so the question stays open.[^1]

A hiking blog's list of New Hampshire fire towers also marks the site as no longer standing.[^4] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the site today, or whether visitors can reach it. Anyone who wants to go should ask whoever manages the land first.
