The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia towers places a tower named "winder" in Barrow County and gives its elevation as 970.[^1] The list states no unit, so we read that as feet, about 296 m.[^1] Its tag, gfc-928, comes from an older Georgia Forestry Commission list of tower locations.[^1] The name is the list author's own label, since names for towers numbered 73 and above are the author's creation.[^1]

The most detailed page we found is Forest Lookouts' entry for Barrow County.[^2] It reports that on February 10, 1995 the Georgia House of Representatives adopted a resolution letting the City of Winder lease the lookout site, roughly two acres, for use as a fire station.[^2] The same page says the parcel is state-owned and lies within Fort Yargo State Park. It also says the state forestry commission retains use of the tower and its facilities in times of extreme fire danger, which suggests a tower still stood on the site in 1995.[^2] The page gives no list number or height, so we take this lookout site to be the list's "winder" tower. No source says so outright.[^1][^2]

Fort Yargo State Park covers 1,816 acres, about a mile south of Winder, and is reached by Georgia State Route 81.[^3]

We found no source that describes the site today, so we cannot say whether the tower stands or what remains of it. None says whether the lookout parcel is open to park visitors. The City of Winder says it operates two fire stations, but no source we read ties either station to the old lookout site.[^4]
