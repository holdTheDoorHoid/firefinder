Rattlesnake Lookout sits 17 miles southeast of Burns, in Harney County, Oregon, at an elevation of 4,140 feet.[^1] The site is listed for the U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service.[^1][^2]

The Forest Lookouts history says the Civilian Conservation Corps erected the tower in the 1930s.[^2] firelookout.com also dates it to the 1930s, and describes a 50-foot wooden platform used mainly for watching wildlife.[^1] Neither source gives a year, a design drawing or a later history, so the construction details here are thin.[^1][^2]

We found no record of who staffed the lookout or of any fire it watched.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list shows the tower standing.[^3] Do not confuse it with Rattlesnake Mountain in Douglas County, which the same list shows as gone.[^3] We found no information about its current condition or public access. Ask the land manager before visiting, and do not climb the structure unless access is confirmed.
