The Emily Lookout Tower stands 68 feet high and uses an Aermotor ladder design.[^1] A Minnesota fire lookout list locates it about 3.5 miles to the south of Emily.[^1]

The build date is uncertain. The same list says it was erected in 1929.[^1] A December 1928 newspaper item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, lists among that year's permanent improvements a tower 68 feet tall, set up southeast of Emily on a hill.[^2] In April 1929 the same paper reported that Taft Kirvan and his wife expected to relocate to Emily, where he would take a job watching from the fire tower.[^2] We found no account of a fire the tower watched.

The sources also disagree on the county. Forest Lookouts and the Forest Fire Lookout Association both say Cass County,[^2][^3] while the Minnesota list appears to place the tower in Crow Wing County.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^3]

We found no recent report on the tower's condition, public access or rental status. We could not confirm who controls the site, so ask before visiting, and do not climb it.
