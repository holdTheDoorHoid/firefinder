Rookie Point Lookout sits in Custer County, Idaho, about 27 miles west-southwest of Challis, in the Challis National Forest.[^1][^3]

The site stands at 7,297 feet.[^1] Forest Lookouts gives its location as 12N-15E.[^3]

The lookout was a cabin from the 1930s, and both the firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts listings mark it gone.[^1][^2]

A user-built Gaia GPS listing, Rookie Point via Yankee Fork Road, describes a moderate one-way route past the point, inside Salmon-Challis National Forest.[^4] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page marks the site as hike-in.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether any permit or closure applies.
