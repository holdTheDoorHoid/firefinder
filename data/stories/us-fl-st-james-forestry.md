The site is a Florida Forest Service ranger station in Franklin County, Florida, known in records as the St. James Forestry Site and the St. James Fire Tower.[^1] Its listed address is 4400 Highway 319 in Sopchoppy.[^1]

The site lies within Tate's Hell State Forest, which covers 212,645 acres in Franklin and Liberty counties.[^1][^2] The forest is near Carrabelle, reached from US 98 along the Gulf coast, and it also lies on St. James Island.[^2]

When the Florida Forest Service was founded in 1928, observation towers were an important part of its fire-control work.[^4]

A map database lists a tower under that same name, Saint James Fire Tower.[^3] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether any tower is open to visitors.
