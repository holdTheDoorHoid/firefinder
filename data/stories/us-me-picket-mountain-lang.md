Picket Mountain (Lang), as the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it, held a fire lookout tower in Franklin County, Maine.[^1] A 2021 list of Maine towers in the Lewiston Sun Journal, drawn from the association's Maine chapter, places it in Lang Plantation and spells the name Pickett Mountain.[^2] The two spellings differ by a single letter, and the association's version matches our records.[^1][^2]

That list dates the tower to 1916 and says it was abandoned in 1921.[^2] The association's own list now marks the tower as gone and gives its position as 45.10203, -70.63853.[^1]

No source we read describes the site today. We cannot say what, if anything, remains on the mountain or whether the summit is open to the public, so check with the landowner before going up to look.

[^1]: Maine Fire Towers, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/me/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Maine fire towers: By the numbers, Lewiston Sun Journal, https://www.sunjournal.com/2021/04/25/maine-fire-towers-by-the-numbers/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
