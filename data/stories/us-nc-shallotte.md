OpenStreetMap lists a "Shallotte Lookout Tower" and tags it as an observation tower.[^1] TopoQuest also places a tower in Brunswick County at the same spot.[^2] A state groundwater site map dated 09-26-2023 labels a "fire tower" northwest of Shallotte.[^3] A list of North Carolina lookout towers gives the Shallotte site as 33.99166, -78.43861 in Brunswick County.[^4] The map's site coordinates sit within about 30 metres of the OpenStreetMap point, so the map and the OpenStreetMap entry probably describe the same structure.[^3][^1] The map says fire tower and OpenStreetMap says observation tower, and the sources do not say which use applied.[^3][^1]

None of these sources gives a build date, tower height or current condition, and none says whether the public can visit.[^1][^2][^3]
