Little Mountain's tower stood on a ridge in Pocahontas County, West Virginia, inside the Monongahela National Forest.[^1] The register places it on the ridge dividing the Greenbrier River watershed, at 3,419 feet (1,042 metres).[^1] The U.S. Forest Service administers the site.[^1] Brian Powell nominated it, and the register added it in January 2017 as US 1642, WV 33.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county table marks the tower as gone and lists its type as unknown.[^2]

For context across the forest, the Mon Forest Towns guide says that of the 22 federally operated towers that once stood across the Monongahela, only three remain accessible in their original locations.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Little Mountain site, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]
