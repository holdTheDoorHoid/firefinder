Pine Island was a forestry station in Koochiching County, Minnesota, and the lookout there was erected in 1937.[^1][^2] The Minnesota DNR says the tower was built to match an earlier tower on the same site.[^2] The Beltrami County tower report describes it as a 100-foot Aermotor stairway tower.[^1]

The DNR ties the 1930s fires on the Big Bog to fire towers built on Ludlow Island and on the Tamarac River.[^3] The sources we read do not name any fire this particular tower watched, and we did not find named lookouts for the Pine Island station.[^3]

In 2011 the tower was moved from Pine Island to the Big Bog State Recreation Area, where it was renovated, rebuilt and reopened to the public.[^1][^3][^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association’s Minnesota list marks the Pine Island site as gone and the Big Bog tower as standing.[^5]

The tower stands in the south unit of the park on Highway 72, near the visitor center.[^2][^4] Visitors may climb the 100-foot tower during regular visitor center hours.[^3] The IFalls guide gives those hours as Monday through Saturday, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m., so check before you go.[^4] A camera on the tower sends a live view to a kiosk in the visitor center.[^3]

Both the county report and the DNR give the height as 100 feet, about 30.5 metres.[^1][^2]
