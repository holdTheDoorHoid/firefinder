The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a standing tower called Iron Belt in Iron County, Wisconsin, at 46.4275 north and 90.35977 west.[^1][^2] The listings give no construction date, builder, owner or staffing history, and the other sources we read do not describe the tower.[^1][^2]

The tower shares its name with the community of Iron Belt, which lies along Wisconsin Highway 77 in the town of Knight.[^3]

In December 2015 the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources said it was getting rid of the towers it used to watch for wildfires.[^4] We found no record that says whether Iron Belt was among them, and the listing still shows it as standing.[^1]

We found no information about the tower's access, owner or current condition. Please do not climb it. Ask the landowner or the local forest office before going anywhere near it.
