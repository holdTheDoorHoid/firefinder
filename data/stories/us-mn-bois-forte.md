The Bois Forte Lookout is a stairway tower on the Nett Lake Indian Reservation in Koochiching County, Minnesota.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register gives 1934 as its build year. One tower footing also carries an inscription dated September 6, 1934.[^1] The register calls it one of three lookout towers on the reservation and does not name a builder.[^1] Survey notes put its overall height at 125 feet.[^1]

The register does not say who staffed the tower or whether it was used to spot fires.[^1] No other fire history turned up in the sources searched for this story.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^2] The register added it on January 8, 2021, at the nomination of Jacob Hufnagle.[^1] Its description also reports recent replacement of the wood steps and landing boards, which it calls in good condition, but gives no date for that work.[^1]

The register names the Bois Forte Band of Chippewa as the body that administers the tower.[^1] It gives no visiting rules, so anyone hoping to see the tower should ask the Band before going.
