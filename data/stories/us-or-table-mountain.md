Table Mountain lies in Multnomah County, Oregon, within the Mount Hood National Forest, about 14 miles northwest of Mount Hood, at 4,540 feet.[^2][^1] The U.S. Forest Service administers the site.[^1] In 1910 the Forest Service built a log cabin there with a platform on its roof.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list classes the site as ground rather than tower.[^4] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register listed it on September 21, 2010, as US 651 and OR 83.[^1] Ray Kresek nominated the site for that listing.[^1]

The cabin is gone. The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as gone, and the Oregon Lookouts history says the cabin was removed.[^2][^3][^4] No source I read describes what remains at the site now, or whether visitors can reach it.
