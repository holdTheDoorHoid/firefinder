Snow Mountain, in the Chain of Ponds area of northern Franklin County, got a wooden fire tower in 1910.[^1][^2] Four years later a 24-foot steel tower with a cab took its place.[^2][^3]

Don Hicks began working as a watchman the day after he graduated from high school in 1966, and his first tower was on Snow Mountain.[^1] A 1967 photo caption shows him looking out of that tower's cab.[^1]

The cab was still in place in 1967, but it has since fallen off.[^4][^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association still lists the tower as standing, though its cab is missing.[^5] Maine ended its staffed fire-tower system after a 1991 budget crisis, and some of its towers were later torn down or fell down without help.[^6]

The summit lies on Penobscot Indian Nation property.[^7] A gate sits on the dirt road off Route 27, and the trailhead lies about five miles beyond it.[^8] The climb gains about 1,880 feet.[^7] Nothing here suggests visitors may climb the bare structure, so leave it alone and check with the landowner before going.
