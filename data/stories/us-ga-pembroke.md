Tom Dunigan's list of Georgia lookout towers has two entries for Pembroke, both in Bryan County.[^1] The first sits at an elevation of 74 feet (23 m).[^1] The second, labelled "pembroke nw", lies northwest of the first and sits at 106 feet (32 m).[^1] Neither entry gives a build date, builder, height or design. The second carries only a one-word note, "Ron", which the list does not explain.[^1]

The name matches the town of Pembroke, which has held the county seat of Bryan County since 1937, according to the New Georgia Encyclopedia.[^2] No source we found ties either entry to the town.

The National Historic Lookout Register says it has 29 lookouts registered in Georgia, and none of the names on its Georgia list matches either Pembroke entry.[^3]

Statewide, the Georgia Forestry Commission started moving away from staffed lookouts toward aerial patrols in the late 1980s, and many of the towers were later scrapped and sold.[^4] No source we found links either entry to that change.

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at either site today, or whether the public can reach them.
