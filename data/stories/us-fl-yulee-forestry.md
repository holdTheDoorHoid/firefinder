The Yulee Forestry Tower is in Nassau County, Florida.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as a 100-foot Aermotor LS-40 steel lookout tower, maintained by the Florida Department of Forestry.[^1]

We found no build year for it. The Forest Lookouts page for the site lists it under the name Becker, with the words Rayonier Corp beside the county name.[^2] Rayonier, which is behind the Wildlight development, has a long timber history in Northeast Florida.[^3] The sources do not say whether the timber company owned the land, the tower or both.[^2][^3]

The Register shows the tower registered on May 22, 2022, as US 1578, FL 25, with Rocco Germani as nominator. It gives the elevation as 22 feet (7 metres).[^1] The 2022 listing is the latest record we found, so its present condition is unknown.[^1]

Do not confuse it with the fire tower at the entrance to Wildlight's Garden District.[^3] The County Insider, a Nassau County publication, calls that tower a tribute to the county's forestry heritage, taking its inspiration from the older fire lookout towers in Florida.[^3]

The Register page has no visit reports, staffing notes or access rules, so the sources do not say whether the public may visit or climb the tower.[^1] The Florida Forest Service, a division of the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services, manages more than 1.2 million acres of state forests and handles wildfire prevention and suppression.[^4] That page does not say whether the agency runs this tower, so ask before you go.[^4]
