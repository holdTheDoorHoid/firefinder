The Story Hill Tower Site is in Aroostook County, Maine.[^1][^2] The Maine list of former lookouts spells the name Storey Hill.[^2]

The register's detail page gives the site's elevation as 933 feet, or 284 meters, and says the tower was located in Washburn, Maine.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site on September 15, 2008, on the nomination of Bob Spear.[^1]

The Maine list marks the tower as gone.[^2] The register does not say what, if anything, remains at the site today, and we found no other source that does. So we cannot say whether anything is left there or whether visitors can reach it.[^1]

The detail page and the Maine list give different coordinates for the site: N 46.800590° W 068.233330° on the detail page, and 46.80255, -68.24990 on the list. Treat either pair as approximate.[^1][^2]

[^2]: Former Fire Lookout Sites, Maine list, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/me/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
