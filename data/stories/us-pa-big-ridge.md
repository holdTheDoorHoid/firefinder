Big Ridge, in Tioga County, had a lookout from 1914: a tree observation post set 40 feet above the ground.[^1] The former fire lookout register dates the same tree tower to 1914 and says it was still in use around 1917.[^2] The Eastern US Lookouts page and the register agree on the year and the tree form.[^1][^2]

The register says the tower is no longer standing.[^2]

A gazetteer places a Big Ridge with an elevation of 1,850 feet near the Bradley Wales Picnic Area and the hamlet of Tiadaghton.[^3] The same gazetteer says the community of Leetonia lies in the Tioga State Forest, about 3 miles southwest of the ridge.[^3]

Today, no source describes what remains of the tree tower, or whether the spot is open to visitors. The Tioga State Forest reference is for the nearby community of Leetonia, not the lookout site.
