The Andover Fire Tower stands at the top of Holt Hill in Andover, Essex County, Massachusetts.[^2][^1] A fire tower went up on the hill in 1924, one of four towers built in Essex County that year.[^2][^1] The current steel tower replaced it in 1970. It is 68 feet tall with a 10-by-10-foot wooden cab.[^1][^2]

The register says the tower is operated by the Bureau of Fire Control of the Massachusetts Department of Conservation and Recreation.[^1] It also says the group of Massachusetts towers this one belongs to are all in active service.[^1] The state's program page says early detection depends on trained observers at 42 operating towers, but it does not name this one.[^3]

We found no account of who staffed this tower over the years, or of any fire it spotted.[^1][^2]

Today the tower remains in active use, and Wikipedia says it is closed to the public.[^2] Please do not climb it, and ask the Bureau before going near it.
