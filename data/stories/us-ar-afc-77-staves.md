AFC 77 is labeled Staves on the Central US Lookouts page, which lists it as a Cleveland County tower of the Arkansas Forestry Commission.[^1] The commission was set up by Act 234 in 1931, and the Encyclopedia of Arkansas credits the agency with a large number of its own towers.[^2]

The same page marks the tower as removed.[^1] No source describes the site today, so there is no record of what remains there or whether visitors can get to it.

Across the state, the count shrank a great deal.[^2] The encyclopedia puts the peak at 122 known fire towers and reports that 48 were still standing in 1994, though how many were still in service is not known.[^2]

[^2]: Forest Fire Lookouts, Encyclopedia of Arkansas, https://encyclopediaofarkansas.net/entries/forest-fire-lookouts-7905/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
