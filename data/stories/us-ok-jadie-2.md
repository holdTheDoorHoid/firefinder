Jadie is a fire tower in McCurtain County, Oklahoma, on land the Oklahoma Forestry Services says is now owned by the Ouachita National Forest.[^1][^3]

The register describes it as a 100-foot Aermotor tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab, and a Central Uplands lookout page gives the same height and cab.[^1][^2] The forestry service gives a taller figure, 110 feet of galvanized steel, so the height is one point where the sources disagree.[^3]

The sources also disagree on when it went up. The register's description says the 1930s, but its own record gives 1950.[^1] The Central Uplands page dates it to the 1930s and says the exact date is not known.[^2] The forestry service gives the most specific account. It says the property was originally part of the Land Utilization Project under an agreement dated October 15, 1952, and that the tower and its house were built in 1953.[^3]

The register says the lookout has had staff from the Ouachita National Forest or the Oklahoma State Forestry Division at different times.[^1] The forestry service says its forest tower observer retired in 2016.[^3]

The register lists Jadie as registered on July 28, 2009, with the Oklahoma Forestry Services and the Tiak Ranger District named as its administrators.[^1]

A March 2025 post from the forestry service lists the lookout as closed.[^3] No source checked describes what the tower looks like now, or whether visitors may go up it.[^1][^2][^3]
