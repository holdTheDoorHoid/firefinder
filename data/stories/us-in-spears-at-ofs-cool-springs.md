The lookout that now stands at OFS Brands' Cool Springs Campus began as the Spears tower, in Hoosier National Forest in Crawford County, Indiana.[^1][^2] The Register says the Indiana DOF erected it in or around 1936, and a county map from 1936 shows it.[^1][^2] The sources I read do not describe its design or height.

The sources I read do not name anyone who worked the tower or any fire it spotted. Mark A's guide quotes a 1965 survey update for a nearby marker. That update refers to the tower as present, but calls the marker "not an occupied station," a survey term that says nothing about staffing.[^2]

Mark A's guide says a 1946 survey entry places the tower on state property, and a January 1960 Outdoor Indiana article includes Spears in a list of 13 state lookouts in District Three.[^2] Mark A also reports a Forest Service web page with a 1973 photo of the tower still standing.[^2] The Register says the Forest Service sold the tower around 1987 so that a newer, taller one could go up, and that the buyer took it apart and put it in storage.[^1] A private source told Mark A the tower was taken apart around 1987 and sat in storage for decades. Mark A suggests it may have held both state and federal ownership at different times.[^2]

A private individual later bought the tower to restore it in Indiana, but died before he could re-erect it.[^1] His son partnered with OFS Brands, an Indiana furniture maker, to restore the tower and set it up at the company's Cool Springs Campus.[^1] The Register added the tower on August 3, 2026, and lists it in Pike County.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list places it in Dubois County and calls it standing.[^3]

The Register says visits to the campus and tower are by invitation only, and that invited guests can climb it.[^1] The campus is used for company retreats, so please do not visit or climb the tower without an invitation.[^1]
