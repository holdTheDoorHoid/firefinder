Mutton Mountain rises on the Warm Springs Indian Reservation in Wasco County, Oregon, about 18 miles south-southwest of Maupin.[^1][^2] The register gives its elevation as 3,983 feet, or 1,214 meters.[^1]

In 1925, a recommendation to build the tower on Mutton Mountain rather than Bald Peter drew a written objection.[^3] In an April letter, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, the writer says that foresters arriving at Warm Springs in spring nearly always chose Mutton Mountain as the sensible spot for a lookout.[^3] The writer still favored Bald Peter, arguing that smoky haze from August to the first good October rain would keep anyone from spotting a distant fire.[^3] In May, a Bend Bulletin item quoted on the same page reported that a lookout tower would be erected on Bald Peter that season.[^3]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register dates a lookout of unknown type on Mutton Mountain to 1933 and says it is now gone.[^1] The firelookout.com listing gives the same year and also calls it gone.[^2] The register added the site on August 26, 2010, on the nomination of Ray Kresek.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, and none says whether the public may visit.[^1][^2] The register names the Warm Springs Indian Reservation as the administrator, and the site lies within the reservation, so ask the tribe before you plan a trip.[^1]
