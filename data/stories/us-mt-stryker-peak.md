Stryker Peak crowns Stryker Ridge in the Kootenai National Forest, about 24 miles northwest of Whitefish.[^1][^3] The Montana History Portal gives its elevation as 7,338 feet.[^3]

The Forest Service built a fire lookout on the peak in 1935.[^3] FireLookout.com describes a 10-foot pole L-4 tower built that year.[^1] Civilian Conservation Corps crews were at work on the access road, and by September 1936 they had completed two of its five and a half miles, the stretch climbing the ridge to the lookout.[^2]

The lookout was used until 1955, when it was abandoned.[^1][^3] A 1978 photo still shows the tower standing.[^1]

In the 1960s the Montana DNRC set up a lookout point about 3 miles southeast of the summit, using a converted school bus with a cupola.[^1] A separate FireLookout.com record for Stryker Ridge dates a bus-and-cupola lookout to the 1960s and marks it gone by 1969.[^5] It may be the same site as the DNRC point, but the sources do not say.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list now marks the Stryker Peak tower as gone.[^4]

None of the sources I read describes what remains at the summit today, or whether the site can be visited.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
