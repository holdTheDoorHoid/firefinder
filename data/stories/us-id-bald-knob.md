Bald Knob was a log cabin lookout on the Clearwater National Forest, about seven miles east of the Canyon Ranger Station in Clearwater County.[^1]

Four of the five sources we read date the cabin to 1924.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The Forest Service's 2016 list of Clearwater lookouts places it in Section 10 of Township 40 North, Range 8 East, at an elevation of 5,332 feet.[^4] The Forest Lookouts site gives the same code, 40N-8E-10.[^3] Use of the cabin ended in the 1940s.[^1][^2][^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association classes the site as a ground lookout rather than a tower, and lists it as gone.[^5] It gives the coordinates as 46.83027, -115.42978.[^5]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
