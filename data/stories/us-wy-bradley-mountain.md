Bradley Mountain lies in Lincoln County, about five miles east of Alpine, within the Bridger-Teton National Forest.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 9,292 feet, and its page is headed as a fire lookout.[^1] The same page marks the site as gone.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wyoming inventory tells a different story. It files Bradley Mountain under a heading that mixes lookout sites with non-lookout structures, with the type unknown and the status gone.[^3] That section says these sites will be shifted onto the lookout listings if documentation shows they were used as regular fire lookouts.[^3] So the two listings disagree about whether a lookout ever stood on the peak.[^1][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
