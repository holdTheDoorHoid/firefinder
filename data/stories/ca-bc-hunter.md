The Open Canada Fire Lookout Index lists a Hunter Fire Lookout in Columbia-Shuswap, British Columbia, marked only by a map point.[^1] The listing has no tower type, build year or height.[^1]

Canada Lookouts, a site that lists lookouts by province, has pages for Mount Hunter (Lower) and Mount Hunter (Upper) in its British Columbia list.[^2][^3] Its upper-lookout page carries the status "Removed".[^2]

Parks Canada describes a Mt. Hunter Lower Lookout and a Mt. Hunter Upper Lookout on trails in Yoho National Park.[^4] It calls the lower one a historic fire lookout tower, and its trailhead is reached from Highway 1 eastbound only.[^4] The index does not say whether its entry means either of these lookouts.

No source I could reach describes what, if anything, stands at the Hunter pin today.
