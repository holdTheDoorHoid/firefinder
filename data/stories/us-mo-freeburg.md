The Freeburg lookout is in Maries County, Missouri, on a towersite the state acquired in fiscal 1948-49. According to the state annual report quoted on the Central US Lookouts page, the site was 10 acres at that time.[^1]

In the 1950s the tower served as a Ground Observer Corps observation post.[^1] In fiscal 1958-59 the wooden cab on top was fully overhauled, the page says, quoting the state's report for that year.[^1]

In fiscal 1970-71 the same report records a new steel lookout tower going up at Freeburg.[^1]

The site is now a conservation area run by the Missouri Department of Conservation, which lists it at 11.8 acres.[^2] A Wikipedia list of Missouri conservation areas describes it as a wooded site with a fire tower.[^3] The department says the stairs are open to visitors, but the uppermost sections are closed to them. Its page does not mention 1970 or steel, so whether that historic tower is the 1970-71 structure is unconfirmed.[^2] Drivers coming from Vienna travel six miles north on Highway 63, then a quarter mile west on County Road 209.[^2]
