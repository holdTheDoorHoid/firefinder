The Warex lookout was a 70-foot pole tower, which its listing calls an L-4 tower.[^1] It stood in the Kootenai National Forest, about 18 miles south-southwest of Eureka, in Lincoln County, Montana.[^1][^2] The site is at 6,242 feet.[^1]

The listing puts the tower in 1934.[^1] Only one page we found gives this tower's history, so the dates rest on a single listing.

The same listing places its destruction in 1972 or 1975 and does not say which year.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a tower, places it at 48.65832, -115.27443, and marks it as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.

[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
