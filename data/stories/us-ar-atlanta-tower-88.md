Forest Lookouts lists Tower 88 as an Arkansas Forestry Commission lookout about a mile east of Atlanta, in Columbia County.[^1] Act 234 of 1931 created the Commission, which also put up many more towers.[^2] Those who staffed such towers often lived alone close by and kept a daytime watch for smoke.[^2]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the tower in 1941.[^1] That description says the tower is built entirely of steel, with an enclosed cabin at the top, and is about 100 feet (about 30 m) high.[^1] It places the tower on high ground about 1,200 feet (about 370 m) northeast of the center of Section 1, Township 19 North, Range 19 West.[^1] The federal station record for the marker, DL1476, gives its position as 33 degrees 6 minutes 46 seconds north and 93 degrees 1 minute 54 seconds west.[^3]

No source describes the site now, including whether the tower still stands or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1][^3]
