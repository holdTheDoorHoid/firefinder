Nesbit Butte sits in the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest in Wallowa County, about 23 miles southeast of Enterprise.[^2][^1]

A May 1934 item in the La Grande Evening Observer described plans for a lookout house and a 30-foot tower on the butte.[^3] The register dates the lookout to 1936.[^1] The history page also lists panorama photos taken on August 24, 1935, and a Forest Service survey disk stamped 1937 was set in the concrete base of the southeast tower leg.[^3] The sources do not line up closely on the build year, but the register and firelookout.com both give 1936.[^1][^2]

firelookout.com lists known lookout personnel as Edward Dmytryk in 1941, Lesley Marks in 1942 and Robert Thorner in 1947.[^2]

The register describes the 30-foot wooden tower as gone.[^1] firelookout.com also lists it as gone, and leaves the end date as a question mark.[^2]

None of the sources says what, if anything, stands on the butte today. The register includes a 2008 photo of the site, credited to Ron Kemnow, but its caption does not say whether any structure is visible in it.[^1] We found nothing on whether the summit is open to visitors, so check with the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest before planning a trip.[^1]
