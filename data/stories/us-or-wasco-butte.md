Wasco Butte sits about 8 miles southwest of The Dalles in Wasco County, Oregon.[^3] A 30-foot L-4 tower went up there in 1936, under the Oregon Department of Forestry.[^1][^2][^3] A 1936 item on the lookout history page says the county had already transferred a small plot on a high point roughly seven miles due west of The Dalles, north of where the Mosier and Chenowith roads meet, for a lookout house on a 30-foot tower.[^2] That direction differs from the southwest bearing firelookout.com gives.

The tower was replaced in 1951 by a treated-timber L-4 tower of the same 30-foot height.[^1][^3]

A 1953 Forest Log item, quoted on the lookout history page, names Janet Fry as the person working the lookout.[^2]

The station reported fires in 1960, including the Eight Mile Fire on June 21 and the Pleasant Ridge Fire on August 3.[^2] It also called in first reports in 1965 and in 1966.[^2]

The register says the lookout has been removed, and firelookout.com says the same, but neither gives a year for it.[^1][^3] Photo captions on the register page, dated September 28, 2003, show an old foundation and three tower leg bases at the site.[^1]

No source we read describes access to the site today, so check with the Oregon Department of Forestry before you go.
