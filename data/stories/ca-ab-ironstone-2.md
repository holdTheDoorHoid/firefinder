Ironstone Lookout is on Willoughby Peak, in the Crowsnest Pass area of south-west Alberta.[^1]

Alberta's wildfire record gives 1966 as the year the lookout was constructed. It lists the building as a cabin, 5 m (16 ft) tall, at 2,078.5 m (6,819 ft) above sea level.[^2] OpenStreetMap tags its tower construction as wood.[^3]

Alberta says observers keep watch at each of its sites, usually from April through October.[^5] A hiker who visited on June 6, 2022 wrote that the lookout did not seem to be manned that day.[^6]

The Alberta record marks the site active.[^2] A blog list titled Abandoned Canadian Fire Lookouts includes Ironstone,[^4] but the same blog's own Ironstone page describes the station as actively managed,[^1] so the sources disagree. We have followed the Alberta record.

Hikers reach the lookout on foot. The route starts at the York Creek staging area south of Coleman, and the 2022 hiker's ascent took about 1 h 40 min.[^6] A 2014 hiker wrote that a sign on the trail tells people not to shoot guns, and that the hiker was nearing a private residence.[^7] A bench on the trail is named for Sarah Thompson. The 2022 hiker reports that Bob Spirko, who wrote up his own ascent in 2015, learned from a local man that she was a former fire lookout.[^6] That hiker stayed away from the buildings, and no source we read says the public may enter them. Keep to the trail and stay clear of the buildings.
