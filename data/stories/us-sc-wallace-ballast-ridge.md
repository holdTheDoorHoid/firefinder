A 1934 newspaper item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, calls the Wallace tower the first in the Florence County fire tower system to be completed.[^1] It was 100 feet high and stood on ten acres donated by the Wallace estate.[^1] The same site places it under Marlboro County, so the sources disagree on the county.[^1]

The item also says the land was cleared so the towerman could grow gardens and crops, and that a four-room house with a sleeping porch was put up for him.[^1] The page names the South Carolina Forestry Commission as the agency.[^1]

Statewide, South Carolina took its fire towers out of service in the early 1990s, and a Post and Courier article says over a hundred towers across the state were shut down.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the Wallace site today, or whether the land is open to the public.
