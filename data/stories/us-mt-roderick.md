Roderick was a fire lookout on the Kootenai National Forest in Lincoln County, Montana, about 9 miles south of Yaak.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 6,600 feet.[^1] Peakbagging.com, which calls the peak Roderick Mountain, gives the mountain's elevation as 6,635 feet.[^3]

Firelookout.com lists a frame cabin on the site in 1921.[^1] In 1942 a 10-foot wood pole tower with an L-4 cab was built.[^1][^3]

The tower was destroyed in 1975, according to firelookout.com, and about 1975 according to peakbagging.com.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks the site as gone.[^2]

Peakbagging.com places the lookout on a small hill about 700 feet southwest of the mountain's high point.[^3] That high point has a benchmark, placed in 1963.[^3] The page carries a photo dated August 10, 2020, but its text does not say what the photo shows.[^3] No source we read describes what stands at the lookout site today, or whether the public may visit it.
