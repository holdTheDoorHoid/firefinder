A 40-foot treated timber fire lookout, with a 14-by-14-foot cab and a catwalk, stood on Walker Mountain in the northwest part of Wythe County, Virginia.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives 1938 as its build year.[^1]

The Appalachian Trail once ran past the tower.[^1] It was moved farther north in 1977 because the ridge lacked adequate water sources.[^1]

The register says the tower was later taken down, leaving only four footers and two concrete steps.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list also marks the tower as gone, and gives its county as Bland and Wythe.[^2]

Today a maintained hiking route, the Walker Mountain Trail, follows the ridge and passes the former tower location.[^1] No source says whether the land beyond that trail is open to visitors. Big Walker Mountain, a separate standing observation tower in the same two counties, is a different structure.[^2]

[^1]: Walker Mountain Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/va/walker-mountain-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Virginia Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
