The Eastern US Lookouts list of Florida towers includes Rocky Hill in Suwannee County.[^1] According to the same list, an auction in 2008 sold the tower for roughly $1,000.[^1]

The U.S. Geological Survey has two records at one spot in the county, named Rocky Hill Fire Tower and Rock Hill Fire Tower, and both are classed as atmosphere sites.[^2][^3]

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1]

[^1]: Rocky Hill - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/rocky-hill1.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Rocky Hill Fire Tower, Adc., U.S. Geological Survey Water Data, https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/USGS-300951082531801/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Rock Hill Fire Tower, Bulk precipitation collector, U.S. Geological Survey Water Data, https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/USGS-300951082531803/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
