The Summit tower stood in Douglas County, in northwestern Wisconsin, at about 46.36576, -92.12227.[^1][^2] A May 1932 item from The Post-Crescent, quoted on a Wisconsin lookout history site, lists a tower under the name "Summit (Chaffey)" among those already completed in district 1 of the Conservation Department.[^1] It is the only date the sources give, and it places the tower as finished by 13 May 1932.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Summit tower as gone.[^2]

OpenStreetMap has a point named Summit Lookout Tower at the spot, but a map marker does not show whether a structure stands.[^3] No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it is open to the public. Do not plan a visit around a tower here.
