The Fairview fire tower stood in Gallia County, Ohio.[^2] The Forest Lookouts list of Ohio towers places the Fairview entry in Gallia County and under the Wayne National Forest.[^1]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register entered the Fairview Tower Site on September 3, 2014, as US 1556, OH 2.[^3] It gives the site's coordinates as 38.681611 north and 82.331500 west, and its elevation as 1,040 feet, or 317 meters.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Ohio towers marks the Fairview tower as gone.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^2][^3]
