The small observation tower on Monroe Hill stands in the northern part of Fox State Research Forest, in Hillsborough, New Hampshire.[^1] One hiking guide dates it to 1934 and says it was used to watch over the forest.[^1] The same guide suggests it may also have been a backup fire lookout.[^1] It also says it is unclear whether today's tower was built around the original one.[^1] We could not open the state's own lookout history to check the date, so 1934 rests on that single guide for now.

A fire tower history on the same hiking site lists the hilltop's south peak, Monroe Hill, at 1,170 feet among the towers it covers.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as an open tower that still stands.[^3] It is filed under "Lookout Sites / Non-lookout Structures," a separate table from the main list of lookout structures.[^3]

To reach it, start at the trailhead on Center Road in Hillsborough.[^1] The route follows an overgrown, grassy road for a tenth of a mile, then turns onto Ridge Trail, which is steep at times.[^1] In a quarter mile the trail levels off and reaches the tower.[^1] A hiker who wrote about the tower in April 2023 called it a very small outlook tower, and found that the views now come in glimpses as the trees have grown.[^4] The guide suggests climbing the stairs for a narrow view between the trees.[^1] We did not find a closure notice or a recent inspection, so please check with the forest before you climb.

One forum poster who visited said it was unclear whether the list's "Monroe Hill" point means the tower or the hilltop, since the coordinates match the hilltop.[^5]
