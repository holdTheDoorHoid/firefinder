Oliver is a fire tower site in Evans County, Georgia, listed in an online index of the state's lookout towers. The index places it at 32.074849, -81.819958 and gives its elevation as 159 with no unit; read as feet, that is about 48 m.[^1]

The index says its towers numbered 1 to 72 were discovered on 1:100,000 USGS topographic maps and NGS datasheets. It also says the names for towers numbered 73 and above are its author's own, so "Oliver" may be a label on the list rather than a name the tower carried.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register and the Former Fire Lookout Sites register each list Georgia towers, and neither lists one called Oliver.[^2][^3]

Today no source describes what remains at the site, or whether visitors can reach it.
