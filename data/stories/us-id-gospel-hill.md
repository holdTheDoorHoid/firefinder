Gospel Hill's lookout was built in 1934. It was a 30-foot pole tower of the L-4 type that stood at the Gospel Hill site in the Clearwater National Forest in Shoshone County, Idaho, at an elevation of 6,457 feet (1,968 meters).[^1][^2]

The U.S. Forest Service administers the site.[^1] It joined the Former Fire Lookout Sites register on January 20, 2011, on the nomination of Harold Coffman.[^1]

The tower's end date is uncertain.[^1][^2][^3] The register says it was probably removed in the late 1950s.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the same guess but puts a question mark on its heading.[^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives only the 1950s and marks the tower removed.[^3] All three treat it as gone, and none pins down the year.

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. None says whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2][^3]
