The Wilson lookout stood in Eau Claire County and was run under the Conservation Department's District 10.[^1] A newspaper item dated May 13, 1932, quoted on the lookout-history page, listed Wilson among the district's towers already finished, so the tower was complete by that date.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list marks the Eau Claire County site as gone.[^2]

The same list has a separate section headed "Relocated / Replica Lookouts," which includes a Marathon County tower called Block.[^2] It labels that tower as the relocated Wilson and lists it as standing.[^2] The entry gives no coordinates and puts "Private" where a location would normally appear, so its exact spot is not published.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the Eau Claire County site today.[^2] The Marathon County tower is listed as standing, but its location is not published, and no source describes public access to it.[^2]

[^2]: Wisconsin Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
