The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists this tower as Elderon (Pike Lake), in Marathon County, Wisconsin.[^1] OpenStreetMap calls it the Elderon Lookout Tower and gives its elevation as 395 meters.[^2] TopoQuest puts it at 1,295 feet above sea level, which matches the OpenStreetMap figure to the nearest meter.[^3]

The Forest Lookouts list gives its survey location as 27N-10E-20.[^4] According to the Town of Elderon's website, Pike Lake is the largest of the town's five lakes.[^5]

WJFW describes how the state's towers worked. When smoke was sighted, the lookout used binoculars and a compass table to pinpoint it, and the azimuth and distance were passed to a dispatch center.[^6] By WJFW's account, 72 towers were still in service when they were decommissioned in 2015.[^6]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association marks the Elderon tower as gone.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site or whether the public can reach it. Anyone curious about the spot should ask the local landowner before going.
