The Georgia tower list places a tower labelled "macon" in Bibb County, at 32.81794 N, 83.73331 W. The list gives its elevation as 515 with no unit; read as feet, that is about 157 m.[^1] The page says names for towers numbered 73 and above are its author's own. This entry is number 203, so "macon" is a label, not an official name.[^1]

The list's map was last revised in March 2016, and the entry carries the tag gfc-601.[^1]

Separately, the Georgia Forestry Commission's own history says that in 1956 the agency and the Georgia Forest Research Council shifted their state headquarters from Atlanta to Macon.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^2]: History of the Georgia Forestry Commission, Georgia Forestry Commission, https://gatrees.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/History-of-the-Georgia-Forestry-Commission.pdf (accessed 2026-10-21).
