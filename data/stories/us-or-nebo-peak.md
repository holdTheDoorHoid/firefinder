Nebo Peak stands in Wallowa County, Oregon, in the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest, at 7,518 feet, or 2,291 meters.[^1][^2]

A log cabin was on the peak by the 1920s.[^2] A note found on its wall in 1923 is quoted by the Forest Lookouts site.[^3] The same page describes the lookout as a retired sheepman.[^3]

The sources disagree about the tower.[^1][^2] The register says a 94-foot treated timber tower with a cabin was built in 1933, and that in 1955 it was trimmed to 72 feet and given a 10-by-10-foot cab.[^1] The firelookout.com listing describes a 30-foot pole tower from 1933 instead.[^2]

The firelookout.com listing names four known lookout personnel: Lee Stickler in 1936, Lynn Johnson in 1941, Jim Jackman in 1943 and Chester Powjowsky in 1946.[^2]

The register says the tower was removed in the 1970s.[^1] The firelookout.com listing says it was destroyed in the 1970s.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout today, or whether the public can reach the summit.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page also reproduces a survey-mark record that the US Geological Survey described in 1954. It gives the Forest Service's location for the mark: 3.5 miles by trail southwest of Lick Creek Ranger Station.[^3]
