Reid Divide was a lookout in the Flathead National Forest, about 12 miles southwest of Whitefish in Flathead County, Montana, at an elevation of 5,928 feet.[^1]

Firelookout.com dates the tower to 1938 and calls it an L-4 design.[^1] One Forest Lookouts page describes a crow's nest in a tree, with a tent camp and an L-4 lookout house.[^2] A second Forest Lookouts page for the same lookout describes a tower of round, native timber with an R-1 cab, so the sources disagree on the cab.[^3]

Firelookout.com says the lookout was abandoned in 1954.[^1] Forest Lookouts marks it as removed.[^2]

Firelookout.com also says a crow's nest two miles east of the lookout is still there.[^1] No source describes the tower site itself today, and none says whether the public can reach the ridge.
