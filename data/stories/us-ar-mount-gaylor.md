Mount Gayler, also spelled Gaylor, is an unincorporated community at the top of Gaylor Mountain in Crawford County, Arkansas.[^1] The mountain is the highest point along the full course of U.S. Route 71.[^1] After the 1929 stock market crash, Edward Bellis, Sr. moved his family to the area and built a gift shop and gas station for travelers.[^1] Burns Gables, a restaurant, opened across the road in 1937.[^1] According to Wikipedia, a steel observation tower went up in 1939.[^1]

The Arkansas lookout list names a "Mt. Gaylor" in Crawford County, with no agency given.[^3] Wikipedia instead places the tower in an area that was once a popular tourist destination.[^1]

Business on the mountain fell away after a new interstate opened.[^1][^2] Wikipedia names Interstate 49, which it says opened in 1999, while a 2012 account credits Interstate 540.[^1][^2] Wikipedia says the gift shop and tower are now closed.[^1] The 2012 account says that as of 2010 the tower was still closed and in need of repair.[^2]

Wikipedia carries a photo captioned as the gift shop and observation tower in 2020.[^1] No source we read describes the tower's condition today. Do not climb a closed tower.
