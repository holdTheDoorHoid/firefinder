The Clinton lookout stands just south of the town of Clinton in Sampson County, North Carolina.[^1][^2] The state's Division of Forest Resources owns it.[^2]

Two sources give 1937 for a 120-foot (37 m) wooden tower built by the Civilian Conservation Corps.[^1][^2] The CCC also built a four-room frame house at the tower, 31 feet by 25 feet, which was completed on September 15, 1938 by Camp P-68.[^1][^2]

The sources disagree about what stands there. A visitor in 1994 found an Aermotor tower, not a wooden one, and was unsure whether it was a second tower or whether the 1930s date was wrong.[^2] A separate North Carolina lookout listing describes a 120-foot creosoted timber tower in North Sampson County, built by the CCC on November 18, 1937.[^1] Whether these are one tower or two is not settled.[^1][^2]

A 1959 USGS map marks a Clinton Lookout Tower, and a 2025 street view shows the tower from East Elizabeth Street.[^2]

A sign at the property entrance, seen on the 1994 visit, identifies the site as the North Carolina Forest Service County Headquarters.[^2] The sources I read do not say whether visitors may approach the tower, so check with the Forest Service before you go.
