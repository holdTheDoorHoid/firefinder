Lookout Mountain, a 6,198-foot summit in the Wenatchee National Forest about 11 miles southwest of Cle Elum, is listed as the site of a tower built in 1930.[^1][^2] The U.S. Forest Service administers the land.[^1] The register entered the site on August 11, 2012, after a nomination by Ray Kresek.[^1] The register and firelookout.com both describe the tower as gone.[^1][^2]

Willhite cites the Kresek Lookout Inventory, which records some kind of tower on the mountain in 1930.[^3] He checked the entire ridgeline on his own visit, and he reports that people who have been to the summit saw no evidence of one.[^3] If a lookout was used there, he thinks the use was brief and may have been no more than a tent camp.[^3] The sources therefore disagree: the register says a tower stood there and is gone, while Willhite's notes turn up no evidence of one.[^1][^3]

Willhite writes that few hikers visit this southern edge of the forest, and that a faint, abandoned trail runs almost to the top.[^3] He notes a benchmark named TANO 1958 on the rocky top.[^3] None of the cited sources describes any lookout remains there today.
