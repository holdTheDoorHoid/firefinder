Morehead Triangle is a fire tower in Rowan County, Kentucky, in the Daniel Boone National Forest.[^2] The county's history of its fire towers lists three in Rowan County: Hickory Flats, Triangle Tower and Sugarloaf Mountain.[^1] Triangle Tower is probably this one. The map database calls it Triangle Lookout Tower.[^3]

The county history puts the tower off Dry Creek Road.[^1] Its cabin has gone, though part of the tower is still there.[^1]

The CCC at Clearfield drew up plans for a recreation center at the tower.[^1] The plan called for a park of about one acre and two observatories on the rock ledges beside it.[^1]

The map database describes the construction as lattice.[^3]

No source describes the site today beyond the remaining part of the tower, so whether the public can visit it is unknown.
