Firelookout.ca lists a Palmerston Fire Tower with a Tweed, Ontario address.[^1] The entry describes the structure as steel and says it appears on the 1977 Pembroke topographic map sheet 31F, third edition.[^1] It gives no build year, height or tower type.[^1]

Across Ontario, the Forest Fire Prevention Act of 1917 set fire protection procedures and divided the province into districts, each under a District Forester.[^2] A historical marker in Ignace says towers were judged the best general method of spotting fires wherever reliable communication was possible. It adds that the observer on a tower learned the surrounding land well enough to see smoke or fire right away.[^2]

No other source found describes the Palmerston site today.
