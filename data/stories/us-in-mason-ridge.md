Mason Ridge Fire Tower stands in Morgan-Monroe State Forest, which began in 1929.[^3] A November 1930 notice in The Forest Worker, as quoted by the Forest Lookouts site, described a new 9,000-acre state forest near Martinsville and said a lookout tower had been erected there.[^2]

The tower's on-site sign dates its erection to 1930, and the register gives the same year.[^3][^1] Mark A, however, quotes a survey note saying sources are confusing about when it went up. That note points to a 1931 state report that mentions an eighty-foot steel tower on the property.[^3]

The register says the tower had been condemned before the Young Hoosiers Conservation Corp (YHCC) rebuilt it in 2009 and 2010.[^1] Crews took two of the three sections down, restored the lower section in place, and re-erected the whole tower.[^1]

Dennis Goen worked as a towerman here from 1963 to 1965, according to an interview from a 2013 state video that Mark A cites.[^3]

Visit Bloomington's April 2026 guide describes the tower as newly renovated and open for use again after a year-long closure.[^4] The same guide says Trail 5 leads to the tower from inside the forest.[^4]

Mark A describes the tower as climbable up into the cab, but says the gate around it is locked most of the time. In mid-June 2026 a tornado struck the forest, and he reports that parts of it are not accessible.[^3] Check the forest's current status before you go.
