The Eastern US Lookouts history site lists a Lakeland tower in Polk County.[^1] Its only entry says the tower was dismantled in the mid-1980s.[^1]

The wider Polk County network helps set the scene. In May 1964, a Lakeland Ledger item quoted on a lookout-history page for the Cypress Gardens tower called that tower one of eight lookout towers spread around Polk County.[^2] The item also tied it to the Florida Forest Service's fire detection network.[^2] The same page mentions a Polk County Headquarters Tower just south of Lakeland.[^2]

I could not find a source describing the Lakeland site today, including whether anything remains or whether the public can reach it. Check with the Florida Forest Service before you go looking for it.

[^2]: Cypress Gardens - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/cypress-gardens.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
