Indian Point is a lookout site on the Salmon National Forest in Lemhi County, Idaho, about 24 miles west of Salmon.[^1] Its elevation is 8,453 feet.[^1][^3]

The only construction date the sources give is 1934, for a cabin. One listing describes it as a log cabin.[^1][^2] The Idaho Fire Lookouts entry gives the same year and elevation.[^3]

Two listings mark the cabin as gone, without a date for its removal.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list also shows the site as a ground lookout that is gone.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the peak today, or whether the land is open to visitors. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page tags the site as a hike-in location, meaning it is reached on foot rather than by road.[^3]
