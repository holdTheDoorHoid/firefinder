The Tennessee lookout list places a tower at Reelfoot in Obion County, at 483 feet (147 m), and marks it removed.[^1] The list gives it the number 9-11, which the page describes as a state forest tower number from a 1976 list.[^1] The same page says its author gave unofficial names to towers that the USGS maps and the state forestry list did not name, so a name on that list is not always an official one.[^1]

Across Tennessee, the Civilian Conservation Corps is reported to have been responsible for building 98 lookout houses and towers in the 1930s.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the Reelfoot site today. Tom Dunigan's page says most former tower sites are now privately owned, and it advises against climbing the towers.[^1] Please ask the landowner before going onto any former tower site.

[^2]: Tennessee Division of Forestry Fire Lookout Towers, 1933 to 1975 (Multiple Property Documentation Form), National Park Service, https://npgallery.nps.gov/pdfhost/docs/NRHP/Text/64501237.pdf (accessed 2026-10-21).
