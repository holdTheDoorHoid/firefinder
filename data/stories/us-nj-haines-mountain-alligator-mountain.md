Area fire wardens built this observation post in 1900, according to the register.[^1] The register says the wardens meant it for watching westward, toward a "great limestone valley" and a ridge in the distance.[^1]

The register names the site Haines Mountain (Alligator Mountain) and places it in the Edison Acres Wildlife Management Area, in Sussex County, New Jersey.[^1] The New Jersey Division of Fish & Game is listed as the administering agency.[^1]

The site was added to the register on July 24, 2016, on the nomination of Robert Spear, as US 1621, NJ 6.[^1] The register's New Jersey list counts eight former lookout sites in the state, and this one is among them.[^2]

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.
