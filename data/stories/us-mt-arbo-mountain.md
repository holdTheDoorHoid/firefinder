Arbo Mountain rises about 19 miles northwest of Libby, in Lincoln County, Montana, on Kootenai National Forest land. The site lists an elevation of 6,244 feet.[^1]

A lookout went up on Arbo Mountain in 1934. Firelookout.com describes an L-4 design built of wood and gives a figure of 10 feet without saying what it measures.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association marks the tower as gone.[^2] Firelookout.com lists it as gone as well.[^1]

The Skyline Trail, part of the National Recreation Trail System, runs near the summit.[^3] Its trail listing says the trail and the lookouts on Arbo, Skyline and Quartz mountains were built for fire suppression.[^3] The listing adds that a hiker can reach a south shoulder about 1,000 feet north of the trail, with views over the Mule Creek and Seventeen Mile Creek drainages.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout site today.
