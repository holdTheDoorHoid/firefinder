Crown Point Lookout was a temporary fire lookout on the Roosevelt National Forest in Larimer County, Colorado.[^1][^2] The register says it was set up because of heavy logging in the area.[^1]

The structure was a house trailer with a cupola measuring 8 by 8 feet, plus a firefinder.[^1] A 1963 Fort Collins newspaper item, quoted on a lookout-history site, describes a fresh station set up on a house trailer, placed in the Sheep Creek logging unit, roughly 45 miles to the west of Fort Collins.[^2] The item says a seasonal Forest Service worker staffed it during the summer.[^2]

The register says the last year of use was 1966, and that the trailer was likely removed in 1967 or 1968.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Colorado list marks it as a removed trailer.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
