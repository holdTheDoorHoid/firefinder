The Jacks Mountain Fire Tower stands on Butler Knob, a peak on the Jacks Mountain ridge in Huntingdon County.[^1][^2] The knob sits within Rothrock State Forest.[^2]

Construction ran from 1922 into 1923, and the reports do not agree on the exact year.[^1] A Huntingdon paper described a 60-foot steel tower under construction in September 1922.[^1] A 1926 report says it was erected during 1922 by the Department of Forests and Waters.[^1] An April 1923 paper said it was about completed, and a 1972 article gives the spring of 1923 as its construction date.[^1] We date the finished tower to 1923.

The tower is a Blaw-Knox steel observation post, and in 1972 it was one of five towers in the Rothrock district.[^1]

The tower was staffed by towermen.[^1] In December 1950 the Huntingdon Daily News named C.G. Weirich as the towerman at Jack's fire tower.[^1] A 1954 Huntingdon Daily News item called Charles G. Weirich of Mount Union an 80-year-old towerman, and reported nine inches of snow at the tower on November 3, 1954.[^1] In 1972 Harry Greenland of Three Springs was the tower operator.[^1]

An undated Wikipedia article says a closed fire tower still stands on the summit.[^2] It also says the tower currently holds a weather station that gives forecasts for the area.[^2] The summit is reached by a rough road, and the article notes views from a nearby scree slope.[^2] Because the tower is closed, please do not climb it.
