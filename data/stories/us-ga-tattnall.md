The historic fire tower near Reidsville, in Tattnall County, may have gone up in the late 1930s on the western edge of town, according to the Tattnall Journal.[^1] For roughly 80 years it was one of the lookouts that guarded the central area of the county against wildfires.[^1] A Georgia tower list on tnlandforms.us gives it the code gfc-116 and places it at 32.07700, -82.09198.[^2]

The Journal reports that this was the tower's third relocation.[^1] The most recent move began when crews took the tower apart and moved it to a new site on Old River Road.[^1] A photo caption in the report places that work at the Georgia Forestry Commission's Reidsville site.[^1]

No source describes whether the tower has been put back up, or whether the public can reach the Old River Road site. Visitors should ask before going, and should not assume the tower is open to climb.

[^2]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
