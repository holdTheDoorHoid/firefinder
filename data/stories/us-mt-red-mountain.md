The Red Mountain lookout in Lincoln County stood about 14 miles west of Eureka, in the Kootenai National Forest, at an elevation of 6,599 feet.[^1] Firelookout.com dates the tower to 1933 and lists it as a 30-foot pole L-4 tower.[^1] In the 1950s the lookout was given an R-6 cab.[^1]

The same listing says the lookout was destroyed in 1959.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks the tower as gone.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Red Mtn. Fire Lookout Tower, Firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/redmtn334.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
