The U.S. Forest Service built a lookout cabin on Elk Mountain in Hood River County, Oregon, in the 1920s.[^1][^2][^3] The register places the site in the Mount Hood region, and the Forest Lookouts page puts it in the Mt Hood National Forest.[^1][^3] The register lists the site at 1,712 feet, or 522 meters.[^1]

The register describes a cabin.[^1] Forest Lookouts dates the Lester Moe and Robert Cooper panorama photos to November 5, 1933.[^3] The register credits its 1933 panorama image to the National Archives Pacific Northwest Region.[^1]

Both the register and firelookout.com say the cabin was gone by the 1940s.[^1][^2]

The register listed the site on August 24, 2010, on the nomination of Ray Kresek.[^1] No source we read describes what remains of the cabin today.[^1][^2]
