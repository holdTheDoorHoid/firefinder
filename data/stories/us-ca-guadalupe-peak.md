Guadalupe Peak is a lookout site in Mariposa County, California.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page for it uses the spelling Guadelope, and it names the California Department of Forestry as the agency.[^1]

According to that page, a lookout with a structure was established there in the 1930s. The page does not describe the structure, give a year, or say who built it.[^1]

The lookout stopped operating in the 1960s.[^1] Both dates on the Forest Lookouts page are given only to the decade.[^1] The firelookout.org index lists the site, under the name Guadalupe Peak, at 37.44972 north and 120.05698 west, and marks it as gone.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, and none says whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Guadelope, Forest Lookouts, https://californialookouts.weebly.com/guadalupe.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: California Lookouts (Alphabetical List), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/ca/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
