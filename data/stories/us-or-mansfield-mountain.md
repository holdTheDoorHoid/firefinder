Mansfield Mountain is about four miles southeast of Vernonia, Oregon, at an elevation of 1,542 feet.[^1] The sources disagree on the county. The firelookout.com page and Oregon's index both place it in Polk County.[^1][^3] The Forest Lookouts Oregon page lists it under Columbia County, with the agency line "Columbia County FPA" and the Oregon Department of Forestry.[^2] Oregon's index gives its coordinates as 45.81210, -123.12287.[^3]

The lookout was a crow's nest with a cabin, listed for 1930. The same page says it was abandoned in 1935.[^1] Oregon's index records the type as a crow's nest and the status as abandoned.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the public can reach it.
