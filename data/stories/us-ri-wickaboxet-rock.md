The Wickaboxet Rock tower stood on a hill in Kent County, in West Greenwich, and sources disagree about when it went up.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives 1926 as the year the 65-foot steel tower began operating.[^1] The Forest Lookouts history site instead quotes a 1934 report saying the tower was built with federal money under a work-project program.[^2] The two dates are eight years apart.[^1][^2]

The register says the tower was decommissioned in 1973, then moved to Cranston's Adult Correction Institution, where it serves as a guard tower.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Rhode Island list has a separate "Cranston ACI" entry, marked gone. That may conflict with the register's guard-tower account, unless the two refer to different structures.[^3] The same list marks the Wickaboxet Hill site itself as gone.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the hill today, or any public access to the hill or the Cranston facility.
