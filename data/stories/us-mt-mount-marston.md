Mount Marston sits in the Kootenai National Forest in Lincoln County, Montana, about five miles east of Fortine.[^2][^3][^1] The Register says the U.S. Forest Service administers it.[^1]

The site got an L-4 cab in 1932.[^1][^3] The present tower, a 10-foot concrete base carrying an R-6 flat cab, was built in 1962, according to the Register and firelookout.com.[^1][^2][^3]

The sources give three different elevations: 7,321 feet in the Register, 7,343 feet on firelookout.com, and 7,330 feet in a 1960 survey station note quoted on the Forest Lookouts page.[^1][^2][^3]

In November 1970 the Daily Inter Lake reported that the lookout had been vandalized, with locks broken off its doors.[^3] The ranger quoted in that notice said valuables were taken out at the end of each fire season, and that the doors were locked only against weather.[^3] The notice added that access was thought to have been by snowmobile.[^3]

In August 2015 lightning caused the Marston Fire.[^3] By August 15 it had grown to between 1,500 and 1,800 acres, and a notice warned that the flames could still overrun the lookout.[^3] An earlier notice said the lookout had already been wrapped before the fire spread.[^3]

The Register registered the lookout as US 1502, MT 80 on January 9, 2021.[^1] Both the Register and firelookout.com describe it as staffed in summer, but neither dates that staffing.[^1][^2] The sources do not say whether the tower came through the 2015 fire unharmed, though the 2021 registration and present-tense descriptions suggest it did.[^1][^2]

None of the sources mentions rental use or public access rules, so check with the Kootenai National Forest before planning a visit.
