Canada Lookouts gives the Stetham tower as 24.4 m (80 ft) high, of steel, with a square cab, and files it under Gogama.[^1] Firelookout.ca places the same site in Greater Sudbury.[^2] Most sources spell the name Stetham.[^1][^3][^4] Firelookout.ca's page is titled Stretham.[^2]

Visitors describe what is left as the remains of a fire tower.[^3][^4] One tower-history page puts the towerman's cabin directly below the base, and says a square steel base outline can be seen at the top.[^4] Firelookout.ca instead locates the observer's cabin at the middle of the steel structure, so the sources disagree about where the cabin stands.[^2]

A visitor's listing added in November 2024 says the hill also carries a modern communications tower.[^3] The same listing says the ladder is cut off well above the ground, and it notes steep drops along the hill's edges.[^3] Please do not climb it.

No source says who owns the land or whether visitors are allowed.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
