Tobacco Row Mountain, in Amherst County, Virginia, once carried a fire lookout tower.[^1][^2] The register entry I checked does not say when that tower went up, who designed it, or who staffed it.[^1]

A microwave site now sits on the summit.[^1] The Long Lines Map and Information site says a Long Lines tower stands on the old fire tower's site, with surviving parts of the fire tower built into it. The original observers' cabin at the top now houses more antennas.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register says the steel tower now sits inside a microwave communications structure that belongs to American Tower Corporation. The entry gives a registration date of April 3, 2018.[^1]

I found no record of notable fires, and no record of when the lookout stopped being used for fire watch. Those gaps should stay open until someone finds a primary source.[^1]

Today the tower is part of a telecom site.[^1] The Virginia lookout list still marks it as standing under the register number US 1274.[^3] No public visit route turned up in my search, so this site should not be treated as open to visitors.[^1]

[^1]: Tobacco Row Lookout, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/va/tobacco-row-lookout/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Tobacco Row - Virginia, Long Lines Map and Information, https://long-lines.com/viewsite/6714 (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^3]: Virginia Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
