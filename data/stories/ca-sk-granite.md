The Canada Lookouts site dates a tower at Granite Mountain to 1962. Its page says smokejumpers put up a new steel tower there that year, 6.1 m (20 ft) tall, and gives a Department of Natural Resources report for 1962 to 1963 as its source.[^1]

The Firetowers of Saskatchewan listing places a tower it calls only Granite at 55-43-02 X 106-35-13.0.[^2]

A towermen list gives Basil Natamogen's service as 1953 to 2001, across four towers that include Pinehouse, Emmaline and Petitte Granite.[^3] His entry says he was full time on the Granite and Petitte towers in 1968 and went back to Granite until 2001.[^3] It does not clearly say which of the two Granite towers those years cover.[^3]

A separate line on the same list says the last tower was manned after mid-August 2001, and that all towers were condemned, with six to be rebuilt. That line does not name Granite Mountain.[^3]

No source we found describes what remains at the Granite Mountain site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
