The Glade Mountain tower stood on a summit in the San Juan National Forest, in Dolores County, Colorado, at 9,164 feet (2,793 meters).[^1] The tower was built in 1941, and the U.S. Forest Service administered it.[^1]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it as US 536, CO 8.[^1] It was registered on September 18, 2009, on the nomination of Susan Epstein.[^1]

The tower was taken down in 1970.[^1][^3] In the same year, Benchmark Lookout was built to replace it.[^4] Benchmark is a 42-foot wooden tower with a 14-by-14-foot cab.[^4][^5]

Two of the register's listings give slightly different coordinates for the summit. The detail page gives 37.77569, -108.58767, and the state list gives 37.77611, -108.58806, so check a map before setting out.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, and none says whether the site is open to visitors. The nearby Benchmark tower is a separate structure, which the state list records as standing, so it should not be taken for the Glade site.[^2][^4]
