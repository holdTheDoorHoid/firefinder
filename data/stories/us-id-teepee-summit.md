Teepee Summit sits about 17 miles northwest of Kellogg, in Shoshone County, at 4,087 feet in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1][^2]

A log cabin went up a quarter mile northwest of the summit in 1933, and a 30-foot pole tower with an L-6 cab followed in 1934.[^1][^2][^3] Two patrol lookouts were also in the area, one half a mile north-northwest of the cabin and one a mile southeast on Teepee Peak.[^1][^2]

The tower was gone by 1950.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under "Removed."[^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as drive-up, but its notes say reaching it was closer to bushwhacking than to hiking.[^2] The same notes also record No. 9 wire and three cement footings on the summit.[^2] No source describes the site today beyond those notes, or says whether it is open to visitors.
