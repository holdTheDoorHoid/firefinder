Quartz Butte sits in Lake County, about 15 miles southeast of Bly.[^3] The register lists it in the Fremont National Forest, administered by the U.S. Forest Service.[^1]

Sources disagree on when the first lookout went up. The Klamath Forest Protective Association's 1931 annual report says it built a telephone line and a lookout tower on the butte, and a July 1931 item puts the association's station on the butte, close to the Ewauna Box Company camp.[^2] The register dates an observation tower and cabin on the site to 1934.[^1] Panorama photographs were taken on May 13, 1934.[^2] The firelookout.com page describes it as a wooden observation tower with a cabin.[^3]

In 1940 the lookout was manned for 150 days. Its reporting station was the Bly station, and telephone was its only means of communication.[^2] A May 10, 1941 Klamath News item said a lookout was about to be sent to Quartz Butte.[^2]

The register dates the abandonment to 1948 and says the lookout is no longer there.[^1] The firelookout.com entry gives the same abandonment year.[^3] Oregon Lookouts marks the tower as removed, with no date.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list also marks it as gone.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today. The register includes a photograph of the site dated 2005, but its caption says nothing about what is there now.[^1] The register gives no access information, so check with the Fremont National Forest before visiting.[^1]
