The Gold Lookout was on Gold Mountain in Douglas County, in southwestern Oregon.[^1][^2] The site sits at 3,518 feet, and Coos FPA administered it.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register dates the lookout to 1935.[^1] Firelookout.com also lists 1935, though it marks that entry unknown.[^2]

Newspaper items reprinted on the Oregon lookouts site point to someone keeping watch on the peak before that.[^3] In April 1924, the Twin Falls Weekly News reported that Mrs. Charles Crouch had kept watch on Gold Mountain through the previous summer, serving as a fire lookout.[^3] A December 1925 Coos Bay Times item listed Gold Mountain, in the Eden Ridge district, among the stations Coos FPA ran.[^3] In June 1931, the Roseburg News-Review reported that Lee Wagner would spend two weeks on Gold Mountain before moving to Bone Mountain, and that Harold Dick of Camas Valley would take the Gold Mountain station.[^3]

If the register's 1935 date marks the first lookout on the peak, those 1924 and 1925 items contradict it. The sources do not settle which is right.[^1][^3]

The register records the lookout as gone by 1966, and firelookout.com gives the same end year.[^1][^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on Gold Mountain today, or whether the summit is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
