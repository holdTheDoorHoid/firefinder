Tailholt Peak is in Valley County on the Payette National Forest, about 23 miles northeast of McCall.[^1] The lookout was a ground cabin, dated to 1920.[^1][^2][^3]

A 1931 newspaper item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, says a government employee had charge of the lookout that summer.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts says it has not yet documented who staffed the lookout.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts page also lists the code 20N-6E-11.[^3]

The cabin was destroyed in 1952.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in one.[^2]

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
