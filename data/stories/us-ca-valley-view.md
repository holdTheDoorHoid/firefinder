Valley View Lookout stands in Glenn County, in the Mendocino National Forest, at 1,545 meters.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says the lookout was built in 1935 as a California Region 5 Plan BC-301 cabin on a 14-foot enclosed timber tower.[^1] A photograph on the register page is captioned 1968 and credited to the Upper Lake Ranger District collection.[^1] The register added the site in January 2020 as US 1897, CA 92.[^1]

The register gives the removal date as unknown.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list marks the site as gone.[^2]

The register says just the foundation is left today.[^1] An OpenStreetMap point named Valley View Lookout carries a fire lookout tag, but the tag does not describe what stands there.[^3] We found no source that says whether the public may visit the site.
