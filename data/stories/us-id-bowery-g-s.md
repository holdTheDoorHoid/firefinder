The Bowery lookout site lies in Custer County, Idaho, about 20 miles north-northwest of Ketchum, in the Sawtooth National Forest.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts files it under the name Bowery G. S., and its page is titled Bowery Guard Station.[^2]

The sources date the site to 1934.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com calls it a camp, while Forest Lookouts and Idaho Fire Lookouts describe a tent cabin.[^1][^2][^3]

Firelookout.com gives the elevation as 10,332 feet (3,149 m).[^1] A map database lists a Bowery Guard Station in Custer County at 6,765 feet (2,062 m), but no source ties that point to the lookout.[^4]

Forest Lookouts and Idaho Fire Lookouts both say the site was abandoned in the 1940s.[^2][^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as a hike-in.[^3]
