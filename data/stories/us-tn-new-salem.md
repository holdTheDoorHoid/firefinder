New Salem was a state forest fire lookout in Jackson County, Tennessee.[^1] The state's tower list puts the site at 1,030 feet above sea level and gives the tower the state forest number 4-21, from a 1976 list of towers.[^1]

Many of Tennessee's fire towers went up in the 1930s, built in partnership with the Civilian Conservation Corps.[^3] The state list says the corps erected 98 lookout towers in Tennessee between 1933 and 1941.[^1] Across the state, the towers together formed the Division of Forestry's fire detection network between 1933 and 1975.[^3]

The state list pairs New Salem with survey mark GB1901.[^1] The National Geodetic Survey datasheet, titled SALEM RM 1, says the Tennessee Highway Department described the mark in 1966.[^2] At that time the station was centered below the lookout tower, 3.5 miles south of Gainesboro, with its disk set in a concrete monument.[^2]

The list now marks the tower as removed.[^1] The only description of the site we found dates from 1966, so no source says what, if anything, remains there today.[^2]
