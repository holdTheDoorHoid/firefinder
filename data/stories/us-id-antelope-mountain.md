Antelope Mountain lookout stood in Bonner County, about two miles east of Clark Fork, on Kaniksu National Forest land.[^1][^2] A log cabin went up around 1923, and a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab came later, around 1936.[^1][^2][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts has no staffing history on file for the site so far.[^3]

The sources disagree about how the tower ended. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says it was removed in 1964.[^1] Firelookout.com, Idaho Fire Lookouts and the Idaho Forest Lookouts page on Weebly all say it was destroyed that year.[^2][^3][^4]

The register lists the site as registered on September 13, 2010, as US 628, ID 1, and gives its elevation as 4,392 feet (1,339 meters).[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives 4932 feet, while the register and firelookout.com both give 4,392 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts marks the site as a hike-in and abandoned.[^3] None of the four sources says what, if anything, is left on the ground today, so the record cannot say whether a cabin footing or tower base survives.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
