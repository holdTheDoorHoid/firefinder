The Birchdale tower stands in Koochiching County, about a mile east of the community of Birchdale, on the south side of Minnesota Highway 11.[^1][^2] The site sits on state forest service land.[^2] The state forest service erected the tower in 1928 as an 81-foot ladder tower, according to the register and the county inventory.[^1][^3]

The sources do not agree on the height. The Forest Lookouts build note says 80 feet, while a 1967 survey note quoted on that site and in the county inventory says 100 feet.[^2][^3] The same survey note describes a metal cabin of the bird-cage type, held up on four legs.[^2]

The county inventory places the tower near the forestry station along the highway.[^3] It also lists a 50-foot wooden lookout built near Birchdale in 1912, but it does not say whether that stood on this site.[^3] The register says a cabin and a warehouse once stood near the tower, and that a county garage is on the site now.[^1]

The inventory's owner column reads Mn MFS, which it explains as the Minnesota Forest Service, now the DNR's Forestry Division.[^3] The register entry is dated January 8, 2021, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^1][^4]

I found no source on whether the tower was staffed or for how long.[^1][^2] Nor did I find one on whether visitors may climb it.[^1][^2] The register names the Minnesota DNR as administrator, so check with the DNR before visiting.[^1]
