Bluejoint Mountain sits at the southern edge of Granby Provincial Park, in what Canada Lookouts calls the Kootenay-Boundary area of British Columbia.[^1][^2] Canada Lookouts gives the elevation as 2,363 m (7,753 ft), though a 2025 trip report puts the summit at 2,320 m.[^1][^2]

A July 2025 trip report found the fire lookout on the summit inactive, and gives its approach as a poor access road followed by a trail up the south ridge.[^2] The writer called the road hard going, with many water bars, and strongly recommended a high-clearance vehicle with AWD or 4WD for Bluejoint Lookout Road.[^2] The broad summit also holds a helipad.[^2]

A 2026 guide places the Bluejoint Mtn Fire Lookout (Granby Provincial Park) in a list of BC lookouts that are confirmed or likely still standing.[^3] The 2025 trip report is the only first-hand description I found, and it places the lookout on the summit.[^2] No source I found says whether the public may visit it today.
