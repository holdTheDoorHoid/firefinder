Thorpe Lookout is in Hubbard County, Minnesota.[^1]

A 2019 blog post about Paul Bunyan State Forest says the old fire tower once stood at Thorpe Lookout, and it recommends the spot for a campfire and a view of the sunset.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^1]

OpenStreetMap has a point named Thorpe Lookout Tower at about the same spot as the Forest Fire Lookout Association coordinates.[^1][^2] It is tagged as an observation tower, but its tags do not say whether a structure stands there.[^2]

The post also recommends a visit and notes that the North Country National Scenic Trail passes through the forest.[^3]

The three sources we read do not describe what, if anything, remains at the spot today.[^1][^2][^3] The 2019 post is the only one that speaks to visiting, so check current access before you go.[^3]

[^2]: Thorpe Lookout Tower, OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/7895315914 (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Paul Bunyan State Forest, Minnesota Bucket List, https://mnbucketlist.com/2019/10/05/paul-bunyan-state-forest/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
