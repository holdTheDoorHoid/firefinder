Redtop Mountain lies in the Kootenai National Forest, about 12 miles west-southwest of Yaak in Lincoln County, Montana. The page gives its elevation as 6,226 feet.[^1]

The lookout was a 30-foot pole L-4 tower with a log cabin for living quarters. The tower and cabin were both built in 1934.[^1]

The page headlines the site as R.I.P. 1934-1958, and says the lookout was destroyed in 1958.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Montana lookouts also marks the Redtop Mountain tower as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the summit is open to visitors. Ask the Kootenai National Forest about access before planning a trip.
