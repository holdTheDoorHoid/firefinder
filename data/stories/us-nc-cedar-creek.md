A historical listing of North Carolina forest lookouts places the Cedar Creek tower in Cumberland County, under the state's Division of Forest Resources.[^1][^3] It dates the tower to 1934 and credits the Civilian Conservation Corps with building it.[^1] The listing gives its size as 99 feet 9 inches of steel, which is about 30.4 metres.[^1]

No source I could read says whether the tower still stands. A State Highway Patrol notice issued in August 2026 describes a Cedar Creek Tower Site at 7109 Cedar Creek Road in Fayetteville.[^2] The Patrol maintains the statewide VIPER emergency radio network, and the notice calls for a new tower site to be built and an old tower site to be decommissioned once its equipment has moved.[^2] The notice does not name the lookout, so it is not clear whether that old site is this tower.[^2]

No source describes whether the public can reach the lookout. Treat any tower on a radio site as off limits unless its owner says otherwise.
