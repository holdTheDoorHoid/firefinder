The Gold Hill lookout in Shoshone County went up in 1938 as a 50-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab on the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The site is listed at 6,284 feet, and its patrol point sat one mile east, atop Boulder Creek Ridge.[^2][^1]

The tower was abandoned in 1948. Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both list it as gone, and the Forest Lookouts county list gives the same two dates.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho index also marks it as gone.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site drive-up, but no source says whether the ground is open to the public.[^2]
