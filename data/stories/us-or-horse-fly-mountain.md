Horse Fly Mountain has two summits in the Fremont National Forest, about nine miles south of Bly in Klamath County.[^1][^2] The register says the first lookout, built of sawn timbers and boards, stood on the southern summit.[^1] Firelookout.com instead lists a platform tower from 1932, put up by the Klamath Forest Protective Association half a mile southeast of the peak, and a 37-foot L-4 tower from 1934 that the present tower replaced.[^2] The register places its first tower, an L-4 on a trestle-type frame, on the northern point in 1934.[^1] The two accounts do not agree on where the early structures stood or when they went up.[^1][^2]

The present R-6 cab, which has a catwalk, sits on a 41-foot treated timber tower and dates from 1961.[^1][^2] The register's built field gives 1960, although its own description gives 1961.[^1] The register dates a standby period to the 1970s, after which the lookout came back into use.[^1]

The register says the lookout has been staffed in recent years, and firelookout.com says it is staffed in the summer.[^1][^2] A Forest Service fuel-break project on the roads near it was signed on February 26, 2025, and is listed as completed.[^3] I found no public access rules or rental listing for Horse Fly Mountain.
