Oakwood was a South Carolina Forestry Commission fire lookout in Aiken County.[^1][^2] The lookout-history site lists the tower as removed.[^1]

By November 1936 the tower had a second job. An item from the Aiken Standard and Review, dated November 13, 1936 and quoted on the lookout-history site, says the Oakwood tower doubled as a Red Cross first-aid site.[^1] The same item says forest fires had dropped in frequency and spread even during a severe drought, and a speaker credited the added fire towers and extra effort.[^1]

A 1964 USGS topographic map of the area, published in 1965, shows a lookout tower near Oakwood, close to the Southern railroad line that passes through the community.[^4] The commission's wildfire history dates its tower system to the 1930s and records that its final lookout tower was shut on October 1, 1993.[^3]

A post in the Aiken Info Facebook group calls the tower the Oakwood/Windsor Fire tower.[^5]

The lookout-history page says only that the tower is removed, so no source I found describes what remains at the Oakwood site or whether it is open to visitors.[^1]
