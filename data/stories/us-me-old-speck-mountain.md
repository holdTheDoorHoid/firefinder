Old Speck Mountain rises at the northern end of the Mahoosuc Range, and a hiking guide places the summit inside Grafton Notch State Park.[^4] The mountain lies in Oxford County.[^1] A wood tower went up in 1914, and a 36-foot steel tower followed in 1919.[^2]

By 1932, fire wardens had a trail up the mountain.[^2] The last watchman, Eric Wight, took the cabin off the tower in summer 1959, according to his 2019 obituary.[^3] WillhiteWeb also dates the removal of the cab to 1959.[^2]

WillhiteWeb says Old Speck, unlike its neighbors, has seen no fire.[^2] We found no fire tied to the tower in the sources we read.

A 38-foot observation platform went up in 1997.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine list marks the tower as standing, and its note says that mark means the cab is no longer in place.[^1] The Summitseeker guide disagrees. It calls the tower restored and tells hikers the steps lead up to a cab.[^4] The sources do not agree on whether a cab stands there today. The 1959 removal has the fuller support.

A spur trail runs from the Appalachian Trail to the summit tower, and the standard route starts at the Grafton Notch parking area.[^4] WillhiteWeb puts the side trail at 0.3 miles, with 40 feet of climb.[^2] We found no closure notice in the pages we read. Check with Grafton Notch State Park before you go.
