Buck Point sits at 5,006 feet in Jackson County, Oregon, inside the Rogue River National Forest.[^1] A 1935 forest history, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, says the Willow Creek road work by the South Fork CCC camp was mainly for protection but was also aimed at developing Buck Point as a lookout fireman station.[^2]

A ground cab went up in 1953.[^1][^3] In 1941 the lookout was staffed for 105 days, and its reports went by telephone to the reporting station at Butte Falls.[^2]

In 1970 the lookout house was replaced by a trailer with a pop-up cupola, which the Forest Lookouts page says was used for a while and then removed.[^1][^2] The register dates the site's abandonment to the 1970s and lists the lookout as gone.[^1]

None of the sources checked describes what remains at the site today. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list still shows the site as a ground-level lookout that is gone.[^4]
