The Bald Mountain lookout in Clearwater County was run by the Clearwater Timber Protective Association.[^1][^2]

The sources disagree on the earliest date. Two sites date a log cabin with a crow's nest to 1914,[^2][^3] while a line in the association's 1915 annual report already lists a Bald Mountain Lookout Cabin.[^1]

By 1923 the site had a pole observation tower with a log cabin. In 1933 it had a 50-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2][^3]

The association's report for 1959 and 1960 says repairs to the lookout were completed in 1960.[^1]

The fate is unclear. Two sites say the tower was destroyed in 1972,[^2][^3] while a third lists it with no date, as removed.[^1]

A 2005 chapter report about Clearwater National Forest towers mentions a Bald Mountain back in the rental program, but it does not describe this site.[^4]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^3] One site labels the tower "Drive-Up", but no source says whether the public can reach it.[^3]
