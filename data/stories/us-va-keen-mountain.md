The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a standing tower on Keen Mountain in Buchanan County, Virginia.[^1] A place-name website says the U.S. Board on Geographic Names catalogues the Keen Mountain Fire Tower as a locale, and gives its elevation as 2,710 feet.[^2]

Keen Mountain is also the name of an unincorporated community on U.S. Route 460, just south of Oakwood.[^3]

We could not find when the Keen Mountain tower was built, who built it, what it looks like, or who has staffed it. In 1928, Virginia's state forestry office launched a statewide program to put up steel lookout towers.[^4] Later, the agency built fire watchtowers at important sites across Virginia.[^5] We cannot tell whether the Keen Mountain tower is part of either effort.

We also found no access or closure information. Check who owns the land before you go, and do not climb the tower if it is closed or on private land.
