The Mount Katahdin lookout began in 1913, when the mountain became a lookout station.[^1] Frank Sewall built a wooden ground house on a ledge on the Abol Slide, a spot where hikers often camped on the way to the summit, and he also served as the watchman.[^1][^3]

A 1916 Forest Commissioners report, as WillhiteWeb reads it, says the observations were taken from the summit.[^1] The same report says that wire for a telephone line to the top was bought in 1915.[^1]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes a tower and says it was removed in the 1920s.[^2] WillhiteWeb describes a cabin and a summit lookout instead, so the sources do not agree on what stood there.[^1][^2]

WillhiteWeb says severe weather led to the lookout's abandonment in 1920.[^1] A forum post that quotes a history of Baxter State Park gives different reasons: a view only in certain directions, and much fog and cloud.[^3]

Hikers kept using the cabin for several years after that, until it collapsed.[^1] WillhiteWeb says it was then known as the Watchmans Camp.[^1]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. WillhiteWeb notes that a trail reaches the summit, and a forum poster says some people have tried to find the cabin's site without knowing whether they succeeded.[^1][^3]
