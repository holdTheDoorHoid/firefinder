The Reed Hill Tower stood in Jackson County, Ohio, at 1,027 feet (313 meters).[^1] The register says it was 75 feet tall, which is about 22.9 meters.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site lists the Ohio Department of Natural Resources for the site.[^2]

By October 1934 the tower appears to have been in use already.[^2] A Chillicothe Gazette note of October 27, 1934, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, says Harry S. Tripp would be returning to the Reed Hill lookout, which sat west of Hamden.[^2] The word "again" suggests he had staffed it before.[^2]

A 1961 map of Hamden, described on the Pastmaps site, includes the Reed Hill Lookout Tower.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Ohio list marks the tower as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site now, or whether the spot is open to visitors.
