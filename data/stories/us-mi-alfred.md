The Alfred Fire Tower is in Dickinson County, Michigan, in the Sturgeon River State Forest.[^2] The tower's listed coordinates are 46.0855219 north and 87.6626501 west.[^2]

A survey description by the U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey is dated 1954, and it places a survey station at the tower's site.[^1] The station sat 44 feet southeast of the tower's southwest leg, and a reference mark was set in the center of the tower's base.[^1] The description places the station about 6 miles southeast of Ralph.[^1]

Michigan's fire tower directory lists the Alfred Fire Tower in Dickinson County, but the entry we could read gives no status.[^3]

A map-archive page for a 1957 USGS topographic map of the area describes the view from the Alfred Lookout Tower.[^4]

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
