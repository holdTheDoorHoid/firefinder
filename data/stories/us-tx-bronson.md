The Bronson lookout tower stands in Sabine County, about a mile and a quarter northeast of the community of Bronson, on Texas Forest Service property.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a standing tower.[^2]

A 1972 National Geodetic Survey description describes a steel structure on four legs, painted silver, with a cabin at the top. It puts the overall height at about 110 feet, or roughly 33.5 meters.[^1]

The sources read do not say when the tower was built, who first staffed it or which fires it watched. The survey description is the only detailed account we found, and it says nothing about staffing or fires.

The same description gives road directions from Bronson, but they date from 1972 and may not match the roads today.[^1] Nothing read says whether the public may visit or climb the tower, so check with the Texas Forest Service before planning a trip.
