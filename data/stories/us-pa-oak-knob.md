The Oak Knob lookout stood in Franklin County.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page describes it as a 30-foot wood tower erected before 1918.[^2] According to the register, a tree tower went up in 1905 and was still in use around 1917.[^1] A regional article on Pennsylvania fire towers mentions a Franklin County state forest fire tower dated 1905, but does not name Oak Knob.[^3]

The register says the tower is no longer standing, though it gives no date for its removal.[^1] The site was added to the register on September 16, 2008.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today or whether the public may visit it. A hiking directory lists an Oak Knob Trail, with directions to Mont Alto State Park, but it does not mention a tower.[^4]
