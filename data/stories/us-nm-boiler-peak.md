Boiler Peak lies in Sierra County, New Mexico.[^2] A Forest Lookouts page places it in the Gila National Forest.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site at about 33.4358 north and 107.9135 west.[^2] It classes the lookout as a ground site rather than a tower, and marks it as gone.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Boiler Peak - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts, https://westlookouts.weebly.com/boiler-peak.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: New Mexico Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
