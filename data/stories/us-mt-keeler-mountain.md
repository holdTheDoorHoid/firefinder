Keeler Mountain sits in Lincoln County, Montana, on the Kootenai National Forest, roughly ten miles south of Troy.[^1][^2][^3] The first lookout here went up in 1924: a 50-foot platform tower with a log cabin, and the cabin still stands.[^1][^2] A 50-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab followed in 1934, and its foundation stones remain.[^1][^2]

The tower standing now is 53 feet tall, built of treated timber, and dates to 1963. It is the third lookout on the site.[^1][^2][^3]

Firelookout.com says the 1963 tower is available for emergencies, which suggests it is not in regular use as a lookout.[^2] The lookout joined the National Historic Lookout Register on August 30, 2015.[^1]

The most recent work is a 2026 condition assessment.[^3] Ahead of the fire season, volunteers teamed up with the Forest Service to assess the structure's condition and explore the possibility of reactivating it as a staffed fire lookout.[^3] Both the Forest Service and the volunteers named lightning protection upgrades a top priority.[^3]

Visitors should not plan to reach it. The access road is closed to protect grizzly bear habitat, and the closure remained in place in 2026.[^2][^3] The site is on the National Historic Lookout Register, but while the area is closed, visitors should stay out.[^1][^3]
