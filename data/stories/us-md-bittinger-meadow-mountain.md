The Bittinger lookout, also called the Meadow Mountain tower, stood on Meadow Mountain near Bittinger in Garrett County.[^1] It was a 60-foot tower put up in 1915, and the state's records call it Maryland's first forestry lookout tower.[^1][^3] Those records also say the state first hoped to set 30 to 35 towers on high points across Maryland, and that the towers were meant to make forest wardens more effective.[^3]

State forestry reports for 1918 and 1919 said the tower was still giving efficient service, which they judged from the observer's daily report cards.[^1] In March 1930 the state built a four-mile telephone line to link a forest guard station at Bond on Savage River with the tower, so that fires near the new state forest land could be reported quickly.[^1]

The Forest Lookout Association's Maryland list marks the tower as gone.[^2] The state's 2007 report on forestry towers covers only towers and tower properties that the Department of Natural Resources managed as of December 2006, and it does not list a Meadow Mountain tower among them.[^3]

No source we read describes the site today.[^1][^2][^3] Nothing we found says what remains on the mountain or whether the ground is open to the public, so check with the landowner before you go looking.
