Chickadee (Brush) was a cabin on the St. Joe National Forest, about 20 miles south-southeast of Avery in Shoshone County, Idaho.[^1] The cabin is dated to 1915 in three listings, and the Shoshone County list gives its location as 42N-6E-8.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts calls the site a hike-in.[^2]

The sources say the cabin was abandoned, but none gives a year for that.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a ground structure that is gone, at 46.9996 north and 115.7388 west.[^4]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. The sources also do not say whether the site is open to visitors, so anyone planning a trip should check access before setting out.
