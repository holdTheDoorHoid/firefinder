Forest Lookouts lists the Wise Mountain lookout under Wise County, Virginia.[^1]

Its one dated item is from March 19, 1950.[^1] The Kingsport Times-News reported that William E. Greear of Dungannon had replaced Douglas Merle Dockery as the lookout on Wise Mountain.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list places the Wise Mountain tower at 36.94287, -82.50869, and shows it as gone.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, and none says whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Wise Mountain, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/wise-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Virginia Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
