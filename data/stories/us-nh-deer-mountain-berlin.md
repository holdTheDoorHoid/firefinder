The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register places Deer Mountain (Berlin) in Coos County, New Hampshire, and marks its tower as gone.[^1] A 2014 hike report calls the mountain Deer Mountain in Berlin and Milan, and says the fire tower that once stood there has been gone for a long time.[^4]

The lists do not agree on the height. One gives the elevation as 2,785 feet.[^2] Another gives 2,770 feet, marks the site as no longer standing, and lists artifacts.[^3]

The 2014 hikers reached the summit plateau by an old fire warden's road off York Pond Road, which is gated.[^4] The road was not too steep, they wrote.[^4] There they found traces of the tower: an old wood foundation and one cement block, both marked with graffiti.[^4] They also saw a fire warden's cabin with a porch, and a stack of firewood still in good condition.[^4]

No source here describes the summit after 2014, and none says whether the land is open to the public.
