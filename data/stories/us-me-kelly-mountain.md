The Kelly Mountain fire tower went up in 1925 in Brighton Plantation.[^1][^2] A local historic resources survey describes its metal frame as somewhat rusted but still structurally sound.[^1][^2] The state issued a 1927 panoramic map centered on the tower.[^3]

I found no source that names the watchmen who staffed Kelly Mountain or the years it was manned, so that part of its history is still missing.

In 2017 the cab came off, and a new observation deck took its place, according to the Fire Lookout Association's annual report for that year.[^4] The association's Maine list still marks the tower as standing, without its cab.[^5]

Today the tower is reached on foot.[^2] The summit track is 0.6 miles long and rocky, and the approach runs along logging roads that a high-clearance car is best for.[^2] A September 2026 trip report describes the ladder as nearly vertical, and says a small opening left its writer wondering how to squeeze through.[^6] The writer climbed only partway and did not go all the way up onto the deck.[^6] The sources do not name the landowner or say whether climbing is allowed, so ask about access before you go.
