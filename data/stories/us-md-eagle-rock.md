Eagle Rock is a summit in Garrett County, Maryland, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maryland list includes a tower there, marked as gone.[^1][^2] The state's Potomac/Garrett State Forest map labels Eagle Rock alongside Backbone Mountain and Lostland.[^3]

Maryland's first forestry lookout tower went up atop Meadow Mountain in 1915. The state's fire tower report says lookout towers were a major part of an effort to further reduce fire danger, and were meant to make the forest warden's work more effective.[^4]

The Garrett County sites on the state's own list of Forest Service towers and properties are Elder Hill, High Rock and Thayerville. Eagle Rock is not on that list.[^5]

A hiking guide describes the top as sandy, with pale rock, boulders to climb and a view of the nearby peaks. It describes a hike of about a quarter mile from the end of Eagle Rock Road and cautions that the boulders are steep and sandy.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains of that tower at the summit today, so check with the state forest before you go.
