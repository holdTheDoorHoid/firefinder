Bidwell Point, also called Elk Creek Butte, is a former lookout site in Glenn County.[^1][^2] It sits at about 1,197 feet, on the Elk Creek topographic map.[^3][^4] That height is read from a survey benchmark nearby, and the table lists no national forest for the site.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a ground-type lookout, now gone.[^2]

A personal note kept on GitHub dates the lookout's construction to 1968, but no other source here confirms that year.[^4]

In the peakbagging table, the label "LO site" marks places where the structure is gone and little or nothing may survive, or where its status is unknown. Bidwell Point carries that label.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the hilltop today, or whether it is open to visitors.
