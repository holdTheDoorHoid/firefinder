The Menzies/Bailloquet Fire Tower is mapped in OpenStreetMap as a fire lookout and an observation tower, at 48.0913219, -84.7737218.[^1]

The map entry carries the tag abandoned:building.[^1] A note on the entry says the tower can be seen from Highway 17 (ON-17) while driving west of Catfish Lake, and from Steephill Dam Road, at the dam and from a spot directly south of the tower.[^1]

Apart from that tag, the entry says nothing about the tower's condition now. Nothing says whether the site is open to the public.[^1]

[^1]: Menzies/Bailloquet Fire Tower, OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/9588299822 (accessed 2026-10-20).
