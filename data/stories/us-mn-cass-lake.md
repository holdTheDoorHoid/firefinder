The Chippewa National Forest put up its third lookout at Cass Lake in 1936, behind the forest supervisor's office.[^1] It was a stairway tower 110 feet tall, built from California redwood, and it was called the Lydick Lookout after the tree nursery to the west.[^1] A forest handbook also describes a 110-foot redwood tower at Cass Lake.[^2]

A separate structure came earlier.[^1] The state put up a short steel tower of about 70 feet northwest of town in 1911.[^1] It is a different lookout from the redwood tower, so the two should not be mixed up.

The county catalog says the redwood tower was removed because it had deteriorated.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Cass Lake tower as gone.[^3]

The catalog describes the supervisor's office building that the tower stood behind as extant.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout site today, or whether the public may visit it.
