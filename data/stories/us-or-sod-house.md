The Sod House lookout sits on a bluff above the headquarters of the Malheur National Wildlife Refuge in Harney County.[^1][^4] The Civilian Conservation Corps built it in 1933.[^1] A June 1935 Burns Herald-Times report says CCC crews were preparing foundation piers for a planned 99-foot steel tower on a prominent point by the lake.[^3] The register describes an 80-foot Aermotor tower with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab.[^1] The firelookout page and a 2012 visit both give 90 feet.[^2][^4]

The tower seems to have been used mostly for watching wildlife rather than spotting fires.[^2][^4] The register says it was still maintained for emergency use.[^1]

On January 2, 2016, a group occupied the refuge headquarters, and the hilltop lookout was being used as a vantage point.[^3]

A May 2012 visit found the tower closed off, with its lowest flight of stairs taken out.[^4] The 2026 directory lists it as abandoned but still standing.[^5] The directory also lists the site as legally accessible to the public, but says that does not mean you will have access to the lookout itself.[^5] Treat the closed tower as off limits.
