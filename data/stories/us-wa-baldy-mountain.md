Baldy Mountain sits in Chelan County, in the Wenatchee National Forest, about 14 miles west of Chelan.[^1][^2] The lookout site stands at 6,421 feet.[^1]

An L-4 cab went up there in 1931.[^1] Both listings agree that the cab is now gone.[^1][^2] The U.S. Forest Service administers the land.[^1] The register carries three photos of the site, credited to Eric Willhite, taken looking north, south and west.[^1]

Ray Kresek nominated the site to the Former Fire Lookout Sites register, which listed it on August 20, 2012, as US 1384 and WA 284.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it. Check with the Forest Service before going up the mountain.
