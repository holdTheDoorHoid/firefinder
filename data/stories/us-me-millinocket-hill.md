The Millinocket Hill lookout stood in Penobscot County, Maine, near the town of Millinocket.[^1][^2] The Historical Society says the tower was a wooden structure put up in 1934, just outside town and near Route 11.[^2] A 1934 state report, quoted by Forest Lookouts, records an emergency wooden tower raised on the hill during that year's dry season.[^3]

The Society calls it a thirty-foot tower and says it was built near enough to town that no watchman's cabin was needed.[^2] Forest Lookouts also quotes a 1932 newspaper item about a fire tower being built at Millinocket Lake.[^3] The sources do not say whether that project became the hill tower.

The Society dates the tower's removal to 1955.[^2] It stood behind the area where the hospital was to be built in the mid-1950s. A very blurry photograph turned up on a photo CD, found while the lot was being cleared for the hospital in 1954.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's statewide list marks the tower as gone.[^1] We found no source describing what, if anything, is left on the hill today or whether the public can reach it.
