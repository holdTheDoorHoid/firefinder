Swan Dive is a steel fire lookout in Alberta, placed at 54.73 N, 115.36 W.[^1] The register gives 2012 as the year the present structure was built, and lists the tower at 30 m (98 ft) high, on a site 1,241 m (4,072 ft) above sea level.[^1]

Alberta says its fire lookouts sit where visibility helps staff detect and report wildfires.[^2] Its 127 lookout sites are each staffed by a lookout observer, generally from April to October.[^3] Alberta's history of its lookouts says towers on high boreal hills let the observer see past any obstacle, and that seasonal staff also recorded the weather.[^4] The register does not list a weather station at this site.[^1]

The register lists the tower as active.[^1]

The register codes the access mode as drive, so a road is the likely route to the site.[^1] Alberta says lookout sites are isolated, and that the people who staff them have to plan for a wide range of possibilities.[^3]

No source describes the site today, including its condition or whether the public may visit.
