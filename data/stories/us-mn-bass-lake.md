The Bass Lake lookout stood in Itasca County, in the Chippewa National Forest.[^1] A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description from 1959 locates the station some 2.5 miles southeast of Wirt, at the lookout tower.[^1] Survey notes from the same year describe a four-legged, self-supporting steel tower with an observation room at the top and an overall height of 85 feet.[^2]

Bids for the sale of the tower were opened on June 8, 1972.[^1] A note on the Forest Lookouts page rates airplane patrols as the stronger option compared with the tower.[^1] The statewide tower list says the tower was sold in 1972 and removed in 1973.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list marks the tower as gone.[^3] The sources we read describe the tower as removed.[^1][^2] Neither source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.[^1][^2]
