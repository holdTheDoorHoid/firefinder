Red Hill Lookout Tower stood in Bradley County, Tennessee, at about 1,162 feet elevation.[^1] Tennessee Landforms lists it as removed and gives its position as 35.0345, -84.9177.[^1] OpenStreetMap records it as an observation tower.[^2] Waterville, an unincorporated community in Bradley County, lies about six miles northeast of the tower, according to Mapcarta.[^3] TopoZone lists it on the McDonald USGS topographic map.[^4]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains on the site or whether it is open to visitors.
