Pass Creek Ridge sits in the Colville National Forest, about ten miles east of Metaline Falls in Pend Oreille County.[^1][^2] The sources give its elevation as about 6,054 feet.[^1][^2][^3] The U.S. Forest Service administers the site.[^1]

Built in 1934, the tower here was a 40-foot pole structure with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2][^3]

The tower came down in the 1950s, but the sources differ on how. The register says it was removed, Firelookout.com says it was destroyed, and WillhiteWeb says removed or destroyed.[^1][^2][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left at the site.[^1][^2][^3] WillhiteWeb's page says its author had not yet visited the site.[^3] A 1967 USGS map, updated in 1971, marks a trail ending at the lookout point, according to WillhiteWeb.[^3]

The register added the site on December 17, 2012, with Ray Kresek as nominator.[^1]
