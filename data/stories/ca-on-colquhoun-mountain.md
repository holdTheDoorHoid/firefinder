A steel fire tower was erected on Colquhoun Mountain in 1929, according to the Canada Lookouts website, which lists it under the Cochrane District, near Driftwood.[^1] A tourism listing for the lookout says the Ministry of Natural Resources erected it that year.[^2]

A French-language BaladoDécouverte entry for the tower says the Cochrane District once had 20 fire towers, each placed so that the observers in all the region's towers could keep radio contact.[^3] According to a website about the province's fire towers, most of the towers from that period were overseen by Ontario's former Department of Lands and Forests, which became the Ministry of Natural Resources in 1972.[^4]

The lookout can be reached on a 7.4 km trail through Greenwater Provincial Park, just west of Cochrane.[^2] The listing says the tower can be reached in every season, though some muddy stretches can become slippery during wetter periods in spring and fall.[^2] It adds that, at present, neither the province nor the municipality actively maintains the park.[^2]

In winter, snowmobilers on the park trails are rewarded with a view from the summit of Colquhoun Mountain.[^3]

None of the sources I could cite describes what stands on the summit today, or whether the public may climb the tower. Do not assume it is open to climb.
