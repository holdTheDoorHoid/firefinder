Sugarloaf Mountain is a British Columbia lookout site listed on the Canada Lookouts website, which gives its position as 50.437 N, 118.401 W.[^1]

The entry dates the lookout to 1947.[^1] It describes a 12 x 12 lookout built of cut beams from the native timber around it.[^1] The entry gives the elevation as 2129, with no unit stated.[^1]

The page also labels the site Bulkley - Nechako. That region name does not fit the coordinates given, so where the site lies within the province is unconfirmed.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, stands on the mountain today.

[^1]: Sugarloaf Mountain, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/sugarloaf-mountain1.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
