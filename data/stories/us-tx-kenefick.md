The Central U.S. lookouts page lists a Kenefick tower in Liberty County, under the Texas Forest Service.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the tower at 30.23998 and -94.89364, and lists it as gone.[^1]

Kenefick lies in Liberty County and had 615 residents at the 2020 census.[^3]

In 1930 the Texas Forest Service entered agreements with landowners for fire protection on 250,000 acres in Polk, Tyler, Hardin, and Liberty counties.[^4] The landowners were three lumber companies and John Henry Kirby.[^4] Two steel lookout towers, one 95 and one 120 feet tall, were erected for that protection unit.[^4] The Central U.S. page does not connect either tower to Kenefick.[^2]

No source describes the site today, and none says whether it is open to visitors.
