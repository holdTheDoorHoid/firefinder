Weches is an unincorporated community in Houston County, Texas.[^4] A lookout tower stood in the village itself, and a Texas Forest Service list gives that agency for it.[^3][^2]

In 1942 the Coast and Geodetic Survey described a survey station at the tower site, which it said was on land owned by the state.[^3] When the station was recovered in 1975, the old wood tower was no longer there.[^3] A steel tower had taken its place, about ten feet east of a USGS benchmark.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas table lists the tower as gone.[^1] Two lists put Weches in Cherokee County instead.[^2][^5] The survey note, the association's table and Wikipedia all put it in Houston County.[^3][^1][^4]

The station description also mentions a road to a farmhouse close to the marker.[^3] The association's table does not say whether its entry refers to the old wood tower or the steel one.[^1] The newest station note is from 1975.[^3] Before going, check with the landowner or the Texas Forest Service, and do not climb any structure you find.
