The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Coeburn site in Wise County at 36.95627, -82.44836.[^1] It files the entry under unconfirmed sites, with its type marked Unconfirmed and its status marked Unknown.[^1] The same list also includes a separate High Knob site in Wise County, at different coordinates.[^1]

Coeburn is a town on the Guest River in eastern Wise County.[^2] The Norfolk & Western Railway extended its Clinch Valley line across the community in 1891, and a local history credits the railroad with helping to create the town itself.[^2]

No source describes the site now. We found nothing that says whether a tower, footings or other remains stand at those coordinates, or whether the spot is open to visitors, so a visitor should not assume a lookout is there.
