Frizzell is a tower in Charlotte County, Florida, in the El Jobean area.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts site files it under that county and gives its location only as T40S R23E, a township and range.[^1] TopoZone gives the tower's coordinates as 26.9703° N, 82.2093° W, with an elevation of about 1 m (3 ft), and lists it among the towers of Charlotte County.[^2] No source we could read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Frizzell - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/frizzell.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Frizzell Tower Topo Map FL, Charlotte County (El Jobean Area), TopoZone, https://www.topozone.com/florida/charlotte-fl/tower/frizzell-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
