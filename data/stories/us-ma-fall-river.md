Fall River's lookout on Copicut Hill began operating on August 1, 1914.[^1] The registry gives that first structure a height of about 60 feet and places it on nearby Copicut Hill.[^2] A local appendix places the tower atop Copicut Hill on Yellow Hill Road, in the city's extreme northeast.[^3]

In 1938 the site received new power and two-way radios.[^1] The Massachusetts Bureau of Fire Control put up the current steel tower in 1966.[^2][^1] The registry puts the tower at 75 feet, with a cab measuring 10 by 10 feet, while the FFLA gives 68 feet.[^2][^1] Guy wires were added in 1970.[^1]

In 1990, vandalism sent a neighboring radio tower down onto the fire tower; the cab was lost and the steel was damaged.[^1] Repairs were made, and the tower was back in service by 1993.[^1] I found no staffing history for it in these sources.

Today the FFLA says the tower sits on state property and still lists it as in service.[^1] The state listing, last updated in May 2016, shows it standing and active.[^4] I found no public visiting information or rental listing. The state says its fire observation towers are not for public access, so treat this one as closed to visitors unless the Bureau says otherwise.[^5]
