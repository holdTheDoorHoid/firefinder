Jolly Mountain sits in the Wenatchee National Forest in Kittitas County, about 14 miles north-northwest of Cle Elum.[^1][^2] The site was established in 1916 as a fire camp, before any cabin was built.[^1][^4] A cupola cabin followed in 1921, and an L-4 cab replaced it in 1936.[^1][^4]

Firelookout.com's list of known lookout personnel has one entry: Montague Easton, for 1933.[^2]

Sources disagree on how the L-4 cab ended. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says it was removed in 1968, while firelookout.com and Willhiteweb say it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2][^4] A third listing marks the site as removed.[^3]

None of the sources describes a surviving lookout structure today. Willhiteweb describes Jolly Mountain as a former lookout site, and reports that signs on the trail show it is open to almost all types of users.[^4] The route starts on gravel road 4315, which runs 5.9 miles to a gate with roadside parking.[^4] From the gate, the final half mile is road walking to the trailhead, and the trail is about three miles one way.[^4]
