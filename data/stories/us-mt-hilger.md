Hilger was a lookout on the Helena National Forest in Lewis and Clark County, Montana, about 12 miles north-northeast of Helena.[^1][^2] The firelookout.com page dates it to the 1950s and calls it an L-4 cab.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a ground lookout rather than a tower.[^3] The page gives its elevation as 5,287 feet.[^1] The association's list places it at 46.771 N, 111.934 W.[^3]

The same page says it was abandoned and is now gone.[^1] The association's Montana list also marks it as gone.[^3]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.
