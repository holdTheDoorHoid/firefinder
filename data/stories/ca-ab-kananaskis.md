Kananaskis Fire Lookout sits in Kananaskis Country, about 350 m (1,150 ft) above Upper Kananaskis Lake.[^1] The Friends of Kananaskis Country guide puts it at just under 2,100 m (about 6,900 ft) above sea level, while Alberta's record gives 2,128.9 m (6,985 ft).[^1][^2]

The site's first lookout went up in 1952, and the present building dates from 1976.[^1] Alberta's record also gives 1976 as the year of construction, and lists the structure as a cabin, 5 m (16 ft) tall.[^2]

Alberta says each site has an observer on watch, usually from April through October.[^3] The guide calls this lookout someone's home and workplace.[^1]

Alberta's record marks the lookout as active.[^2] The guide says it is closed in winter, though it is a popular cross-country ski destination, right on the Lookout trail.[^1]

Visitors can walk up from Highway 40 in about an hour, with 280 m (920 ft) of height gain, or come in from the Boulton Creek campground by the Whiskey Jack trail.[^1] The guide says visitors may use the picnic tables but should stay away from the buildings.[^1]

OpenStreetMap tags the site as private.[^4] The guide and that tag disagree, so treat the site as restricted: keep to the picnic area and clear of the buildings.
