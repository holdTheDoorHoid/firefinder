The Forest Service put up a fire lookout on Washington Bald Mountain in 1918.[^1] It was an enclosed wooden tower, 55 feet tall.[^1] In 1934 a 70-foot steel tower replaced it.[^1] The mountain sits in Township 42 MD in Washington County.[^2][^4]

In the summer of 2007 the new landowners cut the tower down and dropped it.[^2] The new owners, Sustainable Forest Technologies of Milford, were not willing to take on the tower.[^2] The watchman cabin followed in early 2008, when the new landowners removed it.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine list marks the tower as gone.[^4]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
