Gibraltar Rock is a former lookout site in Pend Oreille County, Washington, about 18 miles northwest of Newport.[^2] Firelookout.com lists the Kaniksu and Colville National Forests, but Willhite now describes the land as private timber company property.[^2][^4]

The lookout was an L-4 cab, built in 1935.[^1][^2][^4] The National Historic Lookout Register describes the L-4 as a 14-by-14-foot frame cab that could be mounted on a tall tower.[^5]

Sources disagree on how the cab left the site. Firetower.org and Willhite say it was removed in the 1960s, while firelookout.com says it was destroyed in the 1960s.[^1][^2][^4] The Washington lookouts index also marks the site as removed.[^3]

The site was added to the Former Fire Lookout Sites register on December 14, 2012.[^1]

Willhite's photos show footings, steps near the lookout site, and a footing with some wood still attached.[^4] His photo captions also describe views southwest and west over the Pend Oreille River.[^4]

The land is private timber company property, and Willhite drove to the summit in 2021 because no gate was there.[^4] An open gate is not the same as permission to enter, so ask the owner before you go.
