Winnie was a fire lookout tower in Itasca County, Minnesota. The Fire Lookout Association's county list places it there and marks it gone.[^1] The Forest Lookouts Minnesota page also has a Winnie Dam entry in the same county, which it places in the Chippewa National Forest.[^2] We take the two names to refer to one tower, though no source says so outright.

The Forest Lookouts entry gives one date: on June 8, 1972, offers to buy the tower were opened.[^2] The same entry says flying patrols had proven the better choice over the towers.[^2]

The association's list now marks the tower as gone.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site. A Forest Service handbook places a campsite and picnic area named Winnie Dam at the far east end of Lake Winnibigoshish.[^3] We could not tie that camp to the exact spot where the tower stood. Because the tower is listed as gone, there is no tower to visit.[^1]

[^1]: Minnesota Lookouts (By County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/mn-co/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Minnesota, Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/minnesota.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Chippewa National Forest, U.S. Forest Service handbook hosted by npshistory.com, https://npshistory.com/publications/usfs/handbooks/chippewa/sec8.htm (accessed 2026-10-21).
