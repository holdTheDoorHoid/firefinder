The Adams Hill fire tower stood in Bowie County, Texas.[^1] A list of Texas lookout towers marked as revised on January 24, 2011 calls it the Adams Hill Fire Tower and places it at 33.39 north and 94.455 west.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas index lists the tower as gone.[^3] The Forest Lookouts page for the tower marks it as removed.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3]
