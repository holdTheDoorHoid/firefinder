The Register places the Marion County Airfield Fire Tower in Marion County, Florida, and its entry is short.[^1] It says little is known about the tower, and it records the tower as US 1795, FL 29, registered on January 2, 2026.[^1]

The name suggests a link to the county airport at Dunnellon.[^2] That airport is county-owned and public, and it began as a wartime training field: the Army Air Forces opened it in August 1942, and it was known as the Dunnellon Army Air Field.[^2] No source we read says the tower stands on that field.

A 2025 photo tour by an urban-exploration site calls the place the Dunnellon Ranger Station and dates its completion to 1953.[^3] The site says the Florida Forest Service put up a steel tower and a small one-story office, and it guesses the tower at about 100 feet. The writer's wording is hedged, so treat those measurements as estimates rather than records.[^3]

A Forest Lookouts index lists a Dunnellen entry under Marion County, but the page has no details.[^4] OpenStreetMap has a tower node named Dunnellon Forestry Lookout Tower, tagged as an observation tower.[^5] The place name Dunnellon appears in both, but no page we read says they describe the same structure.

The photo tour says the station was no longer in active use by the mid-2000s, and it points to a wall map marked "Spring/Summer 2008" that an explorer found in the office. It cites no government record.[^3] We found no source that names the people who staffed the tower, the fires it spotted, or the year it last had a watch.

Today the photo tour describes a quiet, overgrown site with a tall steel lookout and a worn office building.[^3] None of the sources say whether the public may visit. Check with the Florida Forest Service before going near it, and do not climb the tower.
