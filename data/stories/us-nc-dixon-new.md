Dixon (new) is a fire tower site in Onslow County, North Carolina. A list of North Carolina lookout towers places it at 34.61277, -77.48777, and gives a second Dixon entry, Dixon (old), at 34.61138, -77.48333.[^1]

A 120-foot (37 m) steel tower was put up near Dixon on February 13, 1937, by a Civilian Conservation Corps crew from Camp S-65.[^2] The lookout-history page that carries this entry credits the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources and cites a 1938 state report for the date.[^2] Which of the two Dixon entries the tower stood on remains unknown.

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the Dixon (new) site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^2]: Dixon - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/dixon.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
