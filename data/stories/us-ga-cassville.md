The Georgia lookout list kept by tnlandforms.us places a tower called Cassville in Bartow County, at 34.24317, -84.86711 and 903 feet (275 m) above sea level.[^1] Its entry carries the code gfc-724.[^1]

Cassville is an unincorporated community between Adairsville and Cartersville, off U.S. Route 41.[^2] The community's area is reported to begin at the Cassville Road-Firetower Road intersection.[^2] The town was originally the county seat, when the county was still called Cass.[^2] During Sherman's 1864 Atlanta campaign, Cassville was destroyed, and the seat then moved to Cartersville.[^2]

Bartow County's Station 2 (Cassville) is at 130 Firetower Road.[^3] The county says the station became full-time in 1981, having long been staffed entirely by volunteers.[^3] No source describes the lookout site now, and none links this station to it.
