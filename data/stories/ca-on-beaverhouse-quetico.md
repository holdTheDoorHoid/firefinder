The Beaverhouse-Quetico fire tower is in Ontario.[^1] A volunteer list of still-standing Ontario towers calls it the Beaverhouse Tower and places it at the Beaverhouse Entry Station.[^1] Quetico Provincial Park has six entry stations.[^2]

Firelookout.ca gives the location as Rainy River, Ontario, and notes that the cab in its photo has since blown off.[^3]

The same list notes that, as of 2013, the Ontario MNR had resolved to take down many of the towers still standing.[^1] It also says the ministry no longer maintains them, and asks visitors not to climb them.[^1]

Quetico's management plan lists Beaverhouse Lake among three northern lakes offering entry, all reached by road.[^2] The volunteer list counts the tower among those still standing, but no source I read describes the site today.[^1]
