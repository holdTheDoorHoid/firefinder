A Georgia lookout tower list revised in March 2016 names a tower "lawson" in Liberty County, at 31.904306, -81.594945 and an elevation of 84 with no unit given (read here as feet, about 26 m).[^1] The list says its towers were discovered on USGS topo maps and NGS data sheets, and that names for towers 73 and above are the maker's own creation, so "lawson" is a label rather than a historic name.[^1] The entry's only source note reads "aerial imagery".[^1] Wikimedia Commons also lists a Lawson Lookout Tower in its Minnesota fire lookout towers category, so a search for the name may bring up that tower instead.[^2] Because the list was last revised in 2016, it may not show the site as it is today. No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at this site now, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Category:Fire lookout towers in Minnesota, Wikimedia Commons, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Fire_lookout_towers_in_Minnesota (accessed 2026-10-21).
