Concord was a fire lookout tower in Gibson County, Tennessee.[^1] The Tennessee Landforms table lists it at 486 feet (about 148 m) and marks it removed.[^1]

The table gives the tower the code 9-3. According to the page, these are state forest numbers in district-then-tower form, drawn from a list of towers compiled in 1976. On that reading, Concord was tower 3 in district 9.[^1]

The same page credits the Civilian Conservation Corps with putting up 98 of the state's lookout towers between 1933 and 1941.[^1] It adds that many towers have since been taken down, and that most former tower sites are now privately owned.[^1]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the Concord site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Tennessee lookout towers, Tennessee Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
