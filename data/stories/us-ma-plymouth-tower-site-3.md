The former-sites register describes a 48-foot steel tower on Telegraph Hill in Plymouth County that opened in 1939 and had staff until 1942.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register says a replacement 48-foot tower was moved to Telegraph Hill in 1939 and used until 1942.[^2]

The Pinehills, the community that includes the hill, says on its own history page that a 48-foot tower was built there in 1939 for fire detection.[^3] The same page says residents climb it today.[^3]

The register lists the site as a former lookout, and it does not say whether a tower still stands on the hill.[^1] The Pinehills page is a community history, not a site survey, so the sources read here do not settle the question.

No source read for this story says whether the public may go up the hill. Anyone interested should ask The Pinehills first, and should not climb a tower that is closed or private.
