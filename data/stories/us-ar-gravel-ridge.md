The Gravel Ridge Lookout Tower stands in Bradley County, Arkansas, on a site the Arkansas Forestry Commission uses.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as a 100-foot Aermotor MC-39 and says its lower-level stairs have been removed.[^1] The Register gives no build date.[^1]

A 1952 survey record describes a station called Bucksnort (AFC 4-1). It describes a four-legged steel structure supporting a 6-by-6-foot cabin, about 110 feet high to the roof apex.[^2] Neither source names the other, so the match rests on the shared Forestry Commission and Bradley County links.[^1][^2] The Register gives coordinates, but the Bucksnort record places its station only by reference to Hermitage and State Highway 15, so the sources read do not confirm the match by position.[^1][^2] The two height figures also differ, and no source explains why.[^1][^2]

The sources read name no staff and no fires for this tower. According to the encyclopedia, Arkansas watchmen frequently lived in solitude at or near their towers and spent daytime hours watching for smoke.[^3]

The Register records a site visit on January 22, 2022, and describes the tower as being on an active Arkansas Forestry site.[^1] It was registered on April 11, 2023.[^1] Because it sits on an active state forestry site, access is not established. Ask the Arkansas Forestry Commission before going, and do not climb the tower.
