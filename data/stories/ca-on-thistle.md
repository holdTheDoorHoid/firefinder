The Thistle Fire Tower is a heavy steel tower with a square cupola.[^1] Its record places it near McWilliams, in Unorganized North Nipissing, Nipissing District.[^1] The record gives no build year, but the tower does appear on a Department of Lands and Forests tower detection map from 1963.[^1]

As of 1985, the lower ladder had been removed.[^1] The record also says the tower was proposed as a repeater station, and that it has dropped off the North Bay topographic map.[^1]

A Wikipedia article on West Nipissing reports a plan to take the tower apart and erect it again at Field, a community on Highway 64 roughly 20 kilometres north of Sturgeon Falls, as a draw for tourists.[^2] The article gives no date for that plan. The record puts the tower at McWilliams, so the two sources disagree on where it stands.[^1][^2]

No source we could read describes what stands at either site today, or whether the public can reach them.
