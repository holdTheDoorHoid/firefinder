The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists an East Leake (Loc 1) tower in Goochland County, Virginia, and marks it gone.[^1][^2] A second East Leake point, listed as Gum Springs in a separate Virginia lookout index, is also in Goochland County.[^5]

The same list places a Rassawek tower in Goochland County, at 37.75534, -78.12578.[^1][^2] It shows that tower as standing and labels it as relocated from East Leake.[^1][^2] The list does not say which East Leake point the tower came from.[^1]

Fluvanna County, Virginia, is home to an archaeological site called Rassawek.[^3] A property called Rassawek also uses the name. Its history page describes planted grapevines and makes no mention of a fire tower.[^4]

The sources we read do not say whether the listed Rassawek point is on either property. No source describes what remains at the East Leake site today, or whether that land is open to the public. Do not climb any tower, and ask the landowner before you go onto any land.
