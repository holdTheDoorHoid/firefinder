The Peavine Mountain lookout site sits at 4,732 feet in the Mount Hood National Forest, in Clackamas County, Oregon.[^1] It began in 1927 as a camp roughly half a mile north of the top.[^1][^2]

In 1933 a 26-foot pole L-4 tower replaced the camp.[^1][^2] A Weebly summary dates the tower to the same year.[^3] The register credits a panorama of the lookout, dated October 10, 1933, to the National Archives Pacific Northwest Region.[^1][^3] In 1935 the summit gained a tent cabin.[^1][^2]

The register says the lookout no longer stood by 1957. Firelookout.com gives the same year and lists the site as gone.[^1][^2] The site was added to the register on September 20, 2010.[^1]

No source read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today. The register page carries a 2011 photograph of the site, captioned with the year and the photographer's name.[^1]
