Stony Creek was a lookout in Shoshone County, Idaho, seven miles southeast of Clarkia.[^1] The statewide list records it as a tower that is gone, at coordinates 46.96676 and -115.12145.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts files it under its St. Joe - Clearwater region.[^2] The agency is listed as PTPA, an acronym that neither source spells out.[^1][^2]

The first structure was a crow's nest, recorded for 1915.[^1][^2] By 1928 the lookout is recorded as a log cabin and a pole tower.[^1][^2] It was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1][^2] Both pages give these three events in the same order: crow's nest, then cabin and pole tower, then abandonment.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in and abandoned.[^2] No source describes what remains there, such as footings or the pole tower, and none says whether the public may visit.[^1][^2]
