An online index of Georgia lookout towers lists a tower called black mtn in Stephens County.[^1] The index gives its position as 34.65910, -83.33748.[^1] The same index also lists a separate Black Mountain Look Out Tower in Union County, which is a different place.[^1] No source we found describes the site today.[^1]

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
