The Hornertown Lookout Tower is in Hickman County.[^1] The Tennessee Land Forms list gives its elevation as 944 feet and its state number as 7-12, and it marks the tower as standing.[^1]

OpenStreetMap records the tower as a lattice observation tower and tags its elevation at 287 metres, which is close to the Land Forms figure of 944 feet.[^2]

The state fire-tower nomination lists Hornertown, in Hickman County, in its table of towers still standing as of March 26, 2014.[^3] The same document lists Moore's Switch and Cobel as wooden towers in Hickman County, and says both were later replaced by steel towers in the 1940s or 1950s.[^3] It does not mention Hornertown's construction, so we do not know whether it was one of the wooden towers or a later steel one.[^3]

We found no build date, staffing record, notable fire or change in ownership for Hornertown.[^1][^2][^3]

The Land Forms list still marks the tower as standing, but it gives no date for that and no access information.[^1] Because we found no visit report or access rule, we cannot say whether the public may visit.[^1] Anyone interested should ask the landowner before going near it.[^1][^2]

[^2]: Hornertown Lookout Tower, OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/356895213 (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^3]: Tennessee Division of Forestry Fire Lookout Towers Multiple Property Submission, National Park Service, https://npgallery.nps.gov/pdfhost/docs/NRHP/Text/64501237.pdf (accessed 2026-10-09).
