A Georgia lookout tower list at tnlandforms.us includes an Andy Mountain entry in Douglas County, with an elevation of 1,349 (the list gives no unit).[^1] The Forest Lookouts website also has a page titled Andy Mountain under Douglas County.[^2] A mapping site describes Andy Mountain as a summit that appears on the Winston topographic map, with an approximate elevation of 1,329 feet (405 m).[^3]

No source we found describes the site today, including whether any structure stands on the summit or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Andy Mountain - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/andy-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Andy Mountain in Douglas County GA (Winston Area), MountainZone, https://www.mountainzone.com/mountains/georgia/douglas-ga/summits/andy-mountain/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
