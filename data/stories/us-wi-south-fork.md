South Fork was a fire lookout tower in Price County, Wisconsin.[^1][^2] The Central US Lookouts page gives its agency as the Chequamegon-Nicolet National Forest.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it at 45.94075 north and 90.13850 west, as a tower that is gone.[^2] A Forest Service page about the Mountain tower says the forest had 14 original lookout towers, but it does not mention South Fork.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.

[^1]: South Fork, Forest Lookouts (Central US Lookouts), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/south-fork.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Wisconsin Lookouts (alphabetical list), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Mountain Fire Lookout Tower, Chequamegon-Nicolet National Forest, U.S. Forest Service, https://www.fs.usda.gov/r09/chequamegon-nicolet/recreation/mountain-fire-lookout-tower (accessed 2026-10-10).
