Pink Hill is a lookout tower in Duplin County, North Carolina.[^2] The town of Pink Hill, which shares the name, is in Lenoir County according to Wikipedia.[^3] The Forest Lookouts page for the state lists the tower under the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources.[^1] That page dates its construction to March 10, 1937, when a crew from Camp S-65 of the Civilian Conservation Corps built a steel tower 99 feet 9 inches tall.[^1] The page cites the state Department of Conservation and Development's 7th Biennial Report (1938) for that entry.[^1] The tnlandforms.us list of North Carolina towers gives its coordinates as 35.05666, -77.80722.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today or whether the site is open to the public.
