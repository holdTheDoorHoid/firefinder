Forest Lookouts files its Alapaha entry under Florida, with the location "Berrien County - 2N-13E-32", and its state index also lists Alapaha under Georgia.[^1] A statewide list of Georgia lookout towers names a tower called alapaha in Berrien County, at 31.373553 north, 83.224914 west.[^2] The town of Alapaha is in Berrien County and lies along the Alapaha River.[^3] A second Forest Lookouts page for Alapaha also gives only "Berrien County".[^4]

The sources disagree about the state. The Georgia list places this tower in Berrien County, Georgia, so the Florida entry looks like a misfiling of the same tower.[^2]

We found no source that gives a build date, design, height, staffing, or any later history. No source describes what remains at the site today, and we found nothing about whether the public can reach it.
