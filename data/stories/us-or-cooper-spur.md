The Forest Service built a fire lookout cab on a ridge of Mount Hood, about half a mile south-southwest of Cloud Cap Inn.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register dates it to around 1930.[^1] Firelookout.com gives 1930 as well.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts site quotes a 1927 Morning Oregonian item. It proposed a new station on the peak and mentioned a cabin already built on the summit several years earlier.[^3] That earlier cabin does not fit the 1930 date, and the sources do not say whether it was the same building. We could not settle this.

The cab is gone. Firelookout.com lists it as abandoned, with no date given.[^2] The register also says the cab is now gone.[^1]

Cloud Cap Inn, the landmark the cab sat near, opened in 1889 and closed as a business in 1946.[^4] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the cab site. We also found nothing about whether the public can reach it. Firelookout.com places the site in Mt. Hood National Forest.[^2]
