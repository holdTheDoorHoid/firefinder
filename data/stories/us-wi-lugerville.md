Lugerville Lookout Tower stands in Price County, at about 45.78 degrees north and 90.52 degrees west.[^1] OpenStreetMap lists it as a tower and records its elevation as 452 metres.[^2]

I could not find a build date, builder or design for Lugerville in the sources I checked, so its early history is unknown. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list shows it as standing, and its row carries no register number.[^1] I also found no record of who staffed it, whether it was damaged or moved, or whether it is rented.

The statewide picture is relevant here, though these sources do not name this tower. A WXPR news report from December 2015 said the DNR had announced it would remove the towers it once used to watch for wildfires.[^3] The sources do not say whether Lugerville is one of the DNR's towers or who owns it now. A 2019 chapter report from the Wisconsin chapter of the Forest Fire Lookout Association said that, as of the fall of 2019, 37 towers had passed to private landowners, 10 had been sold and taken down, 4 had been sold and were awaiting removal, and 56 were still to be dismantled.[^4]

The list shows the tower as standing.[^1] None of these sources describe public access. Ask the landowner or land manager before visiting, and do not climb the tower if it is closed or posted.
