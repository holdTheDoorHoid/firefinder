The Lykes lookout stood in Glades County, Florida.[^1] The Eastern US Lookouts history site dates the tower to 1960, when it went up as a steel tower.[^1] The source gives its height as 100 feet, about 30.5 metres.[^1]

In February 2015 the tower was taken down and relocated to a site in Tennessee.[^1] The page does not name the Tennessee location, and I found no other source that describes the move.[^1]

No source I read describes what remains at the Glades County site today. The tower left Glades County in 2015, so the site there no longer holds it.[^1]

[^1]: Lykes - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/lykes.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
