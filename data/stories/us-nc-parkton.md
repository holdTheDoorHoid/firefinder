The Parkton tower went up in 1929, one mile west of Parkton in Robeson County, North Carolina.[^1] The state's Department of Conservation and Development reported it in the 1928–30 biennial report as a 100-foot (30.5 m) steel tower.[^1] A January 1930 item in the Robesonian, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, said the county then had a 100-foot tower at Parkton.[^1] The same page files the tower under Robeson County and the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources.[^1]

Today, OpenStreetMap, a volunteer-edited map, still has a record named Parkton Lookout Tower, tagged as a lattice observation tower with an elevation of 56 metres (about 184 feet) above sea level.[^2] I did not confirm that this record marks the 1929 site, and I found no source that describes what remains there now or whether the public may visit.

[^2]: Parkton Lookout Tower, OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/357781515 (accessed 2026-10-21).
