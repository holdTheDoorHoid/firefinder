Nelson Point, also called Mill Creek, was a lookout in Idaho County about eight miles northeast of Warren, in the Payette National Forest.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts labels it a hike-in site, and the site is listed at 6,902 feet.[^1][^2]

Two sites date its L-4 cab to 1934.[^1][^2] One site calls the cab a ground house.[^3] A University of Idaho archive photograph of the Mill Creek Lookout carries a 1930 date, four years before the cab's date, so the sources disagree on when the lookout stood.[^1][^2][^4]

The lookout was destroyed in 1966.[^1][^2] One site adds that the ground house was taken down by burning it where it stood that year.[^3]

No source described for this story says what, if anything, remains on the point today, or whether visitors can reach it. Anyone curious about the spot should check with the Payette National Forest before setting out.
