The Falls Fire Lookout Tower is mapped in Alberta, near the peak called Falls Lookout Ridge.[^2] OpenStreetMap, the community-edited map, tags it as an observation tower made of metal and gives its height as 4 m (13 ft).[^1] The same entry is also tagged as a building.[^1] A note on the entry's edit says the change concerned the rural Clearwater, AB area.[^1]

Mapcarta, a map directory, describes access as restricted and says a visitor needs permission.[^2] The OpenStreetMap entry carries an access tag set to private.[^1] Neither source describes a visitor route, and neither says the public may go up or near the tower. Visitors should not plan a trip on the assumption that it is open.

No source we read describes what stands at the site now.

[^1]: Falls Fire Lookout Tower (way 1547946101), OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1547946101 (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Falls Fire Lookout Tower Map - Alberta, Canada, Mapcarta, https://mapcarta.com/W1547946101 (accessed 2026-10-20).
