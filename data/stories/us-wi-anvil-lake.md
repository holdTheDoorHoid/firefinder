The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list places the Anvil Lake tower at 45.96878, -89.05135 and marks it as gone.[^1] The Central US lookouts site lists it in Vilas County, under Nicolet National Forest.[^2]

Not to be confused with the Anvil Lake Campground Shelter in Eagle River, which is a different structure.[^3] The Civilian Conservation Corps built the shelter, and it was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1996.[^3]

According to a 2024 chapter report, 60 Wisconsin lookouts are on the National Historic Lookout Register and the Wisconsin Register.[^4] The report adds that 13 of these towers are listed as gone in those registers.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the tower site, or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1]
