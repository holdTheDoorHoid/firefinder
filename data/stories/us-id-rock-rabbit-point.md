Rock Rabbit Point is a lookout site on Rock Rabbit Mountain in Idaho County, inside the Payette National Forest.[^1][^2] The earliest setup on the mountain was a tent camp and a tree platform, which the register dates to 1929.[^2]

Sources disagree on when the steel tower went up. The register dates a log cabin and a 46-foot Aermotor steel tower to 1937.[^2] Three other pages give 1934 for the tower.[^3][^4][^1] Two of them, Idaho Fire Lookouts and firelookout.com, also date a log cabin at the site to 1929.[^4][^1]

They also differ on how it ended. Idaho Fire Lookouts says the tower was destroyed in 1977, and firelookout.com says it was gone in 1977.[^4][^1] The register says it was removed in 1981, and the Forest Lookouts entry gives no date for its removal.[^2][^3]

Today, the register says the tree platform and the other structures are still on site, and firelookout.com says the log cabin and tree platform are still in place.[^2][^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the lookout destroyed.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the type as ground and the status as standing.[^5] None of these descriptions carries a date, so we cannot say which is current.

The site lies inside the Frank Church River of No Return Wilderness, and Idaho Fire Lookouts describes its access as hike-in.[^2][^4] Anyone planning a visit should check the current condition and access rules with the Payette National Forest first.
