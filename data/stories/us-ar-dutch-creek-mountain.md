Dutch Creek Mountain sits in Yell County, Arkansas, inside the Ouachita National Forest.[^1] In 1933 the Civilian Conservation Corps erected a lookout structure on the summit.[^1]

A survey description tied to a 1956 recovery of the station describes a steel tower on four legs, with a small metal lookout house enclosed in glass at the top.[^1] The same description puts the overall height at about 71 feet (22 m): 68 feet (21 m) to the eaves, plus an estimated 3 feet (1 m) for the gable.[^1]

The lookout was removed in 1972.[^1] No source I found describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today.[^1]

Later, in 2010, the Forest Service signed a decision extending Entergy's special-use authorization for communication facilities at the Dutch Creek Mountain Tower site.[^2] No source I found describes public access to the summit, so check with the Ouachita National Forest before you go.[^2]
