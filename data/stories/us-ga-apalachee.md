In January 1957 a Georgia Forestry Commission ranger announced that the commission's Morgan County fire tower, then standing at Bostwick, would be moved to Apalachee in the same county.[^1][^2] The newspaper report described it as a 100-foot (30.5 m) tower and quoted Ranger Harold Jones saying the move would improve coverage of the forests of Morgan and Walton counties.[^1]

The planned site was close to the Madison-Athens highway, about a mile northeast of Apalachee, on land Marion Cochran had donated.[^1] The article said the new tower would give Morgan and Walton three towers with full-time staff, plus two others staffed during critical fire periods.[^1]

A Georgia lookout tower list at tnlandforms.us includes an Apalachee entry in Morgan County, with a Georgia Forestry Commission number and an elevation of 681 with no unit given.[^3] No source we found describes the site today, including whether a tower still stands there or whether the public can reach it.
