Rich Mountain lies in Great Smoky Mountains National Park, in Blount County, Tennessee.[^1] Tennessee's lookout register lists a tower there at 3,699 feet and marks it removed.[^1] A fire tower once stood at the mountain's highest point.[^2]

A 2011 Clemson thesis says the park abandoned its fire towers in the 1970s, and that within the following decade every tower but four, plus one cabin, was taken out of the park.[^3] Smoky Mountain Living names the four survivors as Mount Cammerer, Mount Sterling, Shuckstack Mountain and the Cove Mountain tower, so Rich Mountain is not one of them.[^4]

The hike begins on Rich Mountain Road, about 2.2 miles from the Cades Cove Loop Road.[^2] Winter closes the road.[^2] A small spur trail from Cerulean Knob reaches the top, where cement blocks from the old tower's base are still there.[^2]

Do not confuse it with the Rich Mountain near Hot Springs, North Carolina, where WillhiteWeb describes a standing lookout.[^5]
