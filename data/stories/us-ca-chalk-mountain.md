A Division of Forestry photograph taken by Mace Pewtherer on October 28, 1953 is captioned Chalks Lookout.[^1] The Internet Archive's note says the photo is believed to show the former Eagle Rock lookout instead, which is also in Big Basin State Park and was photographed that day.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Chalk Mountain as an enclosed tower and marks it gone.[^2]

An environmental review notice describes the site as an existing CAL FIRE communications facility.[^3] It lies in the Coastal Zone, inside a CAL FIRE lease hold area in Big Basin Redwoods State Park.[^4]

A 60-foot wood pole at the site burned in the 2020 CZU August Lightning Complex fire.[^3] A 35-foot emergency repeater was then installed on a temporary basis.[^3]

The same notice describes a replacement tower up to 80 feet tall and says work was planned to start in 2023. The sources do not show whether that work has been done.[^3]

No source describes any remains of the lookout tower on the site today. The sources describe a CAL FIRE communications site inside a lease area, not a public lookout.[^3][^4]
