A fire tower stood on the north shore of the St. Johns River in Duval County, close to the Bayshore Drive bridge over Clapboard Creek.[^1] A federal survey description dated 1934 places the survey station on the peak of a house roof at the Clapboard Creek fire tower, and describes the tower as an 87-foot steel skeleton structure.[^1] The same description gives the top of the tower as 98 feet above high water, and notes that the station is not marked.[^1]

The National Geodetic Survey's datasheet for the station gives its position as 30 degrees, 24 minutes, 25 seconds north and 81 degrees, 30 minutes, 35 seconds west.[^2]

No source reviewed describes the tower or the site as it is now, so whether any part of the tower remains, and whether the public can reach the spot, is not known. Check with the property owner before going to look.

[^2]: Datasheet for station PID BC2010, NOAA National Geodetic Survey, https://geodesy.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=BC2010 (accessed 2026-10-21).
