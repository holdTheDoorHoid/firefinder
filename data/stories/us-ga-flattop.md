The Georgia tower list places a site called "flattop" in Fannin County, at 34.85731 N, 84.50420 W. Its elevation is listed as 3,727 with no unit; read as feet, that is 1,136 m.[^1] The name matches Flat Top Mountain, which Wikipedia says was named for its flat peak.[^2] Wikipedia gives the mountain's elevation as 3,589 feet (1,094 m), citing the U.S. Geological Survey's Geographic Names Information System.[^2] If the list's figure is in feet, the two disagree, and no source says which one applies to the tower site.[^2]

A photo captioned 1940, posted to a Fannin County history group, shows people at the Flat Top Mountain fire tower.[^3] For context, the state forestry agency's history traces its first fire observation tower, on the Ware Hotel in Waycross, to a 1928 forestry fair in that town, though it hedges with the word "perhaps."[^4]

No source describes the site today. None says whether any tower or footing remains on the mountain, or whether the public can reach the summit.
