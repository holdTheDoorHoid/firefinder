Lavender Mountain, a summit in Floyd County, Georgia, is named for George Michael Lavender, a local merchant.[^2] A Georgia lookout tower list revised in March 2016 names a tower "lavender mtn" in Floyd County, at 34.33511, -85.24571 and an elevation of 1,534 with no unit given (read here as feet, about 468 m).[^1] The list says its towers were discovered on USGS topo maps and NGS data sheets, and that names for towers 73 and above are the maker's own creation, so "lavender mtn" is a working label rather than a historic name.[^1] Wikipedia gives the summit's elevation as 1,526 ft (465 m) and its coordinates as 34.336°N 85.168°W, about 4.4 miles (7 km) east of the list's point, so the list may not place the tower on the summit.[^1][^2] A Wikipedia photo caption refers to a house called House o' Dreams on the summit.[^2] No source I could read describes a tower there now, and I found nothing saying whether the summit is open to visitors, so do not assume it is.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Lavender Mountain, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lavender_Mountain (accessed 2026-10-21).
