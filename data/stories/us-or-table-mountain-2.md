Table Mountain sits about 11 miles east of Ashland, in Jackson County, and its first lookout is dated two ways.[^1][^2] The register dates the first lookout to 1930, credited to the Klamath National Forest.[^1] The firelookout.com page dates the development to 1931, with a 30-foot pole live-in tower.[^2] The Oregon Lookouts page also dates work to 1931, and it credits the Crater National Forest with the build.[^3]

The current tower dates to 1958: a 30-foot open timber tower with a flat-top cab.[^1] The Oregon Lookouts page records the 1958 work as 97 percent complete, at a total cost of $4,656.73.[^3]

In 1933 five men from the Moon Prairie CCC camp were putting up a garage and woodshed at the station.[^3] The register says a lookout staffed the site through at least 1975.[^1] That year the Klamath District of the Oregon Department of Forestry operated it while other lookouts were being remodeled.[^3]

The firelookout.com page says the tower has been abandoned for many years.[^2] The 2026 statewide list of standing lookouts also marks it abandoned.[^4]

Today the tower stands on private ground closed to the public, so it is not a place to visit.[^1][^2][^4]
