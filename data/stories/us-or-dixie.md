A 1957 newspaper caption, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, describes the Dixie lookout, in the Dixie Mountain area, as overlooking a large expanse of healthy second-growth forest.[^1] The caption appeared beneath an aerial photograph of the tower.[^1] It also says deer and bear still roam there, as they did more than a century before.[^1] The site lists the tower under the Oregon Department of Forestry and groups it under Multnomah County.[^1]

Do not confuse it with Dixie Butte, a different lookout in the Malheur National Forest.[^2]

A list of standing Oregon lookouts, which counts 156 of them as of July 2026, has no entry for the Dixie Mountain tower.[^2] That list warns that its information may be out of date.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the mountain is open to visitors.

[^1]: Dixie - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts, western US (Ron Kemnow), https://oregonlookouts.weebly.com/dixie.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Oregon's Standing Lookouts - Every Lookout in Oregon, Every Lookout in Oregon (cherylhill.net), https://cherylhill.net/firelookouts/oregons-standing-lookouts/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
