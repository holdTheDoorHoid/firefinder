Iron Creek Point is a lookout site on the Challis National Forest in Custer County, Idaho, at an elevation of 10,736 feet.[^1]

Two listings date a cabin on the peak's east point to 1939.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts site gives a different account. It says that around 1939 the site held a 24-by-24-foot framed tent.[^3] The sources disagree on what was built, so this story does not settle the structure type.

A history compilation of the Challis National Forest lists Iron Creek Point among its station names in staff tables from the early 1940s.[^4]

The Forest Lookouts site says the lookout was abandoned in the 1950s.[^3] The firelookout.com listing shows the site as gone, without a removal date, and so does the Forest Fire Lookout Association list.[^1][^5]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page tags it as a hike-in location.[^2]
