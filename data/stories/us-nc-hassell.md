Hassell Lookout is a steel fire tower in Martin County, in eastern North Carolina, near the Roanoke River.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register dates it to 1936.[^1] A separate lookout history site says a 1938 state report records its completion on September 4, 1936, built by members of the Civilian Conservation Corps at Camp P-67.[^2]

The tower is recorded at 99 feet and 9 inches tall, which is about 30.4 metres.[^2] In June 1938, members of CCC Camp P-67 finished a three-room frame building at the site that combined living quarters with a garage. It measured 24 by 30 feet.[^2]

I did not find a source that says who staffed the tower or when its fire watch ended.

The Register lists the tower as state-owned and administered by the North Carolina Forest Service, and it was added to the register on May 31, 2018.[^1] Earlier that year, a site entry dated March 22, 2018 says the state opened bids on state surplus property, and it places the tower on state-owned land.[^2] The Register added the tower after that notice and still calls it state-owned.[^1] Neither source says whether a sale closed.

I found no dated report on the tower's condition or on who controls access today. Anyone planning a visit should ask the North Carolina Forest Service first, and should not try to climb a tower whose status is unclear.
