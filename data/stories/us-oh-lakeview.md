Lakeview, also listed as Lakeview (Salt Fork), was a fire lookout site inside Salt Fork State Park in Guernsey County, Ohio.[^1][^2]

Salt Fork State Park lies six miles (9.7 km) north of Lore City and is the largest state park in Ohio.[^3]

The Ohio Department of Natural Resources is listed as the administering agency.[^1] The register gives an elevation of 1,080 feet (329 m) and a registration date of September 3, 2014, under US 1563, OH 9.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Ohio list also marks the tower as gone.[^2]

Both lists use the same register number, but their coordinates differ.[^1][^2] The register puts the site at 40.095139 north, 81.664278 west, while the association's list gives 40.09511 north and 81.54984 west.[^1][^2]

The sources found do not describe how the tower was built or what, if anything, remains on the site today.
