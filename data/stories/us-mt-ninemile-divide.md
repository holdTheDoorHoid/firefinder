The Ninemile Divide lookout stood on the divide of the same name in Mineral County. The site is six miles east-northeast of Superior, in the Lolo National Forest.[^1][^2]

The earliest reference we found is from July 1927.[^3] Quoting The Missoulian, the Forest Lookouts page reports that a Missoula school principal was posted on the divide as a summer fire lookout, and that he spent several days fixing up the camp.[^3]

Firelookout.com says the tower was built in 1931. It was a 40-foot pole L-4 tower.[^2] The same page lists Mr. and Mrs. Daniel Demontigny as lookout personnel in 1952.[^2]

The tower came down in 1964.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list marks the site as gone.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
