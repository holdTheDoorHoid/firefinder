Rancheria Rock sits in Wheeler County, about seven miles south-southeast of Fossil, Oregon.[^2] Sources spell the name two ways, Rancheria and Rancherie.[^2][^3]

A lookout was staffed on the rock by the 1930s. A 1936 newspaper said the state had maintained it through fire season for the previous eight years.[^3]

The sources disagree about the cab. Firelookout.com dates the first development to the 1930s and says the L-4 cab was changed later.[^2] The National Historic Lookout Register describes the cab as a 14-by-14-foot Oregon Department of Forestry design, put up in the 1950s in place of an earlier 1930s building.[^1] A 1946 district report already lists a 14 x 14 standard ground-type lookout here, which does not fit a 1950s build date.[^3]

District reports list three fires from this station in 1963 and five in 1964.[^3] A 1963 report said the lookout should be replaced and that it was getting dangerous to use.[^3] The same report says the building had been patched and parts replaced, and in 1966 crews added a new foundation, catwalk, railings and shutters.[^3]

Firelookout.com says the site was abandoned for many years and has seen some preservation work.[^2] The register says the lookout is no longer in active service, calls it one of the most endangered sites in Oregon, and recorded it on March 1, 2003.[^1]

Today the sources disagree about who controls the land. The register places the lookout on BLM land, while firelookout.com says it sits on private property with no public access.[^1][^2] Neighbors and the community have expressed interest in restoring it alongside a nearby state park, and the Oregon chapter of the Forest Fire Lookout Association is handling the communications.[^1] Until the land question is settled, treat the site as off limits.
