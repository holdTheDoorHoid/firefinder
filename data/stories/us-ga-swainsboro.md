Tom Dunigan's list of Georgia lookout towers places a lookout named Swainsboro in Emanuel County, with an elevation of 287. The list gives no unit; if the figure is feet, it is about 87 m.[^1] The list gives it Georgia Forestry Commission number 110.[^1] Its notes say an older Georgia Forestry Commission list described the locations of 203 towers, which it labels gfc-nnn.[^1] Swainsboro is the county seat.[^2]

The same list has a separate entry for a lookout it says was moved from Oak Park, in the same county.[^1] A 2007 Georgia Trend article described a decommissioned fire tower from Oak Park at Flat Creek Lodge, a hunting and fishing complex on a 2,000-acre pine forest about nine miles from downtown Swainsboro.[^2]

No source reached describes what remains at either site today, or whether the public may visit. The Georgia Forestry Commission's Emanuel County unit is at 296 Glenwood Road in Swainsboro.[^3]
