The register places a fire tower on Kinlock Knob, inside the Bankhead National Forest.[^1] The Forest Service says the national forest lies in the northwest part of the state, across Lawrence, Winston and Franklin Counties.[^3]

The register dates the tower to between 1924 and 1930, and the earliest map that shows it is a 1930 Forest Service map.[^1] The register does not record when the tower came down, and it lists the Forest Service as administrator.[^1]

The sources disagree about the county. The register puts the tower in Winston County, while TopoQuest, which labels the point Kinlock Lookout Tower, puts it in Lawrence County.[^1][^2]

The register's photo is captioned "Tower foundations," so foundations are what it shows remaining.[^1] No source describes the site beyond that. The Forest Service lists mountain biking and hunting/shooting among the forest's recreation activities,[^3] but none of the sources says whether the knob itself is open to visitors.
