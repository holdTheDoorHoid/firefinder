Tom Dunigan's list of Georgia lookout towers includes a tower called "pegamore" in Paulding County, at an elevation of 1,121 feet (342 m).[^1] The only note the list gives for it reads "datasheet EE1803," and the list does not explain that reference.[^1] It gives no build date, builder, height or design.[^1]

The same name appears on Pegamore Creek and on Pegamore Lake Dam, an earth dam in Paulding County.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register says it has 29 lookouts registered in Georgia, and none of the names on its Georgia list matches this tower.[^3]

Statewide, the Georgia Forestry Commission started moving away from staffed lookouts toward aerial patrols in the late 1980s, and many of the towers were later scrapped and sold.[^4] No source we found links this tower to that change.

We found no source that describes what, if anything, is left on the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
