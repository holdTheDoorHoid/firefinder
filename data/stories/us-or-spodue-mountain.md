Spodue Mountain Lookout stands in Klamath County, Oregon, on the Fremont National Forest, and the U.S. Forest Service administers it.[^1] The Register calls it the western-most lookout on that forest and says it first went into service in 1965.[^1] Its cab is an R-6 flattop with a catwalk on a 41-foot treated timber tower.[^1] FireLookout.com also gives 1965 and a 41-foot tower.[^2]

Another history page says a road to the summit was built with public works funds in 1963, and that a 42-foot tower with an R-6 cabin was constructed in 1964.[^3] The same page notes a 50-foot tower under construction in 1963, but the finished tower is recorded at 41 or 42 feet.[^3][^1][^2]

The Register's May 2021 visit report, by a former staffer, says the author staffed the lookout in the 2018 and 2019 seasons, and that directions to the tower are signed.[^1] Cherylhill's directory lists it as staffed, and FireLookout.com says it is staffed every summer.[^4][^2] None of these sources names a fire the lookout spotted.[^1][^2][^3]

The 2021 report describes fairly recent paint and notes that the summit is shared with cell-phone towers.[^1] The Register still says the lookout remains active.[^1]

Cherylhill lists the site as accessible to the public, but adds that this does not mean visitors can reach the lookout itself.[^4] Anyone planning a visit should check with the Forest Service first, because none of these sources says the cab is open to visitors.[^1][^4]
