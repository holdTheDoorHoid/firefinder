The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list places a tower called Hi Point (Ziegler Hill) in San Augustine County, at 31.17833 north and 94.13333 west.[^1] The list marks the tower as gone.[^1]

The association's expanded Texas inventory gives about 1937 as the date and 1968 as the removal year. It lists the manufacturer as EMSCO and the height as 100, with no unit given.[^2] Its Moved To column reads "to Poyner."[^2]

The inventory does not say which Poyner it means. The list has only one Poyner entry, Poyner (Fincastle) in Henderson County, which it shows as a standing tower with a date of 1970 and the same manufacturer and height.[^1][^2] Neither list ties the Hi Point tower to Poyner (Fincastle).[^1][^2]

Today the list marks the tower as gone. No source describes what, if anything, remains on the hill.[^1]

[^1]: Texas Lookouts (By County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/tx-co/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Texas lookout inventory, expanded list (PDF), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/TX-expanded.pdf (accessed 2026-10-21).
