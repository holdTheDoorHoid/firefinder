Buck Knob is in Lincoln County, West Virginia, and the register puts the site at 1,170 feet (357 meters).[^1] The West Virginia Division of Forestry administered the tower.[^1]

In May 1959, Asher W. Kelly, the assistant state forester in charge of forest fire control, said several small construction jobs would be done ahead of the fall fire season, and that Buck Knob would get a new observer's cabin.[^2] A May 1962 newspaper item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, mentioned a fire tower ranger at Buck Knob reporting 3.21 inches of rain in a three-hour period.[^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register listed the site on September 16, 2008, on the nomination of Henry Isenberg.[^1]

The register says the tower has been removed, and the statewide list on firelookout.org marks it as gone.[^1][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1]
