The Opportunity Farm lookout is listed in New Gloucester, Cumberland County, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association now lists it as gone.[^1] The Eastern U.S. Lookouts page for New Gloucester has 1950 and 1951 newspaper notes about a New Gloucester fire tower, naming an attendant in 1950 and a watchman in 1951.[^2] That page does not mention Opportunity Farm.[^2] I could not find a build date or design for the tower in the sources I read.

The association also lists a standing tower at the Fryeburg Fairgrounds, in Oxford County, as relocated from Opportunity Farm.[^1] A Fryeburg Fair profile of Howard Hatch, who supervised the fair's tower, says the tower is a reproduction of the Kearsarge (North) fire tower.[^3] It says Fryeburg Fair put up the reproduction in 2009 and refurbished it in 2012.[^3] The profile does not mention Opportunity Farm or any move from New Gloucester.[^3] So the relocation is not confirmed, and as far as these sources show, the fairgrounds tower is a replica rather than the original lookout.[^3] The sources I read do not say whether the tower is open to visitors outside fair week.
