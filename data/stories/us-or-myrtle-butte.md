Myrtle Butte lies in the Malheur National Forest, about 24 miles northeast of John Day in Grant County, Oregon.[^1][^2]

The Forest Service put up a 90-foot tree lookout there in 1924.[^1][^2] A 1968 Sunday Oregonian item, reprinted on the Rex page, credits Ranger Glover Blake with building it.[^2] Firelookout.com records the same 90-foot crow's nest for 1924.[^1]

In September 1924, the Burns Times-Herald reported that the station had shut down for the year and its smoke chasers were off duty.[^2]

Firelookout.com and the lookout index both list the tower as abandoned, with no date given.[^1][^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the butte today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
