A fire lookout tower once stood on the summit of Ferrin Knob, a 4,000-foot peak beside the Blue Ridge Parkway south of Asheville.[^1] A map site lists the spot as a ruin called Ferin Fire Tower, at an elevation of 4,022 feet.[^2] The North Carolina lookout-tower list places a Ferrin Knob tower in Buncombe County. By our calculation, its listed point lies about 12 metres from that map point.[^3][^2]

A hiking guide reports that only concrete footings remain, and that the top has grown back into woods and scrub.[^1] The peak lies in the Pisgah Ranger District.[^1] The walk starts at the Beaver Dam Gap Overlook on the Blue Ridge Parkway, at milepost 401.7, and the climb begins at once on the white-blazed Mountains-to-Sea and Shut-In trail.[^1] With only footings left, there is no tower to climb, so a visit means a hike to the summit.[^1]
