In 1948 the Prospector tower was going up at the entrance to Manitoba's Cormorant forest reserve, just north of The Pas.[^1] A Winnipeg Free Press item dated 15 May 1948, quoted on Canada Lookouts, reported that forest service workers were still building the tower, 25.9 m (85 ft) tall, as a fire-protection measure.[^1] The same item said a log building with five rooms would stand next to the tower, holding an office and a tourist information bureau.[^1]

In April 1968 a Pas Herald notice sought one man to work as a fire ranger and towerman at the Prospector tower, with applications going to the Provincial Building.[^1]

Canada Lookouts is the only source I found for the tower, so these are newspaper items quoted secondhand.[^1] No source I found describes the site today, so whether the tower still stands, and whether the public can reach it, is unknown.

[^1]: Prospector, Canada's Fire Lookouts (Ron Kemnow), https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/prospector.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
