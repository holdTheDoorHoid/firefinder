The Forest Lookouts site lists a Pine Island lookout in Lee County, Florida.[^1]

A 2010 WFTV report, carried by Firehouse, said Florida was phasing out its manned fire towers.[^2] Forestry area supervisor Ray Lovett told the station that cell phones let residents report fires from almost any spot.[^2] The report also gave two other factors: aircraft are now used more, and there is less woodland at risk of fire.[^2]

A cycling site lists a viewpoint called Lookout Tower at Pine Island Harbor, in St. James City, Lee County.[^3] It is not clear from that listing whether it has any link to the old lookout, so visitors should not assume it does.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains of the Pine Island lookout site today, or whether the public may visit it.
