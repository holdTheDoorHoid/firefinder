Watson Mountain sits about two miles north of Nordman in Bonner County, Idaho, within the Kaniksu National Forest, at an elevation of 3,978 feet.[^1][^3]

Listings date the site's crow's nest and camp to the 1930s.[^1][^2][^3]

The sources we read give little more about it. The Idaho listing says its staffing history is not yet documented.[^2]

Two lookout listings mark the site abandoned, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association index does too, giving its type as a crow's nest.[^1][^2][^4] The Idaho listing also calls it a hike-in site.[^2]

Today, no source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit, or whether the site can be reached or is open now.
