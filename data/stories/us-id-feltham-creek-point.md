The Feltham Creek Point lookout went up in 1933 on a peak about 21 miles north of Stanley, Idaho, in the Challis National Forest.[^1][^2][^3] It was an L-4 cabin with a hip roof.[^1][^4] The climbing guide puts the peak about 21 miles northwest of Stanley, so the sources differ on direction.[^5]

None of the sources names anyone who staffed the cabin. Idaho Fire Lookouts says it has no documented staffing history for the site.[^3] The sources do not describe any notable fire connected with the lookout.

The cabin did not last long. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says it was removed in 1967.[^1] Firelookout.com, Idaho Fire Lookouts and the Forest Lookouts Idaho page all say it was destroyed that year.[^2][^3][^4] The sources agree on the year but not on how the cabin ended. The register recorded the site on January 24, 2011.[^1]

No source describes a standing building today. The 2019 climbing guide includes a photo caption that describes what remains of the lookout on the summit.[^5] The same guide says the summit is reached by forest roads, that a 4WD is advisable, and that cars are left just below the summit.[^5] The guide calls the outlook from the top remarkable and says the Sawtooths are in view.[^5] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as drive-up.[^3] None of the sources mentions a closure or a permit rule, but none spells out the site's access rules either.
