The Blueslip fire tower stood in Wright County, Missouri. The lookout-history page spells it Blueslip. The 2015 plan and the 2019 map write Blue Slip.[^1][^2][^3] A 1966 entry on the page says the Conservation Commission obtained land for a steel lookout tower.[^1] The fiscal 1967-68 annual report, as quoted there, records a fire tower going up at Blueslip.[^1]

Two later entries record small works at the site. Two cattle guards went in at the towersite in fiscal 1968-69, and a privy was built at the tower in fiscal 1970-71.[^1]

A 2015 management plan for the Douglas and Wright County towersites includes the Blue Slip Towersite. The plan says these towers are kept mainly for wildfire detection, and it lists recurring vandalism to the towers as a challenge.[^2]

A department site map last updated April 11, 2019, shows the Blue Slip Towersite at 4.6 acres in Wright County. Its legend includes a Fire Tower symbol.[^3]

We found no source that says whether the tower still stands, whether anyone staffs it, or whether the public may visit. The 2019 map is the newest source we found. Check with the Missouri Department of Conservation before you go, and do not climb a tower without permission.
