Sand Creek Lookout stood in Latah County, Idaho, at a point the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists at 46.93164 north and 116.57914 west.[^4] The Potlatch Timber Protective Association is listed as its agency.[^3]

The earliest structure listed was a platform tower, dated to 1921 in both Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts.[^1][^2] The association's annual report for that year, quoted on Forest Lookouts, says the season's work mostly meant connecting lookouts to the telephone, along with a makeshift tower at Sand Creek.[^3]

Both histories also place a 45-foot windmill tower on the north point in 1924.[^1][^2] A State Land Department biennial report, quoted on the same page, records a steel tower fifty feet high built at the site in 1927.[^3] That does not match the windmill tower, and none of the sources says whether the steel tower replaced it.[^1][^2][^3]

Both histories list the site as ruins from 1967.[^1][^2] The association's Idaho list marks the tower as gone.[^4]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, or whether the public can reach it.
