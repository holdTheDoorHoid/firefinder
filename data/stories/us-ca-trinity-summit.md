Trinity Summit is in Humboldt County.[^2]

A lookout station was operating on the summit by August 1931, when a newspaper named a lookout called Crouse there.[^1] In October 1932 Forest Service carpenters went up to put a patrol cabin and a barn on the summit, and both were completed by the end of the month.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Trinity Summit as gone.[^2]

A Six Rivers National Forest project page, last updated on July 22, 2026, carries the name Trinity Summit Restoration Project.[^3] It describes work with the Hoopa Valley Tribe to reduce wildland fire risk to communities and restore ecosystems, at a location east of the Hoopa Square.[^3] The page does not mention a lookout, so it does not show whether anything from the old station remains or whether the summit is open to visitors.[^3]
