Sugar Loaf, also called Sugarloaf Mountain, is in Boulder County, in the Roosevelt National Forest.[^1][^2]

The Forest Lookouts site lists it among its Colorado lookouts and marks it removed.[^1] The page gives no date or description.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Sugar Loaf, at 40.02667 and -105.41861, among its unknown and undocumented sites.[^2] The association says a site in that group would be moved to its lookout lists if evidence showed it had in fact operated as a lookout for wildland fires on a routine basis.[^2]

The two sources disagree. One marks the site as a removed lookout, while the other does not confirm that it was ever a fire lookout.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there. Check with the Roosevelt National Forest before going to look.

[^1]: Sugarloaf, Forest Lookouts, https://westlookouts.weebly.com/sugarloaf.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Colorado Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/co/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
