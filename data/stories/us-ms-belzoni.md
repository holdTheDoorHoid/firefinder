Belzoni is a fire lookout tower in Humphreys County, Mississippi.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page for it marks the tower as not standing.[^1]

No source I found describes the site today, so I cannot say whether anything remains there or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Belzoni - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts (easternuslookouts.weebly.com), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/belzoni.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
