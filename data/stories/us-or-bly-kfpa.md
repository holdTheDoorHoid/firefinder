In May 1928 a newspaper reported that the Klamath Forest Protective Association (KFPA) had set up a fire-protection substation at Bly, staffed by four men.[^1] A June 1931 item says a lookout was posted at Bly on a regular basis.[^1]

The lookout sat on a warehouse roof at the KFPA station, as an open platform.[^2][^3][^4] The register calls it Bly's earliest lookout and places the KFPA station a short distance east of the Forest Service station.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites record and firelookout.com both date the platform to 1934.[^3][^4] On the Weebly page, the platform is described as sitting above a building at the KFPA's Bly headquarters.[^1]

The 1931 item and the 1934 date may describe the same lookout. The sources do not say.[^1][^3]

The KFPA station closed in the mid-1950s. The Forest Service then erected its own 20-foot wooden tower several blocks away. The Former Fire Lookout Sites record says that tower's cab is currently in the town park.[^3]

In June 1935 a minor blaze on the Richardson Ranch near Bly was spotted from the Bly lookout.[^1] A 1967 Klamath District ODF annual report, quoted on the page, says the Bly Guard Station was shut down. The page does not link that closure to the lookout.[^1]

The Weebly page marks the lookout as removed, with no date.[^1] No source I read describes what, if anything, remains of the platform today.
