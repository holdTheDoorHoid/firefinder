Barker Peak, in Riverside County above Banning, was known as Barton Peak when its lookout went up in the 1930s.[^1][^2]

In March 1934 a newspaper item reported that the fourth station on Black Mountain was to move to Barton peak, above Banning.[^1] A later item that month said a tower would go up there, built by workers from the Vista Grande camp.[^1] The Forest Service says the original Black Mountain lookout was moved and reassembled on Barton Peak in 1935.[^3] A Peakbagging photo note credits the CCC with setting the building up on the summit that year.[^2]

In September 1939 a lightning strike at the Barton peak station halted the watch kept by its lookout, Ronald Swart.[^1]

Peakbagging says the lookout had been removed by 1970.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list marks the site as gone.[^4] Peakbagging's table also tags the site as a removed lookout, and notes that no trace may remain.[^5]

A 2013 Peakbagging photo shows the foundation of a ten-foot-square wooden cab that occupied the summit from 1935 into the late 1960s.[^2] A survey benchmark called Barton, placed in 1931, sits near the foundation.[^2] No source we read describes trail access to the summit or whether it is open to visitors.
