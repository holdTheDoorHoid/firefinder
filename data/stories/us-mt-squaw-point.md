The Squaw Point lookout stood 11 miles southeast of Stevensville, in Ravalli County, Montana, on the Bitterroot National Forest.[^1][^2] Ron Kemnow's page gives the survey location as 8N-18W-20.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the site at 46.43430, -113.88400.[^3]

Rex Kamstra's firelookout.com page puts the site at 6,655 feet and describes a 20-foot L-4 tower from the 1930s.[^1] The same page names one lookout person, Laura Roberts, in 1945.[^1]

The sources disagree on how the tower ended.[^1][^2] Kamstra's page says it was destroyed in 1961.[^1] Ron Kemnow's Forest Lookouts page says it was removed that year.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today, and no source says whether the public can visit it.[^1][^2][^3]
