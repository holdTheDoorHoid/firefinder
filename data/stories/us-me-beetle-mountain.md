Beetle Mountain, in Piscataquis County, Maine, was established as a lookout site in 1913.[^1] A 1916 Forest Commissioner's report, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, describes a new station erected there in 1915, underpinned with three-foot peeled spruce and wired down.[^1]

The sources disagree about what stood on the summit in 1920. The inventory on that page, dated 1920, describes the station as a wood ground cabin.[^1] A 360-degree panoramic map of the same year, held by the Maine State Archives, is centered on the "Beetle Mt. fire tower."[^3] We have not resolved which description is right.

Staffing is documented for 1953, when Robert Blackmore worked as lookout for the State Forest Fire Warden Service on the mountain.[^1] A Biddeford Journal item reprinted on the Forest Lookouts page names him.[^1]

Today the FFLA's Maine list shows the site as "Tower Standing*", and its key says the asterisk marks structures whose cab is no longer in place.[^2] We found no date or reason for the cab's removal, and no height, design or access information for the structure today.[^1][^2] Anyone thinking about visiting should check with the landowner first.

[^1]: Beetle Mountain #33, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/beetle-mountain-33.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Maine lookouts list, FFLA (firelookout.org), https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/me/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^3]: Beetle Mt. 1920, Maine State Archives via Digital Maine, https://digitalmaine.com/arc_firetower/8 (accessed 2026-10-09).
