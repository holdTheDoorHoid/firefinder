The Forest Lookouts page files Johnson Mountain under Iron County and the Clark National Forest.[^1] It quotes a St. Clair Chronicle report from November 2, 1939, which says Marvin Goforth was on duty at the Johnson Mountain Tower near Potosi.[^1] The report adds that he went down the hillside with a .22 rifle and shot a large bobcat.[^1]

A photograph record at the Missouri Historical Society is dated August 3, 1963, and describes a lookout station and a Southwestern Bell telephone tower on Johnson Mountain, near Potosi.[^2]

Both the 1939 report and the 1963 record place the site near Potosi, while the Forest Lookouts page files it under Iron County. I have not resolved that difference.[^1][^2]

No source I found describes Johnson Mountain today, including whether the lookout station or the telephone tower still stands. The Forest Lookouts page lists the Clark National Forest as the agency, so ask the Forest about access before you go.[^1]
