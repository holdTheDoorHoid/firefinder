The Spence Mountain tower stood in Klamath County, about 14 miles northwest of Klamath Falls, at an elevation of 5,841 feet.[^1][^2] The register places the site at 42.363744° north and 121.974461° west.[^1] The Oregon Department of Forestry oversaw the site, and a tower went up there in 1955.[^1][^2] The register numbers the site US 1830 and OR 553.[^1]

The tower was later abandoned.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list classes the site as a tower and marks it gone.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site on November 18, 2019.[^1]

None of the cited sources describes what, if anything, remains at the site now. The register says the tower itself is gone.[^1] Ask the Oregon Department of Forestry about access before you go.
