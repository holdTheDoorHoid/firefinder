The Tennessee lookout list places the Britton Lane tower in Madison County, at 583 feet (178 m), and marks it removed.[^1] Its code, 8-23, is a state forest tower number, with the first figure for the district and the second for the tower; numbers like it come from a list compiled in 1976.[^1]

The list's author says that, for towers the USGS maps and the state forestry list left unnamed, the author gave unofficial names, so the Britton Lane name may be the author's own choice rather than an older one.[^1] The list also says that many Tennessee towers have come down and that most former sites are now privately owned.[^1]

Take care not to confuse the tower with the Britton Lane Battlefield, a Madison County site at 425 Britton Lane, a few miles beyond the old Presbyterian church in Denmark. The county's only Civil War battle was fought on that site, and a restored log cabin there served as a hospital during the fighting.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the tower site, or whether the public can reach it.
