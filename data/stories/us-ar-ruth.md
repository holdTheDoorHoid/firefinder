The Ruth lookout's survey record places it about ten miles west and a mile and a half south of Viola, half a mile west of the Baxter-Fulton county line.[^1]

A Coast and Geodetic Survey description from 1953 covers the station. It calls the tower a steel structure about 50 feet high, with an observation cabin on top.[^1]

The central lookouts site lists the tower under the Arkansas Forestry Commission.[^1] Act 234 of 1931 created that agency, which also put up many towers.[^2] Statewide, the peak years for towers ran from the 1930s to the 1970s, when Arkansas had more than 120 of them.[^2]

The page gives the tower two numbers. Its heading reads Unit 4, Tower 16, while the National Geodetic Survey record further down reads Lookout Tower 4 Unit 16.[^1]

No source we found describes what stands at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
