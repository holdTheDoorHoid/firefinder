Hill Point, in Anne Arundel County, Maryland, is on the Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Maryland towers, which marks it as gone.[^1]

The state's list of its own Forest Service towers and properties names one Anne Arundel site, the Long Hill tower and office. Hill Point is not on that list.[^2]

Maryland's first forestry lookout tower went up atop Meadow Mountain in 1915. The state's fire tower report says lookout towers were a major part of an effort to further reduce fire danger, and were meant to make the forest warden's work more effective.[^3]

No source describes the Hill Point site today, so check who owns the land before going to look.

[^3]: Fire Tower Report, Maryland Department of Natural Resources, https://dnr.maryland.gov/forests/documents/fire/Fire_Tower_Report.pdf (accessed 2026-10-10).
