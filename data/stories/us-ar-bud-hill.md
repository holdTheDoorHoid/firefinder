The Bud Hill Lookout Tower stands on a prominent hill about ten miles south of Thornton, in Calhoun County, Arkansas.[^2][^3] The survey description calls it Unit 3, No. 3 of the Arkansas State Forestry Commission, and the Register lists the Arkansas Forestry Commission as its administrator.[^1][^2][^3]

The Register gives no build date. A federal Coast and Geodetic Survey description from 1942 already describes a 100-foot steel tower of standard lookout design, topped by a crow's nest with windows on all four sides.[^2][^3] The Register describes a diagonal internal staircase, while the 1942 description places a stairway in the center of the structure, so the sources disagree on how the stairs run.[^1][^2][^3]

The sources read say nothing about who staffed the tower or what fires it watched. A 1952 survey recovery found the station as described and called the original description adequate, which suggests the tower was still in place that year.[^3]

The Register lists the tower as US 1726, AR 18 and gives April 11, 2023 as its registration date. The entry also notes a site visit dated January 24, 2021.[^1]

Today the Register describes the tower as standing 100 feet tall.[^1] None of the sources read say whether the public can visit or climb it, so anyone planning a trip should ask the Forestry Commission first. One lookout index places the tower in the Ouachita National Forest, which does not match the Register.[^4]
