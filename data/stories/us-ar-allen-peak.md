The Allen Peak lookout stood in Yell County, in the Ouachita National Forest.[^1] The Central US Lookouts page dates it to 1933, when the Civilian Conservation Corps put up a 14 x 14 wood cab on a 30-foot tower.[^1] The Encyclopedia of Arkansas says the CCC built 3,100 towers during the Depression, many of them in the Ouachita and Ozark-St. Francis national forests.[^2]

The sources do not agree on the structure. A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description from 1956, which the page reproduces, calls the lookout a standard steel Forest Service tower and says it is 48 feet high.[^1] That figure does not match the 30-foot tower in the 1933 note, and the sources do not reconcile the two.[^1]

The page marks the tower as removed in 1972.[^1] The Encyclopedia of Arkansas says that aerial observation and, later, satellites made lookout towers less necessary.[^2]

No source describes the site today, so what remains on the peak, if anything, is not recorded. The 1956 survey places the station in the Ouachita National Forest, about 7 miles south-southwest of Rover and about 5 miles north of Aly.[^1] Before going there, check with the Ouachita National Forest about access.
