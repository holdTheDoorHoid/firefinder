The Crowfoot Fire Lookout is in the Columbia-Shuswap region of British Columbia, near Celista.[^3][^2]

A 2026 guide lists a Mobley (Crowfoot Mountain) Fire Lookout near Celista among lookouts confirmed or likely to have standing structures.[^2] The sources read do not say whether that tower is the one Firelookout.ca calls the Crowfoot Fire Lookout, or which summit carries it. The trail page names the route as Crowfoot Mountain and Mobley Lookout.[^1]

The trail page presents the route as a historic one that matters to the Secwépemc communities. It became an access route to the Big Bend Goldrush, and it served sheep herders and foresters.[^1] In winter, the Crowfoot Snowmobile Club looks after the route, with authorization from Recreation Sites and Trails BC.[^1]

In summer it is a hike or a mountain-bike ride. The trail page describes a walk of more than 11 km return from the parking point at the 4WD sheep track to the meadows, or about 20 km return by mountain bike to Mobley Lookout.[^1] It warns that this is remote backcountry with grizzly habitat.[^1]

Beyond the guide's listing, no source describes what remains at the lookout site today, and none says whether the public may climb or enter the tower. Do not climb it unless its access is confirmed as public.
