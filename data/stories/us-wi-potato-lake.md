The Potato Lake fire tower stood in Washburn County, Wisconsin. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list places it at 45.81071 north, 91.64763 west and marks it as gone.[^1]

Wisconsin's fire-tower network peaked at 119 towers, mostly in forested, fire-prone parts of the state.[^2]

A topographic-map site index for Washburn County still carries the name Potato Lake Fire Tower.[^3] No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1]
