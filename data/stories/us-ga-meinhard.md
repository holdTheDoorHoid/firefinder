The TN Landforms list of Georgia lookout towers includes a site named meinhard in Chatham County.[^1] The list places it at 32.185278 north and 81.219167 west, with an elevation of 21 feet (6.4 m).[^1] Its only code is "topo".[^1] The list's note says the towers on its map were identified from 1:100,000 USGS topo maps and NGS data sheets.[^1]

The list says names for towers numbered 73 and above are its compiler's own. This one is numbered 381, so meinhard is a label rather than a recorded name.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, TN Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
