The Victoria Fire Tower Site is in Coffee County, Alabama.[^1] The register puts its elevation at 485 feet, or 148 meters.[^1] The Alabama Forestry Commission administered the site.[^1]

The register lists the site as registered on September 15, 2019, nominated by Andrew Zerbe. Its registry numbers are US 1751, AL 22.[^1]

Alabama once had a great many of these towers.[^2] Thomas Kaufmann writes in an opinion piece that over 200 stood across the state at one time.[^2] He names aircraft as the most common explanation for their decline, since they could cover far more ground than a 100-foot tower.[^2] By his estimate, probably fewer than 75 remain standing.[^2]

The tower is gone.[^1] The register says it stood where a water tank now stands, and that no trace of it is left.[^1] The register does not describe public access, so anyone interested should ask the Forestry Commission before going out there.[^1]
