Stony Mountain is a former lookout site in Yamhill County, Oregon.[^1][^2] The Oregon Lookouts directory lists it under the Siuslaw National Forest with a state co-operative, at township 4 South, range 6 West, section 8.[^1]

The directory marks the lookout as removed.[^1] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Stony Mountain - Forest Lookouts, Oregon Lookouts (Ron Kemnow), https://oregonlookouts.weebly.com/stony-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Yamhill County - Forest Lookouts, Oregon Lookouts (Ron Kemnow), https://oregonlookouts.weebly.com/yamhill-county.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
