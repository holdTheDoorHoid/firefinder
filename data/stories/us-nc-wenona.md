The State Forest Service erected a 99-foot 9-inch (30.4 m) tower at Wenona, in Washington County, in 1948.[^1] The 1948 entry is the only detailed record of this tower that I found.[^1]

Two earlier towers in the same county were Civilian Conservation Corps projects: the one at Plymouth, begun in April 1936, and a tower near Scuppernong, erected in October 1939.[^1] The Plymouth tower has its own entry, and at 99 feet 9 inches it is the same height as the Wenona tower.[^1]

A 2021 list of North Carolina lookout towers gives the name Wenona for a site in Washington County, at 35.72972 N, 76.64138 W.[^2] No source I found says whether the 1948 tower still stands, what remains at the site, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: NORTH CAROLINA - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/north-carolina.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
