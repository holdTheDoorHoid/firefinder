Deadwood Point was a ground lookout on the Challis National Forest, about 10 miles northeast of Stanley, in Custer County, Idaho.[^1][^2] The site is listed at 8,965 feet.[^1]

Both the firelookout.com entry and the Forest Lookouts page place the lookout around 1939.[^1][^2] The firelookout.com entry records a ground cabin that year.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page says it was manned around then as a post for detecting fires.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a ground lookout that is gone, at 44.34781 and -114.83213.[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout at the site today, and none that says whether the public may visit.
