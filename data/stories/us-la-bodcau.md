In November 1946 a Bossier Banner-Progress report, quoted on a Forest Lookouts page, counted three fire towers in Bossier Parish.[^3] The news came from Walter Burge of Bellevue, who had long been involved with the parish's firefighting organization and had for some time been helping build towers both inside and outside the parish.[^3] Two of the three were the Plain Dealing tower near Rocky Mount and the Bodcau tower near old Bellevue.[^3] The third, on Giddens Hill near old Fillmore, had just been finished.[^3]

Wikipedia's list says the Bodcau tower was demolished in 2009 so that more state park facilities could be built.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page for the site records it as removed.[^2] The Wikipedia list also points readers to a Google Street View image of the tower.[^1]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1]
