Quarry Lookout, which the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists as Quarry (Blackberry Hill), is a 100-foot tower in Marathon County. The register describes it as an International Stacy model, with its ladder set in an inside corner.[^1][^2]

The register gives no build year. It says the tower was likely put up after 1940, because a statewide tower survey dated September 1940 does not include it.[^1]

The register lists the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources as the administrator and gives June 19, 2017 as the date the tower was registered.[^1]

In December 2015 the Milwaukee Journal Sentinel reported that the DNR would take all 72 of its fire towers out of service in 2016.[^3] The DNR listing suggests this tower is in that group, but I did not find a source that names it.[^1][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^2]

I found no source on fires, staffing or public access here, and nothing that explains the name Quarry. Do not assume the tower can be climbed.
