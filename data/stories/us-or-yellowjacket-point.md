Yellowjacket Point rises to 5,720 feet in the Umatilla National Forest, 18 miles east of Milton-Freewater in Umatilla County, Oregon.[^1][^2] The register names the U.S. Forest Service as the administrator.[^1]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register says a lookout was built there in 1941 and is now gone.[^1] The register lists the site as US 1148 and OR 496, nominated by Ray Kresek and registered on November 26, 2011.[^1] The firelookout.com listing also gives 1941, and its entry reads "1941: unknown. Gone."[^2]

The Forest Lookouts site marks the lookout as removed and gives its survey location as 5N-38E-13.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left on the point today, or whether the public may visit it. The point lies in national forest land, so check with the Umatilla National Forest before going.[^1]
