Burnt Peak is the site of a former lookout on the Kootenai National Forest, in Lincoln County, Montana, about nine miles southwest of Troy.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 6,300 feet.[^1]

The page lists a log cabin for the 1920s and an L-4 cab for 1930.[^1] It asks whether the log cabin is still there, as ruins.[^1]

On July 16, 2021, NBC Montana reported the Burnt Peak Fire at 1,716 acres, roughly nine miles southwest of Troy.[^3] None of the sources checked links that fire to the lookout site.

Firelookout.com marks the site as gone.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Burnt Peak among Lincoln County's lookout structures and sites, as a ground lookout that is gone, at 48.35505, -116.00718.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
