Gladesville, an unincorporated community, sits in Jasper County, Georgia. The name traces back to a glade near the community's first town site, and an old variant name was "The Glades." Gladesville had a post office from 1853 until 1931.[^1]

A lookout called the Gladesville Lookout Tower appears on the tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers, which places it in Jasper County at 33.22040, -83.75490.[^2] The Eastern US Lookouts page of Georgia forest lookouts gives the county as Jasper and the Oconee National Forest as the agency for the tower.[^3]

No source I could read describes what, if anything, stands at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
