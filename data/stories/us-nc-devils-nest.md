Devils Nest is a fire tower site in Mitchell County, North Carolina.[^1] A list of North Carolina lookout towers places the Devils Nest entry at 36.08261, -82.25181.[^1]

Peter J. Barr dates North Carolina's first long-distance fire detection network to the second decade of the 20th century, when it relied on lookout posts on high peaks.[^2] Over time, builders raised stone or steel towers to lift watchers higher and give them lodging.[^2] The Toe River history blog gives its Mitchell County tower details from Barr's 2008 guidebook, Hiking North Carolina's Lookout Towers.[^2]

The blog lists the year the Devils Nest tower was erected as unknown.[^2] It says the tower was taken apart and burned around 1970.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the Devils Nest site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
