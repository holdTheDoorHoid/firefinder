The Olive Hill tower stands in Hardin County, Tennessee, at about 725 feet above sea level.[^1] The Tennessee Landforms site marks it as standing and gives an index code, FE2243, that the site does not explain.[^1] The Forest Lookouts list also names an Olive Hill lookout in Hardin County.[^2]

We found no build date, builder, staffing history, or fire record for the tower in the sources we read. The index also carries an unexplained note, '7-27', so the page does not confirm the tower's current condition.[^1]

A thesis on the Olive Hill community says that a highway project section including Olive Hill begins at Firetower Road, where that road meets U.S. 64 and State Route 15.[^3] The thesis never mentions the fire tower, so the road's name is at most a loose clue to where the tower might be.

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 76 Tennessee lookouts, and none is called Olive Hill.[^4] We found no information about public access, so anyone interested should ask the landowner before going near the site.
