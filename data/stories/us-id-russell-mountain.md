Russell Mountain stands in Boundary County, Idaho, about 13 miles northwest of Bonners Ferry, at 6,618 feet.[^3][^1] A camp with an alidade, a sighting instrument used in surveying, was set up on the mountain in 1927.[^1][^4]

A cabin followed in the 1930s, according to the National Historic Lookout Register and the Former Fire Lookout Sites register.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com says the same.[^3] None of the sources gives an exact year for the cabin, so the exact date is unknown. The sources do not say why this summit was chosen.

Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both list people who staffed the site.[^3][^4] George Cooper is named for 1932, Bill Edwards for 1933, Jack Hamilton for 1934, Sam Skeels for 1935 and 1936, Robert McDonald for 1946, Garrett Scott for 1949, and Murray Phillips for 1950.[^3][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts also credits Sam Skeels for 1933, citing the Bonners Ferry Herald of 09/07/1933.[^4]

The register says the site was not staffed after 1967.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both date its abandonment to that year.[^3][^4] After that, according to the register, the tower was removed at some unstated point, but the cabin was left in place.[^1][^2] The site was registered with the National Historic Lookout Register on December 7, 2020, and with the Former Fire Lookout Sites register on May 22, 2022.[^1][^2]

Photo captions on the register show tower footings in 2006.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com reports that the cabin remains, while Idaho Fire Lookouts says it is only rumored to still stand.[^3][^4] We found no recent report on the cabin's condition. Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site hike-in, meaning it is reached on foot, and we found no statement on whether the public may visit.[^4] Anyone planning a trip should check with the Forest Service about access and the cabin's safety first.
