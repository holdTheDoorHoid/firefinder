The Stono Mountain lookout is listed in St. Francois County, Missouri, under the Missouri Department of Conservation, at legal location 35N-4E-25.[^1] Stono Mountain, in St. Francois County, has a summit elevation of 1,644 feet (501 m).[^2]

The department's annual report for fiscal 1975-76 records work on the tower road and parking lot, including gravel surfacing.[^1]

No source we found describes the site now, so we cannot say whether a tower or any remains stand there, or whether the public may reach it.
