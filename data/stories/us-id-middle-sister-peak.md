Middle Sister Peak Lookout stands on Middle Sister Peak in Shoshone County, Idaho, about 10 miles southeast of Avery.[^1][^2] The sources disagree on the peak's details. The register gives an elevation of 6,818 feet and places the peak in the Idaho Panhandle National Forest.[^1] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts give 6,898 feet and name the St. Joe National Forest.[^2][^3]

The first structure on the peak was a log cupola cabin, built around 1923.[^1][^2][^3] A 35-foot tower with an L-4 cab replaced it in 1951, but that tower accidentally burned in 1953.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The current 30-foot L-4 tower went up in 1954 and stayed in service until 1989.[^1][^2]

The lookout was restored in the 1990s.[^1][^3] According to the register, volunteers have kept the lookout staffed and in repair since 1992.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts records a 1992 renovation and a second one in 2008 and 2009, after the tower had been vacant. That work was done by a prevention technician and St. Joe fire crew members.[^3]

How often it is staffed is also described differently. Idaho Fire Lookouts reports staffing every summer.[^3] Firelookout.com reports staffing in most summers.[^2] A Spokane Historical photo caption describes volunteer staffing through the summer and fire seasons, and puts it forward as North Idaho's only active fire lookout.[^4]

Today the site is listed as drive-up, according to Idaho Fire Lookouts.[^3] It was registered on the National Historic Lookout Register on October 2, 1995, as US 151, ID 10.[^1]
