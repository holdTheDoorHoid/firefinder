Rogers Mountain Lookout stands about 29 miles north-northwest of Helena, in Lewis and Clark County, Montana.[^3][^1] The Montana Department of Natural Resources and Conservation (DNRC) is listed as its administrator.[^1][^4]

The National Historic Lookout Register dates the cab to 1978.[^1] It calls the cab a latecomer to the lookout age,[^1] and describes a two-story cab with a hip roof, built to live in.[^1] The Register says the cab can be used in emergencies.[^1]

The pages read do not name anyone who staffed the lookout or any fire it watched, so its working life is largely missing from these records.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the structure as standing.[^2] The Register lists April 12, 2022 as its registration date, under US 1538, MT 101.[^1]

The records disagree slightly on elevation. The Register gives 6,915 feet, while firelookout.com gives 6,921 feet.[^1][^3]

The pages read do not say whether the public can climb the tower, stay in the cab, or reserve it. Anyone planning a trip should check with DNRC first.
