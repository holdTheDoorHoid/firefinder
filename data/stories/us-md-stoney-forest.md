The Stoney Forest lookout stood in Harford County, Maryland.[^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts history says the tower was not completed until the fall of 1929.[^1] That page spells the county "Hartford," but the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maryland list gives Harford.[^1][^2]

As quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, the state's 1930 forestry report says the tower's first thorough test came amid the dangerous fire conditions of 1930.[^1] The report adds that the tower "proved very satisfactory" and worked alongside the Foy's Hill tower in Cecil County.[^1] It also describes a steel tower 80 feet high.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maryland list now marks the Stoney Forest tower as gone.[^2]

A state forest with a similar name, Stoney Demonstration Forest, is managed by the Maryland DNR Forest Service, and one of its stated objectives is maintaining it as a forest recreation site. A small gated parking lot sits on Nova Scotia Road, along the forest's southern edge.[^3] No source we read describes what remains at the tower site today.
