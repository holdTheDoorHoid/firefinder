The Saunders Lake fire tower stands in Algoma District, Ontario, with a trail of about 1 km running west from Saunders Lake to it.[^1] The firelookout.ca archive calls it one of the last fire lookout towers built in Algoma District, and says it is in very good condition, cupola included.[^1]

A hiking account gives the route from Highway 17 at Thessalon: Highway 638, then Highway 670, then Shaw Road.[^2] The trail is an old road and is not marked. It starts across the road from the lake's north-west tip.[^2] It gains about 61 m (200 ft) from start to finish.[^2] The firelookout.ca record calls the hike easy, with a gradual climb.[^1] The same account was glad to find the cupola still in place.[^2]

Today, a former tower-man's cabin stands on a point along the lake's north-west shore.[^1]

A still-standing list on the Ontario's Fire Tower Lookouts site includes the Saunders Lake tower without an asterisk.[^3] The list uses an asterisk for towers whose cupola was removed and which now serve as communications towers.[^3] The same list adds that the MNR has stopped maintaining these towers and asks visitors not to climb them.[^3]
