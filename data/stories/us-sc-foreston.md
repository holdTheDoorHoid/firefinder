The Foreston lookout was one of the fire towers run by the South Carolina Forestry Commission in Clarendon County.[^1][^2] The commission's tower system dates to 1930, when its first tower went up near Camden.[^3] Tower operators watched for fires and sent crews out by telephone, and around 1930 the commission began building telephone lines to connect its towers with its firefighters.[^3]

The register and the Forest Lookouts site both record the Foreston tower as removed.[^1][^2] The commission shut its fire tower system down on October 1, 1993.[^3]

A map database still carries a point named "Foreston Lookout Tower" at nearly the same coordinates.[^1][^4] That entry is not evidence that a tower still stands there. No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
