A compiled list of Georgia lookout towers, kept on tnlandforms.us, places a tower labelled "lincolnton" in Lincoln County at 33.80864, -82.47104.[^1] The list gives its elevation as 510, with no unit stated.[^1] The list says its towers were identified from 1:100,000 USGS topographic maps and NGS data sheets.[^1] The label is the compiler's own: the page says names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's creation, and this entry is number 240.[^1]

The entry carries two codes, BLL-50815 and gfc-006.[^1] The page says the BLL numbers refer to coordinates the Georgia Forestry Commission supplied for 118 towers, and the gfc numbers refer to an older Forestry Commission list that described the locations of 203 towers.[^1]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything remains or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
