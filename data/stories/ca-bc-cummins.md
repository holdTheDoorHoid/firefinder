Cummins Fire Lookout is a former BC fire lookout. It lies west of Cummins Lakes Provincial Park and north of Revelstoke.[^1] A web index of Canadian lookouts calls the site CUMMINS MOUNTAIN, gives it a latitude of 52.1 and a longitude of 118.3, and files it under a Bulkley - Nechako label.[^2] That label does not fit Firelookout.ca's location north of Revelstoke, so the two sources describe the site's region differently.[^1][^2]

Firelookout.ca describes the route to the top. The lookout is reached from a branch of the Wood River Forest Service Road, and that road is in turn reached by boat across Kinbasket Lake. From the road, the climb is a 5.5 km (3.4 mi) one-way walk up an overgrown service road to the tower.[^1] The same entry gives two climb figures, 1,060 m (5,250 ft) from the road and 1,600 m (5,250 ft) in total. Both carry the same feet figure, so its climb numbers do not agree with each other.[^1] A British Columbia travel blog lists Cummins as near Kinbasket Lake, with its status uncertain and its access difficult.[^3]

No source I read describes what, if anything, stands at the summit today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
