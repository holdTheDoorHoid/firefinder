Parker Hill is a fire tower in Cannon County, Tennessee. The Tennessee Landforms list gives its elevation as 1,247 feet and marks it as standing. Its number, 5-5, is a district-and-tower number taken from a state list made in 1976.[^1]

I found no build date, builder, height, or design for this tower. The Division of Forestry survey lists Parker Hill in Cannon County among the towers still standing as of March 26, 2014. That is the newest dated record in the sources.[^2] The survey says construction of the state's tower network began in the 1930s, and that the Civilian Conservation Corps is reported to have built 98 lookout houses and towers in Tennessee in that decade. It also says the records are scarce, so the exact number and locations are unknown.[^2] Those figures cover the whole state, not this tower.

The Forest Lookouts page for Parker Hill gives only its name and county.[^3]

The name also belongs to a community. A Cannon County community history places Parker Hill nine miles south of Woodbury, at the western edge of the Highland Rim. It says the community has a store and used to have a school.[^4] The sources do not say whether the tower stands in that community.

Tennessee Landforms says many of the towers have come down, and that most sites are now on private land. It also warns against climbing the towers, because the steps are often missing or decayed.[^1] I found nothing about whether this tower can be visited. Check who owns the land before going to look, and do not climb it.
