Crane Point is a former lookout site in the Malheur National Forest, in Grant County, Oregon, about 28 miles southeast of John Day.[^1][^2]

The register describes a platform with a firefinder stand and a tent cabin.[^1] A 2022 visit report, by contrast, describes a summit platform and a nearby tent camp, and says no cabin or tower was built there.[^3]

Sources differ on the start date. One says the site was a lookout starting in 1930, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites register says the 1930s.[^3][^1] The site was used into the 1960s.[^1][^3]

The tent cabin is gone.[^1] The register says the platform and firefinder stand remained as of 2022.[^1] Two visit reports from 2022 agree on the platform. One says it is still there with the stand, while the other says the old platform and the fire finder stand are all that is left.[^4][^3] The Roaming Civic report describes the platform as fairly sound, though a few boards show rot.[^4]

Visit reports disagree on the walk. One visitor parked and walked the last few hundred feet.[^3] Another drove most of the way and walked about a mile and a half to the platform.[^4] The first report says one access road is closed during hunting season.[^3] The second report says the road was muddy after rain.[^4] We found no closure notice for the site in the pages we read.
