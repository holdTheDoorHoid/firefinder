The Forest Lookouts site lists a tower at Bunker in Shannon County, under the Clark National Forest.[^1] It credits the build to a pine pole project by Civilian Conservation Corps enrollees from Camp Bunker.[^1] The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the tower in 1939.[^1] Its survey point was the finial of a Forest Service tower about four miles southwest of Bunker, a 110-foot (34 m) steel structure topped by an enclosed observation deck.[^1] The pine-pole credit and the steel description do not agree, and the site does not explain the difference.[^1]

A Rolla Herald item dated October 31, 1949, quoted on the site, reports a fatal crash that killed the wife of a Forest Service towerman posted near Bunker.[^1]

The site marks the survey station as removed.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site or whether it is open to visitors today.[^1]

[^1]: Bunker - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts (centraluslookouts.weebly.com), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/bunker.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
