The Cub Hill tower stands at 9405 Old Harford Road in Baltimore County.[^1][^2] The survey gives its DNR land unit as the Baltimore County Project Office, Cub Hill Ranger Station.[^1] The state's deed for the site is dated July 24, 1942, but the survey records the tower's installation date as unknown, and I could not find a build date in the sources I read.[^1]

The survey gives the tower a height of 125 feet and a good condition rating.[^1] It describes the platform as in use for long-term research, with climate and air-monitoring equipment mounted on the tower.[^1] I found no date for when the cab came down. The Forest Fire Lookout Association marks the tower as standing with the cab no longer in place.[^2]

The survey proposes keeping the site for three purposes: a regional forest ranger station, a communications facility, and a central platform for ecological and atmospheric study.[^1] I found no record of staffing or notable fires at this site in these sources.

The survey places the access point on the left-hand side of Old Harford Road, north of Summit Avenue.[^1] A 2002 student web project from UMBC says residents always asked whether they could climb the tower.[^3] That could not happen, so the project shares photographs taken from the tower instead.[^3] Treat the tower as off limits.
