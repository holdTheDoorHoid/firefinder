Chimney Rock is a ridgetop southeast of Roxy Ann, in Jackson County about eight miles north-northeast of Ashland, lying between the Antelope and Lost creek drainages.[^2][^3] A 1952 Medford newspaper item said a forest fire lookout was planned there: a 14-foot-square building with an aluminum exterior, set on a 30-foot tower, at an estimated cost of $4,500.[^3] The same item put the site between Antelope and Lake creeks, so the early sources already disagree on where it sits.[^3]

The lookout built in 1955 seems to have differed from that plan.[^3] A district report describes a 14-by-14-foot ground house built on the rock, with materials hauled up a high line about 250 feet long from the end of the road.[^3] Howard Ash of Eagle Point, a veteran lookout builder, did the construction, and the report gives the cost as $3,510.53.[^3] The register also dates the lookout to 1955 and names the Oregon Department of Forestry as administrator.[^1]

The district placed a lookout on the site on June 18, 1958, to complete its staffing.[^3] William Thompson, a district lookout who had intended to take up the Chimney Rock post once the weather improved, died on June 8, 1958.[^3] Maxine Gibeson of Ashland took over the station in June 1960.[^3] By June 1963 the district said Chimney Rock was one of six lookouts that would not be manned that summer, though the lookouts could be manned again in an emergency.[^3]

The register says the lookout was removed in 1969, while firelookout.com and the Forest Lookouts page say it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2][^3] The register and firelookout.com also call it a 5-foot wooden live-in tower, which does not fit the ground house in the 1955 district report, and we could not settle the design from these sources.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and we found no current access information. The register has a photo captioned "Chimney Rock Lookout site 2007," but the caption does not say what it shows.[^1] Check land ownership and access rules before you go.
