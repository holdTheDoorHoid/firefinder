Hartzfelt Hill sits in the hills south of Olean, in Cattaraugus County.[^1][^2] In 1924 the state Conservation Commission set up a lookout station there, with a 60-foot steel tower and an observer's cabin.[^2][^1] Local landowners and towns paid for it.[^3] The tower was an Aermotor LS-40 model.[^4] A 1923 newspaper said the aim was to keep forest fires from causing serious damage.[^2]

The sources disagree on a few details. A September 1923 plan called for a tower 70 feet high, to be built at Flatiron Rock.[^2] A March 1924 paper described a 70-foot steel tower with a cabin on top, to go on the Forrest Curtis lease near the Rocky Crest sanitarium.[^2] The Commission's report and the 2024 newsletter both give 60 feet, and the newspapers do not clearly say whether they describe the same station.[^2][^3]

The 1923 plans had the state maintaining and staffing the tower for seven months a year during the fire season.[^2] In May 1926 the Democrat and Chronicle reported that the district had thirty small fires that spring.[^2] It credited the Flatiron Rock tower with spotting small fires that were put out quickly.[^2]

The state bought the land around the tower in 1967 for a radio repeater used by law enforcement.[^1] Wikipedia says the tower stopped lookout work at the end of the 1970 season, as aerial detection came into wider use.[^1] The tower was taken down in 1973.[^3][^1] Starr's list marks it removed and gives the local name as Birdsall Hill.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left on the hilltop today, or whether the public can reach it.
