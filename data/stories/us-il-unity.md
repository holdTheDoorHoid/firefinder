Unity was a fire lookout site in Pulaski County, Illinois.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists its type as a platform, not a tower.[^1]

The association gives its position as 37.13611 north and 89.24694 west, and marks the site as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it can be visited.

[^1]: Illinois Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/il/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Illinois Lookouts (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/il/il-co/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
