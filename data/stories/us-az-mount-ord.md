Mount Ord Lookout stands on Mount Ord within the Tonto National Forest.[^1] The mountain takes its name from Major General Edward Ord.[^2] The National Historic Lookout Register places the lookout in Gila County, Arizona, while the Arizona fire-lookout listing gives the county as Gila/Maricopa.[^1][^3]

The current lookout took the place of an earlier 1936 Aermotor MC 39 tower.[^1] The newer structure was constructed in 1983.[^1] It is about 102 feet tall and has a 14-by-14-foot metal cab with a catwalk.[^1]

The sources I could read do not say whether anyone staffed the lookout, or when that work ended. None of them names a fire it reported.

The Rim Country Museum in Payson, Arizona, received the 1936 tower, and the register gives no date for the move.[^1] The current lookout is still standing, according to the Arizona listing.[^3] It was registered with the National Historic Lookout Register on July 20, 2002, after a nomination by Dave Lorenz, the AZ/NM registrar.[^1] The same listing shows the relocated tower at the museum as standing.[^3]

The U.S. Forest Service administers the site, with the Mesa Ranger District as a cooperator.[^1] The top of the mountain also holds a collection of communications towers.[^2] The sources I could read do not say whether the lookout is staffed or open to visitors, so check with the Mesa Ranger District before going up.
