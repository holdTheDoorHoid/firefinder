Saratoga lies in west central Hardin County, where State Highway 105 meets Farm Road 770.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas index lists a tower at Saratoga and marks it gone.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
