Bee Top Mountain Lookout stood on Bee Top Mountain in Bonner County, Idaho, inside the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the mountain's elevation as 6,212 feet.[^2]

Sources agree that an L-4 cab went up on a stone base or foundation around 1938.[^1][^2][^3] The date is not fully settled, though. A Northern Idaho News item from August 27, 1937, quoted by Forest Lookouts, already refers to the Bee Top lookout station.[^3] That suggests the station was in use before the cab's date of about 1938.[^3]

The same 1937 item says Paul Schravendyk had come down from the station for a three-day leave.[^3]

The lookout was destroyed in 1966.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list records the Bonner County site as a ground lookout that is gone.[^4]

No source describes what remains at the site.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts classes the site as a hike-in.[^2]
