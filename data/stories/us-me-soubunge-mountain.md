Soubunge Mountain, in Township 4, Range 11 of Piscataquis County, got a wooden fire tower in 1918.[^1][^2] A 12-foot steel tower replaced it in 1919.[^2]

A 1919 state report, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, describes a two-and-a-half-mile telephone line that tied the tower into the Cuxabexis line at Camp 3, on Ripogenus Stream.[^1] It also says the lookout came with a full camp outfit.[^1] A newspaper item from August 1919 gives the height as 12 feet.[^1]

A Maine History Online caption gives 16 feet instead.[^3] Because the Maine Memory record and the 1919 newspaper both say 12, this entry uses 12 feet.[^2][^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing, though its cab is missing.[^4] Maine's staffed fire-tower network ended after a budget crisis in 1991.[^5] Whether the site is open to visitors today is not settled by these sources, so check locally before going, and do not climb the structure.
