Highland Park is a former lookout site in Catron County, New Mexico, on the Gila National Forest.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a Crows Nest type, now gone, at about 33.76 north and 108.89 west.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the site, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: New Mexico Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Highland Park, Forest Lookouts (Ron Kemnow), https://westlookouts.weebly.com/highland-park.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
