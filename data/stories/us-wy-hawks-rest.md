Hawks Rest is a lookout site in Teton County, Wyoming, in the Teton National Forest. A directory of forest lookouts gives its approximate location as 48N-110W-20.[^1]

On July 15, 1954, the Teton Forest judged it wise to staff two lookouts after two weeks of hot, dry weather. A notice the Jackson Hole Courier printed listed Hawks Rest among other posts due to be staffed the following week.[^1]

A National Park Service account, relayed by Buckrail, says the Bridger-Teton National Forest gave up all of its fire lookout towers after the early 1970s, as airplane patrols became more common.[^2] No source read links that to Hawks Rest specifically.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Hawks Rest among its unknown or undocumented Wyoming sites.[^3] None of the sources read describes a structure or remains there today.

A pack-stock bridge with the Hawks Rest name is a separate structure.[^4] The Hawks Rest Pack Bridge crosses the Yellowstone River in the Teton Wilderness, about 79 miles north of Jackson.[^4] Its original version was built in 1959, and the nearest trailhead or road lies roughly 28 miles off.[^4]
