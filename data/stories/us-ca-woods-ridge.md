Woods Ridge Lookout is a 100-foot Aermotor tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab, in the Groveland Ranger District of the Stanislaus National Forest in Tuolumne County, California.[^1] The Civilian Conservation Corps built it in 1939.[^1][^3]

It replaced a treetop lookout the Forest Service set up in 1913, which sat atop a 100-foot yellow pine that the West Side Lumber Co. had donated.[^1] The register says a residence and garage stand at the site, and a photo caption in its entry dates a residence cabin to 1943.[^1]

In June 1962 a woman took a summer job as a fire lookout.[^2] The Forest Lookouts site quotes an Oakdale newspaper describing her cleaning up quarters that had sat empty for several seasons.[^2] The register says the tower has not been staffed since the 1970s, and a 2011 photo note on Peakbagging says the same.[^1][^3]

As of 2017 the register described the tower as standing but not in use.[^1] The tower was registered on April 24, 2017, as US 1187, CA 118, with Bill Luedeke as nominator.[^1]

The sources read do not describe current access or mention a rental for the tower.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association says its listed rentals are managed through Recreation.gov unless noted otherwise, and its rental list does not show Woods Ridge.[^4] The tower sits on Forest Service land and was not in use in 2017, so its current status is unclear from these sources.[^1]
