Rujada Point, also called Rose Hill, is a former lookout site on a minor summit about a mile to the west-northwest of Rose Hill in Lane County, Oregon.[^1][^2] It sits about 14 miles west-southwest of Oakridge, and the Umpqua National Forest is the agency listed for it.[^2][^3]

In August 1933 an item in The Sentinel, as quoted on the Oregon lookouts page, said material had arrived and work was about to begin, and that timber had been felled to open the view.[^3] A September 14, 1933 item in the Roseburg Chieftain said the Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC) was to build a 10x10 lookout before the first snow, and that size was the norm for secondary lookouts.[^3]

By 1934 the site carried a 40-foot tower of round fir poles, topped with a 10x10 L-5 cab.[^1][^3] Panoramic photographs were made there in February 1934 and retaken in July 1937.[^3]

Sources disagree on how the tower ended. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says it was abandoned and removed, while firelookout.com says it was abandoned and destroyed.[^1][^2] The Oregon lookouts page dates its removal to about 1960.[^3]

The site was registered with the Former Fire Lookout Sites register on March 28, 2011.[^1]

No source we checked describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
