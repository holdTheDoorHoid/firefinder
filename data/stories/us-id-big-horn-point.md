Big Horn Point is in Clearwater County, Idaho, about 18 miles northeast of Pierce. Its elevation is 4,659 feet.[^1]

The Clearwater National Forest is the agency listed for it.[^1][^2] A Forest Service list places it in section 21, township 38 north, range 8 east.[^3]

The site began as a camp in 1935.[^1] In 1937 a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab went up.[^1][^2][^3] A 20-foot L-6 patrol tower stood half a mile southwest.[^1][^2]

Three sources describe the site as ruins: firelookout.com, Idaho Fire Lookouts, and a Forest Service list compiled in June 2016.[^1][^2][^3] A firelookout.org list of Idaho lookouts instead labels the tower "Tower Gone."[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^2] Apart from the ruins and Tower Gone labels, no source we read describes the site today.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
