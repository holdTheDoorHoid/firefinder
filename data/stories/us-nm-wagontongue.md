The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Wagontongue lookout in Catron County as a crow's nest type, and it marks the lookout as gone.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site that covers it places the lookout under the Gila National Forest.[^2]

Wagontongue Mountain sits at the centre of the Wagon Tongue roadless area, an 11,411-acre tract in the Reserve Ranger District of the Gila National Forest.[^3] The Continental Divide National Scenic Trail climbs across the mountain.[^3] For an overnight stop along it, dispersed camping is the realistic choice, and it follows Gila National Forest regulations.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the lookout site, or whether the spot is open to visitors.
