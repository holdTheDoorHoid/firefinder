Staunton is a community in Cook County, Georgia.[^1][^2] A photo blog says its author understands it was once a historic railroad stop, and that it is now hard to tell apart from the nearby city of Lenox.[^2] The tnlandforms list of Georgia towers places a tower named Staunton at 31.28226, -83.46507. Unlike the Statenville entry, it carries no BLL or gfc number.[^1]

Two photographs by Michael Rivera, both taken on 16 August 2014, show a Cook County Forestry Commission tower on MJ Taylor Road.[^3][^4] Both file descriptions say the tower was demolished in 2018, along with all its buildings.[^3][^4] Neither photo names Staunton, so it is unclear whether they show the tower on the list.[^3][^4] If they do, the tower was demolished in 2018.[^3][^4]

The sources found do not describe the site's current condition.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
