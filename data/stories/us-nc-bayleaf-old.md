A Wake County forestry history on the Legeros fire site records a lookout tower on high ground on the west side of Six Forks Road, about eight miles north of Raleigh.[^1] The lease for that site was recorded in 1936, and a steel Aermotor tower of about 99 feet went up there in 1937.[^1] A statewide list of North Carolina towers gives the record as Bayleaf (old), in Wake County.[^4]

Jenny Tilley was the tower's long-time operator.[^1] The Legeros blog says she was one of the first female tower operators in the state and that the tower was closed in 1985.[^2] The forestry history gives 1985 as the last year it was manned.[^1] WRAL says she started on the job when she was 22.[^3]

In 1967 a new site was bought on what is now Howard Road, just west of Creedmoor Road, and the tower was re-erected there in 1968.[^1][^2] Jerry Tilley, her son, told WRAL that they added 20 feet to the tower when they moved it.[^3] The forestry history gives 99 feet for both sites, which conflicts with what Jerry Tilley told WRAL about the move.[^1][^3]

A 2023 WRAL report calls the tower on Howard Road vacant, and the forestry history, last updated in 2017, says it is no longer used.[^1][^3] The forestry history also lists a ranger house and an equipment shed at the same location.[^1] WRAL describes it as rusting in the Raleigh suburbs near Crabtree Valley Mall, and the Legeros blog says it is still standing.[^3][^2] No source we read describes what remains on the Six Forks Road site, and no source says whether the Howard Road site is open to the public.
