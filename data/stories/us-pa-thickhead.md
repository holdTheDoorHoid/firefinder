Thickhead Mountain is in Centre County, Pennsylvania, in an area that the Forest Lookouts site calls the Thickhead Wild Area.[^2] A tree-mounted observation post, built in 1915, stood on the mountain. It was reported as 35 feet above the ground.[^2] The register dates the tree tower to 1915 and says it was still in use around 1917.[^1]

A survey description from 1935 describes an observation tower on the summit, erected by CCC workers, 25 feet tall, with a four-foot light stand on top.[^2] It is not clear from these sources whether this tower replaced the 1915 post.

The register says the 1915 tree tower is no longer standing.[^1]

A photo caption on Flickr places a Penn Lookout Trail sign at a road junction. It describes a narrow, half-mile footpath, inside the wild area of Rothrock State Forest, that leads to a clearing the caption says is likely the tower site.[^3] The caption adds that the view from the clearing has grown over.[^3] We found no source that describes any remains at the site today.
