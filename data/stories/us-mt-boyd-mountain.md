Boyd Mountain rises to 5,349 feet about six miles northwest of St. Regis, in Mineral County.[^1] Both listings place it in the Lolo National Forest.[^1][^2]

The lookout was an L-4 tower that dates from the 1930s, according to the listings.[^1][^2] The second listing gives the same decade and a parenthetical credit to Kresek, with no further detail.[^2] In general terms, a local history column says the L-4 was far and away the favorite live-in design. It says these cabs could sit on the ground or on pole or timber towers as tall as 100 feet.[^4]

The listings disagree on how the tower ended. One says it was abandoned.[^1] The other says it was removed.[^2] A state lookout list marks it as gone.[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the summit, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
