Caribou Hill is in Bonner County, Idaho, and Upper Priest Lake lies about two miles to its southwest.[^1] Both firelookout.com and Forest Lookouts date the tower to 1933 and list it at 40 feet tall with an L-4 cab.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the elevation as 4,592 feet and places the tower in the Pend Oreille TPA.[^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts has no staffing history on record for this site.[^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1971.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com marks its span as 1933 to 1971.[^1] Forest Lookouts attributes its dates to Kresek.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a gone tower.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it. Idaho Fire Lookouts files the tower as a hike-in site that was destroyed.[^2]
