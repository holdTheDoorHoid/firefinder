Anderson Mountain rises about seven miles east of Myrtle Point, in Coos County, Oregon, at roughly 1,401 feet.[^1][^2]

The Coos Bay Times reported in July 1929 that the primary lookout station near Bridge had been completed that spring.[^3] An April 1929 item had said a lookout would be erected on the mountain that summer.[^3] In 1933 a crew of 20 men was building a 60-foot tower there, and a September 1933 report gave each lookout a height of 60 feet, with a cabin on top.[^3] The register and firelookout.com instead describe a 40-foot pole L-4 tower built in 1933.[^1][^2] The sources disagree on the height.

The papers name a few of the people who worked the station. Lloyd Jarvis was the lookout in 1935.[^3] Bob King worked the station in the 1937 fire season and was assigned there again in 1938.[^3] In 1941 William Huff left his job at Albany to report for duty, then faced a two-mile climb uphill to his summer post.[^3]

In July 1955 the Coos Bay Times reported that the station had been abandoned because the tower's timbers were rotting.[^3] The register says the tower was removed in 1956, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.
