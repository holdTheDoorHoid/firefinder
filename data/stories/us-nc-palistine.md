The Palistine lookout was a steel tower on a hill of that name, about 10 miles north of Fayetteville in Cumberland County.[^1] A history page dates it to 1927, when a steel tower of 60 feet (about 18.3 m) went up at Palestine Church, and it cites a 1926–28 biennial report from the North Carolina Department of Conservation and Development for that date.[^1] The page spells the church name Palestine and the hill name Palistine.[^1] In January 1928, The Forest Worker listed a steel tower 60 feet tall on Palistine Hill among the towers newly put into service.[^1] The page files the lookout under the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources.[^1]

A second listing, spelled Palestine, puts a tower in the same county with the NGS point ID EZ4810, and the two names probably refer to one lookout.[^2]

No source describes the site today.
