Huckleberry Mountain is in Douglas County, about 20 miles southeast of Cottage Grove, at 4,754 feet.[^1][^2] The register lists the Douglas FPA as the agency that administered the site.[^1]

A 60-foot pole tower with an 8-by-8-foot cab, plus a ground cabin for living quarters, went up in 1929.[^1][^2] In 1945 a 42-foot pole L-4 tower replaced it.[^1][^2]

Both sources say the 1945 tower was removed in 1970.[^1][^2] firelookout.com's page header reads "R.I.P. 1929-1945," which would end the lookout in 1945 instead. Its own description gives the 1970 removal, so we follow the description.[^2]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the site can be visited. Check with the managing agency before planning a trip.

[^2]: Huckleberry Mtn. Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/or/huckleberry1163.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
