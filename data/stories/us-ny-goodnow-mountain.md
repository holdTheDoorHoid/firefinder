The Goodnow Mountain fire tower stands in the Huntington Wildlife Forest in Newcomb, Essex County, New York, on a summit the register puts at 2,690 feet.[^1][^4] Wikipedia gives the peak as 2,664 feet, so the sources disagree on that figure.[^2]

The 60-foot tower went up in 1922, but the sources disagree on who built it.[^1][^2] Wikipedia credits the Civilian Conservation Corps,[^2] while the register says the state Conservation Department built it.[^1]

The register says the tower was staffed until 1970.[^1] Wikipedia says it closed at the end of the 1979 season, so the end of its fire watch is unclear.[^2]

Later, the tower passed to the SUNY College of Forestry.[^2] The register says the college restored it and that it is open to the public.[^1] The college and the Town of Newcomb also worked together on a restoration in 1995.[^1]

In 2025 the college closed the tower and trail on July 7 for about three months of repairs.[^3][^4] A helicopter flew new galvanized steps, a roof and landings up to the summit, and the project also repainted the tower and restored the cabin at its base.[^3]

Today the summit's view is often described as among the best in the Adirondack Park.[^4] The register describes a 3-mile trail, while the college describes a four-mile trail, so plan for a hike of roughly three to four miles.[^1][^3] The college scheduled a ribbon-cutting for 1 p.m. on October 14, 2025, to mark the reopening.[^3] The sources I read do not confirm whether the reopening went ahead as planned, so check the college's notices before you go.
