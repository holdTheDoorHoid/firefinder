Sand Mountain Lookout stands inside Palisades Park in Blount County, Alabama.[^1] The Register describes it as a 100-foot steel tower formerly held by the Alabama Forestry Commission. It was registered on May 27, 2016, as US 1130, AL 63.[^1]

A 2018 news report says workers kept watch for forest and wildfires from the cab in Blount County between 1949 and 1985.[^2] Neither that report nor the Register gives a construction date, so the sources we read do not say when the tower was built.[^1][^2]

Once technology advanced and the population grew, a person in the cab was no longer necessary, so the tower sat empty.[^2] Still, the county saw it as a landmark and part of its history worth preserving.[^2] Work started in 2016 and wrapped up in early 2018, aided by several grants and local businesses, among others.[^2] Park officials said it is the third fire tower restored in the state and the second opened to the public.[^2]

The 2018 report puts the cab more than 100 feet off the ground, with 133 steps leading up to it.[^2] A 2026 event listing calls the restored tower the park's newest feature and says the climb is free.[^3] An earlier guide, current as of July 2018, advised getting a free permit from the park office first.[^4] In the same report, County Administrator John Bullard said the Alabama Forestry Commission can use the tower whenever needed, including during a large forest fire.[^2]
