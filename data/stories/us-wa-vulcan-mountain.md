Vulcan Mountain rises to 5,204 feet in Ferry County, in the Colville National Forest, about 24 miles north of Republic.[^1][^2]

A September 1915 newspaper item said lookout towers had been built on Vulcan and three other peaks.[^3][^4] In 1931 the Republic News-Miner reported that Willis Ward and R.E. Foote were putting up a fifty-foot pole lookout tower on the mountain.[^4] Willhite says the finished tower, in 1933, stood 55 feet tall, with a 6-by-6-foot cab and a log cabin.[^4][^1][^2]

Firelookout.com lists two known lookout personnel: Paul Taylor in 1923 and James Kasmer in 1945.[^2] In May 1934 the same newspaper reported that location work had started on a road to the lookout, which it said would help fire protection.[^4]

Accounts of the end differ. The register says the tower and cabin were removed in 1952.[^1] Firelookout.com and Willhite say they were destroyed that year.[^2][^4]

Today the road is good for all vehicles, according to Willhite.[^4] The trailhead has no sign, only a berm and room for a few cars, and the old road runs all the way to the site.[^4] The highest knoll marks the tower's location but has no views. A smaller knoll to the right has views and marks the cabin's location.[^4] Willhite found no footings at the tower site and describes the structure as crashed in the nearby woods.[^4] Photo captions show the cabin's foundations and an insulator on a tree near the tower site.[^4] Willhite adds that phone insulators are all around the knobs.[^4]
