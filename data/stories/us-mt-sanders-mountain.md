Sanders Mountain had a fire lookout on the Flathead National Forest in Flathead County, Montana, about 24 miles west-northwest of Kalispell.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 6,003 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list places the site at 48.29037 north and 114.83682 west.[^2]

Firelookout.com dates the lookout to 1934 and describes a 50-foot metal tower with a cabin.[^1]

Firelookout.com also says the lookout had a patrol 2.5 miles south, on Grubb Mountain.[^1] A Forest Lookouts page describes a Grubb Mountain patrol lookout 2.5 miles south of the Sanders Mountain lookout.[^3] It also quotes a July 1930 newspaper item reporting that a pack train had headed into Grubb Mountain to start a lookout camp.[^3]

Firelookout.com gives 1948 as the year the lookout was abandoned, and marks it gone.[^1] The Forest Lookouts Grubb Mountain page also gives 1948 as the year its lookout was abandoned.[^3] The firelookout.com entry is short, and it does not make clear whether its 1948 date refers to the Sanders lookout or to the Grubb patrol.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list also marks the tower gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the Sanders Mountain site today, or whether the public may visit it.
