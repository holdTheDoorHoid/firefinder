Goodman Ridge's lookout is a 5,218-foot site in Umatilla County, Oregon, on the Umatilla National Forest.[^1][^6] The register and firelookout.com say the lookout was built in 1936.[^1][^2]

A 1936 letter says the 66-foot, ring-connected tower took 36 man-days of CCC labor to erect.[^3] An August 1936 newspaper item reports that CCC carpenters had started work on the site.[^3] The sources disagree on height: the register and firelookout.com say 60 feet, while a 1963 USGS data sheet reproduced on Rex's page says 100 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

The tower carried a 7-by-7-foot cab, and a ground cabin provided living quarters.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com names C.J. Waltz as lookout personnel in 1943.[^2] In 1949 the post moved from emergency duty to regular lookout-fireman status.[^3] The register says the tower was used for emergency detection in the 1970s.[^1]

A 2012 visit dates the cab's loss to the winter of 2007-08, while the register and firelookout.com date it to 2008.[^4][^1][^2] Firelookout.com dates the ground cabin's fire to October 2009.[^2] The Forest Service has abandoned the tower, and a 2012 visit found it in poor shape.[^4] Firelookout.org lists the tower as standing, with its cab gone.[^5]

CherylHill's table lists the site as accessible to the public.[^6] In 2012 visitors drove up Skyline Road and walked a spur from Road 3128 to reach it.[^4] The tower is abandoned and in poor shape.[^4] Please do not climb it.
