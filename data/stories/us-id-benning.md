Benning Mountain's lookout was a 40-foot tower topped with an L-4 cab, built in 1930 in the Kaniksu National Forest about 13 miles north-northeast of Clark Fork, in Bonner County.[^1][^3] It stood at the westernmost end of the summit area, about a third of a mile southwest of the high point.[^1]

The two lookout sites that cover it differ on the summit's elevation: one gives 6,099 feet and the other 6,349 feet.[^1][^2]

The tower was abandoned in 1951.[^3] Firelookout.com lists ruins from the same year.[^1] The Idaho lookout site credits both the 1930 and 1951 dates to a source it names only as Kresek.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the tower as gone.[^4]

Today the Idaho Fire Lookouts site describes the place as a hike-in site with ruins.[^2] No source describes what those ruins look like now.
