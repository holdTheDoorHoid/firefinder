Thunder Mountain sits 13 miles west of Yaak, in the Kootenai National Forest of Lincoln County, Montana, at an elevation of 6,011 feet.[^1] In 1940 the site had a 30-foot pole tower of the L-4 design.[^1]

Firelookout.com says the tower was gone in the 1970s.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's state list also marks it as a gone tower, at 48.80818 north and 116.00056 west.[^2]

Montana has a second Thunder Mountain lookout, in Ravalli County, and it is a separate site.[^2] The Ravalli County tower was also a 30-foot pole L-4 design, erected in 1940 and destroyed in 1975.[^3] The two entries share a design and a build year, so the sites are easy to mix up.[^1][^3]

No source we read describes what remains on the summit today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
