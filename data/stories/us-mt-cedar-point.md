Cedar Point is in Lake County, Montana, on the Flathead National Forest.[^1] Firelookout.com describes a 30-foot pole L-4 tower built in 1934 and reports that it was later taken down.[^2]

A federal survey gives more detail on the site. In 1940 the Coast and Geodetic Survey described a Forest Service lookout house on a bare peak about 10.5 miles east of Polson, in the Mission Range, and rated the survey mark in good condition that year.[^3] A 1951 newspaper report placed a fire about a mile east of Cedar lookout, which lies northwest of the Condon ranger station.[^1][^3]

Both pages list the tower as removed.[^1][^2] No source we read describes what remains on the peak today, or whether the public can visit. The survey datasheet's condition history ends in 1940.[^3]
