Piney Hill Lookout is a tower on the Navajo Indian Reservation in Apache County, Arizona.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register places it among the reservation's early towers and dates its construction to the early 1930s.[^1] It is an Aermotor MC-39 tower, 81 feet tall, with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab.[^1]

The register gives two heights for the mountaintop: 8,102 feet in its description and 8,113 feet in its elevation line.[^1] The Navajo Indian Reservation administers the tower, and the Bureau of Indian Affairs is listed as a cooperator.[^1]

The register entry is dated July 20, 2002, as US 439 and AZ 35. The register describes the tower as still active.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as standing.[^2] I did not find a later report on its condition, its staffing, or any fires.[^1][^2]

The register gives no visiting rule for the tower.[^1]
