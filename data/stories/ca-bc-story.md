Story Lookout was a square cabin on a ridge that runs north from Morice Mountain, about 14.5 km from Houston.[^2] Northword reads the name as an informal tribute to Marge Storey, who had a long career as a lookout observer across the North.[^3]

The cabin was built in 1988.[^1][^2] Northword says the Ministry of Forests chose to station an observer on a knob near Houston, mainly because of illegal burning, with wildfire watch as a secondary benefit.[^3] Interior News says the cabin was built to serve wildfire observers.[^1] The two accounts differ on the main purpose, so both are given here.[^1][^3]

Northword says the cabin was used as a lookout for about five years, and that a 1994 change in regulations under the new Forest Practices Code removed the need to watch for illegal burning.[^3] Interior News says satellites, aerial detection and better public reporting ended its role as a fire lookout, and that it gradually came to be used for recreation.[^1]

Northword says the cabin stood idle until the Houston Hikers refurbished the trail to it.[^3] The hikers society dates the first ribboning of the trail to the summer of 2010.[^2] Interior News says Recreation Sites and Trails BC maintains the trail with local volunteers.[^1]

The 2023 Peacock fire burned the lower kilometre of the trail.[^2] Interior News reports a brief closure, a reroute by the hikers society and a reopening in 2024.[^1] The society's own page dates the reroute of the lower section to late 2024, which leaves the exact reopening date unclear.[^2]

The shelter was reported destroyed in the week before 25 September 2026.[^1] The Houston Hikers Society said the structure had rotted and that wind had probably brought it down.[^1] The society had not recommended overnight stays in recent years because of the lookout's poor condition, and had been planning a rebuild; it is now looking at possible designs and seeking volunteers.[^1]

Today, the hikers society lists the trail at 4.4 km, with a round trip of about five hours.[^2] The trailhead moved about 800 m closer to town after the reroute.[^2] The society says the lookout has not yet been renovated, and that overnight stays are not recommended yet.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains of the cabin on the ridge now.
