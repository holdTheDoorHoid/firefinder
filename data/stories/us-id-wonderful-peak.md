Wonderful Peak is in Shoshone County, Idaho, on the St. Joe National Forest, about 4 miles south of Mullan.[^1] The tower carried an L-4 cab and went up in 1935.[^1][^2] A patrol point stood 1.5 miles west, atop Champion Point.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site in its St. Joe - Clearwater region.[^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts names Dan Zobel as the known staff member for 1934, citing a book called Hardship & Happy Times.[^2] That is a year before the 1935 build date both sites give, and neither explains the gap.[^1][^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1972.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks it as gone.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts classes the site as hike-in, so reaching it means walking in.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^3]
