The Forest Fire Lookout Association's New York list gives a Bear Mountain in St. Lawrence County as a platform tower, now gone.[^1] Protect the Adirondacks describes a Bear Mountain in the public Forest Preserve near Cranberry Lake.[^2] The list's coordinates have not been checked against that peak, so it is not confirmed that the list refers to it.

A hiker's route from the summit runs down the mountain's southern spine to a viewpoint over Cranberry Lake, about half a mile on.[^2] The summit itself is wooded and gives no view.[^2] A shorter route to the lake overlook, about a mile, starts from the Cranberry Lake campground, where a day-use fee applies.[^2]

A trail guide notes a trail sign that points to a "Bear Mountain Lookout" one mile away and calls that distance wrong. From that trail junction, it counts 1.1 miles to the summit and 1.6 miles to a rocky outcrop with views.[^3]

No source describes a tower standing on the mountain today. One guide mentions an open bedrock platform at the summit, which holds a USGS summit marker.[^2]
