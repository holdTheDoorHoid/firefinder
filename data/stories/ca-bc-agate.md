Agate Fire Lookout stands on Agate Mountain, not far from Princeton, British Columbia, with a view over the Similkameen valley.[^2] The firelookout.ca listing gives its location as Okanagan-Similkameen.[^1] A visitor's account calls it a former B.C. forestry station.[^2]

According to the account, the tower went into service in 1955 and operated every year through the six-month fire season.[^2] The account says it was eventually abandoned in the 1980s, because newer technology made staffing it impractical.[^2] The writer adds that the local community never took to it, and that it has deteriorated further each year.[^2]

The site has a wood shed and an outhouse, and the undated account says every building, the tower included, is near the end of its life.[^2] At present the tower has no windows or door, and the writer thinks it could topple before long.[^2] A trip report dated September 2021 still finds a fire lookout on the summit, and says the view from it is wide-ranging.[^3]

The accounts disagree about the road. The undated account says one route, starting on the Wolf Creek forest road from Highway 3, is maintained well enough for 2WD vehicles to get close to the top.[^2] Another says the shorter Wolf Creek route has since closed, and describes instead a route from Copper Mountain Road, 31.5 km (19.6 mi) one way, suited to a stock 4x4.[^4]
