The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Shelton tower in Washburn County, Wisconsin, as standing, at 45.73122 north, 91.82779 west.[^1] A second list, from the Forest Lookouts site, also places a Shelton tower in Washburn County and gives no other detail.[^2]

We found no build date, builder, fire history or owner for Shelton in the sources we could reach.

One report says Wisconsin's 72 fire towers were decommissioned in 2015.[^3] The other says the DNR planned to take those towers out of service in 2016.[^4] Neither names Shelton, so we cannot say whether it was one of them.[^3][^4]

The association still lists Shelton as standing.[^1] We found no recent photos, condition report or access rules for it. Before visiting, ask whoever manages the site, and do not assume it is open to climbers.
