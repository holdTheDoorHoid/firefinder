McCormick Ridge is in Boundary County, Idaho, about 11 miles northeast of Coolin, at 6,563 feet.[^1] It stood in the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1]

The lookout went up in 1933 as a 20-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2] A June 1962 newspaper item, quoted on Forest Lookouts, says the Civilian Conservation Corps built six lookouts in the 1930s, and McCormick Ridge was among them.[^3]

According to the same item, the towers had been left idle and unmaintained since 1950, when the Forest Service switched to aerial patrol.[^3]

The sources disagree on when the tower came down. Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both give 1961.[^1][^2] The 1962 item lists McCormick Ridge among the abandoned towers still being toppled and burned, with the work due to finish the next week.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it is open to visitors. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in location, and says the tower is destroyed.[^2]
