Cold Springs was a 125-foot crow's nest in the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest, about 35 miles northeast of the town of Wallowa.[^1][^2] The register and firelookout.com both date it to the 1920s, and the register names the U.S. Forest Service as administrator.[^1][^2]

The register includes a panorama photo of the lookout captioned with the date 8-17-1935, supplied by the National Archives Pacific Northwest Region.[^1]

According to the register, the 1988 Teepee Fire burned the crow's nest.[^1] The site list on firelookout.com names the same fire.[^2] A Forest Service map set for the Tepee Butte Recovery Project in the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest is dated 1988.[^3] It spells the name Tepee Butte, and the sources do not say whether the two names refer to the same fire.

The Forest Association's list records the type as a crowsnest with the status abandoned.[^4] No source I read describes what remains at the site now, or whether the public may visit it.
