McCormick's Creek Fire Tower stands in McCormick's Creek State Park in Owen County, Indiana.[^1] Civilian Conservation Corps Company #589 built the steel tower in 1935.[^1][^2] The park's history says Company 589 worked in the park, and that between November 1933 and July 1935 its men completed the firetower.[^3] The tower is 86 feet tall, with a 7-by-7-foot steel cab.[^1][^2] Indiana Landmarks calls it an uncommon 1930s fire tower design, marked by triangular landings and angled stairs.[^2]

It operated as a fire lookout through 1967.[^1][^2] By the 1970s, planes had become the chief means of spotting forest fires, and such towers were becoming scarce.[^2] The tower joined the National Historic Lookout Register on November 12, 2008.[^1]

Public access ended in the early 1980s, after the tower went unmaintained and fell into poor shape.[^4] Restoration followed. Indiana Landmarks recommended a $10,000 grant and reports a further $85,000 from donors, while Clio puts the total raised at $100,000.[^2][^4] Repairs began in July, according to a December 2018 Indiana Landmarks article.[^2]

The two sources disagree on how long the tower was closed. Indiana Landmarks expected it to open to the public for the first time in nearly 30 years, while Clio says it had been closed for nearly forty years before reopening in August 2020.[^2][^4]

Today visitors reach the tower along hiking trail #4 in the state park, where it is a popular stop.[^2] A June 2019 visit report on the National Historic Lookout Register describes it in excellent condition after a complete remodel.[^1] The Indiana Department of Natural Resources administers the tower.[^1] Check the park's current hours and tower status before you visit.
