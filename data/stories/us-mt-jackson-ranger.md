The Jackson Ranger Station Lookout, in Beaverhead County, Montana, was a platform set on top of a garage rather than a tower.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register gives 1930 as its build year, but its own description says the platform went up sometime in the 1930s, and firelookout.com dates it to the 1930s too.[^1][^2] The register added the lookout on April 14, 2022, under the numbers US 1566 and MT 112, and gives its elevation as 6,558 feet, or 1,999 meters.[^1] Its register entry is the most complete account we found.

Both sources say the platform is still there. It now stands on private property, so it is not a public site.[^1][^2] Visitors should stay off the land and ask the owner before going near it.

[^1]: Jackson Ranger Station Lookout, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/mt/jackson-ranger-station-lookout/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Jackson R.S. Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/jacksonrs.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
