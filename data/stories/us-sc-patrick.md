Patrick was a fire tower run by the South Carolina Forestry Commission in Chesterfield County.[^1] The commission's report for July 1944 to June 1945 says the tower was overhauled and painted in that year.[^1]

The commission says most of its tower sites covered about ten acres and included a house where the operator lived all year, along with the tower.[^2] Towers were the public's point of contact for reporting fires, and they linked to firefighters working in the field.[^2] The commission says many early towers were built of wooden beams, and that every wooden tower was eventually replaced with steel.[^2]

The record now marks Patrick as removed.[^1] The commission closed its fire tower system on October 1, 1993.[^2]

None of the sources checked describes the site today. It is not known whether any footings or other remains survive, or whether the land is open to visitors.
