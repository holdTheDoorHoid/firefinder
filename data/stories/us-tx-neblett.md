Neblett was a fire lookout in San Jacinto County, Texas, at about 30.43 N, 95.23 W.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list gives its structure type as unknown and its status as gone.[^1] A separate Texas tower map, on tnlandforms.us, places a tower entry named "neblett" at the same coordinates and lists a datasheet numbered BL2191.[^3]

The lookout's name closely resembles that of Nebletts Creek.[^1][^4] The creek rises a short distance south of Waverly and flows about eight miles southeast into Winters Bayou. The land it crosses ranges from level to rolling, and deep, sandy loam there supports hardwood forests.[^4]

The list does not describe what, if anything, remains at the site today or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1]
