The peak is listed at 5,585 feet in Shoshone County, on the Coeur d'Alene National Forest about 22 miles north of Wallace.[^1]

The first entry for the site, dated 1931, calls it a camp rather than a cab.[^1][^3] A lookout cab followed in 1934, built to the L-4 design.[^1][^3] That design was a standard 14-by-14-foot building that Region 1 drew up in 1929.[^4]

In early September 1936 the lookout was called in because rain and cool weather had lowered the danger of forest fires, according to a Spokane Chronicle item quoted on the Forest Lookouts site.[^3]

The cab was destroyed in 1959.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in one.[^2]

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the peak is open to visitors.
