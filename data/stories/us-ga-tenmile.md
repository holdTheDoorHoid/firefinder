Tenmile Lookout Tower is listed in Appling County, Georgia, at an elevation of 186 feet (57 m).[^1] A second entry on the same list, called "tenmile old," sits about 82 metres (roughly 270 feet) away by the listed coordinates, at 185 feet (56 m).[^1] For that second entry the list gives a datasheet reference, BS1563.[^1]

No source describes what stands at either spot today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
