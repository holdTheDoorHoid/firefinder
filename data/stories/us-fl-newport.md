Newport Lookout Tower was planned for the east bank of the St. Marks River, just across from the town of Newport in Wakulla County, Florida.[^1] The 28-acre site was a gift to the state from V.G. Phillips of Tallahassee.[^1]

A Tallahassee Democrat item dated January 17, 1937 said a 100-foot (30.5 m) steel lookout tower would be raised at Newport "some time Monday."[^1] The notice came from District Forest William F. Jacobs of the Florida forest service.[^1] The item said enrollees from Civilian Conservation Corps side camp P-68A had done the construction under Project Superintendent T.R. Demery.[^1]

Because the item is an advance notice, it does not confirm when the tower was finished.[^1]

No source we found says whether the tower still stands or what remains on the site today.
