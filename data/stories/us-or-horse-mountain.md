Horse Mountain is a 6,225-foot summit in Lane County, roughly 18 miles from McKenzie Bridge to the southeast, within the Willamette National Forest.[^1][^2][^3]

In July 1931 a newspaper item said burros would haul two lookout houses up to Cascade summits, and it named Horse Mountain as one of them.[^3] A September 1931 item said materials for the mountain's new lookout house had already arrived.[^3] The Weebly history records a 14-by-14-foot L-4 gable-roof house built in 1932, and the register gives the same year for an L-4 cab.[^3][^1]

In July 1948 the Register-Guard reported that a retired railroad man would staff the lookout with his wife.[^3]

The register and the Weebly history both date the cab's removal to 1965.[^1][^3] firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year, so the sources disagree on how it ended but agree on the date.[^2]

No source describes what remains on the summit now.
