The Radisson fire tower stood in Sawyer County, Wisconsin. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list places it at 45.79596 north, 91.22678 west and marks it as gone.[^1]

Wisconsin's fire-tower network peaked at 119 towers, mostly in forested, fire-prone parts of the state.[^2]

The tower shares its name with the village of Radisson, which lies in the Town of Radisson, Sawyer County.[^1][^3] Incorporated in 1952, the village takes its name from Pierre-Espirit Radisson.[^3] No source read says what, if anything, remains at the tower site today.[^1]
