Forest Lookouts lists Standing Stone as a steel fire lookout in Overton County, Tennessee, under the Tennessee Division of Forestry's District 4.[^1]

A 1930s guide to forestry work, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, dates the work to 1936-37. It describes one 100-foot steel lookout tower with an observer's house, built along with a telephone line and park roads.[^1] Tennessee Landforms lists the tower as standing.[^2]

Wikipedia's state park article captions a photo of the state forest as taken "looking northeast from the Goodpasture Mountain Firetower." It also gives Goodpasture Mountain a height of 1,493 feet, the same figure Tennessee Landforms gives for Standing Stone.[^3][^2] We could not confirm that these are one tower, so treat the two names with care.

A National Register form on the state's fire towers says the state forestry division's detection network ran from 1933 to 1975.[^4] The same form presents ten towers, and Standing Stone is not among them.[^4] We found no record of the tower being closed, moved or dismantled, and nothing recent about its condition.

We found no source that names who staffed the tower or which fires it watched. We also found nothing on public access or current use. Standing Stone State Forest is managed by the Division.[^3] None of the sources we read places the tower on that land, so ask the Division before going near the tower.
