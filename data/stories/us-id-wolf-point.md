Forest Lookouts describes Wolf Point as a patrol lookout in Idaho County, on the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1] The site sits about one and three-quarter miles west of Weasel Point Lookout, and the listing gives its location code as 32N-11E-33.[^1]

The Weasel Point entry, the reference point for Wolf Point, lists a 1933 camp.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Wolf Point site, or whether the public can reach it.

[^2]: Weasel Point, Forest Lookouts, https://idaholookouts.weebly.com/weasel-point.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
