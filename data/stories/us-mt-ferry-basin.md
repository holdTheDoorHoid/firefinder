Ferry Basin Lookout sits about six miles southwest of Moiese, in Sanders County, Montana, on the Flathead Indian Reservation.[^1][^2][^3] Some sources call it the Ferry Basin (Sonyok) lookout.[^2] The register gives an elevation of 5,641 feet, while firelookout.com and the tribes' own page give 5,650 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

The first lookout here was a 14-by-14-foot cabin, built about 1935.[^2][^3] In the 1970s it was replaced by the present 20-foot treated timber tower with a flat cab.[^3][^2] The register's build field gives 1970, which matches the newer tower rather than the first cabin.[^1]

The Confederated Salish and Kootenai Tribes' lookout page says the site "is staffed every summer."[^3] The register says the same.[^1] A 1979 newspaper item, quoted on a Montana lookout history site, describes a lookout at Ferry Basin with six years of experience.[^4] That site also notes that the lookout was threatened by the McDonald Fire in the 1994 fire season.[^4]

The register added Ferry Basin on April 12, 2022.[^1] The Confederated Salish and Kootenai Tribe administers it, so any visit depends on the tribe's arrangements.[^1] Check with the tribe before you go.
