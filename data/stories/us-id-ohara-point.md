O'Hara Point was a lookout on the Nez Perce National Forest, about 25 miles southeast of Kooskia in Idaho County.[^1][^2][^3][^5]

All four listings describe a 20-foot L-4 tower.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Three of them date it to 1935.[^1][^2][^4] The Nez Perce National Forest list gives 1934, plus or minus.[^3]

All four listings put the loss in or about 1955.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the tower as gone.[^5]

Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under "Drive-Up."[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or says whether the site is open to visitors.
