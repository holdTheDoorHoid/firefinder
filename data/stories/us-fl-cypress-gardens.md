Cypress Gardens' fire lookout was a 100-foot steel tower on State Road 540-A, just east of Cypress Gardens, the botanical garden and theme park near Winter Haven.[^1][^2]

A report in the Lakeland Ledger, dated May 1, 1964 and quoted on the Eastern Lookouts page, announced that the tower was complete.[^1] It was one of eight lookout towers in Polk County and part of the Florida Forest Service's fire-detection network.[^1] The tower sat in a station complex with a sub-office, a shop and two houses.[^1]

The report said a towerman, an assistant county ranger and their families would live on site once building was done.[^1] A roving ranger and his assistant would also work from the station, which was to have a pick-up tanker and a four-wheel drive tanker for fighting wildfires.[^1] The report also said the tower was already manned when it was finished.[^1]

The Eastern Lookouts page now lists the tower as removed.[^1] On September 23, 2009, the park's owner, Land South Holdings LLC, said the park was shutting down right away.[^2] As of 2011, the park's botanical garden section was kept inside LEGOLAND Florida.[^2]

No source we found describes what remains at the tower's spot on State Road 540-A today, or whether that spot is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
