The Gibraltar lookout stands northeast of Canal Flats, in the East Kootenay area of British Columbia.[^1][^3] The Columbia Valley Greenways Trail Alliance lists the hike to it at 8.43 km and calls the destination an old fire lookout station.[^1] The trail climbs steeply through thick forest, then crosses several avalanche slopes before it reaches a ridge south of the lookout.[^1] From the top, the view looks north over the Kootenay River Valley.[^1]

The listing says a high-clearance vehicle is not needed for the drive, but it warns that the final 8 km is narrow and steep and that drivers should watch for logging trucks.[^1] It also says the route includes one-way roads and needs careful navigation.[^1]

OpenStreetMap lists Recreation Sites and Trails BC as the operator.[^2]

Past a small col, the trail runs along the open slope beneath the lookout tower, so hikers pass below it on the way up.[^1] No source read here describes the cab's condition today, or whether visitors may go inside.
