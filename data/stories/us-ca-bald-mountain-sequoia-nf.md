Bald Mountain Lookout stands in Tulare County on the Sequoia National Forest, and the Forest Service administers it.[^1] An earlier lookout, first shown on a 1935 Inyo National Forest map at a different location, was abandoned in 1936 when a lookout went up at Sherman Peak.[^2] Use of the site resumed in 1951.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register gives 1954 as the build year, when the Sequoia National Forest replaced the earlier tower.[^1] The fire-lookouts.org history page dates the current structure to 1955.[^2] A Region 5 memorandum dated April 6, 1955, quoted on another lookout site, records the purchase of a steel tower and cab to be erected on the forest.[^3] The sources do not agree on the year.[^1][^2]

The register describes a 14-by-14-foot metal cab with a corrugated flat roof on a 40-foot Aermotor tower.[^1] Mules carried the materials in, and the register says the road did not reach the mountain for another decade.[^1] The fire-lookouts.org page dates the logging roads across the plateau to 1967 and says the lookout became accessible to the public that year.[^2]

In 2009 the Forest, citing an engineering assessment, judged the cab "not worth the liability or repair" and planned to remove it in spring 2010.[^2] A lightning-protection inspection, reported in the association's 2009 chapter reports, found the system operationally sound.[^4] Local volunteer groups backed restoring the lookout.[^2] Removal was delayed for lack of money, and by 2011 the lookout was off the threatened list.[^2] The volunteer reports for 2009 and 2010 list it as not staffed.[^2] A 2009 status list in the association's chapter reports, however, marks it as staffed five days with a Forest Service employee.[^4] A page last updated in 2013 says it was then staffed full time in fire season.[^2] The register says the lookout is still active and often passes fire traffic along for the BLM and the Inyo National Forest.[^1]

A Forest Service page, last updated September 4, 2025, calls it a manned lookout in the Domeland Wilderness.[^5] Summer visiting hours run Thursday through Monday, 9:30 am to 6 pm, and entry is free.[^5] To reach it, take the Bald Mountain Lookout road (22S77) from Sherman Pass Road.[^5] The site has no potable water.[^5] For details, call the Kern River Ranger District at 760-376-3781, the contact number the Forest Service lists.[^5]
