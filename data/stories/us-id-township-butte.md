Township Butte is a former fire lookout in Clearwater County, Idaho, about 26 miles northeast of Orofino.[^1] The Clearwater Timber Protective Association is the agency listed for it.[^3]

The site began with a pole platform in 1924.[^1][^3] By 1930 a pole tower 80 feet tall stood there, with a cab measuring 7 by 7 feet.[^1][^3] In 1934 the tower was 50 feet tall and carried an L-4 cab.[^1][^2][^3] A 1954 entry, which the Forest Lookouts page cites to the association's 48th annual report, records maintenance on the structure.[^3]

A list on the Idaho Fire Lookouts site names a Leon Green among the known staff, with no dates, and cites a book.[^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1972, according to all three sources.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts page also marks the site as removed.[^3]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page tags the site "Drive-Up Destroyed."[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground there is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
