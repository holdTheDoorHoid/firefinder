Getaway Point is a lookout site in Shoshone County, on the St. Joe National Forest.[^1][^4] The sources describe a pole tower with a tent on its south point, and they date it to 1939.[^1][^2][^3] The site sits about 20 miles west-southwest of the Red Ives Ranger Station, at 4,891 feet.[^1]

The Shoshone County listing gives the same two entries: a 1939 pole tower with a tent camp, and an abandonment in the 1940s.[^3] The sources do not give a single year for the abandonment; they say only that it happened in the 1940s.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the tower as gone.[^4] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page labels the site drive-up.[^2] None of the sources describes what, if anything, remains there now.
