Sabattus Mountain sits in Lovell, in Oxford County, Maine.[^1][^3] A Lewiston Sun Journal table dates its tower there to 1939.[^1] In a 2021 article, Bill Cobb, director of the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine chapter, said the Civilian Conservation Corps either helped build the tower or built it outright.[^2]

The same table says the tower was taken apart in 1962 and moved to Mount Hope, near Sanford.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register dates the Mount Hope tower to 1962 and says it replaced a wooden tower with a live-in cab on nearby Ridley Hill.[^4] Its entry does not mention Sabattus Mountain.[^4]

The 2021 article describes the Mount Hope tower as still standing and still used to spot fires.[^2] The Register says it is active and staffed each year by volunteers.[^4] The article says standing towers outside its open group are closed to the public, but it does not say which group Mount Hope is in, so whether the tower is open to visitors is not confirmed.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine list marks the Sabattus Mountain tower as gone.[^3] We found no source that describes what remains of the Sabattus Mountain site today, or whether the hill is open to visitors.
