Chester is a fire tower in Ontario, near Dividing Lake and Gogama.[^1][^2] The Canada Lookouts website lists Chester with those two place names.[^1] A list of Ontario fire towers gives the name as Chester Tower, at Dividing Lake, Gogama.[^2]

Ontario's Land Information layer for fire towers has a register entry for the Dividing Lake Ontario Ranger Camp site. It describes an old fire tower there, with a position accurate to within 100 metres.[^3] The entry says the tower is not used, and it gives neither a construction date nor a height.[^3] We found no source that says when Chester was built, who staffed it, or how it has been kept since.

The Forest Rangers list places Chester among the towers it calls still standing, though its entry carries no date.[^2] The same list says the Ontario Ministry of Natural Resources has decided, as of 2013, to remove many of these towers. It adds that the ministry no longer maintains them and asks visitors not to climb.[^2] It also says the ministry removed the bottom 6.1 m (20 ft) of ladder from these towers, because climbing one is hazardous.[^2]

No source we read describes what stands at the Dividing Lake site today, or whether the public can reach it.
