Mitten Mountain Lookout stands in Washington County, Maine. The register lists the site at 452 feet, or 138 meters.[^1]

The register dates the building to the late 1940s and says it came as a kit. The kit came in by train to Columbia Falls and was then trucked to the site.[^1] Forest warden David Grant, helped by Hun Ashe and Arnold Davis, put the structure up.[^1] The register names its first two lookouts, Harold Burgess and Ray Pineo, but gives no dates for their service.[^1]

Mitten was one of many lookouts in Maine's statewide system. That network grew past 100 towers, which were linked by telephone and manned by lone observers.[^2] The Maine Forest Service says the state absorbed privately funded lookouts by 1909.[^2]

Maine's end date is unclear in our sources. According to the register, Maine wound down its fire towers by the early 1970s.[^1] The Forest Service's history says airplane surveillance had led states to start closing their networks by the 1970s, and that Maine held out until a budget crisis in 1991.[^2] We have not settled which date fits, and the register does not say when Mitten itself stopped being staffed.[^1]

Today the register lists Washington County as the administrator, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^1][^3] It was registered on August 28, 2017, as US 1251, ME 12.[^4]

We found no public information about access. Washington County, which the register names as administrator, can say whether the site is open.[^1] Confirm that you may before you climb any tower.
