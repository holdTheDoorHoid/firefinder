This lookout stands above Frontenac Lake in Hubbard County, Minnesota. A Hubbard County tower booklet calls it the Schoolcraft tower and Fire Tower 45, and also the Frontenac Lake tower.[^1] Wikidata places it inside the Paul Bunyan State Forest.[^2]

According to the booklet, the Civilian Conservation Corps erected this Aermotor tower at this site in 1936.[^1] The booklet's 1960 survey notes describe a four-legged steel structure with a metal observation room on top. Its entry also gives an octagonal cab.[^1]

The booklet gives two heights. Its table column reads 81, while the 1960 note puts the overall height at 90 feet.[^1] The owner column reads "Mn MFS," and the part we read does not spell that abbreviation out.[^1]

The booklet's entry also dates the tower to the 1920s, and says it was at a different site before being moved here.[^1] A caption on a Wikimedia Commons photo of the tower gives 1937 for the CCC erection and says it probably came from somewhere else.[^3] A Minnesota lookout list gives 1936 for the erection.[^4] We have used 1936, since the booklet and that list agree.[^1][^4]

We found no names of anyone who staffed the tower and no account of a fire it spotted.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing, under the name Frontenac Lake (Schoolcraft).[^5] We found no recent photograph, inspection or access notice, so its condition today is unclear. Ask whoever manages the site before going near it, and do not climb it.
