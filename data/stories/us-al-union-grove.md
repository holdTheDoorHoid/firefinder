The Union Grove Lookout Tower is near the town of Jemison in Chilton County, Alabama.[^1]

The Alabama Forestry Commission built it in 1948, according to the National Historic Lookout Register, which describes a 100-foot MC-39 Aermotor tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab.[^1] The MC-39 was the most common steel lookout model in Alabama, and forestry crews kept putting up new towers during the war and for a few decades after.[^2]

Thomas Kaufmann, who wrote that essay as a preservationist, is also the person who nominated this tower to the Register.[^1][^2] The Register added the tower on June 23, 2009, under the numbers US 817 and AL 18.[^1]

We found no source that names anyone who staffed the tower or describes a fire it watched.[^1][^2]

The Register lists the Alabama Forestry Commission as the administrator, but it does not say whether the tower still stands or what shape it is in.[^1] We found no recent photo or report and no information about public access, so anyone curious about the site should check with the Alabama Forestry Commission before going near it.[^1]
