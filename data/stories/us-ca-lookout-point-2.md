Lookout Point is a site in Del Norte County that a forest lookout listing places in the Six Rivers National Forest.[^1]

A September 21, 1923 entry, quoted on that listing from volume three of the Chronological History of the Klamath National Forest, which covers 1921 to 1930, describes the guard's communications at the site.[^1] The entry calls for a regular phone line from the guard's camp to the lookout, a mile away, because the emergency wire was unreliable.[^1] The lookout had no shelter.[^1] Its iron telephone was out of order, so the guard had to walk a mile to reach camp and report a fire.[^1]

No source in this record describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Lookout Point - Forest Lookouts, California Lookouts (Forest Lookouts site), https://californialookouts.weebly.com/lookout-point.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
