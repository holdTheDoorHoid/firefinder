Sunnyhill, in Haywood County, Tennessee, sits at about 420 feet (128 m) of elevation.[^1][^4] The Division of Forestry's network of lookout towers ran from 1933 to 1975, and many of its towers were built in the 1930s with help from the Civilian Conservation Corps.[^3] The nomination classes such towers as practical structures erected in the countryside.[^3] The state's 1976 list gave this tower the number 9-6, in the district-and-tower format.[^1][^2]

The list says the coordinates came from an NGS data sheet.[^1] The same list's map plots current and former tower locations, so those coordinates may not mark a standing structure, and the tower is marked as removed.[^1][^2]

The same list reports that most former tower sites are now in private hands.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at this site or whether the public can reach it.
