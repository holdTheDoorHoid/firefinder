The Pierce tower stood in Giles County, Tennessee, at 1,080 feet (329 m) above sea level.[^1] The list numbers it 7-5, a state forest tower number from a 1976 list, which suggests it belonged to the Division of Forestry's network.[^1][^2]

A National Register form on the division's towers credits the agency with constructing 208 fire lookout towers, put up around 1930 to 1970.[^3] Taken together, the towers embodied the division's fire detection system from 1933 to 1975.[^3]

Tennessee Landforms marks the Pierce tower as removed.[^1] That National Register form also says many of the 208 towers are gone or slated for demolition, since they went unused or their leases with private owners lapsed.[^3]

The Landforms list also says most tower locations are now privately owned.[^2] No source describes what remains at the Pierce site today.
