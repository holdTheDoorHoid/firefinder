Davidson Lookout Tower is listed in Laurens County, Georgia. Tom Dunigan's list of Georgia lookout towers gives it an elevation of 355 feet (about 108 m) and places it at 32.34767, -82.92681.[^1] An Eastern US Lookouts list of Georgia towers also names a Davidson tower in Laurens County.[^2]

The map behind the Dunigan list was last revised in March 2016.[^1] No source I found describes the Davidson site today, including whether a tower still stands there or whether the public may visit.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, Tom Dunigan, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Georgia - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/georgia.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
