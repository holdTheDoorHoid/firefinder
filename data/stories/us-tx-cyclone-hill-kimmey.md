Cyclone Hill stood on a timbered ridge inside the Angelina National Forest in Angelina County.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association indexes it as Cyclone Hill (Kimmey).[^4] A federal survey description written in 1949 places the station about 16 miles west-northwest of Jasper.[^1]

The tower was a four-legged steel frame with a glass cab on top, and the survey puts its overall height at about 110 feet.[^1] A Forest Service photo record from 1950 describes the site as a tower, a weather station and a lookout residence.[^2]

A 1959 Forest Service photograph was taken from the tower, looking north across part of an area the record says was to be flooded by Sam Rayburn Reservoir.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas index marks the tower as gone.[^4] No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
