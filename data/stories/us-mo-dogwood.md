Dogwood was a lookout tower site in Douglas County.[^1] The Central US Lookouts record places it at 27N-17W-21, a township, range and section code, and names the Missouri Conservation Commission as its agency.[^1]

In October 1961 the Conservation Commission bought 40 acres for the Dogwood Tower site, at a cost of $1,000.[^1] The Missouri Conservation Commission was created in 1936; before that, wildfires often spread out of control across the Missouri Ozarks.[^2] A Missouri Department of Conservation history article says fire towers were, for decades, the top way to spot fires early.[^2] It also says that in 1966 the department operated 102 fire towers, while the U.S. Forest Service ran 43 in Missouri.[^2]

The record marks Dogwood as removed.[^1] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.
