The Okeetee lookout was a South Carolina Forestry Commission tower in Jasper County.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it as US 920, SC 6 and dates its registration to April 7, 2011.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site quotes a state forestry report for July 1941 to June 1942.[^2] It says the State Forest Service sponsored a tower-site beautification contest that year, scoring each towerman on the appearance and use of the grounds and fields, and that the Okeetee tower unit in Jasper County won.[^2]

The register says the tower has been removed, but it gives no date for the removal.[^1]

A Ridgeland lookout in Jasper County has its own register entry, US 921, and the sources we read do not say whether it and the Okeetee tower are the same structure.[^3]

In February 2026 Jasper County asked for bids to prepare, grade and rock a tower site at 1261 Okeetee Fire Tower Road in Hardeeville, for its emergency services department.[^4] The notice does not say whether a tower stands there now.[^4] No other source we read describes the site today.[^1][^2]
