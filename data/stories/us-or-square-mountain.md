Square Mountain lies in Wallowa County, north of Fingerboard Saddle, between Cow Creek and the Snake River.[^1][^3] The register says a 24-foot pole L-4 tower was built there in 1941.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the same height and year.[^2]

Only one lookout is named for the site, Duley Dale of Enterprise.[^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts history calls Dale the sole lookout ever to staff the tower, and says 1941 was the only year anyone was assigned there.[^3] Firelookout.com instead lists Dale for 1942 and 1943 and spells the name Duly, so the dates conflict.[^2]

The register says the tower was removed in 1952.[^1] Firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as gone.[^4]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
