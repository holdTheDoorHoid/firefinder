The Forest Lookouts site lists Echo Mountain in Powell County, Montana, under the Helena National Forest.[^1] It gives the survey location as 16N-10W-32.[^1] In 1939 the site was recorded as needing improvement.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page is the only source we read for this site, and it does not describe what remains there today.[^1]

[^1]: Echo Mountain, Forest Lookouts (Ron Kemnow), https://montanalookouts.weebly.com/echo-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
