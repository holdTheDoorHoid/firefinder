Jefferson Lookout Tower is listed in Jefferson County, Georgia, at 32.9510° N, 82.3976° W.[^1][^2] The Georgia tower list takes its names for entries 1 to 72 from the USGS name database, and this tower is entry 29, so its name is a USGS name.[^1] The list gives an elevation of 329 with no unit stated, and TopoZone gives an approximate 328 feet (100 m).[^1][^2]

The list ties this tower's tags to Georgia Forestry Commission records. It says the commission supplied coordinates for 118 towers under BLL numbers, and that an older commission list gives locations for 203 towers under gfc numbers. This tower carries one tag of each kind.[^1]

TopoZone places the tower on the USGS Louisville South topographic map.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
