Elk Mountain West, also called Elk West, was a lookout site in Clearwater County, Idaho, eight miles east of Headquarters.[^1][^4] The Clearwater Timber Protective Association, usually written CTPA, is the agency listed for it.[^1][^2][^3] The Clearwater County listing places it at township 38 North, range 7 East, section 7.[^3]

The site's history starts in 1914 with a crow's nest and a log cabin.[^1][^2] A 42-foot platform followed in 1915, and a 50-foot L-4 tower was built in 1934.[^1][^2] Two patrol points sat east of the site.[^1][^2]

Both sources list the lookout as gone.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list agrees.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site drive-up, with an elevation of 5,299 feet.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site today.
