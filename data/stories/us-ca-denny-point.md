Denny Point was a lookout site in Siskiyou County, California, within the Klamath National Forest.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a ground site rather than a tower.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts page quotes the Chronological History of the Klamath National Forest for 1958.[^1] That entry describes Denny Point as a temporary lookout set up to look down French Creek.[^1] Staff were posted there during lightning storms, but the site never won approval as a full-time lookout.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page gives its status as Removed, and the association marks it Gone.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors. The association gives its coordinates as 41.44987, -122.80720.[^2]
