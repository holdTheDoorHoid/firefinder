Round Top Mountain, in the Nez Perce National Forest, had an L-4 cab. The firelookout.com listing calls it a fire lookout cabin.[^1][^2] The site is 28 miles east of Kooskia, in Idaho County, at 6,807 feet.[^1]

The records give 1933 for the cab. Idaho Fire Lookouts marks that year as approximate.[^1][^2]

Both sites say the lookout was abandoned in 1940.[^1][^2] The firelookout.com listing marks it as gone.[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in, meaning visitors walk in rather than drive up.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.

A separate Round Top Lookout, on the Clearwater National Forest, is a different site. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it at 6,560 feet and marks it destroyed in 1972.[^3]
