The statewide list of North Carolina lookout towers places a tower named Scuppernong in Washington County, at 35.91638 north and 76.47055 west.[^1] A second list of the state's forest lookouts, quoting the state's 1940 biennial report, says a Civilian Conservation Corps side camp of nine enrollees, housed in house trailers, was set up near Scuppernong on August 25, 1939 to build a lookout tower.[^2]

The side camp closed on October 27, 1939, the same day the CCC put up a 120-foot (36.6 m) wood tower, treated with creosote, that had a catwalk and an interior stairway.[^2] The second list names the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources as the agency for the Washington County tower.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public.
