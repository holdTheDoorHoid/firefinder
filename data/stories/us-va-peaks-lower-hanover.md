The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list records a tower called Peaks (Lower Hanover) in Hanover County, at 37.72286 N, 77.36418 W, and marks it as gone.[^1] The name matches Peaks Turnout, a 147-acre Hanover County farm bought in 1914 for a girls' industrial school. That school opened in 1915 with 28 students.[^2]

In 1977 the school went all-male under the name Barrett Learning Center for Boys. It later operated as the Barrett Juvenile Correctional Center and closed in 2005.[^3]

A YouTube video is titled "Lookout tower on a 1915 abandoned detention center grounds."[^4] If that describes the same tower as the association's list, it conflicts with the gone entry. The title gives the year 1915 but names no county or site, so the link is unconfirmed.

No source we read says what stands on the land today, who owns it, or whether visitors may go there. Stay off the grounds unless the owner gives permission.
