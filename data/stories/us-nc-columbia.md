Columbia is a town and the county seat of Tyrrell County, North Carolina.[^4] The National Geodetic Survey's record calls the site the Columbia Forest Service Lot.[^2]

In 1954 the Coast and Geodetic Survey described the station as roughly four miles south-east of Columbia, east of State Highway 94.[^2] The same description gives a height of about 120 feet and a four-legged galvanized steel structure, capped by a lookout cage.[^2]

A North Carolina lookout listing dates a 120-foot tower in Tyrrell County to 1946, when the State Forestry Service erected it.[^1] The entry sits under the heading COLUMBUS and cites a state report from 1936 for the 1946 date, which makes that date doubtful.[^1] The Tyrrell County location and the 120-foot height suggest the heading is a misspelling of Columbia.[^1][^2]

The tower list gives the site as 35.86222, -76.21694 and names survey mark EY3217.[^3]

I could not confirm whether the tower still stands; no source I could read in full describes the site today.
