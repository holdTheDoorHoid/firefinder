The county list names the Minnesota Forest Service as owner of the Mirror Lake tower, dated 1933.[^1] It lists the tower at 100 feet, of stairway type, and names Aermotor and a square cab.[^1]

Survey notes from 1952 give a height of about 100 feet, describe the structure as steel, and mention a lookout cabin at the top.[^1]

A note on the county list, dated 2003, says the cab had been removed and the tower was in use as an ARMER site. ARMER is the Allied Radio Matrix for Emergency Response. The state dates its first build to 2004, a year after that note, so the note's date or wording needs checking.[^1][^3] The state also says ARMER now serves as the main communications tool for most state, county and local public safety entities in Minnesota.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Mirror Lake tower as gone.[^2] Its key marks towers that keep their support with the cab removed as Standing, and the 2003 county note describes a tower with its cab removed that was still in use.[^2][^1]

No source we read says whether the steel still stands at the Mirror Lake site today, or whether the public can reach it.
