Little Green Mountain, 19 miles north-northeast of Orofino in Clearwater County, Idaho, had a lookout built in 1933.[^1][^2] The tower was 74 feet tall and of the L-6 type, and the site sits at 4,741 feet.[^1][^2]

A log cabin is listed at the site in 1936.[^1][^2] The site is listed under the Clearwater Timber Protective Association (CTPA).[^1][^3] Forest Lookouts records repairs to the tower in 1954, citing a combined annual report for 1953-54.[^3]

The tower was abandoned in 1957.[^1][^2]

firelookout.com reports the 1936 log cabin as surviving in ruins, though the page gives no date for that observation.[^1] The sources do not say whether the tower itself still stands. Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site as drive-up, which suggests a road reaches it, but it gives no route.[^2] We found no source that says whether the site is open to the public.
