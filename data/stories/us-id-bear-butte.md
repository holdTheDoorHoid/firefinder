Bear Butte is a 5,393-foot peak in the Clearwater National Forest, about 24 miles northeast of Pierce.[^1][^4] Its lookout was a tower with an L-4 cab, built in 1934.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists Warren Yahr as the person who staffed the lookout from 1945 to 1948.[^2][^5] The page attributes that detail to his book, Smokechaser.[^2]

A Clearwater National Forest list compiled in June 2016 gives 1964 as the year the lookout was abandoned.[^4] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts also date the abandonment to 1964 and call the site gone.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts gives no date and says only that the lookout was removed.[^3]

Two platforms are also mentioned: a patrol platform 2.5 miles west atop Bugle, and a 20-foot platform about half a mile northeast.[^1][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout today.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as a hike-in.[^2]
