Lima Mountain, about 23 miles north of Grand Marais in Cook County, once carried a fire tower.[^1] The county tower report describes a ladder tower raised in 1934 or 1935.[^1] North Shore Visitor gives 1935 for its erection.[^2]

Enrollees from Civilian Conservation Corps Camp F-5 at Gunflint worked on the tower, a cabin and a warehouse.[^3] A caption on a historic tower page says the CCC cabin project was finished in May 1936.[^4] That same page describes a square cab with a ladder rather than stairs.[^4]

One tower history says the lookout was used from 1935 to 1956 and removed in 1976.[^4] The county report puts removal around 1976.[^1] North Shore Visitor says 1978.[^2]

Visitors reach the summit on foot. North Shore Visitor describes a grassy parking area about 3.4 miles up the Lima Mountain Grade, then a rocky, poorly maintained trail of about 1.2 miles to the top.[^2] Wikipedia gives the summit as 2,238 feet and the trail as one mile.[^5] Trees block the view, and a few artifacts remain.[^4] The summit sits in a 2,540-acre roadless area next to the Boundary Waters Canoe Area.[^5]
