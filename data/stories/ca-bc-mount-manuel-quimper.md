Mount Manuel Quimper rises straight up from sea level in Sea to Sea Regional Park, just minutes from the town of Sooke on Vancouver Island.[^1][^2][^3] The summit is 546 m (1,791 ft) above the sea.[^3] Visit Sooke says the T'Sou-ke First Nation has long called the mountain Big Mountain.[^3]

Trailpeak dates the summit tower to 1951 and counts it among the last of its type on Vancouver Island.[^1] The Capital Regional District says the former fire lookout was built by the BC Forest Service in the 1950s.[^2]

Visit Sooke says the 1950s structure was recently refurbished and offers commanding views.[^3] Trailpeak says the tower is open to the public and asks visitors to pack out their garbage.[^1]

The main access point is at the end of Harbourview Road, just off Sooke Road.[^3] Trailpeak says the park reserve is open from sunrise to sunset and the parking lot from 8 am to 6 pm.[^1] The CRD gives the hike or bike to the summit as 5.6 km, while Trailpeak lists the trail as 4.3 km one way.[^2][^1] Trailpeak says the foot path to the tower is very steep, and that the only aid needed is a good walking stick.[^1]
