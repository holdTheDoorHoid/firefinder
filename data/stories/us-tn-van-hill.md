Van Hill is a high point in Hawkins County, Tennessee, at about 2,204 feet.[^1][^2] By April 1931 a fire lookout tower stood on it.[^1] A Kingsport Times report dated April 5, 1931, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, said the East Tennessee division of the state fire patrol was preparing six miles of telephone line from a point three miles below Blair's Gap up to that tower.[^1] The work was aimed at cutting the number of fires hurting nearby forests, and the same report counted 135 fires in the district since January.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page files the tower under the Tennessee Division of Forestry, District 1.[^1] Both pages list the tower as removed.[^1][^2] Neither describes what, if anything, is left at the high point today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]
