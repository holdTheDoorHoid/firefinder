Two of the sources describe the Castleford site as a cabin rather than a tower.[^1][^3] It was on Bureau of Land Management land about a mile northeast of Castleford, in Twin Falls County.[^1][^3] The Kemnow list gives its location as township 10 South, range 14 East, section 30.[^3]

Sources date the building to the 1940s.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the same decade but calls the building a camp, so the sources disagree about what it was.[^2] The site's elevation is listed as 3,950 feet.[^2]

Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both describe the site as abandoned, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a ground site that is gone.[^1][^2][^4] The association places it at 42.53315, -114.85124.[^4]

No source describes what remains at the site today. Idaho Fire Lookouts files it as a hike-in site, and none of the pages I read says whether the public may visit.[^2]
