Chipley is a lookout tower in Washington County, Florida, according to the Forest Lookouts website, which lists it among its Florida towers.[^1] The site's page for the tower gives only the name and the county.[^1]

The tower is not on the National Historic Lookout Register.[^2] That register's Florida list names 31 registered lookouts, and its most recent Florida addition is Pine Top Lookout Tower, added on June 6, 2026.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists six Florida sites, and none of them is Chipley.[^3]

No source reviewed describes the tower or its site as it is now, so whether any structure remains, and whether the public can visit, is not known. Ask the landowner or the county before going to look.

[^2]: Florida, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/fl/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Florida, Former Fire Lookout Sites register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/fl/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
