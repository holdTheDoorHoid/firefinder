Canfield Butte sits about three miles north-northeast of Coeur d'Alene, Idaho.[^1] The lookout pages list it under the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1][^2] A crow's nest is recorded on the site in 1932, serving as a patrol point atop the west butte.[^1][^2][^3] Use of the site ended in 1947.[^1][^2][^4]

Today the ridge is a trail area.[^4] A hiker's account calls the site West Canfield Butte and describes a popular trail network that is easy to reach, with hikers, bikers, motorcycle riders and horses using it.[^4] From the Canfield Mountain Nature Trail West parking area, the same account gives 1.8 miles one way to the summit.[^4] Communication towers stand on the ridge, and the route leaves city land to enter what the account calls the cancourse LLC area.[^4]

We found no source that describes a lookout structure on the site today. We also found no agency statement about who may enter, so the hiker's account is the only description of visitors.
