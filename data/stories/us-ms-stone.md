OpenStreetMap maps a Stone Lookout Tower as a lattice tower tagged as an observation tower, at an elevation of 272 feet (83 m).[^1] The note on that entry's most recent edit calls the structure a communication mast, so the record may describe a radio mast rather than a lookout.[^1]

Neither of Mississippi's lookout registers lists a Stone lookout. The National Historic Lookout Register counts 65 lookouts in the state, and the former lookout sites register counts three.[^2][^3]

A 1949 USGS topographic map of the Wiggins area names a Maxie Fire Tower, and a description of that map says the tower looks out over the piney woods.[^4] No source we found says whether it is the same structure as the one in the current record.

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether a tower stands there, or whether the site is open to visitors.
