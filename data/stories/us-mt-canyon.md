Canyon Lookout was a tower in Flathead County, in the Flathead National Forest.[^1][^2] Ron Kemnow's listing also calls the site Canyon Mountain.[^2]

Rex Kamstra's entry gives 1940 for the tower and says it was abandoned in the 1940s, and it now lists the tower as gone.[^1] The entry is brief, and it does not say what the 1940 date marks.[^1]

Staffing is described only indirectly. A University of Montana oral history with Ivan O'Neil, a former Forest Service lookout at Pioneer Ridge in the same forest, names Canyon among nearby lookout stations.[^4] It says some of those stations were not manned, and the summary does not say whether Canyon was one of them.[^4]

The Montana lookout list also marks the tower as gone.[^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and we found no account of public access.
