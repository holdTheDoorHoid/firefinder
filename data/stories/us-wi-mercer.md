Our records place the Mercer tower in Iron County, at 46.16724 north and 90.07127 west, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association marks it as gone.[^1] The Central US Lookouts index gives its location by township, range and section in Iron County.[^2] OpenStreetMap lists the structure as an observation tower with an elevation of 494.[^3]

The Wisconsin Historical Society holds two 1936 photographs connected to the Mercer Ranger Station.[^4][^5] In July 1936, Melvin E. Diemer photographed the station from a watchtower, looking down on it.[^4] Eugene H. Sanborn's October 1936 photograph is captioned as showing a fire lookout at the Mercer ranger station.[^5] The caption does not say whether that structure is the tower in our record, and we found no source that ties the two together.

The state's architecture inventory gives the Mercer Ranger Station building a 1939 build year and records it as demolished in 2023.[^6] That build year falls after the 1936 photographs, so the record may describe a later building.[^6] The sources do not settle the question.

Statewide, Wisconsin resolved in 2016 to shut down its remaining 72 functioning fire lookout towers.[^7]

We found no source that describes the tower site today, so we cannot say what remains there or whether it is open to visitors. The 2023 demolition note concerns the ranger station building, not the tower.
