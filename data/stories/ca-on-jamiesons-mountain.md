Jamieson's Mountain Fire Tower was a steel fire tower with an octagonal cupola, listed near Brudenell, Lyndoch and Raglan in Ontario.[^1] The Firelookout.ca listing gives no year built, height or elevation.[^1]

Ontario's fire towers fell under the jurisdiction of the Department of Lands and Forests, which became the Ministry of Natural Resources in 1972.[^2] As the 1960s went on, aerial fire spotting, and then firefighting crews, took over the towers' work, and fewer towers were in use.[^3] Many were later taken down.[^3]

The cupola was taken to the Ministry of Natural Resources office in Pembroke, where it was restored in 2003.[^2] None of the sources I read says whether the steel tower itself still stands on the mountain, or whether the public may visit it, so do not plan a climb.
