The Corn Creek lookout stood in Phelps County, Missouri, and the Central US Lookouts index places it in the Mark Twain National Forest.[^1] In 1947 the U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey described it as a steel tower on four legs, with an enclosed cage at the top.[^2] The survey placed the station about 9 miles south of Newburg, 104 feet east of the centerline of County Route T. It put the base at 10.5 feet (3.2 m) square and the height at about 60 feet (18 m).[^2]

The federal survey record's history lists a single entry, a 1947 observation of the station.[^2] The lookout index marks the tower as removed.[^1] A photograph on the same page, credited to Bob Frakes, is dated April 2016.[^1]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, or whether the spot is open to visitors.
