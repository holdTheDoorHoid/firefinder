The Georgia lookout list places a tower at Hog Mountain in Lamar County, at 1,020 feet (311 m).[^1] The list says its names for towers numbered 73 and above are its own, and this one is number 244, so "hog mtn" is the list's label.[^1] The list also gives the tower the GFC number 424, and says those GFC numbers come from an older GFC list.[^1]

A MyTopo listing describes a summit called Hog Mountain in Lamar County, with coordinates that match the list's position.[^2] The summit appears on the Barnesville topographic quadrangle, and the nearest populated place, Barnesville, is approximately 1.2 miles to the SSW.[^2] MyTopo gives the summit's elevation as about 1,025 feet (312 m), a little above the list's figure.[^2]

No source describes the site today, so whether a tower or footing remains, and whether the summit is open to the public, is not known.[^1]

[^2]: Hog Mountain - Summit in Lamar, Georgia, MyTopo, https://geo.mytopo.com/feature/georgia/lamar/summit/332001/hog-mountain/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
