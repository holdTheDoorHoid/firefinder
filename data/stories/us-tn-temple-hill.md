Temple Hill, in Unicoi County, Tennessee, was the site of a fire tower shown on a 1935 map.[^1][^2]

The state index places the tower in the Cherokee National Forest, gives its elevation as 3,697 feet (1,127 m), and marks it removed.[^1] The Appalachian Trail site page gives 3,710 feet for the lookout site, so the two sources disagree by 13 feet.[^2]

The site page calls it a former lookout.[^2] The Appalachian Trail passes below the site, and reaching it means a 0.36-mile (0.58 km) detour with about 500 feet (152 m) of climbing.[^2]

According to the site page, the spot is now fully overgrown.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains there today, and no source says whether the ground is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

The state index also reports that numerous towers are gone, and that most former tower sites now sit on private land.[^1]

[^1]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, Tennessee Landforms (tnlandforms.us), https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Temple Hill Lookout Site, WillhiteWeb.com, https://willhiteweb.com/appalachian_trail/fire_lookouts/temple_hill/tower_021.htm (accessed 2026-10-21).
