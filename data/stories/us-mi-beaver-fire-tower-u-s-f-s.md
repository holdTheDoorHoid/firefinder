Beaver Fire Tower U.S.F.S. is listed as a fire lookout in Newaygo County, Michigan, in the Manistee National Forest.[^1] The Michigan Fire Towers directory gives its name with the letters U.S.F.S. attached and places it at about 43.66 degrees north and 85.96 degrees west.[^1] The listing records no National Geodetic Survey marker number for the site.[^1]

No source we read describes the site today, so we cannot say whether any part of the tower remains, or whether the ground is open to visitors.[^1]

[^1]: Beaver Fire Tower U.S.F.S., Michigan Fire Towers, https://michiganfiretower.com/fire-towers/beaver-fire-tower-u-s-f-s/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
