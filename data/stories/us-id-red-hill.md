The Red Hill site is listed under Blaine County and the Bureau of Land Management.[^1]

A 1950 survey description by the Coast and Geodetic Survey places the station at the summit of a small, sage-covered hill, at what the survey calls the Red Hill lookout house.[^1] It puts the station roughly 15 miles to the northeast of Rupert, and about 2.5 miles from Minidoka toward the east-northeast.[^1]

In June 1956 the Twin Falls Times News reported that Doyle Hale and Earl Burk, both of Minidoka, would be in charge at Minidoka's temporary lookout station.[^1] A radio-equipped temporary station was in town, and the Red Hill lookout stood nearby.[^1] Their plan was three hours of duty a day at the station, with the two men taking alternate days.[^1]

No source I found describes the site today, so I cannot say whether the house still stands or what is there now.
