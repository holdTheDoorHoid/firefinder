Bunker Tower stands on the summit of Cheaha Mountain, the highest point in Alabama, inside Cheaha State Park in Cleburne County.[^2] The National Historic Lookout Register lists it as the Bunker Fire Lookout Tower and says the Alabama Department of Conservation and Natural Resources administers it.[^1]

The Civilian Conservation Corps built it from local stone in the 1930s, and the sources give slightly different dates.[^1][^2] The Register lists 1934 as the build year, while its description says the CCC constructed it in 1934 to 1935 using a design from the National Park Service.[^1] Wikipedia says construction began in 1933 and that the tower was completed in 1934.[^2] A historical marker says the tower was dedicated to the park on April 4, 1936.[^3]

The Register's description places the tower atop the park lodge. Wikipedia instead says park offices and a gift shop once filled wings on either side of the tower, so the two accounts do not match on the layout.[^1][^2]

The marker gives the tower's height as about 50 feet, with walls two feet thick.[^3] The Register describes a cab about 10 feet square on a 40-foot stone shaft.[^1]

The marker says the Talladega National Forest occasionally uses the tower to watch for smoke.[^3] I did not find named lookouts or a list of fires in the sources I read.

Wikipedia notes that the tower was added to the Alabama Register of Landmarks and Heritage on December 15, 1989.[^2] The marker says the tower houses the park's museums.[^3]

An America's State Parks guide, updated in October 2026, calls the tower the park's most iconic structure and says it has survived 90 years of storms.[^5] The Encyclopedia of Alabama says people can take the stairway inside up to a viewing deck at the top.[^4] The marker says the park charges an entrance fee and that the tower is reached from Alabama Route 281.[^3] Check the park for current hours and any closures before you go.
