Bald Butte sits about 16 miles northwest of Helena, in Lewis and Clark County on the Helena National Forest, at an elevation of 7,060 feet.[^1][^2]

The only date in Rex's Fire Lookout Page entry is 1930, and it sits beside the words "history unknown."[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list gives the type as unknown and marks the site as gone.[^3]

Kemnow's page gives only the county, the forest and a land description, 11N-6W-10, with no further history.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the butte today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
