The tnlandforms.us list of North Carolina fire towers includes a Chowan tower in Chowan County, placed at 36.15111, -76.65472.[^1] The North Carolina page of Forest Lookouts credits the Chowan County tower to the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources.[^2]

Today no source I found describes the site, so it is not known whether anything remains there or whether the spot is open to visitors.

[^1]: North Carolina fire tower list, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: North Carolina - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/north-carolina.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
