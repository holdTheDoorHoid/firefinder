The survey station for a fire lookout lies about 4 miles west and 1 mile south of the town of Wilton, in Little River County, Arkansas.[^1] The National Geodetic Survey lists the station as "LOOKOUT TOWER 10+3 AFC."[^2]

Act 234 of 1931 set up the Arkansas Forestry Commission, which also built many towers itself.[^3] A 1941 survey description says the structure was built of wood and stood 115 feet (35.1 m) high. Its 8-by-8-foot crow's nest had a catwalk 2 feet wide around it.[^1]

The page is titled "(Wilton ?)" and its heading reads "Unit 10. Tower 3," so the name is still uncertain.[^1]

Statewide, the Encyclopedia of Arkansas says Arkansas had 122 known fire towers at the height of their use, and 48 were still standing in 1994.[^3]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the tower still stands.
