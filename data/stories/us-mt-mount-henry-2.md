Mount Henry, a lookout nine miles northwest of Seeley Lake in Missoula County, stands in the Lolo National Forest.[^1][^2] Montana also has a Mount Henry lookout in Lincoln County, so the two should not be confused.[^3]

In April 1913 the Daily Missoulian reported a plan for three permanent lookout stations, one of them on Mount Henry, which it called the most prominent peak in the Burcell range.[^2] A September 1921 item said a station had been in use there for a number of years.[^2] Firelookout.com dates a cabin on the summit to 1926.[^1] The dates do not line up, and neither source explains the gap.[^1][^2]

A round timber tower, 20 feet tall, carrying an L-4 cab, was put up in 1932.[^2] Firelookout.com describes it as a pole tower at the east end of a summit ridge about four miles long.[^1]

Firelookout.com says the tower was destroyed in 1952.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list now marks the lookout as gone.[^3]

None of the sources describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
