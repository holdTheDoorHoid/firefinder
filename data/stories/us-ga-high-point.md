High Point Lookout stands in Chattooga County, Georgia, inside the Chattahoochee National Forest. The Georgia Forestry Commission administers it, and the U.S. Forest Service is listed as a cooperator.[^1]

The build date is disputed.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register says the tower went up in the 1930s to keep watch over Taylors Ridge and the land around it.[^1] A Forest Lookouts page that quotes a history of the Armuchee Ranger District tells a different story: the state commission put up a steel tower there in 1956, 60 feet tall, with a six-by-six-foot metal cab.[^2] I could not settle which account is right, so the year should be treated as open.

The Register gives the site an elevation of 1,540 feet (469 meters). It accepted the lookout on August 28, 2017, as US 1239, GA 23, on a nomination by Justin Peel.[^1]

Neither source says who staffed the tower or which fires it watched. I also found no record of a closure, a move or a restoration.

Nothing I read says whether the public can reach the tower by road or climb it, or what shape it is in today. Check with the Georgia Forestry Commission or the Chattahoochee National Forest before you go, and stay away from any tower that is posted as closed.

[^1]: High Point Lookout, National Historic Lookout Register (nhlr.org), http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ga/high-point-lookout/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: High Point, Forest Lookouts (easternuslookouts.weebly.com), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/high-point.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
