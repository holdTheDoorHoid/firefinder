Scraggy is a peak in the Little Rockies, about 65 miles southeast of Havre in Phillips County, Montana.[^1][^4] firelookout.com lists an observation tower there from the 1940s and gives the elevation as 5,708 feet.[^1] A Wikipedia list of Phillips County peaks gives the peak, called Old Scraggy Peak, as 5,666 feet.[^5]

The lookout's agency is listed as a private co-op on firelookout.com, and the Forest Lookouts page names the cooperator as private.[^1][^3]

firelookout.com says the tower was abandoned, and the firelookout.org list marks it gone.[^1][^2]

A 2020 hiking account describes the views from the summit, including the old Ruby Gulch Mine below.[^4] No source describes what remains of the tower now. The same account says its author stayed on public land, while the lookout listings name a private cooperator, so who owns the summit today is unclear from these sources.[^4][^1][^3]
