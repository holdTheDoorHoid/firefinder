The Clearwater National Forest lookout sits at 7,370 feet, about eight miles southeast of Lolo Pass.[^1][^4] A camp went up on the ridge in 1919.[^2][^4] In 1922 the Forest Service built a log cabin with a cupola there.[^1][^3] The lookout that stands today went up in 1963. It is an R-6 flat cab on a 12-foot concrete base, and it replaced that cabin.[^1][^3][^4] The site was registered with the National Historic Lookout Register in 2009.[^1][^2]

Sources differ on the cab. The register and the lookout site both describe a flat cab, but the Forest Association's Idaho list gives a two-story cab.[^1][^2][^5]

The Idaho site lists known staff from 1977, with entries for 1990 to 1998 and 2022 to 2024.[^2] It says the lookout is staffed every fire season.[^2] The firelookout.com page says it is staffed during the summer.[^4] The sources I read record no fire or other incident at the lookout.

Today the lookout still stands, and the Idaho site lists it as a drive-up, staffed lookout.[^2][^5] I found no posted visiting rules or rental listing for it, so check with the Clearwater National Forest before you plan a trip.
