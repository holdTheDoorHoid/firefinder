Alberta's wildfire lookout dataset gives 2014 as the year the Rock Island Lake lookout was constructed, and it lists the site as a steel tower 30 m (98 ft) tall.[^1] Alberta Historic Places says steel entered boreal tower construction in the 1920s and gradually displaced wood.[^2]

The dataset lists the lookout as active, under the name Rock Island Lake.[^1] Alberta says each of its 127 wildfire lookout sites is staffed by a lookout observer, generally from April to October, and it describes the sites as isolated.[^3] Its wildfire staff, with Agriculture and Forestry, have adopted BearSmart recommendations for many of the sites.[^3]

A campground bearing the lake's name, Tanasiuk Rock Island Lake Campground, is open seasonally from May to October.[^4] No source we read ties that campground to the lookout, and none describes a visit to the lookout or says whether the public may go there.
