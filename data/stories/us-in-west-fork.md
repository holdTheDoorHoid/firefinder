The Forest Service's table of Hoosier National Forest towers records plans, approved on March 25, 1938, for a 100-foot steel tower at West Fork, with a dwelling, garage, latrine and cistern.[^2] The table places it under a Perry County heading, but Indiana Fire Towers places the site in Crawford County, just north of Perry County.[^1][^2]

According to a 2025 report, the Forest Service gave Loren Brown work at the West Fork lookout tower, which stood close to his farm.[^3] The report says its author interviewed him in 1985, just before he retired.[^3]

Indiana Fire Towers says the Forest Service pages call the tower torn down.[^1] The 2025 report says the Forest Service auctioned it when it no longer needed many of its towers, and that a buyer dismantled it and sold the parts.[^3] Indiana Fire Towers also mentions an online story by one of the lookout men, who paid $75 for the tower as scrap metal and took it down.[^1] The sources describe the removal in different ways.[^1][^3]

Indiana Fire Towers lists the tower as not standing and removed.[^1] The FFLA list also marks it gone.[^4] According to the 2025 report, Loren Brown said parts of the tower can still be seen around the area, including a flight of stairs on a neighbor's deck.[^3] Indiana Fire Towers describes no remains at the site itself.[^1]

The site is in the Tell City district of the Hoosier National Forest.[^1] A forest road, CR 61, approaches it from the northwest.[^1] Indiana Fire Towers calls that road one lane and not in good shape, with almost no place to turn around.[^1] Firetower Road, just north of I-64, ends in a gate.[^1] Indiana Fire Towers says it may once have led to the site but does not today.[^1]
