The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower at Lake Julia in Forest County, Wisconsin, at 45.81327 north and 89.02811 west, and marks it as gone.[^1]

Wisconsin once ran a large network of fire towers. At their peak, 119 were staffed around the state.[^2] The towers dated from the 1930s and 1940s, when they were a key defense for the state's forests.[^2]

The DNR announced in late 2015 that the fire towers would be eliminated. Replacement costs and modern technology were both factors.[^2] A state timeline gives 2016 as the year Wisconsin retired its lookout towers, while WJFW says 72 towers went out of service in 2015.[^3][^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Lake Julia site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
