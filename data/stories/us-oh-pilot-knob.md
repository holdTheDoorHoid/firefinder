The Pilot Knob Tower stood in Dean State Forest in Lawrence County, Ohio, at about 1,000 feet (305 meters).[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register gives it the registry number US 1582, OH 28 and names the Ohio Department of Natural Resources as the administering agency.[^1]

A Chillicothe Gazette note from October 27, 1934, reprinted on the Forest Lookouts site, says Frank Weisenberger would look after the tower.[^2] That same page files the site under Wayne National Forest, which does not match the Dean State Forest name used by the register and by the 1934 note.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Ohio list marks the tower as gone.[^3] An Abandoned Online post from May 2023 carries the title Pilot Knob Tower, but the text we could read is just that title, a date and an author line, so it says nothing about what stands there now.[^4]

No source we read describes what remains at the site now. Dean State Forest is a state forest with published visitor rules, under which motor vehicles are limited to certain roads.[^5] No source we read says whether the tower site itself is open to visitors.
