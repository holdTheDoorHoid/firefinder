The only source that names it is OpenStreetMap, where a point called Fire Tower sits at 33.4844541, -88.8160796 and is tagged as an observation tower.[^1] The comment on the point's latest edit reads "outlook_tower --> observation," which suggests an older tag was changed. It says nothing about who built a structure there or when.[^1]

The two register towers we found closest to the point are the Maben Lookout Tower and the Oktibbeha Lookout Tower.[^2][^3] Maben is a 100 foot Aermotor with a parallel staircase, administered by the Mississippi Forestry Commission, at 33.499815, -88.793285.[^2] By our reckoning from those coordinates, it lies about 2.7 km from the OpenStreetMap point. Oktibbeha is also a 100 foot Aermotor, this one with a diagonal staircase, at 33.421251, -88.890571, about 10 km to the southwest.[^3]

The Mississippi Secretary of State's public lands list, dated July 29, 2020, shows a "Maben Tower" under the Forestry Commission, along with an "Oktibbeha County Work Center." It names no tower called Fire Tower in Oktibbeha County.[^4] The Fire Tower point could be a second mapping of the Maben or Oktibbeha structure, or a different tower. We could not tell which.

We found no build date, staffing history or fire history for this point. We also could not confirm that a tower stands there today, since the map entry alone does not show it. Anyone curious should ask the Mississippi Forestry Commission before visiting, and nobody should climb a structure whose status is unknown.
