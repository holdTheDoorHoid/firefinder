Greens Peak sits in the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest, and Arizona Highways ranks it the loftiest of the rounded volcanic summits in one section of the White Mountains.[^1][^2] A fire lookout, an Aermotor MC24 tower, went up on the site in 1933.[^1][^3]

In 1962 that tower was replaced with a 54-foot metal tower topped by a 14-by-14-foot CL-100 live-in cab with a catwalk.[^1][^3] The register lists the tower as in active service and names the Springerville Ranger District as its cooperator.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register added Greens Peak on March 4, 2002, nominated by Dave Lorenz of the AZ/NM Forest Fire Lookout Association.[^1]

The sources disagree on elevation. The register gives the lookout site as 10,099 feet, Arizona Highways gives the summit as 10,134 feet, and PeakBagging lists 10,135 feet.[^1][^2][^3] I found no staffing history or notable fire record for this tower in the sources I could read.

Getting there depends on your vehicle. Forest Road 117 runs north from State Route 260 near Pole Knoll Recreation Area, and Forest Road 61 leads to FR 61C, which goes on to the lookout.[^2] Arizona Highways says the side trip is steep and rutted enough to need a high-clearance, four-wheel-drive vehicle, and that the lookout is about two miles on foot from FR 117 for those who would rather walk.[^2] I could not confirm whether the tower itself is open to visitors, so check with the Springerville Ranger District before you climb.
