Porcupine Ridge is in the Umatilla National Forest in Morrow County, Oregon, at 5,126 feet (1,562 m).[^1][^3]

Firelookout.com places it about 20 miles west of Ukiah.[^2] It gives the same county and an elevation of 5,126 feet, so the two sources agree on where it stands.[^1][^2]

The register and firelookout.com both date the tower to 1941 and describe it as a 60-foot pole observation tower.[^1][^2] The register also captions a panorama of the Porcupine Ridge Lookout dated July 22, 1935, six years before that build date.[^1][^3] The sources do not reconcile the two dates.

Both the register and firelookout.com now describe the tower as in ruins.[^1][^2]

The register lists the site under the U.S. Forest Service and includes a 2009 photo of the site.[^1] Neither the register nor firelookout.com says what the ruins consist of today or whether the public can reach the site.[^1][^2]
