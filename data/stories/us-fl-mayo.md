The Eastern US lookouts website lists a lookout named Mayo in Lafayette County, Florida.[^1] Map data calls it the Mayo Lookout Tower, places it at 30.0955 north and 83.2346 west, and gives the spot an elevation of 66 feet (20 metres).[^2] Nearby, the same map data puts Dell Hamlet three miles north of the spot and Mayo Village 4½ miles southeast.[^2]

The town of Mayo is the only municipality in Lafayette County and is its county seat.[^3] The 2020 census counted 1,055 people there.[^3]

The map data marks the spot as a tower, but it does not say whether a lookout still stands there.[^2] No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
