Wildhorse Point is the site of a former lookout tower on the Lolo National Forest, about 14 miles west-northwest of Missoula, in Missoula County, Montana.[^1][^2]

firelookout.com dates the tower to 1936 and describes it as a 30-foot pole tower of the L-4 type. It gives the site's elevation as 5,804 feet.[^1] The association's list places the site at 46.92695, -114.32064.[^2]

The page says the tower was destroyed in 1953.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list also marks the tower as gone.[^2]

Beyond that status, no source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Wildhorse Point Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/wildhorsepoint.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
