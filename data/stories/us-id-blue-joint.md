Blue Joint Lookout is listed as a lookout, but the earliest thing the sources describe there is a camp. The site lay in the Bitterroot National Forest, about 12 miles southeast of Magruder Ranger Station, in Idaho County, at an elevation of 8,597 feet.[^1][^2]

Forest Lookouts dates the camp to about 1930 and says it had an alidade and telephone connections.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the same camp with the looser date of the 1930s, while firelookout.com says only that its history is unknown.[^2][^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts and the firelookout.org directory both list the site as gone.[^2][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^2] A Bitterroot Star opinion column says the wider Blue Joint area borders wilderness and lacks roads, though that is a columnist's view rather than a record of the site.[^5] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
