The Wausau Lookout Tower stands in Wayne County, Mississippi, inside the De Soto National Forest.[^1] According to the register, Company 231, based at Camp F-4, built it in 1934.[^1] Wikipedia dates the forest's establishment to June 15, 1936, so the tower predates it.[^2]

The register describes a 100-foot Aermotor tower with a cab measuring 7 feet by 7 feet.[^1] Until 1989 it was the central lookout of the Chickasawhay Ranger District.[^1] The register says nothing about fires, staffing or repairs after that date.[^1]

Today the register places the tower within the historic Wausau Work Center and describes it as the district's only surviving lookout.[^1] Its register number is US 432, and the entry was registered on September 15, 2002.[^1] The register does not describe public access.[^1] Please do not climb the tower. Check with the Chickasawhay Ranger District, which the register lists as a cooperator, before any visit.
