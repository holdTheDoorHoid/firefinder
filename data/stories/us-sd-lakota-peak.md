The Lakota Peak lookout was built in 1955 as a joint project of the Pennington County commission and the state game, fish and parks department.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com dates it to 1954 and describes a 35-foot steel tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab, with a cabin at ground level for the lookout's living quarters.[^3] The register gives the county as Custer, while firelookout.com and the statewide list place the site in Pennington County.[^1][^3][^4]

A July 1955 item from the Rapid City Journal, quoted on a lookout history site, said the new tower had been staffed without a break during its first month in service.[^2] The first lookouts on the site were Mr. and Mrs. Art Scherer, and that same item said four weeks of hot, dry weather had kept them on duty day and night.[^1][^2] Later names on firelookout.com's staff list include Lawrence Mihills and Carl Gibson in 1961, and Jim Casselman and Victor Stanley in 1964.[^3] A phone line reached the site in 1965.[^1]

The register records the tower as removed in 2002, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's statewide list marks the site as gone.[^1][^4] The register added the site on January 4, 2026.[^1]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
