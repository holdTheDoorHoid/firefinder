The Central US Lookouts guide files the Pineview lookout under Barry County, Missouri.[^1] Today the name lives on in the Pine View Tower Trailhead, a Forest Service site in the Mark Twain National Forest.[^2]

The Forest Service calls the Pine View tower the northern access to the Piney Creek Wilderness, which Congress designated in 1980.[^2][^3] The trailhead has three primitive campsites with picnic tables and fire rings.[^2] Two paths of about 1.5 miles each run south from it to Piney Creek, 3.8 miles in all, and the Forest Service lists the site as open.[^2]

Ozarks Walkabout gives the approach as 5.5 miles west of Cape Fair on Highway 76, off Farm Road 2150.[^4]

The Forest Service's page for the site says the spot where the original fire tower stood can still be seen.[^2] None of the sources I could reach says whether a tower stands there now, or whether the public may climb one.
