Muskeg Mountain has a steel lookout tower in Alberta, 30 m (98 ft) tall.[^1] The province's wildfire lookout layer gives 2012 as the year the present tower was built.[^1]

Alberta Historic Places dates the arrival of steel in tower construction to the 1920s, and says it steadily took over from wood.[^3]

Alberta says each of its lookout sites has an observer on duty all day, usually from April to October.[^2] The layer still marks the tower as active.[^1]

The layer lists the access mode as fly, which suggests the site is reached by air rather than by road.[^1] It also does not mark the tower's location as verified.[^1] No public visiting arrangement turned up, and no source describes what the site looks like today. Treat it as a working fire-detection site, not a place to visit.
