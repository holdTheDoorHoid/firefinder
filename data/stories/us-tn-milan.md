Milan is a lookout site in Gibson County, Tennessee, listed at an elevation of 429 feet (131 m).[^1]

The Tennessee Division of Forestry was responsible for building 208 fire lookout towers between about 1930 and 1970, according to a 2014 National Register of Historic Places study of its towers.[^2] That study's table of towers still standing on March 26, 2014 includes Milan, in Gibson County.[^2]

Tennessee Landforms marks Milan as historical.[^1] Its list covers both current and former lookout sites.[^1] The two sources disagree on whether the tower still stands.[^1][^2] The landforms list was last revised in January 2026, so it is the newer source.[^1]

We found no source that describes the current condition of the Milan site, so we cannot say what, if anything, remains or whether the site is open to visitors. Tennessee Landforms notes that many towers are gone and that most lookout sites are now on private land.[^1] It also advises against climbing the towers and notes that steps have often been removed.[^1]

[^1]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, Tennessee Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Tennessee Division of Forestry Fire Lookout Towers, 1933 to 1975, National Park Service, https://npgallery.nps.gov/pdfhost/docs/NRHP/Text/64501237.pdf (accessed 2026-10-21).
