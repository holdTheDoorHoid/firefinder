The Firelookout.ca index lists a Thessalon Fire Tower on Tower Road, in Thessalon, Ontario.[^1] A photographer's pages describe a McCreight's Fire Tower in the Kirkwood Forest, about 10 km (6 miles) north of Thessalon and close to McCreight's Pond.[^2] A May 2002 photo is captioned "Macreights Dam Tower in Thessalon."[^3] No source we read says outright that these are one structure, and a second caption places the tower about 5 km (3 miles) north of Little Rapids instead.[^4]

The photographer's caption gives the height as 30.5 m (100 ft) and describes a steel tower.[^2] We found no source that gives its build date or builder.

The same photographer says Ontario's fire tower system had closed by 1973 and that most of the towers had been destroyed.[^2] A photograph taken on 2 September 2014 shows the steel tower still standing, with a shed at its base that the photographer calls badly vandalized. The cupola at the top was destroyed at some point after 2012.[^2]

A visitor's account on Firelookout.ca gives a different picture. The visit is undated. The writer found the tower gone, but could still see where its footing and bolts had been, and mentions an old cement staircase climbing to the lookout spot.[^1] The photographer's page carries the note "Demolished 2016?" with a question mark.[^2] The sources disagree, and none settles whether the steel survived past 2014.

Only the visitor's undated account reports the tower as gone. No source we read says whether the land is open to the public, so ask before going to look for the site.[^1]
