The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county table covers Wisconsin lookout structures and sites. It lists a tower called Wilderness in Lincoln County, marked gone, with no register number.[^1] Its listed latitude is 45.33472 and its longitude is -90.04071.[^1]

Wisconsin once ran a large network of fire lookout towers, mostly in the forest regions most prone to fire. At its height, 119 towers were in use.[^2] Many surviving towers were raised in the 1930s by Civilian Conservation Corps workers.[^2] In 2015, the 72 towers then in service were taken out of use.[^2]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the Wilderness site today, or whether the public can visit it.
