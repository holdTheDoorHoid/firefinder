Lightning Mountain stands at 6,116 feet in the Kaniksu National Forest, about 11 miles north-northeast of Clark Fork in Bonner County.[^1][^2] In 1923 the site had a crude pole tower and a log cabin.[^1][^2][^3]

All three listings say the lookout was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com also says the log cabin was still there, but it does not say when that was true. The Idaho listing gives only the word Abandoned.[^1][^2]

The Forest Lookouts listing attaches the name Kresek to both the 1923 build and the 1940s abandonment.[^3] Firelookout.com gives the same two dates in a shorter form.[^1]

The Idaho listing describes the site as a hike-in, so a visit would be on foot.[^2] Whether the cabin still stands is not confirmed by any source here.[^1][^2][^3]
