Kane Mountain stands on Green Lake's northwest flank, inside the Shaker Mountain Wild Forest, outside Caroga Lake in Fulton County, and a steel fire tower has stood on its summit since the mid-1920s.[^3][^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register says the site was established in 1925 but only started operating in 1926, while Wikipedia says the tower was erected in 1925.[^1][^2] The Explorer gives 1926 as the build year.[^3] The tower is 60 feet tall, an Aermotor LS-40 with a 7-by-7-foot steel cab, and it was staffed until 1988.[^1][^2][^3]

The Explorer's account names a 1932 fire watcher, James Luff, and describes visitors picnicking in the clearing by his cabin.[^3] That sits uneasily with Wikipedia's date of about 1960 for the observer's cabin.[^3][^2]

Once the state let the tower go, it deteriorated, and the watcher's cabin ended up as a surface for graffiti.[^3] In 2003 the Canada Lake Protective Association and the DEC restored the tower and gave the cabin a fresh coat of paint.[^3] The NHLR's January 2018 visit found the observer's cabin vandalized, open to the elements, and with a hole in the roof.[^1] In 2021 the New York chapter of the Forest Fire Lookout Association, which holds a volunteer agreement with the DEC, received a restoration grant for the cabin.[^4] The DEC provided funding and did most of the roof and porch work, and the cabin got a new roof and porch.[^4]

The station was added to the National Register of Historic Places on September 23, 2001.[^2] The 2018 NHLR visit called the tower in good overall condition, though its glass was not intact and the site was unstaffed.[^1]

Access has changed: a later Explorer hike reports that a landowner closed the property in 2018 and that the South Trail was no longer open.[^3] The author still reached the tower by the East Trail, and the NHLR called the tower easily accessible in 2018.[^3][^1] The sources do not say whether climbing is permitted, so check with the DEC before going up.[^1][^2][^3]
