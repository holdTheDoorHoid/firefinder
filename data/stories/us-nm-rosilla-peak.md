Rosilla Peak is in San Miguel County, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's New Mexico list shows its site as a ground site that is now gone.[^1] The list gives the site's coordinates as 35.73233, -105.62601.[^1] The Forest Lookouts website files the lookout under the Santa Fe National Forest.[^2]

A 1989 Forest Service survey of the region's lookouts includes a photograph of a Rosilla lookout house. Its caption describes a house of the L-4 or L-5 log type, photographed in 1937, and says the house probably was constructed in the early- to mid-1930s.[^3] That places a lookout house at Rosilla by 1937, the year of the photograph.

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
