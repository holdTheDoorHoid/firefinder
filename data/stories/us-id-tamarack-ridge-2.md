The Clearwater National Forest lookout on Tamarack Ridge went up in 1933. It was a 40-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, set on the north point of the ridge.[^1][^2][^3] Two of the sources also list three patrol points from the site.[^1][^2] The lookout's listed elevation is 4,697 feet.[^2][^3]

The Forest Service's list was compiled in 2016 partly from the firelookout.com site, so the two are not fully independent checks on the 1933 date.[^3][^1]

The tower was abandoned by 1940 and was in ruins by then. The Forest Service's list and the Idaho lookout site both give 1940 for that change.[^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the tower as gone.[^4]

Today the Idaho lookout site describes the spot as drive-up ruins.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
