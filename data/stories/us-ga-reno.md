A Georgia lookout tower list includes a tower called reno in Grady County. The list gives the elevation as 269 with no unit, which is read here as 269 feet (82 m).[^1] The name matches Reno, an unincorporated community in Grady County.[^2] Reno had a post office from 1911 until 1933.[^2] State lawmakers made Reno a town in 1913, and its charter was repealed in 1995.[^2]

No source describes the site today, including whether anything remains there or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^2]: Reno, Georgia, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reno,_Georgia (accessed 2026-10-21).
