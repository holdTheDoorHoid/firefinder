The Wawa Fire Tower is a steel tower, according to the firelookout.ca index. The index gives its address as Hawk Junction, Ontario, and its elevation as 452 m (1,483 ft).[^1]

The index says the tower appears on a 1963 map of the province's fire-detection towers, which suggests it was standing by then.[^1] The agency behind that map, the Department of Lands and Forests, became the Ministry of Natural Resources in 1972.[^2] Across the province, the towers were split among twenty-two fire districts, and a towerman's season ran from 1 May to 1 October, though weather could shift those dates.[^2]

The index also places the tower on a 1995 topographic sheet, White River map 42C.[^1] None of the sources found describes what stands at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.[^1]

[^2]: Ontario's Fire Towermen, Ontario's Fire Tower Lookouts, https://forestrangers.github.io/ontariofiretowers/towermen.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
