Pigeon Hill, in Essex County, once held a wooden fire tower 40 feet tall.[^1] The register files the site as Rockport Tower Site #2, under the registry numbers US 12 and MA 12.[^1] The register says the tower went into service in 1929 and that the municipality used it until 1941.[^1]

The register's other Rockport entry, on Pool Hill, describes a tower that went into service in 1931 and was used until 1941.[^2] The register gives the Pigeon Hill site an elevation of 208 feet (63 meters).[^1] Henry Isenberg nominated the site, and it was registered on September 3, 2008.[^1]

The register now lists the site among former lookouts.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains on Pigeon Hill today, or whether the hilltop is open to visitors.

[^2]: Rockport Tower Site #1, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ma/rockport-tower-site-1/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
