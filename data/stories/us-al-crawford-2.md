A 100-foot (30 m) steel tower once stood at Shades Crest and Farley roads in Bluff Park, in Jefferson County, and for most of the 20th century it was the landmark at that corner.[^2][^3] The tower was named for George Gordon Crawford, once president of the Tennessee Coal, Iron and Railroad Company.[^1]

The State Department of Conservation erected it as the fourth fire tower in the county.[^1] A May 1941 Anniston Star item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, called the new tower a lookout for the Shades Mountain area and reported that dedication ceremonies had been held.[^1] The same item said forest fires had damaged about half of Jefferson County's timber in 1938.[^1] The Hoover Sun counts 63 years of service before the tower came down in 1992, which would put its start around 1929.[^2] The two accounts do not agree on the date.[^1][^2]

The tower had a cab at the top, where lookouts spent long hours watching for smoke and passed the location of any fire to crews on the ground.[^2] A 1971 federal survey still described it as an Alabama state fire lookout tower about 100 feet high.[^1]

The tower was torn down in 1992, and its cab was taken off and kept on city property.[^2] A Hoover firefighter later found the cab on a lot at Fire Station No. 4.[^2] The Hoover Historical Society took up its restoration in May 2011 with the City of Hoover, and more than 20 Hoover Public Works employees did much of the reconditioning on their own time.[^2] The project's committee furnished the cab with items such as a kerosene heater, a circular map, a crank telephone and a wooden stool.[^2]

The restored cab was dedicated on Oct. 31 at its new spot on Patton Chapel Road, across from Hoover Fire Station No. 2.[^2][^3]

According to the register, the cab sits in a small park at 1591 Patton Chapel Road, and the City of Hoover administers the site.[^3] A historic marker stands there, and visitors can drive up and walk to the cab to see the furnished interior.[^2] The steel tower itself is gone.[^2] No source describes what remains at its original site at Shades Crest and Farley roads today.[^3]
