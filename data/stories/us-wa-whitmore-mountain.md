Whitmore Mountain rises on the Colville Indian Reservation in Okanogan County, about 13 miles west-northwest of Nespelem.[^2][^1] Civilian Conservation Corps crews put up the first lookout here in 1934 and 1935: an L-4 cab about a quarter mile northeast of the current steel lookout, and a three-legged, 60-foot wooden tower.[^1][^3] The register gives a reported cost of $1,046 for both structures.[^1] A June 1934 newspaper item describes a camp on the mountain with lumber hauled up and a trail being built.[^3] Firelookout.com and a TrailChick photo caption date the wooden tower to 1938, so the early dates are disputed.[^2][^4]

In 1943 a Colville paper noted that many teachers were working as fire lookouts that summer, and it named Georgia Brown on Whitmore Mountain.[^3]

By 1960 the wooden tower was in trouble.[^3] A November 1960 inspection report called it badly deteriorated and unsafe, and proposed a 50-foot steel tower with quarters on top, built 1,200 feet south of the old site.[^3] A 1962 letter discussed moving the new tower a quarter mile southwest, onto private land.[^3]

The present tower is a 53-foot all-steel live-in tower, built sometime in the 1960s.[^1][^2] The register says permission was granted by a private landowner for it.[^1] The L-4 cab still stands on site, though it is in very poor condition.[^1] The steel tower is staffed every summer.[^1][^4]

Lightning started the Whitmore fire on August 3, 2021, and it spread across much of the Whitmore area.[^1] Fire crews cut a hand line around the historic L-4 cab and wrapped it to protect it, and both lookouts came through.[^1][^4]

The register says the lookout is locked, but adds that you can climb the stairs to a viewing platform just below the cab.[^1] I found no source from the administrators on whether visitors may use it, so ask before you go up. TrailChick describes the site as driveable and gives directions that follow BIA Road 67 from Nespelem.[^4] The register lists the Colville Indian Reservation as administrator, and it was registered on April 13, 2022, as US 1565, WA 92.[^1] Ask the administrators before you visit, and do not go up the tower without their permission.
