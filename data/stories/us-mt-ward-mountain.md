Ward Mountain is about eight miles southwest of Hamilton, in Ravalli County, Montana, inside the Bitterroot National Forest.[^1] In March 1934 the Ravalli Republic reported that a tower and lookout had been tentatively approved for the mountain. In July 1934 the Missoulian reported that construction of a lookout had begun.[^2] Firelookout.com gives 1934 as the year the 10-foot pole L-4 tower was built.[^1]

Firelookout.com names lookouts for 11 seasons between 1956 and 1969, and records 1971 as not staffed.[^1] Newspaper items quoted on Forest Lookouts mention lookouts on the mountain between 1959 and 1969.[^2] A 1964 U.S. Geological Survey station description places a survey marker, set in concrete on bedrock, at the center of "the present lookout tower."[^2]

The end of the lookout is less clear. In June 1971 the Ravalli Republic reported that Ward Mountain, on the Darby district, had been closed by the Bitter Root forest.[^2] Firelookout.com instead gives 1973 as the year the tower was abandoned.[^1] The sources disagree on the end date, and we could not reconcile the two.[^1][^2]

Today the tower is gone.[^1] A SummitPost climbing page says the tower stood near the top for years, and that only pieces of it remain.[^3] To drive to the trailhead, the Forest Service says to go four miles south of Hamilton on Highway 93 and turn right onto Roaring Lion Road.[^4] Trail #208 starts there and climbs 6.3 miles to the peak.[^4] The Forest Service lists it as open to hikers, mountain bikers and motorcyclists, and gives about 4,800 feet of climb, while SummitPost says about 5,100 feet.[^3][^4]
