The Lecours Fire Tower is listed by Firelookout.ca in the Thunder Bay District of Ontario.[^1] A hobby list of Ontario towers still standing names a Lecours Tower at Marathon, Ont., which may be the same tower.[^2] Firelookout.ca gives the district and the list gives the town, but no source says how the two relate.[^1][^2]

The list, dated as of 2013, records the Ontario MNR's decision to remove many still-standing towers, and it still includes this one.[^2] Lecours has no star in the list, the mark for a cupola removal.[^2] A Firelookout.ca note describes a flyover in October 2025 and says the tower stands with no cupola.[^1] The two accounts differ on the cupola, and the October 2025 flyover is the later of the two.[^1][^2]

The note's writer was working on the White River in October 2025 and flew over the tower.[^1] The writer was unsure how to reach it on the ground and saw no significant trails nearby, though satellite imagery shows some old logging trails, now very overgrown.[^1] No source describes the site from the ground or says whether the public may visit. The list says the MNR no longer maintains these towers and asks people to refrain from climbing.[^2]
