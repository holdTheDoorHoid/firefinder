The Round Mountain tower in Cleburne County is also listed as Round Mountain (Heber Springs).[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as an Aermotor that appears to be an LS-40 model, and gives the site's elevation as 924 feet (282 m).[^2]

The register gives no build date, and I found no account of who built the tower or when.[^2] It names the Arkansas Forestry Commission only as a cooperator.[^2]

The register lists the tower as privately owned.[^2] According to the Forest Lookouts page, the site and its structures passed into private hands in the 1990s.[^1] The register records a site visit dated July 8, 2016, and the tower was registered in April 2023.[^2]

Because the land is private, I could not confirm whether the public can visit. The Forest Lookouts page also asks visitors to respect the rights of the private owners.[^1] Please keep off the property and do not climb the tower.

[^1]: Round Mountain (Heber Sprs), Forest Lookouts (Central US Lookouts), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/round-mountain1.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Round Mountain Lookout Tower (US 1728), National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ar/round-mountain-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
