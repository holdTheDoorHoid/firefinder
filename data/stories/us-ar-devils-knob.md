Devil's Knob is a fire lookout in Johnson County, on the Pleasant Hill Ranger District, part of the Ozark-St. Francis National Forests.[^1] A Forest Service post shows the tower in 1914 and again today, and a trail listing dates its construction to around 1914, making it one of roughly 121 towers built across Arkansas to prevent fires.[^1][^2]

In October 1938 the Baxter Bulletin reported that Mayford Huffines, who had kept the Matney Knob tower for two years, was moving his family to Devil's Knob tower near Clarksville to keep it.[^3]

According to the Forest Service, few of the state's towers survive, and the survivors no longer serve fire prevention.[^1]

The tower is reached by Forest Service road FSR 1432A, which a trail listing describes as a high-clearance route.[^4][^2] A user review on the same listing warns that vehicles must not go past an old gate on the way up.[^2] No source we read describes the tower's condition in detail, so check with the Forest Service before planning a visit.
