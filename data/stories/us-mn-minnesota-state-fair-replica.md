The Minnesota State Fair Lookout stands on the fairgrounds in Ramsey County, Minnesota. Its listed elevation is 918 feet, or 280 meters.[^1] The tower is 65 feet tall, about 19.8 meters.[^2] The National Historic Lookout Register lists it as US 1067 and MN 10.[^1]

The Minnesota Department of Natural Resources administers the tower.[^1]

We could not find a build date in the pages we read. A visit report dated January 30, 2004 says the tower is now completed, so it was finished by then.[^1] The same report answers no to whether the tower is staffed.[^1] We found no record of fires it watched.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association list calls it a replica, but none of the pages we read says what it copies.[^3] Without a named original, we cannot describe an original site or a move.

The Register registered the tower on January 3, 2015, on the nomination of Paul Munns.[^1]

According to the fair's own page, visitors can climb the tower, weather allowing, to look over the grounds.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list shows it as standing.[^3] We found no statement about access outside the fair, so check with the Minnesota State Fair or the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources before planning a visit.
