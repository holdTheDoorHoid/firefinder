The Pine Mills Fire Tower stands in Wood County, Texas, in the small community of Pine Mills.[^1][^2] The community sits about ten miles southeast of Quitman, where three farm roads meet.[^2]

The earliest date in the Handbook of Texas entry is 1850, when a sawmill community called Liberty Hill stood a short way southeast of the later town.[^2] By about 1905 the nearby timber was used up and the mills had left.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register gives the tower's design only as a belief: a 70-foot tower with a 7-by-7-foot Aermotor cab.[^1] The Register says the cab now holds a radio repeater for the "Texas A&M Fire Service."[^1]

The Register recorded the tower on January 7, 2014, as US 986, TX 3, and names the Texas A&M Forest Service as its administrator.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as standing.[^3]

The available records do not give a build date, a builder, or a list of lookouts who worked here, so the tower's early years are a gap.[^1][^2][^3]

No source says whether the public can visit or climb it.[^1] Before going, check with the Texas A&M Forest Service, and do not climb a tower you have no permission to enter.
