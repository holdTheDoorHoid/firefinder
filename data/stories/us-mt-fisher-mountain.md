Fisher Mountain is in Lincoln County, Montana, about 14 miles south-southeast of Libby.[^1] The page lists the Kootenai National Forest as its agency and gives an elevation of 5,453 feet.[^1] The Fire Lookout Association's Montana list places the site at 48.22757 north and 115.35026 west.[^2]

A cabin and a pole observation tower went up on the mountain in 1928.[^1] The lookout was abandoned in 1942, according to the same page.[^1]

The Fire Lookout Association's list gives its type as a tower and marks it gone.[^2] Rex's Fire Lookout Page, which files the mountain under Lincoln County, gives the land location as 29N-29W-31 and adds no history.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
