Moxie Bald Mountain reaches about 2,630 feet in Bald Mountain Township, Somerset County, Maine.[^1] On the Appalachian Trail, it is the highest spot between the Kennebec River and Monson, and its open granite ledges give views in every direction.[^1]

A wood tower went up on the summit in 1910.[^2][^3] A 12-foot steel tower replaced it in 1919.[^3][^2]

A watchman worked the lookout.[^2] A 1917 report by the chief warden for the Upper Kennebec region describes a new night camp built on the mountain for the watchman.[^2]

The cab was replaced in 1961.[^4]

The steel tower came down in the 1990s, though sources differ on the year.[^5] Central Maine says 1994, and Willhite's lookout history says 1996.[^1][^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's record says the tower was removed by helicopter and set down at the Bolton Hill headquarters east of Waterville, and that it may still be there behind the garage.[^5] Only that record places the steel there.[^5]

The summit today is marked by a small trail sign and the metal bases of the 1919 tower.[^1]
