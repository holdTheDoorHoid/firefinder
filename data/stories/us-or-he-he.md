He He Mountain sits in the Willamette National Forest, in Lane County, about 18 miles north-northeast of Oakridge.[^2] The first lookout was a 10-foot pole tower with a shake cabin, dated to 1919 by the registry and by firelookout.com.[^1][^2] In August 1932 the Eugene Guard, as quoted on a lookout history page, reported that new lookout houses would be built at He-He Mountain and at several other sites.[^3]

In 1934 the pole tower was replaced with a 22-foot round timber tower with an L-4 cab.[^1][^3] In 1958 a treated timber tower with an R-6 flat-roof cab took its place.[^1][^2][^3]

The registry and the Rex's page record its removal in 1968.[^1][^3] firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year instead, so the sources disagree on how it ended.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list also marks the site as gone.[^4] The site was added to the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register on May 7, 2011.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the site now, or whether the public can reach it.
