Rocky Ridge is in the Clearwater National Forest, about 14 miles east-southeast of Pierce in Idaho County, at 6,540 feet.[^1][^2]

The lookout began as a log cabin. Two sources date that cabin to 1917, while a 2016 Forest Service list gives 1914.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Service list records the type as a log cabin, not a tower.[^3] A cupola cabin was added in 1924.[^1][^2][^3]

The sources date the end of the lookout to the 1940s.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives only the decade, not a year.[^2] The firelookout.com page sums up the run as 1924 to the 1940s, a start date later than the 1917 cabin date it gives elsewhere on the same page.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a ground site that is gone.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as drive-up.[^2] No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.[^1]
