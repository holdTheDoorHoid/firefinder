A Georgia lookout list on tnlandforms.us places a tower called Leary in Calhoun County, at about 31.52 N, 84.51 W.[^1] The list gives its elevation as 221 with no unit stated, and attaches one code to it, gfc-202.[^1] The list explains that gfc-nnn codes refer to an older Georgia Forestry Commission list of tower locations.[^1] The list marks some entries "(historical)", and the Leary entry carries no such mark.[^1]

The list says its names for towers numbered 73 and above are its own creation. This entry is number 79, so "Leary" may not be a historical name.[^1] The list's Google map was revised on March 22, 2016.[^1]

The list says nothing about what remains at the Leary site today.[^1] Before going, check who owns the land and whether the public may enter, and do not climb any tower you cannot confirm is open.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
