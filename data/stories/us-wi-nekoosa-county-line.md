The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower called Nekoosa (County Line), on the Adams and Wood county line, and marks it as gone.[^1] Mapcarta gives a Nekoosa Lookout Tower in the Town of Rome, Adams County, Wisconsin, and OpenStreetMap carries the same name.[^2][^3]

A report dated November 30, 1970, from The Daily Tribune, quoted on a hobbyist site about lookouts in the central United States, describes a 60-foot fire lookout on a hill near the Wood-Adams county line that had apparently been blown down.[^4] The tower was owned by the Nekoosa-Edwards Paper Co. In the years before the report, it had mostly served as a backup tower and was not staffed regularly.[^4] Police were checking whether someone may have removed bolts that supported the tower at its base.[^4]

It is not clear that this report describes the Nekoosa (County Line) record. The Association also lists a second Wood County entry, Nursery (Nepco Lake), as gone.[^1]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.[^1]
