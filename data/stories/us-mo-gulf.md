The Gulf lookout tower was finished in the Roubidoux District by late January 1937.[^1] A Houston Herald item from January 1937, quoted on the Central US Lookouts page, says a crew from the CCC camp at Lynchburg had just finished it under Forest Ranger Paul R. Larsson and Foreman B. Lax.[^1] A KRCU history of Southeast Missouri's towers says the Civilian Conservation Corps supplied low-cost labor for many of them.[^3]

The paper calls it the third all-steel, 100-foot tower built in that district.[^1] That is about 30.5 m. Except for its stairs and platforms, the frame was all steel, and it sat on a concrete foundation.[^1] The stairs and platforms climb through the framework to the cabin floor.[^1] The paper says small fires that were hard to spot earlier are now easier to catch and put out before they do serious damage.[^1]

The tower stood about half a mile north of the Gulf Line Pump Station, which lies east of Nebo on the Nebo-Evening Shade road.[^1]

A 1946 National Geodetic Survey station description also refers to it.[^1] The survey point lies 62.10 feet north of a Forest Service lookout, and the description names that tower the Gulf lookout.[^1] The same description places the station about nine miles southeast of the village of Falcon.[^1] Because the survey measured from that tower in 1946, a tower known as the Gulf lookout was standing then.[^1] No source we found says outright that it is the 1937 structure.

The Central US Lookouts page marks the tower removed, with no date.[^1] The 1937 item calls the forest the Gardner National Forest.[^1] Wikipedia says the forest was once known as the Clark National Forest and the Mark Twain National Forest, both proclaimed in 1939, and that the forest in its present form dates from 1976.[^2] We found no source that links the Gardner name to any of those.

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors. The 1946 description places the site on Mark Twain National Forest property, so ask the forest before you go.[^1]
