The Blueberry Hill lookout is in Itasca County.[^3] A compiled list of Minnesota towers describes two towers on the same spot, with the second replacing the first.[^1]

The first tower was dismantled in 1937.[^1] A survey note covering 1934 to 1947 describes it.[^1]

In 1939 a steel ladder tower about 81 feet tall went up in its place.[^1] The list calls it the relocated first Remer tower.[^1]

A Cass County list is less certain about that move. It says the first Remer lookout was probably relocated to the Blueberry site when the second Remer tower went up.[^2] That second Remer tower was a 100-foot tower erected in 1938.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Blueberry Hill tower as gone.[^3]

No source describes the site today, and none says whether it is open to the public.
