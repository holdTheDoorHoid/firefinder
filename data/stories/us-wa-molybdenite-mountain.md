Molybdenite Mountain is a high peak near Ione, in Pend Oreille County, Washington.[^2][^3] The summit lies inside the Colville National Forest.[^4] A lookout once stood on top, and its L-4 cab was built in 1930.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

The Register lists the site at 6,784 feet, while Willhite gives 6,790 feet.[^1][^3]

The sources disagree on how the cab ended.[^1][^2][^3] The Register says it was removed in 1958.[^1] Firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^2] Willhite says the cab was burned in 1958, and his page includes a photo of the cab burning.[^3]

Willhite visited in 2026 and could see no footings on the summit.[^3] He thinks the cab sat on a wooden foundation, which would account for the missing footings.[^3] The firefinder pedestal, a base used for locating fires, is still there.[^3] SummitPost reports that only a handful of beams and a corroded Osborne Firefinder platform are left.[^4] Willhite also notes that a large share of the phone line still lies on the ground along the north ridge.[^3]

Willhite's route heads over the Ione Bridge, spanning the Pend Oreille River, then runs 4.4 miles along Sullivan Lake Road to Dry Canyon Road and onto FR1936.[^3] Measured from Sullivan Lake Road, the trailhead is 7.3 miles along, at a gate on the left.[^3] A SummitPost writer suspected that the lower slopes to the west and south are likely private timber land.[^4] Check current access before you go.
