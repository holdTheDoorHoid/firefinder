Balloon Tree was a fire lookout in Union County, Oregon, in the Umatilla National Forest.[^1][^2] The site is at 5,019 feet (1,530 meters).[^1] Firelookout.com places it about 11 miles north of Elgin.[^2]

The register says the tower was built in the 1930s.[^1] A panorama of the lookout, dated August 2, 1935, is credited to the National Archives Pacific Northwest Region.[^1][^3] The 1935 panorama record lists the tower and camera heights as 65 and 4 feet, while Firelookout.com gives the tower as 70 feet, so the two sources disagree.[^3][^2]

The register and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list both mark the tower as gone.[^1][^4]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
