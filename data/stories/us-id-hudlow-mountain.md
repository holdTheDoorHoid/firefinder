Hudlow Mountain lies about a mile northeast of Hayden Lake in Kootenai County, on Coeur d'Alene National Forest land, at 3,395 feet.[^1] The Forest Lookouts listing gives its location only as a township-range-section code, 52N-3W-35.[^3]

The lookout is recorded with an L-4 cab in 1931.[^1][^2][^3]

The only known staff listed are Mr. and Mrs. William Bush, in 1946.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts ties that entry to a Kellogg Evening News item.[^2]

Both history pages mark the tower gone by 1952.[^1][^2]

The Inland Northwest Land Conservancy says Hudlow Mountain rises over the east side of Hayden Lake and that its summit still holds what is left of a Forest Service fire lookout.[^4] The sources do not describe those remains in more detail, and I found nothing on whether the summit is open to visitors.
