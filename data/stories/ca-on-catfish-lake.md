The Catfish Lake Fire Tower stands about 10 km north of Wawa, Ontario, and about 2 km west of Catfish Lake.[^1] A list of Ontario towers still standing, kept on a hobby website,[^3] gives what appears to be the same site under the name Menzies/Bailloquet Tower.[^2]

As of 2013, the Ontario MNR had decided to remove many towers that were still standing, and the list says the ministry no longer looks after them.[^2] For these towers, the list also says the MNR has taken off the bottom section of ladder, about 6 m (20 ft), and asks visitors not to climb.[^2]

A Firelookout.ca directory note reports that the tower still stands, with no cupola.[^1] The list gives the tower no star, the symbol it uses for a cupola removal, so the two sources differ on that point.[^2] The list is dated as of 2013 and the directory note is undated, so the two may describe different times.[^1][^2] The note's writer did not reach the tower before dark and was not sure of its condition.[^1] No source describes a route to the tower or says whether the land is open to the public.
