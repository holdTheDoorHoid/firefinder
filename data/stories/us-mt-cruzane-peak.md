Cruzane Peak, also called Cruzane Mountain, is in the Lolo National Forest in Mineral County, Montana.[^1][^2] The firelookout.com page spells the name Cruzana.[^3] The Forest Lookouts page gives its legal location as 19N-31W-12.[^1]

In June 1938 the Sanders County Independent-Ledger reported that work had started on the Cruzane Lookout near Saltese, on the St. Regis district.[^1] The item described a tower fifty feet high, crowned by a standard lookout that measured 14 by 14.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page lists the tower as removed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list marks the tower as gone.[^2]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
