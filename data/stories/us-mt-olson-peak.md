Olson Peak in Mineral County, Montana, held a 30-foot pole tower of the L-4 design on the Lolo National Forest, about 7 miles north-northeast of St. Regis, at an elevation of 5,274 feet.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page places it at township 19 north, range 27 west, section 20.[^2]

Firelookout.com dates the tower to 1934 and says it had fallen into ruins by 1992.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks the tower as gone and gives its coordinates as 47.38882 north and 115.05933 west.[^3] The same list has a different Olson Peak in Lewis & Clark County, also marked gone, so check the county before relying on a name alone.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
