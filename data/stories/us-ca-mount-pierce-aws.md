Mount Pierce, sometimes called Pierce Mountain or Monument Peak, is the top of Monument Ridge in Humboldt County's Coast Range.[^2] The Humboldt Meridian, a survey reference line, was established in 1853, and its starting point is on the summit.[^2][^3]

The Fire Lookout Association's California list names a Mount Pierce AWS site in Humboldt County and marks it gone.[^1] Antennas, a geodetic marker and a plaque now occupy the summit.[^2] The iron-bar tower that once marked the summit is gone.[^2] No source we found ties the AWS entry to the antennas or the tower.[^1][^2]

Access is disputed.[^3][^4] The Center for Land Use Interpretation says the summit is Forest Service ground, closed to the public, with a local telemetry site on it.[^3] A hiker's trip report, citing contact with the owner in September 2020, says a logging company owns the land and asks visitors to request permission first.[^4] The same report found the road open for the first 4.5 miles, then gated for the last 3.5 miles to the summit.[^4] Treat the summit as off limits unless the landowner or land manager gives permission.
