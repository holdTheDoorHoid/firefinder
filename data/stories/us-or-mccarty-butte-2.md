McCarty Butte was a fire lookout in the Fremont National Forest, in Lake County, Oregon. It stood about 30 miles east-northeast of Chemult.[^1]

The lookout was built around 1939, as a 12 by 12 foot (3.7 m) non-standard ground cab.[^2][^1] Early descriptions give a wood house on a rock foundation and a 4 by 4 outhouse, with no other building on the site. The estimated cost was $1,500.[^3] It was fitted with an Osborne #6 firefinder, and its water came from Smoke Creek Spring.[^3] The register gives the site's elevation as 5,690 feet (1,734 m).[^2]

In June 1943, The Bulletin reported that Jake Kooyman had been named to staff the McCarty Butte lookout.[^3] The lookout house was painted in 1948, and in 1956 the station was given the air marking number F-4.[^3]

The register says the cab was gone by the 1960s, and firelookout.com says it was gone in the 1960s.[^2][^1] The Weebly history marks it as removed, without giving a date.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the site today. The register places it in the Fremont National Forest, but no source gives a route to it or describes its condition.[^2]
