Grandview Lookout is a 100-foot steel tower in Cullman County, Alabama, administered by the Alabama Forestry Commission.[^1] A 1977 Cullman newspaper item gives its construction date as 1951, while the Register page we read gives no build year.[^2][^1]

A December 1954 item from the Cullman Democrat said the tower was one of two in the county and could see over 400 square miles on a clear day. It also carried a two-way radio, plus maps and tools for pinpointing where a fire was burning.[^2]

A July 1977 item from a Cullman paper reported that a tower at Grandview was manned while one at Bugtussle sat idle.[^2] We found no later staffing record.

The Register notes that the tower is missing the lower section of its stairs, and that it once housed communications equipment but no longer does.[^1] We found no source on public access, so do not assume the tower is open to visitors or safe to climb. Because the Alabama Forestry Commission administers the tower, ask that agency before any visit.[^1]

The Register accepted the tower on May 27, 2016, as US 1133, AL 66.[^1]
