Brewton, a community in Laurens County, Georgia, is not incorporated as a city or town, and its name is sometimes written Bruton.[^2]

A list of Georgia lookout towers, whose page records a Google map revision dated 22 March 2016, names a tower called Brewton in Laurens County and gives its coordinates as 32.5875 and -82.77528.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, and the only source for this tower is that list of names and positions.[^1]

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Brewton, Georgia, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brewton,_Georgia (accessed 2026-10-21).
