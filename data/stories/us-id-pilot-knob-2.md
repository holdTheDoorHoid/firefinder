Pilot Knob Lookout stands in the Nez Perce National Forest in Idaho County, at 7,135 feet, about 14 miles northwest of Elk City.[^1][^3]

The site was first staffed in 1914, when a forest guard was stationed there as a lookout, on duty day and night for fires.[^2][^4] Early staff worked from a tent with a telephone.[^2] In August 1916 lightning struck two of them, and both survived.[^2][^4]

A July 1915 newspaper said a cabin would be built there.[^2] Other lookout histories date the first 10-by-10-foot cab to 1922.[^1][^3] The present 10-foot tower with an L-4 cab was built in 1939.[^3][^4] The cab is a classic 1932-model, hip-roofed design.[^1] Few lookouts carry a Bosworth fire finder instead of an Osborne, and this one is among them.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as drive-up and staffed, and the NHLR says it is regularly staffed each year.[^4][^1]

The staff list on Idaho Fire Lookouts runs from 1914 into 2025. For one long-serving attendant, the list gives 1976 as the start year, while a reader's comment on the same page gives 1985.[^4]

The NHLR registered the lookout on April 24, 2009, and says it is still in service on the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1]
