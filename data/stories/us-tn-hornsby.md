The Hornsby Fire Tower stands in Hardeman County, Tennessee, on an active work center of the Tennessee Division of Forestry.[^1] The Register added it in December 2022 and describes a 90-foot Aermotor tower.[^1] It gives no build date, and the other sources we read give none either.

The state's list gives the tower the number 8-24, and its notes trace such numbers to a list of towers compiled in 1976.[^3] A 2014 table in the National Register document for the state's towers lists Hornsby among the extant towers.[^4]

The sources disagree on the site's details. The Register gives the elevation as 558 feet and places the tower at 35.282954, -88.866625.[^1] The state's waypoint for the tower gives 581 feet and a point at 35.2267, -88.8664, which by our calculation is about six kilometers south of the Register's point.[^2]

We found no account of who staffed the tower or of any fire it watched. The Register does not describe public access.[^1] Because the tower sits on a working state site, anyone interested should ask the Division of Forestry before going near it.
