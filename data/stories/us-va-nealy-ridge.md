Nealy Ridge is in Dickenson County, Virginia.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower there at 37.13570, -82.33777, and marks it as gone.[^1] The post office at Nealy Ridge opened in 1909 and closed in 1961.[^2]

A Dickenson Star headline says only a concrete pier is left of the tower.[^3] Its byline credits the Dickenson County Historical Society's vice-president.[^3] No source found describes the site beyond that pier.
