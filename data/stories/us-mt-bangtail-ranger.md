The Bangtail Ranger Station lookout sat about 14 miles northwest of Livingston, in Park County, in the Gallatin National Forest.[^1] Rex's Fire Lookout Page gives its elevation as 7,700 feet.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site gives its location as 1S-8E-6.[^3] In the standard township, range and section system, that code means township 1 south, range 8 east, section 6.

The page dates the first structures to 1919, an observation tower with a cabin.[^1] By 1931 there was also a cabin on the south slope, about an eighth of a mile below the summit.[^1]

The page labels the station abandoned.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list shows it as a tower marked Gone, with no register number.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, and none says whether it is open to visitors.
