Prospect Hill, above Smethport in McKean County, got a state fire lookout in 1925.[^1][^2] The register describes a steel Aermotor tower built that year.[^1] A local paper reported that deputy forest fire warden Fred Petruzzi had finished erecting it, and that it stood 60 feet tall.[^2]

Its job was to watch for smoke.[^2] A 1925 notice said an observer would be on duty in fire season, and by 1927 a watcher was posted at the top.[^2] In April 1928 that watcher reported the first fire of the season in the Open Brook area, and a crew from Smethport was sent to fight it.[^2] In April 1952 the observer, Frank Oviatt, raised the alarm over a fire that threatened to climb the hill.[^2] From the tower he had a view straight down onto the blaze as it climbed toward him.[^2]

The tower was replaced in 1967 by a taller steel one on the same spot.[^1][^2] A 1967 notice said the new tower would stand 82 feet 6 inches high, with an 8-foot crow's nest on top.[^2]

In 2016 the tower was set for demolition.[^3] Efforts to keep it in place failed, but the county allowed the top section to be saved.[^3] That top was moved to the yard of the Old Jail Museum in Smethport.[^4] Sources differ on how long the move took. A 2022 newspaper says seven years of planning, fundraising and regulatory hurdles, while the project page says it took six years to relocate it.[^4][^3]

Today a portion of the tower is on display at the McKean County Historical Society, whose Old Jail Museum sits behind the county courthouse.[^5][^6] In May 2024 two of four restoration phases were finished.[^6] The planned exhibit is meant to be handicap accessible, and the work still needed funding, materials and volunteers.[^3]

Sources do not describe what, if anything, remains on the hilltop today.
