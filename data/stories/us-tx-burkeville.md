The Texas Forest Service ran a lookout near Burkeville, and the Central US Lookouts directory files it under Newton County.[^1] The survey description places the tower about four and a half miles south of town, between State Highway 87 and Farm Road 1414.[^2]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the tower in 1966, filing it as the Burkeville Lookout Tower under survey mark BK3234.[^2] The survey's observation point sits at the peak of a pyramid-shaped roof on a small house-style cab, carried by a four-legged steel frame. The survey gives the overall height as about 110 feet.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list marks the tower as gone.[^3] No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the land is open to visitors.
