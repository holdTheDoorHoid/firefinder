Manila Park is on the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Utah list, placed in Uintah County at 40.81064, -109.63444.[^1] The list files it under "Unknown/Undocumented Sites." It says a site moves to the lookout lists if evidence shows it was used as a regular wildland fire lookout, and on the copy I read, Manila Park has not moved.[^1]

In the same general area, the Ute Fire Tower Interpretive Site in the Ashley National Forest is a documented lookout, completed in 1937 by the Civilian Conservation Corps. One route to it starts from Manila, Utah.[^2] A Forest Service photo of that tower is captioned Ute Mountain, and the association lists a standing tower called Ute Mountain in Daggett County, at other coordinates.[^1][^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the Manila Park coordinates today.

[^1]: Utah Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/ut/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Ute Fire Tower Interpretive Site, Ashley National Forest, US Forest Service, https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/ashley/recreation/ute-fire-tower-interpretive-site (accessed 2026-10-21).
