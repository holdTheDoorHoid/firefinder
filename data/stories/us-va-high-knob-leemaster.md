The High Knob-Leemaster Lookout stands in Buchanan County, Virginia, at an elevation of 2,250 feet (686 m).[^1] Its name pairs High Knob with Leemaster, an unincorporated community in the same county.[^1][^2] Wikipedia notes that the community's post office ran from 1905 until 1972.[^2]

The Register describes a 110-foot tower of Aermotor make, raised in the early 1930s.[^1] The Register places it on the Coal Heritage Trail in the state's southwest, and names the Bearwallow tower as its sister.[^1]

The sources I read do not describe its staffing or any named fire.[^1][^3]

The Association lists the tower as standing.[^3] The Register registered it on January 23, 2017, as US 1170 and VA 36.[^1] The Association's list also includes other towers named High Knob, in Rockingham and Wise counties, so check the county before planning a trip.[^3]

The Register names an individual, not an agency, as the administrator.[^1] It gives no access arrangement or rental information.[^1] Because the administrator is a named individual, ask before going, and do not climb the tower.
