Smoky Hill was a fire lookout tower in Bayfield County, Wisconsin.[^1][^2] A map listing places the Smoky Hill Fire Tower in Barnes, within that county.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association gives its coordinates as 46.23172 north and 91.49435 west, and lists it as a tower that is gone.[^2] The Central US Lookouts site has its own page for the tower, which also names Bayfield County.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
