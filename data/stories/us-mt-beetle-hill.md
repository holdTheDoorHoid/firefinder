Beetle Hill is a crow's nest site in the Kootenai National Forest, about five miles east-southeast of Yaak in Lincoln County, Montana.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 4,894 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a crow's nest, and the Montana Lookouts county list places it under the Kootenai National Forest too.[^2][^3]

Firelookout.com dates the crow's nest to the 1930s.[^1] The same page now describes the site as ruins, while the Forest Fire Lookout Association list calls it abandoned.[^1][^2]

Firelookout.com's only note on the site's condition is the word "ruins," and that note carries no date.[^1] No source details what remains on the ground, or whether the public may walk up to it. The land is part of the Kootenai National Forest, so ask the forest before going.[^1]
