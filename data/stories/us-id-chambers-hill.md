Chambers Hill was a lookout in Latah County, Idaho, about 10 miles south of Bovill.[^1] The only design the sources give is a crow's nest, dated to 1934.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts site attributes that date to Kresek and names the agency as the Clearwater Timber Protective Association.[^3] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both use the initials CTPA.[^1][^2]

Sources date the abandonment to the 1940s rather than to one year.[^1][^2]

The location is uncertain. Idaho Fire Lookouts gives no exact location, though it labels the site a hike-in.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page lists the code 39N-1E-19.[^3] Firelookout.com lists the elevation as unknown.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under its St. Joe - Clearwater region.[^2]

The three pages we read do not describe what remains of the site today.[^1][^2][^3]
