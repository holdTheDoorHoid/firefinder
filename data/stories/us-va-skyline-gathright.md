The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list includes a tower called Skyline (Gathright). It places the tower in Alleghany County at 37.84609 north, 79.94129 west, and marks it as gone.[^1]

No source we checked describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1]

[^1]: Virginia lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
