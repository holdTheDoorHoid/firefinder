The Lake County fire tower compilation describes the Cloquet Lake lookout as a 60-foot Pittsburg-Des Moines tower of ladder type, owned by the Minnesota Forest Service.[^1] That service later became the Forestry Division of the Department of Natural Resources.[^1] The compilation places the tower at 47.4298 north and 91.4723 west.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it in Lake County at 47.42992 north and 91.47114 west, a slightly different point.[^2]

A 1978 survey note records the tower as gone and says the site was bulldozed.[^1] The Association's Minnesota list also marks the tower as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.

[^2]: Minnesota Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
