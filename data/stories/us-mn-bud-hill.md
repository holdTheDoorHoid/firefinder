The Bud Hill lookout stood in Lake County, Minnesota. The county survey spells the name Budd Hill.[^1] It was a stairway tower of 79.5 feet, numbered 106, and the Minnesota Forest Service owned it.[^1] The tower stood a short distance inland from Gooseberry Park, along County Road 3.[^1]

The Lake County survey dates the move to the Argus site in St. Louis County to 1958.[^1] The statewide tower list gives the same move and the same year.[^4] The St. Louis County survey lists the Argus tower with a date of 1959 and calls it the relocated Budd Hill tower.[^2] The two surveys do not agree on the year, so our timeline lists both.[^1][^2]

The Lake County survey notes that the fire tower was gone from its original site by 1976.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association index lists both the Bud Hill site and the Argus site as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at either site today.
