McKeever Mountain lies about 11 miles east of Coos Bay, in Coos County, Oregon.[^2] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 1,698 feet.[^2]

The lookout grew out of a 1948 arrangement between the Weyerhaeuser company and the Coos Forest Protective Association. A June 1948 Coos Bay Times item, quoted on a Forest Lookouts page, said the company was supplying two new lookout sites, one of them on McKeever Butte.[^1] In August, the Forest Log reported that the new lookout was named for Don McKeever, a Weyerhaeuser research forester who had died a few months earlier when a dead tree came down on the car he was riding in.[^1]

Accounts differ on how the lookout was built. A 1948 Oregon Department of Forestry annual report, also quoted on that page, says Weyerhaeuser supplied a building and relocated it to the site, and that a lookout patrol was set up there.[^1] A 1949 newspaper item expected the company to build a tower, with the protection association supplying the staff.[^1] A 1953 entry describes a cupola-topped lookout house that the company built.[^1][^2]

The lookout was abandoned in 1962.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com names "Weyerhauser Corp." on its page, while the Forest Lookouts page files the site under the Coos County FPA.[^2][^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists McKeever Mountain as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3] A 2026 directory of Oregon's standing lookouts does not include it.[^4]
