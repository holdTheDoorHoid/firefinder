Seville is an unincorporated community in Volusia County, Florida, founded in 1882.[^1] The 2020 census counted 580 residents.[^1] A lookout tower named Seville is listed for the county on a lookout website.[^2] Mapcarta, a map site built on OpenStreetMap data, places the tower near the hamlet of Senyah, an unincorporated community in north Volusia County, and gives the tower's elevation as 49 feet (15 m).[^3]

A Florida chapter report from the Forest Fire Lookout Association, covering 2015 and early 2016, says that a member who had moved to the state operated the Deleon Springs tower and then the Seville fire tower.[^4]

No source I could open describes what stands at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
