Freezeout Ridge, which a 1915 report calls Freezeout Mountain, rises to 5,903 feet about 11 miles east of Clarkia in Shoshone County.[^1][^3] A log cabin and crow's nest went up there in 1912.[^1][^2][^3] John Swanson was the lookout in September 1915, according to a Clearwater Republican item quoted on the Forest Lookouts site.[^3][^2] That same year, a fire committee report from the Coeur d'Alene association calls the station the Clearwater Timber Protective Association's lookout, while the two lookout sites name the Potlatch association.[^3][^2][^1] The Clearwater and Potlatch associations stayed distinct until a merger in 1966.[^4]

In June 1919, a wedding notice quoted on the Forest Lookouts site said the newlyweds would spend their honeymoon at the lookout.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists William Niesham and Lillie Neisham for 1919, and the two sources spell the surname differently.[^2][^3] The same site also lists Henry Thompson for 1920 and a run of names in the 1960s, including Dan Leroy in 1964 and Dan William Green in 1965.[^2]

A log cabin went up at the south end of the ridge in 1923.[^2] A pole tower 30 feet high, fitted with an L-4 cab, followed in 1937.[^1][^3] The tower was destroyed about 1971.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts marks the site as drive-up.[^2] A visit report dated August 14, 2025 says the writers found cement footings from the L-4 tower and think they located the 1923 cabin, but that little else is left beyond the views.[^2] No source says whether the site is open to the public today.
