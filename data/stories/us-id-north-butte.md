North Butte had a 10-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, built in 1932.[^1] The firelookout.com page places it in the St. Joe National Forest, 13 miles south of Avery in Shoshone County, at 6,738 feet.[^1] Both pages give 1969 as the year the tower was destroyed.[^1][^2]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page lists the site as a hike-in.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list places the tower at 47.03805, -115.80070 and marks it gone.[^3]

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit. Anyone planning a trip should ask the St. Joe National Forest about access and conditions before going.
