Rector Mountain lies in Tillamook County, Oregon.[^1] The register and firelookout.com date a crow's nest and a cabin on the site to 1926.[^1][^3] The Oregon lookouts history lists only a crow's nest for that year.[^2]

In September 1929, a Wheeler Reporter item quoted on the Oregon lookouts history said L. H. Kenny, at the Rector lookout, could see only now and then, when the wind cleared the smoke.[^2]

The sources disagree on when the first lookout went up. The register and firelookout.com date it to 1936.[^1][^3] A March 1935 Forest Log note quoted on the Oregon lookouts history says a primary lookout on Rector Mountain was established by Camp Boyington, which had been set up in late 1933.[^2] That note is a year earlier than the register's date.

The history page records the lookout as activated on March 9, 1942, for the Portland Filter Center.[^2] A June 1942 inspection by an Aircraft Warning Service inspector found two observers on duty. It described a 40-foot tower with a 7-by-7 cab and a shake cabin with a bedroom tacked on in haste.[^2]

In 1954 a replacement tower of treated timber went up, with a 14-by-14 cab.[^2] The register and firelookout.com give that tower as 41 feet, while the Oregon lookouts history says 40.[^1][^2][^3]

The Oregon lookouts history says the lookout was abandoned in 1967.[^2] The register and that history say the tower was removed in 1973.[^1][^2] firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and we found no current information on access. The only route description we found dates from 1942, when an inspection report mentioned a trail three miles long from the end of God's Valley Road.[^2]

The register lists the site as registered on November 15, 2010, with the Tillamook FPA as administrator. The Oregon lookouts history page is headed Oregon Department of Forestry, so the two sources name different agencies.[^1][^2]
