The Evadale lookout was a Texas Forest Service tower in Jasper County.[^1][^2] The state list places it at 30.27139, -94.03722 and marks it as gone.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page for the site quotes The Eagle on a contract notice dated September 20, 1962.[^2] The notice awarded O.C. Braly of Overton $48,960 to build eight fire lookout towers for the Texas Forest Service.[^2] An additional $3,805 contract was for reconstruction of the Evadale Lookout Tower.[^2] A reconstruction implies that a tower already stood on the site before 1962, but the sources we read do not say when the tower was first built.[^2]

The Texas list gives the site's name as Evadale and its county as Jasper, the same county the Forest Lookouts page names.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the Evadale site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Texas Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Evadale, Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/evadale.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
