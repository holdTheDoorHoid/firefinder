In January 1963, a report in The News Tribune, quoted on the Forest Lookouts website, said a State Forestry Service fire tower would soon be built on Edwards Road in St. Lucie County.[^1] The tower was to stand on a 400-foot-square (122 m) lot that the St. Lucie County Commissioners had deeded to the state agency.[^1] The tower was already on hand and was to be erected as soon as the lot was ready.[^1] The plans also called for shop and office space on the land, and later two residences on a neighboring lot near the county barn on Oleander Avenue.[^1] The article said the state forestry agency was in year two of its St. Lucie County work on fire prevention, reporting and suppression.[^1]

None of the sources checked describes what remains at the Edwards Road site today, or whether the public can visit it.

[^1]: Fort Pierce, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/fort-pierce.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
