Forest Lookouts files the Potts Mountain tower under Craig County, Virginia, and under the Jefferson National Forest.[^1] A register entry for Bald Mountain, a neighboring Craig County tower built in 1955, says Potts Mountain was the county's other mountain with a tower.[^3]

In June 1936, a Danville Bee item quoted on the Forest Lookouts page reported that a bear raiding sheep in the Potts Creek section had held a fire warden in his steel tower until after supper. The warden proposed immediate action by telephone, and two men with rifles killed the animal.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list places a tower on Potts Mountain and marks it gone, with no removal date given.[^2]

No source read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the public can reach the site. A hiker's page titled Potts Mountain Lookout Site covers a different tower near the Appalachian Trail in Giles County, which its map labels Stony Creek, and says nearly everything the author found refers to the more northern Craig County site, which is not on the trail.[^4]
