Brock Mountain is in Shasta County, within the Shasta National Forest.[^1][^2] On September 16, 1932, the Dunsmuir News reported that two more lookout stations in the forest were being built that summer, so that firefighters could locate and suppress blazes more quickly.[^1] The same article said one lookout already stood on the summit, eight miles east of Bully Hill.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list gives the position as 40.80502 north and 122.13218 west, and marks the tower as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes the summit today, so we cannot say what remains or whether the site is open to visitors.
