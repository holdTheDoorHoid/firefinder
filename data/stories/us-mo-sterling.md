Sterling Lookout Tower stood in Howell County, Missouri.[^1][^2]

OpenStreetMap maps it as a tower with the tower type set to observation, at an elevation of 469 meters.[^2]

TopoQuest gives the elevation as 1,538 feet and says the tower appears on the USGS 1:24,000 topographic map named Cabool SE.[^3] The two elevations agree within rounding.[^2][^3] TopoQuest also lists a populated place called Sterling close by.[^3]

The Forest Lookouts site marks the tower as removed.[^1]

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2][^3]
