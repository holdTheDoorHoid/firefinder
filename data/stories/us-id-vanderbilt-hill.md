The Vanderbilt Hill site lies in Shoshone County, Idaho, about 13 miles southeast of the Red Ives Ranger Station, in the Clearwater National Forest.[^1] Its elevation is 5,692 feet.[^1][^2]

The sources describe a log cabin, which the association and Idaho Fire Lookouts date to 1930.[^1][^2] Both also record a camp set up in 1934 on the ridge north of the summit.[^1][^2] The Forest Service's 2016 list dates the log cabin to 1934 instead.[^3] The sources disagree about when the cabin was built, and the Forest Service list does not explain the difference.[^1][^2][^3]

The site was abandoned in 1941.[^1][^2][^3] The association and the Forest Service list both describe what was left as ruins.[^1][^3]

No source we found describes what stands on the hill today. Idaho Fire Lookouts classes the site as hike-in, the only access detail we found.[^2] The association's list marks it gone.[^4]
