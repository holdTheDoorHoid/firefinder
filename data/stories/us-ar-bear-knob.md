The Forest Lookouts directory lists a fire tower called Bear Knob in Scott County, on the Ouachita National Forest.[^1] Its only entry gives one date: the tower was removed in 1974.[^1]

The Mena and Oden lookout guide does not list a Bear Knob.[^2] It does name a Buck Knob tower in Scott County, and its photo caption ties the image to a 1933 to 1934 Arkansas Civilian Conservation Corps publication.[^2] The guide says Buck Knob is not standing. Photographs of the tower from 1959 and 1966 depict a square cap on top.[^2] One passage in the same guide appears to group a Buck Knob with Polk County towers, so its county details do not agree with each other.[^2] A 1956 federal survey description of a Buck Knob lookout tower says a wooden cabin sits atop a four-legged steel frame, about 50 feet high.[^3]

The sources do not show whether Bear Knob and Buck Knob are the same tower under two names, or two separate towers.[^1][^2][^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the Bear Knob site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
