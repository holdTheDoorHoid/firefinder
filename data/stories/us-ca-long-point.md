Long Point is a lookout site in Glenn County, in the Mendocino National Forest.[^1] The fire lookout index lists it as an enclosed tower and marks it gone.[^2]

The point is a small rise on a ridge, not really a summit.[^3] A hiker who went there rated the views as decent, looking over the Shepherd Creek drainage from three sides.[^3] According to the same trip report, only a concrete foundation survives from an earlier lookout.[^3]

The report describes a gravel road that led out to a trailhead, where a trail drops about 2,000 feet to Shepherd Creek.[^3] We found no closure notice for the site. One hiker's trip report is the only source we have that describes the site today, so check with the Mendocino National Forest before you go.[^3]
