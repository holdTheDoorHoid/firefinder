Wood Duck was a fire lookout tower in Saint Louis County, Minnesota. The Fire Lookout Association's county list places it there, at 48.16818, -93.0965, and marks it gone.[^1] The Forest Lookouts Minnesota page has a Wood Duck entry that lists Saint Louis County.[^2]

No source we read describes the site today or says whether any part of the tower remains. Because the tower is listed as gone, there is no tower to visit.[^1]

[^1]: Minnesota Lookouts (By County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/mn-co/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Minnesota, Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/minnesota.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
