The Big Hill Lookout Tower was near Robeline.[^1] The Wikipedia list gives Sabine Parish as its location, but the Central US Lookouts index and TopoZone both place it in Natchitoches Parish, so this story follows those two.[^1][^2][^3]

The list's history section gives some background on the state's towers.[^1] In 1915 funds went to fire protection work, which made it possible to hire lookout staff.[^1] The first steel fire tower went up near Bogalusa in 1922.[^1] The number of fire towers reached 39 by 1943 and 56 by 1950.[^1]

The list marks Big Hill as torn down.[^1] Its note adds that the site could not be checked on street view or in older aerial photos.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors. Ask the landowner before going onto any land you do not know.
