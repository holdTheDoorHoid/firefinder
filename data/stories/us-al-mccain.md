McCain Tower is in Clay County, Alabama, at about 1,384 feet (422 m) above sea level.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register lists it as US 1142, AL 69. Eleanor McCain nominated it, and the entry was recorded on July 24, 2016.[^1]

The register also names Eleanor McCain as the person who administers the tower, and it gives no government agency for it.[^1] Its photographs carry captions describing a reconstruction, but the register does not say when the work happened or what it involved.[^1]

We could not find a build date, a design, a height or any fire history for McCain Tower. Two searches turned up no news or agency account of the tower either.

Across Alabama, a 1819 News opinion column by Thomas Kaufmann estimates that probably fewer than 75 historic lookouts still stand, and says many are unsafe and illegal to climb.[^2] The register gives no visiting arrangements for McCain Tower, so please do not climb it. Anyone interested should ask the administrator before any visit, and enjoy the tower from the ground.[^1]
