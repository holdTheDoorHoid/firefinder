The Turkey Knob Lookout Tower is in Hardin County, Tennessee.[^1] It is an Aermotor LS-40, a steel model that the Division of Forestry used widely until the mid-1970s.[^1][^2]

Neither source we read gives a build date, a builder, or any fire the tower watched, so those parts of its history are still unknown.[^1][^2]

The Register lists the Tennessee Division of Forestry as the tower's administrator. Jason Johns nominated it, and it was registered on August 7, 2023, as US 1757, TN 61.[^1] The Register also notes a site visit dated January 29, 2020.[^1]

A table of extant towers in a National Register study, as of March 26, 2014, includes Turkey Knob.[^2] Tennessee Landforms lists the tower as standing.[^3]

We found no information on whether the public may visit. Check with the Division of Forestry before you go.
