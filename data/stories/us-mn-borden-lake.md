The fire tower at Borden Lake was completed in 1930, in Crow Wing County, near the town of Garrison.[^1][^2] On May 2 that year, the Brainerd Daily Dispatch reported that a 94-foot tower of a new type had been finished and was already manned.[^2] It was the sixth tower of that type in the district, which was overseen by district forest ranger E.H. Rhodes.[^2]

A telephone link followed. In September 1935 a board approved a State Forestry request to run poles and telephone wire along county road rights of way, for a line from Brainerd to the lookout.[^2] In April 1936 a 23-mile metal telephone line was built, linking the tower with the district ranger in Brainerd.[^2]

Vandals struck the tower in 1970.[^2] On June 26 the Dispatch reported that officials had found five broken windows, a wrecked fire finder, a missing map and a destroyed stool.[^2] On August 21 it reported that someone had broken a trap door and twenty panes of plate glass at the tower.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association now lists the tower as gone.[^1]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the land is open to visitors.
