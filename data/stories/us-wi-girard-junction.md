In 1954 the Coast and Geodetic Survey described a survey station as lying on the hill where the Girard fire tower also stands, in Marinette County, Wisconsin.[^3] The description places the station about 14 and a half miles northwest of Wausaukee, and its directions end at the fire tower and the station.[^3] The Forest Lookouts page for Girard Junction reproduces the description, which does not date the tower.[^3]

TopoQuest gives the Girard Junction Lookout Tower an elevation of 1,282 feet, but lists the feature as a locale rather than a tower.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list marks the Girard Junction tower as gone and gives its coordinates as 45.49710, -88.20283.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
