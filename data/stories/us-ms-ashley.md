The Ashley Lookout Tower is in Copiah County, Mississippi.[^1] A lookout-history site lists it as standing, with the note "Standing (Google Earth)".[^1] That note points to satellite imagery as the basis for the listing.[^1] No source I could read describes what remains at the site today.

[^1]: Ashley - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts (easternuslookouts.weebly.com), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/ashley.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
