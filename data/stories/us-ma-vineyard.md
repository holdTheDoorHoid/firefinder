The Vineyard fire tower stands in the Town of West Tisbury, in Dukes County, Massachusetts, on the island of Martha's Vineyard.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register lists the Mass. Bureau of Forest Fire Control as its administrator.[^1]

The island's first lookout, the register says, was a 50-foot tower built in 1917. It stayed in service until 1944.[^1] Next came a temporary wooden tower near Edgartown. The Forest Lookouts index lists that temporary tower at Airfield Hill, around 1950.[^1][^2] The present tower, 68 feet tall with a 10-by-10-foot wooden cab, was built in 1950.[^1] The sources I read do not explain why the lookout moved to West Tisbury.

I found no record of who staffed the tower or which fires it watched, so that part of its history is still missing.

A Massachusetts state report on fire towers on Cape Cod and the Islands lists the West Tisbury tower among seven active fire observation towers in the area.[^3] The report places it on an unnamed hill on Department of Conservation and Recreation land.[^3] The same report says the road to the tower is maintained by fire-control and forestry staff.[^3]

Public access is not clear from these sources. The report does not say whether visitors may climb the tower, and I found no visitor listing. Treat it as a working state site: check with the state before going near it, and do not climb it.
