The Blacklead lookout stood on Blacklead Mountain in the Clearwater National Forest, in Clearwater County, Idaho.[^1][^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register recorded the site in January 2011, on a nomination from Luke Channer.[^1]

The first lookout went up in 1929 as a 25-foot pole platform tower with a log cabin.[^1][^2] A new log cabin followed in 1932.[^1][^2] In 1934 the 1929 tower and cabin were replaced by a 40-foot pole L-4 tower, and a crow's nest was set on an L-6 tower about two miles north, near Goat Lake.[^1][^3] A Forest Service post, citing the author Louis Hartig, says Oscar Hendrickson built the log lookout, though it gives no year.[^5]

Idaho Fire Lookouts says it has no documented staffing history for the site.[^2] A Forest Service photo caption names George Bradley as a lookout shown in the picture, with no date.[^5]

The lookout is gone.[^1][^4] In a post dated August 17, 2023, Idaho Fire Lookouts reports cement footings and other debris from the 1930s, spread across a large area.[^2] The Forest Service post says the structure has disappeared.[^5]

Sources disagree on two details. Firelookout.com puts the site 11 miles northeast of Powell Ranger Station.[^3] The 2016 Forest Service list gives 11 miles northwest, and the Forest Service post also says northwest.[^4][^5] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the elevation as 7,218 feet, while the register, firelookout.com and the Forest Service list all give 7,318 feet.[^2][^1][^3][^4]

Today a few backcountry trailheads meet at the site, and Idaho Fire Lookouts says it tends to be busy.[^2] The same page labels it drive-up.[^2] The sources we read mention no closure or restriction.[^2] They describe footings and debris rather than a standing lookout, so visitors should expect that.[^2]
