The Standard Peak lookout stood about 10 miles north-northeast of Whitefish, in Flathead County, Montana, on the Flathead National Forest.[^1][^2] Ron Kemnow's page gives the survey location as 32N-21W-14.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the site at 48.54182, -114.24700.[^3]

Rex Kamstra's firelookout.com page gives the elevation as 7,196 feet and dates the lookout to 1929.[^1] It describes a D-1 cupola cabin on a platform.[^1] Ron Kemnow's Forest Lookouts page records panorama photos taken on August 3, 1935.[^2]

Kemnow's page says the lookout was removed, but gives no date.[^2] Kamstra's page says the lookout was no longer there before 1956.[^1] The two accounts agree, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a ground lookout that is gone.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today, and no source says whether the public can visit it.[^1][^2][^3]
