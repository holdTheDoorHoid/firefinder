Round Mountain, in Essex County, once had a fire lookout.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone, classes it as a Crowsnest type, and places it at 44.60199 north, 71.66195 west.[^1] The Eastern US Lookouts site lists its Vermont entry under Essex County and marks it "Removed".[^2] A separate register of former lookout sites has no Vermont entries.[^3] Sable Mountain, also in Essex County, is listed the same way.[^1]

Other Essex County entries on the county list show a mix: Gore Mountain and Stone Mountain are towers marked gone, while Monadnock Mountain's tower is listed as standing.[^4]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
