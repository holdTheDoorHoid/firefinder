The Missouri Department of Conservation erected a fire tower at Seymour, in Webster County, in fiscal 1967-68, according to the annual report as quoted on a lookout history site.[^1] The tower was about six miles north-northeast of Seymour, on the north side of County Route P.[^2] When the National Geodetic Survey described it in 1978, it stood about 110 feet high, with four legs and a cabin at the top, and the survey station sat at the apex of its roof.[^2] The department's 1966 count was 102 fire towers across the state, against 43 run by the Forest Service in Missouri.[^3]

The lookout history site lists the Seymour tower as removed, but it gives neither a date nor a reason.[^1] None of the sources read for this story describes what, if anything, remains on the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
