Bruce Mound Lookout stands in Clark County, Wisconsin, at 1,412 feet (430 meters).[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes a 70-foot tower of a type it calls Baker Latest Rebuilt (1932), with an external cage-protected ladder.[^1]

The register says the tower was built in 1932. The Forest Lookouts history gives 1933 and describes an approximately 73-foot steel tower, so the two sources disagree on both the year and the height.[^1][^2] This story follows the register's figures.

According to the register, a fire burned out the tower's original cab in the 1950s, and the cab was rebuilt the year after. The register does not give the year of the fire.[^1]

A visit report dated April 9, 2018 rates the tower's condition as good. It says the tower is staffed sometimes, when a lookout is available.[^1] The sources I read name no past staff and no other fires.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^3] The register names the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources as administrator, but the same visit report says the tower is located on private property.[^1] The sources do not explain how both hold. Clark County's Forestry and Parks Department runs a recreation site called Bruce Mound that sells season passes and rents its chalet in the off-season, but no source I read ties the fire tower to that site.[^4] Because the register describes the tower as being on private land, do not climb it or go onto the property without the owner's permission.
