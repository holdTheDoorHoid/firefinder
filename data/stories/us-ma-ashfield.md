The register describes a wooden lookout tower that first operated in 1914. It stood on Cran Pond Hill in Franklin County, at about 560 metres (1,838 feet), and the register lists the site as US 15, MA 15.[^1]

A severe windstorm destroyed the tower in the summer of 1918. It was never replaced.[^1]

The site was added to the Former Fire Lookout Sites register on September 3, 2008, nominated by Henry Isenberg.[^1]

Today the register says the site lies on inaccessible private property.[^1] No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains on the hilltop now, so this history cannot say whether any traces survive. Please keep off the private land.

[^1]: Ashfield Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ma/ashfield-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
