Flash Peak Lookout stood about five miles northwest of Avery, in Shoshone County on the St. Joe National Forest, at 5,685 feet.[^1][^2] It was built in 1932 as a 20-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab on top.[^1][^2][^3] The L-4, also called an Aladdin, was a prefabricated lookout house, 14 feet square, produced between 1929 and 1953.[^4]

In August 1934 a wildfire came within about a mile of the station.[^3] A Morning Oregonian account, quoted on the Idaho index, put the station roughly a mile ahead of the fire at midnight and reported that concern was voiced it might be destroyed.[^3] The quoted account does not name the fire.[^3]

The lookout was destroyed in 1956, as all three pages record.[^1][^2][^3] None of them gives the cause or the exact date of the loss.[^1][^2][^3]

Today, no source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit.[^1][^2][^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page lists the site as a hike-in, though it also marks the tower destroyed.[^2] No source says whether the public can reach the site now.[^1][^2][^3]
