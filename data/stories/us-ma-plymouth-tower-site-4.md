The Manomet Hill fire tower stood in Plymouth County, Massachusetts. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it as Plymouth Tower Site #4 and describes it as a 60-foot steel tower.[^1]

A tower went up on Manomet Hill in 1942.[^4] The Pine Hill register says a new tower was constructed there that year, and the Forest Lookouts page says it took over the work from the Telegraph Hill tower.[^3][^4] A second register entry for the same hilltop says the 60-foot tower had been relocated from Pelham.[^2] The sources disagree about where the tower came from, and we could not settle the question.

They also disagree about when staffing ended. The Tower Site #4 record says the tower was staffed until 1968.[^1] The Manomet Hill entry says it was in use until about 1966. The Pine Hill register says the tower served there until 1966, and the Forest Lookouts page says it handed its work to the new Pine Hill tower that year.[^2][^3][^4]

The Pine Hill register describes the tower on Pine Hill as the current Plymouth fire tower, built there in 1966.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains of the Manomet Hill tower today, such as footings or other traces. None says whether the public can reach the hilltop, so anyone planning a visit should first confirm who owns the land.
