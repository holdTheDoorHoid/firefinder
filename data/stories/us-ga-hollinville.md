The Georgia lookout list posted on tnlandforms.us places a lookout tower at a point it calls Hollinville, in Pike County. Each line of the list gives a name, coordinates, a county and a number.[^1] The number for this point is 860, which this record reads as feet of elevation (262 m).[^1] The list was last revised in March 2016.[^1]

The name appears to refer to Hollonville, an unincorporated community in Pike County.[^2] The community sits at the point where Concord Road meets Georgia Highway 362,[^3] and its Wikipedia coordinates put it near the list's point.[^2] The list does not say whether the tower stands in the community itself.

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public. Before going to look, ask the Georgia Forestry Commission or Pike County officials.
