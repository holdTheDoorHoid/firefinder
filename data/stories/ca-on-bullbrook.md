Bullbrook is a fire tower point in the Land Information Ontario tower layer.[^1] The layer places the point at 47.34 N, 82.82 W, to within 10 metres, and marks the entry as verified.[^1] Its tower identifier is TOW-36524-1.[^1]

Most of Ontario's fire towers were put up between the 1920s and the 1950s, when forest fire protection peaked.[^2] Responsibility for them rested with the Department of Lands and Forests, which in 1972 became the Ministry of Natural Resources.[^2]

Today no source describes what stands at the site, or whether it is open to visitors.
