The Bechler River Lookout stood in Yellowstone National Park, in the park's southwest section, in Teton County, Wyoming.[^1][^2] It sat about 18 miles southwest of Old Faithful, at 6,453 feet, or 1,967 meters.[^3][^1]

The first lookout existed as early as 1934.[^1] It was a set of boards strung between two trees, which gave access to the lookout.[^1]

The Civilian Conservation Corps erected the second tower in 1937.[^1] Ten young men from a camp near Old Faithful worked on it through the summer.[^2]

Newspapers from 1937 described a 60-foot tower.[^2] One report said it was built on the ground and then raised into position, and that within two hours of completion it was used to spot a fire in nearby Idaho.[^2]

Firelookout.com describes the 1937 structure as a wooden pole tower.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wyoming list records the tower as gone.[^4] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site in 2022, and the National Park Service administers it.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^3]
