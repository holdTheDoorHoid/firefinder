Chateau Rock is a stack of granite boulders in the Clearwater National Forest, in Clearwater County, Idaho, about 20 miles northeast of Pierce.[^1][^3] The register says the stack is nearly 200 feet high.[^1]

An L-4 cab went up on the rock in 1931. It sat on concrete footings and had a catwalk.[^1] Reaching it meant scrambling over the boulders and then climbing ladders for the last 80 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

A patrol camp stood three miles east, atop Green Point.[^3][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists two smokechasers for 1943, Warren Yahr and Bill Fry.[^2] The register also tells of a sow bear with a cub that a packer had to chase out of the cab.[^1]

The register recorded the site in July 2011, on a nomination from Rod Fosback.[^1]

Since 1961 the cab has existed only as ruins.[^1][^3][^4] None of the sources says what caused the ruin.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site as hike-in ruins.[^2] The Forest Service administers the site, and no source says it is closed.[^1] The route the sources describe is a scramble over boulders and ladders, so anyone going near the rock should take care.[^1] We found no source that describes what remains on the rock now.[^2]
