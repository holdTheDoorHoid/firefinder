In late August 1949 the Bunkie Record reported that the Louisiana Forestry Commission had completed a fire lookout a short distance west of Leesville.[^1] The article framed the structure as an extra safeguard for the woodlands of central Louisiana. It added that the tower would act as the commission's dispatch hub for Vernon Parish and would spot blazes over a wide region.[^1]

A federal survey description written in 1972 placed the tower about 2.5 miles west of Leesville, on Commission land, and said the Louisiana Forestry Commission managed it at that time.[^1]

A Louisiana tower list revised in 2011 still gives a Leesville Lookout Tower at about 31.142 north and 93.292 west, in Vernon Parish.[^2] No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
