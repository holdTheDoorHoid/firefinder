Bonney Butte (ELO) lies in Hood River County, in the Mt Hood National Forest, roughly 1.5 miles north of the main Bonney Butte Lookout.[^1] The Forest Lookouts guide records it as an emergency lookout around 1946 and gives no other date.[^1]

The main site has its own history. According to the register, the Forest Service set up a tent with a fire finder and telephone there in 1925. An L-4 cab followed in 1933 on the south end of the ridge below the summit, and by 1967 the lookout had gone.[^2] The register puts the main site in Wasco County, while the Forest Lookouts guide puts it in Hood River County.[^2][^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at this emergency site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
