Clay Lookout Tower is a fire lookout in Clay County, Georgia.[^1] A Georgia tower list kept at tnlandforms.us gives its coordinates and tags it with two reference codes, BLL-41130 and gfc-203.[^1] The list page carries a revision stamp dated 22 March 2016, so its details may be out of date.[^1]

A Vanishing Georgia photo post describes the state's fire towers as iconic but at risk.[^2] In the late 1980s, the Georgia Forestry Commission moved away from tower operators and chose an air patrol system instead.[^2] Many of the towers were disposed of by scrapping and sale.[^2] Some surviving towers are still put to use when air service cannot be provided.[^2]

No source describes the site today, so it is unknown whether a tower still stands there, what remains on the ground, or whether visitors can reach the place.
