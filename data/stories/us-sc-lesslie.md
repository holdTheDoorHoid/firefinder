The National Historic Lookout Register places the Lesslie Fire Tower in York County, South Carolina, at an elevation of 709 feet, or 216 meters.[^1] The Register added it on January 8, 2021, as US 1477, SC 35, on the nomination of Si Ballenger.[^1] It lists the South Carolina Forestry Commission as the administrator.[^1]

The Commission closed its fire tower system on October 1, 1993.[^2] The Register does not explain how the Commission came to administer this tower, or whether it ever watched for fires.[^1]

The Register also gives no build date, design, staffing history or current condition.[^1] The Commission's history page does not mention the tower.[^2] Until someone with local knowledge checks the tower, treat this record as thin. The Register gives no access information, so anyone interested should ask the administrator before going near it.[^1]
