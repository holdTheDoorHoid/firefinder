Talona Mountain is a summit in Gilmer County, Georgia, with an elevation of 2,090 feet (640 m).[^1] The name comes from Talona, a Cherokee settlement that once stood nearby; today Talona is an unincorporated community in Gilmer County, and a post office there operated from 1886 to 1953.[^1][^2] A published list titled "Georgia lookout towers" places a lookout tower on the mountain at 34.63052, -84.49128 and tags the site "gfc-909".[^3] The list's map was last revised in March 2016.[^3] No source describes what remains on the mountain today or whether the site is open to the public.
