Burlington was a fire lookout in Boone County, about three and three-quarter miles southwest of Omaha.[^1] A National Geodetic Survey station description, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, calls it a four-legged steel tower with a gray metal observation room at the top, about 110 feet overall.[^1] Surveyors recorded the tower in 1961, so it was standing by then.[^1]

The same page marks the lookout as removed but gives no year.[^1] That 1961 date falls inside the period the encyclopedia calls the height of fire tower use, from the 1930s to the 1970s, when Arkansas had more than 120 towers.[^1][^2] The encyclopedia credits aerial surveillance, and later satellites, with making lookouts less necessary.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
