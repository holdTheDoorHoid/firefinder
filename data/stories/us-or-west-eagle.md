West Eagle is a lookout site in Union County, Oregon, on the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page gives its location as 5S-43E-27.[^1] The Oregon lookout list places it at 45.10306 north and 117.42605 west.[^3]

Firelookout.com dates the site only to the 1930s and calls it abandoned. The same page says it may never have been used and may have been a proposed site.[^2] The Oregon lookout list gives its type as unknown and its status as abandoned.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]

[^2]: West Eagle Fire Lookout, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/or/westeagle.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
