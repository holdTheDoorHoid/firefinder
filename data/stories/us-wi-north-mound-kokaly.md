North Mound is a tower site in Clark County, Wisconsin, also known as the Kokaly Lookout Tower.[^1][^2][^6] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^1][^2] TopoZone and OpenStreetMap both put the elevation at about 427 metres, or 1,401 feet.[^3][^6]

The name and county do not match across every list.[^1][^2] An older Forest Lookouts list has a Kokaly entry in Clark County, but it also has a North Mound entry under Eau Claire County.[^4] I could not tie that Eau Claire entry to this tower. I have kept Clark County. The Association lists the tower there at coordinates that OpenStreetMap also gives.[^1][^6]

The sources I could reach do not give a build date, a builder, or a record of who staffed the tower.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^6] WJFW reports that 72 aging towers still in service statewide were decommissioned in 2015.[^5] The sources do not say whether this tower was one of them.[^5]

The Association still lists the tower as standing.[^1] I found nothing about access or visiting, so this story does not encourage climbing it. No source here names the agency that manages the site, so before any trip, ask the local land manager whether the public may go up.
