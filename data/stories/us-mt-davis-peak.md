The Davis Peak lookout was a 40-foot pole tower, which the listing calls an L-4 type and dates to 1946.[^1] It stood on the Kootenai National Forest, about six miles southwest of Thompson Lake in Lincoln County, Montana, at an elevation of 5,340 feet.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Montana lookouts places the tower in Lincoln County and marks it as gone.[^2] The one detailed listing we read says the tower was gone by 1956, and it does not say how the tower came to be removed.[^1]

Today, no source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site. We also found no source on whether the ground is open to visitors, so ask the Kootenai National Forest before going to look for it.
