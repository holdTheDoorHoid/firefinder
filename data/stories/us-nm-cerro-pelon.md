Cerro Pelon is a lookout site in Rio Arriba County, New Mexico. The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a shelter at 36.02676 north, 106.59591 west, and marks it gone.[^1]

The association's New Mexico listing is arranged by county. Two entries have Cerro Pelon in their name, and both are under Rio Arriba. Each row gives a name, coordinates, a type and a status, with no history or description of the site. The second entry, Tree 73/Cerro Pelon (north), is a map board at 36.12173 north, 106.39640 west, and is also marked gone.[^1]

No source I read describes what remains at either site today, or whether either is open to visitors.

[^1]: New Mexico Lookouts (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/nm-co/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
