Squa Pan Mountain lies in Aroostook County, in what was then the Maine Forestry District.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register places its site east of Masardis and southeast of Ashland, on private property.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts history says a 48-foot steel tower was partly erected in 1917, with completion planned for 1918.[^2] The same history says a 26-foot tower was completed in 1918, and a 1920 inventory lists a 26-foot wood tower.[^2] It is not clear whether the 26-foot tower was the steel one planned in 1917.

A 1920 Maine Forest Service panorama map is centered on a tower it calls Scopan Mt., in Township 11, Range 4.[^3] The name looks like a variant of Squa Pan, but the map does not say it is the same tower.

The Forest Lookouts history lists the tower as removed, with no date.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine list shows a Squa-Pan tower standing at a different point, with its cab gone.[^1][^4] The register says it is not known whether Squa Pan still stands.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at this point today.
