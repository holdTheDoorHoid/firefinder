The Monadnock Lookout Tower is not an old fire lookout. It is a park tower in the Cane Creek Mountains Natural Area, in Alamance County, North Carolina, that offers 360-degree views, and the county parks department now lists it as open.[^1] A travel blog post with a June 2025 date in its web address calls it a new lookout tower, so it had opened by June 2025, though we did not find a construction date or a builder.[^2]

The park calls itself a new nature park that opens the county's largest natural heritage area of state significance to the public.[^1] Its page says that once development is finished, the park will total over 1,000 acres and add an observation tower, camping and wildlife viewing areas.[^1] The tower is not in the historic record. The National Historic Lookout Register's North Carolina list, which holds 31 lookouts, did not include a Monadnock tower when we read it.[^3]

We found no staffing history and no record of fire watch use at this site.

Today the tower is open to visitors.[^1] The park lists hours from 8am until closing, and it says that climbing 80 feet brings you just above the tree line.[^1] The tower can be reached on the Northern Approach Trail, which the park rates as strenuous, from the Pine Hill Trailhead. It can also be reached on the Lookout Trail, rated moderate, from the Oak Hill Trailhead.[^1] Plan for the climb, and check the park's hours before you go.
