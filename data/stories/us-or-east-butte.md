East Butte rises in the Deschutes National Forest, about 31 miles southeast of Bend, Oregon.[^1][^4] The first lookout there was an L-4 cabin on a 30-foot wooden tower.[^1][^2]

Sources give slightly different dates for that first structure. A 1992 newspaper item, quoted by a local history site, says the lookout was built in 1932, and the firelookout site says fire detection began there that year.[^4][^2] A 1933 entry on the same history site describes a 14-by-14 gable-roof cabin on a 30-foot round timber tower.[^4]

Early on, an emergency lookout was stationed on the butte in August 1930.[^4] A standard garage went up on the site in 1934.[^4]

In 1959 the lookout, Sylvia Andrews, relayed messages to and from the Aspen Flat fire and kept firefighters informed as the fire front changed. She received a $100 award for that work.[^4]

By 1990 an inspection found the tower and cab had deteriorated and posed safety risks.[^4] In 1995 the lookout duties went to Fox Butte, the old tower was cut down in the spring, and the cabin burned.[^4] The Forest Service rebuilt the lookout as an L-4 gable-roof cab on a 20-foot wooden tower, which the National Historic Lookout Register dates to 1995.[^1] The Forest Lookouts history says the new tower was finished in 1996, when duties came back to the site.[^4]

The Register listed the site on March 1, 2003.[^1] A 2021 visit found the tower staffed, but nobody came out.[^3] A 2023 trip report calls the lookout active and standing.[^5] Its writers had read that visitors are not invited up to the cab, because of heavy traffic from people and ATVs.[^5]

Getting there takes some planning. Visitors park at a gated junction with NF-750, then walk about 1.7 miles of road to the summit.[^3][^5] The butte lies inside the East Fork Rock OHV Trail System, and one account says the OHV trail up the butte is closed from June 1 to October 1.[^3]

The sources also disagree on details. The Register gives 6,371 feet, while the firelookout site gives 6,400.[^1][^2] The Register places the lookout in Deschutes County, while the firelookout site says Lake County.[^1][^2]
