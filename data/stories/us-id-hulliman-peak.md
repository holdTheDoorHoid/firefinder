Hulliman Peak lies in Shoshone County, Idaho, about 24 miles north of Wallace, in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1] The lookout went up in 1934 as a 30-foot pole tower carrying an L-4 cab.[^1][^3] A crow's nest stood about a mile and a half to the north-northwest, atop a spur of Cabin Peak.[^1]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site lists Onnie Takkenan as staff in 1934 and Joe Rogers in 1935, and it gives L. Nemila for 1937.[^2] The first two entries cite Wallace Press-Times reports from June of those years.[^2] A Spokane Chronicle item from September 4, 1936, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, reports that Forest Service officials recalled the lookout, since rainy, chilly weather had eased the fire risk.[^3]

Two sources give 1964 as the year the tower was destroyed.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page gives 1963 instead.[^3] The tower is gone under either date.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the peak as a hike-in site.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit, or whether the site is open to visitors.
