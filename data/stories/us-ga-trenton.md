The lookout at Trenton is listed in the tnlandforms.us survey of Georgia lookout towers, which places it in Dade County at 34.87064, -85.53998.[^1] The list gives an elevation of 1,598, with no unit stated, and tags the tower with the code gfc-701.[^1]

Dade County lies in the state's northwestern corner. Trenton, the county seat, is the county's only incorporated town.[^2]

No source I could reach describes the site today, so it is not known whether a structure still stands there or whether visitors can reach it.

[^2]: Dade County, Georgia, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dade_County,_Georgia (accessed 2026-10-21).
