Wagener was a South Carolina Forestry Commission fire tower in Aiken County.[^1] The commission's first tower was erected in August 1930 near Camden.[^2] The commission closed its fire tower system on October 1, 1993.[^2]

The commission says most of its tower sites covered about ten acres and included a house where the operator lived all year, along with the tower.[^2] Starting in the 1960s, most of the state's tower operators were women, and their husbands frequently worked as firefighters.[^2] Towers were the public's point of contact for reporting fires, and they linked to firefighters working in the field.[^2]

The site record marks Wagener as removed.[^1] None of the sources checked describes the site today, so it is not known what remains there or whether the land is open to visitors.
