The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list names a site called "Bagley – first" in Oconto County, and it marks the tower there as gone.[^1] Its listed coordinates lie a few kilometres south-west of the Bagley tower that stands today.[^1][^2]

The tower that stands today has its own entry in that list, marked as standing.[^1] The state's historic-property record places it on Depot Lane, dates it to October 1939 and credits the Wisconsin Conservation Commission as architect.[^3] The register entry gives the height as 100 feet (30 m), notes a protected ladder on the outside, and says the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources administers the tower.[^2] Until 2009, the Department staffed it each spring during fire season.[^3]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the first site, or whether the public can reach it.[^1]

[^3]: Bagley Fire Tower, Property Record, Wisconsin Historical Society, https://www.wisconsinhistory.org/Records/Property/HI234013 (accessed 2026-10-21).
