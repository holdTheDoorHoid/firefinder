The Shanghai lookout stood in Clearwater County, Idaho, about 5 miles north-northeast of Pierce, at an elevation of 5,166 feet.[^1]

Its agency was the Clearwater Timber Protective Association.[^3] That association and the Potlatch association were formed separately in 1905 and merged in 1966.[^5]

Records date the first structures to 1915, a log cabin and a tree platform.[^1][^3]

A 40-foot steel tower followed in 1927, and a 50-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab in 1933.[^1][^3]

The 1929 tower is placed differently. Forest Lookouts lists a 75-foot pole tower at Shanghai that year, while firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts put a 75-foot pole tower at a patrol point a quarter mile southwest.[^1][^2][^3]

The association's combined 1955-56 annual report describes moving its VHF radio repeater from Bald Mountain Lookout to Shanghai, and says a power line was built for the relay in 1956.[^3]

The association's annual reports for 1957-58 and 1959-60 both describe a new lookout tower and detection facilities at Shanghai, so the completion date is unclear.[^3] The same reports say the line also powered the radio and supplied electricity for lights, refrigeration and cooking.[^3]

The record also lists a 44-foot tower from 1959. Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts say it was moved from Scofield Lookout, while Forest Lookouts calls it an Aermotor MC-40 and does not say it was moved.[^1][^2][^3]

A new receiver station was installed at the lookout in 1962.[^3]

The Forest Lookouts page marks the lookout as removed. Firelookout.com, Idaho Fire Lookouts and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's list say it is gone.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

No source we read says what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.[^1][^2][^3]
