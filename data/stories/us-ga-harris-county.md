The Georgia lookout tower list includes a Harris County Lookout Tower, at 32.77180, -84.90521 in Harris County. Its row is coded GA023 and files the tower under GFC number 305.[^1] The page's map is stamped as revised on March 22, 2016, so the list may not reflect later changes.[^1]

A page on the Forest Lookouts website reprints a newspaper item from The Columbus Ledger, dated October 29, 1955.[^2] The item says Harris County has an organized forestry unit tied to the Georgia Forestry Commission, as do many other Georgia counties.[^2] It also says the county's lookout tower operator watches over more than 147,000 acres, which it describes as the county's total woodland.[^2]

The item names the unit's two tower operators. Henry M. Koone was the operator at Harris Tower, and Idus T. Averett was stationed at William Mountain Tower.[^2] The item gives no location for either tower, so it does not show whether Harris Tower is the tower on the state list.[^2]

We found no source that describes the site today, whether a tower still stands there, or whether visitors can reach it.
