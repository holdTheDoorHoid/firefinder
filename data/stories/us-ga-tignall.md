A list of Georgia fire towers that tnlandforms.us published, last revised in March 2016, places a tower labelled "tignall" (list number GA263) in Wilkes County, at 33.84367, -82.74771.[^1] The list gives its elevation as 642 with no unit; read as feet, that is about 196 m above sea level.[^1] It also gives the tower GFC number 011, from an older Georgia Forestry Commission list that described 203 towers.[^1] The page says its author made up the names for towers numbered 73 and above, and GA263 falls in that range, so "tignall" is a label rather than a documented name.[^1]

Georgia has kept reducing the staff on its fire towers since the late 1980s.[^2] A Georgia Forestry Commission report from 1987 counted 93 towers with full-time staff and 73 with seasonal staff.[^2] A 2006 article drew on a forestry commission spreadsheet to report that 137 of the state's 144 surviving towers were unstaffed.[^2] The same article reported that towers were being sold at auction, and that some were sold as scrap metal.[^2]

No source describes what remains at this site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
