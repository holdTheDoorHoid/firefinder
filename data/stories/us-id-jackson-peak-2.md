This Jackson Peak lookout sat in Boise County on the Boise National Forest, about 10 miles southwest of Lowman, at 7,344 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

Records show an L-4 cab here in 1932.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts history gives its location as 8N-6E-20.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association classes it as a ground lookout rather than a tower.[^4]

The sources describe the lookout as abandoned and gone, and one says it was removed.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a separate, standing Jackson Peak lookout at different coordinates, so the two should not be confused.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at this site today. Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the location drive-up.[^2] Check with the Boise National Forest before you plan a visit.
