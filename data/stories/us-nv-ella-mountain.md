Ella Mountain rises in the Clover Mountains of Lincoln County, Nevada, about 80 miles north of Las Vegas near the Utah border.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register dates the lookout to 1962.[^1] Its cab measures 14 by 14 feet, has a catwalk, and follows a modified R-6 flattop design with slightly slanting windows, set on an eight-foot concrete block base.[^1] UTV Guide gives 1964 as the build year.[^2] It also places the lookout in the Spring Mountains, which does not match the register's Clover Mountains.[^1][^2]

The Bureau of Land Management's Ely District administers the lookout, and the register counts it among a handful of BLM-operated lookouts in the country.[^1] The register also says it is the only remaining staffed structure among Nevada's three standing lookouts.[^1] UTV Guide says it is typically manned from May to October.[^2]

The register recorded the lookout on May 29, 2003.[^1] The register and the Forest Fire Lookout Association both list the tower as standing.[^1][^3]

UTV Guide calls it an excellent destination from Caliente and says the rugged forest roads leading to it are best driven in a vehicle with high ground clearance.[^2] Because the lookout is typically manned only from May to October, check with the BLM before you go.[^1][^2]
