Nine Mile hill, in the eastern part of Langlade County, was the site of the first state fire observation tower in the county.[^1] A report dated July 27, 1928, which a lookout history site quotes from the Stevens Point Daily Journal, says a second state tower was then being started on Hulbert Hill, about 2.5 miles north of Bryant.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a gone tower called Ninemile in Langlade County, which is probably this one.[^2] The same list shows a Hulbert Hill tower in Langlade County as gone too, which may be the second tower from 1928.[^2] A lookout history index also files Nine Mile Hill under Lincoln County, which disagrees with the 1928 report and with the association's list.[^1]

The research found no source describing the hill today, so what, if anything, remains there is unknown.

[^1]: Wisconsin - Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, quoting the Stevens Point Daily Journal of July 27, 1928, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/wisconsin.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Wisconsin Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
