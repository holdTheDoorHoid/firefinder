The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list includes a tower at Winchester in Lee County, and it marks the tower as gone.[^1] The list gives its coordinates as 30.06944, -96.96694.[^1]

Wikipedia places the Winchester community in northwestern Fayette County.[^2] The list puts the tower in Lee County, while the community is in Fayette County, so the county remains unsettled.[^1][^2]

None of the sources read for this story describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Texas Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Winchester, Texas, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winchester,_Texas (accessed 2026-10-21).
