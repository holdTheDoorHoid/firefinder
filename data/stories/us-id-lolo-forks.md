Lolo Forks is in the Clearwater National Forest, in Idaho County.[^2] Firelookout.com places it nine miles southeast of Pierce.[^2]

Firelookout.com dates the lookout to 1935, when an L-4 cab sat atop a log crib.[^2] The Forest Service list gives the same year and design.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts also dates an entry to 1935, but calls it a camp.[^1] The two accounts disagree about what stood there.[^1][^2]

Three sources say ruins remain.[^1][^2][^3] The FFLA's Idaho list, by contrast, calls it a two-story cab and marks it gone.[^5]

The Idaho County list places the lookout at 35N-6E-10, which matches the township, range and section in the Forest Service list.[^4][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site drive-up, but none of the sources says whether the public may visit the ruins.[^1]
