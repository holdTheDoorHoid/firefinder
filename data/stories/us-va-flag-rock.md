Flag Rock is a former fire lookout in Bath County, Virginia, and the Eastern US Lookouts page lists the Virginia Department of Forestry as its agency.[^1]

In October 1929 a Harrisonburg newspaper reported that a steel fire observation tower was nearing completion on Warm Springs Mountain in Bath County.[^1] It was the fourth steel tower of its kind built in Virginia that year, according to State Forester Chapin Jones.[^1]

The same report says a 35-foot steel structure went up on Flag Rock, replacing an old wooden tower that had become dangerous.[^1] It names both Warm Springs Mountain and Flag Rock for that same tower.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list now records the tower as gone.[^2]

A 3.2-mile hiking loop listed by Visit Bath County starts behind the Milk House Market in Warm Springs and climbs to Flag Rock Overlook, which looks down over the Warm Springs valley.[^3] That guide does not mention a tower.[^3] No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the overlook.
