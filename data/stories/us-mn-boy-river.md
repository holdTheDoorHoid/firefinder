The Boy River lookout stood in Cass County.[^3] A 1971 map of the Boy River settlement marks the Boy River Lookout Tower.[^1] The map is based on 1969 aerial photography. It places the settlement at a key junction of waterways, where the Leech Lake River meets the Boy River amid wetlands and timber.[^1] The mapped land lies within the Chippewa National Forest, and the map's description says the tower once served as an important lookout point for managing the forest.[^1]

In 1972 the Department of Natural Resources re-erected the dismantled tower at Hillview, in Otter Tail County.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list marks the Boy River tower as gone.[^3] The same list has a Hillview entry marked gone as well.[^3] The Hillview page does not say whether the re-erected tower still stands.[^2] No source we read describes what remains at the original site today.
