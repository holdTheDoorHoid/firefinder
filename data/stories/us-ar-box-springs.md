The survey station at Box Springs, which the federal record calls Box Springs Fire Tower 97 AFC, lies about seven miles west and half a mile south of Magnolia, in Columbia County.[^1][^2]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey first observed the station in 1950.[^2] Its description gives a steel tower on four legs, carrying a cab at the top, with an overall height of 110 feet (33.5 m).[^2] That 1950 entry is the earliest dated record, and it already describes a finished tower.[^2]

The site page gives the Arkansas Forestry Commission as the agency.[^1] The Encyclopedia of Arkansas says the commission was created by Act 234 in 1931.[^3]

A 1950 survey note also describes how to reach the station.[^2] No source describes what stands at the site now, or says whether the public may visit it. Ask the agency before going, and never climb a structure whose condition is unknown.
