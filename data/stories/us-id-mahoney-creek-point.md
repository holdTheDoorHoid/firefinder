Mahoney Creek Point Lookout is a ground-level L-4 cab in Valley County, Idaho, about 21 miles southeast of Stibnite.[^1][^2] The Register places it in the Boise and Challis National Forests.[^1][^2] The cab was built in 1937, and firelookout.com says it is the only lookout structure the site has ever had.[^1][^2]

No staffing history is documented; Idaho Fire Lookouts says its records do not yet include one.[^3]

Its condition is less clear. Idaho Fire Lookouts dates its abandonment to 2000.[^3] Firelookout.com says the site has sat unused for several years.[^2] When the Register added the tower on October 8, 2011, as US 935 (Idaho 94), it called the tower abandoned and waiting for someone to take it on.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association still lists it as standing.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts files it as an abandoned, hike-in site.[^3] Access is unknown, since none of these sources addresses public visits or climbing.[^1][^2][^3] Check with the Boise or Challis National Forest office before going, and do not climb an abandoned structure.
