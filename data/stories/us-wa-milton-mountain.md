Milton Mountain sits between the forks of Wolf Creek in Okanogan County, on Okanogan National Forest land about 12 miles west of Winthrop.[^1][^2][^3]

The register dates the lookout to 1933 and describes an 18-foot pole L-4 tower.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the same year and height.[^2] A 1933 Wenatchee newspaper item describes a standard lookout house, 14 by 14 feet, on top of a 20-foot tower, so the sources disagree about the height.[^4] A cabin for the site was already planned in the summer of 1932.[^4]

A fireman-lookout named George Haase held the post in 1932.[^3] A July 1932 newspaper item says he had resumed the job, and WillhiteWeb says his fire season began on July 15 that year.[^4][^3]

The lookout was sold in 1951 and removed.[^1][^2][^4] The price was $1.00 in the register, on firelookout.com and on WillhiteWeb.[^1][^2][^3] The register says the tower was sold, while WillhiteWeb says the cab was.[^1][^3]

No lookout stands on the summit now.[^3] A 2022 visit described on WillhiteWeb found no lookout, some sun-dried cut stumps from the lookout days, a sign too worn to read, and loose parts around the ground.[^3] The page also mentions a spot in a rock pile that may be an old cellar or emergency shelter.[^3]

The land is Okanogan National Forest, and these sources describe no closure or permit.[^1][^3] The hike is long. WillhiteWeb lists a hiking distance of 10 miles one way, and its directions end at the Wolf Creek Trailhead. The site is isolated from any maintained trail.[^3]
