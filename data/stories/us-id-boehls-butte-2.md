Boehls Butte is a former lookout in Clearwater County, about 10 miles northeast of Elk River, at an elevation of 3,610 feet.[^1][^2]

The Forest Lookouts list gives the Potlatch Timber Protective Association as the agency.[^3]

A 40-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab went up in 1934.[^1][^2] A 1953 annual report of the association, quoted on the Forest Lookouts list, says the tower was entirely rebuilt that year.[^3] The tower was destroyed in 1972.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts marks the site as drive-up.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site today, and none says whether the land is open to visitors.
