The North Carolina lookout towers list has an entry for Round (Flat) Mountain in Jackson County.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page for Round Mountain names the Nantahala National Forest.[^2]

No source I read describes the site today, including whether a tower stands there or whether the public can reach the summit.

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, TN Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Round Mountain - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/round-mountain2.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
