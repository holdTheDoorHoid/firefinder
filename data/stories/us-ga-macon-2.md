The Georgia tower list places a Macon Lookout Tower in Macon County, at 32.37071 N, 84.01352 W. The list gives its elevation as 433 with no unit; read as feet, that is about 132 m.[^1] The page says towers numbered 1 to 72 are listed from the USGS name database. This entry is number 33, so its name probably comes from there.[^1]

The list's map was last revised in March 2016, more than ten years before this check, so its figures may be dated.[^1]

For context, the state forestry agency's history ties its first fire observation tower to a hotel in Waycross, possibly because of a 1928 forestry fair in that city.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: History of the Georgia Forestry Commission, Georgia Forestry Commission, https://gatrees.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/History-of-the-Georgia-Forestry-Commission.pdf (accessed 2026-10-21).
