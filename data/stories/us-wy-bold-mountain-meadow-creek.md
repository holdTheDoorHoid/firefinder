Bold Mountain Lookout is a cabin on the ground rather than a tower, at about 10,684 feet in Fremont County, Wyoming.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it under the name Bold Mountain (Meadow Creek) as a standing ground lookout.[^2]

The cabin sits on the Wind River Indian Reservation, about 10 miles southwest of Burris.[^1] The reservation is shared by the Eastern Shoshone and the Northern Arapaho tribes.[^3]

The sources I could read do not say when the cabin was built, who designed it, who has staffed it, or whether it ever reported a fire. No source I found names who owns or manages it, so treat those details as unknown.

Because the cabin is on the reservation, anyone interested should ask the tribes before going near it. The sources describe no public access and give no visiting rules.
