Latour Butte is a cinder cone in Shasta County, northwest of Lassen Volcanic National Park.[^1] The lookout on the butte is a 19-foot tower.[^1] The register calls it a CCC building modeled closely on one of two Forest Service plans, the L-101 or the L-301.[^1] The register gives the build years as 1935/1978 and does not explain the second date in its text.[^1] A photo captioned 1944 shows a BC-301 cab on a timber tower.[^1]

The register says the original cab was swapped for the state forestry agency's standard cab design, and that the catwalk, exterior stair and paneling have been replaced too.[^1] It says only the framework, the inside stairway and part of the second floor seem to be original CCC work.[^1]

The forest around the butte became a state forest in 1946.[^2] The register says the lookout station is not readily accessible to the public. It sits some 11 miles from paved road, behind locked gates, though that note is dated 10/91.[^1] The access door has also been removed and the doorway boarded shut, so the tower cannot be entered through its door.[^1]

I could not read the Cal Fire page for the forest, so I have no current word on staffing or condition. I found nothing in the register dated after its 2019 registration.[^1]
