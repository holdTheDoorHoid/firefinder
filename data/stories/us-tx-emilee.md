The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower named Emilee in Tyler County, at 30.83852, -94.27811, and marks it as gone.[^1]

The name belongs to a former community. Emilee, also spelled Emille, lay ten miles northeast of present-day Woodville, in eastern Tyler County.[^2] The community took its name from Emilee McClendon, whose husband, John B. McClendon, was an early settler.[^2]

A post office in the community operated from 1904 to 1946.[^2] Only fifty people were left by 1900, and the latest count on file, from 1948, also gave fifty.[^2] The settlement then vanished, though a few maps still showed it as Emille.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
