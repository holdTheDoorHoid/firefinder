The Kentuck Lookout Tower is in the Talladega National Forest, in Talladega County, Alabama, at about 1,192 feet (363 m) above sea level.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as a 100-foot Aermotor tower with a diagonal staircase, and it lists the U.S. Forest Service as the administering agency.[^1]

The register gives no construction date, so when the tower went up and who built it is not on record in the sources we checked.[^1] We also found no account of who staffed it, what fires it watched, or whether its use ever changed.

The register page notes a site visit on May 1, 2022. The tower was entered on the register on July 24, 2022, with Jason Johns as nominator.[^1] Its condition since that visit is not recorded in anything we could find.

Across Alabama, a 1819 News opinion column by Thomas Kaufmann puts the number of historic lookouts still standing at probably fewer than 75.[^2] The same column says many are unsafe and illegal to climb.[^2] The register gives no current access rules for Kentuck, so anyone curious should check with the Forest Service before visiting and enjoy the tower from the ground.[^1]
