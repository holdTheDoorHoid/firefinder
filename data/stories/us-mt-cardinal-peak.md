Cardinal Peak is a former fire lookout in Powell County, Montana, about 15 miles north-northeast of Seeley Lake, on the Flathead National Forest.[^1] The firelookout.com page gives its elevation as 8,551 feet.[^1] The Forest Lookouts listing places it at township 19 North, range 14 West, section 27.[^2] Those two lookout pages agree on the forest and the county.[^1][^2]

The firelookout.com page dates an L-4 cab there to 1936 and says the lookout was abandoned in 1955.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts listing marks the site as removed.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list gives coordinates of 47.38000 north, 113.36790 west and calls it a ground lookout that is gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
