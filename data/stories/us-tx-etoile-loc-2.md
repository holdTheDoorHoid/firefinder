The Forest Lookouts site lists Etoile under Nacogdoches County and the Texas Forest Service.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists two Etoile sites. The one labeled Loc 2, which is the site in our record, is listed as standing. The Loc 1 site is listed as gone.[^3]

A survey description from 1949, reproduced on the Forest Lookouts Etoile page, places a survey marker 89.59 feet northwest of the center of "Etoile Lookout Tower No. 6." The description refers to the tower as existing in 1949.[^1] It does not say whether that tower was on the Loc 1 or the Loc 2 site, or when it was built.[^1]

The sources we read do not say who built the tower, how tall it was, who staffed it, or whether it watched any fire.

A book-length study, *Fire in the Piney Woods* by Edward Cavallerano, covers Texas lookout towers, the forest service and the Civilian Conservation Corps.[^4] We did not have its text, so we cannot say what it records about Etoile.

The Loc 2 listing is undated, and we found no recent report of its condition or of public access.[^3] Check who owns the land before visiting, and do not climb a tower without the owner's permission.
