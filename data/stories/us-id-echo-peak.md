Echo Peak sits in Kootenai County, Idaho, within the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1][^3][^4] In 1934 a pole tower with an L-4 cab went up on the peak.[^1][^2] The sources disagree on the tower's height: two give a 40-foot pole, while the Forest Lookouts page gives 30 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site says it has no record yet of who staffed the tower.[^2]

In 1946 the L-4 cab moved to Twin Crags Lookout, a separate site in the same county.[^1][^2][^3][^4] One account says the whole 20-foot pole tower moved with it.[^5] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the Echo Peak tower as gone.[^4] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the Echo Peak site today.

A farmer bought the cab in 1963.[^5] A page about the Twin Crags tower says that in 2025 it was still standing with no cab.[^5] The Association's list calls the Twin Crags tower ruins, so the sources disagree about what stands there now.[^4]

Anyone planning a visit to Echo Peak should ask the Coeur d'Alene National Forest about access first.
