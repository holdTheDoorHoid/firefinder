Windfall Peak is in Shoshone County, Idaho, on the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, about 12 miles south-southwest of Clark Fork.[^1] The lookout was a 50-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, built in 1934 on a minor point to the northeast.[^1][^2][^3] The site is listed at 4,605 feet.[^1]

Three sources give 1952 as the year the tower was destroyed.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list places the site at 47.97872, -116.23877, and marks the tower as gone.[^4]

A September 2025 post on Idaho Fire Lookouts reports that nothing of the site remains, footing included.[^2] There is no tower left to climb.[^2]

The site is on National Forest land, and Idaho Fire Lookouts describes it as drive-up.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site in its Coeur d'Alene region.[^2]
