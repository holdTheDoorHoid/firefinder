The Baptiste Lookout sits on a ridge west of Mount Baptiste in the Flathead National Forest, roughly 25 miles southeast of Hungry Horse, Montana.[^1][^2] The current tower dates to 1964 and replaced a 1928 30-foot tower.[^1] Lookout use here began around 1917, and a 30-foot pole tower with a 12-by-12-foot cab went up in 1928.[^2]

The 1964 tower is a 30-foot treated-timber structure with a flat-roofed R-6 cab.[^2] It was staffed every year until 1971 and got little maintenance after that.[^1] Firelookout says it was used for emergencies into the 1970s and then sat abandoned for decades.[^2] In 2005 the lower stairs came out because of vandalism and liability risks.[^1]

The Forest Service assessed the site in 2010 and started rehabilitation in 2011, with completion expected in fall 2013.[^1] Volunteers began annual staffing in 2012.[^1] The register's timeline leaves the finish date a little unclear, and I could not confirm from these sources whether the stairs were put back.[^1]

Today the tower is staffed in summer.[^2] A 2026 Flathead Beacon feature says Mark Hufstetler, who started as a volunteer in 2015, now works for the Forest Service at the lookout.[^3] Because the register says the lower stairs came out in 2005, do not count on climbing the tower. Check with the Flathead National Forest before a visit.[^1]
