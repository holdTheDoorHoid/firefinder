A fire-tower listing on websiteservice.ca places the Cypress Hills Centre Block tower at 49 degrees, 39.742 minutes north and 109 degrees, 28.972 minutes west.[^1] Canada Lookouts' Saskatchewan index also lists a tower called Cypress Center.[^2]

According to Saskatchewan Parks, the Centre Block's amenities include a resort, a swimming pool, trails and zip-lining.[^3] The core area is reached from Highway 21, 27 km south of Maple Creek, and then 3 km west on Highway 221.[^3]

A visitor comment on a Firelookout.ca record for a Cypress tower in Maple Creek No. 111 says the tower is in the Center Block, not the West Block, and that its steel structure was removed in the early 2000s.[^4] No official source we found confirms the removal, and the comment may describe this same tower.

No source we found describes what remains at the tower site today, or whether the site itself is open to visitors.
