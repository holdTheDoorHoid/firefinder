Sharp Top is one of the Peaks of Otter, in Bedford County, Virginia, near Blue Ridge Parkway milepost 86.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts site lists the Virginia Department of Forestry as the agency for the tower.[^1]

A photograph taken on October 29, 1938, shows a stone shelter on the summit, with the base of a wooden watchtower above it.[^3]

A Blue Ridge Parkway guide says a fire lookout once stood on the summit and has since been taken down.[^4] The Forest Lookouts site and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list both show the tower as removed or gone.[^1][^6]

A hiking guide mentions the rocks around a stone shelter at the summit.[^5] The National Park Service describes the summit trail as 1.5 miles long, climbing 1,300 vertical feet, and lists parking and a trailhead among the site's amenities.[^2] No source we read says whether the shelter is open to visitors.[^2][^5]
