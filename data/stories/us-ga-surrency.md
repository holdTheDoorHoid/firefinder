A Georgia lookout tower called surrency is listed in Appling County at 31.72287 N, 82.18977 W, on a list of Georgia lookout towers kept at tnlandforms.us.[^1] The list's name for it matches the town of Surrency, but the list's author says names for towers numbered 73 and above are the author's own creation.[^1][^2] The list's Georgia Forestry Commission reference, gfc-802, belongs to a numbering from an older commission list that described 203 towers.[^1]

The town of Surrency, in Appling County, had 194 residents in 2020.[^2] The state legislature incorporated it as a town in 1911.[^2] It takes its name from Millard Surrency, an early settler.[^2]

No source describes the site today, including whether a tower still stands there.
