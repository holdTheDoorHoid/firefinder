Bunker Hill sits eight miles north of Stevenson in the Gifford Pinchot National Forest.[^1][^2] Panorama photos from July 13, 1934 were taken high off the ground, and WillhiteWeb guesses the vantage point was a platform tower.[^3][^4] WillhiteWeb also relays a recollection from Pete Paladeni, who manned the lookout around 1935 and said there was a small lookout house on the summit.[^3]

The register puts a cabin on the site in 1940, later succeeded by a 26-foot pole tower carrying an L-4 cab.[^1] Firelookout.com lists the 26-foot tower under 1943, and WillhiteWeb has it in place by then.[^2][^3]

In 1942 the site served the Aircraft Warning Service, active from March 9, 1942 to October 15, 1943.[^4] Improvements paid for with AWS funds added sleeping quarters and a woodshed, and afterward the Forest Service kept the site for fire detection.[^4][^3]

Sources differ on how the lookout ended. The register and firelookout.com say it was abandoned in the 1950s and is gone.[^1][^2] WillhiteWeb agrees it was abandoned then.[^3] Oregon Hikers instead says the tower was wiped out in the 1950s.[^5] The Washington forest lookout list marks it removed.[^4]

Oregon Hikers reports five foundation blocks still in place, though the summit is now completely covered by new forest.[^5] WillhiteWeb also mentions a benchmark, about 150 feet south of the former lookout house, sitting on a low rocky knoll.[^3]

WillhiteWeb lists the hike as almost two miles with a 1,200-foot gain, and gives the access as paved.[^3] Oregon Hikers puts the walk at 2.6 miles from the Trout Creek Trailhead.[^5]
