Kentucky Ridge is a lookout tower in Bell County, Kentucky, listed under that name on the Forest Lookouts site.[^1][^2] TopoZone's list of towers in Bell County also names a Kentucky Ridge Lookout Tower.[^2] Nothing in the sources checked describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Kentucky Ridge - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts (easternuslookouts.weebly.com), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/kentucky-ridge.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Map of Towers in Bell County, Kentucky, TopoZone, https://www.topozone.com/kentucky/bell-ky/tower/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
