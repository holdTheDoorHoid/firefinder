Sedgewick Peak Lookout stands in Bannock County, about 36 miles southeast of Pocatello, at roughly 9,170 feet.[^1][^2]

The Idaho Department of Lands had the lookout built over an extended period that the register dates from 1973 to 1977.[^1] Firelookout.com says work started in 1973 and was not completed until 1977, and it describes a concrete base with a frame cab.[^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts describes a two-story building with a concrete base and a frame cab.[^3] Forest Lookouts - Idaho also gives two stories, with a wood frame cab set over concrete.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts says it does not yet have a documented staffing history for the site.[^3] It also lists a 1981 entry reading "Emergency Status" without saying what that meant.[^3]

The register lists the lookout as US 1423 and gives its registration date as September 27, 2020, with Brad Eells as nominator.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives 2011 for the same step, which does not match the register's own page.[^3][^1]

The building now houses radio electronics and communications equipment.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts describes the site as a drive-up radio tower.[^3] No source we read says whether visitors may enter the site or the cab, so check with the Idaho Department of Lands before planning any visit.[^1]
