The Padus Lookout Tower is recorded in Forest County, Wisconsin, at about 45.495 north and 88.652 west.[^1][^2] OpenStreetMap has a point with the same name at nearly the same spot.[^2][^1]

Padus is one of several towns on the Chicago Northwestern Railroad whose origins lie in the lumber trade.[^3]

The Wisconsin lookout list marks the tower as gone.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Wisconsin Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Padus Lookout Tower, OpenStreetMap node 353894215, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/353894215 (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: History of Nicolet National Forest, 1928-1976, chapter 2, npshistory.com, https://npshistory.com/publications/usfs/region/9/nicolet/history/chap2.htm (accessed 2026-10-10).
