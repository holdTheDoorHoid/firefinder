The Lawson Lookout Tower in Marshall County, Minnesota, was built in 1969, according to a county list of fire towers and a Wikimedia Commons description.[^1][^2] The list gives the tower a height of 100 feet, a stair for access and a square cab.[^1] The Commons description places it among the final fire towers put up in Minnesota and says it was named for a prominent state forester.[^2]

The county list names two owners: the Minnesota Forest Service, which a footnote says is now the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources' Forestry Division, and a private party.[^1] The tower was auctioned in spring 2012.[^1] A photo of the tower on Commons is dated July 2011, before that sale.[^2]

The undated list says the tower stands and is most likely private property.[^1] A footnote says a private owner holds the tower and that the public cannot access it.[^1]
