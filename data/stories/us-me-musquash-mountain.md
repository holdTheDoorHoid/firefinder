Musquash Mountain, in Topsfield in Washington County, had a fire tower by 1919. The Maine Forest Service's panoramic map from that year is centered on it, and a 1929 map of the same site survives.[^1][^2] Maine's staffed fire tower network ended after a budget crisis in 1991.[^3] I found no source naming when this lookout was last manned.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^4] A visitor who wrote up a forum trip counted five communications towers on the summit.[^5] The old fire tower had a square cab with a round roof, and the writer found it in poor shape.[^5] The writer climbed a few flights, then stopped, finding many steps badly bent and the handholds too shaky to trust.[^5]

The writer parked at the base of Tower Rd, an unsigned steep dirt road near Musquash Lake, and walked up it. The writer thought a car could have made it to the summit.[^5] I found no source on who owns the summit or whether the public may climb the old tower. Ask before you go, and do not climb it.
