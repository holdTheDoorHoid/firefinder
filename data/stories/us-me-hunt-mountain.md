Hunt Mountain is in Penobscot County, in Township 3, Range 7.[^1] The state forestry department bought steel for a 48-foot lookout tower there in 1924, and a construction crew had finished the tower by late July that year.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's former-site register recorded the Hunt Mountain site in 2008 as US 113, ME 39.[^2] In 1931 the structure was relocated to Lawler Hill.[^2] A 1931 panoramic map from the Maine Forest Service has the Lawler Hill tower at its center, placed in Township 2, Range 6.[^3] The Lawler Hill site is in Aroostook County.[^4]

The register dates the Lawler Hill tower's removal to 1978.[^4]

No source read describes what remains at either site today, or whether either is open to visitors. Both sites are listed as gone.[^5]
