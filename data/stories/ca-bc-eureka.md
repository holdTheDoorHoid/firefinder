Eureka Mountain rises in the Monashee Mountains near Cherryville, to 1,968 m (6,456 ft) above sea level, and its summit holds a fire lookout tower.[^1]

A horse trail to the summit was set up in 1930, and the road was finished in 1953.[^1] The present tower went up in 1957 to serve wildfire management, and OpenStreetMap gives the same start date.[^1][^2]

OpenStreetMap names Recreation Sites and Trails BC, a government body, as the operator. It maps the lookout as a two-level building with a white roof.[^2]

Sources disagree on the regional district.[^1][^3] A road guide places the peak in the Regional District of Central Kootenay, while a map gazetteer places the recreation site in the Regional District of North Okanagan.[^1][^3]

The road leaves the paved BC-6 and runs 7.9 km (4.9 mi), with a climb of 739 m (2,425 ft).[^1] The road is unpaved and has steep inclines, so a 4x4 with good ground clearance is needed.[^1] It is usually open only in summer, since snow blocks it for the rest of the year.[^1]

The site is listed as a recreation site.[^3] None of the sources checked gives a closure or permit rule for the lookout itself.[^1][^2]
