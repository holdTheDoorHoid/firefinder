Forest Lookouts places Riven Rock on Jack Mountain in Highland County, Virginia, and describes it as a lookout station.[^1]

A Highland Recorder item from February 1928, quoted on that page, says the state and county station at Riven Rock was connected by telephone to the switchboard at Monterey and at Doe Hill, and that the chief warden asked local wardens to urge neighbors to respond promptly if a forest fire started in their neighborhood.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list gives the type as unknown and marks the site as gone.[^2]

No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors. Do not confuse it with Sounding Knob, which the Association lists as a separate entry with other coordinates.[^2] The Sounding Knob tower was dismantled in 2002 and has since been re-erected on Jack Mountain, but none of the sources read connects it to Riven Rock.[^3]
