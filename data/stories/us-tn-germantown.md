Germantown was a fire lookout tower in Davidson County, Tennessee.[^1] The Tennessee Landforms list gives its elevation as 870 feet (about 265 m) and its code as DAT001.[^1] It numbers the tower 6-7, and the page says those numbers are state forest tower numbers from a 1976 list.[^1]

Between 1933 and 1941 the Civilian Conservation Corps erected 98 lookout towers across Tennessee.[^1] A post in the Facebook group Dennis Wolf Fire Pictures says a fire lookout once stood in central Germantown, Tennessee.[^2] The readable part of the post gives no county or date, so I could not confirm that it refers to the Davidson County tower on the Tennessee Landforms list.[^2]

The Tennessee Landforms list marks this tower as removed.[^1] A National Register study of Tennessee's towers says many former Division of Forestry lookouts are either taken down or set for demolition, because they were no longer used or their land leases ended.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the Germantown site today, or whether it is open to the public. The Tennessee Landforms list says most tower locations are now private property.[^1]
