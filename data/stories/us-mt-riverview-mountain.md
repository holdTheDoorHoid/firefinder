A log cabin on a platform tower went up at Riverview in 1923.[^1] The site sits about six miles north of Essex, in Flathead County, at an elevation of 6,390 feet.[^1] The lookout's page places it in Glacier National Park.[^1]

The page says it was abandoned in the 1930s.[^1] The association's list gives its type as a platform and its status as Abandoned, and it places the site at 48.36676, -113.61652.[^2]

The association's list has a second Riverview Mountain, in Lincoln County, which it classes as a camp site and also marks as abandoned, so the two should not be confused.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Flathead County site today. None says whether it is open to the public.

[^2]: Montana lookout list, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
