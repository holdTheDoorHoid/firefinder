Olson Tower stands at the south end of Backbone Mountain, northeast of Parsons in Tucker County, West Virginia.[^1][^2] The Forest Service gives its elevation as about 3,736 feet.[^1]

The mountain had a fire tower long before this one.[^1] The state built it in 1922, and the Monongahela National Forest later took it over. It was used until 1963, when the steel Olson Tower replaced it.[^1][^2]

The register lists Olson Tower as built in 1963 and dedicated on September 29, 1963, in honor of Ernst B. Olson, a fire control assistant for the Cheat Ranger District who died in a 1961 car accident near Parsons.[^3] Wikipedia says the name recognizes 28 years of fire control and conservation service to the forest.[^2]

The tower is steel, with 133 steps up to the cab.[^2][^1] The cab is closed to the public, but visitors may climb to just below it.[^1] Wikipedia says the entire walkway to the top is accessible, which differs from the Forest Service rule.[^2][^1]

No source read names a fire this tower spotted.[^1][^2][^3] The register's staffing field reads "No" on one visit report and "Sometimes" on a visit dated July 31, 2019.[^3]

A visit report on the register, undated, says the tower was in decent shape, with some glass broken and a broken lock on the trap door.[^3] It also says the forestry sign marking the tower had been broken and spray-painted.[^3]

The Forest Service says the tower is open when roads are snow-free, and the road is accessible most of the year by dirt.[^1] From Parsons, the route runs north and then along Forest Road 717 to a parking lot below the tower.[^1]

A travel guide says the tower is open from mid-April to December, which does not match the Forest Service rule.[^4] The same guide says the state's first fire tower was built around 1916, yet elsewhere it gives 1922 for this tower.[^4] The Forest Service says the first fire tower in the state stood on Backbone Mountain and was built in 1922.[^1]

The register added Olson Tower on November 27, 2016.[^3] None of the sources read list Olson as a rental.[^1][^4]
