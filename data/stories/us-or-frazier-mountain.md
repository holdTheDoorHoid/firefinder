Frazier Mountain in Union County held a cab rather than a full tower.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says the cab was built in 1953 on a spur ridge northeast of the summit, and it lists the Oregon Department of Forestry as administrator.[^1] The cab was used for emergencies in the 1970s.[^1]

The sources part ways on what happened next. The register says the cab is now gone.[^1] A Rex's Fire Lookout Page entry says a private party bought it and moved it.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association says it was sold and taken away, and now serves as a private cabin roughly 20 miles southeast of Joseph.[^2]

The association's Oregon list records the site as a ground site that is gone.[^4] No source describes what, if anything, remains there, or whether the spot is open to visitors. The site sits at 5,444 feet (1,659 m), about 23 miles north-northeast of Baker City.[^1][^2]
