A Georgia lookout tower list places Marietta in Marion County, Georgia. It gives coordinates of 32.30146, -84.52572 and an unlabelled elevation of 702 (about 214 m if the figure is in feet), and it tags the site with the code gfc-308.[^1] That list was last revised in March 2016.[^1]

For context, the Georgia Forestry Commission's history says the first fire observation tower went up on the Ware Hotel in Waycross, and it suggests a 1928 forestry fair in that city may have prompted it.[^2]

The list is the only source we found that names this tower.[^1] No source we could reach describes what stands at the site today or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: History of the Georgia Forestry Commission, Georgia Forestry Commission, https://gatrees.org/resources/history-of-the-georgia-forestry-commission/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
