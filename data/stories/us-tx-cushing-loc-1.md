The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists two Cushing lookouts in Nacogdoches County, Texas, as Loc 1 and Loc 2, and marks both as gone.[^1] The association gives Loc 1 the coordinates 31.83592 and -94.85356.[^1] That point sits north-west of the Loc 2 point, at 31.76750 and -94.80694.[^1]

A Central US Lookouts index lists a Cushing site in Nacogdoches County under the Texas Forest Service.[^2] The entry gives the name Cushing with no Loc number, so it does not show which of the two sites it means.[^2]

As background, a Texas Escapes history of fire towers says the Forest Service started putting up metal lookout towers at key points in the woods.[^3]

No source I read describes what remains of Loc 1 at the site today, or whether the spot is open to the public.[^1]
