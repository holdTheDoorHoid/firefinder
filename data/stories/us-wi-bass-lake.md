Bass Lake's fire tower stood in the township of Homestead, in Florence County, Wisconsin.[^1] The state's Architecture and History Inventory gives 1935 as the year it was built, and a lookout directory says a steel tower was erected there that year.[^1][^2] The inventory's notes call it one of two Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources (WDNR) fire towers in Florence County.[^1]

According to the same notes, the tower had a metal trusswork base, stood about 80 feet tall, and had a small room at the top.[^1] The inventory record carries a 1984 survey date and says the tower had not been demolished.[^1]

The directory lists the tower as removed, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list marks it gone.[^2][^3] The 1984 record and those later listings disagree about the tower's status.

OpenStreetMap's note says the tower is gone entirely, and the map point sits at its former location.[^4]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
