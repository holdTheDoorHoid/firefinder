The Forest Lookouts website lists this Ashley County tower as "(Crossett ?) Fire Tower 1", with a question mark in the name.[^1] The question mark suggests the name is not settled. The National Geodetic Survey station on the tower is designated DK2075 and carries the name Fire Tower 1.[^2]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the station in 1939. At that time the tower was a steel structure whose glass-enclosed observing platform stood about 125 feet (38 m) high.[^1] The description puts the survey point 4.0 miles southeast of Crossett, and says the point was the finial on the roof of an Arkansas state fire tower.[^1]

The Arkansas Forestry Commission is listed as the agency.[^3] The Encyclopedia of Arkansas says the commission was created in 1931 and also put up many towers.[^4]

The Crossroads Fire Tower is a different tower. It sits north of Crossett, just west of Hamburg,[^5] while this tower's survey point is 4.0 miles southeast of Crossett.[^1]

No source we found describes whether this tower still stands, what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
