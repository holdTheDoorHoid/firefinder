The Texas lookout list calls this site Cleveland (Oak Shade/Tarkington Prairie). It stood in Liberty County, and the list marks the tower as gone.[^1]

A Texas tower table gives 1934 as the date for Cleveland (Oak Shade). It lists the manufacturer as EMSCO and the height as 100, with no unit printed. The table says the tower was removed from this site in 1960 and moved to Kenefick.[^2]

The Kenefick entry in the same table gives 1960 as its date, says the tower came from Cleveland, and lists its removal in 1983.[^2] A forest lookout list gives Kenefick's operator as the Texas Forest Service.[^3] The Texas lookout list also marks Kenefick as gone.[^1]

A map-based list last revised in 2011 still names an Oak Shade Fire Tower at these coordinates.[^4] That list draws on the USGS name database.[^4] No source describes what remains at the Oak Shade site today, or whether the public can reach it.
