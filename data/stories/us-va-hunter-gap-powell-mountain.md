The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a standing tower called Hunter Gap (Powell Mountain) in Lee County, Virginia.[^1] A map database lists a tower in Lee County under the name Hunter Lookout Tower, at an elevation of 2,519 feet.[^2]

Powell Mountain is a ridge about 60 miles long, and Hunter Gap is one of its named gaps, used by State Route 70.[^3]

We could not find when this tower was built, who built it, what it looks like, or who has staffed it. In 1928, Virginia's state forestry office launched a statewide program to put up steel lookout towers.[^4] Later, the agency built fire watchtowers at strategic sites around the state.[^5] We cannot tell whether this tower is part of either effort.

The pages we read do not say whether the land is public or private, or whether visitors may climb. Check who owns the site before you go, and do not climb a tower that is closed or on private land.
