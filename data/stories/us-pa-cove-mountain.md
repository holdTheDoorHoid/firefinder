The Cove Mountain lookout was a tree tower, which the Former Fire Lookout Sites register says was built in 1913 and was still in operation around 1917.[^1] A Forest Lookouts history describes the 1913 structure as a tree observation post 38 feet above the ground.[^2]

In January 1923, two Harrisburg Telegraph items quoted on that same history page dealt with whether the Cove fire tower could be reached just then. The first asked the question. The second quoted a forester as saying the tower could be reached, though the route would run through a foot of crusted snow.[^2]

The sources disagree about the county. The register places the site in Lycoming County. The Forest Lookouts page heads its entry Franklin County, and a January 1923 Telegraph item refers to Fulton County.[^1][^2]

The register says the tower is no longer standing.[^1] Neither source describes what, if anything, remains of the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

[^2]: Cove Mountain, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/cove-mountain1.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
