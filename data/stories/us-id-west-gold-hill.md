West Gold Hill was a lookout on the St. Joe National Forest, in Latah County, about six miles northeast of Potlatch.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 4,661 feet.[^1]

The tower went up in 1916.[^1][^2][^3] All three tower pages give that date for a 52-foot steel tower.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts describe a 6-by-6-foot cab and a ground cabin, and Firelookout.com adds that the ground cabin had living quarters.[^1][^2] Rex's page, citing Kresek, describes a 6x6 covered platform instead.[^3]

A postcard in the Latah County Community Heritage Collection, titled "Gold Hill Tower lookout," has coordinates about 50 meters from this site. It shows a tower with a couple standing at the top.[^4][^5] The collection dates the card to about 1920.[^5]

The tower was destroyed in 1967, according to the same three pages.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone.[^4] None of the four sources describes what remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
