Firelookout.ca lists a steel fire tower at Christian Valley, a community in Nipissing Township, Parry Sound District, in Central Ontario.[^1][^2] The entry gives the site an elevation of 310 m (1017 ft) and a total cost of $835.98 for the steel tower.[^1] Its coordinates are 46.0639, -79.551564.[^1]

The township is incorporated, lies on Lake Nipissing and belongs to the Almaguin Highlands region.[^2] Its communities include Alsace, Christian Valley, Commanda, Hotham and Nipissing.[^2]

A note on the entry says the site is now part of the North Bay district, citing an MNR contact.[^1]

No source I could read says whether anything still stands at the site, or whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2]
