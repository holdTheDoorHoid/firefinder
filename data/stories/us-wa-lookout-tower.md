We could not connect this record to a fire lookout. OpenStreetMap names the point Lookout Tower and tags it as an observation tower. An edit on the OpenStreetMap page, made over 11 years ago, lists it with park trails and benches.[^1] Mapcarta calls it a scenic viewpoint in Skagit County.[^2]

Mapcarta puts the Breazeale Interpretive Center 620 feet northeast of the point, or about 190 meters.[^2] Our own reading of the map coordinates gives the same distance.[^1][^3] The center is on Bay View-Edison Road.[^3] The Washington Department of Ecology manages the Padilla Bay Reserve.[^4] It describes a deck over the bay, reached from the center by a paved trail and a tunnel under the road. It does not mention a fire tower.[^4][^5]

We found no build date, staffing history or fire record for the point. The sources do not say whether the map point marks that deck or some other structure. The record may belong on a list of viewing spots rather than fire lookouts.

The reserve is open to visitors. Its trails and parking lot are always open, and the interpretive center is free to enter.[^5][^4]
