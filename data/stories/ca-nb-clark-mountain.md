A tower was built in August 1951 in the Trousers Lake area of Victoria County, in northwestern New Brunswick.[^1][^2] Fraser Companies Limited built it on its freehold land.[^1] The entry appears in the 116th annual report of the Department of Lands and Mines, which Canada Lookouts files under 1952.[^1] The report lists the tower under Fraser Companies Limited and its subsidiary, Restigouche Company, Limited, and calls it Clark Tower.[^1]

The report also says the construction was not reported in the year the tower was built.[^1] Canada Lookouts headed the entry Clark Mountain, while the report itself uses Clark Tower.[^1]

No source describes the site today, including whether the tower still stands or whether the public can reach it.
