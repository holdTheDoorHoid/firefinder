Rocky Top is a Lincoln County lookout site, but the sources disagree about its dates and its place.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page that the record links to names Lincoln County FPA as the agency and gives the location as 9S-11W-34.[^1] It quotes two Lincoln FPA annual reports.[^1] The 1951 report names Rocky Top among the stations in use that summer, and the 1952 report says it ran part-time in poor visibility.[^1]

The Osbornes Project's Lincoln County list also has a Rocky Top. It is placed at 07 S., 09 W., section 9, and dated 1920 to 1950.[^2] That township and those end dates do not match the Forest Lookouts page, and nothing in the sources says whether the two entries describe one site.

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes a Rocky Point site in Lincoln County at 2,890 ft (881 m). It dates a cabin there to the 1920s and says the cabin had disappeared by the 1950s.[^3] Firelookout.com gives the same dates for its Rocky Point page, which places the cabin 12 miles east of Lincoln City.[^4] The Rocky Top record links that firelookout page, but none of the sources say Rocky Point and Rocky Top are the same place. The register's point lies about 32 km north-east of this record's point.[^3]

Today no source describes what remains at either site, or whether the public can visit.
