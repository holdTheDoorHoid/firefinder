Meissner Point, in Columbia County, carried a fire lookout run by the Oregon Department of Forestry.[^1][^4] The register describes a 40-foot timber tower with an ODF cab, dated to 1953.[^2][^3] Rex's notes date construction to 1952, a year before the register's 1953, and give a total cost of $3,637.47 for a 40-foot tower of treated timber with a 14-by-14 cab.[^4]

In June 1967 the lookout went to round-the-clock staffing.[^4] A 1967 item quoted in Rex's notes gives June 16 as the start date and says the fire index had reached the medium range.[^4] The same notes record that a window was replaced and the lookout got interior and exterior paint in 1953.[^4]

Accounts differ on when it closed. Rex's notes say it was abandoned in 1971, the register says 1973, and firelookout.com says the early 1970s.[^4][^2][^3] The register and firelookout.com both say it was removed sometime afterward, though neither gives a year for the removal.[^2][^3]

No source I found describes what remains on the point today, or whether the spot is open to visitors. TopoQuest lists only a named locale at the coordinates.[^1]
