The Maxville tower site is in Perry County, Ohio, at an elevation of 1,060 feet (323 m).[^1] It was registered as US 1566, OH 12 on September 3, 2014.[^1] The register names it the Maxville Tower Site.[^1] Maxville is an unincorporated community in Perry County that had a post office from 1855 to 1904.[^2]

A Logan newspaper reported on November 22, 1941 that the tower had recently been finished on Isaac Danison's farm.[^3] The Forest Lookouts page files the same tower under the name Kehota and gives its agency as Wayne National Forest.[^3]

The Ohio list shows it as Kehota (Maxville) and marks the tower as gone.[^4]

No source describes what remains at the site today, and we found nothing about whether it is open to visitors.
