The Luce fire tower stood in George County, about 6 miles northeast of Lucedale.[^1][^2] A 1942 Coast and Geodetic Survey station lay 108.73 feet south-southwest of a U.S. Geological Survey bench mark that sat under the tower's center, so the tower was already standing by then.[^2]

When the survey returned in 1958, every mark was in good condition, and its new description still put the station at the fire tower site.[^2]

A 1971 visit by a state highway department crew described the base of the tower as dismantled.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts list for George County marks the Luce tower as removed.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors. The 1971 note places the survey station on property leased by a private person, so do not assume the land is public.[^2]
