The Sideling Hill tower stood in Morgan County, West Virginia, and the West Virginia Division of Forestry administered it.[^1] The register lists its elevation as 2,023 feet (617 meters).[^1]

The register says the tower was built in 1929.[^1] It was added to the Former Fire Lookout Sites register on January 26, 2017, on the nomination of Brian M. Powell.[^1]

The register also records that the tower was taken down later.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list likewise marks it as gone.[^2] West Virginia closed its forest fire towers in 1990, after decades in use.[^3]

Do not confuse this tower with the Sideling Hill in Pennsylvania. A tower there, in Fulton County, was built in 1923 by Pennsylvania's forest department.[^4]

No source we found describes what remains at the Morgan County site, or says whether it is open to the public. The register gives its coordinates as N 39.530833°, W 78.396944°.[^1]
