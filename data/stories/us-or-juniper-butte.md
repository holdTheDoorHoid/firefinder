Juniper Butte was a fire lookout site in Wheeler County, Oregon. It sits 18 miles from Mitchell, on the eastern side.[^1] firelookout.com places the lookout in the 1930s, without a year, and marks it abandoned.[^1] It names the Oregon Department of Forestry as the agency.[^1]

A table of eastern Oregon lookout sites lists a Juniper Butte lookout site at 5,535 feet.[^2] firelookout.com gives 5,531 feet, a difference of four feet.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site's type as unknown and its status as gone.[^3]

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there. Check with the agency named above before you go.
