Firelookout.ca lists a Bangor Fire Tower in Ontario.[^1] The listing's tower type, structure height and year built fields are all N/A, though a line of its text reads "Steel."[^1] The listing also says the tower appears on Pembroke topographic map 31F, third edition, from 1977.[^1]

Across the province, most of Ontario's fire towers were erected between the 1920s and the 1950s. They later came under the Department of Lands and Forests.[^2] Use of the towers fell during the 1960s, and a decision was then made to destroy many of them.[^2]

No source I found says what, if anything, remains at the Bangor site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Bangor Fire Tower, Firelookout.ca, https://firelookout.ca/lookouts/?slug=bangor-fire-tower (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Ontario's Fire Tower Lookouts: Preserving Their History, Ontario's Fire Tower Lookouts (independent site), https://forestrangers.github.io/ontariofiretowers/ (accessed 2026-10-20).
