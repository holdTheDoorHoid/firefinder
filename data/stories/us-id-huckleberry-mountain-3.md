Huckleberry Mountain sits about 20 miles southeast of St. Maries, in the St. Joe National Forest, at 5,662 feet.[^1][^2]

Sources disagree on when the first lookout went up. A fire lookout history page and a newspaper report give 1933. The Idaho Fire Lookouts site and a lookout index give 1936 for a 20-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^2][^3][^1][^5]

The tower was later replaced by a 20-foot treated timber tower with an R-6 flat cab. Two sources date that change to 1974 and two to 1975.[^1][^5][^2][^3] Staffing ended after the 1995 season, when the tower was moved to emergency status.[^2][^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page's known-staff list ends with 1979–1980, earlier than that 1995 end date.[^1][^2]

Years of vandalism and decay led the Forest Service to call the structure a hazard, and it went to the General Services Administration for sale.[^3] Bidding closed on October 24, 2013, with a top bid of $425.[^5][^3] The buyers took the tower down, and it was removed in October 2014.[^2][^3][^4][^5] The new owners say they will move the tower to north central Idaho and put it back up.[^3][^4] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page points to the owners' Facebook page for the rebuild, and I could not confirm from any source I read where the tower now stands.[^1]

What remains on the peak is ruins. A post on the Idaho Fire Lookouts site, dated October 29, 2024, describes some timbers from the R-6 tower and concrete footings from both towers.[^1] The site's listing carries a drive-up tag.[^1] Sources do not say who may enter the ground now. One news account says the tower stood on state land, while others name the St. Joe National Forest.[^3][^2][^1] Check who owns the land before you go.
