The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists a First Mountain tower in Centre County, Pennsylvania. It calls the structure a tree tower dating to 1917 and records that it has come down.[^1] A Pennsylvania lookout list also dates a tree observation post on this mountain to 1917 and gives its height as 35 feet.[^2] Henry Isenberg nominated the register entry, which was registered on September 16, 2008.[^1]

The same register also lists a separate First Mountain site in Clinton County.[^1] That one is a different structure. The Pennsylvania list describes a 30-foot wooden tower put up in 1911.[^2]

The register gives no coordinates for the Centre County site.[^1] No source I read describes what remains on the mountain today, or whether the land is open to the public.

[^1]: First Mountain Tower Site (Centre County), Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/pa/first-mountain-tower-site-centre-county/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Pennsylvania (A-F) - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/pennsylvania-a-f.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
