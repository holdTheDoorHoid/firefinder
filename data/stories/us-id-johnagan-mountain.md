Johnagan Mountain, in the Clearwater National Forest, is listed at 4,387 feet.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com places it about 20 miles east of Pierce and calls the structure a cabin.[^1] The Forest Service's list gives the same 20 miles east of Pierce and places the site in Section 30, Township 37 North, Range 9 East.[^3] Both lookout pages date its L-4 cab to 1934.[^1][^2] The University of Idaho's Keeping Watch page describes the L-4 as an early prefabricated cabin, 14x14, with production running until 1952.[^4]

The cab was abandoned in 1947.[^1][^2] A Forest Service-hosted list of Clearwater lookouts gives the same year, but it was compiled in 2016 and draws in part on firelookout.com, so it is not an independent check.[^3] Firelookout.com marks the site as gone.[^1]

No source describes the site now. Idaho Fire Lookouts files it under hike-in sites, but nothing says what, if anything, remains on the mountain or whether the ground is open to the public.[^2]
