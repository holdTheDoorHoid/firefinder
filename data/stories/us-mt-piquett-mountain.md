Piquett Mountain rises to 8,831 feet in the Bitterroot National Forest, in Ravalli County, Montana, about 20 miles south of Darby.[^1] A climbing guide places it about 5 miles east-northeast of Painted Rocks Lake.[^3]

The firelookout.com page says the site was established as a camp in 1921, and that a cupola cabin built in 1928 stood there.[^1]

The cabin did not last. firelookout.com says it was destroyed in 1966.[^1] Rex's Forest Lookouts page lists it as removed in the same year.[^2] The two sources agree on the year but not on the verb.

A climbing guide on SummitPost says the summit still holds what looks like an old lookout's remains, among them bedsprings, nails, hardware, grounding wire for lightning control and a good deal of melted glass.[^3] The same page mentions a hitching post for stock animals.[^3] The account comes from a climber rather than a survey, so treat the details as a visitor's report.

The hike starts at the Little Boulder Creek trailhead, reached by following the West Fork Road to Painted Rocks Lake and then Forest Road 1130 along Little Boulder Creek.[^3] Motorized vehicles are barred from the area around Piquett Mountain between October 15 and December 1.[^3] No source says the summit is closed.
