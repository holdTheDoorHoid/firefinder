Greasewood Hill is in Tehama County, California.[^1] The Forest Lookouts county index lists it as a ground lookout and marks it gone.[^2]

The earliest dated mention we found is from October 1941. A Red Bluff Daily News report describes two brush fires that burned 160 acres along the Saddle Camp Road. Fire crews from Bakers Camp and Antelope Valley headquarters brought both under control. The first fire started near the Bayless place, in greasewood growth. The second began at 3:30 close to the Greasewood Hill Lookout, just beyond the boundary of Trinity National Forest.[^1]

A California table of lookout sites marks Greasewood Hill with the suffix for a site where the lookout is no longer standing. The table's key says that suffix means the building was taken down, and that little or nothing may be left to see, or that the site's status is not known.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains on the hill today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
