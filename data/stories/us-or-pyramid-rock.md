Pyramid Rock is in Curry County, about 10 miles north-northeast of Gold Beach.[^3] Oregon Lookouts places the site in the Siskiyou National Forest.[^2] The register names the Coos FPA as administrator, and neither source gives a date for that arrangement.[^1][^2]

The register says a 12-by-12-foot cab was built there in 1934.[^1] Oregon Lookouts records a lookout constructed that year and notes that panorama photographs were taken on April 15.[^2]

In August 1935 Charles Hogue was posted as lookout to replace Martin Stenerson, who had taken a position with the Indian Service in Washington.[^2] Shortly before an August 1935 newspaper report, lightning came down the antenna lead, broke two window uprights and cracked the glass, but missed the ranger, M. Stenerson.[^2]

A May 1940 planning document says the post sat unmanned until July 6, 1939, and was kept up until September 30.[^2] In 1941 the post was staffed for 94 days, and its reports reached the Gold Beach station via the West Coast Telephone Company.[^2]

The register says the cab was removed in 1951.[^1] The firelookout.com page says it was destroyed that year instead, so the two sources do not agree on what happened.[^1][^3] Oregon Lookouts also marks the site as removed, with no date.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at Pyramid Rock today. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list marks it as gone.[^4] The register and firelookout.com give no access information.[^1][^3]
