Cougar Creek Lookout was an L-4 cab on a 10-foot log crib, built in 1934 on Challis National Forest land in Custer County, Idaho, about 34 miles northwest of Challis.[^1][^2][^3] An earlier log cabin on the site is dated to about 1930 by one account, and that same account says the 1934 cab sat atop a first floor of log cribbing.[^3]

On August 28, 1937, a newspaper item said CCC enrollees were working on a truck trail to the lookout, through what it called Idaho's primitive area.[^3]

A May 1965 report in The Post-Register said the lookout had last been used in 1942 and had since become a public safety hazard and a potential fire source.[^3] A ranger reported that the building, with its weakened sides and broken windows, would soon be taken down and the site restored.[^3]

All three sites date the lookout's destruction to 1967.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list also marks it gone.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it as a hike-in site and gives no route or distance.[^2] The site sits on Challis National Forest land, and no source states an access rule.[^1][^2]
