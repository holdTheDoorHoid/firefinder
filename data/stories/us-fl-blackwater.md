The Eastern US Forest Lookouts site lists a lookout called Blackwater in Santa Rosa County, Florida, and gives its map position.[^1]

Blackwater River State Forest begins with the same word. It is in Florida's Panhandle, northeast of Pensacola.[^2] The forest's headquarters is in Munson, about 20 miles north of Milton, on State Road 191.[^2]

No source found describes what stands at the lookout site now, or whether the public may visit it.
