The Summit Spring lookout stood in Union County, about 11 miles east of Union, at 6,700 feet on the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest.[^1][^2] The U.S. Forest Service administered it.[^1]

Both sources describe a 13-by-13-foot D-5 cab built in the 1930s.[^1][^2] An item in the La Grande Evening Observer, dated January 7, 1933 and quoted on an Oregon lookouts website, names a fire lookout station at Summit Springs among projects completed in 1932.[^3] None of the sources says outright that this station is the same cab, though the dates fit. A September 1935 entry on the same list records that panorama photos were taken at Summit Springs.[^3]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site on September 29, 2011.[^1]

The register and FireLookout.com both say the cab is now gone.[^1][^2] A third list, on an Oregon lookouts website, marks the lookout as removed.[^3]

None of the cited sources describes what, if anything, remains at the site now. Check with the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest before you visit.
