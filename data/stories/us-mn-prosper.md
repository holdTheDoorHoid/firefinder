The Prosper lookout is in Lake of the Woods County, and a photo caption on Wikimedia Commons describes it as the state's most northerly fire tower.[^2] Wikidata lists the tower within the Lake of the Woods State Forest.[^4]

The Central US Lookouts page gives 1963 as the completion year for an 80-foot steel tower reached by ladder.[^1] The same photo caption says the tower was previously called the Peet tower, roughly 30 miles south of its current location.[^2] None of the sources I reached gives the date of that move, or says whether the 1963 completion refers to the tower standing at this site today.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list shows the tower as standing.[^3]

The sources I reached do not describe current access, climbing rules, or staffing. Check with the managing agency before any visit.
