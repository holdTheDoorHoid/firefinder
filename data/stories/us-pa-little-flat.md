Little Flat stands on a ridge by Tussey Mountain, in Rothrock State Forest in Centre County.[^1][^3] The Register gives its elevation as 2,416 feet, while a State College writer gives 2,420 feet.[^1][^3]

The Register says a steel tower was built in 1922 to replace a wooden one from 1915, and that the lookout was registered on December 31, 2023, with Marc Burns as nominator.[^1] A lookout-history site tells a different early story, recording a tree observation post 25 feet above the ground in 1917.[^2] A May 1972 Daily News item, quoted on the same site, says the tower was constructed in 1922, went into service that fall, and cost $989.91 to build.[^2]

Later that year, a November item from the same paper says a woman was at work on the tower during the autumn fire season.[^2]

The tower is no longer used for fire watch, according to a February 2023 State College article.[^3] The first few flights of steps were removed to keep out vandals, so you cannot climb the tower.[^3] The same writer still finds it a scenic spot worth a visit.[^3]

You can reach it by forest road or on foot. The State College article describes driving up a dirt road and parking about half a mile away, or hiking the Spruce Gap Trail, which the writer calls one of the steepest in the area.[^3] An older 2011 account describes a gravel road that climbs Greenlee Mountain and ends at a parking area by the tower.[^4] The two accounts disagree on how far the walk is, and forest roads change, so check conditions before you go.[^3][^4] Mountain bikers also ride the trails here, and in winter snowmobilers cut their own routes along the forest roads.[^3]
