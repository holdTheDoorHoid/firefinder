Alberta's wildfire lookout dataset records the Keg tower as a steel structure built in 2016.[^1] It gives the tower a height of 30 m (98 ft), an elevation of 950.0 m (3,117 ft), and map sheet 84E/09.[^1] Alberta describes its lookout sites as isolated, and says staff who work them must plan for many possibilities, including encounters with curious bears.[^2] Each of the province's 127 sites has an observer on duty, generally from April to October.[^2] To prevent bear conflicts, Alberta says wildfire staff with Agriculture and Forestry have adopted BearSmart recommendations for many sites, key among them electric fencing.[^2] The dataset lists the tower as active and records a weather station there.[^1] Its access mode is coded as fly, which suggests the site is reached by air rather than by road. No source here says whether visitors may go up the tower.[^1]
