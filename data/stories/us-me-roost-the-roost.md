The Roost, listed in some records as Roost (The Roost), was a lookout tower in Batchelder's Grant, Oxford County, Maine.[^1][^2] A Lewiston Sun Journal table of Maine's towers counts twelve in Oxford County and lists this one among them.[^1] The table gives 1940 as its date and says the tower was removed in the 1940s.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine list places the site in Oxford County and marks its tower gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Maine fire towers: By the numbers, Lewiston Sun Journal, https://www.sunjournal.com/2021/04/25/maine-fire-towers-by-the-numbers/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Maine Fire Towers, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/me/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
