Brush Hill in Sherborn once carried a fire lookout.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes a 40-foot wooden tower there, first operated in 1920 and used until 1940.[^1] The register calls it a municipal tower, which means the town ran it.[^1] It gives the site an elevation of 394 feet (120 meters).[^1]

The town's own history says Sherborn had no official fire department until 1942, when Town Meeting voted to set one up.[^2] The lookout's recorded use had already ended two years earlier, in 1940.[^1]

A 2014 edition of the Bay Circuit Trail guide sends walkers southeast through the Sherborn Town Forest and up a gravel service road to the summit of Brush Hill.[^3] The guide describes that summit as having communications towers and no view, and it does not say whether the summit is the lookout's site.[^3] I found no source that describes what remains of the lookout itself, or whether the summit is open to visitors today.
