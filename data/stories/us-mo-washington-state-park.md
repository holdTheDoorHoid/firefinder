The Missouri Conservation Commission put up a fire tower at Washington State Park, in Washington County, in 1939 and 1940.[^1] The work was done with the aid of the Civilian Conservation Corps and the National Youth Administration.[^1] Reports disagree on its height: a 1939 report gives 67 feet, a 1940 report gives 68 feet for a steel tower, and a 1941 newspaper gives 70 feet.[^1]

The St. Clair Chronicle reported in 1941 that 1,017 people had climbed the tower in June, and that the tower was officially used to spot fires on forest land under state protection.[^1] A new tower was erected at what the record calls a better location in 1956.[^1] In fiscal 1981–82 the entrance road to the tower was moved.[^1] The page's last history entry, from 2013, records a sale at auction for $2,250 but does not say what was sold.[^1]

Today the park covers 2,157 acres and is open to the public for recreation.[^2] No source I found describes the tower or its site now.[^1][^2]
