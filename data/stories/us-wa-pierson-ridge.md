The Pierson Ridge Tower Site is in Ringwood State Park, in Passaic County, New Jersey.[^1] The tower that once stood there was first put up on Windbeam Mountain.[^1][^2]

The Ringwood Company paid for that first tower, built in 1920 to overlook the historic Wanaque Valley, and Section 2 personnel operated it.[^2]

In 1971 the tower was moved to Ringwood State Park, a job done with National Guard and volunteer labor.[^1] The Pierson Ridge register entry describes it as a 60-foot Aeromotor LS-42 with a 7-by-7-foot steel cab.[^1] A second register entry, for the Ramapo Lookout, says the tower was once called the Wind-beam Lookout and gives its model as LS-40.[^3] The sources do not settle which model number is right.

Vandals later destroyed the tower at Pierson Ridge, and the register does not say when.[^1] Its remains were taken to Ramapo Mountain State Forest, where the structure was rebuilt from 1983.[^1]

The sources I read do not describe staffing at the Pierson Ridge site. A 2007 visit report on the Ramapo tower lists it as staffed.[^3]

Today the rebuilt tower has stood at Ramapo Mountain State Forest since 1983, just south of the New York state line.[^3] The 2007 visit report notes that the paint is chipping in places and that directions to the tower are not signed.[^3] A later register page says the tower was still in active use at Ramapo as of 2014.[^2] No source I read says what, if anything, marks the Pierson Ridge spot now, and none say whether visitors may climb the Ramapo tower.[^1][^3]
