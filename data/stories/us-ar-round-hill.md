Round Hill stood in Searcy County, in the Ozark National Forest.[^1]

The Central US Lookouts page dates the tower to 1929, when a 100-foot steel tower was put up through an Emergency Conservation Works program.[^1] A Coast and Geodetic Survey description from 1954, which the page reproduces, places the station about a quarter mile east of the Searcy-Newton county line.[^1] The same description gives the steel tower a height of about 110 feet and a glass-walled house on top.[^1] That is taller than the 100 feet the page gives, and the sources do not reconcile the two figures.[^1]

The page says the improvements on the site were dismantled and moved.[^1] It does not say when this happened or where the tower went.[^1] No source says whether the tower still exists, or what, if anything, remains on the hilltop today.[^1]

The page does not say whether the public can visit.[^1] Do not assume the site is open, and check with the Ozark National Forest before you go.

[^1]: Round Hill - Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/round-hill.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
