The Maxton lookout is in Robeson County, North Carolina.[^1] An online listing of the state's towers files it under the North Carolina Forest Service and describes a new 120-foot (36.6 m) steel tower that the state erected in 1962-64.[^1] The tower went up on land the state had only recently acquired.[^1] The listing cites a 1964 state biennial report for these details.[^1]

No source I could read says whether the Maxton tower still stands, what remains of it at the site, or whether the public can reach that site.

[^1]: NORTH CAROLINA - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/north-carolina.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
