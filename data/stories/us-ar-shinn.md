The Central US Lookouts page lists the Shinn tower under the Arkansas Forestry Commission, in Hot Spring County, on Shinn Mountain.[^1]

A survey station on a wooded knob of the mountain was described by the Coast and Geodetic Survey in 1941.[^1] The description puts it about 10 miles, air line, south-southeast of Hot Springs, and notes that the knob is not the highest point nearby.[^1] The description calls the tower all-steel trusswork, topped by a lookout house under a peaked roof, and gives the whole structure a height of 80 feet.[^1]

The Encyclopedia of Arkansas says Act 234 of 1931 established the Forestry Commission, and that the agency built a substantial number of towers after it was formed.[^2] If the Commission built this tower, it dates from 1931 or later.[^2]

The Central US Lookouts page marks the tower as removed, with no date given.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the public can visit the knob.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Shinn - Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/shinn.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Forest Fire Lookouts, Encyclopedia of Arkansas, https://encyclopediaofarkansas.net/entries/forest-fire-lookouts-7905/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
