Chevelon Butte is a former lookout site in Coconino County, Arizona, on land now part of the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forests.[^1][^4]

A June 1913 item from The Holbrook News, quoted by the Forest Lookouts site, says the Sitgreaves National Forest had spent over $6,000 linking its ranger stations and lookout points to the supervisor's office, so the forest could communicate if a fire broke out.[^1]

The same item says work was just beginning on a tower south of Winslow, on Chevelon Butte, and that the lookout had a telephone connection.[^1] The quoted text joins two sentences without punctuation, and it does not say whether the tower was ever finished.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as ground rather than tower, and marks it gone.[^2] That clashes with the 1913 item, which speaks of a tower.[^1][^2] The Peakbagging table lists it as an LO site at 6,945 feet, read from a nearby benchmark, meaning the building was taken away and little may be left to see.[^3]

Today, no source I read describes what, if anything, stands on the butte now.[^3] None of the sources I read says whether the public may visit, so check with the national forest before going.
