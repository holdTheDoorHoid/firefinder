Wilbur was a lookout site in Morgan County, Indiana.[^1][^2] The Indiana Fire Towers site places it on the Hall topographic quadrangle, in Section 35 of Township 13 North, Range 1 West.[^1] The same site shows a 1955 topographic map of the Hall quadrangle with a lookout tower at Wilbur.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list gives the position as 39.51455, -86.50036.[^2]

The Indiana site lists the structure as not standing and says the location has no known link to a state forest or park.[^1] The Association's list marks the Wilbur tower as gone.[^2]

No source I read describes the site today, so what remains there, if anything, and whether visitors can reach it are both unknown.

[^1]: Wilbur, Indiana Fire Towers (by Mark A), https://www.indianafiretowers.com/wilbur/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Indiana Firetowers, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/in/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
