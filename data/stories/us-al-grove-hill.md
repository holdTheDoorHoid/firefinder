The Register describes the Grove Hill Lookout Tower as steel and places it at the Clarke County office of the Alabama Forestry Commission.[^1][^2] The sources read give no build date, builder or model for it, so its early history cannot be traced from them.

The Register lists the tower at 495 feet, or 151 metres, and records its registration on March 25, 2020, as US 1415, AL 74, with Andrew Zerbe as nominator.[^1] TopoQuest gives the elevation as 485 feet above mean sea level, so the two figures disagree.[^3]

Zerbe's April 2019 visit report rates the tower as in good condition for its age. It records that the tower was not staffed, that it needed paint, and that it carries several antennas.[^1] The report judges the tower probably spared the usual vandalism because it sits at an active Forestry Commission office.[^1]

No fire history, notable fires or later changes appear in the sources read. The most recent condition record found is that April 2019 visit, so nothing confirms the tower's condition today.

Today the Register lists the Alabama Forestry Commission as administrator, but the sources do not say whether the public may enter the office grounds.[^1][^2] Anyone interested in seeing the tower should ask the Commission first rather than walking onto a working state office.
