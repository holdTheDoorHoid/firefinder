Barren Hill is a patrol lookout site in the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1][^2] Sources disagree on when it went up. Firelookout.com, Idaho Fire Lookouts and Forest Lookouts all describe a 40-foot patrol tower built around 1934.[^1][^2][^3] The Clearwater National Forest list instead records a 10-foot L-4 cabin.[^4] A 1981 history by Louis Hartig, quoted on Forest Lookouts, says the Selway National Forest built an L-4 cabin on a 10-foot log tower in 1931.[^3] Forest Lookouts says its own sources seem to conflict on the construction date and tower height.[^3]

Three pages say the tower was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1][^2][^3] The Clearwater list says instead that it was last manned in the 1940s or 50s.[^4]

The Idaho lookout list marks the tower as gone, and Forest Lookouts says it was removed.[^5][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site Hike-In.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.[^2][^3][^4]
