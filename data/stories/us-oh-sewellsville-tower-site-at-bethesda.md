The lookout now at Bethesda, in Belmont County, Ohio, was moved there from Sewellsville.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the original Sewellsville tower as gone, and it lists the tower at Bethesda as standing.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register describes the Sewellsville tower as 90 feet high and lists the site as US 1571, OH 17. It registered the site on September 3, 2014.[^1] The sources we read do not give the height of the tower now at Bethesda.

We could not find when the tower was built, when it was moved, or who moved it. The sources do not name a builder or a date for the move, and they do not say who staffed the tower or which fires it watched.[^1][^2]

The association files Bethesda under a heading for "Relocated / Replica Lookouts." These sources therefore do not say whether the Bethesda tower is the original structure or a replica.[^2]

Today the association lists the Bethesda tower as standing. We found nothing about public access, ownership or condition, so please do not climb it or go onto private land to look for it.[^2]

[^1]: Sewellsville Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/oh/sewellsville-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Ohio Fire Lookout Towers, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/oh/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
