Horse Point Lookout stood in Idaho County, about 12 miles north-northeast of Elk City, in the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1][^4] The site sits at 5,750 feet.[^1]

The lookout went up in 1938 as a 30-foot tower fitted with an L-4 cab, set atop the northeast point.[^1][^4] The Forest Service list calls it an L-4 pole tower and places it in Section 34, Township 31 North, Range 9 East.[^3]

Firelookout.com and the Idaho County list both give 1950 as the abandonment year.[^1][^4] The Forest Service list gives only the 1950s, which fits that date.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association index lists the tower as gone.[^5]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site Hike-In.[^2] No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site now.
