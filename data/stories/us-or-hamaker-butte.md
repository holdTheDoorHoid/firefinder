Hamaker Butte is in Douglas County, Oregon, according to a 2023 USGS topographic map listing.[^2] A TopoZone entry for a recreation site in the same map area gives the same county.[^3] The Forest Lookouts entry sits under a Jackson County heading, although the same page's index lists the butte under Douglas County.[^1]

The page lists the Rogue River National Forest for the site and gives the survey location as 29S-4E-14, meaning township 29 south, range 4 east, section 14.[^1] It describes the site only as an emergency lookout.[^1] The one dated item it lists is a set of panorama photographs taken on July 29, 1933.[^1] A map summary places the butte on the boundary between the Umpqua and Rogue River national forests.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today.
