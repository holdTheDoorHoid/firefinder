Frenchy Butte is in Grant County, Oregon, about 22 miles south of Dayville, on the Malheur National Forest.[^1][^2][^3] The register puts the site at about 5,684 feet, gives its coordinates as 44.159820 north and 119.435900 west, and says the U.S. Forest Service administers it.[^1] Forest Lookouts lists its survey location as 16S-27E-26.[^3]

The lookout here was a trailer, not a tower.[^1][^2] A trailer was set up on the butte in 1954, and both the register and firelookout.com say it was gone by 1957.[^1][^2]

The register recorded the site on June 23, 2011, with Ray Kresek as nominator, and numbers it US 1063, OR 421.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left on the butte today, or whether visitors may go there.
