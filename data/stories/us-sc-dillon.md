Dillon was a South Carolina Forestry Commission fire tower in Dillon County.[^1] A Florence Morning News item dated March 11, 1936, as quoted on a lookout-history site, reported that a steel tower had just been finished near Dillon.[^1]

The same report named two men to the tower: G.W. Byrd of Hamer as towerman, and James L. Campbell, of near Dillon, as "smokechaser." It said they were on duty.[^1]

Statewide, the commission shut down its fire tower network on October 1, 1993.[^2] A Post and Courier item says the system at one point had more than 130 towers.[^3]

Today, no source I found describes what remains at the Dillon site, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1]
