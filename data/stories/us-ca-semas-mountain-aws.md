Semas Mountain sits in Monterey County, California, close to the hamlets of Bradley and Wunpost.[^1] Its elevation is 1,224 feet, or 373 metres.[^1]

The Fire Lookout index lists a Semas Mountain AWS in Monterey County.[^2] It gives the type as AWS rather than tower, and marks the entry as gone.[^2] Its coordinates are given as 35.88258, -120.82185.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts site has a page under the same name.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the summit today, or whether the public can reach it.[^2][^3]
