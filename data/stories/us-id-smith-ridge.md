Smith Ridge Lookout stood in Clearwater County, about 12 miles west-northwest of the Canyon Ranger Station, at an elevation of 5,462 feet.[^1] Firelookout.com files it under the Clearwater-Potlatch TPA and records a 15-foot tower on a pole platform, put up in 1918.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the same 1918 date.[^2]

The tower was abandoned in the 1930s.[^1] Firelookout.com says it was still there in the 1980s.[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts says it has not yet recorded any staffing history for the lookout.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association places Smith Ridge in its list of lookout sites and non-lookout structures, with the status Abandoned.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site Drive-Up, which suggests a road reaches close to it.[^2] None of the sources we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public, so check access before you go.
