Henderson Hill was a fire lookout on the Lolo National Forest, 11 miles northwest of St. Regis in Mineral County, Montana.[^1] It was an L-4 tower, built in 1937 on the northwest end of a ridge.[^1] The page gives the site's elevation as 4,335 feet.[^1] The association's list places it at 47.376 N, 115.321 W.[^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1962.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list still marks the site as gone.[^2] A separate lookout, Cameron Peak, also in Mineral County, was another L-4 tower destroyed in 1962.[^3]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it. Check with the Lolo National Forest before going out to look for it.
