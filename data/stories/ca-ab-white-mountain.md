The White Mountain fire lookout is a steel tower, 30 m (98 ft) tall, standing at 1,021 m (3,350 ft) above sea level.[^1] Alberta's wildfire register gives 2013 as the year the tower was built. It places the site on map sheet 83M/11 and marks the location as verified.[^1]

Alberta treats lookouts as a key part of wildfire detection, and sites them where the view is good.[^2] A provincial map page shows 127 lookout sites across the boreal forest and along the Rockies. Its listing carries a 2009 issue date.[^2] Canada Lookouts, an Alberta lookout index, lists the White Mountain lookout under a Grand Prairie Division heading.[^3]

The register lists the tower as active, reached by road, with a weather station on site.[^1] No source describes public visits, so treat it as a working fire-watch tower rather than a place to visit.
