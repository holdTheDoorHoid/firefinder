The Forest Service built a 20-foot pole tower of the L-5 type on Barlow Butte in 1933.[^1][^2] The site lies in Hood River County, about seven miles south of Mount Hood.[^1][^2] The Register says the tower was never manned, and Firelookout.com says it was never used.[^1][^2]

Oregon Lookouts notes that James Rittenhouse and Robert Snyder made panoramic photos at Barlow Butte on November 7, 1933.[^3]

Both sources say the tower was gone by 1949.[^1][^2]

The Register lists the site as registered on August 16, 2010, as US 568, OR 9.[^1] It records the site's elevation as 5,069 feet (1,545 meters).[^1]

None of the sources we read describes what remains on the butte today, or whether the summit is open to visitors.
