East Leake (Gum Spring) was a fire lookout tower point in Goochland County, Virginia.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association marks it gone, at 37.79403, -77.92526.[^1][^2] Its list also has a second East Leake point, East Leake (Loc 1), at 37.81278, -77.95300, which it marks gone too.[^1]

A separate FOREST LOOKOUTS page, under a Virginia lookouts heading, calls the point East Leake (Gum Springs).[^3] It places it in Goochland County and marks it removed.[^3]

The association also lists a standing tower at Rassawek, which it calls relocated East Leake.[^1] The list does not say which East Leake point that tower came from, so the Rassawek tower cannot be tied to the Gum Spring point from these sources alone.[^1]

No source we read says what remains at the Gum Spring point today, or whether anyone may visit it. Get the landowner's permission before going onto any land there.
