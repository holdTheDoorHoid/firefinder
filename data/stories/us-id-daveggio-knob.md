Daveggio Knob is a lookout site on the St. Joe National Forest, in Shoshone County, Idaho, at 5,882 feet.[^1][^2] Records list a camp there in 1939, and an L-4 cab with a patrol point on the northwest ridge in 1942.[^1][^2]

Both sources give 1963 as the year the lookout was destroyed.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the entry drive-in and destroyed or removed. The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a ground lookout that is gone.[^2][^3]

A June 2025 post on Idaho Fire Lookouts describes a visit to the site. The writers found a road blocked by a tangle of fallen timber, so they parked and hiked up.[^2] They found all four footings, plus other debris such as wire and metal pieces.[^2] At the patrol point, they could see no trace of its earlier use.[^2]

We found no source that says whether the site is open to the public.
