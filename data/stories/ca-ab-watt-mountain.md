The Watt Mountain fire lookout is a steel tower, 30 m (98 ft) tall, standing at 793 m (2,602 ft) above sea level.[^1] A protected natural area takes in the mountain's southeastern flank, roughly 25 km northwest of High Level.[^3]

Alberta's register gives 2003 as the year the tower it lists was built.[^1] The mountain had a tower well before that. In November 1959 the Peace River Record Gazette reported that a couple had stayed at the forestry tower on Watt Mountain through the summer.[^2]

The paper's April 1960 report said helicopters carried forestry crews to the Watt Mountain and Buffalo Head Hills lookout towers, since the roads could not be used.[^2] The same report mentioned that W. Carruthers, his wife and four children would live on Watt Mountain for seven months.[^2]

The register lists the tower as active, reached by road, with a weather station on site.[^1] No source describes public visits, so treat it as a working fire-watch tower rather than a place to visit.
