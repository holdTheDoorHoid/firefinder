The Silver Lake lookout did not start its life in Wisconsin. The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists this tower at Collins Marsh in Manitowoc County as standing, and notes that it was relocated from Silver Lake, Michigan.[^1] I could not find a build date, builder, or design for it in the sources I reached, so its first years remain unknown.

The best-documented part of its history is the move. A 2019 local outdoors post says the tower was taken down in the Upper Peninsula and that three trucks were used to haul it to Collins Marsh.[^2] RD Peters then helped put it back up on the site.[^2] The post does not name the Silver Lake site it came from, so I could not confirm which Silver Lake that was.[^2]

The same post set a dedication for Sunday, September 29, 2019, for the nature center and the tower, in honor of James Koeppel and RD Peters.[^2]

A 2019 post places the tower at the Neustadter Nature Center, 20021 County Road JJ in Collins.[^2] The post gives directions by County Road W from State Highway 10 or 151.[^2] It says visitors may climb the tower only when someone is on hand to open it.[^2] I found no rental record, and the sources do not say how often it is open, so plan a visit around that rule.[^2]

The association's county list prints the latitude as 48.08286, which looks like a typo for the 44.08286 on its state list.[^3][^1]
