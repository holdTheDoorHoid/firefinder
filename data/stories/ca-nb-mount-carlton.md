The Mount Carlton lookout is on a mountain in New Brunswick, now inside Mount Carleton Provincial Park.[^1][^2][^3] Sources spell the name both ways.[^1][^2][^3] The register uses Carlton, while the trail guide and the 1923 newspaper item use Carleton.[^1][^2][^3] The register gives the elevation as 808 m (2,650 ft).[^1]

The first lookout was a log cabin, erected in 1923.[^2][^3] A 1923 newspaper item, quoted on Canada Lookouts, described a cabin on Mount Carleton as the highest elevation yet reached by a lookout in the province, at 823 m (2,700 ft).[^2] A later Department of Lands and Mines report gives 822 m (2,696 ft) above sea level for the same site, which does not match the register's figure.[^2] The register dates the tower to the early 1920s.[^1] A trail page says the tower was used in the early 1900s, which does not fit the 1923 records.[^3]

John Hanna was the sole towerman for many years after the program began in 1923.[^3] Later, two men were posted for safety and isolation, and once radios improved the post went back to one operator.[^3] Towermen were expected by May 15 and worked through a fire season that lasted into October.[^3] The second man used a crank telephone to alert the fire warden at Plaster Rock.[^3] The cabin served fire rangers as a lookout for 45 years.[^3]

In 1939 a project reported by the Department of Lands and Mines replaced the log cabin with a modern frame cupola cabin fitted with improved equipment.[^2] A later annual report says the road to the station was improved with help from the Murphy Lumber Company, in 1955.[^2] Staffing ended after 1968, when airplane patrols took over the work.[^3]

The trail guide places a fire tower on the peak.[^3] A cabin about a third of the way down the mountain, it says, served the towermen when they were off the peak.[^3] The guide calls that old cabin a historical monument to earlier forest fire detection in New Brunswick.[^3]

The trail is a 9.3 km loop that starts near a parking area in the park.[^3] From the front gate, the guide says, drive 5.3 km, turn right, then drive 7.9 km to the parking area.[^3] Park entry is cash only.[^3]
