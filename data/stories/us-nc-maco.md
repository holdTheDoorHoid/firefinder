The Maco tower appears on the North Carolina lookout-tower list for Brunswick County, at coordinates 34.28833 and -78.15666.[^1]

A photograph taken in February 1939 shows the entrance to the Maco fire lookout tower. Its caption credits towerman Earnest Jones with both painting and designing that entrance.[^2] The photo's credit line goes to the Forest History Society, which says it comes from the North Carolina Forest Service's photograph collection.[^2]

A North Carolina groundwater monitoring map of the Maco site, dated March 14, 2022, places the site west of Lake Sutton in Brunswick County and labels a fire tower there alongside a USGS monument.[^3] The map says it is for information only and gives no permission to enter the lands it depicts.[^3]

No source we checked describes what stands there now. Since the map gives no permission to enter the land it depicts, visitors should stay off the site.[^3]
