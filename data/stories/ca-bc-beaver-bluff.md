Beaver Bluff is a knoll that forms part of Allard Mountain, in the Kitimat-Stikine area of British Columbia.[^1] In August 1966 the Terrace "Omineca" Herald reported that George Dupont kept watch from the lookout building, which stood about 914 m (3,000 ft) above the valley.[^1]

From there he could survey the whole Kalum Lake area.[^1] His kit included a two-way radio, a 60-power telescope and three pairs of binoculars.[^1] When he saw smoke, he took a range reading and passed it on to the Terrace Fire Control Centre.[^1]

Most of the year's food came in by helicopter, and George went into Terrace now and then to buy fresh meat and produce.[^1]

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
