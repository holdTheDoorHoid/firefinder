A lookout tower called Moose once stood in Iron County, Wisconsin.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list places it at 46.22096, -90.25861 and marks it gone.[^1]

No source describes the site now, so what, if anything, remains there, and whether the land is open to visitors, is not known.

[^1]: Wisconsin Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
