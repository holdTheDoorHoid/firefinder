The register describes the Pattison Lookout as a 100-foot tower with a stairway in the US Forest Service style. It names McClintock-Marshall Steel Company, a Bethlehem Steel subsidiary, as the manufacturer and gives 1934 as the build year.[^1] The Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources administered it.[^1] The register lists the lookout as US 1241, WI 36 and gives its elevation as 1,216 feet (371 m).[^1]

The register recorded the lookout on August 28, 2017.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Douglas County tower as gone.[^2] The register's description uses the present tense, which does not match that list.[^1][^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
