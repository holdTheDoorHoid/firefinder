The statewide list of Georgia lookout towers has an "alapaha" tower in Berrien County, at 286 feet (87 m) elevation.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site has a page under the same name and county.[^2]

By our calculation, the list's point sits about 1 km south of the published coordinates for the town of Alapaha, which lies in Berrien County beside the Alapaha River.[^1][^3]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
