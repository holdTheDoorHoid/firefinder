Cass County, Minnesota, had a federal fire tower called Whipholt, put up after 1935 at a site the county survey places south of the hamlet and Leech Lake.[^1] The survey lists its owner as the U.S. Forest Service and describes it as a 100-foot stairway tower.[^1]

The survey also says the tower was sold off and taken down in the 1972 Chippewa National Forest tower sale, as other towers in that forest were.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list also marks the tower as gone.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public.

[^1]: Cass County Minnesota Fire Lookout Towers, Wikimedia Commons, https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/29/Cass_County_Towers.pdf (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Minnesota Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
