Eureka Hill is a former lookout in Lincoln County, Montana, about two miles south of the town of Eureka, in the Kootenai National Forest.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 3,175 feet.[^1] That page is a short entry of a few lines, and it is the main source we found for this lookout.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list also gives coordinates for the site.[^2]

Firelookout.com lists a tower here under 1934.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks it as a tower that is gone.[^2]

Firelookout.com says the tower was abandoned in 1948.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today. Nothing we read says whether the site is open to the public, so treat it as unverified and check with the Kootenai National Forest before visiting.

[^1]: Eureka Hill Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com (Rex Kamstra), https://www.firelookout.com/mt/eurekahill.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
