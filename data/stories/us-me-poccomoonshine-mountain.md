A fire lookout once stood on Pocomoonshine Mountain, in Princeton, Washington County, Maine.[^1] A 1919 Maine Forest Service map is a full-circle panorama with the mountain's fire tower at its center.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association spells the name with a double c.[^2]

By 1917 the state had a wooden tower there, 58 feet tall, according to the annual report of the Forest Commissioner, as quoted on a Maine lookout list.[^3]

In 1934 two new steel towers, each 60 feet tall, went up with help from the Civilian Conservation Corps.[^3] One of them stood on Pocomoonshine Mountain.[^3] The report says the new towers were 15 feet higher than the old wooden ones, but that does not match its own figures: 60 feet is only 2 feet taller than the 58 it gives for the old tower.[^3]

The association's county list now marks the tower as gone.[^2] No source I could read describes what remains on the mountain today, or whether the site is open to the public.
