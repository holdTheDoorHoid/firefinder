Camp Ten was a lookout tower in Lincoln County, Wisconsin, near 45.54854 north and 89.50997 west.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.

[^1]: Wisconsin Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Camp Ten, Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/camp-ten.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
