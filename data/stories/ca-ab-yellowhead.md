Alberta's wildfire register describes the Yellowhead tower as a steel tower, 30 m (98 ft) tall, with an elevation of about 1,477 m (4,846 ft).[^1] The register gives 2012 as the year it was built.[^1]

Alberta counts 127 wildfire lookout sites across the province.[^2] Alberta says lookouts are placed at spots with good visibility, which helps staff detect and report fires.[^3] Each site has a lookout observer who watches for fire through the day, generally from April to October.[^2]

The register lists the tower as active, reached by road, with a weather station.[^1] None of the sources read describes public visits, so treat it as a working fire-watch tower rather than a place to visit.[^1][^2]
