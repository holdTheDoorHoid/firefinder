The Newton Firetower stands in Mitchell County, Georgia.[^1] The Register gives its elevation as 166 feet (51 m).[^1] The Georgia Forestry Commission administers the tower.[^1]

The Register's Built field gives 1940, although its description says the tower went up at some point between 1935 and 1940.[^1] It is an 80-foot Aermotor LS-40, a galvanized steel tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab.[^1] The Register does not say who first staffed it or what it was built to watch.[^1]

The Register says that 20 or 30 Georgia towers are still occasionally manned, and this one has a part-time operational forestry station at its base.[^1] It names no fire the tower watched and no past lookout.[^1]

The Register added the tower on January 17, 2013.[^1] Its description says the tower is still occasionally manned, and the Register records no removal, relocation or closure.[^1]

The Register gives no visiting terms.[^1] It lists the Georgia Forestry Commission's Flint District as a cooperator, so ask that office before visiting.[^1]

[^1]: Newton Firetower, National Historic Lookout Register (nhlr.org), http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ga/newton-firetower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
