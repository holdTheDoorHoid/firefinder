Rockridge is a lookout tower site in Heard County, Georgia.[^1] It appears on a numbered list of Georgia lookout towers kept on the tnlandforms.us website, which gives each entry a name, coordinates, county and elevation.[^1] The Rockridge entry places the site at 33.30015, -85.13110, with an elevation of 988 and no unit printed (988 feet would be about 301 m).[^1] The entry also carries the code gfc-416 in its last field, which the page's header labels "GFC number"; the page says nothing more about the code.[^1] The map on that page was last marked as revised on 22 March 2016.[^1]

No source found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it. Check who owns the land before going, and do not climb any structure you find there without permission.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
