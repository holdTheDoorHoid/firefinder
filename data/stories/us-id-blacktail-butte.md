Blacktail Butte sits about six miles east-southeast of Grangeville, Idaho, in the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives its elevation as 4,600 feet.[^2]

In the 1930s an L-4 cab stood on a tower at the west end of the ridge.[^3][^2] The Forest Lookouts page marks that date with a question mark, so the exact year is uncertain.[^3]

A patrol tower stood about two miles northeast, atop North Blacktail.[^4][^2]

Sources agree that the lookout was abandoned around 1948.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the tower as gone.[^6]

Pages for a different lookout, Blacktail (Frisco Peak), say a log cabin stood atop Blacktail Butte in 1926.[^5] The Forest Service list puts that cabin in a separate Blacktail row, not the Blacktail Butte row, so this history starts with the 1930s tower.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in and marks the tower as abandoned and gone.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the butte today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
