Hornet Ridge is a former lookout site in the Kootenai National Forest, about 16 miles east of Libby in Lincoln County, Montana.[^1] The site sits at an elevation of 5,452 feet.[^1]

A 40-foot pole tower of the L-4 type went up there around 1937.[^1] The photograph on the lookout's page is credited to the Fire Lookout Museum.[^1]

The lookout's page dates it from about 1937, with the end year left as a question mark, and says the tower has since departed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the site in Lincoln County at latitude 48.38048 and longitude -115.16481, and lists the tower as gone.[^2]

No source describes the site today, including what remains there or whether the public can reach it. Anyone planning a trip should check with the Kootenai National Forest first.
