Honey Jones Peak lies in the St. Joe National Forest, in Shoshone County, about 12 miles east-northeast of St. Maries, at an elevation of 4,440 feet.[^1] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page gives the same elevation.[^2]

In the 1930s the peak had a crow's nest toward the southeast end of its ridge, a little under the summit.[^1][^2] The association's statewide list types the site the same way.[^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page places it in the same national forest.[^2]

Both site pages list the lookout as abandoned, and the association's statewide list agrees.[^1][^2][^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the peak today.[^1][^2][^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page calls the site a hike-in, and no source says whether the ground is open to visitors.[^2]
