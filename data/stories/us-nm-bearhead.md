Bearhead Peak lies in Sandoval County, New Mexico, on Santa Fe National Forest land.[^1]

A federal survey record dated to 1931, reproduced on the Forest Lookouts site, puts a fire lookout tower about 38 feet high on the peak's highest point, about 2.5 miles south of Bland.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's New Mexico directory lists the Bearhead site as a crow's nest, not a tower, and marks it gone.[^2] The 1931 description and the directory do not match, and the sources do not say which is right.[^1][^2]

The Forest Service's Bearhead Peak Trail, Forest Trail 9, is a 0.5-mile route from the Bearhead Ridge Trail up to the peak.[^3] The Forest Service rates it medium difficulty and allows hiking only.[^3] The agency reports no upkeep on the route since the 2011 Las Conchas Fire.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today.[^3]
