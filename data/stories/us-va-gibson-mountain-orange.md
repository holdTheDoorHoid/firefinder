Gibson Mountain (Orange) was a lookout tower in Orange County, Virginia.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it at 38.18744, -78.15752 and marks it as gone.[^1] Another Virginia lookout list also places it in Orange County.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Virginia - Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Virginia - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/virginia.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
