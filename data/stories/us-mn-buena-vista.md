The Buena Vista lookout stood in Beltrami County, Minnesota. The county survey dates the tower to 1931.[^1] It was an 81-foot ladder tower of Aermotor make, and the Minnesota Forest Service owned it.[^1]

A 1960 survey note describes a steel tower on four legs, topped by a metal observation room.[^2] That note gives the overall height as 85 feet, while the survey table lists 81 feet.[^1][^2] We use the table figure in our records.

The county survey marks the tower as removed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association index lists the site as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today.
