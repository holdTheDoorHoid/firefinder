Wikipedia's list of Louisiana lookout towers places the Lorraine fire tower in Tangipahoa Parish, with Lorraine as its nearest town.[^1] Lorraine is an unincorporated community about 15 miles west of Covington.[^2] We found no build date, builder or height for the tower.

The tower is one of the state's network of lookouts. Louisiana's first steel fire tower went up near Bogalusa in 1922, and by 1943 thirty-nine towers had been built.[^1] No source we read gives Lorraine's construction date, so its place in that run is unknown.

The list says the tower is still standing. It also says the tower looks disused or retired and is visible on Google Street View, and it cites no agency record for the tower.[^1] We found no record of who owns the site, who staffed the tower, or whether it ever saw a fire.

Its current use and public access are unknown, so we cannot say whether a visitor may approach it. Do not climb it.
