The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists two lookout sites called Yellowpine in Sabine County, Texas, labelled Loc 1 and Loc 2. Both are marked gone.[^1]

Loc 1 is listed at latitude 31.25794 and longitude -93.82197.[^1] Yellowpine itself is a community where Farm Road 2426 meets State Highway 87, about five miles south of Hemphill, inside the Sabine National Forest.[^2] Its post office opened in 1902 and stayed open until the early 1950s.[^2] The Handbook of Texas estimates the population at seventy-four from 1968 through 2000.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Loc 1 site today, or whether the public can visit it.[^1][^2] Before going to look, check with the land manager, and do not enter closed or private ground.

[^1]: Texas Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Yellowpine, TX, Texas State Historical Association, Handbook of Texas Online, https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/yellowpine-tx (accessed 2026-10-21).
