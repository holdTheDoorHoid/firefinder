Jug Rock Lookout stood in Shoshone County on the St. Joe National Forest, about 16 miles south of Avery, at an elevation of 6,530 feet.[^1]

The first structure was a log cabin about three-quarters of a mile west of the lookout site, listed for 1915.[^1][^3] Two pages say a trapper built the original cabin in the 1890s.[^1][^2] A platform tower followed in 1919.[^1][^3] By 1939 the site had an L-4 tower.[^1][^3]

The firelookout.com page gives 1954 as the year the lookout was gone, and the Idaho Fire Lookouts page agrees.[^1][^2] A Forest Fire Lookout Association list also marks the tower as gone.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site as a hike-in, with the status abandoned and gone.[^2]

The site lies on national forest land.[^1] No source we found describes what, if anything, remains there today.
