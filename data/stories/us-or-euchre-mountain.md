Euchre Mountain is in Lincoln County, Oregon, about 16 miles northeast of Newport, north of Siletz.[^1][^2]

For several years the Lincoln County Fire Patrol Association used a tree as its lookout. By July 1941 the association said the tree had decayed badly and was unsafe to climb.[^2] The same item says a state forestry crew was building a truck trail to the summit, and a Civilian Conservation Corps camp was making the 100-foot tower.[^2]

Firelookout.com dates the 100-foot tower with a 7-by-7 cab to 1941.[^1] The association's annual report labeled 1942 says the tower was erected that year, so the sources disagree on the year.[^2]

In 1951 the lookout was staffed from April 11 to detect fires during an emergency. A permanent lookout took over on April 25, and the station ran until September 29.[^2]

In 1965 the association's report records a new lookout tower, at a cost of $1,067.58.[^2] Firelookout.com describes a 34-foot live-in tower built from the bottom half of the Norton Hill lookout, and says Euchre Mountain was abandoned in 1968.[^1]

A 1966 report says the lookout made three first discoveries that season, meaning it was the first to spot three fires.[^2]

A 1971 Lincoln County Leader item said the tower had gained national fame after the writer Marcella Coy described her time there as a "fire lady." The same item says the West Oregon Forestry Department used the tower each summer.[^2]

In January 1971 the same newspaper item reported that recent high winds apparently were too much for the tower. It did not describe the damage.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^3] We found no source that describes what remains at the summit today, or whether the road and summit are open to the public. Anyone planning a visit should check with the Oregon Department of Forestry first.
