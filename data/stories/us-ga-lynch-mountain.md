Lynch Mountain sits directly east of Sautee, in the northeast of White County.[^4] A fire tower stood on it by about 1911.[^2] A photograph of that tower, held by the Georgia Archives and shared through the Digital Library of Georgia, has a field-note caption that dates it to about 1911 and names Nacoochee as its place.[^2]

The state forestry department was created by law in 1925.[^3] In 1949 the Legislature set up the Georgia Forestry Commission as that department's successor.[^3] The photograph predates the commission by almost four decades.[^2][^3]

The Eastern U.S. Lookouts directory lists the Lynch Mountain tower under White County and the Georgia Forestry Commission.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today.
