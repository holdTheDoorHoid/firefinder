Mistohay is a fire tower site in northern Saskatchewan, at about 54.49 N, 109.10 W.[^2]

In 1963 Department crews built a tower cabin at Mistohay, according to the Department of Natural Resources annual report for 1963-64, as quoted on Canada Lookouts.[^1] That department is the one the Firetowers of Saskatchewan page says is now called Saskatchewan Environment and Resource Management, or SERM.[^2] The same page gives 1965 twice in its notes for the site, without saying what that year marks.[^2]

The Firetowers of Saskatchewan towermen list gives John Rule four towers, among them Mistohay.[^3] Barbara Brunet is listed at Mistohay for 1998 to 2002.[^3]

No source describes the site today. It is not known whether any part of the tower stands, or whether the public can visit.
