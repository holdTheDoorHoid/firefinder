The White Oak Mountain lookout was a Virginia Department of Forestry tower in Pittsylvania County.[^1][^2] The association's list marks it as gone.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says it stayed in active use until at least 1975. After that it was converted into a communications tower.[^1] The register lists its removal as after 1975.[^1]

The register entry for the site was made on March 24, 2020, with John McDonald as nominator.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list shows a standing tower at Hawthorne, in Montgomery County, and describes it as relocated from White Oak Mountain.[^2] The list gives no date for the move and does not say who moved the tower or how. It files the entry under "Relocated / Replica Lookouts".[^2] The register does not mention a move at all, and it describes a conversion instead.[^1] The Hawthorne claim therefore rests on a single list entry.

We found no details of the Hawthorne tower's build, height, owner, staffing or access. Anyone who wants to see it should check with the landowner first and should not climb it without permission.

[^2]: Virginia, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
