Deescius Fire Lookout is a former BC fire lookout with a view of Tochcha Lake.[^1] The lookout is reached from Tochcha East Road, northwest of Vanderhoof.[^1] Firelookout.ca gives its elevation as 1,535 m (5,040 ft). A web index of Canadian lookouts gives 1,528 m, seven metres lower, and files the site under Bulkley - Nechako.[^1][^2]

The name matches Deescius Mountain, which BC Geographical Names places on the east side of Tochcha Lake.[^3] Its recorded position lies about 100 m from the index's coordinates, so the two may refer to the same high ground.[^2][^3] The same record links the name to a count of caribou horns once found on the mountain.[^3]

No source I read describes what, if anything, is left at the lookout site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
