The Sand Lake Lookout Tower stands in the Town of Sand Lake, in Sawyer County, Wisconsin.[^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as standing and gives its position as 45.80441 north, 91.44012 west.[^1]

The tower was one of five fire towers in Sawyer County that the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources used.[^2] According to the Sawyer County Record, a recent structural check of the state's 72 towers concluded they were unsafe. Most were put up in the 1930s and 1940s, and replacing them was judged too costly at $25 million.[^2] We did not find the year this tower went up or who built it.

The best-documented event we found involving the tower dates to 1966.[^3] A bomber from Barksdale Air Force Base in Louisiana, flying a low-level mission over Ladysmith, clipped treetops and went down.[^3] Langham, a part-time fire-watcher, climbed the Sand Lake tower to guide responders to the crash site.[^3]

The Sawyer County Record reports a Dec. 16 DNR press release announcing plans to remove the towers from service.[^2] The towers were to be taken out of service in 2016, and the DNR would no longer use them to fight fires.[^2] WJFW, by contrast, reports that the 72 towers were decommissioned in 2015.[^4] The two sources disagree on the year, and neither describes the tower's condition today.[^2][^4]

The association still lists the tower as standing.[^1] We found no recent photos, condition report or public access rules for it. Anyone curious about the site should ask whoever manages it before going near the tower.
