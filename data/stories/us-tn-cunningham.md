A tower named Cunningham appears on the Tennessee Land Forms list of lookout towers. The list places it in Montgomery County, gives its elevation as 741 feet, and marks it standing.[^1]

The community of Cunningham is an unincorporated place in Montgomery County, about nine and a half miles south of downtown Clarksville, where State Route 13 meets State Route 48.[^2]

The National Register document on the state's Division of Forestry fire towers includes a list of towers still standing as of March 26, 2014. Cunningham, in Montgomery County, is on that list.[^3]

That document says the Division of Forestry built 208 fire lookout towers between about 1930 and 1970, and that many of the towers erected in the 1930s were built with the Civilian Conservation Corps as a partner.[^3] We could not tie this tower to a particular year, builder or crew, and we found no record of who staffed it or of any fire it watched.

The sources do not say what the tower looks like now, whether it is open to visitors, or who owns the land. Ask the county or the Tennessee Division of Forestry before going near it.
