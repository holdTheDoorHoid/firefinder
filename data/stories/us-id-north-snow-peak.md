North Snow Peak sits in Shoshone County, Idaho, on the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1][^3] In 1934 a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab went up on the peak's northernmost point.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com places the site seven miles east of Lakeview, at 4,630 feet.[^1]

The tower was destroyed in 1953, according to all three history pages.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the structure as gone.[^4]

The most recent report found is a post on Idaho Fire Lookouts dated September 14, 2025. Its writers found four footings from a tower that had been toppled, and no other remains on the site.[^2] The same site page files the lookout as a hike-in, so the site is reached on foot.[^2] No source says whether the site is open to visitors now.
