Swartz Meadow sits about 9 miles south of Grangeville, in Idaho County, at 5,200 feet on the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1][^2]

A tower went up here in 1931: an 80-foot pole tower with an L-6 cab on top.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Service list gives the cab as 7 by 7 feet.[^3] Photos on both lookout sites are credited to the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1][^2]

Sources disagree on when it was destroyed. The Forest Service list gives 1953, while firelookout.com says about 1954 and Idaho Fire Lookouts gives 1954.[^3][^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as drive-up, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the tower as gone.[^2][^4]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
