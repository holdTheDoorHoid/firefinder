The Coast and Geodetic Survey set a survey station on a hill near Conley in 1918.[^1] The station's record places it 2 miles northeast of Conley, at the top of a boulder-covered hill.[^1]

A 1953 note in the same record says a fire tower had been built 75 ft (23 m) southwest of the station.[^1] It measures the station as 75.7 ft (23 m) northeast of the tower's northeast leg.[^1]

The tnlandforms.us list names the point conley, places it in DeKalb County, and cites this same datasheet.[^2] The list gives the point's elevation as 1,035 with no unit. Read as feet, that is 7 ft below the datasheet's NAVD 88 height of 1,042.0 ft (317.61 m).[^1][^2]

The datasheet's last recovery note is from 1973, and it says only that the station was recovered as described.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains of the tower today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^2]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
