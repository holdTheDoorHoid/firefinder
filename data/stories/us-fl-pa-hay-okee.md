Pa-Hay-Okee is a raised observation platform on a boardwalk loop inside Everglades National Park, about 13 miles from the park's main entrance.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts site carries a page under that name.[^3]

The National Park Service says the renovated ceiling of the overlook follows its original 1959 design.[^1] A 1972 USGS topographic map of the area carries the label "Pa Hay Okee Lookout Tower," so the name was already on maps by then.[^4]

Whether a fire tower stood here is less settled. A Florida chapter of the Forest Fire Lookout Association says the lookout is now only an observation deck. It suggests a steel fire tower may once have stood on a low hammock that is now crossed by boardwalks, and calls that an open question.[^5]

The overlook and its boardwalk got a full renovation and reopened in winter 2026. Changes included a broader boardwalk and a roof meant to make visiting more comfortable.[^1] The trail is about 0.16 miles (260 m) round trip, and a trail guide notes that a staircase climbs to the observation platform.[^1][^6] Sources describe only the observation overlook at the site today, and none describes older tower remains.[^1][^5]
