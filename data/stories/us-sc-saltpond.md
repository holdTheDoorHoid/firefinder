The Saltpond lookout was a steel tower, 60 feet tall, on the Atlantic Intracoastal Waterway in Charleston County, South Carolina.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says it was built around the 1940s.[^1]

Forest Lookouts lists the site in Charleston County under Cape Romain National Wildlife Refuge.[^2] The refuge is governed by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, which the register lists as the administering agency.[^3][^1]

The register gives the site's coordinates as 32.998333 north and 79.604722 west.[^1] It says the tower was removed in the 1960s.[^1]

The register added the site on April 7, 2011, on the nomination of Ron Stafford.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can go there.
