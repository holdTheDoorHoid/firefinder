The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists a tree tower at Tannery Hill, built in 1914.[^1] The register gives its road references as US 504 and PA 333, and it says the tower was still used around 1917.[^1]

The register says the tower is no longer standing.[^1] Its entry was added on September 16, 2008.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today. The hill lies in Tioga State Forest near Morris, and the Mid State Trail climbs it.[^2] The trail guide says the path crosses the back of the hill along a plateau, and that it had no defined campsites as of July 2023.[^2] Camping off the trail is allowed under state forest rules.[^2] One described hike starts at a small roadside pull-off on Stony Fork Road.[^2]

[^1]: Tannery Hill Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/pa/tannery-hill-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Tannery Hill via MST, MyHikes, https://myhikes.org/trails/tannery-hill-via-mst (accessed 2026-10-10).
