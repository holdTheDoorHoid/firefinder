The Appleby fire tower stood in Nacogdoches County, Texas, and the page for it lists the Texas Forest Service as the agency.[^1]

The town of Appleby peaked at nearly 1,000 people during World War I, fell to 250 by the early 1950s, and its post office closed in 1958.[^2] A 1948 Coast and Geodetic Survey station description places a survey marker 25 feet southeast of the tower's southeast leg, so a fire tower stood at Appleby by that year.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page marks the tower as removed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas index lists it as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^3]
