The Demers Ridge lookout was a 20-foot pole tower of the L-4 type.[^1] The listing dates it to 1935 and places it on the Flathead National Forest, about 18 miles north of Columbia Falls in Flathead County, Montana.[^1] Its elevation is given as 6,235 feet.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list also marks the tower as gone, and gives its coordinates as 48.64517, -114.20955.[^2] The listing dates its destruction to 1957.[^1]

Today, no source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, and none says whether the site is open to visitors. A hiking route named Demers Ridge Trail is listed in Hungry Horse, Montana, with a length of 8.3 miles, but no source we read ties that trail to the lookout.[^3]
