Silver is a fire lookout tower in Otero County, New Mexico, at about 33.007 north and 105.596 west, according to the state association's list.[^1] The list shows it as standing and gives it no register number.[^1]

I could not find a build date, builder, design, or history for Silver in the sources I could reach. The list's columns cover name, county, coordinates, type, status, and register number, with no date or staffing column.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page for the tower puts it under the Mescalero Indian Agency.[^2] That label shows how the site groups the tower, not who owns the land it stands on.[^2]

Without any dated events, I cannot describe its construction, staffing, fires, or later changes. Its status is listed only as standing, and I found no record of access or a visiting route.

Treat the tower as unknown ground, and do not climb it without the landowner's permission.
