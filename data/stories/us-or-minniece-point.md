Minniece Point is in Linn County, Oregon, on the Willamette National Forest, about nine miles southwest of Detroit.[^1][^2]

In 1931, forest supervisor C. C. Hall announced eleven new emergency lookout stations for the Santiam district, and Minniece Point was one of them.[^3] A telephone link was planned for the following year.[^3] An L-4 lookout cab went up there in 1934.[^1][^2][^3]

Newspaper clippings reproduced on Forest Lookouts name Paul Ratzeburg as the lookout in 1933, 1935 and 1936.[^3] The clippings spell the place several ways, including Mines Rock, Minnis Rock and Minne Rock, so the match with Minniece Point rests on the Forest Lookouts page that groups them.[^3]

The register says the cab was gone by 1966, and firelookout.com gives the same year.[^1][^2]

A visit report dated August 4, 2023, on the register says there are no remnants of the tower.[^1] The visitor reached the site over dirt Forest Service roads and found a small parking area.[^1] The trail is minimally maintained and unmarked, and it is hard to see.[^1] Rope and two permanent anchors suggest that rock climbers use the spot.[^1] The same visitor also praised the views of several volcanic mountains.[^1] Check with the Willamette National Forest before you go.
