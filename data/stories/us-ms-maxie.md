The Eastern US Lookouts site lists a Maxie tower in Forrest County, Mississippi, inside the De Soto National Forest. It gives the survey location as 2N-12W-15.[^1]

The Gulf and Ship Island Railroad stopped at Maxie, and the community's post office operated from 1900 to 1967.[^2] By 2019, only a church and a cemetery still carried the Maxie name.[^2]

In May 1972 a man died after falling from a forest fire watch tower near the Maxie community, according to a newspaper report that the site quotes from The Delta Democrat-Times.[^1] The clip is dated May 15, 1972.[^1]

No source we found describes the tower today, whether it still stands, or whether the public may visit it. Do not climb an old fire tower without the owner's permission.
