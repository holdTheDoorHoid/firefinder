Nakarna Mountain, in the St. Joe National Forest, is listed as a patrol lookout in Benewah County.[^1][^4] The lookout tied to it is Carpenter Mountain, five miles southwest of Fernwood.[^2] Firelookout.com says that lookout began as a camp in 1937, with an 8-foot log crib and an alidade, and gained a 20-foot pole platform in 1938.[^2]

Carpenter's patrol point sat one mile west, atop Nakarna Mountain.[^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts dates the camp to 1939 instead, so the two listings disagree on its start.[^2][^3]

Both listings give 1942 as the year the Carpenter Mountain lookout was abandoned.[^2][^3]

No source describes what remains on Nakarna Mountain today, or whether the summit is open to visitors. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the Carpenter Mountain site as hike-in and abandoned.[^3]
