Moose Mountain is in Bonner County, Idaho, within the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^3] The lookout went up in 1931 as an L-4 cab set on a ten-foot log crib.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts page credits that build date to a source it names only as Kresek.[^3] Its survey location is given as 58N-3E-30.[^3]

One site gives the elevation as 6,543 feet.[^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the lookout under its Northern Idaho pages and describes it as a hike-in site, meaning visitors walk in rather than drive up.[^2]

The three sites agree that the cab was destroyed in the 1950s, though none gives a year more exact than the decade.[^1][^2][^3]

None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
