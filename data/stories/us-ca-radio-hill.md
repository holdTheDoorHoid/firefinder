Radio Hill sits on a hill east of Quincy, in Plumas County.[^1] Work started in May 1938 on a temporary lookout and radio station there, part of the Plumas National Forest fire detection system.[^1] The plan was a remote control hookup that would let the station both broadcast and receive from the forest headquarters in Quincy.[^1]

An April 1938 item described a survey for a lookout and radio site on Cemetery Hill east of Quincy. It also said temporary tents had been used on the hill the previous summer.[^1] The clippings do not say whether Cemetery Hill and Radio Hill are the same place. The May items use only the Radio Hill name.[^1]

Harold Manson was named to be in charge of the new station.[^1] A May 1948 notice said 21 Plumas lookout men would be called to the Radio Hill station on June 1 for special detection training before they manned their own posts.[^1]

In July 1973 the General Services Administration declared the site surplus, a congressman's announcement said. The parcel measured one acre and had a building on it.[^1] On November 29, 1973, the Plumas County Board of Supervisors voted to authorize an offer to buy the site from the federal government.[^1]

Today the summit is a communications site.[^2][^3] A 2024 hiking post describes an antenna complex at the top and the remains of an earlier antenna station further down.[^2] An undated visitor page says the site began as a fire lookout and now carries a range of communications equipment.[^3] The firelookout.org list marks the site as gone, with the type listed as ground rather than tower.[^4] That conflicts with the visitor accounts, which we follow for the summit's current use. We found no source that describes a lookout cab on the summit now.[^2][^3]

Peakbagging's table lists the land as private.[^5] We did not confirm whether the county completed the 1973 purchase.

The posts describe walking up from the high school or from the bottom of Radio Hill Road, and they mention no closure.[^2][^3] Because the land status is unclear, ask locally before you go up.[^2][^3]
