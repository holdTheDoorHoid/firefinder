The Indian Post Office Lookout stood in Idaho County on the Clearwater National Forest, about 13 miles west of Powell Ranger Station, at an elevation of 6,970 feet.[^1][^2]

The lookout was a log cabin topped by a cupola, and the sources date it to 1922.[^1][^2][^3] A photograph from that year in the Bud Moore collection at the University of Montana shows the building under construction.[^4] A June 1922 newspaper item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, said a national forest examiner would visit the lookout station at Indian postoffice during a tour of inspection.[^5]

The firelookout.com listing says the cabin was destroyed after 1922, but gives no date for its destruction.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts also marks it as destroyed.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list shows it as a ground lookout that is gone.[^6]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the land is open to visitors today. Idaho Fire Lookouts carries the label "Drive-Up Destroyed."[^3]
