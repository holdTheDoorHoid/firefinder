Little Round Mountain is in Shasta County, near Montgomery Creek, at 3,020 feet in the Shasta-Trinity National Forest.[^1][^2] In August 1933 the Dunsmuir News reported that a ten-man crew from the Squaw Creek Civilian Conservation Corps camp was building a 26-foot tower with a lookout house on top, and that Melville Still of Mount Shasta was directing the work.[^2] In October the Record Searchlight named Bert Orbell as foreman and said E. Sims was responsible for the lookout, which would close once the rains fell.[^2]

A later site note describes a 24-foot timber tower built in 1933.[^1] The same note says the lookout was destroyed at some point before 1972.[^1] The 1933 reports and the later note disagree on the height, 26 feet against 24, so that figure is uncertain.[^2][^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether visitors can reach it.
