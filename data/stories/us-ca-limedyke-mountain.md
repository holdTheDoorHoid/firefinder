Limedyke Mountain carried a fire lookout tower in Trinity County, California, within the Shasta-Trinity National Forest.[^1] A September 2004 visit report puts the deck 29 feet above the summit and counts 41 stairs in one steep flight with no landings.[^1]

An October 1938 item quoted on the Forest Lookouts page says a woodpecker's beak pierced the tire of an automobile at the lookout house.[^2] By September 2004 the tower was still staffed. The visit report lists it among twelve staffed lookouts on the forest and notes it was the only one of them with a volunteer on duty that year.[^1] The same report judged the condition fair and recommended replacing the exterior wood, the window putty and the interior floor.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Western Region report for 2008 says one lookout was lost to wildfire that year, and names Limedyke.[^3] The Forest Lookouts page instead dates the loss to June 21, 2005, when it says the Lime Complex Fire swept over the lookout and destroyed it.[^2] The two dates disagree, and we have not settled which one is right.

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the site as registered on March 16, 2015, with Eliot Kalman as nominator.[^1] A May 2026 Forest Service release describes a new fire "between the Limedyke Mountain lookout and McClellan Place," using the lookout's name as a landmark.[^4] That release does not say whether any structure stands there. No source we read describes what remains on the summit, or whether the public can reach it. The only road note we found is from the September 2004 visit, which describes the road as in good shape.[^1]
