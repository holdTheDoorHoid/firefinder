Alcorn is on an Ontario list of fire towers that were still standing, which places it at Chapleau Nemegosanda Park, Chapleau.[^2] The list marks it with an asterisk.[^2] Its key says that a marked tower has had its cupola removed and is now used as a communications tower.[^1]

The list says the MNR no longer maintains these towers. It also says the bottom 6.1 m (20 ft) of ladder has been removed, because climbing is hazardous, and visitors are asked not to climb them.[^1]

A note on the list says that, as of 2013, the MNR has resolved to take down many of the towers that still stand.[^2] No other source here confirms whether the tower still stands today, or what the site is like now.
