Roswell was a fire lookout on the Kootenai National Forest in Lincoln County, Montana, about 9 miles north-northwest of Yaak.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 5,612 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list places it at 48.95900 north and 115.76937 west, and classes it as a tower.[^2]

A 10-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab was built there in 1941.[^1]

Firelookout.com says the tower was destroyed in 1958.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the site as gone.[^2]

A Lincoln County list on the Forest Lookouts website names a Roswell Ridge in the Kootenai National Forest.[^3] It gives a legal location and no history, so we cannot tell whether it is this lookout.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the Roswell site today, or whether the public may visit it.
