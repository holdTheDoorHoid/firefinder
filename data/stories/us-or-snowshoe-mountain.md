Snowshoe Mountain was a fire lookout on the Malheur National Forest in Grant County, Oregon.[^1][^2] The county index also gives an alternate name: Snowshoe Mountain (East Flagtail).[^2]

The Oregon Lookouts directory dates construction to 1933 and describes a round timber tower of 30 feet (9.1 m), with an enclosed cab.[^1] It gives 1954 as the year the lookout was discontinued.[^1]

The two directory pages disagree on which section the site is in. The site page places it in section 16 of township 16 South, range 29 East, while the county index gives section 15.[^1][^2] The directory marks the site as removed.[^1] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
