A compiled list of Georgia lookout towers gives a tower in McIntosh County under the entry GA402, "eulonia", at 31.531111 north and 81.428611 west, with an elevation of 17 and no stated unit. Read as feet, that is about 5 m.[^1] The compiler named towers numbered 73 and above personally, so "eulonia" is a label rather than a name recorded on the tower.[^1]

The entry's note reads "topo 1955", and the page does not explain it.[^1] The same list cites 1:100,000 USGS topographic maps among its sources, so the year may refer to a map of that date. That is our inference, not something the page states.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register's Georgia page names 29 lookouts, and none is called Eulonia.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, stands at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Georgia | National Historic Lookout Register, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ga/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
