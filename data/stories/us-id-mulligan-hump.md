Mulligan Hump is a summit in Shoshone County, Idaho, within the St. Joe National Forest, about 10 miles northwest of the Canyon R.S. Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 3,930 feet, while Peakery gives 3,848 feet.[^1][^3]

A fire lookout cabin went up on the hump in the 1930s. The sources give only the decade.[^1][^2]

What remains is described only as ruins. Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both use that word.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list is the outlier, marking the site as gone.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site as "Hike-In Ruins."[^2] A Gaia GPS route titled Mulligan Hump via Larkins Creek Trail describes a moderate one-way trail that goes by the hump.[^5] Beyond the word ruins, none of these sources describes what survives on the ground. Whether the site is open to the public is not stated in these sources.
