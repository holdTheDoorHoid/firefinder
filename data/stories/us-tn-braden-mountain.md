Braden Mountain is a lookout site in Campbell County, Tennessee, listed at an elevation of 2,700 feet.[^1] The Tennessee lookout-tower site gives its code as 2-22 and marks the tower as moved.[^1] The site explains that its codes pair a district number with a tower number, using numbering taken from a 1976 state listing, so 2-22 is tower 22 in district 2.[^1]

A separate account places a Braden Mountain tower in Scott County.[^2] The Encyclopedia of Scott County lists a Braden Mountain tower above Rock House Creek among that county's towers.[^2] It says that tower was dismantled and rebuilt at View Tree Knob, and that the rebuilt structure no longer stands.[^2]

The two sources disagree on the county, and we could not tell whether they describe the same tower.[^1][^2] The same Scott County account also lists another View Tree Knob tower, above Rock House Creek, and says only its base is left.[^2]

According to the Tennessee site, towers were still going up in the state during the 1950s.[^1] The Encyclopedia of Scott County, citing the University of Tennessee, reports a statewide peak of 208 lookout towers, with 142 still standing in 2014.[^2]

No source we found describes the original Campbell County site today. On the Scott County account, the rebuilt tower no longer stands at View Tree Knob, and only the base of the separate tower there is left.[^2]

[^2]: Black Creek Fire Tower, The Encyclopedia of Scott County, https://ihoneida.com/knowledge-base/black-creek-fire-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
