Badger is a place in the Rural Municipality of Piney, Manitoba.[^2] In August 1947 the Carillon News reported that a new fire lookout had been completed about a quarter mile (0.4 km) east of Badger.[^1] The report describes an all-steel tower, 24.4 m (80 ft) high.[^1] I found no other source for the tower, so the 1947 report, as copied on Canada's Fire Lookouts, is the whole record.[^1]

Manitoba's fire towers were once common, but the Manitoba Historical Society says most have since been taken down.[^3] No source I could reach describes the Badger site today, so whether anything still stands there is not known.[^1][^3]
