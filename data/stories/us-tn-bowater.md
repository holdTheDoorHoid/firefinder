Bowater was a fire lookout tower in Sequatchie County, Tennessee.[^1] The Tennessee lookout-tower site gives its code as SQT002, lists its elevation as 2,231 feet, and marks it as moved.[^1]

The author of the site says unofficial names were given to towers absent from the USGS maps and the state forestry list, so the name Bowater may not be an official one.[^1]

According to the same site, towers were still going up in Tennessee during the 1950s.[^1] The Encyclopedia of Scott County, citing the University of Tennessee, reports a statewide peak of 208 lookout towers, with 142 still standing in 2014.[^2]

No source we found says where the structure stands now. None describes the original Sequatchie County site today, including whether anything remains there or whether the land is open to visitors.
