Wolf Creek Peak is in Wasatch County, Utah, within the Uinta National Forest.[^1] A January 10, 1914 item from the Ogden Examiner, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, names the new Wolf Creek lookout station as one of the places on the Uinta forest where a lookout was kept during risky fire seasons.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site in its table of Utah lookout sites and non-lookout structures, with the type given as unknown and the status as abandoned.[^2]

The Forest Service describes a trailhead on the peak, at the upper end of Forest Service Road 091, and a one-mile trail from Wolf Creek Campground to that upper trailhead.[^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the station at the top, so we cannot say what a visitor would find there.
