Blackhawk Mountain is in Idaho County, about eight miles east of Elk City, on the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 6,091 feet.[^1]

The tower, with an L-4 style cab, was built about 1930.[^1][^3][^4]

The sources disagree on its height.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts give 25 feet.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page gives about 28 feet.[^3] A Forest Service list of Nez Perce National Forest lookouts gives 40 feet.[^4] We cannot settle which figure is right from these pages.

Sources date the destruction of the tower and cab to 1960.[^1][^3][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts describes the site as a hike-in that was destroyed.[^2] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. No source we read says whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
