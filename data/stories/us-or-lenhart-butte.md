Lenhart Butte is a summit in Clackamas County, Oregon, about six miles southeast of Sandy.[^3] The Clackamas Marion FPA administered the lookout.[^1][^3]

The Forest Lookouts site quotes a 1939 district annual report saying the Civilian Conservation Corps built a 100-foot tower, a guard station and a garage there that year.[^2] The report said this gave the northern part of the district the detection it had long lacked.[^2] The Sandy Post, in a September 1939 item, gives the new tower as 103 feet, with a cottage and garage.[^2] Firetower.org dates the wooden tower and its 7-by-7-foot observation cab to the 1930s, and firelookout.com mentions a crow's nest on the butte in the 1920s.[^1][^3] A September 1942 inspection letter gives the tower as 110 feet, so the height is not settled.[^2]

Walter Dodson was in charge of the station from mid-July 1939, according to the Sandy Post.[^2] The Army's Aircraft Warning Service ran an observer post there from August 30, 1942, until October 1943.[^2] Ed Ballou and his wife were on duty at the post in 1943.[^2]

A family spent the summer of 1954 at the station, and in 1957 the fire warden hired Burton Blake as a lookout on the butte.[^2] By 1968 the lookout had no one on permanent summer duty, and in 1969 it was not manned.[^2]

Firetower.org says the tower and its ground house were removed in 1972.[^1] Firelookout.com says the tower was destroyed that year.[^3] The Forest Lookouts page lists it only as removed, with no date.[^2] The sources do not agree on whether it was taken down or destroyed.

No source we read describes what remains at the summit today, or whether the public may visit. The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register lists the site at 2,116 feet (645 m) as US 610, OR 51. It was registered on August 26, 2010, on the nomination of Ray Kresek.[^1]
