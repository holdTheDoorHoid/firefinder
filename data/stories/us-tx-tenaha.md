The tower takes its name from Tenaha, a community in northern Shelby County where U.S. Highways 84, 59 and 96 meet.[^1][^2] A photograph credited to Alva B. Williams of the United States Forest Service, dated August 1940, shows the tower beside part of its guard station in the Sabine National Forest.[^2] A Texas page on the Forest Lookouts website lists the tower, under the spelling "TENEHA," in Shelby County and the Sabine National Forest.[^3] A Texas tower list last revised in January 2011 includes a Tenaha Lookout Tower at 31.81306, -93.95028.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^5]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
