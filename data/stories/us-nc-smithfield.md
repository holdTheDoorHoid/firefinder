A North Carolina lookout-tower list on tnlandforms.us places a tower named Smithfield in Johnston County, at 35.50888, -78.27777.[^1] Smithfield is a town in Johnston County and is the county's seat.[^2] Wikipedia gives the town's coordinates as 35°30′50″N 78°21′00″W, about 6.6 km west of the listed point.[^2][^1]

The list starts from 208 North Carolina fire towers credited to the "NC FFLA site" and to Gary Weber, plus a few more found by looking at 1:100,000 USGS topographic maps and NGS.[^1] It warns that it "may include a few observation towers and Coast Guard lookouts."[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the listed point, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1] Nothing we read confirms a standing tower there.[^1] The list was last revised in June 2021.[^1]

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Smithfield, North Carolina, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smithfield,_North_Carolina (accessed 2026-10-21).
