The Gilboa Lookout is a tower in Marion County, Kentucky, at an elevation of about 1,066 feet (325 meters). The National Historic Lookout Register lists it as US 1488, KY 12.[^1] A Forest Lookouts page gives the same county under a Mt. Gilboa heading and adds no further detail.[^2]

The Register lists the administrator as unknown.[^1] It describes the tower as missing portions of its cab and says no history of the tower is known.[^1] The sources give no construction date, builder or design, and no height.

The sources say nothing about who staffed the tower or what fires it watched.

The Register's description of the cab is undated, so it is not clear when the parts went missing or whether the tower has changed since. M. Davis nominated the tower, and the Register recorded it on January 8, 2021. M. Davis also supplied its photograph.[^1]

The sources do not say whether the tower is open to visitors or whether it can be climbed. A partly missing cab is a reason for care: anyone interested should find out who manages the site before going near the tower, and should not climb it.
