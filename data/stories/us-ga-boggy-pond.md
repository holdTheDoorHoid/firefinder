The Georgia lookout tower list places a tower labelled "boggy pond" in Bryan County, at 32.132222 degrees north and 81.569722 degrees west.[^1] Its elevation is listed as 68, with no unit given; in feet that would be 20.7 m.[^1] The page says the list was prompted by a gap: the FFLA site had no Georgia entries in 2010.[^1] Its names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own, so "boggy pond" is the compiler's label.[^1]

The list says it located its towers using 1:100,000 USGS topo maps and NGS survey sheets. This entry carries the note "Ron topo".[^1]

A fishing-guide listing describes a lake called Boggy Pond, about 99 acres in size, in Bryan County, with Pembroke among the nearby towns.[^2] No source found ties the tower to this lake.

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 29 registered Georgia lookouts, and this site is not among the names on its state page.[^3]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything remains or whether it is open to visitors.
