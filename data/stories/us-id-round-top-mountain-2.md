The Round Top lookout stood on Round Top Mountain in Idaho County, Idaho, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites register places it in the Clearwater National Forest.[^1] A 1934 newspaper item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, said materials for ten new lookout towers had been ordered for the Lolo National Forest, and it named a Round Top site in the Powell district as one location.[^2] firelookout.com dates the tower to 1934 and lists it as a 40-foot pole L-4 tower.[^3] The register gives a 30-foot timber pole instead, so the height is disputed.[^1]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site lists one known staff member for the tower, Joe E. Halm, in 1938.[^4] It describes the 1930s structure as a log cabin, which does not match the L-4 cab in the register.[^4][^1]

Its fate is unclear. The register says the lookout was reported abandoned by 1940 and was still standing in 1950.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts also marks it abandoned.[^4] firelookout.com says it was destroyed in 1972.[^3] The accounts differ on when the tower ended, and we could not settle the date.[^1][^3]

Today, the register says some remains of concrete piers may still exist at the site.[^1] We found no source that describes the site in more detail or says whether anything of the tower still stands. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the spot as drive-up, but no source we read says whether the site is open to the public.[^4]
