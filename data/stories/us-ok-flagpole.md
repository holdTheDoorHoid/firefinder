Flagpole Lookout Tower is the fire tower on Flagpole Mountain, which frames the town of Clayton on its west side.[^1][^3] The Forest Lookouts site lists the tower in Pushmataha County.[^2] OpenStreetMap lists a lattice tower of that name, with an elevation of 562 metres.[^3] Wikipedia gives the mountain as 1,562 feet, about 476 metres. The two may measure different points, and the sources do not say which.[^1]

Wikipedia says the tower was once part of a group of staffed towers that kept watch for fires.[^1] The article also says the tower no longer works, and that it is the only one left in Pushmataha County.[^1]

Views are part of the story too. The article describes the view from the tower as broad, reaching many miles in each direction.[^1]

We found no source that says who owns the tower, or whether visitors may climb it or drive up to it. Check with the managers before going near it, and do not climb it unless it is open to the public.
