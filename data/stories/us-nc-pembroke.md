Pembroke's fire lookout was a steel tower in Robeson County, North Carolina.[^1] The state's biennial report for 1928 to 1930 places an 85-foot steel tower, built in 1928, a mile east of Pembroke.[^1] The same report says poles were already up for a two-mile line linking the tower to the local telephone company.[^1]

The sources disagree on the height.[^1] The Robesonian wrote in January 1930 that the county's tower at Pembroke stood 80 feet.[^1] In late February the same paper reported that the state had placed poles on the ground in preparation for extending a telephone line from Pembroke to the lookout, which was on the Pembroke consolidated school's property.[^1] That spot does not clearly match the "one mile east" in the state report, and the sources do not say whether they describe the same place.[^1]

No source I could read describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything of the tower remains, or whether the ground is open to visitors. Check locally before going.[^1]
