Paxton was a steel fire lookout in Shelby County, and the Texas Forest Service is named as its agency.[^1][^2] A National Geodetic Survey station record, reproduced on a Forest Lookouts page, describes a tower on four steel legs, with a glassed-in cab on top, grey paint, and an overall height of roughly 120 feet.[^2] The record's designation carries the year 1958, the earliest date I found for the tower.[^2] The record also names the USGS quadrangle as Tenaha East (2019).[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list now marks Paxton as gone, giving its location as 31.94816 north and 94.16489 west.[^3]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
