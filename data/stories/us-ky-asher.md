The Asher lookout tower stood in Caldwell County, Kentucky, about seven and a half miles north-northeast of Princeton.[^1][^2] Its survey station sat at the center of the tower's top, at roughly 37.218 degrees north and 87.846 degrees west.[^2]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the tower in 1962.[^2] According to that description, the tower was 80 feet overall, stood on four steel legs, and had a metal observation room on top.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts site lists the tower as removed.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.
