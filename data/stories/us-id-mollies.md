The Mollies lookout stood on a peak in the Kaniksu National Forest, in Boundary County, Idaho, about five miles north-northwest of Upper Priest Lake.[^1][^2] In 1927, seven years before the cab, an alidade and a tent went up on Phoebe Tip, a mile north of the peak.[^1][^2] The peak's L-4 cab, a 14-by-14-foot prefabricated live-in cabin design, was built in 1934.[^1][^2][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists Ernest Grambo for 1932, with a pet squirrel, and Theon McMahan for 1938.[^2] That 1932 staffing entry predates the 1934 cab date the same page gives, so the page's dates do not fully line up.[^2]

The cab was given up in 1949, and its remains were destroyed in 1968.[^1][^2][^3] A 2018 hike guide reports that only concrete footings are left, and suggests them as a spot to rest and take in the scenery.[^3]

Visitors reach the summit on foot.[^2] The guide describes a round trip of 3.9 miles with about 1,800 feet of climbing from a trailhead off FS 46, and rates the hike difficult.[^3] It says the forest road to the trailhead is especially rough, and that all but high-clearance 4WD vehicles might find it impassable.[^3] The guide mentions no closure or permit, but it is dated September 2018, so check current conditions with the Forest Service before you go.[^3]
