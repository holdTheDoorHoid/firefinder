Hanover Mountain is a former lookout site on the Nez Perce National Forest, in Idaho County, Idaho.[^1][^2]

The sources describe a smokechaser cabin dated 1931.[^1][^3][^4] The Idaho County page gives the same year but marks it with question marks.[^2] Its patrol point was on Umbrella Butte, three miles to the north.[^1][^3][^4]

The site is at 7,966 feet, about 20 miles east-northeast of Riggins.[^1][^4]

The sources disagree about how it ended. Firelookout.com and the Idaho Fire Lookouts site both say it was abandoned, without giving a date.[^1][^3] The Forest Service list says it burned in the 1960s.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the site as gone.[^5]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page lists it as a hike-in site, and we found nothing on whether the public may visit.[^3]
