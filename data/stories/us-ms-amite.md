The Eastern US Lookouts site has a page for an Amite lookout, listed under Amite County, Mississippi.[^1] Its entry for the site reads "Standing (Google Earth)", which does not say when the imagery was taken or whether anyone has visited the site.[^1]

A GPS tower database also lists an "Amite Lookout Tower" among the towers of Liberty, Mississippi.[^2] The part of that page we read gives no status for it.[^2] No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether a tower stands there now, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Amite - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/amite.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Mississippi Towers (Liberty, MS Towers list), ExpertGPS, https://www.expertgps.com/data/ms/towers.asp (accessed 2026-10-21).
