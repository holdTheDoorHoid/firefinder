A Georgia tower list kept on tnlandforms.us places a lookout labelled "mcintosh" in McIntosh County, at 31.52389, -81.42571.[^1] The page's author says names for towers numbered 73 and above are the author's own creation, so this label is not a name taken from a historical source.[^1]

The entry carries one tag, BLL-64691, and no gfc number.[^1] The list gives coordinates, a county, an elevation and any tags for each tower, but no description of the structure itself.[^1] The page says the Georgia Forestry Commission (GFC) supplied coordinates for 118 towers under the BLL tags, and that an earlier GFC list covered 203 towers under the gfc numbering.[^1]

The page's map was last revised in March 2016.[^1] No source found describes what remains at the site today, so it is unknown whether a tower still stands at those coordinates.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
