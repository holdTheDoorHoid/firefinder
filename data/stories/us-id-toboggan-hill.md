The Toboggan Hill lookout stood in Clearwater County, Idaho, about 12 miles east-southeast of the Kelly Creek Ranger Station.[^1] It sits at 6,149 feet.[^1][^2] The Clearwater National Forest is the agency listed for it.[^1][^3][^4]

The lookout began in 1930 as a 35-foot platform tower.[^1][^2][^4] In 1931 an L-4 cab went up on a 10-foot log base.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts describes it as a two-story log crib.[^2] A crow's nest about a mile to the southeast served as a second lookout point.[^1][^2][^4]

The sources disagree about 1934. Idaho Fire Lookouts says a 35-foot L-6 tower was built that year.[^2] The firelookout.com page says only that such a tower may have been added.[^1]

The lookout was abandoned in 1950.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks it as gone.[^5]

An August 2023 post by Idaho Fire Lookouts describes concrete footings and other remnants lying around the site.[^2] The road was open until recently, but fallen trees have made it passable only on foot.[^2] The writers tried to find the crow's nest. They said its tree appears to have been logged or burned.[^2]

None of the pages we read says whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2]
