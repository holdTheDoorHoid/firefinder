The South Tower site lies about 16 miles southeast of Vetal, in Todd County, on the Rosebud Indian Reservation.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 3,040 feet.[^1]

The sources disagree about its status. The Forest Fire Lookout Association list marks it as a tower that is gone and places it at 43.13092 north, 101.03040 west, while firelookout.com gives the status only as "Unknown."[^2][^1] The same list also marks a North Tower in Todd County as gone.[^2]

The reservation boundary includes all of Todd County, so the site lies inside the Rosebud Indian Reservation.[^4] None of the pages read says who owns the land at the site, so anyone planning a visit should confirm ownership and access first. No source describes what remains at the site today or says whether the public may go there, so its present state is unrecorded.
