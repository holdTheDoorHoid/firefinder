Lick Point Lookout stood in the St. Joe National Forest, about six miles south-southwest of Avery in Shoshone County, at an elevation of 4,200 feet.[^1][^2]

The tower was a 50-foot pole structure with an L-4 cab. It went up in 1934.[^1][^2]

Both websites record the tower as destroyed in 1951.[^1][^2] They agree on the dates and the elevation, and both credit the tower photograph on their pages to the Fire Lookout Museum.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list also files the site as a tower that is gone, which matches both websites.[^3][^1][^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as drive-up, which means it can be reached by road, and no source describes a closure.[^2]
