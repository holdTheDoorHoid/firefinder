The Devil Peak lookout site lies in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, about 11 miles east-southeast of Lakeview.[^1] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page gives its elevation as 4,616 feet.[^2] A camp was set up there in 1931.[^1][^2] In 1934 an L-4 cab went up at the north end of the site.[^1][^2] The lookout was destroyed in 1947.[^1][^2]

The FFLA's Idaho list places the site in Shoshone County, gives its type as Ground and marks it gone.[^3]

A July 2023 note on the Idaho Fire Lookouts page says little is left to see beyond concrete footings for the tower.[^2] The list says gone, while the 2023 note describes footings, so the two sources differ on what remains.[^2][^3] The page labels the site hike-in.[^2] No other source we read describes the site today.
