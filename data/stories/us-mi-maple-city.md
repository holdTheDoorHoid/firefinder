The tower near Maple City in Leelanau County is also called the Cedar tower.[^2] A 1937 item in the Leelanau Enterprise gives its height as 82 feet, places its earlier site north of Glen Lake, and says it was relocated to its current spot in 1928.[^2]

Towermen watched from it through the 1930s and 1950s.[^1][^2] In 1930 a towerman spotted a wildfire in Mason County from the tower, 61 miles away.[^1] In March 1948 the Leelanau County Times reported that the tower was staffed in every kind of fire weather.[^1] By March 1953 the county had no watchman for it yet, and by August 1955 it was staffed seven days a week.[^2] A watchman was named for the 1956 season.[^1]

Newspapers do not agree on where it stood.[^2] A 1937 item places it on a hill south of Maple City, while a 1955 item says the Cedar Fire Tower was one mile east of town.[^2]

No source here describes the site today or says what became of the tower after 1956, so whether anything still stands is not known.[^1][^2]
