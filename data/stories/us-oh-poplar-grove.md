Poplar Grove was a fire lookout tower on Pike State Forest land in Pike County, Ohio.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the Ohio Department of Natural Resources as its administrator, gives the elevation as 1,240 feet (378 meters), and entered the site on September 3, 2014, as US 1584, OH 30.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list gives the county as "Adams/Pike," so the two sources do not agree on the county.[^1][^2]

A panel for the Armintrout tower says Ohio built 39 wildfire lookout towers between 1924 and 1970, and that tower use for wildfire detection ended in 1978.[^3] The panel dates the last descent from a Pike State Forest lookout to the fall of that year.[^3] No source I read ties that closing to Poplar Grove.[^1][^2] A 2017 Ohio Cooperative Living article said the last firewatcher left a Pike County fire tower in 1978, without naming it.[^4] A second Ohio Cooperative Living piece identifies Green Ridge, also at Pike State Forest, as the last tower in use, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association still lists Green Ridge as standing.[^5][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association marks the Poplar Grove tower as gone.[^2]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the Poplar Grove site today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]
