A crow's nest on Three Prong Mountain dates to the 1930s, according to firelookout.com.[^1] The site lies about 11 miles northwest of the Magruder Ranger Station.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the elevation as 8,182 feet.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page gives the legal location as 28N-12E-12.[^3]

Two listings name the Nez Perce and Bitterroot national forests together, and a third names only the Bitterroot.[^1][^2][^3] Both lookout listings say the site was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com adds that the crow's nest was still there in the 1980s.[^1] An Idaho lookout index files it as an abandoned crow's nest.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site as a hike-in.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page credits its photographs to the Kenneth E. Carpenter Photograph Collection at the Bitterroot National Forest Heritage Program Archives, and says permission is required to use them.[^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains there today, or whether visitors can reach it.
