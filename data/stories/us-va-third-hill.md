The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list places a structure called Third Hill in Rockingham County, at 38.65519, -78.92140, and marks it gone.[^1] The entry sits under the list's heading for other or unknown structures, and its type is a possible tower, marked with a question mark, so the kind of structure is not settled.[^1]

A hiking-route site lists a summit called Third Hill in Rockingham County. Its only tower mention is the High Knob Fire Tower, which the page lists among other popular places.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Third Hill site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

Third Hill Mountain, a peak in Berkeley County, West Virginia, shares the name but is a different place.[^3]
