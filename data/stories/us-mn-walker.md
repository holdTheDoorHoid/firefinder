The Forest Lookouts list of Minnesota towers places a 100-foot Aermotor tower at Walker, in Cass County, inside the Chippewa National Forest, and says it was erected in 1937.[^1] A 1942 Forest Service publication for the forest describes the Walker fire lookout as a steel stairway structure 100 feet tall, with a fine view across Leech Lake and its islands.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list marks the Walker tower as gone.[^3] A 2015 report from the association's Minnesota chapter says three of the forest's original 43 fire towers were still standing.[^4]

We found no source that describes the site now, so we cannot say what remains there or whether the ground is open to visitors.
