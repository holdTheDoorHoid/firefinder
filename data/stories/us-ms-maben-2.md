The National Historic Lookout Register lists a Maben Lookout Tower in Oktibbeha County, Mississippi, under the registry number US 1673, MS 36. It was registered on July 10, 2022.[^1] The register describes a 100-foot Aermotor tower with a parallel staircase and gives the build date as unknown.[^1] The Mississippi Forestry Commission administers it.[^1]

A Forestry Commission forester said that, before newer technology took over, fire towers were the main way to pin down a wildfire's exact location.[^2] Kirk Rosenhan, the county fire coordinator, said people in the huts atop the towers watched for smoke, and that when two towers sighted the same blaze, the exact position went to the authorities.[^2] Bill Kitchens, a public outreach forester with the Commission, estimated that the agency began "phasing out" the towers as a detection tool in the late 1980s.[^2]

A 2020 article said the tower on Maben-Sturgis Road was one of two still standing in the county. The other, on Highway 12 southwest of Starkville, was being used as a radio tower. Rosenhan said the Maben-Sturgis Road tower "has just been sitting there."[^2] The register does not name a road, so it is not certain that this is the same structure.[^1]

Kitchens added that the agency has taken the stairs off some towers statewide to discourage climbing.[^2] A July 2022 site visit noted that someone had recently replaced some missing boards.[^1] A radio repeater listing, last updated in March 2024, gives its site as the Maben Fire Tower.[^3]

Today, no source we found describes the site or says whether the public may visit the tower. Do not climb an old fire tower without the owner's permission.
