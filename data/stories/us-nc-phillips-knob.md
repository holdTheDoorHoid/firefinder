Phillips Knob is a lookout tower in Yancey County, North Carolina.[^1] The tnlandforms.us list of North Carolina lookout towers gives its coordinates as 35.93805, -82.29694.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts index for North Carolina pairs the Phillips Knob entry with the North Carolina Forest Service.[^2] It says, citing a 1944 state biennial report, that the state erected a 45-foot 9-inch tower there in 1943.[^2]

A 2008 post on the Toe River History blog lists a Phillips Knob tower erected in 1961.[^3] The post says its details come from Peter J. Barr's book on North Carolina's lookout towers.[^3] The 1961 and 1943 dates disagree, and no source we read says whether they describe the same structure.

The same post says the tower is still up but not open to the public.[^3] That is the latest description we found. No source we read describes the site today.
