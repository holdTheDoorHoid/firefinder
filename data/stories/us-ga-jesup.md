Jesup is a fire tower site in Wayne County, Georgia, that appears on a personal list of the state's towers. The list places it at 31.61849, -81.89938 and gives its county as Wayne.[^1] Its row also carries the codes BLL-65213, usgs and gfc-809.[^1]

The list says it draws on the USGS name database for towers 1 to 72, and that the author made the names for towers 73 and above.[^1] Its list number, GA288, places it in the second group, so its name is the author's own label for the site.[^1]

The page credits GFC with coordinates for 118 towers, and notes that an earlier GFC list gave locations for 203 towers.[^1] The map was last revised in March 2016.[^1]

Jesup does not appear among the 29 lookouts that the National Historic Lookout Register lists for Georgia,[^2] nor among the two former sites in the Former Fire Lookout Sites register.[^3] No source we checked describes what, if anything, remains at the Jesup site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
