Hoyt Mountain is in Shoshone County, Idaho, about six miles southwest of Avery.[^1] The lookout was a 50-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, built in 1934.[^1] The St. Joe National Forest is listed as its agency.[^1][^3]

A patrol point stood two miles west on Boulder Ridge.[^1][^2]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page lists one known staffer, George Terrien, for the years 1940 to 1943, and notes the Former Forest Fire Lookout Registry beside his name.[^2]

Two sources give 1956 as the year the tower was destroyed.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts Idaho county inventory gives the same year but says only that the tower was "gone."[^3]

No source we read describes what remains of the tower or the patrol point today. Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as drive-up, but nothing we found says whether it is open to visitors now.[^2]
