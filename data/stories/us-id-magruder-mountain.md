Magruder Mountain Lookout stood at about 7,428 feet in Idaho County, in the Bitterroot National Forest.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com places it five miles west of the Magruder Ranger Station.[^2]

The earliest entry is a camp on the site in 1933.[^1][^2][^3] By 1940 a log cabin stood atop the north point.[^1][^2][^3]

The only staffing record we found is from 1936. The Forest Lookouts page quotes a September 1936 item from the Ravalli Republic, which said Ralph Wanderer Jr. had worked at the Magruder Mountain lookout for the summer, in the Deep Creek area.[^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts site says it does not yet have a documented staffing history for the lookout.[^1]

The three sources agree that the lookout was destroyed in 1950.[^1][^2][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout, or whether the site is open to the public. Because the lookout stood in a national forest, ask the Bitterroot National Forest about access before going to look.[^1]
