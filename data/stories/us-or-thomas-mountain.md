Thomas Mountain lies near Remote in Coos County, Oregon, midway between the headwaters of Sandy and Rock Creeks.[^1]

The earliest entry, from March 1928, says two new lookout stations and 12 miles of patrol telephone line were to be built that year, supervised by the Coos County Fire Patrol. It also reports a lookout station under construction on Thomas Mountain, eight miles above Bridge on Sandy Creek.[^1]

In February 1936 CCC crews from Camp Bradford were building a four-mile road to Thomas Mountain, where the Coos Fire Patrol planned a new lookout.[^1] The lookout was to watch a district that other lookouts could not see.[^1]

The 1928 entry and the 1936 items do not agree on whether a lookout was already under way, and the page does not reconcile them.[^1] In April 1936 the tower was to be the fourth built by Bradford crews, after towers at Signal Tree, Live Oak and Dutchman's Butte.[^1] In June it was described as the fourth the crews had already built.[^1]

The entry ends with the word "Removed", and it gives no date.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Thomas Mountain, Forest Lookouts (Oregon), https://oregonlookouts.weebly.com/thomas-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
