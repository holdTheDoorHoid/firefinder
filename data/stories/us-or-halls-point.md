Halls Point sits at the north end of Bald Mountain, a north-south ridge that also carries the White Point and Whetstone Point lookout sites.[^1] It lies about two miles north of White Point.[^2] Firelookout.com says the site began around 1912 with a tree crow's nest platform, and that an L-4 tower followed in the 1930s.[^2] The cherylhill.net history calls that same building a cabin.[^1]

The present cab is an R-6 flat-roof design at the north end of the ridge.[^2][^3] It was built partly to replace the old Whetstone Point lookout.[^3] The register says Whetstone, in the middle of the ridge, did not cover the northern country well.[^4]

Most sources give 1956 as the build year.[^1][^2][^4] A 1957 newspaper item quoted on the Oregon Lookouts page said in August that a new lookout would be built soon, and in October that the flat-top structure had recently been finished.[^3] The dates do not match, so treat 1956 as the register's date rather than a settled one.

A three-foot overhang took the place of the heavy shutters that older lookouts used against sun glare. Lighter shutters were to be used in winter to protect the windows.[^3]

The cab was in summer use by 1958. A September 1958 newspaper item quoted on the Oregon Lookouts page describes a couple who had been stationed there for the summer months.[^3] Firelookout.com says it is staffed every summer.[^2] A 2023 visit account lists it as staffed.[^1]

The sources I read name no notable fire or smoke watched from this lookout.

The register says the old Whetstone lookout was burned in 1958,[^4] while the cherylhill.net history says its tower was removed that year.[^1] None of the sources describes a later rebuild or move of the cab.

The Forest Service administers the lookout.[^4] The route turns west from Highway 62 onto Road 64, takes Road 200 at a fork 2.8 miles along, and then drives 5.6 miles to a gate.[^1] From the gate, the lookout is about a 0.3-mile walk up the road.[^1] The sources do not say when the gate is open, so check road conditions with the Forest Service before you go.
