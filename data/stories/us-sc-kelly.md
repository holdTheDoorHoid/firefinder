The Kelly lookout was a South Carolina Forestry Commission tower in Union County.[^1] The Forest Lookouts history site records a sale: in 2010 the tower was sold at auction for $4,024.[^1]

The state's tower network began in August 1930 with a tower near Camden, and its towers ranged from about 55 feet to 120 feet tall.[^2] The agency shut down its fire tower system on October 1, 1993, and Kelly was one of its towers.[^1][^2]

A blog post suggests the Forestry Commission is selling several towers to the highest bidder, and it names the Kelly Tower in Union County among them.[^3] Bidding starts at $500, most of the listed towers currently have no bids, and the purchaser is responsible for removing the tower.[^3]

Today, no source describes what remains at the Kelly site, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1]
