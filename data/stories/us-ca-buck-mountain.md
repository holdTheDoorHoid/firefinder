Buck Mountain is in Del Norte County.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page lists it under the Siskiyou National Forest and then under the Six Rivers National Forest.[^1]

A Siskiyou National Forest guard-placement plan from May 1940, quoted on that page, rated the seen area as excellent. It said lightning was apparently the only past cause of fires, and it judged a somewhat longer season justified here than at Baldy Peak and Summit Valley.[^1]

A Coast and Geodetic Survey description dated 1942 places the survey station on Buck Mountain. At that time the Forest Service had base piers set for a lookout tower just northeast of the station.[^1]

A 1966 recovery note by the same survey describes four concrete bases set for a lookout tower. The station mark sat 43 feet west of the western base.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page has the status label "Removed," and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list marks the tower as gone.[^1][^2]

That 1966 recovery note is the most recent on-the-ground description we found.[^1] No source we read describes the site today, so we cannot say what remains of the bases or whether the site is open to visitors. Anyone interested should check with the forest office first.
