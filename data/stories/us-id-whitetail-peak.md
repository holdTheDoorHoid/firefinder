Whitetail Peak Lookout stood in Shoshone County, in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, about 9 miles south of Clark Fork, at 5,500 feet.[^1]

The tower was a 50-foot pole with an L-6 cab, built in 1934.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com and the Shoshone County list agree on both points.[^1][^3]

The sources do not agree on how the lookout ended. Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both say it was destroyed in 1953.[^1][^2] A Shoshone County forest lookouts list says it was abandoned that year instead.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone and places it at 48.00962, -116.15807.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site as drive-up.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site today, and none says whether the spot is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
