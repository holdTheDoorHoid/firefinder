The Baada Point lookout was a 45-foot steel tower on the Washington coast of Clallam County.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says it was built in 1942.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the same year and height, and places the site about a mile northeast of Neah Bay at an elevation of 140 feet.[^2]

The register lists the Washington State Division of Forestry/DNR as the administering agency, and firelookout.com names the Division of Forestry/DNR as well.[^1][^2]

The register says the tower was gone by 1945, while firelookout.com says it was gone in 1945.[^1][^2] The register added the site on November 26, 2011, as US 1151, WA 59, on the nomination of Ray Kresek.[^1] A regional index of Washington lookouts also lists it under the name "AWS Baada Point."[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public.
