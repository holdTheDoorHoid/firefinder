The Forest Lookouts guide lists Beaver Ridge, in Benewah County, Idaho, as a patrol lookout on the St. Joe National Forest. It gives the land location as 45N-1W-36.[^1]

The guide also has a separate page for a different Beaver Ridge, a lookout on the Clearwater National Forest. That site is not the one described here.[^2]

No source we found describes what remains at this St. Joe site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1]

[^2]: Beaver Ridge - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts (Ron Kemnow), https://idaholookouts.weebly.com/beaver-ridge1.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
