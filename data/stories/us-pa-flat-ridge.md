The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists a Flat Ridge tower in Pike County, Pennsylvania. It describes a wooden tower built in 1910 that was still in use around 1917, and it is no longer standing.[^1] A Pennsylvania lookout list places a 35-foot wood tower built in 1910 in the same county.[^2] The two listings agree on the year and the material.

The register entry carries the registry numbers US 285 and PA 114.[^1] Henry Isenberg nominated it, and it was registered on September 16, 2008.[^1]

The register gives no coordinates for the site.[^1] No source I read describes what, if anything, remains on the ridge today, or whether the ridge is open to visitors.

[^1]: Flat Ridge Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/pa/flat-ridge-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Pennsylvania (A-F) - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/pennsylvania-a-f.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
