The Georgia lookout list includes a tower called luthersville in Meriwether County, at 33.20826, -84.72738.[^1] Its elevation column reads 935 with no unit; read as feet, that is about 285 metres.[^1] Luthersville lies toward the north of Meriwether County.[^2]

The list's compiler says names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own. This tower is numbered 254, so its name may come from the list rather than from a historical source.[^1]

The same list has a separate entry for the Meriwether Lookout Tower, further south, at 33.03013, -84.75687.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today.
