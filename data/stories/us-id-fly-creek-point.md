Fly Creek Point is in Lemhi County, Idaho, in the Challis National Forest, about 21 miles northwest of Challis.[^1][^3][^4] The lookout sat beside the Frank Church River of No Return Wilderness.[^1]

The site became a fire detection point in 1935, when an L-4 ground cabin went up there.[^3] The firelookout.com page gives the same build year for the cab.[^1]

One notable episode came in September 1959.[^3] The Custer County sheriff's office in Challis said the lookout passed word by Forest Service phone that a missing student had walked into a ranch on Warm Springs Creek.[^3] In the 1960s the cabin was used for emergencies.[^1][^2]

The cabin burned in 1993, and our sources disagree on how.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both say a vandal set it on fire over the winter.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page gives a different account: a stranded camper accidentally set fire to the lookout building and a garage near it, later in 1993.[^3]

Today, the Idaho lookout list at firelookout.org marks the site as gone.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts labels its page "Drive-Up Burned," which suggests a road reaches the site.[^2] No source we read describes what remains on the ground or confirms access, so check with the forest before going.
