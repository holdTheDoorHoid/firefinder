The Silverton lookout was a South Carolina Forestry Commission tower in Aiken County.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts history site records a sale at auction in 2010.[^1] A Random Connections blog post names the Silverton Tower near Jackson, SC, among several towers that the Commission seems to be selling to the highest bidder.[^2] Bidding started at $500, most of the towers had no bids when the post was written, and the buyer must take the tower down.[^2] The author adds that moving a tower and reassembling it elsewhere could be astronomically expensive.[^2]

The Commission ended its fire tower system on October 1, 1993.[^4] Columbia Metropolitan magazine reports that decommissioned fire towers have been offered at auction since 2003.[^3]

No source I could reach describes what remains at the Silverton site today, or whether the tower still stands.
