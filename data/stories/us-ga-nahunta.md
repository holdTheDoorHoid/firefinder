The city of Nahunta is the county seat of Brantley County, Georgia.[^1]

A statewide list of Georgia lookout towers gives a tower called nahunta in Brantley County, at about 31.20 degrees north and 81.97 degrees west.[^2] The list says that names for towers numbered 73 and above are its own creation. This tower is numbered 397, so the name is the list's own label.[^2] Its entry ends with the single note "topo".[^2]

A Wikimedia Commons photo dated 29 December 2022 shows a fire lookout on the south side of eastbound US 82, between Hoboken and Nahunta.[^3] The photo's description places a Trail Ridge historical marker beside the tower and says the marker dates from when this road was numbered US 84.[^3]

The photo's description does not say whether this is the tower the list records under the name nahunta. The most recent source found for a tower on this stretch of road is that 2022 photograph. No source says whether a tower here is open to visitors today.
