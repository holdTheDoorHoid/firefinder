Smoky is a wildfire lookout site in Alberta.[^1] An online directory of the province's lookouts lists it by name, in a group labelled Grand Prairie Division.[^1]

Alberta says every lookout site has an observer on duty, usually from April to October, keeping watch for fire through the day.[^4] In October 2024, Alberta Wildfire's information co-ordinator for the Grande Prairie Forest Area said the forest area had eight lookout towers, down from ten because of fires over the year before.[^2] That count covers the whole forest area, not this one site.[^2]

Do not confuse this site with the fire lookout tower now in the Grande Prairie Museum's collection.[^2] A news item from Downstream News Media says that tower dates from the 1930s, first stood in the Smoky River area, and was in use there until the 1970s.[^3] Grande Prairie coverage quotes Alberta Wildfire's co-ordinator saying it was used to train lookout staff rather than as a forestry tower.[^2] The same coverage says it was moved by helicopter in the 70s or early 80s.[^2] The sources disagree on its history, and neither ties it to this site.[^2][^3]

No source we read describes what stands at the Smoky site today, or whether the public may visit. Do not climb or enter any structure without permission, and ask Alberta Wildfire first.
