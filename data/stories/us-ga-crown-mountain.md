The Crown Mountain fire tower is 60 feet tall, an Aermotor design, and sits next to a Georgia Forestry Commission office in Dahlonega, in Lumpkin County.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register dates it to the late 1950s or early 1960s and says it predates the office and workshop on the site.[^1] No source I found gives a single build year, and none names who put it up or why it was placed there.[^1][^2]

The register says the tower is no longer in active use, but the forestry office keeps it maintained.[^1] A Georgia tower list files it under the code gfc-918 in Lumpkin County.[^2] The tower was added to the register on March 14, 2019.[^1]

Access is by arrangement. The register says the tower is posted as state property, but office staff will accompany visitors up it.[^1] The register lists the location as the Lumpkin County forestry office, so ask the office before any visit and do not try to climb the tower on your own.[^1]
