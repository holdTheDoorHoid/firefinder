Wolf Peak sits in the Kootenai National Forest, about 25 miles east of Libby in Lincoln County, Montana.[^1] Its elevation is listed as 5,553 feet.[^1]

A firelookout.com page lists a crow's nest with a tent cabin under 1935.[^1] The same page says the lookout was abandoned in 1941.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list shows the site as a crow's nest with the status abandoned.[^2]

The sources do not agree on the structure type.[^1][^2] The firelookout.com page is headed "Wolf Peak Fire Lookout Tower," but its own 1935 entry and the association's list both describe a crow's nest.[^1][^2]

No source I read describes the site today. Nothing I found says what remains on the ground, or whether the place is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Wolf Peak Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/wolfpeak335.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Montana Lookouts (Alphabetical List), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
