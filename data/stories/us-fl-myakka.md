The Forest Lookouts listing for Myakka describes a steel tower, 80 feet (24 m) tall and fitted with a 7x7 cab, in Sarasota County, Florida.[^1] It dates the tower's erection to 1953 and places it at Myakka State Park, with the survey location T37S-R20E.[^1]

The listing's only later entry is a 2013 notice that the tower was put up for auction, with bid opening set for August 27, 2013.[^1]

No source I could reach says how that auction ended, what remains at the site today, or whether the public can get near it.[^1]

[^1]: Myakka - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts website, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/myakka.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
