Gibbon Ridge is in Umatilla County, Oregon, about 19 miles east of Pendleton, on the Umatilla Indian Reservation.[^1]

A tower was built on the site in the 1930s.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives only the decade, not an exact year.[^2] Firelookout.com also dates the tower to the 1930s and lists the lookout as abandoned.[^1]

The register lists the Bureau of Indian Affairs as the administering agency, and it added the site to its list on November 19, 2019.[^2] The register says the tower is gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or says whether the public may visit. The site lies within the reservation, so anyone interested should ask the tribal government before going there.[^3]
