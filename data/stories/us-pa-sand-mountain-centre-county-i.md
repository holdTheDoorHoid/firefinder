The Sand Mountain fire tower in Centre County was a wooden lookout built in 1910.[^1] A Pennsylvania Department of Forestry report covering 1910 and 1911 lists two fire observation towers erected in those years, one of them on Sand Mountain.[^2] The register's 1910 date falls within those two years.

The register also lists a second Sand Mountain site in Centre County, a tree tower built in 1915 that it says is no longer standing.[^3] That is a different structure, so it should not be confused with this one.

The register says this tower is no longer standing. It was registered on September 16, 2008, on the nomination of Henry Isenberg.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on Sand Mountain today, or whether the site is open to the public.
