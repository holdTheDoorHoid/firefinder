The Sandhill Lookout is a 100-foot steel tower in Clarke County, Alabama, and the Alabama Forestry Commission administers it.[^1][^3] The register gives its elevation as 293 feet, or 89 meters.[^1] I found no record of when it was built, who staffed it, or which fires it watched.

The National Historic Lookout Register entered the tower on May 22, 2022, on a nomination by Jackson Knight, under the number US 1593, AL 77.[^1] The blog Jackson's Ramblings also covers the tower. It calls it Al #105, gives its register number as 1594, and places it in the Scotch Management area.[^2] The register assigns the number 1594 to the Thomasville Lookout, so this account follows the register's numbering.[^4]

The register describes the tower in the present tense and places it in Clarke County.[^1] I found no condition report and no notice about public access.

Before going to look, check with the Alabama Forestry Commission, and do not climb the tower on the strength of this account.[^1]
