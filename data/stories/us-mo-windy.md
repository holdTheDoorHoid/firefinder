The Windy lookout tower was in Oregon County, Missouri, about 14.5 miles east-northeast of Alton.[^1][^2] The Coast and Geodetic Survey described it in 1956 as a U.S. Forest Service lookout tower, a steel framework topped by a small house.[^1] The survey put its height at about 50 feet (15 m), and the Central US Lookouts listing describes a steel tower with no other improvements on the site.[^1][^2] Measured from the Bradley post office, the survey places the tower about 2.75 miles away, to the north-northwest.[^1]

The survey's history section lists only that 1956 observation.[^1] The Central US Lookouts page names Oregon County and the Mark Twain National Forest for the site.[^2] No source describes the tower or its site today, so it is not known whether the structure still stands or whether the public can reach it. Anyone planning a visit should check with the Mark Twain National Forest first.

[^2]: Windy - Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/windy.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
