The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower called Votaw in Hardin County, Texas, and marks it as gone.[^1] Its listed position is 30.41517, -94.65524.[^1] The Forest Lookouts Texas index also has a Hardin County entry spelled "VOLTAW", which is probably the same site.[^2]

Votaw is an unincorporated community in northwestern Hardin County.[^3] The community is named for Clark M. Votaw, Kirby Lumber Company's assistant land commissioner, who was also a vice president of the Santa Fe Townsite Company, the firm that platted its lots.[^3]

No source describes the site now, so it is not known what, if anything, remains there or whether the ground is open to visitors.
