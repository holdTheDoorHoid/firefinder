West Pond Mountain, in Essex County in the Northeast Kingdom, has had a lookout since 1914. A forestry history of Vermont records that the mountain was added as a lookout point that year.[^1] In 1915 the Vermont Timberland Owners Association built a tower and station there, one of several early towers that large landowners paid for.[^1][^3]

In 1936 a Civilian Conservation Corps company put up a steel tower, with a stairway and a cab walled in glass, to replace an older tower, and a new cabin was built for the watchman.[^1] A state report of about 1924 had already listed West Pond among new steel towers, so the sources do not make clear when the steel tower went up.[^1] Watchmen were posted there. A November 1950 notice in the Essex County Herald says Levi Pond had finished his duties on the tower.[^1]

In 1973 the lookout passed into private hands, and the public no longer has access. The site quotes the owner's 2019 request that visitors respect their wishes.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Vermont list still shows the tower as standing.[^2] Because it is privately owned, please do not climb or visit it without the owner's permission.
