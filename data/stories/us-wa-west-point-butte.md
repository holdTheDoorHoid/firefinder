A cupola cabin went up on West Point Butte in 1925.[^1][^2][^3] The butte sits in Skamania County, in the Gifford Pinchot National Forest.[^1] In 1934 the panoramic photographs were taken from the bottom-level windows, about five feet off the ground, not from the upper cupola.[^3]

In 1955 the cupola was replaced with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2][^3] Willhite's page says the new cab went on a slightly higher knob nearby, not the original site.[^3] It advises visitors to go up both knobs, because each once held a lookout.[^3]

The L-4 cab is now gone.[^1][^2] According to Willhite, the cab's footings all remain on the high point.[^3] A 2019 photo of the summit, looking north toward St. Helens, marks the original site at left.[^3]

Access is by road and a short hike.[^3] The page gives a hiking distance of half a mile and an elevation gain of 500 feet.[^3] It describes a west approach, road 54 to road 57 and then spur 5704, and cautions that the last spur has some large potholes.[^3] A trail follows the old road nearly to the top.[^3] Where the road ends, the original trail heads off on the right and climbs by a faint tread to the summit.[^3] No closure appears in the sources read.
