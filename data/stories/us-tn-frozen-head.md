Forest Lookouts files the Frozen Head tower under Morgan County and the Tennessee Division of Forestry's District 2.[^1] It credits the Civilian Conservation Corps with building a steel tower around 1933.[^1] A personal website that reproduces the park's history says the state set aside Morgan State Forest that year, a CCC camp was set up soon after, and a lookout tower was built on the mountain because fire and trespass protection came first.[^3] The Wikipedia article on the park says the CCC arrived in 1933 to build roads and facilities, but that rattlesnakes and prison escapes kept a camp inside the forest from starting until 1938.[^2] The dates do not fully agree.[^1][^2]

The fire tower did not last. Forest Lookouts says the tower was taken apart in 2006 and an observation deck put up on the same spot.[^1] Wikipedia says the park traded the aging CCC tower on the summit for that deck the same year.[^2] Tennessee Landforms lists the tower as replaced.[^5] It is not on the 2014 list of extant Tennessee fire towers.[^6]

Today the summit has that observation deck.[^2] Wikipedia says the deck sits just above the tree line and looks out in every direction.[^2] The land is a state natural area, and hiking is its main recreation.[^4] Wikipedia also mentions a main tower trail, with a short spur from it to a rock shelter near the summit.[^2] No source I found describes the deck's current condition or any remains of the old tower, so check with the park before you go.
