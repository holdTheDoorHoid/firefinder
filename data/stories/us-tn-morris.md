Morris was a fire lookout in Fayette County, Tennessee.[^1] Tennessee Landforms lists the site at 415 feet (126 m), gives the tower the number 9-9, and records the survey code FE2547.[^1]

The federal survey datasheet for that code describes a four-legged steel tower, 93 feet (28 m) high, whose observation platform is enclosed.[^2] The Coast and Geodetic Survey wrote that description in 1958.[^2] The same record dates the first survey observation to 1958, which is a survey date rather than a construction date.[^2]

A recovery note added to the datasheet in 2012 by geocachers says the tower has been taken down and that the top of its observation roof no longer exists.[^2] The datasheet also records that the survey mark was not found on November 26, 2012.[^2]

Landforms also marks the tower as removed.[^1] It is not among the towers listed as standing on March 26, 2014.[^3]

The 2012 note does not say what, if anything, remains of the legs or footings.[^2] No source describes the site today or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
