Hough Mountain had a tree tower in Centre County, Pennsylvania.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts site calls it a tree observation post, established in 1916 and placed 35 feet up.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives the same year and adds that the tower was still in use around 1917.[^1]

The register lists it under the numbers US 325 and PA 154.[^1] It was registered on September 16, 2008, on the nomination of Henry Isenberg.[^1] The register has no map coordinates for the site.[^1]

The register says the tower is no longer standing.[^1] We found no source that describes the site today, so we cannot say whether anything remains on the ground or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Hough Mountain Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/pa/hough-mountain-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Hough Mountain, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/hough-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
