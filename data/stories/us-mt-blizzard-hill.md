Blizzard Hill, also called Blizzard Peak, stands in the Beaverhead-Deerlodge National Forest, about 16 miles north of Butte.[^1][^2][^3]

Firelookout.com dates a cupola trailer on the hill to 1960 and a two-story R-6 cab to 1963.[^2] A photo caption on the Montana site dates a mobile lookout on location at Blizzard Peak to August 1961.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes a different form, an R-6 flat top tower.[^1] The sources disagree on what stood here.

Lawrence Smith staffed the tower in 1969 and 1970.[^1] By his own account in the register, he spent 74 consecutive days at the site in 1969 and 54 in the year after.[^1]

Firelookout.com lists the tower as gone.[^2] The register added the site on May 22, 2022, on Smith's nomination.[^1]

None of the sources says what, if anything, is left on the hill today.
