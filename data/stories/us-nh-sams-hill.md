A Springfield Register item from July 1939, as quoted by Forest Lookouts, said the road to the top of Sam's Hill was understood to be complete, and that work to set up a fire lookout tower there was moving along.[^1] Earlier in the week of June 25, the same item noted, several vehicle loads of workers had been clearing combustible material from the area during hurricane cleanup.[^1] The writer added that the hill's name predates Sam Smith, who later lived in the locality, and that the hill was not named for him.[^1]

Sam's Hill is on NH Family Hikes' list of mountains that once had a fire tower, at 1,683 feet in Charlestown.[^3]

firelookout.org lists the tower as gone.[^2] None of the sources consulted describes what remains on the summit today.
