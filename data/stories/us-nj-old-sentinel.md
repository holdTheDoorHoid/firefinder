Old Sentinel Tower stands on private watershed land in New Jersey, hidden in mature forest. The Register calls it a 100-foot Blaw-Knox tower and says it is not open to the public.[^1]

Sources disagree on the town. The Register places it in Essex County.[^1] A 1990s article's heading says Livingston, another part of the same article gives Northfield Avenue in Orange,[^3] and a 2016 account puts it in East Orange.[^4]

Most of the history runs through Roswell M. Roper, who was water engineer and general manager for East Orange.[^2] A 1945 magazine article, reprinted by the Register, says Roper erected a 100-foot fire lookout tower on the land.[^2]

The dates are not settled. The Register says the tower may have been built as early as 1904, and the 2016 account gives the same date.[^1][^4] The 1990s article says the first, smaller tower on the site went up in 1909, and that three of its concrete footings still sit beneath the larger steel tower.[^3] It dates the larger tower to probably the 1920s, but says the exact date is unknown.[^3]

The 1945 article says the observer could throw a switch to sound a siren at the headquarters, one mile off.[^2] The 1990s article doubts that the tower ever worked with the state fire towers.[^3]

Inside the cab, the Register says a dial telephone was still there in 1998, though there was no fire finder.[^1] In April 2016 a Forest Fire Lookout Association historian found a small table and an old dial telephone in the cab, and noted that all the windows were still intact.[^4] The same account says the resident he met was the son of the man who served as observer during bad fire weather.[^4]

The Register adds that the tower never worked alongside the State Network.[^1] A note in the reprinted 1945 article says the tower was still standing, but the note gives no date.[^2] The Register registered the tower on May 27, 2016, and lists the Orange Watershed Commission as administrator.[^1]

It sits on private land and is not open to the public.[^1] Please keep off the property and do not try to climb it.
