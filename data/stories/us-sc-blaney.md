The earliest record we found for the Blaney lookout dates from 1936. A Florence Morning News item from July that year, quoted on the Eastern US Lookouts page, says the district forester was trying to secure one more tower site near Blaney, which would bring Kershaw County to five towers.[^1]

The State Commission of Forestry's report for July 1937 to June 1938 lists Blaney, in Kershaw County, among seven new steel fire towers erected that year.[^1] The same report credits the Civilian Conservation Corps with putting up additional towers and telephone lines that year, free of charge to the state and to protective associations.[^1] Blaney was one of five sites that also received a set of support buildings.[^1]

The Eastern US Lookouts page marks the Blaney tower as removed, with no date given.[^1] Statewide, a Post and Courier article says the state retired its towers in the early 1990s, and that just 30 were still standing when it was written.[^2]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the Blaney site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1]
