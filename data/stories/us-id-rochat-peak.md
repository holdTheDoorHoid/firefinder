Rochat Peak rises in the Saint Joe Mountains of Benewah County, Idaho. PeakVisor gives its elevation as 5,646 ft (1,721 m).[^3]

The Forest Lookouts site for Idaho lists it as a patrol lookout in the St. Joe National Forest, at survey location 47N-1W-24.[^1]

A page about the St. Joe Baldy lookout on firelookout.com says there were patrol points on a peak it spells "Rockat Peak," 2.5 miles north of St. Joe Baldy.[^2] The Forest Lookouts listing spells the name differently, so matching the two is an inference; neither source states that they are the same summit.[^1][^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
