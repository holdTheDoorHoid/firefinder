The Bourne Fire Tower stands in the Town of Bourne, in Barnstable County, Massachusetts.[^1] The present tower was built in 1947, replacing a 48-foot tower from 1913.[^1] The register describes it as a 70-foot steel tower with a standard 10-by-10-foot wooden cab. It ranks it second among Barnstable County's towers and 23rd among all state towers built.[^1]

A visit report dated September 10, 2006 records the tower as staffed and says the tower was still in active service.[^1] A Massachusetts state document on fire observation towers says seven of its Cape and Islands towers are in the Nickerson Management Complex, and the eighth, in Bourne, falls under the Myles Standish Management Complex.[^2] The sources we read do not name anyone who staffed the tower or any fire it watched.

The National Historic Lookout Register listed the tower on June 30, 2004, on the nomination of Henry Isenberg.[^1]

The register says the tower is operated by the Massachusetts Bureau of Forest Fire Control.[^1] Its most recent visit report we could read is from 2006, so its present condition is unconfirmed.[^1] We found no public visiting arrangement for it, so ask the Bureau before going near it.
