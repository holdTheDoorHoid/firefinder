Beckwith Hill sits in Killingworth, in Middlesex County, Connecticut.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes the tower that stood there as a 60-foot steel structure, and gives the site an elevation of 476 feet (145 meters).[^1] The register added the site on September 9, 2008, under the numbers US 67 and CT 6.[^1] The register's own "next lookout site" link leads to Bald Hill, US 68.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site keeps a list of Connecticut towers, and Beckwith Hill appears on it.[^2] Its entry marks the tower as removed.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the hilltop today, or whether it is open to the public.[^1][^2] A visitor should check locally before going, since none of the sources gives access details.

[^1]: Beckwith Hill Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites register, Forest Fire Lookout Association, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ct/beckwith-hill-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Beckwith Hill, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/beckwith-hill.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
