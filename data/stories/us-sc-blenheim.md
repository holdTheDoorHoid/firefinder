Forest Lookouts lists a Blenheim tower in Marlboro County under the South Carolina Forestry Commission.[^1] TopoQuest places a Blenheim Lookout Tower in Marlboro County at 127 feet above sea level, and says it appears on the USGS Bingham, SC topographic map.[^2]

The Forestry Commission's first fire tower went up near Camden in August 1930.[^3] Across the system, when a smoke was spotted, two towers each took a bearing on it, and the fire was placed where the two readings crossed on a map.[^3] The Post and Courier says the network eventually reached more than 130 towers and served 65 years in wildfire detection.[^4]

The agency says it closed its fire tower system on October 1, 1993.[^3] That date covers the whole network, not any one tower.[^3] A Post and Courier history says the agency closed the last of its lookout towers in 1993, and gives 1994 as the year the system was decommissioned.[^4]

No source we read says whether the Blenheim tower still stands, or describes the site today. A Post and Courier article says only 30 of the state's towers remain standing.[^5] Ask the Forestry Commission or the landowner before any visit.
