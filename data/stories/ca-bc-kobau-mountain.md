Mt. Kobau is one of four sites in the South Okanagan Grasslands Protected Area, and a fire lookout tower once stood on its summit.[^1] Canada Lookouts dates the tower's removal to 2007.[^2]

A trail of about 600 metres (about 1,970 ft), the Mount Kobau Lookout Trail, leads from the parking lot at the top of Mount Kobau to where the tower stood.[^1] BC Parks says the viewpoint there looks out over the Okanagan Valley, Osoyoos Lake and the Similkameen Valley.[^1] The main road to the summit is open to motor vehicles, but it is gravel and maintained only infrequently.[^1]

One hiking blog describes walking through trees burned in the 2015 Testalinden Creek fire on the way to the summit.[^3]

No source I found says what, if anything, remains of the tower on the summit today.
