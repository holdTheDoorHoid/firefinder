The Yellow River Lookout Tower Site is in Covington County, Alabama.[^1] The register says it was built on what was once Conecuh National Forest land.[^1] It lists the elevation as 260 feet, or 79 meters.[^1]

The register dates the tower to about 1940.[^1] The register also says the cab was probably 12 by 12 feet, with a catwalk.[^1]

The register says the tower was removed after 1977.[^1] It says the land is now privately owned because of a land swap, and it lists a private owner in place of the Forest Service.[^1] Statewide, Kaufmann names aircraft as the most common explanation for the towers' decline: they could cover more ground than a 100-foot tower.[^2]

Al Pelham nominated the site. It was registered on March 24, 2020, as US 1946, AL 28.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site now. Because the register lists a private owner, visitors should ask that owner before going onto the land.[^1]
