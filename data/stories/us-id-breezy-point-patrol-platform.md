Breezy Point sits at 6,440 feet in the St. Joe National Forest, and the Idaho Fire Lookouts site says a patrol platform was built there in 1922 for use from the Fishhook Peak lookout.[^1] A firelookout.com entry for Fishhook Peak places that platform on Breezy Point, two miles south of the tower.[^2] The same entry dates the Fishhook tower's start to 1922, when it was developed as a 10-foot pole tower.[^2]

The platform is now listed as destroyed.[^1]

When the site's writers visited, the cement footings of the platform were easy to find.[^1] They also say the spot made an excellent campsite, with a view they found pretty incredible, and the site is still labeled drive-up.[^1] The footings are the only remains a source describes.[^1]
