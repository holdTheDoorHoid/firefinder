Selway Mountain, in Beaverhead County about 32 miles south of Wisdom, had a fire lookout from 1932, when an L-4 cab was set on a log crib base.[^1] In 1942 two Dillon newspapers reported a telephone line running up Bloody Dick Creek to the lookout, along with a new tower meant to improve fire detection.[^2] Until that line was finished, the only contact with the station had been a short-wave radio link.[^2]

The sources disagree on the tower's date and height. The fire lookout website says a 30-foot L-4 tower replaced the cab in 1941.[^1] The 1942 papers describe a tower still being erected that summer, and give its height first as 30 feet and then as 39 feet.[^2] We could not settle which account is right.

The same website gives 1976 as the year the tower was destroyed.[^1] It is the only source we found for that date.

A trail listing describes a 2.0-mile trail from Reservoir Lake Campground to Selway Mountain.[^3] It also says the mountain once had a lookout, and that its top gives a fine look at the surrounding country.[^3] No source we found describes what, if anything, remains of the tower on the summit.

[^2]: Selway Mountain, Forest Lookouts, western US (Ron Kemnow), quoting the Dillon Daily Tribune and Dillon Examiner of May and June 1942, https://montanalookouts.weebly.com/selway-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
