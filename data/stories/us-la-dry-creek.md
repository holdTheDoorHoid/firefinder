The Dry Creek Fire Tower stands in Beauregard Parish, near the community of Dry Creek, at about 30.665 degrees north and 92.995 degrees west.[^1]

Louisiana's first steel fire tower dates from 1922 and was put up near Bogalusa. By 1943 the state had 39 fire towers.[^1] The sources we read do not say when this tower went up, who built it, what it looks like or who staffed it.

The Louisiana list describes the tower as still standing but inactive, and it marks the tower as damaged.[^1] The list cites a June 2015 Crime Stoppers appeal for information about damage to the tower, so the damage had been reported by then.[^1]

We found no public access information for the site. The tower is listed as inactive and damaged, so please do not climb it, and check its current condition with local officials before you plan a visit.

[^1]: List of fire lookout towers in Louisiana, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_fire_lookout_towers_in_Louisiana (accessed 2026-10-09).
