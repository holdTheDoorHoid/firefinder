The Catherine Lake lookout is in Onslow County and is listed as a North Carolina Division of Forest Resources tower.[^1][^2] A tower list on tnlandforms.us gives its position as 34.81083, -77.56083.[^3] The Forest Service's Onslow County office stands on Fire Tower Road in Richlands, but no source we read ties the lookout to that spot.[^4]

The sources we could read in full do not describe what stands at the site now, or whether the site is open to visitors.
