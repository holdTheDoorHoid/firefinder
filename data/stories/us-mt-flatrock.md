Flatrock sits in Mineral County, Montana, on the Lolo National Forest, about 10 miles northwest of St. Regis.[^1] The Fire Lookout website gives its elevation as 5,129 feet.[^1] The association's Montana list gives the coordinates as 47.40738, -115.23447.[^2]

The first structure the website lists is a crow's nest from 1933.[^1] By 1941 the site had a 50-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1]

The website says the tower was destroyed in the 1970s, without a more exact date.[^1] The association's Montana list marks the site as a tower that is gone.[^2]

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today, such as footings or a foundation, or whether the public can visit it. Anyone planning a trip should ask the Lolo National Forest about access before heading out.

[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
