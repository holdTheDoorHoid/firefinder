The lookout on Rock Mountain stood about 7 miles northeast of Stevens Pass, on Wenatchee National Forest land in Chelan County.[^2][^1] It was an L-4 cab, built in 1933.[^1][^2]

The earliest lookout named in the sources is Jim Coston, whom the Leavenworth Echo listed as the Rock Mountain lookout in June 1933.[^3] A year later the Wenatchee Daily World named Haven Bachelor as lookout and said Mrs. Bachelor expected to be up there with him for much of the summer.[^3] An entry dated August 18, 1934 says panorama photos were taken.[^3]

The register says the cab was removed in 1973.[^1] firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^2] The two sources agree on the year but not on how the cab left the summit, and we have not found a third account to settle it.

The Forest Service reports that the lookout no longer stands, but the summit still has views across the Cascade range.[^4] A scramble to the summit starts from the west end of the Nason Ridge Trail, where it meets the Snowy Creek trail.[^4] No source we read describes what, if anything, is left of the lookout on the summit today.
