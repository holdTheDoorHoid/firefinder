The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register lists a Mount Tom Tower Site on US 390 and PA 219 in Tioga County, Pennsylvania, and dates its entry to September 16, 2008.[^1] The entry describes a bald tower that was used around 1917 and is no longer standing.[^1]

The trail guide places the Mt. Tom Loop in Tioga State Forest near Wellsboro.[^2] The loop is a strenuous 3.6-mile route that starts at the Darling Run Access Area and climbs a steep ridge to a vista point near the summit.[^2] The trailhead sits at the main parking lot of that access area, which also serves the Pine Creek Trail.[^2] The guide says the hike's single vista point repays the effort of the climb.[^2]

The register gives only the county and highway for this site, so I cannot tell whether the loop reaches the old tower site.[^1]

No source I read describes what remains of the tower site today.

[^1]: Mount Tom Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites (Forest Fire Lookout Association), http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/pa/mount-tom-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Mt. Tom Loop - Tioga State Forest, PA, MyHikes, https://myhikes.org/trails/mt-tom-loop (accessed 2026-10-10).
