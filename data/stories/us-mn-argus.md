Argus was a stairway lookout in St. Louis County, Minnesota.[^1] The county booklet records it as a McClintock Marshall Co. tower about 79.5 feet tall, and says the name comes from Argus Panoptes, the many-eyed giant of Greek myth.[^1]

The tower was first the Budd Hill lookout in Lake County.[^2] The Lake County booklet says the 80-foot stairway tower, numbered 106, was moved to the Argus site in 1958.[^2] The St. Louis County booklet describes Argus as the relocated Budd Hill tower from Lake County, but gives 1959 as its date, so the two booklets differ by a year.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list marks the Argus tower as gone.[^3]

No source I read describes the Argus site today.[^1][^2][^3]
