Lookout Ridge was a lookout site about 13 miles east of Hayden Lake in Kootenai County, Idaho, on the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, at 5,292 feet.[^1][^2]

The lookout here was a cabin.[^1][^2] The site began as a camp in 1922, and a log cupola cabin went up on it in 1924.[^1][^2] The site also had two patrol points: one a mile to the southeast and one 2.5 miles east, both atop Leiberg Peak.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists Miles Standish as a staff member in 1929, citing a work by Bert Russell about the North Fork of the Coeur d'Alene.[^2]

Firelookout.com puts the loss at about 1950, while the Idaho Fire Lookouts page gives the 1950s.[^1][^2] The firelookout.org index now marks the site as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today. Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site a hike-in.[^2] Anyone who wants to look for it should check current access with the Coeur d'Alene National Forest before going.
