The Varnville Lookout Tower is in Hampton County, South Carolina, and is listed under US 1005 and SC 28.[^1] The register describes a 100-foot steel tower with a steel cab measuring 10 by 7 by 7 feet, built around 1936. The register suggests it is a Bethlehem Steel model.[^1] The register lists the South Carolina Forestry Commission as its administrator.[^1]

The Commission put up its first fire tower near Camden in August 1930, and it closed its tower system on October 1, 1993.[^2] A register entry for the Leesville tower describes about 140 towers the Commission built between the mid-1930s and 1993.[^3] Listing the Commission as administrator suggests Varnville was one of those towers, though no source we read says so outright.[^1][^3]

The register names no tower operator. The number of hours an operator spent in the cab depended on the fire danger. When smoke was seen, two towers each took a bearing on it with a sighting device called an alidade.[^2] The register says this tower's alidade is a different style from most others in the state.[^1] We found no record of fires this tower reported.

The register describes the tower as being in poor shape, and says it now holds radio repeaters.[^1] It does not say when its lookout years ended. If this was one of the Commission's towers, they ended no later than October 1, 1993, when the Commission closed its tower system.[^2]

The register accepted the tower on March 27, 2014, on the nomination of Michael T. Finch, Jr.[^1] We found no visitor information. The tower carries radio equipment and is in poor condition, so it should not be climbed. Ask the Forestry Commission about access before going near it.[^1]
