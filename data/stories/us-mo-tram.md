Tram Tower was a lookout on Tram Ridge in Shannon County, in what the records call the Clark National Forest.[^1][^2]

Construction of the tower began in 1938, when work started on the new site on Tram Ridge.[^1] In April 1938 a local paper reported that the Possum Trot fire tower had been dismantled and would be set up again on Tram Ridge.[^1] Plans for the site also listed a dwelling for the lookout, a garage with a workshop, a fence, a cistern and a latrine.[^1]

In July 1940 a local paper reported that Willie Martin and his wife had moved from Tram Tower to High Tower.[^1]

In 1962 the U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey described a steel tower on four legs, with an observation room enclosed at its top, roughly 75 feet high.[^2] A later survey recovery in 1978 placed the station at the apex of the tower, described it as a four-legged structure about 75 feet high, and rated its condition as good.[^2]

The Central US Lookouts record marks Tram as removed, but gives no date for its removal.[^1] The 1978 survey is the latest description of the tower in the sources found. Neither source says what remains at the site today.[^1][^2]
