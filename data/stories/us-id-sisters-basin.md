Sisters Basin Lookout stood in the St. Joe National Forest, about eight miles southeast of Avery in Shoshone County, at 5,506 feet.[^1][^2] Both pages list a 1932 L-4 tower.[^1][^2]

Lloyd V. Donally, who came to the St. Joe in 1927, recalls that most of the forest's lookouts and buildings were put up in two periods: in 1930 and 1931 by Forest Service crews, and in 1934 and 1935 by Civilian Conservation Corps workers.[^3] His account does not name this lookout.[^3]

Both pages list the tower as gone in 1953, but neither says how it went.[^1][^2]

Do not confuse this site with the lookout on Middle Sister, about 10 miles southeast of Avery. There, a 35-foot tower replaced an earlier log cupola cabin in 1951 and was accidentally burned in 1953.[^4] The sources do not say whether the two losses are linked.[^1][^2][^4]

Neither page describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page labels the spot hike-in, but it gives no route or access details.[^2]
