The Loman Fire Tower stands in Koochiching County, Minnesota, on private land.[^1][^2]

The register dates the tower to 1927 and describes it as an 80-foot ladder tower.[^1] A county survey table lists the Minnesota Forest Service, now the state forestry division of the Department of Natural Resources, as the agency behind it.[^2] A 1967 survey note describes an 80-foot, four-legged structure with a metal cabin on top.[^2] The same note calls the cab both bird-cage style and octagonal, so its shape is not settled.[^2]

The register says the tower, a residence and a garage together once made up a state forestry office.[^1] The survey note adds that the homes there were once a state forestry station.[^2] Neither source names a lookout or records a fire the tower watched.[^1][^2]

Today the tower is privately owned.[^1] The register names William Multer as the private landowner.[^1] The survey table says the owner is a private party and that the site is not accessible.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^3]

The register entry was made on January 8, 2021, with Jacob Hufnagle as nominator.[^1] We could not confirm who built the tower or what it looked like beyond these notes.[^1][^2] Because the site is private, do not visit or climb the tower without the landowner's permission.[^1][^2]
