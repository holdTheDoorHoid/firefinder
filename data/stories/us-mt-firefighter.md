Firefighter Lookout stands on Firefighter Mountain in Flathead County, Montana, about nine miles southeast of Hungry Horse.[^2] The present tower is a 41-foot timber structure with an L-4 cab, built in 1953.[^1][^2][^3]

The site was first established in 1923 with a 12-by-12-foot frame cabin, and a cupola was added the year after.[^2] The 1953 tower stands about a mile and a half northwest of that cabin.[^2] The NHLR says it replaced a log cabin cupola lookout.[^2][^1]

A 1952 newspaper item describes the new tower as a replacement for the Forest Service's lookout, made necessary by the new reservoir.[^3] The Bureau of Reclamation handled the contracts.[^3] Valley Construction Co. of St. Ignatius was awarded the July 1952 contract, $34,840 for the tower, the house and 3.85 miles of access road.[^3]

Reports disagree on who built it. An April 1952 item says Columbia Falls Lumber Company got a $3,217 contract for the tower.[^3] An August 1953 report says the Valley Construction contract also covered erecting the tower and house.[^3]

Firelookout.com reports summer staffing, and its list of known staff has a single entry, for 1957.[^2] The sources I checked describe no fires at the site.

The NHLR says the hip roof was later replaced with a flat roof that has a three-foot overhang.[^1] Firelookout.com says a flat roof was added in later years.[^2]

A Flathead hiking guide calls it an active fire lookout, reached by Forest Service road 38B, a four-mile climb up a wooded ridge.[^4] Vehicles can pass a gate on that road after July 1.[^4]

No rental listing turned up for Firefighter, and none of the sources says whether the cab is open to visitors. Check with the Flathead National Forest before going up.
