Jumbo Mountain sits in Snohomish County, within the Mount Baker-Snoqualmie National Forest, about 4 miles south of Darrington.[^1][^2]

The register dates the site to 1915, when a tent and a platform tower stood there.[^1] Willhiteweb says the camp moved to Jumbo the year after it was first used at another lookout in 1915.[^4] Washington Lookouts dates the Jumbo lookout to 1916, when its duties were transferred from another lookout.[^3]

Willhiteweb names Rowland Atchson as the first lookout man, and says he also held the first post on Gold Hill in 1915.[^4]

Sources say the site was later abandoned, and the register describes it as gone.[^1][^2] Washington Lookouts marks it as removed.[^3]

No source describes a standing structure today. Willhiteweb visited and found faint signs of a trail in the summit rocks, along with some rock work, but no relics.[^4] Willhiteweb suggests the site may be the most difficult former lookout in Washington to reach, with no easy way to the summit and fair gravel road access.[^4]
