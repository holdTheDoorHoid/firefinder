Pillsbury Reservation was an open tower in Sullivan County, New Hampshire, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association now lists it as gone.[^1]

Pillsbury State Park lies mainly in Washington, with a smaller part in Goshen. The land was bought in 1920 and the park opened in 1952.[^2]

A 2015 forum post relays that Iris Baird, the New Hampshire fire tower historian, said the tower had stood on an unnamed peak inside that park.[^3] When the poster visited the summit that year, they found eyebolts, cable and a fireplace, and there was almost no view.[^3]

A commenter in a separate forum thread doubts the list's accuracy. They think the Pillsbury tower was south of the main park, not north.[^4] The exact spot is still unsettled.

The 2015 post is the most recent description I found. No source describes the site as it stands today, or whether visitors may go there.[^3]
