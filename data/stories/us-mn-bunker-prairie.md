Bunker Prairie started out as a different tower. It was first the Wynne Lake Lookout Tower, near Biwabik in northern St. Louis County, and in 1957 it was moved to a site east of Anoka.[^1][^2] It was sometimes called the Anoka tower.[^1] It was a 54-foot ladder tower, and the county list names the Minnesota Forest Service as its owner, a body now part of the state's Department of Natural Resources.[^1]

Anoka County removed the tower in 2000.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts listing for Bunker Prairie also marks it as removed.[^3]

The site is in what is now Bunker Hills Park, a county park complex east of Anoka.[^1] We found no source that describes what, if anything, is still standing or visible there today, or whether the public can reach the spot. If you plan a visit, ask the county first.
