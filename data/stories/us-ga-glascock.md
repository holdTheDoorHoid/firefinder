The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers gives this tower's name as "glascock?", with a question mark, places it in Jefferson County at 33.09640, -82.43530, and tags it BLL-41144.[^1] A 2025 report from the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Georgia and South Carolina chapter calls it the Glascock tower.[^2]

The chapter's 2025 year-end report says that in January 2025, two of its leaders helped a civil engineering firm with the tower's history. The state transportation department had retained that firm for a Glascock County project.[^2] The report mentions Glascock County only in connection with that project, so the Jefferson County placement on the list is the only county given for the tower itself.[^1][^2]

No source I could read describes what, if anything, stands at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^2]: FFLA GA / SC Chapter 2025 Year-End Report, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/cr-gasc-2025.pdf (accessed 2026-10-21).
