Mauriceville, also known as Maurice, lies in north central Orange County, about seventeen miles northeast of Beaumont, where State Highways 12 and 62 cross.[^1] It was founded after two railroads were built, the Texarkana and Fort Smith in 1898 and the Orange and Northwestern in 1902.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list marks a Mauriceville tower as gone, but places it in Newton County.[^2]

A Texas lookout towers list last revised in January 2011 places a Mauriceville tower in Orange County, at 30.24517, -93.82062, the same coordinates the association gives.[^3]

The Handbook places the community in Orange County, and the tower list puts the tower there too, so the Newton County entry looks like the error.[^1][^3] No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^2][^3]
