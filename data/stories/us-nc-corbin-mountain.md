Survey records show a lookout tower on Corbin Mountain from at least 1979.[^1] The survey file places the mountain in Greenville County, South Carolina, a little south of the state line.[^1][^2] A North Carolina tower list gives the same spot as Henderson County, which does not match the survey file.[^3]

A 1979 survey description calls the structure the Corbin Mountain Lookout Tower. It describes an unpainted tower with four legs and a peaked roof, about 15 metres high.[^1]

The survey notes do not agree on where the station sits relative to the line.[^1] The 1979 description puts the station on the state line.[^1] A 1992 recovery note puts it 0.1 mile south of the line, in South Carolina.[^1]

In 1992 a South Carolina geodetic crew gave road directions to the tower, including a sign pointing to the Corbin Lookout Tower.[^1] A 2000 note lists the mountaintop as watershed property owned by the City of Greenville.[^1]

No source I found describes the site today, so it is not clear whether the tower still stands or whether the public can reach it.

[^2]: Corbin Mountain, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/corbin-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: North Carolina lookout towers list, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
