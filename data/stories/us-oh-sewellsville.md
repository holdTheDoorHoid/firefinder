The Sewellsville tower stood in Belmont County, Ohio, at an elevation of 1,362 feet.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes it as a 90-foot-high tower.[^1] It does not give a build date, a staffing history, or any fire the tower watched, and we did not find those details in the sources we could reach.

The register added the site to its list on September 3, 2014.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Ohio list marks the original site as gone.[^2] The same list shows a tower at Bethesda, also in Belmont County, as the relocated Sewellsville tower and as standing.[^2] We could not find when or why the tower was moved, or who moved it.

Bethesda is a village in Belmont County. Its website says it was incorporated in 1910, and that it is home to Epworth Park, which has a lake in the middle.[^3] None of the pages we read ties the Sewellsville tower to that park or to any other spot in the village. We also found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the original site.

Today the Bethesda tower is listed as standing.[^2] We could not confirm its exact spot, its condition, or whether the public may go up it, so do not assume it is open to visitors until the village or its owner says so.
