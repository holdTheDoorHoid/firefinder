The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers places a tower named Ellabell in Bryan County, at 32.12193, -81.48544, with an elevation of 79 (the list shows no unit).[^1] The list gives it GFC number 119, a code that points to an older Georgia Forestry Commission list covering 203 towers.[^1] It also carries the reference BLL-64694, and the list says the GFC supplied coordinates for 118 towers in that series.[^1] The compiler says names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own creation, so "Ellabell" is a label rather than a documented name for the tower.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public. The Forestry Commission's county contacts list its Bryan North unit at 615 Eldora Road in Ellabell.[^2] Check with the Forestry Commission before visiting, and do not climb a tower whose access you have not confirmed.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers (GA tower list with coordinates, elevation and GFC number), tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: County Contacts, Georgia Forestry Commission, https://gatrees.org/gfc-contacts-2/county-contacts/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
