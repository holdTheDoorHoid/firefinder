The Ruby Lookout Tower is listed in Chesterfield County as a South Carolina Forestry Commission lookout.[^1]

The Commission's first tower went up near Camden in August 1930.[^2] The system had operated statewide since the 1930s, and the Commission shut its last lookouts on October 1, 1993.[^3] Across the system, most tower sites covered about ten acres and included a year-round home for the operator, and from the 1960s on most operators were women.[^2]

A state report covering July 1944 to June 1945 says the Ruby fire tower was painted during that year.[^1]

Map data from OpenStreetMap tags the Ruby site as an observation tower of lattice construction.[^4]

No source we read describes the Ruby site today, or says whether the public can reach it.
