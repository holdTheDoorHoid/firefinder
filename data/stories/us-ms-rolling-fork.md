The Rolling Fork Lookout Tower is listed on the National Historic Lookout Register as US 1656, MS 22.[^1] The register places it in Issaquena County, Mississippi, and gives its elevation as 105 feet (32 meters).[^1] It lists a 100-foot Aermotor LS-40 with a straight staircase inside, and it gives the build date as unknown.[^1]

The county is not settled. The Forest Lookouts page for Rolling Fork places it in Sharkey County and lists the Mississippi Forestry Commission as the agency.[^2] We have kept the register's county here, and the two sources still disagree.[^1][^2]

The register records a site visit on July 8, 2022.[^1] It notes that the bottom flight of stairs has been removed.[^1] It also says the Mississippi Forestry Commission vacated the site.[^1] An unclear sentence seems to add that the Issaquena County Maintenance Department now uses it.[^1]

A deadly EF4 tornado hit the town of Rolling Fork on March 24, 2023.[^3] That storm formed in northern Issaquena County, the county the register gives for the tower, but none of the sources we read say whether it reached the lookout.[^3][^1]

The register gives no access information and no staffing history.[^1] Its notes on the removed stairs and on county use do not say whether the public may visit.[^1]
