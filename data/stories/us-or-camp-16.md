Camp 16 appears on the Oregon Forest Lookouts list under Marion County.[^1] The list gives the agency as the Clackamas-Marion FPA and places the site in section 22 of township 8 South, range 2 East.[^1] Its structure type is given as "Tree."[^1]

Lookout trees were a basic fire-watch perch. A ladder or a row of spikes fixed to a tall trunk let rangers or crews climb up and survey the land.[^2] A more elaborate version often built a railed platform atop the trunk, and a tent or shelter at the base housed the watcher.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
