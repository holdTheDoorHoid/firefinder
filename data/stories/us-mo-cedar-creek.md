The Forest Lookouts site lists a Missouri Conservation Commission tower at Cedar Creek, in Taney County.[^1] It gives the site's location as 22N-18W-30, a township, range and section reference.[^1]

The commission's annual report for fiscal 1951-52, as quoted on the site, records that it obtained a towersite and built a tower that year.[^1]

A Coast and Geodetic Survey station, described in 1953, lay roughly two miles south-southeast of Cedar Creek, one mile east of County Highway K.[^1] The survey describes a steel tower on four legs, carrying an observation room, roughly 120 feet (37 m) high to the peak of its roof.[^1]

The site marks the survey station as removed.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site or whether it is open to visitors today.[^1]

[^1]: Cedar Creek - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts (centraluslookouts.weebly.com), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/cedar-creek.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
