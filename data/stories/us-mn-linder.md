The Linder Fire Tower stands in Beltrami Island State Forest, in Beltrami County, and a survey of Beltrami County's towers lists it as standing.[^1][^3] The survey numbers the tower 64.[^1] Before it moved to this site, it was a Game & Fish lookout, and the survey says the Conservation Department's Game & Fish section seemingly erected it.[^1]

The first site lay near Moose River East road in the northwest of the county, where a CCC camp was in the area.[^1] The survey's table gives 1925 for the tower, but its notes say only that the tower may date to that year.[^1] The tower was moved to its present site in 1953.[^1]

The current tower is a ladder-type tower about 80 feet (24 m) tall, with an octagonal cab.[^1] The survey's notes place it among Minnesota's oldest lookouts.[^1] Wikidata puts the site at about 1,237 feet (377 m) above sea level.[^2]

We did not find staffing records or reported fires for this tower in the sources we checked.

The survey lists the tower's owner as the Minnesota Forest Service, which it says is now the Forestry Division of the Department of Natural Resources.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list also shows the tower as standing.[^3] We could not confirm when either source last checked it.

We did not find a source that says whether visitors may climb the tower. It stands in a state forest, but that alone does not show the public may climb it.[^1] Check with the Minnesota DNR before you go, and do not climb the tower unless it is open to the public.
