Slate Point is a crow's nest lookout in Ravalli County, Montana, in the Bitterroot National Forest.[^1][^3] Rex's page places it about 8 miles south of the West Fork Ranger Station, at about 6,260 feet.[^1]

Rex's page dates the crow's nest and a log cabin to the 1920s.[^1] Forest Lookouts gives the legal location as 2S-22W-2.[^3]

The sources differ on the lookout's state. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list calls it Abandoned and gives its position as 45.68968, -114.28180.[^2] Rex's page says the crow's nest is still there and the log cabin is in ruins, but it gives no date for that note.[^1]

No source we read says whether the site is open to visitors, so check with the Bitterroot National Forest before going to look for it.
