The Adams tower stood in Robertson County, at an elevation of 640 feet.[^1] The Tennessee Division of Forestry listed it in District 6.[^2] Its code on the state list is 6-6, and the tnlandforms list says codes like this are district and tower numbers from a 1976 list of state forest towers.[^1] The same page says the Division had 208 tower sites around 1976.[^1]

Both lists mark the tower as removed.[^1][^2] The tnlandforms list adds that many Tennessee towers have been taken down, and that the sites of most of them now belong to private owners.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the Adams site, or whether the public can reach it. Please ask the landowner before going onto the property.

[^1]: Tennessee lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Adams, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/adams2.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
