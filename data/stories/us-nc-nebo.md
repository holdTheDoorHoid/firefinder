Nebo, in Yadkin County, North Carolina, was the site of a fire tower.[^1][^2] The Coast and Geodetic Survey set a survey mark there in 1954, on the highest point about three miles southeast of Boonville.[^2]

A North Carolina Division of Forestry recovery note from 1960 says a fire tower was built 12 feet east of that mark in September 1959.[^2] A 1992 recovery note by the North Carolina Geodetic Survey places the mark on the west side of the fire tower.[^2] The 2018 recovery found the mark in good condition near a chain-link fence.[^2]

No source we read describes the site today, so we cannot say whether the tower still stands or whether the public may visit.[^1][^2]

[^1]: North Carolina lookouts, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/north-carolina.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Nebo (survey mark PID FZ1823), NGS data sheet, National Geodetic Survey, https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=FZ1823 (accessed 2026-10-21).
