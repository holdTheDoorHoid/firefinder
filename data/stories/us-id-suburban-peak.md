Suburban Peak rises to 4,402 feet in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, about 18 miles north of Kellogg in Shoshone County.[^1][^2] The Shoshone County list places it at 52N-3E-31.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list gives its coordinates as 47.81161 north and 116.13685 west.[^4]

A crow's nest was recorded on the peak in 1931.[^1][^2][^3] Around 1934, a 50-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab followed.[^1][^2][^3]

The tower was gone by 1952, according to three sources.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site as "Gone / Destroyed," and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks it as gone.[^2][^4]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors. Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as hike-in.[^2]
