The National Historic Lookout Register lists Isleta Lookout, also called Isleta (Manzanita), in Torrance County, New Mexico.[^1][^2] The register gives no construction date for the tower.[^1]

The tower is an Aermotor 7x7 cab used for observation only, set on a 50-foot open steel tower.[^1] The site also has a residence cabin inside a fenced enclosure.[^1] The register lists the Isleta-Acoma Indian Agency as the administering office.[^1]

Brad Eells nominated the lookout, and the register recorded it on July 7, 2022, as US 1650, NM 49.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's New Mexico list shows the tower as standing.[^2]

The register places the site on the Isleta-Acoma Indian Reservation.[^1] I found no staffing history, no list of fires it watched and no visitor information, so anyone interested in the site should check with the reservation's tribal government before planning a trip.

[^1]: Isleta Lookout, National Historic Lookout Register (nhlr.org), http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/nm/isleta-lookout/ (accessed 2026-10-08).
[^2]: New Mexico Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association (firelookout.org), https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/ (accessed 2026-10-08).
