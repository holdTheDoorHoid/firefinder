The Eastern U.S. Lookouts site has a page for a lookout named High Hill.[^1] The TopoQuest map database places a High Hill Fire Tower in Walker County, Alabama, at 672 feet (205 m) above sea level.[^2] Statewide, the first lookouts of the early 20th century were platforms set in trees.[^3] Soon after, the forest service began putting up wooden towers made from creosote wood.[^3] Many of those wooden towers were later swapped for steel ones during the New Deal.[^3] The TopoQuest listing still shows a tower at that point, but it does not show whether the structure stands today.[^2] Probably fewer than 75 of Alabama's historic lookout structures remain standing today.[^3] Many are in rough shape and unsafe, and climbing them is against the law.[^3] Please do not climb any tower you find.
