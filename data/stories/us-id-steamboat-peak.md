The Steamboat Peak lookout stood on a peak in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, at 4,143 feet.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list places the peak in Shoshone County.[^4] A camp is listed on the site in 1930.[^1][^3]

In 1934 a pole tower 40 feet tall, with an L-4 cab on top, went up about half a mile south of the peak's southernmost point.[^1][^2][^3] The tower was destroyed in the 1950s, and the sources give only the decade, not a year.[^1][^3]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site lists the peak as a hike-in site, with the lookout destroyed.[^2]

None of the sources I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3] Since the tower is gone, there is no lookout to climb.[^2]
