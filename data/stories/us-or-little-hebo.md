Little Hebo sits in Yamhill County, Oregon, on the Siuslaw National Forest.[^1] The register describes a 101-foot steel tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab and a house, built in 1935.[^1] Newspaper items from 1934, quoted on a lookout-history site, show work on a 100-foot tower that year, and in October the forest supervisor feared a strong wind could blow it over unless it was properly anchored.[^3]

The sources disagree on two details. The register says steel, but firelookout.com says the tower could have been wooden.[^1][^2] The register also gives 101 feet where the 1934 papers say 100, so I have used 100 feet.[^1][^3]

In 1958 a 100-foot tower and a 16-by-22-foot ground cabin went up on Table Mountain, about 12 miles south-southeast of Toledo in Lincoln County. The Table Mountain history says that tower was taken apart at Little Hebo and rebuilt there.[^4][^5]

The Little Hebo history and the register date the move to 1959, and the Table Mountain page says the station was to be in use for the 1959 fire season.[^1][^3][^4] The Table Mountain tower was removed in 1973.[^4] firelookout.com calls it destroyed that year, and says it may have been the tower moved from Little Hebo.[^5]

No source I read describes what remains at Little Hebo or at Table Mountain today, and none says whether either site is open to visitors.
