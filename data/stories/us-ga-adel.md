Adel is listed as a Georgia Forestry Commission lookout in Cook County, Georgia.[^1] A list of Georgia lookout towers gives it the GFC number 815, at about 31.11 degrees north and 83.41 degrees west, under the name "adel".[^2] The list says its names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own, so the name is a label rather than a recorded one.[^2]

The same list has a second Adel entry, "adel GFC", about 1.2 km to the west-south-west, carrying the note "new".[^2]

The Georgia Forestry Commission's Cook County unit is based at Barney.[^3] Its operations report, as of July 1, 2020, gives 89,000 forested acres under the unit's protection.[^3]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything remains there or whether visitors can reach it.
