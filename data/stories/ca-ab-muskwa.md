Alberta's register lists Muskwa as a steel tower, 30 m (98 ft) tall, at an elevation of 681 m (2,234 ft).[^1] It gives 2019 as the year of construction.[^1] Its access code is "fly", which points to access by air rather than by road.[^1]

OpenStreetMap maps the site as an observation tower named Muskwa Fire Tower, and tags its construction as steel.[^2]

Alberta's lookouts come in two main forms: steel towers on high ground across the boreal forest, and alpine cabins of one or two storeys on mountain tops.[^3] An Alberta Historic Places article says steel came into use for towers in the 1920s and gradually took over from wood.[^4] The province says observers as a group spot roughly 30% of fires each year.[^3]

Alberta bars the public from climbing lookout towers.[^3] No source describes the site today, though the register marks the lookout as active.[^1]
