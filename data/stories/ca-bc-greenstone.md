Greenstone Lookout stands on the summit of Greenstone Mountain, close to Kamloops in Thompson-Nicola, British Columbia.[^1][^4] The register places it in Greenstone Provincial Park.[^1]

Sources disagree on when the first lookout went up. The register says the federal government set up a Dominion lookout here in the 1920s.[^1] One web account says the first lookout was established in 1916, and another puts it around 1915 to 1916.[^4][^2]

The register says that lookout was later replaced by the cab that stands today.[^1] Both web accounts date that hip-roof cab to the mid-1950s.[^2][^4] A 2023 post by the Four Wheel Drive Association of BC calls the tower 93 years old, which points to about 1930 and matches neither date.[^3]

The register says the lookout was abandoned for many years before it became a warming hut for backcountry users.[^1] Another account says it fell out of use, was fixed up on and off, and was eventually refurbished.[^2] Greenstone Mountain Provincial Park was officially established between 1996 and 1997.[^2]

In 2023 the Logan Lake ATV Club and the Four Wheel Drive Association of BC announced a partnership with Recreation Sites and Trails BC for the tower's ongoing care, with extensive repairs promised for the following year.[^3] The register lists the two clubs as cooperators and gives December 31, 2023 as its registration date.[^1]

Today the cab has a wood stove, which makes an overnight stay possible, even in winter.[^2][^4] The summit is becoming crowded with communications towers, but the lookout still stands apart on the summit.[^4]

Road access begins at the Cherry Creek side. The last stretch is steep and rocky, and many drivers choose to park and hike it.[^2] The two web accounts disagree on the highway for that turn-off: one names Highway 1 and the other names Highway 97.[^2][^4] One blog says an experienced 4x4 driver can reach the lookout in summer, and that snowmobiles can reach it in winter.[^4] The register calls it a warming hut for backcountry users, and none of the sources we read reports a closure.[^1][^4]
