Chediski Lookout is a 121-foot Aermotor lookout on the Fort Apache-White Mountain Indian Reservation in Navajo County, Arizona.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register lists it as US 202, AZ 9 and dates its registration to February 17, 1997.[^1]

The register says the lookout was constructed in 1936 and that an original log groundhouse has been preserved on the site.[^1] We found no source naming the builder or the crew that raised the tower.[^1] The register notes that people often take it for the tallest tower on the reservation, because of the way it projects from a mountaintop.[^1]

Wikipedia gives the peak an elevation of 7,462 feet and says the peak lent its name to the Rodeo-Chediski fires.[^2] The Rodeo-Chediski Fire burned from June 18, 2002, into July, and it began as two separate fires; the Chediski part was first reported near the peak on June 20.[^3] Sources we checked do not say whether the lookout was damaged or spared in that fire.[^3]

The register describes the lookout as in active service, but it gives no date for that description.[^1] The Arizona lookout list we checked shows the tower as standing.[^4] The register names the White Mountain Apache Agency as the site's administrator.[^1] Anyone curious about a visit should start there. Do not climb a tower that its administrators have not opened to the public.
