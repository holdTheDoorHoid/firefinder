Low Pass is a lookout site in Lane County, in section 23 of Township 16 South, Range 7 West, and the Nelson Ridge truck trail leads to it.[^1] The Forest Lookouts (Oregon) page files it under the Western Lane Fire Patrol.[^1]

In April 1938 the Forest Log reported that a joint crew from the Veneta side camp and the main camp had begun clearing the east end of the Nelson Ridge truck trail.[^1] That was a highball, or rush, job, since the trail led to the lookout site, and the aim was to finish it in time for use that summer.[^1] On June 28, 1938, the Eugene Guard reported that the CCC was now building Low Pass Lookout.[^1] A 1938 annual report to the Oregon Department of Forestry records that a road and telephone line had been started for it.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and we found nothing on whether it is open to the public.[^1]

[^1]: Low Pass, Forest Lookouts (Oregon), https://oregonlookouts.weebly.com/low-pass.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
