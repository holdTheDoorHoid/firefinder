Wrens is a city in Jefferson County, with 2,217 residents at the 2020 census.[^1] The state legislature made it a town in 1901, and it became a city again in 1970.[^1] The Wikipedia article gives its location as 33°12′29″N 82°23′15″W.[^1]

The same Georgia lookout list places a tower at Wrens, at 33.22193, -82.43553, with an elevation of 501. The list gives no unit for elevation; read as feet, that is about 153 meters.[^2] The entry also carries a Georgia Forestry Commission (GFC) number, 620, and a code, BLL-50812, that the list does not explain.[^2]

Statewide, the Georgia Forestry Commission began shifting from staffed towers to aerial patrols in the late 1980s, and many towers were scrapped or sold.[^3]

No source we found describes what, if anything, stands at the Wrens site today, or whether the public can reach it.
