Sugarloaf Mountain in Coos County, Oregon, had a lookout on it, but the sources do not agree on when it was built.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com describes a cupola cabin from 1928.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page for the Coos entry says a lookout house was constructed around 1935.[^2] That page places the site at township 29 south, range 12 west, section 13.[^2]

Firelookout.com puts the site ten miles south-southeast of Coquille, at 1,560 feet.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page says the lookout was not on the 1958 inventory of lookouts, and it marks the site as removed.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list records the Coos site as type "Ground" and status "Gone", with no register number.[^3] Firelookout.com gives only the word abandoned, with no date for when it was abandoned.[^1]

A different Sugar Loaf lookout, at the head of the Sixes River in Curry County, was being built in March 1926.[^4] The Forest Lookouts page files that one under the same Coos County patrol association, so the two are easy to confuse.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the Coos lookout, or whether the ground is open to visitors.[^3]
