Nottinghill was a lookout tower in Ozark County, Missouri, inside the Mark Twain National Forest.[^1] The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the tower in 1953 as a survey station.[^1] The station was located by distance and direction from a triangulation station also named Nottinghill, 35.66 feet (10.871 m) away.[^1] The surveyed point was the center of the tower's base, about one and a half miles west of the Nottinghill post office.[^1]

The tower was a four-legged steel structure, painted aluminum, about 50 feet (15 m) tall, with a small open cage at the top.[^1] The survey lists its map as the Willhoit quadrangle, a USGS sheet dated 1968.[^1]

The Central US Lookouts site lists the lookout as removed.[^1] The page does not describe what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1]
