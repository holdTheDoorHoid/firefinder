Tom Dunigan's list of Georgia lookout towers places a tower on Sawnee Mountain in Forsyth County, at an elevation of 1,959 (read as feet, 597 m).[^1] A 2000 trip report by Fred Lobdell gives the summit as 1,960 ft or more, and places the mountain about two miles northwest of Cumming.[^2]

The report says the topographic map of the area showed radio towers and a lookout tower on the mountain. It notes that development on the mountain came after that map was printed in 1992.[^2] The report describes the lookout tower as fairly new, possibly a replacement for an earlier structure, with a spiral staircase wound around its outside.[^2] A house beside it was round and built on a central pillar.[^2] The report's directions say the road bears right at the hairpin, that a left turn there leads into a private driveway, and that the tower is a short distance past that point.[^2]

Wikipedia says radio station WWEV-FM 91.5 broadcasts from a tower on the summit.[^3] Forsyth County Parks and Recreation manages the Sawnee Mountain Preserve on the mountain, and the county reports more than 11 miles of hiking trails.[^3][^4]

None of the sources checked gives the lookout tower's current condition. The 2000 account places the tower beside a house, and its directions point to a private driveway, so treat access as private until a site check says otherwise.[^2]
