The Croatan lookout is listed in Craven County, North Carolina, on the Croatan National Forest.[^1] A statewide list of North Carolina towers places it at 34.96972 north and 76.97277 west and files it under Craven County.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts listing for the state gives a build span of 1928 to 1930 and describes a 100-foot (30 m) steel tower, citing a state report from the Department of Conservation and Development.[^1] The land that later became the forest was not yet a national forest at that time. The federal government acquired it from 1933 to 1935, and President Franklin D. Roosevelt made it a national forest in July 1936, covering about 77,000 acres across Craven, Carteret and Jones counties.[^3]

No source we read describes the site today. We cannot say whether a tower still stands there, what remains, or whether the public may visit. Ask the Croatan National Forest before going, and do not climb any tower without permission.
