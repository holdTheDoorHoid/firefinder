Coyote Mountain rises to 2,870 feet (875 meters) in Lewis County, about 11 miles south-southwest of Morton.[^1][^2]

Two websites date the first station on the summit to 1942, when an AWS "smokehouse" and a platform tower were built.[^1][^2] Willhite says he does not list Coyote Mountain as an AWS site, so he is not sure what that history means.[^4]

In 1955 a standard lookout station went up: a 40-foot tower with a 14-by-14-foot cabin on top.[^3] The cabin was built of plywood and glass, with an aluminum roof that overhangs it as a sun shade.[^3]

Mr. and Mrs. Jack Reed were the lookouts in 1954 and again in 1956.[^4]

The site was abandoned in 1967.[^1] Firetower.org and Willhite both say the removal dates are not known.[^1][^4]

In 2012, Leslie Romer located four footing blocks with rebar, grouped close together.[^4] Willhite searched briefly for the footings in 2017 and did not spot them.[^4] No source describes a standing structure on the summit today.

Access is by permit.[^4] Willhite says a Longview-St Helens permit is needed, after which the site is just a drive-up, and that DNR roads from the north get you very close.[^4] The land is part of a Weyerhaeuser tree farm, so get the permit before going.[^4]
