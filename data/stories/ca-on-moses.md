Land Information Ontario's fire-tower layer lists a Moses Fire Tower at about 46.5653 N, 81.9986 W.[^1] The point is accurate to within 50 metres (164 ft), and the record was marked verified in February 2010.[^1]

A second entry in the same layer, SF-56, is listed with the location description "Moses Twp." It sits about 4 km north-west of this point, and it was marked verified in April 1998.[^1] The layer does not say whether the two entries are the same tower or separate structures.[^1]

No source I could read describes the tower or its site today, or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Fire Tower records, tower layer (LIO_Open10, layer 14), Land Information Ontario, Government of Ontario, https://ws.lioservices.lrc.gov.on.ca/arcgis2/rest/services/LIO_OPEN_DATA/LIO_Open10/MapServer/14/query?where=CLASS_SUBTYPE%3D%27Fire%20Tower%27&outFields=*&outSR=4326&f=geojson (accessed 2026-10-20).
