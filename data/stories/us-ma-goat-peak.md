The Goat Peak Lookout Tower sits on the northwest side of Mount Tom State Reservation, in Holyoke.[^1] Goat Peak rises to 822 feet and is a subsidiary peak of Mount Tom.[^1]

The register dates the tower to 1928 and says it is still kept up.[^1] I could not confirm the 1928 date from a second source. The tower is about 42 feet tall.[^1] A steel frame sits on four concrete bollards and carries a 10-foot-square platform, which visitors reach by three flights of stairs.[^1] As of 2025, the register says new lumber had recently gone into the platform floor.[^1]

According to the register, the tower is currently used for recreation and for watching the southern hawk migration each fall.[^1] I found no source saying it was built for fire watch.

The state's hike guide urges visitors to climb the Goat Peak observation tower, and calls it possibly the finest view on the Mount Tom Range.[^2] The guide puts Goat Peak 0.3 miles up the New England Trail from its trailhead.[^2] A local range site says the reservation has two viewing towers, one at the Visitor Center and a second on Goat Peak, and that the Goat Peak tower is reached by the New England Trail.[^3]

The tower is reached on foot: the register says several trails branch off Reservation Road, and the Massachusetts DCR administers the tower.[^1]
