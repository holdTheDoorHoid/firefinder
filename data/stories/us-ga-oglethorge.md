The Georgia lookout list places a tower it calls "oglethorge" in Macon County, at about 32.30 degrees north and 84.05 degrees west.[^1] The list also carries a Georgia Forestry Commission number, 322, taken from an older commission list.[^1]

The name is close to Oglethorpe, a city that is the county seat of Macon County, so that is the likely town.[^1][^2]

A photograph on Wikimedia Commons, dated 30 December 2014 and credited to Michael Rivera, is filed as "Georgia Forestry Commission Tower, Oglethorpe.JPG".[^3] Its camera position is about 54 meters from the list's coordinates, a distance worked out from the two sets of figures.[^1][^3]

The photograph is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 4.0 license.[^3]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether the tower still stands or whether visitors can reach it.
