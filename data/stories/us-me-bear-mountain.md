The Bear Mountain lookout began on a different peak.[^2] According to the state's 1934 annual report, the steel tower erected on Mount Agamenticus in 1918 was taken apart in the fall of 1934, lengthened by fifteen feet to a height of forty feet, and re-erected on Bear Mountain in Hartford, Oxford County.[^2] Norris Trafton of York provided workers, teams and wagons to move the materials up the mountain.[^3]

A 1935 photographic print in the Maine Memory collection shows a Civilian Conservation Corps crew from Lewiston doing road work on the way to the tower.[^4] The catalog places that work near Waterford, which conflicts with the Hartford location in the other records.[^4] We follow the Hartford location used by the other sources.[^4][^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association registered the site in 2008.[^1] The register places the tower's removal somewhere between 1950 and 1961.[^1] The association's list marks the site as gone.[^5]

No source we read describes the summit today. We found nothing on whether any remains survive there, or whether the mountain is open to visitors.
