The Newport Fire Tower, also called the Simmons tower, is in the Croatan National Forest in Carteret County, about half a mile from the town of Newport.[^1][^3] A 2017 WCTI report says Civilian Conservation Corps workers from Camp Patterson, an African American CCC camp, built it in 1935.[^1] A 2023 photo caption gives its height as 96 feet.[^2]

Before he went to war, Furnifold McLendel Simmons manned the lookout from 1935 until 1943.[^1] The tower was later named in his honor.[^1] By June 1948 the Carteret County News-Times was calling it the Simmons tower, and it reported that a dedication service had recently honored him there.[^3] The paper described him as a crew chief on a B-24 who was killed in an air crash near Peterson Field, California, on April 5, 1944.[^3]

The 2017 WCTI report said the tower was still standing and was the last of six fire towers left on the forest.[^1] It also called the tower the last standing structure Camp Patterson built, and said it was eligible for the National Register of Historic Places.[^1]

The tower sits on national forest land.[^1] The 2017 report, which covered a Forest Service notice, said visitors had left pet memorials at the site, and that staff would remove any that were not taken away.[^1] The notice reminded the public that putting private memorials or remains on the forest is banned.[^1]
