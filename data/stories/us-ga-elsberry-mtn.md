Elsberry Mountain is the highest spot in Dallas, Georgia.[^1] The Dallas article puts its summit at 1,310 feet (400 m).[^1] A compiled list of Georgia lookout towers gives an entry, GA118 "elsberry mtn", in Paulding County, with an elevation of 1,302 and no stated unit. Read as feet, that is 397 m.[^2] The two figures differ by eight feet, and neither source explains the gap.

The list's compiler built it from the USGS name database, which covers towers 1 to 72, and from 1:100,000 USGS topographic maps and NGS data sheets.[^2] The compiler named towers numbered 73 and above personally, so "elsberry mtn" is a label, not a name recorded on the tower.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register's Georgia page names 29 lookouts, and none is called Elsberry.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, stands on the summit today, or whether the public can reach it.
