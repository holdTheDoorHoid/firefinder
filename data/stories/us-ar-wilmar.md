Forest Lookouts lists a tower in Drew County, Arkansas, under the label "Wilmar Unit 5, Tower 4," and names the Arkansas Forestry Commission as its agency.[^1] The Encyclopedia of Arkansas says the Commission was established by Act 234 in 1931 and also built many towers.[^2]

Wilmar itself was a timber center in the early twentieth century.[^3] The Encyclopedia of Arkansas describes it today as mostly a suburb of Monticello, the county seat.[^3] Preserve Arkansas, which keeps an Endangered List of fire lookouts, says about 40 of the state's more than 120 documented lookouts still stand, though the page gives no date for that count.[^4]

The listing marks the Wilmar tower as removed.[^1] No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
