A 1911 state report, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, describes a steel lookout on a hill near Gull Lake, west of Brainerd.[^1] The Cass County list places an early 60-foot steel tower on Dutchman's Bluff, on the north shore, and says it was probably among the first steel towers shipped to the Brainerd area.[^2]

The Cass County list also gives a separate Tower 19: a 100-foot Aermotor stairway tower, erected by the Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC) in 1937 on a hill along the west side of the lake.[^2] The sources do not say whether Tower 19 replaced the 1911 tower.[^1][^2]

A 1949 Brainerd Daily Dispatch item described the lookout on the western shore of Gull Lake and said Minnesota State Forest Service members staffed it throughout the fire season.[^1]

A June 1970 Brainerd Daily Dispatch item quoted a Brainerd forestry office official saying someone had forced the lock on the Gull Lake lookout, though apparently nothing was harmed or missing.[^1] The Cass County list describes Tower 19 in the past tense, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Gull Lake tower as gone.[^2][^3] The sources do not say when or how it came down.[^1][^2][^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains of either tower today.[^1][^2] The Cass County list says Mt. Ski Gull is located on Tower 19's hill, and the sources we read say nothing about public access.[^2]
