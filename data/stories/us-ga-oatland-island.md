Tom Dunigan's list of Georgia lookout towers includes an "oatland island" entry in Chatham County, with an elevation of 13 ft (4 m).[^1] Its note mentions the wildlife center and ends with a question mark, which suggests the placement is uncertain.[^1]

A Vanishing Georgia post describes an old water tower by the center's entrance, which it says is scheduled for demolition.[^2] The post does not call that tower a lookout or connect it to Dunigan's entry.[^2] The same post says the Centers for Disease Control (CDC) used the main building as a laboratory until 1973, that the Chatham County Board of Education has owned the site since then, and that the education center now serves over 20,000 students and visitors a year.[^2]

Today, no source we could reach says whether the old water tower still stands or whether visitors can go near it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Oatland Island, Savannah, Vanishing Georgia, https://vanishinggeorgia.com/2015/04/28/oatland-island-savannah/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
