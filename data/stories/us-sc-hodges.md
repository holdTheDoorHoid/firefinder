A 1945 fire protection plan for Greenwood County called for two lookout towers over one hundred feet high.[^1] The Index-Journal reported on November 22, 1945 that one would stand south-southeast of Hodges, and that the land for the tower near Hodges had been bought from J.W. Ellison.[^1]

Each tower was to sit on a ten-acre site, with a cottage for the tower operator.[^1] Under the terms of the land purchase, if the towers were ever abandoned, the property and its improvements would go back to Greenwood County.[^1] The county provided the two sites but no funds, and the system was under county control.[^1]

A state commission report for 1969-70 says construction started on a house at Hodges Tower, and the 1970-71 report says one at the Hodges site was completed.[^1] The 1969-70 report puts that house "in Berkeley County," but the other items on the same page say Greenwood County, so the Berkeley reference may be an error.[^1]

On July 13, 1993, The Index-Journal reported that the Hodges Fire Tower was one of two Greenwood County towers to be phased out that October.[^1] The commission closed its fire tower system on October 1, 1993.[^2]

No source I found gives a completion date for the Hodges tower, describes what remains of it or its site today, or says whether the public may visit it.
