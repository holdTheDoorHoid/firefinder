The Mount Willing Lookout Tower stands in Lowndes County, Alabama.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register gives its elevation as 348 feet (106 m).[^1]

The Register gives 1938 as the build year, but its own description says the tower dates to 1936, so the two figures disagree.[^1] The description calls it a 100-foot Aermotor MC-39 steel tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab, built by CCC Company 4434, which the Register says was also a junior African-American unit.[^1] By 1942 the Civilian Conservation Corps had built 61 lookout towers across Alabama, according to the Encyclopedia of Alabama.[^3]

The Register added the tower on December 24, 2009, as US 857, AL 35, nominated by Thomas Kaufmann.[^1] A blog post gives the Alabama number as 36 rather than 35.[^4]

The Alabama Forestry Commission is listed as the tower's administrator.[^1][^2] The sources read do not say who staffed the tower or what fires it watched. The Forest Lookouts page for Mount Willing gives only the county and the commission's name, and the Register page has no visit reports.[^2][^1]

None of the sources read describes a move, a fire or a demolition, and none gives a condition check after the 2009 registration. Current condition and access are not recorded in these sources. Anyone planning a visit should contact the Alabama Forestry Commission first.
