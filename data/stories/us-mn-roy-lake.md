Roy Lake's fire tower stood in Clearwater County, southeast of a nearby hamlet and east of Height of Land Road.[^1] The county survey estimates it was built in the 1920s or early 1930s.[^1] It appears on a state tower list from the 1930s, and large-scale USGS maps also mark it.[^1] The tower belonged to the Minnesota Forest Service, which is now the Forestry Division of the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources.[^1]

A survey note from 1938 says the tower had already been removed, and that no sign of it remained on its knoll.[^1]

No source describes the site now. The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^2]
