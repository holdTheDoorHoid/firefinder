The Minnesota Division of Forestry built the Big Sandy Lake fire tower in 1933.[^1][^3] It is a 94-foot steel tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab, made by the Pittsburg-Des Moines Company.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register calls it one of the few lookouts that company manufactured.[^1] The pages we read do not say why this hill was chosen, and they do not record its staffing, its early use or any fires it watched over.

The tower was restored in 1992.[^1][^2] The register added it to its list on September 18, 1993.[^1]

Today the tower stands in Savanna State Forest, with Big Sandy Lake and Lake Minnewawa on either side of it.[^3] The McGregor Area Chamber of Commerce calls it the Big Sandy Overlook Tower and says visitors climb to the top for the views.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as standing.[^4] The pages we read give no current hours or rules, so check with the managing agency before you go.
