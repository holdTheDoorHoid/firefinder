A historical listing of North Carolina forest lookouts places the Chinquapin tower in Duplin County, under the state's Division of Forest Resources.[^1][^2] The listing dates the start of work on a 99-foot 9-inch steel tower to May 1936, and credits the Civilian Conservation Corps.[^1] Funding came from the Emergency Conservation Works program, and the listing puts the cost at $1,877.93.[^1] The listing says erection was begun, but it does not say when the tower was finished.[^1]

In 1961 the site's lease had run out, and the state bought the land that summer.[^1]

No source I could read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit. Before going to look, confirm who owns the land now, since the sources do not say.

[^2]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
