The first Faunce fire tower stood along Faunce Forest Road, south of the tower that stands there now.[^2] The county's list of Lake of the Woods towers calls it the first Faunce Fire Tower.[^2]

A 1934 survey note says the old tower was to be torn down and a new one erected about a mile to the north.[^2] The tower the Civilian Conservation Corps erected in 1935 replaced an older tower, according to the local tourism guide, which places that older tower about three miles to the south along Faunce Forest Road.[^3]

The two sources disagree about the distance. The 1934 note says about a mile, and the guide says three miles. The listed coordinates put the two sites roughly three miles apart.[^1] The county list and the guide both place the old tower south of the current one.[^2][^3]

The tourism guide links the area to the lost village of Faunce, which had a post office from 1917 to 1938.[^3][^4]

The Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list shows this first site as gone.[^1] The county list says all that seems to be left there is a fragment of concrete building foundation.[^2]
