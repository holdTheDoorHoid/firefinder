The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list shows a Gibson Ridge tower in Montgomery County as standing, placed at about 37.11 north and 80.50 west.[^1] Its entry has no National Historic Lookout Register number and no build date, builder or staffing details.[^1][^2] I found no readable history of this tower, so I cannot say when it went up, who built it, or whether anyone staffed it.[^1] Its access and any rental arrangement are not recorded in what I could read, and the list shows only a name, county, coordinates and a status word for it.[^1] The listing is the only record I could find, so treat its status as the association's view, not a fresh inspection. Before going to look, ask the landowner or local officials, and do not climb any tower without permission.

[^1]: Virginia - Forest Fire Lookout Association, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Virginia - National Historic Lookout Register, National Historic Lookout Register, https://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
