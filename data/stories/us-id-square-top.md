Square Top is an 8,503-foot peak in Lemhi County, Idaho, about 42 miles northwest of Salmon, on the Bitterroot National Forest.[^1][^3]

In March 1934 a newspaper item reported tentative approval for a tower and lookout on Square Top, and it placed the project in the Salmon forest.[^3] Forest Lookouts quotes that item. The later pages describe a two-story log cabin built that year rather than a tower.[^1][^2][^3]

The 1934 item names the Salmon forest.[^3] The three pages all list the Bitterroot National Forest.[^1][^2][^3]

All three pages say the cabin was destroyed in 1960.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site hike-in, so the site is reached on foot.[^2] No source we read describes what remains there today, and none says whether the site is open to visitors. Ask the Bitterroot National Forest about the route before you go.
