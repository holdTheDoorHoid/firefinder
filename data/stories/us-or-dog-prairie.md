Dog Prairie is in the Umpqua National Forest in Douglas County, Oregon, about five miles west of Mount Bailey in the Diamond Lake district.[^1][^2] In July 1932 a Roseburg News-Review item, quoted on Rex's Fire Lookout Page, said nine new lookout houses were to be built, and that one would go up at Dog Prairie.[^2] The register and firelookout.com both date the lookout to 1932.[^1][^3]

The sources do not agree on what was built there. The register calls it a wooden L-4 tower, while Rex's page describes a 14-by-14 L-4 ground house.[^1][^2] Rex's page also records panorama photographs dated August 1, 1933.[^2]

The register gives 1965 as the removal year, and its photo caption gives the span 1932 to 1965.[^1] Firelookout.com says it was gone in 1969.[^3] Rex's page gives a removal but no date.[^2] The sources disagree on the year, and we could not settle it.

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or how to reach it. The register lists the coordinates and elevation but says nothing about the ground now.[^1]
