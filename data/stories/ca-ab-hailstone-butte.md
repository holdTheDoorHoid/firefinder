A 2015 hiking report places Hailstone Butte in Kananaskis, Alberta.[^2] Cameron, the lookout on Mount Burke, operated until 1953.[^1] A 2025 blog says that in 1953 the work passed to newer posts on Raspberry Ridge and at Hailstone Butte.[^1]

The Alberta lookout layer gives 1980 as the year of construction for the cabin it lists at the site.[^3] The layer records it as a cabin, not a tower, at 5.0 m (16 ft) tall and 2,370 m (7,776 ft) above sea level.[^3]

The trip report places a fire lookout on the north summit.[^2] Respecting a sign that read "authorized persons only," the hikers went no further.[^2] The layer marks the cabin as active and gives its access mode as drive.[^3] No source read describes its condition today, and none says it is open to visitors.
