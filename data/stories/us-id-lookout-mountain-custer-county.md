Lookout Mountain Lookout is an L-4 ground cab on the summit of a peak in the White Cloud Mountains, inside the Sawtooth National Recreation Area in Custer County, Idaho.[^1][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts and firelookout.com both date the cab to 1933.[^2][^3]

The sources say little about who staffed it. The National Historic Lookout Register notes that the man who later restored the tower had been a lookout staff member in 1961, and Idaho Fire Lookouts has no documented staffing history for the site.[^1][^2]

By the 1970s the lookout had moved to emergency status, and firelookout.com says it was used for emergencies into that decade.[^2][^3]

In 1985 the cab was slated to be burned.[^1] A former 1961 lookout contacted the Forest Service, and Idaho Fire Lookouts says the cab was saved from demolition.[^1][^2] He restored it in 1989, fixing its wooden floors, door, shutters, catwalk and steps, then painting it.[^1][^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register added the lookout on November 27, 2016, with Luke Channer as the nominator.[^1]

Reaching the summit takes a hike. From Stanley, the route runs 9.4 miles on Highway 75, then follows forest road 626 to its end and a trailhead. The trail is about five miles one way and rated difficult.[^5] Wikipedia says a maintained trail goes to the summit.[^4] Sawtooth Trails warns that the peak is dangerous during lightning storms, so watch the weather.[^5]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as a hike-in on emergency status, and the sources I read describe no rental or overnight use for the cab.[^2]
