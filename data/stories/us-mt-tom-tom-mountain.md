Tom Tom Mountain lies 10 miles east of Bigfork, in the Flathead National Forest of Flathead County, Montana, at an elevation of 7,070 feet.[^1]

Firelookout.com lists an L-4 cab there in 1931, and says it was destroyed in 1953.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's state list files the site under "Ground" and marks it gone, at 48.05743 north and 113.86360 west.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains on the mountain today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Tom Tom Mtn. Fire Lookout Cabin, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/tomtommtn.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
