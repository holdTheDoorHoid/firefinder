Benn Knob sits on the Cleveland and Burke county line in North Carolina.[^1] The lookout page, which spells it Benns Knob, names the North Carolina Forest Service as the agency.[^1] A statewide tower list places the summit at 35.56522 north and 81.66039 west.[^2]

A state report covering 1958 to 1960 records a tower cabin constructed on the knob.[^1] A visitor who later filmed the site gives 1959 as the build year.[^3] A survey description written in 1975 places a survey station on the knob, about 20 feet north of the forest service tower.[^1]

The later history is thinner. A visitor's post suggests the tower was taken down and a radio communications tower took its place.[^3] The filmmaker arrived too late to record the original.[^3] A radio-system listing names Benn Knob as one of five sites in the Cleveland P25 system.[^4]

No source I read describes the site in detail today, or says whether the public may go there. The 1975 directions run along a private road and mention a gate 3.6 miles along.[^1] The visitor's report is the only source I found on the tower's fate. It describes a radio tower in place of the lookout, so do not plan a climb.[^3]
