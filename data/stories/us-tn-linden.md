The Linden tower stands in Perry County, Tennessee, and the Tennessee Landforms list gives its elevation as 843 feet.[^1] A 2014 National Register study of Tennessee fire towers, dated June 9, 2014, lists it among the towers still standing as of March 26 that year.[^2] Neither source describes the tower's height, design or current condition in any detail.[^1][^2]

According to the same study, the Tennessee Division of Forestry built 208 fire lookout towers from about 1930 to about 1970.[^2] The sources I reached do not say when this tower was built, who built it, or whether it was ever staffed.

Access is not recorded in these sources, so check with the landowner or the county before planning a visit.

[^1]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Tennessee Division of Forestry Fire Lookout Towers, 1933 to 1975, National Register multiple property documentation form, Tennessee Valley Archaeological Research, https://npgallery.nps.gov/pdfhost/docs/NRHP/Text/64501237.pdf (accessed 2026-10-09).
