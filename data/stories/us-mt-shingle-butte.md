Shingle Butte is in Jefferson County, Montana, within the Helena National Forest.[^1] The Forest Lookouts listing places it at 9N-3W-36, which is township 9 North, range 3 West, section 36.[^1]

A July 1, 1939 item in the Independent, quoted by the listing, calls Shingle Butte, in the McClellan area, an important place for spotting fires and controlling them, and says it was scheduled for improvement.[^1]

A February 1940 Independent item, quoted on the Strawberry Butte page, reports that Helena forest supervisor Percy Melis planned a new permanent lookout in the McClellan creek area, either on Shingle Butte or on another mountain a mile south.[^2] Surveys were to decide the better point.[^2]

The listing says Shingle Butte was located near the current Strawberry tower and came before it.[^1] The Strawberry Butte page says work on a 60-foot tower and cabin there was scheduled for that summer, and a note on the page says the work used labor from the Rimini CCC camp.[^2] The National Historic Lookout Register says the Strawberry Lookout was erected in 1941.[^3]

The listing marks the Shingle Butte lookout as removed.[^1] The register lists the Strawberry Lookout as available for rental.[^3]

No source found describes what remains on Shingle Butte today, or whether the butte is open to visitors.
