Ball Rock is a former lookout site in Tehama County, California, in the Mendocino National Forest.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes the lookout as a Plan 4AR cabin from California Region 5, set on an open timber tower 10 feet high.[^1] The register treats the early to mid-1920s as the probable construction period.[^1] The site's elevation is listed as 6,661 feet (2,030 m).[^1]

A photograph on the register page is captioned with the year 1927, so the lookout existed by then.[^1] The register names the US Forest Service, Mendocino National Forest, as the administrator.[^1]

The register gives no removal date. It says the site holds nothing of the lookout now.[^1]

No source we read describes the site today beyond that, and none says whether it is open to the public.
