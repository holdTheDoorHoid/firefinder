The Cookeville Fire Tower stands in Putnam County, Tennessee.[^1] It is an 80-foot steel tower.[^1] The register dates it to roughly 1944 to 1953.[^1]

We found no builder, tower model, or staffing record for the tower, and no record of any fire it watched.

The register added the tower on May 22, 2022, on the nomination of Nicholas Kesterson.[^1] A 2014 list of surviving state towers includes it, and Tennessee Landforms lists it as standing.[^3][^2]

The tower now houses a radio repeater for the Tennessee Division of Forestry, according to the register.[^1] The register gives the elevation as 1,429 feet (436 meters), which matches the Tennessee Landforms figure of 1,429 feet.[^1][^2]

We found no public access or climbing rule for the tower. Because the register says it houses Tennessee Division of Forestry radio equipment, ask the division before visiting.[^1]
