Cook was one of the South Carolina Forestry Commission's fire towers in Williamsburg County.[^1] The commission's history says most tower sites covered 10 acres and had, besides the tower, a year-round home for the operator, and that the towers were where the public reported fires and a link for field crews.[^2]

A Post and Courier article on the last fire towers profiles a former operator who lost her lookout job in 1993, a year when more than 100 towers around the state closed.[^3] The commission's own history dates the end of its fire tower network to October 1, 1993.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts site lists the Cook tower as removed.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the land is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
