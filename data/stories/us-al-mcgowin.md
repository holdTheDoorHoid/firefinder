The McGowin Lookout Tower is near Chapman in Butler County, Alabama, at about 396 feet (121 m) above sea level.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as a tall lookout, 120 feet high.[^1]

The register says the Civilian Conservation Corps built it in 1936 and that it was known as the McKenzie Lookout.[^1] We found no record of who staffed it or of any fire it watched. The Corps stopped building fire towers when the United States entered the Second World War, and some of its towers were left unfinished, according to a 1819 News column by Thomas Kaufmann.[^2]

The register says the tower was moved from its original site. In 1966 the Alabama Forestry Commission took it to its present location, and the register lists the commission as the administrator.[^1] Thomas Kaufmann nominated the tower to the register, which recorded it on June 23, 2009.[^1]

We could find no second source for the 1936 date, the Corps link or the 1966 move, so the register is the only account we have. The register does not describe the tower's current condition or whether visitors may use it.[^1] Many Alabama lookouts are unsafe and illegal to climb, according to the same column, so please admire this one from the ground and check with the commission before any visit.[^2]
