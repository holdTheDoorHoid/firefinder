A new fire tower went up on Windy Mountain in Park County in 1940, about six miles south of the Crandall Ranger Station.[^1][^2] A Shoshone National Forest handbook printed in 1941 lists fire towers that were under construction or planned, and mentions a new tower on Clayton Mountain as well.[^3]

The tower was expected to give a view over much of the North Absaroka wilderness, including ground burned in the Crandall Creek fire of 1935.[^2] The register says the cabin looks like a modified L-4 and resembles a Region 5 BC-301, and that it stands on an open timber tower 20 feet high.[^1]

The register's photos include a 1959 view of the lookout.[^1]

The register gives no removal date, and says all that remains at the site are the tower piers.[^1] A lookout-history listing marks the tower as gone.[^4]

No source describes the site beyond those piers. The register added the site on May 1, 2022.[^1]
