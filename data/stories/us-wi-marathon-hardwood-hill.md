The Central US Lookouts page for the Marathon tower says the Wisconsin Conservation Commission erected a tower there in 1949.[^1] The same page lists the location as Marathon County, 28N-6E-23.[^1] A 1928 newspaper item on the Central US Lookouts state page says construction had begun on another state fire tower in Langlade County, so the state's tower program was already under way before this one.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as Marathon (Hardwood Hill) and marks the tower gone.[^3] Its Wisconsin list gives the point as 44.88778, -89.76139.[^3]

OpenStreetMap has the same tower as the Marathon Lookout Tower, tagged as an observation tower.[^4]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains on the hill today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
