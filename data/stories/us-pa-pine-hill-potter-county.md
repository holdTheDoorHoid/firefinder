Pine Hill in Potter County had a wooden fire tower that was in use around 1917.[^1] A Department of Forestry report dated December 31, 1917, quoted on the Eastern US Forest Lookouts site, says a 20-foot wooden tower went up before 1917.[^2]

The register gives the location as Potter County and the registry number as US 421, PA 250.[^1] It says the tower is no longer standing, and that the site was added to the register on September 16, 2008, with Henry Isenberg named as the nominator.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on Pine Hill today, or whether the ground is open to visitors. Check who owns the land before you go looking for it.

[^1]: Pine Hill Tower Site (Potter County), Former Fire Lookout Sites register, Forest Fire Lookout Association, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/pa/pine-hill-tower-site-potter-county/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Pine Hill - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Eastern US Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/pine-hill1.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
