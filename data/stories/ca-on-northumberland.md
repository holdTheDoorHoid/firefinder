Firelookout.ca lists the Northumberland Fire Tower as a steel tower at 4863 County Road 45 in Baltimore, in Hamilton Township, Ontario, at an elevation of 150 m (492 ft).[^1] A Forest Rangers fan site says there were towers in the Ganaraska and Northumberland Forests, "north of Port Hope and Cobourg."[^2]

A 2013 blog post describes a climb of the Northumberland Forest fire tower on 11 May 1991. The climb began up a sandy hill, and the blog mentions concrete base posts at the tower's foot.[^3] The author's friend later heard that the tower had been taken down some time after that visit.[^3]

The same post says the author later saw the tower's cab outside a local museum, and that the friend confirmed it was the tower they had climbed.[^3] The Forest Rangers site places the Northumberland Tower at the Canadian Fire Fighters Museum, 95 Mill Street, Port Hope.[^2]

That site also says a Northumberland Forest tower was erected on an island in Rice Lake. It does not say whether that is the same tower.[^2]

The Fire Hall Museum's list of Ontario firefighting museums marks the Canadian Fire Fighters Museum as temporarily closed.[^4] No source I read says whether the cab is on display or open to visitors today.
