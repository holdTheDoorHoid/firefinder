Rose Hill appears in a Georgia tower list, placed in Elbert County at about 34.084 N, 82.759 W.[^1] The list was revised on March 22, 2016.[^1] Its entry carries the number gfc-002, which refers to an older Georgia Forestry Commission list covering 203 towers.[^1] The list says it drew on 1:100,000-scale USGS topographic maps and NGS data sheets, and that the Georgia Forestry Commission provided coordinates for 118 towers.[^1] The list gives the name Rose Hill. It also says names for towers numbered 73 and above were the author's creation, and this one is number 239.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether a tower still stands there.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
