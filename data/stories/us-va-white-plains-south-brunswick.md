The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower called White Plains (South Brunswick) in Brunswick County, Virginia, at 36.63089, -77.94192, and marks it as gone.[^1][^2] The White Plains name also belongs to an unincorporated community, which Wikipedia places in Brunswick County.[^3]

A Forest Lookouts index of Virginia lists South Brunswick and White Plains as two separate entries, both in Brunswick County. The sources do not show whether they name one tower or two.[^4]

Across Virginia, the state forestry office began putting up steel lookout towers in 1928.[^5] A 1991 survey of the department's own properties recorded that it owned a fire tower in each of Virginia's counties.[^5] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public.
