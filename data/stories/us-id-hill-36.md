Hill 36 stood in the St. Joe National Forest, in Shoshone County, about 10 miles west-northwest of Avery, at an elevation of 4,715 feet.[^1]

The sources record a crow's nest here in the 1930s, without a more exact date.[^1][^2] In 1934 the site got a 10-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2] The patrol point sat on Ally Point, two miles to the southeast.[^1]

The tower was destroyed in 1958, and both site pages agree on that year and on the 1934 tower.[^1][^2] The association's statewide list marks the site as gone.[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page calls it a hike-in, and it does not say whether the ground is open to visitors.[^2]
