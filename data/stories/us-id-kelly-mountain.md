Kelly Mountain Lookout stood on Kelly Mountain in Kootenai County, six miles northeast of Coeur d'Alene, at 4,085 feet.[^1][^2]

Sources agree that a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab went up in 1934.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The tower was destroyed in 1950.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts names two sisters, Mamie and Thelma Jardine, as staff and cites a 1943 Coeur d'Alene Press item.[^2] A La Crosse Tribune item quoted on the Forest Lookouts Kootenai County page describes the Jardine sisters in a second season atop Coeur d'Alene mountain.[^3] The two accounts name different lookouts, and neither page reconciles them.

Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site as drive-up.[^2] A visitor's site page describes gravel roads south of Fernan Saddle and says the final road probably calls for a vehicle with some clearance.[^4] Its photos include three captioned footings, and the page says trees block most of the view from the summit.[^4] Idaho Lookouts lists the tower as gone.[^5]
