The Waynesboro Lookout Tower is a 45-foot Aermotor MC-39 in Wayne County.[^1] A National Register study notes that the MC-39's small landings were judged dangerous, and that the later LS-40 model was built with more substantial landings.[^2]

The Register gives no build date, builder or fire record for this tower.[^1] It describes the tower as being on private property and lists the owners as private.[^1]

The Register gives the registration date as August 9, 2023, with Jason Johns as nominator, under the number US 1762, TN 66. It also notes a site visit on November 26, 2022.[^1]

A table of extant towers in the same National Register study, as of March 26, 2014, includes Waynesboro in Wayne County.[^2]

Tennessee Landforms gives a different answer. Its table marks the Waynesboro tower as removed, with no date.[^3]

The sources disagree about whether the tower still stands.[^1][^3] The Register records a site visit in 2022 but does not describe the tower's condition, and the Landforms entry is undated, so we cannot tell which is newer.[^1][^3] Treat its current status as unknown. Because the tower is on private land, check with the owner before going to look at it.[^1]
