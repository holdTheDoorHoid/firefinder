The Burpee Mountain lookout started as a log cabin, put up around 1930 on the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1] The forest's own list places it about 16 miles southeast of Elk City.[^4] It was a patrol lookout, and the sources put it two miles southwest, atop Big Creek Point, so where it stood in relation to the peak itself is not clear.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts has no documented staffing history for the cabin, and its page names no one who worked it.[^2] A University of Idaho photograph of the interior shows a wool-sack bed stuffed with bear grass, and a washtub.[^5]

The cabin was abandoned in 1940.[^1][^2][^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the site as gone, and its entry gives no cause for the loss.[^3]

The pages we read do not describe what, if anything, remains of the cabin today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
