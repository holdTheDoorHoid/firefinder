Whitewater, which the Central US Lookouts site also calls Womac, is a fire lookout tower in St. Francois County, Missouri, listed under the Clark National Forest.[^1] The site lists the code 35N-8E-34 and carries a photograph credited to Bob Frakes and dated January 30, 2016.[^1]

A 1959 topographic map of the Womack area, published in 1983, marks the Whitewater Lookout Tower on one of the area's high points.[^2] The same map also marks a Higdon Lookout Tower on a high point.[^2]

The Clark National Forest was established on September 11, 1939.[^3] On July 1, 1973 the forest was administratively merged with the Mark Twain National Forest, and on February 17, 1976 Mark Twain absorbed it.[^3]

No source I found describes what stands at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
