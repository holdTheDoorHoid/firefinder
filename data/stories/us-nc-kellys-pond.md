Kellys Pond is a four-legged, self-supported steel lookout tower about 2.5 miles southeast of Kinston, in Lenoir County, North Carolina.[^1][^3] When the National Geodetic Survey described it in 1975, the tower was given as 85 feet in height (about 25.9 m), and it was the property of the North Carolina Forest Service.[^1] Its survey mark sits at the top center of the roof, and the mark was recorded as recovered in good condition in 1991, 2005 and 2008.[^1]

A North Carolina Department of Transportation list of historical features for the Kinston Bypass includes the tower under the name Kelly’s Pond Fire Lookout Tower. Its effect decision in that review was no adverse effect.[^2]

No source describes the site today, so this entry cannot say whether the tower still stands or whether visitors may go there.
