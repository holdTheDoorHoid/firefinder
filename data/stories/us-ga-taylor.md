A Georgia lookout tower list published at tnlandforms.us includes a tower under the bare name "taylor", in Long County.[^1] Its coordinates are 31.943529, -81.753868, and the list's note beside it reads "aerial imagery".[^1] Other entries use lowercase names such as "pineland" and "statenville", so this one may be a local place name rather than the tower's own.[^1] The page says the list was prompted by a gap: the FFLA website listed no Georgia towers in 2010.[^1] Its map was revised on 22 March 2016.[^1]

No source describes the site today. Nothing in the sources says whether a tower stands at those coordinates, or whether the public can reach the spot. Visitors should not assume the place is open or that a tower can be seen.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
