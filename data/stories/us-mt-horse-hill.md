Horse Hill is a former lookout site in the Kootenai National Forest, about 26 miles east-southeast of Libby in Lincoln County, Montana.[^1] The site sits at an elevation of 4,963 feet.[^1]

The listing records a frame cabin on the site in 1928.[^1] It dates a 20-foot pole tower of the L-4 type to 1942, and it names a patrol point about half a mile north.[^1] Photos on the page are credited to Tom Ajootian.[^1]

The listing gives the lookout's dates as 1942 to 1975 and says the tower was destroyed in 1975.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the site at latitude 48.33193 and longitude -114.97685, and lists the tower as gone.[^2]

No source describes the site today, including what remains there or whether the public can reach it. Anyone planning a trip should check with the Kootenai National Forest first.
