Rocky Point is a former lookout site in Columbia County, Oregon, in Section 16, Township 4 North, Range 5 West.[^1] Forest Lookouts lists the Oregon Department of Forestry as its agency.[^1]

A state forester's report describes a permanent lookout station set up on the point in 1913, with a cabin for the warden, who was on duty all the time.[^1] The report says a telephone connection to headquarters at Vernonia was meant to make sure any fire was reported promptly.[^1] A later report, covering 1914, says that even through a hazy season the lookout could see most of its territory on almost every day.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site marks the point as removed. It gives no date for that and says nothing about what became of the cabin.[^1] We found no source that describes the site today, what stands there, or whether the public can reach it.

A separate Rocky Point, in Lincoln County about 12 miles east of Lincoln City, had a cabin in the 1920s that was gone by the 1950s. Do not confuse the two.[^2]
