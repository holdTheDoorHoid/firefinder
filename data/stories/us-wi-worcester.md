According to the National Historic Lookout Register, the Worcester Lookout is 100 feet tall, has its ladder in an inside corner, and sits at an elevation of 1,591 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as Worchester (Phillips) in Price County, and the Central US Lookouts site files it under Phillips.[^2][^3]

Its construction date is uncertain. The Register says it was probably built after 1940, because a statewide tower survey dated September 1940 leaves it out.[^1] The Register therefore gives its build date as after 1940 rather than a specific year.[^1]

The Register does not describe the tower's staffing or any fire it watched, and the sources read do not fill that gap.[^1]

In 2015 Wisconsin shut down the 72 towers still working.[^5] The DNR also sought new owners for them and said unsold towers would be torn down.[^4] The sources read do not name Worcester among those towers, so its later history is unclear. The Association still lists the tower as standing.[^2]

The Register lists a registration date of August 16, 2017, and names Tyler Bormann as the nominator.[^1] The sources do not say whether the public may visit or climb it. Anyone planning a trip should check with the Wisconsin DNR first, and should not climb a tower without permission.
