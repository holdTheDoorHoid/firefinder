Federal Hill rises above Milford in Hillsborough County, and the state's Department of Resources and Economic Development administers its lookout.[^1][^2] A first lookout went up in 1911, when a platform was built about 20 feet up a large tree. The Register says it was used on and off until 1918.[^1][^2]

The steel tower with a wood cab that stands today dates to 1929, according to the Register.[^1] A January 1930 newspaper item, quoted by Forest Lookouts, describes a new 45-foot steel tower with an enclosed observation room.[^2] The Register adds that the 1938 hurricane damaged the tower, which was repaired and possibly raised, and that it was raised again in the 1980s.[^1] A 2021 hiking guide gives its height as 75 feet, so the published heights disagree.[^3]

Watchmen were posted here in earlier decades. A 1935 item names Harry W. Pearsons as forest fire watchman, and a 1963 item calls Federal Hill the dispatch tower for five Hillsborough County towers, with Wayne McCalvey as its watchman.[^2] A 2016 Register visit note says the state appears to staff it during fire season,[^1] and a Keene Sentinel article says the state's towers are staffed whenever fire danger reaches Class 3 or higher.[^4]

The tower has needed repairs. Forest Lookouts lists a February 2015 bid notice for cab replacement and steel reinforcement.[^2] In August 2016 a state architect said renovations costing about $84,000 were expected before the end of that year, including taking the cab down by hand.[^2] A Register visit report from September 2016 noted that a cab window had been blown in.[^1] We could not confirm that the work was finished.

A 2021 hiking guide says the cabin is closed to the public, though visitors can climb the tower to the level below the cabin.[^3] The same guide describes a short service track that starts on Ponemah Hill Road, with a gate and room for only a couple of vehicles at the trailhead.[^3] We found no source showing that visitors may enter the cab, so access is listed as restricted. We do not encourage climbing the tower.
