Pisgah Mountain sits in Columbia County, Oregon, where a 20-foot live-in tower went up in 1940.[^1][^2] The register lists the Oregon Department of Forestry as the administering agency.[^1][^3]

The site was activated on October 16, 1942, and listed under the Portland Filter Center.[^3] Its observers were civilian volunteers doing aircraft observation work.[^3] A January 13, 1943 letter says the Army was struggling to keep volunteers motivated in Columbia and Washington counties.[^3] A woodshed and an extra room were added in 1942, and another room in 1943.[^3] Repairs to the windows, doors, flue and ceiling came in 1953, and the inside and outside were painted.[^3]

The register says the tower was removed in 1965, while firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today.
