Red Ridge is a former lookout site in the Mt. Baker-Snoqualmie National Forest, in Snohomish County, Washington.[^1] It sits about eight miles southwest of Glacier Peak.[^2]

An L-4 cab went up on the ridge in 1936, according to the Former Fire Lookout Sites register and FireLookout.com.[^1][^2] The register also lists panoramic photographs dated August 9, 1935, a year before that build year.[^1] The sources do not explain that gap.

The cab was lost in 1967.[^1][^2] The register says it was removed that year, while FireLookout.com says it was destroyed.[^1][^2] The two sources agree on the year but not on how the cab left the ridge.[^1][^2]

The register added the site to its list in 2012, after a nomination by Ray Kresek.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the ridge today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^2]: Red Ridge Fire Lookout Cabin, FireLookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/wa/redridge.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
