Botanie Lookout stands on a peak near the village of Lytton, in Thompson-Nicola, British Columbia.[^1][^2] The register gives its elevation as 1,993 m (6,538 ft) and its build year as 1954.[^1]

The register lists the British Columbia Forest Service as administrator. Wikipedia gives a different owner, saying the lookout is owned and maintained by a local wireless society that uses it for TV, radio and digital transmission.[^1][^2]

In 2021, a wildfire razed the village of Lytton, close by, but the lookout barely survived.[^1]

The register describes the building as in poor condition, with only part of the catwalk left. It also says the lookout still holds communications gear and solar panels.[^1]

Access is hard to pin down. A 2019 trip report describes the road from Botanie Valley Road to the summit as steep and rough, and says every building on top was locked during that visit.[^3] Wikipedia says both rough 4x4 roads were decommissioned, and vehicles can no longer get past a certain elevation.[^2]

We found no source that describes how the public can get in today, so visitors should not count on being able to enter the buildings.
