The Carlton County listing of its fire towers dates the Moose Lake tower to 1939 and names the Minnesota Forest Service as its owner.[^1] It was an 80-foot stairway tower from the McClintock Marshall Co., and the county called it tower number 4.[^1] The listing places it along Tower Road, west of the town of Moose Lake.[^1]

The same listing includes a 1952 survey note that describes the tower as steel, with four legs and about 120 feet high. That figure disagrees with the 80 feet given elsewhere in the listing, so we report 80 feet and flag the difference.[^1] We found no record of who staffed the tower or what fires it watched.

The county listing says the tower is now used for communications.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association still lists it as standing, but it adds an asterisk for towers whose cab is no longer in place.[^2] A visitor's photo page says the steel structure now holds communication gear, and that the stairway sits inside a fence.[^3]

We found no public visiting information. Because the site is fenced and used for communications, treat it as closed to visitors, and ask the owners before going near it.
