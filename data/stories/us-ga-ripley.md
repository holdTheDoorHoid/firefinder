The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers places a tower called "ripley" in Twiggs County, at about 32.723 N, 83.417 W.[^1] Its row carries the source note "Ron topo", and the page says Gary Weber and Ron Stafford provided many of the tower locations.[^1] The list also says that names for towers 73 and above are the author's own creation. Entry GA415 falls in that range, so "ripley" is a label, not a record of an old lookout name.[^1]

A different structure shares the name. The Ripley Fire Lookout Tower on Joe Crihfield Road in Ripley, Tennessee, was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2020. It was built around 1970 for the Tennessee Division of Forestry.[^2] Because it is in another state, it is not the Georgia entry on this list.[^1][^2]

The list gives no height, design, build year, status or access for the Georgia tower.[^1] We found no other source describing what remains at the site today or whether it is open to visitors.
