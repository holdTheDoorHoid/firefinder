A Georgia lookout list on tnlandforms.us places a tower called Leesburg in Lee County, at about 31.73 N, 84.15 W.[^1] The list gives its elevation as 260 with no unit stated, and attaches two codes to it, BLL-41131 and gfc-320.[^1] The list explains the codes: BLL-nnn for coordinates the Georgia Forestry Commission provided, and gfc-nnn for an older GFC list of tower locations.[^1] The list marks some entries "(historical)", and the Leesburg entry carries no such mark.[^1]

The list says its names for towers numbered 73 and above are its own creation. This entry is number 183, so "Leesburg" may be the compiler's name rather than a historical one.[^1] The list's Google map was revised on March 22, 2016.[^1]

The list says nothing about what remains at the Leesburg site today.[^1] Before going, check who owns the land and whether the public may enter, and do not climb any tower you cannot confirm is open.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
