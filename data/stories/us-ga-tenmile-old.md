On a list of Georgia towers, the entry named "tenmile old" sits in Appling County at an elevation of 185 feet (56 m).[^1] Its coordinates place it roughly 82 metres (about 270 feet) from the entry named Tenmile Lookout Tower, which the list gives at 186 feet (57 m).[^1] The list gives a datasheet reference, BS1563, for this entry.[^1]

No source describes what is at this spot today, or whether the public can reach it.
