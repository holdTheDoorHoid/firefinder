The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower called Itasca in Hubbard County, at 47.248 N, 95.169 W, and marks it gone.[^1] We found no source that describes a tower by that name at that spot, so what follows is a lead rather than a confirmed history.

The likeliest candidate is the Arago tower. A 1913 state forester's report said one new steel tower had gone up that year, at Arago, close to Itasca State Park.[^2] A 2025 compilation of Minnesota fire towers lists the Arago tower under Hubbard County, but it gives no coordinates for it, so the two records cannot be checked against each other by location.[^2]

Comparing the two Hubbard County lists, the Forest Fire Lookout Association has an Itasca entry with no Arago, and the compilation has an Arago entry with no Itasca. That pattern suggests the two may be one tower under two names, but no source says so, and we have not confirmed it.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes the site today, so we cannot say whether anything remains there or whether visitors can reach it.
