The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the McCue Ridge lookout as US 1996 and says the U.S. Forest Service administers the site.[^1] The lookout stood in Chelan County, Washington, in the Wenatchee National Forest, low on McCue Ridge.[^2][^3]

The L-4 cab went up in 1931.[^1][^2][^3] Willhite Web says it sat on a 40-foot tower made of timbers cut from the area.[^3] Firelookout.com names Charles G. Brown as known lookout personnel, but it gives no dates.[^2]

Willhite Web also has photo survey panoramas taken on October 3, 1934.[^3]

The cab was abandoned in the 1950s, and the register says the lookout is no longer there.[^1] In 2014 the Chiwaukum Creek Fire burned over the ridge, decades after the cab was gone.[^3]

Today the lookout is gone. The Forest Lookouts page marks the site as removed.[^4] Willhite Web's 2015 visit found two footings just above the snow.[^3] Its inventory of what is left includes concrete footings, telephone wire, pieces of a wood stove, and old cans and dishware.[^3]

Willhite Web names Scottish Lakes High Camp, under a mile off, as the best place to start, and rates the approach an easy hike or snowshoe.[^3] The old spur trail up to the site is difficult to find.[^3] Trees have grown back, so the views are very limited.[^3] Check with the Forest Service for current access rules before you go.
