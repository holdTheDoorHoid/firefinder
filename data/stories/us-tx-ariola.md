The Ariola lookout tower stood in Hardin County, Texas, and its page lists the Texas Forest Service as the agency.[^1]

Wikipedia describes the community of Ariola as unincorporated and places it in Hardin County, and the tower shares the community's name.[^2][^1]

A Texas tower list marked as revised on January 24, 2011 calls it the Ariola Lookout Tower and codes its agency as TFS.[^3]

One state list on the Forest Lookouts site places the tower in Jasper County instead.[^4] The other sources put it in Hardin County, so this story follows them.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page marks the tower as removed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas index lists it as gone.[^5]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^5]
