The High Rock lookout site lies in Pend Oreille County, in the Kaniksu National Forest, at 5,584 feet (1,702 meters).[^1][^2] The site is about 15 miles east of Ione.[^2]

According to both sources, a platform tower went up on the site in 1937.[^1][^2] The register says it is no longer standing.[^1] firelookout.com lists it as abandoned.[^2]

We found no source describing what, if anything, remains of the platform today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

Do not confuse this site with High Rock Lookout in Lewis County.[^3] That tower is in the Gifford Pinchot National Forest and has its own National Historic Lookout Register entry.[^3]
