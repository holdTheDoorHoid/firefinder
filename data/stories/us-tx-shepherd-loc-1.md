The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower called Shepherd (Loc 1) in San Jacinto County and marks it gone.[^1] A Texas towers list on tnlandforms.us gives the same coordinates and pairs them with the survey mark BL1105.[^2]

The mark carries the name Shepherd RM 1 in its National Geodetic Survey datasheet. That datasheet places it in San Jacinto County, dates its monumenting to 1935, and includes a Coast and Geodetic Survey description from 1947.[^3] The 1935 date belongs to the survey mark, not to the tower.[^3]

The 1947 description places the mark about 90 yards from State Highway 150, 5.6 miles northwest along the highway from the Shepherd post office.[^3] The survey places the mark 124 feet east of the tower's northeast concrete footing and describes the tower as abandoned.[^3] The mark's published position is close to the Association's coordinates for Loc 1.[^1][^3] The survey text does not name the tower, but the two sets of coordinates match closely, so this is most likely the Loc 1 tower.[^1][^2][^3]

The Association lists the tower as gone.[^1] The 1947 description refers to a concrete base at the tower, so concrete was still there that year. No source read says what remains at the site today or whether the public may visit it.[^3]

The Association also lists a second site, Shepherd (Loc 2), to the southeast.[^1]

[^1]: Texas Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association (firelookout.org), https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Texas lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/txtowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Datasheet for station BL1105 (Shepherd RM 1), National Geodetic Survey, NOAA, https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=BL1105 (accessed 2026-10-21).
