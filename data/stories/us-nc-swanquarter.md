A federal survey datasheet places a lookout tower at a Civilian Conservation Corps camp on Rose Bay, inside the Swanquarter wildlife refuge.[^1] A North Carolina lookout tower list on tnlandforms.us files a Swanquarter tower in Hyde County under the same survey code, EY3436.[^2] In 1935 the Coast and Geodetic Survey described a station at the center of the ventilator on top of the tower.[^1] A North Carolina State Archives photo collection describes Camp Redington, a CCC camp for Company 424 in Swan Quarter, on Rose Bay about 12 miles east of Lake Mattamuskeet.[^3] The datasheet does not name that camp, so linking it to this tower is an inference.[^1]

The datasheet's two descriptions disagree on the spot.[^1] The 1935 note puts the tower on the west side of Rose Bay.[^1] A 1949 note puts it on the northeast shore, on refuge property, and gives the tower as 90 feet.[^1] That year the station was recovered in good condition.[^1] In 1985 a U.S. Power Squadron recovery could not find the mark.[^1]

The refuge's own page, from the U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service, says the refuge was established in 1932 and that visitors can fish, hunt and bird there.[^4] Visitors reach the refuge either by boat or on a gravel road about two miles long that starts at NC Highway 264.[^4] No source we read describes what remains at the tower site today, or whether that spot is open to visitors.
