Moclips Lookout stood on the Washington coast in Grays Harbor County.[^1] The register lists the Quinault Indian Reservation as its administrator.[^1]

The lookout was built in 1929 as a steel tower with a cabin at its base.[^1][^2][^3] Willhite attributes that description to the Kresek Lookout Inventory.[^3] Both the register and firelookout.com now list the tower and cabin as gone.[^1][^2]

Willhite's page describes no remains at the site.[^3] The Kresek inventory places the lookout in Section 31, T21N, R11W.[^3] Willhite says the East Moclips Ranger Station was inside that section, and that this seems a likely location for the lookout.[^3]

Lookout enthusiasts from the Olympians Club reportedly searched the site earlier, parking on the road and bushwhacking to a knoll. Their notes described recent logging on the knoll and no artifacts.[^3] The page says that information was gained in 2008, but the date of the visit is unknown.[^3]

The register and Willhite's page do not say whether the public may visit the site.[^1][^3] Willhite's page says its author had not visited it yet.[^3]
