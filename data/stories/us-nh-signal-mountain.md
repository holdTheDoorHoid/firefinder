Signal Mountain is in Coos County, New Hampshire, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower on it as standing.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page for the mountain also places it in Coos County.[^2] A forum thread titled for Millsfield says a wind farm was built in that area.[^4]

The earliest date in our sources is 1910. The Forest Lookouts page records a lookout station at the site in 1910, citing a Forestry Commissioner report.[^2] The Weirs Times gives a different start. Its writer dates the first tower to 1911, describes it as a 30-foot wooden structure, and gives the summit as 2,702 feet.[^3] The sources do not say whether the 1911 tower was the same structure as the 1910 station, or whether it was later replaced.

A 1930 item quoted on the Forest Lookouts page says a second wire was added to the circuit at the mountain, which had been a single line.[^2]

The lookout association still lists the tower as standing, with no national register number in its row.[^1] A forum trip report says the structure is in good overall condition, though a few wooden treads are missing. The same report also notes solar arrays, a windmill, and a recently built equipment building on site.[^4] A forum post from a visitor who was there years ago says logging had wiped out the trail.[^5]

We found no record of who staffed the tower, what fires it watched, or who owns it today. The sources do not say whether the public may climb it. A visitor's report of climbing is not permission, and we do not recommend climbing this tower. Check with the state forestry office before going to the site.
