The Idaho Peak fire lookout stands on the summit of Idaho Peak, southeast of New Denver in the Selkirk Mountains, within the Regional District of Central Kootenay.[^1][^2] The Canadian Register of Historic Places gives 1940 as the build date for the Idaho Peak Recreation Site, and it calls the lookout a good example of the 1940s to 1960s style, with a concrete block foundation, a square body and a flat roof.[^2]

The register ties the lookout to the British Columbia Forest Service. It says fire lookouts of that period were posted at sites like this one during the summer months, keeping watch for any sign of fire.[^2] The register adds that local residents and tourists come here each summer.[^2]

In April 2020, local governments in British Columbia recognized the site on the community heritage register under the provincial Local Government Act.[^2] The register lists its current use as a park.[^2]

Today the trail is public, but Firelookout.ca says the lookout itself is locked.[^1] The 2020 register entry describes the lookout as present at the summit.[^2] A hiking guide to the Wakefield Trail route puts the lookout 7.2 km along the route, at 2,273 m (7,457 ft).[^4] A 10Adventures notice says a mudslide has knocked out the road to the trailhead, and the fix could drag on. The notice is undated.[^3]
