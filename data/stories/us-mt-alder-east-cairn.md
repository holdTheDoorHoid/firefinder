Alder (East Cairn) stood in Beaverhead County, Montana, on the Beaverhead National Forest, about 16 miles northeast of Wisdom.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page also calls the place Alder Peak, and gives its legal location as 1S-13W-24.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the site at 45.74116 north and 113.15072 west.[^3] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 9,197 feet.[^1]

Firelookout.com dates a frame cab to 1961.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association gives the type as Ground.[^3]

Both firelookout.com and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's state list mark the lookout as gone.[^1][^3]

No source we read describes the site today. We cannot tell from the sources what, if anything, remains on the ground at the site, or whether the public may visit it.
