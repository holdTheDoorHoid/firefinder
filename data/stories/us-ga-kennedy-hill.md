Kennedy Hill appears on the Georgia lookout tower list kept by tnlandforms.us, which places it in Brooks County at an elevation of 229 (the list gives no unit; read as feet, about 70 m). The list gives its position as 30.91887, -83.60194.[^1] The same list also has a Catledge Hill lookout tower in Brooks County, at different coordinates further south, so the two should not be mixed up.[^1]

Brooks County was created in 1858 from parts of Lowndes and Thomas counties, and Quitman is its county seat.[^2]

No source I found describes the Kennedy Hill site today. That includes whether any structure remains there and whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Brooks County, Georgia, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooks_County,_Georgia (accessed 2026-10-21).
