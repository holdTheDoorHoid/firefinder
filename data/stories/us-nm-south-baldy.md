South Baldy, at 10,783 feet, is the range's high point and the tallest peak in Socorro County.[^1] The Magdalena Ranger District of the Cibola National Forest covers most of the range.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's New Mexico list records a South Baldy site in Socorro County as a crow's nest, and marks it as gone.[^2] The Forest Lookouts website files the lookout under the Cibola National Forest.[^3]

Near South Baldy, atmospheric and astronomical research has been carried out at the Langmuir Laboratory since the mid-1960s.[^1] In 1980 Public Law 96-550 established the Langmuir Research Site, and the act designated a 31,000-acre part of the mountain as nearly roadless, though not wilderness.[^1]

No source I read describes a lookout structure on the summit today, and I found nothing saying whether visitors can reach the old lookout site.
