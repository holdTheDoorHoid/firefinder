The Stiles lookout tower stood in Oconto County, Wisconsin. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list places it at 44.83069, -88.07372 and marks it gone.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page for the tower gives the county and nothing more.[^2]

A state inventory record describes a ranger station built in 1937 on the east side of West Stiles Tower Road, in Stiles Township. The record lists the station as demolished. It does not mention a lookout tower. The road's name is the only possible link to this tower in these sources, and the link is not established.[^3]

The OpenStreetMap point for the tower is named Stiles Lookout Tower. It carries an elevation of 218 meters and is tagged as an observation tower.[^4]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
