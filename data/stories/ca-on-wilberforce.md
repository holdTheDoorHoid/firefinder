The Wilberforce fire tower's listed site is on Black Creek Road in North Algona Wilberforce, in eastern Ontario, at an elevation of 214 m (702 ft).[^1]

Firelookout.ca dates the tower to 1958 and describes it as heavy steel with an octagonal cupola.[^1] The entry gives a total cost of $3,709.52 and names Canada Bridge Co. Ltd. among its details.[^1] The towerman lived at home, the entry notes.[^1]

The tower was shown on a 1963 Department of Lands and Forests Tower Detection System Map.[^1] Wilberforce was one of 15 lookouts overseen from the Pembroke Forest Fire District's headquarters.[^2] The Department of Lands and Forests founded that district in 1922, and its headquarters were in the town of Pembroke.[^2] When a fire was spotted, the towerman took its bearing and radioed it in, and bearings from more than one tower could place the fire on a map.[^2]

The district's towers were phased out once aerial firefighting came into use in the 1970s.[^2]

Firelookout.ca also says the tower appears on Pembroke topographic map 31F, both in its 1977 third edition and in the current fourth.[^1] No source I found describes the Wilberforce site today. Nothing in these sources says whether any of the tower or its footings remain, or whether the site is open to the public.
