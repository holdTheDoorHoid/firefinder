Mule Hill in Jackson County, Oregon, is listed as a platform with a camp, about 26 miles southwest of Crater Lake.[^1] The listing dates the platform and camp to 1935 and names the Klamath FPA.[^1] It gives an elevation of 2,855 feet.[^1]

The listing says it was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1] A national list of Oregon lookouts also records a platform that was abandoned, but it gives no date.[^2]

A different Mule Hill, in Klamath County, has its own separate listing.[^3] Anyone searching by name should check the county.

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
