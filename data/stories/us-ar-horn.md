Horn Mountain, in Lee County, Arkansas, was one of a pair of lookout towers on the St. Francis National Forest, near the Mississippi River.[^1][^2]

The Resettlement Administration built the tower in 1936. Its steel frame rose 110 feet and carried a 7-by-7-foot metal cab.[^1][^2]

The land then passed through several federal hands. It went to the Farm Security Administration in 1937 and to the Bureau of Agricultural Economics in 1938. The Soil Conservation Service ran the site from 1938 until 1954, when the Forest Service took it over.[^1]

The register gives a registration date of July 16, 1996.[^1] The Encyclopedia of Arkansas lists the tower as Horn Lookout Tower in Lee County.[^3]

The register does not say whether the tower was used to watch for fires or who staffed it, and I found no source describing fires it saw.[^1] I also found no visit report or condition note for the tower, so I cannot confirm that the steel tower still stands today.[^1][^2]

Before you go, check with the Forest Service about whether the site is open and what shape it is in. Stay clear of any tower that is closed or looks unsafe.
