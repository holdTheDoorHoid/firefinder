The Boar Tush Lookout is a 100-foot steel tower in Winston County, Alabama. The Alabama Forestry Commission administers it, and the National Historic Lookout Register lists it as US 1134, AL 67, with the state number Alabama #139.[^1]

The Register gives the site an elevation of 930 feet (283 m). It was registered on May 27, 2016, with Andrew Zerbe as the nominator.[^1]

I could not find when the tower was built, who designed it, or who staffed it in the sources I was able to read, so its early history remains unknown here.

The Register says the tower once housed communications equipment but no longer did as of 2016.[^1] It also notes that a tree grows through the structure, while the steel otherwise looked to be in good shape.[^1]

Those 2016 notes are the most recent description I could find, so today's condition and access are not confirmed. Thomas Kaufmann wrote a column in December 2025 saying that many of Alabama's old towers are in poor shape and that climbing a number of them is illegal.[^2] Anyone planning a visit should check with the Alabama Forestry Commission first, and should stay off any tower that is closed or posted.
