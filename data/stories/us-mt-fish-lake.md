The Fish Lake lookout stood in Ravalli County, in the Bitterroot National Forest, 21 miles east of Darby.[^1][^2] firelookout.com gives its elevation as 8,525 feet.[^1] The same site lists it as an L-4 cab dated 1937.[^1] The association's list gives its type as ground.[^3]

The Montana page gives the land description 3N-17W-20 and marks the lookout removed.[^2] firelookout.com says the cabin was gone by 1958, 21 years after its 1937 date.[^1] The association's list also marks it gone, with coordinates of 45.99894 and -113.7498.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors. Because every source records it as gone, a visitor should not expect a standing lookout here.[^1][^2][^3]
