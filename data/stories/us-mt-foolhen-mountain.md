Foolhen Mountain is in Powell County, Montana, about 18 miles northeast of Seeley Lake, at an elevation of 8,535 feet.[^1] Its coordinates on the Fire Lookout Association's list are 47.30739 north and 113.14111 west.[^3]

The site was built in 1938 as a camp with an elevated firefinder platform.[^1] It was abandoned in 1960.[^1]

Rex's Fire Lookout Page places the mountain in the Lolo National Forest, with the land location 18N-12W-21.[^2] The firelookout.com page names the Lolo and Flathead national forests together instead.[^1]

The Association's list gives its type as a platform and its status as abandoned, without saying whether the platform has been removed.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether visitors can reach the site.[^1][^2][^3]
