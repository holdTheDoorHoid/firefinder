The Forest Lookouts site lists a Lowndesville lookout in Abbeville County under the South Carolina Forestry Commission.[^1]

TopoQuest's place database has a Lowndesville Lookout Tower at 34.226499 N, 82.572907 W, at an elevation of 682 feet. That entry gives the coordinates and elevation, but no description of the structure.[^2]

The Post and Courier says South Carolina moved away from fire towers in the early 1990s. It also links the closure of more than 100 towers around the state to 1993.[^3]

No source we found describes what stands at the Lowndesville site now, or whether visitors can reach it.
