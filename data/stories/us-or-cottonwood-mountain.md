The Cottonwood Mountain lookout site lies about 18 miles northwest of Vale, in Malheur County, Oregon.[^2][^1] The Bureau of Land Management administers the land.[^1] A cab went up on the site in the 1960s.[^1][^2] Two listings give the site's elevation differently, 6,482 feet on one and 5,400 feet on the other, so this story leaves the figure out.[^1][^2]

A 1965 newspaper item said two young women from the Burnt River Valley staffed the lookout that summer.[^3] Wilma Morgan was the official lookout, with Margie Elliott as her assistant.[^3] Water was trucked in from Vale, and a battery-powered shortwave radio was their one tie to the wider world.[^3] The clippings place Wilma Morgan at the lookout as late as August 1967, and they also place Ruth Morgan there in 1966 and 1967.[^3]

Both listings now call the cab gone.[^1][^2]

No source I could reach describes what, if anything, remains on the ground today, or whether the public may visit.[^1][^2][^3] Anyone planning a trip should check with the Bureau of Land Management first.[^1]
