The Bartlick Lookout, which locals also called Wildcat Tower, stood near Wildcat Hollow in Dickenson County, Virginia.[^1]

The listing says the tower went up in 1936. The tower was a steel Aermotor model, bought earlier in that summer for $1,448, and CCC crews put it up with a lookout cabin on the site.[^1] Local memory puts the climb at 114 steps to the top.[^1] The listing names the Virginia Department of Forestry as administrator.[^1] The site was added to the Former Fire Lookout Sites register on July 30, 2018.[^1]

The tower was removed in 1978.[^1] The land is now privately owned, and the listing says concrete footers from the tower remain.[^1] A second list of former lookouts marks the tower as gone.[^2]
