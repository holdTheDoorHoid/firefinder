Firelookout.ca lists a Tim Lake Fire Tower in Parry Sound District.[^1] Its own entry says the identification is a guess.[^1] The entry describes a tower symbol on a topographic map, on the north end of a hill, reached by a road called Forestry Tower Road.[^1] It also cites a steel tower about 24 m (80 ft) tall that appears in the general area on a 1963 Department of Lands and Forests tower detection map.[^1]

Tim Lake lies inside Algonquin Park, about 2 km downstream of the Tim River access point.[^2] The park's directions to that access point run along Highway 518 through the village of Kearney, then turn onto the Forestry Tower Road and continue 19 km.[^2]

The record's map point sits about 1.4 degrees of longitude west of the Tim River access point, which is only about 2 km from the lake, so that point may be wrong.[^1][^2]

No source we could read says whether any tower stands near Tim Lake today.

[^1]: Tim Lake Fire Tower, Firelookout.ca, https://firelookout.ca/lookouts/?slug=tim-lake-fire-tower (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Tim River Access Point (#2), Algonquin Park, https://www.algonquinpark.on.ca/visit/camping/tim-river-access-point.php (accessed 2026-10-20).
