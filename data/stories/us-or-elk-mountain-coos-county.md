The Elk Mountain lookout in Coos County stood at 2,328 feet, or 710 meters, about three miles southwest of Sitkum.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com places the site 13 miles east-southeast of Coquille.[^2]

The Coos Forest Protective Association had a second tower ready on Elk Mountain in 1965, after an older tower near Brewster Rock was decommissioned following the summer of 1964.[^4] The register describes the Elk Mountain tower as a 50-foot wooden live-in tower built in 1965.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page quotes a 1965 Coos District annual report describing a 50-foot CT3 tower with a 14-by-14 cabin, built with a $4,000 credit from the State Restoration Fund, tied to the loss of Brushy Bald Lookout.[^3]

From 1965 on, lookouts in the Elk Mountain tower scanned for smoke near Dora and Sitkum. The forests around the East Fork of the Coquille River had earlier been watched from the Brewster Rock tower, and later from Elk Mountain.[^4]

The register says the tower was removed in 1989, while firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2] A report on a Dora event in The World says it was dismantled in the 1980s.[^4] The sources agree on roughly when the tower came down and differ on how it ended.[^1][^2][^4]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today.[^1][^2]
