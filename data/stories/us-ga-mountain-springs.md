Mountain Springs is a lookout tower listed in Jones County, Georgia.[^1] The only source I could find is a list of Georgia lookout towers on tnlandforms.us, which gives a name, a location and a short note.[^1] The list's map was last revised in March 2016, so it may not match the tower as it stands now.[^1] The row for the tower ends with the words "griswoldville gfc-607", and the list's column heading names the last column as the GFC number.[^1]

The word "griswoldville" may refer to Griswoldville, a Jones County village with industry that sat on the Central of Georgia Railway.[^2] The village produced cotton gins before the Civil War and Confederate revolvers during it.[^2] Union forces under General William T. Sherman destroyed the village on his march to the sea.[^2] The list does not explain the note, so any link between the tower and the village is unconfirmed.

No source describes the site today, so I cannot say whether a tower, a foundation or any other trace remains there, or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^2]: Griswoldville, New Georgia Encyclopedia, https://www.georgiaencyclopedia.org/articles/history-archaeology/griswoldville/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
