The Harvey Mountain lookout is in York County, New Brunswick, about 3.2 km (2 mi) north of Harvey Station.[^1]

It was built in 1948 as a 24.4 m (80 ft) galvanized steel tower with living quarters for the observer.[^1] About 1.3 km (0.8 mi) of telephone line on poles linked the tower to the highway, where it joined the Harvey exchange.[^1]

In 1950 a new roof went onto the cupola cabin at the top, and the inside of the living quarters was finished and painted. The department's report says that work completed the station.[^1]

No source describes the Harvey Mountain site today. It is not known whether the tower still stands or whether the hill is open to visitors.

[^1]: Harvey Mountain, Canada's Fire Lookouts (Ron Kemnow), https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/harvey-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
