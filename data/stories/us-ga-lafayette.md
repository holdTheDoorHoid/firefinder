The Lafayette Lookout in Walker County, Georgia, dates from the 1930s.[^1][^2] According to the Register, the tower was put up to observe part of Pigeon Mountain and the town of Lafayette, and the Georgia Forestry Commission is listed as its administrator.[^1] A Georgia tower list revised in March 2016 puts the site at 1,142 feet, while the Register gives 1,141 feet.[^3][^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register registered the tower on August 28, 2017, with Justin Peel as nominator.[^1] On February 12, 2019, the tower was auctioned. It was still standing but had lost its cab, and plans called for taking it down.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register added it as a former site on February 19, 2019, with Victoria Oliver as nominator.[^2]

Both registers record the tower as removed in 2019.[^1][^2] The sources do not say who staffed the lookout, what fires it watched, or what, if anything, stands on the site now.
