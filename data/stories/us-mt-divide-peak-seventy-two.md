The lookout on 72 Peak, also called Divide Peak, was completed in 1931 on the Montana-Idaho state line.[^3][^2] Sources describe it as a 30-foot pole tower of the L-4 type.[^1][^2] A Sanders County newspaper described a tower with a porch all the way around the cab, heavily insulated against lightning, and said it could be reached by car over the Jacks Gulch road, south of Heron.[^3]

Montana listings place it in the Kootenai National Forest, while Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1][^2][^3]

Two sources date its end to 1950 but describe it differently. Firelookout.com says it was abandoned that year, and Idaho Fire Lookouts says it was destroyed.[^1][^2] A Montana listing marks it 'Removed' without a date.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's index lists the tower as gone.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts says a first visit went to the wrong spot, and a return trip found the correct site.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains there now, or whether the site is open to visitors.
