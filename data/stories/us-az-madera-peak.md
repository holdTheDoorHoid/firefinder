Madera Peak is a former lookout site in Gila County, Arizona, inside the Tonto National Forest.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists its lookout as a tower that is now gone.[^2]

The Peakbagging tables list the site as Madera Peak (LO site), at 6,647 feet.[^3] Under that table's key, an LO site is one whose building has been taken down and may leave no trace, or one whose status is unknown.[^3]

No source we could read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the summit is open to visitors.

[^2]: Arizona Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/az/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
