Hamell is a cabin rather than a tower. Alberta Wildfire's lookout layer lists it as a cabin built in 1962, at 2,131.6 m (6,993 ft) elevation.[^1] A trail-map listing places Mount Hamell near Grande Cache, with a fire lookout near its summit.[^2]

The layer marks the site as active and lists its access mode as "fly".[^1] Alberta says each of its lookout sites is staffed by an observer, generally from April to October.[^3]

No source I found says the public may visit Hamell. The trail listing itself asks people on the trail to respect the privacy of whoever is on duty at the fire lookout.[^2] Treat Hamell as a working lookout, not a place to visit.
