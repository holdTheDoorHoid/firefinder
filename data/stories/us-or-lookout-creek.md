Lookout Creek was a fire lookout on the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest, about 39 miles northeast of Enterprise in Wallowa County, Oregon.[^1] The listing gives its elevation as 5,082 feet.[^1]

A listing dates the lookout only to the 1930s.[^1] The same listing says it was abandoned, but it gives no date for that.[^1] A national list of Oregon lookouts marks it as gone.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^2]: Oregon lookout list, firelookout.org (FFLA), https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/or/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
