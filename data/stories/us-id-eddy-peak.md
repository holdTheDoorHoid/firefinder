Eddy Peak is in Boundary County, about 10 miles east of Nordman.[^1][^2] It was a 40-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, named for Ranger Leslie E. Eddy.[^1][^2]

The sources disagree on when it went up. Two say the tower was built in 1941.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page says it was built in the 1930s with Civilian Conservation Corps labor.[^3]

The Priest Lake Timber Protective Association ran the lookout.[^1][^3] It had staff for just one week.[^1][^2] It came down in 1959.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks it as gone.[^4]

No source we read describes what remains of the tower or the site today. None covers access or who owns the site now, so check both before planning a visit.
