The Pigeon Roost Mountain lookout was in Montgomery County, Arkansas, on land inside the Ouachita National Forest.[^1] A local history of the Mena and Oden ranger districts gives 1929 as the year its tower went up.[^2] That page is a local compilation, not an official Forest Service record, so the date is not confirmed.[^2]

A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description, written in 1956, calls the structure a standard steel lookout tower belonging to the U.S. Forest Service and gives its height as 58 feet (17.7 m).[^1] The survey station lies about nine and a half miles east-northeast of Caddo Gap.[^1]

The same local history names Rich Mountain, Bee Mountain and Tall Peak as the only fire towers still standing in those districts.[^2] Pigeon Roost is not on that list, so it may no longer stand.[^2] No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is public.

[^1]: Pigeon Roost Mountain lookout tower, quoting a National Geodetic Survey station description, Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/pigeon-roost-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Lookouts of the Mena & Oden Ranger Districts, Arkansas, argenweb.net, https://argenweb.net/montgomery/lookouts.htm (accessed 2026-10-21).
