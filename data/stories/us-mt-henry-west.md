Henry West stood on the Lolo National Forest, six miles east-southeast of Plains, in Sanders County, Montana.[^1] The page dates the first structure, a log cabin, to 1928.[^1] The page lists a 20-foot pole L-4 tower for 1930.[^1] It gives the elevation as 6,053 feet.[^1] The association's list places the site at 47.430 N, 114.767 W.[^3]

The firelookout.com list of Sanders County tower sites includes Henry West.[^2] The tower was destroyed in 1965.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks the site as gone.[^3]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.
