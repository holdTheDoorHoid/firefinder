The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a gone tower called Sinking Creek Mountain, with the county given as Craig / Montgomery.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register files this tower as the Earn Knob Tower Site, and says it is also known as Sinking Creek Mountain Tower.[^1]

The register says the tower was near the Potts Mountain and Butt Mountain towers.[^1] It dates the tower only to the 1930s and names the U.S. Forest Service as its administrator.[^1]

The two sources disagree on the place. The association's Sinking Creek Mountain point sits at 37.33277 north, 80.38917 west.[^2] The register's Earn Knob point sits at 37.441667 north, 80.181111 west, a different spot.[^1] Both sources use the name Sinking Creek Mountain, but their coordinates do not match, so they do not agree on where the tower stood.[^1][^2]

The association's list marks the Sinking Creek Mountain entry as gone.[^2]

No source we checked describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Earn Knob Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/va/earn-knob-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Virginia lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
