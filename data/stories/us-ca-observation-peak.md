Observation Peak sits on the south side of the Madeline Plains in Lassen County.[^1][^3] Sources give its elevation as 7,964 to 7,970 feet: the register says 7,970, Peakbagging 7,968, and a Lassen County history site 7,964.[^1][^2][^3] The peak was named for the views from its summit.[^3] Visitors climbed it in 1850, and in 1854 a railroad survey party reached the top.[^3]

The Bureau of Land Management built the lookout sometime in the 1960s, according to a 1986 survey that the register cites.[^1] A survey benchmark was placed at Observation Peak in 1931.[^2]

The sources disagree about whether the lookout is still used. The register says it was staffed during the fire season as of 2015, and it lists the Bureau of Land Management's Susanville District as administrator.[^1] Peakbagging describes it as abandoned for some years, with a photo from November 2007.[^2] The register added the lookout to its list in October 2019.[^1]

I found no public visiting information, and the status is unclear. Please do not climb it.
