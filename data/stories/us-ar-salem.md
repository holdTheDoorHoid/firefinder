A federal survey description from 1953 places a lookout tower on the summit of Salem Knob, about 0.8 mile north of Salem in Fulton County.[^1]

The survey describes four steel legs holding up a six-foot-square glass and steel house. It gives the structure's height as 55 feet and says it is unpainted.[^1]

A local history page offers three theories for how Salem got its name.[^3] A third says the town was named for the knob on Pilot Hill, which the forestry service once called Salem Knob.[^3]

A map database lists a KSAR-FM (Salem) tower 0.0 miles from the summit point.[^2] No source we found describes what remains of the 1953 lookout, or whether visitors can reach the summit.
