The Frenchtown lookout stood about three miles east of the town of Frenchtown, in Missoula County, Montana.[^1][^2] The Lolo National Forest is listed as its agency.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com's Missoula West list also includes the site.[^4]

Firelookout.com dates it to the 1930s and describes a 20-foot platform tower with a cabin.[^1] It also lists the elevation as 4,500 feet.[^1]

The sources disagree about what remains. Firelookout.com says ruins, while the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^1][^3] The Association places the site at 47.01129, -114.16339.[^3]

No source we read describes the site today or says whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3] The only description of remains is firelookout.com's word "ruins," and no source dates it. Ask the Lolo National Forest before going.
