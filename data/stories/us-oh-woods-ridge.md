The Woods Ridge Fire Tower stands on a ridge north of Sciotoville in Scioto County, Ohio.[^1][^2] A February 1930 newspaper report, quoted by the Forest Lookouts site, says the tower had been erected the previous October, which places its construction in 1929.[^1] A March 1930 item in the *Forest Worker* counted it among three new 65-foot steel fire towers that Ohio had prepared for service.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site files the tower under the Ohio Department of Natural Resources.[^1]

The first fire the tower spotted that season was a grass fire near Jackson Pike, just past Minford. A fire warden and another man put it out after it reached the woods.[^1] The report gives the burned area as about 20 and a half acres of grass and three and a half acres of woods, and it says a man was alleged to have set it while trying to burn broom sedge.[^1] The same report says the tower had recently gained phone service from the Sciotoville exchange.[^1]

By late October 1934, Fred J. Etterling and Clarence Etterling were manning the tower, with Clarence as the substitute, according to a Chillicothe Gazette item that the same site quotes.[^1] The sources read do not name any other lookouts.

The National Historic Lookout Register accepted the tower on September 3, 2014, as US 1030, OH 18, and gives its elevation as 1,040 feet.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Ohio list shows it as standing, without the asterisk the list uses for towers that have lost their cab.[^3] The sources read do not say when the Ohio Department of Natural Resources last used it as a lookout.

None of the sources read say whether the public can reach or climb the tower, and none describe its condition in detail. Anyone planning a visit should check with the Ohio Department of Natural Resources first, and should stay off any structure that is closed or on private ground.
