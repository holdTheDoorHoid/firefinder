The Dan Ridge lookout stood on the Clearwater National Forest, about 12 miles east-southeast of Powell Ranger Station, in Idaho County, Idaho.[^1][^3] The listed elevation is 7,405 feet.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association gives its coordinates as 46.47255 and -114.46011.[^4]

The tower went up in 1934. It was an L-4 cab on a 30-foot pole.[^1][^2][^3] A Forest Service list of Clearwater lookouts, compiled in June 2016, gives the same year and the same 30-foot pole.[^3]

Three sources agree that the tower was destroyed in 1973.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a tower that is gone.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts marks the site as a hike-in lookout.[^2] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none that says whether the public may visit.
