The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower called Williamsburg in James City County, Virginia, at 37.25476, -76.695, and marks it as gone.[^1][^2] In 1884, Williamsburg itself was made a city in its own right, no longer part of James City County.[^3]

The association also lists a different tower near Toano, in the same county, at 37.43441, -76.83196, and marks that one as standing.[^1] A Forest Lookouts index of Virginia lists a Williamsburg entry in James City County too.[^4]

Across Virginia, the state forestry office began putting up steel lookout towers in 1928.[^5] A 1991 survey of the department's own properties recorded that it owned a fire tower in each of Virginia's counties.[^5] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public.
