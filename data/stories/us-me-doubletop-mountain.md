Double Top Mountain, in Piscataquis County, was made a lookout station in 1913.[^2] A quoted 1917 report says a 48-foot steel tower was partly erected and due for completion in 1918, and a later entry says the work was finished that year.[^2] A 1920 inventory, however, lists a 24-foot steel tower, so the sources disagree about its height.[^2]

In 1919 the Maine Forestry Service made a 360-degree panoramic map centered on the tower.[^1] The state had begun a mapping program in 1917 that gave tower watchmen panoramic maps of the areas they were responsible for.[^3]

The register says the tower was removed between 1996 and 2003.[^4]

The station site is now part of Baxter State Park.[^3] Hikers reach the mountain on trails from Nesowadnehunk Field or Kidney Pond, and the summit is rock and sheer cliffs.[^5] No source I found describes what, if anything, remains of the tower on the summit today.
