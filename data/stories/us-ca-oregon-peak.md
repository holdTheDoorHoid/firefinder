Oregon Peak is a heavily timbered ridge in Yuba County, west of New Bullards Bar Dam.[^1] The lookout sits on Oregon Ridge between Dobbins and Bullards Bar Reservoir.[^2] The National Historic Lookout Register says the site has been used for fire detection since 1921, and it gives 1935 as the build year for the present structures, all built by the Civilian Conservation Corps.[^1]

An Aermotor Company product, the 60-foot tower carries a 7-by-7 observation cabin.[^1] The state remodeled that cab in 1960, giving it a catwalk, a metal door and aluminum sliding windows.[^1] A Nevada State Journal item from November 1946, quoted by Forest Lookouts, reported that construction had started on eight miles of telephone line connecting the Challenge Ranger Station and the lookout.[^3]

In 1992, after the state stopped funding paid lookouts, volunteers began staffing the tower through a program called Volunteers In Prevention.[^2] Oregon Peak became the pilot for similar programs, and many lookouts across California followed its lead.[^2] Oregon Peak and other lookouts have been credited with many first reports of wildland fires over the years.[^2] A Peakbagging photo page, with a photo dated November 21, 2004, describes the lookout as sound and usually staffed in fire season.[^4] Cal Fire's June 2008 list named it among lookouts that might be staffed that year.[^5] A 2021 notice to volunteers said the tower would be closed for the season without enough people to staff it.[^6] That is the latest source I found, so current staffing is unconfirmed.[^6]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^7] The Register gives the registration date as October 30, 2019, as US 1373, CA 196.[^1]

The Register says public access is restricted by a neighboring private campground run for scouting groups, and that the station is 2.2 miles from paved road.[^1] Cal Fire's 2008 list says it does not rent its lookouts.[^5] If you plan to visit, ask Cal Fire or the volunteer program first, and do not climb the tower without permission.
