Round Mountain rises to 6,755 feet in Crook County, Oregon, inside the Ochoco National Forest; firelookout.com gives 6,753 feet.[^2][^3] A cabin built on the summit in the 1930s came first, and a timber tower replaced it in the 1950s.[^1][^2] The two main histories disagree about that tower. One calls it a treated timber R-6 tower, and the other an enclosed L-4 tower.[^1][^2] The one named lookout in the sources is Carol Lochrie, listed for 1952.[^2]

Sources agree that the tower was gone by 1972, but they differ on how it ended.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association register says it was removed that year, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed.[^1][^2] A trail listing says a fire burned it down in 1972.[^3]

Today the summit carries a communication tower, according to that same trail listing.[^3] A Forest Service project record for an AT&T communication site called Round Mountain was signed in September 2020 and is marked completed.[^4] It describes a planned 40-foot monopole tower about 5 miles south of Walton Lake. The record does not mention the summit, so linking it to the summit's communication tower is an inference.[^4] No source found says what, if anything, of the lookout remains under the new equipment. The trail listing gives trailheads at Walton Lake and near Lookout Mountain.[^3]
