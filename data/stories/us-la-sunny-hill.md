The Sunny Hill Lookout Tower is in Washington Parish, Louisiana, near the unincorporated community of Sunny Hill.[^1][^2][^3] That community lies about 11 miles northwest of Franklinton.[^2]

We found no build date, builder or height for the tower in the sources we read. The Louisiana fire tower list gives some background instead. The state's earliest steel lookout dates from 1922 and was put up near Bogalusa.[^1] Thirty-nine fire towers had gone up by 1943, and fifty-six by 1950.[^1] We do not know whether Sunny Hill was one of them.

The list marks the tower as still standing, but it gives no date for that check.[^1] The Central US Lookouts page for Louisiana lists Sunny Hill under Washington Parish and says nothing more about it.[^3]

We found no public access information and no statement of who owns the land. Please ask the landowner or the state forestry office before going near the tower, and do not climb it.
