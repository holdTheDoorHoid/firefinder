Avery Pass sits in the Fremont National Forest in Lake County, Oregon.[^1] According to the Oregon Lookouts directory, around 1910 the ranger based at the Paisley Ranger Station often used this spot for a patrol lookout.[^1] The entry draws on early ranger diaries kept in the forest's archives.[^1]

Most vehicles can reach the pass, according to the Oregon Natural Desert Association's guide to the Fremont, and walkers can head either way from it along the Fremont National Recreation Trail and the Oregon Desert Trail.[^2] The same guide names Morgan Butte, one of a few remaining staffed fire lookout towers, as reachable by most vehicles too.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at Avery Pass today.
