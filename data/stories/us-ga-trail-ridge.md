Trail Ridge is about 130 miles long.[^1] It runs from the Altamaha River in Georgia south to the Santa Fe River in Florida.[^1] A fire observation tower stands at the peak of the ridge, beside U.S. 82 in Brantley County.[^2][^3]

The Civilian Conservation Corps built the tower in the late 1930s, according to a local Brantley County history.[^3] That history says the tower rose high above young pines through the whole twentieth century, and that it went unmanned in the later part of that century.[^3]

The marker next to it was put up by the Works Progress Administration.[^1][^2] It says the ridge formed a natural trail through the coastal lowlands for Native peoples and early white settlers.[^1] The marker database says the marker stands in a clearing at the site of an old fire tower.[^1]

The two sources describe the marker's location differently. The Digital Library of Georgia says the tower is three miles west of Hoboken.[^4] The marker database places the marker 0.4 miles west of Petty Crossing Road, on U.S. 82.[^1]

A photo dated 29 December 2022 shows the tower south of the eastbound lanes of U.S. 82, beside the marker.[^2] The marker database entry was last revised on December 28, 2023. It still places the marker at an old fire tower.[^1] No source describes the tower's condition in detail.

The tower is beside the highway, so it can be seen from the road.[^2] No source names its owner or says whether it is open to climbing. Please do not climb it unless that is confirmed.
