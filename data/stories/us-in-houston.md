Houston was a fire lookout on Houston hill in Jackson County, Indiana, on Hoosier National Forest property.[^1][^2]

The story starts with land. In November 1935, the Jackson County Banner reported that the first land bought in the county for the Pleasant Run unit had been transferred and recorded. It was forty acres near the top of the hill, and the paper reported that it was said the Forest Service would erect a fire tower there.[^2]

A caption on the Indiana Fire Towers site describes a 1986 map that shows a radio tower to the west and the lookout to the east.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the tower as gone,[^3] and the Indiana site's list of towers places Houston among those not standing.[^4]

The Indiana Fire Towers site describes a small turnoff on the north side of the road where the lookout used to be. A modern communications tower stands there now.[^1] No source we read describes public access to the turnoff, so check with the Pleasant Run Ranger District before going there.[^2]
