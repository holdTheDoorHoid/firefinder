French Ridge sits in the Wenatchee National Forest in Chelan County, about 17 miles to the west of Leavenworth.[^1][^2] The lookout was an L-5 cab built in 1934.[^1][^4] The sources we read describe a cab, not a tower.[^1][^2][^4]

In June 1934 the Leavenworth Echo reported that John Wade and Frank Nixon were packing the lookout house in that week.[^3][^4] The paper also said snow in the high country reached as deep as 15 feet in some spots.[^3][^4] Panoramic photos were made from the lookout's roof on August 13, 1934.[^3][^4]

A blog that posts Okanogan-Wenatchee National Forest photos and notes says John (Johan) Olav Kjosness worked summers from 1934 through 1939, and that his lookout posts included French Ridge.[^5] Firelookout.com lists Ralph Walter as known lookout personnel in 1963.[^2]

Removal came in the 1970s.[^1][^2][^4]

Willhite's photo captions mention a foundation, remains and an anchor spike at the site, and one of his views from the site is captioned 2020.[^4] No source we read describes what stands there now beyond those captions.[^4]

Willhite's directions begin in Leavenworth on Icicle Creek Road, run to the trailhead, then cover 2 miles on the Icicle Creek Trail and 4.4 miles on the French Ridge Trail to reach the site.[^4] He lists a hiking distance of 6.5 miles with 3,500 feet of gain.[^4] A 2003 trip report said hikers could overlook the side trail up to the site.[^6] The site is on Wenatchee National Forest land, and no source we read describes a closure or a permit.[^1][^4]
