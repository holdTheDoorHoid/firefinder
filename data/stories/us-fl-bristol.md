Bristol was an 80-foot forest lookout tower in Liberty County, Florida.[^1] A newspaper item dated 17 January 1937, quoted on a regional lookout site, names it as one of two earlier 80-foot towers, the other at Aucilla in Madison County.[^1]

As a trial, the forest service assembled the tower lying on its side on the ground.[^1] A gin pole, cables and a tractor then tipped it upright.[^1] The item gives two reasons for building it that way: the danger to Civilian Conservation Corps enrollees, and safety rules for men working off the ground.[^1] The same item says the Newport tower, due to be raised on Monday, would be the first 100-foot one raised.[^1]

No source I could reach describes the site today.
