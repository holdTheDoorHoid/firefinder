Scout Mountain Lookout stood about 12 miles south of Pocatello, in Bannock County, Idaho, at an elevation of 8,700 feet.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page places it in section 34, township 8 South, range 25 East, inside the Caribou-Targhee National Forest.[^1][^3]

The lookout was a frame cabin built in the 1930s.[^1][^3] Its firelookout.com page is titled Scout Mtn. Fire Lookout Cabin.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list gives its coordinates as 42.67748 and -112.34568, and classes it as a ground structure rather than a tower.[^4]

Two site histories call the cabin abandoned, and that list marks the site as gone.[^1][^2][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under "Drive-Up."[^2] No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.[^1][^2][^3]
