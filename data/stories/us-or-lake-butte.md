Lake Butte is in Grant County, Oregon, on the Malheur National Forest, about 12 miles north of John Day.[^1][^2]

Forest Lookouts lists a 20-foot native timber tower with an L-4 cab, built in 1934.[^3] Firelookout.com instead says the site was established in the early 1930s.[^2] A 33-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab followed in 1938.[^1][^3] A 1946 Coast and Geodetic Survey description puts the tower at about 40 feet, taller than the other sources' figures.[^3]

The Grub Creek fire was reported from the station on July 13, 1961.[^3] In October 1964, shots were fired through the lookout post on a Saturday morning while the lookout was away from the tower.[^3] A Blue Mountain Eagle report, quoted by Forest Lookouts, describes the damage.[^3]

A new 30-foot treated timber tower with an R-6 flat cab replaced the 1938 tower.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts quotes a 1962 annual report saying the tower was completed late that year.[^3] A separate 1962 entry in the Historical Record for Buildings gives its final cost as $6,688.90.[^3] The register and firelookout.com both date it to 1963.[^1][^2]

Forest Lookouts and firelookout.com give 1974 as the year the tower was moved to Antelope Mountain.[^2][^3] The register says only that the move came in the 1970s.[^1]

The moved tower now stands on Antelope Mountain.[^4] Firelookout.com says the Antelope Mountain lookout is staffed every summer.[^4] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the Lake Butte site today. We also found nothing on whether Antelope Mountain is open to visitors.
