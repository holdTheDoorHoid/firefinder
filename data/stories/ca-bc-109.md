The firelookout.ca record lists a 109 Fire Lookout in British Columbia, in Area B (Finlay Valley/Beatton Valley) of the Peace River Regional District, at 956 m (3136 ft).[^1] The record gives its coordinates as 56.786, -121.951.[^1]

The record gives its design as D-6.[^1] A fire lookout photo guide says D-6 refers to District 6 of the U.S. Forest Service, in its Pacific Northwest Region.[^2] The same guide describes a cupola house with a main floor of about 3.7 m (12 ft) square and a second-floor observatory one-quarter that size.[^2] It dates the design mostly to the 1920s.[^2]

No source I found describes the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: 109 Fire Lookout, Firelookout.ca, https://firelookout.ca/lookouts/?slug=109-fire-lookout (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Forest Fire Lookout Tower Photos, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/lktpix.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
