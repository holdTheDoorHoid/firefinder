Moore Knob is in Wise County, Virginia, at 36.88654, -82.52046.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list sorts its entries into lookout structures and other or unknown structures. Moore Knob falls in the second group, with its type recorded as unknown and its status as gone.[^2] The same group also lists tree platforms and platform towers.[^2]
