East Spread stood on Spread Mountain, about 20 miles east of Seeley Lake in Powell County, in the Lolo National Forest.[^1] In August 1935 a Missoulian item said new lookout points were being opened in the upper Blackfoot, one of them at Spread Mountain.[^2] The Independent Record reported in August 1938 that Lolo rangers planned three lookouts that summer, East Spread among them.[^2] The sites were far from roads, so supplies went in by pack mules over mountain trails, and the building work was to be done by Civilian Conservation Corps crews.[^2] By September 1938 the Missoulian reported that construction at East Spread was under way.[^2]

Firelookout.com gives a different build year, 1935, and describes a 20-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1] A 1938 item describes the planned towers as 50 feet tall, so the build year and the height are not settled.[^1][^2]

The Independent Record said staffing the lookouts would allow closer fire watch over the Blackfoot area during fire season.[^2] In 1966 a former Seeley Lake seasonal worker, writing an opinion column, recalled that the district then had four fire lookouts, East Spread among them.[^3] A National Register study says the Lolo planned to use only 26 lookouts in the summer of 1967, and East Spread was on that list.[^4]

Firelookout.com says the tower was abandoned in 1972 and is no longer standing.[^1] Neither page describes what remains on the summit today, and neither says whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
