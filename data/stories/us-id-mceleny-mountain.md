McEleny Mountain is a peak in Lemhi County, Idaho, on the Salmon National Forest.[^1] A climbing guide places its summit on a forested ridge southeast of Hoodoo Meadows, in the Yellowjacket Mountains, at 8,938 feet.[^2]

A forest lookout report from 1924 says that a lookout, patrol and smoke-chaser worker kept a camp half a mile below the summit. The summit itself had no improvements at that time.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today. The climbing guide describes the route in two stages: a trail up the west face, then a brief push through brush to the ridge summit.[^2] It notes that the trail is no longer maintained, and that the top has attractive views.[^2]

The approach starts at a trailhead at the south end of Hoodoo Meadows, reached by forest roads that begin at Panther Creek Road.[^2]
