Juno, an unincorporated Dawson County community, sits along State Route 183 northwest of Dawsonville.[^1] A Georgia tower list places a Juno lookout in the county at 34.46696, -84.17943, and gives 1624 in its elevation column with no unit stated.[^2] The Eastern U.S. Lookouts Georgia index also names a Juno entry in Dawson County.[^3]

The tower list's legend says the Georgia Forestry Commission supplied coordinates for 118 towers, under BLL numbers, and that an older GFC list described 203 towers under gfc numbers.[^2] This Juno entry carries both labels: BLL-64453 and gfc-910.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the Juno lookout site today, and none says whether the spot is open to visitors.
