The Forest Lookouts site lists a Plymouth tower in Orange County, Florida.[^1] Beyond the county, its entry gives only one location detail, "T20S-R27E-", which reads as a survey township and range rather than a place name.[^1]

Its one dated fact is a 2008 auction, which brought $1,500.[^1]

A 2010 WFTV report, carried by Firehouse, said Orange, Seminole and Brevard counties had once had ten such towers between them, and now had two.[^2] Statewide, the report said, the towers were being sold off, partly because of cell phones, which residents can use to report a fire from nearly any location.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains of the Plymouth site today, or whether the public may visit it.

[^1]: Plymouth - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/plymouth.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Fla. Fire Towers Being Sold Off, Firehouse (report by WFTV, 2010), https://www.firehouse.com/community-risk/news/10465752/fla-fire-towers-being-sold-off (accessed 2026-10-21).
