Twentymile Peak is about nine miles south of Bonners Ferry, in Boundary County, Idaho, on the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1] The peak stands at 5,371 feet, and a 40-foot L-4 tower is dated to 1939.[^1][^2]

The sources name one lookout, Harry Crossley, for 1933.[^1][^2] That is six years before the tower date, and the sources do not explain the gap.

The tower was destroyed in 1954.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts entry instead says "Removed," and the sources do not say which word describes what happened.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^4] No source we read describes the site today, so we cannot say what remains on the peak or whether the site is open to visitors. Idaho Fire Lookouts calls the site a hike-in.[^2]
