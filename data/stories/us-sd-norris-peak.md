Norris Peak, south of Deadwood in Pennington County, got a 35-foot steel tower in 1932.[^1][^2] A May 1932 newspaper item said the lookout had been stationed high in a swaying pine or spruce tree the summer before.[^2] The register describes an Aermotor tower with a 7-by-7-foot enclosed cab, plus a small residence cabin with a porch.[^1] Firelookout.com tells a different story, with a crow's nest in 1931 and a 40-foot steel tower with a log cabin in 1932.[^3]

A July 1933 item said a Civilian Conservation Corps sub-camp would soon be established near the peak to build a shelter cabin for the lookout.[^2] In June 1940 the Rapid City Journal called Norris Peak a secondary, or emergency, lookout, used only when fire hazard ran high.[^2] In July 1954 the Pactola ranger and crew brought a spot fire at Norris Peak, near Pactola, under control.[^2] In June 1963 staffing at the Veterans Peak and Norris Peak stations was to depend on the weather.[^2]

Firelookout.com says the tower was destroyed in 1966, but no other source in this research gives that date.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's statewide list marks the site as gone.[^4] The register added the site on May 22, 2022.[^1]

A Black Hills travel listing for Norris Peak places the site near where the now removed tower stood.[^5] No source describes what remains there today, or whether visitors can reach it.
