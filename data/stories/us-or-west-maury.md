West Maury Mountain sits in the Ochoco National Forest in Crook County, Oregon, about 24 miles southeast of Prineville.[^1][^2]

A lookout cabin was built on the mountain in 1954.[^3] It was an L-4 design, about 14 feet square.[^1][^2][^3] It was not built on pilings, as a 1958 newspaper report noted.[^3] A forest service lookout staffed the cabin that year.[^3] A Central Oregonian report from September 1958 says that lookout killed a six-foot bear with a .22 pistol.[^3]

The cabin came down in 1970.[^1][^2] One source says it was removed, and another says it was destroyed.[^1][^2]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout today. A lookout directory lists the site as a ground site and marks it gone.[^4] We also found no information about public access, so anyone planning a visit should check with the Ochoco National Forest first.
