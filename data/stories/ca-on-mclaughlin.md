The McLaughlin Fire Tower is a steel tower in Algonquin Park, in Nipissing District in northeastern Ontario.[^1] The site sits at 558 m (1,831 ft) in elevation.[^1] It is shown on the Department of Lands and Forests tower detection map of 1963.[^1]

Ontario's fire towers were once overseen by the Department of Lands and Forests. In 1972 that department was succeeded by the Ministry of Natural Resources.[^2]

Algonquin's steel fire towers had a wooden lookout cupola at the top.[^3] The park's Fire Tower Trail, at the entrance to the Algonquin Visitor Centre, has a replica of one.[^3] That replica is not at the McLaughlin site, which lies well to the west-north-west of the Visitor Centre.[^1][^3]

One history page on the park's early towers says the steel tower at Big Crow Lake is the only one still standing.[^4] That page does not name McLaughlin, so it does not settle this site's fate.[^4]

No source consulted describes the McLaughlin site today, or whether the public can reach it.
