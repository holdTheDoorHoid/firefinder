Willis was a fire lookout tower in Montgomery County.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list places it at 30.47684, -95.40403 and marks it as gone.[^1]

A second Texas roster lists a Willis tower at the same coordinates, in Montgomery County, and tags it TFS. Notes on the same page tie that code to the Texas Forest Service, which suggests the tag refers to that agency.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register counts five registered lookouts in Texas: Chandler, Love's, Pine Mills, Spring and Woodville. Willis is not among them.[^3]

No source found describes what, if anything, stands at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
