Buck Mountain, in Klamath County, is listed on the Forest Lookouts guide under the Klamath-Lake Counties Fire Protective Association.[^1] The guide's only dated entry is from May 1912, when the Evening Herald reported the association's plans.[^1] The association planned about twenty miles of new telephone line to speed up fire reports, and a telephone would be put on Buck Mountain, "which will be used for a lookout."[^1] The central station was to be at Spencers.[^1]

The association's archive gives a different name for those years. It lists 1911 to 1921 as the Klamath-Lake Counties Forest Fire Association, and 1922 to 1924 as the Klamath Forest Protective Association, or KFPA.[^2] The 1912 notice and the archive appear to describe the same association.[^1][^2]

No source describes the summit today, what remains there, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^2]: Klamath Forest Protective Association Records, 1900-2008, Archives West, https://archiveswest.orbiscascade.org/ark:80444/xv67687 (accessed 2026-10-21).
