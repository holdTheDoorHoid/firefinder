A fire tower under the name Van Creek is on the Georgia lookout list kept on the tnlandforms.us site.[^1] The list places it in Elbert County at 34.13948, -82.74179. Its elevation is given as 682 with no unit; read as feet, that is 208 m.[^1]

The list says its towers were found on USGS topographic maps and in National Geodetic Survey datasheets. It also says the Georgia Forestry Commission supplied coordinates for 118 towers, and an older commission list gave the sites of 203 towers.[^1] The Van Creek entry carries no tag after its elevation, so the list gives it no survey or commission reference.[^1] The list page was last revised in March 2016.[^1]

No other source we found describes what remains at this spot today, or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
