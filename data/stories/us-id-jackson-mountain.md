The earliest documented structure at Jackson Mountain was a log cabin with a platform tower, dated 1914.[^1][^2] A 55-foot pole tower with a pole cab went up in 1924.[^1][^2] By 1929 the site had a 50-foot pole tower with an 8-by-8-foot frame cab.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the site's elevation as 4,685 feet.[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists Herman Walker and LW Hawk as known staff in 1933, both credited to the Fire Lookout Museum.[^1]

The site was abandoned in 1935.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com notes that the 1914 log cabin was still standing in 1983.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association list marks the tower as gone.[^3] No source describes what remains at the site today, and none says whether the area is open to visitors. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as drive-up.[^1]
