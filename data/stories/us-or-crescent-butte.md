A cab was built on Crescent Butte in 1938.[^1][^2][^3] The site is in the Deschutes National Forest, about two miles east of the town of Crescent in Klamath County.[^1][^2]

Sources disagree on how the cab left the summit. The register says it was removed in 1954.[^1] Firelookout.com and WillhiteWeb.com both say it was destroyed that year.[^2][^3] The Forest Association's list records the site as a ground type with the status gone.[^4]

As WillhiteWeb.com summarizes it, a 1958 USGS description of the summit mentions a microwave mast and no lookout.[^3]

No source I read describes what stands on the summit now. A personal guide describes dirt roads behind the town of Crescent.[^3] The sources do not say whether the summit is open to the public, so check with the Deschutes National Forest before visiting.
