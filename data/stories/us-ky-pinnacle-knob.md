Pinnacle Knob is a fire lookout in Cumberland Falls State Resort Park, in Whitley County, Kentucky. The Kentucky Department of Parks administers it today.[^1]

The Civilian Conservation Corps built the tower in 1937.[^1][^2] A Lexington Herald-Leader item dated December 13, 1937, and reprinted on the Forest Lookouts page, said the new steel observatory would replace a temporary wooden tower near the same spot.[^2] It also called the tower the 16th observatory the U.S. Forest Service had completed in the Cumberland National Forest, and said the state parks division had made the site available.[^2]

The steel tower stands 40 feet tall and carries a 14-by-14-foot cab of the Forest Service's R-8 model.[^1][^2] The register describes the cab as live-in and says the tower is the last of its kind in Kentucky.[^1]

The sources read here do not say when the Forest Service stopped using the lookout. The register's visit report from October 2016 records it as unstaffed at that time.[^1]

The tower was restored in 2008. The register gives August 27, 2008 as its registration date, and the Forest Lookouts page records a grand opening on September 27, 2008.[^1][^2] The register now lists it as a state park historic site.[^1]

Today the walk to the tower is a 1.25-mile out-and-back, mostly easy, with a number of staircases on the way.[^3] Visitors park just outside a gate, where there is room for two or three cars.[^3] The top deck can be reached at announced times, during tours the park runs.[^3] The 2016 visitor found the tower in good condition but noted that it needed paint, and could not reach the top because it was locked.[^1] If you want to go up, plan around a park tour and check with the park first.
