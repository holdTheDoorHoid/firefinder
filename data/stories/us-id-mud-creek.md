The Mud Creek lookout stood about 14 miles northeast of Kooskia, in the Clearwater National Forest.[^1][^2] The site sits at 4,517 feet, according to Idaho Fire Lookouts and the Forest Service list.[^3][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site under drive-up access, although the tower is no longer there.[^3]

The site was developed in 1932 with a 130-foot crow's nest tree platform, about half a mile northwest of the later tower.[^1][^3] Around 1936 a 100-foot steel Aermotor tower went up to replace that earlier tree observation point.[^1][^2][^3] The site also held L-4 cab living quarters, and a second crow's nest stood about a mile to the southwest.[^1][^3]

The site was abandoned in 1967.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The tower came down in 1978, a date that the Forest Service list also gives.[^1][^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
