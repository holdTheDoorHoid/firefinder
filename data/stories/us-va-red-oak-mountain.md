Red Oak Mountain Lookout, also called Red Oak Knob, is a cupola-style lookout near Laurel Mills in Rappahannock County, Virginia.[^1] The register dates it to the 1930s and says it is the only cupola-style lookout in Virginia.[^1] The state index lists its type as Ground.[^3]

The sources we read do not say who built it, or give a build year more exact than the 1930s.[^1]

During fire season, the register says, a lookout walked from near Laurel Mills up to Red Oak Knob each day to look out for wildfires.[^1] We found no names, years or specific fires tied to that work.[^1]

The register added the lookout on June 9, 2015, on the nomination of Krissy Reynolds. It gives the elevation as 1,399 feet (426 meters).[^1]

The state index lists the lookout as standing.[^3] The register names a private owner and describes no visiting arrangement.[^1] A March 2020 blog post describes a visit to Red Oak Mountain, but it does not say how the writer got permission or which route was used.[^2] Until the owner says otherwise, treat the site as private.[^1]
