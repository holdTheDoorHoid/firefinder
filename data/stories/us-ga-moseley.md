The Moseley Lookout Tower is listed as a fire tower in Putnam County, Georgia, under the Georgia Forestry Commission.[^1][^2] A Georgia tower list on tnlandforms.us places it at 33.39957, -83.49183, and gives it the code gfc-602.[^1] That list was revised on 22 March 2016, so it may predate later changes at the site.[^1]

In the late 1980s the Commission began replacing its tower operators with air patrols, and many towers across the state were scrapped and sold.[^3] The same post says Georgia's towers are at risk.[^3]

No source I could read describes the site today, or whether the public can visit it.
