The Siltcoos lookout stood on Siltcoos Mountain about seven miles south of Florence, in Lane County on the Siuslaw National Forest.[^2][^1] In July 1932 the road to the top was nearly finished, and lumber was due for the new station.[^3] The sources disagree on how far the site was from the Pacific.[^1][^2] The Register says half a mile, and firelookout.com says a mile.[^1][^2]

The tower was a 20-foot round fir pole with an L-4 cab.[^1][^3] The Register and firelookout.com date its construction to 1933, while the Oregon Lookouts timeline gives 1932.[^1][^2][^3]

Forest guard A. G. Hines and his family settled into the new guard station in late summer 1932, and the station was shut for the season by late October.[^3] In April 1933 he began work as the lookout's forest guard.[^3]

In July 1933 the newly installed fire finder was credited with spotting a fire on Bailey Hill near Junction City.[^3] In 1935 crews were widening the road to the station to 18 feet.[^3]

In 1941 Bill Brown was appointed to run the Siltcoos recreation area and was to be stationed at the lookout after Labor Day.[^3] A 1942 entry lists an activation on March 10 tied to the Eugene Filter Center, with no further detail.[^3]

The Register and the Oregon Lookouts timeline date the tower's removal to 1959.[^1][^3] firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^2]

The Register describes the site only as a former lookout, and no source we read says what remains there now or whether the public can reach it.[^1]
