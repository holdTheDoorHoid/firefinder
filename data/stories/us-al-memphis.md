The Memphis Lookout Tower is in Houston County, Alabama, at about 31.134 north and 85.377 west.[^1] OpenStreetMap, a volunteer-edited map, has a point with the same name and tags it as an observation tower.[^2]

Jackson's Ramblings, a personal site, lists it as "Al # 80" and says it is "Not listed on the National Registry." The author asks anyone with information about the tower to get in touch.[^1]

We found no source that gives a build date, a design, staffing, or the tower's later history. No source we found says whether the tower still stands, what remains at the site, or whether the public can visit it.
