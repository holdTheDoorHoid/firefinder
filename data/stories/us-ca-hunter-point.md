Hunter Point is in the Mendocino National Forest, in Mendocino County, California.[^1] A Forest Service lookout went up here in 1935, a Plan R1 cabin first set on a rock tower nine feet high.[^1] Later a block tower replaced the rock one.[^1] A photograph in the register dated 1943 shows the lookout.[^1]

According to the register, the block tower is all that is left today, and it is used for communications.[^1] A 2009 photo captioned in the register shows the tower in that role.[^1] Mendocino National Forest administers the site, according to the register.[^1]

The register added the site on February 2, 2020.[^1] We found no current description of the site and nothing on public access, so check with the Mendocino National Forest before going.

[^1]: Hunter Point Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ca/hunter-point-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
