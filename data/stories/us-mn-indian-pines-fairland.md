Indian Pines, also listed as Fairland, is a fire lookout in Koochiching County, Minnesota.[^1][^3] Our listings place it on the Red Lake Indian Reservation.[^1][^2] We did not check that against boundary maps.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing, under the name Indian Pines (Fairland), at coordinates near 48.49 north and 94.19 west.[^3] The listings give no year of construction, no builder and no design. We found no account of anyone who staffed the tower or of any fire it watched. The Central US Lookouts page gives a location code, 158N-27N-24, rather than a street address.[^1]

Because the listings place it on the Red Lake Indian Reservation, treat it as permission-only. Ask the Red Lake Nation before any visit, and do not climb the tower without that permission.

[^2]: Minnesota Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/minnesota.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^3]: Minnesota Lookouts (Standing), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/mn-st/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
