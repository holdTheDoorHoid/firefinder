American Mountain is a lookout in Lincoln County, Montana, in the Kootenai National Forest, about 17 miles northwest of Yaak.[^2] A firelookout.com page gives its elevation as 6,591 feet, about 2,009 meters.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list places it at 48.96212 north and 116.03339 west, and classes it as a ground lookout.[^1]

The firelookout.com page dates a cab and crow's nest to the 1930s and says they were still there.[^2] The same page calls the lookout abandoned.[^2] The association's list, however, marks the site as gone.[^1] The two sources do not agree on whether anything still stands.

Neither source describes the site today, or whether visitors may go there.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Montana fire lookouts list, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: American Mtn. Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/american.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
