The Ooltewah Lookout Tower stood in Hamilton County, Tennessee, at about 1,495 feet elevation, according to Tennessee Landforms.[^1] The same entry lists it as removed and gives its position as 35.149, -85.0241.[^1] OpenStreetMap records the structure as an observation tower.[^2]

The entry adds that the top portion is now held by the Ocoee Regional Nature Center in Bradley County.[^1] No source we found describes what, if anything, remains on the original site or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: TN lookout tower: HMT002 Ooltewah, Tennessee Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/m.php?wpt=HMT002 (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Ooltewah Lookout Tower, OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/356895237 (accessed 2026-10-10).
