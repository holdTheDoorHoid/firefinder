The Cooks Hammock Fire Tower stands in Lafayette County, Florida.[^1] The register describes it as a 120-foot Aermotor tower with a standard cab.[^1] The Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services administers it.[^1] The register gives no build year, staffing history or notable fire for the tower.[^1]

A July 2026 Riverbend News report says the steel tower has kept watch over Lafayette County's woods for more than sixty years.[^2] The register says the forest has matured enough to reduce the tower's view.[^1]

The same report says the tower is getting ready to move out of the community.[^2] The excerpt I could read does not say where it is going, who is moving it, or when, so any details of the move remain unconfirmed.[^2]

The register lists no visiting or access information for the tower.[^1] Check with the state, which administers the tower, before you go.[^1]
