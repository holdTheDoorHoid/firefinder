A small cab went up on Ramey Ridge in 1931. Sources describe it as 8 feet by 8 feet, and they split on the material: Idaho Fire Lookouts and firelookout.com call it wooden, while the Rex's Fire Lookout Page calls it a log cab.[^1][^2][^3]

The site lies in Idaho County, in the Payette National Forest, 11 miles south of the Chamberlain Ranger Station.[^1] Sources also disagree on the elevation. firelookout.com gives 7,760 feet, while a 1933 newspaper account quoted on the Rex's page puts the station at 8,400 feet.[^1][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts says it has no documented staffing history for the lookout, and it asks readers to share a photo, story or record if they have one.[^2]

The lookout is gone.[^4] The firelookout.com record says it has been absent for some years, and the Rex's page lists it as removed, without a date.[^1][^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the ridge today, or whether the spot is open to visitors. Idaho Fire Lookouts classes it as a hike-in site.[^2]
