Hemlock Butte in the Umpqua National Forest, Douglas County, once carried a fire lookout tower.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com places the site about 12 miles north of Crater Lake.[^2] The register puts the summit at 6,301 feet (1,921 meters) and gives its coordinates as 43.129050 north and 122.192910 west.[^1]

The register dates the tower to 1934 and says it is now gone.[^1] firelookout.com gives the same year and status.[^2] The Oregon lookouts list marks the site as removed but adds no history beyond that.[^3]

The register records the site's registration date as December 19, 2010, and names the U.S. Forest Service as administrator.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the ground today, and none says whether the public can reach the site. Anyone planning a visit should check with the Umpqua National Forest first, because none of the sources gives current access rules.[^1][^2][^3]
