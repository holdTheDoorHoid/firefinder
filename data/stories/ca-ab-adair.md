Adair is a steel fire lookout in Alberta. Alberta's wildfire register lists the tower at 30 m (98 ft) tall, on a site 394 m (1,293 ft) above sea level.[^1]

The register gives 2013 as the year the tower was constructed.[^1] It places the tower at 59.16, -118.33, on map sheet 84M/01.[^1] The province describes lookouts as a key part of wildfire detection.[^2] It counts 127 lookout sites spread across the boreal forest and along the Rocky Mountains, and says they are placed where visibility is good for spotting and reporting fires.[^2]

Today the register still lists Adair as active, with a weather station on site.[^1] Its access mode is recorded as "Fly", which points to access by air.[^1]
