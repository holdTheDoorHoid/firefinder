Rocky (North) Point is a former lookout site in Linn County, Oregon, about three miles northeast of Sweet Home, at roughly 1,480 feet.[^2][^1] The register lists the site under the Linn FPA.[^1] The Oregon Lookouts page refers to the Linn County Fire Patrol.[^3]

The earliest dated entry on the Oregon Lookouts page is from April 15, 1913.[^3] A newspaper item from the Albany Democrat, quoted on that page, describes a telephone line to Rocky Point and names a Mr. Brady as the person in charge of the station.[^3] A two-story log cabin went up on the point in 1916.[^1][^2][^3]

The register says the cabin was removed in the 1940s.[^1] The firelookout.com listing says it was destroyed in that decade.[^2] Oregon Lookouts dates the removal to about 1945.[^3] The sources agree on the decade but not on whether the cabin was taken down or destroyed.[^1][^2][^3]

I found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1]
