Caddell Mountain's fire tower was first raised on another hill.[^1][^3] The state put it up at Carlin Hill, near Smithfield in Wetzel County, in 1925, and ran it there as a state-owned fire tower until 1946.[^3] That summer it was dismantled and moved to Caddell Mountain in Preston County, where it was re-erected that fall.[^1][^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as an 80-foot steel tower and gives 1946 as its build year.[^1] The Register's own description says the tower came from Carlin Hill, and the Carlin Hill record gives 1925 as its build year. The 1946 date marks the move, not the original build, so the two entries disagree.[^1][^3]

The sources do not say who staffed the tower or what fires it watched.

The Register lists the West Virginia Division of Forestry as administrator and gives December 12, 2013 as the registration date, with Ron Stafford as nominator.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's West Virginia list still shows the tower as standing.[^4] The Carlin Hill record says the Division of Forestry continued to maintain the tower as of 2017.[^3]

The sources do not say whether the public can reach the tower or climb it. Anyone interested should ask the Division of Forestry before visiting, and should not climb it without permission.
