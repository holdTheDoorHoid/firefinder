Silver Creek Lookout went up in 1936 as a standard L-4 cabin, sitting on a concrete base that measures 24 feet on each side.[^1][^2][^3] It is in the Boise National Forest in Valley County, Idaho, about 15 miles southeast of Cascade, at an elevation of 6,778 feet.[^1][^3]

The National Historic Lookout Register says the cab is manned each summer and lists the Emmett Ranger District as a cooperator.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts names a staff record from 1965 and another from about 2019, though it marks the later year as approximate.[^4]

The lookout also shows up in a 1957 newspaper item quoted by Forest Lookouts. It reports that the lookout had seen a fire nearby one Friday night and that searchers were sent out right away. The same item covers a summer forest worker who had gone missing and was later found.[^2]

The cab is still in place. The register added it on June 19, 2017, as US 1207, ID 110.[^1][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts describes it as a drive-up, staffed lookout.[^4] None of the pages I could read say whether visitors may climb to the cab, so treat it as a working lookout and ask the Forest Service before planning a visit.
