Beaver Mountain stands in Dolores County, Colorado, on the San Juan National Forest, at 9,310 feet.[^1][^2] The register gives the site's coordinates as N 37.716450° and W 108.367470°.[^1]

The register dates the tower to the 1910s and its removal to the 1920s.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site and the Forest Fire Lookout Association both mark it as removed.[^2][^3]

The register added the site on September 18, 2009, and its links section includes the Colorado/Utah chapter website of the Forest Fire Lookout Association.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the summit, and none says whether visitors are welcome. The register lists the U.S. Forest Service as the administering agency.[^1] Before heading out, check with the San Juan National Forest.
