Cove Mountain Tower stands on the summit of Cove Mountain, inside Great Smoky Mountains National Park in Sevier County, Tennessee.[^1][^2] The Civilian Conservation Corps built it in 1935 as a 60-foot steel Aermotor tower with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab.[^1][^2]

It was a fire-watch tower until it was no longer used for monitoring forest fire threats. The Park Service has since turned it into an air quality monitoring station, and park staff service its instruments.[^2][^3] According to the Park Service, the second level holds a compact room where instruments measure air quality and weather.[^3]

Hikers can reach the summit on foot, following the Laurel Falls Trail to the Cove Mountain Trail, but they are not allowed into the tower.[^2] The register gives a registration date of March 19, 2007, with the nomination by Peter Barr.[^1]

I could not find a record of who staffed the tower as a lookout, or when it changed over to air quality work. The hiking guide gives its height as 60 feet, but an NPS profile describes the top as 80 feet in the air, and I could not reconcile the two.[^2][^3]

Keep to the public trails, and do not try to climb the tower.[^2]
