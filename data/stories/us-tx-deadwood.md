The Deadwood lookout is in Panola County, Texas, about two miles north-northwest of the small community of Deadwood.[^1] Deadwood is roughly ten miles east of Carthage, in the eastern part of the county.[^2]

A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description written in 1958 describes a glass-enclosed cab on a four-legged steel frame, painted gray, with an overall height of about 100 feet.[^1] A Forest Lookouts photo caption numbers the tower 50 and lists the Texas Forest Service as its agency.[^3]

The sources we read give no build date, builder, staffing history or fire record. The 1958 description is the earliest date we found. Its height is an overall figure, and we found no second source to check it against.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^4]

We found no statement about public access. The 1958 directions from the village say to head north-northwest on Farm Road 123, but a route that old may have changed.[^1] Please do not climb the tower, and check with the landowner before going near it.
