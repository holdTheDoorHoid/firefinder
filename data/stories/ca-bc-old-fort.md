Old Fort is a lookout listed in the Regional District of Bulkley-Nechako, at 55.083 N, 126.383 W and an elevation of 1566 m (5,138 ft).[^1] The federal place-name database lists a peak called Old Fort Mountain at 55.088056, -126.384722, about 0.6 km north-northwest of that listed position.[^2] No source I found describes the site today, so I cannot say what remains there or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Old Fort, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/old-fort.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Place names - Old Fort Mountain, Natural Resources Canada, Canadian Geographical Names Database, https://geonames.nrcan.gc.ca/search-place-names/unique/JBHCH (accessed 2026-10-20).
