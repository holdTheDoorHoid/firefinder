Eagle Butte, in Hood River County, is listed as a lookout site in the Mount Hood National Forest.[^1] The earliest record I found is from August 1917, when rangers in the county's southwest were collecting material for a lookout house on the butte.[^1] The note describes work in progress rather than a finished building.[^1]

Do not confuse this site with the other Eagle Butte in Oregon, the one near Warm Springs in Wasco County.[^2] That tower, a 107-foot (33 m) Aermotor, was built in 1935, and the register lists it as still in service.[^2] It stands within the Warm Springs Indian Reservation, where public access is not permitted.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the Hood River County site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
