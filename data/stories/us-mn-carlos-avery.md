The Carlos Avery tower was an 80-foot ladder tower in Anoka County, and the Minnesota Forest Service owned it.[^2][^3]

A Commission of Conservation report for fiscal 1935 to 1936 says a lookout tower had been erected at Camp No. 1 in the Carlos Avery Game Refuge.[^1] It also says arrangements were made to staff the tower during hazardous periods.[^1]

The county survey says the tower, also known as Camp 1, was relocated here in 1936, soon after the game refuge was set up.[^2] Its earlier site is not known.[^2] The two accounts differ on whether the tower was built at Camp No. 1 or moved there.[^1][^2]

According to the county survey, the tower was taken from its original spot and partly restored in Linwood Township, Anoka County.[^2] The statewide tower list puts that restoration a few miles north of the old site.[^3] The survey lists the restored tower as privately owned, with no public access.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association index lists the refuge site as gone.[^4] No source we read describes what remains at the refuge site today.
