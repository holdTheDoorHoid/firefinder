The Harmony Lookout Tower was in Price County, Wisconsin, on ground about 1,640 feet above sea level.[^2] TopoQuest places it on the USGS Kennan map sheet.[^2] OpenStreetMap has a point under the same name.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list marks the tower as gone and gives its coordinates as 45.57733, -90.56131.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
