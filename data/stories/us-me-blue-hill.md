Blue Hill Mountain stands just under 1,000 feet high in Hancock County, and its summit gives a broad view of the country around it.[^1] In 1947 the Maine Forest Service picked the top as the place for a fire lookout tower.[^1]

Sources do not agree on when the tower went up. A Maine lookout listing gives 1949 as the build year for a 60-foot steel Aermotor tower.[^2] A lookout-history site, drawing on the Maine Historical Society's photo inventory, says a steel tower was set up in 1947.[^3] A photograph of the tower from about 1950 is in the Blue Hill Historical Society's collection.[^1]

The tower came down in 2005.[^1][^3] The Maine lookout listing and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's register both say it was taken away at the landowner's request.[^2][^4]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
