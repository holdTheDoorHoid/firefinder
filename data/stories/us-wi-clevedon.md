The Clevedon Lookout stands in Douglas County, Wisconsin, on a hill that its survey records call Sugar Camp Hill.[^1][^2] The Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources administers it, and the National Historic Lookout Register listed it on August 16, 2017.[^1]

The register does not know when the tower was built and lists its height as unknown. It notes an inside corner leg ladder and says the tower looks like a 100-foot Aermotor.[^1]

A 1958 recovery note for the survey marker on the hill gives directions that include "east to Cleveland fire tower." The sources do not say whether that is this structure.[^2]

I found no record of who staffed the tower or which fires it watched. The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^3] A peak-listing site names a trail, Fire Tower Road, that reaches the summit, but none of the sources say whether the public may reach the tower or go up it.[^4] Check with the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources before you go, and do not climb the tower without permission.
