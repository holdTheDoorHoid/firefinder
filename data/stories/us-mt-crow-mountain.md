Crow Mountain is a lookout site in Lake County, Montana, six miles east of Ronan.[^1] The page gives its elevation as 7,183 feet.[^1]

The only date we have for the lookout is 1973, when it is recorded as a tower.[^1] The same page marks it gone.[^1] The firelookout.com page titles it Crow Mtn. Fire Lookout Tower, while the association calls it Crow Mountain.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it the same way, as a tower that is gone, at 47.52119 north and 113.96790 west.[^2] The firelookout.com regional list for the Flathead-Swan River area names the site too, as Crow Mtn.[^3]

The site lies on the Flathead Indian Reservation.[^1] No source we read says what remains there today or whether visitors may go up. Because the site is on reservation land, anyone thinking of visiting should check with the tribal authorities first.
