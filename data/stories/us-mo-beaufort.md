In the 1954–55 fiscal year, the Missouri Conservation Commission reported that a new fire tower had gone up at Beaufort, in Franklin County.[^1] A November 1955 newspaper notice, quoted on the Central US Lookouts page, described it as a 100-foot tower three miles southwest of town, on Highway EE.[^1] The same notice said that with the tower's completion, all of Franklin County was now in the Meramec Fire Protection District.[^1]

Virgil Fritz of Spring Bluff was the Beaufort towerman.[^1] He would staff the tower during dry spells, and the notice said that as fire danger rose, the detection schedule in the towers got stricter.[^1]

In September 1967, a St. Clair Chronicle notice put the state's bill for vandalism at the Beaufort tower above $1,000, and said two Union men had been convicted of injuring state property.[^1]

The Missouri Department of Conservation's annual report for fiscal 1974–75 says the tower was dismantled.[^1] No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Beaufort - Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/beaufort.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
