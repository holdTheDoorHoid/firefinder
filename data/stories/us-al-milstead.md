The Milstead Fire Tower Site is in Washington County, Alabama, with a listed elevation of 351 feet, or 107 meters.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added it on September 15, 2019, at the nomination of Andrew Zerbe, under the numbers US 1746 and AL 17.[^1] The Alabama Forestry Commission administers the site.[^1]

The register's description is brief.[^1] It says the stands are all that remain, and it adds that a heavily vandalized building, which once held communication equipment, is also on the site.[^1] The description does not say what the stands are, so it is unclear how much of the tower, if any, is still on the ground.[^1]

Thomas Kaufmann, writing an opinion column for 1819 News, says that over 200 of these structures once dotted Alabama.[^2] He names aircraft as the most common explanation for the decline.[^2] He also says the state now keeps around a third of its former lookouts.[^2]

The register does not say whether the site is open to visitors.[^1]
