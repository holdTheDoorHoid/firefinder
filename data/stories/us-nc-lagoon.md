Lagoon is listed as a lookout tower in Bladen County, North Carolina, on tnlandforms.us's list of North Carolina lookout towers. The list places it at 34.52666 N, 78.41416 W, and the page dates a map revision to 16 June 2021.[^1]

Lagoon is also the name of a community. Wikipedia names Lagoon as one of the communities in Colly Township, Bladen County, alongside White Lake.[^2] No source we read ties the tower to that community.

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the tower today, or whether the public may visit the site.

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Colly Township, Bladen County, North Carolina, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colly_Township,_Bladen_County,_North_Carolina (accessed 2026-10-21).
