The Hillmeade lookout stood near Hillmeade in Prince George's County, Maryland.[^1] In 1930 Anne Arundel County contributed $400 toward the planned lookout tower. A state report said the tower would give fire detection over a significant forest area in Anne Arundel County.[^1] A July 1931 newspaper reported that the 100-foot tower had recently been completed.[^1]

In November 1931 lookout-watchman E. O. Brown was in charge of the Hillmeade observatory.[^1] That month he reported a small fire that the Pennsylvania Railroad had started at Tuxedo, eight miles from the tower.[^1] Smokechaser Daniel B. Wiseman put it out before it burned an eighth of an acre.[^1]

The Forest Lookout Association lists the Hillmeade tower as gone.[^2]

A map site still lists a Hillmeade Lookout Tower near Fairwood.[^3] A map label does not show whether anything stands there.[^3] No source we read describes what remains at the site or whether visitors may go there.[^1][^2][^3]
