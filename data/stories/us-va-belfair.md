Belfair was a fire tower in Prince William County, Virginia. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list gives its position as 38.58087 north and 77.43435 west, and it marks the tower as gone.[^1]

The Virginia Department of Forestry's history page says the agency later put up fire watchtowers at important sites across the state.[^2] That history does not name Belfair, so it is only background and does not show who built it.

Because the list marks the tower as gone, no standing tower is recorded at the site today.[^1] No source we checked describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, or whether the ground is open to visitors.

[^1]: Virginia Fire Towers (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/va-co/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: History, Virginia Department of Forestry, https://www.dof.virginia.gov/about/history/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
