Our record for this site is an OpenStreetMap entry named "Lookout Tower." It is tagged as a tower used for observation, and the entry was last edited more than nine years ago.[^1] The entry gives no place name, build date or history.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 31 lookouts in Florida, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register lists six former sites in the state. We found no entry in either list that matches this one.[^1][^2][^3]

Daytona Beach has a county fire training center on Tiger Bay Road, where a four-story drill tower opened in 1988. That is a training structure rather than a lookout, and we found no evidence that it is the same as this entry.[^4]

Until someone identifies this tower, treat the entry as unverified. Do not climb any structure on the strength of a map record alone.
