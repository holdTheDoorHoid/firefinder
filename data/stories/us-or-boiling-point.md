Boiling Point is a former lookout site in Umatilla County, Oregon, about 11 miles southeast of Pendleton.[^1][^2] The register gives its elevation as 3,558 feet, or 1,084 meters.[^1]

The register describes the lookout as a trailer cupola built in 1951. The word "trailer" suggests a cupola mounted on a trailer rather than a fixed tower, but the register does not say how it was moved.[^1] firelookout.com gives the same year.[^2]

The Oregon Department of Forestry administered the site.[^1][^3] The register added it on July 26, 2011, as US 1109, OR 465, after a nomination by Ray Kresek.[^1]

The register says the cupola was gone by 1963.[^1] firelookout.com gives the same year.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list marks the site "Trailer Gone."[^4]

We found no source describing what, if anything, remains at the site, and none saying whether visitors can reach it.
