Flounce Rock is a former fire lookout cabin in Jackson County, Oregon, 26 miles southwest of Crater Lake.[^1] firelookout.com dates the cabin to the 1930s and says it was abandoned.[^1] The same page names the Oregon Department of Forestry as the agency and gives an elevation of 4,138 feet.[^1]

The site shares its name with a rock formation.[^2] A Jackson County place-names database describes that formation as looking like a flounce on a ladies' dress.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site's type as Ground and marks it as gone.[^3] Mountain Zone, a map site, gives an elevation of about 4,144 feet, a few feet more than firelookout.com.[^4]

We found no source that describes the site today, what remains there, or whether the public may visit. Check with the agency named above before you go.
