The Seaburg Point lookout stood 14 miles east-northeast of Riggins, in Idaho County, at an elevation of 6,527 feet.[^1] It was on the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1][^3]

Its history starts in the 1920s with a crow's nest.[^1][^2] In 1927 the site got a 60-foot pole tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab, and by 1929 it also had a log cabin.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists a staffer for 1927, described only as Ted Oliver's brother, and Harold Paul, listed for sometime before 1932.[^2]

A Forest Service history of Nez Perce lookouts includes a photo captioned "Seaburg Lookout Tree, date unknown."[^4]

The lookout was destroyed in 1959, according to firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts.[^1][^2] No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
