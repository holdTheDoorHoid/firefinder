The Williston Lookout Tower stands in Fayette County, Tennessee, on a site the Tennessee Division of Forestry administers.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as an 80-foot Aermotor LS-40 tower.[^1] We could not find when it went up, who built it, or why it was placed there, so its construction remains unknown.[^1][^2]

A Tennessee tower compiler lists a Fayette County tower under the name Ebenezer and gives it state forest tower number 9-8.[^2] The compiler calls these names unofficial and says they were given to towers the USGS maps and the state forestry list left unnamed.[^2] We could not confirm that the Ebenezer entry describes the same site as the Williston record. If it does, the two sources disagree on elevation: the Register gives 577 feet, and the compiler's table gives 594 feet. We use the Register's figure.[^1][^2]

We found no account of who staffed the tower or of any fire it watched.[^1][^2]

The Register lists the tower with an August 6, 2023 registration date.[^1] The compiler's table marks it as standing.[^2]

Today the Register calls the site an active Tennessee Forestry site.[^1] We found no public access arrangement, so visitors should not assume they may go onto the grounds. The compiler advises against climbing lookout towers, because steps are often removed or rotten.[^2]

[^1]: Williston Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/tn/williston-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
