Sutton Top is a mountain in Haywood County, North Carolina, and the Forest Lookouts history lists it under the U.S. Forest Service.[^1] The tnlandforms list of North Carolina towers gives the site the survey code FB2450.[^2]

The only station description we found dates from 1963.[^3] That year the U.S. Geological Survey first observed the survey station, and the sheet places it at a wooden National Forest Service tower that it gives as "41 FT SQUARE."[^3] The sheet does not say whether that figure is the tower's height or its width.[^3] The same sheet codes the mark as a lookout tower and gives the station an elevation of 3,658 feet.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains on the summit today, whether the tower still stands, or whether the site is open to visitors. The survey sheet's history has no entry after 1963.[^3]
