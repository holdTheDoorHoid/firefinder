The Eastern U.S. Lookouts directory says a lookout tower was constructed on Brasstown Bald, in Union County, in 1939.[^1] In 1965, it reports, the lookout was replaced, and the tower it replaced was moved to Southern Forest World in Ware County.[^1] The Georgia lookout list also marks its Ware County entry as relocated from Brasstown Bald.[^2] These are the only sources we found for the move.

Visitors can drive to a lot about half a mile (0.8 km) below the summit, then finish the trip by shuttle or on a short paved walk.[^3] The Forest Service has webcams on the summit's observation tower.[^3] The directory's 1965 entry also mentions a small admission fee to climb a tower, without saying which one.[^1] No source describes the moved tower's condition, or whether it stands at Southern Forest World today.
