The Woodville Fire Tower stands in Tyler County, in East Texas.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register credits the Civilian Conservation Corps with building it in the 1930s, at the Woodville District Office of the Texas Forest Service.[^1]

The Tyler County Heritage Society places CCC Company 891 at Camp Leonard Wood, near Woodville, but its article on the corps does not mention a fire tower.[^2]

The Register describes a 70-foot steel tower with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab. It says the Emsco Oil Derrick Co. supplied the tower and cab.[^1] An external vertical ladder gives access to the cab.[^1]

By the Register's count, 20 of the 142 fire towers that once covered East Texas are still in place.[^1] This one is still standing.[^1][^3]

The available records do not name the lookouts who worked here or the years the tower was staffed. The Register says it is no longer in active service.[^1]

Its cab now houses a radio repeater.[^1] The tower also hosts a lighted Rudolf each holiday season, which the Register says Santa Claus assigned to report whether the children in Woodville have been naughty or nice.[^1]

The Register recorded the tower on March 1, 2000, as US 339, TX 2.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as standing.[^3]

Public access is not stated in the available records.[^1] The Register names the Texas Forest Service as administrator.[^1] Check with that agency before going, and do not climb without permission.
