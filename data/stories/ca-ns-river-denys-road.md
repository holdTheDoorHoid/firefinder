River Denys Road is listed as a fire lookout on the Big Ridge, in Inverness, Nova Scotia.[^1] The name carries an asterisk, which the list uses to mark a tentative label drawn from a nearby landmark or community.[^1]

That list, last updated in September 2009, said Nova Scotia's Department of Natural Resources ran 33 seasonal lookout towers province-wide.[^1] It also warned that some of the towers it listed were likely decommissioned already.[^1]

CBC News reports that the department's towers were taken out of service in 2015 and later dismantled, in favour of aerial surveillance.[^2] The department's statement gave the reason: most wildfire detection now comes from aerial surveillance and calls from the public.[^2] The CBC report does not name this tower, so its fate is unconfirmed.

No source describes the site today, including whether anything stands on the Big Ridge or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: NS fire lookouts, Maritimes Scanning Site (Marscan), http://marscan.com/nsdnrfiretowers.htm (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: 'Not relics from the past': Some firefighters think N.S. should bring back fire lookout towers, CBC News via Yahoo News Canada, https://ca.news.yahoo.com/not-relics-past-firefighters-think-090000982.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
