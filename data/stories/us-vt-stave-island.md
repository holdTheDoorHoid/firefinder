OpenStreetMap has a point named "Stave Island Lookout Tower," tagged as a tower of the observation type.[^1] Stave Island is one of several small Lake Champlain islands included in the town of South Hero.[^2] The same name is used for a seaplane base that AeroCopilot lists at South Hero, Vermont.[^3]

I could find no build date, builder or fire-control role for the tower, and no history of the island that mentions it. A South Hero Historical Society post titled "Stave Island 1893-2019" showed only placeholder text when I read it, so I could not learn from it what the island's history covers.[^4]

Until someone confirms what the structure is, treat it as an unverified tower rather than a fire lookout.
