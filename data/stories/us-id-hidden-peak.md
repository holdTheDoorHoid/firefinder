The first lookout on Hidden Peak was a 16-by-16-foot shake cabin, built in 1927.[^1][^3][^4] An L-4 cab took its place in 1931.[^1][^3][^4] The present structure is an R-6 cab, built in 1961, on a 10-foot concrete base.[^1][^3][^4] A Forest Service statement quoted in an Idaho climbing guide says that cab was placed there three years before the Selway-Bitterroot Wilderness was designated.[^4]

The register says the tower served for emergency use until the 1970s.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists one known staffer, Bill Moore, in 1962.[^2]

The register added the site on July 23, 2012.[^1][^2] It describes the lookout as open to public use, in the Selway-Bitterroot Wilderness at 7,826 feet, and lists it under the Lolo/Clearwater National Forests.[^1] The climbing guide calls the summit seldom visited.[^4] The climbing guide says trails reach the summit, though the hike is long.[^4]
