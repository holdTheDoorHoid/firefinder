The Forest Lookouts record files the Jay lookout under Mark Twain National Forest, in Reynolds County.[^1]

A 1962 Coast and Geodetic Survey description places the station at the base of the lookout tower, about five and a quarter miles east of Bunker and a mile and a quarter north of Bee Fork Creek.[^1] The description calls the tower a four-legged steel structure.[^1] The survey first observed the station that same year.[^2]

Sources differ on the forest's name. The 1962 description puts the station on property of the Clark National Forest, while the record files the tower under Mark Twain National Forest.[^1]

The record lists the Jay tower as removed. It gives no date.[^1]

No source describes the site today. The 1962 survey directions ran south along a track road for 0.05 mile to reach the tower, but the record now lists the tower as removed.[^1][^2]

[^2]: Datasheet HC0774 (Jay Lookout Tower), National Geodetic Survey, https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=HC0774 (accessed 2026-10-21).
