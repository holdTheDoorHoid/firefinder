Mount Hallowell is the high point of the Sechelt Peninsula, and a historic fire lookout tower stands on its summit inside Spipiyus Provincial Park.[^1][^3] The peak rises to 1,228 m (4,029 ft), and from the top you can look out over Pender Harbour, the Strait of Georgia and Vancouver Island.[^1]

A SummitPost page for the peak says the old watchtower sat idle for an unknown number of years.[^3] BC Parks reports that the tower has recently been restored, and that it may be one of the few such towers still standing in the province.[^2]

The park is managed by BC Parks, and hiking trails lead up to the mountain.[^1][^2] The summit trail runs 1.5 km (0.93 mi), is kept in good order, and finishes at the tower.[^1] BC Parks describes the old lookout as a fine place to take in the view, and the park also has more than 55 km (34 mi) of high-clearance 4WD trails on its higher ground.[^1][^2]

A travel guide lists the tower among those believed to still be standing.[^4] No source describes any other structures or remains at the site today.
