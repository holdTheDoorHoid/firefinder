Tom Dunigan's list of Georgia lookout towers places a tower named "wellborn" in Union County.[^1] The list numbers this entry GA358, and the page says names for towers from number 73 on are the author's own creation, so the name is a label.[^1]

The list entry also carries the note "datasheet EE1922". That marker is a 1972 triangulation station in Whitfield County, far to the west of Union County, so the exact location of this entry is uncertain.[^1][^2]

We found no source that describes this tower's construction, staffing, fires or later history. No source describes the site today, so we cannot say whether a tower stands there or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^2]: EE1922 datasheet, National Geodetic Survey, https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=EE1922 (accessed 2026-10-21).
