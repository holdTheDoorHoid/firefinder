A Georgia lookout tower list on the tnlandforms.us website includes a site called siloam, in Greene County.[^1] The list gives its elevation as 661 without naming a unit.[^1]

Wikipedia places the town of Siloam in Greene County.[^2]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known what, if anything, remains there, or whether visitors may go up.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Siloam, Georgia, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siloam,_Georgia (accessed 2026-10-21).
