According to a Georgia Historical Society marker, Riddleville was incorporated as a town in 1859 by state legislative action and named for Anderson Riddle.[^1]

A list of Georgia lookout towers on tnlandforms.us places a tower called "riddleville" in Washington County.[^2] Its entry gives an elevation of 431, with no unit stated, and the code "gfc-625".[^2]

The Eastern U.S. Lookouts directory lists that tower under the Georgia Forestry Commission in Washington County.[^3] It also says that in September 2006 the Commission sold the tower at auction for $700, and the sale required the tower to be taken down within 30 days.[^3] The same directory records similar September 2006 sales for other towers, including one named Prescott.[^3] No source we found confirms that the removal took place.

No source describes what remains at the site today.
