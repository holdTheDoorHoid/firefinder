The National Historic Lookout Register places the Cedar Grove Lookout in Mobile County, Alabama, and says the Alabama Forestry Commission owns it.[^1] The Register gives the site an elevation of 159 feet (48 metres).[^1]

Brendon Minchew nominated the tower, and the Register records its registration as January 2, 2026.[^1] The page carries a 2021 photo credited to Minchew.[^1]

The Register does not give a build year, design, height or builder for the tower.[^1] It also says nothing about who staffed the tower, what fires it watched, or whether it was ever closed, moved or rebuilt.[^1] The Register is the only source I could find that describes this tower.

The Register page does not say whether the public can visit or climb the tower.[^1] Anyone planning a visit should ask the Alabama Forestry Commission first, and should not climb the tower without its permission.

[^1]: Cedar Grove Lookout, National Historic Lookout Register (nhlr.org), http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/al/cedar-grove-lookout/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
