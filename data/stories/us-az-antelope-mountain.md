Antelope Mountain is in Apache County, Arizona. The Forest Lookouts page places the site in the Sitgreaves National Forest, under land description 8N-27E-11.[^2] A SummitPost climbing page says instead that the mountain is on Arizona State Lands, where a permit is required but not typically enforced.[^3]

The Arizona lookout table lists it as a ground site at 34.09631 north and 109.46508 west, and marks it gone.[^1] The Forest Lookouts entry says the same in its own words, calling the site removed.[^2]

The mountain itself rises to 9,003 feet.[^3] The same climbing page says a dirt road reaches the top, and puts the summit less than 2.4 miles from the parking area.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the lookout site today, where on the mountain it stood, or whether visitors may go there.
