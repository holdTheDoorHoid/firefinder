A cabin was built on Paradise Ridge in 1941, though one register says it may date to as early as 1935.[^1][^2] The register places the site in Wallowa County, Oregon, within the Umatilla National Forest, and says the U.S. Forest Service administers it.[^1] The firelookout.com page puts it about 18 miles east-southeast of Walla Walla, at 5,820 feet.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association added the site to its register on August 21, 2011.[^1] It was later abandoned and no longer stands.[^1][^2] The firelookout.com page gives its dates as 1941 to an unknown end, marked with a question mark.[^2]

A Washington index lists a Paradise Ridge entry under Columbia County and marks it removed, which disagrees with the Wallowa County location the Oregon pages give.[^3][^1][^2]

The register puts the site inside the Mill Creek Watershed, and says that watershed is not open to visitors.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains there today.[^1][^2]
