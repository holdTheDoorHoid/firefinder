Green Bonnet was a 40-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, built in 1936 in the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^2] It stood about five miles north of Upper Priest Lake in Boundary County, Idaho, at an elevation of 5,958 feet.[^1][^2]

The tower did not last past 1967.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both say it was destroyed in the Trapper Peak Fire that year, and the Forest Lookouts page says it was burned in a wildfire.[^1][^2][^3] The Boundary County Museum ties the Trapper Peak Fire to lightning strikes in the northwest corner of the county.[^4] The museum says the fire burned for 31 days and covered roughly 16,000 acres.[^4]

The firelookout.org list of Idaho lookouts marks Green Bonnet as gone.[^5] No source we read says what, if anything, remains at the site today. Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the lookout as hike-in, which is the only access detail in the sources.[^2]
