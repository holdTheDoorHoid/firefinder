Two topographic maps of the Tilton area, in Lawrence County, name a Tilton Lookout Tower.[^1][^2] A Pastmaps description of the 1970 map calls it a prominent landmark over the wooded western part of the survey.[^1] The 2012 USGS map's description places it in the central hills as a conspicuous man-made structure, and the map lists a road called Firetower Road.[^2]

OpenStreetMap maps the tower as a lattice structure tagged as an observation tower.[^3]

Neither of Mississippi's lookout registers lists a Tilton lookout.[^4][^5]

No source describes the site today. It is not known whether a tower still stands there, or whether the site is open to visitors.
