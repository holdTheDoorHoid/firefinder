Crockett Knob is in the Malheur National Forest in Grant County, Oregon, about 24 miles north-northeast of John Day.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists its elevation as 4,190 feet, or 1,277 meters, and names the U.S. Forest Service as the administering agency.[^1]

A 1934 L-4 cab stood on the knob, according to the Forest Fire Lookout Association.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the same year.[^2]

In August 1937 the Whitman Forest office asked to build a standard one-car garage and storeroom at the lookout. The Oregon Lookouts page records that the plan was approved for this site on August 6.[^3]

A July 1953 item from the Nyssa Gate City Journal mentions a visit to Roger Stephen, who was on lookout duty at Crockett Knob near Bates.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association says the cab was removed in 1971.[^1] Firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year, so the two sources agree on the date but not on how the cab left the site.[^2] The Oregon Lookouts page lists the lookout only as removed, with no date.[^3]

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it. Check with the Malheur National Forest before planning a visit.
