Boundary Peak Lookout sat about 15 miles southeast of Pierce, in Idaho County, on the Clearwater National Forest.[^1][^2] The site is listed at 5,243 feet.[^1]

The lookout was built in 1934 as a 20-foot L-4 tower on the peak's northeast point.[^1][^2] The Forest Service's list of Clearwater lookouts describes an L-4 cabin for the same year instead.[^3]

The ending is disputed. Two lookout-history pages say the lookout was abandoned in the 1950s and was gone by 1964.[^1][^2] The Forest Service list says it burned in 1954.[^3] The sources do not reconcile the two accounts.

Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site as drive-up.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the tower as gone.[^4] No source we read says what, if anything, remains on the site today, or whether the road to it is still open.[^1][^2]
