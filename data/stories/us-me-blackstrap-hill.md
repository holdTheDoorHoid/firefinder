A state lookout went up on Blackstrap Hill, in Falmouth, Cumberland County, in 1931.[^1][^2] The state's 1931 annual report says a decision was made to end the lack of lookout towers in some districts, and it records a lookout erected on Blackstrap Hill in Cumberland County.[^2] A 1932 panoramic map centered on the Falmouth tower survives in the Internet Archive.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association says the lookout was relocated to Opportunity Hill in 1942.[^1] Its list marks the Falmouth site as gone.[^5]

A hiker's trail report from late May 2026 describes a fire tower on the summit, behind a fence and with commercial communications gear on it.[^3] The report does not say whether that tower is the 1931 lookout or a later structure.[^3] The access road leaves Blackstrap Road, goes through a gate, and carries no sign barring hikers.[^3] The report calls the tower off limits behind its fence, so visitors should stay off it.[^3] The website's disclaimer says trail reports are unchecked and conditions may change.[^3]
