The Silver Mountain tower was a 10-foot pole structure with an L-4 cab. Both pages date it to the 1930s, and neither gives a year.[^1][^2] It stood in the Kaniksu National Forest, about 12 miles west-southwest of Porthill in Boundary County, at 6,535 feet.[^1][^2]

Both sites list the lookout as gone. Neither says when it came down or why.[^1][^2]

Neither page describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today.[^1][^2] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page labels the site hike-in, but it says nothing about trails or whether visitors are welcome.[^2]

Both pages are brief, and neither cites a register, a newspaper or a Forest Service record for these details.[^1][^2]
