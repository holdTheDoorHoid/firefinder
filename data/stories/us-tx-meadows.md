A fire lookout tower called Meadows stood in Gregg County, and the Central US Lookouts listing ties it to the Texas Forest Service.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places it at 32.41194 north and 94.80806 west, and marks it as gone.[^2] A TopoZone list of Gregg County towers also names a Meadows Lookout Tower, placed on the USGS Kilgore NE topographic map.[^3]

Nothing we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the spot is open to visitors.[^1]

[^2]: Texas Lookouts (By County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/tx-co/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Topo Map of Towers in Gregg County, Texas, TopoZone, https://www.topozone.com/texas/gregg-tx/tower/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
