Cemetery Ridge is a peak in the St. Joe National Forest, in Shoshone County, Idaho, about nine miles southwest of Wallace, at 5,591 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

A camp was listed on the site in 1928, and a 10-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab in 1930.[^1][^2][^3] A patrol point two miles southwest, atop Cameron Hill, is listed with the lookout.[^2][^3]

The ridge's name has a story of its own. A December 1936 Independent (Helena) item, reprinted on the Forest Lookouts page, quotes Joe B. Halm of the Forest Service engineering department. He said eleven victims of the 1910 Big Creek fire were buried at the foot of the ridge, which came to be called Cemetery ridge as a result. He also said the name was then made official on maps of the lookout atop the peak.[^1]

In July 1953 a 14-year-old Boy Scout who had been missing from a fishing trip was found on Cemetery ridge, near what an Idaho State Journal report described as a Forest Service lookout with no one stationed in it.[^1]

The lookout was destroyed in 1964.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^2] None of the sources we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
