Pritchard Peak sits in Shoshone County, about 13 miles north of Wallace, inside the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1] In 1939 the site had a camp and a crow's nest, a lookout perch set up in a tree.[^1][^2] The camp was abandoned in 1941.[^1][^2] The two sources disagree on the elevation: firelookout.com gives 4,424 feet and Idaho Fire Lookouts gives 4,400.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts spells the name Prichard Peak, while firelookout.com uses Pritchard.[^1][^2] The Association's Idaho list gives the type as Crowsnest and the status as Abandoned.[^3]

A September 2024 Idaho Fire Lookouts post describes what the writers found on the ground.[^2] They doubt that a vehicle has made it to the summit in the past 20 to 30 years, and the old road, though it still appears on maps, has grown over.[^2] They found flat spots near the top that could have held a camp, but no remains of the camp itself, and no tree they could identify as a crow's nest.[^2] The only items they came across were a length of braided cable and some beer cans.[^2]

The site is listed as hike-in, and the sources describe a camp and a crow's nest rather than a tower.[^1][^2] Expect what the 2024 writers saw: an overgrown road and no camp remains.[^2]
