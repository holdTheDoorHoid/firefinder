Hills Peak is a former lookout site in Douglas County, Oregon, about 27 miles southeast of Oakridge.[^1][^2] The site is administered by the U.S. Forest Service.[^1] A cabin was built on the summit in 1923.[^1][^2] A 1928 newspaper item reported that a line to the peak was about to be built, and that a secondary lookout was planned there.[^3] Panoramic photographs were taken from the site in August 1933.[^3] In 1935 the cabin was replaced with an L-5 cab, which Rex's page describes as a 10 by 10 ground cabin.[^1][^2][^3]

The cab came down in 1966. Firetower.org says it was removed that year, while firelookout.com says it was destroyed.[^1][^2] Rex's page lists the site as removed but gives no date.[^3]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register added the site in February 2011.[^1] None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, remains there now, or whether the public can reach it. Anyone planning a trip should check with the Willamette National Forest first.[^3]
