Simmons Hill lies in Penobscot County, at about 44.995 degrees north and 68.495 degrees west.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower there and marks it as gone.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
