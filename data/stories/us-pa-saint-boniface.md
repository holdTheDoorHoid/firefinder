Saint Boniface, in Westmoreland County, was a steel Aermotor fire tower. The sources do not agree on when it went up or how it came to the site.[^1][^2][^3] The register gives 1935 as its build year and says it was moved from Kecksburg.[^1] The Kecksburg tower's register entry dates that tower to 1924 and says it was relocated in 1938 and took the name St. Boniface.[^3]

A March 1936 Connellsville Daily Courier item, quoted on a lookout history site, reported that work had started on a new tower at Ridge Chapel on Chestnut Ridge, a mile east of Kecksburg, and that demolition of the Kecksburg tower was due to begin soon.[^2] The item planned the new tower at 80 feet and named Richard Johnson of Kingston as the towerman.[^2] The 1935 register date and the 1936 report do not line up, and this story does not settle the date.[^1][^2]

The register says the tower was removed in 1974.[^1]

The name also appears in a 2022 book about the Saint Boniface Woodlands, a property on Chestnut Ridge owned by Saint Vincent Archabbey.[^4] No source we read says whether the tower stood on that land, what, if anything, remains there today, or whether visitors are welcome.
