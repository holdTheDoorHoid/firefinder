The tower stands about 10 miles east of Lodge Grass, on the Crow Indian Reservation in Big Horn County.[^2] The National Historic Lookout Register dates it to the 1930s, but none of the sources I read gives an exact construction year.[^1] A 1957 survey description says the tower was a four-legged wooden structure about 70 feet tall.[^3]

In July 1956, a fire started by lightning in the Wolf Mountains was spotted from the lookout and reported to the Crow Agency station.[^3] The fire was controlled after about four hours, according to a Billings Gazette report reprinted on a Montana lookout history site.[^3] According to the report, all firefighting equipment at Crow Agency was already fighting two other fires, so the information went to the Miller ranch instead.[^3]

The register recorded the tower on April 12, 2022.[^1] It names the Crow Agency office of the Bureau of Indian Affairs as the administrator.[^1] The register's description section has no details yet.[^1]

Because the tower sits on reservation land, check with the administering office before planning a visit.[^1][^2]
