Plowboy Mountain sits about ten miles north of Nordman in Bonner County, Idaho, on the Kaniksu National Forest. The site is listed at 4,877 feet.[^1][^2]

Development began in the 1920s with a pole platform tower and a tent camp.[^1][^3] Living quarters came in 1930, in the form of an L-4 cab.[^1][^3] In 1935 the site got a 50-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, on the eastern-most point of the mountain, and the sources also mention a patrol point two miles to the west.[^1][^2][^3]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site names two watchers.[^2] Rudy Benson is listed for 1933, citing the Fire Lookout Museum, and Robert L. McVey for 1944 to 1947, citing the former Forest Fire Lookout Registry.[^2] A University of Idaho photograph of the lookout is dated 1932, and the library marks that date as approximate.[^5]

Three sources date the site's destruction to 1965.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page classes the site as hike-in, but it gives no trail or access rules, and no source says whether visitors may go there now.[^2]
