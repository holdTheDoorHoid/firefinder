The Forest Lookouts page for Center places the tower in Lawrence County, under the Tennessee Division of Forestry.[^1] It reprints a News-Democrat notice dated October 7, 1955. According to that notice, the state had bought twelve towers, each 80 feet tall and made of steel, and one of them was to go up before early winter, at a cost of about $36,000.[^1] Because the notice speaks of the tower as something still to be built, it is a plan rather than a record of construction.

Tennessee Landforms lists the Center tower as removed.[^2] The 2014 list of extant Tennessee fire towers, reprinted in the National Park Service's multiple property nomination for the state's towers, names two Lawrence County towers, New Prospect and Westpoint, and does not name Center.[^3]

No source I found describes the site today, so I cannot say what, if anything, remains on the ground.
