West Bay was a wooden fire lookout in Bay County, Florida.[^1] A Panama City newspaper item from January 1, 1948 described it as 80 feet high and made of wood, standing two miles north of West Bay.[^1]

We found no build date for it. A Florida Forest Service history of its early towers says that by the fall of 1928 the agency had finished three lookout towers, at its West Bay Unit, Tyler Unit and Satsuma Heights. That history does not say whether the tower that later stood at West Bay was one of them.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts page says the tower was sold at auction in the fall of 2008 for $100 and removed.[^1] Some of its parts went to New York.[^3][^4] In December 2017 Davana LLC erected a tower at the Welcome Center of Grafton Lakes State Park, and a state chapter newsletter reports that the restorer, Dave Vana, said it was originally in the Florida panhandle at West Bay.[^3] The New York register says that tower was built with parts of the West Bay tower from Florida.[^4]

The New York tower stands in a children's playground at Grafton Lakes State Park, near the park's Welcome Center.[^3][^4] The register says the tower has had special accommodations for small children.[^4] For the Florida site, no source we found describes what, if anything, remains at West Bay, or whether the ground is open to the public.
