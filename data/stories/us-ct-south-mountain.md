A 1924 account titled The Work of a Forest Protective Association, quoted on the Forest Lookouts website, opens by saying Hartford County lacked thorough coverage from lookout stations.[^1] It goes on to say that the county's only lookout was a tower at the Bryce cottage, on South Mountain in Bristol.[^1] The account adds that the tower looked out over the county's western section, the area west of Talcott Mountain.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts website lists the tower as removed.[^1]

Today, no source we read describes the site. We cannot say whether anything remains on the mountain, and we found no information on public access. Please check with the Town of Bristol before going up South Mountain to look for the site.

[^1]: South Mountain, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/south-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
