Round Top Mountain, in Gila County, Arizona, has a tower on the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Arizona list, which marks it as gone.[^1] The list places the site at 33.93358 north and 110.01022 west.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts Arizona index places the site on the Fort Apache Indian Reservation.[^2] According to Wikipedia, the reservation lies partly in Navajo, Gila and Apache counties, and it is where the federally recognized White Mountain Apache Tribe lives.[^3]

A map database called TopoQuest still describes a Round Top Mountain Lookout as a tower at 7,155 feet above sea level.[^4] That entry gives no condition, and it does not match the gone status on the Forest Fire Lookout Association's list.[^1][^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.[^1][^2]
