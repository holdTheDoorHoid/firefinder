Becket Mountain, in Berkshire County, once carried a state fire lookout on its summit.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The sources do not agree on when it went up. Willhite's lookout page says a 30-foot steel tower was built in 1913, though it may have been 40 feet, and that a telephone line ran to it.[^3] Firetower.org says the 30-foot steel tower was first operated in 1915 and also had a telephone line.[^1] A 1916 State Forester annual report says the tower had been in use for the past three years, which points to the earlier date.[^2]

In 1916 the tower was taken down and moved to Lair Mountain in Tolland, where a 20-foot extension was added.[^2][^3] The 1916 report calls the new site Lair's Hill.[^2] Firetower.org says it was removed that same year.[^1] Willhite's page notes that some sources give a shorter run, from October 12, 1915 to the end of the 1916 season.[^3]

The summit kept some traces of the station. In 1939 a Massachusetts Geodetic Survey crew recorded old tower foundations there.[^3]

The Appalachian Trail runs over the summit today, and the climb north from the road covers slightly more than half a mile.[^4][^3] According to the trail guide, concrete footings still mark the spot.[^4] Willhite's page agrees the footings are visible today.[^3] Firetower.org reports that only the footings are left.[^1] A visit means walking to the summit; no structure stands there to climb.[^1]
