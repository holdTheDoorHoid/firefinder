The Lincoln County list places the Parsnip Mountain site in the Kootenai National Forest, and the association list gives its latitude as 48.66797 and its longitude as -115.41373.[^3][^2]

A record for 1914 describes a 40-foot pole observation tower with a log cabin.[^1] The 1929 entry lists a log cabin on the north point.[^1] The same page also gives two patrol points: South Fork, 4 miles west, and Garden Ridge, 2 miles southwest.[^1]

The page says the lookout was abandoned in 1935.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list now codes the site as a tower that is gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
