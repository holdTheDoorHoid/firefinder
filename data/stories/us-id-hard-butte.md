Hard Butte is in Idaho County, in the Payette National Forest.[^1] It lies about 12 miles east-southeast of Riggins.[^2]

The earliest date in these sources is 1917, when the site was a camp with an alidade platform.[^1] An L-4 cab went up on the north point around 1933.[^1][^2][^3] In August 1935 The Idaho Statesman reported, as quoted on one Idaho lookout site, that M. C. Lockwood had set out for the Hard Butte station.[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as abandoned, and both it and firelookout.com list the cab as ruins.[^2][^3]

The Forest Service trail page for the Hard Butte Lookout Trail, #508, was last updated March 10, 2026.[^4] It describes a 2.5-mile trail that reaches the lookout at about 8,660 feet.[^4] The north end of the trail branches off Elk Lake Trail #347, and the Forest Service says the trail can be difficult to locate.[^4] The directions run five miles northwest on Highway 55 to Goose Lake and Brundage Road, then four miles north past Hazard Campground to the end of the road.[^4] From there, follow Elk Lake Trail 2.7 miles west.[^4] The Forest Service lists the trail for day hikes and backpacking.[^4] A climbing guide also notes the remains of a fire lookout on the summit.[^5]
