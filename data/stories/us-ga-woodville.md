Woodville is a city in northeastern Greene County, on State Route 77, and had 264 residents at the 2020 census.[^1] The state legislature made it a city in 1911.[^1]

A Georgia lookout tower list places a tower at Woodville, at 33.64667, -83.09715, with an elevation of 738. The list gives no unit for elevation; read as feet, that is about 225 meters.[^2] Its last field reads "topo."[^2] The list's Google map was marked as revised on 22 March 2016.[^2]

A photograph on a map website carries the title "Woodville Fire Tower."[^3]

Statewide, the Georgia Forestry Commission began shifting from staffed towers to aerial patrols in the late 1980s, and many towers were scrapped or sold.[^4]

No source we found describes what, if anything, stands at the Woodville site today, or whether the public can reach it.
