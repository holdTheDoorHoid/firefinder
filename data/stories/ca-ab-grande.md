Grande is a steel fire lookout tower, 30 m (98 ft) tall, at an elevation of 559 m (1,834 ft), according to Alberta Wildfire's lookout layer.[^1] The layer dates the present structure to 2017.[^1] Steel was introduced for Alberta's lookout towers in the 1920s and gradually replaced wood.[^3]

Alberta staffs each of its lookout sites with an observer who keeps watch for wildfires, usually from April to October.[^2] The layer marks Grande as active and lists its access mode as "fly", which suggests the site is reached by air rather than by road.[^1]

No source I found says the public may visit Grande. Alberta describes its lookout sites as isolated working places, where staff have to be ready for things like a curious bear.[^2] Treat Grande as an active lookout, not a place to visit.
