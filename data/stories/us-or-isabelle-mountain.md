Mount Isabelle stands in Jackson County, about 11 miles west of Medford.[^1][^2] The register and firelookout.com both date the first lookout to 1933, when a 50-foot pole tower with a 12-by-12-foot cab went up.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page, which quotes old newspapers, puts construction later.[^3] A 1935 Medford Mail Tribune item says the lookout had been built late the previous fall, which points to late 1934.[^3] The sources disagree on the year.[^1][^2][^3]

In April 1934 the Forest Service had finished surveying a road to the summit, where a fire lookout might be built.[^3] The lookout was kept for emergencies.[^3] The Medford paper reported it closed temporarily in August 1936, and in 1937 it stood unoccupied, reserved for emergencies.[^3] In 1939 the state forest service took it over.[^3] Harry Wickman of Medford went on duty there on June 26, 1940.[^3] The Medford paper's July 1, 1940 item reports that the state forest service had completed a telephone line from Rogue River to the lookout.[^3]

The register says the cab was replaced with an L-4 in 1959.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page records complete remodeling that year.[^3] The register says the L-4 cab was removed in 1967, but firelookout.com and the Forest Lookouts page both say the lookout was destroyed that year.[^1][^2][^3]

A Peakbagging page, which carries a photo dated August 2007, says the summit now has radio gear and no sign of a lookout.[^4] It also places the peak on BLM land, while the register lists the Oregon Department of Forestry as administrator.[^1][^4] The sources disagree on who manages the land.[^1][^4] No source we read says whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^4]
