Hardscrabble Mountain, also called Hardscrabble Ridge, is in the Ochoco National Forest of Grant County, Oregon.[^2]

A lookout went up there in 1939.[^1][^3] The register and Rex's page describe a 40-foot treated timber L-4 tower.[^1][^3] A 1939 Ochoco history entry, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, instead names a 50-foot tower and house as that year's major projects, so the sources disagree on height.[^2] A May 1939 memo also mentions a one-car garage and woodshed for the lookout.[^2]

In October 1950 burglars broke into the station and took two lanterns. Rangers were also asked to look in on other lookouts in case of similar break-ins.[^2]

The register says the tower was removed in 1968.[^1] Rex's page says it was destroyed that year.[^3] The Oregon index lists the site as a gone tower.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
