Cross Plains is a former tower site in Robertson County, Tennessee, at about 807 feet (246 m).[^1][^2] The state's survey numbers it 6-5, from the Division of Forestry's 1976 list of towers, and a Forest Lookouts list places the site in the Division's District 6.[^1][^2][^4] The Division was responsible for building 208 fire lookout towers between about 1930 and 1970, and many were built in the 1930s through a partnership with the Civilian Conservation Corps.[^3] Many of the network's towers were later removed or left awaiting demolition after disuse or the end of a lease on private land.[^3] The survey marks the Cross Plains tower as removed.[^1][^2]

The sources used here do not describe what, if anything, remains on the site today. Most former tower locations in Tennessee are now private property, according to the survey, so check who owns the land before visiting.[^1]
