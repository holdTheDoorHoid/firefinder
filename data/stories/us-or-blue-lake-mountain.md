Blue Lake Mountain stood in the Coast Range, about 24 miles northeast of Tillamook, Oregon.[^2][^3] Crown Zellerbach Company built the lookout in 1955.[^1][^3] It was a 40-foot wooden tower with a live-in cab.[^1][^2][^3]

The Northwest Fire Patrol association ran the lookout under the company's supervision.[^3] In October 1957 the company reported that the season had closed.[^3] The tower had been staffed by Mr. and Mrs. Oscar George of Vernonia, and the company said it would be manned again if the weather changed and unseasonably dry conditions set in.[^3]

The lookout also served as the coordinating station for the company's weather reporting system, which covered all of the company's lookouts in the Lower Columbia River region.[^3]

The lookout was abandoned in 1970.[^2][^3] The register says the tower was gone that year.[^1] The register and firelookout.com also give slightly different elevations, 3,493 feet and 3,506 feet.[^1][^2] The register spells the company name "Zellerback," where the 1957 report and firelookout.com use "Zellerbach."[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list also marks the site as gone.[^4]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, is left at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
