The Lampson Lookout Tower stands in Washburn County, Wisconsin.[^1][^2] The Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list shows it as standing and gives no register number for it.[^2]

Our main lead on the tower is a survey record. The Forest Lookouts page quotes a National Geodetic Survey data sheet with the designation Lampson Lookout and the identifier QN0583, and gives the USGS quadrangle as Trego.[^1] The data sheet does not say who built the tower or who runs it, so we have not named an agency.

We could not find when the tower was built, how tall it is, or what its cab looks like. We also found no account of staffing, fires or later changes in the sources we could read.

The sources do not describe its present condition or ownership. Because its access is unknown, do not assume the tower is open to visitors. Ask before going near it, and do not climb a tower that is closed or private.

[^1]: Lampson, Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/lampson.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Wisconsin lookout structures list, Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
