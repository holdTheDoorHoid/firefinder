The Tennessee lookout list places a tower called Hermitage Springs in Clay County, at 998 feet of elevation. The list says its elevations are based on USGS 10-meter elevation data.[^1]

The list numbers it 4-10, meaning district 4, tower 10, from a 1976 list of state forest towers. The Division of Forestry had about 208 tower sites around that time.[^1]

The list marks the tower as moved. It does not say where the tower went or where it stands now.[^1]

Today no source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the original site, or whether the public can reach it. The list suggests that Google aerial imagery or Street View may show whether a tower is still standing.[^1] It also says most of the sites are now privately owned, and it warns against climbing the towers.[^1]

[^1]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
