East Robeson is a fire tower in Robeson County, North Carolina.[^1][^2] A North Carolina tower list on tnlandforms.us gives its coordinates and county.[^2] The Forest Lookouts listing pairs it with the state's Division of Forest Resources and the East Howellsville Volunteer Fire Department.[^1]

The Civilian Conservation Corps erected the tower in February 1935.[^1] It was a 99-foot, 9-inch (30 m) steel tower with a two-room ground cabin, and it cost $1,714.68, funded through the Emergency Conservation Works program.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page takes those figures from the Department of Conservation and Development's Sixth Biennial Report, which covered 1934 to 1936.[^1]

A 1993 act says the land under the tower, about 0.92 acre, is the property described in a March 7, 1935 deed to the East Howellsville Volunteer Fire Department.[^1] The same act directed the Division of Forest Resources to transfer the tower and that land to the fire department as a gift.[^1] The act places the tower about eight miles east of Lumberton, on Highway 41.[^1]

By January 2014 the tower and its land were being sold at a government surplus auction, with a closing date of January 28, 2014.[^1] Including fees, the winning bid came to $8,922.50.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
