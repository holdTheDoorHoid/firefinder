Bald Peter Mountain sits in Linn County, Oregon, about 11 miles northeast of Sweet Home, at 4,079 feet.[^2][^4] Forest Lookouts files the station under the Linn County Fire Patrol, but our sources do not agree on when the lookout went up.[^3]

The Osbornes Project says the lookout was established in 1912.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page quotes an August 4, 1912 Morning Oregonian item that counts Bald Peter as one of three lookouts north of Foster.[^3] Two other pages give 1937 as the build year.[^2][^4] Photographs by Robert Cooper and Lester Moe are dated August 1, 1933, which is earlier than that 1937 date.[^1][^3] We could not settle which date is right, so this story reports both.

The only note about people on the site comes from Forest Lookouts, which says the Green Peter fire patrolman used it as a patrol post.[^3] We found no names, dates or fires tied to the lookout.

The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register added the site on April 24, 2011, under US 940 and OR 298.[^2] Forest Lookouts marks the station "Removed" but gives no date.[^3] The register and firelookout.com both describe the tower as gone.[^2][^4]

No source we read says what, if anything, still stands on the peak, and we found nothing on public access. The register lists the Linn FPA as administrator, so ask that agency before you plan a visit.[^2]
