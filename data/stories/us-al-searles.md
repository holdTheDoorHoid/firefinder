Searles Lookout stood in Tuscaloosa County, Alabama, at an elevation of 735 feet.[^1][^2] Two registers cover it, the National Historic Lookout Register and the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register. Each page names the other as a sister site, so they are not fully separate checks on each other.[^1][^2]

Neither register gives a build year, and I found no source that says when the tower went up.[^1][^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register describes a 100-foot steel tower, now in poor shape and out of use.[^1] By April 2016, the landowner wanted it removed.[^1] The register names the Alabama Forestry Commission as its administrator.[^1] It lists the registration date as May 27, 2016.[^1]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register later listed the site, with a registration date of June 21, 2022.[^2] It also records removal by October 2016.[^2]

Both registers record the tower as gone, so there is no standing lookout to visit at this site.[^1][^2]

[^2]: Searles Lookout Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/al/searles-lookout-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
