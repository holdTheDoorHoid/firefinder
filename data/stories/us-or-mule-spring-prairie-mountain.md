A cab was built on Mule (Spring Prairie) Mountain in 1930, at about 5,550 feet in Lane County, in the Willamette National Forest.[^1][^3]

The cab was moved in 1953, about a mile southeast to 5,470 feet.[^1][^3] Another page dates a new timber tower with an L-4 cab, built a mile southeast of the first lookout, to 1952.[^2] That page gives the height as 40 feet in one quote and 41 feet in another.[^2] The register adds a caution: USGS figures point to a possible 52-foot tower.[^1] That figure does not match the 40- and 41-foot heights.[^1][^2]

The register says the lookout was abandoned in 1968 and is now gone.[^1] The Oregon Lookouts page is more specific: it records the lookout as burned on August 22, 1968.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
