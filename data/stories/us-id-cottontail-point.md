The Cottontail Point lookout stood in Idaho County on the Payette National Forest, about nine miles north-northwest of Warren, at an elevation of 7,665 feet.[^1]

Two lookout pages describe a 1934 lookout with a 72-foot steel tower and a log cabin, set atop the north end of a ridge.[^1][^2] The county list gives the same 1934 date and 72-foot steel tower, and places the site at location code 24N-6E-32.[^3]

Those pages mark the lookout as gone in 1974, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list also shows the tower as gone.[^1][^2][^4] One page files the site under "Drive-Up Gone," a label that does not say what stands there now.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains on the ridge today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
