Black Mountain, also identified as Black Hill on some forest maps, is a summit in the Cleveland National Forest in San Diego County, southwest of Lake Henshaw.[^1][^2][^3] The FFLA-SDRC page places it on the western edge of the Mesa Grande Indian Reservation.[^2]

The tower was built in 1935. It was a 20-foot open timber design with a 14-by-14-foot live-in wood cab.[^1][^2] Staffing ended in 1975.[^1]

Sources disagree about its end. The register dates its demolition to 1983.[^1] The FFLA-SDRC page has it standing in 1979 and gone by 1986.[^2]

According to the register, a radio repeater site is all that occupies the location now.[^1] Neither page describes public access, so check with the Forest Service before going.[^1][^2] The elevation also differs between sources. The register lists 3,168 feet, while Wikipedia gives the summit as 4,048 feet.[^1][^3]
