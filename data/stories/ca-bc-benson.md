Mount Benson lies on Vancouver Island, 9 km (5.6 mi) west of Nanaimo.[^1] At one time it had a fire lookout on its summit, and a road ran up to the peak.[^1] The Canada Lookouts index lists a Benson lookout at an elevation of 1021 m (3,350 ft), while Wikipedia gives the mountain as 1,023 m (3,356 ft).[^2][^1]

Vehicles can no longer use the road, and the mountain now draws local hikers.[^1] In July 2008 the land was set aside as Mount Benson Regional Park, comprising 212 ha (520 acres) on the upper north-east slope.[^1] The park land had been acquired in 2005 by the Regional District of Nanaimo and the Nanaimo and Area Land Trust.[^1]

There is no road access to the park itself, because all of the old access roads have been blocked off. Visitors start from a parking lot on Benson View Road, by Witchcraft Lake.[^1]

In August 2026 Mosaic Forest Management set up two 360-degree cameras atop Mount Benson.[^3][^4] Their artificial intelligence software watches for wildfire smoke day and night, and staff who receive its alerts can pass them on to the Coastal Fire Centre if needed.[^3] As it scans, each camera takes in roughly 50 kilometres in every direction.[^3]

No source I could read says whether the old summit lookout still stands. The Wave describes the cameras as fixed to a metal tower that also carries other communications equipment, but it does not say whether that tower is the old lookout.[^3]
