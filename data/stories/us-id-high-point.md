High Point is a former lookout site on the western edge of the Island Park caldera, in eastern Idaho.[^1] The register spells the forest Caribou-Targee, while firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts use the Targhee spelling.[^1][^2][^3] The site lies about 22 miles north-northeast of Saint Anthony.[^2] The register gives its elevation as 7,248 feet, while firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts give 7,281 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

The first structure went up in 1922.[^1][^2][^3] The register describes a 20-foot tower with a small observation cabin, plus a ground cabin for a residence.[^1] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both call the first structure a pole platform tower.[^2][^3] A photo caption on the register gives 1924 for the original structure and says it was built around 1922.[^1] A frame cabin replaced the tower in 1934.[^1][^2][^3]

A September 3, 1937 Post-Register item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts Idaho page, says Herman Blazer was placed in charge of the High Point station.[^4] The same item says that, because fires had been increasing on the forest, a new station was set up on Highpoint that year. That does not match the 1922 date, and the sources do not explain the difference.[^1][^4] The item also reports two fires put out the week before, one of them a small fire near Hatchery Butte.[^4] It does not say which station spotted them.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists no documented staffing history, so Blazer is the only named staff member in the sources.[^3][^4]

The structure was destroyed in 1966, and the register also gives 1966 as the removal year.[^1][^2][^3] The register recorded the site on August 12, 2021, on a nomination by Brad Eells.[^1]

The register reports that wood footings and concrete debris are still at the site.[^1] Its photo captions date the footings to 2021 and credit them to Samsara Duffey.[^1] No source describes a standing tower or cabin. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as drive-up.[^3] None of the sources describes a closure or permit requirement.
