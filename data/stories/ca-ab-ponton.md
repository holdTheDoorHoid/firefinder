Alberta counts 127 wildfire lookout sites, spread across the boreal forest and along the Rocky Mountains.[^1] The province describes lookouts as a key part of detecting wildfires, sited where they have a good view.[^1]

Ponton is one of them.[^2] The provincial record lists a steel tower there, built in 2014 and 30 m (98 ft) tall.[^2] It gives the site an elevation of 954 m (3,130 ft) and marks the tower's location as verified.[^2]

The record marks the tower as active, with a weather station, and gives its access mode as fly.[^2] A regional archive says the public is no longer permitted to climb Alberta's lookout towers.[^3] No source we read says the site is open to visitors, and none describes the site today beyond that record.
