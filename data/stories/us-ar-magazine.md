Magazine Mountain is the highest point in Arkansas, in Logan County.[^1][^2] By 1940 a U.S. Forest Service lookout tower stood near the west end of the mountain, and a federal survey station was set directly beneath it.[^2][^3]

In 1938 the mountain was placed in the Ouachita National Forest, and in 1941 it came under the Ozark National Forest instead.[^4] Working with the Forest Service, the mountain opened as a state park in 1998.[^4]

A 1959 survey recovery found the station marks in good condition. Its note describes a microwave tower at the west end of the mountain and does not mention the lookout.[^3]

The state park page places the mountain within the Ozark-St. Francis National Forests and lists hiking trails and a visitor center among its features.[^5]

No source we read says whether the Forest Service lookout tower still stands.
