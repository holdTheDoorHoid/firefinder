Sheep Mountain in Lemhi County rises to 10,865 feet, about five miles south of Gilmore, in the Challis National Forest.[^1][^2] In the 1930s a lookout cab of the L-4 type was set just below the summit and fitted with an alidade.[^1][^2][^3] The firelookout.com page notes the cab was still there in 1983, then records the site as abandoned and gone.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts also lists it as abandoned and gone, and as a hike-in site.[^2]

Today a climbing guide to Sheep Mountain (10,865 feet) says a driver with a four-wheel-drive vehicle and solid driving skills can reach about 9,800 feet on the south ridge. The summit, the guide adds, lies roughly a mile and a half from the road.[^4]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout today.
