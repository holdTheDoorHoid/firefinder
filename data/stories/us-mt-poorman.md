The Poorman lookout was an L-4 cab built in 1931 in the Kootenai National Forest, eight miles north-northeast of Eureka in Lincoln County, Montana.[^1] The firelookout.com page gives its elevation as 7,832 feet.[^1] A Lincoln County list of Forest lookouts also uses the name Poorman Mountain.[^3] Working with the U.S. Forest Service, the Forest Fire Lookout Association completed a national inventory that found more than 8,000 lookouts in 49 states at one time.[^4]

According to the page, the cabin was manned until 1942 and was destroyed in 1977. The page gives no cause.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list files it under Ground at 48.97609, -114.92879 and marks it gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, so visitors should not expect a cabin to be standing.
