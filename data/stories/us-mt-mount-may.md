Mount May sits in the Flathead National Forest, about 24 miles east-northeast of the Spotted Bear Ranger Station.[^1] The site gives its elevation as 6,919 feet.[^1] It dates the lookout to 1937 and describes it as a 20-foot pole L-4 tower.[^1]

After the 1910 fire season, the Forest Service began placing fire-watch stations on high summits.[^3] The Forest kept putting up more lookouts, the 1930s especially, and by 1939 it counted 147 lookouts and towers.[^3]

The site says the lookout was abandoned in 1949 and is now gone.[^1] The Forest Association's Montana list also marks Mount May as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public. Anyone curious should ask the Flathead National Forest about access before going up the mountain.
