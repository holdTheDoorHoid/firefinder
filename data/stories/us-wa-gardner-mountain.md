Gardner Mountain, in the Okanogan National Forest about 14 miles west of Winthrop, once carried a fire lookout.[^1][^2] The register describes a pole platform tower and a cabin, built in the 1930s.[^1] It gives no more exact year, and the register and FireLookout.com both list the tower and cabin as gone.[^1][^2]

Willhite has no other information than a 1930s pole platform tower, and says a cabin is possible.[^3] He also notes that the site appears in DNR records, though no further details are known.[^3] He adds that the 1950 and 1962 USGS parties did not mention a lookout.[^3] The register states that a cabin stood there, which Willhite only treats as a possibility.[^1][^3]

Today the mountain draws climbers. Willhite says most visitors spend a weekend climbing both peaks, and he calls the Gardner peaks the highest point in the Lake Chelan-Sawtooth Wilderness and Okanogan County.[^3] He describes 10 miles up the Wolf Creek Trail to Gardner Meadows, then an off-trail climb, and gives the distance as 12 to 14 miles.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site.
