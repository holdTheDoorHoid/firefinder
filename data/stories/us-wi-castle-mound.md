The Forest Fire Lookout Association's inventory of Wisconsin lookouts lists Castle Mound in Jackson County with the type "Ground" rather than "Tower", and marks it as gone.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Wisconsin Lookouts (Alphabetical List), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
