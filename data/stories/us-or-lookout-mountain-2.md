The Lookout Mountain lookout is listed under the Oregon Department of Forestry's Clackamas-Marion unit.[^1] The site sits about 20 miles southeast of Silverton, in Marion County.[^2]

A tower went up on the peak in 1938.[^1] The Forest Lookouts history describes an 82-foot timber tower with an L-6 cab.[^1] A 1939 district report on the same page gives 100 feet, and firelookout.com describes a 34-foot steel tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab built in 1938.[^1][^2] The sources do not agree on what was built, so treat the height and design here as uncertain.

A 1938 Forest Log item names Chas. Pierce as the lookout on Lookout Mountain.[^1] In 1940, crews from the Mill City CCC camp put up a two-room cabin and a combined wood shed and garage on the mountain.[^1]

A 1962 district report says the Columbus Day Hurricane completely destroyed the tower.[^1] In 1965 a 30-foot steel tower went up with a portable cab.[^1] Vandalism was a constant problem, so the cab was taken apart into sections and removed during each off-season.[^1] firelookout.com instead says the 1965 replacement was a 40-foot crank-up tower with travel trailer living quarters, and that the superstructure is still there.[^2]

The sources disagree on whether anything stands today. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list shows a standing tower.[^3] The Forest Lookouts page has a bare "Removed" note under its 1965 entry, and it does not say whether that means the cab or the whole site.[^1] firelookout.com's entry is headed "R.I.P. 1938-?"[^2] We found no current access information. Ask the Oregon Department of Forestry before visiting, and do not climb any remaining structure.
