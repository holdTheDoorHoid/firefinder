The Big Meadows Lookout Tower stands in Beltrami County, and the Minnesota lookout list marks it as standing.[^2] A National Geodetic Survey station description written in 1960 describes a steel tower on four legs, with an observation room of metal and glass at its top and an overall height of 110 feet.[^1] The same description places the tower on the south edge of the Red Lake Indian Reservation, and the Forest Lookouts page names the Red Lake Indian Agency as the agency.[^1]

We did not find when the tower was built, who built it, who staffed it, or whether it was ever part of a fire. The 1960 survey is the only dated record we found for the tower.[^1]

The tower sits on reservation land,[^1] so we read access as the reservation's call. We found no public access notice. Do not climb it unless the reservation has opened it to visitors.

[^1]: Big Meadows Lookout Tower, National Geodetic Survey station description, Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/big-meadows.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Minnesota fire lookouts list, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
