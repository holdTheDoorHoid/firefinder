The Lem lookout stood inside the Clemons Tree Farm, south of Montesano in Grays Harbor County.[^3] Weyerhaeuser Timber Company owned the farm, which is named for local logger Charles Clemons.[^3][^4] The company dedicated the tree farm in 1941.[^4]

WillhiteWeb says the lookout was likely built in 1940 or within a few years before, and that it was one of four lookouts set aside for fire prevention and experimentation.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes the tower as built in the 1940s.[^1]

Sources agree the lookout is gone.[^1][^2] A 2016 visit described on WillhiteWeb found two footings nearly 20 feet apart, partly buried by moss and pine needles.[^3] The author believed the tower had been removed rather than burned in place.[^3] A 2022 update on the same page says the summit was logged and that four footings were placed about 20 feet downhill, off the east side of the summit.[^3] The footings are the only remains the sources describe.

The site is on private tree-farm land. WillhiteWeb lists it under a Weyerhaeuser general access permit, with separate distances for vehicle and non-motorized permit holders.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the land as private.[^1] Anyone thinking of a visit should check the permit rules first.
