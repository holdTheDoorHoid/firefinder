A survey of Georgia fire towers gives the Bethel site in Clinch County the code GA167, at 30.943 and -82.66336.[^1] The survey lists towers 1 through 72 from the USGS name database, and it says the sites were identified on 1:100,000-scale USGS maps.[^1] For towers numbered 73 and above, the survey says the names are its author's own, so the name Bethel comes from the survey.[^1] Its table has a column for Georgia Forestry Commission numbers, and the Bethel row carries the number 823.[^1] The survey also credits Gary Weber and Ron Stafford with supplying many of the tower positions.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts index of Georgia towers also lists a Bethel in Clinch County.[^2]

No source I found describes the site today, so the public record does not say whether a tower stands there or whether the spot is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Georgia - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/georgia.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
