Charles Butte stands on the St. Joe National Forest in Benewah County, Idaho, about 16 miles south-southwest of St. Maries, at an elevation of 4,471 feet.[^1][^2]

The lookout was built in 1940 as a 30-foot pole platform with cabin living quarters.[^1][^3] The Forest Lookouts site gives the same year and height.[^3]

It was abandoned in 1955.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com reports that the cabin survives, in ruins.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts also describes the site as abandoned, with ruins.[^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in, so it is reached on foot.[^2] The pages we read do not say whether the pole platform still stands.[^1][^2][^3] We found no current access information, so check with the Forest Service before planning a trip.
