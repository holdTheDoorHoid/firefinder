The Sandy Mountain Lookout Tower is in Calhoun County, Mississippi.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register gives its elevation as 422 feet (129 meters).[^1] It lists a 100-foot Aermotor with stairs set on a diagonal.[^1]

The register lists the build date as unknown and names no builder.[^1] It does not say which fires the tower watched, or who staffed it.[^1]

The register recorded the tower on July 21, 2022, as US 1687, MS 47, on the nomination of Jason Johns.[^1] The Mississippi Forestry Commission administers the site.[^1] The only site visit the register records is dated 2/5/22.[^1]

The sources we read do not say whether the tower still stands, or whether it has been moved, burned or taken down.[^1] The register gives no access information, so it does not show whether the public may visit.[^1] The Mississippi Forestry Commission, which administers the site, would be the agency to ask.[^1]

[^1]: Sandy Mountain Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ms/sandy-mountain-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
