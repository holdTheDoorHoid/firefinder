A lookout tower called Hawthorne Trail is listed in Grady County, Georgia, in a lookout-tower list on tnlandforms.us, with an elevation of 349 feet and Georgia Forestry Commission number 222.[^1]

The tower takes its name from the Hawthorne Trail.[^1][^2] A historical-marker page says the name refers to an old trail that William Leonard Hawthorne marked out when he moved his family from Lumpkin County to Grady County, then Early County.[^2]

In September 2006 the Forestry Commission sold the tower at auction for $110.00, with removal required within 30 days.[^3]

Neither the National Historic Lookout Register nor the Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it. The first counts 29 Georgia lookouts, and the second lists two Georgia sites.[^4][^5]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^3]
