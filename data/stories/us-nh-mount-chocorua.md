The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Mount Chocorua, in Carroll County, as a "Map Table" site rather than a tower, and marks it gone.[^1]

A NH Family Hikes list places Mount Chocorua in Albany, at 3,500 feet, among the mountains that once had a fire tower.[^2] The two listings disagree about whether a tower stood on the summit.[^1][^2]

A 1922 note on the Forest Lookouts site says service to the mountain improved when the line was moved from the Liberty trail to the Bee Line trail.[^3] The note is credited to the Forestry Commission's 1921-22 biennial report, and it does not give a build or closing date.[^3] The same page lists the entry as "Removed" and gives no other description of the site.[^3]

We found no source that describes what remains on the summit today or whether the summit is open to visitors.[^3]
