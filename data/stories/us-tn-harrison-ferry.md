Harrison Ferry was a lookout in Warren County, Tennessee. The Tennessee lookout list places it at 1,946 feet (593 m) and marks the tower as removed.[^1]

The list gives the tower the number 5-2. The page says the numbers in its table are district-and-tower state forest numbers, taken from a 1976 list.[^1]

Many listed towers are gone, and most of their sites are now privately owned.[^1] No source I found describes what remains at the Harrison Ferry site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Tennessee lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
