The Cataract fire tower went up in 1949 in Owen County, Indiana, on a 13-acre tract that is now part of Owen-Putnam State Forest.[^1] The land was bought on October 24, 1948, for $391, and the footers were dug that year.[^1]

A November 1949 newspaper item called it Indiana's 24th fire tower. It said the tower would guard the Cagles Mill flood control project and roughly 100,000 acres of Owen County.[^2]

The tower is no longer standing.[^1] The Forest Service's list of Indiana fire towers, under an Indiana Department of Natural Resources heading, names it the Owen Cataract Tower near Cloverdale.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's inventory also marks it gone.[^4]

When the Indiana Fire Towers author visited in April 2024, all four concrete bases were still on the site.[^1] The 13-acre parcel is state forest land along Cunot-Cataract Road and near Ponderosa Road, close to Upper Cataract Falls.[^1] The state's forest recreation guide lists hiking and bridle trails, hunting and fishing across Owen-Putnam State Forest.[^5] No source I could read describes how visitors reach the old footings.[^1]
