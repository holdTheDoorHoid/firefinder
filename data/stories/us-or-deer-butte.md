The Deer Butte lookout sits in Lane County, in the Willamette National Forest, about eight miles southwest of McKenzie Pass, at an elevation of 5,298 feet.[^2]

A lookout was in use there by 1911.[^1] A *Eugene Guard* report from that August, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, says Archie Shough saw a fire in the Santiam reserve from the Deer Butte lookout, and Percy Collier saw it from Castle Rock. Their sightings pinned down where the fire was.[^1]

Firelookout.com dates its crow's nest to the 1920s instead.[^2] The two dates do not agree, so we report both.[^1][^2]

In August 1915, the *Morning Register* reported that Roy Parks was stationed at Deer Butte.[^1] In September 1918, the *Monmouth Herald* named a Miss Pooler as in charge of the lookout, about 20 miles from McKenzie Bridge. She was a high school student from Cottage Grove, and her 10-year-old brother was with her.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page lists panorama photos taken in September 1933 by Robert Cooper, Reino Sarlin and Albert Arnst.[^1]

Firelookout.com says the lookout was abandoned in the 1930s, and the Oregon Lookouts index lists its crow's nest as abandoned too.[^2][^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, and we found nothing on whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3]
