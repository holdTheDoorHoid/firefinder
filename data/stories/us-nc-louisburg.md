The Forest Lookouts directory places the Louisburg fire tower in Franklin County, North Carolina, and lists the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources as its agency.[^1]

The one dated entry concerns a state surplus auction. The directory dates it to March 22, 2018, the day bidding opened.[^1] The same entry places the tower on the Franklin County headquarters property, which the North Carolina Forest Service owns.[^1] The entry does not say whether the tower was part of that auction, or what became of it.

Today no source describes what, if anything, remains on the site, or whether visitors may go there. The North Carolina Forest Service's Franklin County office is at 2177 Fire Tower Road in Louisburg, which is a sensible place to ask. That address does not show that a tower still stands on the grounds.[^2]

[^1]: Louisburg, Forest Lookouts (easternuslookouts.weebly.com), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/louisburg.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: N.C. Forest Service Contacts - Franklin County, NC Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services, https://www.ncagr.gov/divisions/nc-forest-service/contacts/franklin (accessed 2026-10-21).
