Middle Inlet Lookout was a 100-foot Wisconsin standard tower in Marinette County.[^1] It had a protected ladder on the outside, and the register gives the site an elevation of 795 feet, or 242 meters.[^1] The Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources administered it, and the register says it was built in October 1939.[^1]

A 1954 federal survey description of a benchmark at the site refers to a Middle Inlet tower with the year 1935 attached, which would be earlier than the register's date. The sources we read do not explain the difference.[^2]

The Wisconsin DNR decommissioned its fire watch towers across the state in 2015.[^2] A news report from February 2016, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, said that, as a result of the DNR decision, Marinette County had become liable for the two unmanned towers in the county, the ones near Cedarville and Middle Inlet.[^2] The county's forestry and parks committee approved a plan to remove the towers, and the DNR was willing to pay for the work.[^2]

Statewide, the DNR retired 72 towers permanently.[^4] The report says the towers are no longer structurally safe for personnel, that replacing them is prohibitively expensive, and that all 72 will be closed in the spring.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list now marks the Middle Inlet tower as gone, and we found no source describing what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^3]
