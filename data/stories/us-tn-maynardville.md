The Maynardville Fire Tower stands on Hinds Ridge in Union County, Tennessee.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as a 100-foot tower, with an elevation of 1,716 feet (523 meters), and says it overlooks Maynardville and Norris Lake.[^1]

We found no build date or model name for it in the sources we read.

The Register says the Tennessee Division of Forestry administers the tower.[^1] A Forest Lookouts page files it under the Union and Grainger County listing, within the Division's District 2.[^3]

A list of towers still standing as of March 26, 2014, printed in the National Register study, includes Maynardville in Union County.[^2] Tennessee Landforms also marks it as standing and numbers it 2-11, which means district 2, tower 11.[^4] The Register lists its registration date as September 28, 2015, with Christopher Covert as nominator.[^1]

The access accounts we found conflict. A visitor site says the tower is owned by the Department of Forestry and that its top cab is locked.[^5] It also describes a climb of roughly 100 steep stairs, and one reviewer on the same page reports that the lowest set has been taken out.[^5] We found no official notice about visits. Please do not climb this tower, and check with the Tennessee Division of Forestry before any visit.
