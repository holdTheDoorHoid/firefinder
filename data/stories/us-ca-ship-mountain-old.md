The earliest item on the Forest Lookouts page is from July 1914, when a guard was preparing to take up a station on Ship Mountain.[^1] The register says a lookout may have been there as early as 1914.[^2] A telephone line reached the lookout in June 1915.[^1]

Around 1922 a timber tower with a Region 5 Plan 4AR cab went up.[^2] A 1939 forest history says a standard cabin design was hauled 26 miles in and put up in 1922.[^1] Survey notes from 1941 and 1952 describe a two-story wooden lookout house on the peak.[^1]

The Blue Lake Advocate named Al Gastman as lookout in August 1922 and Milton Kingsley as his successor in June 1924.[^1] In September 1924 a lightning storm set many fires across the Siskiyou and Klamath forests, and one lookout on Ship Mountain reported lightning in 2,648 places in his territory that night.[^1]

During the Second World War the peak served as Aircraft Warning Service Station 'Mike 8-1'.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page notes that AWS added sleeping quarters to the site, and that the station was placed on inactive status on October 17, 1943.[^1]

The timber tower was later replaced by a portable trailer lookout.[^2] The register dates that change to the 1960s, while a Forest Fire Lookout Association report on 2008 work says the trailer had been used at the site since 1973.[^2][^3] The trailer was removed in 2007 and the road to the top was improved.[^1] A new two-story station went up in 2008.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list marks the older spot as "Ship Mountain (old)", a ground site that is gone.[^4] The register's coordinates for the lookout match that old spot, but the list places the standing two-story cab about 0.9 km south-southwest of it.[^2][^4] A note on the Coast and Geodetic Survey datasheet, quoted by Forest Lookouts, says the current house stands on peak 4, the southernmost of the four.[^1] The 1941 station sat on peak 3.[^1] I found no source describing remains at the old spot, or saying whether the peak is open to visitors. The register places the site in the Six Rivers National Forest and lists the US Forest Service as administrator.[^2]
