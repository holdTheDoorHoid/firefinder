A fire lookout once stood on Mount Reuben, about eight miles due west of Glendale in Josephine County, Oregon.[^3][^1] The summit lies roughly 24 miles north-northwest of Grants Pass.[^2]

In 1931 the Siskiyou National Forest began establishing a fire patrol lookout station on the summit.[^3] The Forest Lookouts history says a 30-foot tower with an R-1 style cab went up that year, and that the station was established on the summit by January 1932.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives the 30-foot tower with a 14-by-14-foot cab a build year of 1932, so the sources differ by a year.[^1][^2]

Dick Bland of Merlin was named lookout for the first season, and a newspaper item that August called him the fire lookout at Mount Reuben.[^3] A May 1940 plan notes early-June starts in two of three years, with the point staffed until late September in all three.[^3]

The register says the tower was replaced in 1950 by a 30-foot treated timber tower. The Forest Lookouts history dates the new lookout to 1951.[^1][^2][^3] Building maintenance notes from 1962 and 1964 show the building was still being kept up.[^3] The register says the replacement was removed in 1967, while firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2]

The Oregon Department of Forestry is now listed as the administering agency.[^1][^3] The Forest Lookouts index lists the tower as gone.[^4] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether visitors may go there.[^1][^2][^3]
