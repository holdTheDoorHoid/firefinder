The Ralph W. MacAllester Fire Tower stands on Snow Hill in Dover, Massachusetts, on land the Register lists as the Dover Land Trust Reserve.[^1][^3] The Register says fire detection has been carried out on the site since 1939.[^1] The tower began as Dover Tower and was named for Ralph W. MacAllester in 1993.[^1]

An earlier tower came first. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes a 45-foot wooden tower, first operated in 1938 on private land under an agreement, and says it was deemed unsafe in 1946 and destroyed in 1947.[^2] The Register puts the present steel tower at 60 feet, built in 1953.[^1] The sources read do not reconcile the 1938 start date with the Register's 1939 date.

A trail guide lists the site as Dover (Snow Hill) and tells visitors to heed posted signs, respect locked gates, and avoid climbing any fire tower that may be unsafe.[^3] The sources read do not say who staffs the tower now, or whether this tower is open to climbing. Check with the landowner and the state before visiting.
