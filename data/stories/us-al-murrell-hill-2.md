A Murrell Hill Lookout Tower is listed in Russell County, Alabama, on the Omaha topographic map.[^1] TopoZone gives it as 32.2132066 north, 85.0788214 west, with an approximate elevation of 509 feet (155 meters).[^1]

OpenStreetMap has a point with the same name. A fixme tag on that point asks, "Is this still there?"[^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists a Murrell Hill site in the same county, at about 32.345 north, 85.245 west. The register entry found no sign of a tower there, which it attributes to a water tank on the grounds.[^3] We cannot tell from these sources whether the two points mark one tower or two.

We found no source that gives a build date, a design, staffing, or the tower's later history. No source describes the site today or whether the public can visit it, and nothing we found shows whether the tower still stands.
