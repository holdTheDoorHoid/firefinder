Red Mountain is a fire lookout site in Glenn County, California.[^1][^4] The Forest Lookouts page for the county lists Red Mountain by name.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association index describes its structure as an enclosed tower and lists it as gone.[^2] The index gives the site's coordinates as 39.74256, -122.60944.[^2] OpenStreetMap maps a fire lookout building at the site.[^3]

No source we read describes the site today, including whether any part of the tower remains or whether visitors may go there.
