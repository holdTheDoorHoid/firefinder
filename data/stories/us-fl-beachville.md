Beachville, an unincorporated community in Suwannee County, Florida, lies on State Road 247 about five miles northeast of Branford.[^1] Wikipedia places the Beachville Fire Tower in the community and cites the U.S. Geological Survey's place-name database for that entry.[^1]

A listing of eastern U.S. forest lookouts places the tower in Suwannee County.[^2] Map records tag it as a lattice observation tower.[^3]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 31 Florida lookouts, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists six former sites. Neither has an entry for Beachville.[^4][^5]

No source describes the site today, so whether the tower still stands and whether the public can reach it are unknown. A U.S. Geological Survey monitoring record carries the name Beachville Fire Tower, Adc., for an atmosphere site at the mapped spot, but the record says nothing about the tower itself.[^6]
