Buzzville Lookout is a standing tower in Mason County, Illinois, inside the Chautauqua National Wildlife Refuge.[^1][^2][^4] The refuge lies on the Illinois River northeast of Havana.[^3]

The National Historic Lookout Register added the tower on August 8, 2026, as US 1843, IL 4.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as standing.[^4]

The sources read do not give a build date, a height, or who built the tower. The register's description covers the refuge, not the tower, so the refuge's history should not be read as the tower's. The register says President Franklin Roosevelt set up the refuge by executive order in 1936, and Wikipedia gives the same founding year.[^1][^3]

The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service manages the refuge, and the register lists the agency as administering the lookout.[^1][^3] The agency says the refuge offers birding, wildlife watching, hunting and fishing, with viewing platforms.[^5]

We found no source on whether the tower can be entered or climbed, or on its hours. If you want to see it, start by asking the refuge, and do not climb it without permission.
