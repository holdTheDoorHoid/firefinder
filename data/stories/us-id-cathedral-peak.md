Cathedral Peak's lookout went up in 1931 on the easternmost point of the peak, in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, Shoshone County, Idaho.[^1][^2]

It was a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, and the site also had two patrol points, a quarter mile west and about a mile and a half southwest.[^1][^2][^4]

The two sites give different elevations, 4,090 feet and 4,291 feet.[^1][^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1955.[^1][^2][^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list also marks it as gone.[^3]

The Idaho page says its authors combed the ridge and found no trace of the towers.[^2] Their closest find was an open patch they judged suitable for a tower.[^2]

The Idaho page is the only source that describes the ridge itself, and it lists the site as a hike-in on Coeur d'Alene National Forest land.[^2] No source says whether it is open to visitors.
