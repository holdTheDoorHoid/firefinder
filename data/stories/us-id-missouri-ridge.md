Missouri Ridge lies in the Payette National Forest, five miles east of Yellow Pine in Valley County, Idaho, at 8,128 feet.[^1][^2] A lookout tower went up there in 1935: a ten-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, set at the southwest end of the ridge.[^1][^3] The Forest Lookouts page gives its township, range and section as 19N-9E-17.[^3]

The sources disagree on how the tower ended: two give 1960 as the year it was destroyed,[^1][^2] while a third says it was removed during the 1960s.[^3]

The University of Idaho's Archival Idaho collection holds a photograph record titled "Missouri Ridge Lookout."[^4] Its photo text reads "Profile Peak in background."[^4]

None of the pages I read describes what, if anything, is left on the ridge today. The Idaho Fire Lookouts listing tags the site as a hike-in and marks the tower as destroyed.[^2]
