The Upper Shakwa Lake fire tower stands over Upper Shakwa Lake, in the West Spanish Forest.[^1][^2] A 2015 trip report places that forest about 100 km north-west of Sudbury.[^2] The firelookout.ca record and the trip report both describe it as a 30 m (100 ft) fire tower.[^1][^2]

The record says the tower was once a strategic post in Ontario's forest fire protection system.[^1] Like many other Ontario towers, it was abandoned in the 1970s, once spotting from the air and fire-fighting crews had taken over that role.[^1]

The 2015 trip report describes a canoe route through the Shakwa lakes and rivers that began at Rushbrook Lake.[^2] Reaching that lake, the report says, takes hours on dusty logging roads.[^2] The trip was a 130 km expedition.[^2]

The firelookout.ca record says the tower is still there, a solitary watchpost above the Shakwa Lakes.[^1] The trip report found it standing too.[^2]

A still-standing list on the Ontario's Fire Tower Lookouts site names an Oshell Tower at Little Shakwa Lake. The sources here do not say whether that is this tower.[^3]

The list also adds that the MNR has stopped maintaining these towers and asks visitors not to climb them.[^3] No source describes who owns this tower or whether the public may visit it.
