The register dates the North Mountain tower to 1935 and places it in Berkeley County, on the Monongahela National Forest, at an elevation of 1,800 feet.[^1] A forest history of the Monongahela lists a North Mountain Tower from that year among nine towers the Civilian Conservation Corps built during the Depression.[^3]

The sources do not agree on the date or the agency. Eastern US Lookouts says a tower was erected in 1929 and names the West Virginia Division of Forestry as the agency.[^2] The register names the U.S. Forest Service as administrator.[^1]

The site was added to the register in September 2008.[^1] The register and Eastern US Lookouts both say the tower was removed that year.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's West Virginia list marks it as gone.[^4]

The forest history says the forest stopped staffing its towers by the end of the 1970s, and that most of them were torn down, with concrete piers and steps as the only trace.[^3] It does not describe this site. No source describes what, if anything, remains at the North Mountain site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
