The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Roth Rock tower on Backbone Mountain in Garrett County as gone.[^2] The only dated record found is a State Board of Forestry report for 1910-11, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, which says that in autumn 1911 a tower near Table Rock, on Backbone Mountain, was used for fire observations.[^1] The same quote describes a forest observer with a telephone line to the forest wardens, who phoned in fire locations as soon as they were found.[^1] The quoted text does not name Roth Rock, and the sources read here do not show whether it is the same tower.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, and none says whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Roth Rock, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/roth-rock.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Maryland Fire Lookout Towers, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/md/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
