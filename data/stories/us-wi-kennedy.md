The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list places a tower called Kennedy in Price County and marks it gone.[^1] OpenStreetMap has a point with the same name, tagged as an observation tower at an elevation of 492 metres.[^2]

An FCC antenna registration uses the name Kennedy Lookout Tower for a site on County Road E in the Town of Lake, and its coordinates sit close to the lookout listing.[^3] The registration gives a structure height of 91.4 m (299.9 ft) and an overall height of 98.1 m (321.9 ft) above ground.[^3] It lists an FAA issue date of March 11, 1996, and a construction date of August 25, 1997.[^3]

No source we read describes the site as it is today, and none says whether the public can reach it. It is not clear whether this structure replaced the old lookout, or whether any of the original cab or footings remain. Treat the site as off limits unless you have the landowner's permission, and do not climb any tower there.
