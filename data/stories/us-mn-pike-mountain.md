Pike Mountain was a U.S. Forest Service lookout on the highest hill of the eastern Mesaba Range, about 7 miles airline northeast of Virginia, Minnesota.[^1][^2] A 1936 station description, written by the Forest Service, says the survey marker is a standard bronze disk set into outcropping rock at the middle of the tower's base.[^1] The same description names other stations visible from the ground at that point: Jasper, Birch Knob, Gheen and Skibo.[^1]

The description also gives a route from Virginia: north on St. Louis County Highway 7 to County Highway 109, east 2 miles on that road, then a marked side road to a trail that climbs half a mile up the hill to the tower.[^1] It is a 1936 account and says nothing about access today.[^1]

The lookout had been removed by 1973. A county compilation of St. Louis County towers says so, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as gone.[^2][^3]

The sources we read do not describe what, if anything, remains on the hilltop today or whether the public can go there.[^3]
