The original Hornet Peak lookout sits in the Flathead National Forest, about 12 miles northwest of Polebridge.[^1][^2] The lookout dates to 1917, when it had a 15-foot pole platform and a log shed.[^1] The present D-1 log cupola cabin, built in 1922, can be booked through the Recreation Rental program.[^1][^2]

The lookout went unused for years before it was restored, and it can now be rented.[^2]

A full-size copy of that cabin went up at the Missoula Smokejumper Visitor Center in 1993.[^1] The visitor center sits at the Aerial Fire Depot, northwest of the Missoula airport.[^3][^4]

Descriptions of the copy differ. A travel listing says visitors can enter a copy of a lookout tower from the 1930s.[^4] The lookout history page instead says the copy reproduces the Hornet Peak cabin.[^1]

The Forest Service page, in the copy we read, says the visitor center is closed for renovations.[^3] Anyone wanting a tour can email or phone to check on pre-scheduled tours.[^3] The Montana list shows the replica as a standing ground structure.[^5] Because the travel listing says visitors can enter and the Forest Service page says the center is closed, current access is unclear.[^4][^3]
