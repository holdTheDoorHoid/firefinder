The Luskville Fire Tower (tour à feu, or fire tower) stands at the north end of the Chute-de-Luskville (Luskville Falls) trail, in the western sector of Gatineau Park in the municipality of Pontiac, Quebec.[^2][^3] The National Historic Lookout Register (NHLR) gives its elevation as 390 m (1,281 ft).[^1]

The tower was built in 1940.[^2] The NHLR describes a wooden cab set on steel support beams, similar to other metal fire lookouts such as the Aermotor LS-40, with a small balcony outside the cab.[^1]

In the past, forest rangers stayed in the tower to scan the horizon for signs of forest fire.[^2]

The NHLR lists Quebec's Ministère des Forêts, de la Faune et des Parcs as the administrator, with the National Capital Commission (NCC) as a cooperator.[^1] The NCC manages and protects Gatineau Park.[^3]

The NHLR says the cab and tower are still standing, though weathered, at their original location.[^1] The NHLR registered the lookout on December 25, 2022.[^1]

Today the NCC says the tower is not accessible to the public.[^2] The trail is a 4.2 km loop with a 300 m climb up the Eardley Escarpment, and it passes two viewpoints and the tower.[^2] Both the parking lot and the trail close in winter.[^2]
