Crystal Mountain sits in Saline County, inside the Ouachita National Forest.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site lists its lookout structure as built in 1964 and removed in 1979.[^1] If both dates are right, the structure stood about fifteen years.[^1] The encyclopedia says the Civilian Conservation Corps built many towers in the Ouachita during the Great Depression, so this 1964 structure came decades later.[^1][^2] The 1964 construction falls within the period the Arkansas encyclopedia calls the height of fire tower use, from the 1930s to the 1970s, when the state had more than 120 towers.[^2] The encyclopedia also says aerial detection of fires became more reliable and simpler from the 1960s, and that aerial surveillance made lookout towers less necessary.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the ground is open to visitors.

[^2]: Forest Fire Lookouts, Encyclopedia of Arkansas, https://encyclopediaofarkansas.net/entries/Forest-Fire-Lookouts-7905 (accessed 2026-10-10).
