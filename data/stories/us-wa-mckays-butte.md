The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the McKays Butte lookout as US 1372 in Yakima County, Washington, at 4,038 feet.[^1][^2] The register says the Yakima Indian Reservation administers the site, and Willhite Web places it on the Yakama Nation.[^1][^4] The register added the site on August 11, 2012, on the nomination of Ray Kresek.[^1]

The register and Firelookout.com both date the tower to 1935.[^1][^2] Both describe a three-legged wooden tower with an 8-by-8-foot cab, plus a ground house with living quarters.[^1][^2] The register gives the tower a height of 40 feet. Willhite Web says it was 50 feet and credits CCC-ID crews with building it.[^1][^4]

The tower came down in 1965.[^1][^2][^4] The Forest Lookouts page also lists it as removed.[^3] The register also says only a handful of these towers were built, and none is still standing.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left on the butte today.

Willhite Web says the site is on the Yakama Nation and that access is forbidden without special permission.[^4] Ask the Yakama Nation for permission before you go near the butte.
