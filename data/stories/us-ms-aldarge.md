The Aldarge fire tower stood in Benton County, Mississippi.[^1] A survey station beside it lay about seven miles (11 km) northwest of Ashland, on state property.[^2]

In 1966 the Coast and Geodetic Survey set that station 100 feet (30 m) south of the tower. One of its reference marks went into the centre of the tower's base.[^2] The tower had four legs.[^2]

A recovery note on the datasheet, made by Geocaching in 2007, says the tower had been removed by then. The concrete bases of its four legs remained.[^2] The same visit found the concrete base of one reference mark, but not the station mark itself.[^2]

The Eastern US Lookouts list marks the tower as removed.[^1] Its copy of the datasheet dates the survey description to 1967, while the official datasheet says 1966.[^1][^2] This story follows the official record.

No source I found describes the site today. The most recent survey record I found, from 2007, says the tower was removed but its leg bases remained.[^2]

[^2]: NGS Data Sheet, PID EG1663, National Geodetic Survey (NOAA), https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=EG1663 (accessed 2026-10-21).
