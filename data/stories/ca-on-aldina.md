The Aldina Fire Tower stands on the summit of Carson Creek Peak.[^2] One site ranks that peak as the fourth-highest summit in Ontario.[^2]

The route descriptions differ. A 2009 account says to drive west out of Thunder Bay on Highway 11, then turn onto Highway 588 toward Stanley.[^3] A 2016 post says to take Highway 11/17 west, turn left on 590, and drive 19 km along Boreal Road, a gravel road it describes as wide and well graded. It also gives a 3.8 km walk from Boreal Road to the tower.[^2]

A hiking guide says the tower went up in the 1960s and is about 30 m (about 100 ft) tall.[^1]

A 2016 post reports the tower is still up at the summit.[^2] A reader's comment on the hiking guide says its writer visited on May 3, 2025, found the tower still standing, and says a cousin climbed to the top.[^1] One guide also says the cupola is the only part left at the top, a detail no other source explains.[^1]

Another reader comment, on the 2009 page, says access to the top was restricted unless the first section of ladder has been reinstalled.[^3]

One guide says the area has many roads and trails that are not marked.[^1] None of the sources mentions a closure or a permit for the site.[^1][^2][^3]
