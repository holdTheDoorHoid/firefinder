Sentinel Mountain lies inside the Bob Marshall Wilderness, about 24 miles northeast of Seeley Lake, on the Flathead National Forest in Lewis and Clark County.[^1] The summit was first home to a camp set up in the 1920s, and an L-4 cab was built there about 1933.[^1]

A 1937 article from The Ekalaka Eagle, reprinted on a Montana lookout site, describes one season on the mountain.[^2] A Carter County high school student reached the lookout after a 70-mile airplane trip from Kalispell and a 10-mile pack-train ride from the Big Prairie ranger station.[^2] The cabin was a 14-by-14 building, and he carried water up from a stream 2 1/4 miles down the mountain.[^2] The article also said the season was unusually quiet for fires.[^2]

The cab was abandoned in the mid-1950s, and the Fire Lookout site says it is now gone.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list also marks the site as gone.[^3]

No source we found describes what remains on the summit today, or how the site is reached now.
