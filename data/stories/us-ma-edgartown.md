The Edgartown tower was a temporary 60-foot wooden tower, put up on state-owned land in Edgartown in 1944.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout site places it on Airfield Hill.[^1]

In 1946 the US Navy ordered it taken down because it posed a hazard to naval aviation.[^1] During World War II the state leased forestland to the Navy for the length of the war, and that land is today the Martha's Vineyard Airport.[^3] Squadrons used the island for practice between 1944 and 1946.[^3]

The sources disagree about when the temporary tower ended. The Forest Fire site says the Navy had it removed in 1946.[^1] The Register's page for the Vineyard Fire Tower, which is in West Tisbury, says a temporary tower near Edgartown remained in use until the present tower was completed in 1950.[^2] We follow the 1946 date, because the Forest Fire site records a specific removal order.[^1]

No source we read describes the Airfield Hill site today, and we found no information about public access.
