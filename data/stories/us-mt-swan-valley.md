Swan Valley began in 1923 with a 30-foot platform set in a yard at Goat Creek G.S., according to firelookout.com, which places the lookout 12 miles south of Swan Lake in Lake County, Montana.[^1]

In the 1930s a 100-foot tree platform appears in the record, and firelookout.com says it was still there in the 1980s.[^1]

Firelookout.com names the Montana DNRC as the agency.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site at 47.74984 north and 113.82484 west, as a platform.[^2] Both sources describe the lookout as abandoned.[^1][^2]

Rex's Fire Lookout Page pairs the name with the same agency and the survey code 23N-17W-18.[^3]

No source we read says whether any part of the platform survives, or whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3]
