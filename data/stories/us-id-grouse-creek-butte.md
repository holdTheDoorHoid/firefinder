Grouse Creek Butte lies in Elmore County, Idaho, on the Sawtooth National Forest, at legal location 3N-11E-33.[^1][^3] A Forest Service history of the forest's administrative sites also calls it the Grouse Butte Lookout.[^2]

On March 6, 1942, the land was withdrawn from the public domain for a lookout and for grazing the lookout's horse.[^1][^2] A Forest Lookouts page gives the withdrawn tract as 90 acres.[^1] The same history gives the distance as about seven miles to the Shake Creek Ranger Station and nine miles to the Iron Mountain Lookout.[^2]

The history's table lists the 1942 withdrawal under the name Grouse Butte Ranger Station, while its narrative calls the site the Grouse Butte Lookout, so the history itself does not settle whether the site was meant as a lookout or as a station.[^2] The history adds that it has no further information about the site.[^2]

Nothing in these sources describes what, if anything, remains there today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3] Check the Sawtooth National Forest's current notices before any trip.
