Bird Branch was a steel lookout on the Pine Mountain range, in Bell County, Kentucky, about four miles northeast of Pineville.[^1]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey first observed and described the station in 1950. Its description gives a steel tower on four legs, roughly 90 feet tall and gray, with a square cab at the top.[^2][^1] Surveyors tied the base of the tower to a triangulation station named Bird, by a measured line of about 3.3 feet.[^1] Their directions began at the junction of U.S. Highways 25E and 119, about a mile south of Pineville, and ran up a series of forks and hill roads to the tower.[^1]

The survey record still lists the station with a current position, but it does not say whether the tower stands.[^2] The tower's Forest Lookouts page marks it as removed.[^1] We found no source that describes the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
