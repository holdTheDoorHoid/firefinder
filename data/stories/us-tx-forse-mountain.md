The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Forse Mountain site in Sabine County, at 31.52693, -93.98049. It records the structure type as unknown and the status as gone.[^1]

The name belongs to a hill. Mapcarta gives Forse Mountain an elevation of 531 feet (162 metres) and places it close to both Chumley, a hamlet, and Black Ankle.[^2] MountainZone gives the same approximate elevation and places the summit on the Patroon South topographic map.[^3] Its own coordinates, 31.5201799 and -93.9801909, sit about three-quarters of a kilometre south of the point the list gives.[^1][^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.

[^1]: Texas Lookouts (By County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/tx-co/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Forse Mountain Map, Mapcarta, https://mapcarta.com/21729364 (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Forse Mountain in Sabine County TX (Patroon South Area), MountainZone, https://www.mountainzone.com/mountains/texas/sabine-tx/summits/forse-mountain/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
