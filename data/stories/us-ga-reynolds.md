A Georgia tower list places a lookout called reynolds in Clayton County. Its entry carries a note reading GA109-relocated, which points to the Henry County tower that the same list calls greenwood.[^1] That tower was a 100-foot (30 m) Aermotor.[^2][^3] The sources give two model numbers for it, LS-40 and MC-40.[^2][^3]

In 2012 the Forest Fire Lookout Association's first restoration grant of the year went toward restoring the tower and moving it to Reynolds Nature Preserve, near Morrow.[^3] The report describes the move as planned.[^3] By then the tower stood at a former Georgia Forestry Commission headquarters site, south of McDonough off Highway 42, and the commission had sold it.[^3] The Forest Lookouts index says it was sold and would be relocated to the preserve.[^2]

Clayton County Parks and Recreation owns and runs the preserve.[^4] In 1976 the county received 130 acres (53 ha) for preservation from Judge Reynolds.[^4] Wikipedia's summary of the park mentions three miles (5 km) of trails, a Civil War-era farm and an interpretive center. It does not mention a fire tower.[^4]

The list's note says relocated, but no source confirms that the move happened, so whether a tower stands at Reynolds today is unknown.[^1] No source describes the old Henry County site today.[^1][^2][^3]
