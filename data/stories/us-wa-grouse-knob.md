Grouse Knob stands in Pend Oreille County, within the Kaniksu National Forest, about 20 miles south-southeast of Ione.[^1] A crow's nest was on the knob in 1933.[^1][^2] Willhite's page adds that an early road crossed the mountain's west flank and went over Pyramid Pass, just north of the knob.[^2]

The site was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list types it as a crow's nest and marks it abandoned.[^4] Willhite reports that the crow's nest was still there in the 1980s.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts index gives the site a one-word status, "Removed", with no date or explanation.[^3] The two accounts fit together if the removal came after the 1980s, though no source gives a date.[^2][^3]

Willhite's hiking note says to park at Pyramid Pass. From there an abandoned road runs nearly to the top, and old skid trails continue onto the summit plateau.[^2] The note recommends a vehicle with some ground clearance.[^2] It describes the route as clear of brush in 2023, though it will not stay that way.[^2]

The note also calls the summit untouched.[^2] No source I read describes what, if anything, remains there today. The land is part of the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1] No source says whether the summit is open to visitors, so check with the Forest Service before going.
