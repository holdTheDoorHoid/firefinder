Dog Thief Point is a lookout site in Clatsop County, Oregon. Forest Lookouts lists its agency as the Oregon Department of Forestry.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts calls the site Dog Thief Point, and Rex's Fire Lookout Page calls it the Dog Thief Lookout.[^1][^3]

Rex's page lists a crow's nest and a tent cabin at the site under 1928. It does not say they were built that year.[^3]

Rex's page and the Forest Fire Lookout Association both list the site as abandoned. Neither says when that happened, although Rex's page places the word under its 1928 entry.[^2][^3]

Rex's page also says an eight-foot crow's nest was still there. That note is undated, so it may be out of date.[^3] None of the sources we read gives a recent visit, so they do not clearly show the site as it is now.

We found no information about public access. Anyone interested should check with the Oregon Department of Forestry before going to the point.
