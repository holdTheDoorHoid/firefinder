A fire lookout tower went up at Clairville, in Kent County, New Brunswick.[^1] Clairville is a community in Weldford Parish, on Route 465 toward Jailletville, about 5.3 km northeast of Coal Branch.[^2] Canada Lookouts dates the entry to 1958.[^1] The quoted report, the 122nd annual report of the Department of Lands and Mines for the year ending 31 March 1959, describes a new Ajax galvanized steel tower of 24.4 m (80 ft).[^1]

The report says the tower stood on a site overlooking a wide area of forest, near where the Salmon, Buctouche and Richibucto Rivers begin.[^1] It also says the station was to reach ranger offices, and nearby lookouts at Indian Mountain, Laketon, Blackville and Chipman, by VHF radio.[^1]

No source describes the site today, including whether the tower still stands or whether the public can visit it.
