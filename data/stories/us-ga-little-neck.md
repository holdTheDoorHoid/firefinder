A compiled list of Georgia lookout towers, kept on tnlandforms.us, places a tower labelled "little neck" in Chatham County at 32.071111, -81.315833.[^1] The list gives its elevation as 17, with no unit stated.[^1] The name is the compiler's own: the page says names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's creation, and this entry is number 413.[^1]

The page says the list's towers were identified from 1:100,000 USGS topographic maps and NGS data sheets.[^1] It also credits Gary Weber and Ron Stafford with supplying many tower locations.[^1]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything remains or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
