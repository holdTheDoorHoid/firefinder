The Boulder Lake lookout was a small cab on a cliff overlooking Boulder Lake, about 15 miles north-northeast of Missoula, in Missoula County, Montana, within the Lolo National Forest.[^1]

One record gives 1920 as the build year.[^1] The cab was a 7-by-7-foot frame structure.[^1] It had sat unused for years before the 2003 fire.[^1]

A Forest Lookouts page says the 2003 wildfire consumed what remained of the structures.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's 2003 Idaho/Montana chapter report named Boulder Lake among two Montana lookouts lost to fires that year.[^3]

No source we read describes the site now. We cannot say what, if anything, is left on the cliff, or whether the public may visit.
