Shoistal Park was a lookout in Hill County, Montana, about 24 miles southwest of Havre.[^1] Rex's Fire Lookout Page calls it an observation tower and dates it to the 1940s.[^1] No other page we read gives a date for it.[^2][^3] Rex's page gives no elevation for the site.[^1]

Rex's page lists the site as private co-op land.[^1] The Forest Lookouts listing also marks it as cooperator land, private, and gives the legal location as 31N-12E-34.[^2]

Rex's page says the tower was abandoned.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks it as a tower with the status Gone, at 48.39488, -110.16832.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. The land is private,[^1][^2] and none of the pages says whether visitors are welcome, so ask the landowner before going near the spot.
