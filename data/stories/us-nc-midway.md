Midway is one of the North Carolina forest lookouts in an online compilation, which files it under Davidson County.[^1] A North Carolina tower list on tnlandforms.us places the Midway tower at 35.9625, -80.22194.[^2]

No source I could read describes the Midway site today, including whether any part of the tower remains or whether the public can visit it.

[^1]: NORTH CAROLINA - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/north-carolina.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
