The Murrell Hill fire tower site is in Russell County, Alabama, at an elevation the register gives as 535 feet, or 163 meters.[^1] The Alabama Forestry Commission administers the site.[^1] The register says the site's name appears on the commission's list and was located on a topographic map.[^1]

Andrew Zerbe nominated the site, and it was added to the Former Fire Lookout Sites register on September 15, 2019, as US 1748, AL 19.[^1]

The register's description says no trace of a tower was found, and it gives a water tank on the site as the reason.[^1] We found no source that says whether the site can be visited, so ask the Alabama Forestry Commission before going there.

[^1]: Murrell Hill Fire Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/al/murrell-hill-fire-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
