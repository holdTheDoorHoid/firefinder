The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower called Cooperstown (Fauquier) in Fauquier County, Virginia, at 38.52661, -77.56421, and marks it as gone.[^1] A second Virginia list, kept by Forest Lookouts, also places a Cooperstown tower in the county.[^2]

No source I could read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Virginia Fire Towers (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/va-co/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Virginia - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/virginia.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
