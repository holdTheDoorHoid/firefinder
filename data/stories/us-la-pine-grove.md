Wikipedia's list of Louisiana lookout towers places the Pine Grove lookout tower in St. Helena Parish, with Pine Grove as its nearest town.[^1] The gazetteer TopoQuest calls the tower site a locale at an elevation of 167 feet above mean sea level, and says it can be seen on the USGS Pine Grove, LA map.[^2] We found no build date, builder or height for the tower.

Louisiana's first steel fire tower went up near Bogalusa in 1922, and by 1950 the number of towers had reached fifty-six.[^1] The same list says many fire towers still exist. Most are inactive, and many are kept in reserve.[^1] We found nothing about when Pine Grove was built or who put it up.

The list says the tower is still standing, but its notes are mixed. It says the tower is labeled inconsistently on maps and last appears labeled in 2012. The original note says the tower could not be located in older aerial imagery. A later update says it is visible on Google Street View.[^1] We have not seen the tower ourselves, so we cannot settle that disagreement.

We found no record of who owns the site, who staffed the tower, or whether it has ever seen a fire. Its current use and public access are unknown. Do not climb it, and ask the landowner before going near the site.
