Shirttail Peak lies in Shasta County, northeast of French Gulch, near the eastern edge of the Trinity National Forest.[^1][^2]

In November 1943 the Blue Lake Advocate reported that W.H. Williams had spent several months on top of the peak as a forest lookout.[^1] In July 1947 the same paper reported that Mrs. Anna Key of French Gulch had begun her second year as a lookout for the Forest Service there.[^1] She also reported fires on private land that the state's Division of Forestry protected, since the peak sits near the national forest boundary.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list marks Shirttail Peak as gone.[^2] A 2023 Peakbagging table lists the summit without the "LO site" label it gives to lookout sites where a building has been removed or the status is unknown, which leaves its condition unclear.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit now, or whether the peak is open to visitors.[^1]
