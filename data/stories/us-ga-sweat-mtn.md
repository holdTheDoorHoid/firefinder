Sweat Mountain rises in far northeastern Cobb County, in the suburbs north of Atlanta, and is the county's second-highest point after Kennesaw Mountain.[^2] Tom Dunigan's list of Georgia lookout towers places a lookout on it at 1,678, a figure given without a unit; if it is feet, that is about 511 m.[^1] Wikipedia gives the official USGS elevation of the summit as 1,688 feet (515 m). If the list's figure is in feet, the two differ by ten feet, a gap the sources do not explain.[^1][^2]

Years before satellites, a U.S. Forest Service ranger climbed the tower on the mountain each day to report smoke locations for fire control, according to Wikipedia.[^2] That article does not date this work, and its history section cites no sources.[^2]

Wikipedia says the tower has since been removed, links that to suburban growth, and notes a concrete benchmark where one of its feet once stood.[^2]

The summit carries several transmitters, radio towers and antennas.[^2] Wikipedia says the mountain's height makes it highly appealing for radio use.[^2] No source says whether the top is open to visitors. Wikipedia also says the mountain has inspired the naming of nearby establishments, including Sweat Mountain Park.[^2] The county's park page describes wooded walking trails there.[^3]
