The Ruby tower stands in Ruby Township, Chippewa County, and a Wisconsin lookout list marks it as standing.[^1][^2] The sources read do not say when it was built, who put it up, or what the cab looks like.

In January 2008 the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources advertised for a lookout person at the Ruby tower.[^2] The job meant climbing a 100-foot tower and working a two-way radio.[^2] Most of the work fell in April and May, and the schedule depended on fire conditions.[^2] Applications were due February 15, 2008.[^2]

A Chippewa Herald-Telegram item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, reports a break-in at the Ruby tower on June 24, 1993.[^2] Nothing was missing and there was no damage.[^2]

In December 2015 the DNR announced plans to close all of the state's remaining fire towers.[^3] That decision covered the whole state, not Ruby alone.[^3] The sources do not say whether the Ruby tower was closed, taken down, or is still staffed.

Anyone curious should check with the DNR before going near the site. Do not climb the tower.
