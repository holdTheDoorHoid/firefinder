The Longworth lookout is a cabin north of the Fraser River, about 85 km east of Prince George, B.C.[^2] The trail climbs to a ridge above the tree line, and a walk of about 750 m from there reaches a Forest Service lookout at 1,780 m (5,840 ft).[^2]

According to a trail history, the lookout opened in 1929, following severe forest fires in 1927.[^1] The same history says the building still stands on top of Longworth Mountain.[^1]

The cabin had fallen into disrepair before the recent work.[^3] In June 2024 a supplier delivered engineered trusses for the cabin's restoration.[^3] Two accounts say the cabin was fully renovated in summer 2024.[^1][^2]

Reaching it means taking the Giscome-Upper Fraser Highway and the Sinclair/Longworth Road. The trail guide links to a reservation page for the lookout.[^2] The guide warns of high winds, frequent whiteouts and avalanche terrain near the lookout, and says the cabin has no nearby water in summer.[^2]
