Muddy Mountain had a fire lookout tower in Montgomery County, on the Ouachita National Forest.[^1][^2] A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description, dated 1956 in its record, calls it a four-legged steel structure with a sheet-metal house on top, 90 feet above the ground.[^1]

A lookout history of the Mena and Oden ranger districts says eleven fire towers once stood in those two districts, and it names Muddy Mountain among the Montgomery County ones.[^2] The same page lists just three survivors, Rich Mountain, Bee Mountain and Tall Peak, so the Muddy Mountain tower is not among them.[^2] It adds that the Ouachita National Forest once had 43 lookouts and now has six.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Muddy Mountain site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Muddy Mtn Lookout Tower (NGS station description, PID EJ2040), Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/muddy-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Lookouts of the Mena & Oden Ranger Districts, Arkansas, argenweb.net, https://argenweb.net/montgomery/lookouts.htm (accessed 2026-10-21).
