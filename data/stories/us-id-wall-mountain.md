The Wall Mountain site sits at 5,120 feet, about 10 miles north-northeast of Bonners Ferry, in the Kaniksu National Forest of Boundary County, Idaho.[^1][^2] The lookout went up in 1937 as a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, and a patrol point lay two miles to the southeast.[^1][^2]

Three people are named across the listings. firelookout.com gives Frank Heath for 1933 and Pearl "Peppy" Plato for 1944.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives Clifford Middleton for 1939, citing a September 28, 1939 item in the Bonners Ferry Herald.[^2] The 1933 name comes four years before the 1937 build date on the same page, and we could not reconcile the two.[^1]

Both lookout listings date its destruction to 1958, but neither gives a cause.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page gives no year and marks the tower only as "Removed," and the Forest Fire Lookout Association index shows it as gone.[^3][^4]

Today, no source we read describes what, if anything, is left on the summit, or whether the site is open to visitors.
