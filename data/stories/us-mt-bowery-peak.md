Bowery Peak rises to 6,102 feet in Hill County, Montana, on the Rocky Boy's Indian Reservation.[^1] Its lookout was a cabin on the summit.[^1][^2]

A 1957 survey description from the U.S. Geological Survey places the survey mark at the center of a lookout house.[^2] That suggests the cabin was standing by then. The same description puts the survey station, on the peak's highest point, about 26 miles due south of Havre.[^2] The firelookout.com page gives 30 miles south of Havre instead.[^1] The two sources disagree on that distance.[^1][^2]

A state lookout list marks the cabin as gone, without a date.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit, or whether the peak is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3] Because it sits on the reservation, check with the reservation's administration before planning any visit.
