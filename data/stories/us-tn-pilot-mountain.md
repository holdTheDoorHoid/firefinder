Pilot Mountain is a fire lookout site in Morgan County, Tennessee, at an elevation of 2,510 feet.[^1] Tennessee Landforms lists the tower as standing and gives the code GB2610 beside it.[^1]

The 2014 National Register study lists Pilot Mountain among the Morgan County towers standing on March 26, 2014.[^2] The same study credits the Tennessee Division of Forestry with 208 lookout towers built from roughly 1930 to 1970.[^2] We found no build date, design, builder or staffing history for this tower, so we cannot say when it went up or which program built it.

We found no record of notable fires at Pilot Mountain. The 2014 list is the latest dated record we found, and we could not open a later check of its condition.

Tennessee Landforms advises visitors not to climb its towers, because steps are frequently missing or decayed.[^1] We found no public access arrangement for this tower, so please do not climb it. Check with the Tennessee Division of Forestry before going to the site, since the tower's current condition is not confirmed.
