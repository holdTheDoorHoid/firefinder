War Eagle Lookout stands at about 8,200 feet in the Payette National Forest, roughly seven miles northwest of Warren in Idaho County.[^2] The first tower here was a ten-foot log crib topped with an L-4 cab, and two sources date it to 1931.[^2][^3] The National Historic Lookout Register gives 1961 as the build year instead, although its own description mentions the earlier log crib tower.[^1] In 1961 a new structure replaced the original: a Region 6 flat-top cab on a ten-foot concrete block base.[^1][^2][^4]

In August 1944 a Salt Lake Tribune item, reprinted on a lookout history page, described a retired banker and his wife staying at War Eagle and helping fight fires during the war.[^4] A July 1953 newspaper item on the same page placed a blaze near the lookout, ten miles from Warren, that winds fanned.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts keeps a known-staff list that runs from 1971 to 2012.[^3]

The National Historic Lookout Register added War Eagle on November 29, 2020.[^1] The Payette National Forest administers the site, and a regional lookout list still shows the tower as standing.[^1][^5]

Sources differ on the schedule. One says the lookout is staffed in the summer, while the registry says it is generally staffed during the fire season.[^2][^1] Today the site is a drive-up lookout.[^3] Check with the Payette National Forest about current staffing dates and visiting rules before you plan a trip.
