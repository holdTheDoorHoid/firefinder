Blue Joe was a crow's nest in the Kaniksu National Forest, about 17 miles west of Porthill in Boundary County, Idaho, at an elevation of 5,650 feet.[^1][^3] The sources date the crow's nest only to the 1930s.[^1][^3]

Robert Campbell is the only person named on the firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts pages, listed as lookout personnel for 1936.[^1][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list and firelookout.com both describe the site as abandoned.[^1][^2] The association gives its coordinates as 48.97342 north and 116.88601 west.[^2] The Forest Lookouts site gives the legal location as 65N-4W-20.[^4]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site tags Blue Joe as a hike-in site.[^3]

No source I read describes what, if anything, is left at the site now, and none says whether the public may visit it today.
