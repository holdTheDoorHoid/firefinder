A list of Georgia lookout towers on tnlandforms.us has an entry called "wrightsboro" in Columbia County.[^1] The page gives a "google map revised" date of 22 March 2016.[^1] The entry carries only the tag "usgs" and no register number.[^1] The name is shared with Wrightsboro, an unincorporated community in McDuffie County about 18 mi (29 km) to the west-northwest.[^1][^2] Wikipedia says Quakers made the first lasting settlement there, in the 1760s.[^2]

The same list has a second Wrightsboro entry, "wrightsboro GFC", tagged "gfc-021" and "BLL-50809". Its coordinates put it about 1,430 ft (436 m) from this one.[^1] The list does not say whether the two entries are one tower or two.[^1]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at this site today, or whether it is open to the public.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Wrightsboro, Georgia, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrightsboro,_Georgia (accessed 2026-10-21).
