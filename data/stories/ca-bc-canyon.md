The Canada Lookouts index lists Canyon among its British Columbia towers, in the Stikine area.[^1] The entry gives the position as latitude 59.980 and longitude 128.539 (read here as west, since the page prints no hemisphere), and an elevation of 816, which this story reads as 816 m (2,677 ft).[^1] The index says its section gathers information on fire lookouts from across Canada, and it notes that its provincial coverage is far from complete.[^2]

No source I could reach describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors. Anyone thinking of visiting should not assume the spot is open or safe to reach.

[^1]: Canyon, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/canyon.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Canada's Fire Lookouts, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/ (accessed 2026-10-20).
