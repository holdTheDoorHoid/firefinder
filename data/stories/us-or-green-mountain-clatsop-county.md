The Green Mountain lookout stood in Clatsop County, Oregon, about nine miles west of Vernonia.[^1] The site's original lookout, dating from the 1920s, was a 60-foot wooden tower with a cabin.[^2] In September 1934 a Forest Log item, quoted by the Forest Lookouts site, described a new station on the mountain: a 75-foot tower with quarters for the attendant.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register instead gives the 1934 tower a height of 83 feet, with a 7-by-7-foot cab and a 14-by-14-foot cabin for living quarters.[^2]

Donald Kyle was named to lead the new station.[^3] The same site notes a 1956 repainting of the interior and floor.[^3] In 1957 a new 65-foot wooden tower went up, topped by a 7-by-7 Amort cab, and a new cabin was built for the lookout.[^3]

Once the 1934 tower was up, the former cabin, which had an attached garage, served as storage.[^2] The lookout was abandoned in 1964.[^1][^2] A March 28, 1986 visit found the tower in pieces on the ground.[^3] Most of the cab remained on site, though the tower's legs had been taken away.[^3] Two walls and window frames from the 14-by-14 ground cabin lay there too, and the ruins of another cabin stood nearby.[^3]

Both the firelookout.com page and the register list the lookout as gone.[^1][^2] The newest description we found dates from 1986, so no source says what remains on the site today.[^3] No source says whether the site is open to the public, so check access before going near it.[^1][^2][^3]
