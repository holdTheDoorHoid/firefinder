Also known as Red Wing (Sheep) or Sheep Mountain, the lookout was a cabin site in the Challis National Forest.[^1][^2] The sources disagree on the county. Firelookout.com places it 30 miles southeast of Stanley in Lemhi County, while Rex's Fire Lookout Page files it under Custer County.[^1][^2]

Rex's page dates a camp with an alidade to about 1930.[^2] Firelookout.com says the site was developed in 1935 with a cabin and an alidade, and Rex's page gives 1935 for the cabin.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts has no documented staffing history for the lookout.[^3]

Both firelookout.com and Rex's page say it was abandoned in the 1950s, and Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the same decade.[^1][^2][^3]

Firelookout.com marks the lookout "R.I.P." and gives its elevation as 10,910 feet.[^1] No source read describes what, if anything, remains on the site today, or whether visitors may go there.[^1][^2][^3]
