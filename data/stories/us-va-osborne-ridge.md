The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower at Osborne Ridge, at 36.85241, -82.52822 in Scott County, Virginia, with its status as gone.[^1] Its list also has a separate entry for Caldwell, also in Scott County, which the association marks as a possible duplicate of the Osborne Ridge entry.[^1]

MountainZone describes Osborne Ridge as a ridge in Scott County, with an approximate elevation of 2,943 feet (897 m).[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the tower site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Virginia, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Osborne Ridge in Scott County VA (Fort Blackmore Area), MountainZone, https://www.mountainzone.com/mountains/virginia/scott-va/ridge/osborne-ridge-2/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
