Wilhite was a fire lookout tower in Titus County.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list places it at 33.24002, -94.94330 and marks it as gone.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register counts five registered lookouts in Texas: Chandler, Love's, Pine Mills, Spring and Woodville. Wilhite is not among them.[^2]

No source found describes what, if anything, stands at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
