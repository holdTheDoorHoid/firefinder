The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a fire tower at Hurley in Buchanan County, and marks it as gone.[^1] The list places it at 37.40622 and -82.03995.[^1]

Hurley is an unincorporated community in Virginia, in Buchanan County.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.
