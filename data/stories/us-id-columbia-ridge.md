Columbia Ridge Lookout stood 16 miles southwest of Elk City, in the Nez Perce National Forest, at 7,861 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

The lookout was a 50-foot pole L-4 tower, built about 1933.[^1][^2][^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page labels the site drive-up, so the tower could once be reached by road.[^2]

The tower was knocked over in 1963, and the sources give that year as its destruction date.[^1][^2][^3] One header on firelookout.com gives the span as 1933 to 1965, which disagrees with the 1963 date used elsewhere on that page and in the other two sources.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the tower as gone.[^4]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public. Check with the Nez Perce National Forest before visiting the area.
