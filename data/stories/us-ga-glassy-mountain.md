Glassy Mountain Fire Tower stands atop Glassy Mountain in Rabun County, Georgia, inside the Chattahoochee National Forest.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register and Wikipedia both date it to 1941.[^1][^2] The register describes a 55-foot metal tower with a 13-by-13-foot CL-100 series live-in cab and a catwalk.[^1] A ground residence and garage were added in the 1960s.[^1][^2]

The Weebly listing calls the current structure a 55-foot steel K-brace replacement tower, but it gives no date or reason for the replacement.[^3] That does not fit neatly with a 1941 build date, so the history of the current structure is unclear.[^3][^1]

The register notes a Forest Service building at the base that looks well used. Many visitors have reported the site was not staffed, and the register lists staffing as sometimes.[^1]

A 2018 visit report found the tower standing and in good condition overall. It noted rust on the cab and a number of broken window panes.[^1]

The road in is dirt and gravel, and it can take only one vehicle at a time. A locked barrier gate sits about a mile from the tower, and the rest of the way is a walk up the mountain.[^1] The sources do not say whether climbing the tower is allowed, so I would check with the U.S. Forest Service before climbing.[^1]
