In October 1909 state fire superintendents decided to add a lookout on Moosehead Mountain, on the Raquette River northwest of Piercefield in St. Lawrence County, to the observation posts already running: six in the Adirondacks and three in the Catskills.[^1] A 20-foot tower was set up there by June 1910.[^1] By 1916 a 40-foot Aermotor LS-25 steel tower with external ladder access had been erected. The source does not say whether it replaced the first tower.[^1]

W.D. Pond of Childwood was appointed a forest observer for Moosehead Mountain in July 1911, for the fire season, at $60 a month.[^1] In April 1950 a newspaper reported that Mrs. Pearl Eakins of Conifer would again be the observer, and called her the only woman observer in Northern New York.[^1] In 1978 a private party took the tower down and stored it for later re-assembly.[^1]

In 2016 Dave Vana of Davana LLC refurbished the tower and re-erected it in a courtyard at Industry City, a row of industrial warehouses on the Sunset Park waterfront in Brooklyn.[^3][^4] The chapter's 2016 annual report says it went back up in October.[^2] The state newsletter that November marked its birthday, calling it 100 years old and still standing.[^3]

The Todd Group, which installed the courtyard, says Industry City has three public courtyards and that an old fire tower was set up there as a vertical element.[^4] A visitor's blog from May 2021 found that the first flight of stairs had been removed, so the tower cannot be climbed.[^5] We found no later report. Please do not try to climb it, and check with Industry City before you visit.
