The McCool Lookout Tower stands in Attala County, Mississippi.[^1] The tower shares its name with the town of McCool, which is also in Attala County.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register gives the tower's elevation as 714 feet (218 m) and names the Mississippi Forestry Commission as the agency that administers it.[^1] The register entered the tower on July 9, 2023, as US 1743, MS 60, with Jason Johns as the nominator.[^1] I could not find a build date, a designer or any early history for the tower in the sources I read.

OpenStreetMap tags the structure as an observation tower with a lattice frame, which is the only design detail I found.[^3] I also found nothing about who staffed the tower or which fires it watched.

The register describes a site visit on July 8, 2023, and says the roads leading to the tower were locked at that time.[^1] The same entry points to 2021 Google Earth imagery as showing the tower was still in place then.[^1] I found no later report of its condition, so whether it still stands is unconfirmed, and I could not find whether the public may visit.

Please do not try to reach the tower without permission. Ask the Mississippi Forestry Commission about access before planning a trip.
