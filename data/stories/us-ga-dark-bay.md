The Georgia lookout list on tnlandforms.us includes a fire tower called Dark Bay in Atkinson County, placed at 31.3698 north and 82.98712 west.[^1] The page says the list grew out of a gap: in 2010 the FFLA site listed none of Georgia's towers, and the map was revised in March 2016.[^1] This row is number 122, and the author says names from 73 up were invented by the author, so Dark Bay may be a label for the spot rather than what the tower was called.[^1] The Georgia Forestry Commission gave coordinates for the towers the list marks BLL, and this row carries no such mark.[^1] No source I found describes the site today, so I cannot say whether a tower stands there or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1]

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
