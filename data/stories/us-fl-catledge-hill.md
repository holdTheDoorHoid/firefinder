Catledge Hill is a lookout tower in Brooks County, Georgia.[^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts website places it on its Florida list, though the tower's page names only the county.[^1] Mapcarta gives the site an elevation of 207 feet (63 metres).[^3] Tom Dunigan's list of Georgia lookout towers puts it at 30.70048 north and 83.59460 west, in Brooks County.[^2]

Mapcarta also places the Baden hamlet about two miles northeast of the tower.[^3] The Georgia list gives March 22, 2016 as the date its Google map was revised.[^2]

No source reviewed describes the tower or the site as it is now. Whether any of the structure remains, and whether the public can reach the spot, is not known. Check with the county or the landowner before going to look.
