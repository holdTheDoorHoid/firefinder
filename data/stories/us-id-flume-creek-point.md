Flume Creek Point is a former lookout site in Lemhi County, Idaho.[^1] The Boise listing gives its survey location as section 35 of township 17 North, range 16 East.[^1] Its elevation is listed as 9,263 feet (about 2,824 m).[^2]

The sources do not agree on which forest it sits in. One places the point in the Boise National Forest.[^1] Two others name the Challis National Forest.[^2][^3]

Listings describe the site as a camp from 1939.[^1][^2][^3] The Boise listing credits that date to someone named only as Kresek.[^1] The camp was abandoned in 1950.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list also marks it as a camp that was abandoned.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains of the camp today, or whether visitors may go there. One Idaho listing tags the point "Hike-In", meaning it is reached on foot.[^3]
