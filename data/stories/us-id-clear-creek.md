Clear Creek was a 20-foot tower with an L-4 cab in the Nez Perce National Forest, about 16 miles southeast of Kooskia in Idaho County, Idaho.[^1][^3][^4]

The tower dates to 1931 and was abandoned in 1949.[^1][^3][^4] Two listings give its elevation as 4,988 feet.[^1][^2]

The Forest Service's list, compiled in April 2010 from Ray Kresek's Fire Lookouts of the Northwest and other miscellaneous sources, gives the same dates.[^4]

The Forest Service list places the tower in township 31 North, range 6 East, section 32.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it as a hike-in site.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone.[^5]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
