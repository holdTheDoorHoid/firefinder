The Windswept Lookout stands on Windswept Farm in Morgan County, Tennessee, and the National Historic Lookout Register lists it as US 312, TN 5.[^1] The tower is 60 feet high, an Aermotor built of steel, with a cab measuring 7 by 7 feet.[^1] It did not start out on the farm.[^1] The Register says the tower first stood on Walden Mountain near Harriman, 15 miles to the south.[^1]

The Tennessee Forestry Division sold the tower in 1994.[^1] The new private owners rebuilt and restored it, finishing in 1998.[^1] The Register does not say when the tower was first built, and we found no account of who staffed it or of any fire it watched.[^1]

The Register records the tower as registered on March 17, 1999, on the nomination of Rob Norwood, and names Craig Davidson as its administrator.[^1] A Tennessee landforms list places the tower in Morgan County and gives its status as standing.[^2] That list also gives an elevation of 1,506 feet.[^2] The Register gives 958 feet for the site.[^1] The two may describe different spots, and we have not settled which is right.[^1][^2]

The Register credits new private owners with the tower's restoration, and the tower sits on Windswept Farm.[^1] We found no public access information, so anyone interested should ask Windswept Farm before visiting.[^1]

[^2]: Lookout towers, Tennessee Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
