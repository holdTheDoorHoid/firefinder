The Blackburn Lookout Tower was in Claiborne Parish, and the Wikipedia list gives Blackburn as its nearest town.[^1] The Central US Lookouts index also places it in Claiborne Parish.[^2] TopoZone lists it under the USGS map named Blackburn.[^3]

The list's history section gives some background on the state's towers.[^1] In 1915 funds went to fire protection work, which made it possible to hire lookout staff.[^1] The first steel fire tower went up near Bogalusa in 1922, and the second near Urania in 1923.[^1] The number of fire towers reached 39 by 1943 and 56 by 1950.[^1]

The list marks the Blackburn tower as torn down.[^1] The note also says aerial photos from 1998 show the tower.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors. Ask the landowner before going onto any land you do not know.
