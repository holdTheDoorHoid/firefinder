Hunt Peak sits in Boundary County, Idaho, about nine miles northeast of Coolin, in the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1] A camp was set up on the peak in 1927, and an L-4 cab was built there in 1931.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page describes the 1931 cabin as a gable-roofed ground cabin of the L-4 type.[^3]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site names Mr. Ashbaugh for 1927, citing a Forest Service photo collection, and Mr. Ryder Chronic for 1931 and 1932.[^2] A 1935 photo in the Civilian Conservation Corps in Idaho collection shows a Hunt Peak crew, with a foreman from Big Creek Camp F-127, running a telephone line to the lookout tower.[^4]

The lookout was abandoned in 1935.[^1][^2] The firelookout.com page marks the cabin as gone and gives no end year, only a question mark.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the peak as a hike-in site.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
