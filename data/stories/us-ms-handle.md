The register lists the Handle Lookout Site in Winston County, Mississippi, at 596 feet (182 meters), with coordinates near 32.9454 north and 88.8378 west.[^1] It is registered as US 1617, MS 2, on May 27, 2016, on the nomination of Ron Stafford.[^1]

An encyclopedia entry describes Handle as a post-hamlet of Winston County, roughly 15 miles southwest of Louisville, the seat of the county.[^2]

The register notes that by November 2012 the lookout had been felled, and its remains lay on the ground beside its former position.[^1]

No source we read describes the site after 2012, and none gives its access status. The register page links to Google Earth imagery, which we did not view for this story.[^1]
