The Civilian Conservation Corps built a lookout cab on Dow Butte in 1939.[^1] The butte sits in the Lassen National Forest, in Lassen County, California.[^1] The cab was a 14-foot-square R5-C3 design, and the register counts it among just five cabs of that type in Region 5.[^1]

The Forest Service staffed the lookout until 1982.[^1] A former engine crew member recalled that the lookout's occupant was a woman and a retired schoolteacher, and that in 1971 the Bogard Ranger Station engine crew redid the concrete stairs running up from the parking lot.[^1]

A December 1994 item from the Feather River Bulletin, quoted by the Forest Lookouts history, says the lookout had been moved to the Gallatin area.[^2] The item said it would open the following spring as a place for visitors and interpretation.[^2] The register says it has been moved to an interpretive site at Eagle Lake, and a photo page places it at Gallatin Marina on the lake.[^1][^3]

The register lists the cab's registration as October 15, 1997.[^1] The register calls it the first NHLR-listed lookout relocated from its summit.[^1]

Today the Forest Service lists the former lookout as relocated to the south shore of Eagle Lake.[^4] The page mentions interpretive signs and calls the spot a good place to take in the view.[^4] A photo page marks the old summit as the place the lookout was moved from.[^3] We found no source describing what, if anything, remains on the summit.
