Brian Head Peak rises to 11,312 feet (3,448 m) in Iron County, Utah.[^1] The stone observation building stands on the summit, inside the Dixie National Forest.[^3][^2]

The Civilian Conservation Corps built it in 1934 and 1935, with workers from Camp F-16 at Duck Creek.[^2] Crews had to build a road up to the site, and the Forest Service says it is still there and reachable by sedan.[^2] Rock came from the site and from a quarry near the lower bend of the road, and a second building went up nearby to serve as a toilet.[^2]

The Forest Service describes the overlook as a recreation site first, with plaques inside to explain the view, and as an occasional fire lookout second.[^2] The building is also called the "Peak House" or "Brian Head Overlook."[^1]

Sierra Club volunteers restored it in the 1990s, re-tucking the masonry and re-laying the patio.[^2] In mid-November 2022 wind tore the roof off.[^1] Locals who saw the rubble alerted the Forest Service, which then reached out for help rebuilding it.[^1]

The rebuild took two years and drew in the Forest Service, HistoriCorps, the Zion Forever Project, Visit Cedar City, the Town of Brian Head and private donors.[^4] The rebuild reused the methods the CCC crews had employed, to preserve the structure's historical authenticity.[^4] The community celebrated the new structure on the summit, and Hoodline published its report on September 19, 2026.[^1][^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Utah list places Brian Head under "Unknown/Undocumented Sites." The association says a site will be moved to its lookout lists if evidence shows it was used as a regular wildland fire lookout.[^5] The Forest Service calls the fire use occasional, so the sources do not agree on how much the building was used for fire watch.[^2][^5]

Today, a hiking guide describes the building as a public overlook that a short drive or walk reaches, and it advises checking current access before a trip.[^3] The Forest Service page also reports a foundation that remains visible on a trail lower down, east of the overlook.[^2]
