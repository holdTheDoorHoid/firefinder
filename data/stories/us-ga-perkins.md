The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers names a Perkins Lookout Tower in Clay County, at 31.75100, -84.99243. The list gives its elevation as 505, without saying whether that is feet or metres.[^1] The same county has a separate Clay Lookout Tower, at 31.60156, -84.96881, listed under its own entry.[^1]

The page's map was revised on 22 March 2016.[^1] No source I could read describes what remains at the site today, so its current state is unknown. Anyone planning a visit should first check who owns the land and whether the site is open to the public.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
