Estes Mountain is in Custer County, Idaho, on the Challis National Forest.[^1] The Forest Lookouts listing gives its location as township 13 north, range 15 east, section 21.[^1]

The earliest entry is from 1922, when a forest guard named Mr. Wade was posted on the peak to watch for fires.[^1] In 1927 the site served as a secondary lookout and was fitted with an alidade, a sighting instrument.[^1] The listing says other improvements are not known.[^1]

The Forest Service around Salmon and Challis says that, since 1908, it has erected, looked after and staffed as many as 50 lookout sites.[^2] Its history page names Granite Mountain, Bluenose and Sheephorn, plus six others, as old lookouts that survive, unstaffed.[^2] Estes Mountain is not among the names it gives,[^2] and no source found describes the site today.
