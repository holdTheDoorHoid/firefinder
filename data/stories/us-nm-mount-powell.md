Mount Powell sits at 35.46735, -108.24101 in McKinley County, New Mexico.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists its tower as gone.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site files the tower under the Cibola National Forest.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the site now.

[^2]: Mount Powell, Forest Lookouts, https://westlookouts.weebly.com/mount-powell.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
