Snow Hill was a lookout in Monroe County, Pennsylvania.[^1][^4] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives its registry numbers as US 488 and PA 317.[^1]

The register says the site's first tower stood on a building.[^1] A wooden tower came next, and then a steel one.[^1] In 1935 the steel tower was relocated and given the name Dancing Ridge.[^1]

A separate register entry for Dancing Ridge describes a steel Blaw-Knox tower that came from Snow Hill and was taken down in 1975, and it dates the tower's construction to 1935.[^2] We read the 1935 date as the year the tower moved, although that entry uses the word "constructed."[^1][^2] The same entry carries a photo caption that refers to tower remains in 2000, which does not sit easily with the 1975 removal date.[^2]

A 2004 visit report, posted on a survey-marker site, found no tower standing at Dancing Ridge. It found only concrete footings and what may have been a platform at the base of the stairs.[^3] That report concerns Dancing Ridge, not the Snow Hill site.[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the Snow Hill site today, or whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2]
