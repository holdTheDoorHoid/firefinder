Siegel Mountain stands about seven miles south-southeast of Paradise, Montana, in Sanders County, inside the Lolo National Forest.[^1] Firelookout.com describes its lookout as a 50-foot L-4 tower built in 1936.[^1]

In October 1936 the Missoulian reported that Forest Service workers, with CCC crews, were putting up a lookout on the peak, and that Paradise would fall within its observation area.[^2]

The sources spell the name in different ways.[^1][^2] The newspaper wrote Seigle, while firelookout.com uses Siegel.[^1][^2]

Firelookout.com says the tower was destroyed in 1965.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks the Sanders County peak as a tower that is gone.[^3]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^3]
