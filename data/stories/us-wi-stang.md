The Stang fire tower stood in Chippewa County, Wisconsin, where sources place it either at Pine Lake or in the town of Sampson.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page for the tower credits the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources with it.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list places it at 45.28490, -91.47189.[^2]

Newspaper items from the Chippewa Herald-Telegram, as quoted on that page, give a few glimpses of the tower in use. A 1938 item mentions a watchman at the fire tower in Sampson, without naming the Stang tower.[^1] A 1941 item names the "Stang" tower at Pine Lake and says Norman Deminsky was employed there.[^1] That item says Pine Lake, while a 1996 report places the tower in the town of Sampson.[^1]

A Chippewa County Sheriff's Department call entry, as quoted on the same page, records a break-in at the tower reported by the Cornell Ranger Station in September 1996. It was the third break-in there that year. Nothing had been stolen, but the things inside had been tossed outdoors.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page marks the tower as removed.[^1] In December 2015 the Wisconsin DNR announced that its fire towers were being eliminated. Its forest protection director said replacement costs and newer technology were among the reasons.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
