Gold Creek Point is a former Forest Service lookout in Missoula County, on the Lolo National Forest, 12 miles southwest of Seeley Lake.[^1]

A lookout was already on the point by 1921. A Missoulian notice from that September, quoted by the Forest Lookouts site, describes it as in use for years and says it was being remodeled.[^2]

The firelookout.com record dates an L-4 cab on the point to 1933.[^1] The sources we read do not say whether that cab replaced the earlier lookout.

The cab was destroyed in 1953.[^1] The sources do not say how.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a ground lookout, with the status Gone.[^3] We found no source that describes what, if anything, is left on the point today, or whether it is open to visitors.
