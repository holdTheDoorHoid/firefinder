White River Butte was a fire lookout in Powell County, Montana, on the Flathead National Forest.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page gives its location as 21N-13W-28, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list places it at 47.55213 north, 113.28569 west.[^2][^3]

Firelookout.com dates the lookout to 1937, when it was a 10-foot pole tower of the L-4 type.[^1] The Sanders County Ledger describes that design as a 14-by-14-foot wood-frame cab with windows on every side, set either on the ground or raised on a pole or timber tower.[^4] The same firelookout.com record says the White River Butte tower was abandoned and is now gone.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list also marks it as a gone tower.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
