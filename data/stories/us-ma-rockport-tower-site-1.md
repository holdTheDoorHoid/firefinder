Pool Hill, in Essex County, once held a wooden fire tower 40 feet tall.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register files it as Rockport Tower Site #1, under the registry numbers US 20 and MA 20.[^1] The register calls the tower municipal and says it was first operated in 1931.[^1] A second Rockport site, on Pigeon Hill, has its own entry in the register.[^2]

The register gives the Pool Hill site an elevation of 227 feet (69 meters).[^1] It says the tower was used until 1941, and it now lists the site among former lookouts.[^1] The entry was registered on September 3, 2008.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on Pool Hill today, or whether the hilltop is open to visitors.

[^1]: Rockport Tower Site #1, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ma/rockport-tower-site-1/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Rockport Tower Site #2, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ma/rockport-tower-site-2/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
