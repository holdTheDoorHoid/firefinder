Canada Lookouts lists a British Columbia lookout called Swannell in the Bulkley-Nechako area, with an elevation of 1815. The listing gives no unit, but the figure is most likely metres, close to the summit height Wikipedia gives for the nearby mountain.[^1][^2] Its listed coordinates are latitude 53.353 and longitude 125.268.[^1]

Wikipedia describes Mount Swannell as a 1,821 m (5,974 ft) mountain in the Fawnie Range of the Nechako Plateau.[^2] The mountain's listed position is about 135 m from the lookout's coordinates, which suggests the lookout stands on the summit, though no source says so in as many words.[^1][^2]

The mountain is named for Frank Swannell, a surveyor who mapped the region in the 1920s.[^2] Wikipedia also places the summit in the northeastern end of Entiako Provincial Park.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
