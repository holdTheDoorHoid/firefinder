Big Knob is a summit in Madison County, North Carolina, at 35.88694 north and 82.55666 west.[^2] The lookout page names the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources as the agency that ran it.[^1]

The tower went up in 1948.[^1] It was a 73-foot Aermotor with a cab measuring 7 by 7.[^1] A North Carolina Geodetic Survey station, described in 1981, is recorded under the name Big Knob Lookout Tower.[^1] Its directions run along a state road to a gate where state maintenance ends, then follow a graveled road to the top.[^1]

The lookout page dates the removal of the tower to 2009.[^1]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2] The 1981 directions are the only access detail I found.[^1] With the tower gone, a visit should not be planned around climbing it.[^1]
