Buck Ridge is a former fire lookout site in Cumberland County, Pennsylvania.[^1] According to the register, the tower is a Blaw-Knox design dating to 1934, and it reached the site from Staley Knob in the 1970s.[^1]

The Staley's Knob entry, for a site in Adams County, gives a different history.[^2] Its first tree tower dated from 1904, a wooden tower replaced it in 1912, and that tower was replaced again in 1922.[^2] The same entry says the structure moved in 1934 and took the name Buck Ridge.[^2]

The two records disagree on when the tower reached Buck Ridge, with 1934 in one and the 1970s in the other.[^1][^2] Earlier still, the Forest Lookouts site lists a tree observation point 35 feet above the ground that was set up before 1918.[^3]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register recorded the site on September 16, 2008.[^1] The register lists the site as a former lookout, but no source says when, or whether, the tower was taken down.[^1] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
