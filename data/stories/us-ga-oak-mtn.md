The statewide list of Georgia lookout towers includes an entry called oak mtn, in Carroll County, at 1,360 feet of elevation.[^1] The coordinates are 33.56468 north and 85.01744 west, and the entry carries two registry tags, BLL-63469 and gfc-401.[^1]

Carroll County's comprehensive plan update places Oak Mountain just east of the City of Carrollton. It describes the view from the mountain as superb in every direction.[^2]

No source I found describes a tower on Oak Mountain today, so what remains at the site, and whether the public can reach it, is not known. Before planning a visit, check who owns the mountain and whether it is open to visitors, and do not climb any tower without permission.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Carroll County, Georgia Comprehensive Plan Update 2018, Chapter 2: Natural and Cultural Resources, Carroll County, https://www.carrollcountyga.gov/DocumentCenter/View/2449/4-Chapter-2---Natural-and-Cultural-Resources (accessed 2026-10-21).
