Jeru Peak sits about eight miles northeast of Coolin, in the Kaniksu National Forest, at a listed elevation of 6,371 feet.[^1] A camp was recorded on the site in 1931.[^1] In 1934 a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab went up.[^1][^2] The University of Idaho's Keeping Watch page describes the L-4 as an early prefabricated cabin, 14x14, with production running until 1952.[^4] The only lookout the sources name is Leonard Lynch, listed as lookout personnel for 1933.[^1][^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1955.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list gives the tower as gone.[^3]

The sources disagree on the county. One places the peak in Boundary County and the other in Bonner County.[^1][^3] No source describes what remains on the peak today. Idaho Fire Lookouts files the old tower under hike-in sites.[^2]
