The Tejon Mountain lookout site sits at 4,870 feet, or 1,484 meters, in Los Angeles County, California.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register lists it as US 2010 and CA 130.[^1]

The sources disagree on when it was built. The Register says the tower was in service by 1922, according to newspaper reports.[^1] It describes the cabin as small and meant only for observation, set on a short tower.[^1] The county's own reports point to a later date. By June 1936 the county had laid plans for a lookout tower near the Kern-Los Angeles County line, and a county annual report says the tower and an observer's cabin were erected in December 1936.[^2] The Register also dates surviving piers to 1936 and says a newer structure may have been built then.[^1] The Register's Built field reads "Prior to 1922," so the exact 1922 date in our record is too precise.[^1]

A county report for the year ending June 30, 1937 says an observer is posted at the tower under a cooperative arrangement among three counties.[^2] A page about the Sawmill Mountain lookout says its 30-foot steel tower was moved to Tejon by 1935.[^4] We could not confirm that this is the same tower.

The Register describes what is left: the remains of the wood cabin are still on site, and the piers are dated 1936.[^1] It gives no removal date.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list files the entry as a Ground site.[^3] The Register registered the site on April 15, 2020, on the nomination of Brad Eells.[^1]

Today the Register's 2019 photo caption shows the remains.[^1] We found no source that describes a standing tower or what the site looks like now, and no source about public access. The Register names the Los Angeles County Fire Department as the administrator.[^1] Check with that department before going to the site.
