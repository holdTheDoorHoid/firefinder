In 1962 smokejumpers erected a steel lookout tower in the Nisbet Forest, about three miles north of Prince Albert, Saskatchewan.[^1] The Department of Natural Resources annual report for 1962-63 records the work.[^1] A provincial listing gives the tower's height as 24.4 m (80 ft) and describes its frame as heavy steel.[^2]

The following year, department crews built tower cabins, including one at the Nisbet tower.[^1]

A note in the same provincial listing dates a Junction Ranger Tower in the Nisbet Forest to about 1916. The sources do not say whether that earlier structure is this tower.[^2]

I found no source that describes the site today, so I cannot say whether the tower still stands or whether the public can visit it.
