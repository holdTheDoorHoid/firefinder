The Russell Point Lookout Site was a 100-foot steel fire tower near Stevenson, in Jackson County, Alabama.[^1][^2]

The register says the Civilian Conservation Corps built the tower for the Alabama Department of Conservation, on land owned by Alabama Power, and that it was among the last jobs the CCC finished before it shut down.[^1] A July 1, 1942 report in the Alabama Journal, copied on a site about former lookouts, said the state's forestry division had finished the tower and that four more were under construction.[^2] A 1819 News item, "Keeping Alabama's historic lookout towers," adds that the CCC stopped building fire towers when the United States entered World War II.[^3]

The register dates the tower to 1942 and says it was likely removed before 1970.[^1] Today, it reports, just four concrete footings are left.[^1] Alabama Power owns the land, and we found no source that describes public access, so treat the site as private property and do not go looking for the footings without permission.[^1]

The sources do not agree on where the tower stood. The 1942 report places it near Stevenson.[^2] The register's coordinates, 33.8486 north and 85.9133 west, fall more than 100 kilometers south of Stevenson, which lies at about 34.87 north.[^1][^4] We could not settle which is right.
