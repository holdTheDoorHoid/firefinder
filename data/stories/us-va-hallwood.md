Hallwood is a fire tower listed in Accomack County, Virginia, at 37.87658, -75.59448.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks it as standing, with no registry number, owner, or date.[^1] The Forest Lookouts index places it in Accomack County and gives no history beyond that.[^2]

I could not find a build date, height, owner, staffing history, or named fire for this tower in the sources I could open. The only status I have comes from one undated directory entry, so treat "standing" as unconfirmed.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register's Virginia page lists 48 registered lookouts, and Hallwood is not one of them.[^3] There is therefore no register entry to draw on for its history.[^3]

I found no current access, rental, or visiting information.[^1] Before you plan a trip, check with Accomack County officials or the landowner, and do not climb the tower or walk onto its land without permission.
