The Lutts Lookout Tower stands in Wayne County, Tennessee.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as an 80-foot Aermotor LS-40 tower.[^1] The Register lists its build date as unknown, so none of the sources we read say when it went up.[^1]

Aermotor's LS-40 ranked among its most popular steel towers, and it debuted in 1917.[^2] Tennessee's Division of Forestry used it widely.[^2] A National Register study of the state's towers says the design stayed in wide use until the mid-1970s, when other ways of spotting fires took over.[^2]

The sources do not say who staffed this tower or whether it saw any notable fires.

The Register says the tower was once a Tennessee Forestry site and is now privately owned.[^1] A list of towers still standing as of March 26, 2014, printed in the same study, includes Lutts in Wayne County.[^2] Tennessee Landforms also marks it as standing.[^3] It gives the tower the number 7-25, which in the state's 1976 list means district 7, tower 25.[^3]

The Register does not describe any public access to the tower.[^1] Please do not climb it.
