Wing Mountain sits in Coconino County, Arizona, at 35.27358 north and 111.78361 west.[^1] An Arizona list on the Forest Lookouts site places it on the Coconino National Forest.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the structure type as unknown and the lookout as gone.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the mountain is open to visitors.

[^1]: Arizona Lookouts (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/az/az-co/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Arizona - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts, https://westlookouts.weebly.com/arizona.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
