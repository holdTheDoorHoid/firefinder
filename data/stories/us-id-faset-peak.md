Faset Peak Lookout stood in Bonner County on the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, about 6 miles southeast of Lakeview, at an elevation of 5,246 feet.[^1][^2] A log cabin and a 48-foot pole tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab went up in 1931.[^1][^2][^3] L-4 cab living quarters were added in 1943.[^1][^2]

In 1954 a steel tower was moved to the summit from Copper Mountain.[^1][^3] In 1963 a 53-foot treated timber tower with an R-6 cab was built.[^1][^2][^3] The sources list known lookout personnel from 1948 to 1972.[^1][^2]

The R-6 tower moved to Black Mountain in 1975, according to firelookout.com and the Weebly page. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page for Black Mountain gives 1976 for the same move.[^1][^3][^4] The NHLR record also says 1975.[^5] The log cabin burned in a forest fire in the 2010s.[^1][^2] The sources give only the decade.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts, in a post dated November 9, 2024, says the summit is still reachable by vehicle and is a popular spot for single-track and ATV riders.[^2] The post notes that the summit's electrical gear now serves as an electronic site. It also lists remnants of the cabin: some logs, hinges, stove pipe, footings, and an outhouse foundation and vault.[^2] No source here says whether the summit is restricted, and the tower itself is no longer on the site.[^2][^3]

The tower's new home is Black Mountain Lookout in Boundary County.[^4][^5] The NHLR record says the tower was moved in 2019 to a spot about half a mile east-northeast of its earlier Black Mountain site, and that it was to be added to a recreational rental program.[^4][^5] No source here says whether that plan went ahead.[^4][^5]
