The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower called Shepherd (Loc 2) in San Jacinto County and marks it gone.[^1]

A Texas towers list on tnlandforms.us gives the same coordinates but places the entry in Liberty County, with a source tag of TFS.[^2] The two lists disagree on the county.[^1][^2]

The coordinates match the Association's entry exactly, so the two lists appear to describe one site.[^1][^2]

The Association's list labels the two Shepherd sites Loc 1 and Loc 2. Loc 1's coordinates put it northwest of this site.[^1]

A 1947 federal survey description places a survey mark about 5.6 miles northwest of the Shepherd post office, roughly 124 feet from a tower the survey describes as abandoned.[^3] That mark's published position lies in the Loc 1 area, not at this site.[^1][^3]

No source read describes what remains at the Loc 2 site today, or whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Texas Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association (firelookout.org), https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Texas lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/txtowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Datasheet for station BL1105 (Shepherd RM 1), National Geodetic Survey, NOAA, https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=BL1105 (accessed 2026-10-21).
