John Day Mountain sits in Idaho County, about eight miles north-northeast of Riggins, on the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1]

In July 1914 the Idaho County Free Press reported that Mr. Ring had set up three lookout stations, one of them on John Day Mountain at the upper end of Little Slate Creek.[^3]

The lookout recorded here dates to 1939, a 20-foot platform.[^1][^3][^4] One site calls it a three-legged platform tower,[^1][^2] and another calls it a tree platform.[^3] The sources do not say whether Mr. Ring's 1914 station was the same structure.

Two sites date its abandonment to 1967.[^1][^3] A 2010 Forest Service list shows 1942 in its row beside the word abandoned, which does not match 1967.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as hike-in, and a state lookout list marks the platform as gone.[^2][^5] We found no source that describes what remains there now.
