Ulysses Mountain Lookout stands at 7,649 feet on the Salmon-Challis National Forest in Lemhi County, Idaho.[^1] Its 60-foot steel tower carried a 7-by-7-foot cab, and a log cabin served as living quarters for the lookout.[^1][^2][^3] The National Historic Lookout Register gives the build year as 1828 in its data fields, but its own description says 1928.[^1] The other two sites also say 1928, so the 1828 looks like a typo.[^1][^2][^3]

The lookout was regularly staffed through 1965, which Idaho Fire Lookouts gives as its last season, and that site names Robert Smith as known staff that year.[^3] The same site marks the tower as abandoned in 1968.[^3] Forest Fire Lookout says the tower has sat unused since the 1970s, so the sources disagree on when its working life ended.[^2][^3]

The log cabin did not survive.[^2][^3] Photos from 2012 show it still standing, and Forest Fire Lookout says a 2012 forest fire consumed it.[^2][^3] On a later visit, the Idaho writer found the cabin's foundation and burned remnants.[^3] The steel tower remains, but its ladder rungs are broken and its platforms are gone, and the writer decided not to climb it.[^3] An electronics site now sits at the top, though its installation date is unknown.[^3]

The register listed the tower on September 24, 2011, as US 924, ID 87.[^1] A lookout list at firelookout.org shows it as "Tower Standing."[^4] The sources do not describe current road access, gates or rentals, so public access is unknown.[^1][^2][^3] Given the broken rungs, anyone planning a visit should check with the Salmon-Challis National Forest first and should not climb the tower.[^3]
