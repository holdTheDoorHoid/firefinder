The Bismarck lookout is listed in Hot Spring County, Arkansas, on the Central US Lookouts website.[^1]

The Encyclopedia of Arkansas puts the state's peak count at 122 known fire towers. It reports that forty-eight were still standing in 1994, but does not establish whether any were operating.[^2] The same entry names fourteen lookouts on the National Historic Lookout Register, and Bismarck is not one of them.[^2] The people who staffed these towers often lived beside them, and their job was to spot smoke.[^2]

No source we found describes the Bismarck site today, so it is not known whether a tower remains there or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Bismarck - Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/bismarck.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Forest Fire Lookouts, Encyclopedia of Arkansas, https://encyclopediaofarkansas.net/entries/forest-fire-lookouts-7905/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
