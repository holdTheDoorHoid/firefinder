Limber Jim is a former lookout site in Union County, Oregon, on the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest, about 16 miles southwest of La Grande.[^3] The U.S. Forest Service administered it.[^1]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register gives 1936 as the year a tower was built on the site, and firelookout.com gives the same year.[^1][^3] The register says the tower was gone sometime in the 1940s, and firelookout.com lists it as abandoned in the 1940s.[^1][^3] Neither source says who staffed it or what became of the structure.

The photographs complicate the dates. The register captions a panorama "Limber Jim Lookout panoramic 3-30-1935," which would be March 30, 1935.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site records panorama photos taken on September 30, 1935.[^2] Both dates fall before the 1936 build year, and the sources do not explain the gap.[^1][^2]

The register lists the site as US 1838, OR 561, at 6,270 feet (1,911 m). It was registered on November 19, 2019, on the nomination of Tammy McLeod.[^1] We found no source describing what, if anything, is at the site now, or whether the public can reach it.
