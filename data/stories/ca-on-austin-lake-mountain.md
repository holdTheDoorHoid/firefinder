Firelookout.ca lists an Austin Lake Mountain fire tower in Algoma District, Ontario.[^1] The record gives its elevation as 622 m (2041 ft) and its coordinates as 47.52314, -84.30017.[^1]

A note in the record says the tower may stand on this mountain or on a nearby one.[^1] The writer is fairly sure it is on Austin Lake Mountain, because the tower is very prominent and visible from the surrounding area.[^1]

No source here describes the site today or says whether the tower still stands.

[^1]: Austin Lake Mountain Fire Tower, Firelookout.ca, https://firelookout.ca/lookouts/?slug=austin-lake-mountain-fire-tower (accessed 2026-10-20).
