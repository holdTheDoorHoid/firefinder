Ragged Mountain's lookout was a Maine Forestry District station in Penobscot County, and a new station was built there in 1910.[^1] It was fitted with instruments and a cooking outfit.[^1] In 1916 a cabin there burned. The cause is listed as unknown, though the report assumed fishermen or hunters had left the small stove burning. A new 12 by 14 cabin was built in its place.[^1]

The steel tower came later. A 36-foot tower was partly erected in 1917 and finished in 1918.[^1] A 1920 inventory lists a 48-foot steel tower, so the early sources disagree about how tall it was.[^1] A 1919 Maine Forest Service panoramic map, centred on the tower, places it in Township 4, Indian Purchase.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine list still shows the tower as standing. It carries an asterisk, which flags towers where the framework remains but the cab is no longer there.[^3] The list does not say when the cab came down.[^3] Visitor access and ownership are not documented in the sources I could reach, so please do not climb the tower. Ask the Maine Forest Service before any visit.
