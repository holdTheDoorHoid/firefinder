Bald Mountain lies in Baring, in Washington County, Maine, inside what is now the Moosehorn National Wildlife Refuge.[^1][^2]

A 2021 Sun Journal article says the tower there went up around 1937 and, at roughly 100 feet, was Maine's tallest fire lookout.[^2]

A 1951 photo taken from the tower, credited to the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, includes a view of Cranberry Lake.[^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says the tower was removed circa 1954, which is consistent with the 1951 photograph.[^1][^2]

The Maine list gives coordinates for the mountain and marks the tower as gone.[^3] The refuge's own site lists car, foot, bike and horseback as ways to enjoy it.[^4] We found no source that describes what remains on the summit today, or whether a trail reaches it.
