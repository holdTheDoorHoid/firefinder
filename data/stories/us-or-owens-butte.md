Owens Butte, also written Owing Butte, is a lookout site on the Umatilla National Forest in Umatilla County, Oregon.[^1] It stands about eight miles northeast of Ukiah, at an elevation of 4,584 feet.[^1]

The only dated entry on the Fire Lookout.com page is for 1931, and that entry gives no details.[^1] The page says forest maps listed the site as an emergency lookout station, and still did in 1948.[^1]

Both listings call the site abandoned.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association gives its type as unknown and places the site at 45.19180, -118.79310.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^2]: Oregon Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/or/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
