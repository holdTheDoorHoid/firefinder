Thunder Mountain's lookout was a 30-foot pole tower on the Bitterroot National Forest, about 16 miles south of the West Fork Ranger Station.[^1] It went up in 1940 and sat at an elevation of 7,699 feet.[^1][^2] One page calls the tower wooden and the other calls it pole, but both give a 30-foot L-4 design.[^1][^2]

The firelookout.com list names lookouts from 1946 into the early 1970s. Among them are Mr. and Mrs. Kenneth Hoover in 1946 and Bessie Sopher from 1953 to 1962.[^1] Newspaper items reprinted on montanalookouts.weebly.com describe Sopher as a longtime summer lookout.[^3] A 1962 item calls that season her twelfth on Thunder Mountain, which would mean she started in 1951, two years before the list first shows her in 1953.[^1][^3]

The sources disagree on the end date. One page says the tower was abandoned in 1973.[^2] The main firelookout.com page and the montanalookouts page both give 1975 and describe the tower as destroyed or removed.[^1][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association marks the tower as gone.[^4] We found no source describing what remains at the site or whether the public can reach it.
