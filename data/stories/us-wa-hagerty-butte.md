Hagerty Butte rises to about 3,600 feet in Yakima County, Washington, on the Yakima Indian Reservation.[^1][^4] The first lookout went up there in 1935 as a three-legged wooden tower that collapsed within its first year.[^1][^4] In 1937 a 68-foot steel tower replaced it, with a house at the base for the lookout to live in.[^1][^4]

A July 1938 item from the Goldendale Sentinel, reprinted on Rex's Fire Lookout Page, says Homer Lackey spent that summer on the lookout.[^3][^4] The lookout stood near the Vernon Parrish headquarters, and the item says Parrish was employing him.[^3][^4]

The tower is gone.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register and Firelookout.com both list it that way.[^1][^2] Rex's page marks the site as removed.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the hill today.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The register added the site in July 2012.[^1] WillhiteWeb says the site is on Yakama Nation land and that access is forbidden without special permission.[^4]
