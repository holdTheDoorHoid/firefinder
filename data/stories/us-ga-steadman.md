Steadman is a tower site in Richmond County, Georgia.[^1] The tnlandforms list of Georgia towers places it at 33.390412, -82.106239, and the map was last revised in March 2016.[^1]

The list's notes say its entries come from the USGS name database, from towers found on 1:100,000 USGS topographic maps and NGS data sheets, and from coordinates the Georgia Forestry Commission supplied for 118 towers.[^1] Steadman has no commission number on the list. Its source tag reads "topo", which fits the map side of that work, though the list does not say so in as many words.[^1] Its coordinates also carry six decimal places, where the Statenville and Staunton entries carry five.[^1] That may mean the point was read off a map rather than surveyed. This is our inference; the list does not state it.[^1]

No source found describes the site's current condition.[^1]

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
