Bison Mountain is in Powell County, on the Helena National Forest, and the Forest Lookouts site lists it as a lookout location.[^1] Its only dated record is a 1939 plan.[^1] A newspaper item of July 1, 1939, quoted on the site's Treasure Mountain page, reported a plan to move the Treasure Mountain lookout, south of Elliston, to a high point above the old Monarch mine, which the item called Bison Mountain.[^2] Before that, the lookout had been staffed only temporarily.[^2]

The Bison page describes the move only as a plan under consideration in 1939.[^1] The Treasure Mountain page adds a parenthetical note that the move apparently did not happen.[^2]

An archived wildmontana.org event listing describes a loop hike that begins at the Monarch Trailhead and reaches Bison Mountain.[^3] The listing describes the hike as a tour of the Electric Peak Recommended Wilderness Area and gives Elliston as the nearest town.[^3] It does not mention a tower or any other structure.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or says whether visitors may use the summit beyond that hike.
