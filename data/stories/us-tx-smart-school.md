The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower called Smart School in Hardin County and marks it gone.[^1]

A Forest Lookouts index of Texas towers lists the Smart School tower under Hardin County and the Texas Forest Service.[^2] A Texas towers list on tnlandforms.us gives the same county and coordinates, under the name Smart School Fire Lookout Tower.[^3]

An online place-data listing names a Smart School Fire Lookout Tower with a Silsbee address and coordinates close to the others. It classes the spot as a fire station and point of interest, which does not show whether a tower stands there.[^4]

The lists agree on the name and county, and the two that give coordinates match each other.[^1][^3]

No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
