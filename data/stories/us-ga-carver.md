The Georgia lookout list kept by tnlandforms.us places a tower called Carver in Muscogee County, at 32.485319, -84.869063 and 539 feet (164 m) above sea level.[^1]

A 1955 press item quoting a Harris County ranger describes the job as searching for telltale signs of smoke over Harris County's 147,000 acres of woodland.[^2] The same item says quick detection and reporting of a wildfire lets fire-suppression equipment reach the blaze sooner, and that lookout towers are staffed in Harris County and in 134 other Georgia counties whose forestry units are linked to the Georgia Forestry Commission.[^2] It concerns Harris County, not Muscogee County.[^2]

No source describes the site now.

[^2]: Harris County - FOREST LOOKOUTS, FOREST LOOKOUTS (eastern US), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/harris-county.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
