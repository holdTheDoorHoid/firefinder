Eagle Mountain rises in Polk County, Arkansas, inside the Ouachita National Forest.[^1] A Coast and Geodetic Survey station on its highest point was described in 1952.[^1] That description places the station about 13 miles south-southeast of Mena.[^1] The survey note describes a wooden structure on four legs, topped by a lookout cage, and puts its height at about 75 feet (23 m).[^1]

No source I found says whether that cage still stands today.[^1] A trail-guide page is titled Eagle Mountain Lookout and describes a 1.7-mile route that climbs about 800 feet.[^2] The same page says a stock SUV or pickup can manage it.[^2] A user report dated June 8, 2025 says the trail was washed out, with deep muddy trenches.[^2]

If you go, check road conditions first. The survey dates from 1952, and the trail page's condition notes come from user reports.[^1][^2]
