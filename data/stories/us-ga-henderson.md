Henderson is listed as a lookout tower in Houston County, Georgia, at 32.38868, -83.78768. The tnlandforms.us list gives its elevation as 503 with no unit printed, which reads as 503 feet (153 m). The list also gives it the code gfc-333.[^1]

The "gfc" code points to an older Georgia Forestry Commission list; the page says that list gave locations for 203 towers.[^1] The name "henderson" is the compiler's own label. This tower is number 258 on the tnlandforms.us list, and names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's creation.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 29 registered lookouts in Georgia, and this tower is not among them.[^2]

The list's map was revised in March 2016, so it may not reflect the site as it stands now.[^1] No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Georgia lookouts, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ga/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
