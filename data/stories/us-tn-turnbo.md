The Turnbo tower stood in Decatur County, Tennessee, with an elevation of 676 feet (206 m), and the state index marks it removed.[^1]

The index numbers it 8-5. Those are district and tower numbers, and the index draws them from a Tennessee Division of Forestry count of 208 tower sites around 1976.[^1] Its elevations come from USGS 10 m elevation data, adjusted to match benchmarks on USGS topographic maps where those exist.[^1]

The index also reports that numerous towers are gone, and that most former tower sites now sit on private land.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, so check who owns the ground before going to look for it.[^1]

[^1]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, Tennessee Landforms (tnlandforms.us), https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
