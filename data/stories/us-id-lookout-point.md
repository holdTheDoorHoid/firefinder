Lookout Point stood in the Caribou-Targhee National Forest, about 18 miles north-northeast of Dubois, in Clark County, Idaho.[^1] Its listed elevation is 8,716 feet.[^1][^2]

The lookout began as a cabin in 1932.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts page attributes those dates to Kresek.[^3] The cabin was abandoned in the 1950s.[^1][^2][^3] That page gives the site's location in township, range and section terms, as 13N-37E-24.[^3]

Firelookout.org's Idaho list files the site under ground lookouts, not towers, and marks it gone.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts describes it as a hike-in.[^2]

None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^3][^4] They also do not say whether the site is open to visitors, so check with the Caribou-Targhee National Forest before going.[^1]
