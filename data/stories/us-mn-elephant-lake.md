Elephant Lake is a ladder-style steel tower about 80 feet tall, in St. Louis County, Minnesota.[^1][^2] The register says the tower stood at Ash River from 1924 to 1935 before it came to Elephant Lake.[^1] The Civilian Conservation Corps built a tower at Elephant Lake in 1940, and the register says the CCC re-erected it there that year.[^1][^2] The register calls it likely the oldest remaining tower in Minnesota.[^1]

We found no source naming who staffed the tower or any fire it watched.

The register was filed on January 8, 2021, as US 1481, MN 16, with Jacob Hufnagle as nominator.[^1] It says the tower "currently houses communications equipment."[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list instead marks Elephant Lake as gone.[^3] The sources disagree, and we could not settle which is current.

Forest Lookouts places the tower in the Superior National Forest, while the register says the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources administers it.[^2][^1] We found no source on public access, so anyone planning a visit should ask the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources first.
