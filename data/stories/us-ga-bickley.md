The Bickley lookout was a 120-foot steel tower, constructed in 1949 and listed under Ware County and the Georgia Forestry Commission.[^1] The Georgia tower list tags it gfc-835 and places it at 31.40460 north and 82.58788 west.[^2] Bickley is an unincorporated community in Ware County, northwest of Waycross.[^3]

On April 10, 2006, the Ware County Board of Commissioners sold the lookout structure at public auction for $2,075.00.[^1] The county made the sale, although the record is headed with the state forestry commission's name, so who owned the tower at that point is unclear.[^1]

The Georgia list marks the tower "to be removed," and the Forest Lookouts record ends with the word "Removed."[^2][^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^3]
