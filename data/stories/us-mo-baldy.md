Baldy Lookout Tower was a fire lookout in Ripley County, Missouri, within the Mark Twain National Forest.[^1][^2]

A National Archives photograph of the tower is dated 26 April 1940.[^3] Its original caption places it in the Doniphan Ranger District and describes a 14-foot-square cab on a 67-foot (20.4 m) tower.[^3][^2]

A photo caption from the same month names Joe Donley as the Baldy Tower lookout and says he reported a fire to dispatch.[^2]

Wikidata lists the tower as a fire lookout tower at 863 feet (263 m) above sea level.[^4] The National Archives record names the depicted place as the Mark Twain National Forest, Missouri.[^3]

We found no source that describes the tower or its site today, or whether the public may visit.[^1] Check with the Mark Twain National Forest before going.[^1]
