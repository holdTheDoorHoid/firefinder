The Bangor lookout in Kitsap County was a 90-foot wooden tower with a house at its base.[^1][^4] The register dates it to 1953 and names the Washington State Division of Forestry/DNR as the administering agency.[^1] Firelookout.com places it about 10 miles north-northwest of Bremerton.[^2]

No staffing history or notable fires appear in the sources available for this story.

The tower came down in 1971.[^1][^2] A forest-lookouts page also describes an Air Warning Service station: a 14 x 32 cabin on private land. Willhite does not know whether the two are linked.[^3][^4]

Willhite places the site inside the fenced Bangor Trident Base, a naval base. His location rests on secondhand notes. He thinks the 1944 and 1951 aerial photos show the tower, but both predate the 1953 build date.[^4][^1]

Willhite lists access as "very restricted, naval base." No source describes what remains there.[^4]
