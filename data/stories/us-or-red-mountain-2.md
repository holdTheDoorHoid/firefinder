Red Mountain is a lookout site in the Umpqua National Forest, in Douglas County, Oregon.[^1] The first lookout there was a tree tower, built by the forest in about 1920.[^1][^2] A D-6 cupola cabin replaced it in 1928.[^1][^2]

Newspaper items from the 1920s and early 1930s name the lookout as H. P. Looney, also given as Henry Looney.[^3] A 1922 item describes Mr. and Mrs. Looney at the station, and a 1926 item notes that he had charge of it for a third summer.[^3] When a dry spell hit in November 1925, George Newman was placed in the station for a time.[^3]

A February 1930 item says a standard lookout house had replaced the tent once used. An August 1929 item describes progress on the new lookout house. The sources do not settle whether the house was finished in 1928 or 1929.[^3][^1]

The Register says the lookout was staffed into the 1960s.[^2]

In 1985 the cabin went to the Tiller Ranger Station and was set up as a display.[^1][^4] The Register reports that the cabin was restored after the move and is now a historic exhibit there.[^2]

Today the Weebly history says the cabin is on display at the Tiller Ranger Station.[^3] The sources do not give opening hours. We found no source that describes the Red Mountain summit today, so we cannot say what remains there.
