The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list gives a Trinity County tower the name Piney Woods (Piney), at 31.10826 north and 95.04127 west, and marks it as gone.[^1]

A Forest Lookouts page files a Trinity County tower under the name Piney Woods (T).[^2] The two names may belong to one tower, but the sources do not say so.

Edward Cavallerano's 2025 book, Fire in the Piney Woods, begins with the Piney Woods and the conditions that made fire prevention and detection necessary, and it finishes with the location and design of Texas lookout towers.[^3]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Texas Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Piney Woods (T) - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts (centraluslookouts.weebly.com), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/piney-woods-t.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Fire in the Piney Woods: Texas lookout towers, the forest service, and the Civilian Conservation Corps, SearchWorks catalog, Stanford University Libraries, https://searchworks.stanford.edu/selections/in00000760390 (accessed 2026-10-10).
