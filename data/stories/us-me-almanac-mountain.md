The Almanac Mountain tower is on Almanac Mountain, in Lakeville Plantation, Penobscot County, Maine.[^1][^3] The Forest Lookouts site lists it under the Maine Forestry District.[^1]

A lookout station was on the mountain before the tower.[^1] A state report from 1914 records a new station there, equipped with glasses, maps and other gear, along with a half-mile of telephone line.[^1] The 1920 inventory showed no new improvements.[^1]

The steel tower came later.[^1] A newspaper report from April 28, 1921, quoted by the Forest Lookouts site, says a five-man crew was putting up a 48-foot steel lookout tower there.[^1] The sources we read name no observer and no particular fire the tower watched. In 2008 the observer's camp at the site was burned in the fall, and the source does not say how.[^1]

The tower's later history is one of decline. A 2008 Maine lookout chapter report described it as in poor shape and badly vandalized, and said the Maine Forest Service wanted to take it down for safety reasons, with removal possible in 2009.[^2] The Forest Lookouts site says the cab was removed in 2009, leaving only the steel tower on the site.[^1] The state list still shows the tower as standing, with an asterisk that the list says means the cab is no longer in place.[^3]

We found no later record that confirms the tower's current condition, and the sources we read give no access information. The 2008 report treats the tower as a safety problem.[^2] Do not climb it, and ask the Maine Forest Service before going near the site.
