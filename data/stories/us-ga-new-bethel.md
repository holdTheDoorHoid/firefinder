The Georgia tower list places a lookout named "new bethel" in Dawson County, at 34.41361 north and 84.055 west.[^1] It lists the elevation as 1,366, which is about 416 m if read as feet.[^1] As with the other towers numbered 73 and above, the author says the names are the author's own, so "new bethel" is a label and does not show what the tower was called locally.[^1]

The list draws on USGS topographic maps and National Geodetic Survey datasheets, and it includes Georgia Forestry Commission coordinates.[^1] It credits Gary Weber and Ron Stafford with numerous tower locations.[^1] The author says the list was prompted by the FFLA site listing no Georgia towers in 2010.[^1]

The only remark on this entry is "Ron FL relocate." The page does not explain it, so it is unclear whether the tower was moved.[^1]

We found no source that describes the site today, so this account cannot say whether any structure remains there or whether the spot is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
