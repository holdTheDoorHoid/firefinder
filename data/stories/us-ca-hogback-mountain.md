The lookout on Hogback Mountain stands in the Shasta-Trinity National Forest in Shasta County.[^1][^4] The National Historic Lookout Register says the current structure was constructed in 1977.[^1] That date suggests an earlier lookout once stood on the site, but the sources I read do not name it.

The register describes the tower as California Region 5 Plan C16, a revision of the CL-100 all-steel design, with the cab set on a 9-foot brick tower.[^1]

The lookout is tied to the 1992 Fountain Fire. Wikipedia says a lookout atop Hogback Mountain was the first to spot the fire, at about 12:50 p.m. on August 20. It reported a column of smoke behind a ridge, by a historic drinking fountain on Highway 299, and the fire took its name from that fountain.[^2] Wikipedia cites its own sources for this account, so treat it as a lead to check rather than a final record.[^2]

The register lists the Forest Service as the administrator and gives an October 26, 2019 registration date, as US 1345, CA 168.[^1] Its photos date from 2008 and 2009.[^1] I found no later source on the tower's condition or staffing.

Today the Forest Service lists five lookouts and guard stations for rent, and Hogback is not one of them.[^3] I could not confirm whether the public may visit the tower. Check with the Shasta-Trinity National Forest before going, and do not climb it unless the Forest Service says the public may.
