Greenleaf Peak lies about four miles west-northwest of Stevenson, in Skamania County.[^2] Its lookout sat on a 10-foot wooden tower with a live-in cab.[^1][^3]

The register and firelookout.com date the lookout to 1938, but WillhiteWeb says the construction date is not known.[^1][^2][^3] Panoramic images taken from the roof in July 1934 match the cab on that 10-foot tower, and a phone line and flagpole already looked established by then.[^3]

In February 1942 the Forest Service told the state DNR that it was abandoning Greenleaf and Three Corner Rock. The state replied that it would rehabilitate both lookouts, and plans were set in motion for the state to acquire title to Greenleaf.[^3] A benchmark stamped "Greenleaf 1946" was set 47.3 feet from the tower that year.[^3]

The lookout's end came in 1961. The register says it was removed that year, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed. WillhiteWeb reports the destruction date as a claim, and says debris suggests the lookout was pushed over the hillside.[^1][^2][^3]

On a visit in December 2019, WillhiteWeb found a shed just below the summit, a stovepipe, parts of the tower and a rock platform to help flatten the lookout area. The views from the top were almost gone.[^3]

WillhiteWeb's route, checked as of 2020, runs from Highway 14 at the Skamania Lodge road, along gravel roads to a ridge west of the peak, and then a hike of about half a mile with 650 feet of climbing.[^3]
