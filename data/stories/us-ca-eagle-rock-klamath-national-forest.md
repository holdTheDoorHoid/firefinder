Eagle Rock is a former lookout site in the Klamath National Forest, in Siskiyou County, California.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives its elevation as 6,971 feet, or 2,125 meters.[^1]

A chronological history of the Klamath National Forest, quoted on the register page, has the tower under construction in July 1928.[^1][^2] The plan called for a 50-foot structure, built on a 12-foot base and topped by an 8-foot platform.[^1][^2] The same entry noted that the extra height would need more support cable than first planned.[^1][^2] It also says that Frank Joseph would man the tower in emergencies.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list calls the site a platform and marks it gone.[^3] The register added the site on January 1, 2026, with Darin McQuoid as nominator, under the numbers US 2114, CA 171.[^1]

The register's only description is that 1928 history, so no source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1] The register also includes a June 2025 image of the site, credited to Darin McQuoid.[^1] We found no information on public access, so ask the Klamath National Forest before you plan a visit.[^1]
