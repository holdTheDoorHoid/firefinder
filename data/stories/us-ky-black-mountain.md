The Black Mountain lookout stands on the summit in Harlan County, Kentucky, and the Register says it sits at the state's highest point.[^1] None of the sources I read give a build date or name who built it.

The Register's visit report from September 28, 2008 lists the tower as not staffed.[^1] That visit found the cab gone, its pieces on the ground near the base. The stairs were rotten and missing the first run, though the steel frame looked good.[^1] Wikipedia describes an abandoned metal fire lookout with the cab and wooden steps missing.[^2]

The Register lists Penn Virginia Coal Company as the administrator, and says a liability release is required to visit.[^1] Wikipedia says a Penn Virginia company once owned the summit and allowed access with a waiver, and that the state of Kentucky now owns the land, so no waiver is needed.[^2] Wikipedia gives no date for that change, and I could not confirm it from another source.

The road to the summit passes a Federal Aviation Administration radar dome, and radio towers and transmitter buildings stand around the top.[^2] Because the access rule is unsettled, do not climb the tower without first confirming permission.
