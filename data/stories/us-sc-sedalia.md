The Sedalia lookout stood in Union County, South Carolina.[^1] The eastern lookout register lists its agency only as the National Forest, and it marks the tower as removed.[^1]

Across the state, most tower sites covered about ten acres and included a year-round house for the operator.[^2] South Carolina's forestry agency ended its fire tower program on October 1, 1993.[^2]

No source describes what remains of the Sedalia tower, or whether the public can reach the spot. The Forest Service runs Sedalia Campground, open year-round in the Enoree Ranger District of the Francis Marion and Sumter National Forests.[^3] No source ties that campground to the tower's location, so do not assume the two are the same place.
