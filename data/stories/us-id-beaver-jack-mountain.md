Beaver Jack Mountain sits in Idaho County, Idaho, within the Bitterroot National Forest.[^1] It is about three miles north-northeast of the Magruder Ranger Station, at an elevation of 7,068 feet.[^1][^2]

The site began around 1918 as a camp with a tree platform.[^3] A log cabin went up on the mountain's easternmost point in 1920.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts page describes the cabin as built in a grange-hall style, with a gable roof.[^3]

One lookout is named in the sources.[^1][^2] Patricia Joslyn is listed as lookout personnel for 1945.[^1][^2]

The cabin was destroyed in 1957.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page says it was removed that year.[^3]

None of the three sources describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the public can visit it.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site hike-in.[^2]
