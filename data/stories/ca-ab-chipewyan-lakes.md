Chipewyan Lakes is a steel fire lookout in Alberta, 30 m (98 ft) tall and set on a site 615 m (2,018 ft) above sea level.[^1] The provincial register gives 2015 as the year the tower was built.[^1]

The register lists the tower as active.[^1] Its access mode is recorded as fly, which means the tower is reached by air rather than by road.[^1] The register records no weather station on site.[^1]

Alberta operates 127 fire lookouts, and lookout operators spot roughly 30% of wildfire starts annually.[^2] Each operator watches for and reports fires within 40 km (25 mi) of the site.[^2] A lookout observer staffs each site, usually from April to October.[^3]

Today the register still lists Chipewyan Lakes as active and reached by air.[^1] No source found describes the site as it stands now, or says whether the public may visit it.
