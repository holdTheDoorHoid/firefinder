Rivers Peak is in Mineral County, in the Lolo National Forest, about 13 miles southeast of Lookout Pass.[^1] The peak stands at 5,305 feet, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list gives the site's coordinates as 47.34079, -115.46566.[^1][^2]

The lookout dates to 1935, when an L-4 cab stood on a north spur of the peak.[^1] The page, titled Rivers Peak Fire Lookout Cabin, says the cab was destroyed in 1955, twenty years after 1935.[^1] The association's list marks the site as gone.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains of the site today, or whether the spot is open to the public. The page lists the Lolo National Forest for the site.[^1]

[^2]: Montana lookout list, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
