The Forest Lookouts history site files a Fluker lookout under Tangipahoa Parish and gives its location as 2S-6E-Sec.36.[^1] The site arranges its entries by state, with sections for Arkansas, Illinois, Louisiana, Minnesota and Missouri, and this entry sits in the Louisiana section.[^1] Fluker, also known as Hyde, is an unincorporated place in Tangipahoa Parish, and its ZIP code is 70436.[^3]

A station in the Louisiana climate network, named Fluker Fire Tower, is listed at about 30.83 north and 90.57 west, with an elevation of 67 meters.[^2] Its records begin in August 1983.[^2] The station shares the tower's name, but that alone does not show where the tower stood.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.[^1][^2]
