Charity Peak is a former lookout site on the Lolo National Forest in Missoula County, about 14 miles north-northwest of Missoula.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com lists its elevation as 6,378 feet.[^1] Its page is titled "Charity Peak Fire Lookout Cabin."[^1]

The lookout was a cabin set about an eighth of a mile northeast of the summit, and firelookout.com dates it to the 1940s.[^1] The same page says the cabin was abandoned in the 1950s.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list classes the site as a ground structure, not a tower, and marks it as gone.[^2]

Forest Lookouts places the site in Missoula County under the Lolo National Forest.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the cabin, or whether visitors can reach the summit.
