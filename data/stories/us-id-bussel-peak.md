Bussel Peak's lookout was a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, built in 1938 on the St. Joe National Forest, about 23 miles southeast of St. Maries in Shoshone County.[^1][^2] The site sits at 4,473 feet.[^1]

A post by Idaho Fire Lookouts, dated October 29, 2024, describes what the writers found there.[^2] They report the year 1939 written in the cement of the footings, even though the lookout is recorded as built in 1938.[^2] They also found two names written in the cement of another footing.[^2] The writers are confident the two men were part of a Civilian Conservation Corps crew in the St. Joe National Forest in 1939, but they could not identify them.[^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts has no documented staffing history for Bussel Peak.[^2]

Both sources say the tower was destroyed in 1958. Neither gives a cause.[^1][^2]

On the visit described in that post, the writers found footings, remnants of a wood stove, a lot of wire and two benchmarks. The writers say more writing in the cement may once have existed in other spots, but time and weather have worn it to illegibility.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site hike-in and destroyed.[^2] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page does not say whether the land is open to the public.[^2]
