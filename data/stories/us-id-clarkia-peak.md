Clarkia Peak was a 30-foot pole lookout tower with an L-4 cab on the St. Joe National Forest, one mile west of Clarkia in Shoshone County, Idaho.[^1][^3] The site is listed at 3,500 feet.[^1]

The tower was built in 1936 and destroyed in 1962, according to all three lookout listings we read.[^1][^2][^3]

A photo on the Idaho Fire Lookouts page is credited to the Fire Lookout Museum.[^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts classifies the site as drive-up.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^4]

The drive-up label is in the listing, but no source says whether a road still reaches the site. We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
