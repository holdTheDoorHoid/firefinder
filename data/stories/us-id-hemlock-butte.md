The Hemlock Butte lookout in Clearwater County stood at 5,861 feet, about 12 miles east-northeast of Bovill.[^1] Its record begins with a crow's nest in 1914, followed by a log cabin in 1917 and a platform in 1923.[^1] A 50-foot pole tower, listed as 7 by 7 feet, came in 1926, and a 74-foot L-6 tower followed in 1934.[^1] The record ends in 1969 with the lookout destroyed.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

A separate Hemlock Butte, at 6,053 feet, is staffed every summer on the Clearwater National Forest.[^2] Its elevation differs from the 5,861 feet of the lookout described above.[^1][^2] Its present 53-foot tower was built in 1954.[^2]
