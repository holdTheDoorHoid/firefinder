McGowan Peak is a 9,860-foot summit in the Sawtooth Range of Custer County, Idaho.[^2] It overlooks the southwest end of Stanley Lake.[^2]

A Custer County lookout listing describes the peak as a staffed lookout point in 1939, and notes that its facilities are not known.[^1]

The same listing puts the site in the Challis National Forest, under location code 12N-14E-29.[^1] Wikipedia instead puts the summit inside the Sawtooth Wilderness, which lies within the Sawtooth National Recreation Area, so the forest named in the listing needs checking.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the summit today. Wikipedia gives the easiest route to the top as class 3 scrambling.[^2]
