Jureano Mountain Lookout stands in Lemhi County, Idaho, within the Salmon-Challis National Forest.[^1][^2] A 1935 build gave the summit a 7-by-7 observation cabin on a steel tower, replacing an older wooden one.[^1][^4][^5] The register and a local history site put the tower at 60 feet, while a 1982 guide by Bing Young, quoted on a climbing site, says 70.[^1][^4][^5]

Newspaper items quoted on the local history site show the lookout staffed into 1964.[^4] A 1932 item mentions a woman joining her husband there, and a 1951 item says a couple was manning the post.[^4] In July 1956 the lookout's staffer detected a 300-acre fire on Owl Creek.[^4] Items from 1962 and 1964 show seasonal staffing, including a summer lookout stationed there during fire season, and a family leaving in September 1964 when the summer staffer went back to college.[^4] The register and Idaho Fire Lookouts put the last staffing in the 1950s, which these later items contradict.[^1][^3]

A climbing guide says the lookout moved from a primary role to emergency duty and was then abandoned, with no date given.[^5] Firelookout.com says the structures were last in use in the 1950s.[^2] The National Historic Lookout Register registered the site on September 30, 2020, nominated by Brad Eells.[^1]

Today the summit can be reached by road. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as drive-up, and the climbing guide describes a ridge road leading to the top.[^3][^5] The guide says the tower appears to be out of use, warns of caution about climbing it, and advises confirming current road status with the Forest Service before going.[^5]
