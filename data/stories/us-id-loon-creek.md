Loon Creek Point Lookout stood at about 8,364 feet in the Challis National Forest.[^3] Its site now lies within the Frank Church River of No Return Wilderness.[^1]

A 1937 newspaper item, quoted by Forest Lookouts, describes Civilian Conservation Corps enrollees at the Loon Creek spike camp who were building a Forest Service lookout and who also tried their luck at finding gold in the creek.[^2] The sources give 1938 as the build year for an L-4 cab on a log crib, and one describes the cab as ground-mounted.[^1][^2][^3]

The lookout was last staffed full time in the early 1970s.[^1][^3]

A 2007 forest fire destroyed it, according to firelookout.com.[^1] Forest Lookouts names the Red Bluff Fire and gives the date as August 13, 2007.[^2] A firelookout.org report on the 2007 season calls it the only confirmed loss from wildfire among the dozens of fire lookouts the report says were threatened that year.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^3] None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, is left on the site today.
