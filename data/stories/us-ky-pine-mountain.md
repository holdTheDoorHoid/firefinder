The lookout on Pine Mountain near Elva, in Letcher County, Kentucky, was nearly finished by July 1931.[^1][^2] A Lexington newspaper of that month, quoted on a regional lookout history site, said the state forestry department was building it with the national government.[^2] The station was to have telephone links, so a fire could be reported at once.[^2]

The Register describes the structure as an Aermotor LS-60 tower.[^1] A 1944 notice said an association would name a forest keeper to watch for fires from a lookout station on Pine Mountain.[^2] That notice does not say whether anyone was hired, and it does not name Elva, so linking it to this tower is an assumption.[^2]

The Register lists the tower as registered on January 2, 2026, with the Pine Mountain Trail Conference as administrator.[^1] Its entry gives no condition report. We found no source covering the tower's fate between 1944 and that listing.[^1][^2]

The Register says nothing about public access.[^1] We found no visitor information, so check with the Pine Mountain Trail Conference before any visit, and do not climb a structure whose status is unconfirmed.

[^2]: Pine Mountain (Elva), Forest Lookouts (easternuslookouts.weebly.com), quoting the Lexington Herald-Leader (1931) and the Middlesboro Daily News (1944), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/pine-mountain-elva.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
