Mount Shaw is a 2,990-foot peak in Carroll County, in the towns of Moultonborough and Tuftonboro.[^1] The mountain is a remnant of an ancient volcanic ring dike, and it is the tallest peak in the Ossipee Mountains.[^1] The Ossipee range sits north of Lake Winnipesaukee.[^2] The topographic isolation of the Ossipee Mountains gives Shaw 2,330 feet of prominence over the low ground toward the Sandwich Range, one of twelve New Hampshire peaks with more than 2,000 feet of prominence.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Mount Shaw tower as gone.[^3] NH Family Hikes places Mount Shaw among the mountains that once had a fire tower, so the two sources agree the tower is no longer there.[^3][^4]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today.
