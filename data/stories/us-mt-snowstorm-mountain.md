Snowstorm Mountain lies in the Lolo National Forest, about 17 miles north-northwest of Hot Springs in Sanders County, at an elevation of 6,992 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a ground lookout that is now gone.[^2]

The firelookout.com page lists an L-4 cab here in 1933.[^1] The Sanders County Ledger describes the L-4 as a wood-frame cab with windows on every side, and says it was far and away the most popular live-in lookout.[^3] The same article says the cab could stand on the ground or on pole or timber towers as tall as 100 feet.[^3]

The firelookout.com page says the cab was destroyed in 1962.[^1]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
