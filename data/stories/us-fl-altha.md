Altha is the name of a fire lookout tower in Calhoun County, Florida, according to the Eastern US Lookouts index.[^1] The tower shares its name with the town of Altha; Wikipedia places the town in Calhoun County, in North Florida.[^2]

Neither lookout register I checked lists it. The National Historic Lookout Register names 31 lookouts in Florida, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists 6 former lookout sites in the state. Altha is on neither list.[^3][^4]

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
