Beargrass Lookout stood in the Nez Perce National Forest, at an elevation of 5,910 feet.[^1][^2]

All three site pages give 1930, or about 1930, for an L-4 tower.[^1][^2][^3] According to a Forest Service guide to lookout styles, the L-4, also called an Aladdin, measured 14 by 14 feet and was supplied pre-cut.[^4]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page lists staff for 1922, including C.A. Talcott and Jess Upham, and cites a book titled Lochsa by Louis F. Harting.[^1] That date is eight years before the 1930 tower.[^1] The sources we read do not say what stood on the summit in 1922, and they do not name anyone who staffed the L-4 tower.

The tower was abandoned in 1950, and the three site pages agree on that year.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the tower as gone.[^5]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page carries a "Drive Up" tag. No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it now.[^1]
