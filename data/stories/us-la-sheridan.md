The Sheridan lookout tower is in Washington Parish, Louisiana.[^1] Wikipedia's list of Louisiana towers says it is still standing and in active use, but it gives no date for that use.[^1] We found no build date, builder or height.

A U.S. Geological Survey rain gauge carries the tower's name. The station is registered as "Sheridan Fire Tower, LA Daily Rainfall 8405" in Washington Parish.[^2] Its position was interpolated from a map, and its record gives no construction date.[^2] The station sits more than a kilometre from the position the list gives for the tower, so we cannot say the gauge stands on the tower site.[^1][^2]

The list notes that 1945 USGS maps place a Louisiana National Guard target range just south of the tower.[^1] We do not know whether that range is still in use.

The state's first steel fire tower went up near Bogalusa in 1922, and by 1943 thirty-nine fire towers had been built.[^1] We found no source that gives Sheridan's own date.

The station's USGS page listed no data types when we read it, so we cannot tell whether the gauge still records.[^2] We found nothing on who owns the tower, who staffed it or whether it ever saw a fire. Its access is unknown. Do not climb it, and ask the owner before going near the site.

[^1]: List of fire lookout towers in Louisiana, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_fire_lookout_towers_in_Louisiana (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Sheridan Fire Tower, LA Daily Rainfall 8405, USGS Water Data for the Nation, https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/USGS-305100089590000 (accessed 2026-10-09).
