A Georgia lookout list places a tower called "clarking" in Charlton County.[^1] The list gives its elevation as 173 with no unit; if that figure is in feet, the site sits about 52.7 m above sea level.[^1] The same row adds the words "st george" and two register numbers, BLL-65229 and gfc-833. The list does not say whether "st george" is a second name, a town, or something else.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today or whether the public can reach it.[^1]
