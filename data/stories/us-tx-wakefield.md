A Texas Forest Service list places the Wakefield tower in Polk County.[^2] A Tennessee Landforms table gives its coordinates as 31.07389, -94.82611.[^5]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the station in 1949.[^3] The station sat in a clearing about five miles north of Corrigan and 0.35 mile west of U.S. Highway 59.[^3] The tower stood on four steel legs, with a house on top under a pyramid-shaped roof. The survey gives an overall height of about 110 feet.[^3]

The lookout shares its name with the community of Wakefield in northern Polk County.[^4] The Texas State Historical Association says the community's name recognizes W. D. Wakefield, an early settler in the area.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas table lists the tower as gone.[^1] The 1949 description is the only detailed account of the structure here, and apart from that table, no source describes the site today.[^1][^3]

Before going, check with the landowner or the Texas Forest Service, and do not climb any structure you find.
