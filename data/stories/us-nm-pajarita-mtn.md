Pajarita Mountain sits in Otero County, New Mexico, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower there as standing.[^1] The list gives coordinates for it, but no builder, build date, height or NHLR/FFLOS number.[^1]

The oldest record we found is a 1963 topographic map, as described on the Pastmaps site. The map shows a lookout tower at the summit, and its description says Mescalero Apache Indian Reservation lands dominate the survey.[^2] The same description says unimproved roads and a jeep trail were then the only way into the interior. That describes 1963, not today.[^2]

We found no account of who staffed the tower or of any fire it watched. We also found no later report on its condition, so the "standing" label on the list carries no date.[^1]

We did not find any public visiting arrangement. If the land is still reservation land, tribal rules may govern access, and we could not confirm them. Please check before going, and do not climb a structure you cannot reach with permission.

[^1]: New Mexico Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: 1963 Map of Pajarita Mountain, Otero County, NM, Pastmaps, https://pastmaps.com/map/pajarita-mountain-otero-county-nm-usgs-topo-1963-p1967 (accessed 2026-10-09).
