Maternity Ridge was a camp, not a lookout tower.[^1] Firelookout.com dates the camp to the 1930s and lists it as abandoned.[^1] The sources give the decade only, not a single year.[^1] The site lies in the St. Joe National Forest.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list places it in Shoshone County, as a camp with the status Abandoned.[^2] A Shoshone County lookout list gives its location as 45N-3E-7.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts also lists the camp as abandoned.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it. No listing mentions a tower, cabin or cab at the site. Anyone planning a visit should check with the St. Joe National Forest first.
