The Forest Fire Lookout Association's West Virginia list places a Coalwood structure in McDowell County, at 37.37891, -81.65844.[^1] It types the structure as a platform tower and files it under non-lookout structures rather than lookouts. The list marks it gone.[^1] The Eastern US lookouts site also lists Coalwood in McDowell County.[^2]

That placement suggests the association does not count Coalwood as a fire lookout.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: West Virginia Fire Towers, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wv/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Coalwood, Forest Lookouts (eastern US), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/coalwood.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
