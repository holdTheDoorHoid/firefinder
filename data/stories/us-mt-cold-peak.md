Cold Peak was a lookout in Mineral County, Montana, two miles south of St. Regis.[^1] The page places the site in the Lolo National Forest and gives its elevation as 5,001 feet.[^1] Its page is titled Cold Peak Fire Lookout Cabin.[^1]

The page dates the lookout's L-4 cab to 1936.[^1] It says the cab was destroyed in 1955.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a ground-type lookout that is gone, at 47.26812 north and 115.11354 west.[^2] Both sources record the lookout as destroyed or gone.[^1][^2]

Nothing we read says what, if anything, remains at the site today. The sources also do not say whether the spot is open to visitors, so anyone planning a trip should check with the Lolo National Forest first.

[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
