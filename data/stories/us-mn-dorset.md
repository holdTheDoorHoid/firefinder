The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Minnesota lookouts marks the Dorset tower in Hubbard County as gone.[^1] Its listed position is about 46.96 north and 94.98 west.[^1]

A Hubbard County list of towers lists the Dorset tower at 86 feet, a stairway design, erected in 1966.[^2] It codes the owner as "Mn MFS," which we read as the Minnesota Forest Service.[^2]

The list dates the tower's removal to the 1990s.[^2] The same list places it on the west side of Dorset, beside a Great Northern rail line.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the spot or whether it is open to visitors. The rail line beside it is now the Heartland Trail, the only current detail we found.[^2]

[^1]: Minnesota Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Hubbard County Towers (PDF), Wikimedia Commons, https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/36/Hubbard_County_Towers.pdf (accessed 2026-10-10).
