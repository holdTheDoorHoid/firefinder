Mallard Lake sits in Iron County, Michigan, within the Ottawa National Forest.[^1] A November 1971 notice, quoted on a lookout history site, is the only record of the tower in these sources.[^1] It reports that Joseph Harn, the forest supervisor, had sent out bids to remove ten fire towers.[^1] It then lists the Mallard lookout tower as being on the Iron River District.[^1] The notice does not say whether Mallard was one of the ten.[^1]

Around the same time, Michigan was also disposing of its own fire towers.[^2] A Michigan fire tower history page says the state began doing so in 1970, and it ties the decline to aerial surveillance and a growing population.[^2]

No source here describes the site today, so whether anything from the tower still stands there is not known.[^1][^2]
