The Monahan Lookout stood in Vilas County, Wisconsin, on a small, high, wooded knob that scouts picked out in 1934.[^1][^2] The land had been bought for back taxes in 1932 by Edmund and Matilda Drager. Mr. Drager then granted the government an easement so the tower could be built.[^1][^2]

The tower was a 100-foot Model MC-39 Aermotor steel tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab and an inside stairway.[^1] The Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources administered it.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register recorded the tower in 2004, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site in 2023.[^1][^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites page says the tower was removed by August 2023.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list marks it as gone.[^3]

A photo on the Former Fire Lookout Sites page is captioned as a USGS survey marker shown after the demolition, and that marker is the only remnant the sources describe. No source describes the site itself today.[^2]
