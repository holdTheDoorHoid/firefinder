Snowy Summit Lookout stood in Idaho County, about 11 miles east-southeast of Pierce, at an elevation of 5,904 feet.[^1] Firelookout.com places it on the Clearwater National Forest, and Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the same elevation and forest.[^1][^3] The Forest Service list also gives 5,904 feet.[^2]

Firelookout.com dates the tower to the 1930s.[^1] The abandonment in the 1950s appears on firelookout.com, on the Forest Service list of Clearwater lookouts and on Idaho Fire Lookouts.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts says it has not yet recorded any staffing history for the lookout.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site Drive-Up.[^3] That tag suggests a road gets near the site. None of the sources we read describes what remains there now, or whether the site is open to the public.
