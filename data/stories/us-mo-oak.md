The Oak lookout stands in Butler County, on Mark Twain National Forest land.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page gives its location code as 26N-6E-27.[^1] A trail guide to the forest road that reaches it says the tower went up in 1941 as a New Deal project, possibly by the Civilian Conservation Corps, and credits Living New Deal for that information.[^2] The guide describes the tower as in fairly good condition, though it is out of service, and says its first stair was removed for safety.[^2]

According to the guide, the tower still stands roughly half a mile into the trail.[^2] It can no longer be climbed, and the guide calls it a landmark in the area.[^2] The road itself, Forest Service road 3104, is a gentle, scenic gravel road running through the Poplar Bluff sector of the forest.[^2]
