Hollofield was a forestry tower in Maryland's fire lookout network, which began in 1915 and used towers as a communication network against woodland fires.[^1][^2] The tower stood at the northeast edge of Ellicott City in Howard County, reached from Church Lane, on land now part of Patapsco Valley State Park.[^2] I could not find a build date or a design description for it in the sources I read.

A draft state report that covers towers managed as of December 2006 says the forestry tower was taken down and a modern communications tower took its place.[^2] The same report says the upper portion, without its cab, now serves as an observation platform at Merkle NRMA.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Hollofield site as gone.[^3] It also lists a tower at Patuxent River Park as relocated from Hollofield and marked standing with an asterisk, which the list uses for towers whose cab is no longer in place.[^3] The state report's Hollofield section names Merkle NRMA and not Patuxent River Park, so the two sources disagree about where the tower ended up.

Patuxent River Park once had a four-story observation tower that gave the highest vantage point in the park.[^4] After years of service, the tower was torn down.[^4] Hill Hiker's article describes the replacement, built with an inclined elevator so that visitors of all abilities can enjoy the view.[^4] The article does not mention Hollofield, so I have not linked the old tower to it.[^4]

The park's current public listing mentions an observation tower served by an elevator.[^5] I could not confirm that this is the Hollofield lookout. Check the park's current rules before going up any tower.
