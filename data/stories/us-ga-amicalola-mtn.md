Amicalola Mountain is a long ridge that rises near Dawsonville and runs northeast toward Springer Mountain.[^4] The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia towers places an Amicalola Mountain tower in Dawson County at 3,390, which we take to be feet (1,033 m) because the list states no unit.[^1] The list does not say whether this is the Frosty Mountain tower described below. SummitPost says the ridge's highest section is Frosty Mountain, which once held a Forest Service fire tower.[^4]

WillhiteWeb says Frosty Mountain appears to be a smaller summit on Amicalola Mountain. It lists the site as a former lookout in Dawson County, at 3,382 feet.[^2] A USGS party reached the summit in 1966, found a fire tower there, and placed a station benchmark labelled FROSTY MTN FT.[^2]

The tower is gone.[^3][^4] SummitPost says it was taken down, and that only concrete footings remain in the old clearing, partly hidden by undergrowth.[^4] Forest Lookouts marks its Frosty Mountain entry as removed.[^3]

Sources disagree on the county. Forest Lookouts lists Amicalola Mountain as a separate entry in Gilmer County, so it is unclear whether that entry and the Frosty Mountain tower describe the same structure.[^6][^3]

Today the summit lies within Chattahoochee-Oconee National Forest, and the Approach Trail passes over it.[^3][^5][^2] SummitPost calls that route very strenuous.[^4] No tower is left to climb, and the concrete footings are the only remains described.[^4]
