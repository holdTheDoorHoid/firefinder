Miller Mountain lies in the Lolo National Forest, about six miles southeast of Superior in Mineral County, Montana, at 5,385 feet.[^1] A visitor guide for Mineral County names Superior as the county seat.[^4] The county listing places the mountain at 47.08326 north and 114.70717 west.[^2] A Forest Service page puts the Superior Area on the western side of the Lolo National Forest.[^3]

The firelookout.com entry describes a cabin from the 1930s.[^1] The county list classes the site as Ground rather than Tower.[^2]

The two listings disagree on the cabin's status. The firelookout.com entry calls it abandoned, while the county list marks the site Gone.[^1][^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
