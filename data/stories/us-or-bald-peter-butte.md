Bald Peter Butte, a peak in Wasco County, Oregon, rises to 3,725 feet and once carried a fire lookout tower.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register dates the tower to the 1930s, when the U.S. Forest Service put it up, and says it is no longer there.[^1] Firelookout.com also lists the tower as gone and gives only the 1930s, with no further detail.[^2]

The two sources describe the location in different terms.[^2][^3] Firelookout.com places it about 27 miles southeast of Mount Hood, in the Mount Hood National Forest.[^2] A Rex's Fire Lookout Page entry gives it under the Warm Springs Indian Agency, at township 6S, range 11E, section 12.[^3]

The statewide lookout list on firelookout.org also marks the tower as gone.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today. None says whether the site is open to visitors.
