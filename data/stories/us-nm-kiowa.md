Kiowa Lookout sits on Kiowa Mountain in Rio Arriba County, New Mexico, on the El Rito Ranger District of the Carson National Forest.[^1][^2]

The tower was erected in 1923. It is a 50-foot steel Aermotor of the LL-25 model, and the Register counts it as one of only two LL-25 models still in place in the Southwestern Region.[^1][^2]

The observer's cabin at the base dates from 1935, when the Civilian Conservation Corps built it.[^1][^3] Interior stairs now replace the original outside ladder.[^1]

A 1988 thematic National Register nomination of Southwestern lookouts had too little information to say whether Kiowa was eligible.[^3]

The Register accepted Kiowa on December 30, 1996, on a nomination by Kurt L. Winchester, a district ranger. That 1996 entry describes the tower as still in active use.[^1]

A 2020 New Mexico report groups Kiowa with sites marked R, meaning restoration or rehabilitation is needed. It recommends a fresh eligibility review, with nomination if the lookout qualifies.[^3]

Today a lookout listing shows the tower as standing.[^4] No current staffing or rental notice turned up in the sources, so check with the Carson National Forest before planning a visit.
