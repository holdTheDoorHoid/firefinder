Tiff was a fire lookout tower in Washington County, Missouri, on land of the Mark Twain National Forest.[^1] Its record places it at Township 36 North, Range 1 East, Section 9.[^1] That record marks the tower as removed, without giving a date.[^1] The record says nothing about what remains at the site today.[^1]

[^1]: Tiff - Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/tiff.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
