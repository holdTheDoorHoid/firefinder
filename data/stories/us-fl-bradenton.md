The Bradenton Fire Tower is mapped in Manatee County, Florida, at about 27.45 degrees north and 82.50 degrees west.[^1][^2] Map records tag it as a lattice observation tower.[^1] Bradenton is the county seat and the county's largest city.[^3]

A listing of eastern U.S. forest lookouts gives its location as township 35 South, range 18 East, a land-survey grid reference.[^2] Mapcarta also gives the alternate name Manasota Fire Control Headquarters.[^4]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists 31 Florida lookouts, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists six former sites. Neither has an entry for Bradenton.[^5][^6]

No source describes the site today, so whether the tower still stands and whether the public can reach it are unknown.
