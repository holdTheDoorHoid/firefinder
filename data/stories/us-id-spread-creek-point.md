Spread Creek Point is a lookout site in Idaho County, on the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1] It lies about 16 miles east-southeast of the Red River Ranger Station, at 8,178 feet.[^1]

The structure was a 10-foot log tower with log walls, dating from the 1930s, and it was never completed.[^1][^2] The Forest Service's list of Nez Perce lookouts gives the same decade and describes a hewn log tower with dovetailed log walls. It also says the work was never finished.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts and the Forest Service both call the site abandoned.[^2][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site with the type "Tower" and the status "Gone."[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left at the site, or whether it is open to visitors. Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site a hike-in.[^2]
