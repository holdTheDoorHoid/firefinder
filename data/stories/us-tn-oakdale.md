Oakdale was a state forest fire tower in Morgan County, Tennessee.[^1] The state's tower list gives its elevation as 1,832 feet and its state forest number as 2-30, from a 1976 list of towers.[^1]

The tower first stood near the Jr. Mehlhorn Farm, off Highway 328 (formerly Highway 29).[^2] Later it was taken down and rebuilt on M. T. Davidson's farm near Wartburg, in the Lancing area.[^2] The state list still names the entry Oakdale and marks the tower as moved.[^1]

Across the state, the towers together formed the Division of Forestry's fire detection network between 1933 and 1975.[^3]

The genealogy page includes snapshots of the tower at its new home near Wartburg.[^2] The page also says a star at the top of the tower is visible for miles after dark.[^2] It is the only source we found on the reassembled tower, so its condition today is not confirmed.[^2]
