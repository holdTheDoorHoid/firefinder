Round Mountain was a fire tower in Cocke County, Tennessee.[^1] The Tennessee Landforms list gives its elevation as 4,314 ft (1,315 m).[^1] The list files the tower under the Cherokee National Forest, abbreviated CNF, and marks it removed.[^1][^2]

Cherokee National Forest runs a Round Mountain Campground close to the summit of a mountain of the same name in Cocke County.[^3] The campground's elevation is 3,100 ft (945 m).[^3] Sites go to whoever arrives first, and online booking is not offered at this time.[^3]

The two elevations do not match, and the sources do not say whether the campground sits on the old tower site.[^1][^3]

No source found describes what, if anything, remains of the tower today, or whether visitors can reach the old site.
