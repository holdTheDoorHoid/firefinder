Pinehill was a lookout tower on the Lolo National Forest in Flathead County, Montana, about five miles south of McGregor Lake.[^1] Firelookout.com puts the site at 6,410 feet, roughly 1,954 meters.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list gives its coordinates as 47.95586 north and 114.87630 west.[^2]

Firelookout.com dates a 50-foot L-4 tower here to 1940, about 15 meters tall.[^1] The same page says the tower was destroyed in 1962. If the 1940 date holds, it stood for about 22 years before that.[^1] The Montana list agrees that the tower is gone.[^2]

Neither source describes what remains at the site today or whether the public can reach it, so check with the Lolo National Forest, which manages the land, before you go.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Pinehill Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/pinehill.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Montana Lookouts (alphabetical list), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
