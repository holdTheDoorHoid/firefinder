The tower registered as Grass Knob Tower Site #1 stood in Brush Creek State Forest, in Scioto County, Ohio.[^1] The record puts the site at 1,320 feet (402 meters) and names the Ohio Department of Natural Resources as the agency that administered it.[^1]

On October 27, 1934, the Chillicothe Gazette reported that a Grass Knob Tower, north of Otway in northwestern Scioto County, would be manned by Perry Bonner of Mt. Joy.[^2] The two site records do not mention 1934, so whether that tower is this one is unconfirmed.[^1][^3]

The record says the tower was moved from this spot to a site beside US Route 23 and is no longer standing.[^1] The Site #2 record says the tower was moved along that highway from Brush Creek State Forest and was later removed altogether.[^3] The association's Ohio list also marks this site's entry as gone.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at this spot today. Brush Creek State Forest has two bridle trails, and hunting is allowed there in season.[^5] Wikipedia places the forest in Adams County, but the site record places this tower in Scioto County, so the sources disagree on the county.[^1][^5] Check with the state forest before you go.
