Nig Field was a lookout in Campbell County, Tennessee.[^1] The state's tower list puts the site at 3,241 feet above sea level and gives the tower the state forest number 2-14, from a 1976 list of towers.[^1]

A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description from 1961 places the tower on the highest part of Bowman Mountain, about 4.0 miles west of Caryville.[^2] Its glass-walled cab sat on a steel tower with four legs, 70 feet (about 21 metres) tall.[^2] The survey says the tower was locally known as the Bowman Lookout Tower.[^2] The list pairs Nig Field with that survey mark, GB2578, so the two sources give the same tower different names.[^1][^2]

Many of the state's towers went up in the 1930s, in partnership with the Civilian Conservation Corps.[^3] The state list says the corps erected 98 lookout towers in Tennessee from 1933 to 1941.[^1]

The state list now marks the tower as removed.[^1] The only description of the tower we found dates from 1961, so no source says what, if anything, remains on Bowman Mountain today.[^2] The list advises against climbing the towers, and says their steps are often missing or rotten.[^1]
