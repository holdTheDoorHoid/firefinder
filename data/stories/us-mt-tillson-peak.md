The Tillson Peak lookout was a log cabin, and firelookout.com dates it to 1928.[^1] The page places its crow's nest on Packrat Mountain, even though the lookout carries the Tillson Peak name.[^1] The Forest Lookouts index lists Packrat Mountain and Tillson Peak both under Powell County, but neither source we read explains how the two are related.[^2]

The same firelookout.com entry says the lookout was abandoned in 1949.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks the Tillson Peak site as a ground lookout that is gone.[^3]

Firelookout.com places the site in Powell County, on the Flathead National Forest.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it. Anyone interested should ask the forest before going.
