The register lists the Shiloh Lookout Tower in Rankin County, Mississippi, as US 1660, MS 26.[^1] It is a 100-foot Aermotor tower, and the register gives no build date for it.[^1] OpenStreetMap maps the structure as a lattice tower.[^2]

Inside, a straight staircase climbs through 14 landings and 15 flights.[^1] On a visit recorded as 7/9/22, the register found every stair still intact.[^1] The register does not name anyone who staffed the tower or any fire it watched.[^1]

The register says the Mississippi Forestry Commission has vacated the site, but it does not say when that happened.[^1] We found no other published account of the tower's history.[^1]

The register added the tower on July 10, 2022, and lists the forestry commission as its administrator.[^1] We found nothing about public access today, so please do not climb it.[^1] Ask the forestry commission before visiting the site.[^1]

[^1]: Shiloh Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ms/shiloh-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Shiloh Lookout Tower, OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/357997248 (accessed 2026-10-09).
