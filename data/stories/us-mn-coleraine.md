The Coleraine Fire Lookout Tower stands in Itasca County, Minnesota.[^3] A 1934 survey note places it 4.5 miles northeast of Grand Rapids and a mile southwest of Coleraine.[^1]

The sources do not agree on when it went up. The Forest Lookouts site quotes a 1929 entry that records a lookout tower going up that year.[^1] The same site's survey note says the tower was built in 1931 and is 95 feet high.[^1] We report both dates and could not settle which is right.

An antenna went up on the lookout in the fiscal year 1935 to 1936, according to a report the Forest Lookouts site quotes. The same report found the station test on the tower satisfactory.[^1] The report puts the antenna roughly five miles northeast of Grand Rapids, a slightly different figure from the survey's 4.5 miles.[^1]

We found no record of who staffed the tower or of any fire it watched. The Forest Lookouts listing shows "State" beside the county and names no agency.[^1]

Today the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^2] The 1934 survey described the approach: from Coleraine, head south and southwest on Highway 169 for 1.6 miles, and the tower is on the right, about 250 feet north of the highway.[^1] Those directions date from 1934, so they may be out of date.[^1] We found no current access rules, so we cannot say whether visitors may go up. Check before going, and do not climb the tower unless it is open to the public.
