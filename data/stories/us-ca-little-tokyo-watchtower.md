Wikidata lists the Little Tokyo Watchtower in Japanese Village Plaza, in the Little Tokyo district of Los Angeles.[^1]

A Wikidata entry classes it as a fire lookout tower.[^1] The Little Tokyo Community Impact Fund describes it as a Yagura Tower, a replica of a rural Japanese fire tower, which the Korean developer David Hyun built on First Street, where it anchors the plaza entrance.[^2]

The Impact Fund's timeline places the tower under 1983.[^2] CBS News dates Japanese Village Plaza to 1978.[^3] GOLDSEA says David Hyun developed the plaza in 1984.[^4] The timeline says the plaza was to begin a year after the tower.[^2] The dates do not match: CBS's 1978 differs from GOLDSEA's 1984.

A blog that documents Los Angeles County landmarks has a November 15, 2021 post with a photo caption showing its author and a companion at the watchtower, which the caption places outside the plaza.[^5] Wikidata puts it in the plaza, so the two sources differ on its position.[^1]

GOLDSEA describes the plaza, at 350 East First Street, as an outdoor mall and says it draws visitors who want a break.[^4]

No source in this record says whether the tower itself can be climbed.
