The Makinen lookout stood in St. Louis County, Minnesota.[^1] The Minnesota Forest Service owned the tower, and both lists date it to 1937.[^1][^2]

The lists give the tower a height of 100 feet and a ladder climb.[^1][^2] A 1952 survey note disagrees.[^1] It puts the tower at about 80 feet and describes a four-legged, steel-built structure with a lookout cage up top.[^1] The two figures do not match, so we cannot say which one is right.

The county list says the tower was removed between 1961 and 1966.[^1] The statewide list says a Coe Lake tower, two or three miles east-northeast of Makinen, might be the old Makinen tower moved, or might be a different one.[^1][^2] The county list dates that Coe Lake tower to 1969 or 1970, gives the Minnesota Forest Service as a possible owner, and marks it removed as well.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Makinen as gone.[^3] We found no source that describes what remains at the site, or whether the land is open to visitors.
