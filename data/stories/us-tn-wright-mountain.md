Wright Mountain in Fentress County has carried two lookout towers, according to the Register.[^1] The first lookout was a wooden tower the Division of Forestry erected in 1942.[^1] A lookout-history site lists the tower under Fentress County and the Division of Forestry.[^2]

In 1960 the Division replaced the wooden tower with an 80-foot Aermotor tower that has a 7-by-7-foot cab, and that is the tower the Register describes today.[^1] The Register gives September 17, 2011 as the registration date, with Kent D. Taylor as nominator.[^1]

The Register now lists Taylor as the owner and says the tower is privately owned and restored.[^1] It does not say when ownership changed, and I found no date for the restoration in the sources I read.[^1] Tennessee Landforms still lists the tower as standing.[^3]

I found no public access rule and no rental listing in the sources I read. The Register describes the tower as privately owned, so treat it as private property: do not climb it without the owner's permission.[^1]
