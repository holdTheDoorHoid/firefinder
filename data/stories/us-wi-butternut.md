The Butternut fire lookout stood in Ashland County, Wisconsin.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places it at 46.02372 north and 90.49791 west, and lists it as a tower that is gone.[^2] No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Butternut - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/butternut.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Wisconsin Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
