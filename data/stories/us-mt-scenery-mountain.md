Scenery Mountain is in Lincoln County, Montana, in the Kootenai National Forest.[^1][^2] The first structure on the site was an L-4 cab, built in 1936.[^2][^3]

In 1962 an R-6 cab on a 10-foot concrete base replaced it.[^3] A 1962 photo in the register shows the 1936 L-4 with the new R-6 under construction.[^3] The R-6 cab was used for emergencies in the 1980s.[^3] The register lists the site as registered on October 8, 2011.[^3]

The register dates the Forest Service's removal of the cab to 2009, and the 2009 chapter report describes the job.[^3][^4] FireLookout.com dates the removal to 2010.[^2] The chapter report says the chapter declined the job of taking the lookout down because of the lack of access.[^4] Forest Service crews then took the cab off its concrete block foundation, and a forest helicopter carried the materials off the mountain.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today. A Kootenai National Forest trail listing still says the lookout stands at the north end of the Cabinet Mountains Wilderness and is staffed from mid-June to mid-September.[^5] That conflicts with the removal accounts, so we treat the listing as out of date.[^3][^4] The trail, number 649, is reached from Cedar Creek Trail 141, and motor vehicles are not allowed on it.[^5]

During the July 2026 wildfires in the Kootenai National Forest, a trail closure order covered the Scenery Mountain Trail network, and a Montanian update from late July said it remained in place.[^6] Check the forest's current closure notices before you set out.
