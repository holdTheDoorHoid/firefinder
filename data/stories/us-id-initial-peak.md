The Initial Peak lookout stood on a summit in Kootenai County, in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1][^4] It was a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, built in 1934.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the summit's elevation as 4,060 feet.[^2]

All four sources we read agree that the tower was destroyed in 1950.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Only one source we read describes what the site looks like now, and we could not find a date for that description. A photo caption on the WillhiteWeb page says the footing is almost covered.[^3] The same page says that logging has opened sightlines to the south and west from the summit, and that the access road is good enough for most vehicles.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as drive-up.[^2] We did not find a source that says whether the site is open to the public, so check with the Forest Service before you go.
