Haystack Mountain lies about seven miles southeast of Sedro-Woolley, in Skagit County.[^1][^2] Its first structure was not a fire lookout.[^3] It was a cabin for the Aircraft Warning Service, a wartime watch for aircraft.[^4]

The Forest Lookouts page describes a cabin constructed on private land, and gives the station's dates as April 6, 1942 to October 16, 1943.[^3] At rural posts the usual building was a sleeping cabin, and it was hard to get people to staff them.[^4] Observers had to report every aircraft they saw or heard, around the clock.[^4]

After the station closed, the state kept the site.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register and firelookout.com instead say a cabin on the site was abandoned in 1946, four years after it was built.[^1][^2] We take that to be the same cabin, but we could not reconcile the 1943 closing with the 1946 date, and no source explains the gap.

The register names the Department of Natural Resources as administrator and reports that the lookout no longer exists.[^1] We found no source describing what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the public can get there. Ask the Department of Natural Resources before planning a visit.
