Delta Point was an enclosed tower in Shasta County, California.[^2] The Forest Lookouts pages place it in the Shasta National Forest.[^1][^3]

In June 1945 The Dunsmuir News reported that Delta Point was manned by Ray Norberg, a former cowhand from Wyoming.[^1]

The Shasta County list on the Forest Lookouts site gives Shasta National Forest for Delta Point. It gives Shasta-Trinity National Forest for Girard Ridge, another Shasta County site on the same list. The list uses both names, and the sources we read do not explain why.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Delta Point as Gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, stands at the site today, or whether the public can reach it. The association places the point at 40.96373, -122.41212.[^2]
