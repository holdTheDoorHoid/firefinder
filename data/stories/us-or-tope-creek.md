The Tope Creek Lookout sits about 15 miles north of Enterprise in Wallowa County, Oregon.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register credits the U.S. Forest Service with building it in 1936, and says the tower was later passed to the Oregon Department of Forestry.[^1] The state's historic site record gives the same c.1936 date but names the Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC) as builder, so the sources do not agree on who put it up.[^1][^3][^4]

The tower is a timber structure about 103 feet tall.[^1][^4] The sources also disagree about the cab. The Register gives an 8-by-8-foot L-6 cab, while firelookout.com gives 7 by 7 feet.[^1][^2]

The Register dates the end of staffing to the early 1970s and says the tower was then declared surplus.[^1] A staff list on firelookout.com covers 1954 through 1970, but it presents only the personnel the site knows of, so it may not be a full roster.[^2] The same site places a ground house with living quarters in the 1936 build, but a 2022 post on cherylhill.net quotes the owner dating the living quarters to the 1960s.[^2][^3]

Sale details also differ. According to firelookout.com, a state auction in 1978 sold off the land and tower.[^2] The 2022 post gives 1984 for an Oregon Department of Forestry auction, and says Brian Wizard bought the property in 1991 and found the living quarters and cabin heavily vandalized.[^3]

The Register lists Wizard as owner, says he restored the tower, and records its registration on May 19, 1997.[^1] The state record calls the site significantly altered and gives an approximate second date of around 1992 without saying what that date marks.[^4] Both the Register and firelookout.com describe the property as private, and the sources do not mention public access or any rental.[^1][^2] A 2022 account describes a tour of the site during a Forest Fire Lookout Association conference.[^3] Because the tower is private property, anyone who wants to see it should ask the owner first and should not climb it without permission.
