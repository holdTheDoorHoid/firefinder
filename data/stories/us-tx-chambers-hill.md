Chambers Hill was a steel lookout tower in the Sabine National Forest, in Sabine County, Texas.[^1][^2] A National Geodetic Survey station description says the U.S. Forest Service owned it.[^1] The same description says it had four steel legs and a small cabin on top, was painted silver, and stood 110 feet overall.[^1] The Texas lookout table gives a height of 100 feet, so the two sources disagree.[^1][^3] I have used the survey's figure.

The table gives a build year of about 1936, and I found no other source for a build year.[^3]

A Forest Service photograph dated 1960 shows the tower in the background of a view across Red Hills Lake, taken from the dam.[^4]

The table lists the removal date as unknown, and the Texas lookout list now marks the tower as gone.[^3][^2]

Nothing I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it. Anyone planning a visit should ask the Forest Service before going.
