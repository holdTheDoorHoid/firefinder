Floyd Lookout Tower stood in Washington County, Missouri, on land in the Clark National Forest.[^1] OpenStreetMap gives the tower's map point an elevation of 428 metres.[^2]

A May 1955 newspaper item quoted on the Forest Lookouts site describes a cougar that a county conservation agent spotted while on patrol along a "remote Floyd Fire Tower road" in Washington County.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page lists the tower as removed.[^1]

OpenStreetMap still has a point named Floyd Lookout Tower, at about 37.98 degrees north and 90.98 degrees west, tagged as an observation tower. The tags do not say whether anything remains.[^2]

The two sources we read do not describe what remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Floyd, Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/floyd.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Floyd Lookout Tower, OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/358066510 (accessed 2026-10-10).
