Everett is a fire lookout in Beaufort County, listed in North Carolina's lookout directory under the state Division of Forest Resources.[^1] That directory dates the tower to 1936, when the Civilian Conservation Corps built it, standing 99 feet 9 inches high.[^1]

A U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey description from 1954 gives a different figure. It calls the structure a steel tower 110 feet high, with four legs on a concrete foundation, and says the survey point sat at the top of its cage.[^2] The sources do not explain the gap between the two heights.[^1][^2]

A 1967 recovery note from the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers rated the station as in good condition and repeated the 110-foot height.[^2]

An OpenStreetMap entry still names an Everett Lookout Tower at this spot.[^3] No source describes what remains there today, or whether visitors can reach the site.
