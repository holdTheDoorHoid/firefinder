Jersey Mountain sits in the Nez Perce National Forest, about 24 miles south-southwest of Elk City.[^1][^2][^3] A 1930 University of Idaho photo record identifies Foskett as the lookout, with Hugh Eminger serving as smokechaser.[^3] Another 1930 photo record shows a tent at the site that was home to A.W. (Red) Foskett, the lookout.[^5] A 1933 photo in the same collection shows Everett Haney sitting on a front porch.[^4]

A log cupola cabin went up in 1931, and in 1953 a 41-foot treated timber tower with an L-4 cab was built.[^1][^2] Soon after the 1953 tower was built, the cupola was taken off the cabin and its place roofed over.[^1][^2] The two pages describe the L-4 differently. Idaho Fire Lookouts describes an L-4 cab on the tower,[^1] while firelookout.com calls the whole 41-foot structure an L-4 tower.[^2] Both give the same height and material.[^1][^2]

Emergency use of the lookout continued into the 1990s.[^2] A forest fire in 1992 burned both structures.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as drive-up, but no source describes what remains there today or whether the area is open to visitors.[^1]
