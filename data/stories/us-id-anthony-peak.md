Anthony Peak Lookout stood on a peak in Shoshone County, about 13 miles northeast of Bovill, at 4,686 feet, within St. Joe National Forest.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site, citing Kresek, dates the tower to 1936.[^3] It was a 20-foot pole structure with an L-4 cab.[^3] A log cabin stood about a quarter mile north of it.[^1]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site lists two known staff, each from a Kellogg Evening News item: Ragnar Benson in 1960 and Sherman Rouse in 1967.[^2] Firelookout.com lists only Benson, for 1960.[^1]

Firelookout.com says the lookout burned in the winter of 1964 to 1965 and that ruins were left.[^1] The 1967 staff entry does not fit that date, and the sources do not say which is right.[^1][^2]

The Idaho site labels the remains drive-up ruins.[^2] A 1959 U.S. Geological Survey station description of the peak, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, says to pack up a trail for about three miles from the end of truck travel to the top.[^3] The two accounts do not match, so check current access before heading out.[^2][^3]
