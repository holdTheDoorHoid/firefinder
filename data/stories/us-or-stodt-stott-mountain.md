Stodt (Stott) Mountain rises to 3,128 feet in Lincoln County, Oregon.[^1] Construction was under way in 1953, when a Lincoln FPA annual report put the 30-foot split-cedar tower about 15 percent complete.[^3] The register and firelookout.com date the tower to 1954 and describe it as 32 feet tall, so the sources disagree on its height.[^1][^2] The 1954 report lists a 14-by-14 cab, a railing and a lightning arrestor, at a cost of $1,922.98.[^3] The register adds that the cables, bolts, stairs and cab came from other Lincoln FPA towers.[^1]

In 1959 galvanized steel sheet was added below the windows against moisture, and the cab's exterior was painted.[^3]

Staffing is recorded for the late 1960s.[^3] The Forest Log reported in 1967 that Marcella Coy had moved onto Stodt Mountain for her 19th season as a lookout.[^3] By 1969 the district had decided this would be the only lookout manned on a regular basis, and in 1970 it was the only one kept manned through the season.[^3]

The lookout was abandoned in 1972.[^1][^2][^3] The register lists the site among former lookouts and gives no removal date.[^1] No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the summit today, or how to reach it.[^1]
