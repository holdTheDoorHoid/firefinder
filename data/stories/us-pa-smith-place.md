Smith Place was a fire lookout in Clearfield County, Pennsylvania, in Moshannon State Forest.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives its registry numbers as US 486 and PA 315, and calls it a tree tower.[^1]

The register dates the tower's construction to 1914, says it was replaced in 1921, and records its removal in 1974.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site lists the same county and forest and marks the tower as removed.[^2] The register gives the site an elevation of 2,237 feet, or 682 meters.[^1] Its entry was registered on September 16, 2008, on the nomination of Henry Isenberg.[^1]

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2] Anyone planning a trip should check with the forest before going in.
