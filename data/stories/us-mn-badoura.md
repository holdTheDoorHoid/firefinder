The Badoura Lookout Tower stands in Badoura State Forest, south of Akeley in Hubbard County, and the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources administers it.[^1][^3] The tower sits on the grounds of a state tree nursery, which the county list says has been on the site since 1933.[^3][^4]

The Civilian Conservation Corps and the Minnesota Division of Forestry built the tower in 1934 to watch an area of high fire danger.[^1] A Forest Lookouts page gives the same year and describes a 100-foot steel tower.[^2]

The sources disagree about who made it. The Register calls it a 100-foot Aermotor tower with a metal cab.[^1] The Hubbard County list calls it a 100-foot McClintock Marshall stairway tower and numbers it tower 61.[^3]

The Register says the tower was staffed until 1979, when air detection replaced it.[^1]

Today the Register says people from the nearby nursery still use the tower as a radio antenna and an emergency lookout.[^1] The county list notes that it is easy to see from Highway 64.[^3] We found no source that describes public climbing. Nursery staff use the tower,[^1] so the safest way to see it is from the road.
