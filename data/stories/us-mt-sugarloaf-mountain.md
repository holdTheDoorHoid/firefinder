Sugarloaf Mountain is a lookout site about 20 miles east of Libby, in Lincoln County, at an elevation of 5,432 feet.[^1] The page also names the Kootenai National Forest, with no role stated.[^1]

The page dates the L-4 cab to 1930 and says the lookout was abandoned in 1942.[^1] Its page title identifies it as a cabin, and the Montana list gives its type as ground.[^1][^2]

The page marks the lookout gone but gives no date for that.[^1] The list agrees that the site is gone and places it at 48.36117, -115.11780.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, and none says whether the public can visit the site.
