The Lac des Iles tower shares its name with Lac des Îles, a lake that Wikipedia places inside Meadow Lake Provincial Park.[^1] The Firetowers of Saskatchewan list gives the tower's position as about 54.48 N, 109.38 W.[^2] The Canada Lookouts index includes it under the name Lac Des Iles.[^3]

The towermen list credits Chris Wilks with the Dorintosh and Lac Des Iles towers, and John Langford with six towers, Lac Des Iles among them.[^4]

The Department of Natural Resources began putting up towers through the province's north roughly fifty miles apart, a gap chosen so that towermen could talk to one another by radio. That is the general pattern of the network, not a record of this site.[^5]

No source reached describes the Lac des Iles site today. The lake lies in a provincial park, but nothing found says whether a tower or other structure stands at the site, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]
