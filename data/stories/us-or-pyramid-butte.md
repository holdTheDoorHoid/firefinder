Pyramid Butte, in Marion County, got an L-4 lookout house in 1934.[^1][^2] The register puts the summit at 6,095 feet, or 1,858 meters.[^1] Firelookout.com places the butte in the Mt. Hood National Forest.[^4] The house was a 14-by-14 model.[^3]

In August 1934 a newspaper writer passed the butte shortly after leaving Breitenbush Lake.[^3] The writer called the trail one of the steepest and most crooked in the country, and said it was easy to see why pack horses could not take lumber to the top.[^3] A panorama photo entry for the butte is dated June 27, 1934.[^3]

The register says the cab was removed in 1952.[^1] Firelookout.com says it was destroyed in 1952.[^4] A Lakes District historical report covering 1953, as quoted on a collection page, says the lookout was condemned and disposed of that year.[^3]

The sources consulted do not describe what remains on the butte today or say whether the public can reach it.
