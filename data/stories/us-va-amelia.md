Amelia Tower stood in Amelia County, Virginia, and the Virginia Department of Forestry administered it.[^2][^1] The register gives 1965 as its build year and describes a 125-foot Aermotor steel tower, painted gray.[^1]

A 1965 survey description of a station about a mile southwest of the town of Amelia gives a four-legged steel frame, a glass-enclosed cab and a height of 125 feet. That is probably this same tower.[^2]

The two sources agree on the end. The register says it was removed around 2004 to 2005, and the Forest Lookouts page says it was dismantled in 2004-05.[^1][^2]

The register page carries a photo labeled as the tower's footings, credited to Google.[^1] Among the sources read, that photo is the only mention of anything left at the site. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list marks the tower as gone.[^3]

No source read says whether the site is open to the public. Check the land status with the managing agency before any visit.
