Forest Lookouts lists Oakway as a South Carolina Forestry Commission lookout in Oconee County.[^1]

The commission's history pages say more about the network as a whole than about any single tower. Most tower sites covered 10 acres and included the tower and a year-round home for the operator. That acreage let operators raise their own vegetables and feed for household livestock.[^2]

From the 1960s on, most operators in South Carolina were women, often married to firefighters. In many cases the post passed from parent to child.[^2]

The commission shut its tower system down on October 1, 1993.[^2] A Post and Courier article, undated in the copy we read, says the state phased out its towers in the early 1990s and that just 30 are still standing. Some of those 30 are used for radio equipment.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the Oakway site today, or whether the public can reach it.
