The Seminole Lookout Tower carries the name of Seminole, an unincorporated community in Baldwin County, Alabama.[^1] The community lies on U.S. Route 90, about 14.4 miles (23.2 km) east of Robertsdale, and takes its name from the Seminole tribe.[^1] A post office carried the name from 1894 until 1967.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts website files the tower under Baldwin County, listed alongside the Alabama Forestry Commission.[^2] A GPS tower directory lists a Seminole Lookout Tower under Elberta, Alabama.[^3] OpenStreetMap records it as an observation tower and flags its location for checking.[^4]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can get to it.
