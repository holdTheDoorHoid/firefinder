A Georgia tower list compiled by Tom Dunigan includes a lookout called vining in Cobb County. The list gives its elevation as 1,175 and its coordinates as 33.8697, -84.46803.[^1] The page says names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own, so the name may not be a historical one.[^1]

The coordinates nearly match those Wikipedia gives for Mount Wilkinson, a low mountain in southeast Cobb County.[^2] The article gives an earlier name, Signal Mountain, and says the mountain is widely known today as Vinings Mountain. It gives the mountain's elevation as 1,165 ft (355 m).[^2] No source names this mountain as the lookout's site, so the link rests on the coordinates alone.

High-rise office towers now sit on top of the mountain, which is only partly forested.[^2] A Vinings history page says tall buildings spread toward the interstate and up the mountain.[^3] No source describes a lookout standing on the mountain today.

[^2]: Mount Wilkinson - Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Wilkinson (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Celebrating Yesterday and Today in Vinings, Vinings.com, https://www.vinings.com/history/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
