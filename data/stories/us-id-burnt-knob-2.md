Burnt Knob stands at 7,339 feet in Idaho County, on the Payette National Forest.[^1][^2] It lies seven miles north-northwest of Chamberlain G.S.[^1]

A pole tower with an L-4 cab went up in 1933.[^1][^2][^3] The sources disagree on its height. firelookout.com gives 10 feet, while Idaho Fire Lookouts and an Idaho County listing that credits Kresek give 15 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts names Elmer Purdyman as a known staffer, with a 1915 date taken from the Idaho County Free Press.[^2] That date is earlier than the 1933 tower.[^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1960.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list also marks it as gone.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in, and no source describes what remains there today.[^2]
