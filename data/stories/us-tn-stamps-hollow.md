The Stamps Hollow tower stood in Putnam County, Tennessee, at an elevation of 1,988 feet, or about 606 meters.[^1] Tennessee Landforms lists it as removed, under the code PUT002.[^1]

Stamps Hollow itself is a valley in Putnam County, within the 38574 ZIP code area.[^2]

For comparison, Tennessee Landforms lists a standing tower at Cookeville, also in Putnam County.[^1]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the Stamps Hollow site today, or whether the site is open to visitors. Please do not assume a tower stands there.[^1]
