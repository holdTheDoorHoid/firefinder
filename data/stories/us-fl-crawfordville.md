The Forest Lookouts page for Crawfordville, in Wakulla County, reproduces a Florida Times-Union item about the Wakulla lookout tower.[^1] The item, which the page dates May 27, 1930, is datelined Crawfordville, May 26, and says the tower was manned from February 2 and that 2,127 people visited it between then and May 20.[^1]

The figures came from G. A. Young, the assistant state forester in Tallahassee.[^1] The item says the state department in Tallahassee was highly satisfied with the early progress in the county.[^1] Young planned a trip to Wakulla County to bring more property into the fire-protection effort.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Crawfordville - FOREST LOOKOUTS (quoting The Florida Times-Union, May 27, 1930), Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/crawfordville.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
