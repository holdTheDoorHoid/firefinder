The Government Mountain lookout stood about three miles northeast of Noxon, in Sanders County, Montana, on the Kootenai National Forest.[^1] Newspapers of the period called the spot Government Peak, and the Forest Lookouts page files their notices under Government Mountain.[^2] In June 1930 a Sanders County paper noted that both a cabin for firefighters and a lookout tower were finished there.[^2]

The firelookout.com page gives 1930 for the cabin and 1931 for the tower, which it describes as a 50-foot pole L-4 tower.[^1] The June 1930 notice already calls the cabin and tower complete, so the two accounts disagree about when the tower went up.[^1][^2]

Newspaper notices name a run of lookouts.[^2] William Scholes of Noxon was assigned the post in 1930, the first lookout the sources name.[^2] Earl Helms was named for 1931, and a June 1933 notice has him set to be stationed there that summer.[^2] By September 1934 he was home.[^2] A September 1936 notice places an old schoolmate on the peak, and in July 1942 Frank Hoag is called the lookout there.[^2]

The firelookout.com page says the tower was abandoned in 1950.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list marks the site as gone.[^3]

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
