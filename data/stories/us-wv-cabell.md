The Cabell Tower Site marks a former fire lookout in Cabell County, West Virginia.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives its elevation as 1,155 feet (352 metres).[^1]

The register records it as US 1664 and WV 55.[^1] Brian M. Powell nominated the site, and it was registered on January 26, 2017.[^1] The register names the West Virginia Division of Forestry as administrator.[^1]

The FireLookout.org list marks the tower as gone.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout on the site today, or whether the public can reach it. Anyone planning a visit should check with the Division of Forestry before going.

[^1]: Cabell Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register (firetower.org), http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/wv/cabell-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: West Virginia fire lookouts list, FireLookout.org, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wv/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
