Archuleta Mesa lies in the southern San Juan Mountains of southwestern Colorado.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Colorado list places a tower on the mesa, in Archuleta County, at 37.02619 north and 106.98432 west, and marks it as removed.[^2] A Forest Lookouts page for the mesa names the Southern Ute Indian Agency alongside the county.[^3]

No source describes the site today. The sources do not say what, if anything, remains on the mesa, or whether visitors can reach the spot.
