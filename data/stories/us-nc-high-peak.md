The High Peak lookout in Burke County was built in 1934, when the Civilian Conservation Corps put up a steel tower 59 feet and 3 inches tall.[^1] The Forest Lookouts list files the site under the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources.[^1] A North Carolina tower list places High Peak at about 35.72 degrees north and 81.61 degrees west, in Burke County.[^2]

No source describes the site now. Nothing in these sources says whether the tower still stands or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Forest Lookouts, "North Carolina - Forest Lookouts," https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/north-carolina.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: tnlandforms.us, "North Carolina lookout towers," https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
