Stonewall Mountain's lookout stands about six miles north of Lincoln, Montana, in the Helena-Lewis and Clark National Forest.[^4] The summit is given as 8,270 feet in one source and 8,284 feet in another.[^4][^5]

The sources do not agree on when the first tower went up.[^1][^2] A March 1941 newspaper item, reprinted on Rex's page, says a lookout house and tower for fire control were built on the mountain after a Civilian Conservation Corps camp opened at Rimini.[^2] Firelookout.com gives 1945 for an L-4 tower, and a 1965 recovery note quoted on the same page describes a tower built in 1950 and reported removed in 1964.[^1][^2]

Newspaper items name a lookout there in 1951 and 1953.[^2] Firelookout.com says the station was staffed until 1963 and torn down soon after.[^1] A frame cabin on a 10-foot concrete base was proposed around 1990 and built in the mid-1990s.[^1]

In December 2008 the cab lost its roof, which was moved about 70 yards.[^2] A 2009 chapter report describes repairs made after winter damage.[^3] In July 2017, as a wildfire neared, crews put a protective, fire-resistant wrap around the lookout.[^2]

A KRTV feature says a seasonal observer has spent summers at the lookout since 2016.[^4] Firelookout.com says it is staffed in summer, and firelookout.org lists it as standing.[^1][^6]

The road to the summit is unpaved, rocky and steep in places, and runs about 18 miles from Lincoln.[^5] A 4x4 is needed.[^5] DangerousRoads says the road is closed in winter and open from July through October.[^5] Because the cab is staffed in summer, please do not climb it.
