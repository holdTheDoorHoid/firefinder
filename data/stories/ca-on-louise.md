Ontario's open tower layer lists a Louise Fire Tower at about 46.3175 N, 81.3901 W.[^1] The entry is marked as verified and placed within 50 metres of the site.[^1] The layer classes it as a fire tower.[^1]

Most of Ontario's towers were built between the 1920s and 1950s, during the peak years of forest fire protection.[^2] A personal history site on the towers says their use fell steadily through the 1960s as air-based spotting and firefighting gained ground, and that a decision was later made to destroy many of them.[^2]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether any part of a tower remains there. No source says whether the public may visit.

[^2]: Ontario's Fire Tower Lookouts, https://forestrangers.github.io/ontariofiretowers/ (accessed 2026-10-20).
