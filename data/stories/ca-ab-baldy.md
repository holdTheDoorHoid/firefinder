The Baldy fire lookout sits at the summit of Shunda Mountain, overlooking Nordegg, Alberta.[^1] A trail guide titles it the Shunda Mountain (Baldy) fire lookout.[^1]

The Alberta register lists it as a cabin built in 1953. It stands 5 m (16 ft) tall at about 2,082 m (6,831 ft) above sea level.[^2]

From 1912 there were two kinds of lookout in Alberta: mountaintop sites with low, ground-level or stilted buildings, and towers on forested hills.[^3] Expansion of the network picked up in the 1950s, the decade of the cabin's register date.[^3][^2]

A trail guide describes a gated fire road, shared with off-road vehicles, that runs about 2.8 km from a gate on Baldy Mountain Road to the lookout.[^1] Near the summit the guide calls the alpine area fragile, says damage from past use is being repaired, and asks walkers to stay on the road.[^1]

The register codes the site as reached by road and lists it as active.[^2] The guide calls the lookout a work area and a residence, and asks visitors to stay outside the fence unless they are invited in.[^1] No source describes the cabin's condition today, so treat the lookout as restricted.
