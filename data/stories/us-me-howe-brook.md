Howe Brook is a fire tower site in Aroostook County, Maine.[^1][^2] The state archive places it in Township 8, Range 3, WELS.[^1] A Maine Forestry Service panoramic map centered on the tower is dated 1931.[^1] That shows the tower was in use by then, but it does not say when the tower was built, how tall it was or who built it. I could not find those details in the sources I reached.

Maine's network of more than 100 fire towers was linked by telephone, and lone observers staffed the towers, watching for smoke and fire.[^3] By 1909 the state had taken over the lookouts that private owners had funded.[^3] Fire towers were most numerous in Maine between 1930 and 1950.[^4] I found no record of a named watchman at Howe Brook, or of any notable fire it spotted.

Maine ended its staffed network after a budget crisis in 1991.[^3] Some towers were later taken apart or fell down, and others have been put to new use.[^3]

Today the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Howe Brook as standing, with an asterisk showing its cab is gone.[^2][^5] Under that key, the structure is still up.[^5] I found no current access rules or climbing information for the tower. Treat it as unverified, and don't climb it without permission from whoever manages the land.
