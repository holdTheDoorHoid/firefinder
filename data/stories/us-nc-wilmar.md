The Wilmar Fire Tower is in Craven County, North Carolina, and the North Carolina Forest Service is listed as the agency that administers it.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register gives it the number US 1478, NC 28, and records the registration date as January 8, 2021.[^1]

The Civilian Conservation Corps put up the tower in 1935.[^2] A state report from that period describes a steel tower 99 feet 9 inches tall and a five-room ground house, built in February 1935 for $2,149.35 with money from the Emergency Conservation Works program.[^2] A report covering 1936 to 1938 records an extension to the tower's trailer shed, finished on July 24, 1936.[^2]

The sources I could read do not say who staffed the tower or whether it watched any particular fires.

The Register describes the tower as apparently in good shape in 2020.[^1] It also raises the possibility that a 2018 state auction sold it.[^1] Its own estimate of the height, 50 to 75 feet, does not match the 99 feet 9 inches recorded in 1935.[^1][^2] The Register does not name a buyer or confirm the tower's condition after 2020.

Today the sources give no visiting rules and no current owner, so the public's right to enter the tower is unknown. Anyone interested in seeing it should check with the North Carolina Forest Service first, since that is the agency the Register lists as administering it.

[^1]: Wilmar Fire Tower, National Historic Lookout Register (nhlr.org), http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/nc/wilmar-fire-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-08).
[^2]: Wilmar, Forest Lookouts (easternuslookouts.weebly.com), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/wilmar.html (accessed 2026-10-08).
