North Saddle is a former fire lookout site in Clatsop County, Oregon, 12 miles east-southeast of Seaside, at 3,283 feet (1,001 meters).[^1][^2] Firelookout.com files it as Saddle Mountain.[^2]

A camp with a log cabin went up below the summit in 1913.[^1][^2] In 1920 a frame cabin with an observation platform replaced it.[^1][^2] A two-story live-in cabin replaced that one in 1953.[^1][^2]

A photo caption on the register page dates the 1920 cabin's final year to 1951, two years before the 1953 date in the text.[^1]

Both sources say the two-story cabin came down in 1966. The register says it was removed, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed.[^1][^2]

The mountain is now part of Saddle Mountain State Natural Area, a state park.[^3] A 2.5-mile (4.0 km) trail climbs to the top.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, is left of the lookout on the summit today.
