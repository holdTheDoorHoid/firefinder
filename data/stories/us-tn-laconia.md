Laconia's tower stands in Fayette County, on private land.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes an 80-foot Aermotor and notes a small outbuilding on the site.[^1] The register gives no build date, and the sources I read do not say who built the tower or when.[^1]

Tennessee Landforms lists it as state forest tower 9-7, drawn from a 1976 inventory, and marks it standing.[^2] The register names the Tennessee Division of Forestry as a cooperator, but it does not explain what role the agency played at this site.[^1] I found no account of fires, lookout staff or moves here.

The register lists the owner as private and records the registration on December 28, 2022.[^1]

I found no source describing the tower's condition after that registration. Because the land is private, please ask the owner before going near it, and do not climb the tower.

[^1]: Laconia Fire Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/tn/laconia-fire-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, Tennessee Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
