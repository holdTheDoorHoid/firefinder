The Cottonwood lookout stood about 24 miles west of Grangeville, in Idaho County, at an elevation of 4,624 feet.[^1][^2] It was a log cabin with a roof platform, dated 1929, on the north end of a spur below Rattlesnake Plateau.[^1][^2]

The lookout pages and the county list date its destruction to 1965.[^1][^2][^3] The county list marks the land as private, with the location code 30N-3W-23.[^3] One Idaho page's header reads "Hike-In Destroyed": "Hike-In" is its access category, and "Destroyed" agrees with the 1965 date.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a ground site.[^4]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. The sources list the land as private, so visitors should not assume they can reach the site.[^1][^3]
