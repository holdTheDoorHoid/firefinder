Bell Mountain stands about 12 miles southeast of Hailey, in Blaine County, Idaho.[^2][^5] The Bureau of Land Management designed a two-story lookout house for the summit, built in 1963 with a concrete base and a frame cab.[^1][^2][^3]

Mr. and Mrs. Louis Anderson are the known lookouts for 1963 to 1974, and one site also names Thora Anderson.[^2][^3] A July 4, 1970 Idaho Statesman item, quoted on Forest Lookouts, said the Andersons, of Shoshone, would be back at the station east of Bellevue.[^1] The station was last staffed in 1998.[^2][^3]

The lookout was demolished in 2014.[^2][^3] Forest Lookouts dates its removal to September 2014.[^1]

A climbing guide updated in September 2020 says the former lookout site now holds several antenna installations, and that a road runs to the top.[^4] The guide counts two repeater antennas on the summit.[^4] The land is a patchwork of BLM, state and private ownership, so it advises checking the BLM Fairfield map before going.[^4]

The guide's route runs east on Pine Street for about 5.9 miles to Sharps Canyon Road, then about 4.1 miles up to the top, and it recommends a 4WD.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as drive-up.[^3]
