Haystack Mountain, about 30 miles northeast of Seeley Lake, once carried a fire lookout cab.[^1] Rex's Fire Lookout Page dates the L-4 cab to the 1930s, gives the elevation as 8,376 feet, and marks the site as gone.[^1]

The two lookout pages disagree on the forest. Rex's page names the Flathead and Lewis & Clark National Forests, while the Forest Lookouts page lists the Deerlodge National Forest, with the reference 21N-12W-9, and marks the site as removed.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list records the site as a Ground-type structure that is gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
