The Jadie Tower site lies in McCurtain County, Oklahoma, on land now owned by the Ouachita National Forest.[^3]

The sources do not agree on when it went up. The registry gives 1950 as the build year, but its description says the Forest Service built it in the 1930s.[^1] A forest lookout list also says the 1930s, without an exact date.[^2] The Oklahoma Forestry Services data sheet gives 1953 as the year the tower was erected, and says the property had been part of a Land Utilization Project under an agreement dated October 15, 1952.[^3] We have used 1953, because it comes from the agency's own data sheet.[^3] The registry added the tower to its listing in 2009.[^1]

The agency sheet describes a 110-foot galvanized steel tower. The registry says it is 100 feet tall, with a 7-by-7 cab.[^3][^1]

Over the years, the registry says, staff came from either the Ouachita National Forest or state forestry.[^1] The agency sheet says the tower's observer retired in 2016 and lists the station as closed.[^3] The sources do not name any fire the tower spotted.

We could not confirm the tower's condition today. The registry lists it, but none of the sources we read describe a recent visit or inspection.[^1][^3] It is unclear whether the sheet's closed status is still current, and we found no public visiting arrangement.[^3] We would not recommend climbing a lookout that the sheet lists as closed, so ask the Forest Service before going near it.
