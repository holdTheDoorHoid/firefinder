The Copper Mountain Lookout in Kootenai County stood on the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, about 12 miles east of Coeur d'Alene.[^1][^2] It is listed separately from the Boundary County Copper Mountain of the same name.[^5] The first structure was a tree platform, developed around 1918.[^1][^3][^4]

A 40-foot pole tower with a 6-by-6-foot cab went up in 1923, with a log cabin on the ground for living quarters.[^1][^3][^4] In 1934 an L-4 cab was added on the ground for living quarters, and the summit gained road access.[^1][^2][^4] A 50-foot steel tower followed. Three sources date it to 1940, while a Weebly list gives 1942.[^1][^2][^4][^3]

The only named lookout on record is from 1944.[^1][^2] The two towers stood on opposite sides of the summit at least through 1949.[^1]

The L-4 cab was put up for public sale and taken away in 1950.[^1] The steel tower was taken down in 1954 and relocated to Faset Peak.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Firelookout.com gives 1923 to 1942 as the lookout's span in its heading, which does not match its own later entries.[^1] The index lists the tower as gone.[^5]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in, with the lookout removed.[^2] Willhite describes a hike of about two miles one way from Marie Saddle, gaining about 900 feet.[^4] From a saddle on FR 3907 a gated road leads to the summit, and Willhite advises parking and walking or biking it.[^4]

On the summit, Willhite notes that the most recent footings on the high point seem to belong to a steel tower.[^4] USGS notes seem to put the steel tower at the lower point, while an image of it looks more like the high point, so the exact spot is uncertain.[^4] Photo captions mention wire at the lower site, and also glass and nails.[^4] Beyond these footings and captions, no source describes the site today.[^4]
