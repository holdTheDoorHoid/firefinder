Eveleth's fire lookout stood north of town in Saint Louis County, Minnesota, just west of Highway 53.[^2] The county's tower list calls it the relocated Spirit Lake tower from Carlton County and gives an 80-foot stairway tower with a 1947 date.[^2] The only text attached to that 1947 date is a survey reference with no description.[^2]

The Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list shows the tower as gone.[^1] The county list says the site was lost to mining.[^2] Both sources give nearly the same coordinates for the spot, so they very likely describe the same tower.[^1][^2]

I did not find a source that describes the spot today, so I cannot say what, if anything, is left there or whether the land is open to visitors. Check with the local landowner before going to look.

[^1]: Minnesota Lookouts, Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: St. Louis County Fire Lookout Towers, Wikimedia Commons, https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/ce/St_Louis_County_Towers.pdf (accessed 2026-10-10).
