Eubanks Lookout Tower is in Barbour County, Alabama, and is shown on the USGS Eufaula North topographic map.[^1] TopoZone places it at about 31.99 N, 85.22 W, at an approximate elevation of 384 feet (117 m).[^1] OpenStreetMap has a node with the same name about 125 m north of those coordinates, tagged as a lattice tower of the observation type.[^2]

Neither source says whether the tower still stands or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Eubanks Lookout Tower Topo Map AL, Barbour County (Eufaula North Area), TopoZone, https://www.topozone.com/alabama/barbour-al/tower/eubanks-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Node: Eubanks Lookout Tower (358945767), OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/358945767 (accessed 2026-10-21).
