The Carters Mountain fire tower stood on Carter Mountain, about four miles south of Charlottesville, Virginia.[^3] The National Historic Lookout Register dates its construction to 1933 and describes a privately owned steel tower, 100 feet tall, with a cab about eight feet square.[^1]

A federal survey record from 1958 used the tower as a survey point.[^3] It describes an eight-foot-square glass room held up by four steel legs and roughly 100 feet high.[^3]

Before it came down, the tower sat inside a fence and carried communications gear both on its frame and in the cab.[^1][^2] The register listed it on March 24, 2020, and the tower came down in March 2023.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site on December 31, 2023, and both registers record the removal in 2023.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left on the site now. The register lists a private owner and describes no public access, so anyone interested should ask the owner before going onto the land.[^1] The register does hold photographs of the tower taken in January 1997.[^1]
