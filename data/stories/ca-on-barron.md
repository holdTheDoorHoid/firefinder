Firelookout.ca lists a Barron Fire Tower in Algonquin Park, Ontario.[^1] The listing gives no tower type, structure height or year built.[^1]

Its only dated details are two maps. One is Pembroke topographic map 31F, third edition, from 1977. The other is the Department of Lands and Forests Tower Detection System Map from 1963.[^1] Those maps show the tower was marked on them, but the listing gives no build year and no status.[^1]

Algonquin Park's Fire Tower Trail, at the entrance to the Algonquin Visitor Centre on Highway 60, leads to a viewpoint with a replica of the wooden cupola that once topped the park's steel fire towers.[^2] No source I read ties that replica to the Barron listing.

No source I found says what, if anything, remains at the Barron site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Barron Fire Tower, Firelookout.ca, https://firelookout.ca/lookouts/?slug=barron-fire-tower (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Fire Tower Trail, Algonquin Provincial Park, The Friends of Algonquin Park, https://www.algonquinpark.on.ca/visit/recreational_activites/fire-tower-trail.php (accessed 2026-10-20).
