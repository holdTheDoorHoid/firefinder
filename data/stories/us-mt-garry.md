Garry Lookout stood in the Flathead National Forest, in Flathead County, Montana, about 13 miles southeast of West Glacier.[^2] The register gives its elevation as 4,499 feet, or 1,371 meters.[^1]

The lookout went up in 1934.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com describes it as a 30-foot pole L-4 tower, set about half a mile above U.S. Highway 2.[^2]

The register lists 1974 as the year it was removed, but its own description says the lookout was destroyed that year.[^1] Firelookout.com and the Montana History Portal also say it was destroyed in 1974.[^2][^3] The Glacier National Park Archives holds a 1971 photograph of the lookout, credited to David S. Shea.[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout today. The register says the site can be reached by the 2-mile Garry Lookout Trail #499, which starts at U.S. Highway 2 and follows the Middle Fork Flathead River.[^1] Check current trail conditions with the Flathead National Forest before you go, since we could not confirm them.
