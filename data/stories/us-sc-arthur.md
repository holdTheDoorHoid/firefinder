Arthur was a fire tower in Lexington County, South Carolina.[^1][^2] The Eastern US Lookouts site marks it removed.[^1] TopoQuest lists it as the Arthur Fire Tower (historical).[^2]

For context, the South Carolina Forestry Commission says its tower network operated from the 1930s, and that it retired its last forest fire lookout towers in 1993.[^3] The Post and Courier reports that dozens of decommissioned towers remain along rural highways statewide.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the Arthur site today.
