A fire tower once stood on the summit of Kwagama Hill, a mountain 6 km due west of Agawa Canyon.[^1] The Firelookout register places the Kwagama Mountain Fire Tower at Agawa Bay.[^2]

The two sources disagree about why the tower fell. The register says it blew down in a 1965 windstorm.[^2] The hiking guide says a heavy ice storm knocked it down that year.[^1]

The guide says the twisted metal remains of the tower are still visible at the summit.[^1] It does not say whether the site is public or closed, or who controls the gate.[^1]

The guide describes 76 km of rough logging roads from the nearest highway, with a gate at kilometre 16 that is often open.[^1] It advises getting the gate's combination in advance, in case it is closed.[^1] The last stretch is a bushwhack, and the guide says very few trips go up the hill.[^1]

[^1]: Kwagama Hill, Ontario High Points, http://www.ontariohighpoints.com/kwagama-hill/ (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Kwagama Mountain Fire Tower, Firelookout.ca, https://firelookout.ca/lookouts/?slug=kwagama-mountain-fire-tower (accessed 2026-10-20).
