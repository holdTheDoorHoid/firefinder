Cooks Peak, in Boundary County, Idaho, about nine miles west of Bonners Ferry, first held a log cabin lookout in 1930.[^2][^5] A pole tower was added in 1936, and the peak lies in the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^5]

Rex Kamstra's listing names Dan Kimball as staff in 1932, Evan Watson for 1933 through 1935, and Robert Saarf in 1936. Later names are Paul Weber in 1956, Bruce Baker in 1958, Tim Jones in 1959 and Durward Stolp in 1960 and 1961.[^2] A 2024 Bonners Ferry Herald "From the Archives" column, which recalls the early 1930s, says Walter Peel manned the lookout on Cook's Peak.[^6]

Sources disagree on when the lookout was abandoned. Idaho Fire Lookouts and Idaho Magazine give 1958.[^1][^5] Rex Kamstra's listing carries staff into 1960 and 1961, and says the tower was gone by 1964.[^2]

The Forest Service's Cooks Peak Trail #236 leads to the peak, where the old lookout and cabin once stood.[^3] An undated hiking write-up says the tower's footings are still intact and the cabin is decaying.[^4] The Forest Service lists the trail as a recreation route, and the write-up describes about one mile of single-track from the official trailhead to the summit.[^3][^4] No source here reports a closure.[^3][^4]
