Forest Lookouts quotes a May 1929 Charleston Gazette item reporting that the state game and fish commission would begin work on a fire tower near Petersburg, in Grant County, right away.[^1] We did not find when it was finished.

By April 1937 the tower was staffed for the fire season.[^1] Forest Lookouts also quotes the Cumberland Evening Times, which named M.M. Friddle and Vance Belt as stationed at the fire tower on Elkhorn.[^1] The same item named the Big Ridge tower in Lost River State Park as Hardy County's other fire tower, which suggests Elkhorn was in Hardy County too.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a standing tower named Elkhorn in Hardy County, at 38.92887 and -79.04576.[^2] We found no later report on its condition, its staff, or any fire it watched.

A county history by E. L. Judy, dated 1951, uses Elk Horn Rock as a landmark on a boundary that runs to the top of the rock and then along the mountain to the Hardy and Pendleton line.[^3] It does not mention the tower, so we cannot say whether the rock and the Elkhorn tower are the same site.[^3]
