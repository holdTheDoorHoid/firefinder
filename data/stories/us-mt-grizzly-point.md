Grizzly Point stood in the Kootenai National Forest, about six miles southwest of Yaak in Lincoln County, Montana, at an elevation of 6,077 feet.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list records it as a tower in Lincoln County that is now gone.[^2] A separate Lincoln County list also places it in the Kootenai National Forest.[^3]

The firelookout.com guide describes a 10-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, built in 1931.[^1] The same page gives the dates 1931 to 1958 and says the tower was gone by 1958.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list gives its coordinates as 48.76948, -115.78542.[^2] A photograph on the guide's page is credited to the Fire Lookout Museum.[^1]

No source consulted describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
