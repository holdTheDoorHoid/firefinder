Linney Mountain, in Alexander County, North Carolina, appears on the tnlandforms.us list of the state's lookout towers at about 35.93 N, 81.16 W.[^2] The Forest Lookouts website lists the tower under Alexander County and also under a National Forest heading, without naming the forest.[^1]

A March 16, 1972 item from the Statesville Record & Landmark, transcribed on that site, reports that the Forest Service had been running a fire lookout on Linney's Mountain since mid-February. A smoke spotter was to stay there until about mid-May, the worst part of the fire season.[^1]

No source we read describes what stands on the mountain now, or whether the public can reach it.
