A Georgia lookout list on tnlandforms.us includes a tower listed as conyers, in Rockdale County. Its listed position is 33.66250, -83.99639. The list gives its elevation as 905 with no unit; read as feet, that is 275.8 m.[^1]

The name matches Conyers, the Rockdale County town.[^1][^2] The railroad authority named the place after Dr. W.D. Conyers, a Covington banker who negotiated the land for the line. It was first known as Conyers Station.[^2] Conyers became a town on Feb. 16, 1854.[^2] An Oct. 18, 1870 bill created Rockdale County from Newton and Henry Counties.[^2]

For statewide context, the Georgia Forestry Commission's history records a June 1945 airplane test of fire detection. Air patrol was expanded from that test.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, or whether the public may visit.
