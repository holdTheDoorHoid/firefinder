A TN Landforms list of North Carolina lookout towers includes a Giddensville tower in Sampson County, with coordinates near 35.14, -78.24.[^1] The National Register of Historic Places record for the Lovett Lee House places that house at Giddensville, in the same county.[^2]

No source describes the site today, including whether a tower stands there or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, TN Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Lee, Lovett, House (NRHP record 86000567), National Park Service, https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/86000567 (accessed 2026-10-21).
