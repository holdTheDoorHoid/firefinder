Ambrose Point is a lookout site in the Bitterroot National Forest, about ten miles east of Stevensville in Ravalli County, at an elevation of 6,735 feet.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com describes it as a rough platform on poles, about 30 feet tall, erected in the 1930s.[^1] Its abandonment is dated to the 1940s.[^1] The site's pages give conflicting accounts of what came next. One says the platform stood until the winter of 2021 to 2022, when it collapsed.[^1] A second page says it is still there.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list records the type as platform, with the status Abandoned, and places it at 46.50331 north and 113.88190 west.[^3] Firelookout.com credits its photos of the site to the Fire Lookout Museum and Gary Little.[^1]

Beyond these conflicting accounts, no source describes the site today, and none says whether visitors can reach it.
