Cockburn Island is both an island and a township municipality, located in Ontario's Manitoulin District.[^1] According to the township's own history, the township was established in 1881.[^2] The Mississagi Strait divides the island from the western tip of Manitoulin Island.[^1]

No bridge or ferry currently links the island to Manitoulin, Drummond Island or the mainland. Getting there is possible only by private boat in summer, by snowmobile in winter, or by plane or helicopter.[^1]
