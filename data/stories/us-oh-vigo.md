The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register lists the Vigo Tower Site in Ross County, Ohio, at 1,060 feet (323 m) of elevation.[^1] The site was added to the register on September 3, 2014.[^1] Wikipedia describes the community of Vigo as unincorporated and places it in Ross County.[^4] A post office called Vigo operated from 1858 until 1933, and the community also had a railroad station, a hotel, a church, a brick plant and several country stores.[^4]

Another transcribed list names the Ohio Department of Natural Resources as the agency, though that page misspells the name.[^3] The association's Ohio list marks the tower as gone, so there is no standing tower to climb.[^2] Nothing in these sources describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
