The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a fire tower in the city of Suffolk under the name Holland, and marks it as gone.[^1] The list places it at 36.66224 and -76.76154.[^1]

The list uses the name Holland. The state's historic register describes Holland as a village stretched out along a line, in what is now the city of Suffolk, which was once Nansemond County.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.

[^1]: Virginia lookouts list, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Holland Historic District, Virginia Department of Historic Resources, https://www.dhr.virginia.gov/historic-registers/133-0691/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
