The Spearsville lookout tower stood in Union Parish, Louisiana.[^1] The lookouts site lists the Louisiana Forestry Commission as its agency.[^1] A federal survey placed it about 13 miles northwest of Farmerville, 8 miles northeast of Bernice and 7 miles southeast of Spearsville.[^2]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey first observed the tower in 1952.[^2] It described a steel tower on four legs, roughly 115 feet tall, with an enclosed glass cabin at the top.[^2] The surveyors' point sat at the apex of the roof.[^2] The lookouts site repeats that description.[^1]

The lookouts site lists the tower as removed.[^1] No source describes the site today, or whether the ground is open to the public. The federal survey station for the tower is still on record, but its description dates from 1952.[^2]

[^2]: NGS Data Sheet for PID CQ2893, National Geodetic Survey, https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=CQ2893 (accessed 2026-10-10).
