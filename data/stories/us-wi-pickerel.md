Pickerel Lookout Tower stood in Vilas County, Wisconsin.[^1][^2] The Central US Lookouts site files it under the name Pickerel Lake.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list records the tower as gone.[^1]

OpenStreetMap places the tower node at about 526 metres of elevation and tags it as an observation tower.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Wisconsin Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Pickerel Lake, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/pickerel-lake.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Pickerel Lookout Tower (node 353894196), OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/353894196 (accessed 2026-10-10).
