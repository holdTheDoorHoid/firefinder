Lavina Lookout stood on Lavina Mountain in the Central Kootenay area of British Columbia, at 2,238 m (7,343 ft), according to the National Historic Lookout Register (NHLR).[^1] According to the Four Wheel Drive Association of BC (4WDABC), the views take in the north end of Kootenay Lake and the south end of Duncan Lake.[^2]

The NHLR gives 1937 as the build year of the first lookout on this site, and 1959 as the year a two-story flat-cab tower took its place.[^1] The 4WDABC, however, dates the tower to 1974, so the two sources disagree on its build date.[^1][^2] The association took the tower on in 2014, and in 2021 it began hosting a West Kootenay Amateur Radio Club repeater.[^2] By the time the NHLR described it, the tower was no longer staffed.[^1]

The Argenta Creek wildfire burned the tower in summer 2024.[^1][^2] In September 2024 the association reported that the tower seemed to have been reduced to a concrete block base.[^2] The trail was closed afterward.[^2] The association said it could not go up until Recreation Sites and Trails of British Columbia (RSTBC), the holder of its maintenance contract, gave permission.[^2]

By July 2025 the tower walls had been knocked down, and the concrete slab was kept.[^2] In late July 2026 a first rebuild work event cleaned up debris, excavated loose rock around the foundation, and fastened new rebar to the old foundation with epoxy.[^2] Volunteers also built wooden forms for a concrete pour that was still to come.[^2] The rest of the tower material still had to be ordered and delivered to a staging area.[^2]

The association expects the site to remain highly valuable for recreation once access has been deemed safe.[^2] The association's 2026 update does not describe the site as open to the public.[^2]

[^2]: Lavina Fire Lookout Loss, Four Wheel Drive Association of BC, https://4wdabc.ca/2024/07/31/lavina-fire-lookout-loss/ (accessed 2026-10-20).
