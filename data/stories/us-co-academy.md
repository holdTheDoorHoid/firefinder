The Academy Lookout Site was a tower near the U.S. Air Force Academy in El Paso County, Colorado.[^1] It sat on the slope of a backdrop mountain, not far from the Pike National Forest boundary.[^1]

The register describes an approximately 30-foot metal tower of local design, with a cab about seven feet square.[^1] It dates the tower to possibly 1957, when the Air Force Academy put it up.[^1]

The register says the tower was built after a wildfire got away from the Academy's construction site around September 1956.[^1] That fire ran down a draw from the Air Force Academy Chapel toward I-25.[^1] It killed many ponderosa pines considered vital to the campus's parkway-like approach.[^1]

The register says the lookout had only a few years of staffing.[^1] It also says the tower was removed around 1980.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Colorado list marks the tower as removed.[^2]

The site was added to the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register on October 30, 2011, nominated by Kent Argow.[^1]

The register notes that the fire's damage can still be seen today, in how few larger trees there are.[^1] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains of the tower itself. The register lists the land as administered by the U.S. Air Force Academy, and no source says whether visitors can reach the spot, so ask the Academy before going.[^1]
