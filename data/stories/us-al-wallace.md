The Wallace Fire Tower is in Escambia County, Alabama, off Alabama 41, a short drive south of where that highway crosses Interstate 65.[^1][^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register added the tower on January 8, 2021, under the numbers US 1491 and AL 76.[^1] Jackson Knight nominated it, and the Register's entry gives the site's elevation as 369 feet, or 112 meters.[^1]

The Register's description calls it Alabama Tower 62 and uses the past tense.[^1] A blog, which uses the present tense, says it received information that the tower is Alabama Tower 62 on the official state list.[^2] We could not check the state list itself.

We found no source that gives the year the tower was built, who built it, who staffed it, or what fires it watched.[^1][^2]

The Register page shows a September 2025 photo credited to Will Sprinkle, which suggests the tower was still standing then.[^1] We found no information about public access, so anyone who wants to see it should first find out who owns the land and whether visiting is allowed.
