Elk Butte sits in Fremont County, Idaho, in the Caribou-Targhee National Forest, 24 miles northeast of Saint Anthony.[^1][^3][^4] Another Elk Butte, in Clearwater County, is a different lookout and still standing.[^4]

In 1942 a tower with a live-in cab went up here.[^1][^2][^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page gives the site's elevation as 6,372 feet.[^2]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site says it has no record yet of who staffed the tower.[^2]

The sources disagree on what happened in 1966. Two say the tower was destroyed that year.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page says it was removed.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the tower as gone.[^4]

No source we read describes what remains of the tower or the site today.

Anyone planning a visit should ask the Caribou-Targhee National Forest about access first.
