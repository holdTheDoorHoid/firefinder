The Orwig Hump lookout site lies in the Kaniksu National Forest in Pend Oreille County, Washington, about 14 miles east of Ione.[^2] In 1932 the site got a 15-foot tree platform.[^1][^3] A 40-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab went up in 1934.[^1][^3] A patrol platform stood two miles to the northwest, on Tillicum Peak.[^1]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites entry and Willhite both say the tower was removed in 1961.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year instead, and Rex's fire lookout page also lists it as removed.[^2][^4] A USGS map from 1958, with a 1966 update, marks Orwig as abandoned.[^3]

The Forest Service administers the land.[^1] The register gives the elevation as 5,330 feet, though Willhite lists 5,343 feet on older maps.[^1][^3] The register listed the site in December 2012 after a nomination by Ray Kresek.[^1] No source describes what remains at the site today.
