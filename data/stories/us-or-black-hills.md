According to firelookout.com, the Black Hills lookout stood in the Fremont National Forest, about 18 miles north-northwest of Bly in Lake County, Oregon, at an elevation of 5,914 feet.[^1] The firelookout.com page gives only a date line reading "1930s: unknown," so it does not give a year for the lookout.[^1] The page marks it as gone.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list also marks it as gone and gives its type as unknown.[^2]

Today, no source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the land is open to visitors. Anyone planning a trip should check with the forest office first.

[^1]: Black Hills Fire Lookout, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/or/blackhills.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Oregon lookout list, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/or/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
