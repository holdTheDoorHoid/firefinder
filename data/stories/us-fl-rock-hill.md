The Eastern US Lookouts list of Florida towers includes Rock Hill in Walton County, with the U.S. Air Force shown beside the entry.[^1] A U.S. Geological Survey record names a site on Eglin Air Force Base "Bldg 1481 Rock Hill Tower" and classes it as a well.[^2][^3] The name matches the lookout's, but the survey record does not describe a lookout, so the two may or may not be the same structure.

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1]

[^1]: Rock Hill - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/rock-hill.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Walton County monitoring locations, U.S. Geological Survey Water Data, https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/country/US/state/12/county/131 (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: BLDG 1481 ROCK HILL TOWER EGLIN AFB FL, U.S. Geological Survey Water Data, https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/USGS-303545086064601/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
