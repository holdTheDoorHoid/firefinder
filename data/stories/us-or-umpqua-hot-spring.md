In July 1932 the Roseburg News-Review reported that nine new lookout houses were to be built at an estimated cost of $5,300, and that one would go at Umpqua Hot Springs in the upper meadows district.[^1] The Forest Lookouts timeline records a 14 x 14 L-4 ground cabin constructed there that year.[^1][^2]

A July 28, 1942 item in the same paper named Alan Knudtson, son of a local jeweler, as that summer's ranger on the lookout.[^1]

Firelookout.com gives the abandonment as 1965 and calls the site gone.[^2] The Oregon lookout list also marks it as a ground lookout that is gone.[^3] The Forest Lookouts page ends with a single bare word, "Removed," with no date or method attached.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
