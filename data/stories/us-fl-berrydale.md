The Eastern US Forest Lookouts site lists a lookout at Berrydale, in Santa Rosa County, Florida, and gives its map position.[^1]

Berrydale is an unincorporated community in that county and also a census-designated place.[^2] It falls within the Pensacola–Ferry Pass–Brent metropolitan statistical area.[^2] The 2020 census counted 348 residents, down from 441 in 2010.[^2] State Road 4 runs through the community.[^2]

No source found describes what stands at the lookout site now, or whether the public may visit it.
