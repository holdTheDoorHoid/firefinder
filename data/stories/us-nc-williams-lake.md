In February 1940 a Civilian Conservation Corps side camp of eight enrollees, living in house trailers, was based at Williams Lake, in Sampson County, North Carolina.[^1] The crew was to build a house and garage for the towerman there.[^1] The camp was there from February 6 to May 5, 1940.[^1]

On April 29, 1940, a four-room frame house was completed at the tower.[^1] The two entries disagree about the camp: the February entry names Camp P-68, and the April entry names Camp S-68 for the house. I could not settle which is right.[^1]

The tnlandforms list places the tower at Williams Lake, in Sampson County, at 35.14527 N, 78.53916 W.[^2] No source I found says whether the tower still stands, what remains at the site, or whether it is open to visitors.
