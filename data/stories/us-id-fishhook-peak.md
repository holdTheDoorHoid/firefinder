Fishhook Peak, in Shoshone County, Idaho, was developed as a lookout in 1922, with a 10-foot pole tower topped by a metal cupola.[^1][^2][^3] The peak stands at 6,531 feet.[^1][^3]

A log cabin went up about a quarter mile south in 1925.[^1][^3] In 1931 a 20-foot pole L-4 tower was built.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com also notes a patrol platform on Breezy Point, two miles south.[^1]

The 1931 tower was destroyed in 1969.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com dates the lookout's run as 1931 to 1969.[^1]

A post dated October 29, 2024, by the Idaho Fire Lookouts team reports cement footings and one support beam that they believe came from the 1931 tower.[^3] They also found debris they think came from the log cabin, and the post mentions a root cellar.[^3]

The site is listed as hike-in, on St. Joe National Forest land.[^2][^3]
