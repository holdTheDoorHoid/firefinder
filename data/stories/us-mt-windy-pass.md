The Windy Pass lookout stood in the Gallatin National Forest, about 29 miles south of Bozeman, Montana.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site dates a log cabin there to around 1914 and an L-4 cab to 1931.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a ground-level lookout rather than a tower.[^3]

The firelookout.com record says the lookout was abandoned and is gone, but it gives no dates for either.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list also marks it as gone.[^3]

Today the Forest Service rents a cabin called Windy Pass Cabin, on the Windy Pass Trail.[^4][^5] The agency says it was built in 1934 and calls it historic.[^4][^5] It is open from June 16 to October 14.[^4] There is no motorized access, so guests walk or ride a horse the 2.5 miles up the trail, a climb of about 1,300 feet.[^4] Drinking water and firewood are not provided.[^4]

No source we read says the rental cabin is the old lookout or stands on its site.[^1][^4][^5] The lookout's elevation is 9,160 feet on firelookout.com.[^1] The rental cabin sits near the Gallatin Crest at about 8,900 feet, according to the Forest Service and Wild Montana.[^4][^6] We found no source that says whether the old lookout site itself is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
