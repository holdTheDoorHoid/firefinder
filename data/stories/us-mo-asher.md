The Asher fire tower stood on a towersite in Phelps County, Missouri.[^1] According to a state annual report quoted on the Central US Lookouts page, the state acquired 20 acres for the site in the period 1946-1947.[^1]

A June 1947 newspaper item, also quoted on that page, names Asher, on Highway 65, among the sites of 60-foot (18.3 m) wooden towers.[^1] A 1947-48 state manual gives the Missouri Conservation Commission as the owner of the 20-acre towersite.[^1]

In fiscal 1965-66, the page says, a 50-foot (15.2 m) steel tower was put up on the towersite, citing the Missouri Department of Conservation's annual report for that year.[^1] The 60-foot figure belongs to the 1947 wooden towers, so it does not describe the later steel tower.[^1]

The page marks the tower as removed and gives no date.[^1]

A 2019 Phelps County Focus article, quoting Bob Frakes, who has written a book about Missouri's lookout towers, says the Asher site lies on privately owned land.[^2] The article is from 2019, so it may not show who owns the land now. No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site now. The land was reported as private in 2019, so do not go onto it without the owner's permission.
