Big Lake Lookout stood in the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forests in Apache County, Arizona.[^1] Members of the Civilian Conservation Corps erected its 32-foot Aermotor MC-24 tower in 1933.[^1][^2] The register describes a live-in cab measuring 12 by 12 feet.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register added the lookout on September 2, 2009, on the nomination of Dave Lorenz.[^1] Two years later the lookout was lost to fire.[^1]

The Wallow Fire began in Arizona on May 29, 2011.[^3] A fire photographer's account of the blaze places its central part in the Big Lake recreation area.[^3] The register says the Wallow Fire destroyed the lookout in June 2011.[^1] The Big Lake Knoll history page dates the loss to June 4, 2011, when flames overran the tower.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the tower as gone.[^4] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
