Washington Peak is a lookout site in Santa Cruz County, Arizona, on the Coronado National Forest.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it near 31.3533 north and 110.7255 west, with the type not recorded and the status marked as gone.[^1]

The two sources checked do not describe what, if anything, remains at the site today or whether the ground is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Arizona Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/az/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Arizona - Forest Lookouts, westlookouts.weebly.com, https://westlookouts.weebly.com/arizona.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
