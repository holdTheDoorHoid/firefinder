The Zenith lookout is a 100-foot tower of the state's standard design, with an outside ladder the register describes as protected, administered by the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources.[^1] It stands in Lincoln County, west of Tomahawk off Highway 86.[^1][^2]

The register dates its construction to December 1939.[^1] I found no source naming the builder or saying when the tower was first staffed.

A July 1977 newspaper item, quoted on the Central US Lookouts page, reported a burglary at the tower. According to the item, the door was pulled off its hinges, glass was broken and a radio used for communications was damaged.[^2] The report came from a state Department of Natural Resources employee. That hints at some state connection to the site at the time, but the item does not show that anyone staffed the cab.[^2]

A Wisconsin lookout list still shows the tower as standing.[^3] The register accepted it on June 19, 2017, on a nomination by Tyler Bormann.[^1]

In 2015 the Associated Press reported that dozens of the state's forest-fire towers, most dating to the 1930s and 1940s, were being decommissioned. The part of that report I could read does not mention Zenith by name.[^4]

Today I found no source about access, climbing or rental for the Zenith tower. Check with the Wisconsin DNR before any visit, and stay off a tower that is closed or posted as off limits.
