Marshall Knobs is the name of a former tower site in Rutherford County, Tennessee, at an elevation of 882 feet (269 m).[^1] Tennessee Landforms marks the tower, waypoint RUT001, as removed.[^1] The waypoint sits at 35.76671, -86.39245.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register's Tennessee list does not include it.[^3]

A hiking-app listing describes a loop trail of about 3.1 miles that reaches the summit from a trailhead that is easy to reach by car.[^2] The listing does not mention a tower.[^2] None of the sources read for this story describes what, if anything, remains at the tower site today.
