Klone Peak rises to 6,820 feet in the Wenatchee National Forest in Chelan County, Washington.[^1][^2] The U.S. Forest Service administers the site.[^1]

The lookout was an L-5 cab built in 1931 at the highest point of the peak.[^1][^2][^4] Panorama photos were taken there on September 18, 1934.[^3][^4]

The cab came down in 1963: Firelookout.com and Willhite say it was destroyed, while Firetower.org says it was removed.[^1][^2][^4] The site was registered on October 21, 2012, with Ray Kresek as nominator.[^1]

The sources do not say what remains on the summit today. One undated Willhite photo is captioned "Assortment of little items at the summit," but no text explains it.[^4] Willhite's account covers the approach instead. He came in from Maverick Saddle, about 12 hiking miles, and found the trails to the top gave no trouble.[^4] The upper Mad River trails close seasonally to everyone except hikers, in late spring and early summer.[^4]
