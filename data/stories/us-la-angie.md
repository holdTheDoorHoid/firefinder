The Angie tower stood on a hilltop in Washington Parish, on a rise that also held a church.[^1] A survey description on the Central US Lookouts site gives its height as about 80 feet.[^1] It describes four steel legs, orange paint, and an enclosed glass lookout at the top.[^1] That page names the Louisiana Forest Service as the agency involved.[^1]

A Pastmaps reproduction of a 1949 U.S. Geological Survey topographic map labels it the Angie Fire Tower.[^3]

Wikipedia's list of Louisiana towers says it was torn down. The same list says the USGS last showed it on a map in 2012.[^2]

No source we read describes the site today.[^1][^2][^3] The survey record does mention a 1938 bench mark about 54 feet north of the tower's station, a bronze disk set in the top of a concrete post.[^1] No source says whether that marker is still in place.[^1][^2][^3]
