Big Meadows is in Bonner County, Idaho, inside the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^2] The site sits at about 3,543 feet, roughly 17 miles north-northwest of Priest River.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts listing gives its land-survey location as 59N-5W-33.[^3]

A crow's nest and a camp were established there in the 1920s.[^1][^2] A Forest Lookouts page for the site, which credits a source named Kresek, also dates the crow's nest to the 1920s.[^3]

By the 1980s the crow's nest and camp were in ruins.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts records the last documentation of those ruins in that decade.[^1][^2] The Kamstra listing marks the lookout as gone, with dates given as the 1920s to an unknown end.[^1]

Today the site is tagged on Idaho Fire Lookouts as hike-in and abandoned, which suggests it is reached on foot rather than by road.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site now.[^2]
