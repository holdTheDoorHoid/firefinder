The firelookout.ca index lists a Cynthia Fire Tower on Ferguson Trail, near Temagami in Nipissing District, Ontario, at an elevation of 405 m (1329 ft).[^1] Its coordinates are close to, but not the same as, Wikipedia's coordinates for Mount Ferguson, a peak that overlooks Ferguson Bay on Lake Temagami, so this story treats the two as one site.[^1][^2] Wikipedia gives the mountain's elevation as 415 m (1,362 ft), so the two figures do not match.[^2] Wikipedia also says the mountain lies close to where the Cynthia and Aston townships meet.[^2]

The firelookout.ca listing describes a steel tower and says it appears on a 1977 topographic map.[^1] Wikipedia and a Temagami map atlas both date the tower's dismantling to about 1975, so the firelookout.ca record and those two sources disagree on the date.[^1][^2][^3]

The Department of Lands and Forests set up a lookout on the summit, and it was manned.[^2][^3] A Temagami atlas says it was taken out of use in 1966.[^3]

Since the tower was dismantled, there is no lookout on the summit to climb.[^2][^3] No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains there today.[^2][^3]

The fire tower on Caribou Mountain is a separate structure. It was built in 1961, and visitors can still climb it.[^4]
