The Frye Mountain fire tower stood in Waldo County, Maine, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association now lists the original site as gone.[^1] A tower that had been taken down was erected again on the mountain in 1931, according to a state forestry report quoted on the Eastern U.S. Lookouts page.[^2] Wikipedia says the tower was staffed between 1931 and 1991 by spotters trained to find forest fires.[^3]

The sources disagree about when the tower left the mountain. The association's register says the tower was taken apart in 2002 and moved to Swan Island.[^4] Wikipedia gives the same year and places the tower on Swan Island in the Kennebec River.[^3] The Eastern U.S. Lookouts page says the tower was removed around 2002, then moved and reassembled on Swan Island in 2005.[^2] I could not settle which date is right.

The register lists the site as US 106 and ME 32, and gives a registration date of September 15, 2008.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a standing tower at the Swan Island Wildlife Management Area, relocated from Frye Mountain.[^1] The state's Swan Island page lists a wildlife viewing tower among the area's features, but it does not say that this is the former fire tower.[^5] The same page describes shelters and a fishing pond.[^5] I did not find a statement about climbing the old tower, so check the state's current rules before going up any tower.
