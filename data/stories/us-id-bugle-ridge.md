Bugle Ridge Lookout stood in the Kaniksu National Forest in Bonner County, Idaho, about 5 miles northeast of Upper Priest Lake, at an elevation of 6,083 feet.[^1] Priest Lake lies in the Idaho Panhandle, within the Idaho Panhandle National Forests, and was once called Kaniksu Lake.[^4]

The earliest entry is a camp on the ridge in 1933. A cabin is listed there in 1936.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the same 1936 cabin and 1960 destruction dates.[^2] The Forest Lookouts listing attributes its dates to a single source, Kresek.[^3]

The cabin was destroyed in 1960.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in and as abandoned.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the ridge today.
