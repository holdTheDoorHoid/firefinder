Beartrap Lookout stood in Lemhi County, Idaho, in the Salmon-Challis National Forest, at 8,078 feet.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page places it about eight miles west of Shoup.[^4]

The first lookout on the site was a log cabin, built in 1919.[^1][^4] In 1935 a two-story lookout went up: an L-4 cab with a catwalk, set on a log crib base.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

The earliest staffing we found is from 1951, when a newspaper item named Mr. and Mrs. Forrest E. Sedgwick of Shoup as the lookouts on Bear Trap.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts says it has no documented staffing history for the tower.[^3] The two sources disagree. We cite the dated 1951 item and note the gap.[^3][^4]

The Register lists the tower as registered on October 10, 2011.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register lists its site entry as registered on March 24, 2020.[^2]

The tower burned in the Mustang Complex fire in August 2012.[^1][^3][^4] The Register lists it as removed in 2012.[^1]

A photo caption on the Register page reads "Tower remains in August 2019," so the Register holds a photo of the remains from that month.[^1] We found no source that describes the site today or says whether the public can reach it. Check with the Salmon-Challis National Forest before planning any trip.
