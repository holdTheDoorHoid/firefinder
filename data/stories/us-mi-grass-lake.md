The Grass Lake lookout is in Kalkaska County, and the Forest Lookouts page gives the State Department of Conservation as its agency.[^1] A National Geodetic Survey data sheet quoted on that page calls it the Grass Lake Lookout Tower, and places it on the Cote Dame Marie quadrangle, a U.S. Geological Survey topographic map.[^1]

The state's report for 1937 and 1938 planned log cabins for forest fire towermen at the Grass Lake Tower and at two other towers.[^1] The cabins were to be 20 by 26 feet (6.1 by 7.9 m), built of logs.[^1]

No source we read describes the site today, so we cannot say whether any part of the tower remains or whether the public may visit it.[^1]

[^1]: Grass Lake, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/grass-lake.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
