The websiteservice.ca tower list gives Torch's position as 53-40-52.7 X 104-39-48.0, or about 53.68 degrees north and 104.66 degrees west.[^1]

The towermen list on the same site has Ole Rudd at Torch in the 1960s.[^2] Doreen Jensen Svare is listed for 1970 and 1971 at Torch, alongside a second place name marked with a question mark.[^2] Sheryl Fabish is listed as relief at Torch for 1993 and 1994.[^2]

No source we found describes what remains at the Torch site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^2]: Firetowers of Saskatchewan: Firetowermen and Women, websiteservice.ca, https://www.websiteservice.ca/firetowermen.htm (accessed 2026-10-20).
