The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers has a "goodloe" entry in Baldwin County, at coordinates 33.12115, -83.13781.[^1] The entry gives an elevation of 590. The list prints no unit, so that figure reads as feet.[^1] Its last field is "datasheet DF1058."[^1] The page's legend says towers 1 to 72 come from a USGS name database, and that the list also draws on 1:100,000 USGS topographic maps and NGS data sheets.[^1] Names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own creation, so "goodloe" may be a label rather than a name the tower carried.[^1]

No source I could read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
