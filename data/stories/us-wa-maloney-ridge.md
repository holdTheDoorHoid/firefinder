The Maloney Ridge lookout stood in King County, Washington, about a mile south of Skykomish, at roughly 3,364 feet.[^1][^2] Its L-4 cab came from Galena Mountain, where it was built in 1931.[^3] In 1941 the cab was taken apart, carried up to this ridge and reassembled.[^4]

WillhiteWeb dates the move to 1950 instead, and says the site was established that year when the Galena cab arrived.[^5]

During World War II the Aircraft Warning Service manned the station.[^4][^1] An AWS cabin replaced the L-4 cab in 1942,[^1][^2] and another L-4 cab went up in 1953.[^1][^2]

The cab was removed in 1969.[^1] Forest Lookouts says it was removed by burning, while Firelookout.com and WillhiteWeb call it destroyed.[^4][^2][^5]

A WillhiteWeb visit found the rocky perch and the foundations of the lookout still on the site.[^5] Communication towers also stand on the ridge, and the same account says they draw visitors' attention away from exploring the area.[^5] The account also says the lookout stood nearer what is now called Sobieski Mountain, and that its spot sits some distance from where the map places the Maloney Ridge name.[^5]

The road is good gravel, but a winter gate closes it; WillhiteWeb's gate note is dated November 7, 2014.[^5]
