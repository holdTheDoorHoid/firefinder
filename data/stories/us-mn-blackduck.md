The Blackduck Lookout Tower stands in Beltrami County, and the Minnesota lookout list marks it as standing.[^2] A photo page on Wikimedia Commons dates it to 1935, places it in the Chippewa National Forest, and says it is no longer used for fire detection.[^1] That caption is the only source we found for the 1935 date. It is a photo description on Commons rather than an official record, and we found nothing to check it against, so treat the date as unconfirmed.

A 2016 Minnesota chapter report, hosted on firelookout.org, mentions three fire towers in the Chippewa National Forest.[^3] It does not name them, so we cannot say whether the Blackduck tower is one of the three.[^3] The author also wrote that they had not heard whether there had been any further progress involving those three towers.[^3]

We found no current access notice and no climbing status for this tower. Check with the Chippewa National Forest before you visit, and do not climb the tower unless it is open to the public.
