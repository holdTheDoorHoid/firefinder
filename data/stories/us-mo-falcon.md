The Falcon lookout tower stood in Laclede County, Missouri.[^1] The Central US Lookouts page lists the Mark Twain National Forest as its agency and gives its location as 34N-13W-28.[^1] The page marks the tower removed, with no date.[^1]

Falcon, an unincorporated community in eastern Laclede County, lies roughly sixteen miles east of Lebanon on Route 32.[^2] The Gasconade River runs just east of the community, which falls inside the Mark Twain National Forest.[^2] Its post office has operated since 1907, and its ZIP code is 65470.[^2] No source we found ties the lookout to this community.

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it is open to visitors. Because the Mark Twain National Forest is the listed agency, ask the forest before you go.[^1]
