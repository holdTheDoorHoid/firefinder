Falmouth is classed as an unincorporated community in Suwannee County, Florida.[^1] The Forest Lookouts website groups its lookouts by state, and its Florida list includes a Falmouth tower in Suwannee County.[^2]

A report covering 2015 and early 2016 from the Florida chapter of the Forest Fire Lookout Association describes members trying to work out how many of 347 sites on an online listing are still standing. It notes that some of those sites are already gone.[^3]

None of the sources checked describes what remains at the Falmouth site today, or whether the public can visit it.
