Rocky Point Lookout stands in the Clearwater National Forest in Idaho County, about 5 miles north-northeast of the Powell Ranger Station.[^2] It sits on the historic Lolo Indian Trail, the route Lewis and Clark used through this country.[^1]

The first tower here was a 10-foot log crib with an L-4 cab on top, in use from 1934.[^2] The current lookout replaced that cab.[^1] In 1963 a 10-foot concrete base carrying an R-6 flat cab went up in its place.[^1][^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register says the tower is staffed every year and is fairly easy to reach from Powell.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists known staff from the mid-1970s through the early 1990s.[^3] The Forest Service's list of Clearwater lookouts describes the cab as manned.[^4] None of the sources consulted records a fire, a move or a closure at this site.

The register added Rocky Point on April 24, 2009, and the U.S. Forest Service administers it.[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels Rocky Point drive-up and staffed.[^3] No source consulted describes rental terms, visitor tours or climbing rules, so check with the Clearwater National Forest and its Powell Ranger District before planning a visit.
