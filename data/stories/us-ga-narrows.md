The Narrows lookout in Chattooga County was built in 1941 by the Civilian Conservation Corps, according to a history of the Armuchee Ranger District that the Eastern US Forest Lookouts site quotes.[^1] It was a steel structure 42 feet tall, with a wooden cab measuring 12 feet by 12 feet. A catwalk four feet wide, with guardrails, ran around the outside of the cab.[^1] The site is listed at 1,657 feet of elevation.[^2] The Eastern US Forest Lookouts page places the tower in Chattahoochee National Forest.[^1]

The district history says the tower was dismantled in 1978, so the lookout has been gone for nearly five decades.[^1]

Today no source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, and none says whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]
