Beaver Island Fire Tower #2 stood on Beaver Island in Charlevoix County, Michigan, at about 45.65 degrees north and 85.58 degrees west.[^1] The Michigan Fire Towers listing marks it as removed in 1966 and down.[^1] A chronology note on the same page says the U.S. Geological Survey reported by 1966 that the tower had been torn down.[^1]

The same site also has a page for Beaver Island Fire Tower #1, which it places in the Manistee National Forest.[^2] The listing for #2 also gives the PID number QK0850.[^1]

No source we read describes the site today, so we cannot say what remains there or whether the ground is open to visitors.[^1]

[^2]: Beaver Island Fire Tower #1, Michigan Fire Towers, https://michiganfiretower.com/fire-towers/beaver-island-fire-tower-1/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
