Wadleigh Mountain sits in Piscataquis County, in Township 1, Range 12.[^1][^7] A Maine Forest Service panoramic map dated 1919 is centered on a fire tower there, so a lookout stood on the mountain by then.[^7]

In December 1926 the state began hauling steel for a new 36-foot steel lookout tower on the mountain.[^1] A 1935 newspaper item also gave the tower's height as 36 feet.[^1]

In September 1940 a newspaper reported that Linwood Rideout had worked six weeks in a lookout tower on Mount Wadleigh, at Kokadjo, for the Maine State Forest Commission.[^1]

In 2013 Dave Merchant, a member of the Forest Fire Lookout Association (FFLA), created a group called Friends of Wadleigh Mountain, and a restoration proposal went to the state.[^2] That fall, two maintenance trips were made up the mountain as a stabilization effort.[^2] The tower was weather tight for the 2013 to 2014 winter.[^2]

In 2014 the state had budgeted money for structural evaluations but had not yet decided on the proposal.[^3] In 2015 the state agreed to run a structural analysis in 2016 as a step before any restoration plans.[^4] The 2016 report said the project was on hold until the state finished its structural analysis.[^5]

The FFLA Maine chapter's 2017 tower survey recorded the lookout as removed.[^6] Because of the removal, FFLA worked with Parks and Lands to obtain a 12-foot section of ladder for the Squaw Mountain tower at the Greenville visitor's center.[^6]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as gone.[^8] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the public can reach it.
