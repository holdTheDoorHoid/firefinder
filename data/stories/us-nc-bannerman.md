A statewide list of North Carolina lookout towers places a fire tower named Bannerman in Pender County, at about 34.59 north and 77.80 west.[^1] The list says it draws on the NC FFLA site and on USGS topographic maps.[^1] It also notes that a few observation towers and Coast Guard lookouts may be included.[^1]

A TopoQuest entry names the point Bannerman Fire Tower, in Pender County, places it on the USGS 1:24K Stag Park map, and gives its elevation as 19 feet above sea level.[^2] The North Carolina Forest Service's District 8 office serves Pender County.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to the public.
