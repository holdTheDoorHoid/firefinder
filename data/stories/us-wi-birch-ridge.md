Birch Ridge is a lookout site in the Sanborn area of Ashland County, Wisconsin, and lies close to the hamlet of Birch Hill.[^1]

A USGS topographic map, portrayed in 1964 and published in 1965, shows the Birch Ridge Lookout Tower on high ground.[^2] The sheet covers the Cedar and Saxon area.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower at 46.52319 and -90.55046 and marks it as gone.[^3]

Nothing we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors. Ask the landowner or local forest managers before going there, and do not climb or enter any structure you find.
