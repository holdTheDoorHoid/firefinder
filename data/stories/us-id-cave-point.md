Cave Point Lookout stood in Clearwater County, in the Clearwater National Forest.[^1][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives its elevation as 4,588 feet.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the site at 46.70975, -115.53441.[^4]

Records give 1931 for a 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2] The same listings also record an L-5 cab on an 8-foot log crib about a mile north.[^1][^2][^3] The Clearwater National Forest list places the site in section 24 of township 39 North.[^3]

Sources agree that the lookout was destroyed in 1960.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^4]

No source describes what remains at the site. Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site as hike-in, and none of the pages I read says whether visitors may go there.[^2]
