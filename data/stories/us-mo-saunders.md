The Saunders lookout tower was built in Christian County, Missouri, in 1936.[^1] The Central US Lookouts page lists it under the Mark Twain National Forest.[^1] On March 12, 1936, The Douglas County Herald reported that construction had started on the fourth lookout tower in the Pondfork forest unit, three miles south of Chadwick on the old Springdale ranch.[^1] The paper said the site would carry the Saunders name, and that the unit's other three lookouts were at Swan, Hercules and Caney.[^1]

On April 9, 1936, the same paper reported that a camp of Civilian Conservation Corps workers had been set up at the tower site.[^1] A Christian County paper reported on March 25, 1937, that the tower had gone up the spring before, but its telephone link to the Swan tower was not finished until December 1936.[^1]

A 1981 print of a USGS map of Chadwick, dated to 1955, lists the Saunders Lookout Tower among its landmarks.[^2]

No source we found describes the Saunders site today, so we cannot say whether the tower still stands or whether visitors can reach it.
