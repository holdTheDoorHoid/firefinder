The Forest Lookouts site lists a Holum lookout tower in Caldwell Parish, in section 11 of township 11 North, range 4 East. It marks the tower as removed, with no date given.[^1] A USGS topographic map of the area, portrayed in 1984 and published in 1985, lists Holum as a settlement.[^3]

A map database still carries the name.[^2] Mapcarta lists a point called Holum Lookout Tower, typed as a tower, and puts the Holum hamlet about four and a half miles northwest of it.[^2] A map point gives a name and a place, not a condition, so it does not show whether anything stands there.[^2]

No source we read describes the site today or says what, if anything, remains of the tower. Nothing we read says who owns the land or whether the public can reach it, so ask locally before you go, and do not expect a standing tower.

[^2]: Holum Lookout Tower, Mapcarta, https://mapcarta.com/21001100 (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: 1984 Map of Holum, LA, Pastmaps, https://pastmaps.com/map/holum-caldwell-county-la-usgs-topo-1984-p1985 (accessed 2026-10-10).
