The Cemetery Ridge lookout stood in Wallowa County, within the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest, about 33 miles northeast of Enterprise.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association says an L-4 cab was built here in 1940.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the same year and lists the cab as gone.[^2]

Named watchers appear in the record from 1942 to 1951, including Fred Beaudoin in 1942 and Robert Spencer in 1951.[^2]

The cab's later history is disputed. The Forest Fire Lookout Association suggests the cab could have gone to Buckhorn Mountain in 1947.[^1] The Buckhorn Mountain record, however, says its L-4 cab was moved there from Fairchild Point in 1952.[^4] A weebly page quotes a 1954 history of the Wallowa National Forest that says the Cemetery lookout blew over in 1952.[^3] The sources do not agree on what became of the cab.[^1][^3][^4]

No source read describes what, if anything, remains on Cemetery Ridge today.[^2] The Forest Service's Buckhorn Tower Viewpoint is open for day use only.[^5] None of the sources read links that viewpoint to the Cemetery Ridge cab.[^1][^4]
