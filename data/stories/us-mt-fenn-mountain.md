Fenn Mountain's lookout was in Powell County, on the Lolo National Forest, 17 miles east of Seeley Lake.[^1] firelookout.com gives the site an elevation of 8,241 feet.[^1] A 1928 newspaper item quoted on the Montana page gives the lookout's elevation as 8,200.[^2]

firelookout.com dates the cabin to 1929.[^1] A July 28, 1928 item from The Daily Missoulian, quoted on the Montana page, already credits the Fenn Mountain lookout with the ability to detect fires in that district.[^2] The two dates do not line up.[^1][^2]

The same 1928 article reports a planned telephone line from Monture to the top of the mountain, partly because the upper Swan River area still lacked communication links.[^2]

firelookout.com also lists a crow's nest on Conger Point, roughly two miles to the southeast.[^1]

firelookout.com lists the cabin as gone by 1958.[^1] The Montana page marks it removed,[^2] and the association's list shows a gone ground site.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
