Porter Butte sits in Klamath County, Oregon, in the Upper Klamath Lake area.[^1] An August 1937 Klamath News item classed it as a minor lookout and said the Klamath Forest Protective Association covered it.[^1] A Klamath County lookouts blog says the site was built in the 1930s and that the type of structure is unknown.[^2] The blog names the same association as the managing agency.[^2]

The two sources also disagree on the survey location: one gives the code 38S-8E-18 and the other Sec 13 T38S R7E.[^1][^2]

No source describes what is on Porter Butte today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Porter Butte - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts, https://oregonlookouts.weebly.com/porter-butte.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Klamath County Fire Lookouts, Klamath County Fire Lookouts blog, https://klamathlookouts.blogspot.com/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
