The Department of Forestry planned a fire observation tower near Saint Lawrence, in Cambria County, for the summer of 1921.[^1] A July 1921 item from the Indiana Evening Gazette called it Tower No. 3, to stand on land owned by Henry Loidon and to cover parts of Cambria and Clearfield counties.[^1] An August 1921 report in Forest Leaves listed Saint Lawrence in the first batch of 18 towers to go up that summer.[^1]

The register describes it as a steel Aermotor tower, built in 1921 and removed in 1978.[^2] Its entry gives an elevation of about 673 metres (2,207 feet) and was added to the register in September 2008.[^2] No source we read describes what remains at the site today or whether visitors can reach it.
