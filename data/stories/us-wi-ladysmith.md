The Ladysmith Lookout Tower stands in Rusk County, Wisconsin, near the town of Ladysmith.[^1][^2] The Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list shows it as standing, and leaves its register column blank.[^2] We could not find when the tower was built, how tall it is, or who built it.

A 1949 social note from the Chippewa Herald-Tribune, copied on the Forest Lookouts page, describes one man as staying at the tower near Ladysmith.[^1] The note is brief and does not describe how the tower was staffed. It is the only account we found of anyone staying in the tower.

A separate Wisconsin Historical Society record covers the Ladysmith Ranger Station on Highway 8 in Rusk County. It gives 1932 as the station's build year and notes that a crew lived there for three seasons each year.[^3] We found nothing that ties that station to the tower, so we cannot say whether the two share a site.

The same record says the station was slated for replacement in 2003, and that the building was divested from Wisconsin DNR ownership in 2025.[^3] We have not found what became of the tower after those changes.

We found no public access information for the tower. Treat its access as unknown, check before going near it, and do not climb a tower that is closed or private.
