In September 1930, forestry officials reported that an 80-foot (24 m) lookout tower had been finished on Ramer Creek, along the Mossy Head-Niceville road.[^1] A Pensacola News Journal report, quoted on the page, said visitors could climb the tower to look out over the forest.[^1]

The page places the tower in Okaloosa County, Florida.[^1] Mossy Head, which the road's name includes, is an unincorporated community in Walton County.[^2]

The page's agency line names the Choctawhatchee National Forest, then the War Department.[^1] According to the page, the lookout facilities went to the War Department in 1940.[^1]

No source describes the site today, so what, if anything, remains of the tower is not known.
