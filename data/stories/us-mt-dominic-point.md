Dominic Point is a patrol lookout in the Bitterroot National Forest, in Ravalli County, Montana.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site places it two miles west of Willow Mountain lookout.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list shows Willow Mountain as a standing two-story cab.[^2]

A wildfire with the same name, the Dominic Point Fire, was located about 14 miles northeast of Hamilton in July 2010.[^3] It was first reported on July 25, and by July 26 the blaze had spread across an estimated 700 to 1,000 acres of the Bitterroot National Forest.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the Dominic Point site today.[^1]
