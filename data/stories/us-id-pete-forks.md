Pete Forks Lookout stood in the Clearwater National Forest, about 12 miles southeast of Pierce in Idaho County, at an elevation of 5,578 feet.[^1][^2] An L-4 tower went up on the site in 1934.[^1][^2]

The Forest Service list places the tower in Section 9, Township 35 North.[^3] The list draws on Ray Kresek's book, Fire Lookouts of the Northwest, and on Forest Service records.[^3] The tower was destroyed in 1948, a date all three sources give.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts classes the site as drive-up.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's regional register classes the structure as a tower, gives its coordinates as 46.38189 north and 115.58930 west, and marks it as gone.[^4] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.
