The Forest Lookouts site lists a Hornbeck lookout tower in Sabine Parish, in section 30 of township 4 North, range 10 West, and marks it as removed, with no date given.[^1]

The National Geodetic Survey described the tower in 1971. It records a steel structure on four legs, finished in silver paint, with a cabin on top; its overall height is 110 feet.[^2] The same description names the Louisiana Division of Forestry as the owner, and places the tower about two and a half miles southwest of the town of Hornbeck.[^2]

The town itself lies in Vernon Parish.[^3] The survey file and the Forest Lookouts site both put the tower in Sabine Parish, and neither explains the difference.[^2][^1]

The survey file's only history entry is its first observation in 1971, so it does not show when the tower came down.[^2] No source we read describes the site today or says whether any part of the tower remains. Access is also unknown, so ask locally before you go.
