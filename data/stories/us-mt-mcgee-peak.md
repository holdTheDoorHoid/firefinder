McGee Peak is a former lookout site in the Lolo National Forest, about six miles south of Haugan in Mineral County, Montana.[^1] The firelookout.com page dates a crow's nest on the peak to 1935, placed one mile northwest of Crystal Lake, and gives its elevation as 6,401 feet.[^1]

The same page says the site was abandoned in 1941.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list describes it as a crowsnest and gives its status as Abandoned.[^2] We found no source that says the structure was removed.

A Mineral County list also includes McGee Peak, with a township and range and no other detail.[^3]

We found no source that describes the peak today or whether visitors may go there. Check with the Lolo National Forest before going.
