Thunderbolt Mountain stands 8 miles southwest of the Spotted Bear Ranger Station, in the Flathead National Forest. The listing places it in Flathead/Lake County, Montana, at an elevation of 7,910 feet.[^1]

Firelookout.com lists a log cabin on the peak in 1920, followed by an L-4 cab in 1948.[^1] The same listing also names another L-4 cab, two miles to the south, on Gildart.[^1] The listing marks the lookout as gone.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's state list files the site under "Ground" and also marks it gone, at 47.86567 north and 113.67555 west.[^2]

Montana has a second Thunderbolt Mountain, in Jefferson and Powell counties, and it is a separate site.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains on the peak today, or whether it is open to visitors.
