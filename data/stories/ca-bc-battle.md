Battle Mountain lies in the southeast of Wells Gray Provincial Park, in the Thompson-Nicola Regional District of east-central British Columbia.[^1] Its three summits range from 2,306 m (7,566 ft) to 2,369 m (7,772 ft).[^1] The mountain is a well-liked summer destination, and its alpine meadows are known for their bright colour.[^1]

A route description on Trailpeak notes an old fire lookout cabin on the lower summit.[^2] Its writers found the cabin snowbound on March 28, 2017.[^2] From Fight Meadow the route heads north over 52 Ridge and down Bull Valley, then climbs more than 400 m (1,312 ft) to the lower summit.[^2]

The access road is gravel and branches east from Clearwater Valley Road.[^1] The first 2.4 km (1.5 mi) is passable for all vehicles, but a high-clearance vehicle is needed for the next 5 km (3.1 mi) to the parking lot.[^1] The trail begins at the lot and sees heavy use.[^1] A day trip reaches the meadows, but summit hikers should allow two days and pack overnight gear.[^1] The season for hiking here falls between late June and September.[^1]

As of 2026, a Rainlander guide places the lookout among towers whose structures are either confirmed standing or likely still standing but unmaintained.[^3] Wild Bluebell's region-by-region list of towers thought to survive includes it too.[^4] No source I found describes the cabin's condition today, or says whether visitors may go inside it.
