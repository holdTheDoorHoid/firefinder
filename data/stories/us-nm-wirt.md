Wirt is a fire lookout tower in Rio Arriba County, New Mexico, at about 36.807 north and 106.927 west, according to the state association's list.[^1] The list shows it as standing and gives it no register number.[^1]

I could not find a build date, builder, design, or history for Wirt in the sources I could reach. The list's columns cover name, county, coordinates, type, status, and register number, with no date or staffing column.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page for the tower files it under the Jicarilla Indian Agency.[^2] That label shows how the site groups the tower. It does not say whether the tower sits on tribal land or who manages it, and I could not confirm the land status from what I read.

No dated events turned up, so I cannot describe its construction, staffing, fires, or any later changes. The tower is listed only as standing, and I found no record of access or a visiting route.

Treat it as unknown ground, and do not climb it without the landowner's permission.
