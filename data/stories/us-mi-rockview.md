Rockview appears in Mackinac County, Michigan, on the Eastern US Lookouts list of forest lookouts.[^1] For most of the 1900s the state had more than 250 fire towers spread across it.[^2] In 1962 the state turned to aircraft for aerial surveillance, and in 1970 it began selling off its towers.[^2][^3] Today fewer than 25 of the state's towers still stand at their original sites.[^3]

No source I found describes what remains at the Rockview site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
