Andimaul Fire Lookout is in Kitimat-Stikine, British Columbia.[^1] The firelookout.ca directory places it at 55.117, -127.99.[^1]

A local history page about the Andimaul area links to a BC Archives photograph of the view from the Andimaul forest lookout.[^2] The same page describes Andimaul as a flag station on the Grand Trunk Pacific Railway, 254 km (158 mi) from Prince Rupert.[^2]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
