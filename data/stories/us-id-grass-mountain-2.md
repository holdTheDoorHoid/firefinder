Grass Mountain was a lookout in the Payette National Forest, in Idaho County, at 6,262 feet.[^1][^2]

The Forest Lookouts list of Idaho County lookouts describes a tent camp on the site in the 1920s, followed by an L-4 cab.[^3] The firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts entries both date that cab to 1934.[^1][^2] The record also names a patrol point three miles north, at 6,090 feet.[^1][^2] The firelookout.com entry places the lookout 12 miles east of Chamberlain G.S.[^1]

The lookout was destroyed in 1978.[^1][^2][^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it as a hike-in site, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association index marks it as gone.[^2][^4]
