High Top is on a North Carolina list of lookout towers, in Haywood County.[^1] The list gives its coordinates.[^1] It says it draws on the NC FFLA site and counts 208 North Carolina fire towers, and it warns that it "may include a few observation towers and Coast Guard lookouts."[^1]

The earliest record I found for this site is from January 1928.[^2] The Forest Worker announced a new 30-foot steel tower on Hightop Mountain, near Asheville, and said it would be staffed soon.[^2]

A lookout index files that item under a heading for Hill Top Mountain, in Haywood County and the Pisgah National Forest.[^2] The 1928 text says Hightop, and the state list says High Top. These may be one site or two, and I could not tell which.[^1][^2]

No source I could read says what, if anything, remains at the High Top site, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: North Carolina lookouts index, Hill Top Mountain entry (quoting The Forest Worker, January 1928), Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/north-carolina.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
