Lost Creek Lookout stood on the Crow Indian Reservation, about 30 miles south of Billings in Big Horn County.[^1] The reservation is home to the Crow Tribe.[^5] It was established in 1868 and covers parts of Big Horn, Yellowstone and Treasure counties.[^5]

Rex's Fire Lookout Page files the tower under Big Horn County and the Crow Indian Agency.[^2][^3] The tower's listing gives the site an elevation of 7,000 feet and notes a USGS benchmark, QW0543.[^1] The same list places Pryor and Wolf Mountain under the same agency.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the tower at 45.34555, -108.44080, classes it as a tower, and lists it as gone.[^4]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.[^1][^4]
