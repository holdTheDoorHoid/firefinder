The Soctum Hill lookout stood in Sumter County, Alabama.[^1] According to the Forest Lookouts directory, the Allison Lumber Company built the tall, enclosed wooden tower, which the directory calls the first lookout erected in Alabama.[^1]

The directory gives two dates for the tower. Its heading says 1924, but a 1971 Tuscaloosa News report that it quotes says the company built the first fire tower in 1922.[^1] We have used 1922 because the directory ties that date to a named source, a 1971 Tuscaloosa News report, while the 1924 heading gives none.[^1]

According to the same report, dated March 19, 1971, the tower had recently been taken down for safety reasons.[^1]

Today a TopoQuest place listing still shows a historical "Soctum Fire Tower" in Sumter County, at an elevation of 196 feet.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the public can visit it.
