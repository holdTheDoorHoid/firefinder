A statewide list of North Carolina lookout towers places Ansonville in Anson County.[^1] The Ansonville page credits the North Carolina Forest Service and says the State erected the tower in 1944.[^2] It gives the height as 99 feet, 9 inches, citing the 10th Biennial Report of the Department of Conservation and Development for 1944.[^2]

For context, the state's first fire tower went up in Harnett County in 1926.[^3] From 1933 to 1938, the Civilian Conservation Corps lent a hand with 52 new fire towers.[^3]

The Ansonville page marks the tower as removed.[^2] No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3] Because the tower was removed, a visit should not be planned around climbing it.[^2]
