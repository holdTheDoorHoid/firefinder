Emigrant Butte rises in the Willamette National Forest in Lane County, about 25 miles southeast of Oakridge.[^1][^2] The register says a 30-foot wooden platform tower went up there in the 1930s.[^1] Rex's page describes a 20-foot open platform tower around 1940, so the two sources disagree on the height.[^3]

A July 1938 item in The Eugene Guard, quoted on Rex's page, says Charles Wert was posted on the butte for the summer as a Forest Service lookout for fires.[^3]

The register and firelookout.com both say the tower was gone by 1957.[^1][^2] Rex's page dates the lookout's abandonment to the 1950s.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the butte today. Rex's page has a heading for remains with no text beneath it in our copy, and we found nothing on current access. Check with the Willamette National Forest before planning a trip.
