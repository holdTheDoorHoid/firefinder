The Digby fire tower, which the Firelookout.ca index spells Dibgy,[^2] stood on rocky ground directly north of Uphill, in what was the Township of Laxton, Digby and Longford.[^1] The township was in the north of today's Kawartha Lakes.[^1] The index places its record at Sebright instead, so the two sources do not agree on where the tower stood.[^2]

A wooden tower was first built on the site in 1922.[^3] In 1957 it was replaced by a 30.5 m (100 ft) heavy steel tower.[^3] The tower was listed in the Lindsay Fire District, which the same notes say was formed in the 1940s.[^3]

The township's Wikipedia article says the tower was taken out of service in the late 1960s.[^1]

The same article says the area is now mostly swamp, reachable by a newer private road to the west.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains of the tower. Because the road is private, do not assume you may use it.
