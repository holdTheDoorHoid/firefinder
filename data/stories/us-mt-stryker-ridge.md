Stryker Ridge is a lookout site about 21 miles northwest of Whitefish, in Flathead County, at an elevation of 6,906 feet.[^1] Its watch cab started life as a school bus, with a cupola on top.[^1][^2]

The firelookout.com page dates the conversion to the 1960s. It also names the Montana DNRC, with no role stated.[^1] It marks the lookout gone in 1969.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list also marks the site gone, gives its type as bus and cupola, and places it at 48.65201, -114.63624.[^2]

The page carries a photo credited to the Dave Masters collection.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the public can reach it. Ask the agency before planning a visit.
