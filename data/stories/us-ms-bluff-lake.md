The Bluff Lake Lookout Tower stands in Noxubee County, Mississippi, inside the Sam D. Hamilton Noxubee National Wildlife Refuge.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as a 120-foot Aermotor with a diagonal stairway.[^1] A National Geodetic Survey data sheet, reproduced on a Forest Lookouts page, describes a steel tower with an observation cab, 120 feet (36.6 meters) tall.[^2] The two descriptions agree on the height.[^1][^2]

The register lists the build date as unknown, so nobody appears to have recorded when it was built.[^1] It lists U.S. Fish & Wildlife as administering the tower, with the Mississippi Forestry Commission as a cooperator.[^1] The Fish and Wildlife Service also governs the refuge,[^3] but the pages I read do not say how the tower came to stand on refuge land or what the cooperator's role involves.[^1][^2]

I found no staffing or fire history in the pages I read.[^1][^2] The data sheet places the tower near the refuge headquarters.[^2]

The most recent condition note I found is a 2023 listing that marks the tower as standing on Google Earth imagery.[^2] That is satellite imagery, not an inspection.[^2]

The register records a site visit on July 3, 2022, but says nothing about current access.[^1] Goose Overlook is a different structure, a 30-foot platform that looks out over Bluff Lake, and should not be confused with this tower.[^3]
