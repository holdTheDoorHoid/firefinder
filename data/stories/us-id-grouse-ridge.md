Grouse Ridge sat on the Nez Perce National Forest in Idaho County, about 25 miles east-northeast of Elk City, at 6,323 feet.[^1][^2] The Forest Service list gives a crow's nest in 1926 and a log cabin with a cupola in 1928.[^4][^5] Firelookout.com and Forest Lookouts describe the same two stages.[^1][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists Spence A. Dewey as staff in 1945, crediting a registry of former fire lookouts.[^2] The same page names Harry Farris with no year, citing a source titled Faces of the Selway Bitterroot Wilderness.[^2]

Every source that gives a year for the end agrees on 1950, with Forest Lookouts saying about 1950.[^1][^2][^3][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site as hike-in and destroyed.[^2]

No source read describes what remains at the site today. The hike-in label describes how to reach the spot, not whether anything is still standing there.[^2]
