A Georgia tower list places a tower labelled "bowman" in Elbert County, at 34.19638, -82.99167.[^1] Its elevation column reads 717, with no unit given. Read as feet, that is about 219 m.[^1] The label matches the town of Bowman, which lies in Elbert County.[^2]

The list says that names for towers numbered 73 and above are its own, and this tower is numbered 329.[^1] The name may therefore be a label rather than a historical one.[^1] The entry carries the tag "datasheet."[^1] The page says the towers were located using 1:100,000-scale USGS maps and NGS data sheets, but it does not explain the tag.[^1] The page's Google map is marked as revised in March 2016.[^1]

No source found describes this tower's build date, design, staffing or fate. No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
