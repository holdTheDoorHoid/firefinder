A North Carolina lookout-tower list on tnlandforms.us places a tower named Siler City in Chatham County, at 35.70138, -79.48083, and gives the NGS survey ID EZ1268 for it.[^1] The National Geodetic Survey data sheet for EZ1268, designated SILER, gives a NAD 83 (2001) position of 35 42 04.99168 N, 079 28 49.86581 W, which agrees with the list's coordinates to within about 30 m.[^2] The sheet is a survey record.[^2]

An FCC tower record, shown on Cellular Finder, places a tower at 35.7013, -79.4811 on 645 Siler City Fire Tower Road.[^3] It is registered as a tower structure, 120.1 ft (36.6 m) high, with a constructed date of January 15, 2001.[^3] The registration does not say whether the structure is a lookout, so it does not confirm that it is the tower on the list.[^3] On that record, the road's name is the only reference to a fire tower.[^3]

The listed point lies about 3.5 km south-west of the coordinates Wikipedia gives for Siler City itself, 35°43′31″N 79°27′22″W.[^4][^1]

No source we read describes what stands at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.[^1]
