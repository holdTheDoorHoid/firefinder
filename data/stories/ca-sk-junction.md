Junction Fire Tower is listed in Division No. 18, Saskatchewan, at about 54.85 N, 105.59 W.[^1] The Saskatchewan tower list gives its height as 30.5 m (100 ft).[^2]

A towermen list places Maurice Bourassa at Junction from 1999 to 2001. The same entry includes the note "First observer in Junction Tower."[^3]

An older note on the towermen list, by Marvin Torwalt, concerns Burns Matheson, a former field officer, who is recorded as transferring to "Hudson Bay (Junction)" in 1945. The author thinks that by then he no longer manned towers as part of his regular job, and does not say whether that name refers to this tower.[^3]

No source we found describes the Junction site today, whether the tower still stands, or whether the public can visit.[^1]
