The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register lists a Pine Hill Tower Site in Lackawanna County, on US 420 and PA 249, and dates its entry to September 16, 2008.[^1] The entry describes a bald tower that was used around 1917 and removed in 1981.[^1]

A federal survey description from 1955 covers the summit of Big Pine Hill in Lackawanna County.[^2] It describes a steel fire-lookout tower about 90 feet high on that summit.[^2] A surveyor visited survey station LY2745 on 6 August 2004 and recorded it as destroyed.[^3] The surveyor found that the steel tower had been taken down and that a shorter wooden platform had been put up in its place.[^3]

The register entry does not name Big Pine Hill.[^1] The survey station's coordinates, 41.235427° N and 75.635909° W, do not match the point in our records.[^3] I cannot be sure these describe the same tower.

In 2004 a wooden platform stood close to where the steel tower had been.[^3] No source I read describes what the summit looks like now or whether visitors can reach it.
