The Forest Lookouts list records Wilson Hill WMA #1 as a platform. It lists the platform as standing in St. Lawrence County and says it was built with parts of the Whites Hill tower.[^3] We found no other source for that reuse, so treat it as a single listing claim.

Whites Hill, a 1,427-foot mountain near Parishville, had an 80-foot Aermotor LS40 steel lookout that the Conservation Department built in 1950.[^2] The state list places the tower 3 miles south of Parishville, on the west side of Whites Hill Road, and says it entered service in 1951, when it logged 3 fires and 588 visitors.[^1] Once aerial detection took over the job, the tower closed at the end of the 1971 season and was later removed.[^1][^2] Its cabin went to the state Department of Environmental Conservation's Trout Lake Shop, where operations staff use it.[^2]

Wilson Hill is a 4,000-acre state wildlife management area, and the land is an open water wetland next to the St. Lawrence River.[^4] The public use zones stay open all year, but special entry and permit restrictions apply, and hunters must register on the days the area is open to hunting.[^4]

We found no source that describes the platform's condition, whether anyone staffs it, or whether the public can reach it. Ask DEC's Region 6 office in Watertown before you visit.[^4]
