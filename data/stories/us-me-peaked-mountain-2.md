Locals call this small Clifton mountain Chick Hill, while official maps label it Peaked Mountain.[^1] It rises 1,160 feet above sea level, in Penobscot County, Maine.[^1][^2]

A Portland Press Herald item dated April 23, 1947 described the Chick Hill lookout as new, put up about a month earlier.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register gives 1976 to 1993 as the window for the tower's removal.[^2]

Today the summit has open granite ledges and a fenced communications tower.[^1] Only a rusted base remains where an observation tower once stood, to the left of the communications tower.[^1]

The hike to the top starts at a rough gravel parking area and covers about 1.3 miles of rough road that Google Maps calls Fire Road 32.[^1] The write-up notes there are no posted rules.[^1] An unofficial orange-blazed trail crosses private land, so hikers should ask permission before using it.[^1]
