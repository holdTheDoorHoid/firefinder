The Deer Head Lookout sits on a hill outside Conicville, in Shenandoah County, Virginia.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register dates the tower to 1930 and calls it an International Derrick, 40 to 60 feet tall.[^1] The register's location line names the Virginia Department of Forestry.[^1]

The register added the tower on February 27, 2022.[^1] A lookout index from the Forest Fire Lookout Association marks it as gone, which conflicts with the 2022 visit report described below.[^1][^2]

A visit report dated May 23, 2022, by Rocco Germani describes a damaged tower that was still in place when the writer visited.[^1] The writer found the cab's ceiling collapsed, some roof sections missing, and only part of the glass left.[^1] The report also mentions what looks like a large antenna fixed to one corner of the cab.[^1]

Today the sources offer no later picture. The 2022 report says the base sits inside a barbed wire fence.[^1] No source we read says whether the public may visit the site, and none describes the tower after 2022, so its current condition is unknown.

[^1]: Deer Head Lookout, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/va/deer-head-lookout/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Virginia, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
