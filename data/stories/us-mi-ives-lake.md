A fire tower was standing near Ives Lake in Marquette County by October 1922.[^1] That month the L'Anse Sentinel reported that the tower had been erected and that the state was installing a telephone line to connect it with Big Bay.[^1] The paper added that an observer would be posted at the tower through the fire season, and that its view would take in a large area.[^1]

By 1955 the tower was described as abandoned.[^1] A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description from that year, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, puts a survey mark about a mile southeast of the lake, on a wooded hilltop where an abandoned lookout tower stood.[^1] The survey measured from the tower's southwest and southeast anchor legs, so the legs appear to have still been standing then.[^1]

No source we read describes the site today, so we cannot say what remains on the hill or whether visitors may go there.[^1]

[^1]: Ives Lake, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/ives-lake.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
