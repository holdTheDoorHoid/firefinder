The Dalton Ranch lookout stood about three miles south of North Powder, in Baker County, Oregon.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com lists the site as a private co-op.[^3]

A January 1930 report says the Whitman relied on a single cooperator, a busy rancher who watched for fires, organized crews and fought them, and it names him as J. Wylie Dalton of North Powder.[^2] Behind the ranch house, in a fenced enclosure, stood a #2 Osborne firefinder aimed at the east slopes of the Elkhorn range.[^2] The report calls that view a blind spot for the regular lookouts.[^2]

The same report describes Dalton's work on the Daley road fire the previous August.[^2] He stopped work, took his threshing crew, rounded up more men in North Powder, and as foreman handled the rear and parts of the flanks of the fire.[^2]

The register says a lookout tower was built on the site in the 1930s and is gone.[^1] Firelookout.com lists the tower as abandoned.[^3] A panorama photo was taken on September 9, 1936, and the register credits the National Archives for its copy.[^1][^2]

The register lists the site as administered privately.[^1] No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it. Because the land is private, anyone interested should ask the owner before going near it.
