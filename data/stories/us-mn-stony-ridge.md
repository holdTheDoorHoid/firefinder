Stony Ridge was a 94-foot (29 m) Pittsburg Des Moines ladder tower, state tower number 30, that stood along Highway 65 in the George Washington State Forest in Itasca County.[^1] A county list of Itasca towers says the state forest service erected it in 1934.[^1] The same list's table gives 1932 as the date, so the year is not settled.[^1] Survey notes spell the name Stoney Ridge, and the list also calls the site Rock Ridge.[^1]

According to the county list, the tower was later restored, moved to Anoka County, and is now in private hands.[^1] Because it is private, a visitor should ask before assuming access, and nothing read for this story describes a public visit.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association still marks Stony Ridge in Itasca County as gone.[^2] No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the original Highway 65 site, or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
