Lookout Peak Lookout stood on Lookout Peak in the West Mountains, six miles west of Cascade, Idaho.[^1][^4] Sources name the Payette National Forest, and some add the Boise National Forest.[^1][^2][^5]

The lookout began in 1907 as an alidade platform, a stand for a surveyor's sighting tool, with a log cabin about 400 yards below the summit, near a spring.[^1][^2][^3]

Sources list the lookout as abandoned, but none we read gives a year for that.[^1][^2][^6]

In August 2022 lightning started the Four Corners Fire west of Cascade Lake, near Lookout Peak.[^5] A 2023 trip report says most of the surrounding area burned in that fire.[^4]

The 2023 trip report describes a short hike to the summit.[^4] It says a vehicle can get to within two miles of the summit, and that the first 1.5 miles from the trailhead follow the Van Wyck ATV trail.[^4] The writer judged that the summit, which has no trees, seems to have come through the fire.[^4] The report does not mention any remains of the lookout, and no other source we read describes what remains at the site today.[^4]
