Farnham Peak is on the Kaniksu National Forest, in Boundary County, Idaho, at township 63 north, range 1 west, section 5.[^1] Ron Kemnow's Forest Lookouts listing describes the site as a crow's nest, placed atop a tree.[^1] The two sources agree that Farnham Peak carried a crow's nest.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts' history of nearby Fisher Peak lists a 1937 L-4 cab on that peak and notes it was gone by 1964.[^2] The same history places a crow's nest two miles southeast of Fisher Peak, at Farnham Peak.[^2]

No source found describes what, if anything, remains at Farnham Peak today.

[^1]: Farnham Peak - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts (Ron Kemnow), https://idaholookouts.weebly.com/farnham-peak.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Fisher Peak Lookout, Idaho Fire Lookouts, https://www.idahofirelookouts.com/north-idaho-panhandle-region/fisher-peak-lookout/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
