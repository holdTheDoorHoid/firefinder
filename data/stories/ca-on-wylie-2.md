Firelookout.ca lists the Wylie Fire Tower at Laurentian Hills, Ontario, at 45.96532, -77.54564.[^1] It also calls the tower active, one of two such towers in the province.[^1]

A fan-run list of still-standing Ontario towers places Wylie Tower in the Petawawa Research Forest at Chalk River.[^3] The same group's Ontario page says that only two towers remain staffed and fully working, and that both sit in the Petawawa Research Forest, east of Algonquin Park.[^2] That page does not name the two towers.[^2]

Firelookout.ca's "active" label and the still-standing list are the only descriptions of the tower's current condition that I found.[^1][^3] No source I found says whether visitors may go up the tower or what it looks like on the ground.

[^3]: Ontario Fire Towers Still Standing, Forest Rangers fan site, https://forestrangers.github.io/ontariofiretowers/stillstanding2.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
