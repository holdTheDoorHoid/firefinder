Hemlock Knob was a lookout site in Monroe County, Tennessee. The Tennessee lookout list places it at 4,019 feet (1,225 m) and marks the tower as removed.[^1]

The list gives the site the code "cnf" in its info column. The page says the numbers in its table are state forest tower numbers from a 1976 list. It does not say what "cnf" means for this site, though a history note on the page uses "CNF" for the Cherokee National Forest.[^1]

Many listed towers are gone, and most of their sites are now privately owned.[^1] No source I found describes what remains at the Hemlock Knob site today, or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Tennessee lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
