The Carlin Hill tower stood near Smithfield in Wetzel County.[^1] The state owned it, and it stood there from 1925 until 1946.[^1]

A July 1925 newspaper item described a 60-foot steel tower as believed to be pending at Jacksonburg, in the same county, and said the plan included living quarters for a ranger at its foot.[^4] In October 1926 a report said the tower had a telephone connection through the Pine Grove system to the Clarksburg office.[^4]

The sources disagree on the height and the place.[^1][^4] The 1925 item gives 60 feet and Jacksonburg, while the Former Fire Lookout Sites entry puts the tower near Smithfield.[^1][^4] The National Historic Lookout Register and the Forest Lookouts page both describe an 80-foot steel tower.[^2][^4]

In summer 1946 the tower was taken apart and moved to Caddell Mountain in Preston County, where it went back up that autumn.[^2][^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites entry spells the mountain "Cadell," while the other sources write "Caddell."[^1][^2][^3]

That entry lists the original Carlin Hill site as private property, and its photos show tower foundations.[^1] The tower itself still stands at Caddell Mountain, according to the state list.[^5] The entry says the West Virginia Division of Forestry maintained it as of 2017.[^1]

Nothing I found says whether the public can visit the Caddell Mountain tower.[^2][^3]
