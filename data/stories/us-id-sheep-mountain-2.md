Sheep Mountain rises to 9,192 feet in the Challis National Forest.[^1][^2] On August 25, 1932, the Challis Messenger reported that a lookout house was going up on the peak.[^3] firelookout.com describes what went up that year as a cabin, and places the mountain 39 miles west-northwest of Challis.[^1] Rex's Fire Lookout Page gives its township, range and section as 15N-12E-33.[^3]

By 1946 the cabin had been abandoned, according to the history pages that cover it.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list, which places the site in Custer County, now marks it as gone and classes it as a ground structure rather than a tower.[^4]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. One history page lists it as a drive-up site.[^2]
