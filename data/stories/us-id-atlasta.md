The Atlasta lookout stood on Kaniksu National Forest land in Bonner County, about seven miles southeast of Coolin.[^1][^2] Built in 1934, it was a 40-foot pole tower fitted with an L-4 cab.[^1][^3][^4] A 48-foot pole patrol tower with an L-6 cab stood about a mile to the southwest.[^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists Mr. Bob Bond as known staff in 1959, and a photo credit there dates a Bob Bond photograph of the site to 1959 and 1960.[^3]

The sources disagree on how the lookout ended. Firelookout.com, Idaho Fire Lookouts and the Idaho Forest Lookouts page on Weebly all say it was destroyed in 1960.[^2][^3][^4] The register says it was removed that year.[^1] The register also says the ruins of the patrol tower were still there in the 1980s.[^1]

The register lists the site as registered on February 20, 2013, as US 1540, ID 13.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as a hike-in where the tower was destroyed.[^3] None of the four sources describes what, if anything, is left at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
