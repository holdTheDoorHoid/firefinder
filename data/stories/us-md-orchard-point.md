The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a standing tower at Orchard Point in Kent County, Maryland.[^1] The entry is named "Orchard Point (unknown)," and the page gives no history, owner or other detail beyond its county, coordinates and status.[^1]

Orchard Point is also the name of a cape in Kent County, which appears on the USGS Rock Hall topographic map and has a listed elevation of 6 feet.[^2] The association's coordinates and the cape's listed coordinates do not match, so it is unclear whether they mark the same spot.[^1][^2]

I found no build date, builder, owner, or staffing record for a tower at either place in the sources I could reach. A 2007 Maryland Department of Natural Resources report on its forestry towers lists them by county, and Kent County does not appear in that list.[^3]

Without an owner, a visit report, or an access note, these records cannot say whether anything at the point can be visited. Do not climb or enter an unidentified structure, and check with Kent County or the landowner before planning any trip.
