Red Butte stands in the Umpqua National Forest in Douglas County, Oregon, and had a lookout station by 1918.[^1][^4] A 1917 newspaper item quoted on the Forest Lookouts page said the Government would soon set one up on the summit.[^4] A July 1930 item reports that a new lookout house of standard design had just been completed there.[^4] The Register and firelookout.com both describe the earlier structure as a 20-foot pole tower carrying an L-4 cab, and firelookout.com dates it to 1930.[^1][^2][^4]

In 1953 that tower was replaced by the one standing today, a 41-foot treated timber tower with an outrigger L-4 cab.[^1][^2][^4] A 1943 newspaper item quoted on the same page reports that a new tower went up that year. That does not match the 1953 date, and the sources read do not explain the gap.[^4]

Named observers appear at the station from 1918 through the 1940s, several of them married couples.[^4] A 1962 item quoted on the Forest Lookouts page says one observer had already spent two years at the station.[^4] The Air Warning Service built a cabin for its observer on the site in 1942, and the page lists an activation dated October 1942 to October 1943.[^4] No source read names a notable fire from Red Butte.

The Register says the station has seen only emergency use lately, and that the plan is to move it to Lookout Mountain, perhaps into a cabin rental system.[^1] firelookout.com calls the present tower abandoned, and Cheryl Hill's directory lists it as abandoned while still standing.[^2][^3] The sources do not say whether the move has happened.

Today, none of the sources read confirms public climbing or rental access. Cheryl Hill's directory marks the site as legally accessible, which it says is not the same as access to the lookout.[^3] The rental idea is only a possibility in the Register.[^1] Anyone interested should check with the Umpqua National Forest before making a trip, since the Register names the Forest Service as its administrator.[^1]
