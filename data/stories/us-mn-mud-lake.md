The Minnesota Forest Service built the Mud Lake lookout in 1935, about two miles northwest of the Mud Lake Guard Station.[^1][^2] The guard station had an earlier lookout of its own.[^2] A tall white pine served first as a lookout, and then the state put up a three-post, round-cab tower there in the late 1910s.[^2] That tower sat roughly five miles east of Federal Dam and stayed in use until the 1935 tower went up.[^2]

According to the county list, the 1935 tower was 100 feet tall, an Aermotor ladder design.[^1][^2] The 1959 survey notes describe a steel frame on four legs with an observation room at the top, and put its overall height at 115 feet.[^1][^2] The listing and the survey therefore disagree on height.[^1][^2]

The tower was removed after 1985, according to both lists.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone and places the site at 47.27588, -94.02471.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the public can visit it.
