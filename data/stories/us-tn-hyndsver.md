The Hyndsver tower is listed in Weakley County, at 503 feet (153 m) above sea level.[^1] We could not find when it was built, who built it, or who staffed it, and the sources we checked do not say.

The wider program is better documented. The Civilian Conservation Corps built 98 lookout towers across Tennessee between 1933 and 1941.[^2] A National Register nomination for Black Creek, another Tennessee tower, credits a conservation movement with getting fire towers put up and manned.[^3] No source we checked links Hyndsver to that work.

The Tennessee tower catalogue lists Hyndsver as standing.[^1] Its entry gives no design, height or cab details, and it does not say who owns the site or whether visitors may go there. The same catalogue says many towers have been removed and most sites are now privately owned.[^2]

Find out who owns the land and whether the public may enter before you plan a visit. Do not climb a tower that is closed or on private land.
