The statewide list of Georgia lookout towers includes a lookout called nix in Henry County. It gives the site's elevation as 982 feet, at 33.42134 north and 84.26518 west.[^1]

A March 1954 issue of *Georgia Forestry*, a Georgia Forestry Commission publication, reprints an Atlanta Constitution article on Henry County. The article says the county's forestry unit had three lookout towers at its disposal in 1953.[^2] It does not name those towers, so it does not confirm whether Nix was one of them. It also describes how a tower operator or a pilot reports smoke from a wildfire by two-way FM radio to a dispatcher, who sends a crew to the fire.[^2]

No source I found describes the Nix site today, so what remains there, and whether the public can reach it, is not known. Do not climb any tower without permission from whoever manages the land.
