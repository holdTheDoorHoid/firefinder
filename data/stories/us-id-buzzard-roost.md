Buzzard Roost began as a camp in 1919, about 20 miles south-southeast of Avery in Shoshone County, Idaho.[^1][^2]

The site got an L-4 cab in 1935.[^1][^2] The sources place its crow's nest two miles east, atop Mulligan Hump, on the St. Joe National Forest.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts has no documented staffing history for the site.[^2]

Both sources date its destruction to 1954, and neither describes how it was destroyed.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site drive-up. That describes how to reach it, and the page does not say whether the land is open to visitors.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Buzzard Roost as a ground lookout that is gone.[^3] The pages we read do not describe what, if anything, remains on the site today.
