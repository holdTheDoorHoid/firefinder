The Rainier lookout stood on the Fort Lewis Military Reservation in Thurston County, about five miles northwest of Yelm.[^1][^2] The U.S. Army built the first tower here in 1942: a 100-foot timber structure carrying an L-6 cab and catwalk.[^1][^2] It watched over the reservation's land for about 26 years before being replaced by an all-steel tower that the register and firelookout.com both put at 112 feet and date to 1968.[^1][^2] The firm that later dismantled and rebuilt the tower at IslandWood dates that replacement to 1966 instead, and doesn't give the original tower's height at all — it only describes the tower as rebuilt, and inconsistently at that, calling the finished structure 125 feet tall in one place and 118 feet in another.[^3]

The steel tower was decommissioned and sold for scrap around 2000, say the register and firelookout.com; the firm that rebuilt it instead gives 1998.[^1][^2][^3] It was bought again and trucked to Bainbridge Island, where the environmental education center IslandWood wanted it as an observation tower rising through the forest canopy. Rebuilding it took years: the firm Sahale LLC worked on the project from 2002, fabricated more than 23,000 pounds of new steel for stairs, a catwalk and an observation deck starting in 2006, and finished in February 2009, reusing the original galvanized steel members from Fort Lewis and adding an ADA-compliant helical stairwell through the middle.[^3] IslandWood dedicated the rebuilt structure under a new name, Canopy Tower, in May 2009.[^1]

Nothing in the sources found so far says anything about who staffed the Rainier tower, what fires it reported, or exactly when in 1968 the replacement went up. The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register lists it as US 559, WA 5, registered July 22, 2010 on a nomination by Irene Potter, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Washington list simply marks it "Gone."[^1][^4] The Rainier site itself is gone; what survives is the steel from the second tower, standing now as Canopy Tower at IslandWood on Bainbridge Island.
