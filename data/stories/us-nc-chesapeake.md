The Chesapeake lookout is in Gates County, North Carolina.[^1] Its tower was 120 feet (36.6 m) tall.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page credits the 1942 tower to private sources, although it names the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources as the agency.[^1]

A 1980 description by the North Carolina Geodetic Survey gives the tower as 120 feet high with four legs, of the usual Forest Service lookout type, and places the survey station at its center.[^2] The station is about 3.3 miles east of the N.C. 37 junction at Eleanors Crossroads, along U.S. 158 Bypass.[^2] A 1997 recovery of the station, noted by the US Power Squadron, found it in good condition.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts page also lists an auction of surplus state property that opened for bids in March 2018.[^1] The same page places the tower on state land at a Forest Service headquarters site.[^1]

No source we read describes the site today. We could not confirm whether the tower still stands, whether it was sold, or whether the public may visit, so ask the North Carolina Forest Service before going.
