The Civilian Conservation Corps built the Millers Head fire tower in Shenandoah National Park.[^1][^2][^4] Willhite says it was constructed in 1940 and completed in 1941.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page gives 1942 as the construction year, so the two accounts disagree.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list marks the tower as gone, and Forest Lookouts says it was removed.[^5][^2] Willhite found a remaining lookout base at the former site.[^1] Wikidata describes its state of conservation as ruinous, and the sources do not agree on what is left.[^1][^2][^4][^5]

Today the site is reached on foot.[^3] The National Park Service lists the Millers Head Trail as a 1.6-mile round trip that starts just up the hill from the Skyland amphitheater, and it calls the viewpoint the site of a former fire tower.[^3] Willhite's photographs show the base and a view over the Shenandoah Valley.[^1]
