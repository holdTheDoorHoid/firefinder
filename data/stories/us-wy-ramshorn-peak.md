The Forest Fire Lookout Association does not list Ramshorn Peak among its lookouts.[^1] Its Wyoming list places the name in Fremont County under a table headed "Unknown/Undocumented Sites," with no structure type or status given.[^1] The association says such a site would move onto its lookout lists if evidence showed it had been used as a regular wildland fire lookout.[^1]

The name belongs to a peak. Wikipedia gives an elevation of 11,635 feet (3,546 m) and places it in the Absaroka Range, in the Washakie Wilderness on land managed by Shoshone National Forest.[^2] Dubois, the nearest town, lies 16 miles to the south-southeast.[^2] The same article records the first ascent of the summit on July 13, 1960.[^2]

A Forest Service marker near Dubois describes the Ramshorn as a plateau, named for its escarpment. That differs from the summit Wikipedia describes,[^2] so the two sources do not agree on the landform.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today or whether the public can reach it.
