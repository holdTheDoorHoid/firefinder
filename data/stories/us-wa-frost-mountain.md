Frost Mountain rises about 10 miles south of Cle Elum, in Kittitas County.[^1][^2] The U.S. Forest Service administers the site, which lies in the Wenatchee National Forest.[^1]

A lookout went up there in 1954, on a 10-foot treated timber tower with an R-6 flat-roof cab.[^1][^3] Summit panoramas were made in 1934, twenty years before the tower.[^1][^3] Former Fire Lookout Sites captions them "Frost Mountain Lookout," but no source we read explains how that fits with the 1954 tower.[^1]

The lookout was gone by 1988.[^1][^2][^3]

Willhite reports that three foundation posts survive and are in sound condition.[^3] A SummitPost climbing page says the summit's lookout foundation can still be seen.[^4] No source we read describes a standing structure there now.[^3][^4]

Willhite describes a jeep route rising 1.2 miles from the Quartz Mountain Road (Forest Road 3100).[^3] He says hikers can start walking from a gate on the way if it is shut, and he calls the last stretch up to the summit dicey.[^3] A SummitPost route note says the road was passable in an ordinary car as of 2016, though it has some potholes near the trailhead.[^4] It describes parking by a gate on Forest Road 115 and a round trip of about 3 miles with 500 feet of gain to the summit.[^4] No source we read describes a closure or a permit at the site.[^1][^3][^4]
