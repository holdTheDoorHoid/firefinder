The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a ground-level lookout called Pinal Peak (Ferndell), in Gila County, on Tonto National Forest land.[^1][^2] A 1947 survey description, though, calls the lookout on the summit a steel tower about 80 feet high, roughly nine miles airline south of Globe.[^3] The association marks the site as gone.[^1]

Pinal Peak and Signal Peak are the two tallest summits in the Pinal Mountains.[^4] The Signal Peak lookout is a separate tower. The register says it was built in 1934 to replace a wooden tower from 1920.[^5]

A 2022 recovery note says the survey mark on the summit was recovered in good condition. That note is about the survey mark, and it does not say whether the old lookout tower still stands.[^3] Wikipedia says both peaks now carry radio masts and towers.[^4] We found no source that says whether the summit is open to visitors.
