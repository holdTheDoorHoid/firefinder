Winespring Bald is a lookout site in Macon County, North Carolina, listed under the Nantahala National Forest.[^1][^2]

The Forest Lookouts page gives a single date marker, "<1926", with no further detail.[^1] A North Carolina tower list on tnlandforms.us places it at 35.17361, -83.58111 and marks it "lookout no tower."[^2] The same list names Peter Barr's book, Hiking North Carolina's Lookout Towers, as a reference.[^2]

Gaia GPS and the Carolina Mountain Club spell the name Wine Spring Bald.[^3][^4] A Bartram Trail route on Gaia GPS lists Wayah Bald Fire Tower and Wine Spring Bald as separate stops.[^3]

The Carolina Mountain Club also lists an Appalachian Trail route with a side trail to Wine Spring Bald that leaves FS 69B.[^4]

No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains on the site today, or whether a tower stands there.
