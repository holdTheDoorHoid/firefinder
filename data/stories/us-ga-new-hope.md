The Georgia tower list places a lookout named "new hope" in Gilmer County, at 34.748 north and 84.51531 west.[^1] It gives the elevation as 2,063, which is about 629 m if read as feet.[^1] As with the other towers numbered 73 and above, the author says the names are the author's own, so "new hope" is a label and does not show what the tower was called locally.[^1]

The list draws on USGS topographic maps and National Geodetic Survey datasheets, and it credits Gary Weber and Ron Stafford with numerous tower locations.[^1] The author says the list was prompted by the FFLA site listing no Georgia towers in 2010.[^1] The entry itself carries no note beyond its coordinates, county and elevation.[^1]

We found no source that describes the site today, so this account cannot say whether any structure remains there or whether the spot is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
