Gobbler Knob, in Lincoln County on Oregon's north coast, once held a firefinder platform with trailer quarters.[^1][^2] Both went up in the 1950s.[^1][^2] A firefinder was the instrument a lookout used to work out the bearing and range to a possible fire.[^3] The Oregon Department of Forestry administered the site, which stands at 1,125 feet about 18 miles northeast of Newport.[^1][^2] The register places it at 44.82164 north and 123.78005 west.[^1]

By 1959 both sources record the platform and trailer quarters as gone.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the knob to its list in November 2010, under the registry numbers US 708 and OR 119.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the knob today, and neither says whether visitors can reach the site.[^1][^2]
