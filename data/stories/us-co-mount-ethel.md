A March 1911 item in the Routt County Republican listed the Forest Service’s plans for that year, including a lookout station on Mount Ethel on the Park Range and a telephone line linking to it.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Mount Ethel among its unknown or undocumented sites.[^2]

Sources disagree on the county. The Forest Lookouts page and the association’s list give Jackson County, while a gazetteer places the summit in Routt County.[^1][^2][^4]

A 2019 Pastmaps map places the high country around Mount Ethel within the Routt National Forest, where the Continental Divide National Scenic Trail runs along the range’s crest.[^3] No source describes a lookout structure, its remains, or public access to the summit today.
