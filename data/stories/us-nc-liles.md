Liles appears on a list of North Carolina lookout towers, which places it in Halifax County.[^1] TopoQuest, a map site with a place search, goes a step further and names a "Liles Lookout Tower" there at 360 feet (110 m) above sea level.[^2]

In late October 1933 State Forester J. S. Holmes said that eight steel forest fire lookout towers would soon be erected across the state.[^3] One was planned for Halifax County, at a site four miles west of Scotland Neck, and nearby Civilian Conservation Corps camps were to put it up.[^3] The digitised text of the article never mentions Liles, so it is not clear whether that planned tower is the one TopoQuest and the list describe.[^3]

No source I could read says whether a tower still stands at the Liles site, or whether the land is open to visitors. If you plan a visit, ask the North Carolina Forest Service about access before you go.
