Our records place the McCaslin Lookout Tower in Forest County, Wisconsin, at 45.38092 north and 88.45314 west. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list marks it as gone.[^1] OpenStreetMap lists the structure as an observation tower with an elevation of 490.[^2] The Central US Lookouts Wisconsin index also places it in Forest County.[^3]

Statewide, Wisconsin resolved in 2016 to shut down its remaining 72 functioning fire lookout towers.[^4]

We found no source that describes the site today, so we cannot say what remains there or whether it is open to visitors.
