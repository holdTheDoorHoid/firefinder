Hankin Fire Lookout started out as a working fire lookout and was later refitted for winter use, around 2010.[^1]

The lookout stands within the Hankin-Evelyn Backcountry Recreation Area, north of Smithers, on a knob on Hankin Peak's north flank.[^1][^3]

A trail write-up by Morgan Hite describes it as a boxy, two-story cube with an upstairs room glazed on all sides, and a lower floor with platforms and a wood stove.[^3]

The volunteer-run Bulkley Backcountry Ski Society now maintains and books the cabin, in partnership with Recreation Sites and Trails BC.[^1] The Rainlander says the society rents the lookout out for a fee.[^4]

The society's page lists room for six people, a wood stove for heat, and a propane cooking system. Further details are given when you book.[^2]

Wild Bluebell describes Hankin mainly as a base for backcountry skiing, not a summer resort, and the Hesperus Wild write-up notes that visitors come on skis in winter as often as in summer.[^1][^3]

Orange reflectors and tape mark the route, and the cabin lies about 75 minutes past the parking area.[^3] The write-up warns that the access road has potholes and that a low-clearance car may not reach the trailhead.[^3]
