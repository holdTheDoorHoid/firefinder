Steamboat Peak is in Idaho County, in the Payette National Forest.[^2][^1] The lookout stood about five miles southwest of Warren, at 8,530 feet.[^2]

A 12-foot tower with an L-4 cab went up in 1931.[^1][^2][^3]

Forest Lookouts quotes a December 14, 1925 newspaper item, from the Ogden Standard-Examiner, about a lookout family at the Steamboat station.[^3] Lightning electrified the ground around the station, and the lookout, his wife and two small children had to move into a tent.[^3] The page files this item under Steamboat Peak.[^3] The sources do not say whether that station was the same site as the 1931 tower.

Idaho Fire Lookouts and firelookout.com both say the tower was destroyed in 1966.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts lists the tower as removed, without a date.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks it gone.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. Idaho Fire Lookouts classes the site as a hike-in.[^1]
