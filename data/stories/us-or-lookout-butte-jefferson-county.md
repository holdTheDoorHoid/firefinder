Lookout Butte sits on the Warm Springs Indian Reservation in Jefferson County, about 12 miles north-northeast of Mount Jefferson.[^1][^2] The register gives its elevation as 5,321 feet, or 1,622 metres.[^1]

A forestry report for fiscal 1936, as quoted by Forest Lookouts, says a 45-foot tower on the butte was started in the spring of 1936 and would be in service later that fire season.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register places the tower in the 1930s and describes it as a wooden structure with three legs, and firelookout.com dates it to the same decade.[^1][^2]

Both sites say the tower is now gone.[^1][^2]

What remains at the site today is not described in any source we read. The register lists the reservation as the administering body.[^1] We suggest asking the reservation before any visit.
