Tocker Lake is a fire tower on the Firetowers of Saskatchewan website, which places it at 56°48′09.3″ N, 109°07′48.6″ W.[^1] The tower carries the lake's name.[^1] The lake is small and lies in central Saskatchewan, 41 km north-northeast of La Loche.[^2] The lake commemorates Harry Ronald Tocker.[^2]

A Big River-area history says Saskatchewan's Department of Natural Resources set up fire towers roughly fifty miles apart across the north of the province.[^3] The same history says the distance between towers was set so that towermen could keep in radio contact, and that such towers carried a fire finder, a radio set and binoculars.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the Tocker Lake tower site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
