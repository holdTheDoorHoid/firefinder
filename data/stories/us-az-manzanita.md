Manzanita is a former lookout site on the Hualapai Indian Reservation, in Coconino County.[^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists its structure as a crowsnest, and its status as gone, at coordinates 35.84489 north and 113.17306 west.[^1]

The Arizona peak list calls it Manzanita BM and marks it "LO site," a label it uses where the building has been removed or its status is unknown.[^3] The list gives an elevation of 7,392 feet on the Frazier Wells SW map sheet, and marks that height as a benchmark, which means a survey disk is set nearby.[^3] The map sheet is named Frazier Wells SW, and Frazier Wells is a populated place about 30 miles northeast of Peach Springs.[^3][^4]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^2][^3]
