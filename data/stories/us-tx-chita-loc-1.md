The Texas Forest Service had a lookout tower near Chita, in Trinity County.[^1][^2][^3] A survey station description from 1942 describes a tower of all-steel construction, about 125 feet high, with a closed cab on top.[^1] The survey puts the station close to the summit of a partly cleared hill, on state-owned land about nine and a half miles east of Trinity.[^1]

A table of Texas towers gives 1934 in its date column, lists the manufacturer as EMSCO, and places the tower half a mile northwest of Chita.[^3] The table gives the height as 100 feet, which disagrees with the survey's 125 feet. I have used the survey's figure.[^1][^3]

The table says the tower was removed in 1970 and moved to a new site, listed as Chita (Loc 2).[^3] That entry gives 1970 as its build year and 1981 as its removal year, and says it was sold that year.[^3]

The Texas lookout list marks the Chita (Loc 1) site as gone.[^2]

Nothing I read describes what remains at either Chita site today. Nothing I read says whether the public can reach either one.

[^1]: Chita (Loc 1), National Geodetic Survey station description, Forest Lookouts (centraluslookouts.weebly.com), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/chita.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Texas Lookouts, alphabetical list, firelookout.org, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: TX expanded, Texas lookout tower table, firelookout.org, https://firelookout.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/TX-expanded.pdf (accessed 2026-10-10).
