Ranger Peak rises to 8,817 feet, about 15 miles east of Powell Ranger Station in Idaho County, in the Clearwater National Forest.[^1] Rex Kamstra's firelookout.com page dates the lookout to the 1960s with a question mark and marks the rest as unknown.[^1][^3] The forest's own list of its lookouts, compiled in June 2016 from several sources including Ray Kresek's book, Forest Service records and Kamstra's site, also places Ranger Peak in the 1960s. Because it draws on Kamstra's site, it is not an independent check.[^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts site files the peak under its Selway region and names the Nez Perce National Forest, which conflicts with the Clearwater listing. It gives the decade as the 1960s and the date as unknown.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the site as gone.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts also calls the site hike-in, but no source we read says what, if anything, is left on the summit today or whether visitors are allowed.[^1][^2]
