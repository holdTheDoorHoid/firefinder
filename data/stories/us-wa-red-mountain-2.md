Red Mountain rises in Kittitas County, Washington, on the Wenatchee National Forest, and the register gives the lookout site an elevation of 5,703 feet.[^1] Its register point is 47 degrees 23.7 minutes north and 121 degrees 8.7 minutes west.[^1] A lookout cab went up there in 1932, built to an L-5 design.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Washington list gives the site's type as Ground.[^2]

The register dates the cab's abandonment to 1948 and says it is now gone.[^1] Three panoramic views from August 22, 1934, labelled north, southeast and southwest, are credited to the National Archives Pacific Northwest Region.[^1]

No source I found describes the summit today. The register's photo list also includes an undated picture captioned "Debris," credited to Jeff Ruckle.[^1] It does not say what is on the site now.
