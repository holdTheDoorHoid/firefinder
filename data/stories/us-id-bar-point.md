Bar Point Lookout was an L-4 tower in the Clearwater National Forest.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both date the tower to 1934, and the Clearwater list gives the same year.[^1][^2][^4] Forest Lookouts gives the same 1934 L-4 cab and credits the entry to Kresek.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the site's elevation as 6,418 feet.[^2]

The Idaho page's known-staff entry for 1972 names Mr. & Mrs. Brush Bash and cites the Kellogg Evening News.[^2] That date sits oddly beside the 1960s destruction the same page gives.[^2]

Three sources date its destruction to the 1960s.[^1][^2][^4] Forest Lookouts marks it removed instead.[^3] The Idaho lookout list marks it gone.[^5]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site Hike-In.[^2] None of the pages we read describes what, if anything, is left at the site today.[^1][^2][^3]
