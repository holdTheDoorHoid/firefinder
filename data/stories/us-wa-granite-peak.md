Granite Peak is a former lookout site in Pend Oreille County, about nine miles south of Ione. The site stands at 5,238 feet.[^2][^3]

A 10-foot pole observation tower and frame cabin went up in 1928. A 10-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab followed in 1934.[^1][^3] The Washington State Division of Forestry administered the site.[^1]

Lookouts show up in newspapers and histories from the early 1920s. Sam Poston was on the peak in 1922, according to a Spokane Chronicle item quoted on Rex's Fire Lookout Page.[^4] Gerald Dimmick was the lookout in 1926. By late June he had reported four fires in the Dominion Mountain area.[^3][^4] William Lamoon took the post in 1930, starting on June 19.[^3] In 1931 the fire warden, Art Linder, connected the lookout's phone line to Newport.[^3] The only name on firelookout.com's known-personnel list is Lloyd Buckhouse, for 1952.[^2]

The tower is long gone.[^1][^2]

A visitor's account, which is undated, describes a hike of about 4.5 miles one way with roughly 1,200 feet of climbing.[^3] It ends almost at the summit, where the writer found no lookout. What remained was a bed frame, nails and a phone insulator.[^3]

No other source describes the site today.
