Alberta's wildfire register lists a steel tower at Saddle Hills.[^1] It is 30 m (98 ft) tall and stands at an elevation of 967.0 m (3,173 ft), at about 55.62 N, 119.72 W.[^1] The register gives 2016 as the year it was built.[^1]

Alberta says each of its 127 wildfire lookout sites has an observer watching for fires through the day, generally April to October.[^2] An observer's main job is catching smoke and fire early and reporting what they see, lightning included.[^3]

In September 2023 a news report placed a seven-hectare wildfire near the Saddle Hills forest tower. It was listed as being held.[^4] The report says several air tanker drops and ground crews helped check its spread.[^4] Alberta Wildfire also asked everyone in the Grande Prairie Forest Area to take extra care with fire.[^4]

The register lists the tower as active, reached by road, with a weather station.[^1] No source describes public visits to the site, so treat it as a working fire-watch tower rather than a place to visit or climb.
