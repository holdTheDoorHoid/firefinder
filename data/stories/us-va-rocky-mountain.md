Rocky Mountain is a peak in the Blue Ridge, about two miles east of Irish Gap, on the border of Rockbridge and Amherst counties. It is the highest point of both counties.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list gives a Rocky Mountain tower in Amherst and Rockbridge as gone.[^2]

Several radio towers now stand on the summit, and a dirt road that dead-ends there leads to them.[^1] The peak sits inside the George Washington National Forest. The Appalachian Trail passes about 0.3 miles southeast of the summit, and the Blue Ridge Parkway passes about 0.6 miles northwest of it.[^1] The sources I found do not say whether any part of an old lookout remains among the radio towers, or whether the road is open to visitors.
