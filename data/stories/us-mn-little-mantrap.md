Little Mantrap was a fire tower in Hubbard County, just east of Itasca State Park.[^1] A 94-foot ladder tower went up there in 1928, and the county list calls it Fire Tower 69.[^1][^2] The county list codes its owner as Mn MFS, which it defines as the Minnesota Forest Service, now the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources, Forestry Division.[^1] The namesake lake lies to the southwest, the list says.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone, with no date for its removal.[^3]

The county list and the statewide list both say the site is now a state ARMER site, a term neither of them explains.[^1][^2] No source we read describes what stands on the site today or whether visitors may go there.
