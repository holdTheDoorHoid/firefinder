The May Mountain fire tower stood in Island Falls, in Aroostook County, Maine.[^1] A 1920 panoramic map from the Maine Forestry Service is centered on the May Mt. fire tower, which suggests the tower was in place by then.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's 2011 Maine report says four towers were lost that year, and May Mountain was one of them.[^2] The report ties the removal to the MSCommNet Project, a state radio upgrade that is set to affect some mountaintop sites and fire towers.[^2] The chapter spent that year working with the state's project office on many removals and moves.[^2]

The chapter's statewide list marks the tower as gone.[^3] We found no source describing what, if anything, is left on the mountain today or whether the site is open to visitors.
