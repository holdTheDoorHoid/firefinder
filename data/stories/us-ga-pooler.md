An online index of Georgia lookout towers lists a tower at Pooler, in Chatham County, at 32.12103, -81.2604.[^1] It gives the tower two codes, BLL-64693 and gfc-123.[^1] The index's map was last revised on 22 March 2016.[^1]

A 2006 Savannah newspaper report said just two towers in the Savannah area were staffed, one near Midway and one roughly a mile from Ludowici.[^2] The article does not mention Pooler.[^2] It also described the Georgia Forestry Commission's District 10 as stretching from Chatham County to McIntosh County along the coast, and inland as far as Jenkins County.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association had catalogued 360 tower sites, current and former, around the state.[^2]

No source found describes the Pooler site today, including whether a tower still stands there or whether visitors can reach it.
