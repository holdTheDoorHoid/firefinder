Lakeview (Wabaningo) was a Kaniksu National Forest lookout about two miles south of Nordman, in Bonner County, Idaho.[^1][^2] It sat at about 4,025 feet.[^1][^2]

The pages date its start only to the 1930s, when a 20-foot pole platform went up.[^1][^2][^3] It was abandoned in the 1950s.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts page credits both dates to Kresek, so agreement among the pages may not be independent.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^4] The Idaho Fire Lookouts listing also calls it a hike-in site.[^2]

Two Bonner County lookouts carry the Lakeview name. This one is listed as Lakeview (Wabaningo), and the other Lakeview is a separate lookout farther south.[^4]

None of the pages I read says what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors. Check with the Kaniksu National Forest before you go.
