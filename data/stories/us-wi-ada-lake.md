The Ada Lake tower stood in Langlade County. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list places it at 45.37266, -88.74469 and now marks it as gone.[^1] A regional lookout site lists a Lake Ada tower in Langlade County with the land-survey code 33N-14E-4, but gives no coordinates.[^2] Treating that page as the same tower is an assumption based on the shared name and county.

A May 8, 1931 newspaper item, reproduced on that site, reports the collapse of the Lake Ada fire tower, which killed one man. The chief fire warden said he could not state the cause. He also said lookout men were responsible for keeping the towers in repair.[^2]

The same site reproduces an April 29, 1931 item that places a fatal collapse of a 100-foot observation tower at the Eader Lake station in Forest County. The two items name different sites, and the page does not say whether they describe one collapse or two.[^2]

OpenStreetMap still holds a point named Ada Lake Lookout Tower at nearly the same coordinates.[^1][^3] No source describes the site today, so it is unknown what, if anything, remains there or whether the land is open to visitors.
