Peters Creek was a steel fire lookout in Hardin County, on the Shawnee National Forest.[^1] In April 1936 the Shawnee began buying materials for a dwelling, garage-woodshed, cistern and latrine at the Peters Creek tower site.[^2] A National Archives photograph titled "Photograph of Peter's Creek Tower and Improvement" is dated 20 May 1938, so the tower was standing by then.[^3]

A listing from about 1942 calls it the Peters Creek Lookout Station of the U.S. Forest Service. It places the tower about seven miles northwest of Cave-in-Rock, describes views to the south, southeast and southwest, and says visitors were welcome, with parking.[^1]

In August 1972 a Southern Illinoisan advertisement invited bids on this tower and seven others, all steel lookout towers on the Shawnee National Forest.[^1] Bob Frakes, a lookout historian quoted in Illinois Country Living, says nearly all Illinois towers were dismantled or sold off in the early 1970s.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Illinois list marks Peters Creek as gone.[^5]

No source describes what, if anything, is left at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it. For context, the Forest Service says that of the 16 fire lookouts built on the Shawnee National Forest, only the Trigg tower survives.[^6]
