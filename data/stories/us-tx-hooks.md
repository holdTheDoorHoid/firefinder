The Hooks lookout was a Texas Forest Service tower in Bowie County.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts entry for it gives only the name, county and agency.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas index places the tower at 33.45218, -94.29495 and marks it as gone.[^2]

The town of Hooks lies along U.S. Highway 82, about 13 miles west of Texarkana, according to a Texas Escapes profile.[^3] That profile gives the town's coordinates as 33.469439, -94.280385. Comparing those with the index's position for the tower, we calculate that the tower lies about 2.3 kilometres southwest of that point.[^2][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2] Please check with the Texas Forest Service before planning any trip to the site.
