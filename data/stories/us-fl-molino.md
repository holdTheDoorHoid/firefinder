Molino Fire Tower is a lookout in Escambia County, Florida. The Eastern US Lookouts project lists it among the state's towers and gives its land location as 2N-31W-21, a township, range and section code.[^1]

A 2023 notice from NorthEscambia.com places the Florida Forest Service's Molino Forestry Station on Highway 29, at the foot of the fire tower a short distance south of Highway 196.[^2] That implies the tower still stood in October 2023, and it is the newest source we found.[^2]

In October 2023 the station was set to host an open house on Thursday, October 26, as part of Florida Forests Week. The event was to focus on improving forested land and on prescribed fire.[^2]

No source says whether the public may climb the tower. The station belongs to the Florida Forest Service, so ask the agency before visiting.[^2]
