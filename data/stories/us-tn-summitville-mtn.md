Summitville Mountain, in Coffee County, Tennessee, rises to about 1,251 feet (381 m).[^1] The Forest Lookouts list places its tower among the Tennessee Division of Forestry towers in District 5.[^4] The tower was part of a state network that ran from 1933 to 1975, and many of the network's towers were built in the 1930s with help from the Civilian Conservation Corps.[^3]

The state's 1976 list numbered this tower 5-7, in the district-and-tower format.[^1][^2]

The lookout list records the tower as moved, not removed.[^1] No source says where the structure stands now.

The list's map gives the location of towers, past or present, so a marked spot may not hold a tower today.[^2] No source describes the site today or says whether the public can visit.
