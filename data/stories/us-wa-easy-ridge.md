Easy Ridge carried an L-5 lookout cab on the northern point of the ridge, in Whatcom County within the Mt. Baker-Snoqualmie National Forest.[^1][^3] The register and firelookout.com date it to 1935, while Willhite gives 1934.[^1][^2][^4] Panorama photos were taken in late September 1935.[^3][^4]

No one was ever posted to the cab after it was built, though it was used in emergencies.[^1][^4] In 1942 the Aircraft Warning Service looked at putting it to use and decided against it.[^4]

It was gone by about 1973.[^1][^2][^4][^5] Willhite says it burned down that year, the register says it was removed, and a National Park Service appendix gives its destruction as around 1973.[^4][^1][^5]

No source describes a standing structure or any remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6] A 2005 trip report describes a trail that climbs from the Chilliwack River to the former lookout site, and says the route is not maintained.[^6] Willhite had not visited the site when he wrote his page.[^4]
