Bartlett was a fire lookout tower in Saint Louis County, Minnesota, at about 46.9 degrees north and 92.3 degrees west.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list marks it as gone.[^1] No source we read describes what remains at the site today.

[^1]: Minnesota Lookouts (By County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/mn-co/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
