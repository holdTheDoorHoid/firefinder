The Big Mountain Lookout Tower once crowned the summit of Big Mountain, in western Webster County, West Virginia.[^1] According to the Former Fire Lookout Sites register, the view from the top took in the Elk River watershed and the upper end of Sutton Lake.[^1]

The West Virginia Division of Forestry administered the tower.[^1] The register says the cab was lost at some point before 2004.[^1] It places the steel tower's removal somewhere between May 2008 and June 2010.[^1] The FireLookout.org state list marks the site as gone.[^2]

The register's photos show the tower without its cab in October 2007, and show foundations in June 2010.[^1] Those are the most recent dated descriptions of the site we found.

The register also links a geocache at the tower's site.[^1] We found no source that says whether the summit is open to visitors, or what, if anything, stands there now beyond those foundations.

For context, the West Virginia Encyclopedia notes that the state closed its forest fire towers in 1990. It adds that fewer than a dozen remain standing, and none is in service.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added this site on July 8, 2017.[^1]
