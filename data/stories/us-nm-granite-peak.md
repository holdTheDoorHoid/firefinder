Granite Peak was a lookout tower in Grant County, New Mexico, at about 33.19 N, 108.37 W.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^1][^2] Its New Mexico list records each site's county, coordinates, type, status and register number, and the Granite Peak row carries no register number.[^1][^2] The same county list shows Reeds Peak and Signal Peak, both in Grant County, as standing towers.[^2]

No source we checked describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: New Mexico Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: New Mexico Lookouts (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/nm-co/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
