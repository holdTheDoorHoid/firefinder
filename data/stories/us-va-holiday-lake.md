The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a fire tower in Buckingham County under the name Holiday Lake, and marks it as gone.[^1] Its listed coordinates are 37.43819 and -78.66438.[^1]

TopoQuest, a place-name site, calls the spot Holiday Lake Lookout Tower (historical), in Buckingham County, and lists its elevation as 659 feet above mean sea level.[^2] TopoQuest also shows the site on a USGS 1:24K sheet for Holiday Lake, Virginia.[^2] The word historical in that name fits the list's gone status.[^1][^2]

Sources spell the name two ways. The list and TopoQuest use Holiday, while the state register uses Holliday for the nearby park.[^1][^2][^3]

The register says Holliday Lake State Park lies within the Appomattox-Buckingham State Forest and was created in 1937 as one of four "Recreation Development Areas" in Virginia.[^3] TopoQuest puts the park 2.9 miles from the lookout site.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.

[^3]: Holliday Lake State Park, Virginia Department of Historic Resources, https://www.dhr.virginia.gov/historic-registers/006-0051/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
