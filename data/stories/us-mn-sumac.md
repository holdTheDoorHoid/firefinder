Sumac was a 100-foot (30 m) stair tower in Cass County that the US Forest Service owned.[^1] The county list dates it to the 1930s, though only as a probable year.[^1] A Forest Lookouts page for Sumac Hill, placed in the Chippewa National Forest, records that the towerman's residence there was completed in June 1939.[^2]

According to the county list, the tower was disposed of at a 1972 Chippewa National Forest sale of towers and then taken down.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the tower at 47.15 north and 93.92 west, and lists the site as gone.[^3] Neither the county list nor the Forest Lookouts page describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
