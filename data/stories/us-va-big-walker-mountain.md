Big Walker Lookout is a 100-foot observation tower on Big Walker Mountain in Bland County, Virginia, at an elevation of 3,405 feet.[^1][^2] The Virginia Department of Wildlife Resources calls the tower the site's main attraction. The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as an observation tower, coded "Obs Tower," not a fire lookout.[^2][^4]

Stuart Kime built the observation tower and a country store in 1947.[^3] The Kime family has run the site ever since, and it is now in its third generation.[^1][^3] Stuart's son Ron carried on after his father's death.[^3]

The first store burned in 2003. A new store was built just beside the old one, and the swinging bridge that once led visitors to the tower was not destroyed.[^1] The original tower has been rebuilt and restored, and the chair lift up the mountain has been taken out.[^2] The sources do not say when either change happened.

Today the tower is open to visitors who pay an admission fee. They start at the Big Walker General Store, which sits about 12 miles from Wytheville, just off US Route 52.[^1] A deli and gift shop sit at the base of the tower, and the family has plans for hiking trails and a new access road.[^2]

Check hours and fees with the family before you go. The sources we read do not give a current schedule.
