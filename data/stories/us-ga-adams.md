A Georgia tower list includes an entry named adams, placed in Irwin County.[^1] It gives coordinates of 31.68602, -83.38340 and a value of 353 in its elevation column; the list does not state the unit.[^1] The list also says its names for towers numbered 73 and above are its own, so this name may not match a historical one.[^1]

The Eastern U.S. Lookouts directory has an Adams page that also gives Irwin County.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Adams - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Eastern U.S. Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/adams.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
