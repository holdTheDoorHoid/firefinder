Camp Creek Bald Lookout sits in the Pisgah National Forest in Madison County, on the North Carolina and Tennessee state line.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register dates the tower to 1928 and describes a 14-by-14-foot cab on a 21-foot steel tower.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site gives 1929 and a 20-foot tower for a 14 x 14 foot cab, and neither source explains the difference.[^2]

Sometime in the 1960s the tower received one of two prototype round cabs.[^1] The lookout is no longer active. The register says the U.S. Forest Service and a radio club have partly restored it.[^1]

A September 20, 2020 visit report describes poor condition: the paint needs work, some windows are broken, and a visitor could not fully survey the cab because its trap door was locked.[^1] A September 20, 2008 visit report says the catwalk gate was locked and wrapped in razor wire, and rates the tower in very good shape.[^1] The same 2008 report notes a small building next to the tower with commercial and amateur VHF antennas, so treat the site as something to look at from the ground rather than climb.[^1]

The register lists the lookout as added on April 2, 2008, nominated by Peter J. Barr.[^1]
