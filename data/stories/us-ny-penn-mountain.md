Penn Mountain rises in the Tug Hill region of New York, west of East Steuben, and once carried an 80-foot steel fire lookout tower.[^1]

The Conservation Department built the tower in 1950 as an Aermotor LS40 design.[^1][^2] Service began in 1951, with no fires and four visitors reported.[^1]

Sources disagree on the county: Wikipedia and the Forest Fire Lookout Association place the mountain in Oneida County, while CNY Hiking and Bill Starr's list give Herkimer.[^1][^3][^4][^2]

The tower stopped working as a fire lookout in 1971.[^1] Wikipedia says it reopened briefly in 1974 to watch an area that had suffered heavy wind damage near Forestport, then closed again.[^1] It was removed in 1976.[^1] Starr's list and CNY Hiking both mark it as removed.[^2][^4]

Today no source read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit, or whether the site is open to visitors.
