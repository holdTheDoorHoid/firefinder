Bowers Hill is listed as a fire lookout in what is now the City of Chesapeake, Virginia.[^1] Its name is older than the city.[^3] Wikipedia says the name was attached to the area as early as the 19th century, and it describes the hill as a high point in otherwise low, flat country.[^3] The lookout lists spell it Bowers Hill, while Wikipedia's article on the community uses Bower's Hill.[^2][^3] In 1963, voters in Norfolk County and South Norfolk voted to merge into a new city, Chesapeake, and the then-rural community became one of its boroughs.[^3]

The lookout record itself is brief. The Forest Lookouts page gives the city and a single status word, "Removed," and nothing more.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone, and gives its coordinates.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the hill today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
