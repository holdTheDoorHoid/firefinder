This tower began on Missionary Ridge, near Chattanooga, Tennessee.[^2] The tnlandforms catalog describes a 75-foot observation tower there, built in 1893 as a Civil War memorial where Bragg's headquarters were located.[^1] The National Register nomination calls it one of two observatory towers on the ridge for visitors to the Chickamauga and Chattanooga National Military Park. One stood at DeLong point, and the other on a knob that had served as General Bragg's headquarters during the Battle of Chickamauga.[^2]

The sources disagree on the date. The nomination says the tower was constructed around 1895 and that its exact construction date could not be found.[^2] The National Historic Lookout Register says it was originally built in 1865 for the dedication of the Chickamauga Battlefield.[^5]

By 1923 the tower had been declared unsafe and in need of repair or removal, and visitors could no longer climb it.[^2] A decision to tear it down came in 1941. Local supporters pushed to save it, and CCC workers took it apart and moved it to Fall Creek Falls, where it was reassembled and its cab enclosed.[^2]

The year of the move is also disputed. The nomination gives 1941 for the CCC move.[^2] The tnlandforms entry and the NHLR register both give 1943.[^1][^5] Heights differ too: the catalog gives 75 feet for the original tower, while the nomination describes the reassembled tower as eighty feet tall.[^1][^2]

The tower now stands in Fall Creek Falls State Park, in Bledsoe County.[^3] A tnlandforms entry for the Bradden Knob site describes it as located in Fall Creek Falls state park, formerly an observation tower on Missionary Ridge, and lists it as standing.[^3] It is listed on the National Register as the Fall Creek Falls Fire Lookout Tower, with a record dated 2015.[^4] It was registered on the National Historic Lookout Register on May 27, 1993.[^5]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains on Missionary Ridge at the original site today, or whether that ground is open to the public.
