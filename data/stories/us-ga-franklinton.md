Franklinton is one of the Georgia lookout towers on the tnlandforms.us list, whose map was revised on 22 March 2016.[^1] The entry places the tower in Bibb County at an elevation of 376 feet (114.6 m), with the coordinates 32.806111, -83.541944.[^1] The name matches a Bibb County community called Franklinton, which Wikipedia describes as unincorporated and counts within the Macon metropolitan statistical area.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors. Check who owns the land before you go.

[^2]: Franklinton, Georgia, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklinton,_Georgia (accessed 2026-10-21).
