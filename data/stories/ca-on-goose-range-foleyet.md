The Goose Range-Foleyet Fire Tower is listed on the Firelookout.ca register, which places it on Road 1002 in the Unorganized North Sudbury area of Sudbury District, Ontario. The register gives an elevation of 385 m (1263 ft) and coordinates of 48.26261, -82.58947.[^1]

The location is an estimate. The register's author says the spot was worked out from topographic maps and geological surveys, and adds, "I still could be wrong, though."[^1]

Across the province, most fire towers went up between the 1920s and the 1950s, when forest fire protection was at its height. The towers came under Ontario's former Department of Lands and Forests, a body that became the Ministry of Natural Resources in 1972.[^2]

Through the 1960s the towers saw less and less use, as spotting from aircraft and fire-fighting crews took on their role. The government then decided it did not want lawsuits over people hurt climbing unmanned towers, and many towers were destroyed.[^2]

No source I read describes the site today, including whether any structure remains there or whether the public can reach it. Because the location is only an estimate, treat the coordinates as approximate.[^1]
