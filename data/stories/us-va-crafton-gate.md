Crafton Gate is a 120-foot steel tower in Charlotte County, Virginia.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register says it was built in the 1930s, first set up as a lookout serving Charlotte County's lumber business, as the Phenix Fire Tower was.[^1] A Civilian Conservation Corps dispatch dated June 10, 1937, from Company 1390 describes a 120-foot steel fire tower being built on U.S. 360, roughly midway between Keysville and Wylliesburg.[^2] The quoted dispatch text does not name the tower. The Forest Lookouts site places the dispatch on its Crafton Gate page, which is the site's own inference, not something the dispatch states.[^2]

The register says the Virginia Department of Forestry administers the tower and still owns it.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site lists the same agency.[^2] I found no record of its staffing, no list of fires it watched, and no source that describes its condition today.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list shows it as standing.[^3]

The register recorded the tower on September 3, 2014, with a nomination by Kristin Scholetzky.[^1] I found no public access rules, so check with the Virginia Department of Forestry before going to look at it.[^1][^2]
