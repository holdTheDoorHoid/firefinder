Preston became the county seat of Webster County in 1856, when the legislature moved the seat from McIntosh.[^1]

An online index of Georgia lookout towers lists a Preston tower in Webster County, at 32.08014, -84.53653, with the codes BLL-41116 and gfc-316.[^2] The index's map was last revised on 22 March 2016.[^2]

In 2006 a Savannah newspaper report said that Georgia's fire towers had numbered more than 200 at their 1960s peak.[^3] It also reported that 137 of the state's 144 surviving towers were unstaffed.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association had catalogued 360 tower sites, current and former, around the state.[^3]

No source found describes the Preston site today, including whether a tower still stands there or whether visitors can reach it.
