The South Carolina Forestry Commission is listed as the agency for a fire tower on Browns Mountain, in Cherokee County, and the directory marks the tower as removed.[^1] A trail guide says a fire lookout once stood on the mountain, spotting fires in the woods around it.[^2] The guide says the tower is gone, but four large stone pillars that once supported it are still there.[^2]

The guide describes a 0.5-mile hiking side trip from the Kings Mountain National Recreation Trail to Browns Mountain, the highest point on the route.[^2] The trail runs about sixteen miles and links Kings Mountain National Military Park with Kings Mountain State Park.[^2] The guide rates its trail traffic as moderate to heavy.[^2]

[^2]: Browns Mountain, N2Backpacking trail guide, https://n2backpacking.com/hikes/south-carolina/kings-mountain-national-historic-park-browns-mountain/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
