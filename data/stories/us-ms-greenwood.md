The National Historic Lookout Register lists a Greenwood Lookout Tower along US 82 in Leflore County, registered on December 31, 2023 as US 1774, MS 62.[^1] The entry offers almost no background on the tower.[^1] Its only account of the tower's condition dates from summer 2023, when the glass window panes were intact.[^1]

A second register entry, the Leflore Lookout Tower (US 1737, MS 54), was registered on July 9, 2023.[^2] Its coordinates lie about 10 meters from the Greenwood entry's, by our calculation from the posted coordinates.[^1][^2] It describes a 120-foot Aermotor LS-40 standing in the flat Mississippi Delta, administered by the Mississippi Forestry Commission, with a site visit on July 2, 2023.[^2] Two entries that close together may describe one tower registered twice, but no source we read says so, so this story keeps them apart.

The Leflore entry lists the build date as unknown, and neither entry names anyone who worked the tower or when it last served as a lookout.[^1][^2] The newest date we found is from 2023, so we cannot say what the tower looks like today.[^1][^2] Neither entry says whether the public may visit or climb, so treat it as a structure to admire from a distance, and ask the Forestry Commission before going near it.[^1][^2]
