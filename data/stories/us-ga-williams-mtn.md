The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia towers places a tower called "williams mtn" in Harris County and gives its elevation as 935.[^1] The list states no unit, so we read that figure as feet, about 285 m.[^1] Its tag, gfc-307, refers to an older Georgia Forestry Commission list of tower locations.[^1] The name itself is the list author's own label, since the list says names for towers numbered 73 and above are its creation.[^1]

In October 1955 a Columbus Ledger report, as Forest Lookouts quotes it, named two lookout operators in the Harris County Forestry Unit: Henry M. Koone, who manned Harris Tower, and Idus T. Averett, who was stationed at the William Mountain tower.[^2] The report says Harris County is one of the Georgia counties whose organized forestry units are tied to the Georgia Forestry Commission.[^2] The same article spells the name William Mountain.[^2] We take that to be the same site as the list's "williams mtn", though no source says so outright.[^1][^2]

We found no source that describes the site today. None says whether a tower still stands on the hill, what remains there, or whether visitors may go up, so its present state is unknown.
