Hog Heaven was a steel fire lookout on the Flathead Indian Reservation, about 17 miles northwest of Polson in Lake County.[^1] The steel tower went up around 1935. It stood 60 feet tall and carried a 7-by-7-foot steel cab.[^1][^2] A survey description from 1940 puts the tower at about 70 feet, so the sources do not agree on its height.[^2]

The Montana Lookouts page lists the agency as the Flathead Indian Agency, and the 1940 survey calls the tower an Indian Service lookout.[^2]

In 1946 a forest fire burned what the Montana Lookouts page calls the living quarters and firelookout.com calls the cabin.[^1][^2] The tower was removed around 1969.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks the tower as gone.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the public may visit. The site lies within the Flathead Indian Reservation,[^1] so check locally before going.
