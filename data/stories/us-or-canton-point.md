Canton Point is a lookout site in Lane County, Oregon, about 18 miles southwest of Oakridge, on the Umpqua National Forest.[^1] In September 1954 Forest Service crews were building a 40-foot tower and lookout house there, because the area was not well covered for fire detection.[^2] Materials were packed in by mule for three miles from the end of Sharps Creek Road, and a carpenter from Glide handled the construction.[^2] The house was to be built of Douglas fir.[^2] Firelookout.com dates the 40-foot wooden L-4 tower to 1955.[^1]

A July 1963 News-Review item says a Portland State graduate from Eugene was stationed at Canton Point Lookout.[^2]

The sources disagree on how the lookout ended.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com says it was abandoned in 1973 and is gone.[^1] Forest Lookouts says the lookout was removed in 1976.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list also marks the tower as gone.[^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors, so check with the Umpqua National Forest before you go.
