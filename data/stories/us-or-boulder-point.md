Boulder Point sits about 17 miles southeast of Mount Hood, in Wasco County, Oregon, within the Mount Hood National Forest.[^2][^3] Its elevation is listed at 3,554 feet.[^2]

In 1934 a crow's nest was erected in a tree on the point, with a tent and a telephone.[^1] A second listing gives the same year and describes the setup as a crow's nest and camp.[^2] It is briefer than the first and does not mention the tent or the telephone.[^2]

Both listings say the site was later abandoned.[^1][^2]

No source describes the site today, so what remains there, and whether the public can reach it, is unknown.

A different Boulder Point Lookout, in Ravalli County, Montana, is a separate structure; its National Register nomination calls it an example of the 1931 L-4 lookout house design.[^4]
