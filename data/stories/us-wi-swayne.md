The Swayne lookout tower stands in Sawyer County, in northwestern Wisconsin. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's statewide list places it at about 45.69 north and 91.06 west and marks it as standing. The list gives it no national register number, build date or builder.[^1]

OpenStreetMap names it the Swayne Lookout Tower, lists its elevation as 403 meters, and tags it as an observation tower.[^2] The name matches on both sites, which suggests they describe one structure. We could not find a history of the tower in the sources searched. Searches on the name turned up no account of it, so the name may be a local one or a variant spelling. Its construction, staffing and fate remain unknown here.

Wisconsin's wider record is clearer. In 2015 the Associated Press reported that dozens of Wisconsin fire lookouts, most of them raised in the 1930s and 1940s to watch for forest fires, were being removed from service.[^3] The Department of Natural Resources said the 72 towers still in use were not worth the expense of upkeep, repairs or replacement.[^3] The report does not name Swayne, so whether it was among the towers taken out of service is not known from these sources.

The sources read give no access rules for Swayne. A travel article names the Mountain Fire Lookout Tower as the state's sole remaining fire tower open to climbers.[^4] That implies, without saying so, that Swayne is not open to climbers. Check with the landowner before going near the site, and do not climb a tower that is closed or private.
