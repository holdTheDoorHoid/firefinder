Crows Nest Peak, also called Crow Peak, is a summit in Pennington County, South Dakota, within the Black Hills National Forest.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 7,049 feet, while a climbing site gives 7,044 feet.[^2][^3]

In April 1929 a Deadwood newspaper item reported that a field tower would be built at Crow's Nest for rangers to watch for fires. The item said it would be done within one or two weeks and staffed for a spell at the height of the dry season.[^1]

A climbing site says a fire tower was built on the summit in the 1940s and has since been removed.[^3] The sources do not say whether that was the 1929 tower, or whether the 1929 tower was ever built. Firelookout.com and the Fire Lookout Association list both mark the tower as gone.[^2][^5] A 2020 South Dakota Public Broadcasting list of Black Hills towers names Crows Nest Peak among former tower sites, not sites with a standing tower.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today. The peak is on national forest land, but we found no access rules for it, so check with the Black Hills National Forest before going.[^2]
