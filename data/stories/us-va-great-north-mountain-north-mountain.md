The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower called Great North Mountain (North Mountain) in Frederick County, Virginia, at 39.22102, -78.33779.[^1] The listed name matches the Great North Mountain ridge, which ends at U.S. Route 522 in Frederick County.[^2] The Forest Lookouts index for Virginia lists two North Mountain towers, one in Frederick County and one in Shenandoah County, so check the coordinates before you match a name to a site.[^3]

I found no build date, designer, owner, or staffing history for this tower in the sources I could open, and no named fires either. The association's list gives no date for its entry, so it does not say when the tower was built or last checked.[^1]

The association marks the tower as standing, but its entry has no National Historic Lookout Register number.[^1] The register's Virginia page lists 48 lookouts, and this tower is not among them.[^4] That leaves the standing status resting on a single directory listing.

I found no information about access, rental, or visits.[^1] Ask the landowner before going near the site, and do not climb the tower without permission.
