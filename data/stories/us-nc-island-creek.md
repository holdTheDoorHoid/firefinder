Island Creek is listed as a fire tower in Jones County, North Carolina.[^1] A state-wide list places it at 35.02055, -77.14805 and gives its National Geodetic Survey point number as EY2060.[^1] The list cautions that some entries may be observation towers or Coast Guard lookouts.[^1] The Eastern US Lookouts guide lists the site under Jones County and the Croatan National Forest.[^2]

No source describes the site today.

[^2]: North Carolina Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/north-carolina.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
