The Silver Peak Lookout Complex was a fire lookout on the Douglas Ranger District of Coronado National Forest, in Cochise County, Arizona.[^1][^2]

The Civilian Conservation Corps built it in 1938.[^1] The tower was an L-4 type, wood-framed and 14 feet square.[^1] The site also held an outhouse, a storage shed and a water cistern.[^1] The access trail was first cut to suit mules during the lookout's working years.[^1]

The National Register of Historic Places listed the complex on January 28, 1988, with a period of significance from 1925 to 1949.[^2]

One listing site puts the complex at Paradise, Arizona, but the National Register record says Portal vicinity.[^1][^2]

According to Ominous, the tower was lost to fire around 1996, during a thunder-snowstorm.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as gone.[^3]

The site is now in poor shape.[^1] Remaining features include a locked storage shed, tower ruins, and an old water cache perched on a rock pinnacle.[^1]
