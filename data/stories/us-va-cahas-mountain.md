Cahas Mountain is Franklin County's highest peak.[^3] Its tower was one of many that watched over this section of southern Virginia, and the U.S. Forest Service is listed as its administrator.[^1] The register gives the site an elevation of 3,945 feet (1,202 meters).[^1]

The tower suffered repeated vandalism.[^1] The register says the forestry office in Franklin County then decided to take it down, and it dates the removal to about 2007 or 2008.[^1] The register adds that every Franklin County tower has since come down.[^1]

The register says The Nature Conservancy owns the site now.[^1] A Blue Ridge Land Conservancy hike notice, however, says the view from the top of Cahas Mountain is on private land the conservancy conserves.[^2] The two sources do not line up on who holds the land, and the notice names no owner. No source describes what, if anything, is left of the tower. That guided hike up Cahas Mountain is ticketed and limited to 15 participants, and no public access is described.[^2]
