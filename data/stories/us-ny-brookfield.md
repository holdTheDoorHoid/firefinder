The Town of Brookfield in Madison County was set, in 1946, to receive a fire tower from elsewhere.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list shows a Brookfield lookout in Madison County and marks it as gone.[^1]

In July 1946, District Forester Charles E. Baker reported that the Chenango Lake Fire Tower would be moved to Brookfield.[^2][^3] Work on a foundation at Brookfield was under way, and the move would wait until it was finished.[^2] Baker expected the Brookfield tower and the one on Berry Hill in Pharsalia, Chenango County, to give better coverage of the area.[^2]

That same list also marks the Chenango Lake tower as gone.[^1] The 1946 article is the only source we read that links the two towers, and it describes a plan rather than a finished move.[^2][^3]

No source we read describes the Brookfield site now, so we cannot say what remains there or whether the place is open to visitors.[^1]
