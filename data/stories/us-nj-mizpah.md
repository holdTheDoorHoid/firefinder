Mizpah Station Fire Tower stands in Atlantic County, New Jersey, in the Pinelands region near Hamilton Township.[^1][^3] A March 28, 1924 newspaper notice said a large steel tower would help guards locate fires accurately as soon as they started.[^2] Fire Warden Maurice F. Aaron arranged for it, and on June 22, 1924 a paper reported it nearly erected.[^2]

According to the register, the first tower stood near the old Mizpah Hotel. It was a 60-foot Aermotor with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab.[^1] In 1959 it was moved one mile up the road and raised to 100 feet.[^1] Only the register mentions the 1959 move.[^1] The Wikipedia list gives the tower a height of 100 feet (30 m) and an elevation of 106 feet (32 m).[^3]

The register says the tower was recently restored and is the key tower of the southern region of the Pinelands, though it gives no date for the restoration.[^1] It was added to the register on March 15, 2004.[^1] I found no record of who staffed Mizpah or of any particular fire it reported.[^1][^2]

The New Jersey Forest Fire Service administers the tower.[^1] None of the sources I read says whether the public may visit or climb it, so ask the Forest Fire Service before going.
