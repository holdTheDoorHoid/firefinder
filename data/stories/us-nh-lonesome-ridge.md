Lonesome Ridge is a 1,673-foot (510 m) summit listed under Berlin, in Coos County.[^1][^2]

The White Mountain History site describes a class of "secondary" lookouts.[^3] These were lower summits with narrower views, staffed only when fire risk was high.[^3] It names Lonesome Ridge as one example.[^3]

The White Mountain fire towers were organised in 1910, when the timber owners' association, the state and the Forest Service set up a system together.[^1] In New Hampshire, many of those towers were destroyed in the 1938 hurricane.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts list marks the Lonesome Ridge tower as gone.[^2]

None of the sources checked describes the site today. A YouTube video is titled Lonesome Ridge Fire Tower Remains, but its contents could not be checked.[^4]
