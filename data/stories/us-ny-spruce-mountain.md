Spruce Mountain lies within the Saratoga County town of Corinth, and a 73-foot fire tower tops its summit.[^5] The state describes it as a steel Aermotor LS-40 that went up in 1928.[^2]

The tower was staffed until 1988, and the register's 2017 visit reports list it as unstaffed.[^2][^1] The state had come to rely more on aerial detection, and it phased out the towers.[^2] In 1990 it closed the last five fire towers still in use.[^2]

After the lookout ended, restoration began in 2014, with Student Conservation Association members doing the first work.[^2] The state finished the restoration in 2015.[^3][^2] The trail came about through a partnership between the state, Saratoga County and Saratoga PLAN.[^3] One trail-guide banner gives September 2016 as the reopening date, which conflicts with the state's 2015 announcement that the tower was open.[^4][^2] A 2017 visit report notes that radio repeater equipment has been installed on the tower.[^1]

Today the tower is closed.[^3] The Saratoga PLAN trail page, citing the DEC, says the tower itself is closed because of structural concerns.[^3] The public may hike the trail outside the hunting seasons, and it is about 2.4 miles round trip.[^3][^2] For 2026, the hunting-season closure runs from September 19 to December 6.[^3] The route crosses land owned by Lyme Adirondack Timberlands II under a cooperative agreement with the state.[^2]
