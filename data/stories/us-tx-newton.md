The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list includes a tower named Newton, in Newton County, at 30.89038, -93.70708. The list marks it as gone.[^1]

The same list gives a Burkeville tower its own row in Newton County, at 30.93750, -93.66722, also marked as gone.[^1] A lookout directory reproduces a 1966 Coast and Geodetic Survey description of a Burkeville lookout tower. Its observation point was the peak of a pyramid-roofed small house, set on a four-legged steel support, and the overall height was about 110 feet. The tower stood about four and a half miles south of Burkeville, between State Highway 87 and Farm Road 1414.[^2]

Measured between the two register coordinates, the Newton point lies about 6.5 km southwest of the Burkeville row. The survey places its tower about 4.5 miles (7.2 km) south of the town. The two entries may describe one structure, but none of the sources read says so.[^1][^2]

No source found describes what remains at the Newton site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1]

The 2025 book Fire in the Piney Woods describes the location and design of Texas lookout towers, and is a place to look for a fuller record.[^3]
