Elk Mountain is a 4,191-foot peak in Lake County, California, about 10 miles north of Upper Lake.[^2] It lies within the Mendocino National Forest.[^2]

A forest ranger lookout was in use on the mountain by November 1925.[^1] That month the Ukiah Republican Press reported that F.H. Runk was in charge of the forest rangers' lookout on Elk Mountain, and that he had been in Ukiah on business that week.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the lookout as gone and gives its type as unknown.[^3]

Today a dirt national forest road, the Elk Mountain Road, passes about a kilometer east of the summit.[^2] We found no source that describes what, if anything, stands on the summit now, or whether the public may go up there. Check with the national forest before you plan a visit.
