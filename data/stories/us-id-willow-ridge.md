Willow Ridge Lookout stood on a ridge in Idaho County, in the Clearwater National Forest, about 5 miles northwest of the Lochsa Ranger Station.[^1][^3][^4]

It started as a tent camp in 1929. Ranger Ralph Hand set up the camp and put one man on the ridge.[^4][^1] In 1930 a pre-cut L-4 cabin was packed in and put together by Earnest Hartman and Harry Chenoweth. It rested on log sills set directly on the ground, with no tower.[^4][^1][^3]

By 1930 Ranger Fred Shaner was running the district and oversaw the work.[^4] The site was staffed every season until 1934.[^4]

In 1934 a fire burned the cabin.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The site was never staffed again, mainly because there was little to protect.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in.[^2] Louis Hartig's 1981 account places the point at the end of Trail #237, reached by way of Fish Creek.[^4] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the way in is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
