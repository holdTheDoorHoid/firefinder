Land Information Ontario's tower layer lists a Kwinkwaga Lake Fire Tower, placed east of Kwinkwaga Lake. The point is marked as verified and is accurate to within 10 metres [33 feet].[^1] The record leaves its height and construction date blank.[^1]

The design was straightforward: a tall structure on a high point, a resident observer, and a way to report fires to crews on the ground.[^3] At the height of the system, Ontario alone counted 325 lookouts.[^3]

Firelookout.ca also has an entry under the same name, with a Thunder Bay address, marked "Location not verified."[^2]

As of 2013, Ontario's ministry had decided to remove many of the still-standing towers.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at this site today, or whether the public can reach it.
