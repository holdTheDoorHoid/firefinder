Johnson Butte lies in Curry County, on the Siskiyou National Forest, at 3,779 feet.[^1] In 1933 a 20-foot observation tower and a rag camp were set up on the site.[^1][^2] Panorama photos were taken on November 7, 1936.[^3]

A May 1940 Siskiyou National Forest guard-placement plan, quoted on the Oregon page, suggested a season on the premise that lightning was the post's main justification.[^3] In 1941 the lookout was staffed for 84 days and reported to the Gold Beach station by radio.[^3]

In 1947 a 20-foot treated timber L-4 tower replaced the first structure.[^1][^2] The register says that tower was removed in 1968, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2] The Oregon page also lists the tower as removed.[^3] The firelookout.org county list marks the site as gone.[^4] I found no source that describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
