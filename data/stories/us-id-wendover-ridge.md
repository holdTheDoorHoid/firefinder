Wendover Ridge is in Idaho County, in the Clearwater National Forest, about six miles west-northwest of the Powell ranger station.[^1] The listing gives a pole L-4 tower for 1934.[^1]

The tower was destroyed in the 1950s, according to that listing and a June 2016 list of Clearwater National Forest lookouts.[^1][^2] That list is hosted on the Forest Service website.[^2] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page labels the site "Drive-Up."[^3]

None of the three sources describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^3]
