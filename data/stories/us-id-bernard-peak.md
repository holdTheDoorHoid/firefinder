Bernard Peak sits at the south end of Lake Pend Oreille, about three miles south-southeast of Bayview.[^2][^4] Most sources date the first lookout there to 1918, when a 20-foot platform tower went up.[^2][^3][^4][^5] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives 1919 and calls the first tower a pole tower.[^1]

In the 1930s a 40-foot pole tower replaced it.[^1][^4] In 1948 a 40-foot L-4 tower of treated timber went up some 500 feet north of the top.[^1][^2][^5] Rex's page gives 35 feet for that tower.[^4]

Staffing was seasonal.[^4] A Sandpoint newspaper item quoted on Rex's page reports James Chamberlain on the lookout in July 1950, and Mr. and Mrs. Dave Palmer in 1959.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists a Thrash couple for 1953 and Randy Langston for 1970.[^3] Chad Kinzel spent June to September 1967 there, though firelookout.com names him Charles Kinzel.[^1][^2] In 1969, Mr. and Mrs. William Heacox of Spokane were watching for fires.[^4]

In 1961 the winning bidder on a nearby timber sale was to construct a mile of road for access.[^4] WillhiteWeb says the road was built.[^5] In October 1968 vandals broke in, smashing windows and dishes, shattering lantern glass and shooting holes in the floor.[^4] The ranger said the lookout staffer took most of the gear out each fall and boarded the lookout up for winter.[^4]

The tower was destroyed in 1976.[^1][^2][^3][^5]

The trees now block most of the views the lookout once offered, WillhiteWeb reports.[^5] Its photos include a footing dated 8/8/63.[^5] Idaho Fire Lookouts saw signs of several towers around the summit, along with a partly engraved piece of concrete. It guesses a CCC crew left the initials on it.[^3]

WillhiteWeb gives the hiking distance as eight miles one way, with trails running from Farragut State Park to the summit.[^5] It also says a car could reach the summit unless gates or trees are in the way.[^5] We found no closure notice, so check before going. Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site drive-up.[^3]
