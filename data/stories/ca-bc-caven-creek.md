The Caven Creek lookout stood on a mountaintop about 32 km (20 miles) southwest of Jeffray, in the Cranbrook District of East Kootenay, British Columbia.[^1] Canada Lookouts prints its elevation as 1927 with no unit; read as metres, that is 1,927 m (6,322 ft).[^1]

F.B.M. Peterson was a longtime B.C. Forest Service employee who spent 27 years staffing the isolated Caven lookout.[^1] In fire season he was a key employee of the Elko ranger district.[^1]

A June 1955 obituary, which the Canada Lookouts page credits to The Lethbridge Herald, reports that Peterson died at Fernie Hospital after his jeep plunged down a bank near his farm south of Elko.[^1] It adds that he had recently been at the Elko ranger station, awaiting trail conditions that would let him return to his post that season.[^1]

The Canada Lookouts listing marks the site as removed.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains on the mountaintop today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Caven Creek, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/caven-creek.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
