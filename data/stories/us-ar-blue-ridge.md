The earliest dated record for the Blue Ridge lookout is a survey entry, not a construction date.[^2] The Coast and Geodetic Survey described it in 1951 as a steel tower on four legs, topped by an observation cabin, about 120 feet (36.6 m) high.[^2] The station sits about three miles north-northeast of Dierks, in Howard County.[^1][^2]

The site page names the Arkansas Forestry Commission as the agency.[^1] The Encyclopedia of Arkansas dates the commission to Act 234 of 1931, and says it too built many towers.[^3] In Arkansas, tower watchers were the chief means of spotting fires until aerial detection improved in the 1960s.[^3]

The federal datasheet still gives a current position for the station and flags no destroyed mark.[^2] Its history section lists only the 1951 first observation.[^2] That does not show whether the tower stands today. No source I could read describes what is at the site now, or says whether the public may visit it.

Check with the agency before going, and do not climb a structure whose condition is unknown.
