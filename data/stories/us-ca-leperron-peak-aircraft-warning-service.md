LePerron Peak is in Humboldt County, in the Klamath National Forest, which administers the site.[^1] According to the register, a temporary cabin went up there in 1942 for Aircraft Warning Service use.[^1]

The Aircraft Warning Service was the civilian arm of the Army's Ground Observer Corps, created during World War II to spot enemy aircraft heading into American airspace.[^2] Volunteers were organized into it in May 1941.[^2]

A Klamath National Forest chronology, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, records a temporary cabin erected on October 1, 1942.[^3] The register says cabins like this were usually replaced by a purpose-built observation structure soon after, but it does not know whether that happened on LePerron Peak.[^1]

The register gives the removal date as unknown.[^1] The Aircraft Warning Service was deactivated on May 29, 1944, but no source ties that date to this peak.[^2]

The register shows an old photograph of the temporary cabin, captioned as circa 1942.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains on the peak today.[^1][^3]
