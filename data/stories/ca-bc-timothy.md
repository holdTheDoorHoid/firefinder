Timothy is a fire lookout site in British Columbia, listed on the Canada Lookouts website under its British Columbia lookouts, with the region label "Cariboo".[^1] The province's base map marks a fire lookout tower symbol in the same area, under feature number 27368.[^2] The Canadian names database lists Mount Timothy at 51.904444, -121.261667, to within about 100 metres.[^3]

We found no source that gives the year the lookout was built, its design or height, who staffed it, or how it ended. The name now also belongs to Mount Timothy Ski Area, in the South Cariboo.[^4] From 100 Mile House the ski area lies about 30 km to the north, and from Lac La Hache about 18 km to the northeast. Its summit is listed at 1,654 m (5,427 ft).[^4] A profile of the area says the hill had opened by December 1987, with a few runs.[^5]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout today, or whether visitors can reach the site.
