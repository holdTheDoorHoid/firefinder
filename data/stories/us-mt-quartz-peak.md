Quartz Peak Lookout sits in the Kootenai National Forest, about 12 miles northwest of Libby in Lincoln County, Montana.[^1][^2] The first structure on the summit was a cabin, begun in 1934.[^1][^4] A steel tower about 60 feet tall went up the following year, in 1935.[^1][^4]

Sources give slightly different summit heights. The National Historic Lookout Register lists the site at 6,251 feet, while firelookout.com lists 6,255 feet.[^1][^2]

The cabin did not last. A fire in 1954 burned it down, but the tower kept operating after that.[^1][^4] The tower stayed in use until the late 1960s, and it has been abandoned since then.[^1][^4] The sources read do not name anyone who staffed it or any fire it spotted during those years.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list still shows the tower as standing.[^3]

The road that once reached the summit is gone.[^4] A July 2026 trip report describes a 5-mile foot trail with about 800 feet of climbing in its place.[^4] The sources do not say whether the public may enter or climb the tower, and none mention renting this one. Anyone planning a trip should check with the Kootenai National Forest first.

The Register added the lookout on January 9, 2021, as US 1509, MT 87.[^1] Kyle Stetler nominated it.[^1]
