The Benson lookout tower stood near Goshen, in Washington County, Arkansas.[^1] In 1971 the National Geodetic Survey set a survey marker at the site, about two miles south of the village of Mayfield.[^1][^2] Its description refers to the tower's northwest and northeast legs, and a reference mark was cemented into the concrete foundation of the northwest leg.[^2] The directions to the marker turn onto a gravel driveway, where a sign marks a fire tower.[^1]

The 1971 description places the site on private property.[^1] The most recent note we found is a 2005 recovery record by individual contributors, which says the survey marker was in good condition.[^2]

No source we found describes the tower or the site today, so it is not known whether the tower still stands.

Arkansas once had 122 known fire towers. In 1994, forty-eight were still standing, and whether any of them were still operating is not known.[^3] The people who staffed these towers often lived beside them, and their job was to spot smoke.[^3]
