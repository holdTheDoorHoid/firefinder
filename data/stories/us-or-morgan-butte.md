Morgan Butte is a lookout on the Fremont National Forest, about 26 miles north-northwest of Lakeview, in Lake County.[^1][^2] The site did not become a lookout until 1958.[^3] The Round Pass site had first held a house built in 1922, which was replaced in 1930 by a 14-by-14-foot L-4 house.[^4] The house moved in 1958 was that L-4 house, carried by low-boy trailer to the butte that spring.[^4][^2] The lookout duties moved with it, and the register says the Round Pass site then became an OMNI radar site.[^5][^2]

One 2024 visitor says the earlier lookout actually stood on Round Mountain rather than at the pass.[^3] The Round Pass record gives the pass site's elevation as 7,462 feet.[^4]

The current cab went up in 1964 as an R-6 flat-roofed ground cabin with a catwalk.[^2][^5][^3] The register dates a 1985 rehab that put a hip roof on the cab.[^2] One site page dates the R-6 cab to 1958 instead, which the other sources do not support.[^1]

The lookout is staffed through the summer.[^1][^3] A staffer the visitor met in July 2024 was on his fourth season there, and said he had to evacuate in 2020 when the Brattain Fire broke out.[^3] The register listed the site on March 31, 2003.[^2]

On that July 2024 visit, the writer hiked up from the north on the Fremont National Recreation Trail, then drove Road 020 to a gate.[^3] From the gate one can also walk the road up to the lookout.[^3] Check with the Fremont National Forest about summer staffing and road conditions before you go.
