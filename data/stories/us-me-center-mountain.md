The Maine forestry department built its lookout on Center Mountain, in Piscataquis County, between 1917 and 1919. A 16-foot steel tower was partly up in 1917, with plans to finish it in 1918, but the work was still unfinished that year.[^1]

By June 15, 1919, crews had the tower standing, with a telephone connected and the station ready for use.[^1] A newspaper in August 1919 listed it as a 12-foot steel tower in township T4R10, and an inventory from 1920 showed the same height.[^1]

A patrol and lookout worked the mountain in 1919.[^1] They built a bedroom onto the cabin, 8 feet by 14 feet, painted the tower and the house, and cleared the ground around the station.[^1]

A Maine Memory Network photograph from about 1920 shows Forest Department employees posed at the tower.[^2]

The land was private when the tower went up, and the site now lies inside Baxter State Park.[^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association says the tower is no longer standing.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether visitors can reach the former tower spot.
