The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Ohio list records a tower at Carbondale, in Athens County, and marks it as gone.[^1] An Ohio State University Knowledge Bank entry (item 1051) gives its subject as a lookout tower on land belonging to the Carbondale Coal Company, in Athens County.[^2] Carbondale is a settlement in the northwest of Waterloo Township, not an incorporated town, and its name comes from the coal in the hills around it.[^3] Coal mining there began in 1867, shortly after the railroad arrived.[^3] Zaleski State Forest closely surrounds the community, and the area's mines closed years ago.[^3] State Route 56 lies a short distance south of the community.[^3] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.

[^2]: Look-out tower on Carbondale coal Company tract Athens county (item 1051), Ohio State University Knowledge Bank, https://kb.osu.edu/items/b78118ed-387c-4fb6-856c-43c25801ecfa (accessed 2026-10-21).
