The Cedar Key (Suwannee) Fire Tower stands in Levy County, Florida.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as an 80-foot Aermotor LS-40 that still stands in good condition.[^1] The register does not explain the "Suwannee" part of the name.[^1]

The sources reviewed do not say when the tower was built, who erected it, or what it was used for, and no construction, staffing or fire record turned up.[^1][^2] One clue comes from a U.S. Geological Survey groundwater well record. That record is named "DOF-CEDAR KEY TOWER (ROSEWOOD TOWER)" and sits about 57 meters from the register's coordinates (our calculation), so it may mark the same site.[^3][^1] The initials DOF are not explained, and the well record gives no tower height, construction date or design, so the link is an inference rather than a confirmed fact.[^3]

The register lists the tower on July 24, 2016, as US 1090, FL 18, with Robert Spear as nominator.[^1] It gives the site's elevation as 20 feet and names the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services as the agency that administers the tower.[^1]

The register does not describe public access, visiting rules or climbing rules for the tower.[^1] Anyone planning a visit should check with that agency first, and should not climb the tower without its permission.[^1]
