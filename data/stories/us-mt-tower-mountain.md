Tower Mountain is a 9,268-foot peak in Beaverhead County, Montana, on the Beaverhead National Forest, about 18 miles north-northwest of Dillon.[^1] Firelookout.com describes a live-in tower on the site and dates it to 1955.[^1] That entry does not say whether 1955 is the year the tower was built or the year it was abandoned.

A survey description written in 1956 and reproduced on the Forest Lookouts page puts the summit about 13 miles northwest of Dillon, a different distance from the one firelookout.com gives.[^1][^2] The same description gives directions with a road sign reading "TOWER MTN LOOKOUT TOWER," which suggests the tower was standing by then.[^2]

Firelookout.com marks the tower as abandoned and gone, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list records it as gone.[^1][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the site is open to visitors. Check with the forest before going up.
