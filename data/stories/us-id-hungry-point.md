The Hungry Point site lies in the Clearwater National Forest, in Idaho County, at about 5,770 feet.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The lookout was an L-4 cab set on a log crib, built in 1934.[^1][^2] The Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list calls it a two-story cab.[^6] The other sources describe an L-4 cab on a log crib and give no story count.[^1][^2][^3] The L-4 was a 14-by-14 cabin design, prefabricated for live-in use, and such cabins were produced until 1952.[^5]

The lookout was abandoned in 1945 and is listed as gone.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts marks the site as hike-in rather than drive-up.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or says whether it is open to visitors.
