The Caney lookout is a 100-foot steel tower in Ozark County, in the Mark Twain National Forest.[^1][^2] A lookout history site dates it to 1937.[^1]

The tower stands on a hillside along the Glade Top Trail.[^3] The trail was first constructed by the Civilian Conservation Corps in the late 1930s, close to the date of the tower.[^3][^1] The nearby Caney Day Use Area is on an Ozark glade.[^2] The Forest Service adds that Forest staff once kept watch for wildfires from the tower.[^2]

The Forest Service says the old tower is out of service and closed to visitors.[^2] A River Hills Traveler report on a fence project at the day-use area says the tower still stands on the hillside.[^3] Do not climb it.
