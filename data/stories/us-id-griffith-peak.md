Griffith Peak lies nine miles south-southeast of Lakeview, in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1] Sources disagree on the elevation. Idaho Fire Lookouts gives 4,589 feet, while a 1945 newspaper item quoted by Forest Lookouts says 5,800 feet.[^2][^3]

A 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab is recorded for 1934.[^1][^2][^3] A July 1926 newspaper item says a fire from Independence Creek burned out the Griffith Peak lookout and its telephone line, so the peak had a lookout in 1926, eight years before the tower recorded for 1934.[^3]

In October 1945 a newspaper item named Leonine Garnett, 18, as on duty on Griffith Peak.[^3]

The lookout was destroyed in 1953, according to Firelookout.com and Forest Lookouts.[^1][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as drive-up.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
