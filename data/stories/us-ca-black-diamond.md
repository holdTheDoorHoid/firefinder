Black Diamond is a former lookout site in Glenn County, on land of the Mendocino National Forest that the Forest Service administers.[^1] The register gives its elevation as 3,234 feet (986 meters).[^1]

A lookout was built on the site in 1935. The cabin followed California Plan BC-301 and stood on an open steel tower about 30 feet tall, braced in a K pattern. The site also held a garage.[^1] A photo caption on the register page dates one picture of the lookout to 1968, and the picture came from the Upper Lake Ranger District collection.[^1]

The register gives the removal date as unknown.[^1] According to the register, the garage foundation and the tower piers are all that is left.[^1] No source I found describes the site as it is now, so nothing is known here about its view, its road access or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Black Diamond Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ca/black-diamond-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
