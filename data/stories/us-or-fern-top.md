Fern Top is a ridge-top lookout site in Douglas County, Oregon, about 16 miles east of Reedsport.[^1][^2][^3] The register gives its elevation as 1,896 feet.[^1]

The earliest documentation we found is a May 1925 newspaper item.[^3] It reports that a suitable lookout point had turned up on Fern Top, and that a lookout would be posted there that summer.[^3] The earliest description of a tower is a September 1927 letter to the Secretary of the Interior.[^3] It says a 42-foot tower and a small cabin had been put up there for a patrolman.[^3] The land was part of the revested Oregon and California railroad grant, and the letter asked that it be reserved as a lookout station.[^3]

The register and firelookout.com both date the tower to 1932.[^1][^2] That conflicts with the 1927 letter and with a 1928 report of work on the lookout tower, so the sources do not agree on when it went up.[^1][^2][^3]

Lookouts here were seasonal.[^3] The Eugene Guard reported in October 1927 that Arnold Johnson had been stationed at Fern Top and Wallace Creek during the summer.[^3] In September 1934 the Eugene Register-Guard reported that recent rain had ended the season for most lookouts. It also said Cecil Thompson, who had been stationed at the Fern Top ridge lookout, and his wife had come home that week. The couple had married only days before taking up their post at the station.[^3]

The Oregon lookout history records that the site was activated on March 16, 1942, for the Eugene Filter Center.[^3] It says a forest fire destroyed the lookout in 1951.[^3] The register and firelookout.com both give the tower as gone around that time.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes the site today, or says whether it is open to visitors. A National Geodetic Survey station description, which the Forest Service wrote in 1936, says the survey mark was a disk set in a boulder about ten inches below the ground, directly under the tower's center.[^3] None of the pages we read says whether that mark is still there.[^3]
