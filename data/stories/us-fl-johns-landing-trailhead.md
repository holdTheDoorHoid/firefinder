The Welaka Fire Tower stands just behind the entrance sign of the Johns Landing Trailhead.[^1] The trailhead lies on County Road 309, a little south of Welaka, in Putnam County.[^2][^3]

A travel blog describes it as a 100-foot Aermotor steel lookout that the Florida Forest Service runs.[^1] The same blog dates it to somewhere from the late 1930s through the 1950s.[^1] That is the only build period in our sources, and it rests on one blog, so treat it as approximate. Wikimedia Commons places the trailhead inside Welaka State Forest and files the tower among Florida Forest Service towers.[^4]

The blog says the tower has stopped operating.[^1] Fencing keeps the public off its stairs for safety reasons, so visitors cannot use them.[^1] The same blog says the structure is in excellent condition and a well-known landmark for hikers.[^1]

Visitors reach the trailhead from County Road 309.[^2] A 2021 trail guide lists an entry fee of $2 and describes a seven-mile loop of trails.[^2]
