The Forest Service set up a fire camp on the north end of Character Peak's ridge in 1932, on the Coeur d'Alene National Forest in Shoshone County, Idaho.[^1][^2] Around the same time, a crow's nest lookout stood roughly a quarter mile from the camp.[^1]

By 1947 the camp and the nest had both been given up.[^1][^2] The Idaho site says it does not yet have a documented staffing history for the lookout.[^1]

Ray Kresek's book Fire Lookouts of the Northwest, as the site's authors read it, has the nest still standing as of 2010.[^1] In 2021 the Deceitful Gulch Fire hit the area hard.[^1] On the 2022 visit the platform was gone. Two big trees that had once held it still stood, scorched near the base, and spikes remained in one.[^1]

Today it is a hike-in site with no platform to climb. The authors also found old bottles and trash from the camp.[^1] Please do not climb the scorched trees.
