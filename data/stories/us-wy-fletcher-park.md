Fletcher Park's lookout stood in Albany County, about 23 miles northwest of Wheatland, in the Laramie Peak division of the Medicine Bow National Forest.[^1][^2] The earlier structure there was wooden.[^1] In May 1953 the Forest Service announced that the wooden structure was being replaced by a modern steel one.[^2] The lookout listing dates the steel cab to 1953.[^1]

A February 1954 report from the La Prele district said it had erected a new lookout tower at Fletcher Park to guard an area prone to lightning fires.[^2] The steel tower sat atop a rock 250 feet high, and its materials were hauled up the steep slope by rope.[^2] Most of that labor was done by a lookout and a guard during their off hours.[^2]

The two dates do not match. The listing gives 1953 for the steel cab, while the 1954 report describes the tower as newly erected.[^1][^2] The May 1953 report calls the peak Fletcher Peak, and the February 1954 report uses Fletcher Park.[^2]

The listing names Orin Beckwith as lookout personnel in 1950.[^1]

Both lookout histories mark the tower as gone or removed, and the Wyoming directory lists it as a gone tower.[^1][^2][^3] The sources reviewed do not say what remains at the peak today or whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3]
