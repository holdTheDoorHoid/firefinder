The Horseshoe Peak lookout was a patrol crow's nest, not a tower.[^1][^4] It sat atop Murray Peak, 1.25 miles northwest, according to firelookout.com.[^1]

It stood in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, about 12 miles northeast of Wallace, at an elevation of 5,734 feet.[^1] The Shoshone County list places it in Township 50 North, Range 5 East, Section 28.[^3] The earliest entry in these sources is 1939, when it is listed as a crow's nest.[^1][^3]

Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both date its abandonment to 1942.[^1][^2] The Shoshone County list gives 1947 instead.[^3] The sources disagree, and this story gives both dates. The Forest Fire Lookout Association index also lists the site as Abandoned.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site Hike-In.[^2] No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site now.
