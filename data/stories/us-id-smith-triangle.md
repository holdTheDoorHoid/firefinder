Smith Triangle Lookout stood on the Kaniksu National Forest, about seven miles southwest of Porthill in Boundary County, Idaho, at an elevation of 5,922 feet.[^1][^2]

The sources disagree about what the first structure was.[^1][^2] One page describes a 20-foot L-4 tower dated 1934.[^2] The other lists a small cabin in 1937.[^1] The 1934 date fits the staff list, which names a lookout there that year.[^1][^2]

Howard Green is credited with the lookout in 1934, and Ed Johnson for 1935 and 1936.[^1][^2]

Both pages say the lookout was destroyed in the 1950s.[^1][^2]

Today the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the site as gone, with its type recorded as ground.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, is left at the site, or whether the spot is open to visitors. The Idaho Fire Lookouts listing files it as a hike-in site.[^1]
