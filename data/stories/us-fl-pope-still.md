Pope Still is a lookout site in Wakulla County, Florida, within the Apalachicola National Forest.[^1] The spot sits at about 62 feet (19 m) above sea level and appears on the USGS Crawfordville West topographic map.[^2] A Forest Service handbook for the Florida national forests says four towers were built on the Wakulla district, but it does not name them, so the sources do not show whether this site was one of them.[^3] The same handbook says a web of graded roads let crews and equipment reach forest fires quickly.[^3]

Today, OpenStreetMap still maps a tower called Pope Still Lookout Tower at the point, with a note asking "still present?"[^4] No source I could read describes what stands there now or whether the site is open to visitors.
