The Crane Mountain lookout stood on Fremont National Forest land in Lake County.[^1] The register places it at the end of a spur one mile north of the summit.[^1] The site is about 10 miles southeast of Lakeview.[^2]

The register calls the 1952 tower a 10-foot treated timber L-4 tower.[^1] A Forest Lookouts history says the new lookout house was an Aladdin hip-roof model, and that the tower replaced the Willow Point lookout.[^3] A 1959 inspection called the tower 10 feet high, but a 1963 memo refers to a "20' wood tower," so the sources disagree on its height.[^3]

Newspaper notices quoted in that history name the people who staffed it.[^3] Gerald W. Lilje was stationed there in 1959, and Roy E. Jones was manning it in 1960.[^3] The history also says Richard and Dorothy Davie staffed it for part of the 1960 season, and a July 1961 notice has Mr. and Mrs. Ross Bailey manning it.[^3]

The cabin was rebuilt in 1963.[^3] By 1969 the lookout was used only for emergency fire detection.[^3] That year a memo weighed removing the garage and toilet, saying those spots had seen many vandalism problems.[^3]

The register says the tower was removed in 1972.[^1] The firelookout.com entry says it was destroyed that year.[^2] The Forest Lookouts history gives the same year for its removal.[^3]

A photo on peakbagging.com from July 2007 is captioned as showing all that remains of the 1952 lookout.[^4] We found no source that describes those remains in detail or says whether the site is open to visitors today.
