In 1930 the U.S. Forest Service put a lookout tower on the grounds of its Oak Grove Ranger Station, in Clackamas County, about 20 miles southeast of Estacada.[^1][^2] The two listings give different elevations for the site, 2,000 feet and 1,920 feet.[^1][^2]

The tower is gone.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes it that way,[^1] and a separate Oregon lookout list marks it as removed and gives its location as 5S-6E-36.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]

[^3]: Oak Grove Ranger Station, Rex's Fire Lookout Page, https://oregonlookouts.weebly.com/oak-grove-ranger-station.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
