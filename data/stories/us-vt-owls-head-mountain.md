Owl's Head Mountain sits in Caledonia County, Vermont.[^1] At 1,956 feet, it is the highest peak in New Discovery State Park, which lies at the northern end of Groton State Forest.[^2]

The earliest account we found is a 1924 report from the state's forestry commissioner. It describes a road built to a parking area just below the summit, an octagonal stone tower with a wide view, and a cabin for the fire lookout watchman.[^1]

Later accounts credit the Civilian Conservation Corps. Wikipedia says the park was developed in the 1930s by the CCC, and that its major CCC-built facilities were listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2002.[^2] A trail guide says the fire tower itself was built in the 1930s by the CCC.[^3] The 1924 report and these later accounts do not agree on when the stone tower went up, and we found nothing that says whether an earlier tower was rebuilt or replaced.

The final part of the route goes up stone steps and across bare ledge to the top.[^3] We found no names of watchers, no dates of service, and no fires that the lookout is recorded to have spotted.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a stone tower on the mountain as standing.[^4] The state's trail finder includes the Owl's Head trail.[^5] One trail guide describes it as a 2-mile trail that starts off the Osmore Pond picnic connector road.[^3]

We found no source saying the stone tower is open to visitors who want to go inside or climb it. Please keep to the trail and do not climb the structure.
