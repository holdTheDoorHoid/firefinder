Copper Mountain Lookout stood on a summit about three miles southeast of Eastport, in Boundary County, Idaho, within the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^2][^3] The summit is listed at 6,196 feet.[^1] The lookout index places the site at latitude 48.97317 and longitude -116.09882.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts files it under Northern Idaho.[^2]

The first entry in the record is from 1934, when the lookout was a pole tower with an L-4 cab on top.[^1][^2]

Four lookouts are on record for 1946, 1947, 1949 and 1951.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the same names and invites readers to add staffing details.[^2]

The tower is now listed as gone.[^1][^2][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts describes the site as a hike-in.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site today, so a visitor should not expect a tower there.[^2][^4]
