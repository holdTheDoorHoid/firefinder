The Northampton Fire Tower stood in Berkeley County, South Carolina.[^1] The page's header block gives the agency as National Forest, but a callout on the same page names the U.S. Forest Service.[^1] The sources we read do not say when it was built or how tall it was.

Its best-documented period is temporary staffing. In fiscal 1973-74, personnel from the State Forestry Commission began manning the tower on a temporary basis.[^1] That manning was discontinued in fiscal 1979-80.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page now marks the tower as removed, but it gives no date for the removal.[^1] OpenStreetMap still carries a point named Northampton Fire Tower, tagged as an observation tower. That is a map entry, and it does not show whether anything stands there.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today or whether the public can visit it, so check with the Forest Service before going.

[^1]: Northampton, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/northampton.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Northampton Fire Tower, OpenStreetMap node 357119648, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/357119648 (accessed 2026-10-10).
