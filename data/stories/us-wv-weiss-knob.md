Weiss Knob, in Tucker County, West Virginia, had a lookout station in the 1910s.[^1] The Eastern US lookouts page for the knob quotes reports from the state's Forest, Game and Fish Warden.[^1]

The earliest item on the page is from May 1913, when $137.48 went toward trail and telephone line construction.[^1] A 1916 report puts the station at 4,490 feet and says F.M. Glenn supervised it.[^1]

The same report says the station was left unattended for a time during the past year, because a watchman was needed at a different site. It expected a watchman to be posted there again soon.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's West Virginia list still names Weiss Knob, marks its type as unknown, and shows it as gone.[^2]

The knob's slope later held a ski area. A University of Virginia School of Law student, Robert Barton, founded Weiss Knob Ski Area there in 1955, and the state acquired that site in 1959.[^3] Canaan Valley Resort began skiing on that original site in 1971.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains of the station on the knob today, and none says whether visitors can reach the knob.[^1][^2][^3]
