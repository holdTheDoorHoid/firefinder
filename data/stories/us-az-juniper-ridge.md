Juniper Ridge Lookout stands in Navajo County, Arizona, within the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest, at an elevation of 6,985 feet.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register dates it to 1959, when a 54-foot metal tower was fitted with a live-in cab of 14 by 14 feet and a catwalk.[^1][^2] A separate lookout history site gives the same year and describes a K-brace tower with a CL-100 cab.[^2]

The Register says the U.S. Forest Service administers the tower, with the Lakeside Ranger District as a cooperator.[^1] The Forest Service says the tower sees use at the height of fire season, but the sources read name no staff members and no fires it watched.[^3]

The Register lists the tower as US 420, AZ 27, registered on March 4, 2002.[^1] It describes the lookout as being in active service.[^1]

The Forest Service welcomes visitors at the height of fire season, but staff may turn people away when the tower is unusually busy.[^3] The trail page gives directions from downtown Show Low: drive two miles west, turn right onto State Highway 260 toward Payson and Heber, then turn left onto Old Highway 160 to reach the trailhead on Juniper Ridge Trail #640.[^3]
