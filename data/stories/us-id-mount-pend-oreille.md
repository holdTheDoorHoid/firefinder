Mount Pend Oreille is in Bonner County, on the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^3] Its first lookout was an L-4 cab, built in 1930.[^1][^2][^3] A 1929 newspaper item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, had already announced a new station on the peak at the head of Lightning Creek.[^3]

In the 1940s a 40-foot pole tower went up on the summit, topped by an L-4 cab.[^1][^2][^3] The 1940s tower blew down in 1953.[^1][^2][^3] A Forest Fire Lookout Association list marks the site as a tower that is gone.[^4]

An Idaho Fire Lookouts post from August 2023 says the ruins of both L-4 towers are still on the summit.[^2] The post says the spot where the 1940s tower came down is still plainly visible. The 1930s cab's remains lie a short way down the ridge, though they are mostly wood planks now.[^2]

The same post praises the hike from Lunch Peak, says the trail is in great shape, and encourages anyone at Lunch Peak to make the short side trip.[^2] It does not mention a closure or a permit. The post dates from 2023, so check with the Kaniksu National Forest before you go.
