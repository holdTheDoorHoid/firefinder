The Cady Point lookout stood in Whatcom County, Washington, in the Mount Baker-Snoqualmie National Forest.[^1] Firetower.org lists the U.S. Forest Service as the administrator.[^1] Sources date the lookout to 1935, when a two-story L-5 cab was built on the site.[^1][^2][^3]

WillhiteWeb's page says panoramic photographs were shot from the roof, 12.5 feet up, that same year. Its text puts them in September, but its captions and a Weebly listing date them to August 11, 1935.[^3][^4]

We found no source that names who staffed the lookout or describes any fires it watched.

Sources agree the lookout was abandoned in 1970.[^1][^2][^3] Firetower.org and firelookout.com say the site now lies within North Cascades National Park.[^1][^2] A Weebly list of Washington lookouts marks it as removed.[^4]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site. WillhiteWeb's page says its author had not visited the site yet, so it gives no first-hand account of its condition.[^3] Because the site is inside a national park,[^1][^2] the park is the place to ask about access and rules.
