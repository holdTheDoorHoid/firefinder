Ochoco Butte lies in the Ochoco National Forest, about 11 miles southwest of Mitchell in Wheeler County, Oregon, at an elevation of 5,849 feet.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page for the site names the same forest.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Oregon lookouts places it in Wheeler County, gives its type as unknown and marks it as gone.[^2]

Sources say little about the lookout's history.[^1] Firelookout.com gives only a decade, the 1930s, and marks the date as unknown.[^1] The same page calls the lookout abandoned, while the Association's list calls it gone.[^1][^2] Neither source gives a date for either change or says what became of the lookout.[^1][^2]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout on the butte today, and none that says whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
