Keystone Point sits about three miles southwest of Entiat, in Chelan County, and its elevation is listed as 3,850 feet.[^1] A crow's nest was built there in 1934.[^1] The lookout was abandoned in the 1930s; the page gives no single year, so the decade is the most precise date the record offers.[^1] The site is filed under the Wenatchee National Forest.[^1]

A Washington lookouts index dates panorama photographs taken from the site to October 2, 1935.[^2] The same index marks the lookout as gone and gives the code 25N-20E-25 without explanation.[^2]

The pages we could read say nothing about what remains on the summit today, or whether the public may reach it. This account therefore cannot tell a visitor what to expect when they arrive.
