Mineral Ridge is a former fire lookout site in the Lolo National Forest, about 11 miles northwest of St. Regis, in Mineral County, Montana.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 5,500 feet.[^1]

The lookout was an L-4 tower, built in 1937.[^1] Firelookout.com says it was destroyed in 1973, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks it as gone.[^1][^2]

None of the cited sources describes what, if anything, remains on the ridge today, or whether the public can reach the site.
