Chingee is a lookout in Fraser-Fort George, British Columbia.[^1] The Canada Lookouts page for it gives a position of latitude 55.031 and longitude 122.896 (west), at an elevation of 1,287 m (4,222 ft).[^1]

A British Columbia guide to fire lookouts says the province's network held more than 300 lookouts at its height.[^2]

No source found describes the site today, so its condition and whether the public can reach it are unknown.

[^2]: Fire Lookouts of British Columbia: History, Hikes & Where to Stay, wildbluebell.ca, https://wildbluebell.ca/fire-lookouts-british-columbia (accessed 2026-10-20).
