Odart Mountain Lookout stands on an 8,531-foot peak on the White Mountain Apache reservation in Apache County, Arizona.[^1] According to the National Historic Lookout Register, the tower dates from 1934 and is a steel Aermotor MC-39 with a 7-by-7-foot cab. It rises 82 feet above the summit.[^1]

The White Mountain Apache Agency administers the lookout and is also listed as its cooperator.[^1] The register entry was made on January 31, 2002, under the numbers US 463 and AZ 48.[^1] The sources I could read do not say who staffed it over the years or whether it has had any fires.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Arizona list still shows Odart Mountain as a standing tower.[^2] I found no record that the cab was moved, rebuilt, or taken down.[^1][^2]

The sources I could read say nothing about visiting, and the register gives no visiting rule.[^1][^2]
