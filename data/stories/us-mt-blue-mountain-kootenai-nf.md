Blue Mountain rises in the Purcell Mountains on the Kootenai National Forest, in Lincoln County, Montana, about eight miles north-northeast of Libby.[^1][^2] The site's first lookout was a crow's nest in a tree, used in the early 1920s, and a one-room ground house went up in 1928.[^1][^2]

The present steel tower dates to 1935, when the Civilian Conservation Corps erected it.[^1][^2] The sources disagree on its height. The register gives 80 feet with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab, while firelookout.com gives 72 feet.[^1][^2] A new ground cabin followed in 1940.[^1][^2]

Staffing has been seasonal.[^2] An undated profile on firelookout.com says the lookout continues to be staffed in the summer.[^2] The ground cabin was torn down in 2006, according to the same profile.[^2]

The register listed the lookout on May 12, 2000, and the Forest Service administers it with the Libby Ranger District as cooperator.[^1]

Today the road is the main question. An undated travel blog post describes a sign reading "Blue Mountain Lookout – 11 Miles" and a steep, rough forest road that got worse in the last two miles.[^3] The same post describes a travel trailer near the tower, apparently the home of a new host.[^3] Nothing in these sources confirms current public access, staffing or the tower's condition, so anyone planning a trip should check with the Libby Ranger District first.[^1][^3]
