The Ochre Hill Fire Tower stands on top of Ochre Hill, inside Terra Nova National Park in Newfoundland.[^1][^2] Parks Canada's trail page says Ochre Hill Road starts at Route 1 (the TransCanada Highway) and climbs to the tower, and that the trail lies roughly two thirds of the way along that road.[^1]

A travel blog from 2023 says visitors can go nearly to the summit, stopping at a viewing platform. It describes the tower as solid and simple to ascend, and mentions more viewing platforms and red chairs up top.[^2]

An OpenStreetMap editor who had hiked the trail wrote that the tower had been mapped only as a viewpoint, even though it has public access.[^3]

Parks Canada's page still describes the road as leading up to the tower, so a car can apparently reach it.[^1] Apart from the blog's remark that the tower is solid, no source I read describes its condition.
