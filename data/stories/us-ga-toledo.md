Tom Dunigan's index of Georgia fire towers lists a Toledo Lookout Tower in Charlton County, at 30.63884, -82.10567.[^1] The index says its first 72 towers are listed by the USGS name database, so the name is not the index author's own.[^1] The index also explains its register numbers.[^1] The Georgia Forestry Commission provided coordinates for 118 towers under BLL numbers, and an older commission list described 203 towers under gfc numbers.[^1] This tower carries BLL-65198 and gfc-832.[^1]

Mapcarta describes Toledo as an unincorporated place in Charlton County, lying three miles east of the tower.[^2]

No source I found describes what remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Toledo Lookout Tower Map, Mapcarta, https://mapcarta.com/20799170 (accessed 2026-10-21).
