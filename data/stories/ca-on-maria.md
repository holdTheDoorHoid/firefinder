The Maria Fire Tower is listed as a steel tower with an elevation of 412 m (1352 ft).[^1] Firelookout.ca gives its address as Les Lacs-du-Témiscamingue, in the Abitibi-Témiscamingue region of Quebec, but the same page's region field says Ontario, so the page contradicts itself.[^1] The tower's name also appears in Head, Clara and Maria, a municipality whose northern edge is the Ottawa River.[^2]

The only dated record is the tower's appearance on the Department of Lands and Forests Tower Detection System Map of 1963.[^1] A Quebec recreation site gives a general account in which tower-based fire protection started around 1910 to 1911 and later gave way to aerial control, near the close of the 1960s.[^3]

No source I found describes what stands at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
