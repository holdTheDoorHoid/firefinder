Two lists of North Carolina towers give a Graham County lookout two different names. The names are similar enough that the two entries may describe one site, but the sources do not say so.[^1][^2] A North Carolina tower list calls it Hayce (Haoe) and places it at about 35.39 degrees north and 83.97 degrees west.[^1] A Forest Lookouts list files it under Hayoe Bald and credits the Nantahala Forest Service.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts entry is undated. Its source credit points to a Forest Service item held at the D.H. Ramsay Library, Special Collections, at UNCA.[^2]

No source describes the site now. Nothing in these sources says whether a tower or cabin stands on the bald, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^2]: Forest Lookouts, "North Carolina - Forest Lookouts," https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/north-carolina.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
