The Smeads lookout was on Smeads Mountain in Sanders County, Montana, about 5 miles west of Noxon, inside the Kootenai National Forest.[^1][^2] Rex Kamstra's firelookout.com page gives the site's elevation as 5,316 feet and lists a log cabin dated 1930.[^1]

A Sanders County Ledger item from June 1930, quoted on Ron Kemnow's Forest Lookouts page, reported that a pair of men was working to finish the lookout building on Smead Peak.[^2] An earlier Northern Idaho News item from July 1928, also quoted there, named Homer McFarland as the person in charge of the post on Smeads peak.[^2] The sources do not settle whether that 1928 station and the 1930 cabin were the same structure.[^1][^2]

Kamstra's page says the lookout was abandoned in 1935.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list gives the site a status of Abandoned rather than Gone, and lists its type as Crowsnest, which differs from the log cabin that firelookout.com describes.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today, and no source says whether the public can visit it.[^1][^2][^3]
