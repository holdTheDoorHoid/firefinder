The Ukiah Ranger Station lookout stood in the community of Ukiah, in Umatilla County, Oregon, on the Umatilla National Forest.[^1][^2] The U.S. Forest Service administered it, and the site sits at about 3,400 feet.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it as registered on November 26, 2011, as US 1146.[^1]

The tower was built in 1938.[^1][^2] It was a 60-foot treated timber structure with a 7-by-7-foot cab.[^1] Its builders also made room for a water tank alongside the cab.[^3]

The tower is no longer standing.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts list marks it as removed.[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, is left at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors. The Umatilla National Forest is the place to ask before you go.
