The Sand Knob tree tower stood in Huntingdon County, Pennsylvania, and was built in 1917.[^1] The register describes it as a tree tower and says it is no longer standing.[^1] It was filed on September 16, 2008, with Henry Isenberg as the nominator, under the registry numbers US 466 and PA 295.[^1]

A Rothrock State Forest campsite listing in Jackson Township places the Sand Knob peak nearby, close to a mountain saddle called Hubler Gap.[^2] A birding hotspot on eBird is named for the Rothrock SF Owl Gap and Sand Knob trails, and its most recent checklist shown is dated September 11, 2026.[^3]

On its fate, the register says only that the tower is no longer standing.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the tower site today, so a visitor should not expect to find a standing tower.
