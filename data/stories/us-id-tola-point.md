Tola Point is a former lookout site on the Kaniksu National Forest in Bonner County, Idaho.[^1][^3] It sits more than 8 miles west of Coolin, at 3,826 feet.[^1][^2]

The lookout was a 40-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, built in 1934.[^1][^2][^3] A crow's-nest tree platform stood about a mile southeast of the tower.[^1][^2][^3]

A blog post describes a photo from The Smokey Generation as a self-portrait by Ronald Stephens, taken at the lookout in 1945.[^4]

Sources date its abandonment to the 1950s.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list shows the tower as gone.[^5]

In a post dated August 8, 2022, Idaho Fire Lookouts reports that growth now covers nearly all of the site.[^2] The team had to push through that growth to find the footings.[^2] The search turned up three footings and no other remains.[^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site drive-up, but its account describes a bushwhack to reach the footings.[^2] No source we found says whether the site is open to visitors today.
