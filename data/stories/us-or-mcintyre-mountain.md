McIntyre Mountain sits in the Umatilla National Forest in Umatilla County, about 18 miles southeast of Milton-Freewater.[^1][^2] An Oregon lookout history page sometimes calls the spot McIntyre Point.[^1][^2][^3]

The register and Firelookout.com both date the cabin to 1941.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com describes it as a gabled L-4 cabin with a garage.[^2] The register also has photo captions that place views of the lookout in 1947 and 1956.[^1] The Oregon history page says the lookout was built and manned first about 1926, which is earlier than the 1941 date. The sources do not explain the gap.[^3]

The cabin sat on a promontory overlooking the South Fork of the Walla Walla River.[^3] The Oregon history page says it had running water piped to it, a feature lookout towers lacked.[^3]

Firelookout.com names Barnhart Tack as lookout personnel in 1943.[^2] A September 1948 Walla Walla newspaper item, quoted on the Oregon history page, says Ervin Schoesler was stationed at McIntyre.[^3]

The register says the cabin was removed in 1964, and Firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2] A July 1966 newspaper item quoted on the Oregon history page reports the closing of the lookout that year, so the sources disagree on how and when the lookout ended.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
