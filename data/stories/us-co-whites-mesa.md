The Whites Mesa lookout sits inside Mesa Verde National Park in Montezuma County, Colorado, at 7,936 feet.[^1] Its tower came from somewhere else.[^1] The Register says it came from the Apache National Forest in 1963, and the Forest Lookouts summary says it was put up to suit what was needed.[^1][^2]

The Park Service built on only the lower 30 feet of that Aermotor tower, topping it with a platform 14 feet square.[^1] A steel cab measuring 7 by 7 feet went onto the middle of that platform in 1964.[^1]

The sources read name no one who staffed the lookout and no fire it spotted.

A 2019 review rates the tower in good condition, though the wooden platforms could use replacement.[^1] The cab interior is rated fair: its equipment has been removed, and it needs repainting.[^1]

The Register accepted the lookout on March 16, 2010, as US 877, CO 15, on a nomination by Allen Farnsworth, Fire Management Officer. It lists Mesa Verde National Park as a cooperator.[^1]

The sources read do not say whether visitors can see or climb the tower, or whether anyone staffs it now. Because it stands in a national park, check with park staff before planning a visit.
