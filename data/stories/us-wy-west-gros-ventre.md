West Gros Ventre is a site in Teton County, Wyoming, that the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists at 43.53659, -110.79992.[^1] The association files it under the heading "Unknown/Undocumented Sites." Its note says that any of these sites shown to have been used as a regular wildland fire lookout would be moved to the association's lookout lists.[^1]

The Teton National Forest was adding lookout stations in the early 1940s.[^2] A Jackson Hole Courier notice from April 1941 reported four new lookout houses being built, and an August 1942 notice counted 17 fire lookout stations that year.[^2] In July 1954 the Teton Forest judged it advisable to staff the Monument Ridge and Munger Mountain lookouts, and it listed five more positions to be manned within a week.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, stands at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
