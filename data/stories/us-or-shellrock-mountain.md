The Shellrock Mountain lookout was a cabin rather than a tower.[^1][^2] It stood at 4,444 feet (1,355 meters) in the Mount Hood National Forest of Hood River County, Oregon.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the site as US 648 and records its registration on September 20, 2010.[^1]

The cabin went up in 1934.[^1][^2] The register says it was removed in 1945.[^1] The firelookout.com page and the Oregon Hikers field guide both say it was destroyed that year, and the Forest Lookouts page says only that it was removed.[^2][^3][^5]

What remains is very little.[^3] The Oregon Hikers field guide reports that the only trace still on the site is some tangled communications wire.[^3] Reaching the summit takes a short, easy bushwhack up from Surveyor's Ridge Trail, since no maintained trail goes there.[^3] Signs on the approach mark the boundary of The Dalles Watershed and ask walkers to keep to the trail.[^4] Further down the west ridge, a USGS volcano monitoring station runs on solar power.[^3]
