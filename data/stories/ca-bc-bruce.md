The Canada Lookouts register lists a Bruce lookout at 48.767 N, 123.506 W, with an elevation of 704. The page prints no unit for that figure; read as metres, it is 704 m (2,310 ft).[^1] It files the entry under Greater Vancouver, and it does not name a peak.[^1]

Salt Spring Island, which lies in the Capital Regional District, has Bruce Peak as its highest point.[^2] Wikipedia gives that peak as 709 m (2,326 ft) in one passage and as just over 700 m in another.[^2] Read as metres, the register's figure falls between those two.[^1][^2]

Read as metres, the register's height fits Bruce Peak, so the entry may well refer to that peak. The register does not confirm it. Its Greater Vancouver heading also differs from the Capital Regional District placement that Wikipedia gives for Salt Spring, so the link is an inference rather than a sourced match.[^1][^2]

No source I could read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Bruce lookout entry, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/bruce.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Salt Spring Island, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_Spring_Island (accessed 2026-10-20).
