The Georgia lookout list kept by tnlandforms.us places a tower called Carnesville in Franklin County, at 34.36076, -83.2164 and 794 feet (242 m) above sea level.[^1] Its entry carries the code gfc-919, and the list's Google map was last revised on 22 March 2016.[^1]

The entry's name matches the town of Carnesville, which was designated Franklin County's permanent county seat in 1806.[^2]

A 1955 press item quoting a Harris County ranger says quick detection and reporting of a wildfire lets fire-suppression equipment reach the blaze sooner.[^3] It is about Harris County, not Franklin County, and says lookout towers are staffed there and in 134 other Georgia counties whose forestry units are linked to the Georgia Forestry Commission.[^3]

No source describes the site now.
