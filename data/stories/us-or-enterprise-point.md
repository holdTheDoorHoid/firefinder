Enterprise Point is a site in Columbia County, and the Oregon Department of Forestry is the agency listed for it.[^1] The same county list also names Benson Mountain and Clatskanie Mountain as other lookout sites.[^1] In 1934, a field report by P.S. King describes the site as the old Enterprise lookout, roughly a quarter mile from the road.[^1] The report also notes that the old road leading to it had been opened.[^1]

King also reported that no tower had been built there.[^1] He did not think one was needed, since a tower had already been built on Clatskanie Heights, which the report gives as Benson.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains at the Enterprise Point site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Enterprise Point - Forest Lookouts, oregonlookouts.weebly.com, https://oregonlookouts.weebly.com/enterprise-point.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
