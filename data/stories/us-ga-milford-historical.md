The TN Landforms list of Georgia lookout towers names this site the Milford Lookout Tower and marks it historical.[^1] It sits in Cobb County, at 33.86844 north and 84.59771 west, and the list gives its elevation as 1,084 feet (330 m).[^1] The list's note says towers numbered 1 to 72 are the ones in the USGS name database.[^1] This entry is numbered 38, which puts it in that range.[^1]

A second Georgia directory, Forest Lookouts, also lists a Milford entry in Cobb County.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, TN Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Georgia - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/georgia.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
