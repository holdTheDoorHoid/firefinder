The Forest Lookouts website, which keeps pages on lookouts in several states, dates the tower to 1947 and describes it as 60 feet high, with a steel ladder for access and an octagonal cab.[^1] That list and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list both place the tower in Sherburne County, and the association's list marks it as standing.[^1][^2]

We did not find who built the tower or why it was placed where it is. A visitor-written hiking listing describes it as an old lookout tower in the Uncas Dunes Scientific and Natural Area, and says it was probably used in the past as a fire lookout.[^3] We could not confirm that fire use in an official record, so treat it as the listing's own suggestion.

The same listing says the top is not open for climbing, though the tower can be seen from its base.[^3] We found no Minnesota DNR statement about the tower itself, and no record of its staffing or fire history. Please do not climb the tower, and check with the land managers before you visit the area.
