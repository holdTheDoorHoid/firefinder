The first lookout on this spot was a tower that had already stood somewhere else. According to the register, the old 38-foot tower from Chelmsford was moved here and put up in 1918.[^1] A Massachusetts chapter post dates that move to 1919.[^2] The chapter says the tower served to protect only state forest land.[^2]

A new 68-foot iron tower went up in 1930, according to the register.[^1] The chapter's post gives 1932 for the same tower.[^2] Two-way radios came in 1936, and a new roof followed in 1960.[^1]

The lookout shut down when the 1986 fire season ended.[^1][^2] In 2002 the tower sold at auction, was taken apart, and went to a farm in Minnesota owned by the buyer.[^1][^2]

Today the chapter places the former tower site beside Lower College Pond Road, inside the Myles Standish State Forest and near the district fire-control headquarters.[^2] The register lists it at SW Line Road in Plymouth County.[^1] No source I could read says what, if anything, remains on the spot. The register places the tower itself on a farm in Minnesota.[^1]
