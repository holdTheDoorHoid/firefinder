The Forest Lookouts directory names the Arkansas Forestry Commission as the agency for the lookout at Artesian, in Calhoun County.[^1] Act 234 of 1931 created the Commission, which also put up many more towers.[^2] Those who staffed such towers often lived alone close by and kept a daytime watch for smoke.[^2]

A federal survey station carries the name ARTESIAN LOOKOUT TWR 3 6 AFC and is published at about 33 degrees 25 minutes north and 92 degrees 27 minutes west.[^3] The survey record ties the station to the 1981 USGS quadrangle map for Artesian.[^1] A map site lists it as a tower in Calhoun, Arkansas.[^4]

No source describes the site now, including whether anything remains there or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1]
