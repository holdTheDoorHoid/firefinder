Benton is a small community on the banks of the Suwannee River in northern Columbia County.[^1] In 1889 it was linked to Lake City by rail.[^1]

A 1936 state road map of Benton shows a sawmill, a school, farm units and a forest ranger lookout station.[^2] The Forest Lookouts index for Florida lists a Benton lookout in Columbia County and gives its location as T1N R16E.[^3]

No source describes what remains of the lookout site today, or whether the public can visit it. A 2019 Forest Service project record concerns a 250-foot public safety communications tower, and it does not mention a lookout.[^4] Columbia County asked for a special use permit to build and maintain that tower north of the Benton Tower Road (Forest Road 291), and a decision on the request was signed on 28 May 2019.[^4]
