Shell Rock Peak, also called Black Pole, sits in the Payette National Forest about 11 miles south-southwest of Yellow Pine in Valley County, Idaho.[^1][^2] Its elevation is listed as 8,565 feet.[^1][^2]

The earliest date the sources give is 1936, for an L-4 cab.[^1][^2] The lookout was abandoned in 1967.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a gone ground structure, placed at 44.8159, -115.5808.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts files the page under its West Central region.[^1]

The name is easy to mix up. Shellrock Peak, a separate lookout, appears on the same association list as standing.[^3]

Today the sources we read do not describe what, if anything, remains on the peak. Idaho Fire Lookouts classes the site as a hike-in, not a drive-up.[^1] Nothing we read says whether it is open to visitors.
