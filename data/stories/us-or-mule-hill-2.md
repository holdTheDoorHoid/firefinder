Mule Hill is in Klamath County, about 18 miles southwest of Klamath Falls.[^3] The Klamath FPA administered the site.[^1]

A 6-by-6 cab went up on the hill in 1930.[^1] One listing calls it a frame cab.[^3] In its 1931 annual report, the Klamath FPA said it had built a road up Mule Hill and moved the warden there from Hayden Station.[^2] An Oregon lookouts history calls Mule Hill a secondary lookout station.[^2]

The register dates the abandonment to 1950 and says the cab has since fallen down.[^1] The other listing puts the abandonment in the 1950s and describes ruins.[^3] The two sources also disagree on elevation: the register gives 2,855 feet, while firelookout.com gives 5,149 feet.[^1][^3]

One history page says a few boards are still scattered on the site.[^2] The boards remark is undated, and no source says whether the site is open to visitors.
