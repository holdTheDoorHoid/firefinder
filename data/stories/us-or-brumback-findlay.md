The Brumback (Findlay) lookout was a cabin in Wallowa County, in northeast Oregon, at 5,486 feet.[^1] Firelookout.com places it 18 miles northeast of Enterprise.[^2]

The register lists the Oregon Department of Forestry as the administrator of the site.[^1] Both the register and firelookout.com date the cabin to 1941.[^1][^2]

The cabin is gone.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list files the site under Ground and marks it Gone.[^3]

The register added the site on July 27, 2011, on the nomination of Ray Kresek, and numbers it US 1111, OR 467. It shows a panoramic view of the site, courtesy of the National Archives Pacific Northwest Region.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
