The Forest Lookouts listing for Odena places the tower in Gulf County and quotes a Panama City Herald article from August 1967.[^1]

The quoted article reported that a new tower at Odena was close to completion, on U.S. Highway 98 to the south of Port St. Joe.[^1] It was 80 feet (24 m) tall, with a steel house perched on top.[^1] The article said Florida Forestry employees would use the house to keep watch for smoke.[^1] It also said the new tower would replace the current tower near 11-Mile.[^1]

No source I could reach describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can get near it.[^1]

[^1]: Odena #2 - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts website, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/odena-2.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
