Dairy Mountain lookout stood in the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1] It sat north-northeast of Riggins, Idaho.[^2] A 75-foot crow's nest went up in 1927, and a log cabin was added in 1929.[^1]

In 1945 a pole tower went up on the mountain.[^1] The sources disagree on its size. Two sites describe a 40-foot tower with an L-4 cab,[^1][^3] while the Forest Service list gives a 50-foot L-6 tower with a gable roof.[^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists one known staff member, Ted Oliver, for 1927.[^3]

The tower was destroyed in 1960.[^1][^2][^3]

Today the Idaho Fire Lookouts page labels the site "Drive-Up," even though the tower is gone.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the tower as gone.[^4] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, or whether the public can visit it.
