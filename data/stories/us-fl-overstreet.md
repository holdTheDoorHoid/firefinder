The Overstreet Fire Tower stood in Gulf County, Florida.[^1] The Florida Forestry Service administered it.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site reprints a June 1988 item that it credits to the Panama City News-Herald, describing Verna Marshall at work in the Overstreet tower.[^2] By then she had worked as a fire lookout for 28 years.[^2] She gave district headquarters the location and size of a fire at an early stage, and she said that on a clear day she could see 15 to 20 miles around her.[^2]

Hurricane Michael toppled the tower in 2018.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register recorded the site on November 16, 2018, on the nomination of Brian Powell.[^1] The register page credits Bill Fauth with a picture of the tower after the storm.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.[^1]
