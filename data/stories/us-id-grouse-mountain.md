Grouse Mountain, in the Kaniksu National Forest of Bonner County, Idaho, had a lookout by 1914.[^1][^3] That August a newspaper item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, described a lightning-caused fire that the lookout reported to the forest office.[^3] A December 1916 report by the chief warden describes a trail about six miles long cut to the summit, with a telephone line joining the government line already there.[^3]

Three sources give a ten-by-ten-foot frame cabin dated 1921.[^1][^2][^3] Frank Hanson is named as a lookout man in June 1921.[^2][^3] Gilbert Nelson is listed as lookout personnel in 1933.[^1][^2] The sources do not say whether the 1921 cabin replaced the structure in use in 1914.

In September 1928 a Northern Idaho News item said both the warden and the lookout were taken off on a Friday.[^3]

Firelookout.com marks the lookout R.I.P. from 1921, leaves the end date open, and says it is not clear when the cabin was destroyed.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts calls the cabin destroyed, while the Forest Lookouts page marks it removed.[^2][^3] The sources disagree on how it ended, and none gives a date.

The sources read do not describe what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can get there.
