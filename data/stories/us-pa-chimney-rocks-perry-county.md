Chimney Rocks is a former fire lookout site in Perry County, Pennsylvania.[^1] The register calls it a bald tower and says it was used from 1909 to about 1917.[^1] It is no longer standing.[^1]

A 1908–09 Pennsylvania Department of Forestry report, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, describes how rangers could see nearly all of their divisions from such points and locate any fire that might start.[^2] It names a Chimney Rock on the south side of Bower Mountain as used for the same purpose by a ranger who lived in Henry Valley.[^2]

The register says Chimney Rocks and the report says Chimney Rock, but the Forest Lookouts page files the report under its Perry County entry, so this story treats them as one site.[^1][^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
