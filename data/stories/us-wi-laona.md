The Laona Lookout Tower stands in Forest County, Wisconsin. The Forest Lookouts site files it under the county and labels the land as National Forest, giving the location as township 36, range 15 East, section 20.[^1] The Wisconsin Historical Society gives the address as Forest Road 2732.[^2]

The tower was built in 1932 as a steel structure.[^1] The Historical Society's record gives the same year, calls the building the Laona Fire Lookout Tower, and lists its historic use as an observation and fire tower.[^2] The sources we read do not name who built it, who staffed it, or any fire it watched.

Its later history is thin. The Historical Society's record shows survey dates of 1996 and 2024, so the building was still on its books as recently as 2024.[^2] The Historical Society lists no National or State Register listing for it.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list shows the tower as standing, with no register number attached.[^3]

Today the tower is listed as standing.[^3] We found no statement about its current condition or any recent photo, and no source that describes public access. Before going near it, ask the national forest office whether the tower is open to visitors. Do not climb it unless that is clearly allowed.
