The Fitzgerald Fire Tower is recorded as a steel tower at Mackey, Ontario.[^1] The Firelookout.ca register gives the site an elevation of 415 m (1362 ft).[^1] The same record says the tower appears on the Department of Lands and Forests Tower Detection System Map of 1963.[^1]

Most of the towers in Ontario were erected between the 1920s and the 1950s, a period the Ontario Fire Tower Lookouts site calls the peak years of forest fire protection.[^2] Those towers fell under the authority of the Department of Lands and Forests, which became the Ministry of Natural Resources in 1972.[^2] The site's towermen pages say towers were sited to give a good view between each tower and the next, and that planners usually picked high ground.[^3]

No source describes what the site looks like today, or whether visitors can reach it.
