Mount Nebo is about 16 miles northeast of Springfield in Lane County.[^2][^1] A 1933 field report by Samuel L. Miller recommended a tower of at least 75 feet, because the peak was a long ridge with no distinct top.[^3] By June 1934 a road reached the summit and a lookout house had been finished there, and members of the Wendling Civilian Conservation Corps camp had built a 79-foot tower.[^3] The register and firelookout.com give 87 feet instead, for an L-6 tower with a frame cabin.[^1][^2]

A lookout was still on the post in 1942.[^3] That September the Eugene Guard reported that Donald McDonald of Marcola would keep serving at the lookout after another lookout, Ronald Byers, was transferred to Castle Rock.[^3] By 1956 the observation cab had been judged unsafe, and materials were bought to replace it.[^3]

The register and firelookout.com say the tower was gone by 1970.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts history page gives 1971 as the year the lookout was removed.[^3] That page names the Eastern Lane district of the Oregon Department of Forestry as the agency, while the register lists the Eastern Lane FPA as administrator.[^3][^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, and none says whether the public can reach it.[^3]
