The North Carolina lookout tower list includes an entry called Caswell (Yanceyville), placed at 36.42027, -79.33694.[^1] By my calculation from the two published coordinates, that point sits about 38 metres from the Caswell County office of the N.C. Forest Service, which FDmaps lists at 973 Fire Tower Rd.[^1][^2] The office is where State Roads 1572 and 1589 meet, roughly a mile north of the Yanceyville courthouse.[^3]

The sources I could open place the Forest Service office in that area today.[^2][^3] No source I could open describes a tower standing or remaining there.

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Caswell County, FDmaps.com, https://fdmaps.com/caswell-county/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: N.C. Forest Service Contacts - Caswell County, NC Agriculture, https://www.ncagr.gov/divisions/nc-forest-service/contacts/caswell (accessed 2026-10-21).
