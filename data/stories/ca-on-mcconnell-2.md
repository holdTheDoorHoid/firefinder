Land Information Ontario's open data classes the McConnell 2 Tower as a fire tower.[^1] Its point is at 46.776909, -81.706604, and the entry gives the location accuracy as within 50 metres (164 ft).[^1] The same data also holds a McConnell Fire Tower, a separate entry at its own position.[^1] The McConnell 2 entry is flagged as verified, and its location is noted as coming from a dataset the province labels AFFM.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today.
