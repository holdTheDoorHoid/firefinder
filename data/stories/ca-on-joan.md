The Joan Fire Tower was a steel lookout on an island in Nipissing District, in northeastern Ontario.[^1] The register gives its elevation as 364 m (1194 ft).[^1] The register counts it as one of only two Ontario lookouts on an island.[^1]

The register says the tower is absent from Sudbury topographic map 41-I. It also says a 30.5 m (100 ft) steel tower appears at the same spot on a 1963 Department of Lands and Forests Tower Detection System Map.[^1]

A former towerman, Finlay Gillies, is recorded as manning the tower between 1931 and 1947, and possibly longer.[^1] The register says he was profiled in Sylva Magazine, Volume 3, Number 5.[^1]

The register says a tornado destroyed the tower in 1969.[^1] No second source for that date has turned up.[^1] The register also says the tower may be the same as the Bear Island Fire Tower.[^1]

The register does not say what remains at the site today, or whether the island is open to visitors.[^1]
