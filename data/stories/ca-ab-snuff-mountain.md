Snuff Mountain is a wildfire lookout site in Alberta.[^1] An online directory of the province's lookouts lists it by name, and its own entry gives its division as Grande Prairie Division.[^1][^2]

Alberta calls its fire lookouts a key part of wildfire detection and says they are sited where the view is good enough to spot and report fires.[^3] The province counts 127 wildfire lookout sites, spread across the boreal forest and along the Rocky Mountains.[^4] The province says every site has a lookout observer on duty, usually from April to October, keeping watch for fire through the day.[^4]

The province describes lookout sites as isolated and says staff must prepare for a wide range of situations, including a curious bear.[^4]

No source we read describes what stands at the Snuff Mountain site today, or whether the public may visit. Ask Alberta Wildfire before going, and do not climb or enter any structure without permission.
