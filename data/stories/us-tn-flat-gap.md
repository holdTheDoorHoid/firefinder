This tower goes by two names. The Tennessee Land Forms list calls it Jefferson City and places it in Jefferson County, while OpenStreetMap labels the same spot the Flat Gap Firetower.[^1][^2]

The Land Forms list gives its state number as 1-7, taken from a 1976 list of state forest towers, and marks the tower as standing.[^1] The same list gives no build date and no history for it.[^1] The state fire-tower nomination lists Jefferson City, in Jefferson County, in its table of towers still standing as of March 26, 2014, and we found no other mention of it there.[^3] OpenStreetMap maps it as a lattice tower of the observation type.[^2]

Do not confuse this tower with another Flat Gap site. The Mooresburg (Flat Gap) Lookout in Hawkins County is listed on the National Historic Lookout Register.[^4] That register says it was built in 1937 and was first known as the Flat Gap tower.[^4] It is a different tower, and it now carries the Mooresburg name.[^4]

We found no record of staffing, notable fires or changes to this tower.[^1][^2][^3]

The Land Forms page says that many Tennessee towers have been removed and that most sites are now private property. It marks some towers as standing and others as removed, but it does not date those marks.[^1] We found no visit report or access rule for this tower, so we cannot say whether the public may go up it.[^1] Anyone interested should ask the landowner before going near it.[^1][^2]
