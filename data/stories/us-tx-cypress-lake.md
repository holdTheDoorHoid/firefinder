Cypress Lake was a fire lookout in Angelina County. Its site sat 5.3 miles southeast of Diboll and three miles north of the Neches River.[^1] A federal survey description written in 1949 calls it Cypress Lake Lookout Tower No. 15. The description gives a steel tower roughly 110 feet tall, topped by a glass cab.[^1]

The survey page names the Texas Forest Service as the agency.[^1] Because the 1949 description already covers the tower, it was standing by that year.[^1]

A Texas lookout list also carries the site under Angelina County, tagged TFS.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas index marks the tower as gone.[^3] No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
