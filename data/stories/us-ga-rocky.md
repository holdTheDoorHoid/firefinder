Rocky is listed in Whitfield County, Georgia.[^1] It appears on the same numbered list of Georgia lookout towers kept on the tnlandforms.us website, which gives each entry a name, coordinates, county and elevation.[^1] The entry places the site at 34.82400, -85.00779, with an elevation of 1,613 and no unit printed (1,613 feet would be about 492 m).[^1] It also carries a datasheet reference, EE1967, which the page does not explain.[^1] The map on that page was last marked as revised on 22 March 2016.[^1]
