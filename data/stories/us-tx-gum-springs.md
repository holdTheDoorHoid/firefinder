The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list places a Gum Springs lookout in Sabine County, at 31.19167 north and 93.90167 west. It gives the structure type as unknown and the status as gone.[^1]

The association's expanded Texas inventory gives about 1936 as the date and about 1938 as the removal date. It lists the manufacturer and height as unknown.[^2]

The Harry S. Truman Library holds a photograph taken from a Gum Springs Tower in the Winn Ranger District of Kisatchie National Forest, Louisiana.[^3] That tower is a different site, and this story covers only the Sabine County site in Texas.

Today the list marks the Texas site as gone. No source describes what, if anything, remains there.[^1]

[^1]: Texas Lookouts (By County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/tx-co/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Texas lookout inventory, expanded list (PDF), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/TX-expanded.pdf (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Kisatchie National Forest, Louisiana (accession 2009-3046), Harry S. Truman Library, https://trumanlibrary.gov/photograph-records/2009-3046 (accessed 2026-10-21).
