The Spider Lake fire tower stood in Cass County, in the southern area of Foot Hills State Forest.[^2] The county list gives it the number 52.[^2]

The tower went up in 1928.[^2] A Brainerd Daily Dispatch report dated December 29, 1928, quoted by Forest Lookouts, says an 81-foot tower was set up at Spider Lake, west of Pine River.[^1] The county list calls it an 81-foot Aermotor ladder tower erected that year, and gives the owner as the Minnesota Forest Service.[^2]

The sources do not agree on the structure. A 1981 survey note in the county list describes a steel truss tower, about 100 feet high, with a steel cabin on top.[^2] The list does not say whether the tower was rebuilt, or whether that survey describes the same structure as the 1928 tower.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^3] We found no source that describes what remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it.
