A North Carolina forest lookouts list places a Hope Mills lookout in Cumberland County, in an entry credited to the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources.[^1] A separate list of North Carolina lookout towers gives a tower named Hope Mills, at coordinates 34.9725, -78.92583, with the code EB2543.[^2]

No source we could reach describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything remains there or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: North Carolina - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/north-carolina.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
