The Arp lookout tower stands in Smith County, and the Texas Forest Service is named as its agency.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list places it at 32.24016 and -95.02506 and shows the tower as standing.[^2]

The Arp page is short. Beyond the county and the agency, it says nothing about the tower.[^1] Nothing read says when the tower went up, what it is built from, who staffed it or which fires it watched. Nothing read says whether the public may visit it, so check with the Texas Forest Service before planning a trip.
