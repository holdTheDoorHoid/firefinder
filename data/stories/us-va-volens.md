Volens is listed as a tower in Halifax County, Virginia.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list marks it as gone and gives its position as latitude 36.93266 and longitude -79.01329.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts Virginia page also includes an entry for Volens, placed in Halifax County.[^2]

The same Association list has other Halifax County entries, among them Halifax and South Boston, each at its own coordinates. Do not mix them up with Volens.[^1]

Nothing in the sources read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors. Check who owns the land before going to look for it.

[^1]: Virginia fire lookouts list, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Virginia - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/virginia.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
