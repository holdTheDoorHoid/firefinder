Mount Timpanogos has a steel lookout house on its summit, built by the U.S. Forest Service in 1927, and the building still stands.[^1][^3]

A Pleasant Grove club began a campaign for a summit monument in 1922.[^2] The plan grew into a glass-windowed lookout house. The Forest Service joined in, led by ranger Vivian N. West.[^2] West gave the contract to Builders Steel and Iron Co. of Salt Lake City, which fabricated a galvanized steel structure.[^2] Burros carried the window panes up the mountain in wooden boxes.[^2] By late September 1927 the station was complete.[^1]

Sources disagree on the height. A 1927 report gives 12,008 feet, while later accounts put the summit near 11,750 feet.[^1][^2][^3] Inside, a viewfinder had brass pointers set to name the peaks, cities and other sights.[^2]

The 2026 Tribune calls it an observation station.[^3] A ranger at Timpanogos Cave National Monument, who lived in American Fork Canyon in the 1940s and 1950s, recalled that the hut was used for weather observations and had mirror signaling devices.[^2] For decades the hut was also a rest stop on a yearly July group hike to the summit.[^3]

Over the years, people carried off the markers as keepsakes, and strong gusts knocked out many of the windows.[^3] Vandalism over roughly 75 years cost the hut its windows and other equipment.[^2] Only the stone base that once held the viewfinder is left.[^2] In 1980, Utah State Prison inmates climbed up and repainted the building in aluminum, which hid years of graffiti.[^3] Later, a large pyramid-shaped cap went on top as a survey marker.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Utah list still marks the site as gone, but both the 2003 and 2026 reports describe the hut standing.[^4][^2][^3] Each year, tens of thousands of hikers reach the summit on foot. The most popular routes start at Aspen Grove or Timpooneke Campground.[^2]

In September 2026, a group of hikers caught in a fierce thunderstorm near the summit sheltered inside the hut.[^3] Lightning hit the steel shelter, and a 19-year-old from the group was killed.[^3] Nine others in the group had minor injuries.[^3] Check the forecast before you start up.
