Hells Knob's site sits at 1,856 feet (566 meters) in Mingo County, West Virginia, where a fire lookout once stood.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register lists it as US 1678, WV 69, with a registration date of January 26, 2017, on the nomination of Brian M. Powell.[^1] The register lists the tower as gone.[^2]

A Division of Forestry article puts the number of West Virginia fire towers at 70 to 100, depending on the source.[^3] The same article says the division relied on temporary seasonal employees, and quotes a deputy state fire manager saying each tower would be staffed during fire season or other periods of high danger.[^3] That account describes the state's towers in general rather than this one.

No source we found describes the site today, including whether any part of the structure remains or whether the site is open to the public.
