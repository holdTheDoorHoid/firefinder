Baldwin Run sits in Tioga County, inside Tioga State Forest.[^1] The site had a wooden tower from 1899, when the U.S. Geological Survey erected it, and survey engineers used that tower in their work on the region.[^1]

In 1919 a newspaper item said the wooden tower would be repaired until a larger appropriation allowed a steel one.[^1] In 1921 the forestry department called the wooden tower unsafe and ordered a new 47-foot steel observation tower in its place.[^1] By October that tower had been erected.[^1] The register describes it as a steel Aermotor tower built in 1921 that replaced a USGS tower.[^2] A 1939 newspaper item gives the height as 50 feet, so the sources disagree on the height.[^3]

A fire warden, Frank Davis, Jr., spent the fire season at the tower in 1921, and the tower had a telephone connection.[^1] In October 1922 a fire on the Baldwin Run mountains was spotted by the tower's watcher. Volunteers, including high school students, had the blaze contained within hours of arriving.[^1] A 1949 list named Glen Fuller as the towerman on Baldwin Tower.[^1]

In 1935 the tract around the tower was improved, and fireplaces and picnic tables were put in at the site for public use.[^1] The register says the tower was removed about 1964.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today. A 1933 federal survey description, copied on the lookout page, puts a survey mark on state land at the tower.[^1] No source we found says whether the site is open to visitors now.
