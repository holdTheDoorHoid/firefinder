Hilltop was a crow's nest and camp in the Bitterroot National Forest, about 10 miles north-northeast of Lost Trail Pass in Ravalli County.[^1][^3]

The firelookout.com page lists a crow's nest and camp under 1941.[^1] It says the site was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list also marks Hilltop as abandoned.[^2]

The firelookout.com page adds "Still there," without saying what that means.[^1] The two sources describe the site differently. The Association's list says abandoned, which does not say whether anything stands, and neither source calls the site gone.[^1][^2] Do not assume a structure stands there.

No source describes what remains at the site today, and none says whether the public may visit it. Check with the Bitterroot National Forest before going.
