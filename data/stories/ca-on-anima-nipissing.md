The Anima-Nipissing fire tower is recorded on Anima Nipissing Road in unorganized West Timiskaming, in the Timiskaming District of northeastern Ontario, at a listed elevation of 437 m (1,434 ft).[^1] Firelookout.ca also keeps a separate record for a Brigstocke fire tower, about 360 m away by the two listed coordinates and at 394 m (1,293 ft).[^1][^2] That record describes a steel tower with an octagonal cupola.[^2] The Canada Lookouts Ontario index lists the Brigstocke name beside the place name "Anima Nipissing Lake Latchford."[^3] The sources do not show whether the two names describe one structure or two.

Firelookout.ca says the Brigstocke tower was proposed in 1985 as a repeater station, but that plan was never carried out.[^2] The same record notes that it appears on the Ville Marie topographic map, sheet 31M, third edition, from 1995.[^2]

The Ontario Trails Council lists a 1.5 km groomed trail that leads up to the tower.[^4] To find the start, park at the third turn-around on the last road to the left before the government dock on Anima Nipissing Lake and look for a red blaze.[^4] The listing describes the tower as a photo stop with views of Maple Mountain and Rib Mountain, and puts the hike at about 20 to 30 minutes.[^4]

The council says it does not own or manage the trails it lists.[^4] The listing does not say whether the tower can be climbed, so I cannot confirm what stands on the site now.[^4]
