Foley Butte Lookout sits in Crook County, about 15 miles from Prineville, toward the north.[^2] The Oregon Department of Forestry administers it.[^1] The first lookout on the site was a crow's nest, which went into service in 1932.[^1][^2]

Two years later a 60-foot wooden tower replaced the crow's nest. The register describes it as having a cab, and firelookout.com describes it as having a ground cabin, and dates the tower to 1934.[^1][^2] In 1953 the Oregon Department of Forestry put up the present tower, with a live-in cab and catwalk.[^1][^2]

The sources disagree on the height of the 1953 tower. The register gives 40 feet, laminated wood, while firelookout.com gives 50 feet, wooden.[^1][^2] This story does not choose between them.

firelookout.com says summer staffing continued into the 2010s.[^2] The register lists it as registered on January 8, 2003, and says the tower remains in active service.[^1]

firelookout.com says the tower is on private land, and that it is closed to the public.[^2] Treat it as private. This story does not suggest a visit or climbing.
