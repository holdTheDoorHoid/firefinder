Peletier is a lookout tower in Carteret County, North Carolina.[^1] The tnlandforms.us list of North Carolina lookout towers gives its coordinates as 34.74972, -77.09333.[^1] The Forest Lookouts index for North Carolina pairs the Peletier entry with Carteret County and the Croatan National Forest.[^2]

The community of Peletier sits in western Carteret County, with Croatan National Forest to its north and east.[^3] North Carolina Highway 58 runs through the community.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the tower today, or whether the public may visit the site.
