Avery Hill sits about 2 miles northwest of Avery in Shoshone County, at 4,421 feet, in the St. Joe National Forest.[^1]

A tower with a 30-foot pole and an L-4 cab went up around 1939.[^1][^3] A patrol lookout also stood on Trego Point, 1.5 miles to the northwest.[^1]

An earlier mention may point to an older lookout. A July 25, 1925 newspaper report, quoted by the Forest Lookouts site, says the forest lookout at Avery reported a lightning-caused fire near the Bullion mine.[^3] The same report adds that the storm later disrupted wire communication with the district.[^3] Whether that lookout was the same structure as the later tower is not clear from these listings.

The tower was destroyed in 1956.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as drive-up.[^2] None of the sources we read describes what remains on the site today, or whether visitors may go there.
