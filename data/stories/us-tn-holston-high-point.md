Holston High Point is the highest summit of Holston Mountain, a long ridge in the Upper East Tennessee region.[^1] Wikipedia gives its elevation as 4,280 feet, while the state lookout list gives 4,308 feet.[^1][^3]

By 1932 a fire lookout tower had been erected near a survey station, on property leased to the state forestry department.[^2] The survey record puts the mark on the highest part of the western of Holston Mountain's two peaks, roughly eight miles northeast of Elizabethton.[^2]

The state list's Holston High Point entry cites the same survey mark and marks a Cherokee National Forest tower removed.[^3] Wikipedia instead places an old fire tower on Holston High Knob, the fourth-highest point, so the two sites are easy to confuse.[^1]

Today, Wikipedia describes television towers grouped in an antenna farm on the high point's southwest flank.[^1] The sources we read do not describe any lookout structure or footings at the summit today.[^1][^2][^3]

Panhandle Road climbs the mountain for three miles from State Highway 91, and Wikipedia says an iron gate closes it during heavy snow and ice, hunting season, or nesting season.[^1]
