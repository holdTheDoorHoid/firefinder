Lynchburg Lookout Tower stood in Laclede County, in the Mark Twain National Forest.[^1] That forest was established in 1939.[^2]

A Houston Herald report from January 1937, quoted on a page about the Gulf tower, names the Lynchburg lookout beside the Roby lookout as one of the towers that observed parts of the Roubidoux District.[^3] The same report describes a new 100-foot steel tower, finished by a crew from the Lynchburg CCC camp for the Roubidoux District, which the report places in the Gardner National Forest. That was a separate lookout, the Gulf tower.[^3]

A 1939 survey station description by the Coast and Geodetic Survey, quoted on the Lynchburg page, describes the lookout as 100 feet tall, built of steel, with an observation platform enclosed in glass on top.[^1]

The lookout history page marks the tower as removed, with no date or method given.[^1] The research found no source that says what, if anything, remains at the site today or whether the public can visit it.
