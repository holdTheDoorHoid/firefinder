In August 1931 a Jackson County newspaper reported that a district forester had gone to Shoals to oversee the erection of a tower, calling it the state's sixth.[^1] That item may concern the nearby Willow Valley tower instead, because some sources date Willow Valley to 1931 and others to 1933.[^2][^7]

A Forest Service history page lists a Shoals tower in Martin County. It records a plan approved on July 30, 1937, for a 100-foot steel tower, with a dwelling, garage, latrine and cistern.[^3] By 1946 a Coast and Geodetic Survey crew described a four-leg steel lookout tower, 108 feet high, about three miles south-southeast of Shoals on Forest Service land.[^4] The same survey mentions a rangers' house close by.[^5] The plan's 100 feet and the survey's 108 feet do not agree, and the sources do not settle when a tower first stood here.[^3][^4]

The name is also disputed.[^2][^3] A state tower list reproduced by the Forest Service calls it the Lacy tower, near Shoals.[^3] The Indiana guide keeps the name Shoals, since older topographic maps and survey records use it.[^2]

A 1967 survey update says the ranger house had been removed and the lookout tower was no longer in use.[^5] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's database lists the site as gone.[^6]

The guide's author drove there in 2023, taking a gravel road south of Lacy that ended in a loop, where the author saw what was probably a house.[^2] The tower's exact spot could not be pinned down, and the author did not look for base remains, though they believed they were within about 100 feet of it.[^2] The author was also unsure how private the road was.[^2] No source we found describes what remains at the site today.
