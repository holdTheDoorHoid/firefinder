The Piehl tower stood next to Highway 45, near Margaret Lake, in Oneida County, Wisconsin.[^1] A May 24, 1949 item from the Rhinelander Daily News, quoted on the Central US Lookouts page, said the tower then had only a platform.[^1] The same item said a cab was to be built on top so that a fire-watcher could stay on duty there full time.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list records the tower as gone.[^2] OpenStreetMap gives the site an elevation of about 525 metres.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
