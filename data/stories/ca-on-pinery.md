Pinery Provincial Park lies on the southeast shore of Lake Huron in Bosanquet Township, Lambton County, about 6 kilometres south of Grand Bend.[^2] Firelookout.ca says that while the Pinery Fire Tower was active, it could be seen from the park's main gate.[^1]

Most of Ontario's fire towers were built between the 1920s and the 1950s, during the peak years of forest fire protection.[^3] From the 1960s on, aerial spotting and firefighting crews took over, and the towers saw less use.[^3] A fan-run history site says that, as of 2013, the Ontario Ministry of Natural Resources had decided to remove many standing towers. It does not name the Pinery tower.[^3]

After the tower closed, its cab came off, but the structure still stands.[^1] The Firelookout.ca entry does not say who manages the tower or whether it is open to visitors, so do not climb it.[^1]
