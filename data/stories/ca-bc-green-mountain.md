Green Mountain Lookout is a standard British Columbia Forest Service ground cab in Squamish-Lillooet, British Columbia, at an elevation of 2,025 m (6,644 ft).[^1] The register dates it only to the 1950s and describes its views over a remote part of the province as outstanding.[^1] The British Columbia Forest Service is named as the administrator.[^1]

OpenStreetMap, a volunteer-edited map, tags the site as a fire lookout marked "disused:emergency", which suggests it is no longer used for emergency work.[^2]

The register was added on January 2, 2026, on a nomination by Jake Lockwood.[^1]

No source we read describes the site today, so its condition and whether the public can reach it are not known from our reading. Check with the British Columbia Forest Service before planning a visit.

[^2]: Green Mountain Fire Lookout, OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/981906903 (accessed 2026-10-20).
