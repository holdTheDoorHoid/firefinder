The Friendship site in Claiborne County is a survey marker at a fire lookout station.[^1] Its datasheet calls the site the Friendship Fire Lookout Station and adds that it is not a tower.[^1]

The Tennessee Highway Department set the marker in 1964 and described it that year.[^1] Its directions end at the lookout station on top of an open knoll.[^1] The description places the mark beside a lookout building painted green.[^1]

Tennessee Landforms lists the spot at 1,700 feet (518 m) in Claiborne County, under the same survey number.[^2] The 2014 National Register form's list of extant Tennessee fire towers names Springdale in Claiborne County but does not include Friendship.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach the knoll.
