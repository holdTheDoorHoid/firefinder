The Rail Prairie lookout in Morrison County was an 81-foot tower, dated 1930 and owned by the Minnesota Forest Service.[^1]

The Cass County list ties the removal of the Rail Prairie tower to a northward expansion of Camp Ripley.[^2] The Morrison County list says the Rail Prairie tower no longer stands, and it also calls the lookout the Rail Mountain lookout.[^1]

The tower was moved to a site about two miles northeast of Motley, in Cass County.[^1][^2] The Cass County list gives 1952 or 1954 for the move, while the Morrison County list gives 1954.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Motley tower as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the Motley site today or whether the public can reach it.
