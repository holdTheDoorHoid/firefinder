The Saddle Mountain Lookout Site is in Tillamook County, Oregon, at 3,475 feet (1,059 meters).[^1][^2] The Tillamook FPA administers the site.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register dates the construction of the platform and cabin to 1922.[^1] The firelookout.com listing gives the same year and the same two structures.[^2]

The register and the firelookout.com listing agree that the platform and cabin were gone by 1953.[^1][^2] They differ on the word. The register says the structures were removed that year, while firelookout.com says they were destroyed.[^1][^2] Where the sources disagree, we report both.

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit the summit. Anyone planning a trip should ask the administering agency, the Tillamook FPA, before going.

[^1]: Saddle Mountain Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/or/saddle-mountain-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Saddle Mtn. Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/or/saddlemtn45.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
