Shefoot Mountain sits in the St. Joe National Forest, about seven miles northeast of Avery in Shoshone County, Idaho.[^1][^2]

Joe Coddington staffed the summit in 1927 with a tent and a mapboard, and the source says he may have staffed it for several years.[^1]

An L-4 cab went up on the west summit in 1931.[^1][^2] The two sources disagree on how far east the patrol point lies. Firelookout.com puts it 1.25 miles east, atop Turkey Point, while Idaho Fire Lookouts puts Turkey Point 1 1/2 miles east.[^1][^2] The lookout was destroyed in 1961.[^2]

Today Idaho Fire Lookouts reports that no remnants of the lookout could be found.[^1] The only things it lists as left are carvings in the rocks and the view.[^1] The page describes the spot as one often visited by off-roaders, and it suggests visitors may have carried off or burned whatever was left.[^1] It also files the site as drive-up, which means a road reaches it.[^1] Nothing we read says whether the site is open to visitors.
