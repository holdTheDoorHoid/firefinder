Fullhart Knob is the eastern peak of Fullhart Mountain, in Botetourt County, Virginia, close to the Appalachian Trail.[^1][^3] Some sources spell the name Fullhardt.[^1][^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower here as gone.[^2] A visitor's account reports footings of a former fire tower in the shelter area.[^1] The same writer found almost nothing about the lookout's history, and wondered whether one was ever built, because the footings looked so new.[^1]

The summit now holds a trail shelter.[^1] The Roanoke Appalachian Trail Club built the shelter in 1965.[^1][^3] A hiker's blog places the shelter where a former fire tower stood.[^4]

A short side trail leads from the trail to the shelter, about 0.10 miles, with little climbing.[^1][^3] The shelter uses a rain cistern, which the club's listing calls the last of its kind on the Appalachian Trail.[^3]

Beyond the reported footings, no source describes what remains of a lookout on the site today.[^1][^2]
