The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list places a tower called Vicksville (Southampton) in Southampton County, at 36.81042, -77.02613, and marks it gone.[^1]

The same list records a Capron tower that was moved to the Southampton Ag & Forestry Center, where it is listed as standing.[^1] It has its own row and coordinates, separate from the Vicksville entry.[^1]

A Weebly Virginia list also gives a Vicksville in Southampton County.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Vicksville site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Virginia - Forest Fire Lookout Association, firelookout.org, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Forest Lookouts – Virginia, easternuslookouts.weebly.com, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/virginia.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
