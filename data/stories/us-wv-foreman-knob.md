Foreman Knob is in Kanawha County, West Virginia, at an elevation of 1,277 feet (389 m).[^1] The West Virginia Division of Forestry is the agency listed for the site.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register entered it on January 26, 2017, on a nomination by Brian M. Powell.[^1]

A 2022 Charleston Gazette-Mail column lists four former fire towers in the county: Bee Mountain, Foreman Knob, Mt. Desert and Johnson Knob.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's state list marks the Foreman Knob tower as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the knob today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.[^1]
