The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list places a tower called Valley Ridge in Tazewell County, at 37.23017, -81.47444, and marks it gone.[^1] It sits in the list's main table of lookout structures, not in its section for other or unknown structures.[^1]

A second Virginia list, the Forest Lookouts page on Weebly, also names a Valley Ridge in Tazewell County.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Valley Ridge site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Virginia - Forest Fire Lookout Association, firelookout.org, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Forest Lookouts – Virginia, easternuslookouts.weebly.com, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/virginia.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
