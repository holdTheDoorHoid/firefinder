South Fire Tower stood in the Ocala National Forest, in Marion County, Florida.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register counts it as one of eight towers that served that forest.[^1] The register adds that many of those towers were made of wood and have since been taken down.[^1]

According to the register, only the footings of its legs survive today.[^1] A photo of a footing, credited to Edward Seifert, appears on the register page.[^1]

Edward Seifert nominated the site, which was added to the register on March 24, 2020.[^1] The register lists the U.S. Forest Service as the agency that administers it.[^1]

The register does not say whether the footings are open to visitors.[^1] Please check with the Forest Service before going to look for them.
