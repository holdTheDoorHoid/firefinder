The Mission Hill fire tower is in Chippewa County, Michigan, in the Hiawatha National Forest.[^1] A trail guide places the Mission Hill Trail near Brimley and describes it as a 3.6-mile out-and-back trail along Spectacle Lake and Monocle Lake.[^2]

In May 1940, a newspaper reported that a man from Brimley had returned to the Mission Hill tower, which he had staffed for several years.[^1]

In July 1948, a newspaper reported that a bear had killed the three-year-old daughter of a forest ranger.[^1] The family's cabin stood next to the Mission Hill tower, and the report says her father was a towerman at the station.[^1] The 1948 report places the station in the "isolated Marquette National Forest"; the listing gives the Hiawatha National Forest.[^1]

An undated trail app describes the Mission Hill Lookout Tower as the highest point in the area, and it lists the West Tower Road route for high-clearance vehicles.[^3] The trail guide says the road leads to an overlook and trailhead.[^2] Neither page says whether the tower is open to climbing or what condition it is in.[^2][^3]
