Eakin Point is a former lookout in the Bitterroot National Forest, in Idaho County, at about 8,170 feet.[^1][^2] Records date a platform tower and a log cabin there to the 1930s, with the tower placed one mile south of the summit.[^1][^2][^3]

A 1973 entry on the Forest Lookouts site disagrees. It quotes a draft Bitterroot National Forest history by O.F. Schumaker, which describes the point as a tent camp throughout with no building, and says it was abandoned before 1940.[^3] The other entries say the lookout was abandoned in the 1960s.[^1][^2][^3]

The same 1973 entry is the only description we found of the ground itself. It also says the site had been cleared and that lightning protection was set up on poles.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association index lists the platform as gone.[^4] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page labels the site as a hike-in.[^2]
