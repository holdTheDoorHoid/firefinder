Sale Creek was a fire lookout tower in Hamilton County, Tennessee, run by the Tennessee Division of Forestry.[^1][^2] Tennessee Landforms gives it as tower 11 in district 3 on the state's 1976 list.[^1]

The Division's national register form says it built 208 fire lookout towers between about 1930 and 1970.[^3] Its largest number, in the late 1960s, was 208 towers in operation.[^3] Towers went up on both public and private land.[^3] For private sites, the form describes a lease between the state and the owner, with terms of 40 to 99 years.[^3] When a lease ended, the owner could keep the tower or ask the Division to dismantle it.[^3] The form does not mention Sale Creek, so these lease terms describe towers statewide, not this one.[^3]

The 2014 list of towers still standing does not include Sale Creek.[^3] Both Tennessee Landforms and Forest Lookouts mark the tower removed.[^1][^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Hamilton County site, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2] Tennessee Landforms says many lookouts statewide have been removed, and most of their sites are now on private land. It does not say that about Sale Creek.[^1]
