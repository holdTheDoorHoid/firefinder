Thornton Lookout is a steel tower on the Hualapai Reservation, built in 1945.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register gives it as a 100-foot steel tower by Aermotor, of open X-brace construction, topped by a 7-by-7 cabin used only for watching.[^1] The register lists the Indian Emergency Civilian Work Program among its cooperators.[^1] New siding and steps went on in 1998.[^1]

The register says the lookout was still staffed as of 2011, the most recent staffing date I could find.[^1] It was added to the register on May 25, 2022, as US 1598, AZ 81, nominated by Brad Eells.[^1]

Sources disagree on the county. The register and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Arizona list both put the tower in Coconino County.[^1][^3] A Wikidata entry agrees, but its photo file is titled with Mohave County, near Frazier Wells.[^2]

Today the register lists the lookout as administered by the Bureau of Indian Affairs for the Hualapai Reservation.[^1] I found no public visitor information, trail, or listing for it, so whether visitors may approach the tower is unknown. Anyone interested should ask the tribe before going.
