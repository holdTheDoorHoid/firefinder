Mormon Mountain (G.S.) is a former lookout site in Wallowa County, Oregon, about 43 miles east of Wallowa, inside the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest.[^1][^2] The register gives the site's elevation as 5,847 feet, or 1,782 meters, and says the Forest Service administers it.[^1]

A 16-by-16-foot log cabin went up on the site in 1934, together with an 18-foot platform.[^1][^2]

Firelookout.com lists a run of named lookouts after that: Lawrence Potter in 1936 and 1941, Alva Keeler and Phillip Truman in 1942, Alva Keeler and David Reed in 1943, Jesse Earl in several seasons between 1949 and 1962, and Kerry Searles in 1967.[^2]

The register says the lookout was abandoned in 1959 and is gone.[^1] Firelookout.com instead says it was abandoned in the 1960s, and its list of lookouts runs to 1967.[^2] The two sources disagree on the date. Forest Lookouts marks the site as removed.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the site is open to visitors.
