The Forest Lookouts website lists a tower at Wilma in Liberty County, inside the Apalachicola National Forest.[^1] A 1936 Florida State Road Department map of Wilma marks a forest ranger lookout station, though it does not say whether that is this tower.[^2] The Wilma district of the forest is mostly in Liberty County, and Wilma is the headquarters of the ranger in charge of that district.[^3]

A 1967 Coast and Geodetic Survey description of the station records a ranger's statement that the tower had been in the same place since 1934.[^1] The same description rated the station as being in good condition.[^1]

The latest description on the listing is from 1967.[^1] The listing says nothing about whether the tower still stands or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1]
