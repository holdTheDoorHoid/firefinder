Round Top is a 2,982-foot peak in Washington County, Oregon, about 4.3 miles southwest of Timber.[^4] The first lookout here, dated to 1933, was a crow's-nest platform in a tree, with a cabin that housed the lookout.[^1] A 1941 survey description places a lookout house atop a 100-foot fir, which was topped and guyed.[^3] An August 1934 Forest Log item, though, describes work on a pack trail to a lookout still to be built, so the start date is not settled.[^3]

In 1946 a timber tower with a 14-by-14 cab replaced the first structures.[^1][^3] The sources disagree on its height.[^1][^2][^3] The FFLOS register and firelookout.com give 35 feet, while a June 1946 Forest Log item described a 50-foot tower under construction.[^1][^2][^3]

In July 1941 lightning knocked out the ranger at the station, Tom Seth, who recovered and finished his shift.[^3] The Oregon lookouts page also lists an activation on September 9, 1942, for the Portland Filter Center, without explaining the role.[^3] The last staffed season was 1965.[^3]

The FFLOS register says the tower was abandoned that year and removed in 1970.[^1] Wikipedia gives 1946 as the end of the tower and cabin, which conflicts with the 1965 end date.[^4] Firelookout.com says the tower is now gone.[^2] No source found describes the summit today or what, if anything, remains of the lookout.
