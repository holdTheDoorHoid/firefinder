The southern Crane tower is the only one that seems to have a name, and it goes by Indian Springs.[^1] Indiana Fire Towers says Indian Springs and Trinity Springs are both town names near it, and the author thinks both have been applied to this tower.[^1] The Register lists it as US 1838, IN 18 in Martin County, Indiana, with the U.S. Navy as administrator.[^2]

Indiana Fire Towers calls this tower Structure 44 and gives 1942 as its build year, citing a personal resource.[^1] Descriptions of two survey marks, JA1932 and JA1937, say the southern tower was already standing in 1946.[^1] The Navy site it stands on was originally established as a naval ammunition depot in 1941.[^3]

The sources I read do not say who staffed the tower or what fires it saw.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^4] A Forest Service page, last updated in May 2026, says the tower on JT21, which it numbers Fire Tower Three, has a repeater antenna on it.[^5] Indiana Fire Towers places the southern tower on JT21 and calls it Structure 44, the same number the Forest Service gives Fire Tower Three, so the two pages appear to describe one tower.[^1][^5]

The Crane towers sit on restricted federal Naval property.[^1] Indiana Fire Towers reports that the public cannot get in and that climbing them is out of the question.[^1] The Register recorded this tower on August 2, 2026.[^2]
