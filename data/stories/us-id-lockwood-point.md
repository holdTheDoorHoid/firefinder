Lockwood Point lookout was a log cabin on a minor point in Adams County, on the Payette National Forest, about 13 miles south-southwest of Riggins.[^1][^3] The cabin sat at the north end of the point, and Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the site's elevation as 6,960 feet.[^2]

The sources disagree on the year it was built. Two pages give 1933,[^1][^2] and the Forest Lookouts page gives 1932.[^3]

The only lookout we found named is Albert Sipe, listed for 1942.[^1][^2]

The sources also conflict on what became of the cabin. Firelookout.com says it was still standing in 1983.[^1] Forest Lookouts says the lookout was retired in 1966 and burned to dispose of it.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives 1999 as the year it burned.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks it gone.[^4]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the lookout as hike-in.[^2]
