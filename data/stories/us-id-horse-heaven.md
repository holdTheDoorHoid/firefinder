Horse Heaven's history starts with a log cabin in 1929. A 30-foot pole tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab followed in 1931.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The site sits at 8,086 feet (2,465 meters) in Idaho County, Idaho, within the Nez Perce and Payette national forests, 16 miles southwest of Riggins.[^1][^2]

Only one site we read names people who staffed the lookout.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists Mrs. Eula Morton Biba for 1932, citing the Idaho Statesman, and Ivan Decker for 1934, citing the Recorder Herald.[^3] A Forest Lookouts page for a different Horse Heaven, in the Salmon National Forest, quotes a 1934 Recorder Herald item about Decker heading to a lookout of the same name.[^5] The sources do not show which tower that item meant, so the 1934 name is not settled.[^3][^5]

The register lists the tower as removed in 1950.[^1] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts say it was destroyed that year.[^2][^3] The sources agree on the year but not on how the tower ended.[^1][^2][^3]

The register added the site on January 20, 2013, on the nomination of Rod Parks of the Twin River Back Country Horsemen.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2][^3]
