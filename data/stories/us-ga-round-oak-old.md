Round Oak Old is a point in a Georgia tower list revised in 2016, placed in Jones County at about 33.106 N, 83.612 W.[^1] Going by the coordinates the list gives for both points, it sits roughly 2 km (about 6,400 feet) southeast of Round Oak Lookout Tower, which the same list places in the same county.[^1]

The list gives it the name Round Oak Old. It also says names for towers numbered 73 and above were the author's creation, and this point is number 406.[^1] The list says towers were discovered on 1:100,000-scale USGS topographic maps and NGS data sheets, and this entry's source note is only "topo".[^1]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether a tower still stands there.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
