Johnson Peak Lookout stood on a peak in Bonner County, Idaho, about five miles southwest of Clark Fork.[^1][^3][^4] The Coeur d'Alene National Forest is the agency listed for the site.[^1][^2]

The tower went up around 1937 as a 40-foot pole structure with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2][^3] It was destroyed in 1955, a year that all three guides give.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as a hike-in.[^2] The Lookout Structures list on firelookout.org marks the tower as gone.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts describes no remains and says nothing about current access, so no source we read tells a visitor what, if anything, is still on the peak or whether the site is open to the public.[^2]
