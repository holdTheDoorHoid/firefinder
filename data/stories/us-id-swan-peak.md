A camp stood on Swan Peak by 1931, in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest about seven miles northeast of Harrison, Kootenai County, Idaho.[^1][^2] In 1934 an L-4 tower went up there.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts says it has no documented staffing history for the tower.[^2] The Forest Lookouts site does quote an August 1934 Sandpoint newspaper item that calls Jack Noble the forest lookout on Swan Mountain.[^3] The item says a mother bear chased Noble up a small tree, and that his spaniel, Hobo, came to his aid.[^3]

The tower was destroyed in 1955, according to three sources that give that date.[^1][^2][^3] The sources do not say how.

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains of the tower today. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in, so visitors reach it on foot, but it does not say whether the site is open to the public.[^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register uses the number US 831 for a different Swan Peak, on the Salmon National Forest in Lemhi County.[^4] The register says that tower was constructed in 1940, abandoned in 1942, and gone sometime after 1983.[^4] Its elevation and location do not match the Kootenai County tower, so that register number belongs to the Lemhi County site.[^1][^4]
