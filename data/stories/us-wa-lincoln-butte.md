The Washington Lookouts listing for Lincoln Butte places the site in Okanogan National Forest and gives its survey location as 34N-18E-8.[^1] Beyond that, the listing offers one passage, dated August 1927 and signed by H.T. Phelps, with "Six Twenty-Six" in parentheses after the name.[^1] It describes an emergency lookout-fireman named Barren who had come back from a fire on the head of Bridge Creek and was getting ready for bed when lightning was striking all about the peak.[^1] Later that night he woke with no memory of the interval, and his watch had stopped at 8:30 p.m.[^1] The passage refers to the peak without naming it, so the link to Lincoln Butte rests on how the listing presents it.[^1]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Lincoln Butte - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Washington Lookouts, https://washingtonlookouts.weebly.com/lincoln-butte.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
