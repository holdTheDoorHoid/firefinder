The firelookout.ca registry places a Bull Fire Lookout in Cariboo D, Cariboo, BC, Canada.[^1] The entry says a steel tower replaced an earlier wooden one.[^1] That is the only history the registry gives for the site.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is public.[^1]

[^1]: "Bull Fire Lookout", Firelookout.ca, https://firelookout.ca/lookouts/?slug=bull-fire-lookout (accessed 2026-10-20).
