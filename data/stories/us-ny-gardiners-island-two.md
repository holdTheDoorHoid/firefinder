The only source we have for this tower is OpenStreetMap, a volunteer-built map. Its entry carries the name "Gardiners Island Two Lookout Tower," gives a height of 12 metres, and tags the structure as an observation tower.[^1] The page shows the entry was last edited almost two years ago.[^1]

The island has stayed in the Gardiner line since 1639. It is also among the biggest privately held islands in the US.[^2] Treat the tower as off limits unless the owners say otherwise. Nothing we read suggests the public can reach it.[^2]
