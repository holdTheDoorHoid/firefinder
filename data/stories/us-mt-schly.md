Schly was a fire lookout about 15 miles north of Missoula, Montana.[^1] Its firelookout.com entry dates the tower to 1935 and describes it as a 50-foot pole live-in tower.[^1] The same entry says it was destroyed in 1953.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list places it in Missoula County, marks it gone, and calls it a tower.[^2] The firelookout.com entry gives the land as the Flathead Indian Reservation; the Association's row for the site does not repeat that label.[^1][^2]

Both listings spell the name Schly.[^1][^2] The Schley Mountain lookout in Mineral County is a separate site, with its own entry.[^3]

No source I could reach describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the land is open to visitors.

[^3]: Schley Mtn. Fire Lookout Cabin, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/schleymtn.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
