A Charleston Gazette item from September 1932 reports that progress was being made on the Cabin Hill forest fire tower, on Mud Fork mountain in Logan County.[^2] It adds that nearly twenty counties and much of eastern Kentucky were in view from the lookout.[^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives the site as US 1673 and WV 64, with an elevation of 1,813 feet (553 metres).[^1] It was registered on January 26, 2017, with Brian M. Powell as nominator.[^1] The register names the West Virginia Conservation Commission as administrator.[^1]

The FireLookout.org list marks the tower as gone.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^3]: West Virginia fire lookouts list, FireLookout.org, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wv/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
