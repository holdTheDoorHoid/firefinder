The Union (Cliftonville) Fire Tower is an Aermotor LS-40 lookout tower at the Union Forestry Site in Union County, Florida.[^1] The register lists it as US 1812, FL 30 and was added on January 2, 2026.[^1] The Florida Department of Agriculture administers it.[^1]

The register does not say when the tower was built, who staffed it or what fires it watched.[^1] The register names the model as an Aermotor LS-40, and Aermotor built its towers from galvanized steel.[^1][^2]

According to the register, the tower had likely gone out of service by 2025.[^1] We found no other source that describes this tower.[^1]

A photograph on Wikimedia Commons, taken on 31 October 2015 and captioned "Florida Forest Service tower, Union County, Florida," was shot from a spot close to the register's coordinates for the tower.[^1][^3] We think it likely shows this tower. That match is our own comparison, not something the caption states. The photo is by Michael Rivera and licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.[^3]

Today the register does not say whether visitors may go near the tower or climb it. Ask the Florida Department of Agriculture before you go out there.[^1]
