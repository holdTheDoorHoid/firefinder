The Mount Horden Tower is listed at Elsas, an unincorporated railway point in Kapuskasing Township, within the Unorganized North of Algoma District, northeastern Ontario.[^2][^3] It sits on the Canadian National main line, with Agate to its west and Oatland to its east.[^3]

A fan site's list of still-standing Ontario towers includes it at Elsas, without the asterisk the list uses for towers whose cupola has been removed.[^2] The firelookout.ca record gives the tower's address as Agate, Ontario, a railway point west of Elsas.[^1][^3]

A firelookout.ca contributor is not sure the tower still stands.[^1]

No source describes the site today. These sources do not show whether a tower, its footings or any other trace remains, or whether the public may visit.
