The Forest Lookouts website lists a Great Hill entry in Middlebury, New Haven County.[^1] Mapping sites call the structure the Great Hill Lookout Tower.[^2][^3]

TopoZone gives its coordinates as 41.5339852°N, -73.1359443°W and an approximate elevation of 968 feet (295 m), and it places the tower on its Woodbury topographic map.[^3] OpenStreetMap gives an elevation of about 955 feet (291 m) and tags the structure as an observation tower.[^2] The two elevations differ by a few metres.[^2][^3] ExpertGPS also files the tower under Middlebury.[^4]

No source I found describes the site today.
