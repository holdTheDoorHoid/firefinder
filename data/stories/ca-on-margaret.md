The Ontario list of fire towers places Margaret Tower at Biscotasing, a community in the Unorganized North Part of Sudbury District in northeastern Ontario.[^1][^3] The community began in 1884, when the Canadian Pacific Railway founded it on the shore of Lake Biscotasi to support railway construction.[^3]

Ontario's fire towers fell under the former Department of Lands and Forests, a body that became the Ministry of Natural Resources in 1972.[^2]

The list gives Margaret's fate in a few words: it was pulled over and abandoned to decay in the bush.[^1] The same list notes that the ministry decided in 2013 to remove many of the towers that were still standing.[^1]

The list also says the ministry no longer maintains these towers and asks visitors not to climb them.[^1] No source describes what remains at the Margaret site now, or whether the public can reach it.
