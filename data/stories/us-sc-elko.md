Elko Tower is a fire lookout in Barnwell County, South Carolina, with an elevation of 349 feet (106 m).[^1] The Eastern U.S. Lookouts site lists it under the South Carolina Forestry Commission.[^2] That agency shut its fire tower system down on October 1, 1993.[^3] No source says Elko closed on that date.

The National Historic Lookout Register added Elko Tower on January 2, 2026, with a photo by Dan Curry, and it does not describe the tower's present condition.[^1] The Eastern U.S. Lookouts site marks Elko as "Removed" and gives no date for that.[^2] We found no source that dates either the build or the removal, so the tower's present state is unknown.

Before you plan a visit, check with the South Carolina Forestry Commission about the site. Do not climb any structure you find there without permission.
