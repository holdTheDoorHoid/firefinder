The Idamont lookout stood in the Kaniksu National Forest, about 14 miles east of Bonners Ferry in Boundary County, Idaho.[^1] The site had a crow's nest in the 1930s.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list also calls it a crow's nest.[^3]

Both firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts give 1942 as the year it was abandoned.[^1][^2] The association's list marks it abandoned too.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the entry "Hike-In Abandoned," so the site is described as a hike-in.[^2] We found no source describing what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
