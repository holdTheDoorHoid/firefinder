A lookout tower on White Oak Mountain, in Scott County, was nearing completion when the Harrison Times reported on October 5, 1928.[^1] The item, as the Central US Lookouts page quotes it, described a 48-foot lookout tower on the mountain near Waldron.[^1]

The same page records the tower as removed in 1974.[^1]

The page also reproduces a 1971 station description by the U.S. Forest Service. It describes a tower with four steel legs and a metal cabin mounted on top, 85 feet overall.[^1] That description places the tower in Franklin County, not Scott County, and the page does not say whether it describes the same structure.[^1]

OpenStreetMap still shows a White Oak Lookout Tower at the spot this entry covers.[^2] It is tagged as an observation tower and carries a fixme tag questioning its location.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
