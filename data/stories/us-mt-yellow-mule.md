Yellow Mule was a lookout in Madison County, Montana, on the Beaverhead National Forest. It sat about 18 miles southeast of Ennis, at an elevation of 9,200 feet.[^1][^2]

Rex Kamstra's page dates the site to 1915, when a log cabin was put up on a hillside half a mile northwest of Flattop Mountain.[^1] It was a ground-level cabin rather than a tower.[^3]

Kamstra's page says the cabin was abandoned in 1939.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list shows the site as gone.[^3]

Do not confuse this site with Yellow Mule Cabin, a backcountry retreat with a similar name. That cabin was built in 1920 and sits at about 8,650 feet, so its date and elevation differ from the lookout's. We found no source that links the two.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the 1915 cabin, or whether visitors can reach the site.
