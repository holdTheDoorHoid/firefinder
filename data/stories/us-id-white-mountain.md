White Mountain Lookout stood on the Kaniksu National Forest in Boundary County, Idaho, about 16 miles southwest of Bonners Ferry, at 5,005 feet.[^1][^2]

The tower went up in 1937 as a 50-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2] A 1962 news item, quoted by Forest Lookouts, lists White Mountain among six old Forest Service towers that the Civilian Conservation Corps put up in the 1930s.[^3]

Bobby Reed is the only staff member named on either lookout page. Both list him for 1942, and one of them cites a Bonners Ferry Herald item for it.[^1][^2]

The same 1962 item says the towers had been left idle and unmaintained since 1950, when the Forest Service switched to aerial patrol.[^3] In June 1962 the Sandpoint News Bulletin reported that the towers were being knocked down and burned.[^3] Both lookout pages give 1962 as the year it was destroyed.[^1][^2]

A reader's comment on the Idaho Fire Lookouts page describes a current photo showing burned debris, including electrical battery parts, post bases and a short set of concrete stairs. The same comment mentions an abandoned helipad next to the tower site.[^1] It is one visitor's report, not a survey. No source read says whether the site is open to visitors, and Idaho Fire Lookouts describes the lookout as a hike-in.[^1]
