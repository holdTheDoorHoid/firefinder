The Dutchman Lookout Tower stood in Menominee County, Wisconsin.[^1] OpenStreetMap maps it under that name, tags it as an observation tower, and gives its elevation as 403 meters.[^2] The Forest Lookouts site gives its survey location as 29N-13E-12 and names the Wisconsin Department of Conservation alongside it.[^3]

WJFW describes how the state's towers worked. When smoke was sighted, the lookout used binoculars and a compass table to pinpoint it, and the azimuth and distance were passed to a dispatch center.[^4] By WJFW's account, 72 towers were still in service when they were decommissioned in 2015.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the coordinates as 45.00268, -88.87180, and it marks the tower as gone.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site or whether the public can reach it. Anyone curious about the spot should ask the local landowner before going.
