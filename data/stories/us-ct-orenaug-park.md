The Orenaug Park Fire Tower is an 80-foot steel tower in Orenaug Park, in Woodbury, Connecticut.[^1][^3] The Town of Woodbury administers it.[^1] A 2012 visitor wondered whether its open steel lattice design made it look so tall.[^3]

The tower dates from 1911 and was erected to spot fires over the Pomperaug Valley.[^1][^2][^4] The register gives the site's elevation as 504 feet (154 metres), but a contractor and a 2012 blog put the hilltop at 540 feet.[^1][^3][^4] The sources we read do not say who watched from the tower or for how long.[^1]

According to the register, the 2018 work was a repaint rather than a restoration.[^1] The contractor's own project page calls the 2018 job a restoration. It lists sandblasting, structural steel repairs and a new industrial coating.[^4] Both sources agree on the repainting, but they disagree on whether the work counts as a restoration.[^1][^4]

Today the contractor says hikers and visitors are still welcome, and that the tower offers wide views of the landscape.[^4] A 2012 blog describes hiking up a red-marked trail to the tower, reached from the park's entrance on Park Road.[^3] The same blog describes climbing the tower's steel stairs to the top.[^3] The sources do not say whether the stairs are open for climbing today, so check with the Town of Woodbury before going up.[^1]
