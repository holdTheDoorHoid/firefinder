The Robinson Pass lookout was a log cabin built in 1931 in the Okanogan National Forest, about 15 miles northwest of Mazama.[^1][^2] The willhiteweb.com account reasons that a cabin at the pass would make sense, given the long trail that continued north toward the guard stations near the Canadian border.[^3] The account also cites a 1931 Chelan National Forest map that places a Robinson Guard Station at or close to the pass.[^3]

The account is not sure the lookout stood at the pass itself. It wonders whether the lookout was named for the pass but sat on a peak above it, and it passes along reports of debris, including wood and wire, on peak 7619 north of the pass.[^3]

A 1939 forest fire burned the cabin.[^1][^2]

No source we read confirms what, if anything, remains at the site today. The debris reports are secondhand, and the account does not confirm that they came from the lookout.[^3] We found no source that says whether the site is open to visitors.
