The Mikes Knob lookout was built in 1941 on Mikes Knob in West Virginia.[^1][^4] A Forest Lookouts history says the Civilian Conservation Corps did the labor.[^2] The U.S. Forest Service administered the site, and the Register gives its elevation as 4,243 feet, or 1,293 meters.[^1]

The Register has a photo captioned as a fire finder in use at the site in 1946.[^1] We found no record of who staffed the tower or of any fires it watched.

The tower was later moved to the Richwood Volunteer Fire Department station in Richwood.[^1] The Register does not say when the move happened, but a photo caption places the tower atop the Richwood VFD by February 2014.[^1] The blog says the move was made for preservation.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list shows the original Mikes Knob site as Tower Gone.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association list shows a standing tower at Richwood VFD.[^3] The 2024 blog post says it is not open to the public.[^4] Because it stands at a fire station, ask the department before going near it. We found nothing that describes what remains on the original hilltop.

The sources disagree on the county. The Register and the blog say Pocahontas County. The Forest Lookouts page and the Forest Fire Lookout Association list say Greenbrier County, and that list puts the Richwood entry in Nicholas County.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
