Haystack Mountain rises about 14 miles northwest of Salmon, in Lemhi County on the Salmon National Forest.[^1][^5]

A 1918 Forest map already listed a lookout there.[^4] In 1924 a forest report found no developments at the site apart from a horse pasture below the peak, and it concluded that a tower was necessary.[^3] That September the Salmon Herald said building work on the tower and cottage was going well.[^3]

Guy E. Goff was the lookout man in 1925, and in 1926 A.C. Ludwig carried a large load of supplies to the lookout.[^3] By 1926 a visibility map counted Haystack among the forest's primary lookouts.[^4]

Later pages tell a simpler story. Firelookout.com describes a 1930s crow's nest that was abandoned,[^1] and the FFLA list gives it as a crow's nest with the status Abandoned.[^5] Those descriptions do not match the 1924 tower and cottage, and I found no source that explains the difference.[^1][^3]

Today Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site drive-in.[^2] I found no source that describes what, if anything, remains on the summit.
