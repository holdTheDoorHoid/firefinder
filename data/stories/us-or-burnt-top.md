Burnt Top is a 6,323-foot summit in the Willamette National Forest, about 27 miles west of Bend, in Lane County.[^1][^2]

Sources differ on the year the lookout went up. The register and Firelookout.com both give 1934 and describe an L-5 cab.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts quotes a Eugene Guard item from November 1933 saying a crew had completed a lookout house on Burnt Top mountain, along with a telephone line to it.[^3] An October 1933 item from the same paper said a telephone line was being completed from the Burnt Top lookout station to the Horse Lake ranger station.[^3]

The register gives 1965 as the year the cab was removed.[^1] Firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year, and Forest Lookouts also puts the removal in 1965.[^2][^3]

A 1955 U.S. Geological Survey station description put the survey mark at the center of the single-story lookout.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as ground-level and gone.[^4] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit now, or whether visitors may go there, so check with the Willamette National Forest before heading up.
