A lookout list for Georgia places a tower at Lula, in Hall County, at 34.38388, -83.68293, and carries several reference codes for it, including BLL-64456 and gfc-915.[^1]

The headline of a Gainesville Times article on the Lula tower says it has outlasted decades of demolition.[^2] The article says the tower reaches 100 feet (about 30.5 m) and stands next to a Georgia Forestry Commission office in Lula.[^2] A photo caption dated January 3, 2019 describes it as one of the few still in service around Georgia.[^2]

The only dated item I could read is that caption, which places the tower at the Georgia Forestry Commission in Lula.[^2] No source describes its condition since then, or whether the public may visit the site.

[^2]: Dodging decades of demolition: How the Lula fire tower has survived, Gainesville Times, https://www.gainesvilletimes.com/life/dodging-decades-demolition-how-lula-fire-tower-has-survived/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
