Wallace Peak lies in Bath County, Virginia, and the Eastern U.S. Lookouts page files it under the George Washington National Forest.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it under both Bath and Augusta counties.[^3]

The summit was once the site of a fire lookout tower.[^2]

Both lookout lists treat the tower as gone. The Forest Fire Lookout Association shows it as gone, and the Eastern U.S. Lookouts page says it was removed.[^3][^1]

A hiking guide reports that the tower's concrete footings are still on the summit, while the rest of the structure disappeared long ago.[^2]

The guide describes the route as demanding and remote, with a steep climb.[^2] The old tower access road is no longer in use, but the guide says it can still be found with a careful look.[^2]

Tree growth limits the views from the summit, and the guide says there is only a low chance of meeting other hikers on the route.[^2] No other source we read describes the site today.[^2][^1]
