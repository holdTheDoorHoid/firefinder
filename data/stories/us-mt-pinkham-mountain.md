Pinkham Mountain's lookout stood in Lincoln County, Montana, on the Kootenai National Forest, 16 miles south of Eureka at an elevation of 6,322 feet.[^1] The site began as a camp around 1914.[^1] In 1921 a 40-foot platform went up between three trees, along with a log cabin for living quarters.[^1]

In 1924 a 55-foot pole tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab was added, along with a replacement log cabin.[^1] The Kalispell Daily Inter Lake reported that September that the Forest Service was putting up a 70-foot fire lookout on Pinkham ridge, a line Forest Lookouts quotes.[^2] The two heights do not match, so the sources disagree.[^1][^2]

In 1949 a 41-foot L-4 tower of treated timber was built.[^1] Firelookout.com says the tower was gone by 1981, and the Montana list marks it gone too.[^1][^3]

The firelookout.com page gives no publication date, but it says the replacement log cabin is still there.[^1] No source we read says more about the site today, or whether visitors can reach it. Ask the Kootenai National Forest before going.
