A list of Hillsborough County observation towers, dated November 13, 1950, includes a tower at Valrico on Road 60 and gives W.O. Weeks as its towerman.[^1]

A National Geodetic Survey data sheet, described by Hillsborough County in 1983, names its station "Valrico Fire Lookout Tower."[^1]

A 1980 photograph in the county's Environmental Lands collection, titled "Valrico Fire Tower Facing Northwest," shows a wide view of dense forest looking out from the tower's northwest side.[^2] The county places the tower beside Sydney Dover Trails, with State Road 60 to its south and Sydney Washer Road to its east.[^2]

The sources do not show that the 1950 list, the survey sheet and the photograph describe one structure.[^1][^2] The survey sheet places its station 2.7 miles southeast of Valrico.[^1]

The cited pages do not say what, if anything, still stands at the tower or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^2]: Valrico Fire Tower Facing Northwest, Valrico, Florida, Hillsborough County ELAPP, Digital Commons @ USF, https://digitalcommons.usf.edu/elapp/664 (accessed 2026-10-21).
