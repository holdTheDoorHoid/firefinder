The Ouachita Pinnacle lookout was in the Ouachita National Forest, in Garland County.[^1] A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description, dated 1956 in its record, says the lookout house was about 15 feet square, carried on four steel legs, and rose to about 47.5 feet.[^1]

A Friends of the Ouachita trail page carries a photo captioned with the name Ouachita Pinnacle Fire Tower.[^2] A recollection by John Archer, who was then the new district ranger on the Jessieville District, describes a fire reported south of Ouachita Pinnacle, which the account calls the Ouachita Pinnacle Fire. It gives the start date as April 9, 1970, and says the fire burned over ground where timber had recently been logged.[^3] The account is about the fire and does not describe the lookout. The page notes that it was thought to have been written in 2011.[^3]

The same account adds that the trail today passes through the old burn area.[^3] No source describes the tower site today, including whether the lookout still stands or whether the site is open to visitors.
