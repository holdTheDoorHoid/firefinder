The Rich Mountain tower site sits in Greenbrier County, West Virginia, at an elevation of 3,817 feet (1,163 meters).[^1] The register lists the West Virginia Division of Forestry as the administering agency.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added it on January 26, 2017, on the nomination of Brian M. Powell.[^1]

The tower is gone, according to the Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list.[^2]

Do not mix this site up with another Rich Mountain. The tower at the Rich Mountain Battlefield trail near Beverly was taken apart and moved to Snowshoe Resort.[^3] It now sits at Snowshoe Mountain, a ski resort in private hands.[^4]

No source we found describes what remains at the Greenbrier County site, or says whether it is open to the public. The register gives its coordinates as N 38.018333°, W 080.673333°.[^1]
