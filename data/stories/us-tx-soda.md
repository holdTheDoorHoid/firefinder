The Soda lookout was a Texas Forest Service tower in Polk County, Texas.[^1]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the station in 1949, which means the tower was already standing by then.[^1] The survey places the tower about 10.3 miles east of Livingston and 0.3 mile north of U.S. Highway 190. It describes a steel tower on four legs, about 110 feet high, with a glass-walled lookout cab on top.[^1]

A Texas ghost-town guide places the old community of Soda about eight miles east of Livingston, the county seat.[^3] That figure does not match the survey's, so the community and the survey station may not be the same spot.

A federal survey listing for the station reports its surface mark as destroyed, but it says nothing about the tower itself.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list records the tower as gone.[^4] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors. Please do not assume access.
