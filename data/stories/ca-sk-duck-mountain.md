The Firetowers of Saskatchewan pages on websiteservice.ca list a tower named Duck Mountain, at 51°37′34″ N, 101°39′31″ W.[^1] The list gives these figures without compass letters, so the N and W are inferred from the tower's location in Saskatchewan. The site says Maurice Bourassa's efforts made its information possible.[^1]

Duck Mountain Provincial Park, which shares the name, sits about 14 km (8.7 mi) east of Kamsack. The park extends about 12 km (7.5 mi) farther east, to the Saskatchewan–Manitoba boundary. Highway 57 gives road access and links Saskatchewan Highway 5 with Manitoba Highway 83.[^2]

A hiking guide names a Fire Tower Trail in the park, though nothing we read ties that trail to the tower on the list.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the Duck Mountain site today, whether the tower still stands, or whether the public can reach it.
