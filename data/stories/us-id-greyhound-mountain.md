Greyhound Mountain's lookout stood at 8,995 feet in the Challis National Forest, in Custer County, Idaho, about 44 miles west-northwest of Challis.[^1][^2]

Sources disagree on the first building. Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts date an L-4 cabin to 1934.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page gives 1933 instead and describes a standard R-1 gable roof lookout house.[^3]

The cabin was abandoned in 1946, a date all three sites agree on.[^1][^2][^3] A 1959 Coast and Geodetic Survey station description, reproduced on the Forest Lookouts page, describes a white wooden building, 20 feet on each side, roofed in green and accompanied by outbuildings. It was not in use at the time.[^3]

Firelookout.com, Idaho Fire Lookouts and the firelookout.org list all mark the lookout as gone.[^1][^2][^4] None of the sources we read says what, if anything, remains at the site today. Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as a hike-in, the only access detail in the sources.[^2]
