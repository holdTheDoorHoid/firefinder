Fish Creek Lookout stood in Idaho County on the Nez Perce National Forest, at 5,139 feet.[^1][^2] A steel tower and a log cabin for the lookout's living quarters were built in 1931.[^1][^3] The sources disagree on the tower's height: 75 feet on the Idaho Fire Lookouts page and the forest index, and 90 feet on firelookout.com.[^1][^2][^3]

Firelookout.com says the tower was no longer standing by 1968.[^3] The cabin went to Grangeville Park, but the sources disagree on the year. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page gives 1961, and firelookout.com says the 1970s.[^1][^3]

None of the sources describes the mountain site today.[^1][^2][^3] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page carries the label "Drive-Up Cabin Moved," which seems to refer to the cabin rather than the summit.[^1] No source says whether the cabin still stands at the park.[^1][^3]
