Covington Mountain, listed as Covington Mountain (Sauratown), is a lookout site in Stokes County, North Carolina.[^1] A Forest Lookouts list of the state's towers names the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources for the Stokes County site.[^2]

The federal geodetic survey has a station named Sauratown Mtn Lookout Tower. Its point lies about 600 feet (180 m) from the position in the tower list, so the two records most likely describe one site.[^1][^3] The survey listing for that point carries the code XX, and NGS's legend gives X for a surface mark reported as destroyed; the code describes the survey marker, not the tower.[^3]

Today the mountain is a radio site. Wikipedia describes it as a major communications site with no public access, and notes that a private road with a gate stops a short way from the summit.[^4] A page about the mountain's towers reports that its radio structures sit close together, none more than two kilometers from the others.[^5]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout tower today.
