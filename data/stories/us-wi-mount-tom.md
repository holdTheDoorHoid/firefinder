A lookout tower called Mount Tom once stood in Marinette County, Wisconsin.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list places it at 45.30531, -88.05094 and marks it gone.[^1] The Central US lookouts site also lists a Mount Tom in Marinette County.[^2] A TopoZone list of towers in the county names a Mount Tom Fire Tower.[^3]

A gazetteer describes a peak called Mount Tom, 1,004 feet high, in the Town of Middle Inlet.[^4] Whether the tower stood on that peak is not stated in these sources.

No source describes the site now, so what, if anything, remains there, and whether the land is open to visitors, is not known.
