Martin Mountain rises to 9,406 feet in Lemhi County, Idaho.[^1] Its lookout was an L-4 cab set on the mountain's north point in 1934.[^1][^3]

In August 1953 a Forest Service fire spotter, Carl Rennaker of Salmon, fell ill at the lookout.[^3] His wife radioed for help.[^3] Four smokejumpers set out from Idaho City, and a medical student among them diagnosed acute appendicitis.[^3] The four rescuers packed him out by stretcher in about seven hours, over a trail roughly nine miles from the lookout.[^3]

The lookout was abandoned in 1962, and the sources list it as gone.[^1][^3][^4]

The sources do not agree on the forest or the distance. One modern listing places it in the Salmon National Forest, 32 miles northwest of Challis.[^1] A 1953 newspaper put it 50 miles northwest of Challis.[^3] Forest Lookouts names the Challis National Forest.[^3] Today the Forest Service calls the area the Salmon-Challis National Forest.[^5]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it is open to visitors. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it as a hike-in site that is now gone.[^2]
