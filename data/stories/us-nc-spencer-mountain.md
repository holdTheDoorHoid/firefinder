The North Carolina lookout-tower list published by tnlandforms.us includes a tower called Spencer Mountain in Gaston County, at the coordinates it gives.[^1]

The one Wikipedia article we read, on the community of Spencer Mountain, describes a television transmitter on the mountain.[^2] On July 15, 1949, WBTV, the CBS affiliate serving Charlotte, sent out the Carolinas' first commercial TV signal from a transmitter on the mountain.[^2] The station stayed there until 1984, when a new transmitter tower was finished in Dallas, North Carolina.[^2] The old tower has since been shortened, and various groups still use it.[^2]

None of the sources we read ties that transmitter to the lookout listed above.

No source we read describes the lookout site today, or whether visitors can reach it.

[^2]: Spencer Mountain, North Carolina, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spencer_Mountain,_North_Carolina (accessed 2026-10-21).
