Dalton Mountain lies about seven miles south of Lincoln, in Lewis and Clark County, on the Helena National Forest.[^1] In October 1933 Forest Service officials were gathering data for a lookout station and tower on the mountain, to be built with Public Works Administration funds.[^2] A Lincoln man named Alf Wendler was already stationed at a lookout there in July 1933, when a deer spooked his pack horses and he was taken to Helena for medical care.[^2]

The April 1934 plan called it a new lookout station in the Willow Creek district, with the work due to be finished by July 1.[^2] Firelookout.com dates the tower to 1934 and describes it as a 20-foot pole tower of the L-4 type.[^1]

Firelookout.com records that the tower was destroyed in 1971.[^1] No source read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
