Sugarbush was a fire tower in Clearwater County.[^2] The county list also calls it the Rice Lake Lookout Tower.[^2]

Forest Lookouts dates the tower to 1937 and gives its height as 80 feet, with an external ladder.[^1] The county list agrees on the date and height, and gives the owner as the Minnesota Forest Service.[^2]

A 1941 survey note describes the tower as steel, with an enclosed crow's nest.[^2] A 1960 note in the county list gives its overall height as 90 feet, which does not match the 80 feet given for the 1937 build.[^2] The sources do not explain the difference.

The county list says the tower was removed.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone.[^3] We found no source that describes what remains at the site, or whether the public can visit it.
