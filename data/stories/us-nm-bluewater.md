Bluewater Lookout stands on the Sacramento Ranger District of the Lincoln National Forest in Otero County, New Mexico, at about 2,213 metres (7,260 feet).[^1] According to the Forest Service, the Weather Bureau had the tower built in 1917. The agency bought it and erected it on this site in 1937.[^2] The register repeats both dates, but its "Built" field lists only 1937.[^1]

The steel tower stands 45 feet tall and carries a 7-by-7-foot cab.[^1][^3] Sources disagree about who made the cab. The register says it is either an Aermotor LX-24 or an International Derrick Company tower.[^1] The Forest Service page says consensus leans toward International Derrick, but that it is unclear whether the cab is that type or an Aermotor MC-40.[^2] A 2020 report from the New Mexico chapter of the Forest Fire Lookout Association names an Aermotor LX-24.[^3]

A newspaper item dated March 16, 1933, which the Forest Lookouts website reproduces, says a lookout had been stationed at Bluewater Lookout.[^4] If that report is right, a lookout worked from this spot before the 1937 erection date, so the early history may involve more than one structure. The Forest Service describes the observer's cabin and storage shed as Civilian Conservation Corps design and construction.[^2]

The complex was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in January 1988.[^2][^3] The register added the lookout in August 2015, with Mark Gutzman as nominator.[^1] The observer's cabin and shed were restored in 2013.[^3]

In its 2020 year-end report, the New Mexico chapter lists Bluewater as proposed for the New Mexico national forests' cabin rental program.[^3] I found no later source saying the rental has opened. I also found no record of a fire at the site, or of abandonment or a move after 1937.

Today, the Forest Service page, last updated February 19, 2025, says access to the area is on foot and that the tower itself is not available to the public.[^2] The page carries a "Site Closed" label.[^2] The tower is not open to visitors, so please keep off it.
