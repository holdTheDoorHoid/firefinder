The Iron Mountain lookout stood in Boundary County, in the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^2][^3] Both Rex Kamstra's firelookout.com page and Idaho Fire Lookouts describe a 40-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab, built in 1934.[^1][^2]

Both sites say it was destroyed in 1955.[^1][^2] A county list on Forest Lookouts also gives 1955 for the site, with no further detail.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the elevation as 6,426 feet and tags the site as hike-in.[^2] No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.

At least one other Idaho lookout is also called Iron Mountain. That lookout is in Camas County and in the Sawtooth National Forest, and it is on the National Historic Lookout Register.[^4] That site is separate and is not covered here.
