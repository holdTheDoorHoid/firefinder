Culvers Lookout stands on Sunrise Mountain in Stokes State Forest, in Sandyston Township, along the ridge of Kittatinny Mountain near Culver's Lake.[^1][^2][^3]

The site first went by the Normanook Fire Tower. Wikipedia's state list says the tower was first used in 1908, and the registry says the site was established that year.[^1][^2] The state put up a steel tower in 1918. Willhite Web reports that it was built with $250 in federal Weeks Law money and started up in April 1919.[^1][^3]

The tower standing today is an Aermotor, roughly 45 to 47 feet tall with a 7-by-7-foot cab. The registry dates it to 1933. Willhite Web says a new tower built by the CCC went up in 1934, so the sources disagree on the date.[^1][^2][^3]

In 1954 an observer named Robert Jennings was inside when five windows were shot out. Willhite Web says local kids shot at the tower for roughly an hour and a half without knowing anyone was inside, and that he came through unhurt.[^3]

The registry recorded the lookout on August 1, 1992.[^1]

A 2021 account says the New Jersey Forest Fire Service staffs the tower in the fall and spring, and describes an observer working eight-hour shifts there.[^3] The Appalachian Trail runs through the corridor, so hikers pass close by, but a fire access road has a locked gate that keeps motor vehicles out except for authorized people.[^1] Because it is still a working lookout, the cab is best admired from the trail.
