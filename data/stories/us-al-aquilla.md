The Aquilla fire tower site lies in Choctaw County, Alabama, at 479 feet, or 146 meters.[^1] The Alabama Forestry Commission is the agency the register lists for it.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page for the site names the same county and agency.[^2]

The site is in the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, which counts it as a former lookout rather than a standing one.[^1] The register added it on September 15, 2019, on the nomination of Andrew Zerbe, who also supplied the photo.[^1]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains of the tower at the site, and none that says whether the site is open to the public.[^1] A January 2019 report from the Forest Fire Lookout Association notes that the supporting structures under old towers are large and hard to take down, so they tend to be the last thing still standing.[^3] That report does not describe this site.[^3]

An OpenStreetMap point is still named Aquilla Lookout Tower, but a map entry does not show what stands on the ground.[^4]
