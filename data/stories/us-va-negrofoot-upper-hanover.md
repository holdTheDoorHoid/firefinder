The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower called Negrofoot in Hanover County, and its table marks that site as gone.[^1]

The place is Negro Foot, a community in Hanover County, Virginia, that is not an incorporated municipality.[^2] A 1990 historic-resources survey of Hanover County lists a tavern on the Negrofoot property and calls Negrofoot an important stagecoach stop at a crossroads linked to Route 33.[^3]

The association says the tower was relocated to Field Day of the Past, a show site in Goochland County.[^1] That show began in 1992 in Goochland County, and its Who We Are page says it has since moved its operations to Mount Airy Farm on Church Road, in Dinwiddie County.[^4]

No source we could read describes the Negrofoot site today. The only record we could read of the tower after it left here is the Goochland entry.[^1]
