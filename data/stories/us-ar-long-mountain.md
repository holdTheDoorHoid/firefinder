Long Mountain is in Marion County, Arkansas.[^1] The earliest description I found is a 1953 note from the U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey.[^1] It places the survey station about nine miles north and eight and a half miles west of Mountain Home, a quarter mile west of the Marion-Baxter county line.[^1] The note describes a steel tower on four legs, about 50 feet (15.2 m) tall, with a glassed-in room at the top.[^1]

A USGS topographic map of the Bull Shoals area, dated 1972 and published in 1973, names a lookout tower on Long Mountain among its landmarks.[^2]

Ownership is also unsettled. The Central US Lookouts page for the tower names the Arkansas Forestry Commission, but the same site's Arkansas index lists it under the Ouachita National Forest.[^1][^3]

The federal survey station for the tower has a listing code meaning its surface mark was reported destroyed.[^4] That code concerns the survey mark, so it does not by itself show the tower is gone. No source I read describes the site today or says whether it is open to visitors.[^4]
