The Barge Lookout Tower stands in Winston County, Mississippi, at about 586 feet of elevation.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as an 80-foot oil derrick that was converted into a lookout tower.[^1] The register gives no build date, listing the year as unknown, and it does not say who did the conversion or when.[^1]

The register says C.A. Barge Forest Products administers the tower and that the Barge timber company owns it.[^1] That family had a long history in Mississippi timber.[^2] Richard's father, Charles Alexander Barge, had owned sawmills in Mississippi that cut pine and hardwood. When Charles died in 1959, Richard took charge of the family's timberland operation, C. A. Barge Timberlands.[^2] In 1985 Richard set up Barge Forest Products Co., which began as a pole mill.[^2] The family profile does not mention a lookout, so any link between the tower and the company rests on the register's name alone.[^2][^1]

The register does not say who staffed the tower or whether it watched any fire.[^1] The register lists the tower as registered on July 10, 2022.[^1]

The register does not describe the tower's present condition or whether the public can visit.[^1]
