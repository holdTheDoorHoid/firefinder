The Talbert fire lookout was in Barry County, Missouri.[^1] The Central US Lookouts page names the Mark Twain National Forest as its agency and gives the land description as 22N-26W-29.[^1]

A TopoQuest map database lists the feature as "Talbert Fire Tower (historical)" and gives its elevation as 1,479 feet.[^2] The word "historical" suggests the tower may no longer stand, though the database does not explain the label.[^2]

The Missouri Department of Conservation's history of its towers counts 102 towers run by the state and 43 run by the U.S. Forest Service in Missouri in 1966.[^3] The same article gives a height range of 60 to 120 feet and says cost, availability and the surrounding terrain shaped each tower's design and placement.[^3]

No source I could open describes what remains at the Talbert site today, or whether the public can reach it.
