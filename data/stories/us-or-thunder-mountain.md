Thunder Mountain was a lookout in the Mount Hood National Forest, in Clackamas County, Oregon, about 35 miles southwest of Mount Hood.[^1][^2] The register gives the summit at 5,165 feet, or 1,574 meters.[^1]

The sources disagree on the year the tower went up. The register and Rex's Fire Lookout Page both date the Forest Service's 14-foot L-4 tower to 1933.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts gives 1934, and quotes a 1963 inspection that says the same.[^3]

Forest Lookouts says the tower legs were replaced in 1952, and notes that the tower stood on nine leg supports.[^3] In 1963 an inspector found rotting beams and a sagging floor, and wrote that the tower should be replaced. The same report says the public also used the structure, with 40 visitors that year.[^3]

The lookout was deactivated in 1964.[^3] The register and Rex's page both say it was gone by 1965.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list marks the tower as gone.[^4]

The register recorded the site on September 21, 2010, on the nomination of Ray Kresek.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the tower today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
