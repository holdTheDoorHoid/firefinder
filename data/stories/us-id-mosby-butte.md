Mosby Butte is in Power County, Idaho, about 23 miles northwest of American Falls.[^1] The Bureau of Land Management ran the lookout station on the butte.[^1][^3]

The sources do not agree on when the first cabin went up. Forest Lookouts, citing a source it names only as Kresek, gives 1935.[^3] Fire Lookout and Idaho Fire Lookouts both give 1942.[^1][^2] The climbing guide says a lookout was on the butte at least as early as 1935.[^4]

Fire Lookout and Idaho Fire Lookouts name Joe Winter as the lookout from 1942 to 1950, and Mr. and Mrs. Ralph King for 1957.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts quotes an August 1950 newspaper item that calls Joe Winters the operator of the station. A July 1952 item it quotes has Winter on his way back to his Mosby Butte station, two years after the 1950 end date.[^3]

Fire Lookout and Idaho Fire Lookouts list the cabin as gone, with no date.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts marks it as removed, also without a date.[^3]

On the summit, the climbing guide reports no cairn, a USGS benchmark, and what it calls remains of an old fire tower.[^4] That fire tower may be a different structure from the cabin the other records describe.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The guide says a road leads to the summit from a BLM road junction, where a hike begins, and it advises against driving the old two-track road without a high-clearance 4WD with good AT tires.[^4] No source we read gives the site's current condition or any access rule.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
