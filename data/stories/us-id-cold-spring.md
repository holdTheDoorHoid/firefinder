A log cabin went up at Cold Spring in 1931.[^1][^2] In 1939 a 40-foot tower with an L-4 cab is listed for the site.[^1][^2] The site lies in the Clearwater National Forest, about five miles north-northwest of the Kelly Creek Ranger Station, at 6,731 feet.[^1]

Both sites date the end of the tower to 1953.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com says the lookout was abandoned that year and the tower is gone,[^1] while Idaho Fire Lookouts says the tower was removed.[^2]

According to firelookout.com, the 1931 cabin is still there.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts places the cabin in the Recreational Rental Program and tags the site as a hike-in rental.[^2] Beyond the cabin, no source we checked describes what remains at the site today.

[^1]: Cold Spring Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/id/coldspring268.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Cold Spring Lookout, Idaho Fire Lookouts, https://www.idahofirelookouts.com/st-joe-clearwater-region/cold-spring-lookout/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
