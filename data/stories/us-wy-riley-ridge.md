The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a site called Riley Ridge in Sublette County, Wyoming, at 42.48826, -110.49804. The entry gives only a name, a county and coordinates.[^1] The site is not among the association's lookout structures.[^1] Its Wyoming list sorts entries into several groups. Riley Ridge sits in the last one, headed "Unknown/Undocumented Sites."[^1]

The association's rule is that a site moves up to its lookout lists once records show it was regularly used as a wildland fire lookout.[^1] The list gives no structure type, status, build date or staffing for Riley Ridge.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the ridge today or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Wyoming Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wy/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
