The Open Lake Lookout Tower stands in Lauderdale County, Tennessee, and the Tennessee Division of Forestry is listed as its administrator.[^1] The register entry is clear about the tower itself but says little about its past.[^1]

The tower is an 80-foot Aermotor with a parallel internal staircase.[^1] The register gives the site an elevation of 450 feet, or 137 meters, while the Tennessee list gives 460 feet.[^1][^2]

A name badge on the tower mentions Broken Arrow, Oklahoma, so the register dates its manufacture to sometime after 1964.[^1] A separate removal note in the register is dated only as after 1964, and it does not say what was taken down or from where.[^1]

We found no record of who built the tower, who staffed it, or which fires it watched.

The Tennessee list marks the tower as standing.[^2] The register describes it as being in very rough condition, and it was registered on January 1, 2023.[^1] The Tennessee list also reports that many towers have been taken down and that most of the state's lookout sites are now on private land.[^2] It advises against climbing the towers, since steps are often removed or rotten.[^2]

Today we found no public access information and no rental listing for Open Lake. Ask the Tennessee Division of Forestry before going near it, and do not climb it.[^2]

[^2]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
