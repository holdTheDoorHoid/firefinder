Shawneetown Hills was a fire lookout tower in Gallatin County, Illinois, and the Forest Lookouts site has a page for it.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Illinois list gives its coordinates and marks the tower as gone.[^2]

Illinois had roughly 30 lookouts built from 1930 to 1950. Lookouts of that era had a small cab on top of the tower, where a towerman watched for fires.[^3] A May 2021 magazine feature reports that nearly all of the state's towers were removed or sold in the early 1970s.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the site is open to visitors today.
