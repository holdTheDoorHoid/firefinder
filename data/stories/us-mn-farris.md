The Farris lookout started life at another site. The Hubbard County tower list says it was the Steamboat Hill lookout, moved to Farris and erected there in 1952.[^2] It was a 100-foot Aermotor stairway tower, and it kept the number 59 it had carried at Steamboat.[^2]

Farris, once called Graceland, was a rail village in northeast Hubbard County, west of Cass Lake.[^2] A 1959 survey note describes a four-legged steel structure with a metal observation room, and it gives an overall height of 110 feet.[^2] The table entry says 100 feet, so the two figures disagree.[^2]

The same list says the Steamboat tower had apparently been dismantled.[^2] The Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list marks both the Farris and the Steamboat sites as gone.[^1]

I did not find a source that describes the Farris site today. I cannot say what, if anything, is left there or whether the land is open to the public, so check with the local land manager before going to look.
