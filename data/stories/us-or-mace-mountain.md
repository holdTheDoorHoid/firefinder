Mace Mountain, in Douglas County about 26 miles northeast of Roseburg, got a fire lookout in 1934.[^1][^2] A 1933 newspaper item, quoted on Rex's Fire Lookout Page, proposed a 10-by-10 lookout that the CCC would build before winter weather set in; that is the usual size for a secondary station.[^3] The register puts the cab on a minor point one and a quarter miles east of Old Fairview Mountain and calls it an L-5 cab.[^1][^2] Panorama photographs were taken on March 20, 1934.[^3]

The register's photo caption reads 1933 to 1965, which does not fit the register's own date of removal around 1960.[^1] Firelookout.com says the cab was destroyed in 1960, and Rex's page lists a 1960 removal.[^2][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
