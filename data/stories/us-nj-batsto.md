Batsto Station is a fire tower in Wharton State Forest in Burlington County, New Jersey.[^1] Before the tower was built, the lookout here was a rooftop observation floor on the Batsto iron makers mansion, which the register says may have been used for fire detection as early as the 1840s.[^1] The site soon needed more height, and a 60-foot tower went into service in 1924.[^1]

The tower was later raised to a full 100 feet.[^1] The register says it is still in active service, though the sources do not say when the raise happened.[^1] The register gives the site's elevation as 70 feet (21 m), while Wikipedia's table lists both 70 feet and 100 feet (30 m) for Batsto Station.[^1][^2]

The New Jersey Forest Fire Service runs 21 fire towers statewide.[^2] Trained observers use a device called the Osborne Fire Finder to spot a suspected fire and fix its position.[^2]

Kayakers on the Mullica River reported one fire, which had spread to 50 acres when crews reached it.[^3] The same report says the fire was also seen from a tower in Batsto Village, but it does not name that tower, so the link to this lookout is not certain.[^3]

The register lists the tower as registered on March 15, 2004.[^1] Because that entry may be out of date, check with the New Jersey Forest Fire Service before planning a visit. The sources I reviewed did not confirm whether the public can climb it.[^1]
