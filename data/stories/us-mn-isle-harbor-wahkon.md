The original tower stood near the town of Isle.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list includes an Isle Harbor (Wahkon) tower in Mille Lacs County and marks it gone.[^2] The original tower near Isle was moved to Mille Lacs Kathio State Park in 1981, where it still stands.[^1][^2]

The same list has a Kathio State Park entry, described as relocated from near Isle and standing.[^2] Read together, the two entries suggest one and the same tower, though none of the sources spells that out.[^1][^2]

The tower sits on one of the highest hills of the Mille Lacs Moraine, a glacial ridge that acts as the natural dam for the lake.[^1] It is about 100 feet tall, and visitors can climb a stairway to the top for wide views.[^1]

A regional magazine notes that the tower has stood on its hill since the 1981 move.[^3]

The original site is listed as gone, and no source found describes what, if anything, remains there today.[^2]
