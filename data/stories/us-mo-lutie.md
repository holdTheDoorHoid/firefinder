The Lutie lookout sits on Holt Bald, a hilltop in Ozark County, Missouri.[^1][^2] The Missouri Conservation Commission acquired a 40-acre tower site there in fiscal 1950–51, then erected a new tower on it in fiscal 1951–52.[^1]

The structure is a steel frame on four legs, about 120 feet (36.6 m) tall, carrying an observation cabin.[^2] The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the station in 1953.[^2] Surveyors ran a traverse from the center of the tower's base to a triangulation station named Holt Bald, 58.87 feet away.[^2]

According to the survey, the tower stands roughly a mile south of where State Highway 80 meets County Road P. It also notes that Highway 80 became U.S. Route 160 on 1 January 1954.[^2]

No source describes the hilltop today.[^1][^2] The sources do not say whether the tower still stands, whether any part of it remains, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
