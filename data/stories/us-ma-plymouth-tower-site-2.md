The town ran a 90-foot iron tower on Billington Hill in 1889.[^2] The National Historic Lookout Register counts five fire tower locations in town over roughly 125 years.[^2] Also in 1911, the state assumed responsibility for fire detection and erected a tower on Reservoir Hill in Plymouth.[^2]

The former-sites register describes the Reservoir Hill tower as a 48-foot steel structure first operated in 1911.[^1] The register calls it a state tower, and says it was staffed until 1938.[^1]

The register says a dispute with the landowner led to the tower's removal.[^1] It now lists the site as a former lookout.[^1] No source read for this story describes what, if anything, remains on Reservoir Hill, or whether the public may visit it.
