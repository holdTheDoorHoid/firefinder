De Soto Lookout Tower is in Sumter County, Georgia. Tom Dunigan's list of Georgia lookout towers gives it an elevation of 351 feet and places it at 31.98711, -84.05796.[^1] A volunteer-edited OpenStreetMap entry carries the same name, gives the elevation as 107 metres, and tags the structure as an observation tower of lattice construction.[^2]

An Eastern US Lookouts list of Georgia towers includes a De Soto entry for Sumter County.[^3] The map behind the Dunigan list was last revised in March 2016.[^1]

No source I found describes the De Soto site today, including whether a tower still stands there or whether the public may visit.
