Bell Mountain sits in Blaine County, Idaho, about 12 miles southeast of Hailey.[^2] The Bureau of Land Management is the lookout's listed agency.[^1][^2] The BLM designed the two-story lookout house, and it went up in 1963.[^1] It had a concrete base and a frame cab.[^2][^3]

Mr. and Mrs. Louis Anderson were the known lookout staff from 1963 to 1974.[^2] A July 1970 Idaho Statesman item, quoted on another site, reported that the Andersons would return to the station east of Bellevue.[^1] A second site also names Thora Anderson among the staff.[^3] The lookout was last staffed in 1998.[^2][^3]

The lookout was demolished in 2014.[^2][^3] One site dates the removal to September 2014.[^1]

The lookout itself is gone.[^2][^3] A climbing guide updated in September 2020 says the former lookout site now has several antenna installations, and that a road reaches the top.[^4] The guide gives directions from ID-75 in Bellevue, along Pine Street and then Sharps Canyon Road, and recommends a 4WD vehicle.[^4] The guide describes the land as a mix of BLM, state and private ownership, and advises checking the BLM Fairfield map.[^4]
