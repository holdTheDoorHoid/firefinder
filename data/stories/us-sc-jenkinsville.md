The eastern U.S. lookouts record lists a fire tower at Jenkinsville, in Fairfield County, under the South Carolina Forestry Commission.[^1] That record also marks the tower as removed.[^1]

Statewide, lookout towers gave the public a place to report fires and relayed communications to firefighters working in the field.[^2] The state agency shut its network of fire towers on October 1, 1993.[^2]

No source we found describes the Jenkinsville tower site today, so we cannot say what, if anything, remains there or whether the public may visit.

[^1]: Jenkinsville - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/jenkinsville.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Pictures From Our Past, South Carolina Forestry Commission, https://www.scfc.gov/about-us/history/pictures-from-our-past/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
