The Springstead Lookout Tower stands in Iron County, Wisconsin, in the Town of Sherman.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list shows it as a standing tower.[^3] We found no source that says when it was built, who built it, or how tall it is.

Norman Pripps manned the tower for many years, according to the Town of Sherman's memorial page.[^1] The page spells the name "Springsted" in one place. It also places fire rangers atop tall towers in the 1930s and 1940s.[^1] It does not give the years of Pripps's service. After his death, residents paid tribute to him, and a carved sign was set at the base of the tower hill.[^1]

The National Register nomination for the Springstead historic site describes a complex of five buildings on 5.1 acres, including a French Canadian log cabin.[^4] The nomination does not mention the fire tower.[^4]

In December 2015 the DNR announced that it would eliminate its fire towers.[^5] We do not know whether this tower was part of that plan or who owns it today. We found no information about public access, so ask the Town of Sherman before going near it.
