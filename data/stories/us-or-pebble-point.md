Pebble Point is a lookout site in Curry County, Oregon, on the Siskiyou National Forest.[^1] It lies about 25 miles southeast of Port Orford, at an elevation of about 3,000 feet.[^1]

Fire Lookout.com gives the 1930s as its only date, with no detail, and calls the site abandoned.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Pebble Point with an unknown type and a status of gone, and places it at 42.51827, -124.09936.[^2] It is the only source we read that lists the site as gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Pebble Point Fire Lookout, Fire Lookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/or/pebblepoint.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Oregon Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/or/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
