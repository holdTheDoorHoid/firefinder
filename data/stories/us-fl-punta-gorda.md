The tower is 100 feet tall, an Aermotor design topped by a 7-by-7 observation cab, according to the National Historic Lookout Register.[^1] The register places it in Charlotte County, at the Florida Forest Service's Punta Gorda Forestry Station, and gives its elevation as 31 feet.[^1] The register does not give a construction date, so the year it went up is still unknown.[^1]

Brad Eells nominated the tower, and the register listed it on March 13, 2024, as US 1783, FL 28.[^1] The register says the tower remains in service for emergency fire watch, as needed.[^1] I could not find an account of its fire history, staffing or earlier life, so those parts of the story are missing here.

Today it stands on the grounds of the Punta Gorda Forestry Station.[^1] I found no source saying the public may visit or climb it, so check with the Florida Forest Service before planning any trip there.

[^1]: Punta Gorda Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/fl/punta-gorda-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
