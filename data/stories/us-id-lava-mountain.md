Lava Mountain is in Elmore County, Idaho, on the Boise National Forest, about 32 miles east of Boise.[^1][^2] The site sits at about 7,882 feet.[^1][^2] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page gives the same figure.[^2]

A log cabin was built there in 1925.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Lookouts page credits that date to Kresek, so agreement among the pages may not be independent.[^3] Its entry marks the cabin as gone in 1949.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page lists its location as 3N-7E-25.[^3] The firelookout.com page calls it a fire lookout cabin.[^1]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts listing calls it a hike-in site.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a ground-level lookout with the status gone.[^4]

None of the pages I read says what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors. Check with the Boise National Forest before you go.
