Badger Mountain rises to 11,294 feet in Park County, Colorado, inside the Pike National Forest.[^1][^2] According to the Former Fire Lookout Sites register, a steel tower 40 feet tall, with a cab 7 feet square, went up there in 1954.[^1] A 1958 image in the register shows Pike's Peak as seen from the tower.[^1]

The register dates the tower's removal to 1973.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site and the Forest Fire Lookout Association list both mark it as removed.[^2][^3]

The register added the site on September 18, 2009, and its links section includes the Colorado/Utah chapter website of the Forest Fire Lookout Association.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, still stands on the summit, and none says whether the public may visit. The register lists the U.S. Forest Service as the administering agency.[^1] Check with the Forest Service before you go looking for it.
