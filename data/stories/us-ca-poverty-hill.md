Poverty Hill, in Sierra County on the Plumas National Forest, got a timber tower in 1914.[^1] A photograph of the tower under construction that year is credited to the US Forest Service's Mark Swift Collection.[^1] The register describes a 25-foot open timber tower topped by a platform, with no observation cabin, and says the site also had a residence cabin.[^1] The Forest Service administered the site.[^1] The register gives the elevation as 5,517 feet, or 1,682 meters, and lists it as US 2007, CA 127.[^1]

The site joined the Former Fire Lookout Sites register on April 15, 2020, on the nomination of Brad Eells.[^1] The register gives no removal date.[^1]

No source describes the site now, so this record cannot say what, if anything, remains on the hill or whether visitors can reach it.

[^1]: Poverty Hill Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ca/poverty-hill-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
