Egg Lake, listed in the county records as Fire Tower 58, sat in the north end of the Tamarac National Wildlife Refuge in Becker County.[^2] The Civilian Conservation Corps erected it in 1935 as an 81-foot Aermotor ladder tower.[^2] The list places it under the Park Rapids forestry station, with the Minnesota Forest Service as owner.[^2] The refuge came later. The Fish and Wildlife Service says it was established by Executive Order 7902, dated May 31, 1938, about three years after the tower went up.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association marks Egg Lake as gone, with no date.[^1] The county list describes the tower in the past tense, and no source we read gives a date for its removal.[^2]

The county list also mentions a trail to the site and describes the walk as pleasant, especially in the fall.[^2] The refuge's visitor center is open seasonally.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the tower site now, or whether it is marked for visitors.
