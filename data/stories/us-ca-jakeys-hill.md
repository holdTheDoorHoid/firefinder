Jakeys Hill was a fire lookout tower in El Dorado County, California, at about 38.974 north and 120.796 west.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a tower and marks it gone.[^1]

The association files Jakeys Hill under the heading Unknown/Undocumented, with entries drawn from a book by Thornton, *Fixed Point Fire Detection: The Lookouts*.[^2] Its list gives no register number for the tower.[^1]

A separate index, Forest Lookouts, lists a Jakey's Point in El Dorado County.[^3] It may be the same place, but the index gives only the name.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^2]: Additional California Information, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/?p=490 (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Forest Lookouts - California, Forest Lookouts, https://californialookouts.weebly.com/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
