The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers includes a tower it calls "glynn haven." The list places it in Glynn County at 31.184444, -81.378056, and the source note for the entry reads "topo 1956."[^1]

Brunswick is the county seat of Glynn County, which sits in southeast Georgia.[^2]

No source I read describes what, if anything, stands at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^2]: Glynn County, Georgia, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glynn_County,_Georgia (accessed 2026-10-21).
