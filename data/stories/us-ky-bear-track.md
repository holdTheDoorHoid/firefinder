Bear Track was a steel lookout in Lee County, Kentucky.[^1][^3] The Coast and Geodetic Survey first observed the site in 1951. Its description places a steel tower on four legs at the site: 80 feet tall, gray, with an observation room at the top.[^2][^1] A survey line of about 27 feet ran from a triangulation station, also called Bear Track, to the center of the tower's base.[^1] The surveyors' directions ended at the top of a hill, reached by a gravel road.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts index lists the tower as "Lee County - State," and the tower's own page marks it as removed.[^3][^1]

The federal survey record still lists the station with a current position, but it does not say whether the tower stands.[^2] We found no source that describes what remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it.
