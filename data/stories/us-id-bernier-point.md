Bernier Point Lookout stood at an elevation of 5,600 feet in Shoshone County, Idaho, about 13 miles east-northeast of Avery, on the St. Joe National Forest.[^1][^2]

It was a log cabin tower, built in 1935.[^1][^2] A third history page, which credits its dates to Kresek, gives the same year.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places the tower 1.25 miles northwest of Quarles Lookout.[^1]

Each of the three history pages gives 1962 as the year the tower was destroyed.[^1][^2][^3] The association's list of Idaho lookouts marks the site as gone.[^4]

A September 2025 post from Idaho Fire Lookouts says the site sits immediately beside the road.[^2] The writers found cement footings there.[^2] One had been knocked out, and it seems to have carried a plaque that is now unreadable.[^2] No source describes anything else that remains at the site, and none says whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
