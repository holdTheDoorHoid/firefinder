Fleetwood Point is a summit in the Kootenai National Forest in Lincoln County, Montana, near Lake Koocanusa.[^1][^3]

A fire lookout tower once stood on it.[^2][^3] A lookout history page for Alexander Mountain places an 80-foot steel tower on top of Fleetwood Point in the 1930s, 1.25 miles northwest of the Alexander site.[^2] The same page gives 1942 as an abandonment date, right after the tower entry, but it does not say whether the tower or the station was abandoned.[^2]

The sources do not fully agree. The Montana lookout index lists Fleetwood Point with a patrol cabin and a crow's nest, and it mentions no tower.[^1] The Alexander page says a patrol cabin with a crow's nest was still standing in the 1980s, but it files that cabin under the Alexander station.[^2]

Today no tower stands on the summit. A 2026 trip report found the top viewless and said that all that remains is a collapsed hut.[^3] The writers reached Fleetwood Point by following FT-270, a forestry trail, west from Alexander Mountain, and they found no obvious trailhead where that trail meets the forest road.[^3]
