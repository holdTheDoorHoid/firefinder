The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas index lists a tower called Pinckney in Polk County, at about 30.76 north and 94.60 west, and marks it gone.[^1] The Forest Lookouts website has a short page for the same site under the spelling Pinkney, filed under Polk County and the Texas Forest Service.[^2] Both sources place it in Polk County, so we treat the two spellings as one tower.[^1][^2]

The Texas A&M University Press book *Fire in the Piney Woods* covers the state's lookout towers, the Forest Service and the Civilian Conservation Corps.[^3] Its publisher's description says the book looks at where the towers stood and how they were designed.[^3]

Bob Bowman, in his East Texas column, says towers such as the one at Lufkin were built by the Civilian Conservation Corps in 1936.[^4] That describes a regional pattern, not a record for Pinckney.[^4]

Today the index lists the Pinckney site as gone. No source we read describes what, if anything, remains there, or whether the land is open to visitors.[^1]
