Shawnee Lookout is a steel fire tower in the Wayne National Forest, off Sand Run-New Straitsville Road near New Straitsville in Hocking County.[^2] The Civilian Conservation Corps built it, but the sources disagree on the year.[^1][^2] The register says 1936,[^1] Historic Structures says 1937,[^5] and the Ohio Exploration Society, a Hocking Hills guide and Abandoned Online all say 1939.[^2][^3][^4]

The register gives the height as 110 feet.[^1] The Ohio Exploration Society and Abandoned Online say 100 feet.[^2][^4] Abandoned Online describes a 7-foot square cab at the top, and the Ohio Exploration Society says wooden steps once led up to the cabin.[^2][^4]

Historic Structures calls it a fire observation platform, staffed in both the leaf-on and leaf-off periods from the late 1930s to the early 1970s.[^5] The Ohio Exploration Society says it was manned until sometime in the 1970s.[^2]

The Ohio Exploration Society says the tower had fallen into disrepair by 1989, and was then restored and opened to the public, with visitors allowed to climb to the top.[^2] Abandoned Online gives 1994 as the restoration year.[^4] Wind damage in 2004 led to its closure, and the Ohio Exploration Society reported in 2015 that it had not reopened.[^2][^4]

Abandoned Online says the staircase came out in May 2023 because the foundation blocks were deteriorating.[^4] On May 15, 2023, the Forest Service closed the tower to all public access.[^6] The order cites Priority 1 structural problems in the foundation blocks, and it says the tower is red-tagged behind a fence. The order remains in force until the problems are corrected and the order is rescinded.[^6]

As of the Forest Service's last update on June 10, 2025, the order closed the tower and prohibited entering or occupying the area.[^6] The register says the tower was the first in Ohio to be listed and the third nationwide. Its registration date is October 30, 1990.[^1]
