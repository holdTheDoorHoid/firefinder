Otway is listed as a lookout in Carteret County, at about 34.79 N, 76.56 W.[^1] It appears on the tnlandforms.us list of North Carolina lookout towers, which the page says has 208 North Carolina fire towers taken from the NC FFLA site.[^1] The page's Google map was revised on 16 June 2021.[^1] The same page cautions that the list may also contain some observation towers and Coast Guard lookouts, so this entry's type is not confirmed.[^1] No source describes the site now.

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
