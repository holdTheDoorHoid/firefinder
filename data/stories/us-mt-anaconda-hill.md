Anaconda Hill is a lookout site in the Helena National Forest, about 17 miles east-northeast of Lincoln, Montana, at an elevation of 7,153 feet.[^1] A Forest Lookouts page gives its location code as 15N-6W-23.[^2]

The lookout dates to the 1940s, when it was a steel tower with a tent cabin.[^1] It was abandoned in the 1950s.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks the tower as gone.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, is left on the hill, and none says whether the public can reach the site. A support commentary for the Anaconda Hill roadless area, which covers 17,461 net acres, says hunters use the area heavily.[^4] That comment concerns the roadless area as a whole, not the old lookout.[^4]
