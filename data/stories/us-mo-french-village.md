The French Village lookout tower stood in St. Francois County, Missouri, on a towersite managed by the Missouri Conservation Commission.[^1]

The commission's annual report for fiscal 1957-58, quoted on the Central US Lookouts page, says a new tower went up at French Village, and that drive and parking facilities were built for the towersite.[^1] The report for fiscal 1971-72, also quoted there, says the tower gained a concrete block building that housed a radio repeater.[^1]

The Central US Lookouts page marks the tower removed, with no date.[^1] A Wikipedia article on Jacobs Creek, a stream in northeast St. Francois County, describes a mountain with the French Village fire lookout on it, at about 940 feet, where the stream rises.[^2] The article places the community of French Village about two miles east of that point, on Missouri Route Y.[^2] The article uses the present tense for the tower.[^2] The page marks it removed.[^1] Nothing we found settles which is right.

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
