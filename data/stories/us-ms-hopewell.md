The Hopewell Lookout Tower is in Itawamba County, Mississippi, on a site administered by the Mississippi Forestry Commission.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as a 100-foot Aermotor tower, and Aermotor built its towers from galvanized steel.[^1][^3] The register gives the site's elevation as 601 feet, or 183 meters.[^1]

The register gives no build date and does not say who designed or erected the tower.[^1] It also names no one who staffed the tower and no fire it watched.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register listed the tower on June 26, 2022, as US 1621, MS 14, on the nomination of Jason Johns.[^1] The Forest Lookouts index also places a Hopewell tower in Itawamba County, Mississippi.[^2]

The sources we read do not say whether the tower still stands, or whether it has been moved, burned or taken down.[^1][^2] The register's description also says the base could not be reached because of muddy conditions, and it gives no date for that.[^1] No access information is given, so anyone planning to see the tower should first ask the Mississippi Forestry Commission about the site.[^1]
