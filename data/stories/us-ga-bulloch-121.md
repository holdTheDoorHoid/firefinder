A statewide list of Georgia lookout towers includes one entry named "bulloch 121," in Bulloch County.[^1] The county sits in southeastern Georgia, and Statesboro is its seat.[^2] The list gives the tower an elevation of 161 feet (49 m) and a set of coordinates. Its only note reads "aerial imagery."[^1]

A separate entry named "airport" is also in Bulloch County, listed at 175 feet (53 m). By our calculation from the two sets of coordinates, the two points are about 0.57 km (0.35 mile) apart.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register says 29 lookouts are registered in Georgia. None of the names on its list is "bulloch 121," and none is named for Bulloch County.[^3]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
