The Look See Tree is a large white oak at the southwest corner of Arkansas Highway 83 and Pleasant Springs Road, in the Coleman area of northern Drew County, Arkansas.[^1][^3] The Forest Lookouts site lists it as Coleman (Tree).[^2] It is a lookout tree, not a tower.[^1][^2]

Its age is unknown, but by about 1930 it was one of the largest and tallest trees in the area.[^1] The Arkansas Forestry Commission used lookout trees like this one to supplement the small number of fire towers it operated.[^1] Rangers climbed the tree on climbing pegs, and a wood-framed platform was built at the top.[^1] A telephone line let them call in an emergency.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page places the pegs and the platform in the latter part of the 1930s.[^2] The nomination dates the tree's use to about 1930 through 1940. Those dates are hard to reconcile with the Forest Lookouts date, and we did not settle which is right.[^1] We found no named ranger and no particular fire connected with the tree.

After a fire tower took over that work, the pegs and platform stayed on the tree.[^3] The tree was added to the National Register of Historic Places on January 23, 2008.[^3] The Forest Lookouts page says it was still standing and healthy in 2010.[^2]

When the nomination was written, the climbing pegs were still readily visible, most of the platform had disappeared, and the telephone line had been removed. A white porcelain insulator from the line remained on the tree.[^1] We found no public access or visiting information. The nomination lists the property under private ownership, so treat it as private land and ask the owner before going near it.[^1]
