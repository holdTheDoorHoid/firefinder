The Two Medicine lookout, also called Looking Glass, stood on a level ridge with a view of Two Medicine Lake, about seven miles north-northwest of East Glacier.[^1] Its listed elevation is 6,288 feet.[^1] The site lies in Glacier County, on the Blackfeet Indian Reservation.[^1]

The lookout was built in 1937 with a concrete and frame cab.[^1][^2]

The sources differ on how much was lost. A Montana History Portal record dates the loss of the upper floor to 1940.[^2] The firelookout.com page says the lookout was blown away three years after it was built.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list marks the site as gone.[^3]

The most recent record we found is a photograph dated May 1, 1969, from the Glacier National Park Archives.[^2] Its description ends with the words "Remains seen here."[^2] No source we read describes the site today or says whether the public may visit it.
