The Georgia Forestry Commission built the Early County Fire Tower in the town of Blakely in 1952.[^1] It is a 100-foot (30.5 m) Aermotor tower with a metal cab that measures 7 by 7 feet.[^1] The tower was staffed as a fire lookout until 1987.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register lists the tower as US 661, GA 5 and records its registration on March 15, 2006.[^1] The register and a Georgia lookout list disagree on height and position. The register gives 283 feet (86 m) and coordinates of 31.3658° N, 84.9415° W. The list gives 267, with no unit stated (about 81 m if feet), places its point about 1 km from the register's, and marks its link to the register as uncertain.[^1][^2]

According to the register, the tower now serves as a landmark for the community and as a radio site.[^1] The register does not say whether the public can reach the site.[^1]
