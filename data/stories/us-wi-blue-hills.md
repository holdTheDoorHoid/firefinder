The Blue Hills Lookout Tower stands in Rusk County, Wisconsin, at about 1,715 feet above sea level.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as standing.[^2]

We could not find when the tower was built, who built it, or who watched from it. The Central US Forest Lookouts page for Blue Hills gives only a title and a county name.[^3] Until a local record turns up, the early history of the tower is unknown.

Wisconsin once had a large network of fire towers.[^4] WJFW reports that 119 towers were in service at their peak, and that 72 of them were taken out of service in 2015.[^4] Our sources do not say whether Blue Hills was one of those 72, so its current use is unknown.

We found no information about public access. Please do not climb the tower unless the landowner or the managing agency has confirmed that visitors are allowed.
