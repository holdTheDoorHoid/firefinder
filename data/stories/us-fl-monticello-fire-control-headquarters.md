Forest Lookouts lists this tower as "Monticello FC HQ," in Jefferson County, Florida.[^1]

The land was bought from Otto and Elsa Lorenzen on November 4, 1955.[^1] In March 1956 Aermotor of Chicago erected the 100-foot tower for $3,855.29, a price that included materials and labor.[^1][^2] Its cab measured seven feet by seven feet.[^2]

For more than 53 years, the tower kept watch over the woodlands of Jefferson County.[^2] A 2009 newspaper article reported that lookout positions in the state were closed in 1995 or 1996, and that Jefferson County phased out its three towers shortly afterward.[^2]

In August 2009 the Division of Forestry sold the tower at auction for $2,102, and the buyer had 45 days to move it.[^1][^2] A crew from Cross Environmental Services of Crystal Springs began taking it apart on Monday, September 14, 2009.[^2] Forest Lookouts says the tower was dismantled into sections and moved.[^1] By noon on September 16 only the concrete footers were left.[^2]

The paper reported a tentative plan for the parts.[^2] The top 40-foot section, cab included, was to become a viewing tower at Gum Creek Hunting Preserve, north of Greenville in Madison County. The two 20-foot sections were to be put back together there and used as a viewing deck, and the base would probably be lost because of its design.[^2] The same article identifies the 80-foot tower already at Gum Creek as the former Ashville tower, so the sources do not confirm where this tower ended up.[^2]

No source we read describes the site now, and none says whether the public can visit it.
