The Graniteville fire tower stood in Aiken County and was run by the South Carolina Forestry Commission.[^1]

The earliest record we found is a November 22, 1935 report. It said Charles F. Powell of Aiken had been elected to take charge of the tower from December 1, and that his family would move there next week.[^1]

An Aiken Standard report dated March 31, 1936 named the Graniteville tower among the towers already in place in Aiken County, alongside those at Wagener, Oskwood and Silverton.[^3]

In August 1939, the towerman, W.W. New, spotted a lightning strike from the tower. The blaze was quickly contained.[^1]

That October, Willie New, the former Graniteville towerman, was appointed county fire warden, and Leroy Hutchinson, a native of the Talatha section of Aiken County, was named towerman to replace him.[^1]

The eastern U.S. lookouts record marks the Graniteville tower as removed.[^1] Statewide, the Forestry Commission shut its network of fire towers on October 1, 1993.[^2]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the Graniteville tower site today, or whether the public can visit it.
