Flatiron Mountain sits in Lincoln County, Montana, inside the Kootenai National Forest.[^1] The Fire Lookout website places the lookout about 11 miles south of Yaak, and gives its elevation as 5,891 feet.[^1] The association's Montana list gives the coordinates as 48.67514, -115.66608.[^2]

The website dates the lookout's L-4 cab to 1933.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a ground-level structure, not a tower.[^2]

The website says the cab was destroyed in 1953.[^1] The association's Montana list marks the site as gone.[^2]

We found no source that describes what remains at the site today, such as footings or a foundation, or whether the public can visit it. Anyone planning a trip should ask the Kootenai National Forest about access before heading out.

[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
