Buck Peak sits in the Mount Hood region of Hood River County, Oregon, at about 4,751 feet.[^1] In August 1933 a pack train carrying lumber for lookout house construction went off a narrow ledge between Lost Lake and Buck Mountain, and one horse had to be shot.[^2] A panorama of the "Buck Peak Lookout" was taken on November 9, 1933.[^1][^2] The register says the Forest Service erected an L-4 cab on the site in 1934, while the 1933 items suggest that construction was already under way, so the two sources date the work to different years.[^1][^2]

Both the register and firelookout.com put its removal no later than 1950.[^1][^3]

None of the sources checked describes what remains at the site today. Firelookout.com places the site in the Mt. Hood National Forest.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list gives the county as "Hood River/ Multnomah," which differs from the register's Hood River County.[^4]
