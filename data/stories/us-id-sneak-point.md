Sneak Point Lookout stood in Clearwater County on the Clearwater National Forest, about 6 miles east-southeast of the Canyon Ranger Station, at an elevation of 4,550 feet.[^1]

Firelookout.com lists an L-4 tower there in the 1930s.[^1] The Forest Service list of Clearwater lookouts also gives an L-4 tower from that period, but spells the name Sneak Peak.[^2]

In 1940 a camp was set up on the eastern end of a spur that runs from the summit.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the same date for the camp.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts says it has not yet recorded any staffing history for the tower.[^3]

The tower was abandoned in 1941, according to firelookout.com, the Forest Service list and Idaho Fire Lookouts.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site Drive-Up.[^3] None of the sources we read describes what remains at the site now, or whether it is open to the public, so check access before you go.
