Browning appears on Georgia's lookout tower lists as a tower in Wilcox County.[^1][^2] The tower list gives its coordinates as 31.91272, -83.25581, and the page carries a Google map revision stamp dated 22 March 2016.[^1] The Forest Lookouts index gives only the name and the county.[^2] Wikipedia also describes an unincorporated community of the same name in Wilcox County.[^3]

No source I could reach describes the site today, or says whether the tower still stands or is open to the public. Do not climb a tower unless its access is confirmed.

[^2]: Georgia - Forest Lookouts, easternuslookouts.weebly.com, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/georgia.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: Browning, Georgia, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Browning,_Georgia (accessed 2026-10-21).
