The map places a tower called "Lookout Tower" in Chippewa, Michigan, near the Whitefish Point Light.[^1][^2] OpenStreetMap tags the feature as a lattice tower and as an observation tower.[^1] A map listing says the Great Lakes Shipwreck Museum sits about 340 feet from it.[^2]

The lighthouse has a longer record. Whitefish Point sits at the southeastern end of Lake Superior.[^3] Congress established the Whitefish Point Light Station in 1849, and the present light tower went up in 1861.[^3] The sources we read about the station do not mention a lookout tower.

We found no record of when this tower was built, who put it up, or whether it was ever used to watch for fires. The National Historic Lookout Register lists seven Michigan lookouts, and none is named for Chippewa or Whitefish Point.[^4] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists two Michigan sites, Bliss and Paulding, and neither is named for Chippewa or Whitefish Point.[^5] Without a source that calls it a fire lookout, we cannot say it is one.

We also found nothing about whether it still stands, who owns it, or whether visitors may go up. Ask the lighthouse site before going near it.
