The Williams Hill tower stood in Boone County, West Virginia, at an elevation of 1,900 feet.[^1] The register places the site at 38.025163° N, 81.677962° W.[^1] By October 1926 a telephone line ran from the tower to Seth, a community in the same county, and from there messages could be sent on through the Charleston long distance office.[^2] The West Virginia Division of Forestry administered the tower.[^1]

The register of former lookout sites lists the tower as removed.[^1] It was registered on September 16, 2008, on the nomination of Henry Isenberg.[^1] The West Virginia list of lookout towers also marks it as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can get there.
