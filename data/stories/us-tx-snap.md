The Snap lookout was a Texas Forest Service tower in Panola County, Texas.[^1] It shared its name with Snap, a farming community about six miles southwest of Carthage.[^2]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey first observed the station in 1958, and the federal datasheet lists its marker as a lookout tower.[^3]

In the mid-1930s the settlement had a store, a church, a graveyard and several homes, and in 1936 it reported seven residents.[^2] By the early 1990s, the Texas State Historical Association says, little was left but the graveyard and a few widely spaced houses.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list records the tower as gone.[^4] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains of the tower at the site, or whether the land is open to visitors. Please do not assume access.
