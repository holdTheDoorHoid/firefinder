The Hubbart Mountain lookout stood in Flathead County, Montana, about 8 miles southeast of McGregor Lake, at an elevation of 4,351 feet.[^1] The listing on firelookout.com gives a two-room frame cabin for 1956 and a 20-foot wooden tower for 1975.[^1] It lists the agency as Montana DNRC, the Department of Natural Resources and Conservation.[^1]

A history of northern Montana wildfire fighting also mentions the site.[^2] It says that in 1911 timberland owners and other landowners formed the Northern Montana Fire Association, a nonprofit to protect life and property, and that the association had three lookouts, one of them Hubbart Lookout in the Hubbart Dam area.[^2] The article does not date that lookout or say whether it was the same structure as the 1956 cabin.[^2]

The lookout is listed as gone.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list shows its tower as gone,[^3] and the firelookout.com listing gives 2001 as the year it was gone, without a cause.[^1]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors. Anyone interested should ask the Montana DNRC before going there.
