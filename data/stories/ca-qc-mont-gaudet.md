The Mont Gaudet fire tower stands in Lanaudière, Quebec, at an elevation of 764 m (2,507 ft), according to the register.[^1] The register believes it was built around the 1930s, but it is not known when it was abandoned.[^1]

The register gives the lookout's type as unknown and says its cab has been missing for a long time, while still describing the tower as metal-framed.[^1] The register lists the tower as administered by Quebec's ministère des Forêts, de la Faune et des Parcs, the forestry, wildlife and parks ministry.[^1] The tower was added to the register as CAN 13, QC 3 on 25 December 2022.[^1]

[^1]: Mont Gaudet Fire Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/can/qc/mont-gaudet-fire-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-20).
