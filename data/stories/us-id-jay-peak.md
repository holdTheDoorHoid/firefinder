Jay Peak had a 50-foot pole tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab, built in 1930 in the Kaniksu National Forest, about 12 miles south of Bonner's Ferry.[^1][^2] A 36-foot patrol tower stood 1.5 miles southwest on Sand Mountain, and there was also a camp 4 miles southwest on Elmira Peak.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the peak's elevation as 4,580 feet.[^1]

Both lookout-history pages give the same dates, 1930 to 1950, and the lookout was destroyed in 1950.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list marks the tower as gone.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^1]
