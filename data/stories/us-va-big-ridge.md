The Big Ridge Tower Site marks a lookout that once stood atop Big Ridge in Alleghany County, Virginia, inside the George Washington National Forest.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says the tower overlooked two springs resorts, Sweet Chalybeate Springs and Sweet Springs.[^1] The Virginia Department of Historic Resources dates Sweet Chalybeate's development as a commercial resort to 1836, and says it operated until 1918.[^4] The site sits at 3,289 feet (1,002 meters), and the U.S. Forest Service administers the land.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list marks the tower as gone.[^3] The register recorded the site on July 30, 2018, as US 1722, VA 24, on the nomination of Kristin Reynolds.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. None of the sources says whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
