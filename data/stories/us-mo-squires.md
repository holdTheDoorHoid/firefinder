The Conservation Commission bought five acres for the Squires Tower site on February 3, 1962, for $150.[^1] A 100-foot steel tower was erected there in 1962.[^1]

A 2015 management plan for the Douglas and Wright County towersites, issued by the Missouri Department of Conservation, says the towersites are kept mainly for wildfire detection.[^2] For Squires, the same plan says the Forestry Division uses the tower to detect fires.[^2] The plan places the towersite 0.25 miles west of Squires on Route JJ, and gives its legal description as Township 25 North, Range 15 West, Section 18.[^2] It notes no historical or cultural resources at the site.[^2]

No source we found describes the site as it stands today, and none says whether the public may go up the tower, so check with the department before visiting.
