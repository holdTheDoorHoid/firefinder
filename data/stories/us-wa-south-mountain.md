South Mountain is a former lookout site in Mason County, Washington, at 3,070 feet.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the Washington Division of Forestry/DNR as administrator, and it added the site on January 20, 2012.[^1]

The state forestry division put up a new lookout in the summer of 1956.[^3][^4] It had a cab on a 10-foot concrete block base.[^2][^3] The station sat on one of the highest points in the county, about seven miles northeast of the Matlock store.[^4] It replaced the Simpson lookout, which stood to the east at about 1,000 feet of elevation.[^3][^4]

Mrs. Roe Franklin moved to the new station from the Simpson lookout.[^3] The lookouts named on the page are Andy and Nina Harris in 1958, Clark and Irene Frost of Rainier in 1960, Ed Pierce in 1963, and Kay Loertscher in 1964, 1965 and 1966.[^3]

WillhiteWeb dates the cab's removal or destruction to 1976, after about two decades of use.[^3] The register says it was removed that year, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed.[^1][^2] In 2003, crews lowered the hill 15 feet to build new facilities, and the footings came out along the way.[^3]

The 2003 work is the last change to the site that these sources record.[^3] Our sources do not describe the site as it is today, or whether visitors may go there.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
