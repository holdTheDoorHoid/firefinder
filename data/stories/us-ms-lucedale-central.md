A federal survey crew described the Lucedale Central fire tower in 1964, when the Coast and Geodetic Survey took the top centre of its cab as a survey point.[^1] The station sat about five and a half miles west of Lucedale, in George County.[^1] The crew recorded a metal cab glazed on all four faces, a grey conical roof, and a tower roughly 100 feet tall.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts site gives the height as 120 feet, which conflicts with the survey descriptions.[^2] Both survey descriptions give about 100 feet.[^1]

In 1971 the Mississippi State Highway Department visited the station on September 15 and recorded its condition as good.[^1] That recovery note places the tower 4.9 miles west-southwest of Lucedale, north of a county road, on property the Mississippi Forestry Commission owns.[^1] The note describes a glass-enclosed cab atop four steel legs, roughly 100 feet high.[^1]

No source describes the tower or its site after 1971. The federal survey record still carries the station and marks it as a lookout tower, but the data sheet does not say whether the cab still stands.[^1] Research found no source on whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: NGS Data Sheet, PID BH2479, Lucedale Central Fire Tower, National Geodetic Survey, NOAA, https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=BH2479 (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Lucedale Central - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/lucedale-central.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
