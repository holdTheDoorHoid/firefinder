Sponge Mountain is in the Clearwater National Forest, in Idaho County, at 7,349 feet.[^1][^3] Two lookout-history sites and a Forest Service list give 1915 for a crow's nest in this area.[^1][^2][^3]

A 1981 history of the Lochsa Ranger District tells a different story.[^4] According to it, the Gold Meadows lookout was last staffed in 1938.[^4] The next year, a tent camp was pitched on Sponge Mountain as an observation station.[^4] An observer had moved there by 1939.[^5] The history says the point was staffed through the 1941 season and that no structure was ever put up on it.[^4]

The sources disagree on whether a structure ever stood on the mountain, and we could not settle it from what we read.[^1][^4] The Forest Service list and the two lookout-history sites say the site was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1][^2][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today.[^1][^2][^3] A trail guide says the Eagle Mountain Trail, a point-to-point route of about 14.5 miles through the Clearwater National Forest, passes Sponge Mountain, but it does not mention a lookout there.[^6]
