Pegleg Point lies in Shoshone County, on St. Joe National Forest land.[^1][^2][^3] The site sits at 5,739 feet.[^2]

Its first entry is from 1934, when an L-4 tower is listed about half a mile northwest of the summit.[^1][^2] Two patrol points sit half a mile and three-quarters of a mile east along the ridge.[^1][^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1952, according to both the firelookout.com page and the Idaho site.[^1][^2] The Idaho site says it does not yet have a staffing history for the lookout.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks it as a gone tower.[^3]

The Idaho site labels the location drive-up.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains there today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
