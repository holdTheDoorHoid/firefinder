The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers includes a tower named only "pierce," in Pierce County, at 31.37849, -82.12950. The list gives its elevation as 107, without saying whether that is feet or metres.[^1] The entry carries two numbers, BLL-65210 and gfc-804, and the page's key to its columns lists latitude and longitude, elevation and a GFC number.[^1]

The same county has a second listed tower, named bonneyman, at 31.25750, -82.29722.[^1] The page's map was revised on 22 March 2016.[^1] No source I could read describes what remains at the site today, so its current state is unknown. Check with the landowner or the county before going to look.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
