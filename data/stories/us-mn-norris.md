The Norris fire tower is in Lake of the Woods County, at the coordinates the Forest Fire Lookout Association gives for it.[^1] A 2008 state list of vacant or underused buildings places the tower under Forestry and gives 1934 as its year of construction.[^2] That list is the only source we found with a build year. It does not say who built the tower or what it is made of.

We found no record of who staffed Norris or of any fire it helped detect. No source we read describes its height or design.

The same 2008 list says the tower was scheduled for disposal, with an estimated value of $100.[^2] The list also notes that Forestry had 14 fire towers that were under-used and needed review.[^2] The Association's list still shows Norris as standing.[^1] We found nothing newer, so it is not clear whether the state sold, moved or removed the tower after 2008.

Norris Camp shares the name. The National Park Service's record for the camp credits the Civilian Conservation Corps and places it off the Norris–Roosevelt Forest Road in the Red Lake Wildlife Management Area.[^3] The 2008 list also has a footnote saying Forestry holds some buildings of historic interest at Norris Camp that are vacant or underused.[^2] None of the sources we read links the tower to the camp, so we do not assume a connection.

We found no public access or visiting information, so do not assume the tower is open to climb. Check with the land manager before going.
