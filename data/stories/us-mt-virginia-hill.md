The Virginia Hill lookout stood on a ridgetop in the Kootenai National Forest, about seven miles south-southwest of Eureka, in Lincoln County, Montana.[^1] The site is listed at 4,520 feet.[^1]

Work there began in 1936.[^1][^2] The firelookout.com page describes two crow's nests about a quarter mile apart on the ridge.[^1] The Forest Lookouts listing for Lincoln County gives the same year but calls the work a tree observation post.[^2]

The firelookout.com page says the site was abandoned in the 1940s and was still there in the 1980s.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists its type as crowsnest and its status as abandoned, not gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
