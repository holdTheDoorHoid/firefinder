The Abita lookout tower is in St. Tammany Parish, Louisiana. Wikipedia's list of Louisiana lookouts gives Mandeville as the nearest town, and its coordinates nearly match those on OpenStreetMap.[^1][^3] A National Geodetic Survey station description, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, places the tower about three miles south of Abita Springs, a fifth of a mile west of State Highway 59.[^2]

We found no build date. The same survey description was written in 1970 and uses the tower's top centre as its survey point, so the tower already stood by then.[^2] The description says the tower has four steel channel legs and gives its grey cab as 100 feet in height.[^2] A height of about 30.5 meters matches that figure, but the description does not say whether the 100 feet measures the cab alone or the whole structure.[^2]

Wikipedia says the tower is still standing. It says the state Department of Agriculture owns the site and uses it, and that it is unsure whether the tower itself is still in use.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page gives the site as St. Tammany Parish 7N-11E-Sec.13, which is a township and section reference rather than the name of an owner.[^2]

We found no staffing history, notable fires or public access information. Because a state agency uses the site, ask the owner before going near it, and do not climb the tower.
