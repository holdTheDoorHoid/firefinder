The Anglin Lake fire tower stands on the Tower hill at Anglin Lake, at 565 m (1,854 ft) of elevation.[^1][^2] The hill is in Great Blue Heron Provincial Park, which Saskatchewan established in 2013 from two older recreation sites and a large tract of Crown land, about 50 kilometres north of Prince Albert.[^2][^5]

A field note on firelookout.ca gives the tower type as heavy steel and its size as 24 m (80 ft).[^1] The same note says the cupola was taken off in the early 2000s to free up space for a thermal detection system.[^1]

A 2018 blog post says the log cabin on the hill was once a seasonal residence for Anglin Lake's fire tower worker.[^3] The same post reported that the park kept the cabin as overnight accommodation, on a first-come basis.[^3]

The October 2026 field note says the cabin remains in good condition and the tower area is closed off by a fence for safety reasons.[^1] It also reports that the site has no access restrictions and is frequently visited by campers from the park.[^1]

The park's page says the tower tenting sites lie 3 km of hiking from the trailhead, with parking at the Anglin bridge.[^2] Year-round access requires a vehicle entry permit.[^4] From 1 October 2026 the park runs reduced services, with firewood and pit toilets but no power, water or showers.[^2]
