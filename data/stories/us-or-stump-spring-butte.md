Stump Spring Butte is a former lookout site in Baker County, Oregon, about 15 miles southeast of Baker City, on the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest.[^1][^2] The lookout was a 32-foot platform tower, built in 1934.[^1][^2]

Staffing was thin in the late 1950s.[^3] In July 1958 the Baker Record-Courier, as quoted on the Oregon Lookouts page, said Stump Springs, east of Dooley, was staffed only in emergencies.[^3] A year later the Record-Courier reported that Brill Lee would man the lookout in the Burnt River area.[^3] The Oregon Lookouts page files both items under Stump Springs, which appears to be this same lookout.[^3]

The tower came down in 1963.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register and the Oregon Lookouts page both say it was removed that year.[^1][^3] The firelookout.com listing says it was destroyed in 1963, so the sources agree on the year but not on how the tower went.[^2] The site was added to the Former Fire Lookout Sites register on August 21, 2011.[^1]

Today, the register gives the forest and county, but no source I could find describes what remains at the site or whether visitors can reach the ground.[^1]
