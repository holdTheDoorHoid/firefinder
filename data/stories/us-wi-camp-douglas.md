In 1931 Wisconsin's forest fire protection branch was finishing four watch towers in its 11th district, and Camp Douglas in Juneau County was one of them.[^3] By May 1932 a newspaper reported Camp Douglas as completed,[^3] and both registers date the tower to 1932.[^1][^2]

Instead of the far more common outside ladder fitted with a cage, the 68-foot tower had internal dogleg stairs.[^1] The Wisconsin Conservation Commission developed both styles in the early 1930s, but relatively few stair-style towers were built.[^1]

Staffing was irregular and limited to spring fire season, ending in 2015 when Wisconsin shut down its lookout network.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register listed the tower on March 14, 2019.[^1] It was removed in 2023.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site as US 2119 on January 2, 2026.[^2]

The site is listed inside Volk Field Air National Guard Base.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains there today, and none says whether visitors may go onto the base.[^1][^2][^3]
