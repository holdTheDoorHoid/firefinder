The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Trout Brook Mountain tower in Piscataquis County, at 46.15008 north and 68.84415 west, and marks it as gone.[^1]

Maine by Foot files the mountain under Baxter State Park. Its guide describes a 3.5-mile loop that starts along the Five Ponds Trail from Trout Brook Farm Campground, and it says both ascents are steep.[^2] The guide gives the summit as 1,781 feet, with 1,092 feet of elevation gain, and calls the views from the top good. It advises walking the loop counterclockwise, which reaches the summit first and the ridge's views shortly after. It rates the hike difficult and lists pets as not allowed.[^2]

No source we read says what, if anything, remains of the lookout at its site today.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Maine Fire Towers, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/me/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Trout Brook Mountain, Maine by Foot, https://mainebyfoot.com/trout-brook-mountain-baxter-state-park/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
