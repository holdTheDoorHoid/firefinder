The Texas Forest Service owned a fire lookout tower at Elkhart, in Anderson County, Texas.[^1]

A January 15, 1993 notice, reproduced on the Forest Lookouts page, invited bidders to buy, dismantle and haul away the agency's fire lookout towers.[^1] It named the Elkhart tower as one to be sold. Bids were due to the agency's area forester in Henderson, Texas, by 9:30 a.m. on Tuesday, February 23, 1993.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas index lists the tower as gone, at coordinates 31.62167 north and 95.61891 west.[^2] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Elkhart, Forest Lookouts (quoting the Tyler Morning Telegraph), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/elkhart.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Texas Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
