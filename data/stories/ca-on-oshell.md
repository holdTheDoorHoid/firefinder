Oshell Tower is a fire tower listed at Little Shakwa Lake, near Stralak in Ontario.[^1][^2] It appears on Ontario's Fire Tower Lookouts list of still-standing towers.[^1]

The same list notes a 2013 ministry decision to take down many still-standing towers.[^1] The list adds that the ministry no longer looks after them, and asks visitors not to climb them.[^1]

A separate fire tower over Upper Shakwa Lake, in Algoma District, is recorded on Firelookout.ca as a 30 m (100 ft) tower.[^3] The sources here do not say whether that tower and Oshell are the same structure.[^1][^3]

No source describes the Little Shakwa Lake site today, including whether any structure remains or whether the public may go there. The still-standing list is the latest source found for Oshell, and it says nothing about whether the site can be reached.[^1]
