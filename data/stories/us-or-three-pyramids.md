The Three Pyramids lookout stood in Linn County, Oregon, on the Willamette National Forest, about 15 miles south-southeast of Detroit.[^1][^2]

An Oregon lookouts history says the 10 by 10 L-5 lookout house was constructed and staffed by the CCC in 1934, and the register and firelookout.com also date the L-5 cab to 1934.[^3][^1][^2] Panorama photographs were taken there on June 4, 1934.[^3] In July 1939 an Albany Democrat-Herald item described Pyramids as one of two emergency lookouts in the Cascadia district.[^3]

On October 27, 1967, the lookout was burned on site.[^3] A memo dated November 3, 1967 says the Regional Office gave verbal approval on October 25 to burn down three lookouts before AD-112s would reach them. The same memo names Pyramids, Tidbits and Chimney Peak as the three lookouts involved.[^3]

The register says the cab was removed in 1968, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year, a year after the October 1967 burning date on the Oregon lookouts page.[^1][^2][^3]

The register lists the site as a former lookout site, administered by the U.S. Forest Service.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
