Sunrise Point is a lookout site about nine miles south of Superior, in Mineral County, at an elevation of 5,421 feet.[^1] The page also names the Lolo National Forest, with no role stated.[^1]

The page dates a crow's nest and a tent cabin at the site to 1935 and calls the site abandoned.[^1] The Montana list agrees, placing it under "Lookout Sites / Non-lookout Structures" rather than under lookout structures, and gives its coordinates as 47.05853, -114.84575.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the public can visit it.

[^2]: Montana lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
