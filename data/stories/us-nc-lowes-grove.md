Lowes Grove appears on the North Carolina lookout-tower list, with a tower listed in Durham County.[^1] A 2023 WRAL article about a tower in north Raleigh says that Lowes Grove also had a fire tower, but it does not say where that Lowes Grove was.[^2] The same article describes the Raleigh-area towers as a radio network whose operators used triangulation to locate smoke.[^2] Citing a newspaper from the era, it also puts the number of women working in the area's towers at roughly 100, or three in four of the workforce.[^2] That figure covers the area's towers, not Lowes Grove alone.[^2]

A Durham history site, quoting the state's cultural resources department, says Lowes Grove was once a farming community in Durham County and is now surrounded by the Research Triangle Park.[^3]

No source we checked describes what remains of a tower at Lowes Grove today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
