Stony Creek was a lookout about seven miles southeast of Clarkia, in Shoshone County, Idaho.[^2] Clarkia, an unincorporated settlement, lies in the county's southwest.[^4] The Idaho Fire Lookouts site files the lookout in its St. Joe - Clearwater region, and both pages tag it with the initials PTPA.[^1][^2]

The first structure was a crow's nest, dated 1915.[^1][^2] By 1928 the site had a log cabin with a pole tower.[^1][^2]

The sources say the lookout was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1][^2]

Today the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list records the tower as gone.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, is left at the site, or whether the spot is open to visitors. The Idaho Fire Lookouts page files it as a hike-in site.[^1]
