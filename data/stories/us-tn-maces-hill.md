Maces Hill is a former tower site in Smith County, Tennessee, at an elevation of 1,086 feet (331 m).[^1] Tennessee Landforms lists a tower there under waypoint SMT002 and marks it removed.[^1] The waypoint sits at 36.3695, -85.9962.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register's Tennessee list does not include it.[^2]

None of the sources read for this story describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.

[^2]: Tennessee | National Historic Lookout Register, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/tn/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
