A lookout on Lemonade Peak in Shoshone County received an L-4 cabin in 1934.[^1][^2][^3] By 1962 the cab was an R-6 design sitting on a 10-foot concrete base.[^1][^2][^3] The R-6 measures 15 by 15 feet, and its flat roof overhangs the deck to shade it.[^4]

The listings do not agree on the forest. Firelookout.com places the peak in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, while Forest Lookouts names the St. Joe National Forest.[^1][^3] A Forest Service trail page puts the route on the St. Joe Ranger District.[^5] The Idaho Fire Lookouts page names one known staffer, Claude Irwin, for 1967, citing the Kellogg Evening News.[^2]

The lookout was abandoned in 1973.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com also lists the tower as destroyed.[^1] A patrol point sat 1.5 miles southwest, atop Trout Point.[^1][^2]

The old lookout site can still be reached on foot. Forest Service Trail 555 is a 3.3-mile single-track route that climbs gradually from its southern trailhead, which sits at the end of Road 748.[^5] The Forest Service says some of the finest wildflower displays are on the ridge in early June, and that wildflowers are the trail's main draw.[^5] The Forest Service also notes that access to the trailhead varies with the season and weather.[^5]

No source describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout itself.[^1][^2][^5]
