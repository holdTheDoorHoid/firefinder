The Ratlum Mountain Fire Tower stands on private land in the Canton area of Connecticut, on the high point of Ratlum Mountain.[^1][^2] We found no record of when it was built or who designed it.[^1][^2]

An August 2008 post on a backcountry hiking forum says the tower still stands. The post calls it a relatively new tower, which is why it does not appear on the topographic maps.[^2] A reply in the same thread says the property owner built it from Western U.S. fire tower plans and that it was never used officially for fire detection. The forum gives no source for that, so treat it as a claim.[^2]

In August 2021 a Connecticut blog about local towers said the tower can be seen from Route 44 in the center of New Hartford, and that the land is clearly signed as private property. The author says they may be able to get legal access through the family that owns the tower.[^1] The forum also says visitors are not welcome.[^2]

We found no record of anyone staffing the tower or of fires it watched.[^1][^2] Please treat the site as private. Do not trespass or climb it, and ask the landowner before going near the summit.
