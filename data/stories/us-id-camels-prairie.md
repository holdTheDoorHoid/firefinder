Camels Prairie sits in Boundary County, Idaho, about six miles northeast of Coolin, in the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^3] A camp was set up there in 1931.[^1][^2] A 40-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab followed in 1934.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists two known staff at the site: Bob Bond, with a photo captioned 1950, and Ryder Chronic, with a photo captioned 1956.[^2]

The tower was destroyed in 1962.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the tower as gone.[^4] One regional guide labels the site drive-up.[^2]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
