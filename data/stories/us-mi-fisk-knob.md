Fisk Knob is the highest point in Kent County, Michigan.[^2] It lies on the county's northern edge, a short way west of the three-county junction of Kent, Montcalm and Newaygo, and a small open park surrounds it.[^3]

In 1936 a fire tower about 110 feet (33.5 m) high was built at Fisk Knob.[^2] According to the local history, the tower gave wide views over Kent, Montcalm and Newaygo counties.[^2] A newspaper that spring had reported the tower was to go up near Kent City.[^1]

The tower was in use by 1939.[^1] In May 1939 a towerman at Big Rapids spotted smoke from a fire in Walker Township, 44 miles away.[^1] A towerman at the Fisk Knob tower then took a second bearing that fixed the fire's location, and the alarm went out to Walker Township fire fighters.[^1]

A county high-point guide says the parking area is reached by driving less than a mile south along Algoma Avenue, and it names a nearby tower as a landmark.[^3] Its author thought that tower might be a cell tower, so it may not be the 1936 lookout.[^3] No source describes what remains of the 1936 lookout today.
