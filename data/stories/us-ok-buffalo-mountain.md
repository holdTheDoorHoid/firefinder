A central US lookouts listing places the Buffalo Mountain lookout in Latimer County, under the Oklahoma Forestry Service.[^1]

A peak-bagging trip report describes the mountain as a county high point and an existing fire lookout site, so the lookout was still standing when its author visited.[^2] The author left Oklahoma City heading east, and Buffalo Mountain was the first stop of a highpointing trip over Easter weekend.[^2]

The sources do not agree on which hill this is. TopoQuest places a Buffalo Mountain summit in Pittsburg County, at 984 feet, rather than in Latimer County.[^3] Until the Oklahoma Forestry Service or a USGS map settles which summit carries the lookout, treat the location as uncertain.

The trip report is the only source we read that describes the lookout itself, and it gives no date, so it may be out of date.[^2] It does not say whether the public may climb it, so do not assume the lookout is open to visitors.[^2]
