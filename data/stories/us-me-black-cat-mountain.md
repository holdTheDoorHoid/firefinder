The state put up a steel lookout tower on Black Cat Mountain, in Piscataquis County, in 1919.[^2][^4] The sources disagree on its height.[^2][^3][^4] The 1919 annual report gives 48 feet.[^2] A newspaper item from August 1919 gives 42 feet.[^2] A 1920 inventory still listed 48 feet.[^2] A 1920 Maine Memory photo caption and the Maine History Online exhibit both give 60 feet.[^3][^4] The builders also put up a watchman's camp and fitted it with a telephone.[^2]

The station is said to have moved to Trout Mountain in 1931.[^1][^4] Maine History Online gives that move in a caption beside a photo of the Black Cat tower, so the Trout Mountain link is probable rather than proven.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association registered the site in 2008 and lists it as gone.[^1][^5]

No source we read describes the Black Cat Mountain site today.

The fire tower on Trout Mountain now is a different structure.[^6] The Nature Conservancy says a restored tower went up on the summit in 2020, and that it first stood in the longleaf pine forests of Alabama.[^6] The same page says an older tower that once stood on the summit has fallen into ruin on the forest floor.[^6] The summit can be reached on a walk of about 2.7 miles, where the tower stands 80 feet tall.[^7] The Conservancy urges care on the climb and puts the risk on the climber.[^6]
