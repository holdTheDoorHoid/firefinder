The Central US Lookouts page files the tower under Sayersbrook Bison Ranch in Washington County, and dates it to 1953, when H.W. Sayers put it up for use on the ranch.[^1] The ranch is in Potosi, Missouri.[^3]

According to the ranch's history, Frank Sayers bought the first 600 acres in 1928 with apple growing in mind.[^2] The same history says Skip and Connie began raising bison in the 1970s.[^2] The lookout page credits the bison herd and other visitor-friendly activities with making the ranch a tourist destination.[^1] The ranch's history says it grew past six square miles in the 1990s and turned into a tour attraction.[^2]

In April 2015 a press release announced a live and online auction, set for May 6, of about 1,678 acres.[^3] The release said the land would go up for sale in three parcels, or in any mix of those tracts.[^3]

The lookout page dates the auction sale of the ranch to May 2015, and says the plans for its future are unclear.[^1] The ranch's website, which sells bison meat, dates the operation to 1976.[^4]

No source we found describes the tower today or says whether it still stands. The lookout page lists the site as private.[^1] Do not go onto it without the owner's permission.
