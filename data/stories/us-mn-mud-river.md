The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Mud River tower in Beltrami County and marks it as standing.[^1] The association's entry gives only the name, county, coordinates, type and status. It does not name a builder, build date, height or register number, and it does not mark the tower as a former site.[^1] We did not find those details in the other sources we checked.

A travel blog that writes about Minnesota lookouts includes Mud River in its list of lookouts its writers have visited, with the year 2025 beside the name.[^2] The entry we found is only a name and a year, with no description of the site or the tower.[^2]

We also did not find who owns the land, or whether the public may enter it. Please check with the land manager before you go. Until they confirm access, treat the tower as off limits, and do not climb it on the strength of a list entry.
