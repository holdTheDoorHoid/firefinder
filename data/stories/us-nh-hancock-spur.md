Hancock Spur is a site near Lincoln in Grafton County, New Hampshire.[^1][^3]

A White Mountain history article names Hancock Spur, near Lincoln, as an example of an emergency lookout.[^3] The article says such lookouts were set up on strategic points when fire danger ran high, including areas with hazardous fuels from the 1938 Hurricane.[^3] They were linked by telephone line to a central dispatcher.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks Hancock Spur as gone, with an unknown type.[^2] A hiking list of New Hampshire fire towers gives NW Hancock Spur, in the Lincoln and Livermore area, as no longer standing, with artifacts.[^4] The list may refer to the same site, but it does not say what the artifacts are.

No source we read describes the site today or says whether it is open to visitors.
