The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a Trostel tower in Vilas County, Wisconsin, at 46.25243 north and 89.90834 west, and marks it gone.[^1] OpenStreetMap maps a point about 90 meters away under the name Troste Lookout Tower, a second spelling.[^1][^2]

Mapcarta places the tower in the Town of Winchester and gives its elevation as 1,759 feet, or 536 metres.[^3] The village of Winchester lies about two miles south of the tower.[^3]

We found no date for when the tower was built or when it came down.[^1] The association marks it gone. OpenStreetMap still shows a tower point, but a map point does not show whether anything stands there today.[^2] We found no description of the site now and no information on public access, so check who owns the land before going.
