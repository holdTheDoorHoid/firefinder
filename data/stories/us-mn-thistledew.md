Thistledew was a 100-foot (30 m) Aermotor stairway tower on a hill at the north end of Thistledew Lake in Itasca County.[^1] It was state tower number 33, and the Civilian Conservation Corps put it up in 1937.[^1] A 1959 survey note describes a steel structure on four legs with an observation room at the top, and gives its overall height as 125 feet (38 m).[^1]

The county list reports that the tower was bought and later passed to other owners, and that it could now be in Wisconsin.[^1] The list gives no date for that sale or move. A 2001 note in the same list says the station was destroyed.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list marks the Thistledew tower as gone.[^2] No source read describes what, if anything, remains on the hill, or whether the hill is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
