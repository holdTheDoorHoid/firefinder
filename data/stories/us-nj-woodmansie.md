Fire wardens from the area built a timber observation post at Woodmansie. The register says it was made of local timber, with a basic ladder on one side, and it dates the lookout to 1906.[^1]

The register also says many lookouts like this were built in the Pinelands at an early date, and that some went up before the State Forest Fire Service was founded on July 4, 1906.[^1]

The register places the site in Ocean County.[^1] Woodmansie, however, is a place name within Woodland Township, which lies in Burlington County, New Jersey.[^3] A post from the state forest fire service's Section B10 gives the same Burlington County location.[^2]

On September 7, 2015, a 1,012-acre wildfire burned in Section B5 of Woodland Township, and the same post says it was contained the next day.[^2]

The register lists the Woodmansie site among the former lookout sites in New Jersey.[^4] It was added to the register on July 24, 2016, on the nomination of Robert Spear, as US 1620, NJ 5.[^1]

No source we read describes what stands at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.
