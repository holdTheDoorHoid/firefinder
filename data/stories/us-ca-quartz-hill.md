Quartz Hill Lookout is on a summit at the northern edge of Scott Valley in Siskiyou County.[^3][^1] The register records it as administered by the California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection and says it was registered on November 27, 2016.[^1]

The early history is thin. A July 8, 1947 Siskiyou Daily News item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site, named Ray Arnold as working the lookout on Quartz Hill for the Forest Service.[^2] The item cites Glenn Feustal of the Klamath National Forest.[^2] I found no build date or builder in the sources I read.[^1][^2]

One undated trip report found the route hard going. The east approach was gated for private property and no trespassing about a mile before the summit.[^3] The west approach had a locked gate about half a mile short of the lookout.[^3] The writer found the lookout at the top closed up and could not tell whether that was permanent or seasonal.[^3]

Whether the tower still stands is unresolved in the sources I read. Firelookout.org's California list gives the site an FFLOS number, US 2004, CA 124, and the register names the Former Fire Lookout Sites Register as a sister site.[^4][^1]
