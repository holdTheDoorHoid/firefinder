The Mountain Hill lookout, also called Haskell Hill, was erected in Lincoln County in 1931, as part of a program the state decided on that fall.[^1] The program aimed to give districts without a lookout tower one.[^1] Its annual report lists five new 47-foot steel stations and one tower taken down, and it names a new lookout on Mountain Hill.[^1] The Lewiston Daily Sun reported a 47-foot tower there in May 1935.[^1]

In July 2007 the lookout was moved off its hill onto private land.[^1] The Maine chapter's 2007 report says a private party bought the tower in Jefferson, hired Rockport Steel to take it down, and moved it by barge to Lasell Island, where the buyer was setting it up again.[^2] The association's list of Maine towers still marks the Haskell Hill site as "Tower Gone."[^3]

Since the 2007 move, the tower has been on private property, and no source we read describes public access to it.[^1][^2] No source we read says whether the re-erection on Lasell Island was finished, or describes the original Jefferson site today.[^2]
