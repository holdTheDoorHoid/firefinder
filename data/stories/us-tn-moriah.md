Moriah was a fire lookout in Henderson County, Tennessee.[^1] Tennessee Landforms lists the site at 627 feet (191 m) and gives the tower the number 8-14.[^1] That is a district-and-tower number, and Landforms says its table uses numbers from a 1976 list of state forest towers.[^1]

Landforms marks the tower as removed, so it no longer stands.[^1] It does not appear on the list of towers still standing on March 26, 2014, in the National Register document for the state's fire towers.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2] With no standing tower, there is no lookout to visit.[^1]

[^1]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, Tennessee Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Tennessee Division of Forestry Fire Lookout Towers, 1933 to 1975, National Register multiple property documentation, National Park Service, https://npgallery.nps.gov/pdfhost/docs/NRHP/Text/64501237.pdf (accessed 2026-10-21).
