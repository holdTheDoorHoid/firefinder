Escudilla Lookout stood on the crest of Escudilla Mountain in Apache County, Arizona, within the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forests [^1][^2]. In January 1914 a newspaper reported a fifty-foot tower and a ranger's cabin at the mountain's high point [^2].

The Forest Lookouts page says an Aermotor MC-24 tower went up in 1933 [^2]. The register also dates the build to 1933, but it describes a 54-foot tower with a 14-by-14-foot cab [^1]. The Forest Lookouts page places a 54-foot steel tower with a CL-100 cab in 1965, as a replacement for an earlier structure [^2]. Peakbagging dates the rebuilt tower to the early 1970s [^3]. The sources disagree on which date fits that tower [^2][^3].

In 1915 a group of St. Johns Boy Scouts climbed the fifty-foot tower, where Forest officers explained how the protractor on the tower was used to locate fires [^2].

On June 6, 2011, the Wallow Fire swept over Escudilla Mountain [^2]. The register says the lookout was destroyed that June [^1], and Peakbagging says the same [^3]. The Forest Lookouts page describes extensive damage instead [^2].

The register recorded the lookout on September 2, 2009 [^1].

Today, the register and Peakbagging both describe the tower as destroyed, and none of the pages read here reports a rebuild after the 2011 fire [^1][^3]. These sources do not describe current access to the site.
