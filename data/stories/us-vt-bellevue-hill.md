Bellevue Hill is a summit in St. Albans, Franklin County.[^3][^4] In 1933 the state accepted the hill as a park and picnic ground and authorized $1,500 to landscape the summit and erect a lookout tower.[^2] The St. Albans Chamber of Commerce had first set out to raise $500 for a clear title to 35 acres on the summit.[^2] By April 1936 a steel lookout tower stood on the hill, and a stone caretaker's dwelling and public loggia were nearly complete.[^2]

A July 1938 newspaper item reports that vandals smashed windows and defaced walls, and that the house and tower had both been damaged. It also says the state forester had announced the tower would be removed.[^2]

The steel tower, with its 7-by-7-foot steel cab, was moved to Spruce Mountain, which sits within L.R. Jones State Forest in Plainfield.[^2][^1][^3] Sources differ on the year. The Register and the Forest Lookouts page say 1943, while the state forest page says 1944.[^1][^2][^3] The Register says the tower no longer serves fire detection and that the Vermont State Parks Division now maintains it.[^1] A visitor guide lists the Spruce Mountain tower as closed for maintenance in fall 2026.[^5]

Firelookout.org lists the Bellevue Hill tower as gone.[^4] No source I could reach describes what, if anything, remains on the hilltop, or whether the public can go up there now. A visitor who wants to see the tower itself should go to Spruce Mountain, and check the closure first.
