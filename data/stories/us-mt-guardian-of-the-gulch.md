Helena's Guardian of the Gulch is a wooden fire tower about 25 feet (7.6 m) tall. It stands atop the town's most prominent hill, built to watch over Last Chance Gulch.[^1][^2][^3]

Several huge fires in the 1870s were especially destructive, destroying much of downtown and many homes.[^2] Historic Montana says citizens built a first fire tower in 1868, and that fire later destroyed it.[^3]

Sources disagree on the date of the tower that followed. Historic Montana, Helena History and a 2025 news report say 1874.[^3][^2][^7] The National Historic Lookout Register and Wikidata say 1876,[^4][^1] and a firelookout.com page says 1869.[^6]

A watchman worked in the tower.[^2] A telephone installed in May 1878 let the watchman alert firefighters at the Park Avenue station directly.[^2] In 1886 the city added a guardroom, a larger bell and an octagonal cab.[^3][^2][^6] The bell weighed 2,121 pounds, and the tower had to be reinforced to carry it.[^2] The lookout was last used in 1931.[^4][^6]

A 1935 earthquake damaged the tower, and its big bell was displayed on a street until 1939.[^2] A firelookout.com page dates the quake damage to 1936.[^6] A lightning-caused fire in 1950 weakened the structure, and both bells were removed.[^2][^6]

An arson fire in August 2016 damaged the tower again.[^6][^7] In 2017 the city announced plans to remove and reconstruct it.[^2] After the plan drew public outcry, the tower was spared.[^6] A nonprofit called Friends of the Fire Tower formed in 2018 to restore and preserve it.[^6]

The city chose to save the tower and restore it.[^7] Dick Anderson Construction did the work.[^8] Reports differ on the cost, $424,383 in one and a bid of nearly $386,000 in another, and on the start, January or December.[^7][^8] Montana Free Press reports that nearly 70 percent of the tower's original lumber had rotted.[^7]

KRTV reports the work was completed in May and the tower was rededicated with a ribbon cutting.[^8] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's listing shows it as standing.[^5] The register says the City of Helena administers it, and Wikidata places it in Fire Tower Park.[^4][^1] No source describes whether visitors may climb the tower or its cab.[^4]
