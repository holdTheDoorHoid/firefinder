Brown was an 80-foot steel fire tower in Hillsborough County, Florida.[^1] A 1972 Tampa newspaper item, quoted on a regional lookout site, says it was identical to four other county towers and that all five were built in 1937.[^1]

Sources describe its position three different ways. A 1950 list places it at Bullfrog Creek on Road 41. A 1956 report places it on U.S. 301 south of Riverview, and the 1972 item puts it northeast of Ruskin.[^1]

In 1950 the operator was named as Laura Leavine.[^1] In April 1956 the county ranger said that Sylvia Beasley would run the tower, with her husband Frank as assistant ranger.[^1] They replaced a couple who had been transferred to Sarasota County.[^1]

By 2009 the tower was being offered for removal.[^1] An invitation to bid on removing it went out in September 2009, and no award was made.[^1] A second invitation followed in March 2010 for removal of the steel tower.[^1] In April 2010 the tower sold at auction for $860.[^1]

No source I could reach describes the site today.
