Smith Peak stands in Boundary County, about 18 miles northwest of Bonners Ferry, within the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^3] A camp with an alidade, a sighting instrument used in surveying, was developed at the site in 1925.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts credits Clarence Kauffman with staffing that camp.[^2]

In 1928 a Clyde Fickes L-2 Special cab went up, a version of the D-6 cupola that cost half as much.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts spells the builder's name Flickes.[^2]

Known staff from 1933 to 1936 include Sam Skeels, listed for 1933.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts ties Bill Edwards to a 1933 Bonners Ferry Herald item.[^2] Jonas Parshall is listed for 1934, a date Idaho Fire Lookouts extends to 1935, and Robert Mullen is recorded for 1936.[^1][^2]

By the 1950s the lookout was in ruins.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts, on a page dated September 16, 2025, lists the site as "Hike-In Ruins."[^2] The page does not say what remains, such as footings or the cab frame, or whether the site is open to visitors now.
