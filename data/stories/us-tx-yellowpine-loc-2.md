The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists two lookout sites called Yellowpine in Sabine County, Texas, labelled Loc 1 and Loc 2. Both are marked gone. This entry is Loc 2, at latitude 31.28204 and longitude -93.81119.[^1]

The Texas lookout list at tnlandforms.us has a single Yellowpine entry, at 31.28204 and -93.81191 in Sabine County. Its latitude matches Loc 2, but its longitude differs by a small amount.[^2]

Yellowpine itself is a community where Farm Road 2426 meets State Highway 87, about five miles south of Hemphill, inside the Sabine National Forest.[^3] Its post office opened in 1902 and stayed open until the early 1950s.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Loc 2 site today, or whether the public can visit it.[^1][^2][^3] Before going to look, check with the land manager, and do not enter closed or private ground.
