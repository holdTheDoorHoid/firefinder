The Eastern US Lookouts list of Florida towers includes Rose Creek in Columbia County.[^1]

The U.S. Geological Survey has an atmosphere monitoring site in the county named Rose Creek Fire Tower.[^2] A separate groundwater well record near Lake City is named SRWMD DOF - Rose Creek Tower.[^3]

No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1]

[^1]: Rose Creek - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/rose-creek.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Rose Creek Fire Tower, U.S. Geological Survey Water Data, https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/USGS-300610082363301/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: SRWMD DOF - Rose Creek Tower NR Lake City, FL, U.S. Geological Survey Water Data, https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/USGS-300612082363101/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
