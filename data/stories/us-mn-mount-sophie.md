Mount Sophie is a stairway lookout in Cook County, and the county survey places it west of Mineral Center.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as standing. It does not carry the asterisk the association uses for towers whose cab is gone.[^1] We did not find a build date, builder or height for the tower in the sources we checked, so its early history is a gap in our record.

A hiking blog visited the tower in snowy weather and found the steps sound and the landing boards all there. The post does not give the visit date.[^3] The blog is not an official source, and we did not find a statement about who owns the tower or whether visitors may enter the site. Please check with the land manager before you go. Do not climb it unless the public is allowed to.
