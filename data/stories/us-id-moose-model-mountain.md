Moose (Model) Mountain is a former lookout site in the Clearwater National Forest, in Clearwater County, Idaho.[^1] The site sits at 6,603 feet, about six miles northeast of the Kelly Creek Ranger Station.[^1]

A camp stood there in 1918. A log cabin followed in 1923, and in 1931 an L-4 cab went up on a log crib.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the cab as a two-story cab.[^4]

Sources agree the tower was destroyed in the 1950s, though none gives a single year.[^1][^2][^3] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. Idaho Fire Lookouts marks the site "Hike-In Destroyed,"[^2] and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone.[^4]
