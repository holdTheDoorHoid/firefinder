The Long Lake lookout stood in Florence County, on the Nicolet National Forest.[^1] In November 1937, Royal M. Nettleton, a junior forester on the Nicolet National Forest, was up an aspen tree working on a seen-area map for the Long Lake lookout when a hunter shot him.[^1][^2] He died on November 28, 1937.[^2]

Accounts differ on which lookout he was working on.[^1][^2] A June 1938 Rhinelander Daily News item, quoted by Central US Lookouts, says he was trying to get a bearing on the Jones Dam tower from that tree.[^2] The same item says the Nicolet forest then had 16 fire lookout towers.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Long Lake tower as gone.[^3] OpenStreetMap still has a point named Long Lake Lookout Tower, tagged as an observation tower, but that point carries no status, so it does not show whether anything stands.[^4]

No source describes what remains at the site, or whether it is open to the public.
