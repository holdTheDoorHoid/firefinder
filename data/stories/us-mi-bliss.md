The Bliss lookout stood in Emmet County, Michigan, at 950 feet (290 m), and the Michigan Department of Conservation administered it.[^1] The site was added to the Former Fire Lookout Sites register in March 2014.[^1]

A 1982 USGS topographic map of the Bliss area, reproduced on Pastmaps, names a Bliss Lookout Tower among its landmarks, which suggests the tower was standing by then.[^3]

The register says the lookout was probably taken down around 2008, and a communications tower now stands on the site.[^1] A regional list of former lookouts marks Bliss as removed but gives no date.[^2]

No source I found says whether visitors can reach the spot, so anyone planning a trip should check first.
