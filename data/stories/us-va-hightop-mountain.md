Hightop Mountain, in Greene County, Virginia, is the highest point in the South District of Shenandoah National Park.[^2][^3] A WillhiteWeb list of Shenandoah lookouts names a Hightop Lookout.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list marks the Hightop tower as gone.[^5]

The first tower on the summit burned down around 1937 or 1938, according to a site history that says it is based on memory.[^1] Meanwhile, a temporary tower stood until a new one could go up.[^1] By 1944 a third tower was on the summit, a 41-foot metal MC 39 tower with five sets of stairs.[^1] By the late 1950s the park was no longer regularly staffing its five lookout towers.[^4] By the early 1960s it had started taking them apart, scrapping them or selling them.[^4]

The site says the tower's footings still stand in the woods on the summit as of 2026.[^1] It lists Hightop as a former lookout.[^1] The summit is a short side trail, about a tenth of a mile, off the Appalachian Trail.[^1] A parking area at Skyline Drive mile 66.7 serves the Hightop Summit Trailhead, and visitors hike the summit trail from there.[^2]
