Calx Mountain Lookout stands about 24 miles southeast of Libby in Lincoln County, Montana.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^3]

The first lookout here was a platform built in 1921, a crow's nest set up in a tree. It is still there about an eighth of a mile south of the present lookout, though it has fallen down.[^1][^2] In 1934 a 40-foot pole tower of the L-4 type went up on the middle of three summits.[^1] The sources say the 1934 tower was apparently destroyed in 1974.[^1][^2]

The steel tower used today came from somewhere else. The Register says it was moved here in 1984 from Johnson Peak, near Whitefish.[^1][^2] Neither source says why it was moved.

The sources say the lookout has been staffed in recent years, but neither gives a year. I found no record of particular fires it watched.[^1][^2]

The Register lists the lookout as registered on January 9, 2021, as US 1507, MT 85, on a nomination by Kyle Stetler. It gives the elevation as 6,489 feet (1,978 meters). The other page gives 6,480 feet, so the two differ slightly.[^1][^2] The Register names the US Forest Service as administrator, and the other page credits the Kootenai National Forest and the Montana DNRC.[^1][^2] Neither page says whether visitors may climb the tower or stay in it. Ask the Forest Service before any visit, and do not climb the tower if it is closed.
