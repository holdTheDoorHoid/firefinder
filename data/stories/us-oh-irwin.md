The Irwin Tower Site is in Lucas County, Ohio, and the register lists its elevation as 675 feet (206 m).[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Ohio list marks the tower as gone and gives its position as 41.62872 north, 83.78277 west.[^2]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site on September 3, 2014, as US 1561, OH 7.[^1] The register's own website calls it the National Register of Former Lookout Sites and says the Forest Fire Lookout Association backs it, with help from the American Resources Group.[^3] The same website says the country once had over 8,000 fire lookouts across 49 states.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to visitors.
