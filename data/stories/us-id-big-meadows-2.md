Big Meadows is a crow's nest lookout in Bonner County, Idaho, about 17 miles north-northwest of Priest River, in the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^3] Its elevation is 3,543 feet (1,080 m).[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts lists the site under the code 59N-5W-33.[^3]

A crow's nest and a camp were established on the site in the 1920s.[^1][^2][^3] By the 1980s both were in ruins, and the last documented record of those ruins dates from that decade.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts attributes its 1920s entry to Kresek.[^3]

Firelookout.com lists the site as R.I.P., and Idaho Fire Lookouts tags it as abandoned.[^1][^2]

Today, no source describes what, if anything, remains at the site.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it as a hike-in site.[^2] Its photo there is credited to The Fire Lookout Museum.[^2]
