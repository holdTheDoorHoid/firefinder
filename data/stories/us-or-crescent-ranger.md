The Crescent Ranger Station tower was put up in 1936 at the station on the Deschutes National Forest, in Klamath County, Oregon.[^1] A letter from the forest supervisor to the regional office, dated October 15, 1936 and sent in answer to a questionnaire, describes it as a ring-connected tower 54 feet tall.[^1]

The record counts 175 E.C.W. man-hours to dig the holes, build the forms and pour the concrete, and another 175 to raise and finish the tower.[^1] One extra E.C.W. foreman worked on the job for 12 days.[^1]

The tower also served as an emergency observation post.[^1]

The page gives nothing on what remains at the site today, whether a tower still stands there, or whether the public can reach it.[^1]
