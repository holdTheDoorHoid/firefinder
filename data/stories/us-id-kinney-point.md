Kinney Point is in Adams County, Idaho, about 25 miles southwest of Riggins, on the Payette National Forest.[^1]

The site was a camp in the 1920s.[^1] An L-4 cab went up there in 1932, according to Forest Lookouts and firelookout.com.[^2][^1]

The lookout burned in 1951.[^2] Firelookout.com lists it as gone that year, and Idaho Fire Lookouts lists 1951 as destroyed.[^1][^3]

A July 1953 newspaper item said more than 1,000 people were expected at Kinney Point for a Hells Canyon celebration.[^2] The regional head of the Forest Service was to be the main speaker.[^2]

Today the spot is a Forest Service interpretive site overlooking Hells Canyon, with a large wooden sign about the place.[^4] The spur road is rocky and rough, and the Forest Service advises that passenger cars are not recommended.[^4] Restrooms and potable water are not available there.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, of the lookout itself remains.
