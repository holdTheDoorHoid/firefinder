French Mountain lies about 4 miles east of Pierce, in Clearwater County, Idaho, on the Clearwater National Forest, at 5,327 feet.[^1][^2] The Forest Service list places it in section 33, township 37 north, range 6 east.[^2]

A 40-foot pole L-4 tower went up there in 1932, and the site also had two patrol crow's nest platforms.[^1][^2][^3] The lookout was abandoned in 1948.[^1][^2][^3]

firelookout.com's heading dates the lookout from 1932 and leaves the end year as a question mark.[^1] No source I read says the tower was taken down, and none describes what remains on the mountain today. A recreation listing for a French Mountain summit in Clearwater County mentions no structure.[^4]
