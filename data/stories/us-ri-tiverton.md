The Tiverton tower stood behind Tiverton High School, on High School Hill in Newport County, Rhode Island.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says it was built there in 1964 and that the Rhode Island Department of Environmental Management administered it.[^1] It lists the site at 270 feet (82 m).[^1]

The tower had an earlier life in Portsmouth.[^3] A May 1963 notice reprinted on the Forest Lookouts site says the governor had signed authority to condemn an acre of land off Brayton Road in Tiverton for relocating the Portsmouth fire tower.[^2] The Quaker Hill register says that tower was removed in 1963 and re-erected on High School Hill in Tiverton.[^3]

Read together, the 1963 notice and the 1964 build date suggest a moved tower rather than a new one. No source says so outright, so the 1964 date may mark the re-erection rather than a new build.[^1][^2][^3]

The register says the tower was demolished in approximately 2005.[^1] The Forest Lookouts Association lists the tower as gone.[^4] No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the High School Hill site, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^4]
