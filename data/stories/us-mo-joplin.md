The Joplin lookout stood in northern Newton County, Missouri, in the New Hope community, about three and a half miles north of Racine [^1]. The Central US Lookouts page lists the site under the names Joplin, Racine and Redding Mill [^1].

In July 1947 the Neosho Daily Democrat reported that building the Racine tower had stalled for want of money [^1].

The state's annual report for fiscal 1948–49 records the purchase of a 10-acre towersite [^1]. In October 1948 the same paper said fire-control assistance would be available from the site to woodland owners in northwest Newton County, and that a 65-foot observation tower was planned for prompt construction [^1].

A 1949–50 state manual gave the site as 10 acres, with the Missouri Conservation Commission as owner [^1].

On August 20, 1964, the Neosho Daily News reported that the old 65-foot wooden tower had been razed the day before, and that crews would start building a 100-foot steel replacement near New Hope within the week [^1].

The state's annual report for fiscal 1964–65 records a 100-foot steel tower going up at the Joplin Towersite [^1].

The Central US Lookouts page now lists the tower as removed [^1].

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Joplin - Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/joplin.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
