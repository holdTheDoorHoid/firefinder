The Mount Pleasant lookout stands in Caldwell Parish, Louisiana, on State Route 4 about seven miles from Columbia.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register says it is believed to have been built during the 1920s, so that date is an estimate rather than a documented build year.[^1]

Two newspapers from the late 1950s mention it.[^2] A 1957 rainfall report lists the Mt. Pleasant fire tower in Caldwell Parish with 48.31 inches recorded for 1956.[^2] In 1959 the Caldwell Watchman reported that the Louisiana Forestry Commission tower saw an increase in visitors that January.[^2]

According to the Register, the tower was staffed until the early 1980s.[^1] To deter vandals, the lower landings were taken off, and the Register describes the tower as otherwise in good condition.[^1] The Register listed it on December 6, 2017, and names the Louisiana Department of Agriculture & Forestry as its administrator.[^1]

We found no newer condition report and no visitor rules. The lower landings are gone.[^1] Check with the Louisiana Department of Agriculture and Forestry before planning any visit, and do not climb the tower unless the agency confirms access.
