The Clear Lake fire tower stood on Clear Lake Mountain in Township 10, Range 11 of Piscataquis County, Maine.[^1][^2] In April 1929 a Presque Isle newspaper reported that a new lookout station would be erected that summer, a 24-foot steel tower on the mountain in the Musquacook District.[^1] A 1931 Maine Forest Service panoramic map is centered on the tower, so it was standing by then.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's 2017 Maine chapter report records the Clear Lake lookout in T10 R11 as removed that year, one of four changes listed for 2017.[^3] The association's list of Maine towers now marks the site as "Tower Gone."[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the public can reach it.
