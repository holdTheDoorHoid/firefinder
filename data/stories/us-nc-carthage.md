Moore County once had a fire lookout near Carthage.[^1] A North Carolina lookout tower list places it at Carthage, Moore County, at 35.35305, -79.40083.[^1] Before the tower, the Carthage courthouse held a temporary lookout post from 1930 to 1932.[^2]

The North Carolina Forest Service put up a 100-foot Aermotor tower near Carthage in 1951.[^3] Its cab measured 7 feet by 7 feet.[^3] According to a photo caption, the Forest Service staffed the tower after the move, until the late 1990s.[^4] A 1990 move carried the tower to its present location.[^3][^4]

It now stands on Burkemont Mountain, which lies in Burke County.[^4] The National Historic Lookout Register lists it as Walker Top Lookout, on a private tract within South Mountains State Park.[^3] No source I could open says the public may climb it. I also could not find a source describing what, if anything, remains at the original Carthage site.
