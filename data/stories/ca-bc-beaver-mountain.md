Beaver Mountain is a lookout site in the Central Kootenay region of British Columbia.[^1] The Canada's Fire Lookouts page gives its position as latitude 49.15 and longitude 117.433, with an elevation of 1959 and no unit.[^1]

The earliest dated item on the page is from June 1939. It says one project under the government's summer forest programme would employ a ten-man crew on the trail to the Beaver Mountain lookout.[^1] That item is about the trail, so it does not date the tower itself.[^1]

The page records that vandals burned the lookout in 1999.[^1] No source read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Beaver Mountain, Canada's Fire Lookouts (Ron Kemnow), https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/beaver-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
