Swayne Hill, which the Forest Lookouts site also writes as Swain Hill, lies in Butte County, within the Plumas National Forest.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list places a tower there at 39.61650 north and 121.24076 west.[^2]

The earliest dated item we found is an August 1943 notice from the Feather River Bulletin, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page. It places two people on Swayne Hill that month, and names one of them as a student.[^1]

The same California list now marks the tower as gone.[^2] Neither source describes what, if anything, remains on the hill today, and neither says whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

A 2023 peakbagging table of California lookouts lists the site too, but without the "(LO site)" label its key applies to removed buildings or sites of unknown status.[^3]

[^2]: California Lookouts by County, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/ca/ca-co/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: California Fire Lookouts, Table 2, Peakbagging, http://peakbagging.com/Peak%20Lists/CA_Lookout2.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
