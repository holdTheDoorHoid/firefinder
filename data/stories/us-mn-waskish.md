A steel fire tower went up at Waskish, in Beltrami County, in 1938.[^2][^1] The county report gives its height as 99 feet 9 inches, while the Forest Lookouts page gives 100 feet.[^1][^2] It replaced an earlier Waskish tower, which a 1954 survey note says had been moved to another location.[^1]

A 1966 survey note describes the 1938 tower as a self-supporting, four-legged steel frame with a viewing room at its summit, made by Bethlehem Steel Co.[^1]

By 1966 the tower had been sold, and the same note says it stood near Bemidji as the Cloverleaf tower.[^1] The county report calls Cloverleaf the relocated, privately owned second Waskish tower, located southeast of Bemidji.[^1] The 1966 note also says footings remained at the original Waskish site.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Waskish site as gone and Cloverleaf as standing.[^3]

Cloverleaf is privately owned, and the county report's footnote says it is not accessible to the public.[^1] Apart from the Forest Fire Lookout Association's listing of the site as gone, no source I read describes the original Waskish site today.[^3] The last detail recorded there is the 1966 footings.[^1]
