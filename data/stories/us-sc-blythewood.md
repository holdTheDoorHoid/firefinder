The state's report for July 1938 to June 1939 appears to list this tower under the spelling "Blythwood", which drops the e, and says new steel was erected there.[^1] The same report credits the Civilian Conservation Corps with building additional towers and telephone lines that year, free of charge to the state and to protective associations.[^1]

A TopoQuest map record lists a Blythewood Lookout Tower in Richland County, at 488 feet above sea level.[^2]

The Eastern US Lookouts page marks the tower as removed, with no date given.[^1]

The TopoQuest record gives a location and an elevation, but no date and no condition. No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
