Waccamaw is a lookout site in Columbus County, North Carolina, that a Forest Lookouts page describes as a steel tower.[^1][^2] The page gives the tower's height as 93 feet and says it was built in 1929, five miles north of Wananish.[^1] It cites a 1928 to 1930 biennial report from the state's Department of Conservation and Development for that date, and it names the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources as the agency.[^1]

A North Carolina tower list on tnlandforms.us places the Waccamaw at 34.36527, -78.46361, in Columbus County.[^2]

North Carolina's first state-built fire tower went up in Harnett County in 1926, according to the Forest History Society's inventory of the state forest service's photographs.[^3] The same inventory credits the Civilian Conservation Corps with helping put up 52 new fire towers from 1933 to 1938.[^3]

No source I could read describes the site today, including whether the tower still stands.
