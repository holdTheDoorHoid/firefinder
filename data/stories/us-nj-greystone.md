Greystone Station stands on Union Hill in Denville, Morris County.[^2] Among the fire towers in northern New Jersey, the registry says this one is the tallest.[^1]

The New Jersey Forest Fire Service built the tower in 1937.[^1] Its upper part, a 47-foot section with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab, came from an older Edison fire tower from the 1920s. The Aermotor Co. provided an extended base, which lifted the rebuilt structure to 92 feet.[^1] Wikipedia's state list gives the height as 92 feet (28 meters) and the elevation as 909 feet.[^2]

The registry says the tower remains in active service, and it recorded the site on March 3, 2004.[^1] The sources do not describe staffing hours, notable fires or visiting rules, so contact the Forest Fire Service before planning a trip.
