The Webster Tower Site is on Wood Hill in Worcester County, Massachusetts.[^1] The register gives its elevation as 915 feet, or 279 meters.[^1] It describes the tower that stood there as wooden, and that is the only detail it gives about the structure.[^1]

The site was registered on September 3, 2008, on the nomination of Henry Isenberg.[^1] The register files it among its former lookout sites, so it is not listed as an active lookout.[^1]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains on the site today. We also found nothing that says whether the public may visit it, so check access before going to Wood Hill.

[^1]: Webster Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ma/webster-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
