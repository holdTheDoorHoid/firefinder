The tower that is now Fosback Lookout was first built on Monumental Mountain, on the summit between Colville and Rice in Stevens County, Washington.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's record describes a 60-foot pole tower with a cabin from 1945, replaced in 1965 by a 40-foot timber tower with a live-in cab built by the Washington Department of Natural Resources.[^2] The Register gives the same 1965 date and says the new tower took the place of a state tower from 1945.[^1]

A 1945 item quoted by the Forest Lookouts page reports that the first stage of the road to the lookout was finished and that a summit observation cabin was in place, with Dan Reeder of Rice working as the lookout.[^3] A 1952 item says the lookout was to be manned in the near future.[^3] The Association says the tower was last staffed in 1980, while the Register says it was staffed until the mid-1980s.[^2][^1]

The Association says the tower came down in 1996 so that a second radio tower could go on the summit.[^2] The Register says it was sold that year to Rod Fosback, a member of the Forest Fire Lookout Association, who moved it to his own woodland.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page also gives 1996 for the sale.[^3] The firelookout.com page says Fosback rescued the tower and placed it on his hillside property in 1998.[^4] The sources give both years and do not explain the gap.

What stands today is the top section.[^1][^2] According to the Register, the cab is 14 by 14 feet with a flat top and a catwalk, on a 10-foot wood tower.[^1] The Association says Fosback rebuilt the top 10 feet of the tower with its cab on his land east of Colville.[^2] Those accounts agree on the 10 feet but not on the original height, which the Association gives as 40 feet.[^2]

The Register places the lookout on private land above the Little Pend Oreille River.[^1] The Register adds that the Fosback family would staff it as volunteers when fire conditions call for it.[^1] None of the sources describes a visiting arrangement, so treat it as private property and ask the owners before any visit.
