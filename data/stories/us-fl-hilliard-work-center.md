The Forest Lookouts website lists a Hilliard Work Center in Nassau County, Florida, and names FDACS as its agency.[^1] FDACS is the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services, and the Florida Forest Service works within it.[^3]

A photograph on Flickr, taken on 6 February 2023, is captioned as the Hilliard Forestry Station fire tower and names the Florida Forest Service in its title.[^2]

A local government directory gives the work center's address as 3742 Clint Drive in Hilliard.[^4]

No source found describes the site today. Whether the tower still stands, and whether visitors may go up it, is not stated anywhere this research reached. Because the site is a state work center, ask the Florida Forest Service before visiting, and do not climb any tower without permission.
