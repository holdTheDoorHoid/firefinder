Forks, titled Forks (Hallowat Creek) on one site, was a fire lookout in Flathead County, Montana.[^1][^2] A Forest Lookouts page says it is about nine miles west of Big Creek and that its name reflects its location at the junction of Big Creek and Hollowat Creek.[^2] The page spells the creek two ways, Hallowat in its title and Hollowat in its text.[^2]

Its design and date come from firelookout.com, which dates the lookout to 1933 and describes a 30-foot pole tower of the L-4 type.[^1] That page also places the lookout in Glacier National Park, 13 miles north of Whitefish.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page and a 1973 newspaper item put it in the Flathead National Forest instead.[^2]

In August 1951 a Hungry Horse News item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, reported a recent repair visit to the lookout by "Toad" Paullin and Dispatcher Bob Jordan.[^2] The same item made a general remark that wind and deep snow harm towers sitting unstaffed for years.[^2]

In September 1973 a lightning storm caused 25 new fires in the area, according to the Daily Inter Lake.[^2] The largest of them was in the Forks Lookout area of the Glacier View Ranger District, and only one topped an acre.[^2]

Firelookout.com gives 1977 as the year the lookout was destroyed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it only as gone, with no date.[^3]

The Association still lists the site as gone.[^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains there, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3] Anyone planning a visit should check with the Flathead National Forest first.
