The Santa Fe National Forest considered a lookout at Puye Ruins in 1927, to replace Guaje Lookout, in cooperation with the Indian Service and a tour operator.[^1] Work on a telephone line started that year.[^1] In 1928 the Forest and the Indian Service negotiated an agreement to share the cost of manning the site, but it fell through when the Indian Service did not hire a lookout, and there was still no tower.[^1]

In 1931 or 1932 the Indian Service erected a 35-foot steel Aermotor tower with a 7-by-7-foot steel cab, and Guaje Lookout was abandoned.[^1] A new agreement in 1932 made the Forest responsible for hiring, training and supervising the lookout.[^1] By September 1940 the Forest had moved a 10-by-14-foot cabin to the site, and in 1941 it ran a telephone line from the tower to the cabin.[^1]

The report says that during the 1940s Puye was usually staffed only when conditions were extreme.[^1] In 1940 the Forest suggested closing it and moving the lookout to Indian Peak, but the war halted Forest development and the move was put off.[^1]

By 1949 the lookout was unstaffed and reportedly abandoned, and aerial observation and the Los Alamos tower took over its work.[^1] In 1951 the Forest moved the cabin and an Osborne firefinder to the new Clara Peak Lookout, and Puye Lookout was abandoned. The report does not know when the tower was taken down or where it ended up.[^1]

The lookout stood beside the ruins, which sit in Santa Clara Canyon, on reservation land of the Santa Clara Pueblo near Española.[^1][^2] According to the report, very few traces remain at the site. It notes two outhouses, which might have been tied to the ruins rather than the lookout.[^1]

The attraction at the ruins is open from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., weather permitting, and is closed on Tuesdays and Wednesdays. It runs guided tours and says it will close to the public from October 29, 2026, for a special event.[^3] No source says whether the tours pass the old lookout spot. The report is from 2017, and no newer description of the lookout site was found.
