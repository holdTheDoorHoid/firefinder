Bull Hill was a fire lookout in Wise County, Virginia, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list records its tower as gone.[^1] The Eastern US Lookouts page for the site also names only the county.[^2]

The Eastern US Lookouts Virginia index quotes a 1919 description of a different Wise County tower, on Little Stone Mountain, built by the Virginia Coal and Iron Co. in cooperation with the forestry department.[^3] The index files that tower under the name Stonega, as a separate entry from Bull Hill.[^3]

The state's forestry agency later put up fire watchtowers at important sites across Virginia.[^4] Those towers often stood on leased private land, and most were sold for scrap once their leases ended, though some were left standing.[^5]

No source I read says what, if anything, remains at Bull Hill today or whether the land is open to visitors.
