Cloudy Lookout Tower is in Pushmataha County, Oklahoma, according to the Forest Lookouts site.[^1] OpenStreetMap maps a tower of that name, tagged as an observation tower with an elevation of 374 metres.[^2]

For the wider setting, the Oklahoma Forestry Services history notes that the agency once had 23 state fire towers in its protection area.[^3] The same history adds that tower observers have since retired, and that aircraft now fly over the area in their place.[^3]

Wikipedia's article on the town of Clayton says the tower on Flagpole Mountain is the only one still remaining in Pushmataha County, although it is no longer operational.[^4] Taken literally, that leaves no room for a Cloudy tower that still stands.[^4] The article names Cloudy only in a list of other communities in the county, and mentions no tower there.[^4] OpenStreetMap still maps a tower by that name.[^2] No source we read says whether a tower stands at the Cloudy site today, or whether the public may visit it.

Check with the managing agency before going near the site, and do not climb any tower unless its owners say it is open to the public.
