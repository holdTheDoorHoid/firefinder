Cedar Springs Lookout stands on the Jicarilla Reservation in Rio Arriba County, New Mexico, at about 8,291 feet.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register says it was built in 1955 and was the third lookout on the reservation.[^1] Its tower is a 35-foot MC40 Aermotor, with a two-room ground house for living quarters.[^1]

The Bureau of Indian Affairs administers the lookout, and the register says it remains in active service.[^1] It was registered on September 1, 1999, with Dave Lorenz as nominator.[^1] A Forest Lookouts page lists the site under the Jicarilla Indian Agency, and a Forest Fire Lookout Association list shows the tower as standing.[^2][^3]

The public record is thin. It has no staffing or visiting details for Cedar Springs. Because the Bureau of Indian Affairs administers the lookout on a reservation, anyone wanting to see it should ask that office first rather than go up on their own.[^1]
