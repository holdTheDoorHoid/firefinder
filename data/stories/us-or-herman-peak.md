Herman Peak is in the Siuslaw National Forest, in Lane County, Oregon, at about 2,080 feet, roughly 13 miles north-northeast of Florence.[^1][^2]

The first tower went up in 1934. The Forest Lookouts page records a timber tower made of round poles, topped by an L-4 model 1931 cab.[^3] The register calls it a 15-foot pole L-4 tower.[^1] A June 1934 newspaper item had said a lookout house would be built on the peak once a road that Civilian Conservation Corps crews were working on was finished.[^3]

In 1960 a 41-foot treated timber tower with an R-6 flat-roof cab replaced it.[^1][^3] The last staffed year was 1973.[^3]

Sources disagree on when the tower came down. The register and firelookout.com say it was removed in 1974.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page says the cab was dismantled in April 1976, trucked to Iron Mountain, and reassembled on the summit there after a helicopter lifted it up.[^3]

Iron Mountain is a separate site in Linn County on the Willamette National Forest.[^1][^4] Its own L-4 cab was built in 1933, and in 1976 it received the R-6 cab from Herman Peak.[^4][^5]

The Oregon chapter of the Forest Fire Lookout Association listed the Iron Mountain lookout among three Oregon lookouts lost in 2007.[^6] Its 2007 report describes the cab as an R-6 ground cab moved from Herman Peak.[^6] The association made four trips up the mountain to salvage items before the lookout was brought down.[^6]

Today we found no source that describes Herman Peak as it is now or whether anything is left there.[^1] The sources also do not say whether anything remains at Iron Mountain after the 2007 removal.[^4][^6]
