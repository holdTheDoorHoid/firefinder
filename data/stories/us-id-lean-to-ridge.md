Lean-To Ridge lies in the Clearwater National Forest, about 16 miles east of Pierce in Clearwater County, at 5,078 feet.[^1] The tower went up in 1934 as a 40-foot structure with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2] Keeping Watch describes the L-4 as a 14-by-14-foot cabin and the first prefabricated design for living in a lookout.[^3] The listings also name a patrol tree platform eight miles to the southwest.[^1][^2]

The dates do not fully agree. A July 1931 newspaper report about a fire on Hemlock Creek and Lean-to Ridge says the flames were advancing toward the Lean-to Ridge Lookout.[^4] That is three years before the 1934 date in the other listings.[^1][^2][^5]

The same report counted more than 5,000 acres of scorched timber on Hemlock Creek and Lean-to Ridge.[^4]

The tower was destroyed in 1963.[^1][^2][^5] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.[^1][^2][^5] Visitors should not expect to find the tower standing.[^1][^2]
