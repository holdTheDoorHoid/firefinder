The Grantsburg fire tower stood on Tower Hill, the highest spot in the village of Grantsburg, Burnett County, Wisconsin.[^1][^2] The register says the State of Wisconsin built the 110-foot tower in 1932.[^2] The Sentinel reports that some say it has stood on the hill since 1932, after being moved in sections from a former site north of the highway.[^1] A WE ARE Network report gives a different account: it was built in 1937 on the other side of the highway and later moved to its current site.[^3] The sources disagree on the build year. This story uses the register's 1932.

Burnett County DNR staff kept the tower manned until 2011.[^1][^2] Spotting from airplanes started to take over from people in the towers as early as the late 1950s.[^1] The Sentinel says Dorene Snyder worked in the tower for 24 years, and WE ARE dates her time there as a DNR smoke spotter from 1988 to 2011.[^1][^3]

For a few years, the Wisconsin DNR had been offering its fire towers for sale.[^1] Jon Dobbs, a retired medical-equipment business owner who lives in Boulder Junction, bought this one.[^1][^3] The WE ARE Network spells his name John.[^3] According to the register, a private owner bought the tower, and it was dismantled in February 2020.[^2] Jon's wife, Jaci Dobbs, said it would be rebuilt on their land on the highest hill nearby, for private use.[^1] The Sentinel reported the tower was bound for Montreal, Wisconsin, which lies a few miles southwest of Hurley.[^1]

The register and the Sentinel give 110 feet, but Dobbs put the total height at about 95 feet, counting the cab.[^1][^2][^3]

We found no source from after 2020 confirming that the tower went back up in Montreal, Wisconsin. The new home is private property, and no source we read describes public access to it.[^1] The reports describe the tower coming down on Tower Hill, but none describes what remains on the hill today.[^1][^3]
