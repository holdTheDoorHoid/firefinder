The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes a 60-foot iron tower on Soapstone Hill in Petersham. It says the U.S. Army built the tower and used it from 1931 to 1941 to watch for fires sparked by Air Corps bombing practice.[^1] The register also says some of the tower structure still stands today.[^1]

A 1949 photograph in the Massachusetts Archives shows Spotting Tower No. T-616 at Target Area No. 2 in Petersham.[^2] Its catalog entry links War Department structures to bombing-practice target areas in the Quabbin watershed from 1943 to 1945.[^2] Those dates do not match the register's 1931 to 1941 span, and the sources do not show whether this numbered tower is the one on Soapstone Hill.[^1][^2]

A hiker's review on Peakery from November 2025 says the fire tower is not on the summit.[^3] It puts the tower beside the Rabbit Run Rail Bed, on the western side of the summit and close to the reservoir.[^3]

The Soapstone Hill Loop is a 4.3-mile hike that begins at Gate 37, a yellow gate at Quabbin Reservoir in Petersham.[^4] Dogs are barred from trails inside Quabbin's boundaries, and camping is limited to the Federated Women's Club State Forest.[^4] No source we read describes the tower remains as open to visitors, or says what they look like now.[^1][^3]
