Marsh Peak is a rounded summit in the Uinta Mountains, in Uintah County, Utah.[^1][^2] A climbing guide places it among the two easternmost peaks in the range that rise to about 12,000 feet (3,660 m).[^2] Apart from an east-facing cirque, the guide finds the peak not very rugged, rarely climbed, and without a summit register.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association files Marsh Peak under unknown or undocumented sites rather than among its lookouts. The association says only documented, regular use as a wildland fire lookout would move a site onto its lookout lists.[^1]

No trail reaches the top, but the guide calls the climb easy enough for a single day.[^2] Remains of an old radio tower that helicopters serviced sit south of the summit.[^2]

Until a source documents a tower here, treat the lookout label as unconfirmed.[^1]
