Ushta is on the websiteservice.ca list of Saskatchewan fire towers, which gives its position as 52-15-36.2 X 102-38-11.7, or about 52.26 degrees north and 102.64 degrees west.[^1]

Canada Lookouts dates a new cupola on the Ushta tower to 1958. It cites a Department of Natural Resources annual report for the year ending 31 March 1959 as the source of that entry.[^2]

The towermen list on websiteservice.ca gives Wenzel Fialkowski at Ushta from 1973 to 2002, and notes that he then went on to aerial detection.[^3]

No source we found describes what remains at the Ushta site today, or whether the public can reach it.
