In January 1940, a newspaper item quoted on the Forest Lookouts page reported that Fromm Brothers, of Hamburg, had erected a fire tower in the town of Corning, about 12 miles west of Merrill, on land the firm owned.[^1] TopoQuest places the tower at about 1,489 feet in Lincoln County.[^4]

The same item says the builders reused steel from a 150-foot radio tower taken down near Janesville.[^1] It also says the state conservation department was maintaining fire towers in Corning and other Lincoln County towns, and that the Fromm tower brought the county's total to five.[^1]

The Wisconsin lookout list now marks the tower as gone.[^2] Wisconsin's fire towers of that period went up in the 1930s and 1940s, and in 2015 the DNR announced that the towers were being removed.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it.
