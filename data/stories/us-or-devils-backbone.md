The Devils Backbone lookout sat about five miles east of Sandy in Clackamas County, at an elevation of 1,573 feet.[^2] The Forest Lookouts directory lists it among the county's lookouts.[^1]

Firelookout.com names the Oregon Department of Forestry as the agency and dates a crow's nest and a camp there to 1934.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts page gives the code 2S-5E-11, and the Oregon Lookouts index places the site at 45.40848 north, 122.14912 west.[^1][^3] The index classes it as a crow's nest site.[^3]

Firelookout.com says the lookout was abandoned in the 1940s, and the Oregon Lookouts index lists its crow's nest as abandoned too.[^2][^3]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, and we found nothing on whether the public can visit it.[^1][^2][^3]
