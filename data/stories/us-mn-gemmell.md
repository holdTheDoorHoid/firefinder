Gemmell was a fire lookout tower in Koochiching County, Minnesota.[^1] The county's survey of its towers lists it as tower number 82, erected in 1930.[^1] It was a 94-foot ladder tower, possibly an Aermotor design.[^1]

The tower stood in the hamlet of Gemmell, beside an old rail line between Bemidji and International Falls.[^1] The survey lists the owner as "Mn MFS," which it explains as the Minnesota Forest Service, now the Forestry Division of the state Department of Natural Resources.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^3] The county survey says it was removed.[^1]

The old rail line is now the Blue Ox Trail, which follows the old rail grade south from International Falls through Northome.[^1][^2] Koochiching County gave the trail, and the Northome Ridge Riders look after it.[^2] The state's trail page calls it an unpaved route for snowmobiles and off-road vehicles that runs northeast to International Falls.[^4] The trail is open to ATVs in summer and to snowmobiles in winter.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the tower site.
