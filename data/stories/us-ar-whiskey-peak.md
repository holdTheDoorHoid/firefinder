Whiskey Peak is a steel lookout tower on Whiskey Mountain, in Polk County.[^1] In 1952 the Coast and Geodetic Survey described it as about 110 feet high, with four steel legs holding up an enclosed observation platform that was painted gray.[^1] The survey point was the top and center of the tower.[^1]

The survey station sat about 5.5 miles northwest of Wickes and 3 miles west-southwest of Hatton, on the mountain the survey called the highest point in the area.[^1]

The survey calls the structure the Whiskey Mtn lookout tower, while the Central US Lookouts page is titled Whiskey Peak.[^1]

No source I found describes the site today, so I cannot say whether the tower still stands or whether the public can reach it.[^1]
