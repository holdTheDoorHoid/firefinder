Jocko #2 Lookout stands on the Flathead Indian Reservation, on land the Confederated Salish and Kootenai Tribes administer, about 12 miles east of Arlee.[^1][^2][^3] Sources disagree on the county: the register and firelookout.com place it in Missoula County, while the tribal lookouts page puts it in Lake County.[^1][^2][^3]

The National Historic Lookout Register and firelookout.com both date the tower to 1969.[^1][^2] The register describes a 40-foot treated timber tower with an R-6 flat cab, and firelookout.com a 40-foot wooden R-6 tower.[^1][^2] The tribal lookouts page says it was built about 1970, and that R-6 lookouts use a standard 15-by-15-foot frame house.[^3]

A separate lookout also carries the name Jocko #2. It was a 60-foot steel tower built in 1934 in the Flathead National Forest. It was removed, possibly in the mid-1960s, and only its concrete footings remain.[^4]

The tribal page says the tower is now staffed only during emergencies, though it gives no date for that change.[^3]

The register lists the tower as US 1543, MT 106, registered April 12, 2022, and gives its elevation as 7,636 feet (2,327 meters). The other two sources list 7,769 feet.[^1][^2][^3]

The register names the tribes as administrator, but the pages read do not describe how visitors reach the tower or whether entry needs permission. Anyone planning a trip should contact the Confederated Salish and Kootenai Tribes first.[^1][^3]
