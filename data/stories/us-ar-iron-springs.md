The Iron Springs Lookout Tower stands in Nevada County, Arkansas.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register lists it as US 1797, AR 28 and registered it on January 2, 2026.[^1]

The register does not give a build date, a builder or a design for the tower, and we found no other source that describes it.[^1] For context, Arkansas once had a large network of these towers.[^2] The count peaked at 122 known towers, and by 1994 forty-eight were still standing, although the encyclopedia cannot say whether any were in use.[^2]

As of 2024 the register noted that the tower was missing its roof.[^1] We found no record of who staffed it or of any fire it watched.[^1]

Today the register says nothing about who owns the tower or whether the public may go up it.[^1] Until that is confirmed with whoever owns the land, do not climb it.

Please don't mix this one up with the Iron Springs picnic area on Arkansas Highway 7, north of Jessieville in Garland County. That is a separate site, and the Civilian Conservation Corps built most of its facilities in the 1930s.[^3]
