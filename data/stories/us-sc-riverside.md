Riverside was a fire tower in Lancaster County, run by the South Carolina Forestry Commission.[^1] The state's report for July 1939 to June 1940 says a new tower was constructed at the Riverside site.[^1] The same report says the Civilian Conservation Corps built all of the towers, tower buildings and telephone lines at no cost to the state or to protective associations.[^1]

The report for 1957 to 1958 says the wooden tower that stood there was replaced with a steel one.[^1] The record says the tower was sold at auction in 2010.[^1]

According to the commission, the public reported fires to its towers, and the towers linked to firefighters working in the field.[^2] A February 2006 AP story, which does not mention Riverside, said the state was selling a number of its towers.[^3] It also said the Forestry Commission had closed the last one in 1993.[^3]

The record marks Riverside as removed.[^1] None of the sources checked describes the site today, so it is not known what remains there or whether the land is open to visitors.
