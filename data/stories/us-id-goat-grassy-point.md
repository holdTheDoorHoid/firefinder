Goat (Grassy) Point Lookout was a cupola cabin, not a tower, and both lookout pages date it to the 1920s.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives its elevation as 6,375 feet and lists the site under the Clearwater-Potlatch TPA.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list places it in Clearwater County.[^3] Its patrol points lay two miles south-southeast and three miles north.[^1][^2]

The cabin was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list gives its type as Ground and marks it as gone.[^3]

None of the three pages describes what, if anything, remains of the cabin at the site today.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in and does not say whether the site is open to visitors.[^1]
