Twin is a possible fire lookout site in Clallam County, on a hill above an old village that also bore the name Twin.[^1] The village sat on the Strait, between the mouths of the two Twin Rivers.[^1]

The evidence for a lookout is thin. A 1936 Forest Service recreation map and a 1942 Clallam County Metsker map are the only two hints of a possible fire lookout, and it is unclear which of the mountain's several bumps it was on.[^1] Two 1942 USGS benchmarks on the hill make no mention of one either.[^1] Eric Willhite searched seven locations and found little at any of them.[^1][^2] He adds that at some spots the terrain is gentle enough that a tower would not need to sit on the highest point to see well.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts index lists the site under State, with the location given as 31N-10W-28.[^2] Willhite's page gives a different picture of the land. It says a Rayonier Timber permit is required for legal access, which does not fit the index's State label.[^1][^2]

Willhite reports that many overgrown roads wind around the summits, and that reaching the high points takes a bit of off-trail travel.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the hill today.
