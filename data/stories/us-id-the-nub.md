The Nub sits in the Clearwater National Forest, about five miles northeast of the Canyon Ranger Station.[^1] The listings give its legal location as Section 30, Township 41 North, Range 8 East.[^2][^4] The record begins in 1924 with a camp.[^1][^3]

An L-4 cab went up in 1935, and the cab was destroyed in 1958.[^1][^2][^3][^4] All four listings give both dates, and one of them attributes them to Kresek.[^1][^2][^3][^4] No listing records anything at the site after 1958.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the site's elevation as 6,924 feet, and files it as a hike-in.[^3] The firelookout.com page credits its photographs to the U.S. Forest Service.[^1]

No source we read describes the site today. We cannot say what, if anything, remains there, or whether visitors can reach it.
