Clark Creek is the register's name for this lookout site in Union County, northeast Oregon.[^1] Other lists call the same spot Clark Mountain.[^2][^3] The site sits at 5,520 feet (1,682 m), about seven miles southeast of the town of Union.[^1][^2] The register added the site on July 27, 2011.[^1]

The register describes a cabin built in the 1930s and gives no more precise year than that.[^1] The Oregon Department of Forestry administered the site.[^1][^2]

The cabin is gone. The register gives no date for its removal.[^1] The Forest Association's Oregon list records the site as a ground type with the status gone.[^3]

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains on the site today or whether the public may visit it. Ask the Oregon Department of Forestry before planning a trip.
