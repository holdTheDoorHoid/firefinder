Lakes Mountain, also called The Lakes, rises above Anthony Lake in the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest, in Grant and Baker counties.[^1][^2][^3]

The register dates the first lookout, a 12 by 12 cabin with a roof platform, to 1912.[^1] A June 1915 newspaper note mentions a cabin being built at the lookout, and the field guide says it was first constructed in 1915.[^3][^4] The sources disagree on the start date.

Firelookout.com says the first cabin burned by accident in the off-season between 1924 and 1925, and that a cupola cabin took its place in 1926.[^2] The register also gives 1926 for the cupola cabin.[^1] A lookout history site instead describes the 1926 work as an enclosed cupola built onto the lookout house.[^3]

Firelookout.com lists Al Brannan for 1919 to 1921 and Clyde Robertson for 1928 as lookout personnel.[^2] In June 1941 a forestry student arrived at the Anthony Lakes lookout station for the summer.[^3] A July 1951 note says the lookout was being staffed for the summer.[^3] A note from August 1962 says visitors had set a record that year, and that the 500th person to make the hike had been registered.[^3] Firelookout.com lists Leo Poe as the 1962 lookout, while the newspaper note names Leo Mosher.[^2][^3]

The lookout was gone by 1966.[^1][^2] The field guide says it was demolished that year.[^4]

The field guide gives directions from the Anthony Lake Highway, up a spur road, to the summit trailhead.[^4] You can hike from Anthony Lake in about six miles round trip, and a much rougher road starts only 700 feet below the summit.[^4] The summit is a ten-foot scramble over steep boulders with a metal lightning rod on top, and the guide does not mention any remains of the lookout.[^4] Check current conditions with the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest before you go.
