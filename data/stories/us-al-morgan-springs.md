The Morgan Springs Lookout Tower stands in Perry County, Alabama, about a mile south of the Morgan Springs community.[^1][^2] The National Historic Lookout Register gives the site elevation as 486 feet (148 m) and says the State of Alabama designed the tower under the number AL 141.[^1] A 1971 survey description calls it a steel structure on four legs with a cabin for observers.[^2] The sources read do not give a construction date, a builder, a height, or any list of fires it watched.

The sources name different agencies. The 1971 text says the Alabama State Forest Service owned the tower, while the Register names the Alabama Forestry Commission as administrator.[^2][^1]

The Register added the tower on January 2, 2026, as US 1801, AL 86, nominated by Jackson Knight.[^1] A blog post gives the number as "Registry # 141," which is the state design number the Register uses, not its own registry number.[^3][^1] The sources read do not say who staffed the tower, and the Register page has no visit reports.

Nothing in the sources says the tower has been moved, closed or taken down. The January 2026 Register entry is the most recent source read.[^1] The 1971 directions reach the tower through a locked gate and then up a 0.2 mile dirt road.[^2] The blog describes the property as owned by the State of Alabama.[^3] Those directions are more than fifty years old, so anyone planning a visit should check with the Alabama Forestry Commission first. The sources do not say whether the public may go in.
