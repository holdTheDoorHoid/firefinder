Cottonwood Point Lookout stood on Cottonwood Point, about 13 miles north of Priest River in Bonner County, Idaho, on land of the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^3] Its 40-foot tower was built in 1937.[^1][^2][^3] The sources describe the structure a little differently. Firelookout.com calls it a pole tower with an L-4 cab, while Idaho Fire Lookouts describes an L-4 tower and a log cabin.[^1][^2]

The lookout was abandoned in the 1940s, according to all three sites.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the tower as gone.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts reports that a logging operation passed through the area. All that is left is cement footings and some scrap metal.[^2] The page lists the site as drive-up.[^2] It sits on National Forest land, but none of these sources gives an access rule or says whether anyone uses the site now.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho also has a Cottonwood Point lookout in Custer County, the Mid-Cottonwood Point site, which the Association lists as standing. It is a different place and should not be confused with this one.[^4]
