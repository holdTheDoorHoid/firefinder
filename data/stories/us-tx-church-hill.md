Church Hill is an unincorporated community in eastern Rusk County, about 9 miles northeast of Henderson.[^1] The Texas Forest Service ran a fire lookout under the name Church Hill,[^2] and the Central US Lookouts site files it as Church Hill (New Prospect).[^3]

The name comes from a wooded rise on which early settlers raised a church.[^1] Settlement began in the early 1850s with people from Georgia, and Wikipedia gives 20 residents for 2000.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's index marks the tower as gone.[^4] No source I read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the spot is open to visitors.
