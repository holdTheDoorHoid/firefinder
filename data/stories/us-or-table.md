Table Lookout stood on Table Mountain in Lane County, Oregon, at 4,178 feet.[^2][^1] The register says an earlier 30-foot 7-by-7 tower with a ground cabin dated from 1933. A local history instead dates a round, 30-foot timber tower, raised by the Wendling CCC camp, to 1934.[^1][^3]

In 1947 a 14-by-14-foot lookout house was set on a timber tower. The register calls that tower 20 feet tall, and the local history says 25.[^1][^3]

The staffing record I found dates from 1952. The Forest Log reported that William Hastings, 74, had died of a heart attack on July 17 while stationed at the lookout. He worked for the Eastern Lane County Forest Protective association, and his wife shared the post with him.[^3]

Sources disagree on when the tower came down. The register says it was removed in 1979, and the local history says it was dismantled and salvaged that year.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com says it was destroyed in 1978.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^4] No source I read describes what remains at the summit now, or whether visitors can reach it.
