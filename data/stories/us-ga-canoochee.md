A Georgia lookout tower list places a tower called canoochee in Liberty County, at 31.91696, -81.52007.[^1] Its elevation column reads 43, with no unit given.[^1] The row ends with the note datasheet BR1573.[^1]

The list's Google map is marked as revised on 22 March 2016.[^1] The list says names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own creations. Canoochee is numbered 354, so its name may not be a historical one.[^1]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
