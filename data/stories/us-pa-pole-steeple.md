The Pole Steeple Tower Site is in Cumberland County, Pennsylvania.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it as US 429, PA 258.[^1] The register calls it a bald tower that was in use during the early 1900s, and says it has since come down.[^1]

The register added the site on September 16, 2008, on the nomination of Henry Isenberg, and it gives no coordinates for the site.[^1]

The register does not describe what, if anything, remains of the tower today.[^1] A hiking overlook with a similar name, Pole Steeple Overlook, is in the Cumberland County part of Michaux State Forest, next to Pine Grove Furnace State Park.[^2] Its trailhead and parking area are along Old Railroad Bed Road at the park.[^2] No source we read ties the old tower to that overlook, so do not assume the two are the same place.[^1][^2]
