Warroad was a fire lookout tower that stood within the limits of the town of Warroad, in Roseau County, Minnesota.[^1] The Minnesota Forest Service owned it, and the county list gives its height as an 80-foot ladder tower.[^2]

State survey notes from 1934 already describe the 80-foot state fire tower, so it was standing by then.[^2] A 1941 survey note describes an all-steel tower with a six-sided enclosed cabin at the top. It also puts its height at about 100 feet, which does not match the 80-foot figure.[^2]

The tower was dismantled in 1946.[^1][^2] The county list records a later move to the Northwest Angle, in Lake of the Woods County.[^2]

The Lake of the Woods list identifies that tower as the Northwest Angle lookout, erected there in 1951.[^3] It was an 80-foot ladder tower, and the list calls it the northernmost lookout tower in the lower 48 states until it was removed.[^3]

A Forest Lookouts page on the Northwest Angle quotes a 1950s account of an 80-foot fire lookout tower at a lumber camp there, manned during the fire season. The page does not name the Warroad tower.[^4]

No source read describes what remains at the Warroad site today.[^5] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Warroad tower as gone.[^5]
