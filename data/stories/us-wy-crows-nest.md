A 1954 forest map of the Pinedale District marked lookouts on a few scattered peaks.[^2] Three were named: Newfork, Willow Creek and Crow's Nest.[^2] A Forest Service history of the Bridger-Teton says it cannot tell whether these were real structures or temporary fixtures, and that its research turned up no plans or pictures of any of them.[^2]

A column headed "Twenty Years Ago This Week" in the Pinedale Roundup, printed on August 18, 1955, described a dedication on the summit of Crow's Nest lookout.[^1] The service was held near Glimpse Lake and honored a finished loop trail through the Bridger Primitive area.[^1] Because the column looked back twenty years, the dedication probably took place around 1935.[^1]

A USGS map of the Fremont Lake North area, portraying 1968 and published in 1973, labels a Crows Nest Lookout.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wyoming list places Crows Nest in Sublette County and marks it gone.[^4] The list gives its type as unknown.[^4] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the summit today, or whether the peak is open to visitors.
