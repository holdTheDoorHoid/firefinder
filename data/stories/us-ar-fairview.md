Fairview is a former fire tower site on the Ozark National Forest, in Newton County, Arkansas.[^1][^2] Members of the Civilian Conservation Corps manned the tower while it was in use.[^1]

The site of the old Fairview Nursery sits just across Highway 7. The nursery was set up on February 12, 1939 for Company #746.[^1] A hiking-club page says many of its foundations are still in place.[^1]

The fire tower is no longer there.[^1] The Fairview Campground now sits on the site of the former tower. The tower site is recorded as a historical archaeological site, but the hiking-club page does not clearly say what, if anything, of the tower itself remains.[^1]

The hiking-club page lists a $7 per day fee, a vault toilet, drinking water and a parking area. That parking area is also the access point for the Ozark Highlands Trail.[^1] In 2014 the Big Piney Ranger District was considering decommissioning the campground, so check with the district before you go.[^1]
