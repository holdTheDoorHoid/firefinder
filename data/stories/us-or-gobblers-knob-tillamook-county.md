Gobblers Knob is a hill in Tillamook County, inside the Tillamook State Forest.[^1][^4] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives its elevation as 3,052 feet, while a hiking guide gives 3,054 feet.[^1][^4]

The register dates the lookout to 1951 and says the Oregon Department of Forestry administered it.[^1] The register describes a 40-foot steel tower with a small cab, while firelookout.com gives 34 feet and a cab 7 by 7 feet.[^1][^2] On April 26, 1953, The Sunday Oregonian announced new construction that included a secondary lookout on the knob; the Oregon lookouts site quotes the item.[^3] That is two years after the register's build date, and the sources do not reconcile the two.[^1][^3]

A 1956 entry notes that three panes of glass were replaced.[^3] The tower was cut down to 15 feet in 1957, and a bigger cab and a trailer house were added.[^1][^2]

The register puts the lookout's removal in 1962.[^1] Firelookout.com and the Oregon lookouts site say it was abandoned in 1965.[^2][^3] The sources disagree on when the lookout ended.[^1][^2][^3] The register added the site in November 2019.[^1]

A hiking guide says most of the trail is easy gravel road, with a final climb to the summit that is steep and loose.[^4] Undated user reports posted to the same guide describe an impassable washout on the approach road.[^4] No source describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout on the summit.[^1][^2][^3]
