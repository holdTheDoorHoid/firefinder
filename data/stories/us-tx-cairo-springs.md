Cairo Springs lies in southwestern Jasper County, seven miles northwest of Buna, in the area between Farm Road 1004 and the Neches River.[^1] The Texas Tram and Lumber Company set up a logging camp near Yellow Bluff in 1876, and it kept operating at Cairo until 1894. The camp was given up, and the post office shut in 1895.[^1]

The Texas Forest Service ran a lookout here, and the Central US Lookouts directory lists it in Jasper County.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^3]

A Texas State Historical Association entry says the Cairo Springs church and the Cairo Springs lookout "marked the old site in 1990," but it does not say whether the tower was still standing then.[^1]

No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the land is open to visitors.
