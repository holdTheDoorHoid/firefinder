Phillipsburg is a fire tower in Laclede County, listed by the Forest Lookouts site under the Missouri Conservation Commission.[^1] The Department of Conservation's annual report for fiscal 1969-70 says the tower received a new tile floor.[^1]

The Department of Conservation's Goose Creek Conservation Area page lists a 40-acre tract it calls the Phillipsburg Towersite.[^2] The forest spans Laclede and Dallas counties, not far from I-44, and county roads serve it.[^2] No source found says whether that tract holds the lookout site, or whether a tower still stands there today.[^1][^2]
