Jack Mountain sits in the Beaverhead-Deerlodge National Forest, in Jefferson County, Montana.[^2] A 1965 newspaper item, quoted on a Montana lookout site, announced a new fire lookout tower on the mountain, north of Basin.[^1] The tower stood 53 feet tall, was built of treated timber, and carried a lookout cab on top.[^1][^4] It replaced an older, unsafe station on Thunderbolt Mountain that was taken down.[^1]

The register gives 1964 as the build year, but the newspaper item and two other pages say 1965.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

The register describes the tower as seldom used, and says it stopped serving fire detection after 1984.[^2] Firelookout.com says it had been unstaffed for some years before it came down.[^3]

It was torn down in 2011.[^1][^3] A picture-tour page says it was removed for safety reasons that year.[^5]

One peakbagging page says only the foundation posts are left.[^4] The same page also mentions a survey benchmark, placed in 1986, sitting in the middle of the former tower spot.[^4] A picture-tour page says visitors should still plan a hike to the former site, where it describes old log cabins and relics from Basin's mining history.[^5]
