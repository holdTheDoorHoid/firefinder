Spion Kop was a fire lookout on the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, about 24 miles north of Kellogg in Shoshone County, Idaho.[^1] The tower was a 30-foot pole structure with an L-4 cab, put up in 1934.[^1][^2][^4] A patrol point stood about a mile to the southwest, on a summit of 5,142 feet.[^1][^2]

Two sources date the loss of the tower to 1955.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Idaho towers marks it as gone.[^3] The two lookout-history sites give different elevations for the spot, 5,508 feet and 5,502 feet.[^1][^2]

Today only scraps seem to be left.[^2] The Idaho Fire Lookouts site says the remnants are hard to find. They include a board nailed to a tree, a privy hole, scattered cement, two benchmarks, and possibly a hunting blind.[^2] The same site lists the spot as a hike-in, and we found no source saying whether it is open to visitors.[^2]
