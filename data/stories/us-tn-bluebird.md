Bluebird Firetower is a standing lattice tower in Anderson County, Tennessee, at about 1,319 feet (402 metres) elevation.[^1][^2] OpenStreetMap tags it as an observation tower with lattice construction.[^2] Tennessee Landforms calls it Bluebird, and OpenStreetMap calls it Bluebird Firetower.[^1][^2] The Tennessee Landforms list also carries the code GB2537, which we could not explain from any source we read.[^1]

We found no history for the tower. We could not establish when it went up, who built it, or who staffed it, and our web searches turned up no account of a Bluebird tower in Anderson County.

One possible lead is a list of sites for the state's new lookout towers, printed in a Kingsport paper in October 1955, which named a spot for Anderson County near Clinton.[^3] We could not tie that site to this tower.

Tennessee Landforms lists the tower as standing, but we could not confirm its condition, its owner, or whether visitors may go up it.[^1] Ask the landowner before going near it.
