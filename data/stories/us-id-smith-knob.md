Smith Knob Lookout stood within the Payette National Forest, about eight miles northeast of Warren in Idaho County.[^1] The first structure on the knob was a log cabin, built in 1929.[^1][^2] In 1958 a 10-foot concrete base was built, topped by a flat cab that the sources call unusual.[^1][^2] The lookout was last used in the 1970s, though no source gives the year.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts names a few people who staffed it, citing a former registry book: Elaine Puderbaugh for 1967 and 1968, Anna M Weskema from 1969 to 1971, and Judy Puderbaugh in 1972.[^2]

All three sources date the demolition to 2008.[^1][^2][^3] The 2008 Western Region annual report says the Payette NF in southern Idaho removed two unused lookouts that year, one of them the two-story ground cabin at Smith Knob.[^3] FireLookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts describe a concrete base with a flat cab and do not mention a two-story cabin, so the sources do not fully agree on what stood there.[^1][^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, such as footings or a foundation. Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the lookout "Drive-Up" beside its "Demolished" status, but the listing does not say what is there now or whether visitors can reach the site today.[^2]
