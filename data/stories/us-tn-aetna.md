The Aetna tower stood in Marion County, at an elevation of about 1,915 feet.[^1] The Tennessee Division of Forestry listed it in District 3.[^2] Its code on the tnlandforms list is 3-27, and the same page explains that numbers like that are district and tower numbers from a 1976 list of state forest towers.[^1]

The list ties its Aetna entry to survey mark FC1879.[^1] The National Geodetic Survey datasheet for that mark calls the site Whiteside TSFS Lot SC 36 and places it in Marion County.[^3] The datasheet's name for the mark differs from the list's Aetna.[^1][^3]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey described the station in 1961.[^3] The datasheet says the lookout tower at the station was a four-legged wooden structure with a glass-enclosed cab, about 55 feet overall.[^3]

Both lists mark the tower as removed.[^1][^2] The tnlandforms list adds that many Tennessee towers have been taken down, and that the sites of most of them now belong to private owners.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it, so please ask the landowner before going near it.
