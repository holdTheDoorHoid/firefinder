Across Tennessee, the Civilian Conservation Corps erected 98 lookout towers between 1933 and 1941.[^1] A personal inventory of Tennessee lookout towers, published at tnlandforms.us, lists a Spring City tower in Rhea County, at an elevation of 1,826 feet (557 m).[^1] The town of Spring City lies in Rhea County too.[^2]

The inventory marks the Spring City tower as removed.[^1] It lists two other Rhea County towers, Grandview and Smyrna, as standing.[^1]

It also says many towers have since been taken down, and that most former sites are now privately owned.[^1] No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the Spring City site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Tennessee lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Spring City, Tennessee, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spring_City,_Tennessee (accessed 2026-10-21).
