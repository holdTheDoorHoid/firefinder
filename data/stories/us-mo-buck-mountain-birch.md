Forest Lookouts calls this site Buck Mountain / Birch and lists it under the Missouri Conservation Commission in St. Francois County.[^1] A federal survey record names the tower the Birch Fire Tower.[^2]

The Coast and Geodetic Survey first observed the station in 1938. The 1938 description calls it a modest steel structure with a tub-shaped platform at the top, roughly 60 feet tall, about 6 miles northeast of Ironton.[^2]

A 1956 recovery note from the survey gives the condition as good. It describes the tower as 50 feet high, with a crow's nest about four feet square, and places the station in the Clark National Forest.[^2] The 1938 and 1956 survey notes disagree on the height.[^2]

In June 1962 the Conservation Commission acquired 194 acres for the Buck Mountain Tower site, at a cost of $970.[^1]

Forest Lookouts marks the tower as removed.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.[^1][^2]
