The Antonia tower stood in Grant Parish, north of Pollock.[^1][^2] The site record places it in the Kisatchie National Forest.[^1]

A 1984 newspaper report, quoted on the same site, described the tower as 100 feet tall and said it was staffed every day except on rainy days.[^1] On a clear day, the report said, the view from the top could reach as far as 50 miles.[^1]

Rain readings from the tower also made the papers. A 1957 Monroe News-Star item gave 47.95 inches for 1956 and 54.84 inches for the year before.[^1]

Wikipedia's list of Louisiana towers says the Antonia tower was torn down.[^2] Its most recent USGS map label dates from 2012, and the list says the tower could not be found on aerial imagery.[^2]

No source we read describes the site today, so what remains there, if anything, and whether the ground is open to visitors are both unknown.[^1][^2]
