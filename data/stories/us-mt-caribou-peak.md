Caribou Peak is a former fire lookout in Lewis and Clark County, Montana, about 22 miles southwest of Augusta, at an elevation of 8,773 feet.[^1] The Forest Lookouts listing places it at township 17 North, range 8 West, section 36, in the Helena/Lewis & Clark National Forest.[^2]

An Augusta newspaper item dated August 3, 1939, and quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, described a plan to build a lookout that season on a divide between the Helena and Lewis and Clark forests. The item spelled the name Carabau Mountain.[^2]

The firelookout.com page dates the tower to the 1940s and describes it as a 20-foot pole L-4 tower.[^1] Neither source says whether the 1939 plan became that tower.[^1][^2] The same page says the lookout was abandoned in the 1950s.[^1]

Firelookout.com also reports that Google Earth imagery shows a foundation that fits a ground cab alone.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks the site as ground and gone.[^3] No source we read describes anything else at the site today.
