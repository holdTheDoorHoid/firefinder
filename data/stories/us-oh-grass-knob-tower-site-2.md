The Grass Knob fire tower was moved to this spot, along US Route 23 in Scioto County, Ohio.[^1] The record gives the site an elevation of 560 feet (171 meters).[^1]

The tower came here from Brush Creek State Forest.[^1] The record for Site #1 says it stood there and was moved to a location beside US Route 23.[^2]

On October 27, 1934, the Chillicothe Gazette reported that a Grass Knob Tower, north of Otway, would be manned by Perry Bonner of Mt. Joy.[^3] The two site records do not mention 1934, so whether that tower is the one moved here is unconfirmed.[^1][^2]

This site's record says the tower was later taken down completely.[^1] The association's Ohio list also marks this site's entry as gone.[^4]

No source we read describes this spot today, and none says whether it is open to visitors. Check before you go.
