The Stone Corral lookout stood in Del Norte County, California.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page places it in the Siskiyou National Forest.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as Low Divide (Stone Corral), a tower now gone.[^2]

The same Forest Lookouts page dates a 40-foot creosoted sawn timber tower with a cab to 1940, citing *A History of the Siskyou National Forest*.[^1] It also marks the tower as removed.[^1]

An amateur-radio rover location page, last updated in August 2026, carries a user comment that calls the spot a decommissioned fire lookout location.[^3] The commenter says the remains sit at the top of a short, rough trail that needs a 4x4, reached by a road they describe as rough and steep.[^3] The coordinates given for the remains lie about 35 feet from the Forest Fire Lookout Association's point.[^3][^2] The sources do not say whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3]
