Owen Point sits in Ravalli County, Montana, about 14 miles southeast of Stevensville, on the Bitterroot National Forest. Its elevation is 7,691 feet.[^1] Forest Lookouts gives the legal location as township 7 north, range 18 west, section 9.[^4]

Firelookout.com dates a tent cabin and a firefinder platform there to 1937.[^1] A firefinder, such as the Osborne Fire Finder, let lookouts sight the bearing of smoke so that fire crews could be alerted to a wildland fire.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list gives the structure type as a platform, at 46.3675 north and 113.85715 west.[^3] Firelookout.com says the site was abandoned in 1940, and the same association list also calls it abandoned.[^1][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the point today, or whether the public may visit it.
