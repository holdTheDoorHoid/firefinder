Oak Mountain is in Saline County, in the Ouachita National Forest.[^1] According to the Central US Lookouts page, the tower went up in 1928.[^1] A 1940 Coast and Geodetic Survey description gives the Forest Service structure as steel: 100 feet up to the lookout-house floor, then 15 more feet to the roof peak.[^1]

A December 1937 item, reprinted on the same page from The Port Arthur News in Texas, describes a rattlesnake the forest lookouts called Old Spot.[^1] The snake had hung around the tower for two years, keeping the observers company.[^1] The item says a new helper had recently been assigned to the tower, and that after the snake got too close to the helper, it stopped coming back.[^1]

The same page marks the tower as removed in 1966.[^1] A list of lookouts in the Mena and Oden ranger districts calls the site the Oak Mountain Observatory and says it is no longer in existence.[^2]

A National Geodetic Survey list of marks it does not publish gives a survey mark named Oak Mountain Lot a code meaning its surface was reported destroyed.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, stands on the summit now, or whether the public can reach it.
