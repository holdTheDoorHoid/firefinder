A compiled list of Georgia lookout towers, kept on tnlandforms.us, places a Liberty Lookout Tower in Franklin County at 34.36399, -83.21182.[^1] The list gives its elevation as 801, with no unit stated.[^1] A caption on the page's map says towers numbered 1 to 72 are listed by the USGS name database; this entry is number 32.[^1] The list says its towers were identified from 1:100,000 USGS topographic maps and NGS data sheets.[^1]

The page's Google map says it was revised on 22 March 2016.[^1]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything remains or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
