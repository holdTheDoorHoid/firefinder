The Scottsville lookout was a Texas Forest Service tower on a hilltop near Scottsville, in Harrison County, Texas.[^1]

In November 1943, a Marshall News Messenger article quoted on the Central US Lookouts site counted it among the agency's 74 lookout towers. The same article placed it among three towers in Harrison County.[^1]

The article also named the patrolman who worked from it, Carl J. McBrayer, and said that from the tower he could spot fires in the distant parts of the timberland it was meant to protect.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list records the tower as gone.[^2] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains on the hilltop today, and nothing about public access. Please do not assume the site is open to visitors.

[^2]: Texas Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
