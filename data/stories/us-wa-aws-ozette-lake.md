The Air Warning Service set up a station on the east side of Ozette Lake in 1942.[^1][^2] The station, called William 1-1A, was activated on November 23, 1942, under the Port Angeles Filter Center.[^2] Its cabin, listed as 14 x 32, stood on a point that could be reached only by boat, and the Forest Lookouts page says the station was established on private land.[^2]

When the service ended, the state declined the site, and it was left abandoned.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page attributes that account to a report of AWS stations dated May 1, 1944.[^2] Firelookout.com gives 1945 as the year it was abandoned and marks the cabin as gone.[^1] The two sources disagree on the year.[^1][^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Washington list also marks the site as gone.[^3] No source I read describes what remains at the point today, or whether it can be reached.
