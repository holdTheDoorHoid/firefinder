Inverness is listed as a fire tower in Hoke County, North Carolina, on a state-wide list of towers.[^1] The list places it at 35.17972, -79.24111.[^1] Its survey-point column reads "gw topo" and gives no point number.[^1] The list says a few of its entries were added from 1:100,000 USGS topographic maps, but does not say whether Inverness is one of them.[^1] The list also cautions that some entries may be observation towers or Coast Guard lookouts.[^1]

No source describes the site today.

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
