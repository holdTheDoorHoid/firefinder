Horton Ridge rises in Boundary County, Idaho, about 10 miles north-northeast of Coolin.[^1] The Priest Lake Timber Protective Association is listed as the lookout's agency.[^1][^3] In 1932 there was a log cabin at the lookout site.[^1][^2][^4] In 1934 a 40-foot pole L-4 tower was built there.[^1][^2][^4] The summit sits at 6,091 feet.[^2][^4]

The cabin left the ridge in 1970, when it was moved to the Kinner place on Priest Lake.[^1][^2][^4] The tower was taken down in 1977.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

The tower is gone.[^4] Photo captions on Willhite's page name a footing at the site, and another caption refers to a parking area for the trail to Mount Roothaan.[^4] Willhite says the site is a popular starting point for hikes to several Selkirk peaks.[^4] He says the final half mile needs a higher-clearance vehicle, and lower-clearance cars can park at the saddle about half a mile below.[^4] The road offers some views of Priest Lake before the site.[^4] Willhite does not mention any closure.[^4] No source we read gives a current access rule for the site.
