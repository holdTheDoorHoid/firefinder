The Central School lookout is in Angelina County, Texas, about ten miles northwest of Lufkin in a straight line, near the community of Central.[^1] The Texas State Historical Association puts the community eight miles northwest of Lufkin, on U.S. Highway 69, so the two sources disagree on distance.[^2] The community was first known as Central School, a name taken from a schoolhouse that served the area's farms.[^2]

A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description, written in 1948, calls the structure the Central School lookout tower No. 13 of the Texas State Forest Service.[^1] It describes a steel structure on four legs, roughly 110 feet tall, with a glass-enclosed cab at the top.[^1] The description gives no build date, and we found no account of why the tower was placed on this site. The 1948 description is the earliest date we found for it.

We found no record of who staffed the lookout, which fires it watched, or whether it was ever moved, rebuilt or damaged. The sources we read are silent on all of these.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing, under the name Central School (O'Quinn).[^3] No source we read explains the O'Quinn part of that name. An undated East Texas travel page notes that lookout towers remain across East Texas, including one beside U.S. 69 at Central, north of Lufkin.[^4]

The sources do not describe public access. The Forest Lookouts site credits the Texas Forest Service as the agency.[^5] Please do not climb the tower. Before going near it, ask the landowner or the Texas Forest Service.
