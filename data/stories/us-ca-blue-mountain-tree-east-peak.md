The Blue Mountain Tree is recorded at the East Point of Blue Mountain, in the Modoc National Forest, Modoc County, California.[^1][^3] It was never a tower in the usual sense. The lookout was a tree topped with a platform.[^1] A 1945 maintenance plan for the Warner Mountain District says the tree and platform had served for about ten years and had become unsafe to use, and it suggests replacing them with a 20-foot tower.[^1]

A National Geodetic Survey station description from 1948 gives a different picture. It describes the lookout tree as about 40 feet high, with a platform on top.[^1] The two sources do not agree on height, and we could not tell whether the 1945 tower was ever built, or whether the 1948 description refers to the same structure.

We found no record of who staffed the tree or of any fire it watched. Both Forest Fire Lookout Association lists mark the tree as standing, with no register number.[^2][^3] We found no dated source after 1948 for its condition, so its state today is unknown. A separate Blue Mountain tower, a little under a kilometre from the tree's coordinates, is also listed as standing and carries the register number US 1329, CA 152.[^3]

We found no public access information for the tree. Anyone curious should check with the Modoc National Forest first, and should not climb any structure they find there.
