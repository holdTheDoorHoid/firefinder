The Wallace Park Tower stood in the San Juan National Forest in La Plata County, Colorado.[^1] The U.S. Forest Service administered the site, and the Former Fire Lookout Sites register gives its elevation as 10,760 feet, or 3,280 meters.[^1] The register places it at 37.454650 degrees north and 107.718810 degrees west.[^1]

The register dates the tower's construction to 1915.[^1]

Susan Epstein nominated the site, and the register recorded it on September 18, 2009, as US 543, CO 15.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Colorado list also marks the tower as removed.[^2]

The register records the tower as no longer standing.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1] Anyone curious should ask the Forest Service before going out there.
