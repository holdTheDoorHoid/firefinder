The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Minnesota lookouts marks the Eliason tower in Cook County as gone.[^1]

The site is on a wooded summit about seven miles northeast of Grand Marais, in the Superior National Forest.[^2]

A survey note from 1934 describes a 45-foot wooden double tower that the U.S. Forest Service had put up there, and a county list calls it the first tower on the site.[^2][^3]

In 1943 the Good Harbor/Grand Marais lookout was moved to this site.[^3] Before the move, it stood on the peninsula along the east side of Grand Marais harbor.[^3] The Central US page describes the moved structure as an 85-foot steel tower.[^2] The county list calls it an 85-foot ladder tower instead, so the two sources disagree on its construction.[^3]

A 1956 survey recovery note still places the steel tower over the survey station.[^2] The county list marks the tower as removed, and we found no source that gives the year.[^3]

The 1934 description says the wooded hill was owned by the Hedstrom Lumber Co.[^2] No source describes the site today, including who owns the hill now or whether the public can reach it. The most recent survey record we found is a 1956 recovery that noted a station disk set in bedrock.[^2]

[^1]: Minnesota Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Eliason - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/eliason.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^3]: Cook County Minnesota Fire Lookout Towers (PDF), Wikimedia Commons, https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/fe/Cook_County_Towers.pdf (accessed 2026-10-10).
