Wildcat Mountain, in Vilas County, Wisconsin, had a fire lookout tower.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it at 46.18727, -89.64481 with the tower marked gone.[^1] A page on the central US lookouts site names the same mountain and county.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Wisconsin Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wi/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Wildcat Mountain, Forest Lookouts (central US), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/wildcat-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
