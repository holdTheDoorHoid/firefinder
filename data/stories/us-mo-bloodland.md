Forest Lookouts lists a Bloodland tower in Pulaski County, Missouri, under a header that names Mark Twain National Forest. The page marks the tower as removed.[^1]

Bloodland was a former community in southern Pulaski County. A post office there was established in 1898 and remained in operation until 1941. The community is named for a Mr. Blood, who first owned the town site.[^2]

Wikipedia places the community about 3.5 miles west-northwest of Big Piney. The article says it lies inside Fort Leonard Wood today, and that the town was razed to make room for the fort.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the tower site, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2]
