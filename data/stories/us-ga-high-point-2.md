A Georgia list of lookout towers includes a site called High Point in Walker County.[^1] The list places it at 2,327 feet (709 m) above sea level, at 34.86375 north and 85.39257 west. Its note reads "lookout mtn," and it carries the state number gfc-707.[^1]

Do not confuse it with the High Point tower in Chattooga County. That tower has its own entry, numbered gfc-702 and set at a different spot.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page for High Point covers the Chattooga tower, not this one. It dates that tower to 1956 and describes a steel tower 60 feet tall with a six-by-six-foot metal cab.[^2] Those details do not describe the Walker County summit.

No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at this summit today, or whether the public can reach it. Check with the Georgia Forestry Commission before you go, and stay away from any tower that is closed or posted.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: High Point, Forest Lookouts (easternuslookouts.weebly.com), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/high-point.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
