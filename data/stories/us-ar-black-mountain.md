The Forest Lookouts directory lists a fire tower called Black Mountain in Franklin County, on the Ozark National Forest.[^1] Its entry quotes a July 1934 account from the Fayetteville Daily Democrat.[^1] The visitors walked a one-mile foot trail to the tower, which they said was made of wood and about 340 feet tall.[^1] The visitors added that the forest ranger had vouched for the safety of the climb.[^1]

That height rests on the 1934 account alone.[^1] The directory marks the tower as removed, but gives no date.[^1]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Black Mountain, Forest Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/black-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
