The Eastern U.S. Lookouts directory has an Adel page that names Cook County and the Georgia Forestry Commission.[^1] The page gives only those two details.

A Georgia tower list has an entry called adel GFC, placed in Cook County at 31.10931, -83.42671. It gives 232 in its elevation column, with no unit stated.[^2] The same list says its names for towers numbered 73 and above are its own, so this name may not be the tower's historical name.[^2]

No source describes what remains at the site today.

[^1]: Adel - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Eastern U.S. Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/adel-gfc.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
