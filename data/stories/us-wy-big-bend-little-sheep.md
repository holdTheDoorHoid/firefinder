The lookout on Little Sheep Mountain was established after a fire in 1912, according to a lookout page for the site. That page places it in Sublette County within the Bridger-Teton National Forest.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association inventory lists the same place as Big Bend (Little Sheep), which suggests the two names refer to one lookout. The inventory puts the site near 43.32 degrees north and 109.94 degrees west.[^2]

The same inventory lists the site as gone, with its structure type unknown and no year given for its removal.[^2] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Little Sheep Mountain, Forest Lookouts, westlookouts.weebly.com, https://westlookouts.weebly.com/little-sheep-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Wyoming Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/wy/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
