A new cupola was put on the Beaver House fire tower in 1957, according to a Saskatchewan fire-tower listing [^1]. That listing describes the tower as light steel and 24.4 m (80 ft) tall, and gives its position as 53 degrees 20 minutes 32.8 seconds north, 104 degrees 27 minutes 5.9 seconds west [^1]. Canada Lookouts also lists the site among Saskatchewan's towers, on a page with one dated entry [^2].

The Department of Natural Resources annual report for 1963-64, as quoted on Canada Lookouts, records a full overhaul of the Beaver House tower in 1963 [^2]. That is the only work either source records after 1957.

No source I could read describes what, if anything, stands at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Firetowers of Saskatchewan, websiteservice.ca, https://www.websiteservice.ca/firetowers_page4.htm (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Beaver House, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/beaver-house.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
