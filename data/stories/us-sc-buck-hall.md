A regional directory of forest lookouts places a removed tower at Buck Hall, in Charleston County, South Carolina, within the Francis Marion National Forest.[^1]

A Post and Courier article on the forest's towers says the Forest Service once had more than a dozen lookout stations there, and that two of its towers were still standing, one of them the Witherbee tower in Berkeley County.[^2] The article's detailed example is Witherbee.[^2] The tower first stood at a nearby site in the 1930s, was relocated to the Witherbee Ranger District Office in 1952, and stayed in Forest Service use until 1989.[^2]

According to a National Forest Foundation guide, the Buck Hall Recreation Area is a campground on the Intracoastal Waterway that offers water, tent sites, showers and a picnic shelter.[^3] The guide adds that the campground occupies the former grounds of Buck Hall Plantation.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains of a lookout at the site today.
