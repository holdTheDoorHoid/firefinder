Boone is a fire lookout tower in Watauga County, North Carolina. A lookout-history site says the State Forest Service erected a 67-foot tower on the site in 1947, citing a state biennial report from 1948.[^1] The page names the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources as the agency.[^1] A state list of North Carolina towers gives the Boone site's coordinates as 36.23555, -81.70611 and its NGS point ID as FZ2931. The list was last revised in June 2021.[^2]

No source we found describes what stands at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
