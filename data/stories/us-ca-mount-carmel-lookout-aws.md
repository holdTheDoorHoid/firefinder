The Aircraft Warning Service operated a brief post on Mount Carmel in Monterey County, inside Los Padres National Forest.[^1]

The register gives 1942 as the build year, but its own description says the post was constructed in 1943.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page, which cites a book about Los Padres lookouts, also dates the cabin to 1942.[^1][^2] Mules packed the materials in.[^2] The cabin followed the standard AWS design, a ground cabin with an observer's cupola.[^1]

The Aircraft Warning Service relied on volunteers. They logged what they saw and then phoned reports to a regional Army filter center.[^3]

The station was discontinued in 1944 or 1945, once it was no longer needed.[^2][^1] In 1954 the Devil's Peak fire destroyed the cabin.[^1][^2]

The register added the site in December 2019, on the nomination of Brad Eells.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or how the public may visit it.
