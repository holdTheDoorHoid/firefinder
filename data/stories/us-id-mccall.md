The McCall lookout was a wooden tower.[^1] Firelookout.com dates the tower to 1942 and its abandonment to 1950.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the same two years, and files the lookout under West Central Idaho.[^2] Firelookout.com places it at a Forest Service facility in McCall, in Valley County, within the Payette National Forest.[^1] A McCall tourism page says lookouts began appearing in Valley County in the 1920s, prompted by a run of damaging fires in 1910.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the tower as gone, with no date given.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, stands at the site today, or whether the public can visit it. Anyone heading there should check with the Payette National Forest first.
