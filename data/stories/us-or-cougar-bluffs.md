Cougar Bluffs is a cliff in Douglas County, at about 3,973 ft (1,211 m) above sea level.[^2] TopoQuest places it on the Mace Mountain USGS map.[^2] Forest Lookouts lists a Cougar Bluff site on Umpqua National Forest land, at 26S-1W-23.[^1] Its only description is "An Alidade in a roofed pole shelter."[^1]

Today, no source describes what stands at the site, or whether it is open to visitors.
