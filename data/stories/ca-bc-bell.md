The Canada Lookouts index lists a lookout called Bell in the Kitimat-Stikine area of British Columbia.[^1] It gives the site as 56.351 N, 129.442 W, at an elevation of 1264 m (4,147 ft).[^1]

No source I could read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Bell, British Columbia Lookouts, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/bell.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
