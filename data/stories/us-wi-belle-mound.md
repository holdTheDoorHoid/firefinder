Belle Mound is a hill in Jackson County, Wisconsin, about six and a half miles southeast of Black River Falls and six miles northwest of Millston.[^1] The state's conservation department put a steel fire lookout on its highest point in 1928, and the record gives its cab as 7x7.[^1][^2]

The tower was a standard design, a square steel frame with a closed cabin at the top. The survey description puts its height at 25 feet, or about 7.6 m.[^1] A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description from 1934 places that survey mark at the center of the lookout tower, so the tower was in place by then.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page marks the tower as removed, with no date given.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left on the hill today, or whether the land is open to the public. The federal survey record for the hilltop is still on file, but it does not say whether a marker or foundation survives.[^1] Before going up, ask the landowner or local forest managers, and do not climb or enter any structure you find.
