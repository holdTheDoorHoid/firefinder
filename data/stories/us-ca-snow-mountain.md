A station on Snow Mountain in Colusa County was in use by January 1922.[^1] A Mountain Democrat item from that month reports that a forest ranger phoned in from the station on Snow Mountain, where snow on the summit was fifteen feet deep.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Snow Mountain site as gone.[^2] The 60,000-acre Snow Mountain Wilderness, in the Mendocino National Forest, centers on the peak and covers the summit, which lies in Colusa, Glenn and Lake counties. Forty miles of trail run through it.[^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout, so do not assume a structure is standing there.
