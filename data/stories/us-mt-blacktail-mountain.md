Blacktail Mountain rises about six miles west of Lakeside in Flathead County, Montana, to 6,757 feet.[^1] Two web sources tell its lookout history, and they differ on a few dates.[^1][^2]

The earliest mention we found is a 1927 newspaper item, reprinted by Forest Lookouts, about a man hired to staff the Blacktail lookout for the Blackfoot National Forest.[^2] Firelookout.com says a 40-foot pole L-4 tower went up in 1934.[^1] Its own header, however, gives 1935 as the start date for that first tower, so the page disagrees with itself.[^1] A 53-foot treated timber L-4 tower followed in 1962 and may have reused the first tower's cab.[^1]

The two pages also name the agency differently. Forest Lookouts lists the Flathead National Forest with the Northern Montana Forestry association, while firelookout.com lists the Flathead National Forest with the Montana DNRC.[^1][^2]

Staffing records are thin. Forest Lookouts reports that Al Williams was assigned to the Blacktail lookout in June 1938.[^2] Firelookout.com lists Herb Conklin in 1949 and Alfred Williams in 1952.[^1] The sources do not say whether the two Williams entries are the same person. In September 1950 an access road of about 11 miles, starting at the head of Lake Mary Ronan, was finished to the lookout.[^2] The road cut about half a day off the trip to the lookout in case of fire, and it ended the need to pack in with horses.[^2]

Firelookout.com says the second tower was destroyed in 1989.[^1] Forest Lookouts marks the site as removed but gives no date.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the site is open to the public. Check with the Flathead National Forest before trying to find it.

[^1]: Blacktail Mtn. Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com (Rex Kamstra), https://www.firelookout.com/mt/blacktail.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Blacktail Mountain, Forest Lookouts, western US (Ron Kemnow), https://montanalookouts.weebly.com/blacktail-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
