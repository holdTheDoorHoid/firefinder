Halfway Peak is a former lookout site in Shoshone County, Idaho, on the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, at 5,254 feet.[^1]

Firelookout.com puts an L-4 cab on the peak's south point and dates it 1931.[^1] The Idaho Fire Lookouts site gives the same date and design.[^2] Both sites give one end date: the lookout was abandoned in 1942.[^1][^2]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page says little or nothing of the lookout survives.[^2] Its notes report traces of grounding wire and a stray chunk of cement with rebar, and the post itself is dated September 13, 2022.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the site as gone.[^3]

The Idaho page lists the site as a hike-in. We found no source that says whether the public may visit, or that describes any standing structure there today.[^2]
