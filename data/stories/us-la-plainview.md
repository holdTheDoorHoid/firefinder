The Plainview Lookout Tower is in Rapides Parish, Louisiana.[^1]

In 1915 Louisiana set aside money for fire protection, enabling it to hire people to staff lookouts as watchmen or patrol officers.[^3] The state's tally of fire towers stood at fifty-six by 1950.[^3]

A 1951 USGS topographic map, published in 1952 and described by Pastmaps, lists the Plainview Lookout Tower as a landmark near Plainview School.[^2] Pastmaps also notes many old railroad grades in the map's northern sections, a sign of earlier logging in the area.[^2] Its summary adds that the tower underscores how much fire watching mattered in that wooded area.[^2]

No source we read says whether the tower still stands or whether visitors can reach the site.
