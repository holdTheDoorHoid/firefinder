The Central US Lookouts site has a separate page for a Tyler County tower called Chester, and it marks that tower as removed.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a second Barnum entry, Loc 2, in Tyler County as gone.[^2] The Texas lookout index files Chester under Tyler County and names no agency.[^3] The town of Chester sits in the far northwest of Tyler County, near where U.S. Highway 287 meets Farm Road 1745.[^4] The sources do not settle whether Chester is a second tower or the same as the Barnum tower numbered 16. No source describes what remains at the site today.
