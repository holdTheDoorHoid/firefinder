West Plains is a state fire tower site in Howell County, Missouri.[^1] A page quoting the Missouri Conservation Commission's annual report for fiscal 1954-55 says a new tower was erected there.[^1] The Missouri Department of Conservation's 2015 area plan lists the towersite as two acres, acquired in 1955.[^2]

In fiscal 1971-72 the tower road was oil sealed.[^1]

The 2015 plan says the site carries three Department and Howell County 911 radio relays, and that Department staff still use the tower for fire detection.[^2] A 2022 Missouri Conservationist article says the department still runs about 50 fire towers, and that they are no longer required to have full-time staff in fire season.[^3]

The plan describes West Plains as likely eligible for the National Register of Historic Places, one of four towersites it names that way.[^2] The plan's West Plains appendix says the area is not open to the public.[^2] It names three other towersites, Mountain View, Tecumseh and Timber Knob, as open to visitors.[^2]

The 2015 plan is the newest source I found that describes this towersite, so it does not show what stands there today.[^2]
