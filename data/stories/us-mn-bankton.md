An earlier tower at the Molde site in St. Louis County was numbered 103 on a 1939 list.[^1][^2] In 1958 it was moved to Lake of the Woods County and became the Bankton tower.[^1][^2] The county list records the owner as the Minnesota Forest Service, which it says is now the state Department of Natural Resources' forestry division.[^1] It is listed as an 81-foot ladder tower.[^1][^2]

The Central US Lookouts page marks the tower as removed,[^3] and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as gone.[^4] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
