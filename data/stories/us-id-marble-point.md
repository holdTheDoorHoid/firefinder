Marble Point Lookout was a 12-by-12-foot frame cabin topped by a roof platform, built about 1924.[^2][^3] It stood at 5,983 feet in Idaho County, 16 miles south-southeast of Grangeville.[^2]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site places it two miles north of Sawyer Ridge Lookout, not on a prominent point, and labels it drive-up.[^1] The same site does not yet have a documented staffing history for the lookout.[^1]

Sawyer Ridge Lookout, two miles to the south, was first built in 1949 as a 41-foot tower of treated timber with an L-4 cab.[^2][^4] Firelookout.com says Sawyer Ridge may have replaced Marble Point.[^2] Sawyer Ridge's 1949 date falls before Marble Point's 1950 destruction, and both firelookout.com pages say only that it may have been a replacement, so the sources do not settle the question.[^2][^3][^4]

The sources agree that the cabin was destroyed in 1950.[^1][^2][^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it is open to the public. Two sources name the Nez Perce National Forest, one of them alongside the Clearwater, so ask the Forest Service before going to look.[^1][^2]
