Hemlock Butte sits in the Willamette National Forest, about 18 miles southeast of Oakridge in Lane County, at 5,804 feet.[^1][^2]

The lookout was an L-5 cab.[^1][^2] The Oregon lookouts list gives its size as 10x10.[^3] The register and firelookout.com both say it was built in 1933.[^1][^2] The Oregon list gives 1934 instead.[^3] Its entry begins with an August 1932 newspaper item that planned new lookout houses, with Hemlock Mountain among the sites.[^3] The sources disagree by a year, and they do not settle which date is right.

The Oregon list quotes newspapers for the lookout's staffing.[^3] In September 1934 the Eugene Register-Guard reported that Clayton Kelsay had been sent to an emergency post on Hemlock Mountain as a lookout fireman, to stay until the rains began.[^3] In August 1938 the Eugene Guard reported that Gordon Stanely had been burned on the arms at his station, and that Horace Lundberg covered his post for several days.[^3]

The Oregon list says the lookout was abandoned in the 1940s.[^3] The register and firelookout.com say it was no longer there by 1962.[^1][^2] No source gives an exact removal date.[^1][^2][^3]

The register lists the site as registered on May 8, 2011, with the U.S. Forest Service as administrator.[^1]

The Oregon list marks the lookout as removed.[^3] No source describes what remains at the site today or whether visitors can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
