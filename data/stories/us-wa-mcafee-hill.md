McAfee Hill rises only a little from flat ground in Grays Harbor County.[^3] Sometime in the 1940s a summit cabin was used as a fire lookout, and the register says it was gone by 1952.[^1][^2][^3]

Willhite's visit found no footings, though a credible person had told him they remained.[^3] He did find an old garbage pit containing radio batteries.[^3] Beyond that pit, no source describes what remains on the summit today.[^1][^2][^3]

As of 2017, entry required a Rayonier Timber permit.[^3] A seasonal permit allowed driving to the summit during hunting season.[^3] The annual permit ruled out driving in but allowed entry from about mid-January to June 30, outside hunting season.[^3] Willhite's route had many gates and covered 18 miles round-trip.[^3] Whether these permit rules still apply is not stated.[^1][^2][^3]
