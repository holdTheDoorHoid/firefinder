The Monte Cristo lookout stood on Monte Cristo Mountain in the Gifford Pinchot National Forest, in Klickitat County, about seven miles south-southwest of Trout Lake.[^1][^2][^4]

The lookout went up in 1930.[^1][^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register describes an L-4 cab on a tower, and places the site at 4,171 feet.[^1] Willhite's page says the same: built around 1930, with an L-4 cab.[^3]

The Register says the lookout no longer stands.[^1] Firelookout.com marks it as abandoned and gone.[^2] A 1956 USGS survey mentions a monument about 44 feet east of the middle of the lookout house, but does not say whether the lookout was still in use.[^3]

Today the site is reached by trail.[^4] The Forest Service says Trail 53 runs 4.1 miles along a thinly forested ridge to Monte Cristo Mountain, and that remnants of a 1930s-era fire lookout can still be found there.[^4] The trail is open to hikers, bikers and horse riders.[^4] Willhite describes a separate approach he calls the North Trail, with a hiking distance of 2 miles, and its access leaves Highway 141 west of Trout Lake.[^3] He warns that the last mile is a rough, rocky road, and that there is nowhere to park where the trail leaves it, so you may miss the trail entirely.[^3]
