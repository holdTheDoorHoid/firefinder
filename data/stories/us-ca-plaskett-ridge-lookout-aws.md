Plaskett Ridge is in the Los Padres National Forest, in Monterey County.[^1] The post there was a standard Aircraft Warning Service (AWS) structure with a ground cabin and a cupola, which the register dates to about 1943.[^1][^4]

The Forest Service equipped and manned the post, and the Army paid the Forest Service back for all of its costs.[^1] Operators were supposed to pass aircraft activity, and every fire or smoke, on to Forest Service dispatch.[^1] A lookout-history site says the cabin was put to use spotting aircraft during the Second World War.[^2]

The register's list shows the site as Gone.[^3] Its detail page says the removal date is unknown, and that the post's precise spot is unknown too, even though the register gives coordinates for the site.[^1] The register added the site on December 22, 2019, on the nomination of Brad Eells.[^1]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.
