The LaSalle Lookout Tower stands in Clearwater County, Minnesota, in the Mississippi Headwaters State Forest.[^1][^2][^3]

The Minnesota Forest Service owned the tower, which dates to 1931.[^2] The Central US lookouts list gives an 80-foot Aermotor tower with ladder access.[^1] The Clearwater County text also says 80 feet, though its table gives 81.[^2]

The two lists do not agree on everything. A 1941 survey note quoted in the Clearwater County list says the tower was about 100 feet tall, of steel, with an enclosed crow's nest.[^2]

We did not find the names of anyone who watched from the tower, or any particular fire it was used on.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing.[^4] The Clearwater County list says the county rents the tower out for communications, but gives no start date for that arrangement.[^2]

We found no public access information. Because the tower is leased for communications, treat it as off limits and do not climb it.
