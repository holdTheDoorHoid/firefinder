Dorset Peak lies in the town of Danby in Rutland County.[^1][^2] Early notices call the hill both Dorset Mountain and Dorset Peak.[^1]

In 1921 a cabin was put up on the mountain, and building materials for a tower were readied.[^1] In June 1922 the State Forester visited the lookout station, and a newspaper noted that some work was still needed to finish it.[^1] In October 1925 a group of Rutland scouts planned a day trip to repair the fire tower and the observer's cabin.[^1]

Forest Lookouts lists the tower as removed, with no date given.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as an open tower that is gone.[^2]

Hikers now reach the old tower site on foot.[^3] A jeep road that is no longer maintained climbs toward the summit, and a brief side spur reaches South Peak, which marks the site of an old fire tower.[^3] Expect an unpaved access road that is sometimes rough; onX reports that no permit is needed.[^3] The route also goes by a fire warden's cabin that has fallen in.[^3] A 2008 hiking account reports old fire-tower remains and a gas grill on a height of land short of the true summit.[^4]

No source we read says how much of the tower is left. There is no standing tower to climb.[^1][^2]
