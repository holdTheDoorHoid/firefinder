Rocky Butte Area Tower is a 90-foot Aermotor MC-39 tower in Douglas County, Washington, at an elevation of 2,444 feet (745 meters).[^1] Aermotor produced several lookout models over the years, and the MC-39 was one of them.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register knows little about the tower's early life.[^1] The register believes the tower was previously used for fire detection on a reservation before it reached its present site.[^1] It does not name the reservation or give a date.[^1]

The register believes the tower has stood at its present site since at least the mid-1980s.[^1] There it hosts communications equipment.[^1] I found no construction date, no staffing record, and no notable fire history for it.[^1]

A regional photo caption mentions a different tower: a 130-foot lookout west of Rocky Butte that is not in the historic listings. The two should not be confused.[^3]

The tower was registered on January 2, 2026, and the Washington lookout list marks it as standing.[^1][^4] I found no access information.[^1] Because the site now carries communications gear, this story does not suggest a visit.[^1]
