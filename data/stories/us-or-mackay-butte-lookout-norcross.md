Mackay Butte Lookout, which the National Historic Lookout Register calls the Norcross Station, sits in Crook County about 24 miles south-southeast of Paulina.[^1][^3] Newspapers usually called it Mackey Butte, and old photographs show a sign reading "Norcross Forest Guard Station."[^1][^2]

The site began as a camp in 1925.[^1][^3] In July 1925 a report announced a new phone link from Allison Ranger Station to Mackey Butte, and said a fireman would be posted on the butte that year instead of at Allison.[^2] In 1935 a residence cabin, a garage and an exposed fire-finder stand were completed.[^1][^3] In 1940 the fire-finder was enclosed in a 6-by-6-foot ground lookout house.[^1][^3] The NHLR gives both 1935 and 1940 as build years: the first marks the cabin and fire-finder stand, and the second the enclosed lookout house.[^1]

The pages read name no one who staffed the station and record no fire counts.[^1][^2][^3]

The station was abandoned in the 1950s.[^1][^3] By 2019 the NHLR found it still a standing ruin.[^1] Firelookout.com says the 1935 cabin and the 1940 cab were still there, and the garage had become ruins.[^3]

Firelookout.com says the site is private property the public cannot visit, and the NHLR lists it as private land.[^3][^1] Anyone nearby should stay off the property and leave the ruins alone.
