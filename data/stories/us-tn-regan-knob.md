Regan Knob is a fire lookout site in Pickett County, Tennessee. Tennessee Landforms gives its elevation as 1,642 feet and lists the tower as standing.[^1]

A Komoot summit listing for Regan Knob gives the elevation as 480 meters, about 1,575 feet, which is roughly 20 meters lower than the Landforms figure.[^3] The listing describes the knob as a summit and does not mention a tower.[^3] Where the two figures differ, we have used the Landforms value, because it sits in the same tower list as the rest of this record.

The 2014 National Register study lists Regan Knob in Pickett County among the towers standing on March 26, 2014.[^2] The same study credits the Tennessee Division of Forestry with 208 lookout towers built from roughly 1930 to 1970.[^2] We found no build date, design, builder or staffing history for Regan Knob in the sources we checked.

We also found no record of notable fires at this site. The 2014 list is the latest dated record we found.

Tennessee Landforms advises visitors not to climb its towers, because steps are frequently missing or decayed.[^1] We found no public visiting arrangement for Regan Knob, so please do not climb it. Check with the Tennessee Division of Forestry before visiting, since the tower's current condition is not confirmed.
