The Leflore Lookout Tower is in Leflore County, Mississippi, in the Mississippi Delta.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as a 120-foot Aermotor LS-40 on flat Delta land, and gives the site's elevation as 136 feet, or 41 meters.[^1]

Aermotor built its towers from galvanized steel, and the LS-40 was one of the company's models.[^3] The register gives no build date and names no staff member or fire connected with the tower.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register recorded the tower on July 9, 2023, as US 1737, MS 54, on the nomination of Jason Johns.[^1] The Mississippi Forestry Commission administers the site.[^1]

OpenStreetMap has a point at nearly the same coordinates as the register. It is labeled Fire Tower and tagged as an observation tower.[^1][^2] The register's name, Leflore Lookout Tower, is the one we use here.[^1]

The register's only site visit is noted as 7/2/23.[^1] The sources we read do not say whether the tower still stands or has been moved, burned or taken down.[^1][^2] The register gives no access information, and the OpenStreetMap entry lists no access details either.[^1][^2] Anyone interested should contact the Mississippi Forestry Commission before visiting.[^1]
