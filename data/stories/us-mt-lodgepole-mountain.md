Lodgepole Mountain's lookout stood on Flathead National Forest land in Flathead County, Montana, about 21 miles southeast of Essex, at an elevation of 7,211 feet.[^1] Forest Lookouts lists its legal location as 27N-12W-18.[^2]

Firelookout.com dates the tower to 1931 and calls it a 10-foot pole L-4 tower.[^1] The Forest Service's history of the Flathead says the L-4 was a standard design with a gabled roof and windows on every side.[^3]

Firelookout.com says the tower was destroyed in 1961 and gives no cause.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list also marks it as gone.[^4]

The site is inside the Great Bear Wilderness.[^1] The Forest Service history says that wilderness was designated in 1978.[^5] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the ground today, or whether a route leads to the site.
