Dominion Mountain, also called Dominion Peak, stands on the Idaho-Montana state line about 8 miles southeast of Lookout Pass, at 6,032 feet.[^2][^3] A lookout was set up there in 1933.[^1] A Sanders County newspaper item from July 26, 1933, quoted on a Montana lookout list, placed Charles Nelson on the peak as lookout and patrolman, and said he would use a road built in 1932 along the divide for his patrol work.[^3] An L-4 cab was built there in 1934.[^1][^2]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts team says Charles Nelson is the confirmed lookout for 1933.[^1] Its 1938 entry names N.B. Benson, but the date is uncertain, because the footing inscription is hard to read and the team said it needed help deciphering it.[^1] A September 1944 newspaper item, quoted on the Montana list, says Anna Bogdan and another woman worked the lookout that summer.[^3]

Sources date the destruction to 1962.[^1][^2] In a September 2025 post, the Idaho team described cement footings for the cab, with names carved into three of them.[^1] A separate Idaho Fire Lookouts account of a visit to the site calls the ruins a fallen tower.[^5] The footings are linked to the L-4 cab, while that account describes a fallen tower, so the structure type is unclear.[^1][^5] Beyond the footings and the fallen tower, no source describes what remains.[^1][^5]

Sources disagree on the forest. Four sources put the site in the St. Joe National Forest,[^1][^2][^4][^5] and the Montana list gives the Kootenai National Forest.[^3] The Idaho post calls the site a drive-up location.[^1] No source says whether the public may go there.[^1]
