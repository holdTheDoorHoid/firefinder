The tnlandforms.us list of North Carolina lookout towers gives a Palestine tower in Cumberland County, with the NGS point ID EZ4810.[^1] A history page for a lookout spelled Palistine gives 1927 as the year a steel tower of 60 feet (about 18.3 m) went up at Palestine Church, 10 miles north of Fayetteville.[^2] In January 1928, The Forest Worker listed a steel tower 60 feet tall on Palistine Hill among the towers newly put into service.[^2] Both records put the tower in Cumberland County, so they probably describe one lookout, though no source says so outright.[^1][^2]

No source describes the site today.

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Palistine - FOREST LOOKOUTS, easternuslookouts.weebly.com, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/palistine.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
