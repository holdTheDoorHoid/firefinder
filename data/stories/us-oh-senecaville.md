The Senecaville Fire Tower stands in Noble County, Ohio.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register accepted it on September 3, 2014, as US 1026, OH 15, and gives its elevation as 1,198 feet (365 meters).[^1]

The register lists the tower as administered by a private owner, Chuck Kolb.[^1] The register also notes that the tower carries radio gear for the Muskingum Watershed Conservancy District.[^1]

The sources read say nothing about when the tower was built, who built it, how tall it is, or who watched it for fires. The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list of Ohio towers marks it as standing.[^2]

The tower is privately owned, but the register does not say whether visitors may go onto the land or climb it. Ask the owner before going near the site, and do not climb the tower.
