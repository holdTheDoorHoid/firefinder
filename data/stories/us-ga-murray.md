The Murray Fire Tower stands on top of Cohutta Mountain inside Fort Mountain State Park in Murray County, at about 2,760 feet.[^1] The Register names Georgia's Department of Natural Resources as the administering agency.[^1]

The sources read do not say when the tower went up, who staffed it, or what fires it watched.[^1][^2] The Register gives no build year at all.[^1]

What remains is the metal frame.[^1] The Register says the cab is missing and calls the tower a "lost second tower of Fort Mountain."[^1] A Forest Lookouts index lists the same park site under the name Cohutta Mountain, in Murray County.[^2]

An engineering firm's article about the park's stone fire tower says it served as a fire lookout until the early 1960s, when a modern steel tower was built nearby.[^3] The article does not name that steel tower, so the sources cannot say whether this frame is it.[^3]

The Register describes the frame as remaining, but without its cab.[^1] It says nothing about when the cab was lost or who removed it.[^1] The tower was registered on January 8, 2021, as US 1479 and GA 29, after a nomination by Patrick H. Warren, MPH.[^1]

The sources read do not say whether the frame can be visited or climbed.[^1][^2] With the cab gone and no word on access, do not climb it. Anyone going to the park should ask park staff before going to look for it.
