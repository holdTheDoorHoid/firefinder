Sunday Mountain stands about 28 miles northwest of Whitefish, in Flathead County, at an elevation of 5,485 feet.[^1] The firelookout.com page lists the Kootenai National Forest as the agency, and a Forest Lookouts page places the tower on the Fortine Ranger District.[^1][^2]

The firelookout.com page describes a 60-foot steel Aermotor tower built in 1934.[^1] The tower was staffed until 1973.[^1] Accidental burning in 1963 claimed the log cabin used as living quarters, and trailers took its place.[^1] Skillet Mountain, five miles to the southwest, also served as a patrol point.[^1]

In July 1975 the Kootenai National Forest asked for bids on surplus buildings and property.[^2] One item was a steel lookout tower without a cabin on Sunday Mountain, and bids were due by 2 p.m. on August 11.[^2] The firelookout.com page gives 1975 as the year the tower was removed, the same year the forest offered it for sale.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the public can reach the site. A firelookout.org Montana list also marks the lookout as gone.[^3]
