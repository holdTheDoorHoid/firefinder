Across Tennessee, the Civilian Conservation Corps erected 98 lookout towers between 1933 and 1941.[^1] A personal inventory of Tennessee lookout towers, published at tnlandforms.us, calls this one Shelby S.F. and places it in Shelby County at an elevation of 433 feet (132 m).[^1] Its district-and-tower number, 9-18, comes from a 1976 list of state forest towers.[^1]

The inventory marks the tower as removed.[^1] It adds that many towers have since been taken down, and that most former sites are now privately owned.[^1]

Meeman-Shelby Forest State Park, a state park near Memphis, lies in the same county.[^2] None of the sources I read says whether this tower stood inside the park.

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
