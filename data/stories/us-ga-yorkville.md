A list of Georgia lookout towers includes a tower in Paulding County under the simple name "yorkville". It places the tower at 33.92228, -84.98684 and lists its elevation as 1,405, with no unit given. Read as feet, that is about 428 m.[^1] The list also gives the code gfc-721.[^1] The page is marked as revised on 22 March 2016, so the entry may be out of date.[^1]

The name matches Yorkville, a community in Paulding County, Georgia, that is not an incorporated town or city. Wikipedia places it near the junction of Highway 113 and Gold Mine Road.[^2] The same list also places two other towers in the county, Elsberry Mtn and Pegamore.[^1]

No source found describes the site today. This story cannot say whether a tower, footings or any other structure remains there, or whether the public can reach the spot.
