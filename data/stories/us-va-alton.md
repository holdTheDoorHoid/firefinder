The Alton Fire Tower stands in the Cluster Springs quadrangle of Halifax County, Virginia, and the Virginia Department of Forestry administers it.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as an 80-foot Aermotor tower, and a Weebly lookout entry for Virginia gives the same height.[^1][^2]

The sources do not say when the tower was built, and none names the people who staffed it.[^1][^2] The Register records that the tower was registered on September 3, 2014, as US 1038 and VA 18, and gives its elevation as 572 feet (174 m).[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list shows the tower as standing.[^3] These sources record no fire or other major event at the tower.[^1][^2]

None of these sources describes public access or rental use, so a visitor should ask the Virginia Department of Forestry before planning a trip.[^1][^2][^3]
