A steel fire tower went up in 1936 on a summit overlooking Salem Mountain.[^1][^2] A Scranton newspaper reported that preliminary work started on September 4, 1936, employing five men, and that WPA funds would pay for the tower.[^2] The report gave the tower's height as 85 feet and said it would use steel girders.[^2] Those who planned it expected the view from the top to reach 12 miles.[^2] It was meant as a lookout for fire wardens, and the tower was to stand in a broad tract of valuable timberland.[^2]

Sources disagree about the county. The register places the tower in Wayne County.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page files it under Lackawanna County, and it quotes the 1936 report saying the land belonged to the Scranton Coal Company in Carbondale Township.[^2]

The register says the tower was removed in 1985.[^1]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. An OpenStreetMap point still carries the name Salem Firetower, and a note on that point says it may be in the wrong spot.[^3] No source we read says whether the site is open to the public.
