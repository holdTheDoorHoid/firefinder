Erwin Mountain was a fire lookout on the Erwin Game Management Area, about four miles west of Corning in Steuben County.[^1] It was an 82-foot-6-inch International Derrick tower, erected by Civilian Conservation Corps Camp S-123 at Beaver Dams.[^1] The nysffla.org list says the work began in 1940 and was completed in 1941.[^1] Starr's 2011 list gives 1940 as the year it opened and identifies the design as a 1937 International Derrick model.[^2]

Its first year of staffing, 1943, recorded 65 fires and 90 visitors.[^1] Starr's list says it ran three months a year, in April, May and October, and into June and November when fire danger was extremely high.[^2] Regular staffing ended in 1972, when aerial detection led to the tower being closed and listed as inactive.[^1] It still served flood emergency communications until 1978.[^1]

It was declared excess and sold in 1979.[^1] Starr's list, the nysffla.org list and the CNY Hiking list all mark it as removed, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association marks it as gone.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

No source we read describes the Game Management Area site now, including whether anything stands there or whether the public can visit.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
