Alberta's government publishes a map of its wildfire lookout sites. It shows 127 of them, spread through the boreal forest and out along the Rockies.[^1] Observers usually work each site from April through October, keeping watch for fires throughout the day.[^2] The province notes that the sites are isolated, and that staff must prepare for a range of possibilities, including meeting a curious bear.[^2]
