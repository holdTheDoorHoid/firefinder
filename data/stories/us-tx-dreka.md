The Dreka fire lookout stood in Shelby County, Texas, inside the Sabine National Forest.[^1] The earliest record we found is a 1971 survey description by the National Geodetic Survey.[^2] It places the tower about six and a quarter miles north-northeast of Patroon, on the south side of State Highway 139.[^2] The surveyors described a silver steel frame on four legs, topped by a metal cabin and about 110 feet overall, and noted that the U.S. Forest Service managed it.[^2]

A 1984 USGS map of the Patroon North area, as reproduced on Pastmaps, labels a Lookout Tower among its landmarks.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas index now lists the tower as gone.[^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
