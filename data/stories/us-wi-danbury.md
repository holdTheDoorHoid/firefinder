Danbury Lookout stood in Burnett County, Wisconsin, at an elevation of 1,104 feet.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes an 84-foot Wisconsin standard tower with an external protected ladder, built in November 1932.[^1]

The record gets complicated there. An October 20, 1930 newspaper item, quoted on a lookout history site, already describes a state fire tower at Danbury.[^2] It describes the crow's nest as winterized, with the tower closed in and sliding windows fitted, and says a direct phone line connected Webster, Danbury, Gordon and Spooner.[^2] The 1930 and 1932 dates disagree.[^1][^2]

The register recorded the tower on August 28, 2017, and lists the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources as its administrator.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list now marks the Danbury tower as gone.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
