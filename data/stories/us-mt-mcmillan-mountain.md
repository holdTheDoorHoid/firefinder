The McMillan Mountain lookout was in Lincoln County, Montana, on the Kootenai National Forest, about 8 miles south-southeast of Libby at 4,073 feet.[^1][^3]

Early accounts disagree about the first structures. Firelookout.com lists a cabin and two crow's nests for 1920, then a frame cabin for 1931.[^1] Forest Lookouts dates a tent camp with a tree crow's nest to an uncertain point in the 1920s, and a frame living quarters to 1929.[^3]

A Western News item of July 1930, quoted by Forest Lookouts, describes a lookout platform 72 feet up in a tree.[^3] The same item names Dave Foster as the lookout on McMillan Mountain that summer.[^3]

The end date also differs. Firelookout.com says the site was abandoned in 1942.[^1] Forest Lookouts says the lookout site was abandoned in 1944.[^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as Gone.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout today, or whether visitors can reach the site.
