Triangle Hill, in Deschutes County, Oregon, once held a lookout that Firelookout.com describes as a 57-foot crow's nest from the 1930s, now in ruins.[^1][^3]

A separate Forest Lookouts page, Triangle Hill Tree, describes a 57-foot tree lookout that appears to be the same site, though neither source says so outright.[^2] Its 2007 entry says the tree had come down and that much of it had been cut into firewood.[^2] The page says no information has been found on the site, and speculates that it may have served as an emergency post run jointly with the local fire association.[^2]

A statewide index lists the lookout as abandoned.[^3] No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
