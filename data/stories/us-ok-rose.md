The Forest Lookouts list places a 60-foot tower in Mayes County, southeast of Locust Grove, and credits the Oklahoma Department of Forestry.[^1]

TopoQuest lists a Rose Lookout Tower in Mayes County at an elevation of 1,263 feet, and says it appears on the USGS 1:24K topographic map for Rose, Oklahoma.[^2]

The lookout list uses the Oklahoma Department of Forestry, but the state's forestry page uses Oklahoma Forestry Services. That division says it has cared for the state's forests since 1925, and it operates within the Oklahoma Department of Agriculture, Food, and Forestry.[^1][^3]

No source describes the tower today. TopoQuest says it appears on the USGS map, but a map does not show whether the tower still stands or whether the site is open to the public.[^2]
