Oquossoc Bald is the fire tower on Bald Mountain in Rangeley, Franklin County, Maine.[^1][^2][^3]

A 2021 Sun Journal list dates the tower to the 1920s and says it was replaced in 1994.[^1]

The same list calls the tower observation-only. The Forest Fire Lookout Association marks it with an asterisk, which it uses for towers whose cab is no longer in place.[^1][^3][^4]

A hiking site describes a tower cab with Rangeley Lake on one side, but its writers say they have not been there yet.[^5] We followed the newspapers and the association here. An August 2026 Sun Journal piece describes a metal lookout with a top platform and does not mention a cab. It tells readers to climb the lookout for a 360-degree view, and warns they may have to wait their turn.[^2]

Two trailheads, on Carry Road and Bald Mountain Road, serve the trail.[^2] A hiking guide says the trail is on state land and worth visiting.[^6]

We found no recent inspection of the structure, so its soundness is unconfirmed.
