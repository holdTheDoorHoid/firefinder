Little Bunchgrass Mountain held a lookout in Lane County, about 10 miles east of Oakridge, in the Willamette National Forest.[^1][^2] The U.S. Forest Service administered it.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register listed the site on May 8, 2011.[^1]

The register and firelookout.com both date the cab to 1933. It was a 10-by-10-foot L-5 ground cab.[^1][^2] A local history of the forest's lookouts dates the build later. It reports that materials for the lookout house were on their way in April 1934, and that a 10-by-10-foot L-5 lookout house was built in 1934.[^3]

A July 1933 Eugene newspaper item said Frank Singletary had left to serve as lookout fireman on the mountain.[^3] An August 1938 item said Phillip Dimick was then serving as lookout fireman there.[^3]

The register and the local history say the lookout was removed in 1968.[^1][^3] Firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^2] None of these sources says how the cab came down.[^1][^2][^3]

No source here describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
