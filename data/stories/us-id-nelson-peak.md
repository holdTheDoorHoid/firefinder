Nelson Peak rises to 5,968 feet in Shoshone County, Idaho, and sits about four miles east-northeast of Avery.[^1][^2]

The peak lies within the St. Joe National Forest.[^1][^2]

The first structure was a log cabin with a cupola, which the sources date only to the 1920s.[^1][^2] In 1935 a 48-foot pole platform went up on the west summit.[^1][^2] In 1939 an L-4 tower was built on the east summit.[^1][^2] A patrol point lay 1.5 miles to the northwest.[^1][^2]

Both pages say the tower was destroyed in 1969.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com says the ruins of the west-summit platform were still there in the 1980s.[^1]

No source describes what remains of the site today, and none says whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3] The association's list marks the lookout gone.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site Hike-In, which describes how it is reached, not whether it is open.[^2]
