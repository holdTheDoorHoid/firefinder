Tepee Lookout Tower was erected in 1933 by men of the CCC's Paint Lake Camp, on the Ottawa National Forest in Iron County, Michigan.[^1][^2] The 100-foot Aermotor steel tower was built with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab, reached by an outside ladder.[^1][^2]

The register also mentions a groundhouse and a CCC garage at the site, and reports that neither survives.[^1] Staff worked the lookout until the late 1960s.[^1] We found no account of fires it spotted, and nothing we read says the tower was moved, burned or taken down.[^1]

The register entry dates from March 1, 1999, and names the U.S. Forest Service as administrator.[^1] Public information is thin today. The Forest Service's page for Tepee Lake describes the lake as a place for boaters and fishermen, and places it on the Kenton Ranger District.[^3] We found no visiting, rental or climbing information for the tower itself, so please do not climb it.[^1] Check with the Kenton Ranger District before going near the site.[^3]
