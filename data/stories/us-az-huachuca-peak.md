Huachuca Peak had a fire lookout built in the 1930s.[^2] In June 1930 the commanding officer at Fort Huachuca agreed to put a detachment of two or three men on the peak for observation, with the district ranger handling their training. The detachment was expected to stay until July 15.[^2] Forest Lookouts also quotes a Fort Huachuca history that lists a fire lookout and cistern on the peak among quartermaster work done in 1936, carried out by the Works Progress Administration.[^2]

Forest Lookouts places the site on Fort Huachuca in Santa Cruz County, at legal location 22S-19E-26.[^2] Firelookout.org's Arizona list puts the tower in Cochise County instead.[^1] A history of Fort Huachuca's development gives the peak's elevation as 8,410 feet (2,563 m).[^3]

Firelookout.org marks the tower gone.[^1] No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go up the peak.[^1][^2][^3]
