Dunn's Mountain, in Rowan County, had a fire lookout tower on its summit from 1952 until 1971.[^1] The county's forestry history says the first lookout was built in spring 1952 as a platform between two trees, with a third tree cut to serve as the last leg. Windows and a roof came later.[^1]

Survey records describe the summit earlier. The survey station is about 4 miles southeast of Salisbury, and a 1948 mark was set about 30 feet northwest of an old log building near the highest point.[^2]

A 1960 note from the state forestry division says a fire tower was built about 10 feet from that mark in summer 1956. That does not match the county's 1952 date.[^1][^2] In 1957 the tree tower was replaced by a steel tower.[^1] By 1965 the log building was gone, and a fire tower stood to the north on the same hilltop.[^2] The county says the summit tower was taken down in 1971.[^1]

Today the mountain is an 83-acre public park that opened in 2006.[^3] A short, mostly paved trail climbs to the top, and a shuttle runs to the summit on weekends.[^3] A 2014 survey note places the mark in the gravel parking lot of the Dunn Mountain visitor center.[^2] No source I found says whether any part of the lookout remains on the summit.
