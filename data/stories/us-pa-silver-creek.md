Silver Creek Fire Tower stands on Broad Mountain in Blythe Township, Schuylkill County, Pennsylvania, just south of the Weiser State Forest.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register says it was erected in the spring of 1926.[^1] A history page on the Forest Lookouts site describes a 60-foot steel Aermotor tower with a 7-by-7 observation cab, put up by the State Forestry Department.[^2]

The tower had a keeper in its early years. A July 1930 item quoted on the Forest Lookouts page says Mrs. Warren Creasy had been in charge for four years and was back on duty that summer.[^2] A Sunday in May 1930 brought 51 visitors to the tower.[^2]

During World War II the tower served as an Aircraft Warning Service station.[^1] A storm in the mid-1940s damaged the tower and its cab, which were then rebuilt. The new cab has an updated window design.[^1]

By August 2013 the tower was closed. A news report blamed aging and vandalism and said that budget and maintenance staff had fallen short, though hikers still came and a park naturalist led a group hike once a month from spring to fall.[^2] A visitor's blog from July 2021 found it out of use and enclosed by a fence that vandals had broken, and guessed it had been shut for about twenty years.[^3]

The sources say nothing about its condition after 2021, or whether the public may climb it. The register names the Pennsylvania Bureau of Forestry as administrator, but the land is now owned by Blythe Township, so both should be checked before anyone plans a visit.[^1] Treat the tower as closed, and do not climb it.
