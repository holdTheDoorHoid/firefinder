Station Butte sits in the Willamette National Forest in Lane County, at about 1,680 feet.[^1][^2] A lookout history says the spot was used as a patrol lookout point as early as 1907.[^3] Two other sources date a lookout here to about 1919, when a 20-foot crow's nest and a camp were built.[^1][^2] The accounts do not agree on the start date. The 1907 entry describes a patrol lookout point and does not mention a built structure.[^3]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register and firelookout.com both say the crow's nest and camp were gone by 1958.[^1][^2] Neither describes what, if anything, is left on the butte.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list marks the site as Abandoned rather than gone, so the list and the detail pages do not match.[^4]

No source I found describes the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3] Anyone planning a visit should check with the Willamette National Forest first.
