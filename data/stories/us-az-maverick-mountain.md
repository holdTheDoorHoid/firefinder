Maverick Mountain rises to 8,086 feet on the White Mountain Apache Reservation in Apache County, and the National Historic Lookout Register says its tower continues in active service.[^1] A tower about 60 feet tall went up on the site in 1925.[^1] In 1934 an 80-foot steel tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab replaced it, and the register says three Aermotor MC-39 towers were put up on the reservation that year, this one among them.[^1] A travel site calls the 1934 tower 60 feet tall, which is the height the register gives for the 1925 tower, so the two sources disagree about the height.[^2][^1]

The register names the White Mountain Apache Agency as the lookout's administrator, and it was registered on January 31, 2002, when Dave Lorenz, the AZ/NM registrar, nominated it.[^1] I did not find a dated record of who staffs the tower or of its fire history.

The lookout sits on tribal land.[^1][^2] The travel site says visitors need a recreation permit from the tribe's Game and Fish department, and it warns that the access road is unpaved.[^2] The same site invites readers to climb to the top, but it also says access to the tower itself varies.[^2] Please don't climb it. Get the current permit rules from the tribe before you go.
