Brogdon Hill Lookout stands in Gordon County, Georgia, and the Georgia Forestry Commission administers it.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register says the tower is believed to have been built in the 1940s, and that it protects Calhoun and the surrounding area.[^1] I found no exact build year, design details or builder in the sources, so those points remain unknown.[^1][^2]

The register added the tower on August 28, 2017.[^1] A Georgia tower list files it under the code gfc-709 and gives an elevation of 906 (units not stated), while the register gives 897 feet (273 meters), so the two sources differ slightly on elevation.[^2][^1]

The most recent condition report I found is a register visit dated May 28, 2018. It records the tower as still standing with the cab completely removed, junked vehicles around it, and poison ivy growing over it.[^1]

No source I found says whether the site can be visited today. The same report includes a visitor's question about whether the site is on public or private land, and says the steps are completely overgrown.[^1] Until the Georgia Forestry Commission confirms who owns the site and what its condition is, treat it as off limits and do not climb it.

[^2]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
