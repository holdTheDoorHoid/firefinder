This Bald Mountain is a cabin site on the Clearwater National Forest, 26 miles west of Powell in Idaho County, at 6,526 feet.[^1][^3]

Two log cabins are listed there, one dated 1910 and one dated 1912.[^1][^2]

One site asks whether the 1910 cabin burned in the 1910 Idaho fire. It puts that idea as a question, so it is unconfirmed.[^1]

One site's staff list has a single entry, for 1967, naming a John Davis and citing the Kellogg Evening News.[^2]

The two sites with a history both describe ruins.[^1][^2] Neither says what, if anything, still stands there today. One labels the site "Hike-In", but neither says whether the public can reach it.[^2]

Do not confuse this site with the Bald Mountain lookout in Latah County. The National Historic Lookout Register says that tower is on the Clearwater National Forest cabin rental program, and the sources here do not link the two.[^4]
