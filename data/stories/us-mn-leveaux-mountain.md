LeVeaux Mountain, in Cook County, had a U.S. Forest Service fire tower. The county's tower list dates it to 1928 and places it at 47.6157 degrees north and 90.7973 degrees west.[^1][^2]

Notes beside this entry in the county list cite a 1934 survey that used the name Onion, and they also give Onion River Lookout Tower as a name.[^1] The list's layout runs neighboring entries together, so the link to this tower is probable, not certain.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list marks the tower as gone.[^3]

The county list also notes that the Oberg and LeVeaux Mountain trails draw day hikers.[^1] None of the sources read says what, if anything, remains of the tower, or whether its site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
