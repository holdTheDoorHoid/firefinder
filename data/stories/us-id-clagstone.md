The earliest structure listed at Clagstone is a pole platform tower with a frame cabin, dated to 1926.[^1][^3] The firelookout.com entry files the site under the Pend Oreille TPA, six miles northeast of Spirit Lake, at an elevation of about 2,900 feet.[^1] A 20-foot live-in tower is listed for 1936.[^1][^3]

The Forest Lookouts page cites the Sandpoint News-Bulletin for a July 10, 1947 item that names Seth Manning as being on the lookout.[^3] A July 14, 1949 item from the same paper names Virgil Jore and his wife as stationed there that summer.[^3]

Firelookout.com says the lookout was destroyed in the 1940s.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page gives the same decade and attributes it to Kresek.[^3] The 1949 item places Virgil Jore and his wife there that summer, so if both accounts are right, the loss came after that July, late in the decade.[^1][^3]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts site files Clagstone as a hike-in site and marks it destroyed.[^2] No source we checked describes what, if anything, remains on the ground today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
