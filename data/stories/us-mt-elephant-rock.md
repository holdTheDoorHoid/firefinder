Firelookout.com calls Elephant Rock a cabin rather than a tower.[^1] It dates the L-4 cab to 1938 and places it about 20 miles east-northeast of White Sulphur Springs, in Meagher County, on the Lewis & Clark National Forest.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list classes it as a ground lookout.[^3]

In July 1940 a report from Ranger S.C. Eckert, printed in the Montana Standard, said the Elephant Rock fireguard would switch from the telephone to short-wave radio that season.[^2] Dale Cameron handled the radio setup at the site.[^2]

Firelookout.com says the cabin was abandoned in 1963 and is gone.[^1] The montanalookouts page lists it only as removed, with no date.[^2]

Neither page describes what remains at the site today, and neither says whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
