The hamlet of Zama City lies in northwestern Alberta, in Mackenzie County, roughly 80 kilometres north of High Level.[^1] OpenStreetMap maps a tower called Zama Fire lookout and tags it as an observation tower.[^2] The Canada Lookouts index lists its Zama entry under the Peace River Division.[^3]

The area is oil country. Oil fields in the Zama region were found between 1965 and 1969, and the community took its name from the nearby lake, river and oil field it relied on.[^4] Alberta maps its wildfire lookouts in a government data layer, derived from the FIReS database.[^5]

On 21 June 2012 lightning ignited a forest fire near Zama City.[^1] The blaze grew to 100,000 hectares in under a month, and the hamlet was evacuated on 11 July.[^1]

No source I found links the lookout to that fire, describes what remains at the Zama lookout site today, or says whether the public can reach it.
