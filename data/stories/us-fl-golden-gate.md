The Golden Gate Lookout Tower stands in Collier County, Florida, on the site of the Florida Forest Service's Collier Forestry Station.[^1] The register puts the structure at 110 feet, an open steel tower crowned by a 7x7 observation cab, or about 33.5 meters.[^1]

The register says it is unknown when the lookout was built or moved.[^1] It also says the tower "may have been located in 1 or 2 previous locations" and that it might move to the Babcock Ranch station in the future.[^1] We found no date for either its first raising or any move, so its earlier history remains unclear.

The register's photo captions include two from the 1990s, both credited to the Florida Forest Service, and one captioned 2024 and credited to Brad Eells.[^1] Eells nominated the tower, which the register accepted on February 26, 2024.[^1]

The register also links to a 2017 article titled "Their watch has ended: Lee County fire towers relics of past." We could not open that article, so we cannot say how it relates to this tower.[^1]

We found no record of who staffed the tower or which fires it watched. We also found no statement about public access. Because the tower stands on the grounds of a forestry station, anyone interested should ask the Florida Forest Service before visiting, and should not climb it.

[^1]: Golden Gate Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/fl/golden-gate-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
