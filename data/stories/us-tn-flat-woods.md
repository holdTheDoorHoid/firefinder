Flat Woods Lookout stands in Wayne County, Tennessee, west of Columbia, right off State Route 13.[^1] It is a 100-foot steel tower with diagonal stairs.[^1]

The Tennessee Division of Forestry put up many of its lookouts in the 1930s with help from the Civilian Conservation Corps, but records of their construction are scarce.[^2] I did not find a build date for this tower. The register includes a photograph credited to the Tennessee Secretary of State and dated 1942, so the tower existed by then.[^1]

The register lists Flat Woods as registered on January 3, 2015, nominated by Jerry Lutes.[^1] His visit report, dated March 4, 2006, notes that the bottom one and a half flights of stairs had been taken off to deter climbing, that trespass warning signs were on site, and that the tower was unstaffed.[^1]

The register says the Division still runs a radio repeater from the tower.[^1] A Tennessee landforms list also marks the tower as standing.[^3]

The only visit report I found dates from 2006, so the tower's condition today is unknown. Treat the site as closed, and do not climb it.[^1]
