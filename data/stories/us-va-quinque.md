Quinque was a lookout in Greene County, Virginia, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Virginia list places a tower there and marks it gone.[^1]

Forest Lookouts files its Virginia entry under Greene County and records the tower as removed in 1993, the only date the sources give for it.[^2]

Quinque is also the name of an unincorporated community in Greene County, two miles northwest of Ruckersville on Route 33.[^3]

No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the lookout site today, or whether the public can reach it.
