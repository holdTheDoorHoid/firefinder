Mount Toro, a summit in Monterey County, appears on the Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list as "Toro, Mount AWS," in a section titled Non-Wildland Lookouts.[^2] The association marks it gone.[^2]

AWS stands for automatic weather station, a machine-run weather post. Per the encyclopedia entry, operators use such stations to cut human labor or to measure in remote areas.[^4]

Ron Kemnow's catalogue page for the site carries the heading "MOUNT TORO AWS" and no other text that we could read.[^1]

The peak table places the lookout at 3,564 feet, on the Chualar topographic map, and labels the site "Mt Toro (LO site)."[^3] Its key defines an LO site as a place where the building has been removed.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the public can reach it.
