Grapevine Peak in Kern County is listed with a fire lookout that the register dates to about 1940. The register says it may have been built for the Kern County Fire Department before 1940.[^1] It gives the cabin as 14 by 14 feet, similar to a Forest Service L-4 design, on a 30-foot open timber tower.[^1]

The early history is hard to pin down. A July 15, 1941 Bakersfield Californian item, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, says the board authorized Surveyor Thornton to survey a site for a new station atop the peak, at the request of Harold P. Bowhay.[^2] The register's circa-1940 date and this survey for a new station do not line up neatly.[^1][^2]

A 1986 survey leaves the site out, the register notes, yet Ron Kemnow's site lists it as a county structure.[^1]

The register gives no removal date.[^1] It says the summit remains a busy communications site.[^1] Neither page says what, if anything, of the lookout stands there now.[^1][^2]

[^2]: Grapevine Peak - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts (californialookouts.weebly.com), https://californialookouts.weebly.com/grapevine-peak.html (accessed 2026-10-09).
