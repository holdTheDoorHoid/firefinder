The Donovon fire tower is listed as a steel tower in the Timiskaming District of northeastern Ontario.[^1] The Firelookout.ca index places it in the unorganized area of West Timiskaming, at an elevation of 587 m (1926 ft).[^1]

The index marks the location as not verified, so the point on the map is only approximate.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, stands at the site now, or whether visitors can reach it.[^1]

[^1]: Donovon Fire Tower, Firelookout.ca (Open Canada Fire Lookout Index), https://firelookout.ca/lookouts/?slug=donovon-fire-tower (accessed 2026-10-20).
