Tony Point is a former patrol-tower site in Idaho County, Idaho, on the Nez Perce National Forest.[^1] It lies about 2 miles south of the Moose Creek R.S., at 6,136 feet.[^1][^2][^4]

The site began as a patrol tower on the northernmost minor point, dated to 1933.[^1][^2][^4] Rex's Fire Lookout Page lists an L-4 cab on a ten-foot tower under 1934, taken from an undated inventory of the Moose Creek Ranger District.[^3]

Sources agree the lookout was abandoned in 1950.[^1][^2][^3][^4] In 1957 the administration of the site moved from the Bitterroot National Forest to the Nez Perce National Forest.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in, with no current condition given.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list shows it as Tower Gone.[^5] No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
