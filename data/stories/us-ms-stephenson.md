The Stephenson Lookout Tower is recorded on OpenStreetMap as a lattice observation tower, with its map point at 31.2858 north and 91.0038 west, about 127 m (417 ft) above sea level.[^1]

The latest edit to that map point carries the note "Add/modify communication mast.", which hints that the structure there may be a communication mast rather than a lookout.[^1]

No source describes what stands at the site today or whether it is open to the public, so do not climb or enter the structure without first confirming access.

[^1]: Stephenson Lookout Tower, OpenStreetMap, https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/357989738 (accessed 2026-10-21).
