Clear West Peak is a summit in Pierce County, Washington, at 5,643 feet.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register places it in the Mt. Baker-Snoqualmie National Forest, although one Forest Lookouts index names the Snoqualmie National Forest instead.[^1][^3]

A lookout cab went up on the peak in 1933.[^1][^4] It was an L-4 cab, a type that a WillhiteWeb photo guide calls the most popular live-in lookout: a 14x14 wood-frame cab with a hip roof.[^4][^5] George Clisby shot panoramas from the cab roof on August 10, 1934.[^4]

The sources do not agree on how the cab ended. The register says it was removed in 1965, while firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2] Willhite thinks the cab was destroyed in the mid-1960s.[^4]

Today the summit is flat, and Willhite describes it as the site of an old lookout.[^4] No source we read describes any remains there.

Willhite lists the hike at 1.5 miles with about 1,000 feet of gain, and says it starts where the clear-cuts end.[^4] Directions: from Enumclaw, go 22 miles east on State Route 410, then turn onto West Fork Road, Forest Road 74.[^4] His page says the bridge over the White River is washed out. The page is undated, so check road conditions with the Forest Service before you go.[^4]
