Link Lake was a state fire lookout in Itasca County, southeast of Big Fork.[^1][^2] The county list places it at 47.6550 north and 93.4128 west, and numbers it tower 31.[^1] The state erected an 81-foot ladder tower there in 1930.[^1][^2] The owner was the Minnesota Forest Service, now the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources.[^1]

The sources disagree on the height. A 1947 survey note in the same entry puts the tower at about 100 feet and describes a square top about 10 feet on each side.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association marks the tower as gone, but gives no date for its removal.[^3] No source we read describes the site today or says what, if anything, remains there.
