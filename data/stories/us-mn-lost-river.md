The Lost River lookout stood in Koochiching County, Minnesota.[^1] The Minnesota Forest Service owned the tower, and the county list dates it to 1938.[^1][^2]

The county list describes a ladder tower 80 feet tall.[^1] A survey note from 1959 describes a four-legged steel structure with an enclosed observation at the top, and puts the height at approximately 100 feet.[^1][^2] The two figures do not agree, so we cannot say which one is right.

The county list marks the tower as removed, but it gives no date for the removal.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association also lists the tower as gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors. A visitor should not expect to find a tower there.
