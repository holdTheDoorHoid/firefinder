Grassy Top is a long ridge in the Kaniksu National Forest, about 12 miles east of Ione in Pend Oreille County.[^1][^2][^3] A lookout camp was set up there in 1915.[^1][^3]

In 1926 a log cabin was built.[^1][^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register describes it with two platform towers about a mile apart, on the north and southeast points.[^1] One history credits mop-up crews with building the cabin in the final days of a major 1926 fire that tore through Pend Oreille County.[^3] A 1931 entry records that Roy Mackay was struck by lightning.[^3]

In September 1932 a Newport newspaper reported that Lawrence McKay had completed a lookout station on Grassy Top and was starting another on Slumber Peak.[^3][^4] The register dates a 20-foot wooden tower with an L-4 cab, half a mile southwest of the cabin, to 1938.[^1][^2][^3] The sources do not agree on the dates, and they do not say whether the 1932 station was the 1926 cabin or a later building.[^1][^3][^4]

By the 1950s, the site had been reduced to ruins.[^1][^2]

A visitor's undated account says only hidden relics are left. The southwest peak has the most remnants, including footings, and the north tower site still has some.[^3] An unsigned trail climbs the south ridge to the site.[^3] The round trip is about 11 miles with roughly 1,500 feet of climbing.[^3]
