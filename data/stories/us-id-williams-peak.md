Williams Peak Lookout is an L-4 cab on the Payette National Forest in Valley County, Idaho.[^1][^2] The Forest Service gives its elevation as 6,826 feet, while the Register gives 6,842 feet.[^4][^1]

The cab was built in 1932 on a ridge northwest of the summit.[^2][^3] The Register calls it an R1/L-4 ground cabin.[^1]

The Register says the lookout is generally staffed each fire season.[^1] The Forest Service says the lookout still serves fire detection during the summer.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists known staff from 2006 to 2025, with Daniel Moak listed for 2017 through 2025.[^3]

The page also records a fatal helicopter crash.[^3] On August 13, 2006, a helicopter crashed while on a mission on the East Fork South Fork of the Salmon River.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts, relaying a Payette National Forest Facebook post, says four people died, including the lookout Lillian Patten.[^3]

No source describes the lookout as abandoned, moved or burned.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The Register lists it as registered on October 4, 2020.[^1][^3]

The Forest Service calls it an active fire lookout and asks visitors to be respectful of the lookout.[^5] Trail #073 is 7.2 miles long and starts at the Williams Peak Trailhead.[^5] To get there, drive Lick Creek Road from McCall for about 35 miles, then continue about two miles on the East Fork Road.[^4] The Forest Service calls Lick Creek Road rough and narrow.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts describes it as a hike-in staffed lookout.[^3] None of the pages read mentions a rental, and none says whether visitors may enter the cab.[^3]
