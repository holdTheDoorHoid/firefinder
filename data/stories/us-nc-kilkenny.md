Kilkenny is listed as a lookout tower in Tyrrell County, North Carolina, on a list of North Carolina lookout towers kept on the tnlandforms.us website.[^1] The list says it was drawn from the NC FFLA site and places the entry at 35.64527, -76.20583.[^1] The list itself cautions that it may include some observation towers and Coast Guard lookouts, so this entry may not be a fire lookout at all.[^1]

No source describes the site today, so this entry cannot say whether a tower stands there or whether visitors may go there.

[^1]: North Carolina lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
