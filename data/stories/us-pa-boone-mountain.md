The Boone Mountain lookout was a steel Aermotor tower, built in 1921.[^1] The register gives the site's elevation as 1,895 feet (578 meters).[^1] The register places the tower in Elk County, but the Eastern US Lookouts page puts it in Clearfield County, so the sources disagree on the county.[^1][^2]

Before the tower went up, the spot had a bare observation point.[^2] The Eastern US Lookouts page cites a Department of Forestry report dated December 31, 1917, for that.[^2]

The register says the tower was removed in 1974.[^1] Henry Isenberg nominated the site, and the register listed it on September 16, 2008.[^1]

Nothing I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
