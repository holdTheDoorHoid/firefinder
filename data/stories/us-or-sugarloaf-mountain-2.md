Sugarloaf Mountain's first lookout was a 15-foot L-4 cab, dated to 1932 by the register and by firelookout.[^1][^2] The Oregon Lookouts history instead dates the first tower to 1933.[^3] A garage measuring 16 by 18 went up at the site in 1934.[^3]

The present cab went up in 1949.[^1][^2] It is an L-4 on a 32-foot treated timber tower, and the register says it has a catwalk.[^1][^2][^3] A 1948 note in the Malheur Historical Record says the old house was replaced even though it was still fairly good.[^3]

A 2018 visit found the lookout staffed, and the 2026 directory still lists it as staffed.[^4][^5] The register says the site is in active service.[^1] Firelookout says the lookout is available for emergencies.[^2]

The directory lists the site as legally accessible to the public.[^5] The 2018 visit noted a gate on the access road about 0.6 miles below the lookout, so check road conditions before heading up.[^4] Because the lookout is a working station, treat the cab as off limits to climbing.
