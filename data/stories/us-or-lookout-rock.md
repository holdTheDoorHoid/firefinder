Lookout Rock sits on top of a large rock in the Fremont National Forest, about 13 miles northeast of Bly in Lake County, Oregon.[^1][^2] Before any tower went up, a tree with a crow's nest stood near the spot.[^3]

A 1931 newspaper item, as transcribed by one history site, said a fireman from the Finley corrals would be posted there.[^3] The first tower was a 20-foot wooden structure with an L-4 cab, put up in 1932.[^1] A 1959 inspection credits a CCC project with building it.[^3] A 1932 Bend Bulletin item, however, gives the legs as sixteen feet high, so the sources do not quite agree on the height.[^3][^1]

The present cab is a 14-by-14-foot R-6 flattop, built in 1962 on a 20-foot timber tower.[^1][^2] The 1959 inspection had recommended replacing the old tower and house.[^3] A 2024 visitor reported that materials were trucked to the base and hauled up by a high line.[^4]

A 1947 notice named Kenneth Carlson as the lookout the summer before, and the district still needed a capable person with a vehicle for the post.[^3] By the end of the 1960s the tower was no longer regularly staffed.[^4] The Register says the tower went on standby in 1968.[^1] It added the lookout to the National Historic Lookout Register on March 1, 2000.[^1]

The 2021 Bootleg Fire burned through the area. The tower survived, though its timber beams were scorched in several spots.[^4]

Today the sources do not agree on its status. A regional directory lists it as standing.[^5] A 2024 visitor labeled it abandoned, called its future uncertain, and said it needs a lot of work.[^4] Another site notes that it has not been used for many years and may one day join the rental program.[^2] That visitor found the road to the trailhead open, and some class 2 scrambling is needed to reach the base of the rock.[^4] Nothing we read says the public may climb the tower, so check with the Bly Ranger District before planning a visit.[^1]
