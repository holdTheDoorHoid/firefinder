The Evans lookout tower stood in Vernon Parish, near the community of Evans.[^1][^2] Louisiana Highway 111 runs through Evans, and a Wikipedia article on the Clear Creek Wildlife Management Area, which lies in the same parish, mentions the tower in its description of the land.[^2] That article describes a tower known locally as the Evans lookout tower, and it writes in the present tense. Its citation for the tower was retrieved in February 2017.[^2]

Wikipedia's list of Louisiana towers marks the Evans tower as torn down. Its note says the tower still shows in old aerial photographs and appears to have been demolished between 2016 and 2018.[^1]

The two pages do not line up neatly. The article's wording may reflect the years before the demolition the list describes, and neither gives a precise date for the change.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
