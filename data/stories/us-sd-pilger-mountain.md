Pilger Mountain stands in the Black Hills National Forest, at 4,790 feet (1,460 m).[^1] The register says a tower went up there in 1940, and it names the U.S. Forest Service as the administering agency.[^1] A lookout history page dates a steel tower there to 1940.[^2] The register added the site to its list on January 1, 2026.[^1]

A 1962 Rapid City Journal item, quoted on a lookout history page, shows the tower in use that summer.[^3] It says three lightning fires were reported on a Sunday. The first, a spot fire about 21 miles west of Hot Springs, was reported by the Pilger Mountain lookout, and Fall River crews put it out.[^3]

Sources disagree on when the tower came down. The register gives the 1970s.[^1] A lookout history page says the tower was gone in the 1960s.[^2] The 1962 report does not settle the question.[^3]

A peak-listing site reports that a fire tower once occupied the summit and is now gone.[^4] No source I found describes what remains on the summit today, or whether visitors can reach it.

The register places the site in Fall River County.[^1] Another lookout page puts the peak in Custer County, and a South Dakota lookout list does too, with coordinates that differ from the register's.[^1][^3][^5]
