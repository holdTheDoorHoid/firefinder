Redwater was a fire lookout tower in Bowie County, Texas.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places it at about 33.39 north and 94.18 west, and marks it as gone.[^1] The Central US Lookouts site lists the Texas Forest Service as its agency.[^2]

A map database puts the tower on the Leary USGS map area, at an approximate elevation of 364 feet.[^3] Across East Texas, the Forest Service moved from tree-top spotting to metal lookout towers, because spotting from trees was seldom effective.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
