Troy Lookout is a tower in Montgomery County, North Carolina, listed under that name in a North Carolina tower list on tnlandforms.us.[^1] Troy, the county seat, falls inside the Uwharrie National Forest's proclamation boundary, and the town lies on the eastern fringe of the Uwharrie Mountains.[^2] The list gives the tower's coordinates as 35.3425, -79.89166, and adds that its entries may also include a handful of observation towers and Coast Guard lookouts.[^1]

A USGS topographic map of Troy, portrayed as of 2011 at a scale of 1:24,000, lists the Troy Lookout Tower among its landmarks.[^3] OpenStreetMap has a point named Troy Lookout Tower at 35.3426413, -79.8914349, tagged as an observation-type tower.[^4]

No source I could read describes the site today, including whether the tower still stands.
