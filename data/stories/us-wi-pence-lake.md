The Pence Lake Lookout Tower stood in Langlade County, as both the Forest Fire Lookout Association and the Forest Lookouts site list it.[^1][^2] OpenStreetMap records its elevation as 529 metres and tags the structure as an observation tower.[^3]

The tower was still on the map in the 1970s.[^4] Pastmaps describes a 1973 USGS map of Pearson, printed in 1977, where the Pence Lake tower is among the landmarks a reader can locate.[^4]

Across the state, the Conservation Department set up an engineering division in October 1931 to design new towers. It began replacing the old windmill-type towers in 1932.[^5] Wisconsin retired its lookout towers in 2016 and stepped up aerial detection to back up citizen reports.[^5]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association marks the Pence Lake tower as gone, and it gives no National Historic Lookout Register (NHLR) or Former Fire Lookout Sites Register (FFLOS) number for it.[^1]

Nothing I read says what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
