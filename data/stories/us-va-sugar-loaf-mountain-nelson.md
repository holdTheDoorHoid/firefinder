The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower on Sugar Loaf Mountain in Nelson County, at about 37.78 N, 78.82 W, and marks it gone.[^1]

The Lynchburg News & Advance reports that the Sugar Loaf Mountain tower has been taken away.[^2] The paper says the tower had protected Nelson County residents for about half a century.[^2]

HighRock, a design firm, describes restoring a Nelson County fire tower that had sat abandoned for years, turning it into a rustic mountain retreat it compares to a bed and breakfast.[^3] The firm does not name the mountain, so this story does not link that tower to Sugar Loaf.[^3]

With the tower removed, there is no lookout on the summit to climb.[^2] No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site now.
