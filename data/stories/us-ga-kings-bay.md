A tower named "kings bay" appears in Camden County on the tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers, which gives it GFC number 841.[^1] The page says its map was revised in March 2016.[^1] The name matches Kings Bay Base, an unincorporated place in Camden County that hosts the Kings Bay Naval Submarine Base.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register counts 29 registered Georgia lookouts, and its Georgia page does not name Kings Bay.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register lists two former Georgia sites, Lafayette and Yonah Mountain, and does not name Kings Bay either.[^4]

No source describes the site today, including whether it is public.
