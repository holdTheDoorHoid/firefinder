The Cottle Knob lookout stood in Nicholas County, West Virginia.[^1][^2] The Register registered the site on September 16, 2008, on the nomination of Henry Isenberg, and gives its elevation as 3,010 feet, or 917 meters.[^1] The West Virginia Division of Forestry administered it.[^1]

We found no build date, staffing history or notable fire in the sources we read. The Register says the tower has since been removed.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's West Virginia list marks the Cottle Knob site as gone.[^3] It also lists a tower at Pocahontas County High School as relocated from Cottle Knob and still standing.[^3] The list puts an asterisk on that entry, which it uses for towers that keep their structure but no longer have a cab.[^3]

We found no second source for that move, and the list gives no date for it. Treat the school link as a single list's claim.[^3]

Today the sources describe no public access. The list places the tower at a school, so ask the school before going near it.[^3]
