Meadow Camp lies about 14 miles west of Glendale, in Josephine County, Oregon, at an elevation of 3,863 feet.[^1]

Firelookout.com names the Siskiyou National Forest and the Bureau of Land Management as the managing agencies.[^1] A Forest Lookouts page names only the Bureau of Land Management.[^2] A Josephine County lookout list gives the agency as BLM with a question mark.[^4]

Firelookout.com describes a crow's nest here, dated with a question mark to the 1920s.[^1] The Josephine County list gives the construction date as unknown.[^4]

Firelookout.com labels the site abandoned but adds that the crow's nest is still there, though that note carries no date.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon list also shows it as abandoned.[^3] The Josephine County list places it on site as late as 2001.[^4]

The latest dated record we found is from 2001.[^4] No source we read says whether the public can reach the site.[^1][^2][^3][^4] A 2026 directory of Oregon's standing lookouts does not include Meadow Camp.[^5]
