The Texas Forest Service put up a fire lookout on Bird Mountain, in southeastern Anderson County, in 1928. It did so with the Southern Pine Lumber Company and the Houston County Timber Company.[^1] The tower was one of two steel towers. According to 1928 newspaper reports that the Forest Lookouts site quotes, each was 90 feet tall, with a glass-enclosed cabin about eight and a half feet square.[^1]

The two reports disagree about the other tower, at Ratcliff. One puts it in western Houston County and the other in eastern Houston County.[^1]

A Coast and Geodetic Survey station description from 1942 calls the Bird Mountain tower the standard steel lookout type: a square cab on a four-legged trussed frame, painted grey.[^1]

A report dated August 15, 1946 names Forest Patrolman L.P. Johnson, of the Bird Mountain tower, and says one forest fire burned one acre of forest land.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Texas list now marks the tower as gone.[^2] No source we read describes the site today.

[^2]: Texas Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/tx/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
