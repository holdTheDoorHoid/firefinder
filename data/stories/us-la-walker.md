The Walker Lookout Tower stood in Livingston Parish, Louisiana, near the town of Walker.[^1] Fire-protection money was directed in 1915, making it possible to hire lookout watchmen or patrolmen.[^1] The statewide total was thirty-nine fire towers by 1943 and fifty-six by 1950.[^1]

Pastmaps, a site that describes historical USGS maps, mentions the Walker tower in its summaries of prints from 1954, 1964 and 1967.[^2]

The Wikipedia list marks the tower as torn down.[^1] Its note says the tower can be confirmed only on old maps, and it places a Livingston Parish Fire District No. 4 station at the tower's current location.[^1]

That spot now holds a fire district station, so treat it as a fire station rather than a lookout site to visit.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the tower itself.
