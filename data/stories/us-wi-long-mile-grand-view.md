The Long Mile Lookout Tower, also called the Grandview Fire Lookout Tower, stands in the Grand View township of Bayfield County, Wisconsin.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts site gives its location as township 44, range 6 West, section 12, and says a steel tower was erected there in 1932.[^1] The Historical Society's record gives the same year and describes a steel frame structure used as an observation and fire tower.[^2]

The sources do not name who built the tower or who staffed it, and we found no account of a particular fire it watched.

Its later history is less clear. The Forest Lookouts site marks the tower as abandoned.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association still lists it as standing.[^4] The Historical Society surveyed its record in 1995 and noted that the tower had not been demolished.[^2]

A North Country Trail Association hike listing calls the tower decommissioned.[^3] The 5.5-mile out-and-back walk starts from a parking lot on County Road D, about 5.2 miles south of Grand View, and includes a climb to the tower, a short break there, and a return to the lot.[^3] We found no source saying the cab is open to visitors, so treat the walk as a trail visit and check with the land manager before climbing anything.

Statewide, a WJFW feature reports that 72 older towers, then still in service, were retired in 2015.[^5]
