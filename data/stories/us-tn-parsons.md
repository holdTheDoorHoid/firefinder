The Parsons tower is a fire lookout in Decatur County, Tennessee.[^1] Tennessee Landforms, a site that tracks the state's lookout towers, gives its elevation as 670 feet and numbers it 8-4.[^1] The site explains that its numbers are state forest tower numbers, district first and then tower, taken from a 1976 list.[^1]

We did not find any source that says when the tower was built, who designed it, or who has staffed it or watched fires from it. Those parts of its history are unknown.

The sources disagree about whether the tower still stands. Tennessee Landforms marks it as standing.[^1] The National Register form that covers the Tennessee Division of Forestry's fire towers, from 1933 to 1975, includes a list of towers still standing on March 26, 2014.[^2] Parsons is not on that list, and the form does not explain why.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register's Tennessee page, which says it has 76 registered lookouts, does not name Parsons.[^3] We found nothing about access, so we cannot say whether the tower is open or in use today. Anyone curious should ask the landowner before going near it.
