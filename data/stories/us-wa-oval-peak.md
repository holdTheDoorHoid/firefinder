The Oval Peak lookout site lies in Okanogan County, Washington, about 15 miles west-southwest of Twisp.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says an L-4 cab was built there in 1940.[^1]

The register says that cab was removed in 1950, while firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2] Willhite repeats the 1950 loss from the Kresek Inventory, but he has not located any map that marks a lookout or route to the summit, and he doubts a structure was ever there.[^3]

Wikipedia places the peak in the Lake Chelan-Sawtooth Wilderness, which sits inside the Okanogan-Wenatchee National Forest.[^4] The register names the Okanogan National Forest instead.[^1] The summit has a register, and Wikipedia says climbing can be difficult because of talus on the slopes.[^4] No source describes what, if anything, remains of a lookout at the site today.
