Tom Hill is a steel fire tower in west-central Alberta, 30 m (98 ft) tall and standing at 1,289 m (4,229 ft) above sea level.[^1]

Alberta's register dates the present tower to 2019 and lists it as active. It also records a verified location and a weather station.[^1] OpenStreetMap, the volunteer-built map, lists the site as an observation tower.[^2]

The register gives the access mode as "drive", which suggests the site is reached by road.[^1] Alberta counts 127 wildfire lookout sites, each with an observer on duty, usually April through October.[^3]

No source describes the site today beyond the register entry, or whether the public may visit. Treat it as a working fire lookout rather than a place to visit.
