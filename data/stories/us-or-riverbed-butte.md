Riverbed Butte is a former lookout site in Klamath County, Oregon, about 17 miles north of Bly, at roughly 6,000 feet.[^1][^2] The lookout went up in 1934, erected by the CCC's Indian Division.[^3][^4] A 1933 Klamath Agency forestry report urgently called for an emergency lookout east of the Black Hills section.[^3] The tower was a steel Aermotor with a 7-by-7 cab on top, and a cabin at ground level housed the living quarters.[^4] Sources give three heights for it: 60 feet, 61 feet and 67 feet.[^4][^1][^3] The Klamath Indian Agency later gave the tower building number 641.[^3]

By 1969 the tower was manned only in emergencies, such as after lightning storms or on high-hazard days. The living quarters were staffed each year as a fire station.[^3] A 1963 memo described the cab as in poor condition, and a 1972 report rated the steel structure in good condition.[^3]

The register says the tower was used for emergencies in the 1960s and is now gone.[^1] The Klamath County blog and the Oregon Lookouts page both list it as removed, with no date given.[^4][^3] The register dates the lookout only to the 1930s.[^1]

The register and firelookout.com both place the site in the Fremont National Forest.[^1][^2] The Klamath County blog names the Klamath Indian Agency, the Oregon Department of Forestry and the Fremont National Forest as managing agencies.[^4] I found no source that describes what, if anything, remains on the ground, or whether visitors can reach it.[^2]
