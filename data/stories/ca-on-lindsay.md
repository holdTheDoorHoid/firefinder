The Lindsay Fire Tower is listed in the Firelookout.ca registry with the location Bruce Peninsula, Lindsay Township, near a place the listing calls Clark's Corners.[^1] The listing places the tower in the northern Bruce Peninsula, in Ontario, and gives the site's elevation as 207 m (679 ft).[^1]

A local genealogy society lists Clarks Corners among the places in Lindsay Township, and places it on the second concession at Ira Lake.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, stands at the site today, and none says whether the public may visit. Confirm access before going.

[^2]: Lindsay Township, Bruce County Genealogical Society, https://brucecountygenealogicalsociety.ca/townships/lindsay-township/ (accessed 2026-10-20).
