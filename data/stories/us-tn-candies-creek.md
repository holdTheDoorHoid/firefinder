Candies Creek is a former lookout site in Bradley County, Tennessee, at 1,100 feet (335 m) above sea level.[^1][^2] The Tennessee lookout list marks its tower as removed, and its entry reads "3-15 removed."[^1][^2] The list identifies the site by the waypoint code BRT001 and gives its position as 35.2903, -84.8327.[^2]

A list of Tennessee Division of Forestry towers still standing as of March 26, 2014, printed in a National Register document, includes N4AOW Tower in Bradley County. Candies Creek does not appear on that list.[^3]

No source reached describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
