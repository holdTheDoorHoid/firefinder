The McGregor Peak lookout site is in Flathead County, Montana, about 24 miles west-southwest of Kalispell, at an elevation of 4,800 feet.[^1] Forest Lookouts places it in the Flathead National Forest under the legal location 26N-25W-3.[^3]

Firelookout.com says a platform tower and a camp date from the 1930s. The site sits on a southwest spur about half a mile below the summit.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association also lists the site as a platform, with coordinates of 48.04172 north and 114.81099 west.[^2]

Firelookout.com says the platform tower is still there, and it also says the lookout was abandoned.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as Abandoned.[^2] Neither listing calls it gone.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
