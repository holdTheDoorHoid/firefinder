The Lauderdale Lookout Tower stands in Lauderdale County, Mississippi, at about 600 feet above sea level.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register files it under the name Lauderdale (Meridian), and an online lookout index lists it under Mississippi as Meridian.[^1][^2]

The register describes an 80-foot Aermotor tower with a straight stair inside it.[^1] Aermotor built its towers from galvanized steel.[^4] The register gives no build date.[^1] I could not find another source that gives one, and I found no record of who staffed the tower or which fires it watched.

The register's site visit took place on July 3, 2022, and the tower was registered on July 10, 2022, with Jason Johns as nominator.[^1]

When the register visited, the tower was in poor condition: it lacked a roof and windows, and an antenna had been attached to its top.[^1] The same entry says the site was vacated by the Mississippi Forestry Commission, although it also lists the commission as the administering agency.[^1] I found no record of when the commission left or what has happened to the tower since.

A map database entry gives the site an elevation of 190 meters, about 7 meters above the register's figure.[^3] I found no current report on the tower's condition or on whether visitors may go near it. Check with the Forestry Commission before going to the site, and do not climb a tower that is missing its roof and windows.
