The Ash Cave Fire Tower stands in Hocking State Forest, and it was built in 1934.[^1][^2][^3] It is an 80-foot Aermotor tower with a metal cab that measures 7 feet by 7 feet.[^1][^2][^3] A trail guide describes a roughly two-mile round trip between the tower and Ash Cave.[^2]

The sources we read do not name the lookouts who worked here, and none describes a notable fire at the tower. The trail guide says Ohio's fire towers had lookouts posted in them, using binoculars, maps, a radio and a telephone.[^2] The register answers "No" to whether the tower is staffed, but it gives no date for that answer, so the answer may be out of date.[^1]

The Ohio Division of Forestry restored and repainted the tower through its fire tower restoration project, working with the Forest Fire Lookout Association. The register calls that work recent but does not give a date.[^1]

When the weather permits, people may use the stairs and take the risk themselves.[^2] A visitor's comment on the register page says the cab cannot be reached.[^1] American Byways lists the tower as open year-round.[^3] Parking sits north of the tower, at OH-374 and Chapel Ridge Road, and the walk south to the tower is about 550 feet along the Grandma Gatewood Trail.[^2]
