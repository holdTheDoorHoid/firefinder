Georgia's lookout list places a tower named Aultman Still in Worth County, at 31.75285, -83.89004, at 302 feet (92 m) above sea level.[^1] The list codes it BLL-41134, usgs and gfc-216.[^1] A directory of Georgia forest lookouts also lists an Aultman Still tower in Worth County, with no other detail.[^2]

The list's map was revised on 22 March 2016.[^1]

No source found describes the site today, so whether anything still stands there and whether the public can visit are unknown.

[^2]: Georgia - Forest Lookouts, Eastern US Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/georgia.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
