The Eastern US Lookouts site has a page for a Point Washington lookout, and it places the lookout in Walton County, Florida.[^1]

Point Washington is a Walton County community with no municipal government of its own, and it grew up around a sawmill.[^2] The Wikipedia article on the community also describes Point Washington State Forest, which the state bought in 1992 through its Conservation and Recreation Lands program.[^2] The Florida Forest Service manages the forest for multiple uses, from timber and wildlife to recreation and ecological restoration.[^2] Many people use the area for hunting, off-road cycling and hiking.[^2]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2]
