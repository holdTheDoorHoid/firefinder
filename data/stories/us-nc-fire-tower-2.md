A survey mark named FIRE TOWER 2, designation EA2358, sits in Onslow County, North Carolina, on the north bank of the New River.[^1] The Coast and Geodetic Survey described it in 1950, when the station was at the centre of a 135-foot steel skeleton tower painted green.[^1] The station lay about 2,400 feet north of the New River bridge at Sneads Ferry and 250 feet east of N.C. 172.[^1]

The North Carolina Geodetic Survey recovered the mark in 1993.[^1] A 2003 check by the US Power Squadron found it in good condition.[^1]

A North Carolina lookout-tower list gives a Fire Tower 2 in Onslow County under the same code, EA2358. By our calculation, its listed point lies about 12 metres from the survey position.[^2][^1]

No source describes the site now, so it is not known whether the tower still stands.[^1][^2]
