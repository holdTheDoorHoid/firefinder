Hicks, an unincorporated community in Vernon Parish, Louisiana, had a post office from 1887 until 1992.[^1] The Central US Lookouts site has a page for a Hicks lookout that gives its name, its parish and a location code, 3N-6W-Sec.10.[^2] The site's Louisiana index gives the lookout's county as Vernon County.[^3]

Louisiana put up its first steel fire tower near Bogalusa in 1922.[^4] By 1950 the state had fifty-six fire towers.[^4] Most surviving towers are inactive, and a number are held in reserve.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
