The register places a fire lookout on Castle Rock, in Wakefield, Middlesex County.[^1] It describes a 40-foot wooden tower there that began as a temporary station in 1912, operated by the town's fire department under an arrangement with the state.[^1] The register says the station ran until a permanent tower could go up in 1913, and that staffing lasted until that year.[^1] It lists the site among its former lookouts.[^1]

Wakefield's other lookout, on Harts Hill, has its own register entry. That 48-foot iron tower was built on town park land and first operated in 1913.[^2] The register does not say where Castle Rock's permanent tower stood, so the two should not be assumed to be the same.[^1]

The state Department of Conservation and Recreation runs Breakheart Reservation, which is in the Town of Saugus.[^3] Castle Rock is the highest of its six named peaks.[^4] I found no source that confirms this is the same hill as the lookout site, though the names match.

I found no source that describes what, if anything, remains of the 1912 station on the hill, or whether the summit is open to visitors today.
