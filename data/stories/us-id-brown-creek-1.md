A crow's nest went up at Brown Creek #1 Lookout in 1910.[^1][^2] A 100-foot steel tower followed in 1934, at the north end of the ridge.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts calls the tower an Aermotor, while Forest Lookouts gives its model as an MC-39.[^1][^3]

The listings also disagree on the name of the agency that ran the site. Two call it the Clearwater-Potlatch TPA, and one calls it the Clearwater Timber Protective Association.[^1][^2][^3] Forest Lookouts quotes an annual report that records a power line about four miles long built to the lookout in 1960.[^3]

Both firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts say the tower stayed in service into the 1970s.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts also names Mr. and Mrs. Kirkpatrick as staff in 1981, a date that does not match that account, and the sources do not explain the gap.[^1]

The tower came down in 1999.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts lists it only as removed, with no date.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site now.[^1][^2][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site drive-up but does not say whether it is open today.[^1] The Fire Lookout index still marks the tower as gone, at about 46.33466, -115.82524.[^4]
