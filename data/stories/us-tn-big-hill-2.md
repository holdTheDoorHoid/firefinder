Tennessee has at least two Big Hill fire towers.[^1] The one in this record is in Cumberland County. The Tennessee Landforms list gives it an elevation of 2,035 feet and marks it as standing.[^1]

That list is the only source I could read for this tower. It gives no build date, no builder and no staffing history, so those parts of its story are unknown.[^1]

Keep it apart from the Marion County Big Hill tower. The Landforms list places that tower at 2,032 feet.[^1] A National Register nomination for that tower dates it to about 1947, as part of the Tennessee Division of Forestry's fire management program. It says the exact construction date is not known, so 1947 is an estimate.[^2]

The Register's Tennessee list has no entry named Big Hill. Its only Big Hill entry is Big Hill Pond Lookout Tower.[^3] Tennessee Landforms lists a Big Hill Pond tower at 601 feet in McNairy County, a different county and elevation from both Big Hill towers above.[^1]

Access is unknown. The sources do not say whether this tower can be visited, so do not climb it or drive up to it without permission from its owner or the state.[^1]

[^1]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, Tennessee Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Big Hill Fire Lookout Tower National Register nomination, Tennessee Historical Commission, https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/historicalcommission/archive/national-register/2021-nr-listings/thc_nr_big-hill-tower_nomination.pdf (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^3]: Tennessee, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/tn/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
