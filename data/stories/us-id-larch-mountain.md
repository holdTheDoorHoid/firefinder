The Larch Mountain lookout stood in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, about 10 miles south-southwest of Clark Fork in Shoshone County, at 5,306 feet.[^1][^2]

A 30-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab went up in 1934.[^1][^2]

Both sites give 1955 as the year it was destroyed.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list also marks it as gone.[^3]

In a post dated September 14, 2025, Idaho Fire Lookouts describes hiking to the site.[^2] The writers said the tower appeared to have burned.[^2] They found that the alidade stand had been taken off and set down nearby, and that its top mechanism still slides.[^2] They also found three of the cement footings, a chimney, a bed frame and other debris.[^2]

The writers call it a hike-in and say the walk was hot but not difficult.[^2] No source read says whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3]
