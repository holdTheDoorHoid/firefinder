A Georgia lookout tower index lists a tower called Forsyth, in Monroe County in the central part of the state.[^1][^2] The index gives its elevation as 610, read as feet (about 186 m), and its coordinates as 33.04612, -83.93321.[^1]

Monroe County was established on May 15, 1821, and honors James Monroe.[^2] Forsyth is both the county seat and the largest city.[^2] Monroe County belongs to the Macon, Georgia metropolitan area, and 27,957 people lived there at the 2020 census.[^2]

No source describes the Forsyth tower or its site today. It is not known whether a tower or any remains stand there, or whether the public can reach the spot.
