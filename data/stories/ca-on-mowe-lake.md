Firelookout.ca places the Mowe Lake Fire Tower in the Thunder Bay area, at coordinates 48.36516, -90.74429.[^1]

A list on an independent site, titled Ontario Fire Towers Still Standing, names the Mowe Lake Tower at Thunder Bay among the towers that remain.[^2] The same list reports that by 2013 the Ontario MNR had resolved to take down many of the towers still standing.[^2] It also says the MNR no longer maintains the towers and asks visitors not to climb them.[^2]

The site's author says the purpose is to record the history of fire tower lookouts and of the people who staffed them.[^3]

No source describes what stands at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors. The list that counts this tower as standing is undated, does not say whether this tower is among those slated for removal, and no later source confirms it.
