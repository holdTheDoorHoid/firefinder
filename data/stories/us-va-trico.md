Trico, also called the Warren County Fire Tower, was a shared effort between Warren and Clarke counties [^1]. In 1934 the Warren County board of supervisors put up $225 toward the lookout, and Clarke County paid the same amount [^2]. The rest came from the Forest Service, which also did the observations [^2]. A February 1934 Harrisonburg newspaper item described the station on the Blue Ridge near Ashby's Gap, and said the federal service would run it to protect both counties' forests [^3].

In 1957 a USGS party described it as a four-legged tower about 60 feet tall, on Virginia State Forest Service land [^2].

The tower was removed at a date neither source gives, and a ground structure remains [^1][^2]. A 2024 visit found no tower [^2]. A trail sign reading Trico Tower Trail had suggested one might still be there, but the writer said that was not the case [^2]. Photo captions on the page mention a rockpile and another building at the summit [^2]. The Appalachian Trail passes below the summit, and a side trail with 136 feet of climbing leads up to it [^2].

The register lists a private owner as administrator [^1]. The Willhite page places the summit in both Fauquier and Warren counties, while the state list puts it in Warren and marks the tower as gone [^2][^4].
