The Mobray site sits in Grays Harbor County on the Washington coast, at about 656 feet.[^1] The first lookout here was a 12-foot wooden tower put up in 1937.[^1][^2] A 1937 survey crew placed the tower right over the Mobray Benchmark, a survey marker just off the Wynoochee Valley Road.[^2]

A taller 30-foot tower, its cab 8 by 8 feet, took its place in 1942.[^1][^2] A second listing, from firelookout.com, gives the same 1942 date.[^3]

The sources disagree on how the 1942 tower ended.[^1][^2][^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register says it was removed in 1963. Firelookout.com and Willhite both say it was destroyed that year.[^2][^3]

Willhite's visit notes say the 1942 footings survive, though he has failed to locate them several times.[^2] Crews cut back the stand in late 2021, leaving downed trees across the area.[^2] Trees that have grown back now block the view west and north from the harvested ground west of the road.[^2]

The site is a drive-up stop.[^2] Willhite describes the Wynoochee Valley Road as paved, about 17 miles from Montesano, with visitors parking on the roadside near the high point.[^2]
