Buck Mountain's lookout is a 30-foot wood tower built in 1939 on the Coconino National Forest.[^1][^3] It is one of the few remaining towers of its CT-2 design in the United States, and its 14-by-14-foot live-in cab sits on top, with a catwalk.[^1] Wikipedia gives the build date as 1933 or 1939 and places the tower in the Coronado National Forest.[^2] The register and the federal record both point to 1939 and to Coconino.[^1][^3]

The register recorded the tower on April 1, 2001.[^1] Wikipedia gives a National Register listing date of December 14, 1987,[^2] while the federal record shows a January 28, 1988 publication date.[^3] The sources I read do not settle which date is right.

The work I could document is a HistoriCorps project, listed as Phase I and done in partnership with the Coconino National Forest.[^4] I could not find when that work took place, whether the tower now operates as a rental, or who staffs it. Check with the Coconino National Forest before planning a visit or a stay.
