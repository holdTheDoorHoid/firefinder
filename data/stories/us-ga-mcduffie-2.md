A Georgia lookout tower list places a McDuffie tower in Wilcox County, Georgia, with an unlabelled elevation of 381 (about 116 m if the figure is in feet), near 32.00173, -83.48039. It is tagged with the codes BLL-64703 and gfc-521.[^1] Do not confuse it with the National Historic Lookout Register's McDuffie Lookout, which is in McDuffie County, Georgia (registry numbers US 1069, GA 18).[^2]

For context, the Georgia Forestry Commission's history says the first fire observation tower went up on the Ware Hotel in Waycross, and it suggests a 1928 forestry fair in that city may have prompted it.[^3]

The tower list was last revised in March 2016.[^1] No source we could reach describes what stands at this Wilcox County site today or whether it is open to visitors.
