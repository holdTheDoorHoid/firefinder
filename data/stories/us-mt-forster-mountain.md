Forster Mountain was a lookout in Flathead County, Montana, about eight miles southeast of Essex.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page places it in the Flathead National Forest and gives its legal location as 28N-15W-16.[^2] Firelookout.com lists its elevation as 7,736 feet.[^1]

Firelookout.com calls it a fire lookout cabin. It dates the L-4 cab, sited a half mile south of the summit, to 1940, and says the cab was abandoned in 1948.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association classes the site as a ground site, marks it gone, and gives its coordinates as 48.18753, -113.49350.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3] If you plan to look, ask the Flathead National Forest first.
