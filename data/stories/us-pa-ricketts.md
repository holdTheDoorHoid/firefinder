The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register describes Ricketts Tower as an Aermotor-style steel tower dating to 1921.[^1] The register places the lookout in Wyoming County, Pennsylvania.[^1]

A 1955 newspaper item, quoted on the Eastern US Lookouts history page, says the tower was located on North Mountain and names its operator as Nicholas Kravitz. The same item says the tower's coverage took in North Mountain and the southwestern part of Wyoming County.[^2]

The register says the tower was afterward taken elsewhere and rebuilt privately.[^1] The Eastern US Lookouts history lists it as removed, with no date.[^2]

A separate Eastern US Lookouts index puts Ricketts under Luzerne County, not Wyoming.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. No source we found says whether the site is open to the public.
