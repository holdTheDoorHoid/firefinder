The Pinnacle Rock lookout stood in the Mendocino National Forest in Lake County, at 4,562 feet, or 1,390 meters.[^1] The register dates it to 1923.[^1] The register describes a California Region 5 Plan 4AR cabin on a low, open timber tower, with a stair up the rock face to reach it.[^1]

According to the register, the lookout was still up and manned in 1945.[^1] A 1945 Forest Service photo caption says a woman named Dale Nicholas drove her own motorcycle to bring supplies from Clear Lake up to her lookout.[^3] The photo's title spells the name Nichols.[^3]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the removal date as unknown.[^1] A climbing guide says the peak "once had a fire lookout."[^2]

The climbing guide describes a forest drive that ends a short walk from the summit, then a brief climb by rock steps onto summit rocks rated class 2.[^2] It says the road passes below the peak's southwest slope.[^2] It also says the national forest land has no posted restrictions and requires no permits.[^2] No source we read says what, if anything, is left of the lookout itself.
