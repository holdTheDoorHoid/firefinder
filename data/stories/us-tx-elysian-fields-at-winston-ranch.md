The survey page for the Elysian Fields lookout, in Harrison County, lists the Texas Forest Service beside the National Geodetic Survey.[^1] A survey description written in 1958 says the tower stood 100 feet overall, with a glass-enclosed cab on a four-legged steel frame painted gray.[^1]

A 1963 recovery note for the same survey station says a new tower was planned a few feet west of the existing one, to go up soon.[^1] The sources do not say whether that tower was built, or which of the two towers was later moved.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the original Elysian Fields tower as gone. It lists the tower at Winston Ranch, in Nacogdoches County, as standing and relocated from Elysian Fields.[^2][^3] The association spells the county "Nacagdoches," which is a misspelling.[^2][^3] The association gives no date for the move and does not say who moved the tower or why.[^2]

The sources we read do not name anyone who staffed the lookout or any fire it watched.[^1][^2] We also found nothing about public access or the tower's condition today, so please ask the property's owners before going near it, and do not climb it.[^2]
