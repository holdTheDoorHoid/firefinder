Elk Mountain is in Lincoln County, in the Kootenai National Forest.[^1] It lies about 28 miles east-northeast of Libby.[^2]

The site was developed in 1923 with a 10-by-10-foot frame cab and a roof platform.[^1] In 1940 a 10-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab went up about a quarter mile southwest of the summit.[^1] The US Forest Service administers the site.[^1]

According to the register, vandalism and decay have left the tower in ruins.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list instead marks the tower as gone.[^3] The two accounts word the fate differently, but neither describes a tower still in use.[^1][^3]

The register also mentions an L-6 cab about a mile northeast of the lookout, without saying whether it still stands.[^1] The site was added to the register on January 9, 2021.[^1] No source describes the summit today or whether visitors can reach it.
