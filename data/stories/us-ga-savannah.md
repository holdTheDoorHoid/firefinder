Tom Dunigan's list of Georgia lookout towers includes a Savannah Lookout Tower in Chatham County, at an elevation of 36 (read as feet, 11 m).[^1] Its coordinates are 32.04215, -81.18428.[^1] Mapcarta has a page for a tower of the same name in Chatham County, Georgia, and types it only as a tower.[^2] That page places Garden City, which lies just northwest of Savannah, about 5 miles north of the tower.[^2] So the lookout use rests on Dunigan's list alone.[^1][^2]

None of the sources checked gives the tower's current condition or use.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us (Tom Dunigan), https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Savannah Lookout Tower Map - Chatham County, Georgia, United States, Mapcarta, https://mapcarta.com/20788818 (accessed 2026-10-21).
