The lookout on Battle Creek ridge stood about 12 miles northeast of the Moose Creek Ranger Station, in the Nez Perce National Forest. A Forest Service list calls the spot Battle Ridge.[^1][^3][^4]

A camp was recorded here around 1928.[^1][^3]

Sources disagree on when the tower went up. Most give a 10-foot tower with an L-4 cab in 1932.[^1][^3][^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts dates the same cab to 1934.[^2] One page, citing an undated inventory of Moose Creek Ranger District lookouts, describes a 20-foot tower and a 1931 build.[^3]

The tower was destroyed in 1951.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

In 1957 the site was transferred from the Bitterroot National Forest to the Nez Perce National Forest.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^2] No source we found describes what, if anything, remains on the ridge today.
