The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists McCormick as a tower in Tuolumne County, at about 38.18 north and 120.28 west.[^1] It marks the tower as gone.[^1] Forest Lookouts also files McCormick under Tuolumne County.[^2]

A 1998 Stanislaus National Forest heritage-resources report on the Hungry prescribed burn is catalogued under the name McCormick Lookout.[^3] That catalogue name is the only link we found between the report and this lookout. The catalogue holds only the citation, not the report itself.[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether it is open to visitors. Before going anywhere near it, check with the land manager, and do not climb any structure you find there.
