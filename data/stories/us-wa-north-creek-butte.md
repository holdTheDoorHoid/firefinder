North Creek Butte is in Okanogan County, in the Okanogan National Forest, at about 6,580 feet.[^1][^2] An L-5 cab was constructed here in 1934.[^1][^3] The Register credits its 1934 panoramic photographs to the National Archives Pacific Northwest Region, and the panoramas are dated September 18, 1934.[^1][^4] WillhiteWeb also says photos were taken from the cab's roof that same year.[^3]

The lookout was abandoned in 1952, and the cab is gone.[^1][^2][^3]

Today, no source we read gives directions to the site itself. WillhiteWeb offers only a rough outline of how to reach the area and says the exact spot is unmarked.[^3] One photo caption says a tree later took over the North Creek Lookout sign.[^3] The page lists an elevation gain of 2,710 feet but no hiking distance.[^3] Because the cab is gone, there is no structure to climb, and anyone planning a visit should check current access with the national forest first.
