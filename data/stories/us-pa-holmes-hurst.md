The Holmes Hurst tower was a steel Aermotor fire tower put up on Holmes Hill, between Leolyn and Shunk, in Lycoming County.[^1][^3] A newspaper item from August 1921 says the forestry department's new tower had just been hauled by truck to its site.[^3]

An April 1922 item named M.E. McNett, of Leolyn, as towerman on Holmes Hill.[^3] An October 1937 item said the tower near Masten would be taken down after the fall season, while a new one was going up nearby, on higher ground at Sprout Point.[^3]

The register says the tower was moved in 1938 and renamed Sprout Point.[^1] The 1937 item and the register differ by about a year, and we have used the register's 1938 date for the move.[^1][^3] The Sprout Point entry gives 1938 as the build year of that structure, says it came from Holmes Hurst, and gives 1985 as its removal year.[^2]

Forest Lookouts places Sprout Point in Lycoming County's state forest, which that page spells Tiadachton.[^4] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at either the Holmes Hill or the Sprout Point site today, or whether visitors can reach them.
