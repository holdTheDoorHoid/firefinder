Pulpit Mountain's lookout was a 30-foot pole tower, built in 1934 in Lincoln County, Montana, in the Kootenai National Forest.[^1][^3] The firelookout.com page says the tower originally carried an L-4 cab on top.[^1] The county list gives its location as 32N-33W-23.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association list marks the tower as gone and gives its coordinates.[^2] The firelookout.com page says it was in ruins by 1948, and its heading reads "R.I.P. 1934-1948."[^1] That page is a short entry with a photo credited to the Fire Lookout Museum, so the 1934 and 1948 dates rest on this one source alone.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
