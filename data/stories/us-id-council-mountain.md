The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Council Mountain as a ground lookout that is gone.[^1] It stood in the Payette National Forest in Adams County, Idaho, about seven miles east of Council, at 7,900 feet.[^2][^3][^4] A tent camp lookout was set up there in 1909.[^4] A log cabin followed in 1924, according to firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts.[^2][^3]

Forest Lookouts tells a different early story. It quotes a newspaper report dated July 22, 1923, that says the lookout house was destroyed by fire on the night of July 17 and that the cause has not been determined.[^4] The same page dates a replacement ground cabin to 1923 to 1924.[^4] The 1923 report calls the burned house among the most up-to-date in district four and says it cost roughly $800 to build.[^4]

The cab built in 1933 is an R-4 in firelookout.com and Forest Lookouts, but Idaho Fire Lookouts calls it an L-4.[^2][^3][^4] Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both list Leonard Williams as known staff in 1942.[^2][^3]

A 1959 Coast and Geodetic Survey station description calls the lookout house abandoned and gives it a footprint of about 12 feet square, a height of 15 feet, and a pointed roof.[^4] Peakbagging.com's photo caption says the lookout was abandoned by 1959 and gone in 1966.[^5] Forest Lookouts says the structure was disposed of by burning in 1966, and firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts also date its loss to 1966.[^2][^3][^4]

Peakbagging.com has a photo taken August 18, 2019, captioned as showing "what remains" of the lookout, but the caption does not say what is left.[^5] The same caption places the lookout about 0.8 miles north of, and 125 feet below, the Council benchmark.[^5] No source describes the site now. The Council Mountain climbing page says the peak sits in a roadless area with primitive trails around it, but no source gives a route to the lookout site.[^6] Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as hike-in.[^3] No source says whether the public may visit it.
