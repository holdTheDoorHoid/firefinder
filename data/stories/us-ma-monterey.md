The Civilian Conservation Corps built the Monterey Fire Tower on Mount Wilcox, and the Register calls it the first tower the CCC built in Berkshire County.[^1] The sources disagree on the year. A hiking-history page says CCC Camp S-66 erected it in 1934, while the Register's description, the Forest Lookouts page and a 2025 hiking post say 1935.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

The same hiking-history page describes a 60-foot Aermotor MC-39 tower with a 7-by-7 wooden cab, and gives April 1, 1934 as the first day of operation.[^3] The Register describes a 60-foot steel structure with a perched 10-by-10 wooden cab.[^1] The page says a new 10-by-10 cab and chain-link fence went up in 1966.[^3] It also lists a 1939 visit by a Massachusetts Geodetic Survey team that took measurements off the tower's legs, power and two-way radios in 1948, a new roof in 2003, and a 1988 observer.[^3]

A 2025 hiking post says the tower is still used today, and suggests parking at the CCC Camp for a six-mile loop on the Mt. Wilcox Trail in Beartown State Forest.[^4] Beartown State Forest is run by the state Department of Conservation and Recreation, with a listed address of 69 Blue Hill Road in Monterey.[^5]

The hiking-history page says that with no guard, the tower has a fence and cameras. It adds that a visitor who asks permission while a guard is on duty might even be invited up the tower.[^3] The sources do not say the tower is generally open to climbing, so enjoy it from the trail.
