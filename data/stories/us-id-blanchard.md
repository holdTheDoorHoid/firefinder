Blanchard Lookout was a cabin, not a tower. It stood about two miles north of the town of Blanchard in Bonner County, Idaho, at an elevation of 2,900 feet.[^1][^2]

The cabin went up in 1935 and was abandoned in 1939, according to all three lookout pages that describe it.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Lookouts site quotes a January 1927 fire warden's report that mentions "the Blanchard lookout line." The report says a repeating coil at the line's tie-in let the lookout reach head office and the wardens at Priest River.[^3] The sources do not say what stood on the site in 1927, and they do not reconcile that report with the 1935 date.

Idaho Fire Lookouts says it has no documented staffing history for the cabin, and none of the three lookout pages names a lookout.[^1][^2][^3]

The site is filed under the Pend Oreille TPA. The University of Idaho's digital library lists the association as the Pend d'Oreille Timber Protective Association.[^1][^5] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the site as ground, and gone.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. Idaho Fire Lookouts classes the site as hike-in, but none of the sources says whether the public may visit it now.[^2]
