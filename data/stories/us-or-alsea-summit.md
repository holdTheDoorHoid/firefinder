Alsea Summit sits on Alsea Mountain in Benton County, Oregon, about 14 miles southwest of Corvallis, inside the Siuslaw National Forest.[^1][^2][^3]

Sources disagree about when the first tower went up. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register and firelookout.com both date an L-4 tower to 1939.[^1][^2] Newspapers from the period suggest a different timeline.[^3] In August 1937 a lookout named Richard Strating was in charge of a post atop Alsea Mountain.[^3] On December 8, 1938, the forestry department was erecting a new station there.[^3] In September 1939 a crew of CCC enrollees began a road from the summit westward to the lookout station.[^3]

A September 1940 report said a new station would replace a tent that a lookout had been using, and that it would be a tower ten feet high with storage space underneath.[^3] By July 1941 the papers reported that the new towers were in use for the first time, after lookouts had spent the previous year in tents.[^3] In May 1946 the newly organized Polk-Benton District of the State Board of Forestry was to occupy and maintain the station under an agreement.[^3]

The sources disagree about the ending, too. The register says the tower was removed in 1950, while firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page gives no date and lists the site only as removed.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there. Check with the Siuslaw National Forest before you head out.
