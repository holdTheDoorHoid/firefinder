Cedar Mountain is a summit in the St. Joe National Forest, in Shoshone County, Idaho, about five miles north of Avery.[^1][^2][^3]

A two-story frame cabin went up on the summit in 1915.[^2][^3][^4][^5] In the 1930s a 30-foot tower replaced it, though the sources give no exact year.[^2][^3][^4][^5]

The one staff story we found is from 1922. A newspaper report, reprinted on the Forest Lookouts page, describes a 17-year-old lookout, Stanford Larson, who was badly hurt when a cartridge went off as he began to open his revolver while he sat reading in the cabin.[^4][^5] He dragged himself to the telephone, and the report says he was then treated at a hospital in St. Maries.[^4][^5]

The lookout was removed, but none of the sources gives a date for that.[^2][^3][^4]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register recorded the site in September 2019, on a nomination from Tom Taylor. It describes a foundation that a lookout cabin once sat on, along with an old stove, coiled wire and other leftover parts of the lookout.[^1] Sources disagree on what sat on that foundation. The register says a cabin, while the other sources say a 30-foot tower replaced the 1915 cabin.[^1][^2][^3]

Today a hiking write-up on a personal site, which is undated, describes a house site with rock work, a stove part and plenty of metal still on the summit.[^5] The same write-up says four access points reach the summit.[^5] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^2] The sources we read mention no closure or restriction.[^2]
