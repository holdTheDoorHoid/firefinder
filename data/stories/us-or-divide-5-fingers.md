Divide, which the register lists as Divide (5 Fingers), sits in the Siuslaw National Forest in Lane County, Oregon.[^1] Firelookout.com places it about 23 miles northeast of Florence.[^3] Rex's page calls it Divide Ridge (Five Rivers).[^2]

A 41-foot treated timber tower with an R-6 cab went up on the site in 1959.[^1][^2][^3]

A former lookout's personal website says the author worked the tower in the summers of 1960 and 1961.[^4] The same account says the author used a backpack water pump once on a small smoke in a recent slash burn about a mile from the tower.[^4]

Rex's page lists 1972 as the last season the lookout was staffed, and it lists the lookout on emergency status in 1973.[^2] Firelookout.com also gives 1973 as the emergency year.[^3]

None of our sources dates the tower's removal. The register says only that the lookout is gone.[^1] Firelookout.com marks it gone without a date.[^3] The personal account says the tower had been torn down years before the author's later visit.[^4]

The same account describes a return visit in 1972. The hill had been logged, and one of the stumps the author had painted around the tower survived both the tower's destruction and the logging.[^4] No other source we read describes what remains on the site now.

We found nothing on current public access. The register places the site in the Siuslaw National Forest, so check with that forest before planning a trip.[^1]
