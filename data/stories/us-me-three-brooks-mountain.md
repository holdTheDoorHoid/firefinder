The state's forestry department put a steel lookout on Three Brooks Mountain in Aroostook County in 1914.[^1][^2] The 1914 report gives its height as fifty feet from the ground to the portable house, which is seven feet square.[^1] A 1920 inventory lists the tower as 48 feet tall, and Maine Memory gives the site's elevation as 1,578 feet.[^1][^2]

The tower sat in Township 15, Range 6.[^3] The state built a telephone line from the tower to the sporting camps on Square Lake, and a private line ran on to the settlement at Guerette and the town of Stockholm.[^1] A watchman's camp stood about a hundred rods from the tower, near a spring brook that supplied fine water. The camp also had a telephone box, as the tower did.[^1]

J.M. Donahue was the watchman in 1917, the only named watchman we found.[^2] We found no record of a particular fire the tower spotted.

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing, but its cab is no longer in place.[^4] We found no date for when the cab came down.

We found no source describing current access to the site or recent visits, so treat the status as unverified. Do not climb what remains of the tower. Ask the Maine Forest Service before planning any trip to the mountain.
