Hawk Mountain, in Union County, Georgia, has a former fire lookout site on the Appalachian Trail.[^1][^2] Willhite's page gives the summit an elevation of 3,560 feet, but the tnlandforms list puts the mountain at 3,576 feet, so the two sources do not agree.[^1][^2]

Willhite says the lookout was marked on maps in 1935. The old AT went up and over the summit, the page adds.[^1] Its photo captions say a fire tower appears on the 1935 USGS map, and again on the 1946 map, which was updated in 1956.[^1]

In 1972 a USGS party placed a benchmark in the footing of the northwest lookout tower and called it HAWK 1972.[^1] The party described the lookout as a destroyed tower.[^1]

The page is undated. Its photographs are captioned Firepit and Footing, and neither source describes a standing structure on the summit.[^1][^2]

Willhite gives the access as a 0.3-mile alternate trail from the Appalachian Trail, with 500 feet of gain.[^1] The AT no longer climbs over the summit and instead bypasses it on a side traverse.[^1]
