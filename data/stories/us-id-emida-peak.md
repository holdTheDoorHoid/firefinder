Emida Peak, in Benewah County, Idaho, once carried a pole fire tower.[^1][^2][^3] The tower went up in 1939, about half a mile northwest of a point the sources label only as the westernmost.[^1][^2][^3] The St. Joe National Forest is listed as the agency.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com places the site two miles east of Emida.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page gives the land location as 44N-2W-35, a township, range and section code.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the elevation as 4,548 feet.[^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts says no staffing history has been documented for the lookout yet.[^2]

The tower was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1][^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks it as gone.[^4] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or says whether the land is open to visitors. Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the lookout as a hike-in.[^2]
