The Flambeau tower was a lookout in Vilas County.[^1] The Wisconsin lookout list gives its position as 45.99621 north, 89.84492 west, and marks it as gone.[^2] A mapping database lists an observation tower named Flambeau Lookout Tower at nearly the same coordinates.[^3] TopoQuest puts that point at 1,683 feet above sea level, on the USGS 1:24K map named Fence Lake.[^4]

Do not confuse it with the Flambeau Ridge tower in Chippewa County, which the same list marks as standing.[^2]

Wisconsin's fire towers of that period went up in the 1930s and 1940s, and in 2015 the DNR announced that the towers were being removed.[^5]

No source describes what remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it.
