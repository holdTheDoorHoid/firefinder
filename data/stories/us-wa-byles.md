The Byles lookout went up in the 1940s on the Clemons Tree Farm in Grays Harbor County, Washington.[^1][^2][^3] Firetower.org and firelookout.com both describe a 70-foot pole tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab, and WillhiteWeb gives the same measurements.[^1][^2][^3] WillhiteWeb says Weyerhaeuser built it to protect the tree farm.[^3] The company dedicated the tract as a tree farm in June 1941, and the land had already been logged and burned over.[^4]

We found no source that names who staffed the Byles lookout or when it was in use.

WillhiteWeb says the tower may have come down in the 1960s.[^3] A Weebly list of Washington lookouts gives c.1960 as the removal date.[^5] A survey-station note named for the lookout says its lot was reported destroyed in 1960, but the note does not say whether that covers the tower itself.[^5]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register recorded the lookout on November 27, 2011, and says the tower is now gone.[^1] Captions on WillhiteWeb's photographs describe four footings flush with the ground, rebar poking out of them, and a post snapped off one footing.[^3] The land is privately owned, and we found no source that describes public access, so treat the site as private.[^1]
