Olson Mountain Lookout Site is in Jackson County, Oregon, about eight miles north of Butte Falls, at 5,054 feet (1,540 m).[^1][^2] The register places it in Southwest Oregon.[^1]

The register dates the lookout to the 1920s and calls it a crow's nest lookout.[^1] Firelookout.com gives the same decade and also calls it a crow's nest.[^2]

The register says the structure was still there in 1994 and is now in ruins.[^1] Firelookout.com also has it still there in 1994.[^2] The two sources agree on that date and on the 1920s date.[^1][^2]

Both sources list the Oregon Department of Forestry as the agency for the site.[^1][^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added the site in March 2011, with Ray Kresek as nominator.[^1] Neither the register nor firelookout.com says what the ruins consist of today or whether the public can visit the site.[^1][^2]
