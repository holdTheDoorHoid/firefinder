Stevens Peak sits in the Kootenai National Forest, about five miles south of Noxon in Sanders County.[^1] Forest Lookouts gives the location as 25N-32W-18, a land survey code.[^2] FireLookout.com places the site at an elevation of 5,546 feet.[^1]

FireLookout.com dates the lookout to 1930 and describes it as a log and frame cabin.[^1] It was abandoned in 1948.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page gives its status as removed.[^2] FireLookout.com and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list both mark the site as gone.[^1][^3] The association's list places it at 47.91765, -115.77500 and classes it as a ground site rather than a tower.[^3]

None of the three sources I read describes what, if anything, remains at the peak today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
