Bald Mountain (North Piney) was a lookout in Sublette County, Wyoming, inside the Bridger-Teton National Forest.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association classes it as a ground site rather than a tower.[^2]

A Pinedale Roundup item dated July 24, 1941, reprinted on a lookout history page titled Bald Mountain (Big Piney), said the Forest Service had put up a much-needed lookout cabin in Sublette County that summer.[^4] The item does not name North Piney, but other entries on the same page do.[^4] It also said communications would run by telephone and radio to Big Piney, and that the station would be manned on days when the weather station at Cora showed low humidity.[^4]

In September 1942 the Big Piney Examiner reported that Mr. and Mrs. Merritt Hopkins had left the North Piney lookout after about six weeks there.[^4]

An October 26, 1950 Forest Service notice listed properties the agency planned to sell, give away, abandon or destroy. It said the spur to Snider Basin Ranger Station, with a branch to North Piney Lookout, might be included.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association now marks the site as gone.[^2][^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.
