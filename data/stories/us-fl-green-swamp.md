The Green Swamp Tower was on a parcel of land in northwest Polk County.[^3] Its history is told in two ways. The Eastern US Lookouts site says a lookout tower went up at Providence, also in Polk County, in 1963.[^2] In 1990 that tower was dismantled into three sections, moved about two miles north and put back up as the Green Swamp tower.[^2][^1] A 2006 newspaper account gives a different picture. It says the Green Swamp Tower was built in 1963 and does not mention a move.[^3]

The 2006 article describes a 100-foot tower with a cubicle-sized room on top and 132 steps.[^3] By then the tower had been formally retired, but some use continued from time to time.[^3] Its roof still carries an antenna, though the radio inside has been gone for a long time.[^3]

The state phased out full-time fire spotters through the 1990s, and the final jobs ended in 2001.[^3] After that, the Division of Forestry stopped paying for upkeep on towers in some districts, Polk among them.[^3] By 2006 rotting wood hung from some of the steps.[^3]

I could not find any source dated after 2006 that says whether the tower still stands. In 2006 the owner was the Florida Division of Forestry.[^3] The same article calls the structures unsafe.[^3] Do not climb it, and check with the state's forestry agency before you go looking for it.
