Beekman was a Louisiana state fire tower in Morehouse Parish, about 12 miles north of Bastrop.[^2] A Coast and Geodetic Survey station record from 1939 describes it as a steel tower about 100 feet tall with an enclosed glass cab at the top, and gives it the designation Fire Tower 4.[^2]

In 1915 Louisiana directed funds to fire protection work, which allowed lookout watchmen to be hired, and by 1943 thirty-nine fire towers had gone up.[^1] Surveyors in 1939 also observed the tower from five triangulation stations, including one called Beekman.[^2]

Wikipedia's list marks the tower as torn down.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site records it as removed.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts site gives its location as 22N-6E-Sec.4, a township, range and section code.[^2] Wikipedia gives its position as 32°55′46″N 91°51′5″W.[^1] The list's compilers were unable to check the site on Street View or in older aerial photos.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains there, or whether the site is open to the public.
