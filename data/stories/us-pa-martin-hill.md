The Martin Hill lookout stood on Martin Hill in Bedford County, in the Buchanan State Forest.[^2][^3] A tree observation post 25 feet above the ground went up there in 1912.[^1][^2] The state replaced it in 1921 with a steel tower.[^1][^2]

Forester William Byers had the steel tower built that fall at a cost of $610.03, the Southampton Township history says.[^3] The township says the tower stood 68 feet tall and that a small cabin for the watchman sat near its base.[^3] A 1921 item in Forest Leaves said 50 towers had been ordered and that Martin Hill would be one of the first 18 put up.[^2]

The township says a watch person manned the tower each fall during the high fire season, sometimes staying on the mountain for weeks.[^3] Towerman Irvin Ruby reported two fires in 1927 and nineteen the next year.[^3] In May 1930 a careless fire on the hill destroyed 1,150 acres, 950 of them timber.[^3] In November 1975 a stolen car burned, along with four acres of underbrush around the tower.[^2]

The register says the steel tower was removed in 2002.[^1] The township gives a different date, March 17, 2001, and says DER had quietly sold the tower for $300.[^3] The sources also do not agree on elevation. The register gives 2,720 feet (829 meters) for the site, and the township gives 2,700 feet for the tower. A 1921 Bedford newspaper put the top of the hill at 3,096 feet.[^1][^3][^2]

The township describes the summit today as filled with different styles of communication towers.[^3] None of the three sources describes what, if anything, is left of the 1921 tower or its cabin.[^1][^2][^3] The township says the state forest's trails are open to licensed ATVs for part of the year.[^3] Whether the summit itself is open to visitors is not settled by any source we read.[^3]
