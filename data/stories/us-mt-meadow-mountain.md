Meadow Mountain rises to 7,162 feet in the Flathead National Forest, about ten miles southeast of the Spotted Bear Ranger Station in Flathead County, Montana.[^1] The county listing places it at 47.81893 north and 113.37557 west.[^2]

The firelookout.com entry dates its lookout cabin to 1936 and calls it an L-4 cab.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes that design as a 14-by-14-foot frame cab meant for living in.[^3] The register's notes, citing Ray Kresek's book, place the L-4's origin in 1929, when it was still a prototype.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list gives the site type as Ground rather than Tower and marks it Gone.[^2] The firelookout.com entry also marks the cabin Gone.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
