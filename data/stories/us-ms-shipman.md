The Eastern US lookouts list includes a Shipman lookout in George County, Mississippi, and marks it "Removed".[^1] Shipman is an unincorporated community in George County.[^2] The community was known as Brushy until 1907, when it was renamed Shipman.[^2]

A topographic map listing places a Shipman Lookout Tower on the Agricola USGS map.[^3] The listing places the point at about 30.8738 N, 88.5145 W, roughly 262 feet (80 m) above sea level.[^3] The map database files it among the towers of George County.[^3] It classifies the feature as a tower, but it does not say whether a structure stands there now.[^3]

Today, no source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether visitors can reach it.
