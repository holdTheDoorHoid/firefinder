The Flintkote (II) Lookout Tower is an 80-foot Aermotor tower in Kemper County, Mississippi, at about 579 feet, or 176 meters.[^1] The register describes a diagonal staircase and gives no build date.[^1]

Aermotor also made fire lookout towers from galvanized steel.[^2] Wikipedia says hundreds of one 7-by-7 model were used in the southeastern United States, but nothing we read says this tower is that model.[^2][^1]

The register's "Administered by" line names the Mississippi Forestry Commission.[^1] The description, though, says a timber company owns the site now.[^1] The two do not agree, and the register does not say when ownership changed.[^1] The commission was created in 1926 and is authorized to prevent, control and extinguish forest fires.[^3]

The register listed the tower as US 1668, MS 32 on July 10, 2022, nominated by Jason Johns.[^1] The site visit it describes took place on July 3, 2022, and we found no later report.[^1]

We found no record of who staffed the tower, which fires it watched, or whether visitors may go there.[^1] The register says a timber company owns the land, so anyone interested should ask the owner before going on the site and should not climb the tower.[^1]
