Tennessee Landforms lists a Nature Center tower in Bradley County at 850 feet, but the entry is not a standing lookout. Its status reads "sitting," and its remarks describe a display made from the top of a former Ooltewah tower at the Ocoee Regional Nature Center.[^1]

The Ooltewah tower was in Hamilton County and listed at 1,495 feet. Its own entry says the upper part of the tower is now at the Ocoee Regional Nature Center in Bradley County, and the tower is marked removed.[^2]

Because the record describes a display piece, it says nothing about staffing or fire watches at the Nature Center site.[^1]

I could not find when the Ooltewah tower was built, when its top came down, or when it went to the nature center. The nature center's own pages were not checked, so before you visit, confirm that the display is still there and ask about hours and access.

[^1]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, Tennessee Landforms (Tom Dunigan), waypoint BRT003 Nature Center, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/m.php?wpt=BRT003 (accessed 2026-10-09).
[^2]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, Tennessee Landforms (Tom Dunigan), waypoint HMT002 Ooltewah, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/m.php?wpt=HMT002 (accessed 2026-10-09).
