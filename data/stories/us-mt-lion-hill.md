The Lion Hill lookout was in Flathead County, Montana, on the Flathead National Forest, about three miles southeast of Hungry Horse.[^1]

The site had a 50-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab from 1932. The page gives the hill's elevation as 4,830 feet.[^1]

The tower was destroyed in 1950.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list also records the lookout as gone, at 48.35663, -114.01433.[^2]

A 2023 Hungry Horse News item about a proposed Forest Service fuel break says the break would run along Lion Hill Road, Forest Service Road No. 5311, just south of Martin City.[^3] That article does not describe the lookout site. No source we read says what, if anything, remains there today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
