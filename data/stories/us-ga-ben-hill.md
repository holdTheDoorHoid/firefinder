The tnlandforms.us list of Georgia lookout towers records a tower it calls "ben hill" in Ben Hill County, at 31.75242 north and 83.24515 west, with an elevation figure of 363.[^1] The row ties the tower to two reference numbers, BLL-64708 and gfc-513.[^1] The list explains that GFC provided coordinates for towers marked BLL, and that an older GFC list gave locations for towers marked gfc.[^1] The list says towers numbered 1 to 72 take their names from a USGS name database, and that towers were discovered on 1:100,000 USGS topographic maps. It also says names for towers 73 and above are its author's own creation, and this row falls in that range.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts page on Georgia towers has a BEN HILL entry under Ben Hill County, with no further detail.[^2]

No source we found describes what remains at this site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
