The Wacissa lookout is in Jefferson County, Florida.[^1] Land for the site was bought in 1944, and in 1945 an 80-foot (24 m) steel tower with a 7 by 7 foot cabin went up.[^1] By 1959 the Florida Forest Service was running a fire watch station there, and a news report quoted on the same page places a family's house at the tower site.[^1] The steel tower was sold at auction on September 23, 2009, for $2,379.[^1] A July 2010 news report, carried by Firehouse, says Florida's manned towers were being sold off, and that one tower in Leesburg was won at auction by a Georgia man for about $600.[^2]

Today, OpenStreetMap still shows a point named Wacissa Lookout Tower, with a note asking "still present?"[^3] No source I could read says whether the steel tower still stands on the site, what remains there, or whether the public can visit.
