Birch Ridge, also listed as the Birch Hill Lookout Site, stood in the Clearwater National Forest in Clearwater County, Idaho.[^1][^2] It lay about 12 miles north-northeast of the Kelly Creek ranger station.[^2]

The tower was a 40-foot pole L-4 tower built in 1932, according to the register and Rex's page.[^1][^2] A 2016 Clearwater National Forest lookout list gives a 35-foot pole instead.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists one known staffer, Mark E Lawrence, for 1932, and no later staffing.[^3]

The register says the tower was removed in 1959.[^1] Rex's page, Idaho Fire Lookouts and the 2016 list all say it was destroyed that year.[^2][^3][^4] None of them explains how it came down.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register lists the site as US 822, ID 2, and dates its entry to January 20, 2011.[^1]

The register says the U.S. Forest Service administers the site.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the ridge today, or whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
