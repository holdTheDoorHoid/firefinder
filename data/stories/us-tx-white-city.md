The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a lookout at White City in San Augustine County, Texas, and marks it as gone.[^1][^2] The register gives its type only as "Unk", so the kind of structure is not confirmed.[^1] Its listed position is 31.26917, -94.16306.[^1]

White City is a community in southwestern San Augustine County, twenty miles southwest of San Augustine.[^3] The settlement traces to a lumber camp and railway stop on the St. Louis Southwestern Railway, and it gained a post office in 1910.[^3]

No source describes the site now, so it is not known what, if anything, remains there or whether the ground is open to visitors.
