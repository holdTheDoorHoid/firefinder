McKinley Mountain stands at the edge of the San Rafael Wilderness in Santa Barbara County.[^1][^2]

A 2011 blog post says records indicate that a cabin was built on the mountain in 1935, and a 2013 trail account gives the same year.[^2][^3] It stood in a sandstone-ringed clearing east of the official peak.[^2] The cabin was later also used as part of the Aircraft Warning Service, a civilian watch service of World War II.[^2][^3][^4] The 2011 blogger reasoned that spotting aircraft was not the cabin's first job.[^2]

The Forest Service removed the shack in 1974, after high winds had destroyed it.[^2][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list marks the site as gone.[^1]

A 2013 trail account says some remains can still be found at the summit.[^3] The road gives out at McKinley Saddle, and from there a steep, informal half-mile trail climbs to the top.[^2] We found no source that describes those remains in detail, or says whether the site is open to visitors.
