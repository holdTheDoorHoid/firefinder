Birch Lake appears on a Saskatchewan fire-tower listing, which places the site at 53 degrees 28 minutes 55.5 seconds north, 107 degrees 56 minutes 13.7 seconds west [^1]. The listing names Albert Klavins as a towerman there, with three year figures beside his name that the page does not explain [^1].

A family memoir, titled "Birch Lake -The Fire Tower: Chapter 4 of 4," says the author's father took a summer job in 1944 as a fire observer at one of the northern Saskatchewan towers [^2]. The memoir's photo caption says the tower pictured was similar to the one the father worked near Meadow Lake, so it is unclear whether his post was at Birch Lake [^2]. The memoir is a personal recollection, not an official record.

No source I could read describes what, if anything, stands at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^2]: Birch Lake -The Fire Tower: Chapter 4 of 4, McNeill Life Stories, https://mcneillifestories.com/?p=1131 (accessed 2026-10-20).
