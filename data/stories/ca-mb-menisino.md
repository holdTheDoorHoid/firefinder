Menisino is a farming community in the Rural Municipality of Piney, Manitoba.[^1] The municipality says it was already a settlement in the late 1800s, well before the railway arrived.[^1]

A Canada Lookouts page covers a Menisino lookout, but it carries only the name.[^2]

When a fire was spotted, the lookout took measurements of its location and passed the information to other towers, so the fire could be located by triangulation.[^3] Gordon Goldsborough of the Manitoba Historical Society says his research dates the first fire detections by lookout towers to 1913, and estimates that roughly 66 towers were built in Manitoba overall.[^3] The article's provincial figures, however, place the first use of the towers in the 1930s, which conflicts with the 1913 date.[^3]

No source we found describes the Menisino site today, including whether any tower stands there or whether the public can reach it.[^2]
