The Adams Mountain lookout stood in the Umpqua National Forest, about 15 miles southwest of Oakridge in Lane County.[^2] The register's record starts with a 10-foot platform and rag camp, set up in 1918.[^1] In 1933 a 19-foot wooden L-4 tower replaced it.[^1]

A September 1933 newspaper item said a 10-by-10 lookout was to be built by the CCC.[^3] A July 1933 item says a man spent a week on the mountain with a work crew from the Brice Creek CCC camp.[^3] A 1942 report describes the 1933 tower as 19 feet tall, built of round fir timbers, with a 14-by-14 cab that was larger than the planned size.[^3] Getting there meant 26 miles by road from Cottage Grove to a trailhead, then a five-mile hike.[^3]

Our sources name few people. A 1935 item said lookout Fay Stewart was home on leave.[^3] A 1938 item said forest ranger Wayne Mackin would spend that summer at the station.[^3]

The register says the tower was removed in 1958.[^1] Firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year, and neither source says how it came down.[^2] The site was registered on November 17, 2010,[^1] and the Forest Fire Lookout Association list marks it as gone.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit. Ask the Umpqua National Forest before going.
