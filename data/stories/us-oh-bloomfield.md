The Bloomfield fire tower in Muskingum County, Ohio, was still in use in October 1967.[^2] A caption quoted on the Forest Lookouts site shows a helicopter circling the tower to collect a United Fund pledge card, and says a local woman was in charge of the tower.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts page ends by marking the tower "Removed," with no date.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the Bloomfield site, as US 1555, with a registration date of September 3, 2014.[^1]

The firelookout.org list of Ohio lookouts gives the tower a later home. A tower called Berg, in Holmes County, appears there as "(Relocated Bloomfield)" and standing, and its coordinates are listed only as "Private."[^3]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, stands on the Bloomfield site now. Treat the Berg tower as off-limits unless you have the owner's permission.
