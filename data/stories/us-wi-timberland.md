Timberland is a standing fire tower in Burnett County, Wisconsin, according to the Forest Fire Lookout Association.[^1][^2] The association places it at about 45.643 north and 92.094 west, and neither of its Wisconsin lists gives it a register number.[^1][^2]

I could not find a build date, builder, design, or history for Timberland in the sources I reached. Both association lists give the name, county, coordinates, type, and status, with no date column.[^1][^2] That leaves its early years, any fires, and any staffing unrecorded here. I found no record of a closure, move, or rebuild either, though thin sources can hide such events.[^1][^2]

Today I can say little about access. The lists do not say who owns the tower, whether it can be climbed, or whether anyone rents it.[^1][^2] Treat it as unknown ground. Ask the landowner before going near it, and do not climb a tower that is closed or private.
