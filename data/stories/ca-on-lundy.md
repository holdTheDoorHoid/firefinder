The Lundy fire tower is listed at 24.4 m (80 ft) tall, with light steel construction and a square cab.[^1] The Canada Lookouts index places it at Twin Lakes, New Liskeard.[^1] The firelookout.ca register gives its location as the West Timiskaming District of Ontario, with coordinates of 47.5215, -79.89865.[^2]

Across Ontario, most lookout towers went up between the 1920s and the 1950s, when forest fire protection was at its height.[^3] Ontario's Department of Lands and Forests oversaw them, and in 1972 it was renamed the Ministry of Natural Resources.[^3] Through the 1960s the towers saw less use, as aircraft spotting and fire-fighting crews took on more of the work.[^3] The Clay fire tower history site adds that by 2013 the Ontario MNR had decided to take down many of the towers that were still standing.[^3]

No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at the Lundy site today.
