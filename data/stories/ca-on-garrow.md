The Garrow Fire Tower is recorded as a steel tower in Nipissing, Ontario, at an elevation of 455 m (1493 ft).[^1] The register's contributor says the general area is known, but the exact spot is less certain: "I think this is the actual spot, but I do not truly know."[^1]

A list titled Ontario Fire Towers Still Standing gives the tower as "Garrow Tower- Tower Lake, Eldee, Ont."[^2] The list's key says an asterisk marks a tower whose cupola has been removed and which is now used as a communications tower.[^2] The Garrow entry has no asterisk.[^2]

A copy of the list on the Ontario Fire Tower Lookouts site carries a 2013 note saying the MNR had decided to take down many of the towers on it.[^3] That note names no tower, and the list's author says the list is being kept as it is.[^3]

Planners usually picked high ground for a tower, according to the site's towermen pages.[^4] The list is undated, so it does not show whether the tower stands today. No source here says what the site looks like now or whether visitors can reach it.
