The Georgia list of lookout towers places the Centennial tower in Morgan County at 33.584 N, 83.609 W, at 778 feet (237 m) above sea level.[^1] The Eastern US Lookouts site gives its agency as the Georgia Forestry Commission, and it reproduces two 1950 items from Madison newspapers about the tower.[^2]

The Madison Madisonian reported in April 1950 that work on the Centennial Church tower was still underway, and that Mr. V. E. Bulington of Brownwood had been engaged to staff it once it was done.[^2] A Madison Morgan County News item dated May 26, 1950, said the tower was finished and its radio was in place, and that it would enter service the following fall.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register's Georgia index lists 29 registered lookouts, and this tower is not among them.[^3]

No source describes the site today, so I cannot say whether anything remains at Centennial Church or whether the place is open to visitors. Anyone planning a visit should first find out who owns the land, and should not climb any tower that is closed or posted.
