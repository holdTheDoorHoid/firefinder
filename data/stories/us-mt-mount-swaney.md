Mount Swaney sits in the Flathead National Forest, about 20 miles west of Whitefish, at an elevation of 6,349 feet.[^1] The site dates its lookout to 1934 and describes a steel tower with a tent cabin.[^1]

After the 1910 fire season, the Forest Service began placing fire-watch stations on high summits.[^3] The Forest kept putting up more lookouts, the 1930s especially, and by 1939 it counted 147 lookouts and towers.[^3]

The site says the lookout was abandoned and is now gone.[^1] The Forest Association's Montana list places it in Flathead County and also marks Mount Swaney as a gone tower.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether it is open to the public.
