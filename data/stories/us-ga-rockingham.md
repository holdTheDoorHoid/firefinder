Rockingham appears as a tower on the Georgia lookout list kept by Tom Dunigan at tnlandforms.us.[^1] The list places it in Bacon County at 31.54553, -82.41578.[^1] That version of the list carries a March 2016 revision stamp, so the entry may be out of date.[^1]

The entry shares its name with the community of Rockingham, also called Sallie, an unincorporated place in central Bacon County.[^1][^2] Alma, the county seat, lies west of the community, which sits along State Route 32.[^2] A county wildfire plan credits settlers from Rockingham, North Carolina, with naming the community.[^3]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
