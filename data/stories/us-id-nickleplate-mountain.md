Nickleplate Mountain sits in Bonner County, on land the Kaniksu National Forest manages, and a lookout went up there in 1930.[^1][^2] Two pages describe it as a 35-foot pole platform, and one of them credits the dates to Kresek.[^2][^3] The association's list instead files it as a tower.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page also gives the code 61N-5W-10, which is written in the township, range and section form used for land surveys.[^3]

Those pages say the lookout was abandoned in the 1930s, without naming a single year.[^2][^3] The association's Idaho list now marks the tower as gone and places it at about 48.65 north, 116.97 west.[^1]

Nothing I read says what, if anything, remains on the mountain today, or whether the site is open to visitors. Anyone planning a visit should check with the Kaniksu National Forest first.
