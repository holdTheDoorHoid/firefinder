The Forest Lookouts site lists a tower at Canaan, in Gasconade County, as a Missouri Conservation Commission lookout.[^1] It gives the site's location as 41N-6W-11, a township, range and section reference.[^1]

Annual reports quoted on the site record two steps.[^1] In fiscal 1950-51 the commission obtained a five-acre towersite.[^1] In fiscal 1953-54 the commission built a tower at Canaan.[^1]

The site lists the tower as removed.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site or whether it is open to visitors today.[^1]

[^1]: Canaan - Forest Lookouts, Forest Lookouts (centraluslookouts.weebly.com), https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/canaan.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
