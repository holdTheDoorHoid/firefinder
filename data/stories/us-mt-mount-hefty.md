Mount Hefty stands in the Flathead National Forest, about 20 miles northwest of Polebridge, close to the Canadian border.[^1] Its twin summits straddle the U.S.-Canada border.[^3]

Forest Lookouts lists a cabin on the mountain from the 1930s, about a quarter mile south of the border, and marks it as abandoned.[^1] A 2016 Wildsight article, however, describes a fire lookout on top of Mt. Hefty.[^2] The two accounts disagree.[^1][^2]

A hiker's trip report says a ranger told the group that the Forest Service had stopped maintaining the northern section of trail #15 to Mt. Hefty.[^3] The hike starts at the Thoma-Colts Creek (#18) trailhead, about 2.6 miles from the junction with the Mt. Hefty Trail (#15).[^3] The 2016 article lists a round trip of 15.6 km, about three hours.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today.
