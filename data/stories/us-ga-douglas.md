A compiled list of Georgia lookout towers places a tower called "douglas" in Douglas County, at about 33.70 degrees north and 84.77 degrees west.[^1] The list gives it the number 403 with the prefix "gfc", and says those numbers come from an older GFC list, which described the locations of 203 towers.[^1] Its compiler also says that names for towers numbered 73 and above are the compiler's own, so "douglas" is a label rather than a name recorded for the tower.[^1]

The same list has a second tower called "douglas", in Coffee County, which is a different site.[^1][^1]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether anything remains there or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers (GA tower list with coordinates, elevation and GFC number), tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
