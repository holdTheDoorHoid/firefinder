Statenville is a lookout tower site in Echols County, Georgia. The tnlandforms list of Georgia towers places it at 30.70812, -83.02952, and the map was last revised in March 2016.[^1] The list notes that the Georgia Forestry Commission provided coordinates for 118 towers under BLL numbers, and that an older commission list described the locations of 203 towers under gfc numbers.[^1] The Statenville entry carries both numbers: BLL-65193 and gfc-829.[^1]

The most recent dated item is a Wikimedia Commons photograph titled "Georgia Forestry Commission Tower, Statenville," taken on 4 September 2017.[^2] The uploader's description places the picture in Statenville, Echols County, Georgia, and the file is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.[^2] Neither source gives the site's current condition.[^1][^2]
