The Dorset Scenic Lookout Tower stands off Highway 35, just north of the village of Dorset, in the Township of Algonquin Highlands, Ontario.[^1][^2] The township owns and operates the site.[^1]

The original Dorset fire tower was built in 1922.[^1][^2] It was 25 m (82 ft) high and was used as a fire lookout until 1961.[^1][^2] A sign at the lookout and picnic site shares some of the history of the Dorset Fire Tower, including the original tower's installation in 1922 and its retirement in 1961.[^3]

The tower standing today is 30 m (100 ft) high.[^1][^2] The township says the current tower dates from 1967 and was put up so visitors could enjoy the view.[^1][^2]

Visitors can climb to an observation deck that sits 142 m (465 ft) above Lake of Bays, and can also take in views from a tree-top lookout and ground-level viewpoints.[^1] The township says visitors use the tower at their own risk, and it does not permit high-risk use of the tower or the mountain.[^1] Dogs on a lead may go on the park grounds and the trail, but not up the tower.[^1]

More than 10,000 vehicles pass through the gates each year, and in peak autumn a day with more than 600 vehicles is not out of the ordinary.[^1] Fall-colours viewing needs a booking between 19 September and 25 October, and no booking is needed at other times.[^1] The park also offers a picnic area, toilets, an information kiosk, a gift shop and a hiking trail.[^1]
