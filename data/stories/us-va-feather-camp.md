The Feather Camp Lookout Site marks a fire tower that the U.S. Forest Service built in Washington County, Virginia, inside the Jefferson National Forest.[^1] The register dates the tower to 1929 and describes a 40-foot steel tower with a wooden cab measuring 14 by 14 feet.[^1] The site sits at 3,777 feet (1,151 m).[^1]

A 1934 trail guide, quoted by the Virginia's Lost AT archive, mentions the fire tower on a route near the crest.[^2] The same archive holds a black-and-white photograph of the tower dated 1955.[^2]

The register says the tower was later placed in the custody of Virginia Forestry for safekeeping. It was then vandalized in the late 1980s.[^1] After that, the state gave it back, and the Forest Service took it away.[^1] The Forest Lookouts list marks the tower as removed.[^3] The register listed the site on April 10, 2011.[^1]

Today, no source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the public may visit it.
