The Muirkirk lookout stood in Prince George's County.[^2] In July 1925 state forestry officials were looking for a site for a fire watch tower there, after fires had damaged valuable timber, and a newspaper reported that the tower would be 60 feet high and built near the Baltimore-Washington railroad lines.[^1] The steel tower went up in 1926, 60 feet tall, and was in service that year.[^1]

In late 1929 a combined workshop and garage was built at the tower.[^1] During 1930 the forest guard stationed there spent his time, between fire calls, repairing fire tools, preparing signs and building tool boxes for the forest wardens.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association now lists the Muirkirk tower as gone.[^2] A Maryland DNR feature story about the state's fire tower poster describes a Burtonsville fire tower, built in 1926, that was later moved to a site on Columbia Pike (US Route 29) and demolished in 2014.[^3] The sources I could read do not say whether that is the same structure as the Muirkirk tower. No source I read describes the Muirkirk site today.
