The Yellow Butte lookout stood on the highest point of its butte in Douglas County, Oregon.[^1][^3] The Douglas Forest Protective Association administered it.[^2][^3] A 1949 report puts it eight miles southwest of Yoncalla, while FireLookout.com gives nine miles southwest of Drain.[^1][^4]

Accounts of the first tower disagree. A 1949 newspaper report, quoted on the Oregon Lookouts page, says the Civilian Conservation Corps built it in 1932.[^1] A 1934 photo caption shows CCC crews erecting the tower.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites record says a 60-foot wooden tower went up on the site in 1936, and that a 50-foot treated timber tower replaced it in 1943.[^2] FireLookout.com gives the same 1936 and 1943 dates.[^4]

People staffed the station from at least 1935, when a Roseburg paper reported a couple who had been at the lookout all summer.[^1] Newspaper notes from 1937, 1944 and 1947 mention lookouts and a warden at the station.[^1] FireLookout.com says it was staffed into the late 2000s.[^4]

By 1949 the 50-foot pole tower had been judged unsafe.[^1] A new pressure-treated tower, three feet taller than the old one, was raised beneath the same lookout house, and the house itself had to be raised about five feet.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register says the lookout was removed about 2016.[^3] The Former Fire Lookout Sites record says the 1943 tower was removed about 2016.[^2] Oregon Lookouts and FireLookout.com say the tower came down in 2015 and a camera detection system took its place.[^1][^4] The sources disagree on the year, and none says what became of the house.

Today the sources describe a camera at the site, not a tower.[^1][^4] None of them describes what remains on the ground, and none describes public access.[^2][^3] Anyone curious about the site should ask the Douglas Forest Protective Association before going there.
