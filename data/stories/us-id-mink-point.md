Mink Point is a crow's nest in the Nez Perce National Forest, about eight miles southwest of the Moose Creek ranger station in Idaho County.[^1][^2] The site sits at 7,054 feet, according to firelookout.com.[^1]

The crow's nest dates to 1926, and it was abandoned around 1950.[^1][^3][^4] A 2010 Forest Service list, compiled from Ray Kresek's Fire Lookouts of the Northwest and other sources, gives the same 1926 start and marks the 1950 date as approximate.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list classes the site as abandoned, not gone.[^5] Idaho Fire Lookouts files it in its Selway region and describes it as a hike-in site, also abandoned.[^2]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
