Onion Lake is a fire tower site in Saskatchewan [^1]. The Firetowers of Saskatchewan listing places it at 53 degrees 46 minutes 17.6 seconds north and 109 degrees 41 minutes 37.2 seconds west [^1].

Canada Lookouts reports that a new tower joined the fire-detection network at Onion Lake in 1952, in what the source calls the Meadow Lake District [^2]. It describes the tower as equipped with a wind charger, a radio and a cabin, and cites the Department of Natural Resources annual report for 1952-53 [^2].

No source I could read describes what, if anything, stands at the Onion Lake site today, or whether the public can reach it. No source I read says whether this tower sits on First Nation land or what rules would apply to a visit, so check that before going anywhere near it.

[^1]: Firetowers of Saskatchewan, websiteservice.ca, https://www.websiteservice.ca/firetowers_page4.htm (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Onion Lake, Canada Lookouts, https://canada-lookouts.weebly.com/onion-lake.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
