Windy Point is a lookout site listed under the Oregon Department of Forestry, and the Forest Lookouts page gives its location as 1N-6W-15.[^1]

Two other pages describe a Washington County site run by the same agency under the name Woods Point.[^3][^2] Both describe it as a camp.[^3][^2] The register dates a camp there to 1950 and says the camp was no longer there by 1953. firelookout.com gives the same years.[^3][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Woods Point camp as abandoned.[^4]

The two names may refer to one site, but the sources do not confirm it. The register's coordinates for Woods Point are about 9 km north-northwest of the point this record uses for Windy Point.[^3]

Today no source describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it.
