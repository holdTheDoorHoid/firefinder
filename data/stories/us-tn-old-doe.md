Old Doe was a lookout tower in Johnson County, Tennessee.[^1] A Tennessee lookout inventory lists it at 3,694 feet and marks it as removed.[^1] The same inventory says its research into the state's lookout history is still under way.[^1]

Clio says Johnson County once had three lookout towers, and that Kettlefoot is the only one still left there.[^2] The inventory lists Kettlefoot separately, at 3,889 feet and as standing.[^1]

The inventory also reports that many towers have come down, and that most tower sites in the state are now privately owned.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the Old Doe site today, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Tennessee lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Kettlefoot Fire Lookout Tower at Doe Mountain, Clio, https://theclio.com/entry/96121 (accessed 2026-10-21).
