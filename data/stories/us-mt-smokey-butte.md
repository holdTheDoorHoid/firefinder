Smokey Butte lies about 10 miles southeast of Libby, in the Kootenai National Forest and Lincoln County, Montana.[^1] The Montana list puts it at 48.29636 north and 115.40450 west.[^2]

The firelookout.com page lists a tent cabin with a crow's nest for 1935, and says the lookout was abandoned in 1942.[^1] It gives no reason for the abandonment.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list classes it as a crow's nest and marks it abandoned, without a year.[^2]

Neither source says what happened to the structure after it was abandoned, or whether it was taken down.[^1][^2]

Today, no source we read describes what remains at the site, and none says whether the public may visit it.[^1] The sources we found describe the site in only a few lines.[^1]

[^1]: Smokey Butte Fire Lookout Tower, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/smokeybutte.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Montana Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
