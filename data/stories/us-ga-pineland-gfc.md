A tower is listed as "pineland GFC" in Echols County, Georgia, on a list of Georgia lookout towers kept by the tnlandforms.us site.[^1] The list puts it at 30.67070, -82.75757 and tags it BLL-65194.[^1] It gives an elevation of 136, with no unit; read as feet, that is 41.5 m.[^1] The list also says names for towers numbered 73 and above are its author's own creation, so "pineland GFC" may not be a historical name.[^1]

A second Echols County entry, "pineland", lies about 4.8 km (3 miles) to the north-north-west by the listed coordinates, with the tag gfc-828.[^1] Its elevation figure is 141, which read as feet is 43 m.[^1] The sources do not say whether the two entries are separate towers or one.[^1]

The list's page notes that its topographic maps, old and new, were georeferenced.[^1] The list's map was last revised on 22 March 2016.[^1]

No source describes this site today. Whether a tower, footings or nothing remains, and whether the ground is open to visitors, is not recorded in the sources I found.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
