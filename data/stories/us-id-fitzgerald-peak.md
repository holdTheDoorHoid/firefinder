Fitzgerald Peak lies about 14 miles east-northeast of St. Maries, in Shoshone County, Idaho, on the St. Joe National Forest. Its elevation is 4,490 feet.[^1][^2]

A camp is listed on the peak in 1934, and an L-4 tower in 1938.[^1][^2] The tower was destroyed in 1952.[^1][^2] Both listings give the same three dates.[^1][^2]

The Idaho Fire Lookouts page carries a "Drive-Up" tag beside its "Destroyed" label, but it gives no detail on what the site looks like now.[^2] No source I could find describes what, if anything, remains on the peak today, or whether the site is open to visitors. Anyone planning a trip should check with the St. Joe National Forest before going.
