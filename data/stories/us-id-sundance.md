Sundance Mountain, four miles east-northeast of Coolin in Bonner County, Idaho, has been a lookout site since 1928.[^2] The first setup was a tent camp with an alidade on the rock pile, and Idaho Fire Lookouts places a camp 1/4 mile to the west.[^1][^3] Seven years later an L-4 hip-roofed cab went up on a 20-foot tower in 1935.[^1][^2] The register calls that tower a log tower, while firelookout.com calls it a pole tower.[^1][^2]

The register says the 1935 cab survived the firestorm of the Sundance fire in 1967. In 1981 the Idaho Department of Lands put up an R-6 flattop cab with a catwalk in its place.[^1] The sources disagree on the new tower's height. The register gives 20 feet, and firelookout.com gives 15.[^1][^2]

The register says the lookout is now staffed through a volunteer program.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association says it is staffed annually by volunteers, and Idaho Fire Lookouts says volunteers staff it during fire season.[^4][^3] Firelookout.com says it is staffed in the summer.[^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts names known staff going back to the 1930s, including Mr. Frank Cummins, Mr. Ryder Chronic in 1932, and Nancy Glazier in 1979.[^3]

The 1935 cab was replaced in 1981, and all four sites date the present tower to that year.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the lookout as Drive-Up.[^3] The Idaho Department of Lands administers it.[^1][^4] The sources do not say whether visitors may enter the cab, and none of them mentions a rental.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The register added the lookout on April 24, 2009.[^1]
