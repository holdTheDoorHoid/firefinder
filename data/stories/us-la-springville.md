The Springville Lookout Tower stood in Livingston Parish, Louisiana, near the community of Springville.[^1][^2]

A Lake Charles American-Press account dated September 5, 1974, which the Central US Lookouts page quotes, says a telephone pole fell and shorted out a line, and that this set the tower on fire.[^2] The towerman on duty, Colyear Zeigler, got down the escape unhurt.[^2] The fire also consumed the tower's instruments and a two-way radio.[^2]

That account calls the tower destroyed.[^2] Yet a tower stood at the site until March 2018, and the sources we read do not say whether it was rebuilt after the fire.[^1][^3]

The Wikipedia list says the tower had been long retired and was removed in March 2018. It adds that Livingston Parish Fire Protection District #2 posted a video of the tower coming down.[^1]

A WBRZ report dated Monday, March 12, 2018, says the fire district reported that the retired tower was torn down that day. The report adds that the tower had been retired for many years.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left at the site, or whether the public can get to it.[^1][^2][^3]
