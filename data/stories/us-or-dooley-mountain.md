The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says a lookout cab was built on Dooley Mountain in 1936. It was an L-4 cab. The register dates its abandonment to 1947 and lists the cab as gone.[^1] firelookout.com gives the same 1936 and 1947 dates.[^2] Both place the site in the Wallowa-Whitman National Forest, and firelookout.com puts it about 14 miles south of Baker City.[^1][^2]

The record gets murky after 1947. A Forest Lookouts page quotes a July 1958 Baker Record-Courier item that found a couple and their infant son on duty at the Dooley Mountain lookout for the summer.[^3] That is eleven years after the 1947 abandonment date.[^1][^3] Either the 1947 date is wrong or the lookout was used again, and the sources do not settle which. firelookout.com also says the site may not have actually been used.[^2]

A road history of the summit mentions a radar station that has since been dismantled. It says the old road crossed the summit a short distance west of that station.[^4] The history does not say whether the radar station stood near the lookout site.[^4]

We found no source that describes the lookout site today. The register and firelookout.com both list it as gone, but neither describes what remains or whether the summit is open to visitors.[^1][^2]
