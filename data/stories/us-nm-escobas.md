Escobas is a former lookout site in San Miguel County, New Mexico, at 35.50236, -105.70277.[^1] The association's New Mexico table mixes lookout sites with non-lookout structures. Its entry gives the type as a map board, with a parenthetical word, cabin, that the page does not explain, and marks the site as gone.[^1] The association's New Mexico list also gives a Rowe Peak map board at exactly the same coordinates.[^2] The pages do not say whether the two entries are one site under two names.

No source I checked describes what remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to the public. Anyone planning a visit should check with the land manager first.

[^1]: New Mexico Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Return to New Mexico Lookouts (Alphabetical List), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/nm/nm-co/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
