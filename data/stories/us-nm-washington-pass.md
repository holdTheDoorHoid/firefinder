Washington Pass Lookout stands in the Chuska Mountains on the Navajo Reservation in San Juan County, New Mexico, at 9,179 feet.[^1] Among the stations in the reservation's New Mexico section, the register lists it as the highest in elevation.[^1]

The register dates the tower's construction and entry into service to 1936. It is an LS-40 Aermotor, 81 feet tall, with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab.[^1] It lists the Navajo Indian Reservation as administrator and the Bureau of Indian Affairs as a cooperator.[^1]

The register recorded the lookout on July 20, 2002, under the number US 435, NM 10.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a standing tower under that number. Its coordinates sit about a kilometer from the register's figures.[^2][^1]

The sources I reached do not describe staffing, fires, or any later changes to the tower, and I could not find a fuller history.

Because it sits on tribal land, please check with the Navajo Nation before planning a trip. I found no current visiting information.[^1]
