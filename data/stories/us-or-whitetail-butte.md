Whitetail Butte lies in the Umatilla National Forest, about 15 miles northwest of Monument in Wheeler County, Oregon.[^1][^2] The listing gives its elevation as 4,588 feet.[^1]

The sources describe a crow's nest on the butte. The firelookout.com listing gives the year 1930 beside its crow's nest entry and marks the site abandoned.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association index also lists the site as a crow's nest and abandoned.[^3]

In January 1931 the Heppner Gazette-Times reported that a telephone line had been rebuilt between Bull Prairie and Whitetail Butte, to be used in emergencies.[^2] Later that year the same paper reported that the old, abandoned lookout had been rebuilt.[^2]

Both listings still describe the site as abandoned.[^1][^3] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. None says whether the site is open to the public, so do not assume you may visit.
