Taylor Hill is a former lookout site in Washington County, Maine, at latitude 45.13417 and longitude -67.49502.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists its type as Ground rather than Tower, and its status as gone.[^1]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the hill today, or whether the public can reach it.

[^1]: Maine Fire Towers (by County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/me/me-co/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
