The Energy Lookout Tower stands in Clarke County, Mississippi, at about 526 feet of elevation.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as a 100-foot Aermotor MC-39 tower.[^1] The register says the Mississippi Forestry Commission administers the tower.[^1]

The register gives no build date, lists the year as unknown, and says nothing about who built the tower or who staffed it.[^1] I found no other record of either. The register does not mention any fire the tower was used to watch.[^1]

The tower was registered on July 4, 2022.[^1] The register's only condition note says the tower was in good condition as of July 2, 2022.[^1] No later report on its condition was found, so its state today is not confirmed.

The register does not describe public access, so any visiting rules or present use are unknown.[^1] This is a thin record built on one source.[^1] It is best read as a registered structure with an undocumented history.

[^1]: Energy Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ms/energy-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
