Cache Creek is a wildland area about one mile from Jackson's Town Square.[^4] The Forest Fire Lookout Association places its lookout site in Teton County and lists it as gone, with the structure type unknown.[^1]

The Forest Service's Jackson District once operated a sizable network of fire lookouts. Cache Creek was one of its sites, alongside Munger Mountain, Deer Creek and Sheep Mountain.[^2] In the mid-1970s the agency judged some lookouts to be potential liability issues and fire hazards, and it torched the Munger Mountain and Deer Creek lookouts.[^2] The agency's historic context statement says the Jackson District has no historic lookouts.[^2]

A Buckrail article, drawing on National Park Service material, says the Bridger-Teton gave up all its lookout towers after the early 1970s, when airplane patrols became favored and more frequent.[^3] That article covers the whole forest, not this lookout in particular.

No source we read describes what remains at the Cache Creek lookout site today. Jackson Hole Magazine describes the canyon as a place locals bike and hike, but it does not mention the lookout, so it cannot say whether the site is open to visitors.[^4]
