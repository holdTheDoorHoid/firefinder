Mercer is a lookout tower in Liberty County, Florida, in the Apalachicola National Forest.[^1] The only dated record found is a photo caption from November 1935 on the Eastern US Lookouts site.[^1] The caption says the Civilian Conservation Corps built a 100-foot experimental tower with a cabin, using red cypress heartwood.[^1]

The photo is dated 1935, so the tower was standing by then.[^1] The page gives no build date beyond that.[^1]

A map database lists a Mercer Fire Tower in Liberty, Florida, marked as a tower with an elevation of 105 feet (32 metres).[^2] A map listing is not a site report, so it does not show whether a structure stands there now.[^2]

No source describes the site today, including whether the public may visit. Treat the location as unknown, and do not try to climb any tower you find there.
