The Bear Pete lookout was an L-4 cabin in the Payette National Forest, about 15 miles west of Warren.[^1][^3] It carries the same name as Bear Pete Mountain, which rises to about 8,750 feet.[^2][^4]

A climbing guide says the mountain takes its name from Peter "Bear Pete" Brockenau, who earned the nickname from his many bear stories.[^4]

The sources date the cab to around 1930.[^1][^2][^3] The lookout was destroyed in 1966.[^1][^2][^3]

Most sources place the site in the Payette National Forest, but Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the Challis National Forest instead.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

A University of Idaho photograph titled Bear Pete Lookout is dated September 12, 1950.[^4][^5]

Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the site as a hike-in.[^2] The climbing guide says the summit is reached by trail.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the cabin today.[^1][^2][^3]
