Bruin Hill is in the Clearwater National Forest, in Clearwater County, at about 6,504 feet.[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives the same elevation and forest.[^2] It lies about 17 miles east-northeast of the Kelly Creek Ranger Station, in Section 35, Township 40 North, Range 12 East.[^1][^3] A cupola cabin went up there in 1932.[^1][^2] The lookout also had a patrol point on Fern Mountain, about two miles to the west-southwest.[^1][^2] A photo on the firelookout.com page is credited to Harold Coffman.[^1]

The cabin was gone by 1959, according to both the firelookout.com page and the Forest Service's 2016 list of Clearwater lookouts.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
