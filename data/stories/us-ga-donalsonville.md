The lookout listed here as donalsonville is most likely the Seminole County Fire Tower on the National Historic Lookout Register.[^1][^3] The register credits the Georgia Forestry Commission with completing it in 1952, and says it is a later-generation Aermotor design, 100 feet tall, with a metal cab measuring 7 by 7 feet.[^1]

A September 1952 issue of the state forestry magazine reported that a commission forest engineer had inspected Seminole County's terrain to choose lookout sites, and that towers would go up as soon as steel was available.[^2] It also said the state furnished the towers.[^2]

The register says the tower was last staffed in 1988 and remains operational for emergencies. It does not date that second statement.[^1] Its register entry is dated January 2, 2008.[^1]

We found no dated source on the tower's current condition. A Georgia tower list has a "donalsonville" entry in Seminole County, about 3 km from the register's coordinates, and marks it "nhlr?", which suggests the compiler was not sure it matched the register entry.[^3][^1] The list gives its elevation as 171, with no unit, against the register's 142 feet, so the two entries may be different towers.[^3][^1]
