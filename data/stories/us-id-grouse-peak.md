Grouse Peak sits about 8 miles northeast of Challis in Custer County, Idaho, at roughly 8,464 feet, on the Challis National Forest.[^1][^2]

Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both describe an L-4 cab from the 1930s.[^1][^2] Forest Lookouts gives the 1930s structure as a camp and credits the detail to Kresek, so the sources do not agree on what was built.[^3]

Both firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts describe the site as abandoned and gone.[^1][^2] Neither gives a year for its removal.[^1][^2]

A 2017 Forest Service decision, signed 28 August, records that a communication facility at a site called Grouse Peak South, about 8 miles east-northeast of Challis, had changed hands.[^4] That notice does not mention the old lookout, so the sources read do not show whether the radio site occupies the same summit.[^4] Idaho Fire Lookouts tags the lookout as drive-up, but its page says nothing about the road or whether visitors can reach the summit.[^2]
