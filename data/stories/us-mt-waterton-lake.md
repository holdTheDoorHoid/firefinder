Waterton Lake was a lookout inside Glacier National Park in Glacier County, Montana.[^1][^2] It stood about half a mile northeast of the lake's south end, on the slope of Goat Haunt Mountain, above the lake.[^1][^3]

The sources disagree about what was built there. Firelookout.com describes a log cabin from the 1930s, abandoned in the 1940s.[^1] WillhiteWeb says early maps use names that run from lookout tower to cabin, and that 1935 panoramic photos seem to show no lookout structure, perhaps only a table.[^3]

WillhiteWeb says the site was abandoned in the 1940s.[^3] The Porcupine Ridge Lookout, a newer lookout put up in 1939, could see most of the same park country.[^3]

Today the National Park Service describes a one-mile hike that climbs 800 feet to Goat Haunt Overlook.[^4] The trail, the Park Service says, ends at a former lookout site with a view of the Waterton Valley.[^4] WillhiteWeb says current maps show the Goat Haunt site as a campsite.[^3] No source we read describes any remains of the lookout itself.
