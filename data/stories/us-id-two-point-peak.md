Two Point Peak is a summit in Valley County, Idaho.[^2] It lies on the boundary between the Payette and Salmon national forests.[^3]

Bing Young's 1982 history of Salmon forest lookouts describes it as an old lookout, listed in the fire plans of 1919 to 1923.[^3] It adds that the site never had a lookout building.[^3]

A 1924 Salmon forest report places two men at the point.[^1][^2] In August 1928 lightning hit the lookout, melting the alidade and smashing the firefinder's glass.[^1][^2] The post the firefinder stood on was shattered as well.[^1] The lookout that year, Norman Howell, was in his tent a few yards away.[^1]

The records disagree about when the site ended. A 1932 record calls that year the final one in which the site served as a lookout.[^1][^2] Bing Young's history puts the end in the late 1920s, because Shellrock Peak gave a clearer view of the Payette forest side.[^3]

Today the climbing guide places the peak within the Frank Church River of No Return Wilderness.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site now.
