Red Top is a rounded hill in the lower foothills of eastern Madera County, in oak woodland.[^1] In June 1928 the county set about building a lookout station on the summit.[^2] Local property owners offered to do the building work under the supervision of State Forest Ranger J.E. Wofford.[^2] The Madera Sugar Pine company had contributed the lumber, and the Dean Bros. planning mill was to supply glass for all four sides.[^2] The site was on the Billy Brown ranch, and Mr. Brown had promised a deed to the land.[^2]

The dates disagree. The register gives 1934 as the build year, but its own photo caption reads "Construction 1928."[^1] Fire-lookouts.org says the station was constructed "in or about 1938."[^3] The 1928 newspaper reports describe the building work, so 1928 is the best-supported year.[^2]

In May 1929 Madera County and the State agreed to keep a lookout on Red Top Mountain, with the State covering costs above $1,500.[^2] Clarence Reed worked the lookout in the early 1930s.[^2] A 1933 paper said he had finished his season there, and in 1934 it called him the county's lookout on Red Top.[^2] That August a rattlesnake got into the living quarters, and it took him some three hours to find it.[^2] In November 1942 the state forestry division was preparing to build a road to the station, to make it more accessible to automobiles.[^2]

The register describes the tower as a CCC-era, two-story timber tower with a matching BC-301 cab.[^1] Fire-lookouts.org describes a three-story building, with the living quarters in its lower stories.[^3] The register also says the station was severely vandalized.[^1]

Fire-lookouts.org says the lookout ended its use in 1985, when the tower was turned over to the property owner.[^3] The same page says the owner tore it down in 2010.[^3] The register says it was scheduled for dismantling in 2010 but "is still standing as of 2018."[^1] I could not settle which account is right.

The site is on private land.[^3] Fire-lookouts.org says there is no public access, and the register says public access is supposed to be restricted.[^1][^3] Do not go up to it without the owner's permission. The register lists its registration date as December 23, 2019.[^1]
