Marchand was a fire lookout in Perry County, in the Lafayette district of the Hoosier National Forest.[^2] The Forest Service now calls that district Tell City.[^1] A Forest Service list records a plan approved on January 28, 1938, for an 80-foot steel tower with a dwelling, garage, latrine and cistern.[^1] The entry records approval of a plan, not a finished tower.[^1]

A 1958 Derby topographic map shows the tower just east of old Indiana 37.[^3] A 1997 trail map reproduced on the Indiana Fire Towers site also shows a tower near the Mogan Ridge trails.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's list marks Marchand as gone.[^4] The Indiana Fire Towers site says the structure is not standing. A communications tower now occupies the site, and one of four old footers is still visible beside its fence.[^3]

The site is public property just off old Indiana 37, according to the site's author, who visited in June 2024. One old footer sits just outside the fence.[^3] No lookout tower stands there now.[^3]
