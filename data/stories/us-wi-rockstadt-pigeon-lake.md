Rockstadt, also listed as Pigeon Lake, was a fire lookout in Bayfield County, Wisconsin, at about 46.325 N, 91.332 W.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list marks its tower as gone.[^1]

A July 1941 photograph in the Wisconsin Historical Society's Neighborhood House records is captioned as a fire tower at the Pigeon Lake Boys' Camp, a summer camp backed by the Work Projects Administration's recreation program.[^2] The caption does not say where that Pigeon Lake is, so the photo may or may not show this tower.[^2]

Statewide, 72 towers still in service were taken out of use in 2015.[^3] An NHLR entry for the Camp Douglas lookout gives 2015 as the year the state's network ended.[^4] At its height the network had 119 towers.[^3] Wikipedia gives 2016 as the year the state chose to end operations at its final 72 active towers, so the sources differ on the year.[^5]

No source we found describes what, if anything, remains at the Rockstadt site today, or whether the public may visit it.
