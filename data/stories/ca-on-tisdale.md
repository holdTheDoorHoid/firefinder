Firelookout.ca's record for the Tisdale Fire Tower gives its location as Timmins, Ontario, and says it apparently still stands.[^1] A still-standing list on the Ontario's Fire Tower Lookouts site calls it the Tisdale Tower at Schumacher.[^2] The two place names may refer to the same site, though no source connects them.

That list uses an asterisk for towers whose cupola was removed and which now serve as communications towers, and the Tisdale entry carries that asterisk.[^2] If both accounts describe the same tower, it appears to still stand with its cupola removed, and it is now used as a communications tower.[^1][^2]

The list also says that, as of 2013, the Ontario MNR has decided to remove many of these towers. It adds that the MNR has stopped maintaining them and asks visitors not to climb them.[^2]

[^1]: Tisdale Fire Tower, Firelookout.ca (Open Canada Fire Lookout Index), https://firelookout.ca/lookouts/?slug=tisdale-fire-tower (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Ontario Fire Towers Still Standing, Ontario's Fire Tower Lookouts, https://forestrangers.github.io/ontariofiretowers/stillstanding2.html (accessed 2026-10-20).
