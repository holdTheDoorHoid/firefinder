The Millington Lookout Tower is in Shelby County, Tennessee, and the National Historic Lookout Register lists it as US 1763, TN 67.[^1] It is a 100-foot Aermotor MC-39 tower, but it did not begin its life in Millington.[^1] The Register says it once stood near Fordyce, Arkansas, and was moved to Millington in the late 1980s.[^1]

The Register records the tower as registered on August 10, 2023, on the nomination of Jason Johns.[^1] It names Baseball USA Stadium as the administrator.[^1] The town has its own baseball link: a historical marker there says national searches chose Millington for the 1st USA baseball training site in 1985.[^2]

The Register notes a site visit marked 1/2/23, but it says nothing about the tower's condition since then.[^1] We found no account of who staffed the tower or of any fire it watched.[^1]

We found no public access information.[^1] Because of that, anyone interested should contact the administrator, Baseball USA Stadium, before going near the tower.[^1]
