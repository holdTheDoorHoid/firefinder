The Forest Fire Lookout Association's register lists the Franklin County Rising Mountain site as a tree tower built in 1913. The register says it was still used around 1917.[^1]

Other records give earlier dates. A 1908-09 Pennsylvania Department of Forestry report, quoted on the Eastern US Lookouts history page, lists two outlooks built in May, one on Rising Mountain at a height of fifty feet.[^2] A separate register entry for Rising Mountain, filed under Perry County, gives 1909 as the build year for a tree tower.[^3] The same history page also cites a Department of Forestry report dated December 31, 1917. That report lists a 55-foot observation point on the Pennypacker Forest with 1909 as its date.[^2]

The sources disagree on the build year.[^1][^2][^3] The two register entries disagree on the county.[^1][^3] The history page is headed "Perry - Franklin County."[^2]

Both register entries say the tower is no longer standing.[^1][^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today. No source we found says whether the site is open to the public.
