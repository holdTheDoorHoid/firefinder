Garrison Hill, three miles south of Spanaway in Pierce County, once had a fire lookout on land that was Fort Lewis and is now Joint Base Lewis-McChord.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The register and FireLookout.com date a first wooden tower to 1942 and a second one to 1952.[^1][^2] A 100-foot wooden tower with an 8-by-8-foot cab replaced that one in 1957.[^1][^2] Willhite, quoting the Lookout Inventory, gives the 1942 tower and the 1957 rebuild, but not the 1952 tower.[^3] All of the sources say the 1957 tower was abandoned in 1971.[^1][^2][^3]

The site sits just inside the base boundary.[^3] Willhite found not much left but a concrete foundation, and the grass is really overgrown. His photos are captioned 2013.[^3] The site is just off 8th Avenue, a paved public road along the east edge of the base, but there is little to see from the road.[^3]

Willhite says the site is on military land and advises against trying to reach it without permission. He says permission is possible and suggests contacting the base's Adventure Center.[^4]
