Nick Wynn Mountain had an L-4 cab, built in 1940.[^1][^2] It stood on the ground rather than on a tower, in the Bitterroot National Forest, six miles north-northwest of the Magruder Ranger Station in Idaho County.[^1][^3] The firelookout.com page gives the site's elevation as 5,750 feet.[^1]

The cab was destroyed in 1955, and both pages agree on the design and dates.[^1][^2] The Idaho Fire Lookouts site labels it a hike-in site that was abandoned.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list also marks it as gone, with coordinates of 45.77278 and -114.75932.[^3]

We found no source that describes what remains on the mountain today, or whether the public may visit. Anyone planning a trip should ask the Bitterroot National Forest about access first.
