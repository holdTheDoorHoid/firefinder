The Former Fire Lookout Sites register places the Alta Mine Tower Site in the San Juan National Forest, in San Miguel County, Colorado, at 11,040 feet.[^1] It lists the site as US 529, CO 1, with a registration date of September 18, 2009.[^1]

The site shares its name with the Alta Mine.[^1] Wikipedia describes the Alta mining town, a short distance away by the coordinates both sources give, as a significant gold and silver mining place in the Colorado Silver Boom.[^1][^3] Wikipedia says the town was abandoned in 1948, when a fire engulfed the mine complex and ended active mining there.[^3]

According to the register, the tower no longer stands, and a mine tramway is still at the site.[^1] A Forest Fire Lookout Association list for Colorado gives the type as a tram house and the status as abandoned.[^4] A separate lookouts compilation lists the site as removed and places it in the Montezuma National Forest rather than the San Juan.[^2]

No source we read describes what stands at the site today beyond the tramway, and none says whether the public may visit it.[^1][^2][^4] The Alta townsite is on private land, according to Wikipedia, and is mostly surrounded by public land that the Forest Service manages.[^3] Stay off private land, and check with the Forest Service before any visit, since sources disagree on whether the site is in the San Juan or the Montezuma forest.
