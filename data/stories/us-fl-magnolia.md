The Magnolia Fire Tower stands in Orange County, Florida, and was built in 1933.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register calls it an Aermotor MC-39 tower, 100 to 120 feet tall, built for the Florida Division of Forestry.[^1] Aermotor made several lookout models, and the MC-39 was one of them.[^2] Its heights were standardized, though some were customized.[^2]

The register says ownership has passed back to adjacent landowners. It also names the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints as administrator, a role the register does not connect to ownership.[^1] We found nothing about its staffing, fires, repairs or any move, so those parts of its history are still unknown.

The register added the tower in July 2016, on a nomination by Robert Spear.[^1]

Treat it as private land, because the adjacent landowners now own it.[^1] Ask the owners before going near it.
