A lookout-tower list on tnlandforms.us includes a lookout called Hebardville, in Ware County, at 31.24858 north and 82.40262 west, with an elevation of 139 feet.[^1] The list numbers it GA180 and gives two other codes, BLL-65197 and GFC 837.[^1] A genealogy guide places Hebardville in Ware County.[^2] The list's notes say the Georgia Forestry Commission supplied coordinates for 118 towers, which carry BLL numbers.[^1]

Neither the National Historic Lookout Register nor the Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists it. The first counts 29 Georgia lookouts, and the second lists two Georgia sites.[^3][^4]

No source describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can visit it.[^1]
