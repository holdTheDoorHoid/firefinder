Scoop Mountain sits in Stevens County, about eight miles west of Deer Park.[^1][^2] A Willhite page says the mountain is best known for having held a fire lookout on its summit.[^3]

In 1935 a pole platform tower, a tent camp and a phone line were placed on the site.[^1][^3] The Washington Division of Forestry administered it.[^1] The lookout was given up in 1940, and both the register and firelookout.com list it as gone.[^1][^2]

An undated Willhite photo caption says Scoop Mountain and nearby Peak 4024 both have communication towers on top.[^3] As of 2023, a gate on a logging road off Scotts Valley Road admitted only foot traffic.[^3] Scotts Valley Road is gravel, kept in good condition, and any ordinary car can drive it.[^3] The Willhite page gives the hike as 3.5 miles one way, with 1,800 feet of gain, and mostly a road walk.[^3]
