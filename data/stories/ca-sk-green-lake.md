A Canada's Fire Lookouts entry records a steel tower put up at Green Lake, in the Meadow Lake District, in 1950, and cites a Department of Natural Resources annual report for 1950 to 1951.[^1]

The Firetowers of Saskatchewan listing places the Green Lake site at 54-15-19.9 N, 107-46-34.5 W.[^2]

No source read for this story gives a height, staffing or a later history for the tower. No source describes what, if anything, remains at the Green Lake site today, or whether visitors can reach it.

[^2]: Firetowers of Saskatchewan, Website Service (websiteservice.ca), https://www.websiteservice.ca/firetowers_page4.htm (accessed 2026-10-20).
