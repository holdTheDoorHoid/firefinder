The Smith Knob fire tower stands in Scott County, Tennessee, and the county encyclopedia places it in Oneida.[^1][^2] Tennessee Landforms lists the site at 1,654 feet and marks the tower as standing, numbered 2-20 on a 1976 state list of forest towers.[^2]

We could not find when the tower went up, who designed it, what it was used for, or who staffed it. The sources we could read do not cover those details.

The county encyclopedia says Smith Knob and the Black Creek tower are the only two towers in Scott County still run by the Tennessee Division of Forestry. The same entry adds that both remain usable when needed.[^1]

The Division of Forestry still operates the tower.[^1] We list the site as restricted rather than as a public lookout, since we found no public visiting arrangement. Tennessee Landforms advises people not to climb these towers, because steps are often removed or rotten.[^2] Please leave the tower alone and ask the Division of Forestry before going near it.

[^2]: Tennessee Lookout Towers, Tennessee Landforms, https://tnlandforms.us/towers/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
