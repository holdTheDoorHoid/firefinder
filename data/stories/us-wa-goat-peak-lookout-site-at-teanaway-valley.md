Goat Peak sits in Kittitas County, on the Wenatchee National Forest, about 12 miles west of Cle Elum.[^1][^2] The 1927 allotments included $800 for a lookout house on the peak.[^3] The first lookout was a D-6 cupola cabin, built in 1928.[^4][^1]

The only named lookout I found is Adam Ehl, listed for 1933.[^2]

The sources disagree about what replaced the cupola cabin.[^1][^4] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register says a 10-foot treated timber R-6 flat tower went up about 1960.[^1] The Washington Trails Association trip report says an L-4 cabin came in the late 1930s, and a new R-6 flat cabin on a 10-foot tower followed in 1956.[^4] Three sources date the sale to 1969 and say the lookout was moved, and two of them name the Teanaway Valley as its new home.[^1][^3][^4]

The moved lookout sits on a hill behind the Teanaway general store, and the WTA report says you can still see it from Teanaway Road.[^4] It went to a local family,[^4] and I found no source describing public access. Enjoy the view from the road, and leave the property alone.

One 2017 WTA hike started at an unsigned trailhead off Cabin Creek Road and climbed by trail to the old lookout area.[^4] The same report notes trees around the old lookout site.[^4]
