The Mile 13 fire tower appears on the websiteservice.ca list of Saskatchewan towers, at 52°58′00.3″ N, 102°37′40.5″ W.[^1] Firelookout.ca places its entry about 2 km further north, at 52.985095, -102.629752, in Hudson Bay No. 394, with an elevation of 558 m (1831 ft).[^2]

Firelookout.ca describes the tower as a 24 m (80 ft) heavy steel structure.[^2]

The websiteservice.ca towermen list has Gerry Herrod at Mile 13 from 1990 to 2002, and says he went to aerial detection.[^3] Harold Barillo is listed as relief at Mile 13, along with Lobstick and Porcupine, with no dates.[^3]

Firelookout.ca says the cupola was taken off in 2010 to clear room for a thermal detection system, and that the cabin is no longer on the site.[^2]

The entry says the tower is fenced for safety, that access to the site is unrestricted, and that the trail to it is in good shape.[^2] It is a single note from one submitter with no date, so these details are unconfirmed.[^2] Visitors should respect the fence and not climb the tower.
