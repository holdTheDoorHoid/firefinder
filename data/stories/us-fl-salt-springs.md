The Salt Springs Lookout Site lies in Marion County, Florida, off State Road 19.[^1][^2] The community of Salt Springs is located within the Ocala National Forest.[^3]

The register describes the lookout as likely less than 60 feet tall, and says the Florida Department of Forestry administered it.[^1] Robert Spear nominated the site, and the register recorded it on July 20, 2016.[^1]

Forest Lookouts reports that the tower, along with the site's other structures, has been taken down.[^2] The register lists what survives: the original entry road, remnants of an access gate, the tower's footings, and a cement pad tied to an old observer's house, among other relics.[^1] The register points to the original entry road off State Road 19 as a landmark, and says gate remnants can be found there.[^1] We found no source saying whether the site is open to visitors, so ask the administering agency before going.
