The Missouri Conservation Commission acquired 13.5 acres for a towersite in Shannon County in 1946 and 1947.[^1] The state's 1947-48 Official Manual of the State of Missouri listed that towersite as Commission-owned land, still 13.5 acres in size.[^1]

An annual report for fiscal 1956-57 says major repairs were made to the Himont tower.[^1]

In 1962 the U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey described a survey marker about 13 miles northeast of Eminence.[^2] The marker sat 59 feet southeast of a gravel road and driveway junction, which lies 50 feet west of the tower's northwest leg, and its disk was stamped HIMONT 1962.[^2]

The lookouts record lists the Himont tower as removed, without a date.[^1]

No source we read describes the tower itself today. A February 2024 entry on the NGS datasheet rates the survey marker in good condition.[^2]
