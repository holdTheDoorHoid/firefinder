The Former Fire Lookout Sites record says the Olga Coal Company built the tower it lists as Olga No. 3.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register's entry for Olga No. 2 says the company built three towers, and that No. 2 was one of them.[^2] The record for No. 3 names Alawest as the administrator and puts the site at 2,143 feet (653 metres) above sea level.[^1]

A 1986 newspaper report, quoted on the Forest Lookouts website, describes a 40-foot tower on land the Olga Coal Company owned.[^3] The observer then in charge had held the post for five years.[^3] The report calls the structure the Olga Fire Tower and gives no number, so it cannot be tied to Olga No. 3 with certainty.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's West Virginia list marks the site as gone.[^4]

No source read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the ground is open to the public.[^4]
