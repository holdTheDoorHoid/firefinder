Red Eagle Mountain is in Glacier National Park, about two miles south-southwest of Saint Mary, in Glacier County, Montana.[^1] Its lookout was a 60-foot steel tower, a late addition to the park's lookout system.[^1]

Sources disagree about when the tower reached the mountain. The firelookout.com page dates the move here from Swiftcurrent Ridge to 1959.[^1] WillhiteWeb puts the move from Swiftcurrent Ridge in 1960,[^2] and a blog about Glacier's lookouts gives 1960 as the year it was built.[^3]

Most of the park's lookouts shared one design, a two-story wooden building with a cab on top.[^3] The blog counts Red Eagle as one of two notable exceptions.[^3]

The tower was destroyed in 1986.[^2][^3] The firelookout.com page says it was gone that year,[^1] and a Montana lookout list gives its status as removed.[^4]

No source we read describes the site today, including whether anything remains on the mountain or whether visitors can reach the spot.
