Fields Fire Tower stands in Beauregard Parish, near the small community of Fields, between DeQuincy and Longville in southwest Louisiana. The parish once held a cluster of lookout towers — Dry Creek, Gordon, Longville, Merryville among them — keeping watch over this stretch of piney timber country, and Fields was one of that group.

Little about its origin has made it into the historical record so far. A list of the state's lookout towers records Fields Fire Tower as "still standing"[^1], and notes that the tower "is visible here on Google Street View"[^1] — meaning it can be seen from a public road rather than visited up close. No builder, construction date, height, design, or managing agency is documented for it, and it does not turn up in the Forest Fire Lookout Association's or National Historic Lookout Register's parish listings checked for this entry.

For now, Fields Fire Tower is best described as an undocumented survivor: a fire tower still standing on its site in Beauregard Parish, with its history yet to be written down anywhere easy to find. Anyone with local knowledge or Forest Service records of it would be filling a real gap.

[^1]: List of fire lookout towers in Louisiana, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_fire_lookout_towers_in_Louisiana (accessed 2026-10-04).
