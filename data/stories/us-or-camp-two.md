Camp Two was a lookout on the Rogue River National Forest, about 26 miles northeast of Medford in Jackson County, Oregon.[^3][^1] The Oregon Forest Lookouts site calls it an emergency lookout.[^2]

Sources differ on when the tower went up. A forest history quoted by the Oregon Forest Lookouts site dates a 40-foot round timber tower with a 10-by-10 house to 1935, at a point near Camp Two that served as an emergency lookout. The entry says the tower would give coverage over cutover ground along Fourbit and Willow Creeks.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register and firelookout.com both date it to 1936, and both place it at the Owen-Oregon CCC camp site as a 40-foot pole L-5 tower.[^1][^3]

A 1941 entry in the same history says five men were put to maintenance work at the lookout near their camp, and that $100 was set aside for repairs.[^2]

According to the register the tower was later removed, while firelookout.com says it was later destroyed.[^1][^3] The Forest Fire Lookout Association list marks the site as gone.[^4] None of the sources read for this story describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether visitors can reach it.
