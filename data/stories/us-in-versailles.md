The Versailles fire tower stood in what is now Versailles State Park in Ripley County, on land the National Park Service had acquired in the 1930s before the state took it over in 1943.[^1] In the 1930s the Park Service also hired the Civilian Conservation Corps to turn the land into a recreation demonstration area.[^2]

A 1936 county map shows no fire tower, while a 1946 topographic map of the area does, which suggests the tower went up in between.[^1] A state tower list reproduced by the Forest Service includes a Versailles tower.[^3]

The tower's later history is thin. An Outdoor Indiana article from October 1975 has a map that shows a lookout tower, but a September 1980 Outdoor Indiana map of the area does not.[^1] A 1978 table of towers did not list Versailles, and the Indiana fire tower history suggests it may have been taken down by then.[^4] A Forest Fire Lookout Association database lists the tower as gone.[^6]

Today the state park's trail guide says one trail can be reached from the Old Fire Tower Road.[^5] The guide's author visited in 2023, found that road gated, and walked in on a side trail from Trail 1.[^1] Concrete debris sat in undergrowth just north of the road's loop. It might have come from the base, but the author could not tell whether it marked the original site.[^1] No source describes the site in more detail, and the spot sits inside a state park that the state promotes as a destination for visitors.[^5]
