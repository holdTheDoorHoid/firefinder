The Katka Peak lookout stood in Boundary County, Idaho, about eight miles southeast of Bonners Ferry, in the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^3]

It went up in 1932 as a 10-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2] A 1962 Sandpoint News Bulletin story, reprinted on the Forest Lookouts site, says the Civilian Conservation Corps built this lookout and five others in the 1930s.[^4]

Clarence Kauffmann is the name listed for 1933.[^1][^2] Idaho Fire Lookouts also lists George Koppang, citing a Bonners Ferry Herald item dated August 26, 1943.[^2]

According to the same 1962 story, the Forest Service had not used or maintained the towers since 1950, when it turned to aerial patrol to add to its ground watch.[^4] Over the previous dozen years, weather and rot had made the towers risky to climb.[^4] The article reports that the towers were being toppled and burned by Eugene H. Napier, a district fire control officer, and Arlen Rose, a packer from Priest River.[^4] Both guides date the destruction to 1962.[^1][^2]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as a hike-in and describes no remains, so we cannot say whether anything of the lookout or its footings is still on the peak.[^2]
