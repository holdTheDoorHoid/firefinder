Day Hill sits in the town of Wesley, in Washington County, Maine.[^1] A 1918 Maine Forest Service map calls the same tower the Wesley Hill fire tower, with Wesley Mt. or Day Hill as the other names.[^1] The Forest Lookouts history for Wesley Mountain appears to describe the same site.[^1][^2]

That history lists it as a lookout station in the 1910 report of the forest commissioner, and a 1920 inventory records a 55-foot wood tower.[^2] We did not find who built the first tower, or when the current structure went up.

Maine ended its fire tower network after a budget crisis in 1991, and some towers across the state were later dismantled or collapsed.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing, using the asterisk its key reserves for a structure whose cab is no longer in place.[^3] We found no staffing history, closure notice or visit notes for this site. Before any visit, check who owns the land and whether the tower is open.
