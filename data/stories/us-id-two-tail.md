Two Tail was a crow's nest on Two Tail Peak, in Boundary County, in the Kaniksu National Forest.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives its elevation as 4,032 feet.[^1] Forest Lookouts lists the code 61N-2E-3.[^3]

Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts both date the crow's nest to the 1930s.[^2][^1] Both record it as abandoned.[^2][^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as gone.[^4]

Idaho Fire Lookouts describes the site as a hike-in.[^1] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains of the crow's nest, and none says whether the site is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
