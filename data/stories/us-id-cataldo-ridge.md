Cataldo Ridge is in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest, at an elevation the Idaho Fire Lookouts site gives as roughly 3,700 feet.[^1] The site is listed as a hike-in.[^1]

The only date the sources give is the 1930s, when the site was a camp with a firefinder on it.[^1] The same site's regional listing marks it as an abandoned camp.[^2] Both listings describe a camp rather than a tower.[^1][^2]

Neither cited page describes what remains at the site today or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Cataldo Ridge, Idaho Fire Lookouts, https://www.idahofirelookouts.com/cda-region/cataldo-ridge/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Coeur d'Alene, Idaho Fire Lookouts regional listing, https://www.idahofirelookouts.com/category/cda-region/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
