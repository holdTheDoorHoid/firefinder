A lookout on Blacktail Mountain, 10 miles south of Sandpoint in Bonner County, Idaho, began as a camp in 1926.[^1][^2] A mine was located on the site years before the tower.[^2] A 10-by-10-foot cabin went up a half mile east in 1927.[^1]

In 1936 a 40-foot L-4 tower was built atop Blacktail Mountain, one mile south of Butler Mountain, in the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.[^1][^2]

In June 1931 a young lookout named Butler was killed by lightning inside the lookout house during a thunderstorm.[^2][^3] Reports give his first name as James, Ben or Glen, and his age as either about 19 or 23.[^3] One report describes a Sunday evening strike, while the Idaho Fire Lookouts page dates it to June 17, 1931.[^2][^3]

The tower was destroyed in 1947.[^1][^2]

Today the Idaho Fire Lookouts page describes cement footings on Butler Mountain, with grounding cables and wire, and ruins on Blacktail Mountain that look like a cabin.[^2] The page questions whether the Butler Mountain footings belong to the 1936 tower.[^2] It calls the site hike-in.[^2] No source I found says whether the land is open to visitors.
