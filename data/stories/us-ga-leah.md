A Georgia lookout tower list revised in March 2016 names a tower "leah" in Columbia County, at 33.65657, -82.33706 and an elevation of 500 with no unit given (read here as feet, about 152 m).[^1] The list says its towers were discovered on USGS topo maps and NGS data sheets, and that names for towers 73 and above are the maker's own creation, so "leah" is a label rather than a historic name.[^1] The list gives the tower's Georgia Forestry Commission number as 022, and says an older GFC list gave locations for 203 towers.[^1] Because the list was last revised in 2016, it may not show the site as it is today. No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at this site now, or whether it is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
