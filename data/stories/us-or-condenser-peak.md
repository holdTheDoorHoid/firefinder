Condenser Peak rises to 2,920 feet in Polk County, Oregon, about 15 miles west-northwest of Dallas.[^1][^2] The Oregon Department of Forestry administers the site.[^1]

The sources do not agree on what stood here first. The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register and firelookout.com both describe a 24-foot enclosed wooden live-in tower built in 1948.[^1][^2] The Oregon Lookouts page instead records a 500-gallon water tank in 1950 and a three-story enclosed lookout house in 1954.[^3]

By 1969 the lookout had sat unused for several years. The Oregon Lookouts page says it was slated for dismantling after wind damage, vandalism and repair bills.[^3]

The register says the tower was removed in 1967, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed that year.[^1][^2] That date sits uneasily beside the 1969 decision on the Oregon Lookouts page, so the year it came down is uncertain.[^1][^2][^3]

The register lists Condenser Peak among its former fire lookout sites.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, and none records whether the site is open to visitors.
