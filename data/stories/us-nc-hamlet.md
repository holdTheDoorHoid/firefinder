The city of Hamlet sits in Richmond County, North Carolina.[^1] A list of North Carolina lookout towers places a lookout tower named Hamlet in the same county and gives its coordinates.[^2]

The Forest Lookouts listing for North Carolina says the U.S. Resettlement Administration erected a 100-foot (30.5 m) steel tower at Hamlet between 1936 and 1938.[^3] The same entry says the state was to take the tower over later, under a permanent lease.[^3] It cites the state's Department of Conservation and Development for those details.[^3]

The same listing names the North Carolina Division of Forest Resources as the agency for the Richmond County tower.[^3]

The National Historic Lookout Register does not list a Hamlet tower.[^4]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public may visit it.
