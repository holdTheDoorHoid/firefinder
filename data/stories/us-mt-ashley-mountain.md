Ashley Mountain is a heavily timbered peak about 19 miles west-northwest of Kalispell, in Flathead County, Montana, on Flathead National Forest land.[^1][^2]

The Fire Lookout Association says a 40-foot pole tower with a 7-by-7-foot shake cab and log living quarters went up in 1923.[^1] In 1935 a 50-foot pole L-4 tower replaced it.[^1] The association also notes a patrol point half a mile to the southwest.[^1]

The association names two lookouts: Joseph Graham in 1957 and Edward A. Walters in 1958.[^1] A published history of the Flathead National Forest names Ashley Mountain as one of the 21 lookouts manned there in the 1966 season.[^3] That history says that beginning in 1945, parts of several national forests, the Flathead among them, sharply cut back staffed lookouts and relied on routine aerial patrols instead.[^3]

The association says the tower was destroyed in 1976.[^1] A 1959 Coast and Geodetic Survey station description, quoted on a Montana lookouts page, gives the structure as a painted wooden cabin raised on four poles and 60 feet tall overall.[^2] That page lists the record as removed.[^2] The 60-foot figure is taller than the 50-foot tower in the association's notes, and the sources do not explain the difference.[^1][^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains on the peak today.[^1][^2] We found no information on whether the site is open to visitors.
