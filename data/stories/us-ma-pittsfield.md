On Tower Mountain in Berkshire County, the former-sites register lists a wooden lookout that it calls a municipal tower.[^1] The site sits at an elevation of 2,193 feet, and the register says the tower was staffed from 1930 until 1942.[^1] The site was added to the register in September 2008.[^1]

The register now files the site among former lookouts.[^1] A hiking group's account of a June outing over Tower Mountain says the mountain once had a fire tower.[^2]

No source read for this story describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout on the mountain.

Tower Mountain is one of the features listed on the Taconic Crest Trail, which runs through Pittsfield State Forest in Massachusetts.[^3] The hiking group's June walk included a Department of Conservation and Recreation interpretive coordinator, Bob Rando, and a ranger.[^2] The post does not say whether the former tower site itself is open to visitors.[^2]
