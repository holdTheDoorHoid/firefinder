The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Moraine lookout as a tower in Clark County, Wisconsin, and marks it as gone.[^1] Mapcarta places a Moraine Lookout Tower in Pine Valley, Clark County, at an elevation of 1,171 feet (357 metres).[^2] OpenStreetMap carries a point under the same name.[^3]

An FCC-registered communications tower, number 1296428, is listed as constructed and uses the address Moraine Lookout Tower, Ridge Road, Town of Pine Valley.[^4] Its listed coordinates, 44.5634, -90.6588, nearly match the Association's point, and the record gives August 24, 2015 as its construction date.[^4][^1]

We found no source that describes what remains of the lookout itself, or whether the 2015 tower replaced it.[^4] No source we read says whether the public may visit the site.[^1]
