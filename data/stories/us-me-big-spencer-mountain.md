Big Spencer Mountain sits in Piscataquis County, near the eastern shore of Moosehead Lake.[^1][^2] A 1907 issue of Forest Leaves, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, says a lookout station went up there in 1906 to watch 200,000 acres of timberland.[^1] A Daily Kennebec Journal item dated April 6, 1916, by a man who says he helped build the tower, describes the work in a way that competes with the 1906 date, so the first build date is disputed.[^1]

A 1919 report says the tower had been painted both inside and outside.[^1] By 1920 the inventory listed a 14-foot wood tower.[^1] In December 1926 crews were hauling steel and tower materials to a watchman's cabin for a new tower, and a 1927 photo inventory records a 12-foot steel tower with a cab as finished.[^1]

In April 1976 a Kennebec Journal item said Fred Chadwick of Dover-Foxcroft would man the tower, one of the few lookouts still kept.[^1] The same item said visitors could hike the trail to the top, and noted that planes were by then used a great deal for fire patrol.[^1] The Natural Resources Council of Maine says the tower was active for nearly 85 years, until 1991.[^3] Maine's network was shut down after a 1991 budget crisis.[^4]

The cab was removed in December 2009.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as standing, and its key says that mark means the structure is still there with no cab in place.[^5]

Maine Trail Finder says the tower has seen long use but is no longer staffed. Two large banks of solar panels, a communications building and a wooden platform occupy the summit, and the site asks hikers to treat the privately owned gear there with care.[^2] The mountain is part of a state ecological reserve of about 4,240 acres, open to hiking, hunting and birding.[^3][^2] The listed round trip is 4 miles. The trail has rough stretches and some erosion, with short ladders higher up.[^2]

We found no closure notice. We would not climb the tower, which has lost its cab and shares its summit with private equipment.[^5][^2] We found no notable fire tied to this tower in the sources we read.
