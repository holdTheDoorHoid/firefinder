The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Minnesota list includes a tower called Buhl in Saint Louis County, and marks it as gone.[^1] The Central US Lookouts page gives its code as 58N-19W-4, with no further detail.[^2]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Minnesota Lookouts, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mn/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Buhl, Forest Lookouts, Central US Lookouts, https://centraluslookouts.weebly.com/buhl.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
