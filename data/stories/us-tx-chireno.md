The Texas Forest Service ran a steel lookout at Chireno, in Nacogdoches County, about 4.5 miles southwest of the town.[^1] A survey station description calls it Lookout Tower No. 20 of the state forest service.[^1] The survey says it stood on four steel legs, about 110 feet high, with a glass-walled cab on top.[^1]

The build year is unclear. The table's date column gives 1934.[^3] The survey's station name ends in 1949, and the station was described that year.[^1] These sources do not say which year the tower went up, so I have not picked one. The table lists the tower at 100 feet, not the survey's 110, and I have used the survey's height.[^1][^3]

The table says the tower was removed from Chireno in 1960 and moved to Shepherd (Loc 2), about five miles southeast of Shepherd on Farm Road 225.[^3] That site's entry gives 1960 as its build year and 1988 as its removal year.[^3] The Texas lookout list marks the Chireno site as gone.[^2]

Nothing I read describes what remains at the Chireno site today, or at Shepherd. Nothing I read says whether the public can reach either one.
