Squa-Pan Mountain lies in Aroostook County, Maine.[^1] The lookout history files it under the Maine Forestry District.[^2]

The lookout history for Squa Pan says a 48-foot steel tower was partly erected in 1917, with completion planned for 1918, citing the Forest Commissioner's annual report.[^2] The same history says a 26-foot tower was completed in 1918.[^2] The planned height and the completed height do not match, and we could not check the original reports.

The 1920 inventory describes the tower as 26 feet tall and made of wood.[^2] A 1920 Maine Forestry Service panorama map is centered on the tower, which the map calls the Squaw-Pan Mt. fire tower in Township 11, Range 4.[^6] We found no staffing record for this tower in the pages we read.

The fate is unclear. The lookout history lists the tower as removed, with no date.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Maine list still shows a tower here as standing, with its cab gone.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register cannot say whether Squa Pan still stands.[^3]

The register describes a different site, east of Masardis and on private property. It may be the tower that once stood on Haystack Mountain.[^3] Its map point lies south of the point the Forest Fire Lookout Association uses, and the TopoZone point matches the association's.[^1][^3][^4]

The north end of the lake's eastern arm reaches into Township 11, Range 4, where the 1920 map places the tower.[^5][^6] Early settlers knew the lake as Squa Pan Lake, and it was renamed in 2011.[^5] TopoZone places the tower on the "Scopan Lake East" USGS topo map.[^4]

We found no public access record for the Forest Fire Lookout Association's point. The register says its own site is on private property, so do not go onto private land without permission.[^3]
