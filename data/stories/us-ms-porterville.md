The register places the Porterville Lookout Tower in Kemper County, Mississippi, and lists the Mississippi Forestry Commission as its agency.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register dates the tower to around 1937 and identifies it as an International Derrick model from that year.[^1] International Derrick towers are rare in this region, where Aermotor towers are typical.[^1] The register also describes window shades, a five-over-three layout, and a six-pane section near the top center that can be opened.[^1] OpenStreetMap tags the structure as a lattice observation tower.[^2]

We found no record of who staffed the tower or what fires it watched.[^1] The register does not say, and our searches turned up nothing more.[^1]

The register gives a registration date of July 4, 2022, and lists the forestry commission as the agency in charge.[^1] The register also describes heavy woods around the site, and says the base is nearly impossible to reach.[^1] We found no date for the last time anyone used the tower, and no record of later changes to the structure.[^1]

The register gives the elevation as 373 feet (114 meters), while OpenStreetMap lists 118 meters, so the two sources differ by a few meters.[^1][^2] We found nothing about public access or rental today.[^1] Because the Mississippi Forestry Commission manages the site, ask it before going onto the grounds.[^1]
