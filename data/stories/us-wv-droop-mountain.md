Droop Mountain Battlefield State Park, in Pocahontas County, was established on July 4, 1928.[^1] The lookout tower came later: the Clio entry on the park credits the Civilian Conservation Corps, a New Deal work program, with building it in the 1930s and with the park's first major improvements.[^2]

We did not find the year the tower was finished.

A photograph in the Pocahontas County digital archive, dated about 1975, is tagged with Camp Price and with the CCC.[^3]

A Wikipedia photo caption says the tower looks out over the Greenbrier River valley.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Droop Mountain as a standing observation tower.[^4]

We found no record of who staffed the tower or of any fire it watched, and no notice about hours or climbing rules. Check with park staff before you visit.
