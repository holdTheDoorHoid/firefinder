The Dennis Fire Tower stands on Signal Hill in the town of Dennis, on Cape Cod.[^1][^2] A state list places the Dennis Fire Observation Tower atop Signal Hill, on DCR property at 68 Hokum Rock Road.[^1]

The register dates it to 1947 and says it was the final tower built on the Cape. It is a 65-foot steel tower with a 10-by-10-foot cab, described as completing fire detection coverage on the fire-prone Cape.[^2] The Massachusetts Bureau of Forest Fire Control administers it.[^2] Henry Isenberg nominated it for the register, and the register lists the registration date as December 15, 2004.[^2]

The record of who staffed it is thin. A 2015 chapter report counted 38 staffable towers in Massachusetts, with 8 more standing, but it does not say which towers were staffed, or whether Dennis was one of them.[^3]

The most recent work I found dates from 2015. The chapter report lists a "complete tower cab replacement and rebuild" at Dennis, one of nine state towers repaired that year.[^3]

No source I found says whether the public may climb the tower or enter the cab, and none describes its condition since the 2015 rebuild. The register page gives no access details either.[^2] Check with DCR before going near it, and do not climb without permission.
