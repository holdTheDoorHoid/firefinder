Sabao Mountain, 1,119 feet high, sits in the Unorganized Territory of East Hancock, Hancock County, Maine.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site names the Maine Forestry District as the agency for the lookout.[^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower at the site and marks it gone.[^3]

No source we read describes what remains of the site now, or whether the summit is open to visitors.
