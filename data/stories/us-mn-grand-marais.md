The Grand Marais lookout was a steel tower put up in 1928 on a peninsula on the east side of the harbor, in Cook County, Minnesota.[^1][^3] It was 85 feet tall, climbed by ladder, with a square cab.[^1][^3] A social-media post about the county's 1928 towers gives the height as 80 feet instead.[^6] Sources also call it the Good Harbor tower.[^3]

In 1943 the tower was dismantled and re-erected on Eliason Knob, about six miles inland and northeast of Grand Marais, though the 1934 station description places the site about seven miles northeast.[^1][^2][^4] When the Coast and Geodetic Survey described the Eliason site in 1934, a wooden double tower 45 feet tall, put up by the Forest Service, stood there.[^2][^4]

A 1956 survey note still described a steel tower at Eliason. The Cook County list adds that it was removed, but it gives no date.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists both the harbor and Eliason sites as gone.[^5] No source describes what, if anything, remains at either site today, or whether the public can reach them.[^1][^2][^4][^5]
