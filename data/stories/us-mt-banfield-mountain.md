Banfield Mountain stands about 12 miles north-northeast of Libby, in Lincoln County, Montana, within the Kootenai National Forest. Its listed elevation is 6,100 feet.[^1]

The lookout was built in 1923. Firelookout.com describes a pole tower 48 feet tall, with a 7-by-7-foot cab and a cabin.[^1] The same listing names a patrol point three miles northwest, atop Gold Hill.[^1]

A USGS topographic map dated 1963 and published in 1966 marks a lookout tower on the summit.[^3]

The listing says the tower was destroyed in 1975.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone.[^2]

No source describes the summit today or says whether the site is open to the public.
