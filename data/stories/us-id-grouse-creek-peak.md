Grouse Creek Peak lies in Custer County, about 31 miles west-northwest of Challis, on the Challis National Forest.[^1][^3] Forest Lookouts also files it under the name Grouse Creek Point.[^3] Its land location is given as 15N-13E-23, a township, range and section code.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts gives its elevation as 9,454 feet.[^2]

Firelookout.com titles its page Grouse Creek Peak Fire Lookout Cabin.[^1]

An L-4 lookout cabin was built in 1935.[^1][^2][^3] The structure was destroyed in 1970.[^1][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts files the site as hike-in, which means visitors reach it on foot.[^2] No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
