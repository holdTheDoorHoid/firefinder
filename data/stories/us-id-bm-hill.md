BM Hill Lookout stood on the south point of BM Hill, in the Clearwater National Forest.[^1][^2] A Clearwater lookout list from the Forest Service places it about nine miles north of the Powell Ranger Station, at 6,380 feet.[^4]

The tower went up in 1932.[^1][^2][^4] It was a 40-foot pole tower of the L-4 design.[^1][^2] The sources disagree on the county. The Idaho Lookouts list gives Shoshone County, while the Forest Lookouts index files the tower under Clearwater County.[^3][^5]

The tower was destroyed in 1973.[^1][^2][^4] A 2025 post by Idaho Fire Lookouts describes melted glass at the site and judges that the site seems to have burned.[^2]

The same post describes cement footings, scattered ruins and artifacts, and part of a staircase that was still identifiable.[^2] The road to the site is gated, so the visitors walked in about half a mile and had to bushwhack through that stretch.[^2] Beyond that 2025 post, no source describes the site today, and none says whether the land is open to visitors.[^2][^4]
