The Chimney Rocks lookout in Franklin County is described in two different ways. The Former Fire Lookout Sites register calls it a bald tower, used from 1908 until about 1917.[^1] A Forest Lookouts history calls it an open observation point, set up before 1918, so the two sources disagree on whether a built tower ever stood there.[^2]

The register says the structure is no longer standing.[^1] It was entered on the register in September 2008, with Henry Isenberg as the nominator.[^1]

Today the Chimney Rocks name belongs to a hiking circuit in Michaux State Forest. The circuit joins the Appalachian Trail, the Shaffer Rock Trail and the Hermitage Trail, and one of its trailheads is on Rattlesnake Run Road.[^3] None of these sources connects the old lookout to that circuit, so no source describes what, if anything, remains of the lookout site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.[^1][^2][^3]
