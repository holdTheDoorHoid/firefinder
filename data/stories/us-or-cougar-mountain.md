Cougar Mountain, in the Siuslaw National Forest in Lincoln County, got its first cab in the early 1930s.[^1][^3] A September 1931 letter says the Forest Service planned a fireman's cabin there and wanted the material up the mountain before the heavy fall rains.[^2] In 1954 a 41-foot treated timber L-4 tower went up, and Rex's page gives its estimated cost as $4,252.[^1][^2]

Rex's page says a garage went up in 1938, and it lists the post as activated on March 18, 1942, under the Portland Filter Center.[^2] A February 1943 Oregonian story says a man walked to the lookout after a shooting and reported it to Lookout Irvin Hussey.[^2] Rex's page marks 1970 as the last year the lookout was staffed.[^2]

Rex's page says the tower came down after the 1970 fire season.[^2] The register says it has since been removed, and firelookout.com's header leaves the tower's end date open with only a question mark.[^1][^3]

None of the sources we read describes what, if anything, remains on the mountain today. The Former Fire Lookout Sites Register still lists the site.[^1]
