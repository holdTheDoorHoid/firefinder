The Brewster fire tower stands on Deer Park Road inside Nickerson State Park, on Cape Cod.[^1][^2][^3] According to the National Historic Lookout Register, the Massachusetts Bureau of Fire Control put it up in 1949.[^1] The register describes a 68-foot steel tower with a 10-by-10-foot cab.[^1]

The register gives the site an elevation of 102 feet.[^1] It registered the tower on May 14, 2008, as US 731, MA 42, with Henry Isenberg as nominator.[^1]

A state review of the Cape and Islands towers places the Brewster tower atop Deer Park Hill, yet elsewhere the same review lists Brewster and Wellfleet as towers not located on hills.[^3]

The register page lists no staffing or visit reports for Brewster.[^1] A 2015 chapter report from the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Southern New England chapter says the state's maintenance crew made repairs at nine towers that year, and that Brewster's stair platforms were replaced.[^4] Those repairs are the only later work the sources read record.

The tower now sits within the park, reached from Deer Park Road.[^2][^3] The sources read do not say whether the public may climb it or whether anyone staffs it today. Check with the park before a visit, and stay off any stairs or cab that is closed to visitors.
