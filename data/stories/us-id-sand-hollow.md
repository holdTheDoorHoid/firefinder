Sand Hollow is listed as a lookout in Payette County, Idaho.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site for Idaho marks it as private.[^1]

A community with the same name, unincorporated, lies in Payette and Canyon counties, about 10 miles (16 km) north-northwest of Caldwell.[^2]

Since the listing marks it private, visitors should not go there without the owner's permission.

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether any structure stands there.
