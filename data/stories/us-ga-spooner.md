A fire lookout tower is recorded at Spooner, in Clinch County, Georgia.[^1] A list of Georgia towers gives its coordinates as 30.97308, -82.54543, its elevation as 140 (no unit is given; read as feet, that is 42.7 m), and its GFC number as 825.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register lists 29 lookouts in Georgia, and Spooner is not among them.[^2] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists two Georgia sites, neither of them at Spooner.[^3]

None of the sources read describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether it is open to the public. Do not assume a tower stands there. Check with the landowner or the managing agency before going.
