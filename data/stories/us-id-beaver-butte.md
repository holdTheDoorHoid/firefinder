Beaver Butte is in Clearwater County, Idaho, about five miles west-southwest of the Canyon Ranger Station, at 5,040 feet.[^1] The Clearwater Timber Protective Association is listed as the agency for the site.[^3]

The site was set up in 1921, with a twin tree platform on the east point and a crow's nest on the west point.[^1][^3] In 1923 the east point gained a log cabin, and both points got platform towers.[^1][^2]

In 1934 a 50-foot pole tower with an L-4 cab was built on the east point.[^1][^2][^3] The west point is less clear.[^1][^2][^3] Firelookout.com and the Idaho Fire Lookouts page both describe a 100-foot pole L-6 tower built there in 1934.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page also lists a 1934 L-6 tower, but adds a June 1944 note about gathering poles for a 110-foot tower, which it says was erected on the west point.[^3] The sources disagree on that tower's height and on when it was built.[^1][^2][^3]

The Forest Lookouts page quotes the Clearwater TPA's combined 1961-62 annual report. As quoted there, a tower went unstaffed through the fire season, was in poor condition, and was removed that fall.[^3]

The west-point tower came down in 1957. Firelookout.com and the Idaho Fire Lookouts page say it was destroyed that year, while the Forest Lookouts page says the 110-foot tower was removed.[^1][^2][^3]

Firelookout.com and Idaho Fire Lookouts say the east-point tower was destroyed in 1962.[^1][^2] The Forest Lookouts page also puts a removal in 1962, but does not say which point that tower stood on.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts labels the site drive-up.[^2] None of the three sources describes what, if anything, remains there today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1][^2][^3]
