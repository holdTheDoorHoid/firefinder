An item dated August 4, 1960, quoted on the Forest Lookouts site from the Clinton County News, reported that Homer and Rita Kinnett had made a gift of a site for a lookout tower to the Division of Forestry.[^1] The site lay near Sewellton, close to the junction of US 127 and Kentucky Route 55, with ground elevation given as more than 1,100 feet.[^1] The plan called for foundations that autumn and erection of the tower the next spring.[^1] The item also expected the tower, together with Crocus and a second tower planned for Green River Knob, to cover Russell County well.[^1]

The article describes plans, not a finished tower.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page lists the site simply as removed, with no date or reason given.[^1]

The sources used for this record say nothing about what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.[^1]

[^1]: Sewellton - FOREST LOOKOUTS, Forest Lookouts (easternuslookouts.weebly.com), https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/sewellton.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
