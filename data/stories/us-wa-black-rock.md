The Black Rock lookout stood in Whatcom County, Washington, about 17 miles northwest of Mount Baker.[^1][^2]

It was a cab rather than a tower.[^1][^4] The register describes a 12-by-12-foot frame cab built in 1923, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as a ground-level one.[^1][^4]

The register names the Washington Division of Forestry/DNR as the administering agency.[^1] Rex's Fire Lookout Page calls it a State Division of Forestry site and gives the location code 40N-6E-3.[^3]

The site was registered on January 26, 2012, as US 1225, WA 126, on the nomination of Ray Kresek.[^1]

The register reports the cab as destroyed at some later point, but gives no date for it.[^1] firelookout.com marks the end of the site's years as unknown.[^2]

No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site now. None says whether the ground is open to visitors.
