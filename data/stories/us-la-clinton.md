A National Geodetic Survey station description from 1970 places the Clinton tower in a small cleared area about four and a half miles east of town, roughly 200 feet south of State Highway 10.[^2] The same description says the cab is grey, metal and square, with a cone-shaped roof and windows on every side. It gives the height as 110 feet, with four steel channel legs holding it up.[^2]

Wikipedia's list marks the Clinton tower as torn down.[^1] The list places it in East Feliciana Parish near the town of Clinton, and says satellite imagery still showed it as late as 2019.[^1] It adds that Google Street View captured the tower before it was decommissioned.[^1]

We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1]
