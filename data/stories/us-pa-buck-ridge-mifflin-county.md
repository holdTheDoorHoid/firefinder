The register lists a Buck Ridge Tower Site in Mifflin County, with US 230 and PA 59 as its road references.[^1] It describes a tree tower in use around 1917 that no longer stands.[^1] The entry was added on September 16, 2008, and nominated by Henry Isenberg.[^1]

A second history file places a Buck Ridge in Cumberland County.[^2] It describes a tree observation point, 35 feet above the ground, established before 1918.[^2] That is close in date and form to the Mifflin County tower, so the two records may describe the same early lookout or two different ones. The sources do not settle which.[^2]

The register also has a separate Buck Ridge Tower Site in Cumberland County.[^3] That one was a Blaw-Knox tower built in 1934 and moved from Staley Knob in the 1970s.[^3] Where the sources disagree on the county, this story follows the Mifflin County record.

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether it is open to the public.
