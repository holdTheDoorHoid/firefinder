The Sandhills lookout stood in McHenry County, North Dakota, on the J. Clark Salyer National Wildlife Refuge.[^1][^2] The refuge was first called the Lower Souris Refuge, and the Fish and Wildlife Service says it was renamed for John Clark Salyer in 1967.[^3]

The tower's build is recorded in one directory. West Lookouts dates a steel tower to 1937-38 and credits the Civilian Conservation Corps with erecting it.[^1] The same entry adds that a cabin was moved to the lookout site in September 1937 for overnight lodging, and that a telephone line was built from headquarters to the tower in December 1938.[^1]

West Lookouts also records the tower as demolished, with no date given.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as removed.[^2]

No source we checked describes the site today, so we cannot say what remains there or whether visitors can reach it.
