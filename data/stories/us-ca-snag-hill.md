Snag Hill is a lookout tower in Lassen County, California, at about 6,072 feet.[^1][^2] The Lassen-Modoc Unit of Cal Fire looks after it.[^1]

The lookout went up in 1994.[^1] The Lassen Gold Mine Company built it for Cal Fire as a replacement for Hayden Hill Lookout, nine miles to the northeast.[^1][^3] A new mining project needed the Hayden Hill site, so that older tower was torn down.[^1][^3] The register calls its design, spiral staircase included, one of a kind among Cal Fire's lookouts.[^1]

Sources disagree on staffing, and the dates explain part of it. A Peakbagging photo caption for a photo taken August 6, 2002 says the lookout was normally staffed through fire season.[^2] The register says that as of 2015 the tower was staffed when severe weather was forecast.[^1] I have used the register's later note as the current picture, but I found no recent schedule.

In July 2026 a vegetation fire, named the 3-6 Fire, burned about five acres in the Dixie Valley area, near the lookout.[^4] Officials sent aircraft to drop retardant, and ground crews joined in.[^4]

The tower seems to be still in place, since a July 2026 news report places a fire near it.[^4] The register added it on October 20, 2019, as US 1324, CA 147.[^1]

I found no recent note on road access or visiting. Peakbagging puts the site in the Lassen National Forest on one page and in the Plumas National Forest on another.[^2][^3] A CAL FIRE lookout list, in an undated copy I found online, says the agency does not rent its lookouts.[^5] Check with Cal Fire's Lassen-Modoc Unit before planning a trip.
