The Tracy City Lookout Site is in Grundy County, Tennessee, at 2,125 feet.[^1] The register says Grundy County administers the site.[^1]

Two other lists give the name Hobbs Hill to a Grundy County tower and mark it as removed.[^2][^3] Tennessee Landforms gives its elevation as 2,132 feet, close to the register's figure.[^3] A local history describes Hobbs' Hill as a suburb of Tracy City.[^4] No source we read says outright that the two names belong to the same tower.

A historical marker in Tracy City names Civilian Conservation Corps Company 1475 and its Camp S-67.[^5] It says six block-like concrete structures anchored a fire tower that the company erected there in the mid-1930s.[^5] The marker's coordinates differ from the register's, so it may describe a different tower.[^1][^5]

The register's 2006 visit report calls the tower long forgotten and says it was in extreme disrepair. The reporter thought it stood about 60 feet tall and was probably a Carnegie Steel tower.[^1]

Both lists that name Hobbs Hill mark the tower as removed, but neither gives a date.[^2][^3] The 2006 visit is the most recent one we found.[^1]

The 2006 reporter thought the tower might be on private property, with a mobile home about 100 yards away.[^1] We found no source that describes the site since then, so we cannot say what, if anything, remains there.[^1] Tennessee Landforms says most former tower locations are now private property, and it advises against climbing, because steps are often removed or rotten.[^3]
