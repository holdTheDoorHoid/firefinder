Glebe Mountain rises in Windham County, Vermont, on land that covers parts of both Londonderry and Windham.[^1][^2] The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists an open tower on the mountain and marks it as gone.[^1]

In 2022 a VTDigger report quoted a statement that the land would be open for public use once it joined a federal conservation refuge.[^3] The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service would oversee the property, and access to it is expected to improve.[^3] Statewide, Northern Woodlands reports that Vermont was the first state to close all of its towers, and that its last working tower went out of service after summer 1985.[^4]

No source describes what, if anything, remains of the tower, or whether the land is open to visitors now.
