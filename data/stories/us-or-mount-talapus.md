Mount Talapus sits in Multnomah County, Oregon, on the Mount Hood National Forest, about 18 miles north-northwest of Mount Hood.[^1][^2] The U.S. Forest Service administers the site.[^1]

A lookout cabin went up on the site in 1933.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com describes the cabin as abandoned.[^2]

Panorama photos for this lookout are dated June 25, 1934.[^3][^1] The Forest Lookouts page credits Robert Cooper with them, and the register's copy is courtesy of Tammy McLeod.[^3][^1] The date falls within 1933 to 1935, when the Forest Service in Oregon and Washington ran a project to collect panoramas from every fire lookout in the region.[^4] No source we read links these photos to that project; the connection rests on dates alone.

The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists the site as US 1767, OR 508, registered on October 14, 2019, on nomination by Tammy McLeod.[^1]

No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether visitors may go there.[^1][^2][^3]
