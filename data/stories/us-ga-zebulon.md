The Georgia lookout tower list kept at tnlandforms.us includes an entry for Zebulon, in Pike County. It gives the position as 33.08805, -84.33278 and an elevation of 925. The list gives no unit; if the figure is feet, that is about 282 m.[^1] Zebulon is the county seat of Pike County, and the city and the county both take their names from the explorer Zebulon Pike.[^2]

The list was last revised in March 2016, so it may not show later changes.[^1]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Georgia lookout towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/gatowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: Zebulon, Georgia, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zebulon,_Georgia (accessed 2026-10-21).
