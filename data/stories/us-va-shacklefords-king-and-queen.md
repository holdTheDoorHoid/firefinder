Shacklefords is a community in King and Queen County, Virginia. It is not incorporated as a municipality, and it takes its name from the Shackleford family.[^1]

By March 1936 the tower was staffed. A note dated March 16 that year describes a new posting in the Shacklesford fire tower.[^2] The note is credited to a Civilian Conservation Corps company at West Point, Virginia, and the page that reprints it also names the Virginia Department of Forestry.[^2]

A 1949 edition of a map of the area, reproduced by Pastmaps, labels the Shacklefords Lookout Tower as a prominent landmark.[^3]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the tower as gone, and its entry carries no register number.[^4]

No source I found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
