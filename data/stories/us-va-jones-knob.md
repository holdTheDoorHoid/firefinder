Jones Knob is a former fire lookout site in Wythe County, Virginia, at 3,818 feet (1,164 meters).[^1][^2] The register listed it on November 13, 2010, as US 726, VA 6, on the nomination of Dan East.[^1] A firelookout.org county list gives Grayson County instead, so the sources disagree.[^3]

The register says the U.S. Forest Service administered the tower, an 81-foot tower of treated timber built in 1939.[^1] It was used to spot fires near Cripple Creek and covered the southern part of Wythe County.[^1]

The register dates the tower's removal to 1973, and the Forest Lookouts site marks it as removed in that year.[^1][^2]

The register says 18-foot-3-inch concrete footers and a small cistern still remain, and the cistern sits at the exact middle of the four footers.[^1] The register does not say whether the public can reach the site.[^1] The Forest Lookouts entry gives no further detail.[^2]
