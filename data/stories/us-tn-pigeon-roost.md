Pigeon Roost is a fire lookout tower in Humphreys County, Tennessee.[^1] Tennessee Landforms gives its elevation as 716 feet and its state forest tower number as 6-21, from a 1976 list that the same page describes.[^1] The page marks the tower as standing.[^1]

We did not find any source that says when the tower was built, who built it, or who has staffed it. We also found no news story, photograph or access notice about it in the sources we read.

The sources disagree about whether it still stands. The National Register form that covers the Tennessee Division of Forestry's fire towers, from 1933 to 1975, lists the towers still standing on March 26, 2014.[^2] Pigeon Roost is not among them, and the form does not explain why.[^2]

The National Historic Lookout Register's Tennessee page, which counts 76 registered lookouts, does not name Pigeon Roost.[^3] Its present condition and whether the public may visit are both unknown. Please ask the landowner before going to look at it.
