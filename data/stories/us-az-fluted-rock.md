Fluted Rock Lookout sits in Apache County, Arizona, inside the Navajo Indian Reservation. It overlooks a region that shares its name.[^1]

The National Historic Lookout Register says the tower was constructed in the early 1930s. It is a 41-foot MC-39 Aermotor tower with a 7-by-7-foot metal cab.[^1]

A USGS topographic map that portrays the area in 1955, published in 1958, labels Fluted Rock Lookout.[^2] Pastmaps' description of that map calls the lookout and Red Butte key landmarks for orientation on the plateau.[^2] The map gives no build date, but its label suggests the tower already stood by 1955.[^2]

The Register names the Navajo Indian Reservation as administrator, with the Bureau of Indian Affairs as cooperator. It describes the tower as in service but gives no staffing dates. I found no source that names anyone who worked the lookout or describes a fire it reported.[^1]

The Register recorded the lookout on July 20, 2002, as US 437, AZ 33.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Arizona list shows it as standing.[^3]

No source I read says whether visitors may reach or climb the tower. Questions about a visit belong with the administrator the Register names, and I could not confirm what that process involves.[^1]
