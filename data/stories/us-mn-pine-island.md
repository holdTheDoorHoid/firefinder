The Pine Island lookout was erected in 1937 at the Pine Island forestry station in Koochiching County.[^1][^2] The county report lists it as lookout number 89 and describes a 100-foot Aermotor stairway tower.[^1]

The two DNR pages name different towers as the model for its design. The webcam page says it was made identical to an earlier tower on the same site.[^2] The virtual tour page instead names a tower that stood near campground site 1 at Big Bog, and says the two matched in year and style.[^3]

The DNR says the tower came from Pine Island State Forest, and the county report says it was moved to Big Bog in 2011.[^3][^1] The DNR page says it was renovated, reconstructed and opened to the public that same year.[^3]

Today the tower stands in the south unit of Big Bog State Recreation Area, in Beltrami County, east of Upper Red Lake.[^4] The south unit also has the visitor center, which runs Monday to Saturday, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m., and visitors can climb the tower during those hours.[^4] A DNR webcam at the top shows Upper Red Lake live.[^2]

No source we read describes what remains at the Pine Island site today.
