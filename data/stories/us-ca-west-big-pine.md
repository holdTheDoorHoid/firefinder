West Big Pine Mountain is in Santa Barbara County, inside the Los Padres National Forest.[^3] The register dates the lookout to 1934, when a California Region 5 plan BC-301 cabin went up on an open timber tower 10 feet tall.[^1] In World War II a residence cabin for the Aircraft Warning System was added, so the tower could be manned round the clock.[^1]

The register lists the lookout as a former site and says the removal date is unknown.[^1]

A hiker who wrote about a walk up the peak in April 2018 found only a couple of footings and some scattered metal from the lookout.[^2] He thought the higher peak to the east was the one properly called West Big Pine Mountain, and called the lookout's own peak West Big Pine Lookout.[^2] Partway up, he noted an old road cut just above him, and thought a road may once have led up to the lookout's peak.[^2] The sources I read give no current access rules for the lookout site.[^1][^2][^3]
