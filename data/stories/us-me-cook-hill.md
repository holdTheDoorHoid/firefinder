Cook Hill, in Vassalboro in Kennebec County, Maine, once held a fire lookout tower.[^1] The register lists it as US 101, ME 27 and added the site on September 15, 2008, after Henry Isenberg nominated it.[^1]

The index places the site at 44.48906 north and 69.65118 west.[^2] Maine's staffed fire tower network ended after a budget crisis in 1991, and the state's Maine Atlas page says some of its towers were later dismantled or collapsed on their own.[^3] Other towers statewide were restored and opened to the public, the same page adds.[^3]

The register says the tower was disassembled in 2000 and placed in storage.[^1] The index lists the Cook Hill site as gone.[^2] No source I found describes what, if anything, remains on the hill today.
