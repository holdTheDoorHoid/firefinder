Rocky Knob, in Laurel County, was once the site of a state forestry tower.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register dates the tower to the 1930s and lists the Kentucky Division of Forestry as a cooperator.[^1] The site sits at 1,528 feet, or 466 meters.[^1]

The register says the tower was removed in the 1980s.[^1]

Today the site holds several communications towers and maintenance buildings.[^1] The register reports that no forestry tower remains appear to be left on the site.[^1] London City and Laurel County Communications administers the site.[^1] The sources we read do not describe public access, so anyone planning a visit should ask the administrator first.

The site was added to the register on June 9, 2017, on the nomination of Robert Wittenback.[^1]

[^1]: Rocky Knob Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/ky/rocky-knob-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
