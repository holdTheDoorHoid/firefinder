The Earle Lookout Tower was one of the South Carolina Forestry Commission's fire towers, in Williamsburg County.[^1] A Pastmaps summary of a 2011 USGS topographic map of the Trio area places the tower near Earle and calls it a landmark.[^2]

The state's tower network began in 1930 with a tower near Camden.[^3] When a smoke was spotted, two towers each recorded its direction, and the fire lay where the readings crossed on a map.[^3]

The commission's own history dates the closing of its fire tower system to October 1, 1993.[^3] A Post and Courier history dates the system's retirement to 1994, so the two accounts differ.[^4] The Forest Lookouts site lists Earle as removed, with no date.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, and none says whether the land is open to visitors.
