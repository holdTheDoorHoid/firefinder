Quartzite Butte, in the Nez Perce National Forest, was a frame cupola cabin built in 1930.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The Forest Service's Nez Perce list calls the cab an L-4 cab instead, so the sources describe its type differently.[^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists Ted Oliver and Ron Norwood as known staff, under a heading that reads 1929.[^2] That heading falls a year before the 1930 build date, and the listing does not pair the names with years, so the staffing dates are unclear.

The cab was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1][^4] The Forest Service list gives abandonment as its fate too.[^3] Firelookout.com and the Forest Fire Lookout Association both list the site as gone, and the association marks it as a ground site.[^1][^5]

No source describes what remains at the butte today, or whether the site can be reached.
