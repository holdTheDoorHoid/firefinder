Five Points is classed as an unincorporated community in Washington County, Florida.[^1] It lies beside State Road 280, north of Vernon.[^1]

The Forest Lookouts website lists a Five Points tower in Washington County, under its Florida list.[^2]

A report covering 2015 and early 2016 from the Florida chapter of the Forest Fire Lookout Association describes members trying to work out how many of 347 sites on an online listing are still standing. It notes that some of those sites are already gone.[^3]

None of the sources checked describes what remains at the Five Points site today, or whether the public can visit it.
