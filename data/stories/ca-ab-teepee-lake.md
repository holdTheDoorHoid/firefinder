Teepee Lake is a steel fire tower in northern Alberta, 30 m (98 ft) tall and standing at 782 m (2,566 ft) above sea level.[^1] OpenStreetMap, the volunteer-built map, records the same 30 m height and a metal construction.[^2]

Alberta's register dates the present tower to 2011 and lists it as active.[^1] The same entry flags its location as not verified and records no weather station.[^1]

The register lists the access mode as "fly".[^1] No source describes the site today beyond that entry, or whether the public may visit.

Across the province, Alberta counts 127 wildfire lookout sites, each with an observer on duty, usually April through October.[^3] Treat this tower as a working lookout rather than a place to visit.
