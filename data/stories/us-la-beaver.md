Beaver Lookout Tower once stood in Evangeline Parish, and the Forest Lookouts site names the Louisiana Forestry Commission as its agency.[^2] In 1915 Louisiana directed funds to fire protection work, which allowed lookout watchmen to be hired.[^1] Before the first two towers, rungs had been nailed to a pine tree, with a perch built high up on it.[^1] The first steel fire tower in the state went up near Bogalusa in 1922, and a second tower followed near Urania in 1923.[^1] Thirty-nine fire towers had gone up by 1943, and fifty-six by 1950.[^1]

Wikipedia's list marks the tower as torn down.[^1] The Forest Lookouts site records it as removed.[^2]

The list's compilers were unable to check the site on Street View or in older aerial photos.[^1] No source describes what, if anything, remains there, or whether the site is open to the public.
