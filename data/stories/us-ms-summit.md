OpenStreetMap maps a Summit Lookout Tower as a lattice tower tagged as an observation tower.[^1] The town of Summit, in Pike County, shares the name.[^2]

Neither of Mississippi's lookout registers lists a Summit lookout. The National Historic Lookout Register counts 65 lookouts in the state, and the former lookout sites register counts three.[^3][^4]

No source describes the site today, so it is not known whether a tower stands there, or whether the site is open to visitors.
