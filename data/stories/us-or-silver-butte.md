Silver Butte stands about 23 miles southeast of Ukiah in Grant County, Oregon.[^2] A lookout went up there in the 1930s.[^1]

The sources disagree on the tower's height. The register describes a 30-foot pole tower with a 14-by-14-foot L-4 cab, and firelookout.com gives the same 30-foot height with an L-4 cab.[^1][^2] A 1935 newspaper item, quoted by a lookout history site, says an ERA crew working in the forest was wrapping up a 14-by-14-foot house and a 40-foot lookout tower on the peak.[^3] That same crew had earlier run seven miles of telephone wire up to the summit.[^3] The register and firelookout.com place the site in the Umatilla National Forest.[^1][^2] The history site calls it the Whitman National Forest.[^3]

The tower fell down in the mid-1960s, according to the register and firelookout.com.[^1][^2] The register also says a later tower, dated 1980, was removed about 2005.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Oregon county list marks the tower as gone.[^4] No source we read describes what, if anything, remains at the site, or whether the public can reach it.
