Mount Tuscarora stands in Allegany State Park, in Coldspring, Cattaraugus County, near Quaker Lake.[^1][^2]

The state built the tower in 1928. Wikipedia credits the Conservation Department's Division of State Parks with putting up a 60-foot Aermotor LS40 steel tower that year.[^1] A historical marker in the park repeats that a tower went up on Mount Tuscarora in 1928, along with another at Rice Brook.[^3]

In 1931, jurisdiction over many park towers moved to Forest Fire Control. Allegany State Park kept the Mount Tuscarora tower, however, and staffed it whenever fire danger was high.[^1]

We found no record of a particular fire seen from the tower.

Wikipedia says the tower is still standing but needs someone to adopt and restore it.[^1] The Allegany State Park article says that when fire lookouts were dropped in 1971 for air surveillance, park staff took down only one tower, the Mount Irvine tower, during the early 1970s. It names no other tower taken down, Mount Tuscarora's included.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association index lists the tower as standing.[^4] We found no recent condition report and no clear current statement on whether visitors may climb it. Do not climb it without checking with the park first.
