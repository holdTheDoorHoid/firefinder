The Bowery site lies in Custer County, Idaho, inside the Sawtooth National Forest, about 20 miles north-northwest of Ketchum.[^1][^2] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 10,332 feet.[^1] Forest Lookouts lists its location as 8N-16E-25, which means township 8 north, range 16 east, section 25.[^2]

A tent cabin went up on the site in 1934.[^2][^3] Firelookout.com calls the same 1934 structure a camp.[^1] Forest Lookouts files the place as Bowery Guard Station, a name the other two sources do not use.[^2] All three sources agree on the 1934 date.[^1][^2][^3]

Sources say the site was abandoned in the 1940s.[^1][^2][^3]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^3] No source describes what, if anything, remains there today, or whether the site is open to visitors.
