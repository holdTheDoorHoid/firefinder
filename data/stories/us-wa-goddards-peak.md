Goddards Peak lies in Stevens County, about nine miles northeast of Chewelah, on Colville National Forest land.[^1][^4] Its lookout was a crow's nest, recorded on the summit in 1941.[^1][^2]

Pastmaps, describing a 1964 USGS topo map, places the peak at the meeting point of the Kaniksu and Colville forest lines.[^4]

Firelookout.com and the Forest Fire Lookout Association both list the crow's nest as abandoned.[^1][^3] Willhite's page credits the Kresek inventory with the same verdict.[^2]

Willhite lists the lookout site at 5,548 feet, and the page gives no hiking distance yet.[^2] A 1964 USGS map on the same page marks a trail climbing to the summit.[^2]

No source I read describes the summit today, what remains there, or whether visitors can reach it.
