Romance is a lookout tower site in Ozark County, Missouri.[^1] The Central US Lookouts page lists the Missouri Conservation Commission as its agency.[^1]

The commission's annual report for fiscal 1951-52 records that it obtained a towersite.[^1] Its report for fiscal 1954-55 records the erection of a new tower at Romance, in Ozark County.[^1]

In August 1974 a fire broke out near the Romance towersite, close to West Plains. According to The Chillicothe Constitution-Tribune, a snake climbing a fence post short-circuited an electric fence and set off the blaze. The paper judged the cause possibly the strangest ever recorded for a fire.[^1]

No source we could read describes the site today, so we cannot say whether the tower still stands or whether the public can visit.
