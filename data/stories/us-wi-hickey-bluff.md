The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists a tower called Hickey Bluff in Adams County, Wisconsin, at 43.82138 north and 89.70978 west, and marks it as gone.[^1][^2]

A 2015 WXPR report dates the state's fire towers to the 1930s and 1940s, and says 119 were staffed statewide at the peak.[^3]

In December 2015 the Department of Natural Resources announced that it would stop using the fire towers it had spread across the state.[^3][^4] The cost of replacing them and newer technology were factors in the decision.[^3] The year before, the DNR had 60 of the towers staffed.[^3]

Many of the towers sit on private property under easements, and in such cases the owners could decide to take them over.[^4]

The Hickey Bluff listing gives only a location and a status, so no source we read describes what is at the site today or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2] Check who owns the land before going to any old coordinates.
