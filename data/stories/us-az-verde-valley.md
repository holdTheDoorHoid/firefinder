Verde Valley was a ground lookout in Yavapai County, managed by the Prescott National Forest.[^1][^2] In April 1931, William T. Anderson was set to start as a lookout in the Verde district, at a post between Jerome and Clarkdale.[^2] In 1933, because of rain, a Prescott forest supervisor pulled three of the five lookouts from duty, leaving the Verde valley lookout still on duty.[^2]

In 1938 the Forest Service completed a cabin for a fire lookout beside the highway between Clarkdale and Jerome.[^2] A utility corporation gave the ground, and a lookout was kept there from April 1 to July 31.[^2]

By August 1949 the Verde station was the only unstaffed one.[^2] In April 1950, A.R. Cannell, the Verde station's lookout, noticed a blaze along the road below Jerome. Cannell and three men went to the blaze and put it out.[^2]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the Verde Valley lookout as gone.[^1] The two sources checked do not describe what, if anything, remains at the site today or whether the public can visit it.[^1][^2]
