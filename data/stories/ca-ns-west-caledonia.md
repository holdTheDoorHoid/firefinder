West Caledonia was one of the seasonal fire lookout towers that Nova Scotia's Department of Natural Resources ran in Queens, Nova Scotia.[^1][^2]

An unofficial list of the province's towers, drawn from names that scanner listeners reported as used on the radio and last updated in September 2009, places it near Menchan Lake, about 3 km east of Kejimkujik Park, under the "DNR Milton" entry.[^1] The list says it is likely that some of the towers it lists are now decommissioned, so this entry may be out of date.[^1]

The list also says the department operates 33 seasonal towers across the province, and that in the past there were many more.[^1]

The department later decommissioned its lookout towers in 2015 and then dismantled them, CBC News reports, in favour of aerial surveillance.[^3] The department's statement said most wildfire detection now happens from the air or through members of the public phoning 911 or its provincial wildfire centre.[^3] Randall Freeman, who staffed a tower in northern Queens, Nova Scotia, for 18 years, argued that a few helicopters searching the province for smoke could not match the coverage the towers gave.[^3] The article treats the towers as one group and does not name West Caledonia.[^3]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors. Do not assume a tower still stands there.
