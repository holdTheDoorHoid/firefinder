Van Peak sits in Lake County, Montana, inside the Flathead National Forest.[^2][^3] A Coast and Geodetic Survey description written in 1940 places the Forest Service lookout house on a high, bare peak, about seven and a half miles north-northeast of Condon Ranger Station.[^2] The Forest Lookouts page gives the location as township 22 North, range 16 West, section 6.[^2]

Firelookout.com dates an L-4 cab on the peak to 1939.[^1] The Montana list files the site under "Ground" rather than as a tower, and gives its coordinates as 47.69152 north and 113.70085 west.[^3]

The Montana list now marks Van Peak as gone.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, and none says whether the peak is open to the public.[^1][^2][^3]
