A tower named Butler appears on the Tennessee Land Forms list of lookout towers. The list places it in Madison County, gives its elevation as 597 feet, and marks it standing.[^1]

We found no build date, builder, or staffing history for it in the sources we read. The National Register document on the state's Division of Forestry fire towers includes a list of towers still standing as of March 26, 2014, and that list does not name a Butler tower.[^2]

The two sources disagree. The state list says the tower is standing, but the 2014 list does not include it. We could not tell whether the 2014 list missed it, or whether the tower is known by another name.[^1][^2]

There is also a Butler in Tennessee, an unincorporated community in Johnson County that sits on the north side of Watauga Lake.[^3] We found no link between that community and this tower, so the county in our record needs checking against a primary source.

The sources we read do not say whether this tower can be seen, reached or climbed. Ask the landowner or the Tennessee Division of Forestry before going near it.
