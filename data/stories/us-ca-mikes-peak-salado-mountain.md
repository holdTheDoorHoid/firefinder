Mike's Peak, also called Salado Mountain, is a lookout site in Stanislaus County, California, in the Coast Range.[^1]

In June 1939 a crew was building a telephone line to the new fire lookout on the peak, the Modesto Bee reported.[^1] The paper located Mike's Peak near the Kent Ordway place, once the Oak Flat Ranch, in Solado Canton.[^1] It said the district fire warden, O. S. Ball, was overseeing the work.[^1]

In May 1943 the Stanislaus County board of supervisors signed an agreement with the U.S. Forest Service to keep an aircraft warning service and a fire lookout on the peak.[^1] Under the agreement the county would pay the operating costs, and a federal agency would reimburse it later.[^1] The tower lookout would act as a fire spotter between June and September.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists the site as type Ground and status Gone.[^2] Peakbagging lists it under its LO code, which covers a building that has been torn down, possibly leaving no trace, or a site whose status is unclear.[^3]

No source we read describes what, if anything, is left on the peak today, and we found nothing on whether the public can reach it. Check with the landowner or land manager before going there.
