Serpentine - 1 was a fire lookout on the Siskiyou National Forest, about 17 miles west-southwest of Grants Pass in Josephine County, Oregon.[^3] The Oregon Lookouts directory files it in its Josephine County index as Serpentine Mountain - 1.[^2]

Firelookout.com dates a 40-foot (12 m) pole L-4 tower on the north summit of the peak to 1935.[^3] The directory logs panoramic photographs dated October 13, 1938.[^1]

In 1962 the lookout was discontinued, and its duties moved to a replacement tower about a mile south of it.[^1] Firelookout.com puts that replacement on the south summit, as a 41-foot (12.5 m) R-6 tower of treated timber.[^3]

The directory marks the site as removed.[^1] Firelookout.com describes the site as ruins.[^3] No source says what those ruins are, or whether the spot is open to visitors.
