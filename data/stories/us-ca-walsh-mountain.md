Walsh Mountain, also known as Walsh Hill, is a former fire lookout tower in Yuba County, California.[^1][^2] A Forest Lookouts page for the site pairs the county with the Department of Defense and carries one dated entry, 1951.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's California list gives the coordinates as 39.16992 north and 121.30411 west, and marks the tower as gone.[^2]

No source describes the site today. The sources do not say what, if anything, remains on the hill, or whether the site is open to visitors.

[^1]: Walsh Mountain - Forest Lookouts (western US), Forest Lookouts, https://californialookouts.weebly.com/walsh-mountain.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: California Lookouts (By County), Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/ca/ca-co/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
