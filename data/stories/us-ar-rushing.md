Rushing is a settlement in the Ozark hills of Stone County, sitting in Turkey Creek Township on Highway 9.[^3]

The lookout here is a steel tower on four legs, topped with an observation cabin.[^1] The record gives its height as 100 feet.[^1] A Coast and Geodetic Survey description written in 1954 places it in the settlement itself, about 0.1 mile east of State Highway 9.[^1]

The central lookouts site lists the Arkansas Forestry Commission as the tower's agency.[^1] Act 234 of 1931 created that commission, which also put up many towers.[^2] Statewide, the peak years for towers ran from the 1930s to the 1970s, when Arkansas had more than 120 of them.[^2]

No source we found describes what stands at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
