Sidwalter Mountain Lookout, which other sources call Sidwalter Butte, stands on the Warm Springs Indian Reservation in Wasco County, Oregon. The Confederated Tribes of Warm Springs administer it.[^1][^2]

The build year is disputed. The Register says the tower was constructed in 1967 and describes it as a 107-foot Aermotor tower with a metal cab.[^1] Firelookout.com and a 2026 Oregon list both give 1965, and firelookout.com describes a 90-foot steel tower with a 7-by-7-foot cab.[^2][^3] The sources do not explain the gap, so both figures are reported as they appear.

Firelookout.com says the tower was manned, and the July 2026 Oregon list marks it as staffed.[^2][^3] The sources read do not say who worked there or which fires it watched.

A 1987 renovation refreshed the walls, ceilings and paneling, added a new window and steps, and remodeled the living quarters.[^4] The Register lists June 18, 2008 as the registration date, under the number US 739, OR 111.[^1]

The tower is still standing.[^5] The Register says the reservation section where it stands is closed to public access, and the Oregon list marks the site as not accessible to the public.[^1][^3]
