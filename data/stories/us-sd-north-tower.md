A South Dakota lookout list gives a tower called North Tower, in Todd County, at 43.22049, -101.01552, and marks it gone.[^1] Wikipedia places all of Todd County inside the Rosebud Indian Reservation.[^2] Because the county is on the reservation, check access before going; no source gives an access rule for this site. No source describes what remains at the site now.

[^2]: Todd County, South Dakota, Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todd_County,_South_Dakota (accessed 2026-10-21).
