The Weatherly lookout tower stands in Hickman County, Tennessee, at an elevation of about 963 feet.[^1] OpenStreetMap gives the tower point an elevation of 292 meters, close to that figure.[^3]

We did not find when the Weatherly tower was built, who built it, or who has staffed it. The sources we read do not describe any fire it was part of, or any move, sale, or rental.

A National Register document about Tennessee Division of Forestry fire towers includes Weatherly in its list of extant Tennessee fire towers as of March 26, 2014.[^2] The same document also names two earlier wooden towers in Hickman County, Moore's Switch and Cobel, and says both were later swapped for steel towers in the 1940s or 1950s.[^2] It does not say whether Weatherly was connected to either one.[^2]

Tennessee Landforms lists the tower as standing, but the listing has no date of its own; the page carries a revision stamp dated 8 January 2026.[^1] We found nothing on its current condition or public access. Please check with the Division of Forestry or the landowner before visiting, and do not climb it without permission.
