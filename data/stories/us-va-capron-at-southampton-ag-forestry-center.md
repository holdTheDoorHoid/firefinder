The Capron lookout stood in Southampton County, Virginia.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's county list shows a Capron tower at 36.66536, -77.22998, marked as gone.[^1] A separate entry for the Southampton Ag & Forestry Center describes a tower relocated from Capron, which the list shows as standing.[^1][^2] The list gives no date for the move, no reason for it, and does not say who moved the tower.[^1][^2]

We could not find a build date, a design, or the name of the agency or people who ran the Capron tower in the sources we could read. The two listed points are about 16 km apart, but the list does not say how far the tower was moved.[^1][^2]

We have no information about public access at the Ag & Forestry Center. Ask the center before you visit, and do not climb the tower without its permission.

[^2]: Virginia - Forest Fire Lookout Association, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
