Kootenai Point is a lookout site on the Kaniksu National Forest in Idaho, at 5,003 feet (1,525 m).[^1]

Idaho Fire Lookouts lists Dick Maloney as staff in 1935, citing a Bonners Ferry Herald item from August 8 of that year.[^1] In 1936 the same paper reported that Howard Monks, Jr. had finished a season of work as a forestry lookout on Kootenai Point; the site dates that Herald report to September 3, 1936.[^1]

The site's editors say the lookout was unknown to them and that they could find no records of it. They did find a 1936 newspaper clipping that mentions it.[^1] The listing tags the site as hike-in and abandoned.[^1] Nothing in this source describes what, if anything, remains on the summit today, or whether the public can reach it. Check the current notices from the managing forest before any trip.

[^1]: Kootenai Point, Idaho Fire Lookouts, https://www.idahofirelookouts.com/northern-idaho/kootenai-point/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
