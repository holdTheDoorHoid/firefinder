The Barronette lookout stood in Washburn County, Wisconsin, at 45.67394, -91.98040, according to the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list.[^1] That list spells the name Barronette.[^1] A lookout history site spells it Barronett,[^2] which is also the name of a town in Washburn County with 461 residents at the 2020 census.[^3] The listed point sits close to Wikipedia's coordinates for that town.[^1][^3]

The site's Wisconsin index pairs the lookout with the Wisconsin Conservation Department and the land-survey code 37N-13W-21.[^4] The lookout history site dates the steel tower, with its wood cab, to 1934.[^2] The 72-foot figure it gives converts to about 22 metres.

The association's list now marks the tower as gone.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.
