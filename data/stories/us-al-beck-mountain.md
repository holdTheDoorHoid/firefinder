Beck Mountain is in Pike County, Alabama, and the site is listed as administered by the Alabama Forestry Commission.[^1] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register added it on September 18, 2019, after a nomination by Andrew Zerbe.[^1]

The register says nothing of the tower survives.[^1] No source I found describes the summit today beyond that note.[^1]

The register puts the site at 31 degrees 56.1 minutes north and 88 degrees 3.7 minutes west, with an elevation of 38 feet.[^1] A map listing for a Pike County summit with a similar name, Becks Mountain, gives a longitude of 86 degrees west and an elevation of about 673 feet.[^2] If the listing describes the same hill, the two longitudes differ by about two degrees, so the exact spot is uncertain.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Beck Mountain Fire Tower Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/al/beck-mountain-fire-tower-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Becks Mountain in Pike County AL (Ansley Area), MountainZone, https://www.mountainzone.com/mountains/alabama/pike-al/summits/becks-mountain/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
