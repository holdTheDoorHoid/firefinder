A statewide list of Georgia lookout towers has one entry named "butler," in Taylor County.[^1] The name is the same as the county seat, Butler.[^2] The list gives the tower an elevation of 660 feet (201 m) and a set of coordinates. It also tags the entry with two codes, "BLL-41101" and "gfc-326."[^1]

The Forest Lookouts website's Georgia index also lists an entry named Butler for Taylor County.[^3]

The National Historic Lookout Register says 29 lookouts are registered in Georgia, and none of its names is Butler.[^4] The Former Fire Lookout Sites register lists two former Georgia sites, Lafayette and Yonah Mountain, and neither is named Butler.[^5]

No source we found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
