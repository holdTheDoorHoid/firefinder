Red Top was a lookout in the Kaniksu National Forest, about 12 miles southwest of Porthill in Boundary County, Idaho.[^1] Its tower was a 50-foot pole structure with an L-4 cab, and both site histories date its construction to 1938.[^1][^2]

The known staff list runs from Lloyd Hydorn in 1932 and 1933 to Fred Brackebusch in 1963. Names in between include Alvin Flory (1936) and Loren Pitt (1958 and 1959).[^1] Idaho Fire Lookouts credits Ed Long with staffing it in 1957/58.[^2] The list's first two names, from 1932 and 1936, come before the 1938 build date, so the sources do not fully agree on the timeline.[^1][^2]

The sources disagree on when the tower was abandoned. Idaho Fire Lookouts gives 1963.[^2] The firelookout.com page dates the end to 1964, the year work finished on a replacement lookout on Shorty Peak.[^1] Today the Shorty Peak lookout is a rentable cabin run by the Forest Service.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Red Top as a tower that is gone.[^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists the site as hike-in.[^2] No source we read describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public may go there.[^1]
