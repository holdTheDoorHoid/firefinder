The Georgia tower list places a Fletcher Branch Lookout Tower in Long County, at 31.77354 N, 81.81761 W. Its elevation is listed as 71 with no unit; read as feet, that is 22 m.[^1] The list tags the entry gfc-130. The list's map was last revised in March 2016, so its details are more than a decade old.[^1]

No source found describes this tower's build or use. The Georgia Forestry Commission's own history puts the agency's first fire observation tower on the Ware Hotel in Waycross, and ties that tower, tentatively, to a 1928 forestry fair in the same town.[^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains of this tower at the site today.

[^2]: History of the Georgia Forestry Commission, Georgia Forestry Commission, https://gatrees.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/History-of-the-Georgia-Forestry-Commission.pdf (accessed 2026-10-21).
