The Canada Lookouts index lists a British Columbia record called Baldy N.T., in the Okanagan-Similkameen district. It gives the elevation as 2,310, with no unit stated.[^1]

The index also has two pages titled Mount Baldy.[^2][^3] The 1925 Forest Branch report quoted on one says a programme of lookout construction was undertaken that provided for three standard lookouts, and that a primary unit was built that year on Mount Baldy, near Penticton.[^2] A 1926 Forest Service annual report describes a fresh permanent lookout put up on Mount Baldy to cover the Inkaneep and Kettle River Forests.[^3] A 1927 item in the Greenwood Ledge names Wm. Lakeland as running the Baldy lookout, though it does not say which Baldy is meant.[^3]

The second Mount Baldy page is headed Thompson - Nicola, so the index does not clearly say which mountain these reports describe. It is not certain that these reports and the Baldy N.T. record describe the same tower.[^1][^2][^3]

No source I read describes the site today or says whether anything still stands on the mountain.
