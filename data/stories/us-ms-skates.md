The Skates Lookout Tower is in Hinds County, Mississippi, at about 339 feet (103 meters) above sea level.[^1] The National Historic Lookout Register describes it as a 100-foot Aermotor with a straight internal staircase.[^1] The Register lists the Mississippi Forestry Commission as the agency that administered it.[^1]

We found no build date and no builder. The Register gives the build details as unknown, and our two web searches turned up nothing more about its construction.[^1] We also found no record of who staffed the tower or of any fire it watched.

The National Historic Lookout Register added the tower on July 10, 2022, as US 1657 (MS 23), on the nomination of Jason Johns.[^1] The entry is based on a site visit dated July 9, 2022, when every flight of the staircase was still intact.[^1]

The same entry says the Forestry Commission had vacated the site and that it had become extremely overgrown.[^1] It does not say when the agency left. We could not find a later report, so we cannot confirm the tower's condition today. The 2022 visit is the only evidence of its condition, and it is over four years old.[^1]

The Register page does not describe public access.[^1] Anyone interested should check with the landowner or the Forestry Commission first, and should not climb a tower without permission.

[^1]: Skates Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register, http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ms/skates-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
