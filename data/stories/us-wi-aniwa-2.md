The Aniwa tower stood in Marathon County, Wisconsin.[^1] The Forest Lookouts list that records this site gives only the county and a land survey code, 30N-10E-35.[^1] The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Wisconsin list has an Aniwa tower in Marathon County at 45.03104, -89.26429, and marks it as gone.[^2] An OpenStreetMap point called Aniwa Lookout Tower sits at nearly the association's coordinates.[^3]

The sources below describe the state's fire-tower program rather than this tower. A December 2015 WXPR report says the towers then in service were built in the 1930s and 1940s, and that the DNR had announced an end to the fire towers it used to spot uncontrolled fires.[^4] A 2019 Wisconsin chapter report counted 10 towers sold and removed and 56 left to dismantle across the state.[^5] Neither report names Aniwa, so they do not show what happened to this tower.[^4][^5]

No source describes what remains at the site today or whether the public can visit it.
