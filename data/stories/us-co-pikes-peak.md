Pikes Peak, which stands 14,115 feet high, is an ultra-prominent fourteener on the Front Range.[^1] Of the fourteeners in the United States, it sits farthest east, and it lies within Pike National Forest.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Colorado list gives a platform at Pike's Peak, in El Paso County, at 38.84034 north and 105.04375 west, and marks it as removed.[^2] A Forest Lookouts page for the peak names Pike National Forest alongside El Paso County.[^3]

No source describes what, if anything, remains of the platform. The summit is open to visitors.[^1] A toll road reaches it, the cog railway resumed seven-day service on May 27, 2021, and the most popular hiking route, Barr Trail, starts in Manitou Springs.[^1]
