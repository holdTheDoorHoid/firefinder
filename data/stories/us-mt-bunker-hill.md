Bunker Hill is a former fire tower on the Kootenai National Forest, in Lincoln County, Montana, about 10 miles east-southeast of Yaak.[^1] Firelookout.com gives its elevation as 6,516 feet.[^1]

The page gives only the decade, the 1930s, for a pole L-4 tower at the south end, below the summit.[^1] Firelookout.com marks the tower as gone.[^1]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists Bunker Hill among Lincoln County's lookout structures and sites. Its list calls the type a tower and the status gone, with the site at 48.8049, -115.50523.[^2] Both pages describe the tower as gone, so the two sources agree on its status.[^1][^2]

No source describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
