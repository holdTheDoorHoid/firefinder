A lookout-history site lists a lookout at Hobucken, in Pamlico County, under the North Carolina Forest Service.[^1]

The entry cites the state's Department of Conservation and Development and its 20th biennial report of 1964. It dates the new tower to 1962 through 1964 and describes it as a 120-foot steel tower built on a site that had only recently been acquired.[^1]

A tower index on tnlandforms.us gives the lookout the number NCT209 and the state number AE 5936.[^2]

No source I could read describes what, if anything, remains at the Hobucken site today, or whether it is open to visitors.[^1][^2]

[^1]: North Carolina - Forest Lookouts, easternuslookouts.weebly.com, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/north-carolina.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: North Carolina Towers, tnlandforms.us, https://tnlandforms.us/nctowers.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
