The Green Mountain lookout stood at the summit of Green Mountain in Stevens County, at about 5,071 feet.[^3] In July 1938 a large block of timberland, previously administered by the Resettlement Administration, passed to the newly formed Kaniksu National Forest, and the site now sits in the Colville National Forest.[^4][^2]

Three sources date the 30-foot pole tower with its L-4 cab to 1938.[^1][^2][^3] A 1934 newspaper item, though, describes a crew still finishing work at the lookout tower, and Civilian Conservation Corps men ran a telephone line up to the station that September.[^4] WillhiteWeb says the 1938 date is unclear in light of those reports.[^3]

The lookout man moved in during mid-July 1938.[^3][^4] The register says the tower was removed in 1954. WillhiteWeb says it was toppled that year, and firelookout.com says it was destroyed.[^1][^3][^2]

Ruins remain on the summit, according to the register, and WillhiteWeb's photos show footings there.[^1][^3] The views from the summit are limited.[^3] WillhiteWeb describes the way up as a route from Highway 20 along South Fork Mill Creek Road and then on motorcycle trails, roughly two miles one way with about 1,000 feet of climbing.[^3]
