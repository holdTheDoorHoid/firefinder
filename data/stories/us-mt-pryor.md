Pryor Lookout is on the Crow Indian Reservation in Big Horn County, about 36 miles south of Billings.[^2] The Register gives the Crow Tribe as its location and the Crow Agency office of the Bureau of Indian Affairs as its administrator.[^1] It dates the lookout only to the 1930s, and its description section reads, in full, "No information known at this time."[^1] The Forest Lookouts site lists it under Big Horn County and the Crow Indian Agency and adds little else.[^3]

The Register accepted the lookout on April 12, 2022, on the nomination of Kyle Stetler.[^1] The sources I read give no staffing, condition or public access details, so its present state is not documented here. Because the site lies on the Crow Indian Reservation,[^2] check with the Crow Tribe before any visit.
