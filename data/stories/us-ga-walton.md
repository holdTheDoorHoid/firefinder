A Georgia tower list records a Walton Lookout Tower in Dooly County at 32.06045, -83.66600, and gives its elevation as 451, with no unit stated.[^1] A map site that lists the same OpenStreetMap node gives 453 feet (138 metres), two more than the list's figure.[^3] That site also lists the hamlet of Lamb Town about three and a half miles southwest of the tower.[^3] OpenStreetMap tags the structure as an observation tower.[^2] The list's Google map was last revised on March 22, 2016.[^1]

No source I found describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
