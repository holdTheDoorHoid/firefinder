Jasper Mountain was a fire lookout in the Kaniksu National Forest, about 12 miles north of Priest River in Bonner County, Idaho, at an elevation of 3,873 feet.[^1]

Around 1932 a 50-foot pole tower went up on Jasper Mountain, with a log cabin at ground level for living quarters.[^1][^2][^3] The sources disagree on the design. Two of them give L-6, while Idaho Fire Lookouts gives L4.[^1][^3][^2] A 30-foot patrol platform stood about a mile north.[^1][^3]

In August 1936 a Newport Miner item, as quoted on Rex's Fire Lookout Page, reported about 3½ miles of road built from Quartz Creek up to the lookout in two weeks.[^3]

The tower did not last. Both firelookout.com and Rex's page say it was destroyed in the 1960s.[^1][^3] Idaho Fire Lookouts lists it as destroyed too.[^2] Rex's page also says it was removed, and the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks it as gone.[^3][^4]

The most recent account we found is a trip report dated July 6, 2022.[^2] The road had a gate across it, so the writer left the car there and walked in on foot.[^2] The writer described the area as heavily logged.[^2] The writer could still trace the road bed around the mound where the tower had been, but found no footings, cable or trash pit.[^2] The writer saw no sign of the tower itself.[^2] We found no account of the site after that visit, and none that says whether the gate is open to visitors now.
