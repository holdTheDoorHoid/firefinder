Longshoal was a lookout tower in Carroll County, Virginia. The Forest Fire Lookout Association lists it as a tower at 36.87143, -80.71913, and marks it gone.[^1]

USGS topographic maps of the Hillsville area name it.[^2] The late-1960s survey, published in 1971, has Hillsville at its southwest corner. It lists the Longshoal Lookout Tower among several high points that rise above the creek valleys.[^2]

A 2010 Hillsville map, also described on Pastmaps, names the Longshoal Lookout Tower too. That description says the tower is standing and is a conspicuous landmark on the northern ridge.[^3] This conflicts with the Association's gone entry. The 2010 description does not date any structure, so the two disagree and this story does not settle it. Both map pages are USGS topographic sheets, one published in 1971 and one in 2010.[^2][^3]

No source describes the site today.

[^1]: Virginia - Forest Fire Lookout Association, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/va/ (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: 1968 Map of Hillsville, VA, Pastmaps, https://pastmaps.com/map/hillsville-carroll-county-va-usgs-topo-1968-p1971 (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^3]: 2010 Map of Hillsville, VA, Pastmaps, https://pastmaps.com/map/hillsville-carroll-county-va-us-topo-2010 (accessed 2026-10-21).
