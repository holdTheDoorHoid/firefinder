Jewel is a fire tower in Liberty County, which is part of Florida's Big Bend region.[^1][^2] The county is the least populous in the state, with 7,974 people at the 2020 census.[^1] A Florida fire-tower list, drawn from USGS name records and 1:100,000 topographic maps, names the Jewel Fire Tower in Liberty County.[^2] The Forest Lookouts site has a page for it, but that page gives only the name and the county.[^3]

Across the state, Florida's forestry agency had 22 lookout towers and nine crow's nests by 1932.[^4] The agency's history says these were pole lookouts 45 to 70 feet tall, staffed part-time by cooperators and Forest Service crews.[^4]

None of the sources checked describes what remains at the site today, or whether the public can reach it.
