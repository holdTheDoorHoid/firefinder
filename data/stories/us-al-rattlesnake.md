A Forest Lookouts web page lists a Rattlesnake lookout tower in Cleburne County, Alabama, under the Talladega National Forest.[^1] The earliest dated record we found is a 1941 station description by the Coast and Geodetic Survey.[^2] It describes a four-legged steel structure about 125 feet (38 m) tall, with a winding stairway up the center to a glass-enclosed lookout at the top.[^2]

The survey station is the finial on top of the tower, at about 33.767 north and 85.619 west.[^2] The datasheet's history lists only that 1941 observation.[^2]

We found no source that gives a build date, a design name, staffing, or the tower's later history. No source describes the site today, whether the tower still stands, or whether the public can visit it.

[^1]: Rattlesnake, Forest Lookouts, https://easternuslookouts.weebly.com/rattlesnake.html (accessed 2026-10-21).
[^2]: DG3603 Rattlesnake Lot, National Geodetic Survey datasheet, https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/ds_mark.prl?PidBox=DG3603 (accessed 2026-10-21).
