A lookout on Sheep Mountain, an L-4 cab put up in 1929, stood about 7 miles west of Superior, Montana.[^1] The firelookout.com entry dates its abandonment to 1946 and its destruction to 1952, and gives its elevation as 6,723 feet.[^1] The page heading gives its dates as 1929 to 1952.[^1] The entry places it in the Lolo National Forest.[^1] The two sources disagree on the county. The firelookout.com entry says Mineral County, while the Forest Fire Lookout Association's Montana list puts the coordinates 47.18064, -115.05476 in Missoula County.[^1][^2] The same list has other lookouts called Sheep Mountain, so the name alone does not settle which one is meant.[^2]

No source I could reach describes what, if anything, remains at the site today, or whether the land is open to visitors.

[^1]: Sheep Mtn. Fire Lookout Cabin, firelookout.com, https://www.firelookout.com/mt/sheepmtn381.html (accessed 2026-10-10).
[^2]: Montana fire lookouts list, Forest Fire Lookout Association, https://firelookout.org/lookouts/us/mt/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
