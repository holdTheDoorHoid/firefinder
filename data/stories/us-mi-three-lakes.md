The Forest Lookouts site has a Three Lakes page headed Baraga County, but no entries appear beneath the heading.[^1]

A map database lists a Three Lakes Lookout Tower in Spurr Township, at about 46.547 degrees north and 88.168 degrees west. Its entry is classed only as a tower, so it does not show whether a fire lookout stood there.[^2]

Three Lakes is an unincorporated community in Spurr Township, and it had a population of 167 at the 2020 census. The name refers to the community's position close to Beaufort, George and Ruth lakes.[^3]

No source found describes what, if anything, remains at the site today.
