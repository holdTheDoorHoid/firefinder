The Mount Desert fire tower stood in Kanawha County, West Virginia, in the vicinity of Putney.[^1][^2] The register lists the site at 1,650 feet (503 metres) and names the State of West Virginia as the administering body.[^1] It was added to the register in September 2008, nominated by Henry Isenberg.[^1] The Forest Lookouts page also names the West Virginia Division of Forestry.[^2]

The earliest dated mention I found is from December 1927, when a hiking club announced a Christmas-holiday visit that would include two nights at the tower. The same notice said a cabin had been built at the tower.[^2]

A March 1970 newspaper reported that a Department of Natural Resources observer had died of a heart attack on duty at the tower.[^2]

The register says the tower has been removed, and the Firelookout.org list marks it as gone.[^1][^3] Neither source gives a removal date. No source I read describes what, if anything, remains at the site now, or whether the public can reach it.
