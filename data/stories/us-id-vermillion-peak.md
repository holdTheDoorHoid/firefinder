Vermillion Peak Lookout stood on Vermillion Peak in the Nez Perce National Forest, about 16 miles northeast of Elk City, Idaho.[^1][^2] The lookout was a log cabin with a cupola, built in 1924.[^1][^3] The Idaho listing gives the peak's elevation as 7,575 feet.[^2]

The firelookout.com page says the cabin was no longer in use by the late 1930s, and the Idaho listing dates the abandonment to the 1930s.[^1][^2] Both say it may have been replaced by a lookout on Disgrace Butte, two miles southwest.[^1][^2] The Disgrace Butte listing describes that tower as a 1935 pole tower, gone by 1963, and says it may have replaced Vermillion Peak.[^4]

The Forest Fire Lookout Association's Idaho list marks the Vermillion Peak site as gone.[^5] The Idaho listing calls the site a hike-in.[^2] We found no source that describes what, if anything, remains at the peak today, or whether the site is open to the public.
