The Hazelwood Fire Tower stood near Thunder Bay, Ontario.[^1] The Firelookout.ca index gives its address as Thunder Bay, Unorganized.[^2] A hiking guide written after a 2010 visit gives directions: drive about 10 km along Hazelwood Drive, then turn into the driveway on the right, shortly before a powerline. Its writer noted that it was not really a hike, because you could drive right up to the tower.[^1]

The tower is gone. Firelookout.ca records it as removed in 2011.[^2] The hiking guide's writer says it had been taken down after their 2010 visit.[^1]

A reader's comment on the same hiking page reports that four cement anchors, where the tower once stood, are all that is left.[^1] The commenter adds that the anchors were dug up and that the whole area was bulldozed.[^1] Apart from that comment, no source describes the site as it is now. With the tower gone, there is no structure left to climb.[^2]

[^1]: Hazelwood Fire Tower, Superior Hiking, https://superiorhiking.com/hazelwood-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-20).
[^2]: Hazelwood Fire Tower, Firelookout.ca Open Canada Fire Lookout Index, https://firelookout.ca/lookouts/?slug=hazelwood-fire-tower (accessed 2026-10-20).
