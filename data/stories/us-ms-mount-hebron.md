The Mount Hebron Lookout Tower is in Monroe County, Mississippi, at an elevation of 457 feet (139 m), according to the National Historic Lookout Register.[^1] The Mississippi Forestry Commission is listed as its administrator.[^1]

The register describes a 90-foot Aermotor tower with a parallel staircase. It has 13 landings and 14 flights, but its lowest flights have been taken away.[^1] The site description also notes that the Aermotor label was fitted upside down.[^1]

The register lists the build date as unknown, and I found no other account of when the tower went up or who built it.[^1]

The register entry is US 1680, MS 40. It was registered on July 18, 2022, with Jason Johns as nominator.[^1] The site visit it describes took place on July 17, 2022, and the page says the Forestry Commission no longer uses the site.[^1] I could not tell from the page when that changed.

I found no later report of the tower's condition or of public access. Since I could not find access details, do not assume the tower is open to visitors. Ask the Mississippi Forestry Commission before going, and do not climb it without permission.

[^1]: Mount Hebron Lookout Tower, National Historic Lookout Register (nhlr.org), http://nhlr.org/lookouts/us/ms/mount-hebron-lookout-tower/ (accessed 2026-10-09).
