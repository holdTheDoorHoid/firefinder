The Atlanta Lookout Tower, also known as the Atlanta East Fire Tower, stood in Montmorency County, Michigan.[^1][^3] Forest Lookouts' Atlanta entry lists a steel tower erected in 1921, citing the Department of Conservation's 1922 biennial report.[^2] The entry gives no height for that tower, and we could not confirm that it is this one.[^2]

A 1937 item from the Lake County Star, quoted on the same page, describes a 60-foot fire tower roughly 10 miles north of Atlanta that was being replaced by a 100-foot tower that CCC crews were erecting.[^2] The item does not name this site, so we have not linked it to this tower.

Michigan Fire Towers lists the tower as removed and down, but gives no date for its removal.[^1] OpenStreetMap records the tower with an elevation of 309 metres.[^3] We found no source that describes what, if anything, is left at the site, or whether visitors can reach it.
