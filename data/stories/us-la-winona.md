The Winona Lookout Tower is in Winn Parish, Louisiana.[^1] In 1915 Louisiana set aside money for fire protection, enabling it to hire people to staff lookouts as watchmen or patrol officers.[^3] The state's tally of fire towers stood at fifty-six by 1950.[^3]

A 1949 USGS map of the Jonesboro area, as described by Pastmaps, names the Winona Lookout Tower as a landmark, alongside a school and a church.[^2]

A January 22, 1957 item from The Monroe News-Star, quoted on the Forest Lookouts page, mentions the "Winona tower" in Winn Parish while comparing rainfall for 1955 and 1956.[^1]

No source we read says whether the tower still stands or whether visitors can reach the site.
