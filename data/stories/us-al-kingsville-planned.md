According to the Former Fire Lookout Sites register, the state took ownership of this Lamar County site in 1949, intending to put a fire lookout on it.[^1] No lookout was ever built there, and the register dates the state's sale of the land to 1998.[^1] The register now lists a private landowner as administering the site, and gives its elevation as 567 feet (173 metres).[^1]

The site was registered on November 27, 2016, and Andrew Zerbe nominated it. The register's photo, credited to him, is captioned as the site of a planned lookout.[^1]

No source describes what, if anything, remains on the site today. The register describes no visitor access, so anyone curious about it should ask the owner before going on the land.

[^1]: Kingsville Planned Lookout Site, Former Fire Lookout Sites Register, http://firetower.org/lookouts/us/al/kingsville-planned-lookout-site/ (accessed 2026-10-10).
