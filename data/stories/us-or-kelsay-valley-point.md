Kelsay Valley Point is a small point about three miles northwest of Red Cinder Butte, in Douglas County, Oregon.[^1][^2] It sits in the Umpqua National Forest, 34 miles southeast of Oakridge.[^2]

In August 1933 the Roseburg News-Review reported that a road crew would build access to a station then going up at a point the paper spelled Kelsey Point. The Forest Service planned to keep a main lookout there.[^3] The station was built that year, with a 14-by-14 L-4 cab on a 30-foot round timber tower.[^1][^3]

The lookout came down in 1961.[^1][^2][^3] The register and the Oregon lookouts page say it was removed, while firelookout.com says it was destroyed.[^1][^2][^3]

The register has a photograph captioned 1941, but no source we read describes what remains on the point today or whether it can be reached.[^1]
